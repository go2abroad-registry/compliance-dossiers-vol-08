Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – Colombian national tax and customs authority; main taxpayer portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; electronic services platform: https://muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned S.A.S. must first appoint a legal representative resident in Colombia and register the company with the local Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio). The representative then applies to DIAN for the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and requests inclusion in the RUT (Registro Único Tributario). For foreign shareholders, an apostilled and officially translated certificate of incorporation of the parent entity is required. The RUT application may be processed online via Muisca after biometric verification; the NIT is permanent and must be quoted on all invoices, import/export declarations, and tax filings.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned S.A.S. must first appoint a legal representative resident in Colombia and register the company with the local Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio). The representative then applies to DIAN for the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and requests inclusion in the RUT (Registro Único Tributario). For foreign shareholders, an apostilled and officially translated certificate of incorporation of the parent entity is required. The RUT application may be processed online via Muisca after biometric verification; the NIT is permanent and must be quoted on all invoices, import/export declarations, and tax filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Not available. Colombia does not have check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity elections. An S.A.S. is automatically treated as a Colombian tax resident separate juridical person subject to entity-level corporate income tax, VAT, and CREE. No pass-through status is available. A limited tax consolidation regime exists only for qualifying Colombian business groups and requires strict statutory ownership thresholds and DIAN authorization; it is not a routine election for ordinary S.A.S. project logistics companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold for incorporated entities. An S.A.S. engaged in project cargo logistics must register in the RUT and obtain Responsible de IVA status before issuing invoices or performing taxable operations, regardless of revenue. Natural persons may use simplified regimes up to current UVT thresholds, but corporations are unconditionally subject to general VAT registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Colombia qualify for 0% VAT and the right to refund input VAT, provided the export is evidenced through a DIAN-approved customs export declaration (DEX) and the goods leave Colombian customs territory. Certain offshore services rendered to non-residents and services physically performed abroad may also be zero-rated if the recipient is outside Colombia and the benefit is consumed abroad. The taxpayer must be a registered exporter under the RUT and must file the DEX through the DIAN/VUCE customs system.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX / Declaración de Exportación) accepted by DIAN customs system
  • Commercial export invoice bearing IRU/IVA wording and buyer NIT or foreign tax ID
  • Original or electronic Bill of Lading, House Bill of Lading, or multimodal transport document evidencing shipment out of Colombia
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Colombian authorised foreign-exchange intermediary
  • Proof of foreign currency repatriation via Colombia's foreign exchange market (market-rate wire or Letter of Credit settlement evidence)

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta) through DIAN-authorised software or DIAN web services. Each invoice must be transmitted in XML format to DIAN for real-time validation before delivery to the buyer; approved invoices receive the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) or CUNE code, QR stamp, and digital validation. Export invoices must use the special export invoice format. DIAN API integration, invoice numbering control, and reporting under the 'Factura Electrónica' and 'RADIAN' document systems are mandatory for all VAT-responsible taxpayers.

Export zero-rating is strictly audited by DIAN and the National Customs Authority. If documentary or FX repatriation requirements are not met, DIAN will retroactively claw back the 0% rate, assess the full 19% VAT with daily interest, and impose penalties that may range from 100% to 200% of the omitted tax. False export declarations, forged bills of lading, or fictitious export transactions further trigger criminal customs and documentary fraud liability under Colombian Criminal Code and Decree 1165/2019, as well as loss of VAT input credits. Taxpayers must retain the full export evidence bundle for at least five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a managed-float regime under Law 9 of 1991 and Decree 1735 of 1993. Current-account flows (trade in goods, cross-border logistics services, profit remittances) are freely convertible, but every USD or EUR receipt and payment must be flowed through an authorized Intermediario del Mercado Cambiario (IMC); the Colombian Peso is the only legal tender for domestic settlement. Capital-account operations are convertible subject to prior registration with Banco de la República (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales) and mandatory Declaración de Cambio filings. Dividends and profits of the S.A.S. may be remitted abroad without separate FX authorization, provided the applicable 7.5% non-resident dividend withholding is satisfied. Practical hurdle for OOG project-cargo treasury: all hard-currency liquidity must enter and exit through the IMC banking circuit; offshore clearing or non-IMC digital wallets used as primary rails would violate the canalización obligation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For service exports (including out-of-gauge heavy-haul logistics), 100% of the proceeds must be channeled through the Mercado Cambiario within six (6) months from completion of the underlying service, per Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83. Mandatory surrender ratio is 100%: each USD/EUR receipt must either be sold to an IMC into COP or be credited to a Banco de la República-registered USD Compensation Account (Cuenta de Compensación); no partial foreign-retention or offshore holding is permitted. Advance payments and pre-milestone deposits are channeled immediately upon receipt. Non-compliance triggers DIAN penalty proceedings, loss of export-tax incentives, blockage of subsequent FX registrations, and enhanced SARLAFT review by the bank.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Colombia) – the sole monetary and foreign-exchange authority, administering the Mercado Cambiario (official foreign exchange market) under Decree 1735 of 1993, Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83 (as amended) and Resolución Externa 1 of 2018. Enforcement is shared with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (prudential and SARLAFT supervision) and the DIAN (customs, tax and FX violation penalties).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as ancillary collection wallet. Legal constraints: offshore non-bank electronic money institutions (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, DLocal) are not authorized Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario (IMC) and cannot receive or disburse OOG logistics trade proceeds without breaching the mandatory canalización rules of Decree 1735/1993. Permitted scope is limited to (a) incidental receipts of small deposits from foreign sub-contractors, (b) local peso e-wallet services through Colombian SEDPES licensees (Ley 1735 de 2014), and (c) intra-group disbursements that never enter the Colombian FX market; all USD otherwise received abroad must still be repatriated through the designated Colombian IMC account within six months.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Statutory deadlines are set annually by DIAN Resolution; for calendar-year taxpayers the commercial return is generally due between 8 April and 19 April of the following year, according to the last two digits of the taxpayer’s NIT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory transfer-pricing documentation (Local File, Master File and anual informativo form) is required when consolidated gross income in the previous fiscal year exceeds 135,000 UVT (COP 6,353,775,000 at the 2024 UVT of COP 47,065) or net assets exceed 60,000 UVT (COP 2,823,900,000), and cross-border transactions with related parties exceed 45,000 UVT (COP 2,117,925,000).

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not operate a discrete Economic Substance Regulations regime like the Gulf jurisdictions, but the Tax Administration applies a general anti-avoidance doctrine requiring the S.A.S. to demonstrate real and substantive premises, payroll, management, and logistical operations in Colombia; artificial shell invoicing arrangements may be recharacterized and lose the S.A.S. limited-liability shield.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Código Penal, Art. 434B; Estatuto Tributario Art. 771-2)

Statutory sanction: Civil liability: default assessment plus interest and a fine of 100%–150% of the tax evaded. Criminal liability for tax evasion above approximately 5,000 UVT (≈ COP 235 million): imprisonment of 48–108 months.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false return or authorizes the fictitious records may be jointly and severally liable for the tax debt; personal fines, travel bans, seizure of assets, and prison terms may be imposed; the corporate veil will be pierced.
Artificial Shell Company / Abuse of S.A.S. Legal Personality

Statutory sanction: Under the S.A.S. statute and DIAN general anti-avoidance powers, the transactions may be recharacterized; the entity may lose its limited-liability privilege, the tax base may be adjusted, and penalties of 20%–160% of the additional tax may be levied.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow shareholders may be held personally liable for corporate tax obligations if the company’s separate personality is used fraudulently; they may be disqualified from serving as legal representatives in Colombia and may be barred from holding commercial licenses.
AML / UBO Reporting Red Flags (Law 526/1999, Decree 1517/2014 and SIC Precepts)

Statutory sanction: Fines imposed by UIAF and SIC may reach 20,000 SMMLV (≈ COP 26 billion), cancellation of the commercial registry registration, and criminal prosecution for laundering of assets (6–15 years imprisonment).

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for failures in the AML/SOFIA system and UBO registry obligations; they may suffer personal fines, suspension from managing companies, prohibition from contracting with the State, and criminal sanctions.
