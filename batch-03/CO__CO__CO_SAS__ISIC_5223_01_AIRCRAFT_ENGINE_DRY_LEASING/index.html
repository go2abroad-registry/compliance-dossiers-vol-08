Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio del domicilio social (e.g., Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá) operating through the Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES), with supervision by Superintendencia de Sociedades.

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 (S.A.S. regime), Articles 1, 4, 5 and 17; Colombian Commercial Code (Decree 410 of 1971), Articles 110, 353-372; Decree 1074 of 2015 (Single Regulatory Decree for Commerce); Superintendencia de Sociedades interpretive circulars.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (Private Incorporation Instrument) with registered bylaws (estatutos sociales), formalized in the Mercantile Register (Registro Mercantil) via the RUES.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 22 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-in capital required for a Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada; capital may be subscribed with payment deferred up to two years from incorporation; no escrow account is statutorily required. For aircraft engine leasing, subscribed capital should reflect asset acquisition requirements; cash or in-kind contributions (including the leased engines) are permissible.

Funding & unlock path: Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos fire-safety inspection certificate and annual renewal for the registered office; municipal planning verification of land use compatibility (Certificado de Uso del Suelo); structural load inspection may be required by the Chamber of Commerce if the registered office is in a commercial tower; mandatory workplace health and safety program (SG-SST) registration with the selected ARL under Decree 1072 de 2015; emergency and disaster response plan coordination with the municipal risk management council per Ley 1523 de 2012; compliance with the workplace capacity and occupational hazard notification requirements of Decree 1443 de 2014.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Unidad Administrativa Especial de Aeronáutica Civil (UAEAC) — applicable exclusively to the lessee's operational approvals and to any engine lease registration under RAC regulations; the S.A.S. lessor, holding ownership without operational control, requires no aeronautical operating license. No manufacturing or industrial permit applies to a pure dry leasing activity.

Category: Not applicable — dry operating leasing of turbofan aircraft engines is a purely commercial lease activity in Colombia. Financial leasing with purchase option is a supervised activity of Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia; an operating lease structured through a non-financial S.A.S. does not require a financial authorization, banking license, or industrial manufacturing permit.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

DIAN – Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (National Directorate of Taxes and Customs), portal: www.dian.gov.co; operational systems: Muisca (registration and filing) and Factura Electrónica (e-invoicing validation).

E-government filing portal

A Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) must first be incorporated before a Notary and registered with the Cámara de Comercio (Chamber of Commerce) to obtain the certificado de existencia y representación legal. The legal representative must then apply through the DIAN Muisca portal for the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) and the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which is the sole statutory tax ID. A non-resident foreign entity without a Colombian permanent establishment may obtain a simplified RUT/NIT via a locally appointed fiscal representative with a notarized power of attorney, solely for VAT withholding, e-invoicing, and import/export reporting obligations. The RUT is valid for all tax purposes, must be updated within five business days of any change, and must be accompanied by the registration of the DIAN-approved electronic invoicing software solution.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Colombian tax law; the S.A.S. is by default a Colombian tax resident entity subject to entity-level taxation on worldwide income. No hybrid mismatch election is available; however, the taxpayer may elect to apply the permanent establishment attribution rules for a branch operation, may opt into the special dividend distribution treatment under Estatuto Tributario Articles 242 and 245, and may elect to treat cross-border engine leases as export services under Article 481 E.T. where the statutory export conditions are met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Colombia has no general economic turnover threshold for VAT registration; any person, including a foreign entity with a permanent establishment, deriving taxable income from the sale of goods or rendering of services in Colombian territory must register in the RUT and declare IVA from the first transaction. The simplified regime (Régimen Simplificado) applies only to natural persons with gross income not exceeding 3,500 UVT (approximately COP 161 million for 2024), and is unavailable to an S.A.S., which is always in the Régimen Común. For cross-border engine dry leasing, VAT nexus arises only if the engine or its use is physically located in Colombia or if the lessee is a Colombian resident; otherwise the lease is treated as an export service and zero-rated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The dry lease (arrendamiento seco) of aircraft engines rendered by a Colombian S.A.S. to a foreign airline qualifies for the 0% VAT export rate under Article 481 E.T. and Decree 1625 of 2016, Section 1.3.1.19.1, provided that: (i) the lessee has no domicile in Colombia; (ii) the engine is exclusively located and exploited outside Colombian territory throughout the lease term; (iii) the service is technically and commercially linked to the international air transport business; and (iv) the lease payments are received through Colombian foreign exchange channels. Income from such lease is also treated as foreign-source income, reinforcing the zero-rating; any partial use of the engine in Colombian territory disqualifies the export treatment and reclassifies the lease as a taxable IIA subject to 19% VAT.

  • Formulario 510 – Declaración de Exportación (DIAN customs export declaration) duly validated with export exit stamp or electronic authorization
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical removal of the aircraft engine from Colombian customs territory
  • Certificado de Cámara de Comercio evidencing the S.A.S. corporate existence and legal representation
  • Master Lease Agreement / Engine Dry Lease Agreement with the foreign air carrier, stating the engine serial number and foreign place of use
  • Commercial export invoice (Factura Electrónica de Venta) with 0% IVA rate and valid CUFE/CUDE code
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Compensación de Divisas) issued by an authorized Colombian foreign exchange intermediary evidencing the repatriation of lease proceeds
  • Non-residence certificate or domicile certificate of the foreign lessee (when required by DIAN)

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta) is in force for all S.A.S. entities. Invoices must be generated via DIAN-certified software and transmitted in real time to DIAN's SIE (Servicios Informáticos Electrónicos) platform using an XML digital format, a digital signature certificate issued by a DIAN-authorized certification authority, and a unique validation code (CUFE/CUDE). DIAN performs pre-issuance validation; no invoice is legally valid without digital clearance. Additionally, the aircraft engine export invoice must be cross-referenced with the Formulario 510 export declaration in the VUCE/VUPE customs system.

DIAN may retroactively reassess the 0% rate within five years from the filing deadline of the related IVA return, or up to ten years where no e-invoice or RUT registration exists. Upon reassessment, the taxpayer must pay the 19% VAT understatement plus daily late-payment interest (current commercial rate plus 50%) and a penalty ranging from 100% of the understated tax for inaccuracy to 200% for willful misrepresentation or fraud under Article 640 E.T. The zero-rating is automatically clawed back if the lease proceeds are not repatriated within the 180-day term or if the engine is physically used in Colombia; criminal sanctions under Law 1943 of 2018 may also apply in cases of falsified customs declarations, forged bills of lading, or fictitious lease contracts, including asset forfeiture and imprisonment of the legal representative.
