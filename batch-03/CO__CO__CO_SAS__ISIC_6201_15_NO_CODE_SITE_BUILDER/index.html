UBO transparency, Apostille authentication chain, official name clearance restricted wordlist, and pre-incorporation statutory fee budget.

UBO Ultimate Beneficial Owner Screening Rules Official Registry Only

>=5% ownership / control

Applicable AML statute: Ley 526 de 1999 (UIAF creation); Ley 1121 de 2006 (AML amendments); Ley 1258 de 2008 (SAS statute); Código de Comercio Decree 410/1971 as compiled in Decree 1074 de 2015; Superintendencia de Sociedades Circular Básica Jurídica (SAGRILAFT chapter, as amended); Decreto 830 de 2021 (UIAF and PEP regime); Resolución DIAN No. 000164 de 2021 establishing the Registro de Beneficiarios Finales (RBF). A newly incorporated SAS must file its RBF declaration with DIAN within two (2) months after registration and update it annually during the official filing window.
Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible for a fully digital SaaS exporter provided the virtual-office provider is an established commercial co-working or registered-address firm able to accept service of process, Chamber notifications, and DIAN correspondence at the registered address. Each Cámara de Comercio may require the physical address to be verifiable and not a PO Box. The private incorporation document must state the domicile city and full address. Local bank onboarding may request a service contract and monthly invoices from the provider, so a contractual relationship with a reputable provider is strongly recommended. Overall acceptance threshold is high for low-risk, export-only ISIC 6201 activities.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá (Registro Mercantil) and Superintendencia de Sociedades (vigilance only for large SAS; optional for small forms)

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), Código de Comercio (Decreto 410 de 1971) Arts. 98-136, and Decree 1074/2015 Arts. 1.1.1.1-1.2.2.11

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (executed before notary when not all paid-in assets are cash) and Registro del Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 24 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital for S.A.S.; capital may be subscribed partially, but at least one share must be issued and payment terms set. No escrow account is required unless a specific business requires a special regime (not applicable here).

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate (Concepto Técnico de Bomberos) required for commercial office units with occupancy capacity exceeding 50 persons or load factor > 4 people per 10 m²; the company must maintain a basic workplace safety program (SG-SST) under Law 1562/2012, including training records for the legal representative and team. No structural load inspection is required for a software office housed in a commercially approved building, but the lease contract must include a certificate of habitability (certificado de habitabilidad).

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital for S.A.S.; capital may be subscribed partially, but at least one share must be issued and payment terms set. No escrow account is required unless a specific business requires a special regime (not applicable here).

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED: (1) The incorporation of the Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada is ratified and the capital subscription, as set out in the Certificate of Incorporation, is approved and each subscriber accepts the number and class of shares allotted. (2) The Bylaws, as executed, are adopted and declared binding upon all shareholders. (3) The registered office is established at the address stated in the Certificate, within the jurisdiction of the Cámara de Comercio of Bogotá. (4) The Sole Legal Representative and the Principal Accountant are appointed, and each hereby accepts office. (5) The Legal Representative is authorized to complete the registration of the company with the Cámara de Comercio, obtain the NIT-RUT, open bank accounts, and execute all ancillary documents required to commence lawful operations.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Ministerio de Tecnologías de la Información y las Comunicaciones (MinTIC) – Registro de Operadores de Servicios de Tecnología (notified, not licensed)

Category: No operating license required for software design, development, and no-code web application services under Decree 1078/2015; only an optional Voluntary Technology Services Registry (RSTA) is available

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — national tax and customs authority; official portal: www.dian.gov.co; e-filing and administrative services via MUISCA web platform; customs clearance via SIGLO XXI and VUCE.

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the S.A.S. through private document before the Cámara de Comercio (no minimum capital); the RUES mercantile registration automatically assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). (2) Complete and certify the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) via MUISCA/PRO-RUT, declaring ISIC 6201 activity, legal representative, and the IMC foreign-exchange bank account. (3) Foreign shareholders: provide apostilled certificate of incorporation and passport copies; a Colombian tax-resident legal representative is mandatory. (4) Activate the electronic signature (firma electrónica) and register the e-invoicing system within the RUT. (5) If the foreign shareholder operates a Colombian permanent establishment, register the branch NIT; otherwise no additional NIT is required. Total timeline: 3–10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the S.A.S. is a Colombian tax-resident company subject to national corporate income tax at 35% (2024 rate) on worldwide income, including software export revenue; qualifying software-export income may additionally access a limited 5-year 'economía naranja' income-tax renta exenta; no pass-through treatment applies to shareholders. — (1) Incorporate the S.A.S. through private document before the Cámara de Comercio (no minimum capital); the RUES mercantile registration automatically assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). (2) Complete and certify the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) via MUISCA/PRO-RUT, declaring ISIC 6201 activity, legal representative, and the IMC foreign-exchange bank account. (3) Foreign shareholders: provide apostilled certificate of incorporation and passport copies; a Colombian tax-resident legal representative is mandatory. (4) Activate the electronic signature (firma electrónica) and register the e-invoicing system within the RUT. (5) If the foreign shareholder operates a Colombian permanent establishment, register the branch NIT; otherwise no additional NIT is required. Total timeline: 3–10 business days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the S.A.S. is a Colombian tax-resident company subject to national corporate income tax at 35% (2024 rate) on worldwide income, including software export revenue; qualifying software-export income may additionally access a limited 5-year 'economía naranja' income-tax renta exenta; no pass-through treatment applies to shareholders.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. Available elections: (a) voluntary inclusion in the Régimen Simple de Tributación (SIMPLE), a combined income/VAT presumptive regime for gross income up to 100,000 UVT (approx. COP 4,706 million in 2024), generally suboptimal for export-focused SaaS; (b) mandatory VAT 0% on export services (Art. 481 E.T.), formalized through FEV export invoicing; (c) election to offset VAT refundable balances against income-tax liabilities; (d) election of the 5-year economía naranja exemption for qualifying software activities (ISIC 6201 with at least 80% software-derived revenue); (e) Law 2277/2022 anti-hybrid rules, which restrict deductibility of hybrid payments and require no election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

3,500 UVT for ordinary small merchants (COP 164,727,500 for FY2024, UVT = COP 47,065). Non-importing and non-exporting merchants below this threshold may register as 'no responsables' and remain outside the IVA system; however, an exporter of services, including low-code/SaaS providers, is compulsorily required to register as an IVA-responsible taxpayer irrespective of turnover because export zero-rating and refund rights are available only to registered exporters.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

VAT 0% with refund rights (excluido con derecho a devolución) applies to exported services under Art. 481(4) E.T. and Decree 1625/2016 (Arts. 1.6.1.12.5–1.6.1.12.20). The visual low-code/website-builder service (ISIC 6201) qualifies when: (i) the recipient is a non-resident without Colombian domicile; (ii) the service is exploited exclusively outside Colombia (offshore benefit test); (iii) the foreign payer remits the consideration in foreign currency through a Colombian authorised FX intermediary; and (iv) the exporter issues the FEV with the export coding, retains the contract, and files the exchange declaration and Información Exógena. Failure of any condition recharacterizes the sale as a 19% taxable domestic service with full clawback exposure.

  • FEV (Factura Electrónica de Venta) with zero-rated export code, CUFE and QR validation
  • Written service agreement with the foreign client containing exclusive-overseas-use clause and offshore consideration
  • Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas (FX repatriation certificate issued by the Colombian IMC)
  • Declaración de Cambio Formulario No. 5 (exchange declaration for services) stamped by the intermediary
  • RUT excerpt evidencing ISIC 6201 activity and IVA-responsible status
  • For physical/digital goods accompanying the service: DEX (Declaración de Exportación), Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, and commercial export invoice

Mandatory real-time Factura Electrónica de Venta (FEV) under DIAN Res. 000165/2023 for all IVA-responsible taxpayers including software exporters. Each FEV must be transmitted to DIAN via API (WSDL/SOAP web service) or the free DIAN web portal, and must embed a CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica), a QR verification code, and the export-of-services annotation. Purchases from unregistered sellers require the Documento Soporte en Adquisiciones with CUDS filing. The system mandates immediate validation, defined contingency procedures during DIAN outages, and continuous compliance updates issued by DIAN resolution.

DIAN may audit within the 5-year limitation period (Art. 705 E.T.). Rejected zero-rating triggers VAT reinstatement at 19% plus moratory interest, an inexactitud fine of up to 160% of the understated tax, and fines up to 100% on amounts intended to inflate refunds. Late or absent FX repatriation retroactively revokes the export treatment with the same clawback and interest. Criminal tax evasion (Art. 434B C.P.) carries 4–9 years imprisonment when the evasion exceeds 250 SMMLV (approx. COP 325 million in 2024); fraudulent export claims may additionally be prosecuted as aggravated contrabando (Law 1762/2015). Ancillary sanctions include suspension of the RUT and e-invoicing authorization, and exógena information fines up to COP 18,000 per incorrect or omitted record.
