Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá (CCB) — Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES), regulated and supervised by the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC), coordinated with DIAN for the NIT/RUT pre-assignment.

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), Arts. 1, 4, 5, 17, 45; Código de Comercio (Decreto-Ley 410 de 1971), Arts. 19, 110, 354, 372; Decreto Único Reglamentario 1074 de 2015, Book II, Arts. 2.2.2.1.1 et seq. (commercial registry); Ley 2069 de 2020 (minimum-capital liberalization and fast-track registration); Estatuto Tributario (DIAN), Arts. 555-2 et seq. for RUT/NIT.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución de Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (private incorporation instrument), optionally elevated to Escritura Pública if real property is contributed; submitted via the RUES electronic form 'Registro de sociedades'.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 15 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum subscribed capital for the S.A.S.; Ley 2069 de 2020 removed the former one-minimum-wage floor. The paid-in portion must be effectively deposited in a Colombian 'cuenta de constitución' opened in the provisional name of the company, and the issuing bank must grant a deposit certificate proving payment for the Cámara de Comercio. Any unpaid balance of subscribed capital must be settled within two (2) years from execution per Art. 4, Ley 1258 de 2008. No escrow account or trust is mandated by Colombian law.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certification of the office leased premises by the Cuerpo de Bomberos (Bogotá) for occupancy loads above the applicable threshold; structural-load compliance with NSR-10, Title I for the rented office; and workplace-clearance program SG-SST (Decree 1072 de 2015, Book II, Chapter 2.2.4) filed with the ARL, including emergency evacuation plans and occupational-illness prevention for staff.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) for commercial-registry supervision, consumer protection, and personal-data processing registration (Ley 1581 de 2012); municipal Planning Office (e.g., Secretaría Distrital de Planeación in Bogotá) for the land-use compatibility certificate. No sectoral industrial licensing body applies to ISIC 6201 software and cloud services.

Category: Not applicable — Colombian law imposes no sectoral operating or manufacturing permit on pure software programming, financial portfolio-tracking platforms, or SaaS activities (ISIC 6201). The company operates under the CCB mercantile registration, the DIAN RUT, and the municipal certificate of compatible land use (uso de suelo) for its office premises.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — Official portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional platform: MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicio y Control Automatizado); e-services via DIAN's Habilitación and facturación electrónica web services.

E-government filing portal

The SAS must obtain a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) at incorporation. Foreign-owned entities: (1) Execute public deed before a Colombian notary appointing a legal representative resident in Colombia; (2) File the registration in the Cámara de Comercio (Commercial Registry) for the SAS, obtaining the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal; (3) Complete the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) form via MUISCA online portal or at DIAN service point, submitting the certificate of existence, statutes, and representative's identity documents (validated passport if foreign); (4) Obtain the digital signature certificate (Certificado Digital) issued by an authorized certification authority (e.g., DIAN's own or GSE) for e-invoicing and MUISCA filings; (5) Activate the NIT and confirm the VAT (IVA) and Income Tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) obligations assigned; (6) Register the establishment in the DIAN's electronic invoicing system (Resolution 000165 of 2023 framework). Timeline: 7–20 business days for full activation.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — The SAS must obtain a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) at incorporation. Foreign-owned entities: (1) Execute public deed before a Colombian notary appointing a legal representative resident in Colombia; (2) File the registration in the Cámara de Comercio (Commercial Registry) for the SAS, obtaining the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal; (3) Complete the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) form via MUISCA online portal or at DIAN service point, submitting the certificate of existence, statutes, and representative's identity documents (validated passport if foreign); (4) Obtain the digital signature certificate (Certificado Digital) issued by an authorized certification authority (e.g., DIAN's own or GSE) for e-invoicing and MUISCA filings; (5) Activate the NIT and confirm the VAT (IVA) and Income Tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) obligations assigned; (6) Register the establishment in the DIAN's electronic invoicing system (Resolution 000165 of 2023 framework). Timeline: 7–20 business days for full activation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Colombia. Default: SAS is treated as a resident corporation (sociedad nacional) subject to entity-level corporate income tax at 35% (FY 2024), plus applicable surtax (3% for certain industries, not applicable to software). Available elections/regimes: (a) Tax consolidation (Régimen de Grupos Empresariales) after a 5-year prior authorization by DIAN for qualifying parent-subsidiary groups; (b) Simple regime of taxation (Régimen Simple de Tributación – SIMPLE) for small entities meeting revenue thresholds, optional election | not recommended for IVA exports); (c) Optional VAT refund offset mechanism for exporters via the 'ZEE' and free-trade zone regimes where applying for a free trade zone (Zona Franca US) permits a reduced 20% corporate income tax rate on industrial activities, subject to job creation thresholds (not typical for pure software); (d) Transfer pricing safe harbor elections and APA filings under Articles 260-1 to 260-11 E.T.; (e) Annual inflation adjustment elections for inventory costing methods. Hybrid mismatch rules follow OECD BEPS Action 2 via Law 2277/2022, limiting deductions for payments treated as hybrid instruments; no discrete election, mandatory compliance.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For legal entities (SAS), IVA registration via RUT is mandatory from the first taxable transaction, without a minimum economic threshold. For natural persons and other simplified regimes, the obligation triggers when gross income from taxable activities exceeds 3,500 UVTs (Unidad de Valor Tributario = COP 47,065 for FY 2024), i.e., COP 164,727,500, or when holding inventory as of December 31 above 5,000 UVTs (COP 235,325,000). For SAS incorporated as a company, NIT and IVA registration are concurrent at formation regardless of anticipated turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of services qualifies for 0% IVA rate under Article 481(e) E.T. and Decree 1805 of 2010, provided the service: (a) is rendered directly to a foreign beneficiary domiciled outside Colombia; (b) is 'exported' — the real use or effect of the service must occur abroad (criterion of use and enjoyment); (c) is rendered by the Colombian company without a substantive nexus in Colombia; (d) the export is evidenced by a service contract and invoices issued to the non-resident party; (e) foreign currency payments are received through the Colombian official foreign exchange market (exceptions permitted for non-residents' deposits in local accounts). Software-as-a-Service insight dashboards, cloud portfolio tracking, and data analytics for non-resident financial clients fall under the 'technology and software' service category eligible for zero-rating if the technical assistance or the digital service is consumed outside Colombia (Título 1.6 Decreto 1625/2016, arts. 1.6.1.13.15-1.6.1.13.22).

  • Declaración de Exportación de Servicios (export service declaration form filed via MUISCA, encoded as Formulario 630 — 'Información de Exportaciones de Servicios')
  • Commercial service contract executed with the non-resident client specifying the service scope, destination of use, and pricing
  • FEV (Electronic Invoice) issued to the foreign client with CUFE code
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document (only if physical goods are peripherally included; for pure software, digital delivery proof)
  • Certificado de Movimiento de Divisas / Declaración de Cambio (foreign exchange certificate from the Colombian intermediary bank evidencing wire receipt from the non-resident)
  • Proof of payment rails settlement (Stripe/Apple IAP must route through a Colombian FX intermediary to comply with Decree 1074/2015 exchange regime)

Mandatory since 2019 for all DIAN-required taxpayers. Under Resolución DIAN 000165 of November 2023 (effective January 2024), e-invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta – FEV) must: (1) use the 'Facturación Electrónica' platform with real-time DIAN validation via web services or the free mobile/desktop application 'DIAN Radian/FEV'; (2) incorporate a Unique Electronic Invoicing Code (CUFE) or CUDS (for savings/consignment) generated algorithmically from the invoice issuance range pre-authorized by DIAN; (3) transmit each invoice within 5 calendar days of issuance (before the 5th day after issuance); (4) obtain an authorized digital signature certificate (IRT – firma electrónica) for the computing platform; (5) send the final validation message containing the CUFE and the QR code onto the invoice PDF. For SaaS transactions, each monthly subscription or charge requires a FEV. Non-compliance fines under Article 652-13 E.T. (up to 5,000 UVTs per invoice irregularity) and initiatives to disable the NIT apply.

DIAN and the National Customs Authority (part of DIAN) actively scrutinize 'export of services' zero-rating claims. Typical audit triggers: recurring losses, exceptionally high export returns, unregistered accrued services, or FX settlement outside the mandatory 6-month term. Legal consequences: (a) if the documentary bundle is incomplete or the use-and-enjoyment criterion is not met, the invoice becomes a nonexempt sale taxed at 19% IVA, DIAN issues additional tax assessments (período gravable) plus daily default interest (interest at twice the legal lending rate, art. 635 E.T.); (b) 'sanción por inexactitud' under Article 647 E.T. — 200% of the difference between the improperly zero-rated tax and the correct assessment (increased to 500% when the false export is initiated for evasion); (c) fines under Article 652-13 E.T. for falsely claiming export exclusions (up to 20,000 UVTs ≈ COP 941.3 million for FY 2024 per base period); (d) criminal liability under Article 402 Código Penal for 'Fraudulent Exportation' — imprisonment of 6 to 12 years and asset seizure when export declarations are false; (e) retrospective clawback comprising the unpaid IVA plus penalties for all prior VAT returns within the 5-year window (statute of limitations art. 714 E.T. — 3 years in some cases for returns, but DIAN may extend to 5 years in the presence of formal faults).

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% withholding tax on dividend distributions made from profits that have already been subject to the 35% CIT, applicable to non-resident corporate shareholders and resident individuals. For resident corporate shareholders, dividends are generally non-taxable. For non-resident individuals and non-resident companies without treaty protection, the 10% rate applies, with no exemption for the first 300 UVT (such de minimis exemption applies only to resident individuals under current rules).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Fiscal year is the calendar year. For non-large taxpayers the filing window is generally between 9 August and 17 September of the following tax year, determined by the last two digits of the NIT; Grandes Contribuyentes file in April/May.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 42 - Ingresos brutos (Gross income) Report total gross SaaS subscription and financial-tracking software licensing revenue, including recurring cloud analytics fees, recognized on an accrual basis. Exclude VAT collected. For a non-resident-owned S.A.S., include all invoiced amounts to foreign and local clients; cross-border related-party invoicing must be disclosed in the transfer pricing annex and priced at arm's length. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.
Box 44 - Ingresos no constitutivos de renta (Non-taxable income) Record only statutory exclusions such as corrections to previous-year income or reimbursements representing costs paid by customers, if properly documented. Usually nil for SaaS; if a capital contribution or refund from a related party is incorrectly classified here, DIAN will recharacterize it as taxable income. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.
Box 45 - Costos de venta (Cost of sales) Direct costs attributable to delivering the cloud platform: third-party cloud infrastructure, licensed data feeds, API connectivity charges, external sub-processors, and amortization of capitalized software development. Costs paid to foreign related parties (e.g. hosting on a parent group server) must be supported by a valid transfer pricing method, executed contract, and evidence of actual services. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.
Box 50 - Deducciones (Deductions) Operating deductions: salaries and social security of local engineers and support staff, lease and utilities of the service office, marketing expenses, professional fees, IT security compliance costs, and depreciation. Deductions for intercompany royalty or management payments are allowed only if the additive requirements under Articles 124-2 and 631 E.T. are met and the Beneficial Owner/TRC is retained. Payments to blacklisted tax havens may be disallowed. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.
Box 53 - Rentas exentas (Exempt income) Claim only statutory exemptions, e.g. qualified software-export earnings if the taxpayer still holds a valid pre-2023 exemption regime; otherwise leave nil. Foreign shareholders cannot create an exemption where none is due; an unsubstantiated exemption triggers a 200% inexactness penalty. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.
Box 62 - Impuesto de renta a cargo (CIT payable) Compute tax at 35% of the taxable base after allowed loss carryforward and any R&D deductions, deduct prepayments and withholding tax credits. Non-resident shareholders receiving dividends from after-tax profits must separately prepare the dividend withholding form; no shareholder-level expense is deductible in the S.A.S. return. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.
Box 83 - Saldo a pagar o saldo a favor (Balance payable or carryover credit) Final net amount payable to the National Treasury after prepayments and credits; if negative, record refundable tax credit requested for cross-check with DIAN. A carryover credit cannot be remitted abroad; a subsequent refund request may be slowed if the non-resident shareholding structure is not disclosed or the fiscal tax residency of the shareholder is unclear. Late filing: 5% of tax due for each month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax due, with a minimum fine of 10 UVT when no tax is payable (Articles 641, 642 E.T.). Late payment interest accrues daily at the indexed monetary-correction rate. Inaccurate filing is additionally penalized under Article 647 with up to 200% of the understated tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: COP 135,000,000 (approx. USD 37,000) in aggregate related-party transactions in the fiscal year triggers the filing of Form 120 and the Local File. The Master File is additionally required when the worldwide group's consolidated revenue exceeds COP 30,000,000,000 or the local entity's related-party transactions surpass COP 5,000,000,000. All thresholds are annually adjusted by DIAN.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not apply a dedicated Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime, but economic substance is embedded in the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR, Article 631 E.T.), the anti-abuse doctrine, and treaty-benefit realization analysis. A financial-tracking software S.A.S. must evidence real local decision-making, qualified engineering personnel, physical premises, active license/contract management, and a distinct function-asset-risk profile; otherwise DIAN may disregard the legal personality, recharacterize the S.A.S. as a permanent establishment of the foreign beneficial owner, or deny treaty benefits and intercompany deductions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Willful Understatement of Taxable Income

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion is punishable under Law 2277/2022 by imprisonment of 3 to 12 years where the evaded tax exceeds 250 UVT. The tax administration simultaneously imposes an inexactness penalty of 200% of the understated tax (Article 647 E.T.), daily late-payment interest, and disallowance of all claimed loss carryforwards.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives are jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax debt if the understatement results from wilful misconduct; criminal prosecution may include civil restitution of evaded amounts, freezing of personal bank accounts, a travel ban, and imprisonment of the responsible officers.
Shell Company / Sham S.A.S. Without Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: DIAN may apply the corporate veil-piercing doctrine under Article 16 of the Colombian Commercial Code and Article 631 E.T., declaring the S.A.S. non-existent for tax purposes; all income is reattributed to the foreign principal, with tax recalculated at 35%, plus penalties of 200% and interest; the commercial registry may be cancelled and the entity liquidated.

Director personal liability: The limited liability shield is removed: directors and shareholders become personally liable for the full tax deficiency and accrued interest; personal assets are embargoed, travel bans are issued by the DIAN collection department, and continuing operation as a sham entity can support fraud charges carrying imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure and Non-Arm's-Length Intercompany Pricing

Statutory sanction: Unfiled or late transfer pricing documentation triggers fines of up to 1% of the aggregate related-party transaction value per document (Article 634 E.T.); DIAN will adjust the taxable income to arm's length, apply a 10% surcharge on the adjustment, and add daily default interest; total assessment may exceed 300% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve intercompany agreements may be declared jointly liable for the resulting tax deficiency if they knew or should have known of the disconnected pricing; the director's personal residence and shareholder equity can be attached, and a court may issue a prohibition against leaving the country pending payment of the aggregate tax.
Abusive Cross-Border Profit Repatriation and Unsubstantiated Treaty Relief

Statutory sanction: Withholding tax is recharged at the statutory 10% dividend rate or 15%/20% interest-royalty rate plus penalty; the deduction for the payment is denied as a prohibited cost in tax havens; the taxpayer faces the full 200% inexactness penalty and failure-to-withhold surcharge; a criminal complaint for illicit tax conduct may be filed.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the withheld tax not collected and remitted to DIAN; civil restitution includes personal fund recovery of the unpaid foreign payments; the judicial process may impose a property embargo, a travel ban, and imprisonment for up to 10 years where the artificial profit extraction is characterized as aggravated tax evasion.
