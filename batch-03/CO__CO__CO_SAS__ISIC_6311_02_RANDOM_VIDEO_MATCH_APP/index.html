Governing statute

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) inscribed in the Registro Mercantil; Escritura Pública and Registro de Instrumentos Públicos only when aportes comprise real property.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); electronic services portal: Muisca (muisca.dian.gov.co), https://www.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

A foreign shareholder incorporating an S.A.S. must (1) execute the private incorporation deed with a Colombian attorney-in-fact and register the company with the Cámara de Comercio; (2) apply for a provisional NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) through the DIAN RUT (Registro Único Tributario) module on Muisca; (3) appoint a local legal representative/fiscal domicile; (4) formalize the RUT (Formulario 022) online with the support of a certified public accountant (contador público) or lawyer to obtain the definitive 9-to-16 digit NIT. The S.A.S. is automatically classified in the Régimen Común for IVA purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign shareholder incorporating an S.A.S. must (1) execute the private incorporation deed with a Colombian attorney-in-fact and register the company with the Cámara de Comercio; (2) apply for a provisional NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) through the DIAN RUT (Registro Único Tributario) module on Muisca; (3) appoint a local legal representative/fiscal domicile; (4) formalize the RUT (Formulario 022) online with the support of a certified public accountant (contador público) or lawyer to obtain the definitive 9-to-16 digit NIT. The S.A.S. is automatically classified in the Régimen Común for IVA purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election is available for an S.A.S.; it is a capital company taxed as a standalone taxpayer under the Estatuto Tributario (E.T.). Relevant statutory elections include: standard corporate income tax rate of 35%, foreign tax credit election under Art. 254 E.T., optional deferral of losses (Art. 147 E.T.), and voluntary 'gran contribuyente' status. Fiscal transparency (transparencia fiscal) is restricted to investment vehicles such as FIPs and FICs and cannot be elected by an S.A.S.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for legal entities: every S.A.S. carrying out taxable operations in Colombia must register in the RUT and remit IVA under the Régimen Común. The simplified regime (Régimen Simplificado) is reserved for natural persons with gross income below 3,500 UVT (approx. COP 165 million for tax year 2024) and is unavailable to the S.A.S.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports qualify for the 0% export rate under Art. 481 E.T. with credit/recovery of input VAT. Services qualify under Art. 481(e) E.T. and Decree 1625/2016, Art. 1.3.1.13.2 when: (i) the service is rendered to a non-resident recipient without a permanent establishment in Colombia; (ii) the service is used exclusively abroad; and (iii) a written export-services contract (contrato de exportación de servicios) is executed and retained. The real-time video matching and cross-border telephony app supplied to foreign end users through Apple IAP or Paymentwall may qualify as an export of digital services, provided the users are non-resident and the service is not consumed in Colombia.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX / Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (B/L or AWB)
  • Commercial invoice and export-services contract (contrato de exportación de servicios)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de reintegro y declaración de cambio, Banco de la República, Form DCIP-188)
  • RUT evidencing exporter VAT status and, when required, fiscal residence certificate of the foreign recipient

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta, FEV) applies to all business taxpayers under Resolución DIAN 000165/2023. FEVs must be transmitted synchronously through DIAN's Muisca API or a DIAN-authorized software provider using the taxpayer's digital certificate and validated in near real time; each invoice must include the CUFE or CUDS unique tracking code. The video-match app's Apple IAP and Paymentwall revenue streams require FEV issuance for export services at 0%, with supporting electronic 'documento soporte' for acquisitions and advance invoicing (prepago) as applicable.

DIAN rigorously audits export zero-rating and VAT refund claims. Failure to prove export status, to repatriate proceeds within 180 days, or to retain the complete documentary bundle triggers: (i) retroactive VAT assessment at 19% plus inflation adjustment and daily interest (Art. 634 E.T.); (ii) a penalty equal to 100% of the improperly claimed refund (Art. 648 E.T.); (iii) correction penalties up to 160% of the omitted tax on inaccurate returns (Art. 642 E.T.); and (iv) criminal tax-fraud liability under Law 1943/2018 where evasion exceeds COP 300 million, with possible imprisonment. Mischaracterizing Colombian users as foreign recipients for the video-match app exposes the entity to the 19% IVA on Colombian-sourced digital services plus fraud penalties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a managed float with full current-account convertibility for bona fide trade and service operations, provided the transaction is executed through a licensed foreign-exchange intermediary and reported on a 'declaración de cambio'. Capital-account transactions are not prohibited but require prior registration with Banco de la República; inbound foreign investment into the Colombian S.A.S. must be registered in the national FDI registry before funds are repatriated. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are permissible after audited financial statements are filed and Colombian taxes are settled, but the local bank will require the FDI registration to remain active and will apply the statutory dividend withholding clearance. Late or missing registration is the main de facto convertibility barrier because the bank cannot process the onward remittance without it.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, the exporter must channel 100 percent of export proceeds through a Colombian authorized foreign-exchange intermediary and must collect the proceeds within six months from the export date; an extension may be requested only on regulated grounds. There is no mandatory sale or conversion of export currency into pesos; proceeds may remain in a foreign-currency account in Colombia or in an authorized external account, but the full amount must be reported and evidenced by a bank settlement certificate. Services exports such as the app's subscription and IAP revenues are current-account service receipts and are not subject to forced surrender; however, when received through the banking system they must be filed on the applicable foreign-exchange declaration to support tax credits and central-bank statistics.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia); the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia acts as the prudential regulator and foreign-exchange enforcement authority under Decree 1068 of 2015 and Banco de la República Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: Low-Medium, with restricted utility. An offshore digital EMI such as Payoneer, Wise or Airwallex is not licensed by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia as a payment company or SEDPE and cannot operate as the main treasury or custody account of the Colombian S.A.S. without triggering unregistered foreign-exchange activity. It may be used only as a supplemental collection or settlement rail for Apple IAP or Paymentwall if its license permits Colombian corporate onboarding; all settlement funds must nevertheless be forwarded to a Colombian Tier-1 bank, reported on the foreign-exchange declaration, and recorded in the company's statutory accounting and tax records. Using an offshore EMI as the sole account would create a legal blocker for corporate account maintenance, dividend distribution and audit sign-off.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign investor shall wire funds by SWIFT MT103 from its own account to the S.A.S.'s USD or COP account in the selected Tier-1 bank, with the bank instructed to apply the reference 'INVERSIÓN INTERNACIONAL / CÓDIGO DE CAMBIO' plus the Banco de la República FDI registration code. The bank will verify the originator against the registered foreign investor; if the originator or the declared purpose is different, the credit is frozen or reversed. A capital injection cannot be made through a third-party loan or an invoice for services; it must correspond to the subscribed shares in the S.A.S. and be supported by minutes of the shareholders' meeting approving the capital increase and by the updated company registry excerpt.For operating outflows the S.A.S. will use local-origin SWIFT MT103 transfers for vendor fees, royalties and service charges, accompanied by the supplier or intercompany invoice, the service contract, tax withholding certificates and the foreign-exchange declaration ('declaración de cambio por pagos al exterior'). For physical goods or significant equipment procurement, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit, either at sight or deferred, may be issued by the Colombian bank, complemented by documentary collection or bank guarantees where appropriate.
