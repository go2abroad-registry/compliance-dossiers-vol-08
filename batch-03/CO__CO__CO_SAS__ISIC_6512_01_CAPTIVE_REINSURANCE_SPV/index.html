Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio with territorial jurisdiction over the registered office, operating through the Registro Único Empresarial (RUES) and Confecámaras network; operational authorization from the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) for captive reinsurance activity.

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 on the Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.), arts. 1-47; Colombian Commercial Code (Decreto 410 de 1971), arts. 98-110 and 373-394 (registro mercantil); Decreto 2555 de 2010, Libro 4 (financial supervision and authorization of insurance and reinsurance entities, as amended by the SFC captive regime instructions); Circular Básica Jurídica (Circular Externa 029 de 2014) for corporate governance and SARLAFT.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (signed and signature-legalized) or Escritura Pública No. ___ if in-kind real property is contributed; registered in the Registro Mercantil; definitive proof is the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal issued by the Chamber of Commerce.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to the S.A.S. under Law 1258/2008; however, the SFC requires the captive reinsurer to maintain a regulatory minimum subscribed and paid-in capital (per Decreto 2555/2010, adjusted by SFC regulation, reduced floor available for captives covering only parent-group risks), fully paid in cash and deposited in a blocked bank account pending final licensing.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety approval from the Cuerpo de Bomberos (Certificado de Bomberos) for the registered office; structural load inspection and use-of-land certification (Certificado de Uso de Suelo) issued by the Curaduría Urbana or local planning office; workplace occupational clearance under Resolución 0312 de 2019 (SGSST), including ARL affiliation, emergency plan, and electrical safety checklist per RETIE.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN), principal portal https://www.dian.gov.co; transactional gateway 'MUISCA' at https://muisca.dian.gov.co for e-registration, NIT issuance, electronic declarations and invoicing services.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Pre-register the foreign parent/shareholder with DIAN through a local attorney or accredited foreign representative to obtain a DIAN authorization code. Step 2: Incorporate the Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) by public deed before a Colombian notary, appointing a legal representative and a local tax agent; where the captive will operate as a reinsurer, obtain the requisite authorization from the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia. Step 3: Submit the incorporation deed, Spanish-language bylaws, proof of registered address and alien/shareholder identification to DIAN to obtain the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) and the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). Step 4: Complete the RUT positions for income tax, VAT, withholding at source, and electronic invoicing emitter. Step 5: Keep the RUT validated and renew it every two years or upon any material change in tax domicile, shareholding or activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists in Colombia. The S.A.S. is a domestic corporation taxed at entity level on worldwide income. Relevant elective mechanisms include: (i) the fiscal consolidation group election under ET arts. 148-1 et seq., available to qualifying domestic economic groups with 75% or greater common shareholding and prior DIAN authorization; (ii) accelerated depreciation elections for new fixed assets; (iii) annual or multi-year advanced pricing agreements with DIAN on transfer pricing; and (iv) the option to treat certain hybrid instruments consistently under Colombian anti-hybrid rules, but no unilateral elective opt-out is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For legal entities, there is no economic turnover threshold: every S.A.S. performing taxable or potentially taxable operations must register as a VAT responsible within the RUT regardless of revenue volume. Natural persons under the simplified regime historically qualified up to 3,500 UVT, but that regime is not available to a corporate captive reinsurance SPV; a zero threshold applies to legal entities.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Colombia applies 0% VAT (ventas exentas con derecho a compensación) to exports of goods and qualifying export services under ET art. 481 and Decree 1625/2016. A service qualifies when rendered to a foreign person, delivered or used entirely abroad, and not connected with Colombian territory or with a Colombian resident's economic activity. Captive reinsurance premium income is generally an excluded operation (fuera del hecho generador del IVA) rather than zero-rated; nevertheless, management, underwriting and claims services performed by the same Colombian S.A.S. for the foreign parent/reinsurer may be classified as export services and invoiced at 0% if (i) the client is a foreign related party, (ii) the services are economically used outside Colombia, and (iii) full contractual, intra-group, transfer pricing and banking documentation is retained. The SPV must mark its RUT as exporter and file the corresponding export-service declaration to sustain the 0% treatment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) — mandatory where goods are physically exported; for pure reinsurance services, a sworn export-service declaration may be required as evidentiary support.
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill (Conocimiento de Embarque) — when transport or insured goods are involved.
  • Inward remittance certificate (Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas) issued by a Colombian authorized FX intermediary, confirming foreign currency collection.
  • Ceded reinsurance treaty, risk-participation agreement or export services contract, identifying the foreign counterparty and economic use abroad.
  • DIAN-validated electronic invoice (Factura Electrónica de Venta) bearing CUFE and evidencing the 0% rate.

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing through DIAN's ecosystem. The S.A.S. must issue 'Factura Electrónica de Venta' (FEV) using DIAN-approved software or the free MUISCA emulator; each invoice must bear the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación), QR/verification stamp, digital signature and XML/CII schema, and must be transmitted to DIAN for validation before delivery. VAT returns must be filed electronically on form F-100, with automatic cross-checks against invoices, customs declarations and FX reintegro reports. Digital stamping, real-time fiscal clearance and full XML archival are mandatory.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger reimbursement of omitted VAT, daily interest, and a penalty under ET art. 647 increased to 160% of the tax difference when the inaccuracy is considered fraudulent. Improperly obtained or refunded balances are clawed back with an additional 15% sanction under ET art. 670. Criminal tax evasion under Law 1943/2018 may also lead to imprisonment. The DIAN audit window is five years, expanding to ten years for related-party transactions. Cross-validation combines the F-100 VAT return, customs records, electronic invoices, remittance evidence and transfer pricing documentation; failure to repatriate within 180 days may also trigger Colombian foreign-exchange penalties and loss of the 0% rate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is fully convertible for current account items (trade, services and ordinary remittances) via the foreign exchange market, subject to mandatory channeling through authorized intermediaries and exchange declarations. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment in a Colombian S.A.S. captive reinsurance SPV, are freely permitted but must be registered with Banco de la República through an authorized intermediary; profit remittance is permitted after the relevant tax obligation is discharged and requires evidence of duly registered foreign capital and audited financial statements. Non-resident investors do not need domicile or residency for incorporation, but reinvestment and capital reductions must be lodged in the foreign investment registration file to ensure future legal repatriation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds of goods and services must be received within the commercial credit terms authorized by Banco de la República, generally up to six months from the date of shipment or accrual unless an extended pre-financing or foreign-credit arrangement is approved, and must be channelled through an authorized FX intermediary in Colombia. The statutory standard effectively requires 100% channeling and reintegration of the export value into the local financial system; however, the proceeds may be kept in foreign currency in Colombian intermediary accounts only to the extent allowed by Banco de la República and must be converted into COP or otherwise negotiated on the exchange market if needed to settle operational obligations. Non-compliance triggers exchange surcharges (multas cambiarias) and administrative sanctions under the Colombian FX regime.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (central bank) and the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) as foreign exchange administration supervisors, with all transactions executed through authorized foreign exchange intermediaries registered with the Financial Superintendence of Colombia (SFC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for treasury-critical functions. Colombia does not recognize offshore e-money institutions or unlicensed EMIs as authorized FX intermediaries, and a captive reinsurance SPV cannot legally use an offshore EMI as the ultimate clearing bank for inbound or outbound reinsurance premiums, claims or capital flows. Local licensed fintech institutions supervised by the SFC (e.g., specialized digital banks and regulated payment providers) may offer efficient payment initiation rails, but the final legal settlement and FX conversion must occur through a licensed Colombian credit institution or an authorized FX intermediary.
