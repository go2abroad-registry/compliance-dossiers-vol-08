Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus; web portal: https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy

E-government filing portal

Non-resident entities must obtain a Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) by submitting Form TD 2001 (Tax Registration Certificate Application) to the District Tax Office, along with certified M&A, Certificate of Incorporation, registered office address, and proof of identity of directors and ultimate beneficial owners. VAT registration is mandatory for non-resident suppliers of taxable goods/services, with registration completed through the same Tax Portal using Form VAT 101; a VAT registration number is issued within 10-20 business days if a fiscal representative is appointed when required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Non-resident entities must obtain a Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) by submitting Form TD 2001 (Tax Registration Certificate Application) to the District Tax Office, along with certified M&A, Certificate of Incorporation, registered office address, and proof of identity of directors and ultimate beneficial owners. VAT registration is mandatory for non-resident suppliers of taxable goods/services, with registration completed through the same Tax Portal using Form VAT 101; a VAT registration number is issued within 10-20 business days if a fiscal representative is appointed when required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not operate a check-the-box regime. Elections available include: notional interest deduction (NID) election on new equity, tax residence certification election (to confirm central management and control), optional VAT grouping (Form VAT 112), and election to continue old tax rules for amortization of goodwill and IP under transitional provisions. Hybrid mismatch rules under CySec Circular and Income Tax Law 118(I)2002 (art. 18AA) restrict elective treatment but no entity classification election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for Cyprus-established entities; non-established taxable persons have no threshold and are compulsorily registerable from the first taxable supply. Distance sales of goods from other EU member states trigger registration at EUR 10,000 per calendar year.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to: (a) export of tangible goods physically departing Cyprus to a destination outside the EU, evidenced by customs declaration and transport documents; (b) supplies of services tied directly to exported goods (e.g., loading, packing) for goods exported within one year; (c) international transport and related services; and (d) intra-Community supplies of goods transferred to VAT-registered persons in other EU states subject to valid VAT number and listing in VIES. For baby montessori wooden blocks (ISIC 3240), zero-rating is available provided title in goods transfers to a non-resident buyer and goods leave Cyprus within 90 days of dispatch.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document AE0 / SAD)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued by carrier confirming physical departure
  • Commercial invoice referencing contract terms and Incoterms
  • Inward Remittance Certificate showing receipt of export proceeds
  • Proof of VAT identity of overseas recipient (for intra-Community zero-rating)
  • Bank transfer / SWIFT confirmation of export invoice settlement

Cyprus does not mandate continuous transaction controls, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping. Mandatory electronic reporting of VAT returns and VIES periodic declarations via the Tax Portal is required; e-invoicing using PEPPOL is recommended but not compulsory. No real-time interface / API stamping is currently enforced, though the Tax Department has announced gradual transition to EU e-invoicing standards under ViDA.

The Tax Department may reverse zero-rating and charge 19% VAT where the 180-day FX repatriation deadline is missed without justified commercial cause or documentation is falsified. Penalties include interim assessment, 2% interest per month on unpaid VAT, surcharge of 10% on late payment, administrative penalty up to EUR 100 per non-listed transaction, and criminal liability for fraudulent evasion under VAT Law and Criminal Code, with fines up to EUR 40,000 or imprisonment of up to three years. Retrospective clawbacks also apply where goods are re-imported to Cyprus or documentation fails the 'inward timeline' test.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro (EUR) is fully convertible; Cyprus maintains no exchange controls on current or capital account transactions as an EU/EMU member. Non-resident shareholders may freely re-capitalize the Cyprus Ltd and repatriate dividends; profit remittance is not subject to withholding tax or Central Bank of Cyprus approval. The only administrative duty is statistical balance-of-payments reporting to the CB Cyprus for settlements equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cyprus imposes no mandatory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds. The exporter may retain receipts indefinitely in USD, EUR, or any other currency in domestic or foreign bank accounts. The statutory ECB/CB Cyprus reporting framework only requires transaction-level submission for goods exports above the EUR 12,500 threshold; no conversion ratio or liquidation obligation applies.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (CB Cyprus) acting as the national competent authority for monetary stability, banking supervision, and balance-of-payments reporting, in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB) for euro area payment oversight and Eurosystem liquidity operations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility for EU/EEA-authorized Electronic Money Institutions (e.g., Lithuania- or Ireland-licensed EMIs) passported into Cyprus under PSD2; Low feasibility for offshore non-EU digital EMIs (UK, Singapore, Hong Kong) that lost EU passporting rights and would require separate CB Cyprus licensing or an EU-licensed agent. EMIs are permissible only as secondary settlement rails for marketplace receipts; the primary treasury banking relationship must remain with a CB Cyprus-licensed credit institution to satisfy Cypriot law, tax substance, and CBC KYC Directive requirements.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate income tax rate on the worldwide income of Cyprus tax-resident companies (Income Tax Law L.118(I)/2002, s.40).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%: Cyprus does not impose dividend withholding tax on dividends paid by a Cyprus-resident company to non-resident shareholders. The former Special Defence Contribution on dividends and interest income was abolished with effect from 1 January 2024 and never applied to non-residents.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual Form TD is due within 15 months after the end of the tax year (for a calendar-year company: 31 March of the second subsequent year). Provisional tax instalments are due on 31 July, 30 September and 31 December of the tax year; a newly incorporated company must submit its first provisional return within six months from the end of the month in which it commenced business.
