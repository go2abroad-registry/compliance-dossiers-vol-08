Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of Cyprus (Tmima Forologikon Ypiresion), Ministry of Finance; online portal: ARIADNI (ΤΑΧΙSnet) at https://taxportalo.mof.gov.cy and https://ariadni.mof.gov.cy

E-government filing portal

A foreign company intending to register for tax in Cyprus must first obtain a Tax Identification Code (TIC) by filing Form TIN 101 with the Tax Department. For VAT purposes, the company must submit Form VAT 101 (Application for VAT Registration) to the VAT Commissioner, together with certified copy of the certificate of incorporation (or equivalent foreign registration document), proof of permanent establishment or actual place of business in Cyprus if applicable, and identity documents of directors and officers. Non-resident entities without a fixed establishment may still be required to register if they make taxable supplies in Cyprus or distance sales exceeding the distance selling threshold. Registration is completed through the Tax Department’s ARIADNI portal or by physical filing, after which the company receives a TIC and, where applicable, a Cyprus VAT number (CY + 8 digits + 2-letter check code). Companies are also required to register with the Social Insurance Services and, if employing staff, the Inland Revenue for PAYE. If the entity is incorporated in Cyprus but has no trading activity, it must still obtain a TIC and file dormant returns unless exemption is confirmed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: Worldwide income for Cyprus tax residents; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income and gains from immovable property located in Cyprus. Cyprus has a unitary national tax regime with no sub-state corporate taxes. Private limited companies are statutory taxpayers at 12.5% corporate income tax. No check-the-box or pass-through election is available for a Cyprus Ltd under domestic law; however, EU/EEA entities may be classified as transparent for certain hybrid mismatch rules under the EU Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive (ATAD) implementation.

Available election forms

Cyprus domestic law does not provide an elective check-the-box regime equivalent to the U.S. classification election. However, a Cyprus Ltd may elect to be treated as a tax-transparent entity for U.S. federal income tax purposes under the U.S. check-the-box rules by filing IRS Form 8832 if it is a disregarded entity or partnership eligible under U.S. regulations. Under Cyprus tax law, entities may elect group relief under Section 19 of the Income Tax Law, elect tonnage tax regime for qualifying shipping activities, elect intellectual property (IP) box regime under Section 6B of the Income Tax Law for qualifying intangible assets accounting for nexus ratio, and elect the notional interest deduction (NID) under Section 9C for new equity. For hybrid mismatch purposes, Cyprus law allows a taxpayer to make an election to align tax treatment of transparent entities or to adjust the treatment of hybrid financial instruments, subject to conditions under the EU ATAD. The Cyprus Tax Department may issue advance tax rulings on application.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full liberalisation exists under Article 63 TFEU and the OECD Code of Liberalisation of Capital Movements. The euro is freely convertible on both current and capital accounts; no CBC licence is required for inbound equity, outbound dividends, or trade settlements. Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is subject to a 0% Cyprus dividend withholding tax (no domestic WHT; no Special Defence Contribution for non-residents; EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive available to qualifying EU corporate shareholders). Residual hurdles are documentary only: signed dividend minutes, audited financial statements, CRS/FATCA self-certification, and CBC AML source-of-funds verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Cyprus imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, no prescribed conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline. Exporters may retain foreign-currency balances indefinitely with authorised commercial banks. The only binding obligations are (i) CBC Balance-of-Payments statistical reporting by the receiving bank for cross-border payments of or above €12,500 (ECB reporting threshold), and (ii) customs export declarations (EXD / Single Administrative Document) under the Customs and Excise Law. Export VAT is zero-rated.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου, CBC), operating as the national competent authority within the Eurosystem; the euro is issued and its external value is governed by the European Central Bank (ECB).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory for Cyprus-resident entities with annual turnover exceeding EUR 10,000,000 and aggregate related-party transactions exceeding EUR 2,000,000; Master File mandatory where the multinational group's consolidated turnover reaches EUR 750,000,000; Country-by-Country Reporting threshold EUR 750,000,000 group revenue. Documentation must be contemporaneous, maintained in EUR, and produced within 30 days of a Tax Department request.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, the 'management and control' test is the statutory basis for tax residence, which requires physical presence, strategic decision-making, employees and premises in Cyprus. The GAAR under Section 33A of the Income Tax Law and the 2022 TP Administrative Guidelines recharacterize artificial structures; treaty access requires real economic substance; the IP Box follows the OECD nexus approach, effectively imposing substance discipline on all Cyprus-licensed trading entities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded plus default interest; ex officio assessment by the Tax Department; criminal conviction carries fines and imprisonment of up to 5 years under the Income Tax Law and the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns commit a criminal offense; courts may order personal restitution of evaded taxes, freeze corporate and personal assets, impose travel bans, and impose custodial sentences; disqualification as directors is common.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length recharacterization with income adjustment taxed at 12.5%, plus a 10% surcharge and default interest; documentation failures trigger fixed penalties and evidentiary prejudice; repeat violations may be escalated as tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve non-arm's-length pricing may be jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax; creditor protection rules under the Companies Law (Cap. 113) enable fraudulent-trading claims against directors; banking facilities may be withdrawn, and disqualification orders issued.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Loss of Cyprus tax residence; repatriation of profits to the foreign parent's jurisdiction; denial of treaty benefits; penalty assessments for failure to maintain contemporaneous records; the Registrar of Companies may strike off the entity and banks may close its accounts.

Director personal liability: Nominee and shadow directors face civil liability for fraudulent trading where insolvency follows; criminal liability for conspiracy to mislead foreign tax authorities; cross-border enforcement instruments permit personal travel bans and asset-freezing orders.
Money Laundering / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Offence under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law (L.188(I)/2007 as amended): corporate fines up to EUR 1,000,000 and imprisonment of up to 14 years; confiscation and freezing orders; the body corporate and directors may be named in criminal proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML compliance officers face personal criminal prosecution for negligent failure to implement AML controls; asset seizure, travel bans, and extradition exposure apply; the entity's licence and bank relationships are terminated.
Late Filing / Non-Payment of Corporate Tax

Statutory sanction: Fixed late-filing penalty of EUR 100 plus EUR 50 per month of delay (statutory cap of EUR 500); late payment interest; 5% surcharge on overdue provisional tax; best-of-judgment assessments and enforced collection proceedings by the Tax Department.

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for fraudulent trading if the company continued trading with no reasonable prospect of settling its tax debt; courts may order wrongful-trading restitution, disqualify directors, and impose a travel ban until settlement is secured.
