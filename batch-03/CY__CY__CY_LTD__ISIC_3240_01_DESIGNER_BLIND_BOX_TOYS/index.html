Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DRC), Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Nicosia, Cyprus — online via the Ariadne e-Registration portal.

Governing statute

The Companies Law, Cap. 113 (as amended by Laws 76(I)/2021 and 2(I)/2022); Sections 4, 5, 7, 9, 11, 12, 21, 23, and 170. Registration of a private company limited by shares is effected under Section 5 upon delivery of the Memorandum and Articles, statutory declaration, and notice of registered office; the registered office obligation is set out in Section 21; mandatory filing of beneficial ownership data is required under the Companies (Amendment) Law provisions transposing EU AML obligations.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association (M&A) together with Form HE1 (Application for Registration of a Private Company), statutory declaration of compliance, notice of registered office address, and consent forms of the first directors and secretary.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 33 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private company limited by shares under Cap. 113; incorporation is permissible with a single share of €1.00 nominal value issued to the founding subscriber. No escrow or deposit account requirement is imposed by the Registrar; however, authorized capital above €1,000 attracts a proportional registration fee, and banks typically require an initial operating deposit for account activation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Fire Service of the Ministry of Justice and Public Order covering the production floor, spray booth, and raw-material storage areas; structural and building-plan approvals from the District Town Planning Authority under the Streets and Buildings Regulation Law, Cap. 96 and the Town and Country Planning Law; workplace clearance by the Department of Labour Inspection (DLI) under the Safety and Health at Work Law 89(I)/1996, including machine guarding, electrical installation inspections, extraction and ventilation systems for solvent vapours, painter's booth electrical classification, and first-aid provisions.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private company limited by shares under Cap. 113; incorporation is permissible with a single share of €1.00 nominal value issued to the founding subscriber. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance; official portal: Tax For All (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy).

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus-incorporated private limited company is automatically registered with the Tax Department upon incorporation via data transmission from the Registrar of Companies; the Tax Department issues the Tax Identification Number (TIC/TIN). The company must maintain a registered office in Cyprus and appoint a company secretary. For VAT purposes, the company must pre-register through the Tax Department's district office or Tax For All portal before making taxable supplies, submitting certificate of incorporation, registered office address, identity of directors, and expected turnover. A non-resident entity with a Cyprus branch must first register the overseas company with the Registrar of Companies, then apply to the Tax Department for tax registration and to the VAT district office for a Cyprus VAT number.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Cyprus-incorporated private limited company is automatically registered with the Tax Department upon incorporation via data transmission from the Registrar of Companies; the Tax Department issues the Tax Identification Number (TIC/TIN). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box or entity transparency election exists under Cyprus law; a Cyprus Ltd is a separate non-transparent taxable person for corporate income tax and VAT. Election-based reliefs include group loss relief (75%+ group relationship) and notional interest deduction (NID) on new equity; the participation exemption is automatic for qualifying dividends and capital gains. Hybrid mismatch treatment is mandatory under Cyprus adoption of EU ATAD and cannot be elected away.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 of taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months for Cyprus-established persons; no registration threshold applies to non-established persons making taxable supplies in Cyprus

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for supplies of goods physically exported from Cyprus to a destination outside the EU, including export sales of designer blind box toys to non-EU customers, provided the export is evidenced by a customs declaration accepted by Cyprus Customs and the goods leave the EU. Supplies of services to non-EU recipients with effective use and enjoyment outside Cyprus may be zero-rated if the supplier holds appropriate documentary evidence. Intra-EU distance sales to consumers are not zero-rated in Cyprus and must be declared via OSS at the destination member state.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Single Administrative Document / AES filing accepted by Cyprus Customs)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note evidencing dispatch from Cyprus and exit from the EU
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT confirmation showing settlement from the foreign buyer within 180 days
  • Proof of physical export (customs endorsement, carrier manifest, or port/terminal release receipt)

Cyprus currently has no general real-time fiscal clearance or e-invoice stamping mandate for B2B/B2C invoices; invoices can be issued on paper or electronically under EU VAT invoicing rules. VAT returns, EC sales lists and OSS schemes are filed electronically through the Tax For All portal. The EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) e-invoicing requirements are not yet transposed/effective in Cyprus.

The Tax Department verifies zero-rating against customs exit data, transport documents, and inward remittance evidence. If export proof is missing or the 180-day repatriation condition is not satisfied, output VAT becomes payable at the applicable rate from the supply date, input VAT deductions are reversed, and VAT refunds already paid are clawed back with statutory interest. Administrative penalties may be assessed up to the amount of tax understated, and fraud cases trigger criminal prosecution and fines/imprisonment under Cyprus VAT and Customs legislation. Assessments/customs post-clearance recovery may be made within six years, or without time limit in cases of fraud or wilful default.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus has complete current and capital account convertibility as a Eurozone member State, governed by Article 63 TFEU and the OECD Code of Liberalisation of Capital Movements. No Central Bank approval, exchange control permit, or prior declaration is required for inbound investment, profit remittance, dividend distribution, trade settlement, or intercompany financing. The Cyprus corporate tax regime applies a 12.5% unified corporate income tax; dividends paid to non-resident shareholders attract zero Cyprus withholding tax and zero Special Defence Contribution (SDC), while SDC at 17% applies only to distributions benefiting Cyprus-resident tax individuals. The temporary 2013 crisis-era capital controls were fully lifted by 2015 and have not been reimposed. For the designer blind box toys operation, euro-denominated flows with EU counterparties settle instantly via SEPA; USD and CNY/HKD-denominated e-commerce receipts are freely convertible to EUR on the domestic interbank market.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Cyprus imposes no surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline for export proceeds derived from designer toy sales. All USD, GBP, CNY, EUR and other currency export receipts may be retained in any currency, held domestically or offshore, and deployed freely for working capital, hedging, or investment. The commercial bank only applies transaction-amount thresholds for Balance of Payments statistical reporting to the Central Bank of Cyprus; underlying export invoices, air waybills, bills of lading, and customs declarations (Intrastat/Extrastat) must be retained for VAT and annual statutory audit substantiation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), established under the Central Bank of Cyprus Law of 2002 (Law 138(I)/2002); the national competent authority for banking supervision, anti-money laundering supervision of credit institutions and EMIs under Law 188(I)/2007, and custodian of Cyprus Eurosystem membership obligations; operates within the European System of Central Banks with no residual exchange-control powers following the repeal of the Exchange Control Law (Cap. 199) in 1994.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Viability rating: High for payment collection, FX conversion, and mass disbursement automation; Medium as the sole treasury rail. Cyprus-regulated EMIs operate under the Payment Services and Electronic Money Services Law of 2018 (Law 94(I)/2018), supervised by the Central Bank of Cyprus and EEA-passportable. Revolut Business, Wise, Payoneer Europe, and Lemonway can onboard fully remotely to connect with Shopify Payments, Stripe, and pay-out Chinese suppliers in CNY at competitive mid-market rates. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route shareholder or related-party capital through an MT103 SWIFT wire from a commercial bank or via SEPA Credit Transfer for EU investors, with the beneficiary account at a Bank of Cyprus or Hellenic Bank operating account. Use explicit remittance identification in Field 70 to avoid compliance routing delays: UNCREDITED CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION FOR SHARE ISSUE for equity; LOAN AGREEMENT DATED [X] FOR INTERCOMPANY CREDIT for shareholder loans; TRADE PAYMENT FOR DESIGNER TOY GOODS INVOICE [NUMBER] for trade inflows. Provide the supporting documentation simultaneously at the receiving bank: board resolution approving the transaction, allotment form (Form HE30) filed with the Cyprus Registrar of Companies or executed share transfer, signed loan agreement with commercial terms, and source-of-funds evidence being 6 to 12 months bank statements of the remitting entity. Amounts above EUR 10,000 trigger standard CDD under Law 188(I)/2007; cumulative inflows above EUR 50,000 in a 12-month window trigger enhanced due diligence and UBO re-verification. No Central Bank of Cyprus approval is required; the bank reports the transaction solely for Balance of Payments statistical purposes. For e-commerce funding, Shopify Payments and Stripe remit daily merchant settlement payouts as EUR or USD credit transfers referencing the payout ID, which must be reconciled automatically in the treasury module.Clearing rails matrix for the designer toy supply chain: (1) SEPA Credit Transfers for EU resin, paint, packaging, and subcontracted production suppliers with immediate settlement; (2) SWIFT MT103 direct remittances for Chinese, HK, Vietnamese, and Taiwanese vendors settling raw materials and outsourced roto-casting, with the commercial invoice number, HS Code 9503, and Incoterms specified in Field 70; (3) SWIFT MT202 cover payments through correspondent banks where split into MT103 and MT202 COV two-message architecture for USD-denominated beneficiary banks in China; (4) Commercial Letters of Credit for large production runs, issued by the Cyprus bank confirmed by a Chinese advising bank at sight or 90-day usance, covering sea and air freight shipments; (5) Documentary Collections D/P or D/A at sight for medium-value repeat orders; (6) Open account terms of 30 to 60 days for trade-credit-insured Eurozone wholesale customers that purchase blind box collections. The bank's FX desk executes spot conversion of EUR into USD, CNY, or HKD at order execution equal to the prevailing domestic interbank rate; the treasury function must pair inward merchant settlement currencies with outward supplier currency requirements to minimize bid-offer leakage. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Viability rating: High for payment collection, FX conversion, and mass disbursement automation; Medium as the sole treasury rail. Cyprus-regulated EMIs operate under the Payment Services and Electronic Money Services Law of 2018 (Law 94(I)/2018), supervised by the Central Bank of Cyprus and EEA-passportable. Revolut Business, Wise, Payoneer Europe, and Lemonway can onboard fully remotely to connect with Shopify Payments, Stripe, and pay-out Chinese suppliers in CNY at competitive mid-market rates. Critical legal constraint: client balances held by EMIs are safeguarded under Directive 2009/110/EC and are not deposits protected by the Cyprus Deposit Guarantee Scheme; liquidity risk and platform concentration risk therefore mandate daily sweeping of EMI balances to tier-1 banks. EMIs also require a full bank-grade KYC file and may refuse complex intercompany financing flows; they are appropriate for merchant receivables concentration and FX conversion, not for LC issuance or long-term capital warehousing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 500,000 aggregated controlled transactions in a fiscal year triggers mandatory Local File documentation for the Cyprus resident entity. EU Country-by-Country Reporting and Master File obligations apply where the ultimate parent group's consolidated annual revenue reaches EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus applies an implicit Economic Substance Doctrine through its statutory management-and-control test, OECD BEPS alignment, and the EU ATAD General Anti-Abuse Rule. A Cyprus designer-toy trading entity must demonstrate real local direction, day-to-day decision-making, staff, servicing capacity, premises, and operational risk control. Pure letterbox arrangement with directors located abroad and passive collection of e-commerce royalties will be challenged for tax residency and treaty access; banks and tax authorities may require economic substance declarations and supporting payroll/lease records.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal sanctions under Cyprus Income Tax Law (Law 118(I)/2002): fine up to EUR 10,000, imprisonment up to three years, or both; tax assessments reopened for six years; 10% surcharge, monthly late-payment surcharge up to 50%, and interest at 5% per annum on evaded tax

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in fraudulent understatement can be criminally prosecuted in their personal capacity; corporate veil may be pierced for restitution of unpaid tax and interest, with travel bans, asset freezes, extradition under extradition treaties, and director disqualification for up to five years
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of Cyprus tax residence benefits, IP Box relief, and DTA/treaty access; recharacterization of profits by the Tax Department based on economic substance; administrative penalties up to EUR 20,000; compulsory strike-off under the Cyprus Companies Law Cap. 113 and dissolution proceedings

Director personal liability: If directors signed declarations without substance or continued trading while insolvent, they become personally liable for group liabilities and tax debts; disqualification orders and personal fines are enforceable against named directors; Cyprus courts may impose civil damages for fraudulent reliance and foreign travel bans pending investigation
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Prosecution under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law (Law 188(I)/2007): imprisonment up to five years, criminal fines up to EUR 100,000, and confiscation of tainted assets; freezing orders, disgorgement of proceeds, and revocation of business licenses are statutorily available

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly permit laundering through the Cyprus Ltd face personal criminal liability, unlimited restitution, asset seizure, enforcement of travel bans, and imprisonment; the corporate veil is pierced by statute where officers are implicated as instrumental perpetrators or accomplices
