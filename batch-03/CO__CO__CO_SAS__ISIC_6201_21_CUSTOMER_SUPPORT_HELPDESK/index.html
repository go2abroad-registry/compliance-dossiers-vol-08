Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); official portal: www.dian.gov.co, MUISCA electronic platform

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a SAS must first register the company with the Cámara de Comercio through the RUES system; DIAN then issues the NIT automatically upon company registration. The company must update the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) online, obtain an electronic signature certificate, and activate the NIT for electronic invoicing. Non-resident foreign suppliers of digital services without a local entity must register for a foreign-entity NIT through DIAN’s special procedure and may appoint a tax representative where required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Colombian SAS is taxed as a corporation at entity level, not as a pass-through, and territorial income principles apply for the corporate income tax. — A foreign entity establishing a SAS must first register the company with the Cámara de Comercio through the RUES system; DIAN then issues the NIT automatically upon company registration. The company must update the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) online, obtain an electronic signature certificate, and activate the NIT for electronic invoicing. Non-resident foreign suppliers of digital services without a local entity must register for a foreign-entity NIT through DIAN’s special procedure and may appoint a tax representative where required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Colombian SAS is taxed as a corporation at entity level, not as a pass-through, and territorial income principles apply for the corporate income tax.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Colombian law. An SAS may elect the SIMPLE combined regime if eligible (gross income below 80,000 UVT), and certain new small enterprises may elect the ZESE or other temporary tax incentive regimes, but no hybrid-mismatch or pass-through classification election is available for income tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic turnover threshold triggers compulsory VAT registration; registration is required from the first peso of taxable supply or import. Foreign digital service suppliers supplying services to non-business consumers in Colombia must register for VAT and each invoice/import operation, regardless of turnover. The optional SIMPLE regime, available to small taxpayers with gross annual income up to 80,000 UVT, is not an exemption from VAT registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, zero-rating is available only when the goods are physically exported from Colombia to a destination outside Colombian customs territory and the export is evidenced by a valid DEX customs declaration. For services, including helpdesk, conversational bot and cloud support services, zero-rating applies when the service is rendered to and 'used or enjoyed' abroad by a non-resident customer, provided the service falls under Article 481 of the Colombian Tax Statute and meets the material export-verification rules.

  • DEX electronic export declaration accepted by DIAN with exit confirmation
  • Commercial invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Packing list and transport evidence
  • Certificate of inward foreign exchange repatriation or Exterior Remittance Certificate
  • Contract or service agreement demonstrating the offshore recipient and offshore use for services

Electronic invoicing is mandatory for all VAT-responsible entities and must be issued through DIAN-authorised software generated under the factura electrónica de venta with real-time prior validation. Invoices are transmitted via API/web services to DIAN’s MUISCA platform; each invoice receives a unique electronic identification code (CUFE or CUDS) and digital stamp before delivery to the customer. Continuous electronic audit file submission is required, and deviations are subject to administrative sanctions.

DIAN conducts transfer-pricing and export-fraud audits with a five-year statute of limitations; incorrect or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger retrospective VAT clawback, default interest, and severe administrative penalties, including inaccurate-invoice penalties of up to 100% of the omitted VAT. Documentary falsity or intentional abuse may constitute tax fraud and smuggling-related offences carrying criminal penalties and possible disqualification from tax benefits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia maintains a partially regulated capital account. Current account transactions, including service exports from helpdesk and software activity, are generally freely convertible through the foreign exchange market on the basis of bank-supported declarations. Capital account operations, including foreign direct investment into a CO_SAS, profit remittances, loan principal repayments, and outbound investment, must be registered with Banco de la República to acquire legal repatriation rights. For an ISIC 6201 helpdesk subsidiary, profit remittance is permitted after payment of Colombian corporate income tax and the applicable dividend withholding tax on non-resident shareholders, provided the original equity injection was registered and the remitted amount is supported by audited financial statements and documentary evidence of goodwill, invoicing, and operational substance in Colombia.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds derived from software, helpdesk, support, and cloud services rendered by the Colombian S.A.S. must be channeled exclusively through an authorized intermediary of the Colombian foreign exchange market (a supervised commercial bank). The statutory channeling obligation must be completed within the regulatory timeline set by Banco de la República, commonly no longer than six months from the date of service billing or receipt, with authorized extensions available in justified events. The mandatory conversion ratio is 100% for foreign currency proceeds from service exports, unless the bank has formally enabled a permitted foreign currency deposit account and the bank registers the exemption; no partial voluntary conversion ratio applies. The channeling requirement is enforced through the submission of the corresponding foreign exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio) to the receiving bank, with the service invoice, signed contract, and proof of actual delivery of support services kept in the AML/KYC dossier.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), acting through the Junta Directiva and the Comité de Reglamentos de Cambio, with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia authorized to supervise regulated foreign exchange intermediaries.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI accounts, such as Wise Pro, Payoneer, Payoneer B2B, or Deel-type payment partners, have no standing to execute the mandatory Colombian foreign exchange declaration or to register foreign investment with Banco de la República. They may serve only as auxiliary settlement rails for small-value inbound subscriptions or platform payouts where no Colombian legal FX obligation arises, but they cannot replace a licensed Colombian intermediary for dividend repatriation, export proceeds channeling, foreign capital entry, or tax remittance. Use of an offshore EMI as the principal treasury account creates a high compliance risk for the CO_SAS and may lead to unregistered capital flows and administrative sanctions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholders must remit capital to the Colombian S.A.S. via SWIFT MT103 directly to the conventional account opened with a Tier-1 Colombian bank, using a mandatory reference to 'foreign investment' and the corporate tax ID (NIT). The receiving bank will direct the transaction to the proper foreign exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio por Inversión Internacional) contained in the Colombian FX regime; the funds must be registered with Banco de la República through the bank's online system before being released for operating purposes. All supporting evidence, including the signed capital contribution minutes, apostilled shareholder resolutions, source-bank records, and the investor's identification documents, must be uploaded to the compliance queue. Proceeds cannot be routed through a private individual's account, a non-bank PSP, or an offshore EMI; such routing invalidates the investment registration and blocks future dividend repatriation.Outbound disbursements for international software vendors, cloud infrastructure, subcontractors, and shareholder distributions must be executed with SWIFT MT103 payment instructions from the Colombian screen-based bank platform, using correct FX purpose codes and the underlying invoice or board resolution. For larger capex or one-off procurement with counterparties requiring payment guarantees, a commercial letter of credit is permissible under UCP600 and is normally issued by the Colombian bank's trade finance desk. Local COP-denominated operating disbursements to Colombian vendors, employees, and tax authorities may use ACH/PSE-type domestic clearing rails. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Deadline falls in the period from the 9th to the 21st calendar day of April following the fiscal year-end (fiscal year must be 31 December for a standard S.A.S.), the exact date being assigned by DIAN based on the last two digits of the taxpayer's NIT. Extensions are not granted beyond this DIAN calendar; quarterly prepayments (pagos trimestrales) are compulsory during the year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The mandatory Local File and Master File obligations apply to an S.A.S. with total gross assets or gross revenues exceeding 100,000 UVT (COP 4,847,192,000 for 2025) or to taxpayers whose foreign-related-party transactions exceed 10,000 UVT (COP 484,719,200 for 2025). Additionally, a formal Transfer Pricing Informative Return (Form 120) must be filed annually when the taxpayer reaches the 100,000 UVT threshold or carries out cross-border transactions with related parties above the de minimis amount, even if the annual return shows no tax. The S.A.S. helpdesk company must also provide a contemporaneous TP study for any cross-border intercompany cloud hosting, software royalties, management service fees, or technical support charges.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not operate a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime for ordinary commercial companies; however, economic substance is consequential for (i) claiming benefits under tax treaties, (ii) qualifying as a Colombian Holding Company (CHC), and (iii) rebutting the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR / abuso en materia tributaria) contained in Article 869 of the Tax Code. A shell S.A.S. without real staff, premises, activity, or decision-making in Colombia will not be recognized for treaty or CHC purposes. In the software context, DIAN may scrutinize the true situs of server operations, support functions, client contracts, and intellectual property ownership to prevent conduit arrangements. Transfer pricing penalties of up to 200% of the understated tax and the automatic disallowance of the payment are triggered when substance and documentation fail to demonstrate arm's length behavior.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

International Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Underreporting

Statutory sanction: Under Colombian tax criminal law (Código Penal Tributario, Law 1943/2018 as complemented by Law 2277/2022), tax evasion exceeding 250 monthly minimum wages (approximately COP 325,000,000 for 2025) is punishable by imprisonment of 48 to 108 months and fines up to 15,000 SMMLV; false or inflated cost invoices generate a penalty of up to 200% of the understated tax, plus cancellation of the RUT (tax registration) and criminal referral. DIAN may impose daily administrative fines of up to 10,000 UVT and secure the disqualification of the taxpayer from public procurement.

Director personal liability: Directors and administrators of the S.A.S. who intentionally conceal revenues or who approve the use of simulated contracts are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall and related penalties under Article 794 of the Tax Code and Article 78 of Law 1258/2008. Criminal judges may impose travel bans, asset embargoes, and confiscation of luxury property; upon a final criminal conviction, the director faces imprisonment for up to 9 years and civil restitution of at least 300% of the evaded sum.
Shell Company Abuse and Substance Sham

Statutory sanction: DIAN may disregard the shell under the economic substance doctrine and reattribute the revenue to the Colombian entity, imposing a 35% tax plus a penalty of 200% of the underpaid tax and a monthly late-payment interest surcharge. For violations of the SAS law, the Superintendencia de Sociedades may dissolve the company and order the liquidation of assets for sham transactions.

Director personal liability: Under Article 42 of Law 1258/2008, shareholders and directors who use the S.A.S. as an instrument of fraud, tax evasion, or abuse of corporate assets are personally liable. The civil court can pierce the corporate veil and order the director to pay all debts of the entity, including withholding taxes and social security obligations. The legal representative may also be barred from leaving Colombia while criminal and tax investigations are pending, and in aggravated cases, the director is sentenced to 6 to 12 years' imprisonment.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Disclosures

Statutory sanction: Money laundering aggravating penalties under Law 1762/2015 authorize imprisonment of 10 to 30 years if illicit proceeds are disguised through corporate vehicles. The S.A.S. can be fined up to 1,000 SMMLV (approximately COP 1.3 billion) for failing to maintain UBO records; and the commercial registration can be cancelled, leading to involuntary dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal criminal responsibility for approving transactions that involve unverified third-party funds. Under Colombian anti-money laundering jurisprudence, a director who knowingly signs off on suspicious client onboarding or uses PayPal/Stripe as straw intermediaries can be indicted for 'lavado de activos,' resulting in preventive imprisonment, a ban on managing commercial companies, and the seizure of all personal and corporate assets linked to the alleged illicit activity.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Cross-Border Cost Shifting

Statutory sanction: DIAN will adjust the taxable income and disallow the overstated deductions, applying a 35% additional CIT plus a penalty of 200% of the understated tax on the adjustment. Failure to file Form 120 triggers a fine of 5% of the aggregate value of the related-party transactions or up to 10,000 UVT, and the TP documentation itself, if false, can generate a 'sanción por inexactitud' of up to 100% of the tax determined by DIAN.

Director personal liability: The administrator who signs the Form 110 swear that the expenses are true and arm's length becomes personally liable for the assessed tax adjustment if the supporting evidence is proven fictitious. Severe cases may be prosecuted as aggravated fraud, carrying imprisonment of 6 to 9 years, a ban on holding public office, and an order to pay restitution of the full tax debt plus interest.
Failure to Remit Withholding and VAT on Cross-Border Payments

Statutory sanction: Withholding agents who fail to declare and pay at source are liable for a fine of up to 200% of the amount not withheld or paid and daily late interest; DIAN may automatically reject the deduction of the underlying payment. The S.A.S. can also be held jointly liable for the foreign beneficiary's Colombian tax deficiency when the payment was made without proper withholding.

Director personal liability: Directors who personally decide to omit withholding liabilities are subject to personal joint liability for unpaid taxes and penalties. If the amount exceeds 4,000 UVT (approximately COP 193,887,680 for 2025), the director may face criminal tax evasion prosecution, leading to imprisonment of 3 to 7 years, civil restitution, and a travel ban pending payment of the full tax damage.
