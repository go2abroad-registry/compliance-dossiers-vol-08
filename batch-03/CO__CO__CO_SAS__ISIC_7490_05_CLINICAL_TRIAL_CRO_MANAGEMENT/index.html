Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN). Official portal: www.dian.gov.co; primary transactional platform: MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicios y Control Automatizado) at muisca.dian.gov.co.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity (e.g., a French parent or a foreign shareholder) that will participate in a Colombian S.A.S. or that itself carries on business in Colombia must obtain a Colombian NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) via the RUT (Registro Único Tributario). Step-by-step: (1) Appoint a legal representative or attorney-in-fact domiciled in Colombia, evidenced by a notarized power of attorney, apostilled and officially translated into Spanish; (2) file the foreign entity's certificate of existence (certificado de existencia y representación legal) or equivalent registration documentation, apostilled; (3) submit the RUT application telematically through the MUISCA platform using the credentials of the Colombian-appointed representative, indicating the relevant tax obligations (income tax under Articles 12 and 20-24 of the Estatuto Tributario, VAT, withholding, and if applicable PE registration); (4) obtain the NIT and activate the DIAN electronic signature (firma electrónica) for the representative; (5) if holding Colombian-source revenue, register as a VAT responsible agent and request authorization to issue electronic invoices under the 'validación previa' scheme. Processing time is generally 5-10 business days with complete documentation; the mere acquisition of a NIT/RUT by a foreign entity does not automatically create a permanent establishment, but actual service-delivery activities in Colombia by the CRO should be re-characterized as PE risk under Article 24 of the Estatuto Tributario, reincorporating the OECD/BEPS test. For a newly incorporated S.A.S., the same procedure is completed by the SAS's legal representative and the projected tax ID is issued simultaneously with the RUT.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope. A Colombian Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) is a capital company subject to Colombian corporate income tax (CIT) at the headline rate of 35% on its worldwide income (territorial extension for worldwide income of Colombian residents, with foreign tax credit relief under Article 12 of the Tax Statute). The SAS is not a pass-through entity for Colombian tax purposes; dividends paid to foreign shareholders are subject to remittance withholding tax (7.5% under the general rate, reduced only if the shareholder is a resident of a treaty jurisdiction). The S.A.S. is not inherently a tax-exempt entity. If the SAS operates as a branch/PE of a foreign entity, the PE is likewise taxed at entity level on Colombian-source income, with branch-remittance WHT on remittances under Article 407 of the Tax Statute. — A foreign entity (e.g., a French parent or a foreign shareholder) that will participate in a Colombian S.A.S. or that itself carries on business in Colombia must obtain a Colombian NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) via the RUT (Registro Único Tributario). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope. A Colombian Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) is a capital company subject to Colombian corporate income tax (CIT) at the headline rate of 35% on its worldwide income (territorial extension for worldwide income of Colombian residents, with foreign tax credit relief under Article 12 of the Tax Statute). The SAS is not a pass-through entity for Colombian tax purposes; dividends paid to foreign shareholders are subject to remittance withholding tax (7.5% under the general rate, reduced only if the shareholder is a resident of a treaty jurisdiction). The S.A.S. is not inherently a tax-exempt entity. If the SAS operates as a branch/PE of a foreign entity, the PE is likewise taxed at entity level on Colombian-source income, with branch-remittance WHT on remittances under Article 407 of the Tax Statute.

Available election forms

Colombia does not have a U.S.-style 'check-the-box' election. The relevant statutory elections are: (i) the SIMPLE regime (régimen simple de tributación), optional for an S.A.S. whose prior-year gross income does not exceed 80,000 UVT (approximately COP 4.0 billion for FY2025) and that is not engaged in certain excluded activities; SIMPLE consolidates CIT, local taxes and VAT withholding into a single rate between 7% and 14% plus a 0% to 7% industry addition; (ii) the election to apply treaty-reduced dividend rates subject to OECD/BEPS beneficial ownership and substance tests; (iii) the mandatory application (not an election) of hybrid mismatch adjustment rules enacted by Law 1819/2016 (Articles 128-1 to 128-23 of the Tax Statute) which require a classification comparison of hybrid instruments/entities with foreign jurisdictions (e.g., French S.A. vs. Colombian S.A.S. classification) rather than a filing election; (iv) the election to deduct expenses on a cash or accrual basis for certain controlled entities? Not applicable by default. There is no Colombian check-the-box election for entity classification as a disregarded entity; the S.A.S. is always a taxable entity in Colombia.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no de minimis turnover threshold for legal entities (S.A.S.) providing taxable services in Colombia: registration as an IVA responsable (general regime) is mandatory from the first taxable supply. The simplified IVA regime (régimen simplificado) is available only to natural persons with prior-year gross income below 3,500 UVT and meeting qualitative conditions; it is not available to an S.A.S. Non-resident suppliers of digital services to Colombian final consumers are also subject to mandatory simplified registration without any turnover threshold. For export-oriented CRO services, the SAS must be registered as an IVA-responsible entity with an 'exportador de servicios' status granted by DIAN.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported services are excluded from IVA (effectively 0% VAT) under Article 481(1) of the Estatuto Tributario (amended by Law 1819/2016) when all of the following conditions are met: (i) the services are performed within or from Colombian territory; (ii) the services are used or enjoy exclusive benefit abroad; (iii) the final beneficiaries are non-residents with no domicile or permanent establishment in Colombia; (iv) the consideration is received in foreign currency and the corresponding foreign exchange is surrendered to the Colombian foreign exchange market within the legal period; and (v) the taxpayer has prior registration as an 'exportador de servicios' in the RUT with DIAN. For a clinical-trial CRO, qualifying services include protocol design, clinical data management, biostatistical analysis, pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, central laboratory services, and medical writing, provided that the sponsor is a foreign biopharma without a Colombian PE and the data and intellectual outputs are consumed by the sponsor outside Colombia. Services performed in Colombia for a local affiliate with a Colombian PE would not qualify. The taxpayer must also file the annual informative return of export services and maintain documentary evidence of the foreign beneficiary's status.

  • Declaración de Exportación de Servicios (Electronic Export Services Declaration) filed through the MUISCA platform with DIAN, including the service contract, scope of work and value
  • Factura Electrónica de Venta para Exportación (Electronic export invoice) containing the CUFE code and the DIAN validación previa acceptance number
  • Certificado de cambio / Inward Remittance Certificate (Foreign Exchange Certification) issued by an authorized Colombian financial intermediary evidencing repatriation and sale of the foreign currency on the Colombian exchange market within the 180-day deadline

Mandatory electronic invoicing (factura electrónica) with real-time fiscal clearance is fully operative under the 'validación previa' model: since 1 August 2024 all VAT-responsible taxpayers must issue invoices only through DIAN-authorized software (software propio or software tecnológico) and transmit each invoice in UBL/CII XML format to DIAN via certified SOAP/REST APIs; the invoice receives a DIAN authorization number before being issued to the client. Each invoice bears a unique digital stamping code (CUFE – Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) generated from the taxpayer's NIT and invoice data. Export invoices must use the specific 'factura electrónica de venta para exportación' scheme, with the export service declaration filed in MUISCA. A copy of every authorized export invoice is transmitted in real-time to DIAN; failure to obtain prior validation renders the invoice ineffective and may trigger VAT recharacterization.

DIAN has five (5) years from the due filing date to review and reject an export zero-rating position; where the conditions of Article 481 E.T. are not met, DIAN will retroactively assess the 19% IVA that should have been charged, plus moratory interest calculated from the original due date of each filing and from any IVA refund previously claimed, and will apply the inaccuracy penalty under Article 647 E.T. (10% of the tax difference, or 20% when triggered by false, inexact or unlawful invoices/documents). Unjustified export zero-rating claims that lead to a final IVA assessment are also subject to the general limitations on fiscal audit and to penalty for claim-excess under Article 648 E.T. for excessive compensations/refunds. In case of fraud, the criminal tax evasion provisions of Article 271 of the Colombian Penal Code (as amended by Laws 2010/2019 and 2277/2022) may apply, with imprisonment of 48-108 months when the defrauded amount exceeds the statutory threshold (300 monthly minimum wages); additionally, the exporter may be suspended or removed from the 'exportador de servicios' registry and barred from issuing zero-rated export invoices for a period of 24 months.
