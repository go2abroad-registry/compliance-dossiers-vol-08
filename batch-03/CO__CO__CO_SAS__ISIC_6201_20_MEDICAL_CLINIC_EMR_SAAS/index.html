Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-in capital for S.A.S. The subscribed capital may be paid in installments, and the term for full payment cannot exceed two years. No escrow account is required by law, except that in-kind contributions must be supported by an independent valuation and sworn statement.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); taxpayer portal: MUISCA (https://muisca.dian.gov.co)

E-government filing portal

The Colombian S.A.S. is required to register with the Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio) upon incorporation. As part of that registration, DIAN automatically assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). The company must then complete its RUT (Registro Único Tributario) profile, obtain an electronic signature suitable for DIAN procedures, and appoint a legal representative with authority to manage tax filings. For a foreign-owned S.A.S., the incorporating resolution, private deed, or notarized instrument must identify all foreign shareholders and their respective identification documentation. The legal representative must be a resident individual authorized to interact with DIAN and may be granted power of attorney through notarial or apostilled documents. VAT registration and e-invoicing activation occur through the same RUT system. Foreign persons acting directly as service providers or suppliers must also obtain a NIT via the DIAN simplified enrollment process, but the local S.A.S. is the standard entity for software export operations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No entity classification election is available. Colombian legislation does not provide a check-the-box regime or any analogous US-style classification election. A Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) is mandatorily treated as a corporation and tax resident of Colombia for income tax purposes. No fiscally transparent alternative is available at the S.A.S. level. Colombia applies domestic anti-hybrid mismatch rules for cross-border arrangements, and affected taxpayers must assess and disclose any hybrid mismatch resulting in a double deduction or deduction-without-inclusion outcome.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold applies to corporate entities; every S.A.S. engaged in taxable activities must register in the RUT as an IVA responsible party before commencing operations. For natural persons, a simplified-regime threshold may apply, but it is not available to the corporate entity.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of services qualifies for the 0% VAT rate when the service is used or exploited exclusively outside Colombia, the recipient is domiciled abroad and is not a Colombian entity or individual, and the supplier is a Colombian resident. For the EMR SaaS service to a foreign medical clinic, the supplier must evidence through the commercial agreement, technical configuration, and usage logs that (i) the clinical users and data processing are located outside Colombia, (ii) the software is installed or accessed for a foreign operator, and (iii) no Colombian-sourced benefit or taxable consumption occurs. Exports of goods are zero-rated upon physical departure from Colombia under the appropriate customs export procedure.

  • DIAN-validated electronic export invoice (factura electrónica de venta) bearing the applicable CUFE/CUDS stamp and referencing the export service contract.
  • Foreign-destination evidence package comprising the executed SaaS agreement with the non-Colombian medical clinic, technical service-certificate, remote access or cloud-delivery logs demonstrating exclusive offshore consumption, and, for physical goods, the Declaración de Exportación and Bill of Lading or Airway Bill.
  • Foreign-currency payment evidence including the inward remittance certificate issued by a Colombian authorized foreign exchange intermediary or the Certificado de Liquidación de Divisas proving repatriation of export proceeds.

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing. The S.A.S. must issue all sales invoices through a DIAN-authorized Operador Tecnológico or directly through the MUISCA system. Each invoice must contain either a CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) or CUDS (Código Único de Documento Soporte) and be transmitted to DIAN in XML format for validation before or at the time of issuance. The e-invoicing system requires electronic delivery to the recipient, storage in DIAN archival environments, and compliance with the technical API or web-service requirements established by DIAN. Digital stamping and validation status are managed exclusively by DIAN in real time.

DIAN may audit the export zero-rating claim at any time within the general statute of limitations. If the zero-rating is disallowed, DIAN will retroactively assess VAT at the 19% rate on the gross export invoice, together with default interest and the penalty for omission or inaccuracy, which commonly equals 100% of the amount omitted and can be increased to 200% when the misstatement was willful or involved fabricated documentation. Fraudulent export declarations, sham offshore contracts, or false inward-remittance certificates may also give rise to criminal liability for tax fraud, money-laundering, or customs offenses, as well as suspension from the e-invoicing and exporter regime.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) operates under a free floating exchange-rate regime with full convertibility for current-account transactions, including trade in goods and services, dividend and profit remittances, and interest payments, subject to mandatory channeling through the authorised foreign-exchange market. Capital-account transactions, including foreign direct investment, equity subscriptions, and financial swaps, are assessable and must be formally registered with Banco de la República. Profit remittance by a foreign-owned S.A.S. is lawful once annual financial statements are approved and corporate income tax, withholding tax on dividends, and any applicable remittance taxes are settled. No exchange-denial or administrative restriction applies to genuine operating inflows/outflows, but a hard-currency shortage or a 60% limit on foreign participation would trigger heightened regulatory review. For a fully-owned non-resident S.A.S., the practical limit is a greater documentary burden, not convertibility failure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Colombian foreign-exchange rules, proceeds from exported goods must be repatriated and channeled through an authorised intermediary within six (6) months from the date of the shipping document or customs declaration. For exported services, the same six (6) month term is counted from the date of invoice or completion of the service. The proceeds must be surrendered in the formal FX market at the bank's prevailing reference rate; the mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts intended for local use is 100%. Holding export proceeds abroad requires prior authorisation of Banco de la República. Failure to surrender within the statutory term may result in administrative fines and potential criminal exposure under Colombian foreign-exchange regulations.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia) in coordination with the Foreign Exchange Commission (Comisión de Cambios). Foreign-exchange regulation and enforcement are principally governed by Decree 1735 of 1993, the External Regulations (DCIN 83, Circular Financiera), and the supervisory oversight of the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for offshore digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) accounts. Colombian law does not grant foreign EMI licences the status of a regulated banking or foreign-exchange intermediary; holding operational treasury funds and receiving export proceeds in Colombia requires settlement through a licensed commercial bank or a locally licensed SEDPE (Sociedad Especializada en Depósitos y Pagos Electrónicos). A local digital wallet with a licensed custodial bank may be used only as a complementary collection rail, not as the primary treasury or forex clearing channel. All high-value SaaS billing, export receipts, and profit remittance flows must pass through a formal banking account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All non-resident shareholder equity contributions must be wired from the investor's bank to the Colombian S.A.S. capital-escrow account using SWIFT MT103 for same-day value, referencing the company's NIT and the 'Registro de Inversión Internacional' (foreign-investment registration) procedure. The wire must be presented to the Colombian bank's foreign-exchange desk together with the formal investment declaration (Form 1 under Banco de la República's exchange forms) and a statutory audit confirmation. FX conversion to COP occurs at the market rate; the bank obtains a 'cambio' supporting statement and registers the capital flow with Banco de la República. A notarized capital subscription agreement and proof of inbound wire from the beneficial owner's account are required to secure profit-repatriation rights.SaaS license fees, cloud-service payments, and subcontractor expenses must be settled through a regulated bank-managed FX channel. A SWIFT MT103 wire instructing the Colombian bank to convert COP and transfer USD/EUR is made, accompanied by a service-import declaration (Form 12 or equivalent) and the underlying contract or commercial invoice. For physical or embedded-hardware components, a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) may be used when a transport document and customs entry are required.
