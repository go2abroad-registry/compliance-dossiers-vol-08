Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cyprus Tax Department (Τμήμα Φορολογίας / Department of Taxation), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus. Electronic filing portal: TAXISnet (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy). VAT registration, VAT returns and EU sales lists are lodged through the Tax Department's ARIADNE (Αριάδνη) electronic VAT platform. Customs export processing is handled by the Cyprus Customs and Excise Department (Τμήμα Τελωνείων) under the EU Export Control System (ECS/ECS2), requiring a CY-prefixed EORI number.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 – Company registration: A Cyprus private limited company (CY_LTD) is incorporated with the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DRCOR); Cyprus imposes no minimum capital requirement and foreign shareholding is fully permitted. Step 2 – Automatic income-tax TIN: Upon incorporation, DRCOR transmits the company profile to the Cyprus Tax Department, which automatically allocates an 8-digit Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); no separate income-tax application is required for a Cypriot-incorporated trading vehicle that is managed and controlled in Cyprus. Step 3 – Foreign-company TIN: A foreign entity that has a Cyprus permanent establishment or central management and control must file the income-tax company registration form (T.D. 2001 or the electronic equivalent) with the Tax Department, attaching certified constitutional documents, certificate of registration/good standing, board resolution, power of attorney, and proof of the Cyprus establishment. Step 4 – VAT registration: File Form VAT 101 (Application for VAT Registration) through the Tax Department's ARIADNE electronic VAT portal. The Cyprus VAT registration certificate bears the VAT number in the format 'CY' + 8-digit TIN + one check letter. Non-EU-established applicants must simultaneously appoint a tax representative established in Cyprus under the Cyprus VAT Law. Step 5 – Customs/EORI: Obtain a Cyprus EORI number (prefix 'CY') from Cyprus Customs to lodge electronic export declarations and transport manifests. Step 6 – Ancillary registrations: Where the company has Cypriot payroll, register with Social Insurance Services (GESY) and the employers' PAYE/health levy.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus domestic law has no check-the-box or look-through entity classification election; a Cyprus limited company is inherently opaque and taxed at entity level (12.5% corporate income tax on worldwide income for resident companies). Available statutory elections relevant to a bitumen trading group include: (i) group relief election for intra-group loss surrender; (ii) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on new qualifying equity; (iii) Intellectual Property (IP) box election (80% deemed deduction on qualifying IP income); (iv) tonnage tax election for qualifying shipping/maritime operations; (v) deemed dividend distribution election for close companies under the Special Defence Contribution regime; (vi) pooled foreign tax credit election; and (vii) merger/division roll-over elections. Hybrid mismatch rules implementing ATAD II (Law 141(I)/2021, in force 1 January 2022) apply mandatorily and non-electively to hybrid entities, hybrid instruments and double-deduction arrangements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Republic of Cyprus is a Eurozone member and the euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account. There are no domestic exchange-control laws restricting the use or transfer of euros. Dividend distributions and branch profit remittances by a Cyprus-resident private limited company are generally free from statutory withholding taxes to non-resident shareholders, subject to EU anti-money-laundering, sanctions screening, and standard bank verification requirements. The Central Bank of Cyprus retains prudential and balance-of-payments statistical monitoring but does not impose conversion requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory export-proceeds surrender rule exists for Cyprus exporters. Export receipts for bitumen and asphalt products sold on containerized wholesale terms may be retained in euros or in any permissible foreign currency and need not be converted. There is no fixed statutory timeline for forced repatriation; proceeds should be domiciled with a Cyprus/EU-licensed banking institution under normal commercial settlement terms and be supported by export documentation, freight documentation, and customs declarations for tax and CBC statistical reporting purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza Kyprou), Nicosia, Cyprus

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium as a supplemental digital rail only. Legal constraints are significant for offshore non-EEA electronic money institutions; such entities generally lack the required Cyprus/EU authorisation and cannot provide compliant SEPA or euro clearing services. An EU-licensed EMI with a valid passport into Cyprus, including a Cyprus-authorised Electronic Money Institution under the Payment Services Law and Central Bank of Cyprus supervision, may be used for ancillary low-value payments, but it must not replace the primary tier-1 Cypriot banking relationship. Offshore EMIs are not acceptable for trade settlements involving letters of credit or import/export cargo financing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injections should be executed as a SWIFT MT103 credit transfer to the Cyprus Ltd corporate account using the beneficiary IBAN and BIC. The payment message field 70 must state 'Capital Contribution – Equity Subscription' and reference the board resolution and share allotment number. On the Cypriot bank's declaration form, where requested, the payment should be categorised as a direct-investment capital transaction. The supporting verification bundle must include the board resolution authorising the capital increase, minutes of the share allotment, updated register of members, evidence of source of funds from the investor's bank, and certified KYC/CTF forms.
