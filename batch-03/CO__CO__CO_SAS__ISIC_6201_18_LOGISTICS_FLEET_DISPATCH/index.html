Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá / Registro Único Empresarial (RUES), under the supervision of the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), as amended; Código de Comercio arts. 98, 110-112, 353; Decree 1074 of 2015 (RUES); Law 222 of 1995 (commercial oversight).

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución e Inscripción en el Registro Único Empresarial (RUES), evidenced by the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 14 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No legal minimum subscribed capital under Ley 1258/2008; payment terms are free but full payment of subscribed capital must occur within two years from incorporation; no bank escrow account is required for cash contributions unless the bylaws impose such mechanism.

Funding & unlock path: For the registered office, obtain the fire safety approval from the Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos de Bogotá when the premises exceed 100 m²; perform structural load inspection for server racks and equipment; issue the Sistema de Gestión de Seguridad y Salud en el Trabajo (SG-SST) program under Resolution 0312 of 2019, and secure the occupational risk insurance enrollment before commencing employee duties.

Paid-in capital rule: No legal minimum subscribed capital under Ley 1258/2008; payment terms are free but full payment of subscribed capital must occur within two years from incorporation; no bank escrow account is required for cash contributions unless the bylaws impose such mechanism.

RESOLVED: That the subscription and payment obligations of all shares in the initial capital of the Company be approved as set forth in the incorporation document, and that the share registry reflect each founding shareholder's ownership. FURTHER RESOLVED: That the registered office of the Company be established at Calle 93 No. 14-76, Oficina 501, Bogotá D.C., Colombia, and that all corporate purposes be ratified. FURTHER RESOLVED: That [Name] be appointed President and Legal Representative and [Name] be appointed Secretary; both officers, jointly or severally as provided by law, are authorized to execute and file all registrations with the Cámara de Comercio and DIAN, and to retain counsel for corporate governance compliance.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial license is mandated for ISIC 6201 (computer programming and related activities); the Minister of Commerce, Industry and Tourism (MinCIT) is the relevant sector authority for software export benefits, while DIAN remains the regulatory enforcement body.

Category: Not applicable — Fleet Route Optimization SaaS and Real-Time Telematics software services are subject to free-regime commercial activity under Decree 1074/2015; no manufacturing, technical, or concesión permit is required for the software scope.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – portal www.dian.gov.co; transactional portal Muisca for e-filing, RUT and e-invoicing

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must appoint a Colombian legal representative/attorney-in-fact with a notarized power of attorney, prove valid corporate existence, and file the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) with DIAN through the Muisca portal to obtain an NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). Where the vehicle is an S.A.S., the entity is first registered with the local Cámara de Comercio and thereafter obtains and activates its NIT in the RUT with DIAN.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity must appoint a Colombian legal representative/attorney-in-fact with a notarized power of attorney, prove valid corporate existence, and file the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) with DIAN through the Muisca portal to obtain an NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). Where the vehicle is an S.A.S., the entity is first registered with the local Cámara de Comercio and thereafter obtains and activates its NIT in the RUT with DIAN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Colombia has no U.S.-style check-the-box election; an S.A.S. is a separate taxpayer. Available elective mechanisms include the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (SIMPLES) for taxpayers under the annual UVT threshold, the holding company regime for foreign passive income under Law 2010 of 2019, and contracts for tax stability or megainvestments for qualified projects. Hybrid mismatch rules under Law 2010 and Decree 599 of 2022 govern denial or symmetric treatment of deductible hybrid payments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic activity threshold applies. Any person performing taxable sales of goods or services in Colombia must register in the RUT as a VAT responsible party before commencing operations. Non-resident digital service suppliers are required to register, charge and remit VAT without any local turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of tangible goods from Colombia to abroad qualifies for the 0% VAT rate upon proving physical departure. Services, including logistics fleet optimization and telematics SaaS, qualify for zero-rating where the recipient is a foreign person not domiciled in Colombia and the service is consumed or used exclusively outside Colombia. The supplier must be VAT registered and maintain contemporaneous evidence of the foreign recipient, service performance, and actual export use.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación submitted via MUISCA/SICEX)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill / electronic transport document
  • Commercial invoice and packing list marked 'EXPORTACION'
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas)
  • Signed SaaS subscription agreement or service contract with foreign counterparty

All VAT responsible taxpayers must issue electronic invoices using an DIAN-authorized software provider or DIAN's free electronic invoicing application. Every invoice must include the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) and its XML payload must be transmitted to and validated in real time through DIAN's Muisca WebService (API). Digital certificates (X.509) and SHA-384 signatures are mandated; export invoices require digital stamping and, where appropriate, an electronic 'documento soporte'.

DIAN will retroactively deny the zero rating, reclassify the operation as subject to 19% VAT, reject all related input VAT refunds and credits, and assess the omitted tax with moratory interest plus the 'sanción por inexactitud' of up to 200% of the understated tax under Articles 648 and 650 of the Tax Code. Fraudulent export documentation can further trigger criminal export fraud proceedings, loss of export incentive status and registration disqualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a free-market floating exchange rate with full convertibility of the peso (COP) on the country's official exchange market (Mercado Cambiario). Current-account transactions, including service exports, licensing fees, and operational payments, are freely convertible and remittable once properly documented. Capital-account transactions, including foreign direct investment, equity contributions, and cross-border loans, require compliance with Banco de la República's exchange regime, notably the mandatory investment registration and the filing of the corresponding exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio). Profit remittance in the form of dividends is permitted for non-resident shareholders after the applicable withholding tax is settled and the underlying investment is duly registered with Banco de la República; failure to register the foreign investment within the six-month window from the date of disbursement creates administrative penalties and may lead to disallowance of the capital repatriation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Colombian exchange regulations require all proceeds from the export of goods to be channeled through the foreign exchange market within six (6) months counted from the date of the bill of lading (shipment date). For the export of services, the channeling term is four (4) months from the date of issuance of the corresponding invoice or equivalent receivable recognition. The regulation does not impose a mandatory percentage conversion ratio; rather, it mandates the intermediation of the full export receivable through an authorized foreign exchange intermediary. Exporters may either sell the foreign currency to the intermediate bank at the prevailing representative market rate (TRM) or hold it in an authorized foreign-currency deposit account with a Colombian commercial bank or through a registered cuenta de compensación. All exchange operations must be booked on the corresponding exchange declaration and supported by the commercial invoices, transport documents, tax registry (RUT), and underlying service or sales contracts. Failure to adhere to the timelines may trigger fines and administrative sanctions by the DIAN, and will be considered a foreign-exchange violation under Circular DCIN 83 and Law 9 of 1991.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia) is the monetary and foreign-exchange authority; the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit (MHCP) issues exchange regulations; and the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) acts as the administrative and enforcement body for foreign-exchange controls, money-laundering prevention, and trade-related customs verification.
