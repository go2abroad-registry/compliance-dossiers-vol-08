Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) - national tax and customs authority. Portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; transactional platform: https://muisca.dian.gov.co (MUISCA).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Execute the S.A.S. deed of incorporation before a Colombian notary; non-resident shareholders act through a legal representative or apoderado holding an apostilled power of attorney. Step 2: Register the company with the local Cámara de Comercio to obtain commercial registration. Step 3: Register in the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) via MUISCA using the representative's digital certificate; DIAN assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which concurrently operates as the corporate tax ID, VAT ID and customs ID. Step 5: Where the non-resident parent receives Colombian-source amounts prior to entity formation, the parent must itself obtain a NIT through a Colombian fiscal representative to avoid excess withholding.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: the S.A.S. is a separate Colombian corporate tax resident; income tax operates under the source rule (Arts. 12-13 E.T.) with Colombian-source business income taxed at the general 35% corporate rate, and non-resident shareholders bear additional dividend taxation at distribution under Arts. 242-245 E.T. No pass-through treatment is available to an S.A.S.

Available election forms

(i) SIMPLE tax regime (Arts. 903-916 E.T.): optional combined income-plus-IVA regime for an S.A.S. whose annual gross income does not exceed 100,000 UVT; election is made in the RUT by the date DIAN indicates. (ii) Transfer pricing documentation elections (Art. 260-1 E.T.): selection of the transactional profit method used in the local and master file. (iii) Anti-hybrid and GAAR rules (Arts. 118-1 et seq. E.T. per Law 2010/2019) allow re-characterization of mismatched cross-border payments, but Colombia has no check-the-box election. (iv) Optional e-invoicing authorization as 'proveedor tecnológico' (Resolution 000165/2023) is available for group service companies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary de minimis for corporations: the S.A.S. must register as IVA-responsible (Régimen Común) in the RUT from its first taxable supply of bakery goods and services, regardless of turnover (Decree 1625/2016, Art. 1.6.1.2.1). The optional SIMPLE regime is available only where annual gross income does not exceed 100,000 UVT (COP 4,979,900,000 for FY2025, UVT 2025 = COP 49,799); above this ceiling, traditional IVA and income tax reporting is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (a) tangible goods physically exported when evidenced by the DEX customs declaration, and (b) services exports under Art. 481 E.T. if: the buyer has no residence/domicile in Colombia, the service is used exclusively outside Colombia (e.g., offshore master-franchise know-how, royalties and central-kitchen technical assistance), the consideration is paid by the non-resident without Colombian intermediary intervention, and the service is declared as an export in MUISCA. Local franchise sales attract 19% IVA, except basic basket items (e.g., bread) excluded at 0% under Art. 424 E.T.; pastries and confectionery are standard-rated.

  • DEX - Declaración de Exportación (approved customs export declaration issued via MUISCA)
  • Bill of Lading (BL) or Airway Bill (AWB) evidencing physical exit of goods
  • Certificado de cambio / Formulario No. 4 (Declaración de Cambio por Exportaciones de Bienes) issued by the Colombian intermediary bank evidencing FX repatriation
  • Commercial export invoice matching the DEX in value, quantity and incoterms
  • For services exports: certificate of non-residence of the buyer, the executed service/license agreement, and delivery evidence showing exclusive use abroad
  • Packing list and inspection/certificate of origin where required by destination customs

Mandatory real-time e-invoicing ('Factura Electrónica de Venta', FEV) per DIAN Resolution 000165/2023: the S.A.S. must issue XML invoices through a DIAN-authorized technology provider and submit them to MUISCA for pre-validation ('validación previa') via SOAP/REST web services; the approved invoice is stamped with a CUNE code and QR before delivery to the customer. Export transactions require an export-type electronic invoice with incoterms and customs-office data. Non-compliance blocks the counterparty's input VAT credit and triggers sanctions under Arts. 652 and 652-13 E.T.

Exporters' VAT refunds (saldo a favor) are subject to accelerated DIAN verification (30 working days under Art. 855 E.T. if the complete documentation bundle is filed; refunds prescribe after 5 years). False or inflated export claims trigger: (i) the 100% inexactness penalty on the difference or refund improperly obtained (Art. 648 E.T.); (ii) moratory interest under Art. 635 E.T. from the refund application date; (iii) retroactive clawback of the principal plus interest through official correction within the 5-year statute of limitations; (iv) criminal tax fraud under Arts. 434A-434G of the Criminal Code (imprisonment of 48-108 months where evasion exceeds 400 monthly minimum legal wages, approximately COP 586M for FY2025); and (v) cancellation of the RUT and prohibition on issuing export invoices for recidivist false exporters.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia maintains a managed-floating regime for the Colombian peso (COP). Current-account transactions, including goods exports, service fees, royalties, interest and dividends, are generally convertible and free from prior central-bank licensing, but transactions at or above the USD 10,000 threshold must be channeled through an authorized FX market intermediary and supported by a Declaración de Cambio. Capital-account flows, particularly inbound foreign direct investment into the CO_SAS and eventual equity or capital repatriation, are not subject to prior government approval but are mandatorily registrable with Banco de la República through the intermediary bank under the Registro de Inversiones Internacionales. Profit-remittance hurdles for the non-resident franchisor/shareholder include the proper legalization of the original FDI registration before any dividend equals USD 10,000 or more is wired abroad, the full payment of Colombian corporate income tax on distributable earnings, the application of the statutory dividend withholding for non-resident shareholders, the production of the corporate distribution resolution and audited financial statements, and the obligation to route the remittance through the registered local bank account using the correct exchange concept account. Any unregistered, fragmented or informally channeled remittance triggers exchange-infraction fines, possible recharacterization and a bank-side compliance hold on the outgoing wire.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds generated by the CO_SAS in its capacity as an exporter of central-kitchen pastry products, and as the recipient of franchise royalties on the outbound master-franchise model, must be fully canalizada (channeled) through the organized FX market or into a previously registered compensation account. The statutory channeling deadlines are six months from the date of shipment for goods and thirteen months for services and technology or royalty flows. For proceeds equal to or exceeding USD 10,000 per transaction, settlement must pass through an authorized intermediary producing a Declaración de Cambio; sub-threshold proceeds may be received through ordinary local or compensation accounts but remain subject to annual exchange reporting and full traceability. There is no percentage-based mandatory conversion into COP; the legal duty is complete repatriation through regulated rails rather than a fixed composition of currency. The exporter may lawfully hold foreign currency after channeling, but settlement through parallel brokers, unregulated crypto corridors or offshore EMI ledgers constitutes an uncanalized exchange infraction carrying fines, FX-market disqualifications and adverse customs and tax administrative consequences.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (the Central Bank of the Republic of Colombia), acting in coordination with the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit and the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) as the supervisory authorities for the organized foreign-exchange market. The governing framework comprises Law 9 of 1991, the Central External Regulation DCIN 83 (adopting Resolution No. 8 of 2000 and its subsequent amendments), Decree 1068 of 2015, and the SFC's SARLAFT and foreign-exchange compliance circulars.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

FEASIBILITY RATING: LOW as a primary treasury rail; LIMITED only as a de-minimis auxiliary collection channel. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Payoneer and Airwallex are not licensed by the SFC and are not authorized FX market intermediaries under the Colombian exchange regime. They cannot issue legally valid Declaraciones de Cambio, register foreign investment inflows with Banco de la República, or lawfully settle export proceeds in COP. Receiving material foreign funds through such platforms exposes the CO_SAS to uncanalized-flow exchange infractions, loss of the bank-side positive-pay protection, and evidentiary gaps for transfer-pricing and audit defense. Their use is justifiable only for immaterial incidental collections below the USD 10,000 per transaction threshold, provided a written treasury policy designates the EMI as a sub-agent of a tier-1 bank and monthly reconciliation is performed. Locally licensed SEDPEs such as Nequi and Daviplata are permissible solely for domestic low-value COP digital payments covering POS, staff payroll and franchise network disbursements, never for cross-border receipts or conversions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The non-resident shareholder or subscribing master-franchisor instructs its offshore bank to transmit USD, EUR or CNH via SWIFT MT103 to the CO_SAS capital account at a tier-1 authorized intermediary. The MT103 must show the true ordering party in field 50K and the exact CO_SAS beneficiary account in field 59, with the payment purpose in fields 72 or 77B stating FDI capital contribution, SARLAFT source verification, and the applicable foreign-investment concept code from the Banco de la República Form No. 5 catalogue for direct-investment cash contributions. For receipts at or above USD 10,000, the intermediary bank generates the Declaración de Cambio and the corresponding exchange registration entry. The bank's compliance gate verifies four elements: the Chamber of Commerce certificate confirming authorized and paid-in capital headroom, the foreign investor appearing in the SAS shareholder register, the remitter identity matching the registered investor under a positive-pay protocol, and the board or shareholder minutes pre-approving the capital subscription or increase. The funds are negotiated into COP at the free market rate if capitalization in COP is elected, and the full principal must be posted to the capital escrow sub-ledger with daily reconciliation. Artificial fragmentation of a single subscription into multiple sub-USD 10,000 tranches is expressly forbidden as exchange fraud; the CO_SAS should bring the full injection in one or a pre-disclosed series of wires and complete the Banco de la República investment registration before any dividend or liquidation distribution is projected.The primary outbound rail is the SWIFT MT103 channel for master-franchise royalties and technical-assistance fees payable to the Chinese franchisor; each payment order must cite the underlying royalty invoice, attach the transfer-pricing support file prepared under the TNMM or royalty-comparison method, identify the applicable royalty concept code for technology-transfer and services in the exchange declaration, and undergo dual-signature authorization by the legal representative and the CFO. Commercial settlements for imports of pastry ingredients, custom baking equipment and packaging use documentary letters of credit governed by UCP 600, opened at sight or on a usance basis in USD or CNY, with the full commercial-document package including invoice, bill of lading, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and packing list. Documentary collections on a documents-against-payment or documents-against-acceptance basis are employed for recurring consumable imports below USD 25,000 to reduce LC costs. If the CO_SAS maintains a Banco de la República-registered compensation account abroad or in a local USD account, eligible inbound export and royalty receivables may be offset against approved outbound payables only through the periodic canalización reconciliation, never by bypassing the organized FX market; each intercompany invoice is paid gross through a regulated intermediary in accordance with transfer-pricing requirements and Colombian FX law. The bank reviews outbound flows against artificial-benefit pricing and improper valuation indicators before release.Deliverable USD/COP outright currency forwards contracted with authorized local banks over tenors from one to twenty-four months, supported by documented commercial exposure comprising royalty payables, import payables and export receivables, and executed under an ISDA master or a Colombian-form standard master agreement.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/COP cash-settled in USD or in the COP difference, permissible when the underlying exposure is genuine, identified in the treasury hedge policy and documented for transfer-pricing and tax purposes.Cross-currency swaps (USD/COP) transacted with tier-1 counterparties for multi-year hedging of royalty streams, central-kitchen capex or foreign-currency financing tenors exceeding one year.USD/COP currency options comprising calls and puts for contingent hedging of commodity-price movements in pastry inputs and of franchise-capex tender exposures; contracts must be written through supervised financial intermediaries.Exchange-traded USD/COP futures on the Bolsa de Valores de Colombia (Grupo BVC) with central clearing, used for rolling short-term tactical hedges of seasonal import payment peaks.FX swaps (spot-forward swaps) executed at local banks to cover temporary liquidity mismatches between same-day COP collections and maturing USD payables while keeping the net currency exposure unchanged.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: FEASIBILITY RATING: LOW as a primary treasury rail; LIMITED only as a de-minimis auxiliary collection channel. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Payoneer and Airwallex are not licensed by the SFC and are not authorized FX market intermediaries under the Colombian exchange regime. They cannot issue legally valid Declaraciones de Cambio, register foreign investment inflows with Banco de la República, or lawfully settle export proceeds in COP. Receiving material foreign funds through such platforms exposes the CO_SAS to uncanalized-flow exchange infractions, loss of the bank-side positive-pay protection, and evidentiary gaps for transfer-pricing and audit defense. Their use is justifiable only for immaterial incidental collections below the USD 10,000 per transaction threshold, provided a written treasury policy designates the EMI as a sub-agent of a tier-1 bank and monthly reconciliation is performed. Locally licensed SEDPEs such as Nequi and Daviplata are permissible solely for domestic low-value COP digital payments covering POS, staff payroll and franchise network disbursements, never for cross-border receipts or conversions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Transfer Pricing Informative Return are required when the taxpayer's gross income or total assets equal or exceed COP 4,706,500,000 (100,000 UVT) and related-party transactions exceed COP 470,650,000 (10,000 UVT) for the taxable year. Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated gross revenue exceeds the DIAN-established threshold.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not apply the UAE-style Economic Substance Regulation as a standalone code, but the DIAN may deny treaty and domestic benefits through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule, the beneficial-ownership requirement in DTAs, and the substance-over-form doctrine in Articles 871-1 through 871-5 of the Colombian Tax Code. The tax authority will examine the presence of real offices, qualified payroll, decision-making functions, risk assumption, and commercial substance in the jurisdiction of residence when assessing treaty relief, transfer-pricing structures, and financing or royalty operations.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Omission of Cash Sales

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion under Law 2277/2022: imprisonment of 6 to 10 years and fines of 150% of the omitted tax; civil adjustment of omitted tax plus monthly late interest; loss of deduction rights for the affected fiscal year.

Director personal liability: The DIAN may pierce the corporate veil and hold directors jointly liable for civil restitution when the evasion was ordered or knowingly tolerated; directors may face travel bans through pending tax claims, disqualification from managing Colombian companies, and criminal imprisonment upon personal conviction.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Royalty Drain to Offshore Affiliates

Statutory sanction: DIAN may recharacterize the transaction, disallow deductions, assess income tax at 35% on the disallowed amount, apply transfer-pricing penalties and the 100% inaccuracy fine, plus monthly interest from the original due date.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve unrelated-party arrangements may be personally liable for the underassessment if the transaction was structured solely to strip Colombia-source profit; travel restrictions may be imposed while the case is under investigation.
Abusive Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The corporate form may be legally disregarded for tax purposes; all income is reallocated to the actual owner or master franchise; threatened penalties under Law 2277/2022 may include penalty of up to 100% of the tax avoided and cancellation of the commercial registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders face civil restitution for unpaid corporate debts when the entity is treated as an alter ego; personal asset attachment and bankruptcy proceedings may be opened; criminal charges for conspiracy to commit tax fraud carry imprisonment up to 12 years.
AML/CFT Deficiencies in Cash-Intensive Bakery Operations

Statutory sanction: Colombian AML law permits fines of up to COP 2,400,000,000 for non-compliant non-regular financial entities; supervisors may order suspension or revocation of the mandatory sanitary and commercial operating license.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are subject to personal fines, travel bans during investigation, disqualification from holding public or corporate office, and criminal prosecution for money-laundering facilitation with imprisonment of 10 to 20 years.
