Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá (or the Cámara de Comercio of the city of the registered office) acting as keeper of the Registro Mercantil; corporate supervision by the Superintendencia de Sociedades; fiscal registration by DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales).

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 (Sociedades por Acciones Simplificadas), Articles 4, 5, 8, 9, 17, 22 and 45; Código de Comercio (Decree 410 of 1971), Articles 110, 111, 354; Decreto Único Reglamentario 1074 of 2015, Book II on mercantile registration; Law 1429 of 2010 on company formalization.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation deed under Article 5 of Law 1258 of 2008) filed for recording in the Registro Mercantil, together with the Formulario de Registro Único Tributario (RUT) and the resulting Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 24 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Law 1258 of 2008 imposes no minimum paid-up capital. The sole shareholder may subscribe capital with payment deferred up to 24 months (Article 8); no escrow account is mandated, but contributions must be paid into the company account and non-monetary technology contributions must be appraised under Article 9.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certificate (Certificado de Bomberos) for office occupancy, structural load and occupancy clearance from building management, installation of extinguishers and evacuation signage, compliance with NTC 2050 / RETIE electrical standards, and registration of the workplace under Sistema de Gestión de Seguridad y Salud en el Trabajo (SG-SST) per Decreto 1072 of 2015 if staff are hired in Colombia.

Paid-in capital rule: Law 1258 of 2008 imposes no minimum paid-up capital. The sole shareholder may subscribe capital with payment deferred up to 24 months (Article 8); no escrow account is mandated, but contributions must be paid into the company account and non-monetary technology contributions must be appraised under Article 9.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – portal: www.dian.gov.co, with taxpayer transactions through MUISCA and electronic invoicing systems.

E-government filing portal

A Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) must be incorporated before a notary and registered with the competent Cámara de Comercio; DIAN then assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). The company must complete the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) online or at a DIAN service point, update the RUT with the ISIC 6201 business activity, and obtain the applicable electronic invoicing authorizations. Foreign shareholders may hold ownership without residency; foreign legal representatives require an appointed local agent for tax domicile notice purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Colombia has no federal check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. The S.A.S. is a resident corporate taxpayer taxed at the entity level; no pass-through regime is available. Qualified taxpayers may elect the Simplified Taxation Regime (Régimen Simple de Tributación – SIMPLE) under Articles 903–916 of the Tax Code when statutory turnover thresholds and ownership conditions are met. Export-related zero rating for VAT is mandatory when statutory requirements are satisfied, not an elective regime. Hybrid mismatch rules apply under domestic anti-deferral and CFC rules to the extent provided by Colombian law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general turnover threshold for VAT registration in Colombia; any person carrying out taxable activities is obliged to register as a VAT responsible party from the start of operations. For eligible small taxpayers electing the SIMPLE regime, the statutory annual gross income ceiling applies; for 2025 this equivalent is set by law in UVT (unidad de valor tributario), with the applicable UVT value announced annually by DIAN.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to goods exported from Colombia where actual customs export is evidenced and the exporter legally repatriates the foreign currency. For services, including SEO content analytics SaaS and software-related services, zero rating applies when the service is rendered by a Colombian tax resident or domiciled entity, the service is used exclusively outside Colombia by a non-resident customer, the contract and consideration reflect a genuine cross-border transaction, and the service is not economically linked to taxable activities within Colombia. The analysis under Article 481 of the Tax Code and regulatory guidelines must be documented in the company's transfer pricing and VAT files.

  • Declaración de Exportación (DEX) accepted by DIAN/VUCE for goods exports
  • Commercial invoice describing the service and contractual export nature
  • Export service certificate or the approved form evidencing cross-border service provision
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for physical exports
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Declaración de Cambio issued by an authorised local bank proving receipt of foreign currency
  • Evidence of the foreign client's tax residence and exclusive foreign use of the software/analytics service

VAT registered taxpayers must issue the factura electrónica de venta (FEV) through DIAN-authorised software or an authorised technology provider. The invoice must be transmitted in real time to DIAN's electronic invoicing clearance platform and must contain the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) for sales invoices and the CUDS (Código Único de Documento Soporte) for supporting documents. DIAN validates and sets the digital stamping; credit notes and debit notes must also be transmitted in the same electronic format.

DIAN may retroactively disallow zero-rated treatment and claim VAT plus default interest when export documentation, foreign use evidence, or currency repatriation does not meet the statutory conditions. The taxable review period generally covers the last three to five tax years depending on the omission. Penalties include the inaccurate return penalty for omitted VAT, which may reach 100% or, in cases involving fraudulent supporting documents, up to 160% of the disputed amount. Criminal exposure may arise under Colombian anti-smuggling and tax fraud rules when false export declarations are used.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is fully convertible for current-account flows, including cross-border SaaS and software service exports, provided the payments are channeled through the Mercado Cambiario (MCR) via an authorized IMC and a Declaración de Cambio is filed. Capital account inflows and outflows are liberalized for registered foreign direct investment (FDI): equity contributions and intra-group loans become freely remittable once registered in Banco de la República's International Investment Registry (Registro de Inversores Internacionales) within 12 months of disbursement. Profit repatriation to non-resident shareholders is permitted after the 35% Colombian corporate income tax (2025 rate) and a 7.5% dividend withholding tax on the distributeable amount; an unregistered investment loses the FX right to remit dividends and capital.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For service exports within ISIC 6201 (SEO analytics SaaS), 100% of the foreign-currency proceeds must be channeled (canalizado) through the MCR and repatriated (reintegro) to an authorized IMC within 6 months from the invoice date, or from the contractual payment due date when such due date is later. The proceeds may be temporarily held in a foreign-currency account only during the pending reintegro window and must then be sold to or deposited with the IMC; statutory extensions are available if the exporter proves a documented collection delay by the foreign client. The export is declared electronically through the IMC using the service-concept code applicable to informatics and software services, and no prior regulatory license is required for the SaaS activity itself.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia); foreign-exchange policy is set by its Board of Directors (Junta Directiva) under Ley 9 de 1991, restated in Resolución Externa 1 de 2018 (compiling Resolución Externa 8 de 2000). The Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia authorizes and supervises the foreign-exchange intermediaries (IMCs/mercado cambiario) that execute compulsory exchange declarations.

Low-Medium as the primary treasury rail but Medium as a collection overlay. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Mercury, Airwallex, Payoneer) and payment aggregators (Stripe, Paddle) are not authorized Colombian IMCs; they cannot register FDI, file exchange declarations, or perform the statutory reintegro to Banco de la República. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholders inject equity or subordinated debt via SWIFT MT103 in USD or EUR to the SAS's COP account at the IMC; the IMC applies the market exchange rate (TRM) and files the electronic Declaración de Cambio para Inversión Internacional Internacional, assigning a registration code. The SAS must record the investment in Banco de la República's International Investment Registry within 12 months of disbursement, supported by notarial capital-increase minutes, updated RUT, and the SARLAFT source-of-funds questionnaire; funds must not be fragmented or mislabeled to avoid reclassification as operating revenue. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35% (general CIT rate, Article 240 E.T., as maintained by Law 2277 of 2022 for fiscal years 2023 onward; financial sector surcharge of 3 percentage points applies only to credit institutions)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Dividends paid to non-resident shareholders: (i) 35% income tax on the portion of the dividend that corresponds to profits not previously taxed at the corporate level (i.e., exempt or specially taxed profits); and (ii) 10% withholding tax on the portion distributed from profits that have already borne 35% CIT. Combined effective burden on pre-tax earnings is approximately 41.5%. Statutory rate applies unless a Double Taxation Agreement (DTA) reduces the 10% component, typically to 5%–15% for qualifying substantial shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual income tax return for the fiscal year ending 31 December is due between early April and mid-May of the following year, with the exact deadline determined by the final two digits of the NIT (tax ID). For a NIT ending in '23' (payload composite key), the 2025 filing window for tax year 2024 typically falls on 8–12 May 2025; the DIAN publishes the definitive calendar by resolution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: For tax year 2024 (UVT = COP 47,065), mandatory documentation thresholds are: Local File (Información Local) – gross income above COP 6,118,450,000 (130,000 UVT), or gross assets above COP 5,177,150,000 (110,000 UVT), or net equity above COP 4,706,500,000 (100,000 UVT); Master File (Información Maestra) – the Colombian taxpayer has gross income above COP 7,530,400,000 (160,000 UVT) and belongs to a consolidated group with total joint income above COP 11,766,250,000 (250,000 UVT); per-counterparty transaction report (Form 2284) – when transactions with each foreign related party exceed COP 2,118,000,000 (45,000 UVT) per type. Even below these thresholds, all intra-group services, licence fees and SaaS cost-sharing arrangements must be supported by contemporaneous economic substance evidence.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not operate a Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime, but a functional equivalent exists through: Article 869 E.T. general anti-abuse rule requiring legitimate business purpose; Article 260-1 E.T. arm's-length rule; CFC rules (Articles 882–886 E.T.); DTA beneficial-ownership conditions; mandatory Beneficial Owner Registry (Registro Único de Beneficiarios Finales); and DIAN Circular 000014/2020 on transactions with non-residents. A Colombian S.A.S. attracting software development revenue with only a registered address, no payroll, no management meetings in Colombia, and no technical staff will be treated as a shell; DIAN may disregard its existence, reattribute the SaaS income to the non-resident principal, and levy the 35%/10% dividend/deemed-distribution WHT plus a 100%–200% inexactitude penalty.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Criminal Tax Fraud

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of 4 to 9 years under Article 434-A of the Criminal Code; fines equal to 100% of the evaded tax; monthly interest under Article 635; late-filing penalty of 5% per month capped at 100%; asset seizure and mandatory filing of corrected returns. DIAN may also impose a 200% penalty when the false claim relates to exempt income (rentas exentas) without legal support.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and controlling directors are jointly and severally liable under Article 793 E.T. for the unpaid tax and criminal fines; the DIAN may request travel bans (impedimento de salida del país) and preventive embargoes on personal assets pending fraud investigation; criminal liability attaches to the administrator who signs the false return.
