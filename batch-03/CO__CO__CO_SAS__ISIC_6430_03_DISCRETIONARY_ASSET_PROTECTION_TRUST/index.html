Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) - official national tax and customs administration; principal portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional e-services via the 'Sede Electrónica DIAN' platform (successor to Muisca), including RUT, e-invoicing validation gateways, and taxpayer document services.

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the S.A.S. through the 'Cámara de Comercio' of the registered office, filing the private incorporation document and registering in the Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES); the Chamber assigns a provisional NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) upon issuance of the Chamber certificate. (2) Within 30 calendar days after the incorporation date, formalize the definitive tax registration with DIAN by submitting the 'Registro Único Tributario (RUT)' application, either through the integrated single-window procedure (VUE) or directly via the DIAN portal; the RUT must reflect the company as an IVA responsible in the 'régimen común' and include responsibility code R.99 if export zero-rating of trust-administration services will be claimed. (3) Obtain and register the legal representative's digital signature certificate (certificado de firma electrónica) issued by a DIAN-accredited certification authority to enable electronic invoicing and RUT updates. (4) File the regulatory authorization of the 'sociedad fiduciaria' license granted by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (Decreto 663/1993, Article 29) and update the RUT with the special activity code ISIC 6430 (trust administration) and the high-risk AML supervisory designation. (5) Open a corporate bank account and register the foreign-exchange intermediation data with the local financial entity to receive and formally settle the offshore fee remittances in compliance with Banco de la República Circular DCIN-83; the applicable foreign-exchange declaration ('Declaración de Cambio', forms 5 or 6) is required for each remittance received from the foreign trustee.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the Colombian S.A.S. is by law a resident capital company subject to national corporate income tax at 35% (for tax year 2024) on worldwide income; there is no pass-through or transparent regime for S.A.S. companies. The irrevocable offshore spendthrift trust administered by the S.A.S. is a separate non-resident structure and is not a Colombian taxpayer; the S.A.S. pays tax on its fiduciary fee income, while Colombian-resident beneficiaries of the offshore trust become taxable on distributions only when such distributions are actually received, subject to the anti-deferral and controlled-foreign-corporation provisions of the Estatuto Tributario.

Available election forms

Colombia does not provide a check-the-box or free entity-classification election. The relevant statutory elections available to a licensed fiduciary S.A.S. are: (i) the voluntary update of the RUT to include IVA responsibility 99 ('exportadora') enabling the zero-rating of export-fiduciary services; (ii) the optional exclusion from municipal ICA (Impuesto de Industria y Comercio) territorial taxation where the service is exclusively exported, available under some local ordinances, but not applicable at the national level; (iii) the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' is statutorily prohibited for entities subject to supervision by the Superintendencia Financiera, therefore unavailable to this S.A.S.; (iv) group income-tax consolidation under Article 124-4 of the E.T. (introduced by Law 2277/2022) for Colombian financial groups with direct or indirect ownership of 90% or more, subject to prior DIAN authorization; (v) the formal election to present annual transfer-pricing documentation and, where applicable, advance pricing agreements with DIAN; (vi) hybrid-mismatch rules under Law 1819/2016 and Law 2277/2022 are mandatory, with no affirmative election permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de-minimis turnover threshold for legal entities: a commercial S.A.S. must be registered in the RUT as an IVA responsible taxpayer from the date of incorporation regardless of actual revenue. The simplified IVA regime ('régimen simplificado') is reserved to natural persons whose gross sales, consignations, and income do not exceed 3,500 UVT (COP 164,727,500 for 2024, with the UVT set at COP 47,065 for tax year 2024); that regime is never available to a corporate fiduciary. Registration is therefore compulsory ab initio, with no local-currency threshold applicable to the S.A.S.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% IVA) applies under Article 481(e) of the Estatuto Tributario and Decree 1418/2016, as amended by Decree 1550/2021, to services rendered to a foreign recipient that (i) has no permanent establishment in Colombia, and (ii) receives the full economic benefit and use of the service outside Colombian territory. For the fiduciary administration of an irrevocable offshore spendthrift trust, the S.A.S. must demonstrate: the trust is governed by foreign law; the settlor, protector, and all discretionary beneficiaries are non-resident and non-domiciled abroad; the S.A.S. holds no Colombian situs for the trust assets and maintains no day-to-day management office in Colombia that decides discretionary distributions; the asset-protection instructions (including the selection of offshore financial institutions, custodians, and investment accounts) are executed from the foreign jurisdiction; and the management fee is invoiced to and paid by the foreign trustee from funds outside Colombia. Prior to invoicing, the S.A.S. must obtain the 'Certificado de Exportación de Servicios' from DIAN through Form 12565 or Form 13106, enter the service in the DIAN exporter registration ('Registro de Exportadores de Servicios'), and maintain the certificate current; absence of the certificate causes the automatic application of 19% VAT to the fees.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for any physical trust asset movements, or, for pure fiduciary services, the offshore custody-bank asset statement and the contractual shipment/transfer records evidencing movement of trust assets;

Mandatory DIAN-approved electronic invoicing applies to all taxpayers since 2023. The S.A.S. must issue the 'Factura Electrónica de Venta' (FEV) exclusively through a certified technology provider ('Proveedor Tecnológico'/'Operador Tecnológico') or through DIAN's free e-invoicing platform. Each invoice is transmitted in real time to the DIAN fiscal backend via the REST/JSON-XML API under the 'validación previa' modality, meaning DIAN must validate and authorize the electronic document before the invoice is delivered to the foreign trustee. Every FEV must contain the unique 'CUFE' code for sales invoices or 'CUDS' for purchase-support documents, a QR matrix, the issuer's digital certificate, year/date/operation type, and the R.99 export indicator for zero-rated service fees. The S.A.S. must transmit and receive the DIAN acceptance receipt by 11:59 pm of the same business day of issuance, retain the XML authorization receipt, and file the aggregate monthly IVA declaration (Form 300) and the annual 'Información Exógena' report, including the e-invoice listing, through the DIAN portal. Purchase-side substantiation requires the 'Documento Soporte en Adquisiciones' (DSA) for any domestic expense that does not carry an FEV.

DIAN may review export zero-rating claims within the general 3-year assessment period (5 years where tax returns were not filed or where deficiencies are linked to unregistered transactions). If the supporting documentation is incomplete, unsatisfactory, or the service is recharacterized as performed in Colombia, the S.A.S. loses the 0% rate and DIAN retroactively assesses the 19% IVA, plus monthly default interest (the rate published monthly by DIAN, approximately 1.25% per month for 2024). The taxpayer is also subject to the 'sanción por inexactitud' under Article 648 E.T., as amended by Law 2277/2022, equal to 100% of the omitted VAT; where the export certificate was obtained fraudulently, the penalty may be calculated on the total invoice value and the exporter is removed from the exporters register. Failure to repatriate the foreign currency within 180 days constitutes a foreign-exchange infringement under Banco de la República Circular DCIN-83, triggering administrative surcharges, loss of the REF and zero-rating certificate, and disqualification from future export programs. In cases of intentional evasion, criminal sanctions apply under Law 1762/2015 (Article 434-A of the Criminal Code): imprisonment of 48 to 108 months and fines of up to 200% of the tax evaded; additionally, where the offshore trust administration is used for money-laundering purposes, the S.A.S. may be organized and closed by the Superintendencia Financiera in coordination with DIAN and the UIAF, and the responsible officers may face forfeiture and asset-freezing measures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia maintains a free-market floating exchange system (COP) with selective exchange controls. Current-account transactions (fiduciary service fees, dividends, royalties and interest) are freely convertible through authorized exchange intermediaries without prior central-bank approval, but must be channeled through the Mercado Cambiario. Capital-account flows (inbound trust corpus, equity injection, external credit) are unrestricted, yet registration with the Registro de Inversiones Internacionales is mandatory before any profit repatriation, capital liquidation or external-debt amortization; unregistered investments forfeit repatriation rights. Dividend remittances are lawful once the underlying investment is duly registered, provided taxes are settled and the Declaración de Cambio is filed with the intermediary. No material operational hurdle exists for profit remittance by a licensed fiduciary, although source-of-funds documentation and underlying trust transaction evidence will be scrutinized by the beneficiary bank and the SFC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Service exporters (including a fiduciary earning management fees from offshore trusts) must channel export proceeds through the Colombian exchange market within 6 months from service accrual or invoice date; goods exporters have 6 months from the export declaration, extendable once by up to 6 additional months. Channeling is required for each operation exceeding USD 10,000: 100% of the receivable FOB or service value must be liquidated into the Mercado Cambiario by sale to, or deposit with, an authorized intermediary (commercial bank or foreign-exchange brokerage). Amounts up to USD 10,000 per operation are exempt from the channeling obligation. Receipts collected outside the statutory timeline constitute an exchange violation under Banco de la República rules, triggering fines and loss of the right to reintegrate capital; all supporting documents (service export declarations, invoices, SWIFT MT103 receipts) must be retained for five years.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (the Central Bank of Colombia), acting jointly with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) for bank licensing, solvency and AML/CFT supervision, and with the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) for exchange-control enforcement; the operative legal basis is Law 9 of 1991, Decree 2555 of 2010 and the Banco de la República Circular Reglamentaria Externa DCIN-83 (Régimen de Cambios Internacionales).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW for primary treasury custody; MEDIUM as an ancillary payment gateway. Colombian law (Law 1735 of 2014, Decree 2555 of 2010) reserves deposit-taking and payment-account services to SFC-supervised credit establishments and licensed SEDPEs. An offshore digital EMI (Wise, Mercury, Revolut, etc.) cannot lawfully hold the entity's client trust funds, act as the settlement bank, or serve as the clearing hub because it is not an authorized Colombian exchange intermediary, cannot receive regulated fiduciary deposits, and would circumvent the mandatory exchange-channeling rules. Offshore EMIs may support non-client disbursement flows only (e.g., market-data subscriptions, software-as-a-service) with express prior consent of the onboarding bank, provided no trust corpus and no foreign-exchange reintegro is executed through them; any such use must be disclosed in the SAGRILAFT/AML file and reconciled monthly.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital must arrive via SWIFT MT103 (single remittance) to the entity's COP or USD account at an authorized Colombian exchange intermediary, using the beneficiary name and NIT, the full Colombian account number (no IBAN), and the intermediary's SWIFT/BIC; the narrative must cite the foreign-exchange purpose code: 330 for investment/equity subscription, 444/451 for external or supplier credits, or the trust-corpus purpose code applicable to foreign investment. Within 10 business days of receipt, the bank or brokerage files the Declaración de Cambio (Formulario No. 1), and the investment must be registered in the Registro de Inversiones Internacionales on the Banco de la República platform before any future dividend or principal repatriation. The bank's compliance verification includes: investor identity and ultimate ownership, board resolution in Spanish authorizing the contribution, apostilled trust deed or subscription agreement, and source-of-funds certificates. Amounts at or below USD 10,000 may be received freely but remain subject to documentary retention and suspicious-transaction reporting.Outbound settlements exclusively use regulated clearing rails: SWIFT MT103 for beneficial foreign wires (trustee fees, beneficiary distributions, debt-service payments, dividend remittances), each backed by the relevant Declaración de Cambio (Formulario 14 for services, Formulario 10 for returns to foreign investors) and with withholding taxes remitted to DIAN via Formulario 100/110 before transfer. Commercial letters of credit (cartas de crédito) governed by UCP 600 are used for trade-based trust-asset settlements, with Colombian issuing banks confirming documents, fee-invoice evidence and the sanctioned goods/services list; local Colombian disbursements ride the automated clearing house rails (CUD/ACH via SEBRA and CENIT). All wires pass the bank's OFAC/UN/EU/UK sanctions screening; no crypto, cash-courier, pre-paid-card or informal value-transfer rails are permitted under the Colombian exchange regime.Deliverable COP/USD forward exchange contracts (forwards) with authorized local banks, requiring documented underlying foreign-currency exposure (USD-denominated trust fee receivables).European-style plain-vanilla COP/USD options (calls and puts) with licensed Colombian or OECD-regulator counterparties, recorded at mark-to-market under IFRS 9 hedging rules.Cross-currency swaps (permutas financieras de moneda) matching long-term USD trust receivables with COP operating costs, subject to SFC derivative authorization and ISDA/CSA documentation.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) only with regulated international counterparties when the hedge tenor exceeds available domestic liquidity or the counterparty is foreign; settlement in USD without COP delivery, requiring full underlying-exposure documentation.Combination interest-rate/FX swaps and structured collars meeting the entity's board-approved hedging policy, provided valuations are independent and margin/collateral arrangements comply with SFC prudential limits.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: LOW for primary treasury custody; MEDIUM as an ancillary payment gateway. Colombian law (Law 1735 of 2014, Decree 2555 of 2010) reserves deposit-taking and payment-account services to SFC-supervised credit establishments and licensed SEDPEs. An offshore digital EMI (Wise, Mercury, Revolut, etc.) cannot lawfully hold the entity's client trust funds, act as the settlement bank, or serve as the clearing hub because it is not an authorized Colombian exchange intermediary, cannot receive regulated fiduciary deposits, and would circumvent the mandatory exchange-channeling rules. Offshore EMIs may support non-client disbursement flows only (e.g., market-data subscriptions, software-as-a-service) with express prior consent of the onboarding bank, provided no trust corpus and no foreign-exchange reintegro is executed through them; any such use must be disclosed in the SAGRILAFT/AML file and reconciled monthly.
