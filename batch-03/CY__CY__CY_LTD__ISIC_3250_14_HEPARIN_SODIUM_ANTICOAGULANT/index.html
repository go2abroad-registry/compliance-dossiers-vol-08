Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department, Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus (Φορολογικό Τμήμα, Υπουργείο Οικονομικών). Official e-portal: ARIADNE (https://taxportal.ariadne.gov.cy). Customs and export-related matters: Cyprus Customs and Excise Department (THESEAS/ECS portal).

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company is automatically registered for corporate income tax upon incorporation; the Registrar of Companies HE number (e.g., HE77) serves as the TIN, and no separate CIT registration certificate is required. For VAT, the entity must submit Form VAT 101 (Application for Registration) to the Tax Department, attaching the certificate of incorporation, registered office certificate, and declaration of officers; non-established foreign entities supplying taxable goods or services in Cyprus are registrable regardless of turnover and may be required to appoint a VAT representative. A pharmaceutical manufacturing licence from the Cyprus Pharmaceutical Services is also a statutory prerequisite for heparin production.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not apply a US-style check-the-box regime but allows specific elections: (i) group loss relief within a 75% qualifying group; (ii) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) on new equity; (iii) Intellectual Property Box election granting 80% exemption on qualifying profits; (iv) accelerated capital allowance elections; (v) foreign tax credit versus local exemption choices; and (vi) VAT group registration election. EU ATAD hybrid-mismatch rules statutorily deny benefits where hybrid instruments or entities create double non-taxation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for persons established in Cyprus; non-established taxable persons supplying goods/services in Cyprus are registrable without a turnover threshold; cross-border intra-EU distance-sales threshold: EUR 10,000 (applicable to the EU OSS scheme).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) the supply or dispatch of goods physically transported out of the customs territory of the EU, evidenced by a customs export declaration and independent transport documents, and (ii) services closely connected to exported goods or supplied B2B to non-established persons under the general place-of-supply rules. The exporter must hold the contractual position to effect the export, the physical exit must occur within 90 days of the supply, and documentary evidence must be retained for at least six years (ten years in fraud cases). For heparin sodium APIs, batch-release certification and GMP documentation parallel the tax evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD Form / ECS electronic declaration bearing the MRN)
  • Bill of Lading / CMR Consignment Note / Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT Wire / Letter of Credit payment evidence
  • Commercial Invoice and proforma contract
  • Certificate of Origin and pharmaceutical Batch Release Certificate
  • Customs exit confirmation report

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory API-based invoice clearance regime is in force in Cyprus as at 2025. B2G e-invoicing is mandated for transactions with Cypriot public authorities under EU Directive 2014/55. Export declarations must be filed electronically via Cyprus Customs ECS/THESEAS and ICS2 systems. All taxable persons must issue VAT-compliant invoices (paper or electronic) preserving authenticity, integrity, and legibility; adoption of the EU ViDA e-invoicing and reporting standard is expected from 2028 onwards.

Cyprus imposes no statutory deadline requiring physical FX repatriation; the 180-day figure represents the commercial and audit documentation benchmark for substantiating export zero-rating. Unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger retrospective VAT assessment at 19% with clawback of any input VAT refunded, statutory late-payment interest at the Commissioner's prescribed rate, predetermined administrative fines under s.50A of the VAT Law (up to EUR 20,000 per offence category), criminal prosecution under s.50 (fines and/or imprisonment), extension of the reassessment window from six to ten years in cases of fraud, and possible Customs Code enforcement including goods forfeiture and exporter penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. Cyprus applies no foreign exchange restrictions under EU law (Regulation (EC) No 1883/2005). Profit remittance is unrestricted, subject to corporate income tax (12.5%), withholding tax (if any) under double tax treaties, and the availability of distributable reserves as confirmed by audited financial statements. Directors are obliged to file financial statements and pay dividends in accordance with the Companies Law, Cap. 113.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender requirement or mandatory forex conversion ratio for export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained in any currency indefinitely without a repatriation timeline. Reporting obligations are limited to the Central Bank of Cyprus's Balance of Payments statistical framework, where the authorized local bank files a Form 21 direct return for transactions exceeding EUR 12,500. All supporting commercial documentation (invoices, packing lists, transport documents) must be preserved for at least seven years.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou), operating within the Eurosystem and subject to the European Central Bank's single monetary policy. The Ministry of Finance and the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission (CySEC) retain ancillary supervisory roles.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Regulated Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) holding a Cypriot license or an EU passport under PSD2 can operate, but their transactional rails are generally limited to MT103-style transfers and do not support documentary trade finance (e.g., letters of credit, guarantees). Offshore digital EMIs without a valid EU passport are legally prohibited from servicing Cyprus entities. The pharmaceutical sector is additionally classified as medium-risk for AML, requiring enhanced due diligence that often outweighs the convenience of an EMI.

Defense evidence: Current tax residence certificates, detailed source of wealth and source of funds statements, certified excerpts from the Cyprus Registrar of Companies (D.R.O.C.), a full chain of ownership with passport copies, and a board resolution endorsing the onboarding. Defense evidence: A formal PEP declaration, evidence of legitimate business rationale, the applicant's independent asset management history, a signed indemnity for enhanced monitoring, and an AML compliance officer approval in writing. In exceptional cases, a personal interview with the PEP is conducted.

Root cause: Newly incorporated entity with aggressive revenue forecast and no trading history in pharmaceutical API trading

Defense evidence: Enforceable supply and offtake contracts from reputable pharmaceutical counterparties, warehouse or logistics agreements, comprehensive cash-flow projections, and prior-year transactions if the shareholder has a related operational history. The defence must include evidence that the goods are real, generally a pre-shipment inspection report or an active purchase order from an FDA/EMA-listed entity.

The bank may commission an external forensic trade audit before activation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital is routed through SWIFT MT103 with the correct EU Balance of Payments purpose code D.9 (direct investment equity). Remittance must name the Cyprus IBAN of the beneficiary and include the reference 'SHARE CAPITAL' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN'. The receiving bank requires a board resolution authorising the capital increase, a statutory share application form, the updated share register, and an auditor's confirmation that the funds are not derived from illegal activity. No exchange control approval is necessary. For loans, a signed shareholder loan agreement and an upfront facility letter are required.Trade settlements are executed via SWIFT MT103 (single payment) or MT103+MT202 for high-value payments requiring USD or GBP clearing. For documentary trade, the company utilises MT700 to open irrevocable letters of credit conforming to UCP 600. Euro payments clear directly through TARGET2, while USD wires clear through the CorrespondeNet network of the bank's New York correspondent, typically a 'World's Bank' or JPMorganChase; GBP flows clear via CHAPS.
