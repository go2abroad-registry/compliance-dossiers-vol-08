Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Confecámaras - Registro Único Empresarial (RUES); local execution by Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá, with oversight by Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Law 1258 of 2008) establishing the Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada, Arts. 4-5 (no minimum capital and private incorporation document), Arts. 17-20 (registered office and share capital), in subsidiary application with Código de Comercio Arts. 110-373 and Decreto 2153 de 1992 (Chamber of Commerce registry rules).

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation document) with notarial signature authentication; public deed available as an alternative

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 18 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum legal capital; share capital is freely fixed in the bylaws, may be zero, and unpaid subscriptions may be paid in installments up to 2 years; no escrow account or proof-of-payment record is required at formation.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain the occupancy and fire safety certificate (Concepto de Bomberos) from the official fire corps for the registered office; implement the occupational health and safety system (SG-SST) under Decree 1072 of 2015 and Resolution 0312 of 2019; secure a structural load and land-use concept for office occupancy.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum legal capital; share capital is freely fixed in the bylaws, may be zero, and unpaid subscriptions may be paid in installments up to 2 years; no escrow account or proof-of-payment record is required at formation.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, that the incorporation document of the Company, subscribed by all founding shareholders, is ratified and accepted in its entirety. RESOLVED FURTHER, that the initial share subscriptions and the capital payment schedule set in the bylaws are confirmed; that the registered office at the address stated in the charter is adopted; and that the following officers are appointed: Legal Representative, Alternate Legal Representative, and Treasurer-Secretary, each to serve until replacement. RESOLVED FURTHER, that the opening of corporate bank accounts, the recordal of intellectual property license agreements before the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio, and all ancillary filings with the Chamber of Commerce, DIAN, and municipal authorities are authorized. All resolutions take effect immediately upon adoption.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) - Delegatura de Propiedad Industrial

Category: No manufacturing or operating license is mandatory for ISIC 6420 IP patent-licensing activities; voluntary recordal of patent and trademark exploitation license agreements before the SIC for erga omnes effect (Registro de Contratos de Licencia de Explotación).

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

DIAN – Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales; official portal https://www.dian.gov.co and MUISCA transactional portal https://muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

After notarial incorporation of the S.A.S. and registration with the local Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio), the entity must register electronically with DIAN's MUISCA platform to obtain the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) and the NIT (tax identification number). Required submissions include the notarized bylaws, certificate of incorporation and legal representation, proof of registered office, foreign-tax identification details, and the designated legal representative's documentation. Electronic registration automatically triggers VAT, income tax, and withholding agent obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Colombia does not provide check-the-box elections permitting pass-through classification; S.A.S. corporations are mandatorily taxed as juridical persons on worldwide income with a foreign tax credit. Available elections are limited to procedural or group regimes such as the consolidated income tax filing for parent-subsidiary groups, advance pricing arrangements, and foreign tax credit carry-forward. No hybrid-mismatch entity election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic-turnover threshold applies; all suppliers of taxable goods or services in Colombia, including non-resident digital-service providers, must register for VAT from the first taxable operation. A narrow exception exists only for certain occasional imports of services by non-VAT-registered individuals, not for a resident S.A.S. holding company.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Colombia zero-rates services that qualify as servicios exportados under the Colombian Tax Code (Article 481(f), Law 1943/2018 and regulatory decrees). A Colombian licensor (CO_SAS) may zero-rate IP royalties and sub-licensing fees when the licensee is a foreign non-resident, the licensed rights are actually exploited outside Colombian territory, the service is not economically connected to Colombian assets or Colombian-end consumers, and the exporter has registered with DIAN as an export-service provider. The license agreement, royalty invoices, proof of foreign withholding tax, and technical/exploitation reports must substantiate effective external use of the IP. Documentary and factual compliance is tested at refund and audit stages.

  • Export of Services Registration Form (Formulario de Exportación de Servicios) submitted through DIAN MUISCA
  • Customs export declaration or equivalent DIAN 'Constancia de Exportación' where a customs record is generated for bundled goods-related supplies
  • Bill of lading, airway bill, or digital delivery certificate – only where the IP transfer is attached to tangible media or physical goods
  • Inward remittance certificate and SWIFT confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign currency from the non-resident licensee
  • Signed and notarized licensing/sub-licensing agreement and supporting royalty calculation ledger

Mandatory electronic invoicing (factura electrónica de venta) is integrated with DIAN's real-time fiscal clearance. Every invoice must be transmitted through DIAN-approved software or the MUISCA web/API portal, digitally signed, and obtain a valid CUFE code before issuance. Invoices cannot be suppressed or altered without transmitting an adjustment event to DIAN. Export supply chains must transmit the relevant export-service event codes to preserve the zero-rating evidence chain.

If DIAN determines that the zero-rating criteria were not met (e.g., the royalty is actually attributable to Colombian use, documentation is deficient, or repatriation did not occur), the assessment is clawed back to the 19% VAT liability plus default interest and a late-assessment penalty of up to 20% of the tax under Articles 634–636 of the Colombian Tax Code. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims may trigger fiscal-fraud criminal exposure and disqualification from future export regimes. Non-repatriation within 180 days adds exchange-control penalties through Banco de la República and may invalidate the exporter's registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian Peso (COP) is freely convertible for current-account operations (royalties, services, profit remittances) within the regulated Mercado Cambiario; all obligatory exchange operations must be channeled through an authorized Intermediario del Mercado Cambiario (IMC) with electronic Declaration of Exchange filing. Capital-account operations (inbound and outbound direct investment) require no prior license but must be registered with BDBR via Formulario No. 4 before returns are remitted abroad. No statutory ceiling limits profit remittance, but remittances face documentary gates: current Form 4/5 registration, tax withholding at source paid, and transfer-pricing documentation for related-party royalty streams; conversion is executed at the free IMC market rate or at the TRM rate published by BDBR for accounting and tax purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods-export proceeds: 80% of FOB value must be channeled through an IMC within 6 months of shipment. Service and IP-royalty export proceeds (the CO_SAS primary inbound stream from foreign sub-licensees): 100% of the invoiced value must be channeled through an IMC within 6 months of service delivery. Channeling does not compel conversion: funds may be retained in domestic foreign-currency accounts or in BDBR-registered compensation accounts; only the routing through the IMC and the exchange declaration are mandatory. Non-compliance may trigger monetary fines under Law 9 of 1991 enforced by the SFC and DIAN within their respective competences.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (BDBR) – Junta Directiva (Central Bank Board); the exchange control regime is jointly administered with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) for supervision of authorized foreign exchange intermediaries (IMC) and with DIAN for certain exchange-control declaration and tax records.
