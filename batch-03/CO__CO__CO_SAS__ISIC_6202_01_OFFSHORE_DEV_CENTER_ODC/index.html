Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — official portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; transactional and registration services are processed through the 'Sede Electrónica DIAN' and the MUISCA system.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Colombian SAS obtains its statutory tax and VAT identification through the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), assigned by DIAN upon registration of the company with the Cámara de Comercio. The legal representative must then complete the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) through DIAN's virtual channels using the company's digital certificate, declaring the principal activity under ISIC 6202, the SAS's status as 'Responsable de IVA', and the address/domicile in Colombia. The RUT is the unified TIN/VAT ID; no separate VAT registration exists. Foreign shareholders are not required to hold a Colombian NIT unless they personally receive Colombian-source income subject to withholding.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Colombian SAS obtains its statutory tax and VAT identification through the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), assigned by DIAN upon registration of the company with the Cámara de Comercio. The legal representative must then complete the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) through DIAN's virtual channels using the company's digital certificate, declaring the principal activity under ISIC 6202, the SAS's status as 'Responsable de IVA', and the address/domicile in Colombia. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch pass-through election exists under Colombian law. A Colombian SAS is mandatorily classified as a resident domestic legal entity subject to entity-level corporate income tax on worldwide income. The company may elect the SIMPLE tax regime only when meeting statutory turnover and shareholder eligibility thresholds, but such election does not transform the entity into a pass-through; it only simplifies the calculation and payment of national and local taxes. The SAS may also elect differential VAT treatment for qualifying export services by timely filing the required export-supporting substantiation with DIAN.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

COP 0 for legal entities; a Colombian SAS is required to register as 'Responsable de IVA' from its first taxable sale/service, with no de minimis turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% VAT when physically exported from Colombian customs territory and removed from the national customs domain. Export services, including offshore software development and related digital services, qualify for 0% VAT if the service is rendered from Colombia, the recipient is a non-resident entity or individual without domicile/permanent establishment in Colombia, and the service is used or exploited outside Colombia. The exporter must have RUT status as 'Responsable de IVA' and maintain contemporaneous evidence showing that the benefit of the service is realized abroad; if any part of the service is used in Colombia, the 19% VAT rate applies to that portion.

  • Export Customs Declaration (Declaración de Exportación / Formulario No. 140 or electronic DEX) validated by DIAN through the customs system
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or equivalent transport document evidencing the physical movement of goods out of Colombia
  • Commercial invoice issued as a valid electronic invoice containing the DIAN CUDE and QR code
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate issued by an authorized Colombian bank evidencing collection and repatriation of export proceeds

The SAS must issue electronic sales invoices (factura electrónica de venta) through a DIAN-prequalified technological provider or its own authorized software using web services/API. Each invoice must be transmitted to DIAN in real time, pass schema and legal validation, and obtain a CUDE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) and a QR code for fiscal clearance. The SAS must also transmit the standard e-invoicing events to DIAN, including dispatch, acceptance, rejection, and payment confirmation, via the electronic invoicing portal.

DIAN subjects export zero-rating claims to strict documentary and substantive scrutiny. If the export condition is not proved, or the required customs, invoicing, foreign-exchange, or service-use documentation is missing, DIAN may claw back the VAT exemption, assess the full 19% IVA together with default interest, and impose the inaccuracy penalty (sanción por inexactitud), which may amount to up to 160% of the omitted tax. In cases of fraudulent export declarations, criminal tax evasion liability arises under Colombian tax criminal law, carrying imprisonment and substantial fines, and the statute of limitations for reassessment is extended where fraud or false documentation is present.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia maintains a free-market exchange regime without exchange controls on current-account transactions. Capital-account flows (inward foreign direct investment, financial credit, portfolio remittances) require formal registration with Banco de la República via an authorized local intermediary. Dividend and profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is freely permitted after corporate tax returns, compliance with foreign investment registration (if applicable), and proof of audited distributable profits; applicable dividend tax obligations must be settled and evidenced.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Service export proceeds (including software development and related BPO) must be channeled through the Colombian foreign exchange market through an authorized intermediary in compliance with the 'canalización' obligation. Reintegration (surrender) must occur within 6 months from the date of receipt, extendable up to 12 months with prior Banco de la República approval. There is no statutory conversion ratio; however, once channelized, the intermediary converts into COP unless the exporter maintains a 'Cuenta de Compensación' (pre-approved foreign-currency account) for permitted operational needs, requiring monthly reporting to the central bank.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia) and the Foreign Exchange Administration (Régimen Cambiario) through authorized intermediaries; foreign investment registration and exchange control compliance are overseen by the Bank's International Business Department.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Payoneer Fit: High

Medium (qualitative rating: Low-Medium). Offshore digital EMIs (Wise_B2B, Payoneer, etc.) are recognized as auxiliary payment corridors for cross-border trade settlements but cannot replace the required regulated financial institution relationship in Colombia. EMI accounts are not authorized 'foreign exchange intermediaries' under Decree 1735/1993; therefore, export proceeds must still be channelized through a licensed Colombian bank. EMIs may receive USD/EUR for low-risk invoicing, but cumulative balances trigger Banco de la República reporting if they are deemed a foreign bank account. Best practice: use EMIs for ancillary tax payments and small disbursements, with a domestic operational account as the primary treasury receptacle.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident equity injections into the S.A.S. must be remitted via SWIFT wire (MT103) to the company's domestic Operational Account at an authorized intermediary with the purpose code 'FDI – Capital Contribution' referencing the registration form 'Formulario No. 5' (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales). The remitting bank must use the company's exact RUT and accounting reference; the receiving Colombian bank will verify the foreign investment registration before crediting. Capital can also be injected in-kind (technology equipment) but must be declared. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

7.5% withholding tax on dividends paid out of profits already taxed at the corporate level (Article 242-1 ET); 35% withholding on dividends distributed from income that was tax exempt, subject to treaty override.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing deadline is the ninth (9th) calendar day of the fourth month following the fiscal year-end (e.g., for a December year-end, due 9 April of the following year). Deadlines are staggered by the last digit of the tax ID (NIT). For offshore services entities generating recurring income, quarterly advance payments (RAC) are due in February, May, August, and November of each fiscal year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File preparation is triggered when prior-year gross income or net worth equals or exceeds COP 6,640,000,000 (approx. 135,000 UVT for 2024); alternatively, when cross-border related-party transactions exceed COP 2,210,000,000 (approx. 45,000 UVT). Form 2280 ('Información de Operaciones con Vinculados del Exterior') must be filed electronically with DIAN by mid-July of the following year.

Economic substance scope: Colombia enforces economic substance through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (Article 658-1 ET), CFC rules (Articles 101-103 ET), and the place-of-effective-management/PE doctrine (Article 20-1 ET). The ODC must maintain its own management, staff, office, payroll, and billing infrastructure in Colombia. Interposition of a zero-tax IP-holding vehicle in a BVI or Panama entity will trigger CFC inclusions, TP adjustments, and disallowance of deductible payments. There is no standalone ESR filing, but DIAN may disregard any transaction lacking a valid business purpose.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: DIAN civil evasion penalty of up to 160% of the understated tax under Article 648 ET, plus compound arrears interest; criminal sanction of 4-9 years imprisonment under Article 434A of the Criminal Code; cancellation of the entity's export software benefits and fiscal status.

Director personal liability: Corporate veil pierced where the S.A.S. is operated as an alter ego of the foreign parent; directors and the parent-appointed legal representative may be jointly liable for the entire unpaid CIT, subject to asset freezing, travel bans (orden de captura), and personal property seizure. Professionals signing the false return may be barred from appearing before DIAN.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: DIAN re-adjustment of income to arm's length plus a penalty equal to 10% of the adjusted differential amount under Article 260-9 ET; failure to file Form 2280 attracts a fine of up to 15,000 UVT (approx. COP 737,000,000 for 2024); deduction disallowed and recharacterized as constructive dividend.

Director personal liability: The local finance manager and statutory director who certify the return without substantiating documentation are personally liable for penalties; DIAN may issue direct 'proceso de cobro' against directors' bank accounts and impose a 5-year ban on representing Colombian legal entities.
Abusive DTA Relief Through Letter-Box Holding

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits under the General Anti-Abuse Rule (Article 658-1 ET) and applicable Limitation-of-Benefits clauses; recharacterization of the income as taxable remittances subject to 20% withholding; penalty of up to 100% of the evaded tax; criminal fraud prosecution with imprisonment of up to 8 years.

Director personal liability: Directors signing the TRC declaration or executing dividend payments may be held jointly civilly liable; the foreign parent's local agent may be personally subjected to confiscation of S.A.S. shares, travel restrictions, and a nationwide prohibition on holding representative authority in Colombian entities.
AML / Offshore Payment Red Flags

Statutory sanction: UIAF (Financial Intelligence Unit) and Superintendence of Companies administrative fines up to 200% of the transaction amount; account freezing and revocation of company registration; criminal money-laundering imprisonment of 10-20 years under Law 1453/2011, plus confiscation of laundered assets.

Director personal liability: Directors who operate the Wise/Payoneer account without signed AML declarations and background checks are individually liable for each transaction; passport revocation by Migración Colombia, immediate freeze of personal and corporate bank accounts, and criminal trial for money-laundering when the offshore payment rail is found to be a conduit.
