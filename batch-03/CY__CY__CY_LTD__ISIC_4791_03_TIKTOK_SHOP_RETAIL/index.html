Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cap. 113 as amended in 2012; a nominal authorised share capital (commonly EUR 1,000) may be adopted without an escrow or deposit requirement, and no capital duty is levied on issue

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification and structural load compliance are required by the Cyprus Fire Service and municipal building control only for leased office or fulfilment premises; occupational health and safety registration under Law 89(I)/1996 applies where staff are physically employed. For a virtual-office arrangement, the lessor's building safety clearance is sufficient.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cap. 113 as amended in 2012; a nominal authorised share capital (commonly EUR 1,000) may be adopted without an escrow or deposit requirement, and no capital duty is levied on issue

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance; taxpayer portal ARIThMOS (taxportal.mof.gov.cy) and the Department's official site taxdep.mof.gov.cy; export clearance supervision by the Customs and Excise Department of the Republic of Cyprus.

E-government filing portal

STEP 1: Incorporate the Private Limited Company (Ltd) with the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver to obtain the HE registration number (HE plus six to seven digits). STEP 2: File the Company Registration/Taxpayer Registry application with the Tax Department to obtain the Tax Identification Code (TIC), which is 8 digits plus a check-letter (a 9-digit identifier is reserved for non-resident persons). STEP 3: Submit the VAT registration application (Form VAT 101) concurrently or within 30 days of exceeding the EUR 15,600 taxable turnover threshold; the issued VAT identification number has the format CY + 8 digits + 1 letter. STEP 4: Foreign entities establishing a Cyprus permanent establishment or deriving Cyprus-source income must register through the same portal and may appoint a local tax representative; foreign sellers without a Cyprus establishment generally use the Non-Union OSS in a chosen EU member state of identification rather than a Cyprus VAT ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a Cyprus-resident Ltd (managed and controlled in Cyprus) is subject to income tax on worldwide profits at a flat 12.5% corporate rate; a non-resident Ltd is taxable only on Cyprus-source income; the entity is a distinct taxable person for VAT with intra-EU and export trading capacity; no Tax_Exempt_Zone status applies to ISIC 4791 social commerce retail.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Cyprus. Ltds are mandatorily entity-level taxed; fiscal transparency applies only to partnerships, limited partnerships, European Economic Interest Groupings (EEIGs) and certain collective investment vehicles. Elective mechanisms available: (1) 75% group loss relief between qualifying Cyprus-resident group companies; (2) group VAT registration election under Article 44 VAT Law 94(I)/2000; (3) capital allowances elections and depreciation method elections; (4) ATAD-compliant anti-hybrid adjustment regime (no elective hybrid-mismatch positioning; mismatches are neutralised by compulsory rules); (5) One-Stop-Shop / Import-One-Stop-Shop elections for cross-border B2C distance sales and intra-EU distance sales of goods and electronic services.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is part of the eurozone; full convertibility under EU Treaty provisions. Both current and capital account transactions are free from exchange controls. Profit remittance is unrestricted; there is no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Cyprus tax-resident company to non-resident shareholders, subject to availability of distributable reserves and due corporate formalities.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. As an EU member state and eurozone participant, Cyprus imposes no statutory requirement for surrender, mandatory conversion, or holding of export receipts in local currency. Export proceeds are freely held in any currency or offshore/onshore account.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility for EU/EEA-licensed EMIs (e.g., Wise Europe) via passporting into Cyprus; these can serve as auxiliary payout rails for TikTok Shop settlements. Offshore, non-EU EMI accounts are not permissible as primary settlement accounts due to local banking and AML supervisory constraints; they may only be used for non-core prefunding with enhanced due diligence and are subject to rejection by Cypriot correspondent banks.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 aggregate controlled transactions in a tax year; intra-group financing transactions exceeding EUR 250,000; Master File required if worldwide group consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750 million

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not operate standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as in the UAE; however, EU ATAD, GAAR, and substance-over-form rules require genuine economic activity. To secure tax residency, IP Box benefits, and treaty access, the company must maintain management and control, employees, premises, and decision-making in Cyprus

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Underdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 100% to 200% of undercharged tax plus interest at 5.88% per annum; criminal conviction may result in a fine not exceeding EUR 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to three years

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where directors knowingly signed false returns or authorised concealment. Directors may be personally and severally liable for unpaid taxes, subject to civil restitution, departure prohibition orders, asset confiscation, and criminal imprisonment
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Cyprus GAAR may disregard the entity for tax purposes; tax residency may be reallocated to the place of effective management; denied IP Box/treaty benefits and assessed at higher effective tax rates with penalties up to 100% of avoided tax

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for fraudulent trading under the Cyprus Companies Law, personally accountable for falsely asserting tax residency; travel restrictions and disqualification orders may be issued
Money Laundering through Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law, conviction carries imprisonment up to 14 years and a fine up to EUR 500,000; proceeds of crime may be frozen and confiscated

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners, and compliance officers who knowingly participate in laundering or fail to appoint an AML compliance officer face personal criminal liability, travel bans, and extradition
Persistent Non-Filing and Non-Payment

Statutory sanction: Late filing penalties of €200 base plus €100 per month of delay up to €1,200; interest at 5.88% per annum on unpaid tax; estimated assessments and enforcement proceedings may be initiated by the Tax Department

Director personal liability: Directors may be assessed personally to the extent of the company's shortfall after asset exhaustion; courts can issue payment orders, asset freezes, and restrictions on leaving Cyprus
