Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); official portal https://www.dian.gov.co; transactional clearance platform MUISCA (https://muisca.dian.gov.co). Electronic invoicing and export declarations (DEX) are processed through DIAN web services/API.

E-government filing portal

For the local S.A.S.: (1) incorporate as an S.A.S. before the Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio) using a private incorporation instrument; (2) file the Pre-RUT through DIAN's online portal using the legal representative's digital certificate, entering the CIIU codes for ISIC 9000 creative/artistic activities and related software/publishing sub-activities, and electing the responsible-IVA regime; (3) obtain the definitive RUT with the assigned NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). The S.A.S. is a Colombian tax resident even at 100% foreign ownership. For a non-resident foreign entity with Colombian-source activity, DIAN issues a foreign-entity NIT after the entity appoints an attorney-in-fact (apoderado) in Colombia and files apostilled constitutional documents, the notarized power of attorney, and foreign tax-residence evidence; this pathway also applies to non-resident digital-service suppliers required to charge Colombian IVA under Law 2155/2021.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Colombian S.A.S. is not pass-through; it is taxed as a corporation on worldwide income at the 35% general corporate rate (Law 2277/2022), with supplementary shareholder dividend withholding (7.5% for resident individuals, 10% for non-residents on profits already taxed at entity level). The S.A.S. is tax-resident because it is incorporated under Colombian law; no territorial election or check-the-box regime exists. — For the local S.A.S.: (1) incorporate as an S.A.S. before the Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio) using a private incorporation instrument; (2) file the Pre-RUT through DIAN's online portal using the legal representative's digital certificate, entering the CIIU codes for ISIC 9000 creative/artistic activities and related software/publishing sub-activities, and electing the responsible-IVA regime; (3) obtain the definitive RUT with the assigned NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — A Colombian S.A.S. is not pass-through; it is taxed as a corporation on worldwide income at the 35% general corporate rate (Law 2277/2022), with supplementary shareholder dividend withholding (7.5% for resident individuals, 10% for non-residents on profits already taxed at entity level). The S.A.S. is tax-resident because it is incorporated under Colombian law; no territorial election or check-the-box regime exists.

Available election forms

Colombia has no check-the-box or domestic hybrid-entity election; legal-form classification is mandatory. Statutory elections available to the S.A.S. include: (i) the Régimen Simple de Tributación (SIMPLE) for eligible entities with gross receipts no greater than 100,000 UVT (approximately COP 4,706 million in 2024), replacing ordinary income tax, IVA, and consumption tax with a combined cash-based tax; (ii) election of monthly versus bimestrial IVA filing cadence where the taxpayer is an exporter seeking refund efficiency; (iii) the option for non-resident digital sellers to appoint one DIAN-recognized agent for VAT compliance; and (iv) anti-hybrid ordering and deduction-recharacterization rules under ET Articles 129–131 applicable to payments subject to hybrid mismatch treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary turnover threshold applies to a Colombian S.A.S.: registration as an IVA-responsible taxpayer is mandatory whenever the company engages in any taxable sale of goods or services, makes imports, or intends to rely on export zero-rating. The simplified IVA privilege (Régimen Simplificado) is available only to natural persons with gross income below 3,500 UVT (approximately COP 164 million in 2024) and is not available to corporations or S.A.S. entities. Non-resident digital-service platforms are subject to mandatory registration from the first sale to a Colombian consumer, without a permanent-establishment threshold, pursuant to Law 2155/2021.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: any physical export covered by an approved DEX is zero-rated for IVA. Services: the webtoon publishing and syndication service qualifies under ET Article 481 where the service is rendered to a person not domiciled in Colombia and is used or exploited exclusively abroad, or falls within the statutory deemed-export digital services list (software, cloud/digital platform services, licensing of intangibles to non-residents). For the CO_SAS earning Apple IAP and Google Play Billing revenue, zero-rating requires: (i) the contracting counterparty is Apple, Google, or another non-resident platform/end-user; (ii) the licensed webtoon IP is consumed or monetized outside Colombia; (iii) the entity retains geolocation and platform settlement reports supporting foreign consumption; and (iv) proceeds are settled in foreign currency. Any portion of revenue attributed to Colombian domestic consumers constitutes taxable domestic digital services at 19% IVA and must be invoiced accordingly.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX / Declaración de Exportación) accepted electronically in MUISCA for goods, and the Declaración de Cambio por Servicios (Form No. 6) for service exports
  • Transport document for goods — Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or multimodal/commercial evidence of departure from Colombian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Cambio) issued by an authorized Colombian foreign-exchange intermediary evidencing settlement of export proceeds within the statutory window
  • Commercial contract, licence agreement, and the FEV electronic invoice identifying the non-resident counterparty and the exclusive foreign use/exploitation of the digital content
  • Non-residence evidence of the customer, such as a foreign tax-residence certificate, Apple/Google intermediary reporting, and platform settlement statements

All IVA-responsible S.A.S. entities must issue electronic invoices (Factura Electrónica de Venta — FEV) through a DIAN-authorized technology provider or DIAN's free billing tool; each FEV is transmitted to DIAN through MUISCA/API and pre-validated in near-real time before dispatch. The invoice must carry the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) and QR code as the functional digital stamp. Purchases from non-invoicing suppliers require the transmission of a Documento Soporte en Adquisiciones. Goods exports require an export declaration (DEX) accepted in MUISCA; service exports are documented through the forex service-settlement form (Form No. 6). Noncompliance triggers penalties under ET Article 652, ranging from 1% of unfactured operations to up to 1,500 UVT per irregular invoice, with establishment-closure powers.

DIAN applies heightened scrutiny to exporters claiming input-VAT refunds: the zero-rating claim must reconcile with the DEX, the FEV set, the FX settlement certificate, and the non-resident-use evidence; the general fiscal-review period is five years from the statutory return due date, with extension where no return is filed. If zero-rating or the refund is disallowed, DIAN claws back 19% VAT on the underlying export value plus monthly moratory interest, and imposes a 160% inaccuracy penalty on the omitted tax (ET Articles 647–648), a 20% surcharge on unjustly obtained refunds (ET Article 651), and invoicing penalties under ET Article 652. Intentional fraud using fictitious foreign buyers, falsified FX declarations, or mischaracterized domestic Apple/Google revenue triggers criminal tax-evasion liability and, for goods, contraband forfeiture sanctions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is freely convertible under a managed-float regime. Current account transactions, including platform commissions, royalties, technical/creative services and profit remittances, are permitted without quantitative exchange controls but must be channelled through the regulated foreign exchange market (authorized IMCs, compensation accounts or exchange houses). Capital account transactions, notably equity injections by non-resident shareholders of the S.A.S., are permitted without previous authorization but require mandatory registration with Banco de la República via Form 1 within the statutory window. Profit distribution to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted once corporate income tax at 35% is settled; a dividend remittance surtax of up to 10% may apply under Article 245 of the Tax Code on the portion of the distribution not taxed at shareholder level. No special hurdle applies to the digital webtoon syndication business; the ability to remit is a matter of tax clearance rather than exchange authorization.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Colombia does not impose a mandatory conversion (surrender) ratio; however, 100% of export receipts are classified as obligatorily channelled foreign exchange and must be repatriated through the foreign exchange market. For exports of services, including digital webtoon licensing, platform revenues and multilingual chapter-unlocking revenues, the statutory channeling term is six (6) months counted from the date of provision of the service (invoice issuance). Channeling is effected through an IMC or a foreign-currency compensation account (Cuenta de Compensación), and the exporter must present Form 2 (Declaración de Cambio por Exportación de Servicios) within five (5) business days. Exported proceeds may remain in USD in a compensation account without conversion; conversion to COP is at the exporter's discretion. Any failure to channel within the deadline triggers administrative penalties and recharacterization risk; the exporter must document the receivable with the DIAN electronic invoice.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia) is the sole foreign-exchange administration authority, acting through its Board of Directors, the Foreign Exchange Office (Oficina de Cambio) and the normative framework of Resolución Externa No. 1 de 2018 and Circular Reglamentaria DCIN 83. Policy coordination rests with the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit; DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) enforces tax, customs and anti-money-laundering consequences of FX operations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium as a standalone rail. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, offshore Neo-banks) are not authorized foreign-exchange market participants in Colombia: they cannot complete Form 1/Form 2 declarations with Banco de la República, cannot issue channeling certificates required by DIAN, and cannot provide the legal audit trail for export proceeds. Use of an EMI is legally viable only as a temporary collection float that is promptly swept to a tier-1 IMC account, with every inflow traceable to the corresponding DIAN electronic invoice. Permanent reliance on an offshore EMI without a domestic IMC account exposes the S.A.S. to FX-control penalties, SARLAFT adverse classification, and potential DIAN assessment of un-repatriated revenue. For the low-risk webtoon trade, a direct tier-1 bank relationship is the recommended clearing path.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident equity contributions are routed as foreign direct investment and must be verifiable end-to-end. Protocol: (i) the shareholder issues a SWIFT MT103 in USD or EUR to the S.A.S.'s IMC account or USD compensation account in Colombia; Field 70 (Remittance Information) must quote the NIT of the S.A.S., the corporate registration number, and the FX purpose description 'Aporte de capital social - Inversión internacional directa' matching the Banco de la República purpose code for Form 1; (ii) the receiving IMC files Form 1 (Declaración de Inversión Internacional) within three (3) months from the capital augmentation; (iii) the S.A.S. updates the Cámara de Comercio registry and obtains a fresh Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal; (iv) the beneficial-ownership register (RUBF) is updated; and (v) USD may remain in the compensation account or be converted to COP at spot through the IMC.
