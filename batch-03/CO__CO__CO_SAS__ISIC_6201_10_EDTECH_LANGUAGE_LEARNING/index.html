Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Direccion de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); official web portal: www.dian.gov.co; electronic services access via the DIAN Transaccional portal (SIE) using a digital certificate (certificado digital) issued by authorized certification entities from the ONAC list.

E-government filing portal

1) Register the S.A.S. deed and bylaws with the local Chamber of Commerce (Camara de Comercio) to obtain the private registration number; 2) Within 30 days, apply to DIAN via the RUT (Registro Unico Tributario) electronic process for the NIT (Numero de Identificacion Tributaria); a foreign beneficial owner must provide a valid passport, proof of registered office abroad, and appoint a local legal representative with Colombian tax residence; 3) Obtain and activate the electronic signature (firma electronica) and digital certificate, and designate an authorized accountant (contador publico) for SIE filing; 4) If the entity will render services outside Colombia, the RUT must be updated to include the activity code for 'exportador de servicios' before issuing zero-rated invoices; 5) If the S.A.S. opts for the SIMPLE regime, an additional registration must be completed in the Administration module of DIAN before the annual option deadline.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

1) Optional Regimen Simple de Tributacion (SIMPLE): election available for S.A.S. entities with gross annual revenues below COP 3,500 million (2024; the threshold is updated annually); combines income tax, VAT, and industry/trade tax into a sliding gross-revenue rate from 1.8% to 14.5%; cannot be revoked before the minimum 2-year period; 2) No Check-the-Box election exists; Colombia does not have an entity classification election for corporations; S.A.S. is mandatorily a separate tax subject taxed on worldwide income at 35% corporate rate (2025: 35%, plus a 3% surtax for certain high-revenue years, and a 7% or 9% permanent establishment surcharge in applicable cases); 3) Optional VAT grouping (Grupo de entidades) is not available for S.A.S. as it is reserved for joint venture contracts (CUFs) and business consortia meeting the 50% equity control test; 4) Hybrid mismatch elections are not expressly codified for local law; Colombian transfer pricing rules (Chapter III of the Tax Code) must be reviewed for cross-border hybrid instruments; 5) Foreign tax credit election is available per-treaty or unilateral under Article 254, at the taxpayer's option.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold applies for domestic S.A.S. entities performing taxable VAT activities: registration in the RUT and charging of IVA is mandatory from the first day of business. For foreign digital service providers (no local establishment), DIAN requires VAT registration and collection on B2C sales once their total Colombian digital service sales exceed COP 3,100 million per annual period (Decree 1416 of 2021); below that, they may voluntarily register. For the SIMPLE regime, the annual gross revenue cap is COP 3,500 million.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Colombian S.A.S. may invoice services at 0% VAT (excluded/export services) when: a) the service is performed, developed, or provided from Colombian territory; b) the service is effectively used, consumed, or exploited exclusively outside Colombia, even if partially delivered electronically; c) the sale is made to a non-resident entity or to an entity operating in Colombia that is a special 'maquila' zone or a foreign-domiciled beneficiary; d) the service is not embedded in or added to the purchase price of tangible goods that will be consumed in Colombia. For EdTech subscription software sold via Apple IAP / Google Play Billing: if the platform operator (Apple/Google, as a foreign digital intermediary) purchases the license from the Colombian S.A.S. for redistribution to global users, the supply qualifies as an exported service; however, if the end user is located in Colombia and the platform collects VAT, the Colombian S.A.S. must NOT charge VAT twice, and the supply to the platform is treated as a VAT-neutral B2B export subject to zero-rating documentation. Zero-rated services must be declared in Form 300 (IVA) but do not generate output VAT; input VAT is creditable (descontable) only if the export service is evidenced by a valid export certificate. The S.A.S. must complete the 'exportador de servicios' registration in the RUT and, for services, sign the service export declaration (Declaracion de Exportacion de Servicios) under Resolution 031 of 2019.

  • Customs Export Declaration for services (Declaracion de Exportacion de Servicios - DES) validated by the DIAN Customs Office when the service is classified as an export; for pure digital services without physical customs movement, an 'export certificate' (certificado de exportacion) issued by the DIAN is acceptable
  • Commercial invoice or FEV (Factura Electronica de Venta) detailing the service, the contractual clause on exclusive foreign use, and the 0% VAT rate
  • Written service agreement (contrato) identifying the foreign beneficiary, its tax residence, and the place of use of the service
  • Proof of payment from abroad: inbound wire transfer credit advice (comprobante de transferencia) or confirmation from the foreign counterparty's bank
  • Foreign beneficiary tax residence certificate (certificado de residencia fiscal) issued by the foreign tax authority, or sworn statement if no tax treaty applies
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Fondo de Inversion de Remesas, i.e., the 'certificado de no repatriacion' is not required; the DIAN requires the forex reimbursement voucher demonstrating the funds entered the national exchange market through an authorized intermediary broker)

Mandatory Factura Electronica de Venta (FEV) with real-time DIAN validation for all registered taxpayers; issuance requires the FEV via the DIAN web service (Servicios Informaticos), API integration (JSON/XML), or an authorized technological operator (proveedor tecnologico); the invoice must be electronically transmitted to the DIAN prior to delivery to the purchaser and must contain the mandatory CUFE/CUDS hash and QR code; a separate numeration authorization is not required, but the RUT and digital certificate must be active; interim documents (e.g., proforma, 'cuenta de cobro') are forbidden for sales to final consumers; DIAN resolution No. 000165 of 2023 governs the 'Validacion Previa' (pre-validation) scheme, under which the DIAN must return an XML acknowledgment with the 'documento equivalente' status before the document is legally binding; invoices below COP 5 UVT (~COP 233,000 in 2025) may be substituted by simplified receipts (tiques) if issued through an approved fiscal registry (registraduria elevada).

DIAN may audit zero-rated export invoices within a 5-year statute of limitations. If the zero-rating is deemed fraudulent or unjustified, the tax authority will retroactively assess the 19% IVA plus default interest (calculated at the Banco de la Republica's lending rate from the original due date) and impose: a) a penalty of up to 200% of the omitted tax (Article 648 of the Tax Code) for inexact declarations; b) an additional penalty of up to 100% of the tax for repeated infractions; c) criminal liability under Article 402 of the Penal Code for 'falsedad en documentos privados' if the export documentation is falsified, with imprisonment from 48 to 108 months in aggravated cases; d) the export qualification is void if the foreign exchange is not repatriated within the 180-day statutory term (extendable only in cases of force majeure or with prior authorization from the Banco de la Republica); failure to repatriate triggers a fine of up to 10% of the non-repatriated amount and loss of the zero-rated treatment, converting the supply into taxable local services; e) directors and legal representatives may be jointly liable for the payment of the contested tax if they sign inexact declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is freely convertible for current-account flows, but Colombia does not maintain full capital-account freedom. Inward direct investment, external loans, repatriation of capital, dividends, royalties, and service payments must be routed through an IMC and evidenced by a Declaración de Cambio. The EdTech revenue stream from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing qualifies as an export of services and is convertible without prior central-bank authorization, provided the proceeds are channeled through an IMC in accordance with the statutory deadlines. Dividend and profit remittance is lawful after the foreign investment has been registered with Banco de la República (Formulario No. 3 – Inversión Internacional) and applicable dividend withholding tax has been satisfied; unregistered investment forfeits the procedural right to obtain foreign exchange for remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Colombia imposes no mandatory percentage surrender and no forced conversion of export receipts; however, the full amount of service-export proceeds (including digital education subscriptions settled by app stores) must be repatriated and channeled through the IMC system. The statutory channeling window is six months for services and up to twelve months for goods; software products and technology-enabled education services may, upon proper classification, access the extended window. The proceeds may be retained in a foreign-currency deposit account with an authorized bank in Colombia or a registered foreign compensation account (cuenta de compensación), but channeling must be completed and evidenced in the applicable Declaración de Cambio. Late or omitted channeling gives rise to administrative sanctions and, where foreign assets are not declared to DIAN, to tax and foreign-wealth penalties.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (the Central Bank of Colombia), exercising exchange-control powers jointly with the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit under Law 9 of 1991 and External Resolution No. 8 of 2000, as amended; daily enforcement of the regulated exchange market (Mercado Cambiario) is performed by Authorized Exchange Intermediaries (Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario, IMC), while DIAN receives the ancillary tax-filing data.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Regulated Colombian financial-technology channels are viable: licensed electronic payment and digital-deposit companies (Sociedades Especializadas en Depósitos y Pagos Electrónicos, SEDPE) such as Nequi and Daviplata can serve as domestic collection and disbursement front-ends. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Mercury, and similar neobanks) have no regulated bank status in Colombia and cannot satisfy IMC channeling. Their viability is limited to temporary USD aggregation of Apple and Google store payouts before a regulated sweep into Colombia, with an overall feasibility rating of Medium for collection support and Low for any treasury, FX, or clearing function.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders must wire the capital contribution in USD to the S.A.S.'s account with an IMC bank. The wire must identify the beneficiary NIT, the S.A.S. name, the SWIFT BIC of the Colombian institution, and the narrative 'FDI capital contribution to Colombian S.A.S. (Código 1020)'. The bank prepares the Declaración de Cambio (Formulario No. 3 – Inversión Internacional) within five business days of receipt; the completed registration certificate must be retained as proof of investment for future dividend repatriation and capital refunds. Parent-loan disbursements must instead follow the external-debt channel: register the credit with Banco de la República where the aggregate facility exceeds the statutory threshold, present the external-debt declaration at disbursement, and comply with the minimum stay and registration covenants before servicing interest and principal.Use SWIFT MT103 for contractually documented cross-border IP licensing, trademark royalties, and software vendor payments; use MT202 covers for liquidity concentration. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Regulated Colombian financial-technology channels are viable: licensed electronic payment and digital-deposit companies (Sociedades Especializadas en Depósitos y Pagos Electrónicos, SEDPE) such as Nequi and Daviplata can serve as domestic collection and disbursement front-ends. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Mercury, and similar neobanks) have no regulated bank status in Colombia and cannot satisfy IMC channeling. Their viability is limited to temporary USD aggregation of Apple and Google store payouts before a regulated sweep into Colombia, with an overall feasibility rating of Medium for collection support and Low for any treasury, FX, or clearing function.
