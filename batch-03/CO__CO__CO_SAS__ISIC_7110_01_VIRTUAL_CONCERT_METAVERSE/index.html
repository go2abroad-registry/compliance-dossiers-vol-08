Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio with territorial jurisdiction over the registered office; integrated through the Registro Único Empresarial (RUES).

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), Arts. 1, 3, 5, 9 and 22; Código de Comercio, Arts. 98-110; Decreto Único Reglamentario 1074 de 2015, Book 2, Part 2, Title 1.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) registered in the Mercantile Registry; a public deed is required when the shareholders contribute real property or other assets transferable only by public instrument.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 17 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a SAS. Subscribed capital, its value, and installment terms are freely determined by the shareholders in the bylaws; any unpaid subscribed portion must be paid within two years. Cash contributions must be deposited in a Colombian bank account opened in the company's name before registration; no separate escrow account is required under Ley 1258 de 2008.

Funding & unlock path: No fire safety certification, structural load inspection, or physical venue clearance is required because the company operates without a public performance venue. If administrative offices are later leased, the company must obtain the fire department's Concepto Técnico de Seguridad Humana and the applicable electrical safety inspection under Ley 1523 de 2012, and comply with occupational safety obligations under Ley 1562 de 2012.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a SAS. Subscribed capital, its value, and installment terms are freely determined by the shareholders in the bylaws; any unpaid subscribed portion must be paid within two years. Cash contributions must be deposited in a Colombian bank account opened in the company's name before registration; no separate escrow account is required under Ley 1258 de 2008.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable - the virtual concert and digital ticketing activity does not operate a physical industrial, manufacturing, or venue establishment. General corporate oversight rests with the Superintendencia de Sociedades, and personal-data processing supervision with the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC).

Category: No statutory operating license is required for virtual performance hosting and digital ticket tokenization in Colombia. If musical works are used, the company must obtain copyright authorization from SAYCO-ACINPRO and may register creative works at the Dirección Nacional de Derecho de Autor.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) - official national tax and customs authority; taxpayer portal: www.dian.gov.co; electronic tax services: 'Servicios Tributarios DIAN' (https://www.dian.gov.co).

E-government filing portal

Foreign legal entities, including non-resident digital-service suppliers, must first appoint an attorney-in-fact or legal representative in Colombia with sufficient powers. The representative submits the duly legalized and apostilled constitutional documents (certificate of incorporation, bylaws, and board resolution appointing representative) to DIAN, either at a regional DIAN section or through the online RUT pre-registration system, using the form 'Registro Único Tributario (RUT)'. DIAN then assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). A foreign digital-service provider without a Colombian establishment may use DIAN's simplified electronic registration mechanism for non-residents and will be issued a provisional NIT/RUT; this registration does not in itself create a permanent establishment for income-tax purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope_Notes: The Colombian S.A.S. is a corporate income-tax resident because it is incorporated under Colombian law; it is taxable at entity level on worldwide income. For non-resident suppliers of digital services, only Colombian-source income is taxable, generally through withholding at source, and the non-resident is not a Colombian tax resident solely by reason of NIT/RUT registration. The entity is not a pass-through and no partner-level flow-through exists.

Available election forms

Not applicable: Colombia does not have a check-the-box or comparable entity-classification election; the S.A.S. is compulsorily taxed as a separate corporation under the Colombian Tax Code (Estatuto Tributario, ET). No hybrid mismatch fiscal-transparency election is available. Limited statutory choices exist for depreciation rates, inventory valuation, optional consolidated corporate income-tax filing under common control, and for small taxpayers the option to join the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (RST), but these do not alter the entity's default tax identity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - There is no general monetary de minimis turnover threshold for IVA registration in Colombia. Any person habitually engaged in the sale of taxable goods or services in Colombian territory, and any non-resident supplier of digital services to Colombian final consumers not registered as an IVA responsible, must register for IVA through the RUT. Small taxpayers with gross income below statutory limits (currently up to 3,500 UVT per year) may elect the simplified 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (RST), but that regime is an alternative aggregation of certain taxes, not a threshold for VAT registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% IVA rate applies to exports of tangible goods leaving Colombian customs territory, to services rendered to non-residents and used exclusively abroad where the benefit is realized offshore, and, with defined conditions, to digital services tied to foreign recipients. For virtual concert and metaverse ticket services, the taxpayer must prove the recipient is a non-resident, the service is effectively used outside Colombia, and the transaction lacks substantial local taxable use. Proof must be contemporaneous and documentary, including the applicable customs or service-export declaration.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación - DEX/DUA) issued by DIAN's customs module
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill / international transport document evidencing exit from Colombian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Formulario de Declaración de Cambio) evidencing collection through an authorized local foreign-exchange intermediary
  • Export or service commercial invoice referencing the NIT, the zero-rated nature, and the foreign buyer/service recipient

Electronic invoicing is mandatory for all IVA-responsible parties. Invoices must be generated through DIAN-authorized software or DIAN's free electronic invoicing application, and each sales transaction must be transmitted to DIAN in real time for validation as a 'factura electrónica de venta'. The electronic invoice must carry the unique electronic invoicing code 'CUFE' (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) and a QR link to DIAN for verification. The issuer must transmit acceptance or rejection events from the purchaser, and all operations are subject to DIAN's API-based clearance and periodic event reporting; no physical tax-stamping is used.

Zero-rating claims are subject to specialized DIAN audit under Articles 481 and 483 ET and the regulatory rules in Decreto 1625/2016. If conditions are not met or documentation is falsified, DIAN retroactively claws back the VAT not paid, adding daily interest and applying the 'sanción por inexactitud' for tax omission of between 100% and 200% of the greater amount of tax assessed if there are false, inaccurate or incomplete data (Article 648 ET). Persistent or intentional misrepresentation may also trigger criminal tax-fraud proceedings, suspension from the exporter registry, and seizure of goods or assets under the Colombian customs regime.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a unified free-floating exchange-rate régime with daily publication of the Tasa Representativa del Mercado (TRM). The Colombian peso is fully convertible for current-account transactions, including cross-border ticket sales, royalties, service fees, and dividend remittances, without prior central-bank approval. Capital-account inflows, including foreign direct investment into a Colombian S.A.S., may enter freely but must be reported through an authorized foreign-exchange intermediary (Intermediario del Mercado Cambiario, IMC) and registered with Banco de la República; absence of registration prevents future profit remittance and capital repatriation. Profit distribution to non-resident shareholders is permissible after satisfaction of the 35% corporate income tax and the 10% statutory dividend withholding tax (subject to any applicable tax treaty relief), with certified financial statements and a valid foreign-investment registry record. There is no separate remittance tax. Virtual-concert revenues denominated in USD or USDC are not subject to exchange-control approval if received within Colombia's current-account framework and repatriated within the statutory timeline.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Ticketing and metaverse-service revenues qualify as servicio de exportación under Decreto 1068 de 2015. The Colombian exporter must transfer 100% of the gross foreign-currency proceeds into the national territory through the foreign-exchange market within six months following the fecha de exigibilidad (due date) of each invoice. The proceeds must be sold to an authorized IMC; no partial surrender ratio is available. Receipts settled in USDC must first be converted to fiat USD through a compliant crypto exchange or treasury counterparty and then channeled through the IMC within the same deadline. Failure to channel or late channeling triggers daily monetary fines and penalty scales of 10% to 200% of the unrepatriated amount under the régimen cambiario, plus potential SARLAFT regulatory scrutiny for the financial network.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), acting jointly with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) for authorization and prudential supervision, and the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) for foreign-exchange enforcement. The governing framework is the Political Constitution of 1991, the Organic Statute of the Financial System, Decreto 1068 de 2015 (Único Reglamentario del Sector Hacienda y Crédito Público), and Resolución Externa No. 8 de 2000 of the Junta Directiva del Banco de la República.
