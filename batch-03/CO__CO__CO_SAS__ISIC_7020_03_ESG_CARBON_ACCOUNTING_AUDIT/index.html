Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio of the company's domicile (for professional services, typically Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá) operating the Registro Único Empresarial (RUES)

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), arts. 1, 4, 5, 15-18 and 22; Código de Comercio (Decreto 410 de 1971), arts. 98-127; Decreto 1074 de 2015, arts. 2.2.2.38.1 et seq. (merchant registration)

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) inscribed in the Registro Mercantil; public deed (escritura pública) required only when real property or significant in-kind contributions are made

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 15 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; Ley 1258 permits subscribed capital payable in installments up to 2 years (art. 4). No escrow or bank-account capital lock is required by law, although the registered subscribed capital must be reflected in the by-laws.

Funding & unlock path: Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos (Bogotá) Fire Safety Certificate (Certificado de Bomberos) for the leased/owned office issued after on-site inspection; municipal occupancy permit (uso de suelo y propiedad horizontal clearance) from the Departamento Administrativo de Planeación; workplace safety program per Resolución 0312 de 2019 with affiliation of staff to an ARL (Administradora de Riesgos Laborales); optional structural load report for open-plan floors in multi-tenant buildings.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital; Ley 1258 permits subscribed capital payable in installments up to 2 years (art. 4). No escrow or bank-account capital lock is required by law, although the registered subscribed capital must be reflected in the by-laws.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial licensing authority applies — ISIC 7020 (management consultancy and carbon audit) is an unregulated professional activity; general oversight rests with the Superintendencia de Sociedades for corporate law and DIAN/Secretaría Distrital de Hacienda for taxes.

Category: No sectoral operating permit required; the only registrations are the establishment registration (Registro Anexo de la Matrícula Mercantil) and ICA enrollment.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN), through its official portal www.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishes a Colombian branch or maintains a local legal representative and applies to DIAN for a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT). The entity must complete the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) electronically or in-person, provide the incorporation or foreign registration certificate (apostilled and translated), identify the representative, state the intended economic activities, and obtain the NIT before issuing invoices or operating. The RUT is the unified taxpayer identification and registration record.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity establishes a Colombian branch or maintains a local legal representative and applies to DIAN for a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT). The entity must complete the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) electronically or in-person, provide the incorporation or foreign registration certificate (apostilled and translated), identify the representative, state the intended economic activities, and obtain the NIT before issuing invoices or operating. The RUT is the unified taxpayer identification and registration record.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Colombia does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election for Sociedades por Acciones Simplificadas (S.A.S.). The S.A.S. is solely subject to entity-level corporate income tax. No hybrid mismatch elections are available under domestic law; taxpayers may apply the group consolidation regime (Régimen de Consolidación) for entities under common control, and may elect the SIMPLE taxation regime (Régimen Simple de Tributación) if small and meeting statutory thresholds, but this does not alter the default entity-level classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis VAT registration threshold; any entity carrying out taxable sales of goods or services in Colombian territory must register for VAT through the RUT with DIAN. For the optional Régimen Simple de Tributación, the gross revenue threshold is set in UVT (about COP 100,000 UVT per calendar year, adjusted annually), but VAT registration remains mandatory for all taxable operations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Colombia qualify for the 0% VAT rate under the export regime. Services qualify if the recipient has no domicile or permanent establishment in Colombia, the service is used or exploited exclusively outside Colombian territory, and the consideration is paid by a non-resident through the authorized foreign exchange market (when remittance is required). The supply must be directly related to the export or performed abroad; supporting documentation must evidence the fulfillment of these conditions.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign exchange settlement (Certificado de Reintegro) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial Invoice issued to the foreign purchaser

Electronic invoicing (factura electrónica de venta) is mandatory for all VAT-registered taxpayers. Invoices must be issued through DIAN-authorized software or DIAN's free system, transmitted in real time to DIAN's services, and validated before issue. The invoice must incorporate the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) or CUDS (Código Único de Documento Soporte) as a digital stamp. APIs and web services of DIAN are used for authentication, transmission, validation, and registration of invoices and supporting documents; deviations trigger automatic rejection or penalties.

DIAN may audit zero-rated exports retroactively. If the taxpayer fails to evidence the physical export, effective provision of offshore services, or timely foreign exchange repatriation, DIAN will assess the VAT at 19% plus applicable overpayment indexation, a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded or omitted, and interest for late payment. False or fictitious export declarations can give rise to criminal tax evasion charges, customs penalties, and disqualification from refund mechanisms. The applicable statute of limitations for DIAN assessments is generally six years, extendable in cases of non-declaration or fraudulent conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

COP is fully convertible for current-account transactions including service export earnings, dividends and interest remittances, subject to mandatory channeling through the official foreign exchange market ('canalización') by an authorized intermediary... Capital-account flows (FDI, foreign loans) are freely transferable but require registration / exchange declaration... For a carbon-audit SAS with 100% non-resident ownership, no prior central bank approval is needed; but the foreign investment must be filed through the 'Declaración de Cambio' with code 1001 within the statutory window (file within 6 months from channeling) to legalize future dividend/equity repatriation... Withholding applicable: 5% dividend WHT for non-resident shareholders (Tax Code), plus no exchange-surplus authorization required...

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Resolución Externa 1 of 2018, the mandatory surrender / conversion ratio for service export proceeds was abolished... The obligation is now timely 'canalización' (channeling) of export receivables through an authorized FX intermediary within 6 months from the invoice/service-completion date (per DIAN Circular DCIN-83, Section 03.04). 100% of the remittance must be accompanied by the electronic exchange declaration... proceeds may be collected in foreign currency and credited to a foreign-currency account at a local intermediary (cuenta de compensación) without forced sale to the market... failure = administrative fines & tax disallowance...

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (BANREP) ... operative powers under Law 9 of 1991, the Foreign Exchange Statute (Estatuto Cambiario, Decree 1068 of 2015), Resolución Externa 8 of 2000, Resolución Externa 1 of 2018 ... jointly administered by DIAN (exchange declarations) and supervised on the financial side by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed no later than the date published annually by DIAN for the relevant fiscal year. For a calendar-year taxpayer, the deadline generally falls between early April and late May of the following year, determined by the last digit of the taxpayer's NIT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File obligations arise where the taxpayer's gross income or gross assets exceed approximately COP 4,250,000,000 (100,000 UVT) and related-party transactions in the fiscal year exceed approximately COP 425,000,000 (10,000 UVT). Master File obligations apply where the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds approximately COP 16,500,000,000. Thresholds are updated annually using the UVT.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations. However, the General Anti-Abuse Rule in Article 658-1 E.T. and substance-over-form principles allow DIAN to disregard artificial arrangements. The SAS must demonstrate real people, premises, management decisions, and operational functions in Colombia; otherwise, the entity may be treated as a conduit or shell for tax avoidance purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Article 402 Law 1819/2016, tax evasion carries imprisonment of 4 to 9 years and a fine of 100% to 300% of the evaded tax. Administrative civil penalties can reach 200% of the amount sought to be evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives, and accountants who sign a false return can be jointly liable for unpaid tax and subject to asset seizures, travel bans, and disqualification from serving as officers of Colombian companies. Corporate veil-piercing is permitted under Article 42 Law 1258/2008.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The tax adjustment is computed at the arm's-length price, with a penalty of 100% to 300% of the resulting tax underpayment. Separate fines apply for missing documentation, up to 1,000 UVT.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve false transfer pricing documentation or who fail to implement the required TP master file can be held personally liable for resulting tax deficiencies and may face criminal liability if fraud is established.
Shell Company / Abuse of Corporate Personality

Statutory sanction: DIAN may disregard the SAS as a separate taxpayer, disallow all intra-group deductions, recharacterize the transactions, and impose fines based on the amounts involved. The commercial registry may also be cancelled for deceitful operation.

Director personal liability: Under Law 1258/2008, judges may pierce the veil when the SAS is used for fraud or improper conduct, making shareholders and directors personally, civilly, and fiscally liable for the company's obligations.
AML Non-Compliance in Carbon Offset Brokerage

Statutory sanction: Fines may reach several thousand UVT depending on the financial volume; registration with the Financial Superintendency or applicable AML authority may be ordered; egregious cases result in administrative liquidation and criminal proceedings under Colombia's criminal code.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face travel bans, personal fines, disqualification, and imprisonment of up to 12 years for laundering money derived from environmental fraud or unlawful offset schemes.
