Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; transactional portal: https://muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

A Colombian SAS obtains its Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) upon incorporation by filing the Certificate of Existence and Representation (Cámara de Comercio) and registering with DIAN through the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) procedure. Foreign entities without local presence need a Colombian appointed representative or attorney-in-fact to register for a NIT/VAT ID via the same RUT process, physically or through an authorized online intermediary, and must submit common corporate documents (certified copy of the deed/charter, power of attorney, foreign registration evidence, identification of legal representative) translated into Spanish and apostilled, if required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Colombian SAS obtains its Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) upon incorporation by filing the Certificate of Existence and Representation (Cámara de Comercio) and registering with DIAN through the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) procedure. Foreign entities without local presence need a Colombian appointed representative or attorney-in-fact to register for a NIT/VAT ID via the same RUT process, physically or through an authorized online intermediary, and must submit common corporate documents (certified copy of the deed/charter, power of attorney, foreign registration evidence, identification of legal representative) translated into Spanish and apostilled, if required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box elections exist under Colombian tax law; corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) is imposed at the entity level. An eligible S.A.S. may voluntarily opt into the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (RST), an elective special tax regime for small and medium entities, which substitutes CREE and some consumption withholding taxes. Hybrid mismatch regulations (Law 2010 of 2019) are mandatory and not elective, mandating jurisdictional tax characterization consistency for structured arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Legal entities, including S.A.S., have no turnover threshold and must register for VAT upon commencement of any taxable activity. Natural persons and informal entities become VAT responsible when their annual gross income exceeds 3,500 UVT (approx. COP 174 million for UVT 2025 valued at COP 49,799) or when otherwise meeting the qualitative nexus criteria in Article 437 of the Colombian Tax Statute.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to exports of goods and to services supplied for use or exploitation exclusively outside Colombia, provided the recipient is a non-resident, the service is not linked to subsequent local services, and the exporter obtains an official 'Exports of Services' certificate (Constancia de Exportación de Servicios) and complies with DIAN's documentary evidence requirements. For software and MMORPG in-app purchase activities, the exporter must evidence that the digital service is effectively consumed abroad, that intellectual property rights remain or are licensed to a foreign counterparty, and that the service is reflected in the exporter's periodic VAT declarations as zero-rated export income. Pure online services to foreign platforms qualify for zero-rating, but services linked to domestic end-consumers or related-party use may be taxed at 19% and subject to income tax source rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Colombia mandates electronic invoicing (facturación electrónica) for all VAT-registered taxpayers with real-time pre-clearance (validación previa) through DIAN's technological platform. Invoices must be generated using DIAN-authorised software or the free DIAN portal, digitally signed with an XAdES-EPES signature, include a compulsory QR code generated by DIAN, and be transmitted as XML. At the point of sale, real-time receiver acknowledgement from DIAN is generally required before the operation is legally perfected; non-compliant invoicing triggers penalties and the denial of input VAT credits.

DIAN audits export zero-rating claims using cross-border data exchanges and transfer pricing documentation under Articles 260-2 and 260-3 of the Tax Statute. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days results in full VAT recapture (clawback) plus monetary interest and a penalty of up to 160% of the tax previously zero-rated, invoiced by a formal assessment. Falsifying the export documentation or misrepresenting the offshore recipient triggers criminal penalties under Law 599 of 2000 (Criminal Code) for fraud, in addition to tax fraud penalties under Article 647 of the Tax Statute; retrospective clawback is not statute-barred for fraudulent or sham transactions, and senior management and export document preparers can be held jointly liable.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

COP is freely convertible on the current account for operating transactions; Colombia maintains a managed exchange-control regime for capital account operations. The MMORPG publishing entity receives hard currency (USD/EUR) settlement from Apple and Google: these are service exports (exportación de servicios) that must be channeled through the IMC and registered in the Foreign Exchange Declaration (Declaración de Cambio). Profit remittance to shareholders is permitted only if the foreign investment has been registered in the International Investment Register (Registro Internacional de Inversiones) maintained by Banco de la República; dividends may be remitted via IMC after the corporate income tax and the dividend tax (going to non-residents) are settled and the investment registration is active. Unregistered foreign shareholding results in non-registrable capital outflows, including dividend remittances and capital repatriation, so the FDI registration step is mandatory within the treasury blueprint.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from the exploitation of software and publishing services must be channeled through the IMC within 6 months of accrual (Decree 1735/1993, Article 75); the foreign currency so obtained must be monetized (sold/converted into COP) within 5 business days of receipt, unless the exporter applies the proceeds to an authorized foreign payment purpose (payment of external credits, import settlements, or a permitted foreign currency obligation). A Foreign Exchange Declaration (Formulario de Declaración de Cambio - Exportación de Servicios) must be filed with the IMC for each batch of receipts. The statutory conversion ratio is 100% of net export revenue (gross proceeds minus app-store commissions deducted by Apple/Google, evidenced by payout statements) and the proceeds are exempt from the obligation after the 6-month term if properly channeled but still require the declaration. Failure to channel within the term triggers fines by the Superintendencia Financiera / tax consequences, although small service exporters under the threshold may qualify for simplified declaration procedures in the IMC.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), with Foreign Exchange Directorate (Dirección de Cambios Internacionales) administering the exchange control regime under the legal framework of Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1735 of 1993, Resolution 8 of 2000 and the External Communications (Comunicaciones Externas DCIP); exchange transactions must be channeled through authorized intermediaries (Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario – IMC) and be reported in the Sistema de Información Cambiaria for registration.
