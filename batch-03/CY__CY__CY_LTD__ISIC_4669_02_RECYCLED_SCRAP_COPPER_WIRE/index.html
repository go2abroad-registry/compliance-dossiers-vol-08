Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (Τμήμα Φορολογίας) — TaxisNet portal: https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy; customs and export controls by the Department of Customs and Excise (Τμήμα Τελωνείων).

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus Private Limited Company (HE77) is automatically registered for income tax upon incorporation and obtains an 8-digit Tax Identification Number (TIN) coinciding with the company registration number. Before commencing taxable activity, the company or its appointed tax representative files Form E.F.V. 101 with the VAT Service of the Tax Department, attaching the certificate of incorporation, articles of association, registered office certificate, and director identification. The VAT number is issued in the format 'CY' + 8 digits + check digit. For physical consignment exports, the company must additionally obtain an EORI (Economic Operators Registration and Identification) number from the Customs and Excise Department.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus Ltd is a separate corporate taxpayer assessed at 12.5% on worldwide income; no pass-through or fiscal transparency classification exists. Participation exemption protects non-Cyprus dividend and permanent establishment income; the jurisdiction is Unitary_National with no sub-tax distinctions; non-resident ownership is fully permitted without affecting tax classification.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available under Cyprus law. Available statutory elections: (i) group relief consolidation for 75%+ EU/EEA resident subsidiaries; (ii) VAT group registration under VAT Law s.31(1); (iii) Cyprus IP Box election (Prorights) based on substantive nexus under s.25B; (iv) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on qualifying new equity; (v) tonnage tax election for qualifying vessels; (vi) deemed tax-residency determination is an evidenced-based management-and-control test rather than a formal election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 (mandatory registration if taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period exceeds EUR 15,600; non-resident suppliers must register from the first taxable supply in Cyprus; higher thresholds apply to intra-Community distance sales of EUR 35,000 and intra-Community acquisitions of EUR 10,251.61).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (i) direct exports and physical consignment of recycled scrap copper wire to non-EU destinations under VAT Law s.5(1)(a), provided the goods are dispatched to a third-country destination and the supplier obtains proof of export within 90 days from the date of supply; and (ii) intra-Community supplies under s.5(1)(b) where the buyer holds a valid EU VAT number and proof of intra-Community dispatch is secured within 5 months. Domestic Cyprus sales of copper scrap are excluded from the standard charge and are subject to the special reverse-charge rule under s.38A. The supplier must hold contractual evidence of risk transfer, freight terms (CIF/FOB/CIP), and foreign destination. Export refunds are claimable on each quarterly VAT return; excess credits are refundable through the Tax Department within 3 months of application.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Electronic Single Administrative Document) with EORI number validation
  • Bill of Lading (or CMR/Air Waybill) evidencing physical dispatch to the non-EU destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT confirmation proving receipt of proceeds from the foreign buyer

PEPPOL-compliant e-invoicing is mandatory for public-sector contracts (EU 2014/55/EU); B2B electronic invoicing is permitted but there is no real-time fiscal clearance, AI-based continuous control, or digital stamping regime currently in force in Cyprus. Periodic VIES recapitulative statements, Intrastat declarations, and the Cypriot Electronic Submission of Data (ESDE) framework must be filed electronically via TaxisNet. EU-level ViDA real-time digital reporting is not yet applicable to Cyprus and is expected only from 2030 onward.

Zero-rated export claims on recycled metals are subject to heightened scrutiny by the Cyprus Tax Department and EU anti-MTIC metal fraud task forces. If the export evidence is missing, untimely, or commercially inconsistent, the supply is reclassified as taxable at 19% with retroactive assessments up to 7 years, statutory interest (currently 8.5% per annum on VAT), and administrative penalties of up to 200% of the tax deficiency. Fraudulent zero-rating, false customs declarations, or artificial round-tripping of scrap triggers criminal prosecution of directors under VAT Law s.45, punishable by fines and imprisonment, in addition to customs fraud penalties under the Customs Code and AML actions for suspicious cross-border metals payments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus, as a Eurozone member, applies full free movement of capital under EU Treaty provisions. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible absent general foreign-exchange restrictions. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and capital repatriation are unrestricted, subject to standard corporate law requirements, tax clearance, and anti-money laundering compliance. The Euro is legal tender and all corporate treasury obligations can be settled in EUR or any freely traded currency. Practical hurdles arise only from enhanced due diligence for high-risk jurisdictions or sanctions screening, not from convertibility limitations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export receipts exists under Cypriot law. Export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency accounts with authorized banks, used for maritime or trade disbursements, or transferred offshore without central-bank approval. However, all cross-border payments above statistical reporting thresholds must be reported to the Central Bank of Cyprus for balance-of-payments compilation, and invoice/packing documentation must evidence the underlying physical consignment of recycled copper wire.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου / Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

EU-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and Payment Institutions passported into Cyprus may be considered viable for ancillary treasury rails such as virtual IBANs or payment initiation, but they are not authorized to hold capital escrow accounts or provide credit-linked trade settlement. Non-EU offshore digital EMIs cannot lawfully operate in Cyprus without an EU license or passport and are generally rejected for corporate account opening. Feasibility rating: Medium for EU-licensed EMI passporting; Low to Not Viable for offshore digital EMIs.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the Cyprus Ltd is routed via SWIFT MT103 from the subscribing shareholder's corporate bank account to the company's Cyprus escrow account. The wire must carry the ECB/BoP purpose code 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' (code 'EQ/DA') in the remittance instructions. The CBC-compliant verification package comprises: board resolution approving the share allotment; signed subscription agreement; share register update; negative confirmation of bearer or unregistered shares; source of funds evidence; bank account signatory interview; and a legal opinion if the investor is an offshore entity. Funds may be held in a blocked capital escrow account pending completion of KYC and registration of the share capital increase with the Cypriot Registrar of Companies.Outbound settlements for recycled copper wire consignments are cleared through: (i) SWIFT MT103/ MT202 for open-account telegraphic transfer payments from the buyer; (ii) Irrevocable Documentary Letters of Credit (LC) issued by the buyer's international bank, confirmed by a top-tier Cyprus or EU bank; or (iii) Documentary Collections (DP/DA) where buyer credit quality is acceptable. The LC must require presentation of clean-on-board ocean bills of lading, commercial invoice, independent quality/weight certificate, certificate of origin, and all permits required for importation of recycled copper. Payments are made to the Cyprus operating account set up with dual authorisation and automated sanctions screening. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Cyprus imposes no withholding tax on outbound dividends to non-resident shareholders; the special defence contribution on dividends for company payers has been abolished since 1 January 2021.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed by the earlier of (i) 15 months after the end of the accounting period, or (ii) 31 December of the year following the end of the accounting period. For an accounting year ending 31 December 2024, the filing deadline is 31 December 2025.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory de-minimis exemption is codified; the Cyprus Tax Department operates a risk-based, request-driven transfer-pricing regime. Contemporaneous Local File and Master File are practically required where annual related-party goods turnover exceeds EUR 1,000,000, services or financial transactions exceed EUR 500,000, or where the Cypriot entity operates within an MNE group with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750,000,000 (the mandatory Country-by-Country Report threshold; the CBCR form is filed in Cyprus on ARIADNE together with notification).

Economic substance scope: Cyprus has no UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code, but the doctrine operates indirectly through the Cyprus corporation residence test (management and control exercised in Cyprus), the IP Box nexus requirements, the ATAD-compliant interest limitation rule, and the 2017 administrative TP framework. For an ISIC 4669 recycled-scrap trader, substance is established by physical office premises, locally resident directors engaged in procurement/logistics decisions, documented board minutes, bank accounts in Cyprus, and evidence that negotiation, risk assumption, and freight/logistics management are performed in Cyprus. Failure to demonstrate substance exposes the company to general anti-abuse rulings, recharacterization of profits, denial of NID and treaty benefits, and CFC attribution in shareholder jurisdictions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction under the Cyprus Income Tax Law: fine of up to EUR 20,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 5 years; civil recovery of the underpaid tax plus a 10% assessment surcharge and 5% annual interest. The Cyprus Tax Department can also raise a jeopardy assessment with immediate collection measures.

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors who sign false returns or direct concealment are prosecuted as principals, with travel bans and sequestration orders issued under the Tax Collection Law. Upon conviction, automatic disqualification as director for up to 5 years applies; the director's personal bank accounts can be frozen under a foreign-asset investigation order.
Trade-Based Money Laundering (AML)

Statutory sanction: Venue: Cyprus AML Law 188(I)/2007, as amended by Law 15(I)/2020. Legal person exposed to criminal fines of up to EUR 1,000,000, publication of the sanction, suspension of the Cyprus VAT registration, and seizure of bank accounts; the financial institution is obligated to file a suspicious transaction report to MOKAS, triggering an asset freezing order. Repeat or systemic misconduct leads to an administrative penalty of EUR 500,000 and loss of the operating licence.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly involved in ML can be sentenced to imprisonment for up to 14 years and face confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime Law covering the company's assets. Directors found to have tolerated the conduct are individually liable for civil restitution to the repatriating creditor and subject to EU-wide travel bans in extradition proceedings.
