Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the full authorized capital must be subscribed at incorporation, but payment may be deferred under the terms fixed in the by-laws. No mandatory escrow account requirement. The S.A.S. may be formed with one share of nominal value greater than zero.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); official portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional portal: muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

After commercial registration of the S.A.S. in the Cámara de Comercio, the legal representative must file the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) application with DIAN before starting taxable activities. Filing can be completed online through the MUISCA platform or at a DIAN assistance point using the digital certificate of the legal representative or a Colombian attorney-in-fact under a notarized special power of attorney. DIAN assigns the Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT), which serves simultaneously as income tax ID, VAT ID, and customs ID. A non-resident foreign entity requiring a Colombian tax identification for invoicing, withholding, or investment may also obtain an NIT/RUT through a Colombian fiscal representative.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — After commercial registration of the S.A.S. in the Cámara de Comercio, the legal representative must file the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) application with DIAN before starting taxable activities. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

There is no Colombian 'check-the-box' election for foreign tax classification; an S.A.S. is always a Colombian taxpayer at the entity level. Elective regimes available include the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (RST) for eligible SMEs subject to turnover and activity limits, and optional consolidated taxation for Colombian corporate groups under Article 132 of the Tax Code. Anti-hybrid rules restrict deduction of hybrid mismatch payments, but no pass-through election is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary turnover threshold for corporate entities. Any S.A.S. engaged in taxable transactions must register as an IVA responsible party in the RUT before operations. For natural persons, a non-responsible regime applies when annual gross income is below 3,500 UVT (approximately COP 164 million for 2024), but this exemption does not apply to companies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and certain services qualify as excluded from IVA (zero-rated). For services, including digital stock photo licensing and generative visual asset subscriptions, the service must be supplied to a person without residence or domicile in Colombia, must be used or exploited exclusively outside Colombia, and the consideration must be received from abroad. The exporter has the right to discount input VAT and to claim a refund of the resulting positive balance through DIAN.

  • Commercial invoice issued to the non-resident client evidencing the asset license, subscription, or generative service and the consideration received
  • Validated electronic sales invoice (factura electrónica de venta) accepted by DIAN, containing the CUFE/CUNE code
  • Customs Export Declaration (declaración de exportación/DEX) and customs authorization for physical goods; for digital services, the export service contract or license agreement and proof of provision
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or equivalent transport document for physical exports
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Certificate of Foreign Exchange Reintegration (certificado de reintegro de divisas or declaración de cambio por servicios) issued by a Colombian authorized financial intermediary

DIAN Resolution 165 of 2023 and prior regulations mandate electronic invoicing ('factura electrónica de venta') for all IVA-responsible taxpayers. Invoices are generated in XML, transmitted in real time to DIAN or an authorized technology provider, validated by DIAN, and returned with a 'validada' status. Each invoice must contain a unique DIAN validation code (CUFE/CUNE) and a QR representation; integration through DIAN web services or the authorized provider's API is compulsory for billing software. This digital validation operates as fiscal clearance/stamping. Supporting documents ('documento soporte') must be issued for transactions that are not subject to invoice.

DIAN subjects export-based VAT refunds to automated risk-scoring and documentary verification. If the documentation does not prove the export nature, the non-resident status of the counterparty, or the actual receipt of funds from abroad, the refund may be denied, blocked, or retrospectively clawed back with default interest and a penalty for 'inexactitud' under Article 647 of the Tax Code, which can reach up to 200% of the improperly refunded amount. Foreign exchange reintegration violations may also be penalized by Banco de la República, and intentional tax evasion may trigger criminal prosecution under Articles 434A-434C of the Tax Code, including custodial penalties. The general statute of limitations for DIAN to verify annual returns and refunds is five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia has a managed (partial) convertibility regime. Current-account transactions, including service exports and royalty income, are freely convertible and can be channeled through the foreign exchange market without prior approval, subject to mandatory reporting and the four-month collection timeline. Capital-account operations are controlled and must be registered with Banco de la República. Foreign direct investment (FDI) must be registered using Form 4 (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales). Profit remittance to foreign shareholders is permitted only after the FDI registration is valid and a 'Certificado de Reintegro de Utilidades' is produced; such remittances attract the statutory remittance tax (currently 12% under Law 2277/2022). The principal non-tariff hurdles are mandatory conversion of export-of-services proceeds to Colombian pesos, the four-month reintegro period, and the prior FDI registration requirement for dividends.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For exports of services, 100% of foreign-currency proceeds must be collected and sold into the official foreign exchange market (i.e., an authorized bank) within four (4) months from the earlier of service provision or contractual due date. A 'Declaración de Cambio' must be filed with the local bank upon collection. Non-compliance results in a penalty of 5% of the outstanding unsettled amount for each month or fraction, plus interest. Extensions are possible only in exceptional facts, but digital platform receipts are routinely inspected, so strict adherence is expected.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Banrep) and the Financial Superintendence of Colombia (SFC) as supervisory regulator.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Mercury) are not authorized Colombian foreign-exchange intermediaries and cannot serve as the principal repository for service-export proceeds. Their use as payment-orchestration rails for disbursements (e.g., royalty payouts to foreign contributors) is feasible provided the entity independently meets the Banco de la República channeling and registration requirements. Feasibility rating: Medium for ancillary operations, Low for primary treasury and dividend repatriation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For equity injection by a foreign shareholder, the funds must be remitted to the local bank using an international SWIFT MT103 with the correct purpose code (typically 'FIN.INV/EQ' or the bank-assigned code for foreign direct investment) and the recipient's NIT and account number. Upon receipt, the bank countersigns the exchange declaration and issues an investment certificate; within 12 months from the contribution date, the company must file Form 4 ('Registro de Inversiones Internacionales') with Banco de la República. Until registration, the deposit is held in a 'capital escrow' sub-account or a restricted account; after registration, the funds are reclassified as equity, enabling future profit remittance and capital repatriation.The primary outbound clearing rail for settling royalties, licensing fees, and global content contributors is a standard SWIFT MT103 wire transfer submitted via the local bank's corporate banking interface. For large software procurement or acquisition of AI infrastructure, the bank can issue an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (LC) at sight under UCP600, confirmed by the beneficiary bank. All trade settlement payments must be declared in the 'Declaración de Cambio' under the corresponding service-import exchange concept number, and applicable withholding taxes (RETEIVA, RETEFUENTE) must be discharged. For recurring micro-payouts, batch-processing through SWIFT service bureaus or an RPA-driven file upload to the bank is acceptable.Delivery forward contracts (FX forwards) for up to 24 months with authorized local banks or registered foreign counterparties.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) in USD/COP, permitted for hedging virtual export revenue without requiring physical peso delivery.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) for matching foreign-currency loan and revenue structures.Foreign-currency options (European-style) to hedge committed revenue when the banking counterparty is licensed by the SFC.
