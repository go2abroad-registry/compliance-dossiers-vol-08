Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio del domicilio social; filing processed through RUES (Registro Único Empresarial y Social) under the public oversight of the Superintendencia de Sociedades.

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Colombian Simplified Stock Company / S.A.S. Act), arts. 2, 5, 17, 21 and 40; Código de Comercio, arts. 98, 110, 373 and 374; Decreto Único Reglamentario 1074 de 2015, as amended.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) submitted for registration; public deed if selected by incorporators or required for certain in-kind contributions.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 17 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital for S.A.S. entities; capital may be subscribed and paid within a maximum of two years. No government escrow deposit is required for cash contributions; in-kind contributions must be described and appraised in the incorporation document.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety technical concept (concepto técnico de bomberos) for the physical office; favorable land-use certificate (uso de suelo) issued by the municipal planning office; structural-load and occupation-capacity certification from a licensed architect or engineer; and compliance with the mandatory workplace health and safety system (SG-SST) under Resolución 0312 de 2019.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital for S.A.S. entities; capital may be subscribed and paid within a maximum of two years. No government escrow deposit is required for cash contributions; in-kind contributions must be described and appraised in the incorporation document.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED by the founding shareholders as the first corporate governance act: (1) The private incorporation instrument of the S.A.S. is approved, and the employer-of-record and contractor payroll corporate purpose is adopted. (2) The subscribed share capital is accepted; shareholders ratify their payment obligations under the bylaws. (3) The registered office is fixed at the address stated in the deed within Colombia. (4) The sole legal representative and alternates are appointed with full powers to open bank accounts and execute payroll and employment contracts. (5) The legal representative is directed to complete incorporation, NIT, and labor licensing filings. This resolution is effective immediately and recorded.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – Colombian national tax and customs authority; official portal www.dian.gov.co; electronic registration and filing platform MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicio y Control Automatizado) at muisca.dian.gov.co.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the S.A.S. before a notary and register with the local Cámara de Comercio; 2) upon registration, DIAN automatically assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria, 9-digit code plus verification digit), which is reflected on the RUT (Registro Único Tributario); 3) access MUISCA with the NIT and the mandatory digital signature (Firma Electrónica DIAN) to update economic activity ISIC 7810, VAT status as Responsable de IVA – Régimen Común, and withholding-agent (Agente de Retención) status; 4) for a foreign non-resident company without a local entity, obtain an NIT by filing the RUT enrolment through MUISCA, appointing a Colombian attorney-in-fact, and registering the foreign company's permanent-establishment status for any Colombian-source services; 5) request the electronic invoicing authorization (Habilitación de Facturación Electrónica) in the same MUISCA session before the first taxable transaction.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation – the Colombian S.A.S. is a resident tax entity taxed at the entity level on worldwide income under the Impuesto de Renta (35% headline rate for tax year 2025); pass-through treatment is not available. Colombian-source income remains the territorial nexus for non-resident shareholders receiving dividends, which are subject to a 10% dividend withholding tax at the shareholder level. — 1) Incorporate the S.A.S. before a notary and register with the local Cámara de Comercio; 2) upon registration, DIAN automatically assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria, 9-digit code plus verification digit), which is reflected on the RUT (Registro Único Tributario); 3) access MUISCA with the NIT and the mandatory digital signature (Firma Electrónica DIAN) to update economic activity ISIC 7810, VAT status as Responsable de IVA – Régimen Común, and withholding-agent (Agente de Retención) status; 4) for a foreign non-resident company without a local entity, obtain an NIT by filing the RUT enrolment through MUISCA, appointing a Colombian attorney-in-fact, and registering the foreign company's permanent-establishment status for any Colombian-source services; 5) request the electronic invoicing authorization (Habilitación de Facturación Electrónica) in the same MUISCA session before the first taxable transaction.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation – the Colombian S.A.S. is a resident tax entity taxed at the entity level on worldwide income under the Impuesto de Renta (35% headline rate for tax year 2025); pass-through treatment is not available. Colombian-source income remains the territorial nexus for non-resident shareholders receiving dividends, which are subject to a 10% dividend withholding tax at the shareholder level.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists under Colombian law. Available statutory elections: (i) optional accession to the Régimen Simple de Tributación (SIMPLE) under E.T. Articles 905 et seq., replacing income tax, VAT and local industry/commerce tax with a single turnover-based tax at rates of 1.8%–14.5%, available only if prior-year gross income does not exceed 100,000 UVT (approximately COP 4.98 billion in 2025) and other eligibility conditions are met; (ii) the de minimis No Responsable de IVA status for natural persons and small goods vendors with gross income not exceeding 3,500 UVT (approximately COP 174.3 million in 2025), which is unavailable to a service company such as an EOR staffing platform; (iii) voluntary registration as a withholding agent for domestic and cross-border payments; (iv) election of the simplified formula for the 10% advance instalment of corporate income tax for taxpayers with prior-year net revenue not exceeding certain UVT limits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary exemption threshold: any enterprise providing taxable services in Colombia must register in the RUT and obtain VAT status before commencing activities, regardless of turnover. De minimis thresholds apply only to goods-only merchants (income not exceeding 3,500 UVT, approximately COP 174.3 million in 2025), which is unavailable to a staffing/EOR services taxpayer; the SIMPLE regime turnover ceiling is 100,000 UVT (approximately COP 4.98 billion in 2025).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: a legal export qualifies for the 0% excluded VAT rate when the goods leave Colombian customs territory under a DEX export declaration filed by the exporter or an authorised customs agent and the sale is made to a foreign counterparty with shipment abroad. Services: a service qualifies as an export of services under E.T. Article 481 when (i) the recipient is a non-resident entity with no domicile or residence in Colombia, (ii) the service is used exclusively outside Colombia, the benefit, use or appreciation occurring entirely abroad, and (iii) the payment is received in freely convertible foreign currency and channelled through Colombian foreign-exchange intermediary rules; the exporter must issue the electronic export invoice and hold RUT status with export activities. Practical caveat for ISIC 7810 EOR/staffing services: DIAN restricts zero-rating where the workforce or the operative benefit of payroll, contractor management or back-office services occurs in Colombia, so the S.A.S. must document that the contractual benefit is consumed abroad and should obtain a protective advance ruling or consult DIAN before applying 0% IVA.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX – Declaración de Exportación) for tangible goods, complemented by the electronic export invoice (Factura Electrónica de Exportación) with CUFE code for services
  • Bill of Lading (oceanic) or Airway Bill (air) evidencing exit of goods from Colombian customs territory with the consignee located outside Colombia
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Declaración de Cambio por Exportación de Servicios issued by an authorised Colombian foreign-exchange intermediary, evidencing legal repatriation of the export proceeds in freely convertible currency

Mandatory electronic invoicing through DIAN's MUISCA platform: all VAT-responsible taxpayers must issue the Factura Electrónica de Venta (FEV) in XML under UBL 2.1, signed with the DIAN digital certificate and transmitted via DIAN-authorised Proveedores Tecnológicos (API/SOAP web services, REST/JSON integration). Each invoice must contain the unique CUFE/CUDE code and a QR code. DIAN assigns each taxpayer an emission profile of validación previa (real-time fiscal clearance: the invoice is validated against MUISCA before delivery to the buyer) or validación posterior (clearance within a set period). Export transactions require the Factura Electrónica de Exportación with a designated export type code, recorded alongside the relevant foreign-trade declarations. No physical fiscal stamp is affixed; the legal digital seal is the CUFE/CUDE code accompanied by DIAN's electronic acceptance event.

DIAN may retroactively disallow the 0% export treatment and assess the full 19% IVA on the gross invoice, plus default interest (intereses moratorios) from the original due date of the VAT return. False or inexact export claims trigger the 100% to 200% sanción por inexactitud under E.T. Articles 648 and 649 on the resulting VAT or income-tax differences, and improper refund or offset claims may incur the additional sanction for unjustified refunds under E.T. Article 670. Fraudulent customs or exchange declarations can constitute criminal tax fraud under Código Penal Article 402 and Law 2277 of 2022, carrying imprisonment and fines equal to the evaded tax; repeat offences and sham invoicing arrangements may additionally lead to DIAN collection proceedings, precautionary seizure of assets, loss of the exporter's special registration, and exclusion from government procurement registers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is freely convertible at a unified floating market rate. Current-account transactions, including service exports and employment-related cross-border payments, are settled without quantitative limits but must be routed exclusively through authorized FX intermediaries and declared on the electronic Declaración de Cambio (Formulario No. 7). Capital-account inflows through the foreign direct investment (FDI) route are open to non-residents and must be registered with the Banco de la República to secure future capital and profit remittance rights; profit remittances are permitted once Colombian taxes are settled, subject to the statutory dividend withholding rate (generally 10% for non-resident shareholders, with possible treaty relief). No compulsory surrender to the central bank and no exchange-profit tax apply, but all operations require formal FX-channeling documentation and a documented source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Colombia operates a channeling (canalización) regime rather than a conversion or surrender quota: 100% of foreign-currency export proceeds from goods and services must be repatriated and channeled through an authorized FX intermediary, or credited to an approved foreign-currency or compensation account, within the term endorsed on the export declaration or the service contract, generally up to six months after shipment or the due settlement date for services. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the exporting S.A.S. may retain USD/EUR in a local foreign-currency account or sell the proceeds to the interbank market at the prevailing market rate. Evidence is the bank-encoded Declaración de Cambio, and failure to repatriate or channel within the applicable timeline exposes the company to exchange-control penalties under Law 9 of 1991 and Resolution Externa No. 8 of 2000.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), acting jointly with the Ministerio de Hacienda y Crédito Público and the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia, is the foreign-exchange control authority under Law 9 of 1991, Resolution Externa No. 8 of 2000, and Decree 1068 of 2015; the DIAN administers export declarations and customs documentation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, as a restricted gateway only. Foreign digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) are not locally chartered financial institutions and cannot obtain authorization under the Colombian Statutory Financial System to operate as banks or FX intermediaries; they may be used for cross-border collection, contractor payouts, and international card issuance, but all domestic COP payroll, social security, and tax obligations must be settled through a Colombian regulated financial institution. Using an offshore EMI as the principal treasury ledger raises SARLAFT concerns and FX-channeling non-compliance risk unless funds are cascaded into a licensed onshore bank account with proper Declaración de Cambio support.

Commercial Registration Renewal (Renovación de la Matrícula Mercantil) Annually between 1 January and 31 March; no extensions $30 USD Late renewal after 31 March triggers a 50% surcharge on the renewal fee plus moratory interest; renewal is suspended, and failure to renew for two consecutive years causes administrative cancellation (strike-off) of the mercantile registration.
Annual shareholders' meeting for approval of financial statements (Estados Financieros) and management report; minutes recorded in corporate books Within 3 months after fiscal year-end (31 March for a calendar-year entity); the approved statements must be filed with the Chamber of Commerce within 30 days of approval $5 USD Persistent omission enables the Superintendencia de Sociedades to impose an administrative fine of up to 50 SMMLV and to compel the meeting; unapproved or unfiled statements constitute a compliance breach under Ley 222/1995.
Annual corporate income tax return (Form 210 – Declaración de Renta y Complementario) Between mid-April and mid-June of the following tax year according to the last two digits of the NIT, per the annual DIAN resolution $0 USD Late-filing sanction under ET Art. 641: 1% of tax payable per month or fraction of delay, capped at 100% of the tax; if no tax is due, 1% of gross income capped at 1,000 UVT with a 10-UVT minimum; moratory interest accrues until payment.
Annual final beneficial owner declaration (Declaración de Beneficiarios Finales – Form 350) Annually in April–May by a two-digit NIT schedule fixed by DIAN resolution; reflects shareholding as of 31 December of the preceding year $0 USD Untimely, false, or omitted declarations are subject to fines under ET Art. 651 of up to 10,000 UVT per filing; the company's electronic invoicing and tax compliance certificates may be blocked until the declaration is accepted.
Transfer pricing documentary return (Form 120) where the EOR platform exceeds applicable thresholds Same window as the corporate income tax return (Form 210) according to the NIT schedule $0 USD Delayed or omitted filing triggers the ET Art. 641 extemporaneity sanction and, when mandatory, an additional fine of up to 10,000 UVT under ET Art. 651; no strike-off effect.
