Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance — competent for corporate income tax (CIT), VAT, and social insurance withholding; electronic filing via 'Ariadni' / Tax For All portal (https://taxis.gov.cy/); customs administration for export goods is under the Customs & Excise Department (Τελωνεία Υπουργείου Οικονομικών), responsible for export declarations and EORI issuance.

E-government filing portal

Locally incorporated Cyprus Ltd: registration is automatic — the Department of Registrar of Companies forwards incorporation data to the Tax Department, which issues a 9-digit Tax Identification Number (TIN) without further application; VAT number is applied for separately (format CY + 8 digits + 1 letter). Non-resident foreign entity establishing a Cyprus branch/PE: file an application for income tax registration (Form TIN-101) with the District Tax Office within 3 months of commencing taxable activity, together with certified memorandum/articles, proof of registered office, and director identification. Non-established VAT supplier: no threshold applies — file electronic Form VAT 101 via Tax For All before the first taxable supply, attaching certificate of incorporation, certificate of registered office, board resolution appointing a tax representative (where required), audited financials/parent financials, and proof of local place of business. For export operations, obtain an EORI number from the Customs & Excise Department through the DPS (Declaration Processing System) portal prior to lodging any customs export declaration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus private limited company is a separate taxable person subject to Corporate Income Tax at the headline rate of 12.5% on worldwide income; tax residence is determined by central management and control. No fiscal transparency is available for companies (pass-through treatment confined to partnerships). Participation exemption applies to qualifying dividend income and capital gains from disposal of shares, subject to the 1% or substantial-holding anti-abuse conditions. The territory of the Republic of Cyprus is a normal-tax jurisdiction with no exempt-zone or offshore/international-business-company distinction in the statute. — Locally incorporated Cyprus Ltd: registration is automatic — the Department of Registrar of Companies forwards incorporation data to the Tax Department, which issues a 9-digit Tax Identification Number (TIN) without further application; VAT number is applied for separately (format CY + 8 digits + 1 letter). Non-resident foreign entity establishing a Cyprus branch/PE: file an application for income tax registration (Form TIN-101) with the District Tax Office within 3 months of commencing taxable activity, together with certified memorandum/articles, proof of registered office, and director identification. Non-established VAT supplier: no threshold applies — file electronic Form VAT 101 via Tax For All before the first taxable supply, attaching certificate of incorporation, certificate of registered office, board resolution appointing a tax representative (where required), audited financials/parent financials, and proof of local place of business. For export operations, obtain an EORI number from the Customs & Excise Department through the DPS (Declaration Processing System) portal prior to lodging any customs export declaration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus private limited company is a separate taxable person subject to Corporate Income Tax at the headline rate of 12.5% on worldwide income; tax residence is determined by central management and control. No fiscal transparency is available for companies (pass-through treatment confined to partnerships). Participation exemption applies to qualifying dividend income and capital gains from disposal of shares, subject to the 1% or substantial-holding anti-abuse conditions. The territory of the Republic of Cyprus is a normal-tax jurisdiction with no exempt-zone or offshore/international-business-company distinction in the statute.

Available election forms

Cyprus has no US-style check-the-box election and no elective hybrid 'blend' regime; EU ATAD hybrid mismatch and CFC rules apply mandatorily. Available statutory elections include: (1) Group loss relief election under Income Tax Law s.26(19) — surrender of tax losses within a 75% group, filed with the annual tax return; (2) Optional IP Box regime — election/opt-in for the 80% deemed deduction of qualifying intangible property profits subject to commensurate-in-substance substance requirements; (3) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) claim on new qualifying equity capital — supported by a capital computation schedule submitted with the CIT return; (4) Deemed dividend distribution (Special Defence Contribution) arrangement for close companies to align deemed distributions with actual distributions; (5) Foreign tax credit election — relief by credit against Cyprus tax for foreign source income; (6) VAT elections — option to tax immovable property, election for monthly/quarterly fiscal periods, and partial-attribution method elections for input VAT recovery.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€15,600 per annum for taxable persons established in Cyprus; €10,000 pan-EU threshold for intra-Community distance sales of goods and electronically supplied services; non-established foreign suppliers have NO threshold — compulsory registration before the first taxable supply in Cyprus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT is applied to the supply of goods (including wholesale physical trade of prilled urea and potassium chloride) dispatched or transported by or on behalf of the seller to a destination outside the EU, provided that (i) the goods leave the EU within 90 days from the time of supply, (ii) the exporter holds a validated customs export declaration (Electronic SAD/EX-A with MRN), and (iii) transport documents (B/L, CMR, AWB) evidence dispatch from a Cyprus/EU port to a non-EU destination; title and risk of loss must pass to the foreign purchaser — a genuine supply of goods, not a carriage/charter service, is required. Intra-Community supplies to a VAT-identified purchaser are exempt with credit equivalent to zero-rating (0%) subject to the customer's valid EU VAT number and EC Sales List reporting. Input VAT incurred on export purchases and overheads is deductible and may be recovered by exporters through periodic VAT refund claims (Form VAT 13) or the monthly refund scheme; non-established EU suppliers may use the Directive 2008/9/EC electronic refund portal and non-EU claimants the 13th Directive process. Supporting export evidence must be retained for at least 6 years.

  • Customs Export Declaration — Single Administrative Document (SAD/EX-A) with validated MRN (Movement Reference Number) through the Cyprus Customs DPS/ECS system
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or CMR waybill (road) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing dispatch from a Cyprus/EU point to a destination outside the EU
  • Certificate of Origin and any applicable phytosanitary/safety data sheet for urea-classification products
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 / Letter of Credit documentation matching the export value
  • Sales confirmation or INCOTERM-linked contract evidencing transfer of title and risk to the foreign purchaser
  • EC Sales List and Intrastat declaration (where the goods are dispatched to another EU Member State)

No mandatory real-time B2B e-invoicing, no fiscal clearance API stamping, and no digital tax stamp regime is currently in force in Cyprus. Electronic filing of the VAT return (Form VAT 4), EC Sales Lists, and Intrastat declarations is compulsory via the Tax For All/Ariadni portal. E-invoicing is mandatory only for B2G invoices under EU Directive 2014/55/EU through the PEPPOL network. Tax invoices may be paper or electronic subject to authenticity and integrity safeguards; compliance with the future EU ViDA framework is under preparation but not yet enacted.

VAT audits are conducted by the VAT Audit Service of the Tax Department. Where zero-rating is not substantiated within the 90-day export evidence window, or the goods are diverted for EU consumption, the supply is assessed at 19% VAT with statutory interest. Late filing and late payment penalties escalate from 5% to 10% and up to 100% additional tax where the Tax Department determines fraud or gross negligence. In cases of deliberate false zero-rating declarations, the Cyprus courts may impose criminal fines up to €10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 3 years, together with retrospective clawback of all input VAT reclaimed and denial of refunds until full substantiation is provided. Limitation periods: 5 years for assessments generally, extended to 10 years in cases of fraud, false statements, or obstructive conduct; directors may be held jointly and severally liable for VAT debts of the company.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a member of the euro area and applies the EU free movement of capital principle under Article 63 TFEU. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible without pre-approval from the CBC or the Ministry of Finance. The euro is freely convertible into other currencies, and funds may be credited or debited from a Cyprus corporate account in EUR or any major tradable currency. Profit remittance by a Cyprus private limited company in the form of dividends, interest, royalties, management fees or branch profits is not subject to exchange-control permits. The only material hurdles are documentary and AML-driven: the company must have lawful distributable profits evidenced by audited financial statements or a solvency statement permitted by the Cyprus Companies Law; the bank must obtain a current beneficial-ownership declaration, board minutes resolving the distribution, tax residency confirmation, and evidence that the beneficiaries are not sanctioned persons or under active PEP/high-risk screening. Non-resident shareholders may freely receive dividends without CBC approval, although the bank may require a domestic withholding tax analysis when the shareholder is a non-EU tax resident.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cyprus has no statutory export-proceeds surrender, repatriation or mandatory conversion obligations. Export receipts from prilled urea and potassium chloride sales may be retained in EUR or any convertible currency in a Cyprus-licensed bank account; there is no regulatory timeline for bringing proceeds onshore, no required conversion ratio and no obligation to deposit export earnings with the Central Bank of Cyprus. The only applicable obligations are statistical and customs related: the receiving bank reports cross-border payments to the CBC for ECB balance-of-payments statistics, and the exporter must file Intrastat/Extrastat declarations and Union Customs Code export declarations with the Cyprus Customs Department where thresholds or non-EU shipment rules apply. Such reporting is informational and does not restrict the use or timing of export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα Κύπρου, CBC), operating within the European System of Central Banks and the European Central Bank framework. The CBC is the licensing, prudential and AML/CFT supervisory authority for credit institutions and payment/e-money institutions in Cyprus. There is no separate foreign-exchange administration; exchange-control competences are absorbed by EU rules on free movement of capital and the CBC's balance-of-payments and sanctions-compliance functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-Low for primary treasury reliance, and High only as an auxiliary payment channel for small-value operational disbursements. An electronic-money institution (EMI) authorized in an EU/EEA State may passport into Cyprus by notification to the Central Bank of Cyprus under the Payment Services and Electronic Money Laws. A non-EU/EEA offshore EMI may not lawfully provide payment accounts or money-remittance services to a Cyprus-resident corporate client without a Cyprus authorization or a valid EEA passport; such offshore EMIs are therefore not a viable clearing rail for capital injection, export proceeds or LC settlement. Cyprus-licensed or passported EMIs can execute SEPA and some SWIFT transfers, but they cannot issue or confirm trade letters of credit or offer borrowing or currency-swap facilities because those are regulated banking/credit activities. The recommended use is a limited auxiliary wallet linked to a primary tier-1 commercial bank account, with the wallet never holding more than EUR 50,000 at any given time, to preserve reconciliation quality and avoid enhanced AML complexity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injections must arrive via SWIFT MT103 from a bank account held in the name of the investing shareholder; third-party funding is unacceptable and will trigger automatic enhanced due diligence. The remittance must include the Cyprus company's full legal name, the Cyprus IBAN, the account bank's SWIFT BIC, and an instruction in field 72 or field 70 reading E.g., 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION pursuant to board resolution dated [date]' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN per loan agreement dated [date]'. For equity contributions, attach to the bank a certified copy of the board resolution, evidence of the capital increase filed with the Cyprus Registrar of Companies, the updated register of members, and an internal source-of-funds declaration from the investor. For shareholder loans, provide a signed loan agreement with commercial terms, an interest rate and repayment schedule, a serviceability assessment, and confirmation that the lender is the same UBO/shareholder. The Cyprus bank will classify the receipt for ECB balance-of-payments purposes under direct investment or other capital flows, but no CBC exchange-control approval or conversion license is required. If the overseas jurisdiction imposes outward-remittance restrictions, the investor must first obtain its local central bank approval and present that approval to the Cyprus bank. The entire inbound path must be documented in the corporate treasury file to support the accounting treatment and the 12.5% corporate income tax filing.For international commodity settlements, the primary clearing rails are SWIFT-based bank transfers and documentary credits. Use SWIFT MT103 for post-shipment cash payment of invoices and for margin calls, and MT700/MT710/MT760 for irrevocable documentary credits, standby LCs and guarantees. For prilled urea and potassium chloride purchases, the recommended mechanism is an irrevocable documentary letter of credit payable at sight, with confirming bank involvement where the supplier demands payment risk mitigation. The paying bank must receive a complying presentation including the commercial invoice, original negotiable bill of lading, certificate of origin, independent weight and quality certificates, phytosanitary or other authorization if applicable, and a sanctions-end-use certificate. EUR disbursements to EU counterparties must clear through TARGET2/SEPA; USD and CNY disbursements require correspondent-bank clearing via the receiving bank's nostro/loro relationships. The disbursement instruction must cite the LC number or the underlying trade contract reference, and the amount must exactly match the invoice and the Incoterms (CIF, CFR or FOB). Before execution, the Cyprus bank will screen the beneficiary and the paying agent against EU/UN/UK/OFAC sanctions lists and may request a copy of the export/import customs declarations to confirm physical goods movement. Cash, travellers cheques, bearer instruments and non-bank money-service third parties are absolutely prohibited for trade disbursements.Delivered FX outright forwards (FXA) between EUR and the relevant trade currency (USD, CNY, GBP, CHF), offered by CBC-licensed banks under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or bank master forward agreement, with credit-line support and MiFID II suitability documentation.FX swaps (deliverable swap transactions combining a spot and a forward leg) for temporary conversion of EUR into USD or CNY to fund trade payments and supplier advance requirements; permitted subject to counterparty credit limits, EMIR trade reporting and internal treasury approval.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CIRS) used to hedge long-term shareholder loans, import/export receivables or physical commodity positions denominated in USD or CNY where both FX and interest-rate exposures are present; subject to ISDA/CSA margining and EMIR regulatory reporting.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for restricted or partly convertible currencies (most notably offshore CNY, RUB, TRY or INR) may be entered into with an authorised non-resident OTC counterparty or through a Cyprus-bank hosted swap line, provided the hedge is supported by an underlying trade contract and the bank obtains board evidence that the transaction is commercial, non-speculative, and is reported as a derivative for EMIR/MiFIR purposes.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is required when the aggregate annual value of controlled transactions of the Cyprus company exceeds EUR 750,000; Master File and CbC report duties arise at group consolidated turnover exceeding EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus applies substantive engagement rules through EU Code of Conduct criteria and OECD BEPS requirements. A Cyprus commodity trading company is expected to maintain real physical premises in Cyprus, adequate employees and management who make procurement, freight, and pricing decisions in Cyprus, board minutes showing actual commercial control, bank accounts in Cyprus, and sufficient working records; failure to demonstrate this substance supports TP adjustments, re-categorisation as a conduit, and the withdrawal of tax residence certificates.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under Cyprus income tax legislation; fines up to the amount of tax evaded plus a further penalty of up to 100% of the assessment; mandatory repayment of avoided tax; confiscation of the proceeds; imprisonment for up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign fraudulent returns or instruct concealment are jointly and severally liable with the company. The corporate veil can be pierced to impose civil restitution, disqualification from acting as directors, seizure of personal assets, travel bans, and custodial sentences.
Transfer Pricing Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: A TP adjustment bringing the taxable profit back to the arm's length level, with additional 12.5% corporation tax on the uplift, statutory interest, late-payment penalties, and possible penalty for incomplete contemporaneous documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly implement weak or sham pricing policies may be liable for negligent or fraudulent statements to the Tax Department. Courts may pierce the veil and render directors personally liable for the extra assessment, lift passport/travel restrictions, and impose disqualification orders.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Commissioner may annul the Cyprus tax residence certificate, re-characterise the arrangement under beneficial-ownership principles, deny deductions, exclude treaty benefits, and assess tax at 12.5% plus interest and penalties; administrative fines follow under anti-abuse provisions.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors can be stripped of limited liability protection and held personally accountable for tax debts in the case of wilful misconduct or reckless trading; travel bans and asset-freezing orders are routinely pursued in substance-abuse cases.
AML Violations and Sanctions Breach

Statutory sanction: Cyprus AML legislation imposes fines on legal persons of up to EUR 1,000,000 or an amount calculated from turnover or underlying proceeds; criminal penalties include imprisonment up to 5 years, confiscation, and suspension of bank accounts; regulatory disqualification of the company's AML officer is mandatory.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal indictment for permitting or participating in laundering, travel bans and passport surrender during investigation, personal fines, seizure of beneficial ownership assets, and imprisonment.
