Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus, Ministry of Finance (official portal: TAXISnet at https://taxisnet.mof.gov.cy/; e-services for VAT registration and customs clearance through the Cyprus Customs Department)

E-government filing portal

Non-resident entities must apply for Cyprus VAT/TIN registration via TAXISnet. Required steps: (1) Complete and submit the standard VAT application (TD 101 / electronic application) in Greek or English, executed by authorized signatories; (2) attach notarized/certified copies of constitutive documents (Memorandum and Articles of Association), certificate of incorporation (or foreign equivalent if applying as an overseas entity), power of attorney appointing a Cyprus tax representative (mandatory for third-country entities without EU establishment), and identity documents; (3) submit a statement of intended trading activities in the aseptic oat milk manufacturing sector; (4) obtain issuance of VAT identification number and corporate TIN within approximately 2 to 4 weeks; (5) register for Cyprus customs/ECS (Exports Control System) at the Customs Department for export declarations and Trader Portal access.

Required steps: (1) Complete and submit the standard VAT application (TD 101 / electronic application) in Greek or English, executed by authorized signatories; (2) attach notarized/certified copies of constitutive documents (Memorandum and Articles of Association), certificate of incorporation (or foreign equivalent if applying as an overseas entity), power of attorney appointing a Cyprus tax representative (mandatory for third-country entities without EU establishment), and identity documents; (3) submit a statement of intended trading activities in the aseptic oat milk manufacturing sector; (4) obtain issuance of VAT identification number and corporate TIN within approximately 2 to 4 weeks; (5) register for Cyprus customs/ECS (Exports Control System) at the Customs Department for export declarations and Trader Portal access.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not permit a 'check-the-box' pass-through election. Available statutory elections include: (a) VAT grouping election (VAT Law No. 94(I)/2000 Art. 45) permitting associated enterprises to elect to be treated as one taxable person; (b) election to apply the VAT margin/one-off exemption or fixed-rate partial exemption method based on approved standard sector method; (c) option to tax on intra-EU removal to other Member States with acquirer registration proof; (d) group relief election for transfer of tax losses between 75%-owned resident/subsidiary companies (Income Tax Law Art. 26); (e) notional interest deduction on new equity capital (Art. 9(ZZ), Income Tax Law) made by election in the tax return; (f) election for tonnage tax is not applicable to manufacturing; (g) special election for IP Box not applicable to oat milk production unless patents/software are exploited. All elections are subject to advance filing conditions with the Tax Department.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 in taxable supplies (excluding exempt-with-deduction VAT) over the preceding 12 months or a prospective 12-month period; distance sales to Cyprus trigger the EUR 35,000 EU threshold unless exceeded, after which local VAT registration is obligatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods from Cyprus to a third country (outside the EU) qualifies for 0% VAT where the physical dispatch/transportation occurs within three months from the tax point (VAT Law 94(I)/2000, Sec. 33 and EU VAT Directive Art. 146). For the UHT oat milk product, zero-rating also requires that the goods leave the EU customs territory and are declared in the export customs procedure. Services related directly to export transactions (transport, loading, storage, commissions) supplied to a customer outside the EU are likewise zero-rated under ordinary place-of-supply rules if the recipient is a taxable person outside Cyprus/EU and the benefit of the supply is enjoyed outside the EU. The supplier must hold a valid commercial evidence trail.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document (SAD) — electronic export declaration lodged in European Export System (ECS) with MRN
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR consignment note (in the name of or for the account of the exporter) showing physical departure from Cyprus/EU
  • Commercial invoice (itemized UHT oat milk products, HS code 2202.99 or 2106.90 as applicable, quantities, pricing)
  • Packing list and weight/volume certificate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer proof reflecting payment receipt in Cyprus from foreign purchaser
  • Purchase/contract evidence connecting supplier, exporter, and foreign customer, together with proof of purchaser's EU VAT registration or local tax identification status

As of the 2025 position, Cyprus does not impose mandatory real-time fiscal pre-clearance or digital stamping for all B2B/B2C invoices. Taxpayers are required to issue sequential numbered invoices within 30 days and preserve electronic/xml versions for audit. Cyprus is transposing EU Directive 2014/55/EU for B2G e-invoicing; the Tax Department accepts e-invoices issued through certified software and integrators, and is expected to implement the ViDA digital reporting requirement (real-time B2B database) following public consultation; no API clearance with digital stamping protocol is currently operational.

Export zero-rating is audited under Cyprus VAT Law Art. 46-48, Customs Law 2004, and Dispute resolution mechanism. If the Tax Department establishes that no exports actually taken place or documents are false, it will assess output VAT at the standard rate (19%) plus statutory late-payment interest and impose administrative penalties up to 100% of the assessed VAT difference, together with criminal liability for tax evasion; in addition, purchases and input VAT credits connected with the export may be clawed back retroactively.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility. Cyprus adopted the euro (EUR) on 1 January 2008 and maintains a completely liberalised capital and current account. Business profits, dividends, branch remittances and liquidation proceeds may be converted and transferred abroad without Central Bank of Cyprus approval, exchange-control permit, or profit-remittance tax. Dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are generally subject to 0% Cyprus withholding tax where conventional participation conditions are met. A UHT oat-milk production company receives the same treatment as any other Cyprus-domiciled commercial entity: no screening, quota, or EUR-allocation mechanism applies to the import of capital for plant construction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Cyprus retains no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory repatriation timeline, and no compulsory conversion ratio for export receipts. Proceeds from sales of UHT products to buyers in third countries may be held freely in EUR or in foreign currency. The only administrative duty is statistical: every cross-border payment, inbound or outbound, equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500 (or equivalent) must be reported by the bank through the Central Bank of Cyprus International Transactions Reporting System (I-TRS), and standard export documentation under the EU Union Customs Code applies.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou), the Republic's monetary, banking-supervisory and payment-system authority, operating within the euro-area framework of the European Central Bank and the Single Supervisory Mechanism. Exchange-control competence is exercised through the Central Bank of Cyprus together with the free-movement-of-capital provisions of Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-to-High feasibility as a complementary payment layer, never as the sole banking infrastructure. A Cyprus-authorised Electronic Money Institution under the Electronic Money Law (N.81(I)/2012, as amended) or an EU/EEA EMI passported into Cyprus under the Revised Payment Services Directive (PSD2) may support multi-currency virtual IBANs, SME FX conversion, payroll execution, and sub-EUR-12,500 operating disbursements. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot arrange documentary Letters of Credit, cannot provide genuine credit facilities, and frequently lack host-to-host SWIFT for corporates; accordingly, inbound capital injection, plant capex payments, and LC-supported import settlements must run through tier-1 supervised credit institutions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity and shareholder-loan injections are routed by SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the parent or investor bank to the CY_LTD EUR account at a tier-1 Cyprus bank. Every transfer must carry a compliant Balance-of-Payments purpose classification for the Central Bank of Cyprus I-TRS: for equity subscription use the 'direct investment - equity capital' classification; for intercompany funding use 'direct investment - intercompany lending'; and for advance payments against imported UHT-line machinery use the 'goods - advance payment' classification with the proforma invoice attached through the bank's trade desk. Supporting documentation includes the share-subscription resolution, executed loan agreement, certificate of the UBO register, and confirmation that the remitting bank is not a shell or non-bank pass-through. Cash or third-party aggregation is prohibited; the full UETR of the SWIFT message must align with the source-of-funds documentation.Three clearing rails are authorised for trade settlements. Rail 1: straight SWIFT MT103/2 telegraphic transfer for prepayments, sample orders, spare parts and open-account balances below EUR 500,000, referencing the commercial invoice and proforma. Rail 2: irrevocable documentary Letter of Credit opened by SWIFT MT700 under ICC UCP 600 for the principal UHT filling lines, aseptic packaging machines, and bulk raw-material imports, with the confirming bank in the seller's jurisdiction where required. Rail 3: documentary collection (SWIFT MT400 / collection instructions) for mid-value settlements where security is required without the cost of a full LC. All outbound payments at or above EUR 12,500 require Central Bank of Cyprus I-TRS reporting; the relationship bank's trade desk verifies invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, certificates of origin, insurance certificates, and inspection certificates before releasing funds; EUR settlements clear via T2 (formerly TARGET2), USD legs via CLS/CHIPS, and GBP legs via CHAPS, each with T+2 value dating.Deliverable outright EUR/USD and EUR/GBP forwards (up to 18 months tenor) under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with Credit Support Annex, executed bilaterally with the relationship bank.EUR/USD FX swaps and rolling rollover swaps used for short-term liquidity bridging of trade payables and seasonal working-capital peaks.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in EUR/CNY and EUR/INR, cash-settled in EUR, applicable to restricted or non-convertible currency exposures in the packaging-materials supply chain.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CCIRS) to convert a USD- or CNY-denominated trade-finance or equipment-finance liability into EUR, subject to EMIR trade reporting and the NFC- clearing-threshold assessment.Exchange-traded FX futures and options on regulated EU venues, cash-settled and deployed exclusively as documented IAS 39/IFRS 9 hedges of projected USD, GBP and CNY receipts from oat-milk export sales.
