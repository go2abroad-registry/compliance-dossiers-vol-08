Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); main portal: www.dian.gov.co; electronic services via the MUISCA platform (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicios y Control Automatizado), including the mandatory electronic invoicing system.

E-government filing portal

The Colombian S.A.S. must first complete notarial and Chamber of Commerce registration, then obtain the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) and NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) through the DIAN portal, under the MUISCA platform, before commencing any taxable operations. Non-resident shareholders are not required to obtain a local NIT unless they derive Colombian-source income subject to tax; in such cases, registration by the foreign taxpayer may be effected through a duly empowered representative or legal agent in Colombia.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the S.A.S. is a Colombian tax-resident corporation taxed on worldwide income; corporate income tax headline rate of 35%, plus a 3% surcharge on taxable income exceeding approximately COP 1.2 billion; dividend remittances to non-resident shareholders are subject to dividend withholding tax and, for dividends from earnings subject to corporate tax, an additional assessed tax.

Available election forms

No check-the-box regime is available in Colombia; entity classification is determined solely by legal form and tax residence, and no elective passthrough treatment exists for a Colombian S.A.S. Hybrid mismatch considerations are governed by the OECD Multilateral Instrument and the Andean Community rules, but Colombian domestic law does not grant elective hybrid mismatch status to domestic or cross-border entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

COP 0: no general turnover threshold for IVA registration; any individual or legal entity, resident or foreign, that performs taxable activities in the national territory, including foreign suppliers of digital services to Colombian consumers, must enroll in the RUT and be registered as an IVA responsible party. Limited simplified rules apply to occasional taxpayers and small artisan sellers, but are not applicable to a regular S.A.S. engaged in the sale of specialty tea in a fixed salon.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Colombia are excluded from IVA and qualify for zero-ratification provided the exporter holds a valid customs export declaration and proves the goods have left Colombian customs territory. Services rendered by the specialty tea salon may qualify as exempt/zero-rated if they are genuinely provided to non-residents or used exclusively abroad, such as digital platform services or consultancy related to international distribution, subject to Article 481 of the Estatuto Tributario and applicable DIAN interpretive rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) submitted electronically through DIAN customs systems
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of the goods from Colombia
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas) issued by an authorized Colombian foreign exchange intermediary, evidencing collection in the established term

Electronic invoicing is mandatory for all VAT-responsible taxpayers: the S.A.S. must issue the factura electrónica de venta through an authorized software or service provider, transmit the invoice in real time to DIAN's validation web services/API before delivery to the purchaser, and include the DIAN-generated digital validation code (digital stamping). The invoice must also be made available to the consumer through a digital channel or QR code, and the seller must retain the electronic invoice record with the DIAN imputation for fiscal audit purposes.

DIAN may audit export-supported VAT refunds for up to five taxable years; any misstatement, fictitious export, or fraudulent documentation triggers revocation of the zero-rating and of any refund, reclassification of the transaction as locally taxable, default interest, penalties of up to 200% on improperly obtained refunds or compensations, and potential criminal liability for tax fraud and customs smuggling. Retrospective clawback also applies where the exporter fails to maintain the underlying commercial and transport documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is fully convertible for current-account transactions (imports, exports, services, transfers) and for most capital-account transactions without prior government authorization, but every regulated operation must be settled exclusively through an IMC and evidenced by an exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio). Inbound FDI in an S.A.S. is free, but must be channeled and registered in Formulario No. 3 to preserve profit-remittance and capital-repatriation rights; unregistered or off-market investment is 'descanalizada' and cannot legally exit through the FX market. Profit remittance hurdles: (i) funds must be legally registered FDI, (ii) profits must be evidenced by audited/filed financial statements and shareholder meeting minutes, (iii) dividend remittances require a Formulario No. 3 declaration and are subject to 10% non-resident dividend withholding tax (plus the underlying 35% inflationary? No, don't add tax) – hence all treasury intents should carry underlying documentation. External credits from non-resident shareholders are equally allowed but must satisfy the external-debt registration and term/interest rules of Decreto 1735/1993 and Resolución 8/2000; a compulsory reserve deposit (encaje) may be reinstated when ordered by the Junta Directiva (currently not active).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters must 'canalize' 100% of export proceeds through the Colombian FX market: for goods, within 6 months from the date of shipment (fecha de embarque); for services (relevant if the SAS bills foreign clients for software or lounge consulting), within 6 months from the invoice maturity date of the service. Fulfillment may be by (a) sale of the foreign currency to an IMC for COP, or (b) deposit into a foreign-currency checking/savings account at an IMC in Colombia, in both cases filing Formulario No. 2 (goods) or Formulario No. 5 (services). Retention abroad requires a previously authorized compensation account (cuenta de compensación) granted by the Banco de la República in exceptional cases. Late or partial channeling is an exchange infraction sanctionable by DIAN with fines and the loss of the right to use the official FX market for the uncovered amount.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Junta Directiva del Banco de la República); exchange-control supervision exercised by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) over Authorized Intermediaries (Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario – IMCs) and enforcement/penalty assessment by the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); legal basis: Constitución Política de Colombia (1991), Ley 31/1992, Estatuto Cambiario (Decreto 1735/1993), and Resolución Externa 8/2000 (Régimen de Cambios Internacionales).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility as primary treasury rail: offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Mercury, Airwallex) hold no Colombian IMC license, cannot issue local COP account numbers, cannot settle POS/credit-card merchant funds in COP, and are not accepted by DIAN/Banco de la República for Formulario declaration purposes. Permitted only as ancillary offshore receivable channels, provided all funds are immediately re-channeled (recanalización) into the Colombian IMC account within the term prescribed by Resolución 8/2000 to avoid descanalización penalties. Regulated onshore fintech alternative: SEDPES (Sociedades Especializadas en Depósitos y Pagos Electrónicos, e.g., Nequi, Daviplata) may serve as low-value e-money wallets and POS collection taps, but they do not replace the IMC bank account for cross-border FX or corporate disbursements.

Annual corporate income tax and complementary return (Declaración de Renta y Complementarios) Annually under DIAN's published tax calendar; for an S.A.S. with a 31 December fiscal year, filing windows generally fall between July and September of the following year, determined by the final digit of the NIT. $0 USD Late-filing penalty of 5% per partial or full month on the greater of tax due, capped under the Tax Code, plus default interest; repeated failure enables DIAN default assessment and suspension of the RUT, affecting ability to operate, invoice, or obtain public certificates.
