Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum capital is required for a S.A.S. (Ley 1258 de 2008, Art. 4). Shareholders freely fix the subscribed and paid capital; deferred payment of cash subscriptions may extend up to 24 months, while in-kind contributions must be fully paid at execution. An escrow account is not statutorily mandated for standard incorporation, but cash subscriptions should be evidenced by a temporary bank deposit certificate attached to the constitution document.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate (Certificado de Bomberos) from the municipal Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos, use-of-soil and structural load clearance from the local planning office (Curaduría Urbana / Alcaldía), and workplace safety registration with the ARL and Ministerio de Trabajo compliance for office premises.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum capital is required for a S.A.S. (Ley 1258 de 2008, Art. 4). Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

DIAN – Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (National Tax and Customs Directorate); official transactional portal: Muisca (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicio y Control Automatizado) at https://www.dian.gov.co.

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned S.A.S. registration sequence: (1) incorporator executes private deed and registers the company with the Cámara de Comercio of its domicile, obtaining the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal and mercantile pre-registration data; (2) within 30 days the legal representative, holding a Colombian digital certificate, activates the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) in Muisca, which automatically assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria); (3) the RUT must be updated to reflect statutory tax roles: 'Responsable de IVA – Régimen Común', 'Declarante de Renta', and 'Exportador de Servicios' (calidad de exportador); (4) complete electronic invoicing 'habilitación' by registering internal billing software or engaging an authorized technology operator to obtain the CUFE validation scheme; (5) non-resident shareholders require their own NIT only for dividend withholding and transfer-pricing compliance, obtainable by appointing a Colombian apoderado who files the RUT pre-registration with the shareholder's passport data.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation – The S.A.S. is a resident juridical person taxed as a separate taxpayer on worldwide net income under the Estatuto Tributario. Territorial scope: Colombian-source income plus foreign-source income attributable to a Colombian permanent establishment; the corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) is 35% for 2025, with no pass-through option. Dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are subject to a final 10% withholding at the distribution date, applying the imputation/credit mechanism that avoids double taxation on already-taxed profits. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation – The S.A.S. is a resident juridical person taxed as a separate taxpayer on worldwide net income under the Estatuto Tributario. Territorial scope: Colombian-source income plus foreign-source income attributable to a Colombian permanent establishment; the corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) is 35% for 2025, with no pass-through option. Dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are subject to a final 10% withholding at the distribution date, applying the imputation/credit mechanism that avoids double taxation on already-taxed profits. Municipal ICA (Impuesto de Industria y Comercio) applies at 0.2%–1.4% on gross consulting fees depending on city.

Available election forms

No US-style 'check-the-box' or voluntary hybrid-mismatch elections exist; Colombia applies anti-hybrid and CFC rules automatically under Law 2010/2019 and Law 2277/2022. Available elections relevant to this ISIC 7020 profile: (i) Régimen Simple de Tributación (SIMPLE) – elective integrated income/VAT regime for entities with prior-year gross ordinary revenue not exceeding 3,500 UVT (approx. COP 174,296,500 for 2025); consulting services are taxed at progressive rates of approximately 2.2%–14.5% on gross revenue, replacing the separate 19% VAT and income-tax advance; (ii) Deducción por inversión en activos fijos productivos – allows an extra 30% depreciation-deduction election on qualifying new fixed assets; (iii) bimestral VAT export-refund election under ET Art. 855 for exporters with periodic saldo a favor; (iv) voluntary ICA 'régimen de retención' alternatives available at the municipal level. Tax consolidation is not available to standalone S.A.S. entities; group statements are consolidated only for financial reporting.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic or turnover threshold exists for commercial companies: an S.A.S. providing taxable consultancy services is objectively required to register as a VAT responsible (Régimen Común) from the first taxable transaction (ET Art. 437). The only quantitative exclusion applies to natural persons without a commercial establishment whose annual gross income does not exceed 3,500 UVT (COP 174,296,500 for the 2025 UVT of COP 49,799) and who do not import taxable goods; these persons remain 'no responsables de IVA'. The CO_SAS entity must therefore register in the RUT and obtain prior authorization for electronic invoicing regardless of revenue level.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A foreign market-entry consultancy service (ISIC 7020) qualifies for the 0% VAT export rate under ET Art. 481 and Decree 1893/2019 only if the following cumulative tests are satisfied and documented: (i) the client is a non-resident entity with no permanent establishment or branch in Colombia; (ii) the consultancy is developed and its economic benefit is realized exclusively outside Colombian territory, including overseas delivery of feasibility reports, regulatory gap analyses, market-entry strategies, and remote or offshore advisory sessions; (iii) the consideration is effectively paid from abroad by a non-resident payer through a foreign-currency remittance, external bank account, or an approved B2B cross-border rail such as Wise B2B, and is not charged to a Colombian domicile; and (iv) the S.A.S. holds the 'calidad de exportador' marker in its RUT and reports the operation as an export of services in Muisca, including issuance of an FEV with the foreign client's TIN and CUFE. Services that generate benefits within Colombia, such as local regulatory lobbying, domestic branch assistance, or negotiating with Colombian government authorities, are taxable at 19%. Qualified exporters may file bimestral VAT returns and request refund of the resulting saldo a favor, with the right to accelerated refund where the exporter has maintained an impeccable compliance record.

  • Customs Export Declaration – for services: the DIAN export-of-services declaration (Form 504 / Muisca export module) identifying the ISIC 7020 transaction, the foreign client, and the legal basis for the 0% rate; for any physical deliverables (e.g., printed feasibility kits or market-study prototypes) a DEX (Declaración de Exportación) must be concurrently filed through the VUCE system.
  • Bill of Lading – required where physical goods accompany the consultancy (airway bill or ocean bill of lading evidencing goods leaving Colombian customs territory); for purely offshore services, the functional equivalent is the signed technical-services contract, the FEV with CUFE, and the delivery acceptance certificate from the foreign client.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate – certificado de canalización de divisas issued by a local market-intermediary bank (or Banco de la República Form 520/530) demonstrating that the Wise B2B or bank-wire proceeds were collected and channeled through the Colombian foreign-exchange market within the statutory term.

Mandatory end-to-end electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta – FEV): all taxable services must be issued and transmitted in real time to DIAN through Muisca, either via the DIAN-authorized web service (FIT) or through an authorized technology operator. Each FEV must contain the statutory CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) or the CUDS code for support documents, plus a QR, and must be validated by DIAN before it becomes fiscally operative; invoices that fail validation are legally ineffective. The taxpayer's billing software must be previously 'habilitado' by DIAN and all purchase vouchers from non-invoicing suppliers must be reported through the Documento Soporte en Adquisiciones a No Obligados a Facturar. As of 2025, DIAN has fully fiscalized the real-time clearance cycle, including 100% inspection of XML payloads, UUID conformity, and cross-referencing with customs and FX data.

DIAN may reopen export zero-rating positions for 5 years (10 years where no return was filed) and may retroactively recharacterize the service as taxable at 19% where any of the four zero-rating conditions is missing. Violations trigger: (i) full VAT assessment plus monthly moratory interest (currently around 1.5% per month, compounded); (ii) inaccuracy penalty up to 200% of the omitted tax (ET Art. 648); (iii) export-commodity misdeclaration penalties under customs law; (iv) clawback of any VAT refund obtained without entitlement, increased by 100% under ET Art. 668 plus interest; (v) criminal liability for fraudulent export invoicing or false quality-of-exporter declarations under Law 1762/2015, with imprisonment of 48 to 144 months for aggravated contraband; and (vi) administrative consequences including cancellation of the 'calidad de exportador', exclusion from the accelerated refund procedure, and mandatory electronic-invoicing audit for subsequent periods.
