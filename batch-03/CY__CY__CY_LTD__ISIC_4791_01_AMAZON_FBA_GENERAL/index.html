Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; e-portal at taxportal.mof.gov.cy

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity without a place of business in Cyprus must apply for Cyprus VAT registration (Form TD101) through the Tax Department Portal, appointing a tax representative where required. Upon approval, the Cyprus VAT/TIN number is issued, formatted as CY + 8 digits + 1 alphabetic check character. TIN for corporate income tax purposes is the same VAT identification number used for direct tax filing.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope (tax-resident companies taxed on worldwide income; non-resident companies taxed only on Cyprus-source income) — A foreign entity without a place of business in Cyprus must apply for Cyprus VAT registration (Form TD101) through the Tax Department Portal, appointing a tax representative where required. Upon approval, the Cyprus VAT/TIN number is issued, formatted as CY + 8 digits + 1 alphabetic check character. TIN for corporate income tax purposes is the same VAT identification number used for direct tax filing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope (tax-resident companies taxed on worldwide income; non-resident companies taxed only on Cyprus-source income)

Available election forms

No check-the-box or broad hybrid-mismatch election exists; ATAD-aligned fixed rules apply. Available election: VAT grouping under section 34 of the Cyprus Value Added Tax Law 94(I)/2000, allowing closely related entities to be treated as one taxable person, subject to conditions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for Cyprus-established taxable persons; non-established foreign suppliers making taxable supplies are generally registrable regardless of threshold, subject to distance-sale/Union-OSS special rules where applicable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to goods dispatched or transported from Cyprus to a destination outside the European Union provided the seller (or carrier or agent on the seller's behalf) removes the goods outside the EU within the statutory period and holds the official customs export declaration and commercial transport evidence. Services qualifying as exports to a non-EU recipient are zero-rated where the effective use and enjoyment is outside the EU and the supplier retains documentary evidence; certain ancillary services to export goods are zero-rated when directly linked to the export transport.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/EX-A) with ARN confirmation
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR / Community Transit Document evidencing dispatch outside the EU
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Proof of exported consignment reaching the non-EU destination (e.g., customs clearance confirmation from destination customs)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank payment receipt evidencing receipt of export proceeds in a Cyprus or EU bank account

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control (CTC), or digital stamping e-invoicing system is currently in force for domestic B2B supplies. VAT registration, VAT returns, Intrastat declarations, and related filings must be submitted electronically through the Cyprus Tax Portal. E-invoicing is permitted and recognized for VAT purposes; B2B e-invoices must be archived to a reliable, truthful, and legible format in accordance with VAT record-keeping rules.

Any claim for export VAT zero-rating is subject to on-demand documentary verification and audit by the Cyprus Tax Department. Where substantiation is incomplete or fraudulent, the zero rate is denied, the taxpayer becomes liable for VAT at 19% plus statutory interest, and administrative penalties of up to 200% of the assessed amount may apply. Knowingly providing false customs declarations or fabricated shipping and remittance evidence constitutes criminal evasion, carrying fines of up to EUR 10,000, imprisonment up to six years, and the reversal or 'clawback' of any VAT refund previously paid, together with legal interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Republic of Cyprus has been a euro-area member since 1 January 2008; the euro is fully convertible on both the current account and the capital account. Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union guarantees unrestricted free movement of capital and payments both within the EU and vis-à-vis third countries, subject only to narrowly construed public-policy justifications, FATF/AML safeguards and prudential derogations consistent with Article 65 TFEU. No Cypriot statutory gate, exchange control or prior-authorisation regime applies to profit repatriation, dividend distributions, debt service, intercompany payments or repatriation of foreign direct investment by a Cyprus private limited company. In practice, convertibility is constrained not by black-letter law but by bank-level compliance gatekeeping: every outgoing cross-border credit transfer from a Cyprus credit institution must carry a valid purpose code, be compatible with the account's declared economic profile and pass CBC-mandated transaction-monitoring screens. Dividend remittances require corporate-law distributable capital confirmation, an audited profit position and, where applicable, tax residence and withholding-certificate documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory export-proceeds surrender rule, no statutory conversion ratio and no obligation to repatriate foreign-currency receipts within any fixed calendar window. Amazon FBA exporters and marketplace sellers may lawfully retain USD, GBP, EUR or any freely convertible currency in their commercial bank accounts and are permitted to net multi-currency treasury positions. The operative obligation is statistical and AML-reporting rather than pecuniary: under the EU Balance of Payments framework and Central Bank of Cyprus reporting instructions, commercial banks are bound to report cross-border goods, services and financial-account flows together with the applicable purpose code to the CBC, and the exporting company must preserve the complete audit trail. For an Amazon FBA seller the mandatory record set comprises Seller Central settlement and currency-conversion reports, the monthly payout summary, FBA logistics and fulfilment invoices, customs declarations, VAT OSS returns and shipping carrier bills of lading, all retained for no less than five years for CBC and AML/CFT file reviews.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), acting as the national prudential supervisor, financial stability authority and operator/paymaster of the euro TARGET2 payment infrastructure; monetary policy and supervision of the euro area reside with the European Central Bank (ECB), which also holds the definitive primary mandate over Eurosystem clearing and settlement sites.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

High, subject to a licensing qualifier. Airwallex and Stripe operate under EU-passported Electronic Money Institution or payment-institution licences (e.g., Irish, Maltese or Czech authorisation) and may lawfully open segregated e-money accounts for a Cyprus corporate beneficiary under the PSD2 passporting regime; they are fully viable for inbound Amazon marketplace settlements, gateway payout routing, multi-currency notional pooling and intercompany FX conversions. The binding legal limitation is that an EMI does not hold banking deposits and is not covered by the €20,000 Deposit Guarantee Scheme; funds held with an EMI are e-money claims, not fully ring-fenced deposits, and the EMI may not undertake credit or lending activities. The treasury blueprint therefore restricts EMI balances to intra-day collection, settlement float and FX conversion, with an automated end-of-day sweep into a tier-1 CBC-authorised credit institution account. Notification to the CBC under the Passporting Rules is the responsibility of the EMI itself; the Cyprus entity must nevertheless meet the EMI's own KYC/transaction-monitoring standards, which mirror the sixth AML Directive and the CBC Integration Guidelines.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing is executed over SWIFT MT103 from the remitting international bank to the Cyprus credit institution, quoting the Cyprus benefactor's IBAN and national clearing code, with the EBA/ISO 20022 payment purpose code clearly denoting an equity contribution (e.g., 'INVS' foreign direct investment or 'AUGM' capital increase, subject to the receiving bank's coding schedule). The receiving Cyprus bank concurrently compiles the CBC Balance of Payments statistical return and requires the company to deliver the full capital-pack: executed and notarised board minutes authorising share allotment, updated register of members, certificate of incorporation and Articles, a completed CBC client information sheet, and precured source-of-funds evidence from the remitting institution. For shareholder loans or intercompany funding, the inbound message must carry the loan purpose code and be accompanied by a notarised cross-border loan agreement, the intercompany loan accounting entries, and transfer-pricing documentation sufficient to resist recharacterisation by the Cyprus tax authorities. USD or GBP capital injections are accepted and may be converted to EUR at the bank's prevailing mid-market rate with transparent conversion fee disclosure; post-credit the amount must be booked on the corporate ledger as issued share capital or shareholder loan payable in accordance with Cyprus Companies Law Cap. 113 and the Tax Department's capital-duty/registration filing rules.The preferred outbound rail for Amazon FBA settlements within the EEA is SEPA Credit Transfer, which provides low-cost, T+1 value-dated euro clearing; for non-EEA counterparties and major currency payments the operative rail is SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfer routed through the Cyprus bank's correspondent network. Amazon operational drains — FBA fulfilment fees, selling-plan charges, advertising (PPC) spend, long-term storage fees and returns processing — are managed via Amazon marketplace collections and direct-debit mandates operated from the same EUR account. For high-value bulk inventory sourcing from Asia, a confirmed irrevocable documentary letter of credit under UCP600 is permissible and issued from the Cyprus account, but for cost-sensitive open-account flows the recommended structure is T/T payment through MT103 accompanied by the purchase order, supplier pro-forma invoice, transport documents and customs declarations. Every outbound MT103 must carry the correct purpose code, state the exact underlying invoice number and value, and list the counterparty's SWIFT BIC to pre-empt CBC transaction-monitoring freezes. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A mandatory Cyprus Local File is required when the annual aggregate value of related-party transactions exceeds EUR 750,000. Intercompany financing arrangements are separately reportable where the aggregate principal of loans with related parties exceeds EUR 250,000. A Master File and Country-by-Country Report are required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not operate a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime in the UAE/Bermuda sense. However, the Cyprus Tax Department applies the OECD Principal Purpose Test, the EU Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive general anti-abuse rule, and Cyprus Income Tax Law Article 33 to deny treaty benefits or statutory incentives where a company lacks real economic substance. A Cyprus Amazon FBA company should evidence local board meetings, business management decisions taken in Cyprus, permanent office or contractual premises, full accounting records, physically present staff, and locally held bank accounts. Shell arrangements using nominee directors and foreign decision-making will invalidate tax residency, treaty access, and the 12.5% headline rate benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Reassessment for the full omitted revenue, disallowance of all deductions, interest on underpaid tax, and a civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed. Criminal prosecution for fraudulent tax evasion exposes the company and signatories to fines and imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil where the Cyprus Ltd is a facade for the controlling non-resident owner. Directors may face civil restitution orders, asset freezing, travel bans, disqualification, and personal criminal liability for knowingly signing false returns.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Cyprus Tax Department may adjust taxable income to an arm's length level, disallow the excess deduction, levy the 12.5% corporate tax plus interest and penalties, and impose specific transfer pricing documentary penalties for non-production of the Local File within the statutory demand period.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally exposed where they orchestrate circular payments to tax-haven affiliates to strip profit from the Cyprus entity. The court may treat the diverted profit as a distribution to the shareholder and require directors to restore the funds, with attachment of personal assets and cross-border travel restrictions.
Shell Company Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: The Cyprus Tax Department may disregard the Cyprus tax-resident status, reassess the entity as managed and controlled outside Cyprus, deny treaty benefits, and impose the maximum penalty regime for incorrect returns. Registrar of Companies strike-off action under Companies Law Cap. 113 follows for failure to file statutory returns.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who lend their names to a shell without active oversight face civil disqualification, personal fines, and criminal liability for false declarations to the Registrar and tax authority. Controlling foreign owners may be pursued for unpaid corporate liabilities on an equitable basis where the company is treated as an alter ego.
Cross-Border VAT and Permanent Establishment Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Foreign VAT assessments, interest, penalties, denial of input VAT recovery, and customs obligations under the Union Customs Code. In some EU member states, Amazon may be jointly liable for unpaid VAT of a non-established FBA seller. Local tax authorities may also impose a corporate income tax assessment on the foreign PE and levy additional withholding tax on deemed profit remittances.

Director personal liability: Directors or statutory representatives of the Cyprus Ltd may be named personal respondents in foreign VAT collection proceedings, particularly where goods have been moved into an Amazon fulfilment centre without a valid EU VAT registration. Travel bans and seizure of assets in the destination countries are available enforcement mechanisms.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Cyprus AML Law (Law 188(I)/2007, as amended) provides for administrative penalties, fines, suspension of operations, and criminal prosecution for wilful non-disclosure. The Unit for Combating Money Laundering (MOKAS) may issue freezing orders over bank accounts and payment rails.

Director personal liability: Senior managers and compliance officers can be held criminally liable for AML failures with custodial sentences. Directors who facilitate the concealment of ultimate ownership face personal fines, director disqualification, civil restitution to creditors, and travel bans pending criminal or extradition proceedings.
