Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department (Τμήμα Φορολογίας / Department of Taxation), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; official e-portal: Tax For All / Tax Department Cyprus (https://taxdep.mof.gov.cy) with VAT Online e-filing, VIES EC Sales List interface, and integration with the Registrar of Companies.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the private limited liability company (Ltd) with the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver of Cyprus, obtaining Certificate of Incorporation (ELF code HE77). 2) The Tax Department automatically issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN / Tax File Number) upon incorporation for corporation tax purposes. 3) File VAT registration with the Cyprus Tax Department using Form T.D. 401 (Application for VAT Registration) through the VAT Online portal, attaching the certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office address, directors and ultimate beneficial owners details, expected turnover, and foreign VAT registration evidence if applicable. 4) Upon approval, the Tax Department issues a Cyprus VAT number in the format CY plus 8 or 9 digits, registered for VIES intra-Community reporting. 5) Register separately for Social Insurance and file employer obligations if staff are hired. 6) Appoint a statutory auditor and company secretary as required by the Companies Law Cap. 113; a foreign entity may obtain VAT registration directly through a VAT representative where no fixed establishment exists.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus is not a check-the-box jurisdiction; corporations are taxed at entity level under Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 at a uniform 12.5% rate. A company may elect Cyprus tax residence by establishing management and control in Cyprus (Art. 2), or rely on an applicable double tax treaty tie-breaker if effective management is elsewhere. Election-type reliefs include the Notional Interest Deduction on new equity (Art. 9B), the IP Box regime election (Art. 9D) with an effective 2.5% rate on qualifying intangible income, and participation exemption selection for dividends and capital gains subject to statutory conditions. VAT grouping elections (VAT Law 94(I)/2000, Art. 12A) permit related Cyprus entities to file as a single taxable person. Hybrid mismatch rules under ATAD 1 and 2 apply, requiring taxpayer self-assessment notices and disallowing double deduction benefits; no open pass-through election exists for a Cyprus limited company.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 taxable turnover from supplies of goods and services in any rolling 12-month period in Cyprus; EUR 7,800 annual intra-Community acquisitions of goods by a non-established taxable person; EUR 10,000 annual cross-border B2C distance sales/electronically supplied services threshold in other EU Member States, subject to One Stop Shop (OSS) election. Voluntary registration is permitted for businesses below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Section 58 of the Cyprus VAT Law 94(I)/2000, the supply of goods is zero-rated where the exporter proves that the goods were dispatched or transported outside the EU by the supplier or on its behalf, with physical export completed within 90 days of supply, evidenced by a Customs Export Declaration routed through the EU Electronic Export System (ECS) with Movement Reference Number. Section 59 zero-rates intra-Community dispatches of goods to a customer holding a valid VAT identification number in another EU Member State, provided the supplier issues a compliant invoice, retains transport evidence (CMR or equivalent), and reports the dispatch in the VIES EC Sales List. Offshore services, international freight and ancillary transport services directly connected to export goods, and B2B services supplied to non-EU customers whose place of supply is in the customer's jurisdiction are out of scope/zero-rated on production of customer business-status evidence. For pre-packaged spicy tallow hotpot soup base, zero-rating additionally requires compliance with EU food export health certification, destination-country import permits, and payment documentation evidencing genuine commercial export; excess input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports is repayable by the Tax Department quarterly upon substantiation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) with Movement Reference Number (MRN) and digital customs release confirmation from the EU Electronic Export System.
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list matching exactly the declared merchandise, weights, and consignee.
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note evidencing physical removal of goods from the EU customs territory.
  • Proof of exit from the EU (stamped or electronically recorded ECS exit confirmation showing date and exit customs office).
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 / Letter of Credit documentation evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds in the Cyprus exporter's bank account.
  • EU export health certificate or veterinary/sanitary attestation for pre-packaged food products and any destination-country import licence.

Cyprus operates mandatory real-time fiscal reporting through the Ariadne RTI (Reporting of Tax Information) platform administered by the Tax Department. Businesses supplying designated high-risk goods (cigarettes and processed tobacco, ethyl alcohol, liquid fuels, cement and construction materials, with phased expansion to food wholesalers and pre-packaged food distribution) must transmit invoice data in real time via certified ERP/accounting software or API before the invoice is considered legally issued; the system applies digital invoice numbering, cryptographic sequence stamps, and electronic clearance validation. All Cyprus VAT-registered persons must issue EN 16931-compliant electronic invoices in B2G transactions, retain SAF-T-like structured audit files, file periodic VAT returns (Form VAT 4) and VIES EC Sales Lists, and comply with Intrastat statistical declarations. No general digital stamping duty applies, but the Tax Department can require invoice pre-authorisation for export refund claims and cross-validates Ariadne data with Customs ECS/ICS movement records.

The Cyprus Tax Department and Customs have statutory power to reopen zero-rated transactions retroactively for up to the full limitation period, re-characterise unsubstantiated exports as domestic taxable supplies at 19% VAT, and assess the resulting tax with default interest and administrative penalties. Incomplete, contradictory, or late export documentation, circular payments, sham bills of lading, unregistered foreign counterparties, or goods not leaving Cyprus within 90 days trigger clawback of all deducted input VAT previously refunded and denial of future refund claims. Fraudulent export zero-rating (fictitious exports, missing-trader carousel chains, or invoice forgery) constitutes a criminal offence under VAT Law 94(I)/2000, arts. 62–65 and the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law, punishable by imprisonment of up to five years and/or fines, in addition to demands for the full VAT, interest, and surcharges. Cross-border audit cooperation through Eurofisc, OLAF, and the ECS customs movement database enables real-time traceability of goods and funds, and the Tax Department may require bank guarantees, asset freezes, or navigational controls before issuing export VAT refunds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus has no exchange-control law; full euro convertibility exists on both current and capital accounts under Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. Capital import and export, profit repatriation, dividend payments, and repayment of shareholder loans are unrestricted and require no prior Central Bank of Cyprus authorisation. Practical hurdles are limited to AML/KYC documentation, CBC balance-of-payments statistical returns, CRS/FATCA reporting, and, for China-resident shareholders, a potential dividend withholding tax: 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where applicable, otherwise 17% Cypriot WHT reduced to 5% (minimum 25% shareholding) or 10% under the China-Cyprus Double Taxation Treaty.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Zero mandatory surrender regime: there is no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate export receipts within a fixed number of days. Export proceeds from the distribution of pre-packaged hotpot soup base may be retained in foreign-currency accounts (USD, CNY/CNH, EUR) or converted freely without Central Bank of Cyprus approval. The only obligations are statistical and evidentiary: exporters must reflect export transactions in the quarterly CBC balance-of-payments/Intrastat returns, and the receiving bank may require commercial evidence (invoice, Bill of Lading, letter of credit) to record the foreign-exchange credit under its internal trade policy.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου); full name in English: Central Bank of Cyprus. It is the competent authority for banking supervision, foreign-exchange statistics, and AML/CFT supervision of Cyprus-licensed credit institutions, operating within the Eurosystem where the European Central Bank (ECB) holds monetary authority for the euro.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High for secondary FX collection and working-capital accounts, but Low as the sole or primary treasury rail. An EU-licensed electronic money institution (EMI) such as Wise Business, Revolut Business, or Airwallex may passport into Cyprus under the Electronic Money and Payment Services Law (Law 122(I)/2018) and EBA guidelines, permitting rapid multi-currency account opening and low-cost FX. However, EMIs cannot issue, advise, or negotiate documentary credits under UCP600, cannot serve as a CBC-licensed correspondent for TARGET2/MT700 clearing, and sustained flows above EUR 500,000 through an EMI create an imbalance typology risk for the primary relationship bank. The Cyprus Ltd must therefore maintain its principal treasury, LC, and settlement infrastructure with a CBC-licensed credit institution.

However, EMIs cannot issue, advise, or negotiate documentary credits under UCP600, cannot serve as a CBC-licensed correspondent for TARGET2/MT700 clearing, and sustained flows above EUR 500,000 through an EMI create an imbalance typology risk for the primary relationship bank. The Cyprus Ltd must therefore maintain its principal treasury, LC, and settlement infrastructure with a CBC-licensed credit institution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 2,000,000 aggregate annual value of controlled transactions triggers the Local File; EUR 5,000,000 group gross turnover triggers the Master File; Country-by-Country Report obligations apply at EUR 750,000,000 consolidated group revenue.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not maintain a separate ESR code equivalent to Cayman/UAE. The Cyprus tax residence test is applied through management and control (board meetings, decision-making and registered office in Cyprus). Treaty access and IP-box relief require substantive activities; a distribution company must retain employees, inventory management, invoicing, warehousing/logistics and full corporate records in Cyprus. Minimal substance or year-end restructuring will be attacked under the Cyprus GAAR and EU ATAD anti-abuse framework.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Financial penalty of up to 100% of the evaded tax; additional administrative fines of €100-€500; interest at 5% per annum on unpaid amounts; criminal prosecution may impose fines up to EUR 5,000 and imprisonment up to five years.

Director personal liability: Where the Cyprus Ltd is used as a façade to divert taxable profit, courts will pierce the corporate veil. Directors face civil restitution, personal assessment for unpaid corporate tax, travel bans and imprisonment.
Shell Company / No Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of Cyprus tax residence; refusal of TRCs and treaty benefits; recharacterisation of income to the shareholder; additional 12.5% corporate tax plus penalties and interest; potential administrative strike-off.

Director personal liability: A director signing false substance declarations faces personal indemnity for taxes, legal costs, travel bans and criminal prosecution for knowingly facilitating tax evasion.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Compensating adjustment to the arm's-length profit plus late-payment surcharge and statutory interest; penalties up to 5% of the adjusted transaction value; disqualification from TP simplification regimes; and reputational exposure in EU joint tax inspections.

Director personal liability: Directors responsible for false or missing TP documentation are jointly liable for the additional corporate tax and may be named in criminal tax proceedings; a travel ban may be imposed until full settlement.
Anti-Money Laundering / UBO Reporting Failures

Statutory sanction: Under Cyprus AML/CFT law, administrative fines up to EUR 1,000,000; imprisonment up to five years for false UBO declarations; suspension of bank accounts; potential cancellation of the company registration.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face disqualification orders, personal asset forfeiture, travel prohibitions and imprisonment if they consented to or wilfully ignored suspicious transactions.
