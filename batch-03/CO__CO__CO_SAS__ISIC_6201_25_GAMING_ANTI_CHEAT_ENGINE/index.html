Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio (Chamber of Commerce) of the registered domicile, operating through the Registro Único Empresarial (RUE) online portal; the incorporation certificate is issued as the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal.

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada regime), Articles 1, 5, 17 and 22; complemented by Código de Comercio (Decreto 410 de 1971) Articles 110 and 119, and Decreto Único Reglamentario 1074 de 2015.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private constitutional contract) with annexed bylaws (Estatutos), filed electronically through the RUE portal and authenticated by the registered legal representative; public deed is required only if real property is contributed.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 21 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under Article 5 of Ley 1258 de 2008, there is no statutory minimum subscribed or paid-in capital. The S.A.S. may be formed with zero capital or with capital payable in installments over two (2) years. Nevertheless, the Chamber of Commerce charges a registration tariff scaled to the subscribed capital, our Dossier recommends a nominal paid-in part (e.g., 10%) to support bank onboarding and the Beneficial Owner filing before DIAN.

Funding & unlock path: The registered office must obtain the Certificado de Bomberos (fire-safety certificate) confirming structural load, fire-exit and extinguisher compliance; additionally, under Resolución 2400 de 1979 occupational health clearance and ARL (Riesgos Laborales) enrollment are required before the first employee works on the premises.

Paid-in capital rule: Under Article 5 of Ley 1258 de 2008, there is no statutory minimum subscribed or paid-in capital. The S.A.S. may be formed with zero capital or with capital payable in installments over two (2) years. Nevertheless, the Chamber of Commerce charges a registration tariff scaled to the subscribed capital, our Dossier recommends a nominal paid-in part (e.g., 10%) to support bank onboarding and the Beneficial Owner filing before DIAN.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLUTION I – SHARE SUBSCRIPTION. The incorporation instrument is ratified and the subscribed capital is acknowledged, with ten percent paid in and the balance payable within the legal term. RESOLUTION II – REGISTERED OFFICE. The principal seat is established at the address stated in the constitutional contract in Colombia; the legal representative is authorized to execute lease, utility, and internet service agreements. RESOLUTION III – OFFICERS. The Legal Representative and Deputy Legal Representative are appointed with powers to open bank accounts (SWIFT and Stripe channels), sign contracts, and represent the Company before DIAN and the Chamber of Commerce. RESOLUTION IV – ADMINISTRATIVE. The Legal Representative is authorized to complete registration formalities, obtain NIT/RUT, and sign accessory documents.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial manufacturing license is required for ISIC 6201 software-only operations. The Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio (SIC) is the residual authority for commercial regulations, trademark registration, and personal-data registry oversight.

Category: Not applicable – no statutory operating permit is required for kernel-level anti-cheat middleware development. Voluntary trademark registration before SIC is recommended; if the company later self-hosts hardware servers, a municipal land-use permit and environmental compliance for electronic waste (RAEE – Resolución 1512 de 2010) will apply.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); official portal www.dian.gov.co; transactional fiscal registration and electronic invoice portal: MUISCA (muisca.dian.gov.co)

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Appoint a resident legal representative through a properly notarized and apostilled power of attorney. Step 2: File the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) electronically in MUISCA, indicating entity type S.A.S. and 'domicilio en el exterior' where applicable; DIAN assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria). Step 3: Activate the taxpayer attributes including 'Responsable de IVA' (Régimen Común) and, if applicable, withholding agent status. Step 4: Obtain electronic invoicing habilitación through a DIAN-authorized technology provider (Proveedor Tecnológico). Step 5: Register the bank account for export proceeds with a Colombian intermediary bank and open the foreign currency market (Mercado Cambiario) operational line.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the Colombian S.A.S. is a separate and autonomous taxpayer, subject to corporate income tax, VAT, and complementary taxes; no pass-through or fiscal transparency applies; Colombian source rules apply for outward services.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists. Mandatory entity-level taxation applies. Available statutory elections include: (i) Régimen Simple de Tributación (SIMPLE) for entities with prior-year gross revenues up to 100,000 UVT (approximately COP 4,980 million in 2025); (ii) election of monthly VAT refund cycles for goods exporters or quarterly/annual recovery cycles; (iii) application for 'exportador de servicios' certification to streamline refund processing; (iv) tax group consolidation for qualifying business groups subject to prior DIAN authorization; and (v) request for an advance pricing/characterization agreement with DIAN for cross-border service transaction treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic turnover exemption threshold for corporate taxpayers: an S.A.S. is automatically classified in the VAT common regime and must register in the RUT before the first taxable event. The simplified-regime threshold (gross income not exceeding 3,500 UVT, approximately COP 174.3 million in 2025) is limited to natural persons trading goods and does not extend to companies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated under Article 481 of the Estatuto Tributario. Services qualify as 'Servicios Exportados' at the 0% VAT rate upon cumulative compliance with: (i) the service is used exclusively abroad or in the production of goods/services abroad; (ii) the recipient is a non-resident and not domiciled in Colombia; (iii) the benefit is not exploited in Colombia; (iv) the consideration is paid in foreign currency and repatriated through the Colombian foreign exchange market; and (v) the service is not associated with goods physically located in Colombia. Kernel-level anti-cheat middleware and SaaS licenses supplied to foreign gaming operators generally satisfy these requirements; a documented ownership and revenue-mix analysis should be maintained to substantiate the export characterization.

  • RUT evidencing the 'exportador' attribute
  • Factura Electrónica de Exportación (export series FEV bearing the CUFE)
  • Export contract or service agreement with the foreign counterparty
  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX) and Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (required for physical media components)
  • Declaración de Cambio, Form 1, and Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas issued by the Colombian intermediary bank
  • Management letter and accounting support evidencing the VAT credit pool (IVA descontable) and refund application using Formulario 350

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta) with DIAN pre-validation for all responsible taxpayers, including exporters. Each invoice is transmitted as XML to the MUISCA web services through an authorized Proveedor Tecnológico; DIAN performs real-time validation of the numbering range, RUT attributes, and tax items; upon acceptance the taxpayer affixes the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) as the digital stamp and a QR code. Export transactions require a special export-series electronic invoice reconciled with customs documentation. DIAN event codes (CODI, COUS, COMA) must be tracked. Failure to issue compliant electronic invoices invalidates VAT credits and expense deductibility and triggers penalties under Articles 652 through 653 of the Estatuto Tributario.

DIAN may audit export zero-rating positions for up to five years and may retroactively disallow the 0% rate where substantive, documentary, or foreign-exchange repatriation conditions are unmet. Consequences include: collection of the 19% IVA plus monthly default interest; the inaccuracy penalty equal to 100% of the greater of the omitted VAT or the refund improperly obtained; revocation of the exporter status; disablement of the electronic invoicing system; and criminal prosecution under Articles 402 and 403 of the Código Penal for fraudulent tax refunds, punishable by 48 to 108 months of imprisonment and fines equal to the value obtained. Separate foreign-exchange infractions for non-repatriation are sanctioned by DIAN with fines up to 150% of the uncollected sum.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% withholding on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders when sourced from profits that have already borne CIT; distributions out of non-taxed/exempt profits are treated as ordinary Colombian-source income and taxed at 35% (Art. 245/246 E.T.).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Calendar fiscal year. Return due in August-September of the following year according to NIT ending digit on the annual DIAN tax calendar (e.g., FY2024 returns: 16 August through 11 September 2025).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and informative TP return (Form 120/220 equivalents subject to DIAN resolution) arise when gross assets or gross income in Colombia exceed approximately COP 4,706,500,000 (100,000 UVT) AND cross-border controlled transactions exceed approximately COP 47,065,000 (1,000 UVT per related party/per transaction). Lower thresholds or zero-threshold reporting apply for transactions with non-cooperative/low-tax jurisdictions and for taxpayers with net worth/income above the ordinary threshold.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not have a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime, but the DIAN aggressively applies substance-over-form, business-purpose, and beneficial-ownership doctrines under GAAR Article 869 E.T. and transfer-pricing Article 260-3 E.T. For the CO_SAS gaming anti-cheat entity, economic substance is demonstrated by payroll, actual executive/engineering decisions residing in Colombia, server/development infrastructure, and real commercial risk; lack of substance is recharacterized as a sham, causing disregarded transactions and treaty denial.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal sanction under Article 434A Criminal Code (Ley 1762/2015): imprisonment 48-108 months, a fine equal to 20%-50% of the evaded tax, and exclusion from state contracting; DIAN may nullify returns and impose 100%-200% penalty on omitted amounts plus daily interest.

Director personal liability: Directors/legal representatives who sign or order false returns are jointly and severally liable under Article 794 E.T. for the corporate tax debt; they may face criminal imprisonment, personal asset embargoes, a judicial exit/travel ban, and suspension of their commercial registration, without access to corporate veil protection.
Shell Company / Artificial Structure (Sham SAS)

Statutory sanction: DIAN disregards the legal personality under the veil-piercing doctrine (Law 1258/2008, Art. 42; Art. 869 E.T.), determines CIT at the real beneficiary level, and applies recharacterization penalties; the registry may cancel the mercantile registration and impose fines up to 100% of transactions.

Director personal liability: Shareholders and managers of the artificial SAS are held civilly and criminally liable for all unpaid corporate taxes; foreign directors risk personal seizure, a foreign-exchange and travel restriction order, and up to 20 years of imprisonment if the sham is used to launder proceeds through UIAF-reportable payment rails.
AML / Money Laundering / Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Criminal Code Art. 323 (financial assistance for money laundering): imprisonment 9-15 years (extended for aggravated laundering) plus a fine of 1,300 to 50,000 statutory minimum monthly wages; UIAF and Fiscalía can seize bank, Stripe, and SWIFT records; VIOLATION OF anti-terrorism finance laws carries additional 10-20 years.

Director personal liability: Directors or compliance officers who ignore red flags or permit the payments are treated as co-authors of laundering; personal and corporate assets are confiscated, directors face immediate travel bans, disqualification from office, and up to 25 years in prison under the aggravated regime, with the corporate veil being pierced to reach shareholders.
