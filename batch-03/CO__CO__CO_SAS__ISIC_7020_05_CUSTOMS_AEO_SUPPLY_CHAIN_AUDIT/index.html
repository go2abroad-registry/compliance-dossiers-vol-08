Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio del domicilio social operating the Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES), under oversight of the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio.

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada regime) arts. 4, 5, 21, 25 and 41; Código de Comercio arts. 110, 354 and 421; Decreto Único Reglamentario 1074 de 2015 (Title 4, Mercantile Registry).

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private constitutional document) accompanied by the Formulario Único de Registro Empresarial (RUES form); a public deed (Escritura Pública) is required only where real property is contributed to capital.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 30 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None. Ley 1258 de 2008 (art. 5) eliminates minimum capital for S.A.S. companies; subscribed capital must be paid in full within two years unless a shorter by-law term is stipulated; no escrow account or capital deposit is a precondition of incorporation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certificate issued by the Cuerpo de Bomberos verifying egress, extinguishers and signage; structural compliance with NSR-10 (Norma Sismo Resistente) certified by a licensed engineer for the office building; occupational clearance via worker registration with an ARL and implementation of the minimum SG-SST standards under Resolución 0312 de 2019.

Paid-in capital rule: None. Ley 1258 de 2008 (art. 5) eliminates minimum capital for S.A.S. companies; subscribed capital must be paid in full within two years unless a shorter by-law term is stipulated; no escrow account or capital deposit is a precondition of incorporation.

(4) Filing of the registration and tax applications.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable to ISIC 7020 consulting/audit services; physical-office permitting falls under Secretaría Distrital de Planeación (zoning), Cuerpo de Bomberos (fire safety), and ARL/Ministerio del Trabajo (occupational risk).

Category: No industrial operating permit required; operational clearances comprise the Matrícula Mercantil (already issued) and the Concepto de Uso del Suelo for the registered office.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) - Colombia's national tax and customs authority. Official portal: www.dian.gov.co; primary digital channels: MUISCA (tax ID, RUT, returns, payments), the Facturación Electrónica dashboard (electronic invoicing), and VUCE/SIGLO XXI (customs and foreign-trade clearance).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Execute incorporation documents of the S.A.S. and register them in the Cámara de Comercio of the head-office city to obtain the Certificado de Existencia y Representación Legal. Step 2: Enroll in DIAN's Registro Único Tributario (RUT) online through MUISCA, obtaining the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), selecting 'Responsable del IVA - Régimen Común' and the applicable customs status (exportador/importador/AEO) for the ISIC 7020 scope. Step 3: Obtain the digital certificate (firma electrónica) of the legal representative for electronic filing and e-invoicing. Step 4: Register foreign shareholders' identities and ultimate beneficial owners where the local AML framework so requires. A 100% non-resident owner may hold and govern the S.A.S., but the Colombian-resident S.A.S. itself is the taxpayer and must procure its own NIT/RUT before making any taxable supply in Colombia.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The S.A.S. is a distinct legal person and a Colombian tax resident, subject to entity-level corporate income tax at the general 35% rate (2025) and liable for IVA, ICA (municipal industry and commerce tax), and complementary levies; its shareholders are taxed only upon dividend distributions, with withholding at source for non-resident shareholders. No pass-through or fiscal-transparency election exists; the S.A.S. cannot flow its tax attributes through to foreign owners.

Available election forms

Colombia has no check-the-box regime; legal-form classification is imposed by law. Elective mechanisms relevant to an S.A.S.: (i) Régimen Simple de Tributación (RSRT/SIMPLE), a combined income and gross-receipts tax election for S.A.S. with prior-year gross income not exceeding 3,500 UVT (approx. COP 174.3 million in 2025, UVT = COP 49,799); (ii) a group may petition DIAN for autorización de consolidación fiscal (tax consolidation) of controlled subsidiaries; (iii) hybrid mismatch limitation rules (Law 2010 of 2019) restrict deductions or impose entity-classification recharacterization for certain hybrid instruments and entities without granting an elective opt-in; and (iv) non-resident owners may characterize the S.A.S. as a service provider or lessee for withholding purposes under DIAN guidance. AEO certification, being a customs compliance status, does not of itself alter any tax election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no de minimis turnover threshold for legal entities: any S.A.S. performing habitual taxable sales or services must register as an IVA-responsible common-regime taxpayer from its first taxable supply. Natural persons can use the simplified IVA regime only while prior-year gross income is at or below 3,500 UVT (2025: approx. COP 174.3 million). Export-oriented S.A.S. businesses must register even if all sales are zero-rated, and digital-service suppliers supplying Colombian B2B or B2C services must register under the same IVA rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Article 481 E.T., exports of tangible goods are zero-rated (0% IVA) when physically shipped under a valid Declaración de Exportación (DEX) and when the exporter collects the consideration in convertible currency through the Colombian foreign-exchange market or an authorized offset. Export-linked services (e.g., AEO and supply-chain security audits performed for non-resident clients), and offshore services in general, qualify as 'servicios exportados' at 0% when the beneficiary is a non-resident without Colombian domicile, the service is not used or exploited in Colombia, and the transaction is paid from abroad. The S.A.S. must evidence each service's cross-border destination and maintain a technical audit file (contract, scope report, counterparty identification) ready for DIAN inspection. Inward VAT on export-related inputs may be deducted and claimed as a refund (saldo a favor) through bimonthly IVA declarations.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación - DEX) submitted through the DIAN/VUCE channel with the official validation number
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (air) issued by the carrier, with data consistent with the DEX
  • Electronic export invoice (Factura Electrónica de Exportación) with CUNE and 0% IVA statement
  • Commercial invoice and packing list in the language of the contract
  • Certificate of origin or preferential-origin evidence when a tariff preference is claimed
  • Inward Remittance Certificate: the foreign-exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio por Exportaciones) or constancia de canalización certifying that the hard-currency proceeds were settled in Colombia

Since 2023-2024 (Resolución DIAN 000165 of 2023 and amendments), the FEV (Factura Electrónica de Venta) is mandatory for nearly all taxpayers. The invoice must be generated in XML/UBL 2.1 through DIAN-authorized software providers or DIAN's free web platform, transmitted to DIAN in near-real time, and validated to obtain a CUNE (Consecutivo Único de Numeración Electrónica), the fiscal digital stamp, before it may be delivered to the buyer. Buyers must emit the 'Recibo de Bien o Servicio' acceptance event. Export invoices use the dedicated Factura Electrónica de Exportación module and must be reconciled with the customs export data so that the 0% IVA regime is accepted; any mismatch triggers customs block and denial of VAT refunds.

DIAN may retroactively revoke the 0% rating where the export document bundle is incomplete or contradictory, the DEX has been cancelled, the buyer and seller are related without a transfer-pricing analysis, or repatriation is late. Consequences include: (i) reclassification of the supply as taxed at 19%, with assessment of the VAT and moratory interest; (ii) the 'sanción por inexactitud' of 200% of the additional assessment (or of the overclaimed balance) under Article 647 E.T., aggravated by up to 300% in serious fraud cases; (iii) suspension or loss of AEO and exporter status; (iv) criminal prosecution for customs fraud under Law 1762 of 2015 with imprisonment of up to 8 years; and (v) extension of DIAN's statutory assessment window from 5 to 10 years for unreported or fraudulent claims, enabling full retrospective clawback of refunded balances.
