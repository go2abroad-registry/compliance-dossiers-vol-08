Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cap. 113. A Cyprus Ltd may be incorporated with a single EUR 1.00 share; standard practice is EUR 1,000–2,000 issued capital. No escrow or capital-deposit verification is required at registration; the paid-up amount must be received by the company and entered in the register of members, and is subject to 0.6% capital stamp duty (capped).

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; official portal: https://tax.mof.gov.cy

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor establishing a Cyprus private limited company must first obtain a company incorporation certificate and an HE identification number from the Registrar of Companies. The company must then register for tax and VAT with the Tax Department within the statutory post-incorporation period, submitting the completed registration forms, certified constitutional documents, registered office address proof, and identity documentation of directors and ultimate beneficial owners. A Tax Identification Code is automatically linked to the HE company number; non-resident entities with a Cyprus VAT liability may be required to appoint a tax representative if they have no fixed establishment.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign investor establishing a Cyprus private limited company must first obtain a company incorporation certificate and an HE identification number from the Registrar of Companies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not maintain a check-the-box regime; a Cyprus private limited company is mandatorily classified as a separate taxable entity under the Income Tax Law and is not eligible for pass-through treatment. Available elections relevant to commodity trading include group loss relief elections, foreign tax credit elections, notional interest deduction elections on new equity, and the intellectual property box election where applicable. Hybrid mismatch adjustments under EU ATAD apply, but there is no free-standing entity election for tax transparency.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for goods and EUR 7,800 for services; a non-established supplier making any taxable supply in Cyprus is registrable regardless of turnover, and EU distance-selling rules also apply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically transported or dispatched outside the European Union by or on behalf of the seller, including bulk raw cane sugar sold on FOB/CIF terms supplied from Cyprus or imported and re-exported under customs supervision. The supplier must possess and preserve contemporaneous evidence of removal from EU territory within the statutory timeframe. Offshore B2B services supplied to a recipient established outside the EU may also be zero-rated where the place of supply is outside Cyprus.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document (SAD)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent international transport documentation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/LC settlement evidence demonstrating foreign-currency receipt
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and certificate of origin
  • Proof of physical export from Cyprus or another EU customs office

Cyprus does not currently operate a real-time fiscal clearance mandate, digital stamping, or mandatory API-based e-invoicing clearance for B2B commercial transactions. Tax invoices must conform to EU VAT Article 226 invoicing requirements and be retained electronically or in paper form for inspection; the Tax Department accepts electronic invoices without a pre-clearance step.

The Tax Department may deny zero-rating where the export evidence bundle is incomplete, unsupported, or contains documentary irregularities, thereby raising an additional VAT assessment at the standard Cyprus rate of 19% on the full export value. Late VAT and interest penalties accrue, and fraudulent or reckless zero-rating claims expose the taxpayer to statutory fines and criminal prosecution; records and customs documentation must be retained for at least six years to withstand retrospective clawback and audit adjustment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a full member of the Eurozone, and the euro is freely convertible. Under the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union, restrictions on the movement of capital between Cyprus and third countries are prohibited, subject to standard EU safeguards; consequently, there is no distinction between current-account and capital-account convertibility for lawful commercial transactions. Profit remittance and capital repatriation are not subject to any exchange-control approval or currency-conversion tax, but cross-border payments must comply with EU Anti-Money-Laundering Regulation, sanctions screening, CRS/FATCA reporting, and standard bank documentation; dividend remittance may be subject to 0% withholding tax when the participation exemption conditions are satisfied and the recipient holds sufficient substance in Cyprus.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement to surrender, convert, or repatriate export proceeds within a specified timeline in Cyprus, and no mandatory conversion ratio applies to the euro. Exporters may hold foreign-currency proceeds in domestic or foreign bank accounts. Tax and AML compliance require that export proceeds be received through regulated credit institutions within commercially reasonable terms, accurately recorded in the audited financial statements, supported by underlying invoices, bills of lading, and customs declarations, and reported in the annual VAT and corporate income tax filings as applicable.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), being the national monetary authority and the competent prudential supervisor for all credit institutions, payment institutions, and electronic money institutions operating in the Republic of Cyprus, acting in coordination with the European Central Bank and the national Ministry of Finance for exchange-control, sanctions, and balance-of-payments reporting matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for EU-authorized electronic money institutions used as ancillary payment/fx rails; Low for non-EU offshore digital EMIs. A Cyprus-incorporated commodity trader may use a fully licensed EU EMI for low-value operational wires and fx conversion, but the primary treasury structure for physical vessel shipments of raw cane sugar must be anchored in a full commercial bank because maritime trade settlements, documentary credit issuance, and banker's acceptances require a banking counterparty with SWIFT correspondent relationships and trade finance capacity. Non-EU EMIs holding Cyprus accounts are generally not viable due to EEA passporting termination, sanctions-risk restrictions, and limited access to MT103 documentary trade settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan funding is routed via SWIFT MT103/MT202 from the parent or lender bank in EUR or USD; the Cyprus beneficiary bank requires the transactor to quote the EU Balance-of-Payments purpose code corresponding to Direct Investment – Equity Capital or Intercompany Lending, accompanied by the board resolution, updated share register, capital contribution declaration, financing agreement, and confirmation of the Cyprus entity's bank account. Because Cyprus maintains no exchange-control approval regime, the principal compliance gate is the banks' internal AML/KYC review, sanctions screening, and the substantiation of the commercial or corporate purpose of the inbound wire. Funds designated as share capital are credited to a Capital Escrow Account or a dedicated operational account and recorded in the registered office books; a final reclaim or dissolution distribution follows the Companies Law procedure.Settlement of physical VHP raw cane sugar import purchases and export receivables is cleared through SWIFT MT103 cash telegraphic transfers for direct open-account invoices or through irrevocable documentary letters of credit issued on SWIFT MT700/MT707/MT798 under UCP600 rules for vessel cargo transactions. The outbound payment instruction must cite the underlying invoice and LC reference, use approved counterparty bank accounts only, include a valid EU BoP purpose code for merchandise trade, and be supported by the negotiation set comprising commercial invoice, bill of lading, inspection certificate, cargo-pol/value certificate, and insurance documents.
