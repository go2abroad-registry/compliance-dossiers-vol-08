Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Cyprus Tax Department (Tmima Forologias), Ministry of Finance, Nicosia, operating the ARIADNI electronic Tax Portal (taxportal.mof.gov.cy); indirect taxation is administered by the VAT Service within the Tax Department; export customs declarations are processed by the Cyprus Customs and Excise Department (Teloneio) through the THESEAS national export system.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident company without a Cyprus establishment obtains a Cyprus VAT number by submitting Form VAT 101 to the VAT Service through the ARIADNI portal, accompanied by certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, articles of incorporation, directors' passports, proof of the intended economic activity and, where applicable, the appointment of a tax representative; for corporate income tax purposes, a non-Cypriot company that creates a permanent establishment in Cyprus (including via a place of management, branch, agent with authority, warehouse or installation site) must register with the Corporate Income Tax section of the Tax Department within 60 days of commencing business, obtaining the statutory Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) in the format of 8 digits plus a trailing alphabetical character; an EU or third-country supplier that makes distance sales to Cyprus consumers, holds Cypriot immovable property, or performs taxable supplies in Cyprus is registrable for VAT from the first euro of turnover without the domestic threshold; the Cypriot VAT number is expressed as CY + 8 digits + one letter, and foreign entities are enrolled simultaneously in the VIES system for intra-Community transactions.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the CY_LTD (private limited company incorporated under Cyprus Companies Law Cap. 113, with registered office and place of effective management in Cyprus) is a non-transparent, corporation-level taxpayer subject to corporate income tax at the flat rate of 12.5% on worldwide income, as well as VAT as a taxable person; no pass-through treatment is available under the unitary Cypriot system; territorial reliefs applicable to the CY_LTD include the foreign permanent establishment dividend exemption, disposal-of-share gains exemption, and tax-free restructuring relief. — A foreign non-resident company without a Cyprus establishment obtains a Cyprus VAT number by submitting Form VAT 101 to the VAT Service through the ARIADNI portal, accompanied by certified copies of the certificate of incorporation, articles of incorporation, directors' passports, proof of the intended economic activity and, where applicable, the appointment of a tax representative; for corporate income tax purposes, a non-Cypriot company that creates a permanent establishment in Cyprus (including via a place of management, branch, agent with authority, warehouse or installation site) must register with the Corporate Income Tax section of the Tax Department within 60 days of commencing business, obtaining the statutory Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) in the format of 8 digits plus a trailing alphabetical character; an EU or third-country supplier that makes distance sales to Cyprus consumers, holds Cypriot immovable property, or performs taxable supplies in Cyprus is registrable for VAT from the first euro of turnover without the domestic threshold; the Cypriot VAT number is expressed as CY + 8 digits + one letter, and foreign entities are enrolled simultaneously in the VIES system for intra-Community transactions.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the CY_LTD (private limited company incorporated under Cyprus Companies Law Cap. 113, with registered office and place of effective management in Cyprus) is a non-transparent, corporation-level taxpayer subject to corporate income tax at the flat rate of 12.5% on worldwide income, as well as VAT as a taxable person; no pass-through treatment is available under the unitary Cypriot system; territorial reliefs applicable to the CY_LTD include the foreign permanent establishment dividend exemption, disposal-of-share gains exemption, and tax-free restructuring relief.

Available election forms

Cyprus does not operate US-style check-the-box or general hybrid-transparency elections; the statutory elections available to a Cyprus tax resident company include (i) group relief election for the surrender and offset of tax losses within a 75% group, (ii) annual election of the Notional Interest Deduction (NID) on new qualifying equity as a capital allowance, (iii) election of the IP Box regime, granting an 80% deduction on qualifying intellectual property profits on an arm's length basis, (iv) option to tax (waive the exemption on) the rental of immovable property under the First Schedule, Part II of the VAT Law, (v) elections addressing hybrid mismatch outcomes under the ATAD II implementation, including the offsetting election and the dual-inclusion income election, and (vi) deemed dividend mitigation elections documented with the Tax Department, using the deemed distribution provisions of the Special Defence Contribution Law 117(I)/2002 to align actual distributions with the required 70% post-tax profit distribution rule.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 per annum for taxable turnover in goods and EUR 7,800 per annum for taxable turnover in services for Cyprus-established businesses; non-established foreign suppliers are registrable from the first euro of Cyprus taxable supplies, while intra-Community distance sales of goods to Cyprus consumers are covered by the EUR 10,000 EU-wide cross-border threshold applicable since 1 July 2021 under the VAT e-commerce package (Cyprus previously maintained a national distance selling threshold of EUR 35,000).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Manufactured exothermic reaction self-heating meals physically dispatched from Cyprus to a destination outside the EU are eligible for the 0% export VAT rate under Section 30 of the Cyprus VAT Act; supplies to a taxable person established in another EU Member State qualify for zero rating as intra-Community supplies only where the recipient holds a valid EU VAT identification number, the goods leave Cyprus within 90 days of the time of supply, and the VIES record supports the transaction; the self-heating heating pack integrated into the final food kit is classified as part of the complete manufactured article and is exported at 0% without separate VAT implication, and related offshore B2B services (e.g., freight, inspection and warehousing organised for the export supply) follow the general place-of-supply rules, being zero-rated or outside the scope where the recipient is established outside the EU.

Cyprus does not currently impose a general real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control, or mandatory digital stamping regime; the immediate digital compliance obligations are the electronic filing of VAT returns, VIES recapitulative statements, Intrastat declarations and administrative data through the ARIADNI Tax Portal using web services, batch XML and the national digital certificate; for business-to-government transactions, Cyprus has implemented mandatory e-invoicing under EU Directive 2014/55/EU through the eProcurement gateway; the future mandatory B2B e-invoicing framework is expected to follow the EU 'VAT in the Digital Age' legislative package, requiring structured e-invoicing and transaction-level reporting, but is not yet in force, so taxpayers may voluntarily use European Standard EN 16931 (UPB) formats.

The Cyprus Tax Department applies audit scrutiny to export zero-rating claims through the THESEAS customs clearance trail, bank inward remittance tickets and VIES records; where the zero rate is claimed without genuinue export, the Assessment and Penalty provisions of the VAT Act (Law 94(I)/2000), in conjunction with the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law 4/1978, allow the tax commissioner to claw back the VAT previously relieved, plus interest at 9% per annum, to impose civil penalties of up to 100% of the tax involved, and to extend the limitation period from 4 years to 6 years in cases involving fraudulent conduct; deliberate document falsification or participation in carousel-style export fraud may be prosecuted criminally, with fines of up to EUR 10,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 3 years for each offence, in addition to customs penalties for false export declarations under the Customs Code and the freezing or confiscation of the export refund and connected assets.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a full member of the European Union and the Eurozone; the euro is fully convertible for both capital and current account transactions. There are no general exchange control restrictions on capital inflows or outflows for resident or non-resident investors, provided that transactions comply with EU anti-money laundering, counter-terrorist financing, sanctions, and statistical reporting obligations. Profit remittances, dividends, and liquidation proceeds may be freely transferred abroad subject to standard bank due diligence, tax clearance certificates where applicable, and the absence of any EU or Council of Ministers restrictive measures. The practical hurdles are documentary and compliance-driven, not regulatory; banks may apply internal enhanced scrutiny to intra-group loans, high-risk jurisdictions, and large one-off payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory obligation requiring the surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds into euros for Cypriot exporting entities, and no prescribed timeline for repatriation of export receipts. Exporters must, however, lodge the export declaration with the Cyprus Customs, retain evidence of the underlying commercial transaction, and ensure that all euro or foreign currency receipts are credited to a licensed Cyprus bank account. The Central Bank of Cyprus applies statistical monitoring under Regulation (EC) No 1849/2006 and the EU Balance of Payments compilation framework, but does not impose any conversion ratio or time constraint. The company should adopt a 30-day internal treasury policy to convert or hold foreign currency receipts according to its cash flow and hedging plan.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα Κύπρου) in coordination with the Cyprus Ministry of Finance and the Central Agency for the Prevention of Money Laundering (MOKAS)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low to Medium with strict limitations. Cyprus does not authorize pure offshore digital EMI passports unless they hold an EU Electronic Money Institution license and are either passported into Cyprus under PSD2 or are registered with the Central Bank of Cyprus as authorized payment institutions. Non-EU EMIs (e.g., UK-based, US-based) generally cannot onboard Cypriot companies for ongoing corporate treasury services unless they obtain a Cypriot branch or agent license. The primary constraint is the inability to maintain a Cypriot IBAN for clearing euro payments and the heightened AML/KYC burden on digital cross-border flows. EMIs can serve as complementary collection gateways, but the operational account for tax, trade, and compliance should reside with a Tier-1 Cypriot bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant protocol begins with a board resolution authorizing the issuance of shares, followed by the transfer of funds from the non-resident shareholder's bank account to the entity's Capital Escrow Account held at Bank of Cyprus or Hellenic Bank. The wire must include a precise payment purpose code in SWIFT field 72/77B; for share capital subscription use 'G3DR' (Purchase of equity) or 'G3DS' (Issuance of equity) as referenced in the ECB Balance of Payments manual and the Cyprus Statistical Collection Framework. The sending bank must carry the complete UBO name and tax identification number in the remitter field. The receiving bank will apply verification against the most recent CRS and AML records; a share allotment document and a notarized certificate of acceptance of capital contribution must be lodged with the bank within five business days. The company should also file with the Cyprus Registrar of Companies the capital increase form HE24 and, if applicable, the non-resident ownership declaration with the Central Bank of Cyprus under the consolidated prudential statistics.For trade settlements, the entity will primarily use SWIFT MT103 for direct bank-to-bank euro or foreign currency wires, with a mandatory 'BEN' or 'SHA' charge configuration as agreed with the commercial counterparty. For large raw-material purchases (e.g., chemicals, food-grade aluminum containers, non-woven exothermic pads), the company may adopt a Documentary Letter of Credit (Commercial LC) under UCP600, opened through a Tier-1 Cyprus client bank with an advising bank in the seller's country, incorporating a 90-day usance clause where feasible. The disbursement package must include the invoice, proof of shipment (bill of lading or air waybill), inspection certificate, insurance certificate, and any required import permit for food-contact materials. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when related-party transactions exceed: EUR 1,000,000 for goods; EUR 250,000 for services; or EUR 100,000 for financial transactions. A Master File is required where the group achieves EUR 750 million consolidated revenue.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not operate a dedicated Economic Substance Regulations code equivalent to UAE or BVI; however, the doctrine is embedded in the Income Tax Law's central management and control test, OECD BEPS standards, and the Tax Department's TP guidelines. A company claiming Cyprus tax residence must have a registered office and core decision-making, board meetings, and commercial substance such as personnel, premises, and records maintained in Cyprus.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties of up to EUR 100,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 5 years. Civil assessment of underpaid tax, interest at 10.75% p.a., and administrative fines up to 100% of the tax evaded are imposed.

Director personal liability: Individuals directing the evasion are criminally prosecuted, may be banned from corporate directorship, and may face travel bans. Directors may be jointly and severally liable for the company's tax debts if assets were removed or the company was left insolvent.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Tax Department may reclassify the company as non-resident and disregard the Cyprus entity for tax purposes. Treaty benefits are denied, and the full foreign source income is reassessed with interest and penalties. Administrative fines up to EUR 50,000 apply under anti-abuse rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who participate in the sham are personally assessed for taxes and penalties from date of commencement of the arrangement; director disqualification orders for a period up to 12 years may be issued by the Cyprus courts.
AML / KYC Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law, fines of up to EUR 250,000 and/or imprisonment up to 14 years are prescribed. The company's assets may be forfeited and the business license suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors and the appointed compliance officer bear criminal liability if KYC/AML obligations were not implemented. Courts may impose travel bans, freeze personal bank accounts, and order restitution of the laundered amounts.
