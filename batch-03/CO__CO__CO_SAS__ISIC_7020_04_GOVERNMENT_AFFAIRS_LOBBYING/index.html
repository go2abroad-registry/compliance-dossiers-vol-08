Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-in capital: an S.A.S. may be formed with zero or nominal capital (e.g., COP 1,000 ≈ USD 0.24). Under Ley 1258/2008, art. 3, the subscribed capital must be fully paid within two (2) years from incorporation; no escrow or blocked-account requirement applies. Inbound contributions from foreign shareholders must additionally be reported to Banco de la República via the summary foreign-exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio, Form No. 3).

Funding & unlock path: Municipal Fire Safety Certificate (Certificado de Bomberos) verifying evacuation capacity, extinguisher placement, and electrical load; occupancy and structural-safety endorsement from the building manager or Curaduría Urbana (certificado de seguridad estructural) for the leased offices; mandatory workplace-conditions compliance under the S.G.S.S.T. regime — ARL risk classification (clases I-II), first-aid equipment, and accident-reporting procedures; optional exterior-signage permit if the office displays public-facing corporate identification (publicidad exterior visual, Ley 140 de 1994).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – central tax and customs administration; electronic portal DIAN MUISCA and Cámara de Comercio registration integration.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned S.A.S. must be incorporated before a notary and registered with the competent Cámara de Comercio; simultaneously request NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) from DIAN through MUISCA using the corporate registration certificate, valid bylaws, proof of address, appointment of legal representative with standing power, and foreign shareholder identification. Foreign investors need an appointed agent in Colombia and must obtain RUT (Registro Único Tributario) plus NIT before commencing taxable operations or hiring locally.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The S.A.S. is a separate juridical and tax-paying person subject to Colombian corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) and complementary taxes on worldwide income for tax resident entities; non-resident shareholders are taxed only on Colombian-source income and dividends at source, with no check-the-box pass-through available.

Available election forms

Colombia has no US-style check-the-box election. Statutory elections affecting entity tax classification include: (i) no automatic conversion absent reorganization; (ii) optional simplified taxation regime (Régimen Simple de Tributación) for eligible corporations with qualifying gross income, replacing income, VAT and municipal industry-commerce taxes in some respects, subject to size thresholds and shareholder composition; and (iii) conventional anti-hybrid and CFC rules under which Colombian law disregards hybrid instruments without elective conformity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold for VAT-responsible status: any person performing taxable sales, rendering taxable services within Colombia, importing taxable goods, or agreeing to supply taxable services from abroad is required to register as a VAT responsible party from its first taxable event. Simplified business regime thresholds (e.g., up to 3,500 UVT) are only for natural persons or certain simple taxpayers and do not apply to the S.A.S. corporate entity.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT/IVA rate applies to export of goods (sale and physical removal from Colombian customs territory) and export of services (offshore services rendered to foreign residents that are used exclusively abroad and paid by non-residents). The responsible exporter may take Credits for underlying input VAT and request refund (devolución), subject to actual repatriation of foreign currency and DIAN validation of the service's extraterritorial use. Lobbying and government-affairs related services to foreign clients may qualify only if the benefit is localized abroad and the client is a foreign entity.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) registered in the VUCE/SIGLO system and accepted by DIAN customs control
  • Bill of Lading (BL) or Air Waybill evidencing physical exit of goods from Colombia
  • Commercial Invoice for Export aligned with e-invoicing requirements
  • Certificate of Inspection/Packing List where applicable
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Reintegro Económico) from a Colombian authorized FX intermediary evidencing collection within the mandated deadline

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta) for all VAT-responsible taxpayers, including the S.A.S.; invoices must be generated and transmitted in real time to authorized DIAN clearing technology operators through the DIAN MUISCA web-service APIs, must contain UUID plus DIAN validation code and QR Code (Código Único de Facturación), and must be accepted/rejected by the recipient before issuance is complete; non-compliant invoices invalidate input VAT credits.

DIAN may audit documentary and economic substance, reject refund, and retroactively claw back VAT credits with daily interest (sanción por inexactitud up to 200% of the omitted or improperly determined amount under Articles 648 and 671 of the Tax Statute), impose a fine of up to 100% of the improperly recovered VAT in cases of falsified customs documents, and criminally prosecute invoice and customs fraud, plus denial of subsequent export refunds for up to 2 years following unsuccessful fraudulent claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a managed-floating exchange-rate system with a market-clearing COP/USD rate and full convertibility for current-account transactions, including service exports and outward profit remittances that are channeled through authorized foreign-exchange intermediaries. Capital-account transactions are liberalized but subject to mandatory declaration, channeling and registration with Banco de la República's International Investment Registry: foreign direct investment (FDI) into a Colombian S.A.S. must be registered to create the legal entitlement to future dividend and capital repatriation. Profit remittance is not quantitatively limited or sequenced, yet the effective tax claw-back consists of a 10% dividend withholding tax for non-resident shareholders and a 7.5% remittance tax for other payments to foreign beneficiaries; the principal practical hurdles are the need to present the FDI registration certificate at the time of each remittance and to file and retain the corresponding exchange declaration (Declaración de Cambio / Form DC-1) for every operation above the regulatory threshold. Convertibility obstacles arise mainly from delays in obtaining the investment-registration certificate, mismatched corporate-purpose codes in wiring instructions, and documentary gaps in proving the underlying commercial reality of lobbying services.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters of services (including ISIC 7020 government-affairs advisory and cross-border lobbying consulting) are subject to mandatory canalización: 100% of foreign-currency proceeds must be channeled through the Colombian foreign-exchange market, either by crediting a domestic foreign-currency account at an authorized intermediary or by selling the currency to the intermediary, within 6 months from the date services are completed or the receivable falls due, as established by Banco de la República External Communications. Colombia does not impose statutory conversion into COP; proceeds may be retained in USD domestically or in a registered foreign compensation account (cuenta de compensación). Each collection must be evidenced by a certificate of channeling (comprobante de canalización) and the associated Form DC-1, and the entire compliance file (export document, invoice, SWIFT credit advice, channeling certificate) must be retained for at least five years.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (the Central Bank), jointly with the Financial Superintendence of Colombia (Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia) and the National Directorate of Taxes and Customs (DIAN), under the foreign-exchange regime (Régimen Cambiario) established by Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1068 of 2015 and the External Communications of the Central Bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: LOW as a primary treasury rail. Colombian law reserves deposit-taking and current-account banking activity to institutions licensed by the Financial Superintendence of Colombia; an offshore fintech EMI cannot legally provide a COP-denominated operating account to the S.A.S. resident entity, cannot issue compliant comprobantes de canalización, and cannot support the FDI registration obligation. An offshore digital bank may be used solely as an auxiliary multi-currency account for disbursements to foreign subcontractors outside the Colombian system, provided no Colombian export-service collection is received there and no payment is made from it for local costs. Any attempt to collect Colombian exports through an unregistered foreign account without a compensation-account authorization breaches the foreign-exchange regime and is not acceptable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

External equity contributions into the S.A.S. must be received via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own bank account into the Colombian intermediary's nostro or the recipient's foreign-currency account, with the sender identified as the registered investor and field 70 referencing the purpose 'Inversión directa de capital - aporte al capital social' and, where practical, the investor's tax-identification number. On credit, the local bank performs the channeling, issues the comprobante de canalización, and files the declaración de cambio with the classification code for direct-investment equity contributions (e.g., code 2110); the company must then obtain the Registro de Inversiones Internacionales certificate from Banco de la República within the applicable statutory window, best practice being five business days. Maximum integrity is achieved by wiring exclusively from the UBO's own account, avoiding third-party interposed funding, and formalizing the shareholder-resolution and share-issuance documents in Colombia before the transfer is cleared. Any inbound receipt not matched to the filing of exchange forms or the FDI registration creates a risk of nullity, fines, and the loss of future remittance rights.For disbursements to foreign sub-consultants, suppliers and advisors, the treasury must use SWIFT MT103 messages with a clear purpose description and invoice reference, executed by the local bank after a parallel purchase of US dollars or euros through the foreign-exchange channel; the corresponding Form DC-1 declaring the service payment must be filed and retained. For substantial procurement, commercial letters of credit (advised or confirmed via SWIFT MT700) and standby guarantees provide the appropriate instrument. Domestic creditors entering the Colombian system are paid through ACH Colombia or the bank's online platform in COP. All foreign-currency payments must be supported by an economic-substantiation document (contract, invoice, delivery note or acceptance record) to sustain both FX-compliance and tax-deductibility in a DIAN audit. Aprobación y depósito de los Estados Financieros de Propósito General (approval and public registry deposit of annual financial statements) Shareholders' meeting to approve year-end financial statements no later than 31 March of the following year; deposit with the Chamber of Commerce concurrently with the annual commercial renewal $0 USD Non-deposit may block the annual renewal, trigger Chamber-imposed fines, expose directors and officers to civil and statutory liability, and after repeated non-compliance contribute to cancellation of the registration and strike-off.
Información Exógena y Anexo de Precios de Transferencia (annual exogenous information return and transfer pricing annex, if thresholds are met) Due per DIAN annual resolution, generally during the second quarter of the year following the reporting tax year, based on the taxpayer's NIT digit schedule $0 USD Fines under Article 651 of the Colombian Tax Code for late, inaccurate, or omitted information (up to UVT-denominated caps per data item), plus default interest and risk of forced tax assessment; does not by itself trigger strike-off but may impair corporate standing.
