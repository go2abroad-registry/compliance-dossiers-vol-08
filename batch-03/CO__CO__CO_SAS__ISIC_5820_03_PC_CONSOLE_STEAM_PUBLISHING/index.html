Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is legally required for a Colombian S.A.S. Share capital may be subscribed and paid in installments as provided in the bylaws, and no escrow account is required; in-kind contributions must be paid immediately upon incorporation and be appraised by appropriate valuation.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN), Colombia — official portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional platform: MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicios y Trámites Automatizados); electronic-invoicing clearance via DIAN web services/API.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the S.A.S. in the Chamber of Commerce and obtain the certificate of existence and legal representation. Step 2: The legal representative registers the company in the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) through MUISCA to obtain the NIT, electing the common VAT regime, corporate income tax and withholding obligations. Step 3: Acquire a DIAN-accepted digital signature certificate and activate the electronic-invoicing web service. Step 4: Receive the definitive NIT/RUT. Non-resident shareholders require no NIT for the S.A.S.; a non-resident foreign digital-service seller without a Colombian PE may use the simplified DIAN registration for foreign digital services and appoint a tax representative in Colombia.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box regime exists. Available elections/regimes: (i) consolidated taxation for 100%-held corporate groups authorized by DIAN (Art. 886 ET); (ii) Colombian Holding Company (CHC) regime for qualified foreign dividends and PE profits (Art. 895 ET); (iii) application for Free Trade Zone (Zona Franca) status with a reduced 25% income tax rate for exported services; (iv) anti-hybrid rules (Arts. 851-2 and 852-1 ET) denying deductions for hybrid arrangements with no elective override.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No threshold for legal entities: VAT registration is mandatory from the first taxable transaction. Simplified (Régimen Simplificado) status is limited to natural persons with annual gross income below 3,500 UVT and total assets below 1,600 UVT; 2025 UVT = COP 49,799. Foreign digital-service suppliers must register from the first sale regardless of value.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT is available under Art. 481 ET and Decree 2247/2014 where: (a) the contracting party and beneficiary of the digital publishing/licensing/distribution services (Steam/PlayStation Store) is a non-resident entity; (b) the digital game or licence is consumed exclusively outside Colombia; (c) the export service is evidenced by a DEX or a DIAN-validated CUFE electronic export invoice; and (d) the consideration is received and repatriated through Colombia's foreign-exchange market. Input VAT on development, marketing and commission costs remains creditable.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación — DEX) for physical media; for pure digital deliveries the DIAN-validated electronic export-service invoice with CUFE replaces the DEX
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill — required only when software is shipped physically (discs/cartridges); not applicable to digital Steam/PlayStation distribution
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas with Declaración de Cambio modality 10 issued by the commercial bank, evidencing collection from the foreign store operator

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta — FEV). Each invoice is generated in XML and transmitted to DIAN's validation web service through an authorized system (DIAN free software, proprietary software, or certified technology provider); DIAN immediately validates and applies the digital stamp (CUFE, Código Único de Facturación Electrónica), after which the seller delivers the XML to the buyer. Credit notes, debit notes, export invoices and electronic equivalent documents are submitted through the same API endpoints; no paper or non-cleared invoice is fiscally valid.

DIAN cross-checks every zero-rated export claim against Banco de la República foreign-exchange records and the bank's Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas. Non-repatriation, domestic consumption, irregular invoices, or invalid CUFE cause reversal of the 0% rate, retroactive assessment of 19% VAT plus moratory interest, the 100% inexactitude penalty (Art. 648 ET), the improper refund/compensation penalty (Art. 670 ET), cancellation of exporter status, and criminal liability of the legal representative for tax evasion under Art. 434B ET (imprisonment of 4–9 years).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia maintains a managed free float regime with full convertibility for current account transactions and registered capital account operations. Foreign investment for a SAS video game publisher (software publishing service exports) is generally freely convertible provided that: (i) the investment is registered with Banco de la República via the electronic International Investment Register (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales - el canal de inversión extranjera) for FDI statistics and future capital/dividend repatriation; (ii) all international payments are routed through an authorized Intermediary of the Exchange Market (banco comercial autorizado), never through unregulated parallel channels; (iii) remittances are documented with the applicable Declaración de Cambio (foreign exchange declaration) forms. Profit/dividend remittance by a Colombian SAS (ISIC 5820 digital publishing) is unrestricted up to 100% of registered after-tax profits, subject to: (a) standard corporate income tax of 35% for fiscal year 2025 under Ley 2277 de 2022 (tasa de renta), (b) the 5% additional dividend withholding tax for non-resident shareholders on post-CIT distributable profits (tasa de retención por dividendos: 10% + 5% surcharge under Ley 1819 de 2016), (c) presentation of the annual registry update proving the foreign shareholder's capital participation. No mandatory reinvestment quota or prudential freeze applies to software/Steam publishing operations. However, the SAS must channel the USD receipts from Valve or PlayStation Store into the Colombian banking system within the legal terms; the peso conversion rate is market-determined (TRM – Tasa Representativa del Mercado) and applies for tax and FX declarations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Colombian Foreign Exchange Regime (Resolución Externa 1/2018 del Banco de la República and Decree 1068/2015), proceeds from exports of services (including digital content distribution, Steam publishing royalties, and console store sales) must be channeled to the domestic economy through a Colombian authorized exchange intermediary. The statutory channeling period for service exporters is six (6) months computed from the date of issuance of the invoice or the contractual billing milestone, whichever is earlier, and must be documented via the Formulario Declaración de Cambio (Declaración de Cambio por servicios – código 5010 / 5011). Conversion of export proceeds into COP is mandatory if the funds are not retained in a foreign currency denominated deposit account (Cuenta en moneda extranjera) held in a Colombian banking institution. Practically, 100% of the USD receipts from Steam/Valve and PlayStation Store must be either: (i) settled into COP within 180 calendar days, or (ii) deposited into a registered USD account at a Colombian authorized bank and converted when used for operational expenses, with each conversion evidenced by exchange declarations. Foreign currency accounts (cuentas en moneda extranjera) must be opened exclusively with authorized intermediaries; transactions executed after the statutory timeline without channeling constitute exchange infractions (infracción cambiaria) under Article 18 of Law 9 of 1991, subject to fines equal to 100%-200% of the non-surrendered amount. For pure intangibles exported without physical customs documents, the export proceeds must be justified by the Elastic (ISIC) service invoices additionally evidencing the contract with Valve (Steamworks Distribution Agreement) and the platform statement of net revenue.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia) jointly with the Dirección de Cambios Internacionales (Foreign Exchange Department) and the Junta Directiva del Banco de la República; the foreign exchange regime is codified under Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1068 of 2015 (Decreto Único Reglamentario del Sector Hacienda y Crédito Público), and Banco de la República Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83. The Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia supervises authorized foreign exchange intermediaries (intermediarios del mercado cambiario).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low-to-Medium. Colombian tax and FX regulations require all resident SAS transactions in foreign currency to be intermediated through (i) an authorized Colombian bank (intermediario del mercado cambiario) or (ii) an account in a foreign bank registered with Banco de la República as a Cuenta de Compensación. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) are not authorized as standalone settlement channels for export proceeds without a local license. If used only as a secondary collection vehicle, they must (a) receive USD from Valve/PlayStation Store, (b) be linked as payout method to the Colombian SGD/Deceval-type settlement?; (c) the final USD must be transferred within 180 days into a Colombian authorized intermediary for FX purposes. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections (FDI equity contributions) from non-resident shareholders into the Colombian SAS (CO__CO_SAS) must follow the mandatory bancarized route: (1) the shareholder wires USD from its home bank to the SAS's USD account at the Colombian Tier-1 intermediary (e.g., Banco de Bogotá account in USD with correspondent: Bank of America, New York or JPMorgan New York); (2) SWIFT MT103 reference must include the transaction narrative: 'Aporte social / capital increase CO__CO_SAS' plus the national tax ID (NIT) of the SAS; (3) within one (1) business day of collection the bank requires the Formulario No. 2 'Declaración de Cambio por Inversión Extranjera Directa Aporte de Capital' (DC – Inversión Directa, código 0151), accompanied by the certificate of incorporation (Cámara y Comercio), a SSS (Sociedad SAS) board/minutes approving the capital increase, and the foreign exchange broker's FX conversion order if converting into COP; (4) Registration of Constitutive Investment with Banco de la República (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales) must be executed within the same month, producing a Registro individual report of the amount converted and the shares payable; (5) Retention: no Colombian withholding tax applies to capital injections (aportes), but stamp taxes (impuesto de timbre) at 0% for digitalized capital contributions for micro and small?; for the standard SAS no capital tax applies at entry. Use of a central US/local branch conversion is possible only through the bank's 'Mesa de dinero' for the official TRM; any delay beyond 30 days risks recategorization as overdue channeling subject to sanctions; if the injection exceeds USD 100,000 equivalently, the bank engages automatic reporting to UIAF (Inteligencia Financiera) triggering mandatory identification of the foreign shareholder's bank and source of wealth.Outbound treasury flows for the SAS (royalty/license fees to developers, advertising purchases on Steam, payment for sub-license or content acquisition, commission remittances to publishers) use the following clearing rails: (a) SWIFT MT103 for all non-documentary payments denominated in USD/COP; (b) Commercial Documentary Letters of Credit (LC at sight) for payments tied to physical deliverables (e.g., boxed console media, specialized hardware), although for pure ISIC 5820 digital services the standard is a bank transfer against an electronic invoice; (c) COP wire through CUD (Cámara de Riesgo Central, now operated by Cámara de Compensación de Pagos) for domestic suppliers; and (d) SWIFT gpi (global payment innovation) to the beneficiary's bank for high-value royalties (>USD 1,000,000) guaranteeing same-day confirmation. Each MT103 must contain: purpose code '5210 Registro de regalías por uso de software / derechos de autor', the beneficiary NIT or international ID, and the reference 'Factura #xxxx con base en contrato de distribución'. Withholding tax obligations: the SAS must withhold US income tax at 33% on outbound royalties/rendering of services (Art. 408 E.T.) and remit the withholding to DIAN within the monthly preliminary tax term; for payments to US treaty counterparts, the limitation of benefits provisions under the US-Colombia Tax Treaty (2012) reduce source tax only if a valid Certificate of Tax Residency is attached before the disbursement date. FX cover: the SAS may buy USD spot from the Colombian bank at TRM + spread, and must complete the corresponding Declaración de Cambio de Servicios / Transferencia al exterior (Form TC-4) for each outward remittance; payments for third-party service imports are allowed freely, but outward royalties remitted to foreign related parties must satisfy the transfer pricing evidence file (Documentación Maestra y Local) to avoid double taxation and adverse adjustments.Currency Forwards (Forwards COP/USD) traded with Colombian authorized intermediaries, executed under ISDA/CMA/Formato Latinoaméricano, physically deliverable in COP or USD and permitted for hedging of the SAS's exporter proceeds; cannot be used for speculative accumulation beyond 110% of underlying cash flows.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDF) / Deliverable Forwards on USD/COP referenced to the TRM, allow hedging of projected Steam and PlayStation Store receivables without the need to hold USD; E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Low-to-Medium. Colombian tax and FX regulations require all resident SAS transactions in foreign currency to be intermediated through (i) an authorized Colombian bank (intermediario del mercado cambiario) or (ii) an account in a foreign bank registered with Banco de la República as a Cuenta de Compensación. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) are not authorized as standalone settlement channels for export proceeds without a local license. If used only as a secondary collection vehicle, they must (a) receive USD from Valve/PlayStation Store, (b) be linked as payout method to the Colombian SGD/Deceval-type settlement?; (c) the final USD must be transferred within 180 days into a Colombian authorized intermediary for FX purposes. The SEDPE (Sociedad Especializada en Depósitos y Pagos Electrónicos) regime (Ley 1735 de 2014 and Fintech Law Ley 2024 de 2020) licenses local payment institutions, but these do not have independent FX sales authorization unless they are officially authorized as foreign exchange intermediary; therefore, the treasury blueprint mandates a local Tier-1 bank for all foreign exchange filings, with EMIs used strictly as consolidated payout reporting (not legal clearing).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File thresholds are referenced to UVT (Unidad de Valor Tributario). For 2024, 1 UVT equals COP 47,065; therefore 30,000 UVT equals approximately COP 1,411,950,000. A Colombian S.A.S. must prepare and file the Local File and transfer pricing declaration when its gross income or total assets exceed this threshold and it engages in cross-border related-party transactions (e.g., intercompany royalties, management fees, or platform payout structures). Master File is required when the multinational group has consolidated global revenues above 600,000 UVT and meets the OECD transfer pricing documentation criteria.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not have a bespoke offshore Economic Substance Regulation (ESR) regime separate from its general tax code. However, DIAN applies substance-oriented controls under the general anti-abuse rule (Article 869 ET), the permanent establishment concept, and the controlled foreign corporation (CFC) rules. A Colombian S.A.S. must demonstrate actual local economic activity - employees, office, decision-making, and business risk - to avoid re-characterization as a shell conduit for foreign shareholders. Non-resident digital publishers arranging a structurally passive Colombian entity without meaningful functions should expect PE and GAAR challenges.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Article 647 ET, DIAN may impose a penalty of 200% of the omitted tax, plus default interest under Article 635 ET. Criminal tax evasion under Article 425 of the Colombian Criminal Code carries imprisonment of 4 to 9 years and fines equal to the same amount as the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives who sign fraudulent returns or approve falsified accounts are jointly and severally liable for the omitted tax, may face civil restitution, and may receive court-ordered travel bans pending criminal proceedings. Active participation in evasion can lead to personal criminal conviction.
Transfer Pricing Disqualification

Statutory sanction: DIAN is empowered to adjust the transfer price to the arm's length value, increasing the taxable base and the CIT liability. The same adjustment carries a penalty of up to 5% of the adjusted value or 20% of the underpaid tax, plus default interest; absence of a contemporaneous TP study triggers a mandatory penalty equal to 3% of the value of the affected transactions, not less than 30,000 UVT? (in line with Article 260(8) ET).

Director personal liability: Directors who directly negotiate and approve related-party agreements without obtaining a proper arm's length economic study expose themselves to joint liability for the resulting tax assessment and interest. Criminal liability can attach if the transfer pricing documentation is knowingly fabricated to hide a constructive dividend.
Shell Company / Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: DIAN may disregard the legal personality of the shell S.A.S. under GAAR (Article 869 ET), reallocate the income to the foreign principals, and impose a penalty equal to up to 100% of the tax avoided. The entity may also be characterized as a permanent establishment of the foreign shareholder, subjecting both the entity and the foreign parent to Colombia-source taxation and registration obligations.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who consent to the use of the corporate vehicle without actual substance can be held personally liable for the tax reassessment, have their assets attached by DIAN, and may face a travel ban during fraud investigation. In serious cases, the criminal filing of false constitutional documents triggers imprisonment for 3 to 7 years for documentary falsification.
AML / Criminal Proceeds

Statutory sanction: Financial institutions must report suspicious transactions to the Financial Information and Analysis Unit (UIAF). Non-compliance or active structuring may result in account freezing, administrative fines under the Colombian AML regime up to 1,000 UVT per infraction, and criminal money laundering charges under Article 323 of the Criminal Code carrying 10 to 15 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly bypass bank due-diligence requests or orchestrate layered payments through shell accounts face criminal conviction, personal civil liability for the proceeds, asset freezing, and pretrial detention with travel bans. Directors protecting the foreign principal in laundering software-publishing royalties can be tried as co-authors of the offense.
