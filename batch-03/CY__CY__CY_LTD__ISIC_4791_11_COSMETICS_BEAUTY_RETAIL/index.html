Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required under Cypriot law; there is no escrow or pre-deposit requirement. The Company may register with a small issued share capital, e.g. a single ordinary share of €1, or adopt an authorized share capital of €2,000 divided into 2,000 ordinary shares of €1 each.

Funding & unlock path: No fire safety certificate is required for a pure administrative e-commerce office. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus, Ministry of Finance; online e-filing portals: Ariadne VAT e-service and Tax Portal e-filing system (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy).

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company must first be incorporated and registered at the Registrar of Companies, obtaining its HE/Company Registration Number. The company is then required to complete registration with the Cyprus Tax Department within 60 days of incorporation or commencement of business, which results in the allocation of a Cyprus Tax Identification Code. VAT registration is a separate application submitted through the Ariadne VAT portal; registration is mandatory within 30 days after taxable supplies exceed EUR 15,600 over the preceding 12 months, or are expected to exceed that threshold in the following 30 days. Foreign entities without a fixed establishment may register as non-established taxable persons for VAT, and for cross-border B2C distance sales they may elect OSS or IOSS instead of a full local VAT registration where applicable.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Cyprus private limited company must first be incorporated and registered at the Registrar of Companies, obtaining its HE/Company Registration Number. The company is then required to complete registration with the Cyprus Tax Department within 60 days of incorporation or commencement of business, which results in the allocation of a Cyprus Tax Identification Code. VAT registration is a separate application submitted through the Ariadne VAT portal; registration is mandatory within 30 days after taxable supplies exceed EUR 15,600 over the preceding 12 months, or are expected to exceed that threshold in the following 30 days. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid classification election; a Cyprus Ltd is mandatorily treated as a separate entity-level taxpaying company. Available administrative elections include group loss surrender between 75% group companies, VAT group registration for closely related persons, and the qualifying IP box election. EU anti-hybrid mismatch rules apply and override arrangements designed to exploit classification differences; no elective hybrid-branch treatment is recognized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months or expected in the next 30 days, calculated excluding VAT; for cross-border distance sales of goods into other EU Member States, the EU-wide B2C distance-selling threshold is EUR 10,000 per calendar year, while non-established suppliers have no local threshold when making taxable supplies considered to be located in Cyprus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical export of goods from Cyprus to a non-EU destination qualifies for the 0% VAT rate when the goods are declared to Cyprus Customs, accepted for export, and removed from the customs territory evidencing by an approved export declaration and transport documentation. Intra-EU dispatches to another Member State may be zero-rated only where the purchaser's EU VAT number is valid, an EC Sales List is filed, and transport/removal evidence is retained. Input VAT attributable to exported goods remains recoverable through the Cyprus VAT return or refund procedure provided the export evidence package is maintained. Domestic B2C retail sales of cosmetics and personal care products to Cyprus consumers remain subject to the 19% standard rate; zero-rating does not depend on the online or DTC character of the seller.

  • Valid Cyprus EORI number registration
  • Approved Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) with Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Commercial invoice showing exporter, consignee, HS classification for cosmetics, quantities, price, Incoterms and payment terms
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or courier shipment manifest proving physical removal from Cyprus
  • Packing list
  • Bank settlement evidence including SWIFT credit or Inward Remittance Certificate, supplemented by Shopify Payments/PayPal payout reports

Cyprus does not currently impose real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory digital stamping, or tax-authority API validation for retail invoices. B2B and B2C e-invoicing is voluntary, except for public procurement B2G invoices, which must be e-invoiced through a PEPPOL-compliant solution. Taxpayers must issue valid VAT invoices, preserve their authenticity and integrity, and retain underlying export/customs records in accordance with the Cyprus VAT Law.

Cyprus Customs and the Tax Department may cross-check export declarations, MRN exit records, transport documents and banking/payment settlement evidence. Invalid or contradictory export substantiation may cause withdrawal of zero-rating, retrospective assessment at 19% VAT, statutory interest, monetary penalties for false declarations, and criminal prosecution for VAT evasion. The standard VAT assessment limitation period is generally six years, with an unlimited extension in cases of fraud or intentional wrongdoing. Retrospective clawback may also include denial of input VAT recovery and recharacterization of the supply as domestic retail VAT due in Cyprus.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus has adopted the euro (EUR) and maintains full current-account convertibility with no exchange-control approval requirement for trade or service transactions. The capital account is open to EU/EEA and third-country investors under Article 63 TFEU, subject to EU sanctions screening, AML/CFT controls, and BoP/IoP statistical reporting by banks. Profit remittance through dividends is unrestricted for non-resident shareholders and, subject to beneficial ownership documentation, no Cypriot dividend withholding tax applies to non-residents. Payment intermediaries must tag each wire with an EU BoP purpose code; mis-tagged or undocumented receipts may be rejected or suspended by the beneficiary bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory foreign-currency surrender requirement or mandatory conversion ratio applies to Cyprus exporters. EUR, USD, and GBP export receipts may be retained in commercial bank settlement accounts without mandatory repatriation timelines; however, transfer pricing, VAT, and Intrastat reporting obligations require arm's-length invoicing and contemporaneous records of the underlying cross-border e-commerce transactions.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Greek: Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), functioning within the Eurosystem and cooperating with the European Central Bank (ECB) under the Single Supervisory Mechanism (SSM) for significant credit institutions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility: MEDIUM. Cyprus-licensed and EU-passported electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Europe, Revolut Bank UAB, and Payhawk can open remote multi-currency accounts for a Cyprus Ltd; however, EMIs cannot provide statutory capital escrow services, bank guarantees, or traditional deposit accounts. PayPal and Shopify settlement should route to a mainstream credit institution to avoid payment-gateway scheme restrictions. EMI rails are suitable for operational sub-ledgers, FX conversion, and supplier disbursements but not for capital injections or tax-deposit holding.
