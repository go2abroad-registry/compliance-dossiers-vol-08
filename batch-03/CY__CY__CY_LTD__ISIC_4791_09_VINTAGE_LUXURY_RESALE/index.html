Governing statute

Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113 (as amended), §§4-6, 11-12 (mode of incorporation and certificate of incorporation), §23 (name restrictions), §46 (registered office), §§51-53 (directors and company secretary); implemented under the Companies (Registration) Rules and the Registrar's administrative practice, with AML/UBO gates under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Law L.13(I)/2021 and the Beneficial Ownership (Companies) Law L.14(I)/2021.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department, Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus / TaxisNet Portal (www.taxdepartment.gov.cy); for VAT: Ariadne VAT filing system

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company is automatically registered for income tax upon incorporation with the Registrar of Companies, which issues a Tax Identification Code (TIC/HE number). To obtain a VAT Registration Number, the taxpayer files form VAT-101 with the District VAT Office (or electronic submission via TaxisNet) within 30 days of exceeding the €15,600 taxable turnover threshold; non-Cyprus-established foreign entities must register before making any taxable supply and may be required to appoint a fiscal representative. Documents required: Incorporation Certificate, certificate of registered office address, identity documents of directors, and evidence of actual or intended economic activity in Cyprus.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Cyprus private limited company is automatically registered for income tax upon incorporation with the Registrar of Companies, which issues a Tax Identification Code (TIC/HE number). To obtain a VAT Registration Number, the taxpayer files form VAT-101 with the District VAT Office (or electronic submission via TaxisNet) within 30 days of exceeding the €15,600 taxable turnover threshold; non-Cyprus-established foreign entities must register before making any taxable supply and may be required to appoint a fiscal representative. Documents required: Incorporation Certificate, certificate of registered office address, identity documents of directors, and evidence of actual or intended economic activity in Cyprus.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Cyprus. Statutory elections include group loss relief under Art. 14 of the Income Tax Law, the Notional Interest Deduction on new equity (Art. 9AB), election for capital allowances on intangible assets (Art. 9A), and the election to treat intra-group disposals as continuing ownership (restructuring provisions). Hybrid mismatch elections follow EU ATAD rules applied through domestic legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 (annual taxable supply turnover; €35,000 for distance selling into Cyprus; no threshold for non-established suppliers)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Cyprus VAT law zero-rates sales of movable goods physically exported outside the EU by the supplier, provided the supplier obtains proof of dispatch within 90 days of the supply and the commercial/transport documents evidence that the recipient is located outside the EU. Online resale through marketplaces is eligible only if the Cyprus supplier acts as principal and the goods leave the EU in the supplier's name. Supplies of ancillary services directly related to the exported luxury goods (packaging, authenticity certificates, insurance) may also be zero-rated if they are contractually part of the export transaction.

  • Customs Export Declaration (validated in Cyprus Customs El.SYS, SAD/EX1)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing departure from EU territory and non-EU destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer receipt proving payment received from the non-EU buyer

Cyprus does not impose a real-time e-invoice clearance or digital stamping mandate. Mandatory electronic filing of VAT returns and VIES/Intrastat returns through the TaxisNet portal and Cyprus Customs 'El.SYS' export system. B2G e-invoicing to the Cypriot public sector must use PEPPOL-compliant formats. All VAT and export supporting documents must be electronically archived for six years.

The Cyprus Tax Department submits zero-rating export claims to desk and field audits, requiring physical and documentary traceability (export customs records, courier tracing, bank remittances). In case of insufficient or false export evidence, the zero-rating is clawed back retroactively with the standard 19% VAT assessed on the original sales value, subject to a six-year limitation period (twelve years on allegations of fraud). Civil penalties include an additional 50% of the tax with interest at 3.5% per annum, and criminal prosecution under the VAT Law may lead to fines up to €100,000 and imprisonment up to five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a euro-area member; the euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account under Article 63 TFEU and the liberalized Cyprus exchange-control framework. There are no general prior exchange-control permits for dividend distributions, profit remittances, loan repayments, or equity repatriation by non-resident shareholders. Cyprus imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders of a private limited company, and the only substantive hurdles are corporate-law formalities, audited accounts, tax clearance, and bank-level source-of-funds verification. CBC balance-of-payments statistical reporting is required through the mandated credit institution for cross-border transactions, but this is reporting, not permission.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender, conversion, or holding-period rule applies in Cyprus. Export proceeds from vintage luxury resale are freely retainable in EUR or foreign currency. The exporter must maintain transaction-level evidence, invoices, customs declarations, and payment references for CBC balance-of-payments statistics and bank sanctions screening. No statutory timeline or minimum conversion ratio exists.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου, CBC), acting as the euro-area national central bank, banking supervisor, and authorizing/statistical authority for foreign-exchange and balance-of-payments purposes; CySEC supervises payment institutions, EMIs, and FX/investment firms where relevant.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a collection/payout rail, Low as the sole treasury settlement account. Cypriot-licensed or EEA-passported EMIs under Law 82(I)/2012 and PSD2 may open business accounts remotely with an IBAN and can aggregate Stripe and e-commerce card settlement, but they are not licensed credit institutions, cannot provide regulated capital escrow or treasury overdrafts, and are often treated by auditors as restricted cash. Offshore EMIs without a Cyprus passport or EEA branch must not be used for statutory share-capital verification. Optimal structure: use the EMI or payment processor solely for card settlement and seller payouts, with the senior treasury account and capital escrow held at a tier-1 Cypriot commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share capital is transferred from the shareholder's bank to the Cyprus Ltd's designated capital/escrow account via SWIFT MT103 or SEPA credit transfer. Remittance information must state: 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION – CY_LTD – [Share Subscription Agreement date]'. The receiving Cypriot bank will classify the flow as BPM6 direct-investment equity capital and not as trade settlement. Required verification includes the board resolution authorizing the account and share issuance; share subscription agreement and form HE2 allotment return; UBO certificate and passport of the transferor; source-of-funds evidence; and tax identification number. No CBC foreign-exchange approval is required. Non-EUR remittances are converted at the receiving bank's spot rate, and the dealing confirmation must be retained.Outbound payments to overseas resellers, suppliers of vintage handbags, watches, and jewellery, and logistics providers are executed from the operational account via SWIFT MT103 for USD/GBP and via SEPA or Instant SEPA for EUR. High-value authenticated purchases may be settled by commercial letter of credit under UCP600 when the consignor requires bank payment risk mitigation. Each payment must reference the underlying consignment or purchase contract, invoice, and customs declaration. No prior Central Bank approval is required; banks conduct sanctions screening and CBC statistical reporting. 12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for dividends paid to non-resident shareholders. Cyprus does not impose a statutory withholding tax on outbound dividends. Domestic Special Defence Contribution at 17% applies only to dividends distributed to Cyprus tax-resident individuals and, in specific cases, Cyprus tax-resident companies.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File threshold: aggregate amount of controlled transactions in any category exceeding EUR 750,000 per tax year. Master File threshold: consolidated group turnover exceeding EUR 750,000,000 with related-party transactions. These Cyprus transfer pricing documentation and filing rules apply from tax year 2022 onward.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not have a general standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) filing regime similar to the UAE. However, economic substance is enforced through: (i) the Cyprus tax residence test requiring management and control in Cyprus; (ii) EU ATAD CFC rules and Cypriot general anti-abuse rules; (iii) OECD nexus rules for the IP Box regime; and (iv) the Cyprus Companies Law requirement of a registered office and registered secretary. A CY Ltd engaged in online luxury resale must have real local employees or adequate outsourcing, an office, locally made commercial decisions, and contemporaneous evidence of its operating substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of omitted income; interest and administrative penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax; on criminal conviction, imprisonment of up to 5 years and/or a fine of up to EUR 50,000 (subject to judicial determination).

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns or orchestrate fraudulent bookkeeping can be held jointly and severally liable for the company's unpaid tax; criminal liability may extend to the individual directors, with potential restitution orders, travel restrictions, and director disqualification orders.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of Cyprus tax residence certificate; tax recharacterization and local substance-based adjustments under general anti-abuse and CFC rules; deregistration from the Tax Department; and potential nullification of treaty relief where the Cypriot company lacks beneficial ownership.

Director personal liability: Directors may incur personal responsibility under Cyprus Companies Law Cap. 113 for fraudulent or wrongful trading, civil restitution of misapplied corporate assets, travel bans pending liquidation proceedings, and disqualification from acting as directors.
AML / KYC Deficiencies in Payments and Escrow

Statutory sanction: Under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law, administrative fines of up to EUR 1,000,000 or 10% of annual turnover for legal persons, plus confiscation of proceeds; criminal conviction for money laundering may result in imprisonment of up to 14 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and designated compliance officers can be criminally prosecuted for knowledge-based money laundering; personal fines, imprisonment, travel restrictions, and confiscation orders may be imposed together with civil liability to compensate affected third parties.
Transfer Pricing / Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: A transfer pricing adjustment increasing taxable profits at 12.5%; late-payment interest and administrative penalties for missing documentation, including a EUR 500 penalty per unfiled TP return and daily fines for continued non-compliance; risk of CFC/ATAD recharacterization.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve transfer prices to evade Cypriot tax may face joint and several liability for penalties under general tax law; in extreme cases, criminal prosecution for fraud may attach personal liability leading to restitution orders and imprisonment.
