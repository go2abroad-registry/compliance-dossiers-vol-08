Not permissible as the sole registered office (Geschäftsanschrift) for a licensed cold-chain storage GmbH. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern – BZSt) for VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.); local municipal Tax Office (Finanzamt) for Steuernummer and income/corporate/trade tax; electronic registration and filing portal: ELSTER.

E-government filing portal

After commercial register admission, the GmbH submits an electronic income-tax registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) via ELSTER to the local Finanzamt to obtain the Steuernummer. The GmbH then files Form Uml1VAT with the BZSt to obtain the VAT identification number, stating the German business address, intended intra-Community transactions, and B2B services. Foreign parent shareholders must provide certified constitutional documents and proof of legal representation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or pass-through regime is available for a GmbH. The GmbH is a non-transparent resident corporation subject to corporate income tax, solidarity surcharge, and trade tax. Hybrid and reverse-hybrid classifications are governed by §4k EStG. Optional VAT Organschaft consolidation may be elected if financial, economic, and organizational integration exists with the controlling German parent.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration from the first taxable turnover; no economic threshold applies to an ordinary GmbH. Small-business relief under §19 UStG is optional where prior-year turnover was at most EUR 22,000 and current-year turnover will not exceed EUR 50,000, but commercial cold-storage and export logistics generally exceed such limits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical exports of goods to destinations outside the EU are zero-rated under §4(1)(1) and §6 UStG, provided the goods leave Germany and the EU and are transported directly by the seller or a commissioned carrier. Cold-storage and logistics services physically connected to those exported goods are eligible for zero-rating or tax-free treatment if the exporter's documentation proves the direct connection to the export movement. Certain services supplied to non-EU recipients or goods placed under customs transit may be zero-rated or shift the VAT liability to the recipient under §6a and §13b UStG; services relating to German land remain taxable.

  • Electronic export declaration through ATLAS with MRN and Ausfuhrnummer
  • Commercial invoice and export packing list
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill, CMR road consignment note, or combined transport document evidencing physical exit
  • Customs proof of export receipt stamped or processed by the German or EU customs office (franchise certificate for postal consignments)
  • Inward payment evidence or bank remittance statement showing receipt from the foreign buyer
  • Certificate of origin and, where applicable, pharmaceutical cold-chain temperature records and health certificates

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls exist. Domestic B2B e-invoicing is mandatory from 1 January 2025 in the XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.0.1 format, transmitted interoperably. B2G invoices must be electronic. VAT returns and prepayments are filed electronically through ELSTER; no closure stamp or API pre-clearance is required by the tax authority.

Germany imposes no foreign-exchange repatriation deadline because there are no exchange controls. Export zero-rating is strictly audited on documentation and physical movement. Missing or inconsistent export proof triggers a VAT correction at 19%, late-payment interest of 6% per annum, and penalty assessments under §§25b and 371 AO. Intentional or fraudulent false zero-rating claims constitute tax evasion under §370 AO, punishable by imprisonment of up to ten years and fines up to EUR 50,000; limitation periods extend from four to ten years depending on fraud and concealment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro (EUR) is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions. Germany applies the EU free movement of capital regime (TFEU Articles 63-66); there are no exchange controls, no official settlement rates, and no licensing requirements for capital injections, shareholder loans, profit distributions, or trade-related payments. Dividends and profits may be remitted to foreign shareholders without FX approval, subject only to the German withholding tax framework (25% Abgeltungsteuer plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge, total 26.375%, potentially reduced under an applicable Double Taxation Treaty) and to the legally required shareholder resolution and audited distributable profit calculation. Non-resident ownership is expressly permitted for a GmbH, and no 'remittance hurdle' certificate is required for the cold-storage entity's revenues.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory surrender, repatriation, or conversion ratio requirements for export receipts. The cold-storage entity (ISIC 5210) may invoice and receive EUR or any foreign currency (USD, GBP, CHF) and maintain the proceeds in the same currency without mandatory conversion. However, under Section 67 AWV, any cross-border payment or capital flow exceeding EUR 12,500 (or its equivalent) must be reported electronically to the Deutsche Bundesbank within 7 days of value date. The report is statistical in nature, requiring the correct AWV transaction type code (Zahlungsart) and counterparty reference, and a 'goods or services' classification code reflecting the logistics service fee; failure to report attracts administrative fines but does not affect the legality of the payment.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (central bank and external-sector statistical authority under the Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV / Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance)), acting in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB) within the Eurosystem and with Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin) for bank supervision. For foreign-exchange and cross-border capital control matters, the Bundesbank is the sole reporting and monitoring authority; no separate foreign-exchange ministry approval is required for companies resident in Germany.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-High as a complementary rail, Low-Medium as a sole treasury rail. German-authorised or EEA-passported Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs, licensed under the Zahlungsdiensteaufsichtsgesetz / ZAG) can provide multi-currency IBAN accounts, fast SEPA and SWIFT execution, and competitive EUR/USD FX for low-value payouts. Legal constraints: EMI balances do not benefit from statutory deposit insurance (Einlagensicherung), EMIs cannot pay interest or provide credit facilities, and they cannot issue or settle commercial letters of credit, escrow/fiduciary accounts, or committed hedge lines. The blueprint should therefore use an EMI for payment-rail diversification and instant cross-border disbursements while preserving the core treasury, share-capital escrow, and documentary LC operations in a tier-1 credit institution.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Routing protocol: (a) the shareholder remits exclusively from a corporate bank account bearing the shareholder's exact registered legal name, in EUR, via SWIFT MT103 to the GmbH's German IBAN; (b) field 70 (Remittance Information) must state 'Kapitaleinlage / Share Capital Contribution' together with the applicable Bundesbank AWV Zahlungsart statistical code for equity contributions; (c) for any cross-border payment exceeding EUR 12,500, the treasury must file the AWV cross-border statistics report electronically with the Bundesbank within 7 days; (d) the receiving German bank issues the statutory certificate of capital availability (Bankbescheinigung) required for the commercial-register notarisation of the contribution; (e) where the inbound value is in USD or another foreign currency, the bank converts at prevailing spot limits and credits EUR to the operational account; no repatriation or clearance certificate is required. For shareholder loans instead of equity, the loan must be documented at arm's length, bear arm's-length interest, and be vetted against the German interest-restriction rules (Section 4h EStG / Section 8a KStG) and the thin-capitalisation jurisprudence; loan drawdowns also require AWV reporting and a stated purpose code distinguishing intercompany finance from trade flows.Clearing rails: (1) SEPA Credit Transfer and SEPA Instant Credit Transfer for EUR disbursements to EU counterparties, settling T+1 or same-day; (2) SWIFT MT103 for non-EEA single payments with full correspondent-banking routing; (3) SWIFT MT202 COV for cover payments in foreign-currency legs; (4) Commercial Documentary Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707) for procurement of temperature-controlled equipment, medical logistics infrastructure, or pharmaceutical freight where the supplier requires bank-backed payment undertakings; (5) SEPA B2B Direct Debit for recurring utility, warehouse-energy, and high-volume sub-carrier collections. Each outbound disbursement must be individually tagged with the underlying commercial invoice number, warehouse note, and transport reference to prevent TBML-related bank inquiry; payees must have completed KYC at the time of payment and must match the bank-recorded beneficiary exactly, with no third-party settlement structures.Outright FX forwards (Devisentermingeschäfte) in EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, and EUR/CHF, with tenors aligned to the actual customer billing and procurement cycles, executed under an OTC FX master agreement and pre-assigned credit line.FX swaps (Devisenswaps) used for temporary cross-currency liquidity bridging between EUR and USD operating balances.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for currencies subject to posting or convertibility restrictions (e.g., CNH/CNY or certain CEEMEA currencies), permissible in Germany without special licence where the underlying is a genuine trade receivable or payable of the cold-storage business.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) for multi-year EUR/USD financing of capital expenditure in the refrigerated warehousing capacity expansion, including a EUR/USD basis-swap component.Zero-cost collars and vanilla FX options under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with Credit Support Annex, economically unrestricted under the AWG/AWV and eligible for hedge accounting where the HGB Section 254 or IFRS 9 effectiveness criteria are met.Exchange-traded EUR/USD futures on Eurex as a transparent alternative to OTC forwards where the credit-line appetite of the treasury team is limited.
