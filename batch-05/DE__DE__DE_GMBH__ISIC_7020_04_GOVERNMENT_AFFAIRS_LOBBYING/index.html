Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (Federal Central Tax Office), with local Finanzämter; electronic system: ELSTER (Elektronische Steuererklärung). VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) is issued by BZSt via ELSTER or form USt 1 TG.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned GmbH obtains its tax number by registering the company with the Commercial Register (Handelsregister), followed by submission of the registration notice to the locally competent Finanzamt; corporate income tax number and VAT ID are issued subsequently. A separate VAT identification number application (USt 1 TG) is filed with the Bundeszentralamt für Steuern; the GmbH must also register for trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) with the local municipality. Non-resident shareholders must disclose ownership and beneficial ownership in the tax registration forms.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the GmbH is a resident legal entity with unlimited corporate income tax liability; subject to corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15.0%, solidarity surcharge at 5.5% of the corporate income tax, trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at local municipal rates (standard effective 7%–17%, typical 15%–16%), resulting in a combined effective rate of approximately 30–33% on retained profits. — A foreign-owned GmbH obtains its tax number by registering the company with the Commercial Register (Handelsregister), followed by submission of the registration notice to the locally competent Finanzamt; corporate income tax number and VAT ID are issued subsequently. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the GmbH is a resident legal entity with unlimited corporate income tax liability; subject to corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer) at 15.0%, solidarity surcharge at 5.5% of the corporate income tax, trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at local municipal rates (standard effective 7%–17%, typical 15%–16%), resulting in a combined effective rate of approximately 30–33% on retained profits.

Available election forms

Germany does not provide check-the-box pass-through election for GmbH, except for certain hybrid transparent U.S. LLCs recognized under Germany’s anti-hybrid rules. Available elections include: (i) fiscal unity (Organschaft) election to group income and VAT/trade tax between parent and GmbH if control and profit-pooling requirements are met; (ii) trade tax grouping election without profit-pooling; (iii) tax transparency election for a German-Recht partnership requiring explicit statement in the partnership agreement; (iv) application of German anti-hybrid mismatch election to re-characterise hybrid entities and instruments under § 4k EStG.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration applies to all entrepreneurs; small-business (Kleinunternehmer) exemption applies where prior-year turnover does not exceed EUR 22,000 and current-year turnover is not expected to exceed EUR 50,000. For intra-Community distance sales and cross-border remote services to Germany, an EU-wide EUR 10,000 threshold applies before German VAT registration is required.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically leaving Germany to a non-EU country qualify for zero-rating under § 6 UStG if the exporter is the supplier and provides sufficient documentary evidence (commercial invoice, export customs declaration and transport document). For cross-border services supplied to a business customer in a third country, German VAT generally does not apply if the place of supply is the customer’s jurisdiction (B2B reverse charge); business customers outside the EU can be invoiced net without German VAT if proof of business status is obtained. Export refund procedures require VAT to be reclaimed through periodic VAT returns; no special export licence is required for lobbying/consulting services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic ATLAS/Ausfuhr- und Abfertigungskonzept)
  • Bill of Lading, CMR consignment note, or IATA Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment confirmation
  • Commercial invoice and proof of recipient’s foreign residence status

Since 1 January 2025, every German business must be able to receive structured electronic invoices (e-invoicing) in B2B transactions using XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.x formats. Mandatory transmission obligations phase in from 1 January 2027 for businesses with prior-year turnover exceeding EUR 800,000 and for all businesses from 1 January 2028. Germany does not operate an Italian-style real-time fiscal clearance platform (SII) or digital stamping; e-invoices must be transmitted through EDI/Peppol-compatible infrastructure, with no pre-clearance but a retention obligation of 10 years.

Under § 370 AO, fraudulent export VAT evasion is punishable by up to five years’ imprisonment (or up to ten years in aggravated cases); forfeiture and seizure of assets, fines, and full recovery of the claimed zero-rated VAT plus late-payment interest (1.5% monthly surcharge up to 25% under § 240 AO) may apply. Tax audits rigorously examine export documentation; where proof is missing or insufficient, the Finanzamt retroactively reverses the 0% rate, assesses 19% VAT with interest, and imposes fraudulent-benefit penalties including surcharges of 5% to 25% of the evaded tax; officers may face criminal prosecution and debarment from holding directorship in German companies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains a fully convertible currency regime under EU law for both capital and current accounts. Profit remittances to foreign shareholders or owners are unrestricted and require no prior central bank approval, provided standard corporate tax filings and beneficial ownership reporting are completed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Service export receipts from lobbying and government relations advisory activities must be collected in EUR and repatriated within statutory timelines. No mandatory surrender to the central bank applies to current account transactions; voluntary surrender may be arranged through licensed banks upon client request.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank and European Central Bank (ECB)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Fintech electronic money institutions are subject to BaFin licensing and enhanced AML obligations. Offshore digital EMIs are not viable for corporate clients due to regulatory restrictions and correspondent banking requirements; traditional banking rails remain the only compliant option.
