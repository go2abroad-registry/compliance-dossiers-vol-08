Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent Local Court (Amtsgericht) acting as Commercial Register Court (Registergericht) for the companys registered office.

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung, GmbHG), principally Sections 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 9c, 10, 11, 12, 13, 35 and 35a; German Commercial Code (Handelsgesetzbuch, HGB), Sections 8 and 10; German Civil Code (Bürgerliches Gesetzbuch, BGB), Section 181 where applicable.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarised Handelsregisteranmeldung, accompanied by the notarised Articles of Association (Gesellschaftsvertrag) and managing directors' declarations required under GmbHG Section 8.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The statutory share capital of a GmbH is at least EUR 25,000. Before registration, at least EUR 12,500 must be paid in cash where the capital is subscribed in full; each cash contribution must be paid at least to the extent required by the managing directors' registration declaration. Contributions must be made to an account controlled by the company in formation and remain available to the company. In-kind contributions require a specified contribution description, valuation documentation and full contribution before registration. Germany has no universal statutory escrow-account requirement, but the notary and registry may require evidence of capital availability. The stated fee is an indicative registry and notarisation budget converted into USD; actual court, notarial and publication charges vary by share capital, document complexity and exchange rate.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain building-use and fire-safety clearance from the local building and fire authorities where required; maintain compliant escape routes, emergency exits, fire detection and extinguishing systems, refrigeration emergency controls and fire-load management. Complete structural and floor-load verification for cold rooms, racking and heavy pallets. Perform workplace risk assessments under the Occupational Health and Safety Act, provide statutory safety training and personal protective equipment, and coordinate inspections with the applicable Berufsgenossenschaft. No general national operating licence exists solely for wholesale trading, but food, veterinary, premises, fire, occupational and equipment clearances may be mandatory for the actual site and activities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains full convertibility for both current account and capital account transactions pursuant to EU regulations and national FX law. Resident companies encounter no capital controls on profit remittances or repatriation. Hurdles are confined to standard tax filings and beneficial ownership verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender of export proceeds applies under German law. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency without conversion to Euro. No statutory timelines or conversion ratios exist; conversion decisions rest with the corporate treasurer subject to internal risk policies and AML obligations.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High feasibility for cross-border EMI through licensed providers such as N26 Business or Wise Business, subject to BaFin oversight and full KYC/AML compliance; offshore digital EMIs are permitted for EU-resident entities with appropriate regulatory approvals.
