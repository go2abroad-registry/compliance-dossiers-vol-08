E-government filing portal

Step 1 — File the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) via ELSTER with the Finanzamt competent for the GmbH's registered seat, disclosing the beneficial owners, corporate form, and intended dropshipping activities; obtain a Steuernummer (typically 4–8 weeks). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — GmbH is a corporate tax resident (unbeschränkt steuerpflichtig) taxed on worldwide income under §1 KStG; profit is subject to 15% corporation tax (Körperschaftsteuer) plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on the CTE base (effective 15.825%) plus municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer, effective rate 7%–17% depending on the Hebesatz multiplier of the municipality); there is no pass-through classification available to a GmbH under German tax law (it is always a separate taxable entity); worldwide territorial scope is applied with a full participation exemption (§8b KStG) for qualifying foreign dividends and capital gains, whereas dropshipping trading profits are fully taxable at the German place of effective management.

Available election forms

Organschaft election under §§14-19 KStG and §2(2) Nr. 2 UStG — integrate a financially, economically, and organisationally dependent group company into a single taxpayer for corporation tax, trade tax, and VAT (fiscal unity), requiring a written profit-and-loss pooling agreement (Ergebnisabführungsvertrag) filed in the Commercial Register for a minimum of five years; trade-tax loss-offset election under §10a GewStG; group VAT invoice validation via VIES; hybrid mismatch forced-alignment rules under §4k EStG; no US-style check-the-box election exists — entity classification is governed exclusively by the German Civil Code and KStG.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany applies unrestricted convertibility for both current-account and capital-account transactions under the EU free movement of capital (TFEU Articles 63-66), the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG), and the AWV. No exchange-control approval is required for the receipt of dropshipping settlement proceeds, the payment of foreign suppliers, or capital inflows. Profit remittance by way of dividend distribution is governed exclusively by corporate-law solvency and balance-sheet tests under HGB (sections 268, 272, 279), which require distributable retained earnings and the absence of insolvency; no exchange-control clearance is required. Outbound dividends are subject to the German capital-yield withholding tax of 26.375% (25% Abgeltungsteuer plus 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag on the withholding tax), reducible to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive where the foreign parent holds at least 10% for at least 12 months, or to a treaty rate by the applicable double-taxation agreement. The euro is the Company's functional currency; cross-border payments are fully free, but all payments to or from non-residents exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported statistically to the Deutsche Bundesbank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany imposes no statutory surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds. A German GmbH may lawfully retain receivables in foreign currency. The only binding obligation is statistical: every cross-border payment received from a non-resident exceeding EUR 12,500 (including multiple related payments that aggregate above this threshold within a calendar month) must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank no later than the fifth working day of the month following the month of receipt, using the AWV payment-reporting channels (Meldevordrucke or the Bundesbank's electronic reporting interface). The report must identify the counterparty, the payment purpose, and the amount. Late or false reporting is an administrative offence under AWG section 33.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Central Bank of the Federal Republic of Germany, Frankfurt am Main) - the German central bank and member of the Eurosystem; responsible for monetary policy implementation, banking statistics, and the mandatory statistical reporting of cross-border payments under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung - AWV). Banking licensing and conduct supervision is exercised by the Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin). Germany maintains no separate FX control authority or foreign-exchange licensing body.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium feasibility with strict legal constraints. EEA-licensed electronic-money institutions and payment institutions (for example N26 Business, Penta, Qonto, or Solarisbank-based white-label platforms) may provide payment services in Germany under the PSD2 passporting regime and are supervised by BaFin or their Home State competent authority. Viability is rated Medium for automated dropshipping because: (i) digital onboarding and multi-currency IBANs efficiently absorb Stripe and PayPal settlement flows; (ii) an EMI is not a credit institution and may not grant credit or take deposits, and client funds are not covered by the statutory German deposit-guarantee scheme unless safeguarded in a partner credit institution; (iii) high-velocity cross-border e-commerce settlement frequently triggers automated compliance freezes, source-of-funds holds, and unplanned account terminations; (iv) payment finality and commercial credit lines are unavailable. The recommended architecture is a zero-balance collection-sweep account at an EMI for PSP payouts only, with the Tier-1 commercial bank remaining the treasury anchor and sole custody bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital must be injected through a regulated banking channel only. For an existing GmbH, the shareholder remits via SWIFT MT103 (non-EUR corridor) or SEPA Credit Transfer (EUR corridor), with the remittance-information field unmistakably stating 'Einlage in das Stammkapital' or 'Zuzahlung in die Kapitalrücklage', and with an ISO 20022 Purpose / bank-specific capital-contribution purpose code where the ordering bank supports such codes. The receiving German bank will generally require proof of the remitter's identity and a source-of-funds declaration where the remitter differs from the registered shareholder; the funds become unrestricted immediately for a pre-incorporated GmbH only after the notary's confirmation of availability and the Commercial Register filing. For the incorporation phase, the deposit must be made to a blocked special account (Sperrkonto/Sonderkonto) with a German credit institution. Any inbound contribution exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank under the AWV statistical-reporting obligation.Trade disbursements are executed over three clearing tiers: (1) SEPA Credit Transfer for EUR payments within the EEA and SEPA Instant (SCT Inst) for time-critical payouts up to EUR 100,000; (2) SWIFT MT103 with the mandatory UETR (Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference) for USD, GBP, CNY, or other non-euro supplier payments to overseas dropshipping vendors; and (3) documentary trade instruments, namely Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP 600) or documentary collection (D/P, D/A), for volume-stock imports exceeding the bank's risk thresholds. Receivable settlement from Stripe and PayPal is not a separate treasury rail: the PSP aggregates card-network transactions and settles in EUR via SEPA credit transfer to the GmbH's collection IBAN; accordingly, the Company should maintain one dedicated EUR collection IBAN per marketplace to enable automated reconciliation. Every outbound disbursement must carry the AWV statutory purpose code and be backed by a supplier invoice, customs declaration, and transport document, all retained for the statutory German 8-year commercial retention period.Outright over-the-counter FX forward contracts (Devisentermingeschäfte) - permitted without a BaFin licence for non-financial corporates hedging genuine commercial exposure in EUR/USD or EUR/CNYFX swaps (Devisenswaps) - permitted for rolling management of short-term USD receivables against USD supplier payablesCross-currency swaps (Zins- und Währungsswaps) - permissible only when combined with a drawn EUR credit facility and a matching USD payable streamExchange-traded currency options (buy side only) - permitted as a premium-capped hedging instrument under the GmbH treasury policy; naked selling of options is not recommended and requires specific board authorisationNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) - legally permissible in Germany under the AWV for closed or restricted currencies; not market-effective for EUR/USD or deliverable EUR/CNY onshore exposure and therefore excluded from the standard dropshipping hedging programmeCash-flow natural hedge - permitted and preferred: the GmbH may hold USD multi-currency settlement accounts at a Tier-1 bank and defray USD-denominated supplier payables directly out of USD-denominated PSP or marketplace receivables, thereby eliminating the need for derivative instruments altogether

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium feasibility with strict legal constraints. EEA-licensed electronic-money institutions and payment institutions (for example N26 Business, Penta, Qonto, or Solarisbank-based white-label platforms) may provide payment services in Germany under the PSD2 passporting regime and are supervised by BaFin or their Home State competent authority. Viability is rated Medium for automated dropshipping because: (i) digital onboarding and multi-currency IBANs efficiently absorb Stripe and PayPal settlement flows; (ii) an EMI is not a credit institution and may not grant credit or take deposits, and client funds are not covered by the statutory German deposit-guarantee scheme unless safeguarded in a partner credit institution; (iii) high-velocity cross-border e-commerce settlement frequently triggers automated compliance freezes, source-of-funds holds, and unplanned account terminations; (iv) payment finality and commercial credit lines are unavailable. The recommended architecture is a zero-balance collection-sweep account at an EMI for PSP payouts only, with the Tier-1 commercial bank remaining the treasury anchor and sole custody bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (Körperschaftsteuer) plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag) on the corporate tax, plus municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) at a rate of 7%–17% depending on the municipality, resulting in a combined headline CIT incidence of approximately 30%–33%.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

25% capital yield tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge, resulting in an effective 26.375% domestic withholding tax on dividends distributed by a German GmbH to its non-resident shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory under §90 Abs. 3 AO for cross-border controlled transactions exceeding EUR 1,000,000 per fiscal year; Master File: required for multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of EUR 750,000,000 or more. German tax authorities may nevertheless demand documentation below these thresholds where there are specific indications of pricing irregularities (transaction audits).

Economic substance scope: Germany does not have an Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime in the style of the UAE or the Cayman Islands. Anti-abuse substance requirements arise from §42 AO (General Anti-Abuse Rule), treaty-limitation-on-benefits clauses and the German Federal Fiscal Court's substance jurisprudence. A GmbH must maintain physical business office, local management, employees or IT infrastructure, operational contracts and a real decision-making function for ordering, pricing and returns; otherwise the entity can be disregarded, profits reallocated to the foreign principal, treaty benefits denied, and a 5%–10% surcharge under §162 Abs. 4 AO applied for incomplete transfer pricing documentation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion (Steuerhinterziehung)

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine under §370 AO for the GmbH up to EUR 10 million; imprisonment of natural persons up to 5 years in ordinary cases, up to 10 years in aggravated cases; confiscation of profits, mandatory late-payment interest and extended assessment to 10 years.

Director personal liability: The managing director (Geschäftsführer) is criminally liable as the executor; jointly and severally liable civilly for the GmbH's unpaid taxes under §69 AO; the court may impose travel bans, seize passport/ID, prevent departure under §§ 38–39 OWiG; director disqualification up to 5 years.
Shell company / minimal economic substance

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the GmbH under §42 AO; denial of treaty benefits; recharacterization of German-source income as direct income of the foreign beneficial owner; assessment of withholding tax of 26.375% on deemed distributions; extension of tax assessment period to 10 years; potential retroactive reassessment of five years.

Director personal liability: Directors jointly and severally liable for taxes due on recharacterized profits; personal collection orders possible; on criminal evidence of fraudulent concealment, imprisonment up to 5 years and revocation of the GmbH's commercial registration under § 396 FamFG.
AML / payment-processing abuse

Statutory sanction: Violations of the German Anti-Money Laundering Act (GwG): administrative fines up to EUR 100,000 for minor cases, up to 5% of annual turnover for serious corporate breaches; criminal liability under §261 StGB for money laundering with imprisonment up to 5 years (10 years for gang-related cases); seizure of the company's assets.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are prosecuted for money laundering; additionally, directors face civil liability for damages to banks under §823 BGB; travel bans and arrest pending investigation; extradition risk for EU and international arrest warrants.
Transfer pricing documentation failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty surcharge under §162 Abs. 4 AO: 5% of the amount of the adjusted taxable profit, minimum EUR 5,000, maximum EUR 100,000 per violation; daily penalty of EUR 1,000 for each day of failure to submit documentation; extension of the regular assessment period to 5 years and an increase to 10 years for incomplete documentation.

Director personal liability: The Geschäftsführer is personally liable to the extent the GmbH cannot cover tax and penalties due to negligent non-compliance; the tax authority can seize a manager's assets via Haftungsbescheid; in egregious cases, criminal charges for tax evasion with imprisonment up to 5 years and removal from the commercial register.
