Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (GmbHG), §§ 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 35–44, 57; Commercial Code (HGB) §§ 8–16; Notarisation per GmbHG § 2 and German Notarisation Act (BeurkG).

Formation / constitutional filings

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern – BZSt) for VAT identification numbers and federal tax administration; local tax office (Finanzamt) with jurisdiction over the GmbH's registered seat for corporate income tax, trade tax, and VAT returns; Main Customs Office (Hauptzollamt) via the ATLAS system for export clearances; electronic filing portal ELSTER (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Notarize the GmbH articles of association and register with the Commercial Register (Handelsregister) at the local Amtsgericht. Step 2: Submit the tax registration questionnaire ("Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung") electronically via ELSTER to the competent Finanzamt, declaring the activities including manufacture and export of sterile nitrile gloves. Step 3: Receive the tax number (Steuernummer) for corporate income tax, trade tax, and VAT file administration. Step 4: Apply to the BZSt for the VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer), mandatory for intra-EU supplies and compliant invoicing. Step 5: Obtain an EORI number (DE + 9 digits) from the Hauptzollamt for ATLAS export declarations. Step 6: Non-resident foreign entities with no domestic seat must register direct with the BZSt or the competent Finanzamt through a fiscal representative; the identical questionnaire procedure applies to obtain a Steuernummer and VAT ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (GmbH is a body corporate taxed at entity level: corporate income tax 15% plus solidarity surcharge 5.5% (aggregate 15.825%) and municipal trade tax typically 14-17%; resident GmbH is subject to worldwide taxation, and no pass-through status is available under German law)

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election. Available elections: (1) fiscal unity (Organschaft) for VAT under §2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG and for corporate income tax and trade tax under §§14, 17 KStG; (2) small-business exemption under §19 UStG if the GmbH's revenue stays within the statutory limits; (3) VAT grouping and intra-Community supply exemption election supported by the customer's valid VAT ID; (4) OSS/IOSS scheme elections for distance sales and low-value import consignments; (5) German hybrid mismatch rules under §4k EStG (ATAD) restrict deduction-election opportunities; (6) election for VAT refund by foreign entrepreneurs per the §18 Abs. 9 UStG special procedure.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold applies to an established German entity; every entrepreneur is registrable for VAT. The small-business election under §19 UStG may apply where previous-year turnover is no more than €25,000 and current-year projected turnover does not exceed €100,000. Non-established foreign suppliers are registrable upon any taxable supply in Germany; the EU distance-sales threshold is €10,000 across all Member States (OSS Union scheme), below which registration is required solely in the supplier's home Member State; intra-EU acquisitions by VAT-registered businesses are taxable without threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of sterile nitrile gloves to destinations outside the EU are zero-rated pursuant to §4 Nr. 1a in conjunction with §6 UStG where the goods physically leave the EU customs territory and the supplier substantiates export in accordance with §§8-10 UStDV (ATLAS export declaration; simplified proof for shipments up to €100). Intra-EU deliveries to VAT-registered medical wholesalers are exempt under §4 Nr. 1b and §6a UStG subject to the customer's valid VAT ID and transport/arrival evidence. Services ancillary to export (e.g., labelling, quarantine release, storage directly linked to the exported goods) also qualify for zero-rating if rendered to non-EU entrepreneurs or used in export; the supply of medical gloves at 7% reduced VAT rate may be claimed where the goods qualify as medical protective equipment in domestic downstream sales, but headline export zero-rating applies to all qualifying exports.

  • ATLAS electronic customs export declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung) with Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Commercial invoice compliant with §14 UStG including VAT ID, Incoterms, and unit values
  • Packing list detailing sterile nitrile glove cartons, net and gross weights, and harmonized tariff classification (HS 3926.20 or 4015.12)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR consignment note evidencing the goods left Germany
  • Proof of export (Ausfuhrnachweis) — the customs export accompanying document or ATLAS confirmation print-out
  • Gelangensbestätigung (confirmation of arrival) where transport is effected by the buyer or for intra-Community movements without customs documentation
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT LC/TT settlement extract evidencing foreign-exchange inflow
  • EU preferential origin documentation (EUR.1 or supplier's declaration) where duty preference is claimed at destination

Mandatory B2B e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) is phased in: from 1 January 2025 all resident businesses must be able to receive structured e-invoices (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD compliant with CEN EN 16931); from 1 January 2027 resident businesses with prior-year turnover above €800,000 must issue e-invoices for domestic B2B supplies; from 1 January 2028 all resident businesses must issue e-invoices. There is no real-time fiscal clearance requirement for domestic invoices today, but the EU ViDA initiative will mandate digital reporting (clearance) for cross-border transactions by 30 June 2030. Export and intra-EU export documents remain paper/PDF-compliant with the ATLAS customs platform; intra-EU recapitulative statements must be filed via ELSTER. Sterile nitrile gloves must additionally satisfy EU MDR traceability, which is not a fiscal clearance.

Export zero-rating is routinely challenged in German VAT audits (Außenprüfung). Failure to produce valid proof of export within the verification period converts the sale to a taxable domestic supply at 19%, triggering retrospective VAT clawback, accrual of interest at 0.5% per month under §233a AO, and administrative late-filing penalties. Intentional evasion constitutes tax evasion under §370 AO (imprisonment 6 months to 10 years, fines up to EUR 500,000); negligent failure is sanctioned under §378 AO with fines up to EUR 50,000. Where an invoice falsely identifies a supplier or customer, the issuing entity is liable for the VAT under §25a UStG. Customs false declarations may additionally attract penalties under §382 AO and the Union Customs Code Article 42, revocation of AEO status, and, for foreign entrepreneurs, mandatory appointment of a fiscal representative with joint and several liability under §§21-22 UStG.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates a full-convertibility regime: the euro is freely convertible within the Eurosystem, and there is no exchange-control licensing, no capital-import approval, no surrender obligation and no destination restriction on either current-account or capital-account flows for a German GmbH (TFEU Articles 63-65). Capital-account flows (share-capital injections, shareholder loans, intercompany credit, profit distributions) are unrestricted; the prerequisites for remitting dividends to non-resident shareholders are a notarised shareholders' resolution, approved annual financial statements showing distributable profit, and statutory withholding tax of 26.375% inclusive of solidarity surcharge, reducible to 0% under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive for qualifying 10%+ EU corporate shareholders or to treaty rates (5%-15%) under double-tax agreements with the UBO's residence jurisdiction. Current-account flows (export receivables, import payables, royalties, service fees) are free of FX-approval, with the sole compliance duty being Bundesbank statistical reporting for each cross-border payment of at least EUR 12,500. Inbound FDI into the high-speed dipping nitrile-glove manufacturing sector is outside AWV investment-screening scope (Sections 55-60 AWV) because the operation is not classified as critical infrastructure, making Germany a transparent and highly bankable treasury domicile.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory regime: zero surrender requirement. German law imposes no mandatory conversion ratio, no repatriation timeline and no currency nomination for export receipts; the GmbH may retain 100% of USD, CNY or third-currency proceeds indefinitely in foreign-currency accounts held at licensed German banks. The only binding duties are statistical: under AWV Section 67(1), inbound payments for exported goods of at least EUR 12,500 (or equivalent) must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank within five working days of receipt, identifying the exporter's reference, the exact CN commodity code (CN 4015.12 for powder-free sterile nitrile examination gloves), the country of the non-resident payer, the invoice amount and the currency. Payments below the threshold are nil-reported but must remain traceable in the corporate banking archive to defeat pattern-monitoring 'structuring' allegations. The bank's treasury module automatically generates the AWV report from MT103 reference data before the five-working-day deadline.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Frankfurt am Main) is the designated central monetary, statistical and balance-of-payments agent for cross-border payment and foreign-trade reporting under the Außenwirtschaftsgesetz (AWG) and Außenwirtschaftsverordnung (AWV). Banking-conduct, licensing and AML supervision of corporate account relationships rests with the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin), while formal foreign-exchange and foreign-trade policy competence is exercised jointly by the Bundesbank and the Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Climate Action (BMWK).

Turnover: A GmbH is exempt from German statutory audit if it qualifies as a small corporation under HGB § 267(1), i.e. at the balance-sheet date it does not exceed more than one of the following three thresholds: total assets EUR 6,000,000; net turnover EUR 12,000,000; average annual employees 50.
