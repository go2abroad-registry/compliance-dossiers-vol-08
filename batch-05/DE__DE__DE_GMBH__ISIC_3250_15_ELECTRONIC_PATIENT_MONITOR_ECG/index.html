Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

The competent local court (Amtsgericht) acting as Commercial Register (Handelsregister) for the GmbH's registered-office district; filing is transmitted electronically by a German notary.

Governing statute

German Limited Liability Companies Act (Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung, GmbHG), particularly sections 2 (formation and notarization), 3 (articles), 5 (share capital), 7 (registration and capital contributions), 8 (registration documents), 10 (registration particulars), and 11 (pre-registration liability). The Commercial Code (Handelsgesetzbuch, HGB), including sections 8 and 10, governs the Commercial Register.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized application for first registration in the Commercial Register (Anmeldung zum Handelsregister), accompanied by the notarized articles of association or model protocol and statutory supporting documents.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 35 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum stated share capital is EUR 25,000. For a cash formation, before registration at least EUR 12,500 in aggregate must be paid in, and each share must receive at least one quarter of its nominal amount. Contributions in kind must be fully performed before registration and require statutory documentation and valuation disclosures. No general statutory escrow-account requirement applies; funds are ordinarily paid into an account opened for the company in formation and must be available to its managing directors. The stated USD 0 is not a representation that formation is free: German court and notarial fees are payable under statutory fee schedules, vary with transaction particulars, and are invoiced in euros.

Funding & unlock path: Before use, confirm that the premises have the required building-use approval and comply with applicable state building codes, fire-safety conditions, escape-route requirements, and any fire-authority conditions. A separate fire certificate is not universally required and depends on the premises and permit conditions. Assess structural loading and technical installations through competent professionals where required by the building approval or applicable safety rules. Comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Act, Workplace Ordinance, applicable technical rules, and accident-insurance requirements; conduct and document workplace risk assessments and obtain any required inspection of work equipment or installations.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

No single federal industrial licensing board grants a general manufacturing permit for this activity. The competent authority depends on the facility's Bundesland and municipality: the local trade supervisory authority (Gewerbeaufsichtsamt or equivalent) administers workplace and trade controls; the relevant state medical-device market-surveillance authority administers medical-device oversight; and the municipality's building authority handles building approvals. The local trade office administers Gewerbeanmeldung.

Category: No general federal manufacturing operating licence is prescribed solely for assembling patient monitors. Required authorizations depend on the facility and activities. Before placing monitors on the EU market, the manufacturer must comply with Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR), including applicable conformity assessment, CE marking, technical documentation, quality-system, registration, and post-market obligations. A notified body is required where the applicable MDR conformity-assessment route requires one. Applicable German medical-device operator and market-surveillance requirements must also be assessed.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains full convertibility for current account transactions and capital movements by virtue of EU internal market principles and the Foreign Trade and Payments Act (AWG). No exchange control licences are required for the inflow or outflow of funds, and profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are not blocked by FX administration. The only practical hurdles are German withholding tax on dividend distributions at the statutory rate of 26.375% (including solidarity surcharge) unless reduced under a double tax treaty or relieved under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, plus compliance with financial sanctions screening and AML documentation obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany does not impose any mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds; exporters may hold foreign currency balances indefinitely at domestic or foreign banks. Under Sections 67 to 72 of the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (AWV), every cross-border payment received by a German resident, including export receipts, exceeding EUR 12,500 or the equivalent must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank by the fifth working day of the month following the payment, with details of the amount, currency, and the statistical payment code. The statutory mandatory conversion ratio is therefore zero percent; any conversion is at market rates and deemed a treasury decision, not a control regulation.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank and the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht, BaFin)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium as a supplementary clearing rail and Low as the sole treasury account. Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) licensed by BaFin under the German Payment Services Supervision Act (ZAG) may lawfully open German IBANs and provide cross-border payment initiation, but they cannot accept deposits, extend credit, provide overdraft facilities, or hold capital escrow balances on behalf of a GmbH in the manner of a full commercial bank. Offshore EMIs without a German licence or EU passport cannot lawfully provide payment accounts to German residents and must be avoided for any operational treasury function; use of such an EMI would create AML and supervisory exposure.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Non-resident opaque shareholder structure with multi-tier holding companies and no visible economic activity.

Root cause: Non-resident opaque shareholder structure with multi-tier holding companies and no visible economic activity.

Defense evidence: Commercial register extract (Handelsregisterauszug), notarised articles of association, full shareholder register, certified passport copies of all natural person beneficial owners, proof of residential address of each director, tax compliance certificate (Unbedenklichkeitsbescheinigung), and a written beneficial ownership declaration together with a source-of-funds explanation for the underlying shareholder capital.
Medical device export exposure to jurisdictions subject to EU sanctions or dual-use export control scrutiny.

Root cause: Medical device export exposure to jurisdictions subject to EU sanctions or dual-use export control scrutiny.

Defense evidence: Detailed product classification under the German Export Control List, a valid export licence issued by the Federal Office for Economic Affairs and Export Control (BAFA), end-user certificates for the target markets, technology transfer compliance statements, and documentary evidence that patient monitoring hardware is not designed or adapted for military use.
Expected account throughput disproportionate to declared business plan or unusual cross-border counterparty patterns.

Root cause: Expected account throughput disproportionate to declared business plan or unusual cross-border counterparty patterns.

Defense evidence: Comprehensive corporate business plan, signed supplier and customer contracts, purchase orders, anticipated invoice volume forecasts, historical financial statements if available, and a detailed liquidity forecast explaining multi-currency inflows and outflows associated with the ECG patient monitoring production cycle.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital contributions must be routed from the non-resident parent or shareholder to the GmbH's designated capital escrow account at a German commercial bank using SWIFT MT103 or MT202 instructions. The wire must reference an AWV statistical purpose code for equity contribution, for example the code corresponding to 'Erwerb von Gesellschaftsrechten' or 'Einzahlung auf das Eigenkapital einer Gesellschaft', and must include an explanatory payment purpose text such as 'Eigenkapitalzufuhr an GmbH'. The bank must be provided with the notarised share subscription statement (Übernahmeerklärung), the current commercial register excerpt, and a declaration by the managing director confirming that the transferred funds are equity and not trade proceeds; for amounts above EUR 50,000, the receiving bank may require a further anti-money-laundering source-of-funds questionnaire.Trade payments for procurement and supplier settlement shall be executed through SWIFT MT103 single-customer credit transfers for direct payments and SWIFT MT202 or MT202 COV for correspondent bank transfers. For larger component purchases from Asian or non-EU suppliers, the appropriate rail is a documentary letter of credit opened by the German house bank under UCP600 with SWIFT MT700/MT701 advice, or a documentary collection for lower-risk transactions. All outbound disbursements must comply with the AWV reporting thresholds and must state the correct statistical purpose code; payments to blocked or sanctioned counterparties are prohibited. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (Körperschaftsteuer) plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on the corporate tax amount plus municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer, typically 14-17% depending on municipality, average effective combined rate approx. 30-33%)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

26.375% (25% Abgeltungsteuer plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on the WHT amount) for dividends distributed by a German GmbH to individual shareholders; for corporate shareholders the rate is 26.375% if no participation reduction; treaty relief may reduce to 0% for substantial corporate holdings if EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive (85% or 100% participation, two-year holding) conditions apply

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: German TP documentation thresholds (Administrative Principles 2019/2021): Local File required if related-party transactions ≥ €10 million (10% materiality threshold per transaction category) and intercompany service provision ≥ €1 million; Master File required if consolidated group turnover > €100 million and related-party transactions exceed €5 million; documentation must be submitted no later than 60 days after receipt of a tax audit request, or 30 days for extraordinary events (per § 90 (4) AO)

Economic substance scope: Germany does not apply the OECD ESR (Economic Substance Regulations from UAE/Caribbean); however, functional substance is mandatory under the OECD transfer pricing guidelines and German case law: income allocation to a German GmbH requires own employees, premises, decision-making functions (people functions), and entrepreneurial risk capacity; lack of substance for a German distributor/contract manufacturer triggers recharacterisation under § 1 AStG, and for post-BEPS it can lead to a PE attribution and an exchange of information. For ISIC 3250 assembly entity employing engineers, quality staff and calibrators, ensure at least 5-10 skilled medical-device employees, real office/production lease, actual board decisions in Germany, and bear commercial risk; otherwise deemed tax evasion and false declaration risk

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Steuerhinterziehung)

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to €50,000 in tax misdemeanour cases; imprisonment up to 5 years; in aggravated cases using professional money-laundering or false documents, imprisonment from 6 months to 10 years; back taxes and interest up to 6% p.a. from the due date; if a company evades more than €1 million, AG-statute additional enforcement; felony under § 370 AO

Director personal liability: Civil joint and several liability against the GmbH and all directors for unpaid corporation taxes, trade tax, and payroll taxes if the director caused or failed to prevent the offence; criminal conviction leads to personal fines, disqualification from serving as managing director for up to 5 years, travel bans (risk of flight: forfeit passport, issue of international arrest warrant), asset freeze; piercing the corporate veil (Haftungsdurchgriff) when the GmbH is undercapitalised or dirties its corporate separateness; imprisonment of managing director applies to both intentional evasion and gross negligence under § 69 AO; administrator liability under § 69 AO also for customs/export violations
False Declaration and Forgery (Falsche Steuererklärung)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 5 years or fine (criminal §§ 370, 370a AO) for outright false declaration; fine up to €25,000 under § 379 AO for negligent violation of filing duties; automatic 6% interest on assessed underpayments; loss of all tax treaty benefits; tax liabilities may be assessed in full with penalty surcharge of 25% (Zuschlag)

Director personal liability: Director and statutory accountant (Steuerberater) are jointly liable if they signed false declarations; impunity not available due to reliance exemption if the board of directors knew about the falsehood; personal criminal record, travel ban, and seizure of personal assets (both real estate and bank accounts) to satisfy the public treasury's claims
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance (Einkunftskürzung / missbräuchliche Verrechnungspreise)

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment: compulsory upward adjustment of income (positive adjustment), plus 6% interest; late documentation penalty under § 162 AO: from €1,000 up to €1,000,000 (5% of the adjusted increase, cap €1m); in case of failure to submit, 5% of the adjustment amount up to €1m; criminal tax evasion charge? (TP non-compliance itself is a violation but usually chooses a tax assessment penalty); additional penalty for non-submission of specific record-keeping under § 162 (4) AO; German tax court confirms in unilateral adjustment; if the adjustment exceeds €25 million, special internal revenue inspector involvement and public notification of audit findings

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for the documentation penalty if they signed or failed to appoint a tax representative (rd § 69 AO); personal liability extends to interest claims (6% p.a.); in severe cases with deliberate falsification, treat as tax evasion; travel bans, asset seizure; also a legal disqualification from serving as a director in any future GmbH
Shell Company and Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Reclassification as a mere shell; all German-source income attributed to a permanent establishment of the foreign parent, imposing full German taxation on book profits; trade tax on the full attributable profit; additional registration under § 14 AO; in cross-border context, German tax office will apply the German business criteria § 10 AO and refuse treaty protection under the GAAR (General Anti-Avoidance Rules in DTA: Principal Purpose Test), and retrospective assessments with 6% compound interest; for VAT purposes, the shell cannot deduct input VAT and becomes liable for full output VAT on sales with no offsetting recovery; fines for VAT evasion § 26c UStG up to €25,000; criminal prosecution for abuse of legal forms

Director personal liability: Personal liability under § 69 AO for any tax not collected by the shell; civil liability to the tax creditor; criminal charge of bankruptcy fraud (§ 283c StGB if business is insolvent while extracting profits); title embedding of personal assets to cover the tax debt; permanent ban from future board appointments in tax-sensitive industries
Withholding Tax Failure / Dividend WHT Evasion

Statutory sanction: Back-withholding plus late payment interest; fiscal surcharge if not withheld at source; administrative penalty up to €25,000 for negligence and criminal evasion if intentional; possible assessment on the withholding agent personally as a secondary debtor (§ 44 EStG, § 48 EStG); in cross-border setting, treaty shopping refund refused and additional withholding charged; in the EU context, abuse of the Parent-Subsidiary Directive opens the case under ATAD GAAR with retroactive adjustment

Director personal liability: The managing director is personally liable for the full amount of unpaid withholding tax and as a tax collector (Steuerentrichtungspflicht) under § 44 EStG and § 34 AO; also personal criminal liability if the director knowingly failed to withhold; travel ban, European arrest warrant in high-amount cases, and restriction of business licence (GmbH dissolution can be ordered)
AML and Beneficial Ownership Declaration Failure

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine up to €5 million or 2% of annual worldwide turnover for anti-money-laundering obligations (GwG); for transparency register offences: fine up to €1 million plus publication of the offence; with repeated or serious AML breaches, the company may be dissolved and its registration revoked; German customs and BAfA export control enforcement (medical equipment can trigger dual-use, but ECG is not in the control list? The medical device itself does not require export license; however, installed software might trigger EC-rule 2D002/G; if export to China, necessitate general export authorisation or individual licence, bringing export control violation risk; violation under § 19 & § 20 AWG with imprisonment up to 3 years and fines)

Director personal liability: Directors are criminally liable for failing to report UBOs and are subject to civil damages to the State; in severe AML cases, banking relationships are terminated, which collapses the GmbH's payment rails (Swift_LC and TT_Wire); managing directors may personally face seizure of personal assets, confiscation of profits, and prohibition from managing any business for up to 5 years in accordance with § 6 GwG organisational duty breach
