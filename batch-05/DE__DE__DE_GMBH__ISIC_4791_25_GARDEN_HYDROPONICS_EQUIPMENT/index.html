Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices are conditionally permissible for an online retail GmbH that has no physical storefront. The registered seat must satisfy section 4a GmbHG, meaning it must incorporate an actual permanent address in Germany capable of receiving judicial service, official correspondence and tax notifications. A purely dematerialized virtual office without an administrative or correspondence reachable location in Germany is not permissible and will lead to refusal of registration. For the present e-commerce hydroponics equipment business, a German business-center address or a Steuerberater service address is generally accepted, provided the managing director's administrative files and statutory records are available at that address. No formal escrow account is mandated, but a dedicated GmbH formation deposit account is used.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) and local Tax Office (Finanzamt); e-filing portal Elster (www.elster.de)

E-government filing portal

A GmbH obtains a corporate income tax number and VAT registration certificate from the competent local Finanzamt by submitting a Registration Questionnaire for tax purposes (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung), filed electronically via Elster; for VAT ID, application is made to BZSt after local tax registration. Foreign entities without local establishment may register directly with BZSt in certain cross-border situations or through a VAT representative, providing articles of association, proof of residence, and expected turnover.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; resident GmbH is subject to corporate income tax (15%), solidarity surcharge, trade tax and VAT at entity level; worldwide income taxable unless treaty/PE relief applies.

Available election forms

No check-the-box; a GmbH is per se corporation. Elections available include: Organschaft (tax-group) election for corporate income tax and trade tax; small-business VAT exemption under §19 UStG; use of OSS (One-Stop-Shop) for e-commerce; hybrid mismatch elections restricted by ATAD/anti-hybrid rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for resident corporations; mandatory registration upon commencement of business. For intra-EU distance sales of e-commerce goods, the EU-wide threshold is EUR 10,000 of distance sales (OMP), above which German VAT applies to B2C supplies unless OSS is used. Small-business scheme (§19 UStG) is available for entrepreneurs with prior-year turnover up to EUR 22,000 and current-year up to EUR 50,000 (GmbH must verify eligibility).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export supplies to third countries are zero-rated (0%) if the supplier transports/ships goods out of the EU; proof of export must be kept. Intra-Community supplies to EU businesses with valid VAT ID and EC Sales List are zero-rated. Services connected to goods (e.g., freight, handling) may be zero-rated if related to export. For e-commerce exports, the seller must maintain evidence of dispatch from Germany and arrival at non-EU destination.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Ausfuhranmeldung) containing the Movement Reference Number (MRN)
  • Commercial Invoice / Export Invoice with Incoterms and HS codes
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / CMR consignment note
  • Proof of exit from EU Customs Territory (registry receipt or customs release notification)
  • Current export packing list and loading list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit advice) for export proceeds if applicable

Real-time clearance is not yet implemented. Electronically filed VAT returns (Voranmeldungen) and annual VAT declarations via Elster are mandatory. From 1 Jan 2025, German B2B providers must be able to receive structured e-invoices (XRechnung/ZUGFeRD); from 1 Jan 2026, domestic B2B suppliers must issue e-invoices; no digital stamping or API clearance.

Fraudulent or erroneous zero-rating triggers immediate VAT assessment at 19%, plus late-payment interest at 0.15% per month; input VAT deductions are clawed back; tax evasion under §370 AO may result in imprisonment up to five years, unlimited fines, and seizure of assets. Assessments remain enforceable for up to 10 years in case of evasion, 4 years for gross negligence, in line with German General Tax Code (AO).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany maintains full current-account and capital-account convertibility. There is no approval-based exchange-control regime for ordinary treasury operations, no obligatory conversion of foreign currency into EUR, and no restriction on the repatriation of profits or dividends by a GmbH. EU free movement of capital under Articles 63–65 TFEU applies. The only operating constraints are statistical and compliance-orientated: cross-border payments above EUR 12,500 must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank under the AWV, and all treasury flows must remain compliant with EU/UN sanctions and German anti-money-laundering law. Profit remittance from the GmbH is legally unrestricted but remains subject to German withholding tax and any applicable tax treaty or EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive relief at source.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no surrender requirement. A German exporting GmbH is not obliged to convert export invoices into EUR, need not repatriate proceeds to a designated German bank account, and may retain foreign currency indefinitely in correspondent or nostro accounts. The only mandatory element is statistical reporting: any cross-border payment above EUR 12,500 — including export-proceeds receipts — must be filed electronically with Deutsche Bundesbank within the prescribed five-banking-day window using the applicable AWV purpose code. No conversion ratio or timing obligation exists.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank — German central bank, responsible under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung, AWV) for foreign-exchange statistics, cross-border payment reporting and capital-transaction monitoring. Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin) acts as the prudential regulator for banks and financial-market conduct, while BAFA handles any residual export-control licensing.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for operational settlement flows, Low for statutory share-capital deposits. An EEA-regulated e-money institution (EMI) under PSD2 may passport into Germany and provide an IBAN for receivables, supplier payments and treasury operations; offshore non-EEA EMIs without a German branch and no verifiable identity infrastructure are generally unsuitable because they cannot satisfy German GwG (Anti-Money Laundering Act) obligations for GmbH onboarding. Critically, an EMI cannot provide the statutory deposit confirmation required by § 8(2) GmbHG for the EUR 25,000 initial capital of a German GmbH; only a KWG-licensed credit institution is recognized for that purpose. Therefore, the capital escrow account must be a domestic commercial-bank account, while EMI rails can be used only as a secondary liquidity-clearing option.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the GmbH minimum capital, route a SWIFT MT103 wire from the foreign shareholder's bank to the GmbH's dedicated capital account opened at a German KWG-licensed commercial bank. The account must be in the exact legal name of the company, the payment reference must clearly indicate the share-capital contribution, and the bank issues the statutory § 8(2) GmbHG confirmation after final credit. The payment is reportable to Deutsche Bundesbank under the AWV when above EUR 12,500; it must be coded under the capital-transaction/direct-investment purpose category rather than a goods or services code. After GmbH registration, the funds are released for operational use; any early disposal before registration exposes the managing directors to personal liability. For supplementary working-capital injections, use a standard cross-border MT103 credit transfer with the correct statistical purpose code, ensure sanctions screening is clean, and verify that the paying bank's correspondent routing is on an acceptable German/EEA network.Euro-denominated supplier and trade payments settle via SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) using TARGET2/Clearstream infrastructure. Non-EUR disbursements use SWIFT MT103 bank-to-bank transfers with exact beneficiary account and BIC identification. For high-value or documentary import transactions, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (L/C) is opened via SWIFT MT700, advised and confirmed through a German correspondent bank; payments against complying presentations are cleared by accepted drafts or deferred-payment clauses. All outbound trade transfers above EUR 12,500 must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank within the statutory deadline using the applicable AWV payment-purpose code. The commercial invoice value, customs declaration and SWIFT payment should be reconciled exactly.
