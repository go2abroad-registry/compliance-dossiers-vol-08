Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern, BZSt) for the German VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer, USt-IdNr.); the competent local Tax Office (Finanzamt) administers corporate income tax, trade tax, wage tax, and domestic VAT registration. Tax registrations and tax questionnaires are generally submitted electronically through the ELSTER portal.

E-government filing portal

A German GmbH obtains its tax number by submitting the electronic Tax Registration Questionnaire for Corporations (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) through ELSTER to the competent Finanzamt after incorporation and commencement of activities. The application should include the commercial register extract, articles of association, registered address, business activities, managing directors, bank details, expected turnover, accounting period, and beneficial ownership information where requested. The Finanzamt issues the tax number. The GmbH separately applies to the BZSt for a USt-IdNr., normally through the BZSt online application process or through the Finanzamt. A foreign entity without a German establishment must register with the competent Finanzamt if it makes taxable supplies in Germany that are not shifted to the customer under the reverse-charge mechanism; it may apply directly for a German tax number and USt-IdNr. and must provide foreign incorporation, tax residence, business activity, representative, and supply documentation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

A GmbH is generally subject to German corporate income tax at entity level and cannot elect United States-style check-the-box classification. German partnerships may elect corporate taxation under Section 1a of the German Corporate Income Tax Act, but that election does not apply to a GmbH. German hybrid-mismatch rules under Section 4k of the Income Tax Act can deny deductions or tax income where arrangements produce deduction-without-inclusion or double-deduction outcomes. No general hybrid election converts a GmbH into a transparent entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general turnover threshold below which a business making taxable supplies in Germany is exempt from VAT registration. A taxable person generally must register from the beginning of taxable activities, subject to applicable reverse-charge rules and other exemptions. The German small-business exemption under Section 19 of the VAT Act applies where prior-year domestic turnover did not exceed EUR 25,000 and current-year turnover does not exceed EUR 100,000; the exemption is not available where the relevant conditions are not met and does not eliminate registration obligations in all cross-border situations. EU cross-border B2C supplies may instead be reported under the EU One Stop Shop where eligible; the EU-wide EUR 10,000 threshold applies to specified intra-EU distance sales and telecommunications, broadcasting, and electronically supplied services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Germany to destinations outside the European Union may qualify as VAT-exempt with input-tax deduction where the goods are transported or dispatched to a third country and the supplier proves exportation through customs or equivalent documentary evidence. Intra-EU supplies of goods may qualify for VAT exemption where the customer is a VAT-identified business in another EU Member State, the goods are transported to that Member State, and the supplier satisfies reporting and evidence requirements. Cross-border services require place-of-supply analysis rather than a universal zero-rating rule. B2B electronically supplied services generally follow the customer-location rule and may be subject to reverse charge or local VAT. B2C electronically supplied services within the EU generally follow the consumer-location rule and may be reported through the EU OSS. A mobile application distributed through Apple or Google may involve platform-specific deemed-supplier rules, with the platform accounting for VAT on qualifying B2C electronic services while the developer accounts for its supply to the platform under the applicable contractual and place-of-supply rules.

  • MRN and customs export confirmation or equivalent electronic exit confirmation
  • Commercial invoice and transport or dispatch records
  • Bill of lading, air waybill, consignment note, or courier export record, where applicable

Germany does not operate a general real-time VAT invoice-clearance system or mandatory digital tax-stamping regime. From 1 January 2025, German-established businesses must be capable of receiving structured electronic invoices for domestic business-to-business transactions. Mandatory issuance of structured e-invoices is phased in: generally from 1 January 2027 for businesses whose preceding-year turnover exceeds EUR 800,000 and from 1 January 2028 for all affected domestic B2B transactions, subject to statutory transitional rules. EN 16931-compliant formats, including XRechnung and suitable ZUGFeRD versions, are generally accepted. Ordinary PDF invoices are not structured e-invoices. Invoices must satisfy German VAT invoice requirements, including supplier and customer identification, invoice date, sequential number, description, consideration, tax rate, VAT amount, and applicable reverse-charge or exemption references. No routine invoice-by-invoice pre-clearance API with the Finanzamt is required.

Germany has no universal statutory 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation deadline for export VAT relief. VAT exemption or zero-rating may be denied or retrospectively clawed back if export, transport, customer-status, place-of-supply, or platform evidence is incomplete, contradictory, or fraudulent. The taxpayer may be assessed for unpaid VAT, statutory late-payment interest, and administrative penalties. Incorrect VAT returns may trigger additional tax assessments and correction obligations. Intentional or reckless tax evasion can result in criminal prosecution, fines, imprisonment of up to five years for tax evasion, or up to ten years in especially serious cases. Input VAT refunds may be reduced, suspended, or denied where documentary and substantive requirements are not met. False customs declarations may additionally result in customs duties, import or export-related penalties, and criminal liability under applicable customs and tax laws.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany uses the euro and has no general exchange controls. Current-account transactions, including payment for services, royalties, imports, exports, dividends, interest, and ordinary operating expenses, are freely convertible and transferable subject to sanctions, anti-money-laundering, tax, and payment-services controls. Capital-account transactions, including foreign direct investment, capital contributions, intercompany loans, acquisitions, and profit repatriation, are generally unrestricted. A GmbH may remit dividends and other distributable profits to non-resident shareholders after adoption of the annual accounts, satisfaction of German corporate-law distribution requirements, and deduction or treaty-based reduction of withholding tax. Dividend distributions are normally subject to German withholding tax at 25 percent plus the solidarity surcharge, subject to exemptions, tax treaties, or refund procedures. Hidden profit distributions, insufficient distributable reserves, unresolved tax liabilities, sanctions, and deficient beneficial-owner evidence may delay or prevent remittance. Foreign-investment screening under the Foreign Trade and Payments Act may apply to qualifying acquisitions or investments in sensitive sectors; ordinary mobile utility software activity is generally outside mandatory sector-specific screening unless the factual business involves critical infrastructure, defense, security, or other protected activities.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Germany has no mandatory export-proceeds surrender or compulsory conversion regime. Export receipts may remain in euro or foreign-currency accounts and may be converted at the company's discretion. There is no statutory deadline for repatriation or conversion and no mandatory conversion ratio. Payment reporting to the Deutsche Bundesbank under the Foreign Trade and Payments Regulation may apply to qualifying cross-border payments and transactions above the applicable reporting threshold, subject to statutory exemptions and current Bundesbank reporting instructions. These statistical reports are not currency-conversion requirements and do not authorize evasion of sanctions, tax, customs, or AML obligations.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank, within the Eurosystem; prudential and anti-money-laundering supervision is also relevantly exercised by the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for operating payments and collection; Low to Medium for statutory GmbH capital formation, complex treasury, and unrestricted cross-border banking. An EU or EEA electronic-money institution or payment institution may provide IBANs, SEPA transfers, card acquiring, and certain foreign-payment services under passporting or local authorization. It is not a German deposit-taking bank unless separately licensed as a bank; safeguarded e-money is not equivalent to insured deposits. Offshore or non-EEA digital EMIs generally cannot provide regulated payment services in Germany without an applicable authorization, passport, local establishment, or reliance on an authorized provider. The notarial formation process, Handelsregister registration, source-of-funds review, safeguarding model, account-ownership evidence, beneficiary transparency, payment-purpose controls, and platform risk policy may make a German bank preferable for the capital contribution account and core treasury account. Apple and Google settlement accounts should be disclosed with platform agreements, expected volumes, settlement currencies, chargeback exposure, and payment-flow diagrams.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For a GmbH under formation, route paid-in share capital to a bank-designated capital contribution or escrow account in the company's name or formation structure, using the notarized formation deed, articles, shareholder list, managing-director details, and bank-required purpose reference. After registration in the Handelsregister, obtain the bank's release or conversion of the account to the operational account and retain the registration extract and capital-payment confirmation. For post-registration equity, use a documented capital increase, shareholder resolution, notarial deed where required, updated shareholder list, and bank evidence. For shareholder loans, execute a written loan agreement stating principal, currency, maturity, interest, repayment, subordination if relevant, and transfer-pricing basis. Use SEPA Credit Transfer for euro payments and SWIFT or correspondent-bank transfers for non-SEPA currencies. Use accurate payment references such as share capital, capital reserve, shareholder loan, intercompany funding, dividend, royalty, or invoice settlement; do not describe a capital contribution as trade revenue. Maintain remitter-beneficiary consistency, source-of-funds evidence, sanctions screening, invoice or agreement support, and reconciliation to the general ledger. Bundesbank external-payment reporting under the Foreign Trade and Payments Regulation may apply above the applicable threshold, including for certain capital, loan, and service flows; confirm the current threshold, exemptions, and reporting channel before execution.Use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer for eligible euro-area payments, TARGET services through the relationship bank for bank-to-bank euro liquidity, and SWIFT cross-border transfers for non-SEPA currencies or correspondent-bank payments. The legacy SWIFT MT103 format is being replaced or supplemented by ISO 20022 payment messages, principally pacs.008 in bank-to-bank processing; use the format accepted by the originating bank and destination bank. For imports and material supplier obligations, retain purchase orders, invoices, customs documents where applicable, delivery evidence, sanctions screening, and three-way accounting match. A commercial documentary letter of credit may be issued or advised through the relationship bank, using the applicable UCP 600 framework and, for electronic presentation, eUCP where agreed. Documentary collections may use URC 522. For recurring SaaS, cloud, advertising, app-store, and contractor payments, maintain contracts, tax and VAT treatment, withholding-tax analysis, beneficiary verification, and transfer-pricing support where related parties are involved. Bank charges, intermediary deductions, value dates, cut-off times, rejected-payment procedures, and payment repair controls should be documented in the treasury policy.Deliverable forward exchange contracts for forecast or committed foreign-currency payables and receivables, subject to bank suitability, documentation, limits, and confirmation of the underlying exposure.Non-deliverable forwards where the relevant currency, counterparty, and settlement structure are legally and operationally available; settlement is generally in a permitted reference currency rather than physical delivery.Currency swaps and cross-currency swaps for documented funding or balance-sheet exposures, subject to bank credit approval, collateral terms, valuation, and applicable regulatory requirements.Foreign-exchange spot transactions for operational conversion and settlement.Foreign-exchange options and collars for documented exposures, subject to bank approval, product governance, suitability, valuation, and disclosure requirements.Hedging with an authorized EU credit institution or investment firm should be documented under an ISDA or equivalent master agreement where appropriate. EMIR reporting, risk-mitigation, clearing, margin, and intra-group exemptions must be assessed based on the parties, instrument, and classification. The GmbH should avoid speculative dealing unrelated to identifiable business exposures and should maintain hedge designation, exposure forecasts, counterparty limits, confirmations, valuation records, and accounting documentation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium for operating payments and collection; Low to Medium for statutory GmbH capital formation, complex treasury, and unrestricted cross-border banking. An EU or EEA electronic-money institution or payment institution may provide IBANs, SEPA transfers, card acquiring, and certain foreign-payment services under passporting or local authorization. It is not a German deposit-taking bank unless separately licensed as a bank; safeguarded e-money is not equivalent to insured deposits. Offshore or non-EEA digital EMIs generally cannot provide regulated payment services in Germany without an applicable authorization, passport, local establishment, or reliance on an authorized provider. The notarial formation process, Handelsregister registration, source-of-funds review, safeguarding model, account-ownership evidence, beneficiary transparency, payment-purpose controls, and platform risk policy may make a German bank preferable for the capital contribution account and core treasury account. Apple and Google settlement accounts should be disclosed with platform agreements, expected volumes, settlement currencies, chargeback exposure, and payment-flow diagrams.

Statutory sanction: Criminal penalties including fines up to 10 times the evaded tax amount and imprisonment up to 5 years; civil restitution orders

Shell Company
