Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Minimum share capital EUR 25,000; at least EUR 12,500 in cash must be paid into a German blocked escrow account (Gründungskonto) opened for the GmbH in formation before registration. Each cash contribution must as a minimum be one-quarter of its nominal amount, and total cash contributions must reach one-half of the minimum capital. If the company is a single-shareholder GmbH, security must be provided for the unpaid part of contributions. The bank confirmation of payment must evidence that the paid amount is at the undisposed disposal of the managing director.

Funding & unlock path: Office premises must comply with the Landesbauordnung fire-safety rules: functioning emergency exits, extinguishers, smoke detectors where required, and unobstructed evacuation routes. The workplace authority (Staatliches Gewerbeaufsichtsamt) may require adherence to Arbeitsstättenverordnung occupational safety obligations. No structural load inspection is required for standard office occupancy; a structural certificate may be needed if additional server equipment or security vaults are installed.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum share capital EUR 25,000; at least EUR 12,500 in cash must be paid into a German blocked escrow account (Gründungskonto) opened for the GmbH in formation before registration. Each cash contribution must as a minimum be one-quarter of its nominal amount, and total cash contributions must reach one-half of the minimum capital. If the company is a single-shareholder GmbH, security must be provided for the unpaid part of contributions. The bank confirmation of payment must evidence that the paid amount is at the undisposed disposal of the managing director.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern – BZSt) and local tax administrations (Finanzämter), with ELSTER as the statutory federal tax portal

E-government filing portal

A foreign GmbH with German VAT nexus must register with the competent local Finanzamt for a Steuernummer, filing the official registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) through ELSTER; for intra-Community supplies, the entity obtains a Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (German VAT ID) by application to the BZSt using the BZSt-Online-Portal (BOP). In either case, the foreign entity must appoint a national VAT-filing representative if no German-resident controller or permanent establishment is available; the application requires certified incorporation documents, shareholding and director details, and the intended economic activity declarations.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign GmbH with German VAT nexus must register with the competent local Finanzamt for a Steuernummer, filing the official registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) through ELSTER; for intra-Community supplies, the entity obtains a Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer (German VAT ID) by application to the BZSt using the BZSt-Online-Portal (BOP). In either case, the foreign entity must appoint a national VAT-filing representative if no German-resident controller or permanent establishment is available; the application requires certified incorporation documents, shareholding and director details, and the intended economic activity declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists. The relevant German election is Organschaft (fiscal unity): a GmbH may pool corporate income tax and VAT with a controlling German resident entity by satisfying financial, economic, and organizational integration and executing a valid domination and profit-and-loss transfer agreement (Beherrschungs- und Gewinnabführungsvertrag). Trade tax consolidation follows where the subsidiary is integrated under the same conditions. Hybrid-mismatch outcome adjustments are mandatory under §4k EStG and the German ATAD implementation, not elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Small-business exemption under §19 UStG applies when turnover in the previous year did not exceed EUR 22,000 and the current calendar year is projected not to exceed EUR 50,000; however, foreign entrepreneurs with taxable supplies in Germany generally must register for VAT from the first legally effective taxable transaction, unless the reverse charge shifts the tax liability to the German recipient or the transaction is intra-Community distance sales below the EUR 10,000 Union-wide threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of physical goods to non-EU destinations qualify for 0% VAT under §6 UStG if the seller proves that the goods have been removed from the Community (proof of export under §8-10 UStDV). Supplies of services to a third-country entrepreneur are not subject to German VAT where the recipient's place of business is decisive under §3a(2) UStG; B2B supplies to EU entrepreneurs are in principle place-of-supply-shifted and subject to reverse charge. The GmbH must have documentary evidence in its records that the recipient is an entrepreneur and that the service's place of supply is outside Germany.

  • German customs exit confirmation (ATLAS/AES export declaration with Movement Reference Number "MRN")
  • Transport document (chartered bill of lading, airway bill, CMR, or rail consignment note) irrefutably demonstrating physical dispatch after the transaction
  • Commercial invoice, purchase order, and shipping contract evidencing the sales transaction
  • Bank transfer proof or foreign-exchange inward remittance certificate, where the exporter elects to evidence payment pursuant to §8(2) UStDV

Since 1 January 2025, domestic B2B suppliers must issue structured electronic invoices (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.x) as the standard form and all recipients must be able to receive them; the mandate applies to all domestic B2B invoices on 1 January 2028. For public procurement, XRechnung/Peppol BIS formats are mandatory. There is no pre-clearance or digital-stamping tax control system; invoices are archived digitally without real-time validation, and on-demand clearance by Finanzamt remains part of VAT audits.

Zero-rating is examined on documentary proof and may be retrospectively denied where documentation is missing, unverifiable, or contradictory. Rejected export claims trigger VAT reassessment, i.e., the seller becomes liable for the headline 19% VAT on the full invoice amount plus default interest (currently 6% per annum) and monthly late-payment surcharges of 1% on the overdue tax. Intentional fraud can be prosecuted as tax evasion under §370 AO with imprisonment up to 10 years, repeated evasion without reasonable cause attracts penalties under §378 AO; §371 AO self-disclosure can mitigate only before discovery.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Federal Republic of Germany is a euro-area Member State with fully liberalised capital and current-account convertibility. There are no exchange controls or administrative approval requirements for cross-border fund transfers, dividends, royalties, or loan repayments, pursuant to Articles 63-66 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union and Section 1 et seq. of the AWG. Profit remittance by a GmbH is unrestricted, subject only to statutorily imposed statistical reporting (AWV Section 67, threshold EUR 12,500) and compliance with EU/UN sanctions. Outbound dividend payments attract a German withholding tax of 26.375% (25% capital gains tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge) unless reduced to zero or a lower rate under an applicable double-taxation treaty, the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, or the German partial-income system for corporate shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. The German regulatory framework imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory repatriation, and no forced conversion ratio for export receipts. Exporters may retain foreign currency in freely accessible non-resident or domestic foreign-currency accounts. Reporting obligations arise solely under AWV Section 67: cross-border payments received or made by a German resident that exceed EUR 12,500 (or the equivalent) must be reported to the Deutsche Bundesbank by the resident or its collecting bank for balance-of-payments statistics, typically within 2-4 weeks after receipt with the standard AWV code for the underlying transaction.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Französische Straße 16, 10117 Berlin), exercising delegated foreign-trade and payments-statistics administration under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Act (Außenwirtschaftsgesetz – AWG) and Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung – AWV), in coordination with the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin) for banking and payment-services supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium for a low-risk financial-news-feed entity (ISIC 6391). An EEA-licensed e-money institution (EMI) may operate in Germany via the EU passport under the German Payment Services Supervision Act (Zahlungsdiensteaufsichtsgesetz – ZAG) and may facilitate EUR/GBP/USD treasury settlement with reduced costs and faster API integration. However, an offshore non-EEA EMI (e.g., UK or US headquartered) faces an effective licensing barrier: it must provide services through a German agent with prior BaFin authorisation, which is materially unattractive for a GmbH. Furthermore, fintech/EMI balances are not covered by the German statutory deposit-insurance scheme, and the mandated notarial confirmation of the paid-in share capital requires credit proof from an EU credit institution; therefore the EMI route is viable only as a supplementary disbursement rail, never as the primary onshore capital-escrow account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The GmbH's statutory share capital (minimum EUR 25,000 nominal, at least 50% paid in on formation) must be wired via SWIFT or SEPA to a dedicated bank-designated escrow account using reference field 70 with the phrase 'Stammeinlage zur Errichtung der DE_GMBH' and the AWV/Bundesbank statistical purpose code for capital contributions. The originating transfer should be routed from a recognised credit institution, either the shareholder's EU bank account in EUR or, for foreign-currency proceeds, a correspondent-bank conversion into EUR. The bank's credited-value confirmation, together with the notarial filing deed, serves as the proof of payment for the management confirmation and commercial-register registration. Corporate income tax return (Körperschaftsteuererklärung) with reconciliation of statutory balance sheet to taxable income, filed electronically via ELSTER Due 31 July of the calendar year following the tax year; automatically extended to 31 December when prepared by a licensed German tax advisor (Steuerberater); further extension to 31 May of the second following year available on application in most federal states $0 USD Late-filing surcharge (Verspätungszuschlag) under Section 152 AO of 0.25% per month on assessed tax, minimum EUR 25 per month, cap EUR 25,000; tax assessment by estimation (Schätzung) under Section 162 AO; coercive fines (Zwangsgeld); interest of 0.5% per month on arrears under Section 233a AO.
Trade tax return (Gewerbesteuererklärung) for municipal trade tax assessment, filed electronically via ELSTER Due 31 July following the tax year; extension practice identical to corporate income tax (31 December for advisor-prepared returns; case-by-case extension to 31 May of the second following year) $0 USD Late-filing surcharge and estimated assessment under Sections 152, 162 AO; municipal enforcement of assessed trade tax with statutory interest; managing directors risk personal liability for unpaid trade tax of the GmbH in insolvency or liquidation.
Annual value-added tax return (Umsatzsteuererklärung) reconciling VAT advance payments, filed electronically via ELSTER Due 31 July following the calendar tax year; extension to 31 December when prepared by a tax advisor; monthly or quarterly VAT advance returns (Umsatzsteuervoranmeldungen) remain separately due by the 10th day after the end of each period $0 USD Late-filing surcharge under Section 152 AO; late-payment interest of 0.5% per month under Section 233a AO; estimated VAT assessment upon non-filing; criminal liability for intentional VAT evasion or gross misdeclaration.
Transparency Register notification and annual in-file confirmation of ultimate beneficial owners (Wirtschaftlich Berechtigte) Notice must be given immediately and at the latest within 4 weeks of knowledge of any change in beneficial ownership (direct/indirect interest or voting control of more than 25%; details required for tiers >25%, >50%, and >75%); no fixed anniversary filing exists, but the GmbH must annually audit and reconcile its UBO entry against the shareholder list and update the register whenever the entry no longer reflects the legal status $0 USD Administrative fines up to EUR 150,000 per violation under Section 56 GwG, including for outdated, false, or incomplete UBO data; repeated and serious violations can lead to elevated fines and further daily penalty payments.
Shareholder resolution approving the annual financial statements (Feststellung des Jahresabschlusses) and discharge of managing directors, minuted in the corporate minute book Approval within the first 8 months (medium/large GmbH) or 11 months (small GmbH) of the following fiscal year under Section 42a GmbHG $0 USD No direct administrative fine; however, absence of an approved financial statement prevents timely Bundesanzeiger publication and thereby triggers the Section 335 HGB penalty procedure; managing directors additionally face personal liability under Sections 41a and 43 GmbHG for failure to maintain orderly corporate records and to convene the shareholders promptly.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15.0% German corporate income tax (Körperschaftsteuer) plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag) on the corporate tax, equating to 15.825% combined federal/national burden; municipal trade tax (Gewerbesteuer) is superimposed at a statutory tax base of 3.5% multiplied by the municipal collection rate (Hebesatz), typically 400%–490%, adding approximately 14.0%–17.2%, producing a combined full statutory tax-rate corridor of 29.8%–33.0% before dividend withholding considerations.
