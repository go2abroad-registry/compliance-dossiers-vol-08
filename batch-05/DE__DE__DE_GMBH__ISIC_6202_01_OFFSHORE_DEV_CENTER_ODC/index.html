Conditional and restricted: a German GmbH requires a domestic registered office ('Sitz') under §4a GmbHG and must maintain a real, accessible operational address to accept service of process and public notices. Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Handelsregisterabteilung beim zuständigen Amtsgericht (Commercial Register Division of the Local Court) for the registered seat of the GmbH, in electronic coordination with the Advisory Chamber of the notary.

Governing statute

Gesetz betreffend die Gesellschaften mit beschränkter Haftung (GmbHG), principally §§1, 3, 4a, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 39; supplemented by the E-Commerce Regulation (E-Commerce-Verordnung) and Part 1 of the Verordnung über das Handelsregister (Handelsregisterverordnung).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized Anmeldung zur Eintragung einer Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung (Application for Registration of a GmbH in the Commercial Register), submitted electronically by the German notary.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 30 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum nominal share capital is EUR 25,000 pursuant to §5(1) GmbHG. At the date of registration, no less than EUR 12,500 in cash contributions must have been made in full and be unconditionally and irrevocably at the free disposal of the managing director(s) (§7(2) and §8(2) GmbHG). Payment must be documented by a bank confirmation of an unrestricted deposit account held in the name of the GmbH; no formal escrow account is required, but the managing director's unrestricted disposal right is subject to verification by the registry court.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety: certification of compliance with the local Landesbauordnung, including fire alarm and extinguishing systems, escape routes, and a simple fire safety regulation for the leased office floor; the Trade Office and the occupational accident insurer (Berufsgenossenschaft) carry out risk-based inspections. Structural load: for rented office space the building certificate must state an office-rated load class; a structural inspection is required only if the ODC will house a dedicated server room exceeding 500 kg/m² floor load, in which case the board obtains a Statiker (structural engineer) certificate. Occupational clearance: proof of compliance with Arbeitsschutzgesetz (ArbSchG), Arbeitsstättenverordnung (ASchVO), Bildschirmarbeitsverordnung (§§9–10), and company registration with the Berufsgenossenschaft for information technology and telecommunication workers prior to commencing paid work.

Paid-in capital rule: Minimum nominal share capital is EUR 25,000 pursuant to §5(1) GmbHG. At the date of registration, no less than EUR 12,500 in cash contributions must have been made in full and be unconditionally and irrevocably at the free disposal of the managing director(s) (§7(2) and §8(2) GmbHG). Payment must be documented by a bank confirmation of an unrestricted deposit account held in the name of the GmbH; no formal escrow account is required, but the managing director's unrestricted disposal right is subject to verification by the registry court.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable – no industrial licensing board; the relevant general authority is the Gewerbeamt (Trade Office) and the mandatory membership body is the Industrie- und Handelskammer (IHK), which performs public-law oversight of commercial businesses.

Category: No dedicated operating or manufacturing permit is required under §§6, 14, 15 GewO or the 4. BImSchV; a pure offshore software engineering and maintenance service occupies a non-regulated commercial activity. The company must only submit the Gewerbeanmeldung and obtain the IHK membership confirmation as an accessory formality.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt) and the local Finanzamt (tax office). Official portals: ELSTER (https://www.elster.de) for electronic tax registration and filings; BZSt (https://www.bzst.de) for VAT identification number and central tax matters.

E-government filing portal

After notarial incorporation and entry into the commercial register, the GmbH must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) through ELSTER with the competent local Finanzamt within approximately four weeks of commencing business. The Finanzamt issues the Steuernummer (tax number) covering corporate income tax, trade tax and VAT. The separate VAT identification number (Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer) for cross-border and intra-Community transactions must be applied for from BZSt. A foreign parent company must supply a certified commercial register extract, articles of association, proof of the registered office, and identification of the managing directors to complete the procedure.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Germany does not provide a US-style check-the-box election; a GmbH is per se a corporation. Available elections include the Organschaft fiscal unity (Secs. 14 ff. KStG and Sec. 2 Abs. 2 GewStG) requiring a profit-and-loss pooling agreement, majority voting rights and financial integration. Hybrid mismatch rules under Sec. 4k EStG are mandatory and not elective; no pass-through entity classification exists for a GmbH.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis VAT registration threshold for non-resident suppliers: any taxable supply with the place of supply in Germany triggers German VAT. Domestic small-business exemption (Sec. 19 UStG) applies only to German-resident entrepreneurs, with a prior-year turnover ceiling of EUR 22,000 and a current-year ceiling of EUR 50,000. EU cross-border distance sales and digital services are subject to the annual OSS threshold of EUR 10,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically delivered to recipients outside the EU are zero-rated under Sec. 6 UStG when export is evidenced by the customs export declaration, transport documents and supporting trade records. For offshore software development services supplied from Germany to a non-EU foreign business, the B2B place-of-supply rule under Sec. 3a UStG places the supply in the recipient's jurisdiction, making the German supply outside the scope of German VAT; such services are not taxable as German exports, and input VAT on related costs may be deducted by the German entity or recovered by the non-resident business under Sec. 18(9) UStG.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ATLAS/Ausfuhranmeldung)
  • Commercial invoice (Handelsrechnung)
  • Bill of Lading or CMR consignment note or Air Waybill, as applicable
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 bank transfer evidence for export proceeds
  • Export contract with Incoterms and delivery terms
  • Statement of work and place-of-supply evidence for offshore software services (recipient's business establishment and jurisdiction)

From 1 January 2025, all German-resident VAT payers must be able to receive structured electronic invoices. B2B e-invoicing issuance becomes mandatory from 1 January 2027 for high-turnover taxpayers and from 1 January 2028 for all other taxpayers, using XRechnung or ZUGFeRD formats. B2G invoices must already be transmitted via the Peppol network or XRechnung. Germany currently imposes no real-time fiscal clearance, no API-based invoice clearance with the tax authority, and no digital stamping requirement.

The German tax authorities will deny export zero-rating where documentation is incomplete, inconsistent, or fraudulent, and will retroactively assess 19% VAT together with statutory late-payment and under-declaration interest. Knowingly creating false export evidence constitutes tax evasion under Sec. 370 AO, punishable by imprisonment of up to ten years, fines, forfeiture of the tax advantage, and joint and several liability for managing directors and advisers; any input VAT recovery or invoice deduction is simultaneously clawed back.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates a fully convertible current account regime under EU law and the EU Payments Services Directive. Capital account transactions are liberalized with mandatory prior notification to BaFin for direct investments exceeding EUR 1 million; portfolio investments below this threshold face no prior approval. Profit remittances from operational activities are unrestricted for EU-resident entities, subject only to standard AML/KYC verification and German Central Tax Office reporting obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds applies to software services receipts in Germany. All current-account inflows from offshore clients are freely convertible and remittable without statutory timeline or ratio requirements. Banks may request voluntary surrender documentation solely for AML monitoring purposes.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank
