E-government filing portal

Step 2: File the business registration (Gewerbeanmeldung) with the local trade office (Gewerbeamt), which automatically notifies the competent local Finanzamt. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Germany has no US-style 'check-the-box' or pass-through election. A GmbH is statutorily opaque and tax-resident where its statutory seat or place of effective management is located; it bears unlimited tax liability on worldwide income at entity level (15% corporate income tax plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge on that tax, plus municipal trade tax at effective rates of approximately 14% to 17%, yielding an aggregate income-tax-equivalent burden of roughly 23% to 30%). Available statutory elections include: (1) Organschaft (fiscal unity) — a German GmbH may elect to be consolidated for VAT purposes under Sec. 2(2) No. 2 UStG and for corporate income tax/trade tax under Sec. 14 KStG with the parent company, provided financial, economic and organizational integration exists and, for income tax purposes, a valid profit-and-loss transfer agreement (Ergebnisabführungsvertrag) is concluded; (2) the small-business exemption (Sec. 19 UStG) is NOT available to a GmbH, only to natural persons and certain partnerships; (3) group approaches to financing structure are governed by anti-hybrid rules under Sec. 4k EStG (implementing ATAD), with no elective opt-outs; a GmbH cannot elect transparency and, for foreign tax classification, German law follows the local entity's legal form (per se corporation).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible in both current and capital account transactions; Germany maintains no general foreign-exchange control regime. Cross-border payments and profit remittances are unrestricted, subject to (i) mandatory balance-of-payments reporting to Deutsche Bundesbank for payments exceeding EUR 12,500 per transaction, (ii) corporate-law capital maintenance and distribution rules under GmbHG Sections 30 and 31, and (iii) dividend processing subject to German withholding tax (25% Abgeltungsteuer plus 5.5% solidarity surcharge) modifiable under applicable double-taxation treaties. Profit remittance hurdles are therefore administrative and tax-related, not exchange-control-related.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Germany imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts; proceeds may be retained in EUR or foreign currency and held in onshore or offshore accounts. Only ex-post statistical reporting applies: cross-border export payment receipts exceeding EUR 12,500 must be reported to Deutsche Bundesbank under AWV Section 67 by the seventh day of the month following receipt, in electronic form via the Bundesbank External Transactions Reporting portal.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (German Central Bank), exercising external-sector statistical and monetary reporting functions under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung – AWV); prudential banking supervision is vested in the Federal Financial Supervisory Authority (BaFin).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

High operational viability for disbursement-side payment aggregation and FX conversion via EEA-authorized electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise Europe SA, passporting into Germany under PSD2; Medium-to-Low as a substitute for the primary treasury bank. Funds held with an EMI are not covered by German statutory deposit insurance under the Deposit Guarantee Act (Einlagensicherungsgesetz), and settlement of stock purchases, customs duties, and VAT requires a domestic credit-institution account. Recommended structure: Tier-1 German bank as principal treasury account and EMI rails for TikTok Shop payouts and small-ticket cross-border conversions.
