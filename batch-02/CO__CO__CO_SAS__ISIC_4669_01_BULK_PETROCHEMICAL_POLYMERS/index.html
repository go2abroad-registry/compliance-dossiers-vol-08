Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) - Colombian National Tax and Customs Directorate. Institutional web portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; electronic taxpayer platform (MUISOCA) for RUT registration, NIT issuance, and e-invoicing: https://muisca.dian.gov.co.

E-government filing portal

Every Colombian entity or foreign entity with Colombian tax obligations must enrol in the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) and obtain a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which serves as both the income tax ID and the VAT ID. For a Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.): (1) incorporate the S.A.S. by private deed filed with the Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio), which simultaneously processes the initial RUT opening; (2) complete the electronic RUT pre-registration via the MUISOCA platform or at a DIAN office, stating the primary ISIC activity 4669 and the taxpayer's exporter status; (3) obtain the NIT and the electronic RUT certificate. A non-resident foreign entity without a permanent establishment that still has Colombian VAT obligations (e.g., taxable digital services rendered to Colombian residents, or acting as a consignor/seller where the local buyer cannot self-invoice) must register through a general attorney-in-fact in Colombia holding a notarized and authenticated power of attorney; DIAN then assigns a foreign-entity NIT and records the status 'responsable del IVA' in the RUT.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A Colombian S.A.S. is a resident legal entity automatically taxed at entity level on its worldwide income under the general corporate income tax regime (35% statutory rate for tax year 2025, plus the 10% surcharge applicable only to certain extractive/energy sectors, which does not apply to petrochemical wholesale trading). Pass-through treatment is not available; there is no Colombian federal/state dual taxation and no preferential entity classification for a standard S.A.S.

Available election forms

Colombian law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election for domestic business entities. A Colombian S.A.S. is mandatorily an income tax payer and VAT responsible party. Available statutory elections: (i) the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (SIMPLE) election under Estatuto Tributario Articles 903-916, available to eligible small taxpayers (legal entities with annual gross income up to approximately COP 3,500 million), which consolidates income tax, VAT, ICA and other taxes into a progressive rate on gross income; (ii) the election by a non-resident provider of digital services to register with DIAN and collect VAT on services supplied to Colombian consumers; (iii) the election by a foreign entity to maintain a branch (establecimiento permanente) and file under a Colombian NIT through a resident legal representative; (iv) the exporter's election to apply the proportional VAT-credit methodology for the refund of VAT borne on export inputs; (v) no hybrid-mismatch election exists: Colombia's anti-hybrid rules introduced by Law 2277 of 2022 operate automatically to disallow double deductions and to include the corresponding income. A Zona Franca (free trade zone) regime, if elected, provides a reduced 20% CIT rate and VAT exclusion on eligible transactions, but the input file does not place the S.A.S. in a Zona Franca.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

COP 0 (no de minimis economic turnover threshold). Under Article 437 and related provisions of the Estatuto Tributario, any person or legal entity engaged in the sale of goods or rendering of taxable services in Colombian territory, or which imports goods, is mandatorily a VAT responsible party subject to RUT registration from the first taxable transaction. A wholesale S.A.S. trader cannot elect out of the general VAT system; however, an eligible micro-business may instead be covered by the SIMPLE regime, which charges a consolidated rate that includes a VAT component. For imports of polyethylene/polypropylene granules, DIAN assesses VAT at customs (19%) at the time of importation, regardless of the importer's registration status.

Colombia has a mandatory electronic invoicing regime (facturación electrónica) applicable to all VAT responsible persons. Invoices must be issued as 'factura electrónica de venta' in XML (UBL 2.1) through DIAN-authorized technology providers using DIAN's web services (API) integrated with the MUISOCA platform. Each invoice must contain a unique CUFE or CUDS code (digital stamp/QR hash) and must be transmitted instantaneously to DIAN; the invoice is legally valid only after the platform returns the validation envelope (aceptación). The electronic invoice must be delivered to the buyer, and all transactions must be reported to DIAN in near real time. For taxpayers classified as high fiscal risk or engaging in designated wholesale fuels/trading activities, DIAN Resolution 000165 of 2023 and related rules impose 'validación previa' - a real-time fiscal clearance that requires DIAN's approval of the invoice before the transaction can be released to the buyer. Documentation-support equivalents ('documento equivalente electrónico') are required for purchases from persons not obliged to invoice.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia applies a managed-float regime with full convertibility on the free exchange market for current-account transactions and for capital-account flows that are registered with Banco de la República. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted in principle, but every conversion must be executed through an IMC and declared on Formulario No. 5; foreign direct investment (equity into an S.A.S.) enters under Segment 45 (International Investments) and must be registered without prior exchange-control authorization. Dividend and capital-repatriation remittances are permitted after registration and after certification that Colombian corporate tax and the 10% dividend-withholding levy (when applicable) have been settled. The operative hurdles are documentary and procedural: mis-coded declarations, late Form 5 filings or unregistered shareholdings trigger sanctions under the exchange-control regime, but the regime does not impose quantitative caps or discretionary rationing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Resolution 8 of 2000 (as amended), 100% of merchandise export proceeds must be channeled through the official exchange market by sale to an IMC or credit to a local foreign-currency account within the statutory period, generally six (6) months from the date of the transport document for non-South American destinations, with shorter periods for Andean-region trade; no fixed surrender ratio exists because conversion occurs at the free-market rate. Proceeds must be matched to the corresponding export customs declaration (Declaración de Exportación) within the same window. For the petrochemical trading S.A.S., all documentary-credit (LC) and open-account export collections must be received via SWIFT MT103 into the Colombian account and declared under Formulario No. 5 within the channeling term; failure to channel within the deadline constitutes the exchange-control violation of non-canalización and attracts fines and DIAN enforcement.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (central bank) and Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (financial supervisor). Foreign-exchange regulation is governed by Law 9 of 1991, Banco de la República Resolution 8 of 2000, the DCIN-83 regulatory handbook, and the DIAN customs framework; all conversions and cross-border payments must be channeled through duly authorized foreign-exchange intermediaries (Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario, IMCs) and declared on Formulario No. 5 (Declaración de Cambio).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Restricted / Low viability. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Payoneer, Mercury) are not registered Colombian IMCs and cannot lawfully serve as the primary treasury hub because exchange-control rules oblige all import, export and capital flows to be channeled through an IMC and declared on Formulario No. 5. An offshore EMI account is only tolerable as a non-native receive-only auxiliary wallet for minor receivable collections, and its use for supplier settlements or export collections creates channeling breaches, DIAN customs reconciliation problems and enhanced UBO/AML risk. For this Colombian S.A.S., on-boarding at a tier-1 local bank is the only compliant clearing path

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires a SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident parent or shareholder to the S.A.S.'s COP or USD account at the chosen IMC, with Field 70 Remittance Information stating 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / APORTE DE CAPITAL SEGUN ACTA No. X — INVERSION EXTRANJERA DIRECTA' and Field 72 stating 'FORM 5 SEGMENT 45 — REGISTRO BANCO DE LA REPUBLICA'. Simultaneously with the credit or conversion, the IMC files the Formulario No. 5 (Declaración de Cambio, Segment 45 – International Investments, Concept 4501: subscription of shares and capital contributions), which perfects the foreign-investment registration. Backup documentation includes the share-issuance minutes, updated company bylaws, RUT and NIT, good-standing certificates, and the UBO forms. The foreign investment must be registered with Banco de la República within two (2) calendar months after perfection of the contribution; no prior exchange-control license is required for equity, but the ex-post registration is mandatory and the State reserves the right to deny channeling if the underlying shareholder is not identified.Supplier settlements for bulk polyethylene and polypropylene imports are executed via (i) direct SWIFT MT103 transfers for open-account trade, or (ii) Irrevocable Documentary Letters of Credit issued by the Colombian IMC (MT700 issue, MT707 amendments, MT202/MT202 COV cover reimbursements) under UCP600 and URR725. Advance payments for imports require a secured mechanism (LC or guarantee letter issued by a bank, per Resolution 8 of 2000) and strict reconciliation with the final customs declaration. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid to non-resident shareholders from profits already taxed at corporate level; 15% if distributed from earnings not previously taxed (source wages or untaxed income); treaty reductions may lower 10% to 5%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: COP 4,706,500,000 (100,000 UVT for tax year 2024) in gross income or gross assets; transactions with entities in preferential tax regimes (tax-haven blacklist) trigger mandatory full TP documentation irrespective of value.

Economic substance scope: Colombia has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations; however, corporate tax residence is based on incorporation or effective management in Colombia, and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR, ET Art. 869) empowers DIAN to recharacterize shell arrangements lacking real local office, personnel, storage, and decision-making in metal/polymer wholesale trading; financial indicators must reflect actual economic activity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Underreporting Gross Income

Statutory sanction: DIAN correction: liquidación oficial with sanctions for inaccuracy of 100% of omitted tax; criminal evasion penalties under Colombia Criminal Law for amounts above 200 UVT: imprisonment from 4 to 9 years and fine of up to 60% of evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives can face criminal fraud and tax-evasion charges; a court may pierce the veil when the S.A.S. is a mere conduit, imposing civil restitution and travel bans.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Mandatory TP adjustment: additional tax plus penalty of 100% of the untaxed excess margin, annually; DIAN can ignore invoices from related providers without TP documentation; reiterative penalties for omission of supporting Form 120.

Director personal liability: CFO and tax manager personally liable for negligent signing of false certification under ET Art. 571; criminal liability for document falsification; disqualification to manage corporate affairs in Colombia.
Trade-Based Money Laundering and AML Violations

Statutory sanction: UIAF (Financial Intelligence Unit) sanctions: fine up to 1,000× minimum monthly wage applicable; criminal anti-money-laundering law (Art. 323) imposes imprisonment of 8 to 15 years and fines of 100-1,000 minimum wages; exclusion from banking and trade corridors.

Director personal liability: Banking and AML legal framework holds the legal representative and beneficial shareholders personally accountable; travel bans, asset freezing, and up to 20 years of incarceration for organized crime involvement in bulk LC fraud.
Dividend WHT Evasion and Artificial Loan Surrogation

Statutory sanction: DIAN recharacterization as dividends: additional 10%-15% WHT plus 5% per month late payment penalty up to 200% of the unwithheld tax; disallowance of the deductible payment and refusal to admit the DTA when no TRC is present.

Director personal liability: The S.A.S. becomes jointly liable for withheld but unremitted amounts; directors can be personally obligated to pay the corporation's tax debt when they actively use corporate assets for personal benefit; criminal conviction for fraudulent retention up to 5 years, plus prohibition from serving as corporate director in Colombia.
