Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None under the Taiwan Company Act for a WFOE limited company; however, the Financial Supervisory Commission imposes a prudential paid-in capital expectation of at least TWD 20,000,000 (approximately USD 645,000) for a securities proprietary trading entity. The capital must be remitted into a Taiwan bank after the MOEA Investment Commission foreign investment approval is granted, evidenced by a bank capital verification certificate, and the inward remittance must be cleared by the Central Bank of Taiwan. The amount is not a blocked escrow account under the Company Act, but it remains conditionally restricted by the foreign investment remittance approval and cannot be repatriated or distributed until the FSC license is obtained and tax no-objection clearance is issued.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for civil-defense industrial permits; however, the Company must obtain a fire-safety inspection certificate from the Taipei City Fire Department for the registered office and co-location space, a valid building occupancy and structural load certificate from a licensed engineer for rack installations, and occupational safety and health compliance under the Taiwan Occupational Safety and Health Act for all employees.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, National Taxation Bureau (regional National Taxation Bureaus) is the registration and collection authority; electronic portals: MOF Tax Portal at tax.nat.gov.tw and MOF Electronic Invoice Integration Platform at e-invoice.nat.gov.tw

E-government filing portal

1) Establish Taiwan tax nexus: fixed place of business, dependent agent concluding contracts, or taxable sales of goods/services in Taiwan. 2) Appoint a Taiwan resident tax/accounting agent and, for the WFOE/branch form, register the legal presence with the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA). 3) Compile the notarised parent incorporation certificate, constitutional documents, board resolution appointing the local managing officer, identity credentials, Taiwan fixed-place address proof, Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) licence for algorithmic market-making, and AML/KYC compliance evidence. 4) File the tax registration application (Application for Tax Registration / Unified Business Number) with the governing National Taxation Bureau via the MOF Tax Portal or in person; a foreign digital-service supplier without a fixed place may use the dedicated foreign e-commerce VAT registration module. 5) On approval the authority issues the eight-digit Unified Business Number (TIN), which functions as both the Business Tax (VAT) identifier and the profit-seeking enterprise income-tax registration number. 6) Complete Uniform Invoice registration and apply for the e-invoice digital certificate and Turnkey transmission account. 7) For pure offshore electronic services to Taiwan individuals, registration is required only if annual taxable sales exceed NT$480,000. 8) Keep the TIN registration file current and file monthly/quarterly indirect tax returns according to the assigned filing cycle.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None (Taiwan does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election). Procedural options only: a small-scale business operator may elect to become a general VAT taxpayer to claim input credits, and a foreign electronic-service supplier may elect the simplified VAT registration route; neither election alters entity classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for resident/in-residence businesses: registration is mandatory before commencing any taxable sale in Taiwan. Small-scale operators with average monthly sales below NT$80,000 for goods or NT$40,000 for services use a simplified quarterly assessment and do not issue Uniform Invoices; foreign suppliers of electronic services to Taiwan individuals must register when annual sales exceed NT$480,000. A licensed financial/securities entity may instead fall under the 2% non-value-added business tax on gross revenue under Article 11 of the Business Tax Act.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act to goods physically exported from Taiwan and to services supplied to foreign enterprises or individuals where the services are used outside Taiwan or are incidental to exported goods; international transport and certain offshore services also qualify. For algorithmic market-making, export zero-rating can be claimed only if the services are sold to a foreign counterparty and are not treated as used in Taiwan; purely proprietary trading on the WFOE's own account is not an exported service and creates no zero-rated output VAT. Excess input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports may be refunded under the VAT refund provisions rather than carried forward.

  • Customs Export Declaration filed through the Customs Administration of the Ministry of Finance
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or carrier/forwarder cargo receipt evidencing physical departure from Taiwan
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the Customs Export Declaration
  • Export sales contract, purchase order or market-making service agreement with the foreign counterparty
  • Foreign exchange receipt / inward remittance certificate issued by an authorised bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Service completion or delivery records demonstrating that the service was performed outside Taiwan for zero-rated exported services

Taiwan operates a mandatory Uniform Invoice regime rather than a continuous real-time fiscal pre-clearance system. Standard VAT taxpayers must transmit invoice data through the MOF Electronic Invoice Integration Platform (e-invoice.nat.gov.tw) using the e-invoice Turnkey API and a MOF digital certificate; each e-invoice carries a unique invoice number and random anti-fraud code validated on the platform. B2C e-invoices are stored in the cloud with the MOF verification seal; paper Uniform Invoices are likewise reported through the same information pipeline. Small-scale businesses are exempt from issuing Uniform Invoices but must submit prescribed purchased/sales reports. Failed transmissions, stale invoices, or non-matching TINs expose the taxpayer to rejection and penalty risk.

The National Taxation Bureau will compare the Customs Export Declaration, transport documents, contract terms and bank remittance records. If any statutory document is missing, inconsistent or non-contemporaneous, the zero-rating is disallowed and the exporter is assessed 5% output VAT on the transaction with interest. Intentional falsification of customs or foreign-exchange documents triggers Article 41 criminal liability for tax evasion under the Business Tax Act, Article 51 administrative fines of up to five times the evaded tax, possible business suspension, and retroactive clawback of any refunded VAT within the statutory collection period (7 years for fraud/non-filing cases). Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day deadline, absent an approved extension from the central bank, may also cause the zero-rating to be revoked.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime under PBOC oversight. Capital account transactions remain strictly controlled and require prior SAFE approval for inflows, outflows, equity investments, and most cross-border capital movements. Current account transactions are generally convertible, enabling profit remittances, dividends, and trade settlements subject to corporate income tax clearance and supporting documentation. Profit repatriation faces no material convertibility hurdles once tax obligations are met.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mandatory surrender of 30% of foreign exchange export proceeds to the bank within five working days for eligible trade transactions, with possible extensions upon justification. Proprietary algorithmic trading activities fall outside surrender obligations unless reclassified as export-related.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability; Chinese regulations prohibit unlicensed offshore electronic money institutions for domestic entities. Cross-border EMIs require SAFE filings and domestic licensed channels with full AML/KYC compliance.
