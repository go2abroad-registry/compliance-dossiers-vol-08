Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA) via its Electronic Tax Bureau online portal (etax.chinatax.gov.cn) and the local in-charge tax service offices; the Unified Social Credit Code system administered by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) serves as the primary tax identifier.

E-government filing portal

For the Chinese WFOE: after SAMR registration, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code is automatically the Chinese Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); the WFOE must complete tax registration information confirmation with the in-charge STA office through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days after the business license issue date, then register for VAT as either a small-scale or general taxpayer, register for corporate income tax (CIT), payroll/environmental/culture-related levies if applicable, and open a bank settlement account. For a foreign non-resident entity without an establishment or place in China that earns Chinese-source income, the payer/withholding agent must file withholding registration using the non-resident's foreign TIN and supporting contract documents with the STA; where the non-resident needs to issue invoices or reclaim input VAT, it must apply for a temporary tax identification number/temporary tax registration through the in-charge tax authority.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed by the PRC on its worldwide income; no pass-through treatment for CIT or VAT). — For the Chinese WFOE: after SAMR registration, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code is automatically the Chinese Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); the WFOE must complete tax registration information confirmation with the in-charge STA office through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 days after the business license issue date, then register for VAT as either a small-scale or general taxpayer, register for corporate income tax (CIT), payroll/environmental/culture-related levies if applicable, and open a bank settlement account. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed by the PRC on its worldwide income; no pass-through treatment for CIT or VAT).

Available election forms

None; China does not operate a check-the-box entity classification regime or hybrid mismatch classification election under the Enterprise Income Tax Law. For VAT, the WFOE may elect to be treated as a general VAT taxpayer if its annual taxable turnover is below RMB 5,000,000 and it maintains sound accounting records, or conversely may remain a small-scale taxpayer where eligible.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration threshold; below that, small-scale taxpayer status applies and a monthly VAT-exemption cap of RMB 100,000 turnover is currently available; all active business taxpayers must nevertheless complete tax registration before commencing operations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For tangible goods, the exporter must remove goods from China under an export customs declaration and complete the export sale to an overseas buyer. For services, 0% VAT is available only for listed cross-border taxable services under Finance and Taxation [2016] No. 36 Annex 4 and STA Announcement No. 29 of 2016, supplied to overseas companies/individuals and consumed entirely outside China. The financial newswire/data-feed supply may qualify as a cross-border information/information-system service if the recipient is overseas, the subscription content is not consumed by a Chinese establishment, the payment is received in foreign currency, and the service contract, performance record, and bank FX receipts are retained.

  • Export Customs Declaration (Export Goods Declaration Form) for physical shipments
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill evidencing actual export from China
  • Commercial invoice, export sales contract and packing list
  • Bank foreign-exchange settlement/collection certificate or inward remittance certificate from the accepting bank
  • For cross-border digital services: signed cross-border service contract, overseas recipient identity/qualification documentation, performance and usage logs showing overseas consumption, zero-rated e-invoice, and foreign-currency receipts from the SWIFT/Stripe payment rails
  • Export tax rebate application file with the VAT general taxpayer election record

The STA Golden Tax IV platform requires issuances through the national Electronic Invoice Platform or approved e-invoice service providers using the taxpayer's digital certificate; electronic invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near real time, red-letter (credit note) invoices must be reported, and fully digital electronic invoices carry a unique invoice code and digital stamp for anti-fraud matching. Special VAT invoices are integrated with the STA's automated cross-check of purchase-side credit claims, sales-side declarations, and export customs records; there is no separate per-invoice pre-clearance approval for ordinary VAT invoices.

If the STA determines that zero-rating/export refund documentation is false, incomplete or not timely collected, it will claw back the refunded/discharged VAT, impose a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% on the unpaid VAT, and impose an administrative penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid tax under the Tax Collection Administration Law. Fraudulent export declarations and false export-rebate applications are treated as criminal offences under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law, exposing controllers to imprisonment and fines; the STA may also apply a 10-year retrospective clawback period for fraudulent tax evasion, refer the taxpayer to a tax dishonesty blacklist, and restrict exit of legal representatives and responsible personnel.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating RMB regime under PBOC/SAFE. Current-account convertibility is effectively full after banks pass a documentary authenticity review, meaning the WFOE may receive foreign currency for newswire subscription fees, convert at market rates, and remit service import payments without pre-approved quotas. The company must file balance-of-payments (BOP) declarations and keep contracts, invoices, tax payment records, and settlement trace logs. Capital-account convertibility is restricted. The Taiwan shareholder's equity contribution must first pass SAFE FDI information registration, then be wired to a designated Capital Account, and subsequently applied only to permitted operating purposes (no securities, entrusted loans, or prohibited negative-list investments). Repatriating profits requires a statutory audit, enterprise income tax settlement, board declaration, and bank verification; absent those, the remittance is blocked. RMB trade settlement can flow through CIPS, but free capital-account cross-border RMB movement remains limited. Overall, the WFOE can achieve operational FX liquidity, while its strategic capital stock remains regulated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory physical surrender ratio applies to export collection: 0% required conversion. However, the full amount of export proceeds must be repatriated to the PRC banking system and credited to the onshore foreign-currency current account by the contractual payment due date; offshore retention is prohibited without SAFE approval. Any balance above the bank-approved retention cap must be converted into RMB or transferred out under documentary review. For goods trade, if the receivable is not collected within 90 days after customs declaration, the WFOE must file a delayed collection report in the SAFE Trade Monitoring System. For digital news service receipts that are not goods-related, the repatriation trigger follows the invoice due date and the same delayed-reporting mechanism is used where the bank expects prompt inward settlement.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low and legally constrained. Under PBOC payment-institution rules, offshore digital EMIs are not permitted to open domestic sovereign settlement accounts, perform onshore RMB/foreign-currency conversion, or act as the WFOE's primary treasury rail. Fintech EMIs such as Airwallex, Revolut Business, or Stripe Treasury may be used only as front-end collection or gateway software, with all funds ultimately swept into a licensed Chinese bank account through SWIFT or domestic clearing. Using an EMI as the custody or FX execution layer would expose the entity to unlicensed payment activity and blocked settlement.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% (CIT Law Article 37, gross withholding at source on dividends remitted to non-resident shareholders); no additional PRC business tax or surtax on dividend repatriation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be lodged within five months after the fiscal year-end (on or before 31 May for calendar-year taxpayers); quarterly provisional returns are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter, and any balance of CIT payable must be settled by the annual deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory when the annual amount of related-party transfers of tangible property reaches or exceeds RMB 200 million; intangible property transfers reach or exceed RMB 100 million; financial asset transfers reach or exceed RMB 100 million; or related-party service transactions reach or exceed RMB 40 million. Master File mandatory when the enterprise conducts cross-border related-party transactions and the group's prior-year consolidated revenue reaches or exceeds RMB 1 billion. Country-by-Country Report mandatory when the MNE group's consolidated revenue reaches or exceeds RMB 5.5 billion in the prior fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not operate a sectoral UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations regime, but economic substance is enforced through (i) the General Anti-Avoidance Rule in CIT Law Article 47 permitting re-characterisation of any arrangement without a reasonable commercial purpose; (ii) the special tax adjustment rules in SAT Announcement 2017 No. 6 which require a substance-over-form analysis of the WFOE as a tested party; and (iii) the beneficial-ownership test in SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9, under which the Taiwan shareholder must carry on substantive business activities. The WFOE must maintain a mainland office, editorial newsroom staff, local data-centre control, independent pricing and contracting functions, assume credit and operational risk, and record board decision-making in China so that the Core Income-Generating Activities of the syndicated financial newswire business are attributable to the Chinese entity and not to a shell parent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by Revenue Omission and Offshore Parking

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63 and 64 impose a fine of 50%-500% of the evaded tax, a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, asset seizure and publication of the violation; Criminal Law Article 201 punishes aggravated evasion with imprisonment of not more than seven years and an additional criminal fine of up to five times the evaded amount.

Director personal liability: A director who orchestrates the concealment is personally liable for restitution of the evaded tax and penalties; the court may issue an exit ban under Article 44 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, freeze personal assets, impose travel restrictions on the grounds of a serious tax violation, and, upon criminal conviction, sentence the director to up to seven years' imprisonment, disqualification from corporate management, and inclusion in the nationwide dishonesty blacklist.
Shell-Company Treaty Abuse and Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 2017 No. 6 authorise the tax bureau to ignore the conduit, re-characterise the structure, deny treaty benefits, reclassify non-deductible interest or licence fees as deemed dividends, and re-impose the full 10% WHT with a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge and fines under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63; a false beneficial-ownership declaration may additionally be punished under anti-money-laundering statutes.

Director personal liability: Directors of both the China WFOE and the Taiwan conduit may have the corporate veil pierced for civil recovery of the unpaid tax and interest, be blacklisted as serious dishonest judgment debtors with exit bans, face personal asset freezing, and be prosecuted for fraud or tax evasion where they knowingly signed false residency certificates or beneficial-ownership statements, carrying a maximum seven-year custodial sentence.
Thin Capitalisation and Transfer Pricing Manipulation on Intercompany Debt

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 46 and Cai Shui [2008] No. 121 disallow interest on the excessive debt portion, recharacterise it as a deemed dividend distribution subject to 10% WHT, and permit a secondary transfer-pricing adjustment with compound interest at the benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points; under SAT Announcement 2017 No. 6 a special tax adjustment may be accompanied by a fine of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false loan agreements or inflated interest invoices are jointly and severally liable for the disallowed amount and the deemed-dividend WHT, may be placed on the personal tax enforcement blacklist with exit and travel bans, subjected to asset seizure, and criminally prosecuted for fraudulent invoicing or tax evasion if the manipulation is deliberate, with imprisonment exposure of up to seven years.
