Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — National Tax and Customs Directorate of Colombia. Portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional platforms: MUISCA (muisca.dian.gov.co) for RUT, export declarations and refunds; Gestión Electrónica (gestion.dian.gov.co) for electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica). Complementary authorities: Banco de la República (foreign-exchange/expatriation regime) and the Cámara de Comercio / Confecámaras (RUES commercial registry used to trigger initial NIT issuance).

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the S.A.S. digitally before the Chamber of Commerce under Law 1258 of 2008; no minimum capital is required (a nominal one-peso subscription is accepted) and 100% foreign ownership is permitted. 2) Obtain the provisional NIT automatically through the Chamber's integration with DIAN (Ventanilla Única Empresarial). 3) Complete the mandatory Registro Único Tributario (RUT) filing before DIAN (virtual appointment or in-person), electing the statuses Responsable de IVA (régimen común), income-tax payer, withholding agent, and specifying the CIIU 1074 economic activity and export-registration data. 4) Secure a DIAN-accepted digital signature certificate (e.g., Certicámara, GSE) to execute filings and e-invoices. 5) Appoint the legal representative in the RUES and, if balance-sheet thresholds of Art. 203 C.Com. are met, a revisora fiscal. 6) Open a local bank account, enroll a foreign-exchange intermediary for export reintegro, and ensure the RUT export flag is active before filing the first DEX.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The Colombian-resident S.A.S. is taxed as a standalone corporation at a 35% headline income-tax rate for FY2024/FY2025 (Art. 240 ET; Law 2277 of 2022), without pass-through. Territorial_Scope: a modified worldwide regime — Colombian-source income is taxed; foreign-source dividends and capital gains are exempt when derived from profits already taxed at comparable levels abroad (Art. 235-2 ET). Export sales of goods manufactured in Colombia (ISIC 1074/NAICS 311412 frozen dumplings) are Colombian-source. Tax_Exempt_Zone: an authorized Zona Franca Permanente Especial (single-user) can cut the corporate rate to 20% and grant VAT/customs suspension for the factory's imports of inputs and machinery.

Available election forms

Colombian law does not provide a check-the-box or analogous entity-reclassification election; hybrid and reverse-hybrid outcomes are neutralized automatically by the OECD/BEPS-aligned rules (Arts. 960-1 to 960-9 ET), which are mandatory. Available elections: (i) Opt into the simplified Sistema de Tributación SIMPLE (Arts. 903-915 ET) when annual gross income does not exceed 100,000 UVT (≈ COP 4,979,900,000 in FY2025); SIMPLE consolidates income tax and national sales tax into one return at sector rates (range 1.8%-14.5%). (ii) Apply to DIAN for single-user permanent free-zone (Zona Franca Permanente Especial) status for the food-processing plant, obtaining a 20% income-tax rate and customs/VAT deferral. (iii) Claim treaty benefits (dividend/interest/royalty limitation, MAP, or APA) under Colombia's double-taxation conventions. (iv) Satisfy the documentary and substance conditions of Art. 235-2 ET to exempt foreign dividend/capital-gain income from Colombian corporate tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital account convertibility is regulated and non-fully free; foreign direct investment into an S.A.S. requires prior or subsequent registration with Banco de la República ('Registro de Inversión Internacional'). Current account convertibility, including dividend remittances to foreign shareholders, is operationally open provided the investment is duly registered, the foreign shareholder is identifiable, and the repatriation is channelled through an authorized intermediary. The Colombian peso (COP) is freely convertible onshore but statistically controlled; profit remittances are additionally subject to proof of payment of corporate income tax and the 'CERT' procedure for repatriation. Offshore convertibility is limited and non-deliverable mechanisms are necessary for COP hedging.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Colombian Regimen Cambiario, export proceeds from the sale of prefabricated dim sum and other goods must be repatriated and converted into COP through an authorized foreign exchange intermediary within 6 months from the date of shipment (embarque). The full amount of the export collection is subject to mandatory conversion to Colombian pesos on the day the foreign currency is received into the local account; no statutory percentage retention in foreign currency is allowed for unregistered exporters unless the exporter obtains a prior 'Compensation Account' authorization from Banco de la República. The exporter must also submit the export declaration and the Exchange Declaration (Declaración de Cambio) electronically within the term fixed by the Central Bank, preserving all maritime/airway waybills and customs documentation.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia), acting jointly with the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit through the Regimen Cambiario under Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1735 of 1993, and Circular DCIN 83 of 2021

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low to Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Revolut, or other non-Colombian chartered e-money institutions) are not authorized to open deposit accounts or perform seisin of Colombian peso funds under the supervision of the Superintendencia Financiera. Their utility is limited to ancillary payment processing outside the statutory 'Mercado Cambiario' obligations. Mandatory foreign exchange transactions, such as export proceeds conversion and import payments exceeding USD 50,000, must be channeled through a local authorized bank with a SWIFT relationship. Therefore, full treasury liquidity and compliance cannot be sustained through a fintech EMI; a physical local bank relationship is legally indispensable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the initial registered capital (minimum capital is statutory zero for S.A.S., but a planned injection is necessary for the prefabricated dumpling factory), the foreign investor must cause a SWIFT MT103 transfer from the shareholder's bank to the S.A.S.'s authorized local commercial bank, clearly evidencing the beneficiary and the purpose code for direct foreign investment. Within the banking channel, the S.A.S. must complete the 'Registro de Inversión Internacional' with Banco de la República's Electronic Foreign Exchange System (e.g., via the 'Formulario No. 10' or the Special Frequency Registration system) before the influx date or at most within two months after the collection. The remittance must be accompanied by a board resolution of the S.A.S. approving the capital increase, the updated certificate of existence, and a clear indication that the proceeds represent 'direct foreign investment in capital' for ISIC 1074. No third-party remittances or contributions through cash/crypto exchange are permissible.For procurement of inputs (e.g., flour, steam-cooking ingredients, packaging film), the principal treasury rails are SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for supplier payments and irrevocable commercial letters of credit (UCP600) for large or repeat shipments. Inbound import payment orders must be registered with the authorized bank and the customs declaration accompanies each transaction; if aggregate payment exceeds USD 50,000 or equivalent, the importers must present the 'Registro de Importación' and the exchange declaration to Banco de la República. The bank will assign the relevant foreign exchange classification code and may request the underlying invoice, bill of lading, and FDA-equivalent veterinary certificate for the frozen food ingredients. For export settlements, the S.A.S. may also use SWIFT MT103 to receive foreign receivables and then undergo mandatory conversion; export proceeds must not be routed through virtual accounts or non-regulated payment processors.Plain vanilla USD/COP deliverable forwards executed with an authorized local bank against a registered underlying trade exposure (export or import).Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in USD/COP referencing the Colombian official exchange rate (TRM) with offshore counterparties, permissible for hedging COP risk.Cross-currency swaps (USD/COP) under the local market rules, only when the principal is supported by a commercial export/import transaction or foreign debt registration.FX options (call or put on COP vs. USD) offering protection on recognized trade cycles, subject to documentation of the S.A.S.'s hedging policy and underlying commercial transaction.
