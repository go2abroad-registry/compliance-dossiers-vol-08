Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a S.A.S. The authorized, subscribed, and paid-in capital are freely determined in the bylaws. The subscribed capital may be paid in deferred installments within two years under Article 5 of Law 1258 of 2008. No escrow account is legally required for a standard cash-funded S.A.S.; however, in-kind contributions must be valued and evidenced in the incorporation document.

Funding & unlock path: The warehouse/office must obtain a Concepto de Protección contra Incendios from the Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos, demonstrate structural load compliance to the local Curaduría Urbana, enroll workers with an ARL, and implement the SG-SST system under Resolution 0312 de 2019, including evacuation drills and workplace risk assessments.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); electronic portal: DIAN website (www.dian.gov.co) and the MUISCA transactional web platform, including the Unified Tax Registry (RUT) module.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Colombian S.A.S. must obtain a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) as the single statutory TIN/VAT ID before commencing business. Procedure: (i) incorporate the S.A.S. by private deed and register the company with the local Cámara de Comercio (commercial registry); (ii) appoint a legal representative resident in Colombia or a lawful attorney-in-fact (apoderado) authorized to act before DIAN; (iii) complete registration in the RUT through the DIAN virtual office, declaring the company’s address, ISIC-4791 economic activity, and responsible VAT status; (iv) activate the electronic invoicing digital certificate (firma electrónica) issued by a DIAN-authorized certification provider; and (v) for non-resident digital sellers without a fixed establishment, use the non-resident registration mechanism for VAT identification where DIAN assigns a special foreign-seller NIT.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — The S.A.S. is a Colombian juridical person taxed at entity level under the general corporate income tax regime; it has no look-through or pass-through treatment; profits are taxable at the corporate rate and distributions to shareholders are separately subject to dividend withholding tax. — A foreign-owned Colombian S.A.S. must obtain a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) as the single statutory TIN/VAT ID before commencing business. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — The S.A.S. is a Colombian juridical person taxed at entity level under the general corporate income tax regime; it has no look-through or pass-through treatment; profits are taxable at the corporate rate and distributions to shareholders are separately subject to dividend withholding tax.

Available election forms

Colombia does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election. The S.A.S. may elect: (i) the ordinary corporate income tax regime or, where eligible, the Simplified Taxation Regime (Régimen Simple de Tributación – SIMPLE), which consolidates income tax, municipal industry and commerce tax, and VAT in a single instalment; (ii) status as an IVA 'responsable' under the general regime; (iii) application for Free Trade Zone (Zona Franca) treatment conferring a reduced income tax rate provided the relevant registration and investment commitments are met; and (iv) for hybrid mismatch situations, compliance is mandatory under Law 1819/2016 — no elective protective classification exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

COP 0 — No general monetary registration threshold applies. The S.A.S. engaged in the retail sale of musical instruments and studio audio equipment must register with DIAN as a VAT 'responsable' before the first sale in the national territory. For reference, the optional SIMPLE regime has an eligibility ceiling of approximately 80,000 UVT; the standard 19% IVA regime applies to all corporate sellers above or below that threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Sales of musical instruments and studio audio gear by a Colombian S.A.S. to foreign buyers qualify for the 0% IVA export rate where the goods physically depart Colombia through customs control and are evidenced by an accepted export declaration. The 0% rate applies to definitive exports of tangible goods, including shipments via international courier under simplified export procedures, and to services rendered from Colombia to foreign jurisdictions where the service is exclusively used abroad and territoriality requirements are met. The supplier must be an IVA-responsible person, maintain the relevant export contracts, and retain documentary proof of dispatch, destination, and foreign receipt.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación – DEX) accepted by DIAN/MUISCA with authorization of definitive exit
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or courier consignment note (Master/HAWB) evidencing physical departure from Colombia
  • Commercial invoice consistent with the DEX and the foreign buyer
  • Packing list (relación de contenido)
  • Inward remittance certificate (certificado de reintegro de divisas) issued by the local commercial bank or the foreign-exchange declaration (declaración de cambio)
  • For zero-rated services, the Certificate of Foreign Use or Certificado de Ausencia de Causación (CAC) supporting exclusive foreign consumption

Mandatory nationwide electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta – FEV) applies to every VAT-responsible S.A.S. Invoices must be generated through DIAN-approved software or the authorized technological platform, formatted in UBL 2.1 XML, signed with the DIAN-validated digital certificate, and transmitted to DIAN in the applicable validation mode ('Validación Previa' or 'Validación Posterior') with accepted/rejected status event processing. Each valid invoice must display a two-dimensional digital stamp containing the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) and DIAN web verification code. The S.A.S. must also interoperate with RADIAN for electronic invoice financing where applicable. Online payment gateways such as Stripe and PayPal do not replace or discharge the Colombian seller’s fiscal e-invoicing duty.

DIAN strictly audits zero-rated export claims and refund applications. The 0% rate and any input VAT refund are lost if export proceeds are not repatriated to Colombia within 180 days of the export declaration, or within the additional period expressly authorized by the foreign-exchange regulations; failure triggers retroactive collection of 19% IVA on the export value plus default interest from the date of dispatch. Inaccuracies or fraudulent refunds expose the S.A.S. to the statutory inexactitude penalty of up to 200% of the tax determined, a specific sanction of 100% of improperly obtained refunds, suspension from RUT and electronic invoicing, and criminal liability for fiscal fraud under Law 1943/2018, including imprisonment for culpable personnel.
