Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio (Chamber of Commerce) of the company's registered domicile (e.g., Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá) operating through the RUES; DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) for tax registration; Superintendencia de Sociedades for corporate oversight.

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 (Articles 1-5, 17 and 45), as complemented by the Código de Comercio (Articles 98-110, 372-376) and Decree 1074 of 2015 (Registro Único Empresarial y Social); private-incorporation regime under Article 5 of Law 1258/2008.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) registered in the Registro Mercantil / RUES; where real property is contributed, the instrument must be elevated to a public deed (Escritura Pública) before a Notary and subsequently registered with the Chamber of Commerce.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 34 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital; capital is freely fixed in the bylaws under Law 1258/2008 art. 17 and payment terms are agreed by the shareholders. Shares must be fully subscribed and paid within the term stated in the bylaws. No escrow account is required; however, foreign-investment contributions must be paid into a Colombian bank account in the company's name and registered with the Banco de la República within five (5) business days of receipt.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certification (Concepto Técnico / Certificado de Bomberos) from the Cuerpo de Bomberos of the municipality, including extinguishers, emergency exits, and signage; structural-load and racking inspections if heavy-machinery parts storage is conducted on site; occupational health and safety clearance through the mandatory SGSST program under Resolution 0312 of 2019, employee medical assessments, emergency-response planning, and ARL (occupational-risk) coverage before employees engage in administrative or warehouse duties.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital; capital is freely fixed in the bylaws under Law 1258/2008 art. 17 and payment terms are agreed by the shareholders. Shares must be fully subscribed and paid within the term stated in the bylaws. No escrow account is required; however, foreign-investment contributions must be paid into a Colombian bank account in the company's name and registered with the Banco de la República within five (5) business days of receipt.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No industrial operating license applies to the pure wholesale trade of used construction machinery (ISIC 4659); the authorities that may intervene are the Cámara de Comercio (mercantile registration), the municipal Alcaldía (ICA and land-use certificates), the Cuerpo de Bomberos (fire safety), and, only for importation of used heavy equipment, the Ministerio de Comercio, Industria y Turismo (MINCIT) through VUPE and the Ministerio de Ambiente for environmental import clearance.

Category: Not applicable — no manufacturing or industrial operating license is required for wholesale remarketing of secondhand hydraulic earthmovers and rollers; the activity is governed by the mercantile registration and municipal operating permits.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – national tax and customs authority; electronic portal: https://www.dian.gov.co and transactional platform: https://muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

1) File the S.A.S. deed with the Chamber of Commerce to obtain the business certificate; 2) Register the company with DIAN in the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) electronically or in person, selecting the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and requesting VAT responsibility code 'R' and 'Exportador' status; 3) Wait for the NIT to be assigned; 4) Obtain digital certificate (certificado digital) from a certified CA to enable e-invoicing and customs operations.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (resident S.A.S. taxed on worldwide taxable income; no pass-through or check-the-box classification) — 1) File the S.A.S. deed with the Chamber of Commerce to obtain the business certificate; 2) Register the company with DIAN in the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) electronically or in person, selecting the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and requesting VAT responsibility code 'R' and 'Exportador' status; 3) Wait for the NIT to be assigned; 4) Obtain digital certificate (certificado digital) from a certified CA to enable e-invoicing and customs operations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (resident S.A.S. taxed on worldwide taxable income; no pass-through or check-the-box classification)

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist. Available elections include: (i) Colombian Holding Company (CHC) regime for qualified holding companies; (ii) Free Trade Zone (Zona Franca) user election giving a 20% income tax rate subject to requirements; (iii) Simplificado annual regime (SIMPLE) for small entities with annual income below 100,000 UVT and under 100 employees; (iv) tax consolidation election for groups satisfying control and economic interest tests under Law 2010 of 2019.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For a Colombian legal entity, no economic threshold applies: registration as a VAT-responsible taxpayer is mandatory upon undertaking any taxable sale or service. For non-resident digital service providers, a 30,000 UVT threshold applies (approx. COP 1,260,000,000 in 2024), but this is not relevant to a domestic S.A.S. exporter.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of tangible goods (including used construction machinery) are zero-rated for VAT provided the seller is a VAT-registered exporter, the goods are physically shipped from Colombian customs territory to a foreign destination, the transaction is evidenced by a final export declaration, and the export proceeds are repatriated within the legal term. Used equipment qualifies only if the seller holds and can transfer title free of liens and the equipment is not otherwise excluded by special environmental or antique trade legislation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) transmitted electronically through DIAN/VUCE
  • Bill of Lading (signed ocean bill of lading or air waybill evidencing physical shipment)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificate of foreign currency compensation issued by the Colombian intermediary bank)
  • Commercial invoice identified as export sale and marked 0% IVA with CUFE
  • Pre-shipment inspection certificate for used machinery (if required by destination country or DIAN)

Mandatory Facturación Electrónica de Venta (electronic invoicing) with near-real-time transmission to DIAN. Taxpayers in the e-invoicing system must issue XML invoices signed with a certified digital certificate, compensate the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica), and obtain DIAN's validation prior to delivering the invoice. For exports, the same electronic invoice description is used with additional 'Exportación' identifiers, and the invoice must reference the accepted export declaration. Reverse-charge or e-invoice equivalence rules apply to foreign-to-local services but not to the underlying export sale.

DIAN may reopen VAT returns for up to five years and recharacterise any export as a local taxable sale if the mandatory export formalities are missing or inaccurate. Consequences include payment of the 19% IVA on the taxable base, default interest, a penalty for tax inaccuracy of up to 100% of the omitted tax, and in fraud cases penalties under Article 308 of the Criminal Code together with imprisonment for tax evasion. DIAN cross-verifies export declarations, e-invoices, customs shipping records and FX remittance certificates.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso operates under a managed float and is de jure convertible for current-account transactions (goods trade, freight, insurance, technical services, and profit-related remittances) when settled through the official exchange market (mercado cambiario) involving an IMC. Capital-account operations, including equity contributions to the S.A.S., are liberalized but must be registered with the Banco de la República foreign-investment registry (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales) so that subsequent dividend and capital repatriation flows can be legally processed; in practical terms, profit remittance is conditional on (i) prior registration of the direct investment, (ii) processing through an IMC with the applicable Declaración de Cambio, (iii) available audited retained earnings, and (iv) satisfaction of the domestic dividend withholding rules. Unregistered capital contributions or remittances of proceeds deriving from non-registered equity will be blocked by the IMC and recharacterized for exchange-control and tax purposes, generating administrative rather than monetary convertibility hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory framework: Law 9 of 1991, implemented by Resolution No. 48/2000 of the Junta Directiva del Banco de la República and Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83 of 2007. Export proceeds from goods, including secondhand hydraulic excavators, rollers, and spare parts, must be canalized into Colombia by sale to or deposit with an IMC, or by crediting an authorized foreign-currency account in Colombia or a Banco de la República-approved compensation account abroad, within six (6) months following the date of receipt of the foreign-currency payment. There is no mandatory percentage surrender or forced conversion into COP; exporters may retain USD balances in permissible accounts, but the IMC filing (Declaración de Cambio por Exportaciones, supported by the DIAN export declaration and the commercial invoice) must evidence channeling within the statutory window. Failure to channel within the six-month term constitutes an exchange-control infraction attracting fines and monetary sanctions, in practice up to 200% of the uncanalized amount, imposed by Banco de la República, with additional DIAN penalties in cases of misdeclaration.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), acting through the Junta Directiva and jointly with the Ministerio de Hacienda y Crédito Público; the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia supervises the authorized foreign-exchange intermediaries (IMCs), while DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) governs the underlying customs and foreign-trade declarations.
