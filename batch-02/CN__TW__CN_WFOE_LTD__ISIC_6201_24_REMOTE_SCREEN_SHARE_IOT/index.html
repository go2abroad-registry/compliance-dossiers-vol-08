Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices and commercial mailboxes are generally not permitted for WFOEs in China. The registered office must be a physical address that is capable of receiving official correspondence and is accessible for inspection by the SAMR, tax authorities, and other regulators. For a software company engaged in remote desktop access and IoT telemetry, a virtual office would likely be rejected by the local tax bureau for the purpose of issuing VAT invoices and by banks for opening a corporate bank account. A physical lease agreement for at least one year is required, and the address must be consistent with the business license. Some free trade zones (e.g., Shanghai FTZ) may allow shared office spaces, but they must be registered as a legal business address and provide a lease certificate.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Taiwan limited company. For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the paid-in capital must equal or exceed the amount approved in the MOEAIC Foreign Investment Approval; the full amount must be remitted into a Taiwan bank account in the company's name before registration and verified by a Taiwan licensed CPA. There is no general blocked escrow account requirement, but the capital must remain traceable and un-disbursed until the company has obtained its registration certificate.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), acting through the local tax service authorities at the WFOE's registered address, and administered digitally through the State Taxation Administration Electronic Tax Bureau platform and the State Administration for Market Regulation one-stop enterprise registration system.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE first obtains an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) from the State Administration for Market Regulation; the USCC automatically constitutes the taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration number. Within 30 days after issuance of the business license, the legal representative or authorized agent must complete tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau portal, confirming the taxpayer type (resident enterprise), registered address, legal representative, ISIC 6201 software and information technology services industrial classification, capital structure, and bank account details. The Electronic Tax Bureau issues a digital tax registration certificate; the same USCC is used for all VAT filing and invoice issuance. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in mainland China may separately obtain a non-resident TIN through the Electronic Tax Bureau platform or through its PRC withholding agent by submitting its certificate of incorporation and tax residence certificate. For the Taiwanese shareholder/counterparty, such non-resident TIN is used for PRC withholding tax reporting; the Taiwan-invested WFOE itself remains a PRC resident enterprise

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a dual current/capital account convertibility regime. The RMB is convertible on current account items such as trade in software services, remote support subscriptions, and bona fide cross-border service fees, provided the transaction is based on genuine commercial contracts and supported by invoices, contracts, and tax certificates. However, capital account transactions remain tightly restricted; FDI equity injections, shareholder loans, dividend distributions, and capital outflows require SAFE registration or approval, a clear commercial purpose, and use of designated foreign exchange accounts. For this mainland WFOE, all export revenue from remote desktop access and IoT telemetry services must be repatriated to China, reported under service trade foreign-exchange declarations, and cannot be netted or retained offshore without SAFE permission. Profit remittance is permitted only after annual tax filing, audit completion, and CPA confirmation of distributable profit, with corporate income tax and withholding tax settled. Foreign exchange transactions are subject to the actual-demand verification principle, and any anticipated hedging must be tied to real underlying exposure. In practice, this means current account convertibility is nominally available but documentary-intensive, while capital account mobility remains tightly controlled and liquidity-constrained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The mandatory fixed-percentage surrender rule has been abolished. Export proceeds must be repatriated into mainland China through a licensed collecting bank and registered in the SAFE trade-monitoring system within the applicable timeline. For goods exports, the standard collection period is generally within 90 days from the date of customs declaration, while service exports such as software and IoT telemetry are expected to be settled and repatriated promptly once due. The WFOE may elect to retain 0% or up to 100% of foreign currency earnings in its foreign exchange settlement account, or convert them into RMB, subject to bank verification of the underlying export contract, customs manifest where applicable, invoice, and collection entry. The conversion ratio is not a statutory fixed rate but must match the stated commercial purpose and the actual-demand principle.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Not viable as a primary treasury rail. A mainland-incorporated WFOE must use a licensed Chinese bank with SAFE visibility for all onshore FX and cross-border settlement. Offshore digital EMIs, including UK or European e-money institutions, cannot open a local RMB account for the WFOE and are not designated FX banks. Using Stripe or Paddle as a merchant of record for global software and IoT subscription sales is acceptable for customer-facing collection, but settlement into China must be routed through an onshore acquiring or settlement arrangement with proper service trade foreign-exchange declarations. EMIs cannot act as flow-through vehicles for FDI capital injection, capital account remittance, or outbound trade disbursements, and any attempt to use an EMI as a treasury intermediary would violate the PRC foreign exchange control framework.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder should remit through its corporate account, never a personal account, to the WFOE's Foreign Exchange Capital Account at a SAFE-designated bank using SWIFT MT103. The remittance instruction must reference the FDI SAFE registration number and the correct balance-of-payments purpose code for equity capital injection. Upon receipt, the bank verifies the FX registration and underlying investment documents; a PRC CPA then issues a capital verification report. The funds cannot be settled into RMB before bank verification, and the capital account foreign exchange settlement form must be completed. Use of funds must align with the WFOE's registered business scope and the negative list. Inbound proceeds from Stripe or Paddle must not enter through the capital account; these are current account software subscription receipts and must be collected through a normal settlement account with service trade declarations and tax filings.
