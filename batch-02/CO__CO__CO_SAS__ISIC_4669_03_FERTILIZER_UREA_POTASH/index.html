Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — National Tax and Customs Authority of Colombia; electronic taxpayer services via DIAN Virtual/MUISCA portal (muisca.dian.gov.co); institutional portal: www.dian.gov.co.

E-government filing portal

The S.A.S. is first incorporated through the Cámara de Comercio/RUES; the legal representative then files the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) with DIAN to obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), the sole statutory tax ID, and to activate tax responsibilities (income tax; IVA as responsable del régimen común; electronic invoicing; customs/exporter registration for goods exit). Non-resident shareholders must appoint a locally domiciled tax attorney-in-fact; RUT filing and all subsequent obligations require the DIAN digital certificate (firma electrónica).

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — The S.A.S. is first incorporated through the Cámara de Comercio/RUES; the legal representative then files the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) with DIAN to obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), the sole statutory tax ID, and to activate tax responsibilities (income tax; IVA as responsable del régimen común; electronic invoicing; customs/exporter registration for goods exit). Non-resident shareholders must appoint a locally domiciled tax attorney-in-fact; RUT filing and all subsequent obligations require the DIAN digital certificate (firma electrónica).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election exists; classification is statutory under the Commercial Code and Estatuto Tributario. Available statutory mechanisms: (i) optional Régimen SIMPLE de Tributación (ET Arts. 903–916) for entities below the annual UVT gross-income ceiling, consolidating income tax, VAT and municipal tax — the election must be weighed against export VAT-refund positioning; (ii) mandatory CFC/transparencia fiscal internacional pass-through attribution (ET Arts. 881–889, Law 1819/2016) for controlled non-resident entities (not an election); (iii) transfer-pricing documentation elections and advance pricing agreements under ET Arts. 260-1 et seq.; hybrid mismatch deductions are restricted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary exemption threshold for legal entities: every S.A.S. performing taxable wholesale activities must register with DIAN as an IVA-responsable (régimen común) in the RUT from commencement of operations; the SIMPLE alternative is restricted to taxpayers under the statutory UVT-based gross-income ceiling (for legal entities, 100,000 UVT, approximately COP 4.7 billion for tax year 2024) and may not suit export-refund optimization.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of prilled urea and potassium chloride sold to non-residents and physically removed from Colombian customs territory qualify as ventas exentas (zero-rated) under ET Art. 481, removing output IVA while preserving the exporter's refund right over attributable input VAT (saldo a favor). The S.A.S. must hold RUT exporter registration, a definitive export declaration (DEX), transport evidence, and comply with FX repatriation and transfer-pricing substance. Note: domestic fertilizer sales may be VAT-excluded under the ET Art. 424 agro-input exclusion, without refund right, so input VAT segregation between domestic excluded sales and export volumes is critical.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación — DEX) validated by DIAN customs through the authorized customs broker; contains fiscal, tariff, statistical and value data for the urea/potash shipment
  • Bill of Lading (sea) or equivalent transport document (air waybill or CMR for land exports) evidencing physical removal of the goods from Colombian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / foreign-exchange settlement evidence (certificado de negociación de divisas and declaración de cambio) issued by the authorized FX intermediary proving collection within the statutory repayment term

Mandatory real-time e-invoicing: the S.A.S. must issue factura electrónica de venta (FEV) through a DIAN-authorized provider or direct API; each invoice XML set is transmitted to DIAN's web-service validation before issuance and receives an aceptada/validada status (Resolución DIAN 000165/2023 technical specs); the client PDF must show the DIAN QR digital fiscal stamp. Electronic transporte/remission documents and the documento soporte for purchases from non-invoicing suppliers are also mandatory for goods flows.

DIAN cross-audits export IVA refund claims (devolución de saldos a favor) against the DEX, bills of lading, export invoices and FX settlement records; any inconsistency triggers rejection, retrospective clawback and moratory interest. Sanctions: ET Art. 670 (devolución o compensación improcedente) fines up to 100% of the amount refunded; ET Art. 648 (inexactitud) penalties up to 200% (160% for periods under Law 2277/2022) of the tax difference; Law 1762/2015 customs contraband fines of 100%–300% of the customs value with confiscation; criminal exposure (customs/tax fraud) with imprisonment for executives; and suspension from exporter privileges upon repeat non-compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a managed-float currency regime with full current-account convertibility for trade in goods and services and regulated but open capital-account convertibility. Foreign direct investment (FDI) may enter and exit freely but must be registered with Banco de la República under the Registro de Inversión Internacional to secure lawful profit remittance and capital repatriation. Decree 2438 of 2020 and Resolution 5044 of 2020 as amended eliminated the historical 5-year minimum denationalization holding period and the USD 100,000 registration threshold, substantially removing administrative hurdles for foreign-owned SAS entities; dividend and capital remittances remain subject to Colombian withholding taxes but require no additional FX authorization once the investment is registered and the corresponding declaración de cambio is filed with the IMC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Exporters of physical goods, including prilled urea and potassium chloride, must channel 100% of export proceeds into the Colombian FX market within 12 months counted from the date of collection (fecha de recaudo) of the proceeds. Channeling is satisfied by selling the foreign currency to an IMC in exchange for COP, or by depositing the funds into a registered foreign-currency compensation account (cuenta de compensación) held at a Colombian financial institution or a registered account abroad. No partial conversion ratio is applied to compensation-account balances, but the full offshore balance remains subject to the channeling obligation before any unreported disposal; non-compliance exposes the SAS to exchange-control fines by Banco de la República and triggers enhanced AML/KYC reassessment by its banking counterparts.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), with the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit (Ministerio de Hacienda y Crédito Público) and the National Tax and Customs Directorate (DIAN) exercising complementary oversight; all foreign-exchange transactions must be channeled through authorized intermediaries of the foreign-exchange market (intermediarios del mercado cambiario, 'IMCs').

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Colombia has no dedicated EMI license category; non-bank digital payment providers operate as Sociedades Especializadas en Depósitos y Pagos Electrónicos (SEDPEs) supervised by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia and cannot issue documentary credits, trade loans, or FX intermediation. Offshore digital EMIs lack access to local COP clearing rails (SEN, CUD, ACH) and are functionally inadequate as primary treasury counterparties for high-value urea/potash wholesale transactions; they may serve only as auxiliary AP/AR settlement tools for sub-threshold incidental payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Incoming shareholder equity contributions must be routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 to the SAS's account at an authorized IMC, referencing the company NIT and the foreign-exchange declaration form (Declaración de Cambio por Inversión Internacional). The IMC files the declaración under the direct investment category, and the SAS must register the investment with Banco de la República within 30 calendar days of receipt; remitter and beneficiary details must exactly match the FDI registration to ensure lawful profit repatriation. Disbursement in COP occurs at the representative market rate (TRM); no minimum capital verification is required for SAS formation.Trade settlements for prilled urea and potassium chloride must be executed through the IMC using SWIFT MT103 wire transfers or irrevocable documentary credits (SWIFT MT700) issued by the Colombian bank in favor of overseas suppliers. Each outbound payment requires a Declaración de Cambio de Importación (Formulario No. 1) with the import-of-goods purpose code '4202', filed at the time of payment, and must be reconciled against the DIAN import declaration and underlying invoice/packing documents. Actualización de Beneficiario Final (annual UBO confirmation of direct and indirect beneficial owners) Annually within the decree-specified window, currently the first semester of each year (recent cycles: 1 February through 30 June / 31 July); any qualifying change of control or 5%-or-greater shareholding during the year must be updated within one month of the event. $0 USD Superintendencia de Sociedades may fine both the entity and the legal representative; the Chamber will refuse to issue commercial registry certificates or process the annual renewal until the UBO filing is regularized, and notarial, banking and import procedures are consequently blocked.
Declaración Anual del Impuesto de Industria y Comercio (ICA), with complementary Avisos y Tableros (municipal gross-receipts tax on wholesale trading) Annually by 30 April of the year following the tax year in most municipalities; certain large taxpayers must also remit monthly or bimonthly advance installments during the year. $0 USD Municipal surcharges and interest apply at roughly 2% per month of delay, capped at 100% of the ICA due; the taxpayer is flagged in the municipal registry, official certificates are suspended, and clients' withholding certificates cannot be generated until full settlement.
