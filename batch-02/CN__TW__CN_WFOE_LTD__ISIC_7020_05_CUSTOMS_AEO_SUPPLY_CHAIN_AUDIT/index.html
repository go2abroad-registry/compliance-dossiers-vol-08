Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to management consulting under PRC law. Registered capital is subscribed in full in the Articles of Association; the paid-in amount must be contributed within the schedule agreed in the Articles, and a foreign-invested WFOE must deposit the subscribed capital in a bank foreign-exchange capital account prior to or immediately after registration. No formal escrow is required, but capital remittance is subject to SAFE information reporting.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China (国家稅務總局); official portal: www.chinatax.gov.cn; electronic tax bureau: etax.chinatax.gov.cn with provincial instances. For Taiwan-side counterparty matters: Taiwan Ministry of Finance (財政部) and regional National Taxation Bureaus, e-tax portal: etax.nat.gov.tw.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE limited liability company through the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) one-stop registration; the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (統一社會信用代碼) printed on the business license (營業執照) is the statutory TIN and VAT ID. Step 2: Complete supplementary tax registration in the e-Tax Bureau within 30 days of license issuance, including legal representative, financial controller, banking accounts, accounting method and taxpayer-type election. Step 3: Elect VAT status: small-scale taxpayer (annual taxable sales at or below RMB 5,000,000) or general VAT taxpayer (mandatory above RMB 5,000,000 and required for input VAT credits and export refunds). Step 4: Obtain customs registration as an import/export enterprise through the China International Trade Single Window, acquiring the 10-digit customs code, electronic port IC card and AEO facilitation profile. Step 5: Open a foreign exchange settlement account at a designated bank, register with SAFE for trade-in-foreign-exchange monitoring, and activate the export tax refund (出口退免稅) filing module in the e-Tax Bureau. Step 6: For services supplied to Taiwan or other overseas recipients, file the cross-border zero-rating or VAT exemption record with the local STA and retain service delivery and consumption evidence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 2, taxed on worldwide income at 25%; no pass-through or check-the-box eligibility exists for Chinese LLCs. Territorial scope: the WFOE is taxed on China-source income and offshore income; non-resident counterparties such as a Taiwan parent or customer are taxed on China-source income by withholding, generally 10% but potentially reduced under the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement effective for 2017 onward. A free trade zone location does not create a tax-exempt zone; only selective preferential regimes such as encouraged-industry 15% rates apply, which are not assumed here.

Available election forms

PRC law provides no check-the-box or hybrid entity classification election; all WFOE LLCs are entity-level taxpayers. Available statutory elections: (a) VAT status election between small-scale simplified levy (currently 1% or 3%) and general taxpayer treatment (6%, 9%, 13% with input VAT credits) when annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000; (b) export tax refund mechanism election: exemption-offset-refund (免抵退) for production and qualifying service exporters versus levy-then-refund for trading companies; (c) cross-border service treatment election between zero-rating for statutory offshore outsourcing, software, R&D and information-system services and VAT exemption for certification-consulting and audit-type services under CaiShui 2016 No. 36 Annex 4; (d) treaty benefits application under the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement to cap Taiwan withholding rates on dividends at 5% (for beneficial owners holding at least 25% capital with substance), interest at 10% and royalties at 10%; (e) enterprise income tax elections such as R&D super-deduction (additional 100% deduction) and advanced-technology-service enterprise status at 15%. Hybrid mismatch arrangements are constrained by the general anti-avoidance rule (EIT Law Articles 47-48) and special tax adjustment rules; no elective hybrid-mismatch safe harbor exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic-nexus turnover threshold exempts an incorporated WFOE from VAT registration; registration is compulsory at incorporation. The operative threshold is general VAT taxpayer registration: annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (approximately USD 690,000) mandate general taxpayer status with standard rates and input VAT credits; at or below RMB 5,000,000, small-scale simplified treatment at 1% or 3% may be elected, but it disqualifies the entity from claiming input credits and export tax refunds. Foreign suppliers without a PRC establishment that provide services into China are subject to VAT withholding by the PRC payor, and Taiwan suppliers rendering services into Mainland China through the WFOE engagement are subject to the same reverse-charge or withholding mechanism.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: the WFOE must hold general VAT taxpayer status, be the named exporter on the customs export declaration, ensure physical departure from the PRC customs territory, receive matching export proceeds in foreign currency, and issue the zero-rated export e-Fapiao; the exported goods must have previously borne genuine input VAT that is creditable. For offshore services including AEO certification, security auditing and consulting supplied to Taiwan or other overseas recipients and consumed entirely outside Mainland China: statutory zero-rating applies only to enumerated categories such as offshore service outsourcing, business process management, software, R&D and information-system services; certification-consulting and audit-type services generally qualify for VAT exemption rather than 0%, meaning no output VAT is charged and associated input VAT is non-reclaimable unless the engagement is restructured as zero-rated offshore service outsourcing (離岸服務外包) satisfying trade-in-services classification and delivery documentation. Substantiation must demonstrate that the recipient is outside the PRC and that the benefit and performance occur wholly offshore, evidenced by contemporaneous contracts, delivery records, communications and intellectual-property usage logs.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報關單) bearing the electronic release record and customs declaration number
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (海運提單或空運單) proving dispatch from a PRC port and arrival outside Mainland China
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Export Proceeds Collection Record (銀行收匯水單或涉外收入申報單) issued by the settlement bank or SAFE monitoring system
  • Zero-rated or VAT-exempt e-Fapiao generated through the digital e-invoice platform
  • Export sales contract, packing list and weight memo, plus customs inspection or AEO security-audit documentation where goods are subject to statutory inspection

China mandates the fully digital e-Fapiao (數電票) system deployed nationally through the e-Tax Bureau; legacy tax control equipment is being phased out. All VAT invoices require real-time issuance with authority pre-assigned invoice quotas, digital stamping or digital signature, and immediate transmission to the Golden Tax Phase IV (金稅四期) clearance engine. Taxpayers may generate invoices through the web portal or certified API integration; Golden Tax IV automatically cross-matches invoice data with China International Trade Single Window customs declarations, bank foreign-exchange settlement records and corporate registration data. For export transactions, the zero-rated e-Fapiao must reference the customs export declaration number and be matched to the customs release manifest; discrepancies trigger real-time exception reporting, refund holds and audit flags.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to ex-post desk audits and Golden Tax Phase IV data forensics. False or inflated claims trigger: (a) administrative clawback of refunded VAT and exempted tax, daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, and fines between 50% and five times the tax evaded under the Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 63-66; (b) criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 204 (fraudulent export tax refund) and Article 205 (false issuance of special VAT invoices), with imprisonment of up to five years for significant amounts, five to ten years for huge amounts or serious circumstances, and up to life imprisonment for especially huge amounts or especially serious circumstances; (c) customs enforcement including revocation of customs registration or AEO certification for up to three years and joint blacklisting under the customs-tax memoranda; (d) a five-year statutory audit look-back period with no time limitation for fraud, together with cross-strait tax and customs information exchange under the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement and customs cooperation framework. Any false representation in AEO security-audit reports additionally exposes the WFOE to professional disciplinary action, client-jurisdiction licensing suspension and contractual indemnity claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB (CNY) is fully convertible on the current account in law for genuine trade in goods and services, including consulting, certification, and AEO security-audit fees. Convertibility is administrative rather than free: banks must verify the underlying transaction through SAFE trade-in-services and trade-in-goods monitoring systems, and any single service-trade outward payment exceeding USD 50,000 requires an advance Service Trade and Other Cross-Border Payments Tax Filing Form (税务备案表) accepted by the local tax authority. The capital account remains non-convertible absent registration: FDI capital contributions must be registered with SAFE through a designated bank, paid into a dedicated Capital Escrow Account, and used within a negative list that excludes securities investment, wealth-management purchases, entrusted loans, non-business real estate, and equity reinvestment outside the registered scope. Profit remittance (dividends) is treated as a current-account outward item but is practically conditional on audited statutory financial statements evidencing distributable reserves, a valid profit-distribution resolution, verified settlement of corporate income tax and the 10% withholding tax on the Taiwan non-resident corporate shareholder, and presentation of the FDI foreign-exchange registration voucher to the remitting bank. Accumulated losses, unresolved tax arrears, an unreconciled capital account, or a negative-list mismatch will block the remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's trade-in-goods aggregate-verification framework, export proceeds arising from customs-cleared goods must be repatriated into a domestic current-account foreign-exchange account rather than retained offshore; for spot receivables the general compliance expectation is collection within 90 days after export declaration, and for registered usance transactions within the contractual maturity, reported through the enterprise trade monitoring system. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio into RMB: the former forced-surrender quotas were abolished in 2012, and A/B-class enterprises may elect between retaining foreign exchange in a current-account forex account or settling into RMB on a voluntary-settlement (意愿结汇) basis against verified underlying trade items. C-class enterprises and entities flagged for overdue receipts, mismatched customs data, or AML red flags are downgraded, subjected to list-based verification, and may be required to settle foreign exchange compulsorily. For this WFOE, the primary service receipts are trade-in-services fees rather than goods-export proceeds, so the goods-trade surrender mechanics apply only to any incidental customs/AEO-advisory fees tied to physical export documentation; pure consulting fees are verified under the trade-in-services regime instead.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) is the sovereign monetary authority of the People's Republic of China; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) is the PBOC-affiliated central foreign-exchange regulator and exercises delegated authority over currency convertibility, current-account verification, capital-account registration, financial-institution FX retail business, and cross-border payment statistics. All regulatory requirements for a mainland China WFOE funded from Taiwan must be resolved against PBOC AML/KYC rules and SAFE foreign-exchange directives.

Foreign Investment Annual Business Operation Report (外商投資事業營運狀況年度申報) Annually by May 31 following the reporting year (calendar-year basis), reflecting the audited/approved annual financial data (paid-in capital, revenue, profit, shareholding structure) $0 USD Unreported or inconsistent foreign-investment data may trigger Commission compliance review, suspension of preferred remittance/earnings-repatriation facilitation, and delay or denial of reinvestment or expansion approval; continuing default may restrict the foreign-investment approval and cause unapproved equity transfers to be void.
