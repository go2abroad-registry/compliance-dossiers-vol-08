Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None as a statutory minimum for this ordinary digital content WFOE under the Foreign Investment Negative List; the registered capital is freely fixed by the shareholders in the Articles of Association. Under the Company Law (2023 Revision), subscribed capital must be fully paid within five years from the date of establishment unless a shorter or longer period is lawfully specified. Cash contributions must be wired into a dedicated capital contribution account opened in the company's name at a bank authorized to receive foreign invested capital. This account is restricted in the sense that funds are held for paid-in capital verification and may not be disbursed for operating purposes until the business license is issued and the account is converted into a basic deposit account; no government-controlled escrow blocking mechanism applies beyond this standard capital verification arrangement. For the Taiwanese shareholder, remittance must also satisfy SAFE foreign exchange registration and cross-strait banking documentation requirements.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; electronic filing and registration through the China Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) portal.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation, the local Administration for Market Regulation issues a business license embedding the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the statutory taxpayer identification number. The Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise must complete tax registration with the competent State Taxation Administration local office within 30 days of license issuance using the e-Tax portal or in-person appointment, submitting the business license, articles of association, legal representative identity document, registered address proof, bank account details, and investor identity/corporate existence documents. The tax authority activates the VAT taxpayer profile (small-scale or general taxpayer) and issues a tax registration record. Foreign non-resident entities without a Chinese establishment may obtain a withholding tax identification number through a local withholding agent via the e-Tax portal.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 aggregate annual taxable turnover for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; all newly incorporated entities must register for VAT irrespective of turnover and maintain small-scale taxpayer status until the threshold is exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exported services applies where the Chinese WFOE provides qualifying cross-border services, including digital streaming and vertical micro-drama distribution, to non-resident customers, the services are consumed entirely outside China, the export income is received in foreign exchange from abroad, and the transaction is not prohibited by law. The 0% VAT rate is available with refund of attributable input VAT. Physical goods exported across the customs border also qualify for 0% VAT with export rebate under the applicable export VAT refund regime.

  • China Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) for physical goods
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill for physical export deliveries
  • Signed streaming/content licensing agreement with overseas platform or non-resident customer
  • Foreign exchange inward remittance certificate issued by the Chinese settlement bank
  • Zero-rated export filing and supporting evidence submitted via the China Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) portal

The State Taxation Administration mandates digital electronic invoicing through the national fully digitalised electronic invoice system. All VAT invoices must be issued via STA-certified tax control equipment or the e-Tax portal API, cleared in real time into the Golden Tax Project IV database, and carry an anti-fraud digital stamp. Offline manual invoicing and unregistered invoice templates are prohibited.

Failure to substantiate the zero rate with the required documentation within the statutory deadline triggers VAT reassessment at the prevailing standard rate, a late payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on unpaid tax, an administrative penalty ranging from 50% to five times the evaded tax, and retrospective clawback of any VAT refund issued. Fraudulent export VAT claims are prosecuted under the Criminal Law and the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection, carrying penalties up to life imprisonment and asset confiscation in severe cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime under PBOC and SAFE. Current account transactions, including cross-border licensing fees, online streaming platform revenues and short-drama distribution royalties, are generally eligible for free conversion after factual verification against genuine trade and service background. Capital account operations, including the initial Taiwanese equity injection, remain subject to FDI foreign exchange registration, negative-list compliance and settlement-use restrictions. A Taiwan-invested WFOE may settle its foreign currency capital into RMB only for permitted operating purposes, and cannot use the converted RMB for entrusted loans, securities investment, real estate, or purchases of assets unrelated to the business. Profit repatriation to the Taiwanese shareholder must be supported by audited financial statements, corporate income tax payments, dividend distribution resolutions and a withholding tax certificate, with the surplus currency converted through a domestic licensed bank. There is no free-floating convertibility for pure financial transactions; all settlement practices require an underlying transaction dossier. The real-economy digital content export model of the WFOE, however, benefits from a straightforward current-account adjudication path provided the platform settlement evidences and subscriber dashboards are preserved.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For digital software/audio-visual service exports and overseas streaming receipts, the WFOE must repatriate foreign currency proceeds into China through a domestic designated bank and match the relevant service trade declaration to SAFE's non-trade administration horizon. There is no mandatory forced surrender ratio; conversion to RMB is voluntary, but the bank must maintain the Service Trade Foreign Exchange Declaration filing and the actual contract/invoice reconciliation within the required record retention period. For physical merchandise exports such as DVDs or promotional merchandise, the same repatriation obligation applies and the export receipts must be verified against customs declarations under SAFE's trade monitoring system within the statutory verification period, generally within 180 days unless a longer settlement period is registered under the trade credit framework.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs, including Stripe Treasury-like platforms, Wise Business, Revolut Business, and Airwallex, have no legal standing to hold or clear WFOE RMB in mainland China; PBOC/SAFE rules require corporate FX and RMB clearing through licensed banks. Such EMIs cannot issue a mainland bank account number or accept a Taiwan-invested WFOE as an onshore client for treasury clearing. Their only permissible role is ancillary foreign collection support, namely receiving platform payouts from global app stores and then forwarding the net amount via SWIFT into the WFOE's domestic bank; the WFOE remains fully responsible for SAFE service-trade declaration and tax evidence for every inbound transfer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwanese investor must first complete the mainland foreign-invested enterprise approval and SAFE FDI foreign exchange registration, then wire the equity capital from its Taiwan bank account through SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's domestic designated bank using the purpose of capital injection under FDI registration and the FDI foreign exchange code as filed. Upon receipt, the bank will confirm the remittance against the registered expected capital amount, issue the FDI receipt post-back, and allow the WFOE to convert the foreign exchange into RMB only after the CPA capital verification report and SAFE settlement confirmation are filed. Any inward remittance exceeding the registered capital or without matching FDI registration will be parked in a non-resident temporary account and returned unless the WFOE amends its business license and foreign exchange registration before the bank's verification cutoff.For overseas streaming distributors and content buyers, the WFOE may use SWIFT MT103 direct transfers for licensing fees, but service trade contracts must be filed in the bank under China's service trade settlement rules. For high-value international co-production or distribution advances, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit can be used with payment terms against presentation of the distribution contract, copyright assignment certificate, final master copy delivery, inspection report and clean signed invoice. Documentary collections may be used for smaller territorial licensing deals, with the commercial invoice, complete-master delivery receipt and downloaded-subscriber report as the accompanying documents. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File: required when aggregate related-party transactions in the prior fiscal year equal or exceed CNY 1 billion. Local File: required when annual related-party transactions reach: (a) tangible asset transfers of CNY 100 million or more; (b) intangible property transfers of CNY 100 million or more; (c) financial asset transactions of CNY 100 million or more; (d) other related-party transactions of CNY 40 million or more. For the micro-drama WFOE, the relevant threshold is most likely 'other related-party transactions' (royalties, content services, platform fees) of CNY 40 million; below the threshold, contemporaneous documentation should still be prepared on a risk-based request under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42.

Economic substance scope: PRC law does not apply the UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations code, but applies an equivalent substance-over-form doctrine: (1) the Beneficial Owner six-factor test of SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9 requires the WFOE to have actual personnel, premises, decision-making authority, risk-bearing and operational substance; (2) the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 120 of the Tax Collection Administration Law allows recharacterization of shell structures; (3) the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement is implemented by domestic legislation and is not a covered-tax-agreement under China's BEPS MLI, so PRC anti-abuse rules apply strictly; a Taiwan-owned WFOE that books app-store revenue in China but makes all decisions in Taipei is vulnerable to denial of DTA rates and deemed-dividend recharacterization.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Suppression of OTT/Streaming Revenue and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Recovery of underpaid tax plus 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge; administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law; criminal tax evasion under Article 201 of the Criminal Law with fines of 1-5 times the evaded amount and imprisonment of up to 7 years where the evaded amount exceeds RMB 100,000 and constitutes more than 30% of the tax due; revocation of VAT invoice issuance rights and business license.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil under Article 20 of the PRC Company Law; directors and the Taiwan-nominated legal representative jointly and severally liable for civil restitution; travel bans under the exit-exam procedure; personal disqualification from new company registration and imprisonment as joint offenders where fraudulent conduct is proven.
Transfer Pricing Abuse and Special Tax Adjustment

Statutory sanction: Special Tax Adjustment under SAT Announcement 2017 No. 6: upward income adjustment, retroactive reassessment for up to 10 years, interest at the benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points from the due date of the underpaid tax, and where false related-party reporting is established, a penalty of up to 50% of the underpaid tax; burden of proof shifts to the taxpayer where documentation is withheld.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who execute the intercompany agreements are subject to secondary adjustment collection; exit-exam denial and travel bans; civil joint liability for amounts assessed where the board authorized the artificial pricing.
Dividend WHT Evasion through Constructive Distribution and Unreported Shareholder Loans

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the unpaid receivable/loan balance as a deemed dividend under GAAR and the beneficial-owner doctrine; WHT assessment at 10% (or 5% if treaty substance is proven) on the deemed amount plus 0.05% daily surcharge; administrative penalty up to 50% of the un-withheld tax; Article 47 EIT Law recharacterization and potential criminal sanction if foreign-exchange evasion is also established.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution by directors who authorized the cash-flow arrangement; SAFE foreign-exchange violation fines and individual travel restrictions; criminal liability for concealing foreign-exchange receipts under the Regulations on Foreign Exchange Administration where cross-border fund flows were disguised.
AML, Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Denial of the 5%/7.5% cross-strait treaty rates with WHT re-assessed at the full 10% statutory rate; AML institutional fines of up to RMB 2 million plus individual fines under the PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law; freezing orders on WFOE bank accounts; Criminal Law Article 191 money-laundering offense with fines and imprisonment of up to 10 years where collected funds are derived from fraudulent subscription schemes; delisting from e-tax normal-taxpayer status.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face personal asset forfeiture, travel bans, prohibition from acting as directors of any PRC entity, personal liability for the unpaid WHT and AML fines, and criminal prosecution for participation in a money-laundering or tax-evasion conspiracy.
