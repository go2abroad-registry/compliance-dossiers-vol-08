Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio with territorial jurisdiction over the company's registered domicile (e.g., Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá), operating through the national Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES) and the Ventanilla Única Empresarial (VUE).

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada, Arts. 1-50); Código de Comercio (Decreto 410 de 1971, Book II, Arts. 98-120 and 372-374); Decreto 1074 de 2015 (Régimen Único del Comercio); Circular Única de la Superintendencia de Sociedades.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation deed with full bylaws), elevated to Escritura Pública before a Notary only if real property is contributed as paid-in capital.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 11 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. Authorized capital must be fully subscribed at incorporation; the paid portion may be distributed in installments with a maximum deferral of two years (Ley 1258 de 2008, Art. 4). No escrow bank account is legally mandated; banking onboarding requires source-of-funds verification under anti-money-laundering rules.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety inspection and certificate from the municipal Cuerpo de Bomberos; structural and occupancy verification per NSR-10 seismic building regulations; Certificado de Uso del Suelo from the Curaduría Urbana confirming light-commercial warehouse use; mandatory workplace health and safety program (SG-SST) with risk classification registered with an Administradora de Riesgos Laborales (ARL) in accordance with Resolución 0312 de 2019.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — official portal www.dian.gov.co; taxpayer service portal MUISCA (muisca.dian.gov.co).

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor establishing a Colombia S.A.S. must execute incorporation via a public deed or private document filed with the Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio); the S.A.S. then must register with DIAN to obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and inscribe the RUT (Registro Único Tributario). The legal representative (extegratorily appointed by shareholders) must personally file the RUT application, declaring the company's address, business activities, and tax regime. Foreign shareholders are not required to have a local TIN for the company formation, but the S.A.S. as a domestic juridical person receives a unique NIT automatically upon DIAN approval; foreign direct investment must be registered with Banco de la República if financing is deposited in local currency.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (a S.A.S. is a capital company subject to Colombian corporate income tax at a 35% rate for 2024-2025; resident companies are taxed on worldwide income with specific source attribution rules, while branches and non-resident-owned entities without a permanent establishment are subject to more limited territorial sourcing). — A foreign investor establishing a Colombia S.A.S. must execute incorporation via a public deed or private document filed with the Chamber of Commerce (Cámara de Comercio); the S.A.S. then must register with DIAN to obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and inscribe the RUT (Registro Único Tributario). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (a S.A.S. is a capital company subject to Colombian corporate income tax at a 35% rate for 2024-2025; resident companies are taxed on worldwide income with specific source attribution rules, while branches and non-resident-owned entities without a permanent establishment are subject to more limited territorial sourcing).

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available to foreign entities for a Colombian S.A.S. The S.A.S. is irrevocably taxed as an entity under Colombian corporate income tax. Taxpayers meeting size and turnover limitations may opt into the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (SIMPLE) for a blended tax payment replacing CIT, VAT, and municipal industry and commerce tax, but off-price wholesale apparel traders of substantial scale are generally ineligible. Group companies can voluntarily prepare consolidated tax returns only when authorized by DIAN under strict common-control and substance requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No economic threshold is required for VAT registration; any natural or juridical person habitually engaged in the sale of goods, provision of services, or import of tangible goods must register in the RUT and obtain a specific VAT registration and/or e-invoicing authorization irrespective of turnover. For small merchants below COP 8 billion annual gross income, access to the SIMPLE regime may be available, but a standard S.A.S. apparel liquidation trader should maintain a full VAT registration as a sales taxpayer (responsable de IVA) once involved in taxable transactions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from Colombia to foreign markets qualify for a 0% VAT rate. The sale must meet the definition of an export under Decree 1165/2019 and the Customs Statute: goods must physically leave Colombian territory permanently, be documented in a duly submitted and confirmed export customs declaration (Declaración de Exportación - DEX), and the exporter must receive and collect the export proceeds through the Colombian foreign exchange market within the statutory period. For services rendered to non-residents for use abroad, a separate 0% regime applies. The off-price wholesale apparel trader must prove that the invoice corresponds to actual goods, the end-user is a foreign purchaser, and cargo has been dispatched to a foreign destination; if the goods are sold to a foreign purchaser but are ultimately consumed in Colombia, the zero-rating is denied.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX/Declaración de Exportación) approved by DIAN customs in the VUCE/VUPE system
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of goods from Colombian territory
  • Inward Foreign Exchange Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Reintegro) or documentary evidence of wire transfer showing payment of the export price from the purchaser to the exporter
  • Commercial invoice of export sale, marked with the export VAT exemption statement
  • Export packing list and certificate of origin (if applicable for FTA preference)
  • Mandatory annual export reconciliation report to the Grupo de Cumplimiento of DIAN

Since 2019, all VAT-responsible taxpayers in Colombia must issue electronic invoices (Factura Electrónica) through DIAN-approved technology providers; the invoicing system uses a 'CUDE' (Código Único de Documento Equivalente) or 'CUFE' (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) digital stamp and requires pre-validation by DIAN through the Muisca web portal or machine-to-machine API. Invoices must be transmitted in real time and may be subject to DIAN rejection before legal issuance; large taxpayers must also file daily export-related electronic declarations through the VUPE or foreign trade single window. Digital stamping is mandatory, with no general postponement for wholesale garment exporters; the unique transaction code is embedded in the invoice XML and validates the fiscal status to the recipient.

Export zero-rating is subject to rigorous ex-post audit by DIAN. The exporter must make the goods available for physical inspection and provide customs, banking, and currency exchange records; any inconsistency in the B/L, payment instrument, or declared quantity automatically generates a cross-screening mismatch. If DIAN rules that no genuine export occurred or a refund involved fraudulent documentation, the responsible party will be required to pay the over-credited VAT (calculated retroactively at the 19% rate), a penalty of up to 200% of the tax surcharge for fraudulent refund abuse, plus default interest. Criminal liability is triggered under Article 402 of the Colombian Criminal Code for fraudulent export refunds or smuggling; on conviction, the exporter faces imprisonment of 48 to 108 months and confiscation of the goods or assets. Additionally, false customs declarations are sanctioned under Law 1762/2015 with fines measured on the customs value of the goods and temporary or definitive suspension of the exporter's registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso is a floating currency, and Colombia maintains a largely liberalized capital-account regime. However, all transactions classified as exchange market operations under the Colombian foreign exchange code must be channeled through authorized intermediaries under the mandatory canalización obligatoria regime. Profit remittances are permissible for registered foreign direct investment without prior administrative approval, but the entity must ensure full tax compliance and file the required exchange declarations through the authorized bank. Conversion into COP is generally frictionless for licensed commercial operations, provided the underlying transaction and supporting documents are valid.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods exporters must collect export proceeds and channel them into the Colombian foreign exchange market through an authorized intermediary within six months of the export declaration date, unless Banco de la República grants a timely extension. The full amount of the export proceeds is subject to the reintegro obligation, meaning the funds must be sold to the market or placed in a permitted foreign-currency account if authorized. There is no partial conversion ratio, and the exporter cannot retain the foreign currency indefinitely outside the authorized market without falling under the statutory penalty regime.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia as the supervisor of authorized foreign exchange intermediaries and the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) as the customs-linked exchange control enforcement body

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low legal viability for core treasury operations. Offshore digital EMIs and fintech payment providers that are not authorized by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia cannot act as foreign exchange intermediaries, cannot register export proceeds, and cannot file Colombian exchange declarations. Such platforms may be used only for ancillary payments and are not acceptable substitutes for the mandatory canalización obligation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from non-resident shareholders must arrive by SWIFT transfer to the Colombian S.A.S. account at an authorized commercial bank. The bank will classify the incoming funds under the foreign-exchange declaration purpose code for foreign direct investment and file the corresponding cambial declaration with Banco de la República through the approved electronic system. Required supports include the share subscription agreement, updated Cámara de Comercio certificate, corporate resolution approving the capital increase or initial contribution, proof of source of funds, and the FDI registration certificate or registration application.Cross-border disbursements for inventory purchases and trade settlements must be executed through SWIFT MT103 via the authorized Colombian bank.
