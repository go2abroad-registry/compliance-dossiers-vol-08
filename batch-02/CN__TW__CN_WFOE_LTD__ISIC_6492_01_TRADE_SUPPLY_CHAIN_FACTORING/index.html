Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Mainland China: State Taxation Administration (国家税务总局, STA), with provincial and municipal Electronic Tax Bureaus (电子税务局); portal https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn and https://www.chinatax.gov.cn. Taiwan: Ministry of Finance, Taxation Administration (財政部賦稅署), administered locally by the National Taxation Bureaus (國稅局); portal https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw. For the PRC WFOE, the operative registration portal is the STA electronic tax bureau of the jurisdiction where the WFOE is registered.

E-government filing portal

For the Chinese WFOE, the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, USCC) on the SAMR business license is the statutory taxpayer identification number under the multi-certificate integration system; no separate tax registration certificate is issued. The WFOE must: (1) complete taxpayer registration in the STA electronic tax bureau within 30 days of business license issuance; (2) verify VAT taxpayer type and elect or apply for general VAT taxpayer status if annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 or if voluntary classification is beneficial; (3) file banking deposit account information, accounting standards, and legal representative/CFO identification; (4) apply for e-invoice quotas and digital tax certificates; (5) register with the local National Taxation Bureau in Taiwan for an 8-digit Profit-seeking Enterprise Uniform Number (營利事業統一編號) only if a Taiwan fixed place of business or PE is established. In the absence of a Taiwan PE, Taiwan-source factoring fees payable to the WFOE may be subject to Taiwan withholding tax and case-by-case non-resident tax registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The PRC WFOE is a resident enterprise taxed as a corporation on worldwide income under the Corporate Income Tax Law, and it is a VAT taxpayer in its own right. It is not pass-through and is not eligible for check-the-box flow-through treatment. A Taiwan branch or PE, if any, is subject to Taiwan Profit-seeking Enterprise Income Tax on Taiwan-source income under Taiwan's territorial rules; no special tax-exempt zone status is presumed for the WFOE.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election is available in Mainland China or Taiwan for a WFOE. In the PRC, the principal statutory elections are: small-scale VAT taxpayer treatment where annual turnover is below RMB 5,000,000, versus voluntary general VAT taxpayer registration; CIT preferential elections such as high-tech enterprise 15% rate, encouraged-industry concession, and accelerated depreciation/amortization. In Taiwan, a Taiwan PE may in limited non-resident cases elect deemed-profits taxation rather than actual-income assessment if books are inadequate, but the WFOE itself cannot elect transparent treatment; hybrid mismatch outcomes are constrained by PRC CFC rules, Taiwan's CFC/Anti-hybrid legislation, and BEPS Action 2, with no standalone taxpayer election to alter entity classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

China: every enterprise is required to register for tax/VAT at incorporation irrespective of turnover; there is no zero-turnover de minimis for a juridical person. Mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration arises once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold, small-scale taxpayer treatment applies (currently a 3% levy with temporary reduced rates as published). Taiwan: a profit-seeking enterprise must register for Business Tax before commencing business, and there is no turnover threshold for company registration; only small-scale sole proprietors may use the simplified registration threshold of monthly taxable sales below NT$300,000, which is not available to a WFOE or corporate branch.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A PRC general VAT taxpayer may claim zero-rating and input-VAT refund for physical goods exported from the PRC customs territory if the goods are actually shipped abroad, title passes to a non-resident buyer, export proceeds are collected within the statutory FX repatriation period, and the complete export declaration/invoice/transport/payment chain is evidenced. Cross-border factoring, reverse factoring, and trade-finance services may qualify for zero-rating or VAT exemption only if the services are expressly within the cross-border services catalogue in Caishui 2016 No. 36 and annexes, are consumed entirely outside China, and are provided to an overseas recipient without a PRC establishment; otherwise the services remain taxable at 6%. Taiwan: exported goods and services wholly used or consumed outside Taiwan are zero-rated or exempt from Business Tax when evidenced by export declarations, contracts, invoices, and foreign-exchange receipts.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口货物报关单 / Customs Export Goods Declaration Form)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (B/L or AWB evidencing export shipment)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Collection Certificate (出口收汇核销)
  • Export sales contract, commercial invoice, and packing list
  • VAT special invoice (增值税专用发票) or export invoice evidencing prior input tax
  • Customs valuation, HS classification, and pricing documentation
  • Proof of offshore service consumption and non-resident recipient status for cross-border factoring/services, where applicable

China: mandatory digital e-invoicing through the STA 'fully digitalized electronic invoice' (全面数字化的电子发票) system using XML/OFD format; general VAT taxpayers must issue special VAT invoices through tax-authority-controlled systems with digital signatures and instantaneous transmission to the STA. ERP/API integration or tax-UKey issuance is required in practice; red-letter/negative invoices must be processed through the e-tax platform before the seller may offset output VAT. Taiwan: profit-seeking enterprises must issue statutory Uniform Invoices (統一發票) for Taiwan business receipts, upload e-invoice data to the e-invoice integration service platform (電子發票整合服務平台), and comply with quarterly filing, invoice serial numbering, and digital stamping/QR-code requirements. Taiwan does not operate a full real-time pre-clearance VAT system, but the platform provides near-real-time invoice-data exchange and audit visibility.

China: fraudulent zero-rating or export-refund claims trigger immediate clawback of refunded VAT and disgorgement of input credits, a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, administrative penalties of 50% to five times the tax under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law, customs fines and confiscation under the Customs Law, and criminal liability for export-tax-refund fraud under Article 204 of the Criminal Law (imprisonment up to life and confiscation for especially large amounts). The trade-finance documentation used to substantiate VAT refunds is also subject to anti-money-laundering and SAFE foreign-exchange scrutiny. Taiwan: false export zero-rating or refund claims cause recapture of tax, late-payment interest, statutory fines (including penalties up to multiple times the tax involved where evasion is established), and criminal prosecution for tax evasion under the Taiwan Tax Evasion and Penalty framework; cross-border supporting records are additionally subject to joint audit and information exchange.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi is generally convertible for bona fide current-account transactions, including eligible trade payments, service payments, and profit distributions, subject to bank review, supporting documents, tax compliance, customs or trade data, and applicable foreign-exchange reporting. Capital-account transactions, including foreign-invested enterprise capital contributions, outbound investments, debt financing, guarantees, and certain intercompany flows, remain regulated and may require registration, quota, filing, bank review, or SAFE approval. A WFOE may normally convert distributable after-tax profits and remittance amounts, but must first complete statutory audit and tax settlement, satisfy withholding-tax obligations, document board or shareholder approval, and establish that the remittance is not an unauthorized capital withdrawal or disguised related-party payment. Cross-border transactions involving Taiwan-related counterparties may receive enhanced bank, sanctions, anti-money-laundering, commercial-substance, and cross-Strait regulatory scrutiny.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no general PRC rule requiring ordinary enterprises to mandatorily convert a fixed percentage of export proceeds or surrender all export foreign exchange within a universal statutory period. Eligible export proceeds may generally be retained in a foreign-exchange current account or converted at the enterprise's discretion, subject to SAFE rules, bank verification, customs and trade-data consistency, and enterprise foreign-exchange classification. Banks may require export declarations, commercial contracts, invoices, shipping documents, customs records, collection evidence, and transaction-purpose information. Enterprises classified as Category B or C may face enhanced review, restricted settlement procedures, or additional documentation. Export proceeds used for factoring, reverse factoring, or related-party settlement must remain traceable to the underlying trade and must not be used to disguise capital-account inflows, unauthorized lending, or circular funds. The applicable bank should confirm current SAFE implementation rules and any transaction-specific reporting or settlement deadline before execution.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), together with the People's Bank of China for monetary, payment-system, and capital-account settlement matters. Commercial banks act as designated foreign-exchange banks and conduct transaction-level authenticity and compliance reviews.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium. An offshore electronic-money institution cannot ordinarily replace a PRC domestic bank for a WFOE's RMB basic account, capital account, foreign-exchange settlement account, statutory tax payments, payroll, or regulated factoring collections. Offshore EMI wallets may support limited vendor or customer payments only where the provider is licensed for the relevant corridor and the transaction is processed through lawful cross-border payment arrangements. The WFOE must not use an offshore wallet to bypass SAFE registration, bank authenticity review, capital-account restrictions, foreign-exchange reporting, beneficial-ownership checks, or PRC payment licensing requirements. Taiwan-related wallet and payment arrangements require separate review of cross-Strait restrictions and the provider's authorization in both relevant jurisdictions.

Defense evidence: Certified corporate registry extracts for every ownership layer; constitutional documents; ownership and control chart to the natural-person UBO; passports and residential evidence; director and shareholder resolutions; notarized or legalized documents where required; explanation of Taiwan-related ownership and control; sanctions and politically exposed person screening results.
Factoring activity appears to constitute unlicensed lending, deposit-taking, payment services, or banking business.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Taiwan-related wallet and payment arrangements require separate review of cross-Strait restrictions and the provider's authorization in both relevant jurisdictions.
