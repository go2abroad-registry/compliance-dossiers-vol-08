Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — official website: https://www.dian.gov.co; operational portal: MUISCA (https://muisca.dian.gov.co), including the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) subsystem.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Colombian S.A.S. must register with DIAN before commencing taxable operations. The procedure is: (i) incorporate the S.A.S. before a notary and register it with the local Chamber of Commerce, (ii) obtain digital signature (firma electrónica) for its legal representative, (iii) apply for RUT inscription (inscripción en el RUT) through the MUISCA portal or a DIAN service center, submitting the certificate of existence, the bylaws, and an apostilled/power-of-attorney for foreign shareholders or administrators, appointing a Colombian legal representative for tax purposes as applicable, and (iv) receive the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which is the single statutory tax identification number. VAT registration as responsable del IVA and withholding-agent registration are contained in the same RUT/NIT profile.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Colombian S.A.S. must register with DIAN before commencing taxable operations. The procedure is: (i) incorporate the S.A.S. before a notary and register it with the local Chamber of Commerce, (ii) obtain digital signature (firma electrónica) for its legal representative, (iii) apply for RUT inscription (inscripción en el RUT) through the MUISCA portal or a DIAN service center, submitting the certificate of existence, the bylaws, and an apostilled/power-of-attorney for foreign shareholders or administrators, appointing a Colombian legal representative for tax purposes as applicable, and (iv) receive the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which is the single statutory tax identification number. VAT registration as responsable del IVA and withholding-agent registration are contained in the same RUT/NIT profile.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or foreign hybrid classification election exists under Colombian domestic law. The S.A.S. is mandatorily taxed at entity level as a resident corporation on its worldwide income. The only material elective option relevant to this manufacturing scope is the optional ‘Régimen Simple de Tributación’ (SIMPLES) for eligible very small taxpayers; no hybrid mismatch bunching/subpart F style elections are available to foreign shareholders.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No Colombian peso turnover threshold applies. Every legal entity carrying out taxable acts such as selling goods, producing or importing taxable food products, or rendering services must register in the RUT/NIT as responsable de IVA before commencing operations; registration is unconditional for a commercial manufacturer regardless of annual gross receipts.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for the zero rating/exclusion from Colombian IVA when: (i) the S.A.S. is registered as an exporter in the RUT; (ii) the goods are sold to a foreign buyer and physically leave Colombian customs territory; (iii) the corresponding export declaration (DAE) is presented and accepted through DIAN’s customs systems; (iv) the export invoice identifies the foreign customer and states the export destination; and (v) the export proceeds are channeled through and settled in the Colombian foreign exchange market within the legal term. Input VAT borne on export-oriented production is creditable; excess credits may be refunded after DIAN review.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DAE – Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport document evidencing physical departure from Colombian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / foreign exchange settlement evidence issued by the Colombian intermediary bank

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta) is required for all S.A.S. commercial taxpayers. Invoices must be generated in XML with a unique CUF (Código Único de Facturación), an embedded QR code, and sent in real time to DIAN’s online validation and clearance services (MUISCA / authorized electronic invoicing platform). DIAN processes the document at the moment of transmission; acceptance, rejection or error staging is returned digitally. Physical dry stamps are no longer used; proof of VAT treatment is the DIAN-validated XML and corresponding acknowledgement.

DIAN performs substantive audit verification of export invoices, customs declarations, physical shipment evidence and FX settlement. Fraudulent or improperly substantiated export zero-rating followed by an input VAT refund triggers: (i) mandatory retrospective clawback of the refunded amount with updated default interest; (ii) monetary penalties of up to 100% of the inaccurately claimed excess refund under the Colombian Tax Code; (iii) suspension, linked-benefits denial or cancellation of the exporter RUT status; and (iv) criminal tax fraud exposure under the Colombian Tax Code Article 647 and contraband-related prosecutions when forged customs documentation is used. The ordinary statutory prescription period for DIAN to assess and recollect is 5 taxable years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a managed-float foreign exchange regime. Current-account transactions are substantially convertible, but capital-account operations, including foreign direct investment and cross-border financing, must be channelled through authorized financial intermediaries and registered with Banco de la República. Profit and dividend remittances from a Colombian S.A.S. are legally permitted once the relevant corporate tax obligations, annual registrations, and foreign-exchange declarations are duly filed, and the investment has been properly registered in the Registro de Inversiones Internacionales.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Decree 1735 of 1993, exporters of goods must channel the full invoice value of their proceeds through the Colombian foreign exchange market within six (6) months from the date of shipment. There is no stipulation of partial surrender; the proceeds must be repatriated or placed in an authorized local foreign-currency compensation account (cuenta de compensación) before conversion to Colombian pesos. Each collection is evidenced by the corresponding foreign-exchange declaration and linked to the customs export declaration (DEX/DUS).

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (including the Comisión de Cambios for foreign exchange regulation)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-conditioned feasibility. Non-resident digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as global fintech payment platforms are not authorised by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia and cannot hold local COP-currency accounts, provide domestic clearing, or issue local IBAN-type routing codes. They may support off-shore treasury layers by executing cross-border supplier payments outside Colombia, but all statutory cash flows, payroll, tax payments, and regulated capital injections must pass through a locally licenced financial intermediary. Feasibility rating: Low for domestic treasury, Medium as an ancillary cross-border disbursement rail for low-value overseas supplier payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital contributions from non-resident shareholders must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 to an account held with an authorised Colombian commercial bank. The remittance instructions must include the S.A.S. tax ID (NIT), the legal name of the investee, and the foreign investment registration number assigned by Banco de la República. The bank will receive the funds, verify the underlying board/shareholders' resolution approving the capital increase, collect the mandatory foreign-exchange declaration, and credit the capital escrow account. The foreign investment must be registered under Banco de la República's Registro de Inversiones Internacionales before the funds are used for working capital or trade disbursements, utilising the applicable concept code for direct foreign investment in Colombia.Trade settlements to overseas raw-material suppliers will use SWIFT MT103 for telegraphic transfers and documentary commercial letters of credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707) for large import consignments, particularly where phytosanitary certificates and pre-shipment inspection are warranted. Domestic peso clearing for suppliers in Colombia should run via the local ACH (ACH Colombia) or the CUD high-value cheque and electronic clearing system offered by Banco de la República.
