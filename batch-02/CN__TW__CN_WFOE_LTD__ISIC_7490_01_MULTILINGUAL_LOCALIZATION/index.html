Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Taiwan (Tax Administration); operational portal: National Taxation Portal (e-tax portal at etax.nat.gov.tw)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested entity incorporated in Taiwan (e.g., a branch or subsidiary) must complete company registration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs, after which the Taiwan tax authority automatically issues a Business Administration Number (BAN, uniform invoice number). The BAN serves as the VAT registration number. The entity must then register for VAT (Business Tax) with the local National Taxation Bureau, obtain a unified invoice registration, and set up tax filing credentials through the e-tax portal. Non-resident foreign enterprises without a fixed place of business apply for temporary Tax ID numbers via withholding agent registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-invested entity incorporated in Taiwan (e.g., a branch or subsidiary) must complete company registration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs, after which the Taiwan tax authority automatically issues a Business Administration Number (BAN, uniform invoice number). The BAN serves as the VAT registration number. The entity must then register for VAT (Business Tax) with the local National Taxation Bureau, obtain a unified invoice registration, and set up tax filing credentials through the e-tax portal. Non-resident foreign enterprises without a fixed place of business apply for temporary Tax ID numbers via withholding agent registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

TAIWAN CHECK-THE-BOX UNDER INCOME TAX ACT: No general check-the-box regime for entity classification. Hybrid mismatch rules under the Income Tax Act and the Company Act may recharacterize payments or entities where treaty/tax outcomes are inconsistent. Elective application of qualified foreign intermediary rules for withholding, and elective consolidated business tax filing for affiliated companies under the Business Tax Act are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold for VAT registration; any enterprise selling goods or services in Taiwan, or importing goods, must register as a business tax entity. Small-scale businesses (monthly turnover below TWD 80,000 for goods or TWD 40,000 for services) may apply for a reduced rate of 1% subject to approval.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act to the export of goods, provision of services to foreign enterprises or non-residents where the services are used entirely outside Taiwan, exported services related to goods located outside Taiwan, and sales of goods/services in connection with offshore trade. For multilingual localization services provided to foreign clients, zero-rating is available where the service benefits a foreign recipient; supporting documentation must prove the foreign status of the client and that the service is not used in Taiwan.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for goods)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (for goods)
  • Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate (Inward Remittance Certificate)
  • Foreign-client contract and invoice (for services)
  • Statement certifying the service is used/provided outside Taiwan

Mandatory electronic uniform invoices (eUI) for most VAT-registered businesses. Integration with the MOF e-invoice platform; exporters and B2B service providers must transmit invoice details digitally. Compliance via Turnkey network protocols, JSON/XML APIs, and platform certificates. No real-time fiscal stamping in the EU/CIS sense; filing is periodic with real-time submission of e-invoice data to the national platform.

Fraudulent or incorrect zero-rating claims trigger: (i) recovery of unpaid tax, (ii) a fine of up to 15 times the amount of the undercollected tax under the Business Tax Act, (iii) penalty for failure to issue or preserve uniform invoices, and (iv) criminal liability for tax evasion/forgery of documentation under the Tax Evasion and Tax Collection Law, including imprisonment up to 5 years. Retroactive clawback periods apply for up to 7 years for executions and up to 15 years for tax evasion-related penalties.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is freely convertible for current-account items, including cross-border service-trade receipts and profit remittance, upon genuine-transaction documentary verification; capital-account items remain restricted and flow under a registration-based regime. For this Taiwan-invested CN WFOE: (i) inbound FDI registered capital is admissible under the Foreign Investment Law and the Negative List regime through a bank-mediated SAFE FDI onboarding registration, evidenced by the Business Registration Certificate; (ii) the 2015 voluntary settlement reform (SAFE HuiFa [2015] No. 19) permits the WFOE to convert FDI capital into RMB at its discretion into a settlement-pending account, subject to a negative-use list excluding equity out-investment, securities purchases, unrelated real estate investment, and entrusted loans to third parties; (iii) profit distribution to the Taiwan shareholder is a current-account remittance payable after (a) the annual statutory audit, (b) corporate income tax final settlement, (c) assessment and payment of 10% dividend withholding tax on the non-resident Taiwan shareholder, noting that the 2015 Cross-Strait Anti-Double-Taxation Arrangement is not in force and treaty-reduced rates are not reliably available, (d) a valid board or shareholders' resolution, and (e) electronic tax filing for cross-border payments when the remittance exceeds USD 50,000; (iv) TWD is not freely convertible onshore, and all WFOE contracts and invoices should be denominated in USD or RMB to avoid TWD liquidity friction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since the SAFE 2012 Reform Announcement and the 2008 Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, there is no mandatory surrender ratio or forced conversion timeline for service-trade export proceeds. A localization WFOE exporting multilingual translation and dubbing services must: (i) repatriate service-trade receipts into China through its bank-designated current-account foreign-currency operating account within the contractual payment terms; (ii) permit the receiving bank to verify contract-invoice-delivery consistency and to record the receipt in SAFE's cross-border receipts and payments monitoring system; (iii) retain the right to hold the foreign currency balance in USD or to settle voluntarily at its own discretion; and (iv) ensure that receivables are remitted by the invoiced client, or by a formally designated remitter with an instruction letter, to prevent false-trade or false-receipts compliance flags.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, exercising delegated authority from the People's Bank of China (PBOC) over onshore foreign-exchange administration, settlement and sale, and balance-of-payments declaration; PBOC operates the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS) for RMB clearing. The Taiwan leg is subject to the Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) and the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) at the outward remittance source, with Bank of China Taipei Branch acting as the designated cross-strait RMB clearing bank.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: Under Article 208 of the PRC Company Law (2023 revision), every limited liability company must prepare annual financial statements and have them audited by a licensed accounting firm; no revenue or asset threshold exempts the WFOE. A formal statutory audit is in any event required where the entity claims tax loss carry-forwards in the annual CIT settlement, distributes dividends, reduces capital, undergoes share transfer with valuation, or provides audited statements under bank covenants. Small-scale accounting treatment (Small Enterprise Accounting Standards) is available only to qualifying domestic micro and small enterprises; a WFOE serving cross-border clients must apply full Accounting Standards for Business Enterprises (CAS).
