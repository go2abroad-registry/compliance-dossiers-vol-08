Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) - Colombian national tax and customs administration. Official portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional platform: MUISCA (muisca.dian.gov.co); 'Sede Electrónica' for electronic filing, RUT management and e-invoice validation.

E-government filing portal

STEP 1: Incorporate the S.A.S. under Law 1258/2008 by private document, register with the competent Cámara de Comercio and obtain the Certificado de Cámara de Comercio. STEP 2: File the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) application with DIAN via the MUISCA online portal or at a DIAN 'punto de contacto'; non-resident shareholders must appoint a resident legal/tax representative under a notarized power of attorney. STEP 3: DIAN issues the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) and a formal RUT certificate classifying the company as 'responsable de IVA' and merchant. STEP 4: Obtain a DIAN-recognized digital signature ('firma electrónica') required for mandatory electronic filing and e-invoicing. A foreign seller transacting in its own name for a Colombian taxable nexus or permanent establishment must likewise obtain a NIT/RUT through an appointed agent before doing business.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: Colombian-resident S.A.S. (Law 1258/2008) is a separate taxpaying entity with no pass-through status. It is taxed on worldwide income at the 35% corporate income tax rate for tax years 2023-2025; no check-the-box election exists to be treated as a flow-through. Municipal industry-and-commerce tax (ICA) applies to trading gross income but is deductible for corporate income tax. Dividends remitted to foreign shareholders are subject to dividend withholding tax, subject to treaty relief. Territorial concessions are available only through the statutory regimes described in the elections field.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election is available. Statutory options include: (1) SIMPLE regime (Arts. 905-922 E.T.) for qualifying small taxpayers (gross income ≤ 100,000 UVT, approx. COP 4.98 billion in 2025), substituting ordinary income tax/VAT obligations; (2) Zona Franca user status authorized by DIAN, taxing qualifying operating income at 20% (Law 1004/2005, Decree 2147/2016); (3) Colombian Holding Company (CHC) regime under Art. 895 E.T., exempting qualifying foreign dividends and capital gains where the S.A.S. holds ≥10% of foreign entities, reports ≥50% foreign-source assets or income, and meets economic substance rules; and (4) fiscal consolidation (Arts. 885-897 E.T.) for Colombian groups. Hybrid mismatch rules introduced by Law 2277/2022 are mandatory and non-elective.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

COP 0 - no statutory turnover threshold for the ordinary IVA regime. Any person making taxable sales, rendering taxable services or importing goods must register in the RUT and be classified as 'responsable de IVA' from the first transaction. The only quantitative threshold is for the optional SIMPLE regime: gross income ≤ 100,000 UVT (approx. COP 4,980,000,000 for 2025; 2025 UVT = COP 49,799).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Article 481 E.T. zero-rates exports of tangible goods (e.g., surplus commercial aircraft rotables and avionics) and qualifying cross-border services. Conditions for goods exports: (i) the S.A.S. is registered as an exporter with DIAN; (ii) a definitive export is completed under the Colombian customs regime with a valid Declaración de Exportación; (iii) the buyer is non-resident and title/passage occurs outside Colombia; (iv) payment is received in foreign currency through the Colombian foreign-exchange market. Zero-rating permits full recovery or refund of input IVA attributable to export purchases. Sales of spare parts and supplies to international air carriers for international operations may also qualify for zero-rating under Article 481(f). Services-export zero-rating requires additional documentary compliance under Decree 1625/2016, Arts. 1.6.1.12.13-1.6.1.12.20 (foreign recipient, use exclusively abroad, evidence of FX settlement).

  • Declaración de Exportación Definitiva (customs export declaration issued through DIAN/VUCE electronic systems)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Colombia
  • Factura Electrónica de Venta de Exportación (electronic export invoice containing CUFE and referencing the export declaration)
  • Declaración de Cambio / Inward Remittance Certificate issued by an authorized foreign-exchange intermediary
  • Certificate of Origin, where preferential tariff treatment is claimed or where DIAN requires it
  • Registro Nacional de Exportadores (valid DIAN exporter registration)

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica de Venta - FE) applies to all IVA-responsible taxpayers. Under DIAN Resolutions 000012/2023 and 000165/2023, each FE must be transmitted in XML to DIAN's MUISCA/Sede Electrónica platforms through a registered technology provider or DIAN's free web issuer (with API integration), validated in real time, and embedded with the CUFE/CUDS code and QR digital-stamping element. The invoice is cleared online before issuance to the buyer; electronic equivalent documents are required for certain purchases from non-responsible parties. Taxpayers must transmit acceptance/rejection events and retain records for at least 5 years. Export invoices must reference the customs export declaration.

DIAN verifies every export VAT refund claim (solicitud de devolución y/o compensación) and may audit the supporting customs, transport and foreign-exchange documentation for up to 5 years after the annual return due date. If a refund is determined improper, Article 670 E.T. imposes a penalty equal to 20% of the refunded amount, plus daily default interest under Article 634 E.T. and monetary indexation under Article 867 E.T.; overstated refunds may also trigger the 100% 'inexactitud' penalty under Article 647 E.T. Falsified export declarations, fabricated bills of lading or simulated FE transactions carry criminal liability under Law 1762/2015 (customs fraud and smuggling), including asset-seizure and money-laundering exposure. DIAN may additionally offset clawbacks against future refunds, suspend the exporter's registration, and reassess underlying transactions by presumption where commercial substance is lacking.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is fully convertible for current-account transactions, including trade-related settlements through an authorized foreign exchange market intermediary ('IMC'). Capital-account transactions, including inbound foreign direct investment into a Colombian S.A.S., are permitted but require prior or subsequent registration with Banco de la República through the foreign exchange declaration forms. Profit and dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted after the applicable tax form and the corresponding foreign exchange declaration have been filed. Surplus commercial aircraft parts distribution, classified as ISIC 4659, constitutes a goods-import/export activity for exchange purposes, and therefore all payments for imports and collections from exports must be channelled through the official foreign exchange market. The sale of used/certified aircraft rotables is not treated as a financial flow in itself and does not require a separate convertibility license; however, the underlying trade must evidence LC, commercial invoice, and customs declaration to support the exchange transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, the exporter must channel the full export proceeds to Colombia through an authorized foreign exchange intermediary within the statutory period of six (6) months after the date of shipment, or within the period expressly extended by Banco de la República in case of documented collective financing cycles. The proceeds must either be converted to COP at a market-determined rate through the IMC, or be credited to a foreign currency deposit account held with a local commercial bank supervised by the Superintendencia Financiera. The exporter must present the corresponding 'Declaración de Cambio' (Exchange Declaration) to the IMC, together with the airborne export or customs exit certificate, commercial invoice, bill of lading or airway bill, and proof of payment. There is no legal mandatory percentage conversion ratio; the entire export value is subject to the channeling obligation, but the exporter is free to hold the foreign currency in a local foreign-currency account if such account has been duly opened with an authorized bank. The use of compensating arrangements for aviation parts swaps is permissible only under a prior approval from the Banco de la República's exchange office.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia) acting through the foreign exchange market administration and the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia, with policy coordination from the Ministerio de Comercio, Industria y Turismo for export-related matters

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for the physical Colombian treasury layer. Colombian law reserves the taking of deposits, issuance of payment instruments, and the provision of settlement/fx services to supervised banking institutions. Offshore Financial Technology companies and Electronic Money Institutions cannot independently hold COP or settle through the Central Clearing System (CENIT) or the ACH system. An EMI, such as an EU-based e-money institution or a U.S. Money Transmitter, may be used only as a complementary invoicing or cross-border collection gateway in USD/GBP/EUR, provided the ultimate settlement enters Colombia through a local authorized bank. Any attempt to structure liquidity through an unlicensed EMI would trigger enhanced scrutiny by the Superintendencia Financiera and potentially classify the operation as an illegal handling of public funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound foreign direct investment into the Colombian S.A.S. must be routed through an IMC in Colombia using SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 if between banks) from the investor's overseas bank to the local bank. The payment instruction must carry the correct purpose code for direct investment in Colombia, and the beneficiary bank will request submission of the 'Declaración de Cambio de Inversión Internacional' (Form 3) or the simplified Form 3 if the investment is under the automatic authorization threshold. The bank will verify the nature of the contribution (cash versus in-kind), the source of funds, the beneficial ownership statement, and the value of the issued shares. Liquid injected capital may initially rest in a non-interest bearing escrow/current account pending completion of the companies registration and the issuance of a certificate of exchange registration by Banco de la República. Bank transfers must identify the Colombian S.A.S. RUT number and the exact corporate reference to avoid classification as a loan subject to Form 3 D-1.Outbound settlements for procurement of surplus aircraft parts from overseas suppliers are executed through SWIFT MT103 over correspondent bank relationships with the local commercial bank, or through the documentary letter of credit ('Carta de Crédito Comercial') governed by ICC UCP600 rules. The invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, insurance certificate, and any airworthiness release (FAA 8130-3/EASA Form 1) must be presented through the local bank for compliance screening. For parts purchases requiring escrow, the local bank or an authorized trust company may open a controlled escrow account in COP or USD, disbursing only upon delivery confirmation by a certified aviation inspector. Payments directly to offshore escrow agents are permissible when licensed under Colombian exchange regulations and supported by foreign exchange Form 4.
