Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A Chinese WFOE must maintain a fixed, physical, verifiable registered office within the administrative district where the business license is issued. A pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally rejected by the market supervision administration, tax authorities, social insurance authorities, and banks for account opening. In approved free-trade zones or recognized business incubators, a shared or incubator address may be accepted, but the lease must be in the company's name, the premises must be available for inspection, and the address must support actual business operations. For a medium-risk VPN and digital encryption SaaS company, banks routinely require a physical site visit, on-site signage, lease verification, and proof of personnel and equipment before opening settlement accounts. Use of a non-conforming virtual address materially increases the risk of tax registration denial, bank account refusal, and administrative penalty.

No statutory minimum paid-in capital for a Taiwan limited liability company. Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (財政部), Taxation Administration (賦稅署); frontline enforcement by the five regional National Taxation Bureaus (國稅局). Official portal: Ministry of Finance Tax e-Portal (財政部稅務入口網) at https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw, including the dedicated Cross-Border Electronic Services VAT registration platform.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested Taiwan subsidiary (functional WFOE) must first complete Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) company registration, then register for the 8-digit Unified Business Number (營利事業統一編號) and VAT status with the local National Taxation Bureau, accompanied by the responsible person's identity documents, articles/registration approval, and place-of-business proof. A foreign enterprise with no Taiwan fixed place of business but selling cross-border electronic services (VPN/SaaS) to Taiwan individuals must file the online cross-border e-service VAT registration via the MOF e-tax platform, appoint a Taiwan fiscal representative or licensed tax agent, obtain a Unified Business Number, and thereafter file simplified quarterly VAT returns and issue electronic receipts.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (with Territorial_Scope for the non-resident foreign seller leg): The Taiwan WFOE is a resident 'profit-seeking enterprise' taxed at 20% corporate income tax on worldwide income under Income Tax Act Article 3. Dividends paid to the Chinese parent are subject to 21% non-resident withholding; the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Arrangement is signed but not yet in force, so the domestic withholding rate applies. Undistributed retained earnings may incur a 5% unappropriated-surplus tax under Article 66-9. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (with Territorial_Scope for the non-resident foreign seller leg): The Taiwan WFOE is a resident 'profit-seeking enterprise' taxed at 20% corporate income tax on worldwide income under Income Tax Act Article 3. Dividends paid to the Chinese parent are subject to 21% non-resident withholding; the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Arrangement is signed but not yet in force, so the domestic withholding rate applies. Undistributed retained earnings may incur a 5% unappropriated-surplus tax under Article 66-9. Foreign enterprises without a Taiwan fixed place of business are taxed only on Taiwan-source income, generally on a withholding basis.

Available election forms

Taiwan has no US-style check-the-box regime; pass-through treatment is unavailable to limited companies and classification follows the Company Act and Income Tax Act. Available statutory elections: (i) organizational-form election between a Taiwan subsidiary and a branch of the foreign parent at MOEA registration; (ii) small-business operator election for 1% VAT when monthly sales are below NT$200,000 (goods) or NT$100,000 (services); (iii) election by foreign cross-border e-service providers to file simplified quarterly VAT returns, with a deemed-tax-base method acceptable when accounts are incomplete; (iv) Blue Return election (accountant-attested) to access certain tax preferences; (v) no hybrid-mismatch or check-the-box election exists, and cross-border financing structures creating deductible/non-deductible mismatches may be recharacterized under Taiwan's general anti-avoidance rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic businesses: VAT registration is compulsory before commencing any taxable business, with no general monetary exemption. Small-scale operators with monthly turnover below NT$200,000 (goods) or NT$100,000 (services) may be taxed at 1% but remain subject to registration. Foreign cross-border electronic-service suppliers with no Taiwan fixed establishment: mandatory registration when Taiwan B2C turnover exceeds NT$480,000 in the immediately preceding 12 months; below-threshold suppliers may register voluntarily, while B2B-only foreign suppliers are cleared through the domestic customer's reverse-charge mechanism.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Business Tax Act Article 7 to (a) exported goods and (b) services supplied from Taiwan that are used abroad or directly connected with exported goods. For the VPN / network-privacy SaaS, the Taiwan WFOE must evidence that subscribers are located outside Taiwan, the service is economically enjoyed abroad (offshore server deployment, foreign geo-IP consumption records), and consideration is received in foreign currency from non-resident counterparties. Services effectively used in Taiwan, rendered to Taiwan-domiciled individuals, or consumed for onshore network access are taxable at 5%; a mixed domestic/foreign subscriber base requires apportionment of zero-rated and taxable turnover.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) and customs-approved manifest for any tangible hardware or pre-loaded firewall/VPN appliances exported
  • Export sales contract or order evidencing the offshore service scope and payment terms
  • Foreign-currency inward remittance certificate (外匯收入結匯證明書) or bank debit/credit advice proving collection of export proceeds
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or courier receipts for physical exports, substantiating shipment out of the Taiwan customs territory
  • Digital-use substantiation pack: overseas server deployment records, subscriber geo-location/IP logs, and non-resident customer certifications demonstrating use abroad

VAT-registered taxpayers must issue Uniform Invoices (統一發票). Electronic Uniform Invoices (電子統一發票) must be transmitted through Ministry of Finance-certified Value-Added Service Centers or the Turnkey direct-connection interface to the E-Invoice Integrated Service Platform (財政部電子發票整合服務平台). B2C cloud invoices must upload within 48 hours of issuance and B2B e-invoices within 7 days; the platform enforces serial-number control and real-time receipt authentication, with no separate fiscal stamping or cryptographic stamp duty. Foreign cross-border e-service suppliers under the simplified regime issue electronic receipts instead of a Uniform Invoice sequence and file quarterly returns on the dedicated e-service platform.

Zero-rating claims are documented per filing period; failure to assemble the substantiation bundle by the statutory filing deadline triggers automatic disallowance and a 5% VAT assessment. The National Taxation Bureau may retrospectively reassess within 5 years, extended to 7 years for fraudulent or concealed claims, charge daily tax-arrears interest, impose administrative penalties up to 300% of the evaded tax (with higher multiples for deliberate invoice forgery or sham export schemes), and refer criminal tax-evasion prosecutions carrying imprisonment of up to 5 years. Repeated or fictitious export zero-rating arrangements may additionally bar the taxpayer from using the simplified cross-border e-service regime and trigger withholding-based collection for the foreign seller leg.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates under partial current account convertibility for trade-related services and digital SaaS transactions, permitting WFOE profit remittances after SAFE approval and tax clearance. Capital account transactions remain non-convertible, requiring prior SAFE registration for inbound capital and outbound profit repatriation, with documented hurdles including beneficial ownership verification and absence of sanctions exposure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Digital service receipts must be surrendered to designated banks within five working days of receipt under current account rules, mandating conversion at the prevailing exchange rate unless SAFE grants specific exemption for strategic technology exports; failure triggers administrative penalties and potential license suspension.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for offshore digital EMIs due to PBOC and SAFE prohibitions on unlicensed cross-border electronic money issuance; Chinese WFOE must route through licensed domestic channels or obtain explicit SAFE approval for any offshore wallet integration, subject to full AML/CFT compliance and capital controls.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard Enterprise Income Tax rate for resident enterprises; 20% statutory rate for small low-profit enterprises with effective reduction.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% withholding tax on gross dividends paid to a non-resident corporate shareholder without a Chinese permanent establishment, under PRC EIT Law Articles 3 and 37 and SAT Public Notice 37/2015. Dividends to a non-resident individual are subject to 20% withholding under the Individual Income Tax Law unless treaty-reduced.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The tax year is the calendar year. The annual return must be filed and tax settled within 5 months after year-end, i.e., no later than May 31 of the following calendar year. A filing extension may be applied for before the deadline under the Tax Collection and Administration Law, but the payment obligation is preserved.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly prepayments are due within 15 days after the close of each quarter: April 15, July 15, October 15 and January 15 for the fourth quarter. The annual settlement return remains due by May 31.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when any related-party transaction threshold is met: tangible asset transfers above RMB 200,000,000 per year; intangible or financial asset transfers above RMB 100,000,000; other related-party transactions, including services and financing, above RMB 40,000,000. Master File is required when the offshore consolidated group's prior-year revenue exceeds RMB 5,500,000,000. Related-party transaction disclosure forms are also required in the annual CIT return whenever reportable related-party transactions exist.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not administer a separate Cayman-style Economic Substance Regulation. Instead, economic substance is tested through the Beneficial Owner rules under SAT Announcement 9/2018, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Article 47, and functional/risk analysis. A Taiwan-parented WFOE that is a shell or merely owns VPN/IP in name without employees, servers, decision-making functions and operating substance in China is exposed to denial of treaty benefits, recharacterization of internal payments, and special tax adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Additional tax assessment plus 0.05% daily late surcharge; administrative penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax. Criminal conviction under PRC Criminal Law Article 201 carries fines and imprisonment of up to 7 years for serious evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives, and financial officers who prepare or sign false returns may be held criminally liable, jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax when corporate funds are commingled, subject to asset preservation orders, exit bans, and travel restrictions.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Chinese tax authorities may deny treaty or preferential treatment, disregard the shell entity, recharacterize fee payments as constructive dividends, impose 10% dividend withholding tax, recalculate taxable income under the arm's-length principle, apply late surcharge and interest, and levy administrative fines under the Tax Collection Law.

Director personal liability: Actual controllers and directors directing the artificial structure may be treated as beneficial owners; when the corporate form is abused, courts may pierce the veil, making directors personally liable for civil restitution, unpaid tax, and related fines. Travel bans and criminal liability for fraud or tax evasion apply upon proven concealment.
