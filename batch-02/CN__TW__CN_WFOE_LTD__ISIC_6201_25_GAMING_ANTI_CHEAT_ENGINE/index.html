Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

PRC Company Law adopts a subscription capital system (认缴制); no statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an ISIC 6201 software-development WFOE, and the 2022 Foreign Investment Negative List does not restrict this sector. The Articles of Association must state the subscribed capital and a contribution schedule; no escrow account, bank verification certificate, or paid-up capital deposit is required at incorporation. The sole shareholder must remit the subscribed capital within the contractual contribution period to satisfy bank KYC reviews and subsequent operational fundings.

Structural: the light office load (not exceeding 3.5 kN per square meter) requires no separate structural permit, but the lease must confirm floor-load compatibility through the building property certificate. Occupational health: a workplace hazard identification statement must be filed with the local health commission; no respiratory or chemical-exposure controls are applicable to binary middleware R&D workstations, and no civil-defense shelter obligation arises for a pure software-engineering office of this WFOE.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, STA), exercised operationally through the provincial/municipal STA bureaus at the WFOE's registered domicile. Unified national filing portal: National Electronic Tax Bureau platform (全国统一规范电子税务局) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn. Tax–customs–foreign-exchange data is cross-matched through the Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期) information system and the SAMR 'one-stop' (一网通办) registry integration.

E-government filing portal

(1) Complete SAMR name reservation and obtain the foreign-invested WFOE business license; the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, USCC) printed on the license is, by PRC law, the sole statutory TIN and VAT ID for all tax filings, e-invoicing, customs registration, and foreign-exchange settlement. (2) Tax registration is triggered automatically via the SAMR–STA real-time data exchange; the local STA must confirm the taxpayer type (general vs small-scale VAT taxpayer) and configure the e-invoice (数电票) quota within 10 working days of the license issuance. (3) Complete post-registration formalities: legal representative's and financial officer's real-name/face authentication with the STA, binding of RMB and foreign-currency settlement bank accounts, establishment of statutory accounting records, and submission of the cross-border service zero-rating/exemption filing (跨境应税行为零税率/免税备案) in respect of Taiwan-region gaming clients. (4) Taiwan-investor documentation (identity certificates or Taiwan MOEA company registration) must be certified under the cross-strait notarization and document verification system; the non-resident Taiwan holding company requires no separate PRC TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 2, taxed on worldwide income at the 25% EIT rate (reducible to 15% upon High/New Technology Enterprise or Technology Advanced Service Enterprise certification). The entity is a standalone VAT taxpayer: general taxpayer status is mandatory once annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; otherwise small-scale status at a 3% collection rate may apply. No pass-through or transparent tax treatment exists for a WFOE LLC. Profit distributions to the Taiwan-region shareholder are subject to dividend withholding: the standard rate is 10% EIT withholding, reduced to 5% under the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement (2015) where the Taiwan beneficial owner directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE's equity and satisfies substance/bona-fide-holder conditions. Territorial scope: anti-cheat middleware and SaaS services supplied to Taiwan-based game publishers and wholly consumed outside mainland China are treated as offshore cross-border services; integration, installation, or support services performed inside mainland China remain within the VAT taxable territory and the EIT China-source income scope.

Available election forms

PRC law does not provide a check-the-box regime. Available elections and elective treatments: (1) small-scale VAT taxpayer election for annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000, with a VAT exemption for monthly sales not exceeding RMB 100,000; (2) per-transaction election between cross-border service zero-rating (0% output VAT with input-VAT refund under the exemption–offset–refund method) and pure VAT exemption (no output VAT, no input refund) under Caishui〔2016〕36号 Annex 4 and STA Announcement 2016 No. 29 — the zero-rating election is generally preferential for a refundable input-VAT base; (3) software-enterprise VAT immediate-refund election (即征即退): if the kernel-level anti-cheat engine is licensed as a self-developed software product at the 13% goods rate, VAT collected in excess of 3% is refundable; (4) EIT deemed-profit method (核定征收) in exceptional cases with STA approval where accounting is inadequate; (5) hybrid-mismatch adjustments under STA Announcement 2016 No. 42 are compulsory, not elective, and require arm's-length pricing and contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation for all license/SaaS fees exchanged with the Taiwan group.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable sales (rolling 12 months or four quarters) exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (¥500万) makes general VAT taxpayer registration compulsory (STA Order No. 43 of 2018, codified under the VAT Law 2026). Below that threshold the WFOE may elect small-scale taxpayer status at a 3% collection rate; small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales ≤ RMB 100,000 are VAT-exempt (policy extended through 2027). General taxpayer registration is nevertheless strongly advisable for an export-oriented WFOE because only general taxpayers may claim input-VAT refunds on zero-rated cross-border services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Kernel-level anti-cheat middleware, SaaS integrity-monitoring subscriptions, and SDK license deliveries to Taiwan-based game publishers qualify for VAT zero-rating (0% output VAT with refund of attributable input VAT) where all of the following conditions are met: (a) the service falls within the zero-rated cross-border catalogue — 'software services' and 'information system services' — under Caishui〔2016〕36号 Annex 4; (b) the Taiwan recipient is a non-resident enterprise with no permanent establishment in mainland China; (c) the service is rendered and wholly consumed outside mainland China, which for remote server-to-server delivery from mainland hosting points to Taiwan gaming servers is accepted by the STA as qualifying; (d) the WFOE has filed the cross-border zero-rating/exemption registration (跨境应税行为零税率/免税备案) before the transaction month and issues compliant e-invoices naming the Taiwan entity; (e) input VAT attributable to zero-rated export transactions is segregated or fairly apportioned from domestic-sale input VAT and carried into the monthly exemption–offset–refund (免抵退) computation. Alternatively, the transaction may be classified as VAT-exempt (免税) with no refund; the zero-rating election is preferable whenever the WFOE holds material input-VAT credits.

All PRC VAT taxpayers must issue Fully Digitalized E-Invoices (数电票) in real time through the National E-Invoice Service Platform under Golden Tax Phase IV. Each invoice is validated by the STA at the moment of issuance (fiscal clearance at creation), with automatic cross-matching of e-invoice data, VAT returns, customs declarations, and foreign-exchange receipts; there is no physical fiscal stamping or legacy invoicing. Large-volume exporters may connect through the Tax–Enterprise Direct Link (乐企) API for machine-to-machine issuance and clearance. Under the 2026 VAT Law electronic invoices carry full legal effect and paper special VAT invoices are being fully phased out; any issuance attempted outside the authorized platform is blocked, and any mismatch between e-invoice data and cross-border zero-rating filings triggers an immediate tax-bureau audit alert.

The STA and customs jointly audit zero-rating claims under the export-tax-rebate integrity system. Non-compliance triggers: mandatory clawback of all refunded VAT amounts plus daily late-payment interest of 0.05% (万分之五); administrative penalties of 0.5x–5x of the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, and 1x–5x of the fraudulently obtained refund under Article 66; fines up to RMB 100,000 for false filings; revocation of the zero-rating/exemption filing and a 2–3 year blacklisting from future export-refund processing. Where fabricated contracts, inflated license fees, sham cross-strait arrangements, or artificial foreign-exchange round-tripping are detected, the STA may re-characterize the revenue as domestic taxable services at 6%, impose transfer-pricing adjustments with a 5-year lookback and interest, and refer criminal prosecution for fraudulent export-refund (骗取出口退税罪) under Criminal Law Article 204 — the criminal threshold begins at defrauded refunds above RMB 50,000, aggravated at RMB 500,000 and RMB 2,500,000, with penalties from 5 years' imprisonment up to life imprisonment plus fines of 1–5 times the amount fraudulently obtained. Compliance with the 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation deadline under SAFE administration and retention of the complete export substantiation bundle for the statutory recordkeeping period (generally 10 years) are indispensable defenses against retrospective clawback.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese yuan (CNY) achieves de facto current-account convertibility for service-trade operations: software licensing proceeds and anti-cheat middleware subscription fees may be collected from or disbursed to overseas counterparties without case-by-case SAFE approval, subject to bank-led verification under SAFE cross-border monitoring systems and to the cross-border trade payment tax recordal procedure (对外支付税务备案) where the payment amount requires it. Capital-account items remain controlled: FDI capital injection, return of capital, equity transfer and foreign debt all require SAFE registration or filing obtained through the delegated bank, evidenced by the FDI Business Registration Certificate (业务登记凭证). Dividend and profit remittance to a Taiwan-resident shareholder is treated as a current-account debit for operational purposes but requires a closed verification chain: a statutory annual audit report, board resolution approving the distribution, a tax clearance or tax payment certification covering withholding obligations (including cross-strait tax treatment), and a valid FDI registration confirming the shareholder's equity chain. No separate SAFE approval is required at each dividend remittance once the bank has verified the above documentation package and the enterprise is not on any SAFE watchlist. Practical hurdles remain: capital-account RMB funds derived from voluntary FX settlement (意愿结汇) must observe the four-no usage prohibitions (no out-of-scope spending, no securities investment, no non-self-use lending, no non-compliant wealth management purchase); pilot-zone enterprises can rely on the Capital Account Income Payment Facilitation policy (资本项目收入支付便利化) to pay vendors directly from the settlement account; and any company-level offshore account holding of business revenue is prohibited without prior SAFE approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory surrender ratios for export or service-trade foreign-exchange proceeds; the WFOE may retain earnings in its Foreign Exchange Settlement Account and convert voluntarily. Statutory repatriation and verification obligations nevertheless apply. For goods-bearing exports (anti-cheat hardware modules), the SAFE total-amount verification system generally requires export proceeds to be received within 90 days of customs release for A-class enterprises, extendable to 180 days with documented buyers-credit arrangements; non-collection triggers an overdue-receivables exception report to the verifying bank and, if unresolved beyond 365 days, administrative penalty exposure under PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations Article 48 (fine of up to 30 percent of the unlawful foreign-exchange amount). For service-trade exports (software licensing, SaaS or anti-cheat engine subscriptions), there is no fixed statutory recovery window, but bank-side proportional monitoring applies: if contracted service revenue is not collected within 180 days from invoice issuance, the bank will flag the imbalance on SAFE's cross-border flow monitoring platform and demand a written justification, an aged-receivables schedule and a collection plan. All proceeds must ultimately be deposited in a domestic-designated account; parking operating receipts in offshore wallets without SAFE registration is a punishable violation under the same regime.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) is the apex monetary and foreign-exchange administrative authority; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), an operational bureau under PBOC, exercises delegated FX control through domestic-designated banks. For the Taiwan dimension, the Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) and the Taiwan Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) Investment Commission govern outgoing investment into mainland China; cross-strait RMB clearing is executed under the 2012 Cross-Strait Currency Clearing Agreement with Bank of China Taipei branch as the Taiwan-side RMB clearing bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium for offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Stripe Treasury, Interstellar). Such entities cannot hold a PBOC-granted non-bank payment institution license and therefore cannot open a domestic payment account or function as the WFOE's primary onshore collection rail without a licensed Chinese banking partner; unlicensed cross-border collection would violate the PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and PBOC/SAFE cross-border payment-institution licensing frameworks. Viable architecture: the Taiwan parent (or a Hong Kong treasury vehicle) maintains the Stripe account and Airwallex multi-currency wallet; Stripe settles card receipts into that offshore treasury wallet; the treasury vehicle then wires the contracted intercompany consideration into the WFOE's Bank of China account via SWIFT MT103 with the correct service-trade purpose declaration. In this structure the EMI is an offshore treasury utility, not an onshore account provider, and stays compliant. Feasibility rating: Medium for the offshore-treasury model; Low for any model seeking direct onshore EMI settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant sequence: (1) the Taiwan parent obtains Taiwan MOEA Investment Commission approval for the mainland software venture and files the outward-remittance authorization with its Taiwan bank; (2) the WFOE completes the combined SAMR/MOFCOM Foreign Investment Information Report, obtains its unified social credit code, and then secures the SAFE FDI Business Registration Certificate through the delegated Bank of China branch; (3) the WFOE opens the FX Capital Account in its own name at the same delegated branch and obtains the capital-account account number; (4) the Taiwan parent bank executes a SWIFT MT103 wire using purpose field code 622013 (foreign-invested enterprise capital injection) with the remitter stated as the Taiwan shareholder and beneficiary as the WFOE; alternatively, a cross-border RMB remittance via CIPS can be used with purpose code for FDI equity injection; (5) on receipt the delegated bank pairs the credit with the SAFE-registered FDI certificate number, verifies the remitter against the shareholder registry, and posts the funds to the FX Capital Account; (6) the WFOE exercises voluntary settlement (意愿结汇) into the Capital Fund Settlement-and-Payment Account, or, if the bank is a payment-facilitation pilot, applies the Capital Account Income Payment Facilitation regime to pay operating vendors directly; (7) the bank files all transactions to SAFE's cross-border capital monitoring system and the WFOE files the annual FDI information report through SAMR. For RMB-denominated settlement with Taiwan clients and distributors, clearing is executed through CIPS with Bank of China Taipei as clearing bank, which eliminates FX conversion and streamlines documentary compliance.
