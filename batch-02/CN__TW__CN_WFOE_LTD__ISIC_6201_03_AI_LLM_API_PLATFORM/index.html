Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is convertible for current-account transactions (AI/LLM API licensing fees, cloud usage, software exports) after the domestic bank verifies the contract, invoice and tax filing where required. Capital-account transactions remain controlled: FDI paid-in capital, intercompany loans, equity transfers and foreign guarantees require SAFE registration or approval. Profit remittance to the Taiwanese shareholder is treated as current-account investment income and is legally remittable after the WFOE completes statutory audit, final CIT settlement, withholding tax payment, and provides the bank with a board resolution, audited distributable-reserves computation and SAFE remittance form.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China no longer imposes mandatory export surrender ratios or fixed forced-conversion quotas on export receipts. Export and service-trade proceeds must be collected into the WFOE's domestic foreign-currency account and supported by underlying trade/commercial documentation; the WFOE may retain or voluntarily sell the FX to a domestic bank. SAFE uses an A/B/C compliance classification for trade-related flows: an A-class enterprise enjoys automatic settlement and conversion, while B/C classification triggers a 90-day enhanced verification window, restricted foreign-currency account operation and stricter documentary review.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); for Taiwan-origin investment, Taiwan's Central Bank and the MOEA Investment Commission also regulate the investor's outflow side.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for statutory treasury functions. Offshore digital EMIs cannot open the WFOE's statutory FDI capital account or serve as the primary SAFE-reported collection bank. They are feasible only as a secondary API-revenue collection gateway when they route through a licensed PRC bank, retain full audit trails and do not hold the WFOE's export receivables offshore. Crypto/USDC settlement rails are not a recognized corporate clearing system in China; crypto can be used only after conversion into fiat through licensed on/off-ramps and must be excluded from equity and trade-payment flows.

Box 8 - Tax Credits and Net Tax Payable Application of R&D investment credits, foreign tax credits, minimum-government-investment credits, and any approved investment tax incentives; the resulting net corporate income tax payable or refundable is then carried to the payment settlement line. The WFOE may claim foreign tax credits for taxes paid on foreign-source income; withholding taxes borne by the foreign parent on inbound royalties or dividends are not creditable on the WFOE's own return and must be reimbursed separately under DTA if applicable. For late filing without extension: a fixed penalty of NT$1,500 if no tax is due; if tax is due, a surcharge of 10% of the assessed tax under Article 108 of the Income Tax Act. For late payment: a surcharge of 1% of the unpaid tax for every two days, capped at 10% of the unpaid amount, plus default interest at the inflation-adjusted one-year postal savings rate. Intentional understatement or evasion may trigger an additional penalty of up to two times the evaded tax under Article 110 and criminal referral under Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Taiwan WFOE is required to prepare and submit transfer pricing documentation (the Local File) if its aggregate controlled transactions with related parties exceed TWD 50 million in the tax year; the Master File is required for a multinational group with consolidated annual revenue above TWD 27 billion, along with the prescribed administrative thresholds for controlled services and intangibles. Even when below the explicit threshold, contemporaneous documentation is recommended for all intercompany AI/LLM licensing, cloud cost recharges, and derivative revenue allocations.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not apply Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, but it applies an economic substance principle through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (Article 12-1 of the Income Tax Act), Article 43-1 on controlled-transaction adjustment, and the domestic/foreign income anti-abuse rules. A Taiwan WFOE deriving AI/LLM API revenue must maintain real employees, active decision-making, risk bearing, core intellectual property ownership, and independent profit ability in Taiwan. If the WFOE is recharacterized as a shell or conduit, the tax authority may ignore the separate existence, reallocate income to or from the non-resident parent, impose Article 43-1 adjustments, deny deductible intercompany payments, and apply the 21% withholding tax to any deemed dividend distribution.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Reporting

Statutory sanction: Under Article 110 of the Income Tax Act, the tax authority imposes a surcharge of up to two times the evaded tax; moreover, Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act criminalizes fraudulent tax evasion with imprisonment of up to five years, short-term detention, and/or a fine. The evaded tax plus default interest is collectible, and the WFOE may lose the benefit of any incentive credits.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns or knowingly participate in the evasion are jointly and severally liable in civil proceedings for the restored tax and penalties; the tax authority may impose a travel ban under Article 23-1 of the Tax Collection Act when the tax debt exceeds the statutory threshold; criminal conviction may result in imprisonment and disqualification from serving as a director.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Article 43-1 of the Income Tax Act, the tax authority may recharacterize and price the related-party transactions at arm's length; the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 12-1 permits disregarding the arrangement and taxing the economic substance. The resulting additional tax is subject to a penalty of up to two times, with possible denial of all intercompany deductions and the reclassification of the repatriated payments as taxable dividends.

Director personal liability: Directors and controlling officers face personal liability if the shell structure is used to evade corporate tax; they may be ordered to pay civil restitution of the tax, penalties, and interest; the tax authority may obtain a court order for civil recovery, impose a travel ban, and, in aggravated cases, prosecute for tax fraud leading to imprisonment.
Anti-Money Laundering / Crypto Payment Conduct

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Control Act, a non-compliant institution is liable for administrative fines of NT$1 million to NT$5 million for each violation; if the funds are derived from criminal conduct, Article 14 imposes money-laundering liability of up to seven years' imprisonment and a fine not exceeding NT$5 million, with forfeiture of the laundered property.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML compliance officers are personally subject to administrative penalties and may be disqualified from management; they can face civil monetary penalties and restitution for funds laundered through the platform; an ongoing AML investigation may trigger bail restrictions, travel bans, and ultimately criminal conviction with imprisonment.
