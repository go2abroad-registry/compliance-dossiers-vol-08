Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); administered locally by the municipal/state-level STA service bureaus where the WFOE is domiciled; digital platform: National E-Tax Bureau (etax.chinatax.gov.cn) and the state unified VAT invoice management system.

E-government filing portal

A PRC WFOE is automatically enrolled in tax registration upon incorporation under the multi-certificate integration system: the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the business license is the statutory TIN and VAT registration number. Within 30 days of business-license issuance, the WFOE must complete post-registration formalities at the competent STA local bureau: (i) real-name authentication of the legal representative and financial officer; (ii) filing of accounting/bookkeeping standards; (iii) tax-item identification covering Corporate Income Tax, VAT, urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, stamp duty, and other local levies; (iv) invoice type and monthly issuance quota approval; and (v) confirmation of the electronic tax registration record. A foreign-incorporated parent investor (including a Taiwan resident corporate investor) without a Chinese establishment must separately obtain a non-resident tax registration code for China-source income withholding and filing purposes; because the 2015 Cross-Strait Taxation Arrangement is signed but not yet in force, Taiwan investors currently rely on the domestic 10% withholding framework rather than treaty rate relief.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China does not provide a US-style check-the-box entity classification election. The WFOE is mandatorily a PRC tax resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide income at 25%. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT small-scale taxpayer status where annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000, with a simplified 3% levy rate (temporarily 1% under Caishui [2023] No. 19), versus mandatory general taxpayer registration once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; (ii) simplified VAT computation election for enumerated supplies; (iii) tax-free reorganization or deferred-recognition elections for qualifying asset restructurings under Caishui [2009] No. 59; (iv) deemed-profit taxation only upon STA approval where books cannot be audited; and (v) beneficial-ownership documentation filings by non-resident investors to claim reduced withholding rates where an effective treaty applies. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available; thin capitalization, CFC, and general anti-abuse rules neutralize hybrid arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold applies to a newly incorporated WFOE; VAT registration is mandatory upon establishment for all limited liability enterprises. The small-scale taxpayer regime applies where annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000; once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, the WFOE must register as a VAT general taxpayer. The voice-streaming information service revenue is chargeable at 6% VAT, while the statutory headline/goods rate is 13% and the cross-border export rate is 0%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports qualify for the 0% VAT rate with input-tax refund where a customs export declaration is filed. Cross-border services qualify for zero-rating or VAT exemption only for enumerated offshore services wholly consumed outside China, such as software services, information-system services, offshore outsourcing, R&D, design, and technology transfer, per Caishui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4. The voice-streaming/interactive-audio platform may claim zero-rating or exemption only where the service recipient is a registered overseas enterprise (for example Apple or Google as app-store operators), the service is fully consumed outside mainland China, the invoice is issued to the overseas counterparty, and consideration is received in foreign exchange from abroad. The WFOE must complete the export VAT filing and offshore-service exemption/zero-rating record-filing with STA before the first qualifying supply; sales to PRC users through App Store or Google Play channels remain within domestic VAT territorial scope and cannot be zero-rated.

  • Bill of Lading or airway bill, supplemented for services by electronic delivery logs evidencing offshore consumption
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher) proving collection from the overseas counterparty
  • Cross-border service contract and tax-compliant invoice issued to the overseas customer

Golden Tax Phase IV and the fully digital e-invoice regime apply: invoices must be issued through the unified E-Tax Bureau platform with real-time data upload, centralized tax-control encryption, and automatic digital stamping at the moment of issuance. VAT taxpayers must use STA-approved API or browser-based invoicing modules, obtain issuance quotas before invoicing, and support real-time reconciliation and red-letter reversal workflows. Paper invoices are retained only in transitional cases; cross-border invoices and zero-rated supply documentation must be electronically archived and made available to STA inspection.

STA audit review may retrospectively claw back refunded VAT with daily late-payment surcharges (0.05% per day on the underpaid tax) and impose administrative penalties of 50% to five times the amount of tax evaded or fraudulently refunded under Tax Collection Law Articles 63 and 64. Export-refund qualification may be suspended for up to three years, and previously refunded input VAT is reversed. Fraudulent export-refund claims constitute a criminal offense under Criminal Law Article 204, punishable by imprisonment up to life and confiscation of property where the sums are huge. Responsible officers face personal liability, travel and payment restrictions, credit blacklisting, and an extended retrospective audit window of up to ten years in cases involving fraudulent intent.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current-account purposes once a bank has performed statutory verification; this includes trade in services, software/app-store revenue, royalties, salaries, and profit remittances. For the WFOE, after-tax profits and legitimate dividends may be remitted to the Taiwan shareholder without prior SAFE approval, but only after submission of the board dividend resolution, audited annual financial statements, tax clearance certification, stamp tax payment evidence, and bank-side construction of a current-account profit-remittance file. Capital-account transactions remain controlled: FDI equity injections require an FDI registration number and can be received only into a bank-designated Foreign Exchange Capital Account; use of converted RMB capital is restricted by SAFE's negative list, which prohibits use for securities investment, entrusted loans, repayment of unrelated external debt, or speculative property purchase. The WFOE must maintain a clear separation between capital-account proceeds and current-account operating-fund flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since the 2009-2012 SAFE reforms, there is no mandatory surrender ratio for export receipts in foreign currency; the mandatory conversion ratio is zero. For physical goods exports, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic foreign-currency settlement account within the normal trade credit period recorded in the sales contract, ordinarily within 90 days after the export customs declaration; any longer deferral must be registered in SAFE's trade-monitoring system. Once repatriated, the WFOE may retain 100% of the foreign exchange in its settlement account or sell it to a licensed bank at its own discretion. For the digital service revenue receivable from Apple and Google, the flows are current-account service export receipts; they must be received through the WFOE's onshore foreign-currency settlement account, declared through the bank's digital service-trade verification system, and supported by app-store settlement statements; no surrender percentage applies.

Central bank supervision: The State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) within the People's Bank of China (PBOC) system is the controlling foreign-exchange regulator for the PRC WFOE. PBOC sets RMB monetary policy and exchange-rate management policy; SAFE administers foreign-exchange receipts and payments, current-account verification, capital-account registration, and all cross-border banking supervision. The Taiwan parent company is separately regulated by Taiwan's Investment Commission (Ministry of Economic Affairs) for making permitted outbound investments into mainland China.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The WFOE must first engage its appointed bank as the FDI registration bank, which inputs the FDI basic information into the SAFE Digital Foreign Exchange Administration Platform and obtains the FDI registration code before any inward remittance is accepted. The Taiwan parent must obtain the required Taiwan Investment Commission approval and remit the capital contribution from its Taiwan bank in USD, HKD, or RMB. The MT103/MT202 wire must name the remitter as the Taiwan shareholder, the beneficiary exactly as the WFOE's licensed Chinese/English company name, and the beneficiary account as the specific FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account. The remittance purpose must be stated as 'investment in registered capital' or '注册资本投资款' and must include the SAFE FDI registration code; generic purposes such as 'loan', 'business expense', or 'service fee' are rejected. The receiving bank verifies the entry against the SAFE registered capital amount, reports it under the capital-account class of FOREIGN-DIRECT-INVESTMENT EQUITY INJECTION, and then applies the RMB conversion under the capital-use negative list. If the Taiwan parent remits RMB, the payment must clear through a CIPS participant bank with RCPMIS reporting and the same FDI registration reference.For service imports, software royalties, cloud-hosting fees, content-moderation contracts, and app-store platform fees, the primary clearing rail is an onshore-bank SWIFT MT103 from the WFOE's foreign-currency settlement account. The bank requires the underlying contract, a valid invoice, the SAFE service-trade transaction classification, and, where the payment is a China-sourced taxable item, the corresponding tax-withholding or non-tax filing certificate. For physical goods imports, such as branded merchandising hardware, the WFOE may use commercial letters of credit under ICC UCP600, documentary collection, or direct T/T, with customs entry papers and cargo inspection evidence. Profit remittances to the Taiwan shareholder are executed as MT103 dividends after audit, board approval, tax clearance, and current-account verification. For payments received from Apple and Google, settlement comes to the WFOE from the app stores as a current-account service receipt, not as consumer person-to-person transfer; the bank credits the foreign-currency account and completes service-trade digital declaration using app-store statements, tax invoices, and platform payout reports.Deliverable RMB/USD and RMB/HKD forward contracts offered by PBOC/SAFE licensed onshore banks, supported by genuine app-store receivables and forecast royalty flows.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps for long-term USD-denominated intercompany debt or capital-exposure management.FX swaps between RMB and foreign currency for short-term liquidity smoothing of operating cash flow.FX option and option-combination products sold by qualified PRC banks, provided the underlying exposure is real and the product passes suitability and real-trade-background checks.NDF overlay: Onshore non-deliverable forwards are not a permissible hedging instrument for a PRC WFOE. Any offshore NDF overlay is restricted to the Taiwan parent's own treasury positions and must never be booked, funded, or settled through the PRC WFOE's banking rail.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Low for the PRC-regulated core treasury. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, or Revolut Business cannot open a PBOC-recognised RMB basic deposit account, cannot hold the SAFE-registered FDI capital-account funds, and cannot make SAFE declarations on behalf of the PRC WFOE. An EMI may be used only as an auxiliary receivable wallet operated by the Taiwan parent outside China; directing app-store proceeds through an offshore EMI before repatriation would create a non-compliant detour around SAFE bank-side verification and would undermine the WFOE's audit trail and AML profile. All onshore operating funds and capital injections must clear to a licensed onshore commercial bank.
