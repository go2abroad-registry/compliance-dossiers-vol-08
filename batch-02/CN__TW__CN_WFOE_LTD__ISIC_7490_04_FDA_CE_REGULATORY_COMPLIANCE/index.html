Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No paid-up capital minimum and no blocked escrow account required for a WFOE in the ISIC 7490 consulting sector; the sole shareholder must still stipulate a registered capital amount in the Articles of Association and must fully pay the subscribed contribution within five years from the issue of the business license under Article 47 of the Companies Law. If the future expansion plan adds a restricted sector from the Foreign Investment Negative List, the relevant licensing regime may impose a minimum amount or a capital contribution schedule; absent such restriction, the contribution may be in cash, technology, or other lawful non-cash assets.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Taxation Administration, Taiwan, through the local National Taxation Bureau (NTB) with jurisdiction over the WFOE's registered address. Online electronic portals: MOF e-Tax Portal (https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw), MOF E-Invoice Platform (https://www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw), and MOEA Company and Commerce Registration Service (https://gcis.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Obtain investment approval from the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) Department of Investment Services under the Statute for Investment by Foreign Nationals; the Taiwan WFOE is foreign-owned and has no minimum capital obligation. Step 2: File company incorporation with MOEA using the Company and Commerce Registration Service (gcis.nat.gov.tw), submitting the articles of incorporation, Taiwan registered office address, shareholders and director list, and paid-in capital bank certificate if any. Step 3: The MOEA one-stop service transmits the incorporation record to the local National Taxation Bureau for mandatory business tax registration under Article 28 of the Business Tax Act; the WFOE is automatically assigned a Taiwan Unified Business Number (UBN) that is the TIN for corporate income tax, business tax, customs, and electronic invoices. Step 4: If the WFOE will issue standard VAT invoices, submit the Uniform Invoice User application to the local NTB and activate the Turnkey electronic invoice account on the MOF E-Invoice Platform. Step 5: Register the WFOE for bimonthly business tax returns and annual corporate income tax settlement under the Income Tax Act; appoint a local tax agent or accounting firm to administer filings. Step 6: Open a foreign-currency bank account with a Taiwan bank and register the exchange settlement account with the Central Bank of Taiwan under the Management of Foreign Exchange Act; this account is used to collect export consideration and to generate the foreign exchange settlement certificates needed for zero-rated export claims.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

TWD 0/no turnover threshold: every business operator must apply for business tax registration before commencing business. The operational threshold for issuing standard 5% uniform invoices is average monthly sales exceeding TWD 200,000; at or below TWD 200,000, the WFOE may be classified as a small-scale business and taxed by assessment at 1% or 2% unless it elects to issue uniform invoices.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Article 7 of the Business Tax Act zero-rates exports of goods and exports of services, including qualifying offshore services. The Taiwan WFOE's FDA 510(k) and CE Mark regulatory consulting services qualify as zero-rated exports when the customer is a non-resident, the service is performed for a foreign-market regulatory outcome, the benefit and use of the service occurs outside Taiwan, and the consideration is received in foreign currency and remitted to Taiwan. To substantiate the 0% rate, the WFOE must retain the service contract, foreign-currency invoice, evidence of service performance, and the bank's foreign exchange settlement certificate; periodic bimonthly business tax returns must reflect the zero-rated output tax and claim any input tax refund due.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry/Export Manifest) - mandatory where physical goods or regulatory samples are exported; for pure consulting services this document is substituted with the service contract and performance evidence.
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill - mandatory for tangible exports of goods or samples; not required for the service-only FDA/CE consulting activity.
  • Commercial invoice / service agreement stating the foreign-resident customer, the FDA 510(k) or CE Mark compliance milestones, total consideration, and settlement currency.
  • Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate (FIRC/Water Memo) issued by the Taiwan bank - evidencing inward remittance or settlement of foreign currency export proceeds into the WFOE's Taiwan bank account within the statutory deadline.

Taiwan requires electronic uniform invoices for VAT-registered businesses. Invoices are issued from MOF-controlled serial-number blocks and transmitted via the certified Turnkey connection to the MOF E-Invoice Platform within the statutory transmission window, generally no later than 48 hours after issuance. The platform validates the invoice number, taxpayer UBN, amount, and tax code, then records the digital signature; there is no physical fiscal stamp, and anti-fraud control is achieved through encrypted XML submission, serial-number duplication checks, and e-Invoice platform reconciliation.

If the WFOE cannot produce the export declaration (for goods) or the service contract, performance evidence, and foreign exchange settlement certificate (for services), the National Taxation Bureau will reverse the 0% export claim and assess output business tax at 5%, plus late interest from the original filing date. Under Articles 43 and 51 of the Business Tax Act, the underpayment is additionally penalized by up to three times the evaded tax. For fraudulent schemes, the Tax Collection Act and Business Tax Act impose criminal fines and imprisonment, and the assessment and recovery window is extended to seven years from the due date for intentional non-declaration, allowing retrospective clawback of previously allowed zero-rating.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese renminbi remains only conditionally convertible under the capital account, although current account receipts and payments have been, in principle, freely convertible since China accepted Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement. An inward direct investment by a Taiwan-registered investor into a Mainland China WFOE is treated as foreign-direct-investment type capital, and the equity contribution must be remitted into a dedicated SAFE-registered FDI foreign exchange capital account; such funds cannot simply enter a basic RMB account before completing the foreign exchange registration and the foreign investment information reporting. Current account transactions, including cross-border consulting fees and royalties, are settled through licensed banks after authenticity review. Profit remittance by the WFOE to its Taiwan shareholder is permissible once the annual audit and final tax settlement are completed, withholding taxes have been paid, and a payment order with supporting tax certificates, audit report, and board resolution has been submitted to the bank. SAFE filing, real-demand verification, and regulatory approval are still required for large overseas disbursements, cross-border guarantees, foreign debt, and reinvestment. Therefore, the PBOC/SAFE framework restricts free fund navigability but provides a predictable compliance path for legitimate FDI and operating flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic designated foreign exchange account within 90 days from the date of export customs declaration unless a deferred collection report is filed with SAFE; there is no mandatory conversion ratio, and the WFOE may retain 100 percent of eligible export proceeds in foreign exchange or settle all or part into RMB. For service-trade exports of medical device regulatory consulting under ISIC 7490, there is no surrender requirement, but every receipt must be booked through a licensed bank under the applicable SAFE current account transaction code, supported by the underlying contract and invoice, and reported through the SAFE cross-border receipt and payment monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through its State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Very low viability; offshore fintech EMIs are legally barred from servicing a Mainland China WFOE as deposit-taking institutions. An offshore EMI cannot open an onshore RMB settlement account, cannot receive FDI capital contributions under SAFE rules, cannot perform FX settlement and sale business, and cannot satisfy China’s basic deposit account supervisory regime. The WFOE must hold its operating and treasury accounts at a SAFE-approved domestic bank. An EMI may only be considered as an ancillary offshore treasury wallet outside Mainland China and cannot be the primary clearing rail for capital injection, trade payments, or profit remittance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder shall pass a board resolution, execute the equity subscription agreement, complete the foreign investment information reporting through the national investment information system, and file the FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE through the receiving bank. The inward remittance must be sent via SWIFT MT103 from the Taiwan corporate bank account directly to the WFOE’s FDI foreign exchange capital account in the name of the WFOE, using the SAFE cross-border receipt and payment transaction classification code for inward direct investment equity contribution. Payment details must reference the WFOE’s registered name, the SAFE FDI registration number, and a clear purpose as equity injection; a third-party payer is prohibited. After receipt, the bank will perform capital verification against the registered total investment. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual Enterprise Income Tax reconciliation return is due within five months after the end of the fiscal year, i.e. no later than 31 May for a calendar-year taxpayer; quarterly advance payments are due within fifteen days after the completion of each calendar quarter, i.e. 15 April, 15 July, 15 October and 15 January.
