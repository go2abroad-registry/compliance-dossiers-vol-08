Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual offices and commercial mail-forwarding addresses are not acceptable as the sole registered office for an ISIC 6430 charitable endowment foundation structured as a PRC WFOE. The PRC Company Law and the Regulations on the Administration of Foundations require a fixed business premises that can accommodate the filing cabinet, board minutes, UBO records, grant contracts, and financial books. SAMR and the tax authorities regard a mailbox-only address as an indicator of shell-formation risk. Local branches of the tax bureau and commercial bank account-application teams will inspect the premises or require the lease registration, utility bills, payment vouchers, and photographs before issuance of tax registration and bank account opening. A virtual office may be used only for secondary correspondence after a physical operating address and statutory premises are established.

Statutory minimum paid-up capital: None under the Taiwan Company Act for a limited liability company without special licensing. However, the subscribed capital must be fully paid in and remitted to a temporary capital verification account opened in the proposed company name at a Taiwanese bank; the funds must remain blocked for the account holder's operations pending the certified public accountant's capital verification report and the successful company registration filing. No formal state-mandated escrow regime applies beyond this bank-level verification-and-hold instruction.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Step 1: For a foreign non-resident enterprise creating a Taiwan fixed place of business, first complete the required juridical person, branch, or foundation registration with the Taiwan central competent authority and obtain establishment approval and business registration. Step 2: Before business commences, and generally within 15 days after the establishment approval, file the profit-seeking enterprise tax registration with the competent National Taxation Administration district office or use the e-Tax Service Portal. Step 3: The National Taxation Administration will assign the statutory tax registration number (Uniform Number/Tax ID) used for business tax, Uniform Invoices, customs declarations, and all tax return filing. Step 4: A foreign non-resident entity with no Taiwan fixed place of business that supplies cross-border electronic services to Taiwan individual consumers is exempt from registration until annual Taiwanese-source B2C digital service turnover exceeds TWD 480,000; once the threshold is crossed, it must register under the cross-border electronic services business tax facility, appoint a Taiwan tax agent, and file the same business tax returns. Step 5: A charitable endowment foundation should also apply for recognition under Article 4 of the Income Tax Act as an educational, cultural, public welfare, or charitable organization so that qualifying grant and endowment income remains exempt while nonexempt business or investment income remains taxable at the entity level.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No registration threshold for a Taiwan fixed place of business; registration is mandatory before taxable business begins. For a foreign non-resident supplier of cross-border electronic services, registration is triggered only when annual B2C sales to Taiwan individual consumers exceed TWD 480,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A Taiwan-registered enterprise may claim the 0% business tax rate for (1) goods physically exported from Taiwan customs territory to an overseas buyer, and (2) qualifying offshore or cross-border electronic/digital services supplied by a Taiwan business to an overseas recipient or actually consumed outside Taiwan. The claimant must establish through customs and commercial records that the goods or services left the Taiwan economic territory, that a genuine sale occurred, and that the foreign currency consideration was, or within 180 days will be, repatriated to Taiwan. Input VAT attributable to zero-rated export supplies is creditable and may be refunded through the bi-monthly business tax return.

  • Customs export declaration (export entry or export manifest) filed with Taiwan Customs evidencing physical export of goods
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing carriage to the overseas consignee
  • Foreign exchange settlement certificate or inward foreign remittance record issued by a Taiwan foreign-exchange bank
  • Uniform Invoice or export invoice issued to the overseas buyer showing zero-rated export sale
  • Export contract, purchase order, pro forma invoice, and packing list proving transaction terms, value, and counterparty
  • For exported services: service agreement, offshore usage or consumption evidence, and delivery/receipt records confirming the recipient is outside Taiwan

Taiwan requires taxable profit-seeking enterprises to issue Uniform Invoices for taxable supplies. Electronic uniform invoices are issued through the Ministry of Finance E-Invoice Integrated Service Platform using approved turnkey or API interfaces (JSON/XML); B2B invoice details are captured during the bi-monthly business tax return cycle, while B2C e-invoices must be transmitted to the cloud platform within the prescribed upload window (generally 48 hours). Taiwan does not operate real-time fiscal clearance or anti-fraud digital stamping; integrity is enforced through unique Uniform Invoice numbering, statutory business tax ID cross-checks, and the platform's anti-duplication validation controls.

The National Taxation Administration and Taiwan Customs may audit zero-rating claims within the statutory assessment period, generally five years and seven years for proven fraud. If the documentation bundle is missing, inconsistent, or counterfeit, the zero rate is denied, output business tax is retroactively assessed at 5%, input tax credits or refunds are clawed back with interest and late surcharge, and administrative penalties up to five times the evaded tax may be imposed. Export proceeds not repatriated within the 180-day deadline may be recharacterized as domestic taxable sales, resulting in additional 5% business tax, interest, penalties, and possible criminal liability for deliberate tax evasion or forgery of export and foreign-exchange documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible for current account items (trade, services, dividends) subject to authentic-transaction verification, but strictly controlled on the capital account. The WFOE's inbound equity injection is a capital-account event requiring SAFE FDI registration, a dedicated capital escrow account, and post-settlement use verification. Outbound grant and profit remittances are subject to single-payment tax filing (对外支付税务备案) for amounts above USD 50,000 and SAFE purpose-code declaration. Taiwan-sourced funds are treated as cross-strait FDI requiring MOEAIC investment approval on the Taiwan side and SAFE FDI registration on the mainland side; full repatriation of registered capital is permitted only after liquidation or equity transfer, whereas current-account profits are remittable annually upon audited financial statements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished blanket mandatory FX surrender in 2012. Goods-trade export proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic FX account within 90 days of customs declaration; services-trade proceeds within 180 days; exporters may freely hold or convert retained FX and must report through the SAFE Trade Monitoring System. The charitable endowment WFOE (ISIC 6430) has no core export exposure; the only applicable obligation is repatriation and declaration of incidental receipts (e.g., licensing or consulting fees) under the relevant timeline, with no mandatory conversion ratio unless a local SAFE circular imposes a minimum settlement percentage for cross-strait transactions.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) governs all FX and cross-border RMB flows for the mainland WFOE; the Taiwan Central Bank (CBC) supervises the Taiwan-side RMB clearing conducted through Bank of China (Taipei branch).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low (feasibility rating 2/10). Offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, Payoneer, Currenxie, Wise) are not licensed by PBOC/SAFE to open corporate current or capital accounts for a mainland WFOE; they may only aggregate small-value e-commerce receipts through licensed Chinese PSPs (Alipay, Tenpay). Such EMIs cannot handle FDI capital injection, capital escrow, or charitable grant outflows, and their use would breach SAFE capital-account rules and trigger AML red flags. They are excluded from the primary treasury rail and serve at most as a supplementary collection conduit for small charitable donations routed through a licensed PSP partner.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Taiwan shareholder funds must be remitted via SWIFT MT103 from Bank of China (Taipei branch) or a Taiwan OBU to the WFOE's designated capital escrow account at the mainland lead bank. The remitter must exactly match the registered shareholder; field 70 must state 'Cross-Strait FDI – Registered Capital Contribution' and reference the SAFE FDI registration certificate; the correct SAFE capital-account transaction code must be quoted. On receipt, the bank reports to SAFE's Capital Account Information System and credits the foreign-exchange capital account. RMB conversion is permitted under discretionary settlement (意愿结汇) into a settlement-pending-payment account (结汇待支付账户), with each subsequent RMB payment supported by invoices or contracts. The Taiwan side must hold MOEAIC investment approval and CBC outward-remittance clearance.Grant and operating outflows clear via (i) SWIFT MT103 USD wires using SAFE current-account purpose code 721090 for grants or 122990 for service fees, each payment above USD 50,000 requiring prior outward-payment tax filing (对外支付税务备案); (ii) RMB cross-border payments through CIPS using Bank of China (Taipei) as clearing bank for Taiwan-side credits; and (iii) documentary Commercial Letters of Credit only if the foundation undertakes permitted trade-service activities under ISIC 6430 — in practice, the charitable structure precludes L/C usage, and direct verified remittance is the sole compliant rail for Taiwan grant distribution.Onshore RMB/USD forward contracts (远期结售汇) with a licensed bank under the real-demand principle, tenor 3-12 months, backed by forecasted current-account receipts or FDI-related outflows.Onshore RMB/USD FX options (purchased European options or zero-premium collars) permitted for verified current-account exposure, subject to SAFE real-demand verification and prohibition of speculative positions.Cross-currency swaps (货币掉期) converting floating-rate endowment income into fixed-rate RMB or USD, executed under a SAFE-approved derivative framework agreement.Cross-strait RMB/TWD implicit forwards settled via USD crossing at Bank of China (Taipei), permissible solely for verified grant or trade flows.Onshore synthetic forwards (差额交割) for RMB/TWD or other non-deliverable currency exposures are permissible under CIPS real-demand rules; direct RMB/USD NDFs are prohibited onshore, and offshore NDF participation by the WFOE requires prior SAFE no-objection.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low (feasibility rating 2/10). Offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, Payoneer, Currenxie, Wise) are not licensed by PBOC/SAFE to open corporate current or capital accounts for a mainland WFOE; they may only aggregate small-value e-commerce receipts through licensed Chinese PSPs (Alipay, Tenpay). Such EMIs cannot handle FDI capital injection, capital escrow, or charitable grant outflows, and their use would breach SAFE capital-account rules and trigger AML red flags. They are excluded from the primary treasury rail and serve at most as a supplementary collection conduit for small charitable donations routed through a licensed PSP partner.
