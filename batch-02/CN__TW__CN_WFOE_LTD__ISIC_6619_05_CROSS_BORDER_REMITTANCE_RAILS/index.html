Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum registered capital applies for an ordinary WFOE under the PRC Company Law and Foreign Investment Law, which adopt the subscribed capital and self-commitment system. However, because ISIC 6619 cross-border remittance services require a PBOC non-bank payment institution business license, the paid-in registered capital must be at least RMB 30 million for regional remittance business, or RMB 100 million if the company applies for nationwide clearing and remittance service coverage. The capital must be paid in cash from the foreign shareholder's own offshore account, remitted by SWIFT to the company's designated foreign exchange capital account, and monitored by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange. There is no blocked escrow account requirement imposed by SAMR, but the paid-in capital remains subject to SAFE capital account control and may not be withdrawn without lawful capital reduction or regulatory approval.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for civil defense industrial fire certification because no physical factory, chemical storage or warehouse is involved. The standard administrative office lease must satisfy the fire code for commercial buildings, including a fire safety acceptance inspection or fire compliance certificate from the local fire and rescue authority, adequate fire extinguishers, emergency exits and emergency lighting, and, where the employee headcount crosses the statutory threshold, a workplace safety registration with the local Emergency Management Bureau under the PRC Work Safety Law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China; online registration and compliance portal: STA Electronic Tax Office (eTax / China Tax Service).

E-government filing portal

A China-resident Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation and automatically receives the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which functions as its statutory Taxpayer Identification Number. Within 30 days of business license issuance, the legal representative or authorized agent must complete the tax registration information confirmation through the STA Electronic Tax Office by filing the business license, articles of association, legal representative identity document, registered office proof, opening bank certificate, ownership and investment schedule, and a statement of business scope. The local STA validates the record, determines VAT small-scale or general taxpayer status, and activates the applicable monthly or quarterly filing obligations. A foreign non-resident enterprise without a Chinese permanent establishment that derives China-source income or has withholding obligations must separately obtain a non-resident tax identification number at the competent STA sub-bureau in the payor's jurisdiction, using the non-resident's home-country tax identification evidence and a completed non-resident tax registration questionnaire. For the WFOE itself, no separate application is required because the Unified Social Credit Code is the tax identity; the entity is treated as a resident limited liability company subject to entity-level Corporate Income Tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not maintain a U.S.-style check-the-box election or a hybrid mismatch entity election. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is mandatorily classified as a resident enterprise for Corporate Income Tax under the Enterprise Income Tax Law and cannot elect pass-through status. Separate VAT small-scale and general taxpayer status is governed by turnover thresholds and is not an entity classification election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All incorporated companies must complete VAT registration with the STA at establishment, with no universal turnover exemption for the WFOE. Mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; entities with annual turnover below RMB 5,000,000 may be treated as small-scale VAT taxpayers, and a newly formed WFOE may voluntarily register as a general taxpayer through the eTax platform where anticipated revenue or input-credit requirements justify it.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods, zero-rating requires physical export from Chinese customs territory, customs release of the goods in the name of the exporting WFOE, receipt of foreign exchange settlement, and retention of the complete customs and transport documentary chain. For services and intangible assets, the supply must be a qualifying cross-border taxable service consumed wholly outside China with a recipient located outside the PRC. The WFOE must hold a written cross-border service contract, evidence of actual service delivery outside China, outbound payment documentation, and foreign exchange repatriation records. For the Taiwan cross-border remittance rails, 0% VAT will be recognized only where the service is supplied to an offshore recipient for use in an offshore clearing or corridor market, is not consumed or exploited in China, and is not an onshore RMB clearing service; otherwise the supply remains taxable at 6%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) issued in the name of the WFOE and bearing the customs release registration number
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent multimodal transport document evidencing physical movement from Chinese customs territory to the overseas consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) issued by the designated foreign-exchange bank evidencing repatriation of export or service proceeds within the statutory deadline
  • Signed cross-border service contract, commercial invoice, and delivery or service-consumption evidence confirming offshore use of the Taiwan remittance corridor
  • SWIFT payment and settlement records for the cross-border money transmission rails, including MT103 or MT202 confirmations where applicable

The PRC operates mandatory electronic invoicing under the fully digitalized electronic invoice framework and Golden Tax Phase IV. VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-approved electronic invoice service platform or certified third-party systems integrated by API with the electronic tax office. Each fully digitalized e-invoice carries a unique automatically generated invoice code and cryptographic tax-control code; real-time fiscal upload to the STA occurs at issuance, and the invoice must pass central authentication before the buyer may claim input VAT credit. Anti-fraud digital stamping is performed through centralized invoice data validation, digital signatures, QR code verification, and matching against the taxpayer's VAT declaration and customs records. For business-to-business financial services, fully digitalized e-invoicing is the mandatory issuance channel and paper-only invoicing is not permitted for the zero-rated cross-border corridor.

Failure to substantiate zero-rating causes the tax authority to assess VAT at the applicable rate, with the financial services rate of 6% applying to the remittance service, together with late-payment surcharges. Under Articles 52 and 63 of the Law on Tax Administration of the PRC, fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger unlimited retrospective recovery and a penalty between 50% and 500% of the underpaid tax. Use of false VAT invoices, fictitious export declarations, or forged FIRC documentation may additionally give rise to criminal liability under Article 205 of the Criminal Law. The customs authority may separately impose penalties for inaccurate export declarations, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange can penalize failure to repatriate foreign exchange within the 180-day deadline. Any VAT refund or 0% treatment already granted may be clawed back with interest if the customs declaration, Bill of Lading, service delivery evidence, or Inward Remittance Certificate is not produced on audit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

In the PRC operating jurisdiction, the RMB is convertible on the current account for legitimate trade and service payments, but capital-account convertibility is strictly regulated and subject to SAFE registration, quotas, and underlying-risk verification. The WFOE may settle incoming foreign-currency service fees, remittance-agency commissions and FDI equity injections through domestic designated banks, provided the relevant transaction documents, tax filings and SAFE codes are presented. Current-account outflows, including payments to Taiwan suppliers, licensing fees and dividend remittances, require documentation establishing commercial substance, an invoice or contract, proof of tax withholding, and a bank-verified SAFE transaction code. Profit remittance is not blocked but is operationally heavy: the annual PRC audit must be completed, corporate income tax and dividend withholding tax paid, and a board resolution plus SAFE registration submitted. Capital-account proceeds held in a capital escrow account must be used only within the approved business scope and cannot be commingled with current-account funds. Export/service receipt proceeds must be repatriated into China; no fixed statutory conversion ratio is imposed, but each bank enforces SAFE's quota-based retention limits. On the Taiwan side, TWD/USD clearing and outward remittances are monitored by CBC under the Statute for Regulation of Foreign Exchange, and cross-strait payments require clear transaction purpose codes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods exports, SAFE requires 100% repatriation of the export foreign-currency proceeds to a PRC-designated bank within 90 days from customs clearance; the mandatory conversion percentage is 0% under the current post-verification regime, meaning the WFOE may either voluntarily settle the proceeds into CNY or retain them within its current-account FX quota. For service-trade export receipts such as cross-border remittance agency fees, proceeds are expected within 30 days after the invoice value date and must be credited to the entity's onshore FX account; any balance exceeding the bank-aggregated current-account quota must be converted or justified to SAFE.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange of the People's Bank of China (SAFE); counterpart Taiwan-side authority: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low; not viable as primary treasury infrastructure. Offshore digital EMIs are not recognized as SAFE-designated foreign exchange banks and cannot lawfully open or maintain corporate current-account or capital-account balances for a PRC WFOE. They may only serve as software or payment-aggregation providers if they avoid deposit-taking and use a licensed onshore settlement bank; any custody or settlement of FX would expose the WFOE to unlicensed cross-border payment activity. The Taiwan parent should settle through a licensed Taiwan commercial bank or a bank with cross-strait correspondent capability.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder must instruct its CBC-regulated bank to send a SWIFT MT103 to the Chinese bank's designated receiving branch. The wire should reference the WFOE's SAFE FDI foreign-exchange registration number and use the SAFE-determined balance-of-payments purpose code for FDI equity injection. The Chinese bank will debit the funds to the WFOE's capital escrow account, complete the SAFE information registration, and issue a bank receipt for CPA capital verification. Conversion of the USD/TWD proceeds into CNY may be executed at the spot rate quoted by the receiving bank, with the capital-account funds restricted to the approved business purposes.Operational payments to Taiwan suppliers, service providers and agent partners should be cleared by SWIFT MT103 direct credits using a current-account FX transaction code. For goods-backed cross-strait trade, the WFOE may use commercial letters of credit or documentary collections; remittance payouts to expatriate beneficiaries are settled by MT103 to the correspondent bank, followed by local cash-pickup clearing/ATM disbursement through the licensed payout network. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on outbound dividends paid by a Chinese WFOE to a non-resident enterprise shareholder under CIT Law Article 3, Article 37, and Article 59, before any relief under the Cross-Strait tax arrangement or exception for Chinese-listed shares.

Deadline: · Frequency:

By May 31 of the following tax year for the annual return and self-assessment; quarterly prepayments are due by April 15, July 15, October 15, and January 15, or the next business day if the statutory date falls on a non-working day.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File contemporaneous documentation is mandatory in the PRC where annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million for tangible assets, RMB 100 million for financial assets, RMB 100 million for intangible assets, or RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions. Master File is required if total annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion, or if cross-border related-party transactions involve two or more jurisdictions and exceed RMB 500 million.

Economic substance scope: The Chinese GAAR under CIT Law Article 47 and general anti-abuse doctrine allow re-characterisation of a remittance SPV that lacks real commercial substance. The WFOE must demonstrate employees, licensed payment acceptance infrastructure, settlement interfaces, AML/CFT compliance staff, board decision-making, and risk management in mainland China as Core Income-Generating Activities. If the WFOE is a mere channel for Taiwan-controlled flows, the tax authorities may disregard the entity, re-attribute profits to the actual service provider, impose WHT on deemed distributions, and apply economic-substance and payment-licence tests administered by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange and the People's Bank of China.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing and Cross-Border Profit Shift

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41, 44, 48 and Implementing Regulations; SAT Announcement 42/2016. The tax authority adjusts the transaction to arm's length, disallows inflated deductions, imposes underpayment interest at market rate plus penalties under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection Article 63 of 50%-5x of the tax shortfall, and may extend the statute of limitations to 10 years for deliberate concealment.

Director personal liability: A director who approves false transfer-pricing records is personally liable for joint restitution under Company Law if the limited liability is abused to evade taxes; the tax authority may impose an exit ban under the collection law until tax is paid. Criminal Law Article 201 exposes responsible directors to up to 7 years imprisonment and fines for fraudulent tax declarations; a court may pierce the corporate veil and order personal asset recovery where the WFOE is a pure conduit.
Tax Evasion and Dividend Re-characterization

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47, Article 3, Article 37, and Notice 2017 No. 37 re-characterise the payment as a dividend; the deduction is denied; 10% dividend WHT is collected from the WFOE as withholding agent; late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day and a 50%-5x administrative penalty for false declaration apply; criminal liability arises under Criminal Law Article 201 for intentionally evading tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign sham service agreements and approve the outward payment are jointly liable for the unpaid WHT; they may be blacklisted and denied exit by Chinese frontier inspection under the exit-restriction administrative measure; criminal prosecution of the responsible officer can lead to asset freezing, civil restitution to the tax authority, and imprisonment from 3 to 7 years where the amount is enormous and exceeds 30% of the legally payable tax.
Money-Laundering Shell and Payment-Licence Fraud

Statutory sanction: Anti-Money Laundering Law Articles 32-33, Criminal Law Article 191 on money laundering, and Criminal Law Article 225 on illegal business operation. The central bank may revoke the payment business qualification, impose fines up to RMB 5 million and confiscate illegal gains; the tax authority may impose an additional 50%-5x tax penalty under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection Article 63 for knowingly false tax returns.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced: directors who control the shell are liable for unpaid tax and AML fines with personal assets; they are placed on the individual exit blacklist by the tax and immigration authorities; criminal conviction for money laundering carries up to 7 years imprisonment for related laundering acts, and evasion of tax can yield an additional term of up to 7 years, to be served concurrently or consecutively as ordered by the court.
