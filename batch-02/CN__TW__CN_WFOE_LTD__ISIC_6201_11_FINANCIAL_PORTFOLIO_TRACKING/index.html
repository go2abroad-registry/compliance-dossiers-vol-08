Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; local STA tax service bureau at the WFOE's registered address; online operations through the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) and the STA Electronic Invoice Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — incorporate the WFOE with the local Administration for Market Regulation (AMR) and obtain the Business License of Enterprise Legal Person containing the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2 — within 30 days of business-license issuance, complete tax registration with the local STA via the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax); the USCC is the statutory TIN and VAT registration number, and no separate paper tax registration certificate is issued. Step 3 — elect VAT taxpayer status as general VAT taxpayer (mandatory when cumulative 12-month taxable turnover reaches CNY 5,000,000; voluntary below threshold) or small-scale taxpayer. Step 4 — activate the VAT invoice issuance quota and digital invoice seal through the Electronic Invoice Service Platform, complete the electronic filing/collection agreements, and bind the legal representative and accounting personnel to the e-Tax record. Step 5 — for a foreign non-resident investor/parent without a permanent establishment in China, no Chinese TIN is generally issued or required; China-sourced payments are covered by the WFOE's non-resident withholding registration and reporting. If the foreign party performs taxable activities in China through a branch or service operation, that branch/operation must separately register via the same e-Tax portal and obtain a USCC-based TIN.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero percent VAT (export zero-rating) or cross-border VAT exemption is available to a Chinese-resident VAT taxpayer for digital software/cloud services, including the personal wealth analytics and investment tracking application, when: (i) the recipient is a Taiwan/overseas enterprise or individual; (ii) the service is classified as software services or information technology services under the VAT reform rules; (iii) the service is wholly consumed outside mainland China; and (iv) the WFOE files the cross-border service declaration through the Electronic Tax Bureau and retains the statutory supporting documents. If the service is consumed in mainland China or the export conditions are not proven, domestic VAT applies (6% for modern services, or the applicable small-scale simplified rate).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC maintains a bifurcated foreign exchange regime under the Foreign Exchange Control Regulations and SAFE administrative rules. Current account transactions are effectively convertible upon genuine verification by a designated foreign exchange bank, covering software subscription receipts, licensing fees, and ordinary operating disbursements. Capital account transactions remain controlled; a Taiwan-invested WFOE must obtain SAFE foreign exchange registration, open a dedicated capital account for FDI equity injection, and then convert registered capital into RMB for permitted operating purposes. There is no statutory mandatory surrender ratio for export proceeds because SAFE abolished compulsory surrender in 2012; however, all current account foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated through designated banks with supporting contracts or invoices and reconciled through the trade-in-services monitoring system. Profit remittance to Taiwan shareholders is lawful after annual statutory audit, corporate income tax settlement, and submission of audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and a board resolution to the settlement bank, with non-resident dividend withholding tax applied. The overall assessment is tight administrative control rather than full currency inconvertibility: the WFOE may conduct profitable RMB conversions if every transaction is backed by genuine commercial documentation and proper SAFE filing. Capital account liberalization is gradually expanding through pilot policies, but the bank compliance burden remains heavy.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE imposes no mandatory export proceeds surrender ratio; the statutory conversion ratio is 0% and the WFOE may retain foreign exchange or settle into RMB. For goods-trade exports, the 180-day collection rule applies from the customs declaration date under the Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration Measures. For software and services revenue, receipt must occur by contractual due date and be verified by the settlement bank. All proceeds must be repatriated to China unless an offshore retained account is approved; unrepatriated or overdue amounts trigger SAFE monitoring suspension and bank restrictions.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, a vice-ministerial agency operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central banking authority for foreign exchange control and cross-border capital account administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Overseas digital fintech EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, and Revolut Business are not licensed as Chinese payment institutions and are legally barred from opening local RMB settlement accounts or performing SAFE-declared current account conversions for a China-incorporated WFOE. They may only act as front-end collectors of foreign subscription receipts in limited scenarios and must downstream the funds into a licensed Chinese bank account for tax and SAFE reporting. The overall feasibility rating is Low for use as the primary treasury rail because such EMIs cannot provide compliant capital account registration, tax withholding documentation, or PBOC-recognized clearing. The WFOE should contractually use them solely for overseas collection and maintain Bank of China or ICBC as the authoritative clearing bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must route capital injection through SWIFT MT103 wire transfers using SAFE's foreign direct investment purpose code for equity capital contribution, with the beneficiary name exactly matching the WFOE's SAFE foreign exchange registration certificate. The receiving bank credits the funds into the capital account (foreign exchange capital account), then a PRC certified public accountant issues a capital verification report. RMB settlement is performed after confirming the WFOE business is not on the foreign investment negative list and after submitting the FDI report through the bank's national foreign exchange monitoring system. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Overseas digital fintech EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, and Revolut Business are not licensed as Chinese payment institutions and are legally barred from opening local RMB settlement accounts or performing SAFE-declared current account conversions for a China-incorporated WFOE. They may only act as front-end collectors of foreign subscription receipts in limited scenarios and must downstream the funds into a licensed Chinese bank account for tax and SAFE reporting. The overall feasibility rating is Low for use as the primary treasury rail because such EMIs cannot provide compliant capital account registration, tax withholding documentation, or PBOC-recognized clearing. The WFOE should contractually use them solely for overseas collection and maintain Bank of China or ICBC as the authoritative clearing bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: RMB 200 million for annual related-party tangible property transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible or financial asset transfers; RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions. Master File is generally required when the group's consolidated annual revenue reaches RMB 2.5 billion and related-party transaction thresholds are met.

Economic substance scope: China applies a substance-over-form doctrine under its General Anti-Avoidance Rule and the Enterprise Income Tax Law. Although there is no offshore-style Economic Substance Regulations filing in China, tax authorities will examine the Core Income-Generating Activities, employees, premises, risk exposure, and decision-making functions of both the Chinese WFOE and the Taiwan shareholder before granting treaty benefits or allowing related-party deductions. A shell entity with no real business substance will be disregarded and income will be recharacterized.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company and Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 47 and Implementation Regulations authorize the tax authority to recharacterize the transaction under the General Anti-Avoidance Rule; the tax authority may impose special tax adjustment within 10 years, add interest, and impose late payment surcharges and penalties up to 50% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Under PRC Company Law Article 20, a court may pierce the corporate veil if the shareholder or director abuses the limited liability structure to harm creditors or the tax authority; directors may be ordered to restore assets personally, subjected to asset freezes, travel bans and tax blacklisting, and in serious cases may face criminal prosecution for tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201 with imprisonment up to seven years.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 46, together with SAT Bulletin 2016 No. 42, allow a transfer pricing audit, upward adjustment of taxable income, disallowance of excessive deductions, charging of interest on the adjustment, a daily late payment surcharge, and an evasion penalty under Article 63 of the Tax Administration Law of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve false transfer pricing documentation or knowingly cause the company to underpay tax may be jointly and severally liable for the tax debt; the public security authorities may place them on exit control lists, freeze personal assets, and impose criminal liability for false tax filings, with imprisonment terms of up to seven years under Criminal Law Article 201.
Failure to Withhold on Outbound Dividends and Abusive Treaty Claim

Statutory sanction: Under PRC Tax Administration Law Article 69, a withholding agent that fails to withhold and remit tax is liable to a penalty of between 50% and 300% of the amount that should have been withheld; the tax authority may also collect the underpaid tax from the withholding agent and impose daily late payment surcharges.

Director personal liability: Practicing directors who caused the company to breach withholding obligations may be personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; the tax authority may blacklist the company and its responsible directors, impose travel bans, restrict exit from China, and pursue criminal investigation for intentional tax non-remittance, leading to imprisonment and confiscation of personal assets.
