Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio (Chamber of Commerce) of the registered office, operating under the Registro Único Empresarial (RUES) and the Superintendencia de Sociedades.

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada regime), Arts. 1, 5, 9, 17 and 21; Colombian Commercial Code (Decree 410 of 1971), Arts. 352-364 and 110-115; Sole Regulatory Decree 1074 of 2015, Title 1, Chapter 2; Resolution 100-000004 of 2021 (RUES operational standards).

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) containing the corporate bylaws (estatutos), filed for registration in the Mercantile Registry; public deed (escritura pública) is required only when the corporation holds real estate assets.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 28 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum legal capital is required for an S.A.S. The incorporation instrument must state authorized, subscribed, and paid capital; at least one share must be subscribed and paid on incorporation. The remainder of the subscribed capital may be paid in installments within two years (Law 1258 of 2008, Art. 9). No escrow or blocked bank account is required by corporate law. For the wholesale fuel license, the Ministry of Mines and Energy may apply a minimum net worth and working capital test set by Resolution 40137 of 2021.

Funding & unlock path: Certificado de Bomberos (fire safety clearance referencing NFPA 30 and Colombian fire regulations), structural safety certificate (Certificado de Seguridad Estructural), land-use certificate (Certificado de Uso de Suelo) issued by the Curaduría Urbana, and the mandatory occupational health and safety program (SG-SST) registered with the ARL (Aseguradora de Riesgos Laborales) under risk category III-V. Additional mobile and port access permits from DIMAR and the Superintendencia de Puertos e Transporte are required for waterfront bunkering operations.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum legal capital is required for an S.A.S. The incorporation instrument must state authorized, subscribed, and paid capital; at least one share must be subscribed and paid on incorporation. The remainder of the subscribed capital may be paid in installments within two years (Law 1258 of 2008, Art. 9). No escrow or blocked bank account is required by corporate law. For the wholesale fuel license, the Ministry of Mines and Energy may apply a minimum net worth and working capital test set by Resolution 40137 of 2021.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN). Official portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional e-filing and e-invoice validation platform: MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicio y Control Automatizado). Customs export declarations and national tax collection are administered by the same authority.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned S.A.S. obtains its NIT as follows: (1) appoint a legal representative domiciled in Colombia; (2) execute the incorporation deed before a notary and file it with the competent Cámara de Comercio, which automatically generates the provisional NIT; (3) complete the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) registration with DIAN, activating the responsibility codes for income and complementary tax (ordinary regime), VAT (IVA) at 19%, withholding at source, national fuel tax codes if applicable, and the foreign-exchange settlement registration; (4) obtain an authorized digital signature and e-invoicing certificate (firma electrónica) from an accredited certification entity; (5) register the fiscal address and legal representative in MUISCA. Foreign shareholders must submit apostilled corporate existence certificates and register their shareholding data with the Chamber of Commerce. If the S.A.S. acts as principal exporter of hydrocarbons, it must additionally obtain export authorization from the Ministry of Mines and Energy and DIAN's fuel-export registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The S.A.S. is a Colombian tax resident corporation subject to national corporate income tax at 35% on worldwide income, with a foreign tax credit for foreign-source income. No pass-through or check-the-box classification exists under Colombian law. Territorial scope: modified worldwide taxation, Colombian-source income fully taxed and foreign-source income taxed with credit relief. The operation is not located in a Zona Franca; therefore the special tax-free zone regime does not apply.

Available election forms

Colombia does not permit US-style check-the-box elections. Available statutory mechanisms include: (a) income tax consolidation filings for Colombian 'Grupos Empresariales' upon DIAN authorization; (b) election to file as a Large Taxpayer ('Gran Contribuyente') with centralized filing and monthly VAT settlement; (c) the 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (SIMPLE) regime is statutorily available but is excluded for wholesale fuel brokerage and is therefore unavailable; (d) foreign tax credit is granted by default, with no alternative to elect a deduction once the credit is applied; (e) the VAT export zero-rating election under Article 481 E.T. for qualifying services rendered to foreign beneficiaries without a Colombian permanent establishment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover threshold for mandatory VAT registration in Colombia: any S.A.S. performing taxable services or goods sales must register in the RUT and assume IVA responsibility before the first transaction. Registration is mandatory from incorporation regardless of capital or turnover. The SIMPLE regime (available up to 100,000 UVT, approximately COP 472,000,000 in 2024) is statutorily excluded for hydrocarbon wholesale brokerage.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports under Article 481 E.T. are zero-rated (0% VAT) with the right to refund of input VAT, provided the goods are physically exported and covered by a definitive export declaration. Services, including brokerage commissions on crude oil and refined products, qualify only if: (i) the beneficiary is a foreign entity without a Colombian permanent establishment; (ii) the service is economically used abroad; and (iii) the consideration is received in foreign currency and settled through the local foreign-exchange market. In a pure commission mandate, only the principal exporter may claim zero-rating for the goods; the commission is exempt only when the brokerage service is performed entirely abroad. If the S.A.S. acts as principal (buy/resell), title and risk must pass outside Colombia and the S.A.S. must hold the hydrocarbon export registration; otherwise the commission is taxed at 19%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX – Declaración de Exportación) with definitive acceptance
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch abroad
  • Foreign exchange settlement certificate (Certificado de Compra de Divisas) issued by a local intermediary bank
  • SWIFT MT103 and/or standby letter of credit evidencing receipt of payment

  • Apostilled corporate documents of the foreign beneficiary for service exports

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing (factura electrónica de venta – FE) through DIAN-validated technology providers; issuance requires prior 'habilitación de numeración' in MUISCA. Each invoice must bear the CUFE/CUDS unique digital code and QR code validated through DIAN's web service; clearance is granted in real time, and non-validated invoices are invalid for cost deduction and VAT crediting. For exports, the electronic export invoice must be pre-submitted to DIAN's export module with the bill of lading attached digitally; fuel sales require special hydrocarbon transaction codes and monthly operational reports to DIAN.

DIAN's fuel and foreign-trade units automatically cross-check export declarations, e-invoices, and bank FX-settlement records. Failure to repatriate and settle export proceeds within the 180-day term triggers fines of up to 200% of the non-settled amount, plus prohibition on further exporting. False or inflated export zero-rating claims subject the taxpayer to a 100% surcharge plus interest on the under-declared tax, a penalty of up to 300% of the refund improperly obtained, loss of the right to apply for further refunds for the next two taxable periods, and criminal export-fraud prosecution (4–9 years imprisonment, aggravated to up to 12 years for hydrocarbon smuggling). The retroactive audit window is 3 years, extended to 5 years in cases of omitted declarations or exchange-regime breaches.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso operates under a managed floating exchange-rate framework. Current-account transactions, including maritime fuel oil exports, brokerage commissions, and transportation charges, are freely convertible when channeled through the regulated foreign-exchange market, i.e., through authorized commercial banks, finance corporations, or foreign-exchange houses. Capital-account transactions are partially controlled; foreign direct investment in the Colombian S.A.S., foreign loans, and equity contributions must be registered with Banco de la República using Formulario No. 5 (investments) or Formulario No. 6 (external credit). Profit remittance by dividend distribution to a non-resident shareholder is legally permitted after evidence of registration and after the applicable supplementary dividend withholding tax is discharged; the withholding is generally 10% on the dividend paid to non-residents under Article 245 of the Colombian Tax Statute, provided the distributing entity is fully compliant with corporate income tax obligations. There are no restrictive convertibility quotas for physical commodity trade, but each FX transaction must carry the correct statistical purpose code and complete underlying documentation; an incorrect code or a mismatch between the invoice, B/L, payment beneficiary, and beneficiary bank is the principal practical convertibility blocker.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For exports of refined petroleum products and marine fuel oil by wholesale/trading entities (ISIC 4661), the exporter must first obtain the liquid-fuel wholesale commercialization resolution from the Ministerio de Minas y Energía. The customs export declaration (DEX) must classify the merchandise accurately, and the resulting foreign currency must be repatriated and channeled through the regulated exchange market via an authorized domestic intermediary. The mandatory channeling period is 13 months from shipment, and the statutory obligation covers 100% of the net export value after only pre-authorized and fully documented deductions such as freight, insurance, inspection fees, legal commissions, and demurrage. There is no surrender quota requiring the exporter to sell the foreign exchange to Banco de la República; the exporter may retain foreign currency in a permitted domestic foreign-currency account, but title and the channeling evidence must flow through a Colombian authorized intermediary. The bank will demand the DEX, the invoice validly issued under DIAN e-invoicing rules, the bill of lading or warehouse delivery order, and the actual SWIFT credit in order to close the exchange-market Formulario for each export lot.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia), acting jointly with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) as the prudential and banking regulator, and the Unidad Administrativa Especial Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) for customs and foreign trade compliance. The exchange-control regime is principally codified in Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1735 of 1993, Banco de la República Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-083, Resolution 8 of 2000 of the Comité de Cambios, and Circular Externa 026 of 2021 and 032 of 2021 issued by the SFC on money-laundering and terrorist-financing risk management.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Colombian exchange-control law requires that export proceeds, trade settlements, and capital injections be routed exclusively through authorized domestic foreign-exchange intermediaries. Offshore electronic money institutions not licensed by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia cannot issue a valid Declaración de Cambio and therefore cannot lawfully serve as the primary treasury rail for the S.A.S.; using an offshore EMI as a collection point for commodity receivables would breach the mandatory channeling obligation and create an AML red flag with tier-1 correspondents. A Colombian-regulated fintech may be used only as an initiating payment channel or as a wallet layer over a tier-1 account, never as the depositary of trade proceeds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The non-resident shareholder executes the S.A.S. capital contribution by SWIFT MT103 wire to the company's USD-denominated account with a Colombian authorized intermediary; the wire must reference the shareholder corporate name and the exact wording 'Aportes de Capital S.A.S. — Inversión Internacional' together with the internal investment registration number, if previously assigned. Within 20 business days of receipt, the S.A.S. must file Formulario No. 5 with Banco de la República registering the foreign direct investment; the same form is updated upon subsequent capital increases and upon dividend remittance. The bank will verify that the remitter is the registered shareholder, that the account beneficiary matches the commercial registration of the S.A.S., and that the purpose code corresponds to foreign direct investment (not to service payments or loan disbursements); a loan-financed injection requires a separate Formulario No. 6 and a formal external credit agreement.Physical marine fuel oil and distillate settlements are executed using SWIFT MT103 wires for direct supplier payments, with each wire carrying the applicable exchange-control purpose code derived from the underlying DEX or import/export registration; the bank requires the invoice, the delivery evidence, and the beneficiary bank confirmation before release. Where credit enhancement is required, the treasury rail is a commercial letter of credit issued under UCP600 for the documentary trade cycle, or a Standby Letter of Credit issued under ISP98 and transmitted by MT760; the Standby L/C serves as a performance or deferred-payment guarantee to suppliers, shipowners, and storage operators while the principal settlement flows by MT103 against presentation of conformity documents: invoice, B/L, certificate of quality and quantity, and insurance certificate. The Colombian issuing bank will treat each disbursement as part of the Declaración de Cambio, automatically reconciling the outgoing trade dollars with the corresponding customs declarations, thereby preserving full auditability of the foreign trade corridor.Deliverable COP/USD forward contracts with Colombian authorized financial intermediaries, expressly tied to the maturity of underlying export receivables or supplier payment obligations; these forwards are permissible without prior Banco de la República authorization when the counterparty is a licensed local institution.Plain-vanilla FX swaps between COP and USD for short-term liquidity alignment of operating accounts and Standby L/C margin calls, where the notional quantum is consistent with underlying trade flows.Limited use of non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed offshore for the COP/USD pair; although internationally available, NDFs are operationally acceptable to the treasury only for non-locally registered subsidiary flows, since a Colombian-resident S.A.S. meets the same commercial need with onshore deliverable forwards that are automatically eligible as exchange-market hedges.Cross-currency interest rate swaps with licensed investment-grade counterparties, used to hedge multi-year inventory financing or capital-injection debt in foreign currency, provided the hedged notional is documented against the registered foreign exposure.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low. Colombian exchange-control law requires that export proceeds, trade settlements, and capital injections be routed exclusively through authorized domestic foreign-exchange intermediaries. Offshore electronic money institutions not licensed by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia cannot issue a valid Declaración de Cambio and therefore cannot lawfully serve as the primary treasury rail for the S.A.S.; using an offshore EMI as a collection point for commodity receivables would breach the mandatory channeling obligation and create an AML red flag with tier-1 correspondents. A Colombian-regulated fintech may be used only as an initiating payment channel or as a wallet layer over a tier-1 account, never as the depositary of trade proceeds.
