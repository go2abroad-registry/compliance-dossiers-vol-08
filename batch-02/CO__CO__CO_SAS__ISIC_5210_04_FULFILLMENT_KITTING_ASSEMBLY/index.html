Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio del domicilio social (e.g., Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá) operating the Registro Único Empresarial (RUE); oversight by Superintendencia de Sociedades; tax enrollment at DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales).

Governing statute

Law 1258 of 2008 (Ley 1258 de 2008), Articles 1-6, 17-19, and 22 governing the Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.); supplemented by the Colombian Commercial Code (Código de Comercio), Articles 98-106, 110, 372-374, and Decree 1074 of 2015 (regulatory decree); tax registration under E.T. Articles 555-1 and 555-2.

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument) with attached by-laws, filed for commercial registration in the Registro Único Empresarial (RUE).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 18 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital for S.A.S.; at least one share must be subscribed, and at least one share's par value must be paid in at incorporation. In-kind contributions require an expert valuation. No escrow account requirement under Colombian law.

Funding & unlock path: Certificado de Bomberos (fire safety certificate per NFPA retrofitted standards) for the warehouse; structural load inspection under NRS-10 (Reglamento Colombiano de Construcción Sismo Resistente); ARL occupational clearance for racking, forklift and manual handling operations per Resolución 0312 de 2019.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum capital for S.A.S.; at least one share must be subscribed, and at least one share's par value must be paid in at incorporation. In-kind contributions require an expert valuation. No escrow account requirement under Colombian law.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, that the incorporators hereby subscribe in full to the authorized share capital of the Company, Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.), the contributed amounts being acknowledged as paid into the corporate treasury and recorded in the register of shareholders. RESOLVED FURTHER, that the legal domicile and registered office are established at the address declared in the incorporation document, serving for all statutory and administrative notices. RESOLVED FURTHER, that the legal representative is authorized to execute all company registrations, commercial enrollments, tax filings, and bank account-opening procedures.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

None mandatory — Ministerio de Industria, Comercio y Turismo (MinCIT) classifies ISIC 5210 as a storage/logistics activity; municipal zoning approval is the only locational requirement.

Category: Not applicable — no manufacturing license required; certification may be voluntarily obtained under NTC-ISO 9001 or BASC for secure 3PL operations.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — Official transactional portal: www.dian.gov.co (MUISCA platform for electronic filings, RUT, and tax returns).

E-government filing portal

For a Colombian S.A.S. with non-resident shareholders: (1) Incorporate the S.A.S. before a Notary and register the deed with the Cámara de Comercio of the registered domicile; (2) Enroll the company in the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) via MUISCA, submitting the pre-RUT form to obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which DIAN assigns automatically upon acceptance; (3) Obtain a digital signature certificate from an authorized certification entity (e.g., Certicámara) to sign electronic invoices and tax declarations; (4) Register the S.A.S. as a full VAT responsible party (responsable del régimen común) and complete the DIAN electronic invoicing activation (habilitación de la factura electrónica de venta) through an authorized software operator or owning platform; (5) Apply for exporter status in the RUT and, if physically handling outbound goods, obtain the customs authorization (autorización aduanera) required for a 3PL fulfillment warehouse.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A S.A.S. is a standalone income tax taxpayer (impuesto sobre la renta, 35% statutory rate for tax year 2024 and subsequent years), an IVA responsible party, and a municipal Industry and Commerce Tax (ICA) subject. No pass-through or fiscal transparency election is recognized for a standard S.A.S. — For a Colombian S.A.S. with non-resident shareholders: (1) Incorporate the S.A.S. before a Notary and register the deed with the Cámara de Comercio of the registered domicile; (2) Enroll the company in the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) via MUISCA, submitting the pre-RUT form to obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria), which DIAN assigns automatically upon acceptance; (3) Obtain a digital signature certificate from an authorized certification entity (e.g., Certicámara) to sign electronic invoices and tax declarations; (4) Register the S.A.S. as a full VAT responsible party (responsable del régimen común) and complete the DIAN electronic invoicing activation (habilitación de la factura electrónica de venta) through an authorized software operator or owning platform; (5) Apply for exporter status in the RUT and, if physically handling outbound goods, obtain the customs authorization (autorización aduanera) required for a 3PL fulfillment warehouse.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — A S.A.S. is a standalone income tax taxpayer (impuesto sobre la renta, 35% statutory rate for tax year 2024 and subsequent years), an IVA responsible party, and a municipal Industry and Commerce Tax (ICA) subject. No pass-through or fiscal transparency election is recognized for a standard S.A.S.

Available election forms

Colombia does not offer a US-style check-the-box election. Available elections include: (i) the Régimen Simple de Tributación (SIMPLE) under Law 2010/2019, available to a S.A.S. with annual gross revenues not exceeding COP 100,000 million, replacing income tax, ICA, and simplifying IVA; (ii) election to be taxed as a Colombian Holding Company (CHC) under Art. 772-1 E.T. (Law 2277/2022), which permits a 0% dividend withholding and exemption on qualified foreign-source income; (iii) the option for exporters to elect the documentation-based treatment of exported services to access the 0% IVA rate; (iv) transfer-pricing documentation elections (choose the simplified or comprehensive report threshold).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No numeric threshold is prescribed for corporations: VAT liability begins upon the first taxable act in the national territory, regardless of turnover. A de-minimis simplified regime exists exclusively for natural persons with annual gross income not exceeding 3,500 UVT (approximately COP 164.7 million for 2024) and is unavailable to a S.A.S., which is therefore always mandatorily registered as a responsable del régimen común.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and qualifying exported services are taxed at 0% IVA (excluded with right to refund of input VAT), provided: (i) goods physically depart from the national customs territory, evidenced by an accepted DEX (Declaración de Exportación) and the actual dispatch from the kitting/fulfillment warehouse; (ii) for services, including fulfillment, kitting, and light assembly, the recipient must be a non-resident entity without a Colombian permanent establishment and the economic benefit of the service must be realized exclusively abroad, supported by a written export services contract (contrato de exportación) filed with DIAN; (iii) the service provider must be registered in the RUT as an exporter; and (iv) for physical shipments handled by the S.A.S. in a bonded or non-bonded warehouse, the customs formalities must be completed by the S.A.S. or its customs agent, with the commercial invoice, packing list, and transport document aligned. Input VAT embedded in raw materials, consumables, and logistics inputs is reclaimable through the periodic IVA return (Form 300) or through the refund process (Form 210) when a saldo a favor arises.

  • DEX (Declaración de Exportación) — electronic customs declaration accepted by DIAN
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical dispatch from the Colombian fulfillment center
  • Commercial Invoice (factura comercial) in Spanish or English issued by the S.A.S.
  • Packing List (lista de empaque) detailing kitted units, weights, and HS codes
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de cambio) issued by a Colombian authorized foreign-exchange intermediary, evidencing collection of export proceeds
  • Copy of the executed export services contract (for services/fulfillment contracts) and certificate of use of services abroad

Direct real-time clearance is mandatory. Every sale or service invoice must be issued in UBL 2.1 XML format containing a CUDE (Código Único de Documento Electrónico), generated with the taxpayer's DIAN-issued digital certificate, and transmitted to DIAN within a maximum pre-validation window of 10 minutes through the DIAN web service or an authorized technology operator. DIAN performs real-time validation, assigns the fiscal hash/digital stamp, and records the operation in the MUISCA central register. Exporters and 3PL companies must also transmit electronic payroll (nómina electrónica) and the electronic inward-freight documents (documento equivalente) applicable to foreign inbound cargo.

DIAN may disallow the refund and impose: (i) a 25% penalty on the improperly obtained refund (Art. 670 E.T.) plus default interest; (ii) a 100% penalty on the tax evaded for inexactitude or fraud (Art. 647 E.T.); (iii) an additional two-fold (200%) surcharge and loss of exporter status where documents are falsified, with criminal prosecution for aggravated tax evasion under Art. 402 Código Penal (between 4 and 9 years' imprisonment); (iv) administrative penalties for non-compliance with the foreign-exchange repatriation deadline (infracción cambiaria), including fines calculated on the un-repatriated sum and suspension of the exporter registration; and (v) retrospective clawback of VAT refunds for a limitation period of up to 5 years, extended in cases of criminal conduct, together with transfer-pricing adjustments if export prices materially deviate from the arm's length standard.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

7.5% on dividends paid from profits already taxed at the corporate level. A 35% withholding tax applies to distributions out of untaxed profits, plus a 3% remittance surcharge where applicable. In practice, properly taxed profits attract only the 7.5% WHT at dividend distribution.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due on the 9th business day of the ninth month after the fiscal year-end for NIT ending in 0 or 1, with later dates up to the 21st business day for higher NIT endings. The Colombian fiscal year is the calendar year. Deadlines are published annually via DIAN Resolution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Approx. COP 2.1 billion (45,000 UVT) in intercompany transactions with foreign related parties triggers mandatory Local File and Transfer Pricing return filing. Master File also required when multinational group consolidated revenue exceeds approx. 100,000 UVT (approx. COP 4.7 billion).

Economic substance scope: Colombia applies the OECD-aligned economic substance principles through general anti-avoidance rules (GAAR) and the substance-over-form doctrine in the Colombia Tax Code (Article 869 et seq.). The SAS must maintain real physical presence, operational staff, inventory control, local decision-making, and sufficient capital relative to the logistics functions performed. A profit allocation of at least a routine return on warehouse/fulfilment functions is expected.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Suppression of Income

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of 4 to 9 years under Article 434A of the Colombian Criminal Code. Administrative fine of 20% to 160% of the omitted tax plus default interest. Seizure of bank accounts and goods.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives and accountant-directors are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax when fraudulent conduct is proven; they may face pre-trial detention, travel bans, asset freezing, and criminal imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment of COP amount denied as a deduction; penalty of 20% to 160% of the understated tax; specific TP penalty of up to 3% of the unadjusted transaction value or 150-1,500 UVT depending on the seriousness.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign TP documentation with false information or who knowingly ignore comparable benchmarks may be personally liable to the extent of the resulting tax, and may be barred from corporate management for up to 10 years.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Total disregard of the entity for tax purposes, reattribution of income to the foreign parent, 35% tax on the deemed Colombian PE, and a 7.5% dividend WHT on any distribution. The underlying corporate registration may be canceled by the DIAN.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held civilly liable to third parties and the tax authority for the liabilities of the sham vehicle, with personal asset exposure and potential criminal charges for fraudulent misrepresentation.
AML / Money Laundering Conduit

Statutory sanction: Money laundering penalties of 10 to 30 years’ imprisonment and heavy fines equivalent to 10,000-50,000 SMMLV. Forced extinction of the company's assets and cancellation of the commercial registration.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally prosecuted for authorizing transactions with funds potentially derived from illicit activity; they face criminal sentences, fines, travel restrictions, and international extradition from treaty partners.
Improper Dividend Remittance

Statutory sanction: The Colombian SAS is liable to pay the full undeclared WHT plus default interest and a 100% penalty of the missing amount. The foreign recipient may also lose treaty protection and face an additional 3% surcharge.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize the distribution without legal support are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withholding sum and can face criminal sanctions for intentional evasion.
