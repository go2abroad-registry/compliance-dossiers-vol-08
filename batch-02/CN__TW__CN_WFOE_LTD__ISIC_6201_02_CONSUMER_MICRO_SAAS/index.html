E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity without a fixed place of business in Taiwan that provides cross-border electronic services to Taiwanese individuals may apply for a Taiwan Unified Business Number (tax identification number) by submitting the applicable application form, identification documents, and service-related evidence to the National Taxation Bureau. A foreign entity establishing a wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) in Taiwan must first obtain approval from the Ministry of Economic Affairs, complete local corporation or branch registration, and then file business tax registration with the National Taxation Bureau within 15 days of registration issuance. For a non-resident digital service provider, no local entity is required; the tax ID is issued solely for Business Tax registration purposes.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Article 7 of the Value-added Business Tax Act, sales of goods for export and services used or consumed outside Taiwan qualify for zero-rating. For goods, the seller must export the goods and possess a customs export declaration. For digital software-as-a-service subscriptions, zero-rating applies when the service is provided to a non-Taiwanese purchaser or is used wholly outside Taiwan, and payment for the service is received in foreign currency; the service provider must retain documentation proving the foreign/useful-on-outside nature of the transaction.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) with corresponding Export Declaration Form
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate or Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Sales contract or purchase order demonstrating the transaction is an export and the service/goods destination is outside Taiwan

Taiwan enforces the Uniform Invoice system. Registered businesses must issue Uniform Invoices, and those using electronic Uniform Invoices must transmit data using the statutory E-Invoice Turnkey Platform (XML message format) to the Ministry of Finance; the Ministry assigns the Uniform Invoice number. Taiwan does not operate a continuous real-time fiscal clearance API for every taxpayer; transmission is periodic, and anti-fraud control is achieved through central Inventory of Uniform Invoices, taxpayer invoice numbering, and audit checks. Foreign cross-border e-service suppliers with no fixed place of business may issue simplified electronic receipts through the Tax Web portal.

The National Taxation Bureau may require production of all supporting export documents, including foreign exchange repatriation records, within 180 days of the export sale. If zero-rating cannot be substantiated or the 180-day repatriation deadline is missed, the zero rate is rescinded, the 5% Business Tax is assessed, and a penalty of not less than five percent and not more than three times the underpaid tax may be imposed under the Value-added Business Tax Act. Serious fraudulent export schemes involving forged documents or fictitious zero-rated sales are also subject to criminal liability and retrospective clawback under the Tax Collection Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a modernised but asymmetric convertibility regime. On current account, RMB is fully convertible under IMF Article VIII: a WFOE may freely convert receipts for trade, dividends, royalties and services once the bank verifies transaction authenticity against supporting contracts, invoices and tax certificates. On capital account, however, convertibility remains administratively controlled: inward direct investment requires SAFE's Foreign Exchange Registration for Foreign-Invested Enterprises (FDI registration), the opening of a dedicated capital settlement account, and a CPA capital verification report; outward direct investment, cross-border loans, and securities investments require separate filings and quotas. Profit remittance exposes the core hurdle: dividends cannot be routed out until the annual audit is completed, corporate income tax is settled, a board resolution is passed, and the bank confirms through the payment verification system that current-account funds are genuine; the Taiwan parent is then subject to non-resident withholding income tax at 10% absent an effective double-tax agreement between the mainland and Taiwan. In practice, the SAFE negative list prohibits the use of settled capital proceeds for principal-guaranteed wealth management, entrusted loans, offshore securities, or non-self-use real estate. The practical consequence is that the WFOE needs a licensed commercial bank to orchestrate both the capital-account registration and the current-account verification, while treasury planning must build in lead time for audit, tax clearance, and SAFE examination before every dividend distribution. Overall, convertibility is functional but not frictionless; compliance documentation determines the speed of capital movement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration Framework (2012 reform), export proceeds must be repatriated to a domestic current-account foreign exchange settlement account within 30 days of the contractual collection maturity date, and no later than 90 days from the date of export customs declaration. If collection is overdue by 90 days, the bank automatically reports the position to the Trade in Goods Monitoring System and the enterprise must file a written adjustment report within 30 days; prolonged overdues result in down-grading from the A-list facilitation tier to B-list or C-list status with full document-based review of every FX transaction. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0% - China has abolished the mandatory surrender quota; the exporter may retain 100% of export proceeds in a foreign currency settlement account or convert voluntarily at any time, provided the underlying customs declaration, invoice and collection match are verified through the monitoring system. Funds must not be left offshore; the full amount of the export invoice value must enter the domestic banking system within the statutory timeline.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), including its local branches and the national Foreign Exchange Monitoring System, as the sole regulator for capital account registration, foreign exchange accounts, trade verification, and cross-border settlement in the People's Republic of China.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low - subject to hard legal constraints. Offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, Multinet) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in mainland China and cannot serve as the primary settlement account for a domestic WFOE. The PBOC Non-Bank Payment Institution licensing regime (effective 2024) grants payment business licences only to onshore entities; foreign EMI aggregators may cooperate with licensed Chinese payment institutions or banks as technical facilitators for export collection, but the WFOE's statutory treasury, capital injection registration, and tax/AML accountability must route through a licensed domestic commercial bank and SAFE-registered current-account bank. Hence an offshore EMI is legally barred from acting as a deposit or routing bank; it may only act as a downstream collection convenience for micro-subscription turnover after the primary domestic banking layer is established.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: first, complete SAFE Foreign-Invested Enterprise establishment and FDI registration to obtain the Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate; second, open a dedicated Capital Settlement Account at a domestic commercial bank; third, the Taiwan shareholder remits equity funds via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 with remittance information clearly stating equity injection or increase of investment capital, using the ISO purpose code for investment rather than current account; fourth, upon receipt, the WFOE submits the inflow report and performs settlement into RMB under SAFE capital account settlement facilitation rules, ensuring funds are not used for entrusted loans, principal-guaranteed wealth management, securities investment, or non-self-use real estate; fifth, within the statutory corporate registration timeline, engage a licensed Chinese CPA to issue a capital verification report and update the SAFE FDI registration records with paid-in capital details; if the Taiwan shareholder remits RMB directly, the cross-border RMB direct investment route may be used but still requires the same SAFE registration and verification discipline.For subscription-software exports or cloud infrastructure procurement, use SWIFT MT103 for low-value recurring current-account payments with invoices and contracts attached to the bank's FX verification system; use commercial Letters of Credit for mid-to-high value hardware or co-location purchases; use documentary collections (D/P or D/A) for counterparty-verified trade scenarios where a full L/C is not cost-efficient. All outbound disbursements require the bank to verify underlying transaction authenticity and, where tax is due, the non-resident withholding tax payment certificate must be presented for cross-border royalties. Dividend remittances to the Taiwan parent require the board resolution, audited financial statements, corporate income tax clearance, and SAFE current-account payment verification.Onshore deliverable RMB forward FX contracts with real trade background and SAFE-registered bank counterpartyOnshore FX swaps and cross-currency swaps restricted to the actual residual exposure of the WFOE's cross-border receipts and obligationsPlain-vanilla RMB FX options purchased with the right to buy or sell, declared against an identified underlying exposure, at a qualified domestic bankNDFs (non-deliverable forwards) are strictly prohibited onshore for mainland entities and may only be quoted offshore at the Taiwan-parent level without direct onshore booking

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low - subject to hard legal constraints. Offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, Multinet) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in mainland China and cannot serve as the primary settlement account for a domestic WFOE. The PBOC Non-Bank Payment Institution licensing regime (effective 2024) grants payment business licences only to onshore entities; foreign EMI aggregators may cooperate with licensed Chinese payment institutions or banks as technical facilitators for export collection, but the WFOE's statutory treasury, capital injection registration, and tax/AML accountability must route through a licensed domestic commercial bank and SAFE-registered current-account bank. Hence an offshore EMI is legally barred from acting as a deposit or routing bank; it may only act as a downstream collection convenience for micro-subscription turnover after the primary domestic banking layer is established.
