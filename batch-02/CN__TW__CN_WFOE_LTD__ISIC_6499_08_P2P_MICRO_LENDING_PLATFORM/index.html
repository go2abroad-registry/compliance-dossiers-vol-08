Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局), official e-filing portal www.chinatax.gov.cn (electronic tax bureau: etax.chinatax.gov.cn). Taiwan-region taxable activities are administered separately by the National Taxation Administration, Ministry of Finance, Taiwan (財政部賦稅署, www.mof.gov.tw), which levies profit-seeking enterprise income tax (20%) and business tax (5%); cross-strait double taxation relief is governed by the 2015 Cross-Strait Agreement on Avoidance of Double Taxation and Enhancing Tax Cooperation (海峡两岸避免双重课税及加强税务合作协议).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The WFOE obtains its Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, 18 digits) upon incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation; this UCC functions as the de facto TIN and VAT ID. Step 2: the STA performs automatic taxpayer registration and issues the electronic tax registration record (电子税务局税务登记). Step 3: the WFOE designates a legal representative and a tax filing agent (办税人员), completes the taxpayer classification election (general taxpayer versus small-scale taxpayer), and signs the three-party bank-tax direct-debit agreement (三方协议). Step 4: the WFOE activates the tax digital certificate (税务UKey) and enrols in the fully digital e-invoice platform (数电票). Foreign shareholders need no separate PRC tax ID for the entity; non-resident withholding tax profiles are set up at the time dividends, interest or royalties are paid offshore.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise subject to 25% Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income; no pass-through election exists; profits of any Taiwan-region branch are taxable within the PRC worldwide base, with foreign tax credit relief only under the Cross-Strait agreement.

Available election forms

Available elections: (1) VAT status — small-scale taxpayer (annual turnover up to RMB 5,000,000; simplified levy of 1%–3% with no input VAT credit) versus general taxpayer (mandatory above RMB 5,000,000 or voluntary; 6% output VAT on financial and technology services with input VAT credits); (2) export tax refund method — production-enterprise exemption-crediting-refund (免抵退) versus trading-enterprise exemption-refund (免退税); (3) CIT preferential-rate claims — High/New-Technology Enterprise (15%) or Small Low-Profit Enterprise (effective 5% on taxable income up to RMB 3,000,000); (4) thin-capitalisation ratio election for related-party debt (2:1 general, 5:1 financial enterprises); (5) no hybrid-mismatch check-the-box regime — PRC CFC and GAAR rules recharacterise hybrid funding arrangements; Taiwan-region hybrid instruments are reviewed under STA anti-avoidance rulings.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

China: VAT exemption for small-scale taxpayers at monthly taxable sales ≤ RMB 100,000 (annual ≈ RMB 1,200,000); compulsory general-taxpayer VAT registration when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; however, a licensed P2P micro-lending WFOE must register for VAT from day one because it issues taxable invoices and supplies financial services (loan services and direct-charge financial services) at 6%; financial institutions receive no turnover exemption. Taiwan-region branches: business tax registration is compulsory upon licensing with no monetary threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (零税率) with refund of input VAT applies only to exported goods and to the closed list of cross-border services (such as research and development, software design, industrial design, copyright transfer and offshore service outsourcing) that are performed and consumed entirely outside China and paid in foreign currency. Pure financial services, including loan interest and P2P platform transaction fees, are not zero-ratable; at most they qualify for cross-border VAT exemption (跨境应税行为免征增值税) where the borrowing client is a non-resident and the lending arrangement is wholly supplied and consumed offshore, with no PRC situs of collateral, guarantee or performance. The WFOE must evidence the offshore consumption, the contractual performance place, the invoice issued to the non-resident, and collection in convertible currency. Goods-export zero-rating would apply only to a separately operating export goods line, never to lending cash flows.

Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期) requires real-time data collection across invoicing, bank settlement, customs and corporate filings. New WFOEs must use the fully digitalised electronic invoice (数电票) platform of the STA; each invoice contains a 20-digit digital invoice number, an anti-counterfeit QR code (防伪码) and an XML/OFD data file transmitted to the national e-invoice service platform through government API. High-volume fintech lenders are obligated to integrate the electronic tax bureau API (电子税务局接口) for automated issuance, real-time fiscal clearance and loan-to-invoice reconciliation; any offline reconciliation gap triggers automatic VAT risk-control alerts and blocked invoice quotas.

STA and Customs jointly screen export-rebate claims through the Golden Tax risk engine; red flags include transactions with offshore related parties, unmatched customs declarations, and missing FX receipts. Consequences: (1) retrospective clawback of the refunded VAT with statutory interest and late-fee surcharges (0.05% per day); (2) administrative penalty of 50%–500% of the misdeclared tax under Tax Administration Law Art. 63; (3) criminal liability under Criminal Law Art. 204 for fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds — fine of 100%–500% of the defrauded amount and imprisonment from up to 5 years to life for amounts that are large or especially large; (4) SAFE enforcement for failure to repatriate export proceeds — warnings, fines of up to RMB 300,000, suspension of foreign-exchange receipt qualification, and downgrade of the WFOE's trade-inspection integrity rating; (5) unlimited clawback period for fraudulent rebate claims; legal representatives, tax filing agents and beneficial owners bear joint and several liability.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains IMF Article VIII current-account convertibility, but the capital account is subject to SAFE registration and quota controls. Renminbi in the cross-border context is convertible only through designated onshore banks, and each capital movement must be justified by an FDI registration, foreign debt filing, or special licence. Profit remittance from a WFOE requires verified audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement certificate, bank FX reporting, and evidence of no outstanding regulatory liability. Taiwan treats TWD as generally externally convertible, but a Taiwan shareholder transferring funds into mainland China must use legitimate cross-strait banking channels and satisfy Taiwan CBC and Ministry of Finance AML/CFT screening; no automatic onshore convertibility exists for speculative positions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The WFOE's licensed micro-lending operations do not generate goods export proceeds; therefore, the goods-export surrender regime is not a primary treasury input. For any ancillary cross-border service receipts, SAFE's trade-in-services supervision requires repatriation to a domestic FX account by the bank verification deadline (normally based on the contract payment schedule, with the annual trade credit category determining the relaxation or acceleration of the timeline). There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio under the current goods-trade foreign exchange administration framework. The enterprise may elect to retain up to 100% of qualified current-account receipts in a FX account or sell it to a designated bank in the onshore RMB market; however, the bank must verify the goods-trade declaration and receipt book, and any pre-received payment exceeding 30 days must be reported in SAFE's monitoring system.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC) regulating the TWD settlement side and cross-strait RMB clearing arrangements through the Bank of China Taipei Branch.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low as the primary treasury rail, Medium only as a technical payment aggregator for non-client operational expenses. Offshore digital EMIs are not permitted to open virtual account ledgers for a mainland WFOE's client funds, do not hold PBOC settlement licences, and cannot clear RMB loan originations or repayments. They may be used for limited cross-border opex payables and supplier payments, but all lending principal and repayment flows must be settled through PBOC-licensed onshore third-party payment institutions and SAFE-regulated bank accounts.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The lawful route begins with a Taiwan-side board resolution which is notarised, then the mainland WFOE completes FDI basic information registration through the local market supervision authority and SAFE e-recordation. The bank designated for the Capital Escrow Account must receive an SWIFT MT103 from the Taiwan shareholder remitting bank; the remittance field must state the purpose as 'FDI capital subscription / cross-border RMB investment capital' and provide a matching FDI registration certificate or SAFE system record number. The receiving bank validates the source of funds, records the payment in RCPMIS (for RMB) or the SAFE capital project information system (for FX), and credits the funds only to the dedicated Capital Escrow Account. Funds in the capital account may then be settled into RMB for operational purposes under the bank's supervision against submitted invoices, contracts, salaries, rents and loan-origination operating costs.Operational cross-border vendor payments should use SWIFT MT103 with the standard SAFE current-account purpose code for service fees or royalties, supported by an invoice, contract, tax withholding certificate and bank payment application. Commercial letters of credit (LC) are not a relevant clearing rail for a micro-lending treasury: no commodity export/import flows are anticipated. Loan disbursements and repayments must be executed through China National Advanced Payment System (CNAPS) ACH direct debits to borrower deposit accounts and licensed local wallet payout systems (e.g., Alipay/WeChat Pay channels used through PBOC-regulated payment institutions). Under no circumstances may loan principal be routed out of the WFOE to an offshore wallet or to a Taiwan account in a manner that turns the internal lending platform into a cross-border money remitter.Onshore RMB/USD forward contracts with trade or foreign-debt backing, transacted through a SAFE-approved designated FX bank after underlying verification.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) used solely to hedge the WFOE's registered foreign-debt or shareholder loan interest/principal exposures, not for speculative open positions.Offshore non-deliverable forwards executed by the Taiwan parent (outside SAFE onshore supervision) to hedge anticipated cross-strait cash remittances, provided that such instruments do not create onshore RMB settlement obligations and comply with Taiwan CBC derivatives conduct rules.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% deemed withholding rate for dividends paid by a PRC resident enterprise to foreign non-resident corporate shareholders without a permanent establishment in China; under the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Arrangement, the rate may be reduced to 5% where the Taiwan resident beneficial-owner enterprise directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE's paid-up capital, otherwise 10%.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly provisional CIT return within 15 days after each quarter-end; annual reconciliation return within 5 months after fiscal year-end. For a calendar-year WFOE, the annual return is due by May 31.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File is required where aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1,000,000,000. Local File thresholds are: tangible asset transfers above RMB 200,000,000; intangible asset transfers above RMB 100,000,000; financial asset transfers above RMB 100,000,000; and other related-party transactions above RMB 40,000,000 in a single year.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not apply offshore-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR). Substance is nonetheless enforced through place-of-effective-management rules, beneficial-ownership doctrines, controlled-foreign-company rules, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR), and special tax adjustment powers. A licensed micro-lending WFOE must maintain actual headcount, physical office, underwriting and credit decision-making, risk-management systems, regulatory licence and books in China; artificial Taiwan-based conduit arrangements may be disregarded or recharacterised.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Law Article 63 imposes penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax plus 0.05% daily late-payment interest; Criminal Law Article 201 provides for imprisonment, criminal fines and asset confiscation for serious tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives may face exit bans (出境限制) for unpaid tax liabilities; courts may pierce the WFOE's corporate veil under PRC Company Law where assets are stripped, making directors and controlling shareholders jointly and severally liable; responsible officers may be criminally prosecuted.
Unlicensed Financial Business / Illegal Micro-Lending

Statutory sanction: PRC Criminal Law Articles 176 and 225 impose penalties for illegal public deposit-taking and illegal business operation, including imprisonment, fines and confiscation; the platform is subject to administrative closure, revocation and disgorgement of illegal gains.

Director personal liability: Directors, beneficial owners and officers who approved or controlled the unlicensed lending scheme face personal criminal liability, travel restrictions, asset freezing and disqualification from corporate office; cross-border fund movement can trigger mutual legal assistance and confiscation.
Anti-Money Laundering Violation

Statutory sanction: PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law imposes rectification orders, fines, licence revocation and confiscation of illegal gains; criminal liability under Criminal Law Article 191 applies for knowing receipt or transfer of proceeds of crime.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives, senior managers and AML compliance officers may be personally fined, disqualified from management positions and subjected to exit bans; intentional facilitation can result in imprisonment and personal asset forfeiture.
Transfer Pricing / Special Tax Adjustment

Statutory sanction: PRC tax authorities may adjust taxable income, impose special-tax-adjustment interest at the basis rate plus 5 percentage points, and apply penalties of 50% to 500% for false declaration or underpayment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly set non-arm's-length pricing or strip profits into Taiwan group companies are exposed to joint liability where corporate form is abused; exit restrictions and collection actions may be pursued against the legal representative.
Withholding Tax / Dividend Repatriation Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Law Article 69 imposes a penalty of 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld; the tax authority may also collect the under-withheld tax from the withholding agent or the non-resident recipient.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial officers responsible for withholding may be personally ordered to make good the unpaid tax if corporate assets are insufficient, and may face travel bans and administrative or criminal fines.
Shell Company / Treaty-Shopping Abuse

Statutory sanction: Beneficial-ownership denial, reversion to the 10% statutory withholding rate, GAAR recharacterisation, back-dated interest, penalties of 50% to 500% and possible criminal referral for fraud.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the corporate veil and hold directing shareholders personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; persons concerned may be subject to travel bans, asset freezes and disqualification from serving as directors.
