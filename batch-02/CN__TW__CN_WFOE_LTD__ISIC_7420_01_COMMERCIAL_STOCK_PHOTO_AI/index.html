Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A bare virtual office or commercial mailbox is not acceptable for a Chinese WFOE. SAMR requires a fixed, lawful, non-residential registered address with verifiable use rights; tax authorities require the same address to be capable of receiving inspection and service of process. In free-trade zones and qualified incubators, a registered-address hosting agreement may satisfy the registration formality, but Chinese banks will usually require a dedicated suite, lease invoice, and an on-site KYC visit before opening a settlement account. For this commercial stock-photo AI business, a serviced office or qualified incubator suite with an actual desk, employees, signboard, and business records is the minimum to avoid being labelled an abnormal tax registration. A pure virtual mailbox risks account refusal, tax invalidation, and regulatory inspection failure.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement for a Taiwan limited company. The Articles of Incorporation must state the total issued capital and the par value per share. All subscribed capital must be paid into a temporary corporate bank account in New Taiwan Dollars, retained until incorporation and released only after the company registration certificate is issued. A Taiwan-certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report unless the capital is less than the threshold exempting CPA audit by the MOEA. Foreign currency contributions must be remitted through a licensed bank and converted. The Board may also establish a shareholder loan in escrow, but such loan cannot be represented as paid-up capital.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current account transactions, including cross-border service income from stock-photography licensing and AI visual-asset royalties, subject to documentary authenticity verification by the settling bank. The capital account remains managed and only partially convertible: FDI equity injections, capital account receipts, and offshore financing are admitted through SAFE registration and restricted-use rules. Profit remittance by the Taiwan-invested WFOE entails three mandatory conditions before the dividend can leave China: (i) completion of the PRC statutory annual audit by a domestic CPA; (ii) full settlement of PRC corporate income tax, including the 10% non-resident enterprise withholding tax on dividends to the Taiwan shareholder (no PRC-Taiwan double-tax treaty applies); and (iii) submission of the board dividend resolution, audited financial statements, tax filing and tax clearance evidence, and the cross-border service trade tax filing record (税务备案表) to the remitting bank. Once those documents are verified, dividend remittance is processed as a current account outflow without separate SAFE approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The mandatory foreign-currency surrender regime (强制结汇) was abolished in 2012. Under SAFE's Trade in Goods and Trade in Services monitoring frameworks, export proceeds must be repatriated into the WFOE's domestic foreign-exchange accounts; for goods exports the standard collection window is monitored at ninety (90) days from customs declaration for advance receipts and one hundred eighty (180) days for open-account proceeds, with extensions obtainable through the SAFE monitoring system where contractual payment terms exceed the deadline. For digital service exports such as stock-photo and AI visual-asset licensing, the repatriation obligation is verified through digital-delivery contracts, marketplace platform settlement statements, and tax filings. There is no statutory conversion ratio: once repatriated, the WFOE may freely retain the foreign currency or convert it into CNY at the onshore market rate, provided each settlement is traceable to an underlying commercial transaction and recorded under the applicable SAFE purpose code.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (中国人民银行, PBOC) acting as the central monetary authority, together with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局, SAFE) as the delegated foreign-exchange regulatory administrator. Commercial banks are designated as settlement banks executing FX controls under SAFE's centralized registration and monitoring systems.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Currencycloud, Skye Pay) are rated Medium viability. They are legally usable as multi-currency collection accounts for overseas marketplace proceeds (e.g., Adobe Stock, Alamy, Stripe-settled offshore entities) provided the EMI does not carry out onshore payment business in China; all onshore collections from mainland customers must be settled through a PBOC-licensed payment institution or a domestic bank account. Stripe currently holds no onshore acquiring license in China, whereas PayPal operates through its licensed PRC subsidiary (PayPal/GoPay). Accordingly, the treasury structure should separate offshore PSP collection accounts from a domestic RMB settlement account, with repatriation by SWIFT MT103 into the WFOE's current-account FX settlement account under the export-proceeds rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Routing sequence: (1) Execute the cash equity injection per the WFOE's amended articles of association; (2) complete FDI FX registration with SAFE via the chosen bank and obtain the Business Registration Certificate containing the FDI registration code; (3) open a dedicated Foreign-Exchange Capital Account (外汇资本金账户) at the bank; (4) the Taiwan shareholder remits via SWIFT MT103 with purpose code identifying equity investment and reference 'FDI registration No. [XXXX]'; the ordering customer must exactly match the registered shareholder and the beneficiary must exactly match the WFOE name; (5) upon receipt the bank verifies the registration and credits the capital account; (6) convert into RMB under the discretionary/voluntary settlement (意愿结汇) mechanism and park liquidity in the RMB funds settlement account, with each disbursement supported by invoice/contract evidence; (7) observe the negative-list usage restrictions, which prohibit deployment into securities, real estate beyond the licensed business scope, or entrusted loans; (8) for Taiwan-sourced capital, the outward remittance by the Taiwan shareholder must be pre-approved by the Taiwan Investment Commission (MOEAIC) where applicable, and cross-strait notarized ownership documents must be presented to the bank.Royalty and licensing fees to global contributors are cleared by SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers from the WFOE's current-account FX settlement account, using SAFE service-trade purpose codes under the intellectual-property-royalties category; each single payment above the regulatory threshold requires the cross-border service trade tax filing record (税务备案表) obtained online before the bank will release funds. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when annual related-party transactions aggregate NT$50 million or more. Master File is required when the ultimate parent company's consolidated annual revenue exceeds NT$60 billion or when the Taiwan entity is designated as a significant contributor. Country-by-Country reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue exceeding NT$60 billion.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations code. However, under Taiwan's general anti-avoidance principle and the substance-over-rule doctrine, a Taiwan WFOE must demonstrate real business substance through local employees, active board management, local bank accounts and commercial contracts in Taiwan; a pure conduit arrangement is subject to income recharacterisation and penalty assessment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The tax authority will reassess underpaid corporate income tax plus a penalty of one to three times the underpaid amount under Article 110 of the Income Tax Act. Interest runs monthly from the due date. The five-year audit window applies, and favorable tax credits are withdrawn.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute fraudulent returns face criminal liability under the Criminal Code (forgery and fraud), with imprisonment up to five years. The court may pierce the corporate veil, impose travel bans, order civil restitution from personal assets, and hold directors jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall.
Shell Company or Artificial Conduit Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Under the Tax Administration Act and substance-over-form doctrine, the arrangement is disregarded; the Taiwan WFOE is reassessed on an arm's-length basis, denied deductions, charged 20% corporate income tax plus 5% retained earnings surtax, and fined for non-genuine registration.

Director personal liability: Where the legal entity is a mere facade, directors and ultimate beneficial owners may be held personally liable for unpaid taxes. Civil restitution, freezing orders, travel bans and, in egregious cases, imprisonment for criminal tax avoidance are available.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The tax authority adjusts cross-border related-party income to an arm's length price under Article 43-1 of the Income Tax Act. The adjustment results in additional taxable income, additional 20% tax, late payment interest, and a penalty of one to three times the deficient tax for unreasonable transfer pricing.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign fraudulent or grossly negligent transfer pricing disclosures are exposed to personal liability in the event of corporate insolvency. Personal criminal liability attaches only where intentional concealment is proven; in that case imprisonment and travel bans may be ordered.
AML and Payments Compliance Breach

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Money Laundering Control Act results in corrective orders and an administrative fine of up to NT$5 million. If hidden income is later discovered, the tax evasion penalty of one to three times the tax is added.

Director personal liability: Directors and AML compliance officers may be personally fined up to NT$1 million. Money laundering involving criminal proceeds carries imprisonment of six months to five years, travel bans and confiscation of assets.
