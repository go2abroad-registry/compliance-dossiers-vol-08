Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — National Tax and Customs Directorate of Colombia; operational portal: www.dian.gov.co and MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicios y Control Automatizado) for RUT/NIT, e-invoicing, and customs export declarations.

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the S.A.S. by private document or notarial deed and register it with the Mercantile Registry of the competent Cámara de Comercio; foreign shareholder documents and powers of attorney must be apostilled and translated into Spanish. (2) Appoint a legal representative domiciled in Colombia and grant a special power for tax registration. (3) File the pre-RUT via DIAN's MUISCA portal and obtain the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria); for an S.A.S. submitted digitally, the NIT is issued upon RUT validation, typically the same day. (4) In the RUT, the entity must be marked as 'Responsable del IVA Régimen Común', with CIIU activity 4791 (retail sale via internet and social platforms), the exporter status for outbound e-commerce, and the municipal ICA registration where applicable. (5) Obtain a DIAN-enabled electronic signature certificate (e.g., Certicámara or GSE) and request the e-invoicing 'Resolución de Habilitación de Facturación' in MUISCA to activate electronic billing. Lead time: 3–10 business days for full enablement; NIT issuance can be same-day for a digital RUT filing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Colombian-resident S.A.S. is a separate taxable entity subject to corporate income tax at the general rate of 35% on worldwide income. No pass-through or check-the-box classification is available. Dividends paid to non-resident shareholders carry additional dividend withholding surcharge. Territorial_Scope is residence-based for CIT, while non-resident shareholders remain taxable only on Colombian-source items. Tax_Exempt_Zone status is not available to a standard S.A.S., but free-trade-zone certification requires separate DIAN/zoning qualification.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Colombian law; anti-hybrid rules from Law 2010/2019 apply mandatorily. Available elections include: (i) 'Régimen SIMPLE de Tributación', an elective aggregate levy combining income tax, VAT, national consumption tax, and municipal ICA for taxpayers with prior-year gross income not exceeding 80,000 UVT (approximately COP 3.98 billion for 2025), subject to activity limits and owner eligibility; (ii) the new-company CIT progression under Law 2069/2020 (Art. 100 E.T.) for newly incorporated small SAS entities with gross income capped at 45,000 UVT, phasing in the 35% tariff through reduced rates in the first four tax years and full tariff from year five onward; (iii) voluntary election of the VAT exclusion treatment for export of services under Decree 2150/2017 and Article 481 E.T.; (iv) for transfer-pricing purposes only, the designation of a group-wide 'Grupo Empresarial' regime; Colombia does not allow consolidated income tax filing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

COP 0 — there is no monetary VAT registration threshold for a corporate S.A.S. seller; registration in the RUT as a 'Responsable del Régimen Común' is mandatory from the first taxable act (sale) executed in Colombia. The simplified-regime threshold of 3,500 UVT applies exclusively to natural persons and is unavailable to corporations. For non-resident foreign digital-commerce providers, Law 2010/2019 requires IVA registration with no turnover threshold if services are used in Colombia; a foreign marketplace platform (e.g., TikTok Shop acting as intermediary) has the same no-threshold nexus rule.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods physically departing Colombian customs territory are excluded from IVA at 0% under Article 481 E.T. For an S.A.S. engaged in social-commerce retail (ISIC 4791), eligibility requires: (i) a customs export declaration accepted by DIAN; (ii) physical dispatch of the goods abroad within the fiscal period; (iii) the foreign buyer as principal in the transaction; and (iv) receipt of payment via the authorized foreign-exchange market. Digital and platform-related services (marketing, advertising, marketplace fees) supplied to non-domiciled clients qualify as export of services when the benefit is wholly used outside Colombia and the recipient has no Colombian domicile. Both categories generate refundable input VAT balances where creditable IVA exceeds output IVA.

  • Declaración de Exportación (DEX / vDEX) — the DIAN-accepted electronic customs export declaration constituting legal evidence of the shipment
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / multimodal transport document evidencing effective removal of the goods from Colombian national customs territory, issued by the carrier
  • Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas (Inward Remittance Certificate) issued by a Colombian authorized foreign-exchange intermediary proving crediting of the export proceeds

Mandatory real-time e-invoicing applies to all IVA taxpayers. The S.A.S. must (i) hold the DIAN 'Resolución de Habilitación de Facturación Electrónica'; (ii) issue each sales invoice in the DIAN-standardized UBL/XML format; (iii) transmit the document to DIAN's MUISCA web-services API prior to delivery; (iv) receive the validation/accreditation response and embed the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) and DIAN QR code; and (v) preserve the signed XML for the statutory 10-year period. DIAN performs near-real-time validation against the RUT and VAT patterns. TikTok Shop payout reports do not substitute for the DIAN electronic invoice, and all social-commerce sales must flow through the e-invoice pipeline.

DIAN reconciles the DEX, e-invoice, transport documents, and foreign-exchange settlement records in targeted exporter audits. If a 0% rating is invalidated, the export is reclassified as a domestic taxable transaction, giving rise to a 19% VAT assessment on the invoice price plus daily default interest (Art. 634 E.T.), the inexactness penalty of 100% of the omitted tax (160% where the export is simulated, nonexistent, or fraudulently documented under Art. 648 E.T.), retroactive recovery of any input-VAT refunds, and collection surcharges. Serious cases trigger criminal liability for customs fraud and smuggling under Law 1762/2015. Failure to repatriate export FX within the 180-day deadline subjects the exporter to exchange-regime fines of up to 2,000 monthly minimum statutory wages and adjustments, in addition to the tax clawback.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a free-floating convertible currency regime for current-account transactions and for most capital-account transactions routed through the Official Foreign Exchange Market (MCR, Mercado Cambiario Regulador) via authorized intermediaries (CAMs). Profit remittance in the form of dividends and branch profits is convertible without exchange-rationing restrictions once the foreign investment has been registered on Form No. 1 (Registro de Inversiones Internacionales) with BanRep, and once the distribution has been approved in accordance with the Company by-laws and Colombian commercial law. Remittance obstacles are primarily documentary: promoter financial statements, proof of corporate income tax on distributable earnings, payment evidence of any statutory withholding, and a completed exchange declaration filed by the local bank. Capital inflows require lawful channeling and registration; repatriation is conditional on full registration. Unregistered capital inflows are not entitled to future access to the MCR for exit, and may be deemed illegal possession of foreign exchange.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Resolution 8 of 2000 and subsequent amendments, export proceeds for goods must be channeled through a CAM within six months from the date of the export customs dispatch. For services, the channeling period is generally the contractual payment term subject to a maximum of five months after the service is completed or the agreed payment maturity date, unless an extension is authorized by BanRep. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to Colombian pesos; the exporter is required to bring the proceeds into the country through an authorized intermediary and may retain foreign currency in a local external-circulation account. The exporter must submit an exchange declaration (Form No. 4 for goods, Form No. 5 for services and other concepts) documenting the repatriation. Failure to channel proceeds within the statutory term can trigger fines, presumptions of illegal exchange operations, and cross-reporting to the UIAF.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (BanRep), operating as the central bank and foreign exchange authority through the Comisión de Cambios, with enforcement by the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) and the Unidad de Información y Análisis Financiero (UIAF). Regulatory basis comprises Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1068 of 2015, Decree 2555 of 2010, and Banco de la República Resolution 8 of 2000.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility is Low-Medium as a principal treasury rail and Medium as an ancillary collection/payment gateway. Offshore Digital EMI institutions such as Wise, Payoneer, and Mercury are not licensed by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia to conduct deposit-taking or payment-account services for Colombian residents. They cannot satisfy the mandatory channeling requirement for foreign-exchange settlement, which requires a local CAM. A foreign EMI may be used as a non-Colombian payment collector to compress settlement timing on TikTok Shop payouts, but the underlying sales proceeds must be repatriated in original currency to the Colombian S.A.S. bank account with a CAM. The contractual and documentary structure must show the EMI as a mere billing/collection processor, not as the official account of the Colombian entity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For a capital injection from the foreign shareholder, the structured protocol is a SWIFT MT103 wire from the investor's bank to the Colombian S.A.S. bank account at a CAM. The receiving bank must be instructed via the payment narrative to document the transfer as inbound direct investment and to complete the exchange declaration Form No. 1 (Inversión Internacional), citing the specific subcode for foreign direct investment in a non-financial company. The investor must provide the bank with a letter of instructions, the corporate resolution approving the capital increase, a copy of the shareholder registry and NIT certificate, and, where the contribution exceeds a high threshold, proof of the source of funds and a certificate of non-involvement in tax havens. After the wire is cleared, the S.A.S. must file the Registro de Inversiones Internacionales with BanRep through the CAM within the statutory registration window (recommended within 30 days; legally required before repatriation). The bank will record the COP exchange transaction using the TRM (market reference rate) of the day.Payments to overseas suppliers, third-party fulfilment centers, and platform advertising vendors must be made through the local CAM to preserve FX legality. For standard low-value, high-frequency trade settlements, an MT103 customer transfer initiated via the local bank's electronic treasury module with full invoice reference is appropriate. For large wholesale purchases from Asian apparel or electronics suppliers, an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (sight or deferred payment) issued by the Colombian bank is recommended, supported by the import declaration. Each outgoing payment requires a corresponding exchange declaration: Form No. 3 for importation of goods and Form No. 5 for services and other transfers. The CAM will maintain a sub-ledger that ties the outbound foreign-currency payment to the import declaration and to the Colombian company's invoicing sequence.Deliverable Colombian peso / U.S. dollar forward contracts (FX forwards) traded with a licensed local CAM under the Official Foreign Exchange Market.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing the peso/dollar TRM, settled net in pesos or dollars, subject to the existence of an underlying commercial exposure.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (cross-currency swaps, CCIRS) used to hedge long-term balance-sheet exposure on foreign-currency debt or investments.Plain-vanilla over-the-counter foreign exchange options (COP-USD calls and puts), entered into only with an authorized Colombian financial institution and designed for hedging, not speculation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility is Low-Medium as a principal treasury rail and Medium as an ancillary collection/payment gateway. Offshore Digital EMI institutions such as Wise, Payoneer, and Mercury are not licensed by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia to conduct deposit-taking or payment-account services for Colombian residents. They cannot satisfy the mandatory channeling requirement for foreign-exchange settlement, which requires a local CAM. A foreign EMI may be used as a non-Colombian payment collector to compress settlement timing on TikTok Shop payouts, but the underlying sales proceeds must be repatriated in original currency to the Colombian S.A.S. bank account with a CAM. The contractual and documentary structure must show the EMI as a mere billing/collection processor, not as the official account of the Colombian entity.
