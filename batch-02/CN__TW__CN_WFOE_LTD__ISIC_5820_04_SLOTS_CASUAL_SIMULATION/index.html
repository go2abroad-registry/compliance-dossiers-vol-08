Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office or commercial mailbox addresses are generally not permissible for a WFOE in China. The registered address must be a physical location within the jurisdiction where the company is incorporated, and it must be suitable for business operations. Tax authorities and banks require a physical presence for verification and correspondence. For the software publishing industry (ISIC 5820), a virtual office may be rejected because the business requires a registered operational address for licensing, tax filings, and bank account opening. The local SAMR and tax bureau may conduct on-site inspections to confirm the existence of the office. Therefore, a physical office lease is mandatory.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-in capital exists for a Taiwan limited company following the 2009 capital deregulation; however, the Investment Commission tests declared capital adequacy against the submitted business plan. The entire paid-in capital must be remitted from abroad by the sole foreign shareholder into a Taiwan foreign-currency corporate account, settled into New Taiwan Dollars and verified by a licensed CPA before MOEA filing. No escrow is mandated except where the Investment Commission imposes a schedule-linked capital drawdown condition, in which case the unutilised balance remains ring-fenced in the designated account until each tranche is released.

Funding & unlock path: Prior to occupying the registered office, obtain (a) a fire-safety compliance certificate and fire-protection equipment verification under Article 10 of the Fire Services Act (消防法) from the local Fire Bureau; (b) an occupancy and structural safety certificate from the Taipei City Construction Management Office confirming floor load capacity and use-group classification; and (c) an occupational hazard workplace clearance under the Occupational Safety and Health Act (職業安全衛生法) covering risk assessment, first-aid equipment, emergency evacuation drills and employee health-examination records. Casual workers engaged in simulation testing of the casual game must be covered by labor or accident insurance before deployment, and the company shall maintain a fire-safety management plan approved by the Fire Bureau with a named safety officer.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

CNY is convertible on current-account items (Article VIII, IMF Articles of Agreement), including service-trade receipts from Apple/Google application-store payouts, subject to documentation-based authenticity verification. Capital-account items remain strictly controlled under a positive-list regime: inbound FDI requires SAFE foreign-exchange registration through a delegated bank; conversion of capital-account proceeds follows the negative-list 'payment-verification' (支付结汇) model, and since 2020 under SAFE Circular 汇发〔2020〕8号, capital-income payment facilitation allows direct use for permitted operating expenses with post-event sampling checks. Profit/dividend remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is classified as current-account investment income but is gated behind three prerequisites: audited annual financial statements, completed corporate income tax filing with no outstanding tax liability, and the SAFE/tax service-trade outward-payment tax recordal form (服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表). No separate government approval is required, but free remittance is blocked by a de facto tax-compliance and equity-post-verification test.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory foreign-exchange surrender in 2012; there is no statutory conversion ratio. However: (i) all service-trade proceeds attributable to the WFOE must be repatriated into a domestic bank account and offshore retention requires SAFE approval; (ii) goods-trade activity requires entry on the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Income/Expenditure Enterprise Directory (货物贸易外汇收支企业名录), while service-trade receipts are declared under the Cross-Border Income/Expenditure Reporting Code (涉外收支申报) within five working days of crediting; (iii) settlement into RMB is voluntary and foreign-currency current-account retention is permitted, but settlement must be supported by a genuine-transaction document package (contract, invoice, Apple/Google payout statements); (iv) for app-store receivables paid on net-30/45 cycles, each inbound MT103 must be reconciled against the corresponding payout report to avoid SAFE anomalous-payment flags.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行) as monetary authority; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局) as the delegated foreign-exchange regulator operating under PBOC. For this Taiwan-invested Chinese WFOE, all FX administration is executed through SAFE local branches via delegated commercial banks, including FDI foreign-exchange registration, settlement and sale of foreign exchange, cross-border receipt/payment declaration, and capital-account facilitation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium (conditional). Licensed cross-border payment institutions such as Payoneer, PingPong and Airwallex may collect Apple/Google IAP proceeds into their Hong Kong/Singapore/UK settlement rails and remit to the WFOE through a PRC partner bank under the SAFE cross-border payment-institution pilot license (跨境外汇支付业务试点) or the PBOC cross-border RMB payment pilot. Legal constraints: (i) EMIs are settlement agents, not deposit-taking banks, and the WFOE remains the VAT/CIT taxpayer of record with full SAFE declaration responsibility; (ii) the EMI must hold a valid PRC payment license or act through a licensed local bank partner, otherwise receipt constitutes illegal cross-border payment activity under PBOC/SAFE 2019-2020 circulars; (iii) social-casino-adjacent content may fall under the prohibited business list of certain EMI acceptable-use policies, though clean Apple/Google billing records mitigate this risk; (iv) funds floated offshore must be disclosed and are exposed to non-repatriation penalties. Recommended structure: use the EMI only for collection acceleration while maintaining the primary USD account at a Tier-1 bank for final clearing and SAFE declaration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Protocol for the Taiwan-parent capital injection: (1) complete FDI foreign-exchange registration with SAFE through the delegated bank by submitting the Foreign Investment Law filing, business license, shareholder resolutions and Taiwan-investor qualification documents; (2) obtain the FDI Foreign-Exchange Registration Certificate (外汇登记凭证) and open a Foreign-Currency Capital Account (资本金账户) or an RMB Capital Account; (3) instruct the Taiwan shareholder's bank to wire via SWIFT MT103 with field 70 narrative 'FDI REGISTERED CAPITAL INJECTION - SAFE FILE NO. [登记业务编号]' to the WFOE's capital account; the remitter must be the registered foreign investor directly and third-party remittance is prohibited, with purpose category 'Construction of Enterprise' and SAFE standard transaction code 622011; (4) upon crediting, the bank completes cross-border receipt declaration (涉外收入申报) within five working days; (5) conversion to RMB is voluntary, either spot settlement into the settlement-pending RMB account (结汇待支付账户) with negative-list usage restrictions, or retention in USD for operating needs; payments under the capital-account facilitation policy do not require per-transaction verification when inside the negative list and are recorded in the enterprise internal compliance book.Clearing rail matrix: (i) local CNY payments (salaries, rent, cloud hosting, domestic marketing) settle via CNAPS/e-banking from the RMB basic account; (ii) cross-border USD/EUR payables (Google Ads, AWS, overseas CDN, Taiwan-IP license fees, dividends) settle via SWIFT MT103 for single payments or MT202 for batch treasury concentration, each preceded by the SAFE service-trade outward-payment tax recordal (税务备案表) stamped by the bank; (iii) CNY cross-border settlement to RMB-region suppliers rides the CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) rails with bank-to-bank messaging, reducing FX cost; (iv) commercial letters of credit (SWIFT MT700) are not suitable for ISIC 5820 digital service flows and should be reserved for physical equipment or software-licensing procurement where the vendor insists, given high documentation cost for intangibles. Dividend remittance to Taiwan follows the outbound protocol: board resolution, audited CIT-completed financial statements, withholding tax at 10% on dividends or a reduced rate if the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement's limitation-on-benefits is successfully claimed by the beneficial owner, plus VAT/service-tax formalities via the 税务备案表, then bank outbound MT103 with the investment-income purpose code.Onshore CNY/USD FX forwards (远期结售汇) - permitted only with genuine underlying exposure under the real-demand principle (实需原则); the WFOE may lock conversion of forecast Apple/Google USD receipts into CNY for payroll and CNY operating expenses, subject to a bank-approved credit line or margin.Onshore CNY FX swaps (外汇掉期) - same-day or maturing swaps combining spot CNY conversion with a future reverse leg, used for rolling USD-RMB working-capital needs.Onshore CNY FX option structures (人民币外汇期权及组合) - plain-vanilla options or option combinations such as risk-reversals and collars for hedging USD IAP income, contingent on documented exposure and premium paid in CNY.Offshore USD/CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDF) - available through Hong Kong or offshore counterparties for residual exposures lacking documented onshore trade background; settlement made in USD against the CNH fixing; onshore PRC banks cannot execute NDF for customers.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CCIRS) - permissible for converting USD-denominated shareholder loans into CNY liabilities when the loan is registered with SAFE; usage is restricted to matching actual funding flows under the real-demand framework.
