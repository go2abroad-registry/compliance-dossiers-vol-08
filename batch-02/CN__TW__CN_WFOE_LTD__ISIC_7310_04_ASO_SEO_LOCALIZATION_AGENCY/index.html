The bank will hold these funds without release until the CPA capital verification report is submitted and the company registration is approved, unless a written release is obtained from the foreign exchange bank based on the investment approval letter.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Taiwan (Republic of China) - Tax Administration, operating through the National Taxation Bureaus; electronic portal: Ministry of Finance Tax e-Filing Portal (https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw) and e-Invoice Integration Service Platform (https://www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity must first complete the legal registration of its Taiwan branch or business unit with the Ministry of Economic Affairs and obtain the branch establishment approval. Immediately after that legal registration, the foreign parent must apply to the competent National Taxation Bureau for tax registration, submitting the following statutory documentation: certified certificate of foreign incorporation or registration, articles of incorporation and company charter, approved Taiwan branch registration, power of attorney and identification of the Taiwan responsible person, proof of Taiwan business premises, and the Taxable Business Registration Application. The National Taxation Bureau will verify the information, register the foreign taxpayer in the Taiwan tax ledger, and assign an eight-digit Unified Business Number (UBN), which serves as both the Taiwan Tax Identification Number and the VAT registration number. Foreign e-commerce and digital service providers with no fixed Taiwan place of business may instead complete the simplified cross-border electronic services tax registration on the Ministry of Finance e-Tax portal, obtaining a Taiwan VAT registration identity without establishing a permanent establishment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Taiwan operates under a managed floating exchange rate with current account convertibility broadly unrestricted. Legal-entity current account receipts and payments, including service fees from app store optimization and localization work, may be freely transacted through Authorized Foreign Exchange Banks, subject to prescribed remittance declaration thresholds. Capital account transactions, including inbound capital injection into a Taiwan-registered entity, foreign investor equity, or repatriation of sale proceeds, remain under CBC oversight and require supporting documentation such as investment approval, audited financial statements, and shareholder resolutions. Taiwan does not mandate surrender of export proceeds; receipts may remain in foreign-currency deposit accounts. Profit remittance by foreign shareholders is permitted after tax clearance and compliance with CBC settlement rules. Mainland China, by contrast, maintains stricter convertibility controls through SAFE. A Chinese WFOE must register an FDI enterprise with SAFE to open a capital account, and every injection must be verified against the FDI registration. Current account items such as payments for advertising services are convertible after genuine transaction verification, but funds cannot be diverted to equity investment or prohibited uses. Repatriation of profits from the WFOE requires an audited statutory financial report, tax completion certificate, board resolution on profit distribution, and bank verification. The asymmetry between Taiwan and China creates structural friction, especially for TWD app-store settlements flowing into a CNY entity; funds must first be collected by a Taiwan operating account, reflected in service invoices, and then settled through SAFE-monitored trade or service transaction rails. Round-tripping, over-invoicing, and mismatched payment purpose codes are major regulatory exposure points for an ASO and SEO localization agency dealing with many small-batch digital advertising payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan does not impose mandatory surrender of export or service export proceeds. Exporters and service providers may retain foreign currency proceeds in foreign currency deposit accounts or convert them voluntarily. Export receipts should be collected within 180 days of shipment or service completion unless extended with CBC approval. For Mainland China, SAFE also does not require forced surrender; eligible trade and service exports can be settled by authorized banks through the goods and services trade monitoring system. In both regimes, the carrier bank is responsible for verifying underlying transaction documents and assigning the correct foreign exchange purpose code before settlement or crediting.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC) as the primary foreign exchange and monetary authority for Taiwan; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China for all onshore CNY capital account and trade-related foreign exchange administration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs, including Wise B2B and Payoneer, are not licensed as deposit-taking banks in Taiwan and cannot serve as the primary corporate treasury account or statutory capital escrow depository. They are viable only as supplementary payment facilitators routed through licensed Taiwan banks or partner financial institutions, and their use is further constrained in Mainland China where WFOE capital and trade settlements must pass through SAFE-regulated onshore bank accounts. The overall viability is low as a stand-alone banking solution and medium only for low-value cross-border app-store commission payouts under a sponsoring bank arrangement. Heavy reliance on EMIs creates upstream KYC opacity, sanctions screening gaps, and potential frozen-funds exposure during account reviews, so a Tier-1 domestic bank relationship must remain the operational backbone.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the CN WFOE must be injected through the SAFE-registered FDI capital account using a proper SWIFT MT103 remittance from the Taiwan parent, with the payment purpose code described as foreign direct investment capital injection. The remitting bank in Taiwan must classify the outward transfer under CBC capital account reporting and retain the board resolution, FDI registration certificate of the Chinese WFOE, share subscription agreement, and formulaic capital verification report. Upon receipt, the Chinese settlement bank will verify the SAFE business registration and require the enterprise to submit a capital account statement within ten business days. The Taiwan-origin funds cannot enter through Wise B2B, Payoneer, or any other EMI unless the carrying bank is a licensed AFEB and SAFE data exchange is completed. For profit distribution, the WFOE must complete the annual statutory audit, file tax returns, obtain the profit distribution board resolution, and then apply for TWD or USD remittance through the designated onshore bank using the current account purpose code for profit remittance by foreign investors.Operational disbursements for ASO and SEO localization should be cleared through SWIFT MT103 for direct vendor payments, with proper purpose codes such as advertising agency services, software licensing, or localization consultancy. Commercial documentary letters of credit are appropriate for sizable software development or translation vendor contracts where performance and shipping documents are verifiable. Documentary collections may be used for smaller localization service trades, but the bank should always have the underlying service contract, invoice, delivery acceptance report, and proof of regulatory licensing in the destination market. For Apple, Google, and other app-store developer payments, the rake-off should be made from a Taiwan-operated bank account or a dedicated commercial card linked to the Tier-1 bank, with the corresponding transaction feed retained for anti-layering review. Wise B2B and Payoneer should only be used for low-value test payments and never as a substitute for a SWIFT-cleared vendor payment when the purpose code is capital or large service trade.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The local file is mandatory when related-party transactions in a single category exceed RMB 40 million (or total related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million) in the annual tax year; the master file is required when total related-party transactions exceed RMB 100 million and the group’s consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 5 billion; below these thresholds, the WFOE must still file the annual related-party transaction reporting form (同期资料/年度关联业务往来报告表) through the STA’s e-filing system, and a contemporaneous TP documentation file must be ready within the statutory deadline (by the filing date for local file, and within 30 days of the STA request for master file).

Economic substance scope: The PRC applies the substance-over-form and general anti-avoidance rules (GAAR) under EIT Law Article 47 and Implementation Regulation Article 120; a WFOE that merely invoices localization services without performing core functions—such as app-store optimization strategy, keyword research, translation quality control, and ad-buying decisions—will be recharacterized as a shell entity, its income may be reallocated to the Taiwan parent, and the cost-plus mark-up must reflect the risks borne and functions performed; the OECD BEPS Action 5 economic substance analysis is also relevant for any Taiwanese IP licensing structure; for the WFOE to qualify as the principal or limited-risk distributor/agent of the App Growth Agency, it must demonstrate local employees with technical competence, actual decision-making authority to bind the agency, payroll and social insurance records, tangible office premises, and a bank account through which client receipts flow.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Disguised Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Article 41 and Article 47, the STA may apply the GAAR and make a transfer pricing adjustment, add the under-reported income to taxable profits, and levy a deficiency interest surcharge; under Tax Administration Law Article 63, evasion of EIT exceeding RMB 50,000 triggers an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax, confiscation of illegal gains, and blacklisting of the enterprise; a criminal referral is mandatory if the evaded amount exceeds 30% of the taxable income for a single year and is over RMB 50,000.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial supervisor of the WFOE may be personally liable for the unpaid corporate tax plus the 0.05% daily surcharge and the penalty; if the enterprise is a mere conduit or shell, the court may pierce the corporate veil and hold the Taiwan parent and its directors jointly and severally liable for the WFOE’s tax debts; on criminal conviction, directors, financial officers, and tax-return preparers face up to 7 years’ imprisonment and fines for tax evasion, and may be subjected to a departure ban (出境限制) and a blacklist preventing them from serving as directors of any Chinese enterprise for up to 5 years.
Phony Transfer Pricing Documentation / Thin Capitalization / Round-Tripping

Statutory sanction: Article 114 of the Tax Administration Law and Notice 2017 No. 6 (STA TP Management Measures) allow the STA to disregard the transaction, recharacterize the debt as equity, apply the 2:1 thin capitalization ratio, disallow the excessive interest expense, and deem the payment a dividend subject to 10% WHT plus VAT; a late or incorrect related-party form is punishable by a penalty of RMB 10,000 to RMB 50,000; the underpaid tax is subject to a 5-year limitation period if there is reasonable disclosure, but if concealment is proven, the limitation period is extended to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the compliance certificate certifying false transfer pricing documentation are personally exposed to criminal liability for tax fraud; if the WFOE cannot pay the assessed tax, the tax authority may impose a property preservation order freezing bank accounts and real estate of the legal representative and the beneficial owners, and may limit the legal representative’s exit; a Hong Kong or Taiwan court may order directors to restore misappropriated payments to the WFOE’s estate in a shareholder derivative action, and personal bankruptcy proceedings can be triggered by the restitution order.
Failing to Withhold and Pay WHT on Cross-Border Dividends, Interest, or Royalties

Statutory sanction: Under Tax Administration Law Article 69, a withholding agent that fails to withhold and remit the required tax is liable for the tax itself plus a 50% to 300% penalty on the unwithheld amount; if the WFOE fails to file the withholding return on time, a separate penalty of RMB 2,000 plus RMB 2,000 per day up to RMB 20,000 applies; interest at 0.05% per day accumulates from the due date until payment; if the Taiwan parent is found not to be the beneficial owner and the treaty relief is revoked, the WFOE becomes jointly liable for the full 10% WHT plus penalty and interest.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and chief financial officer are civilly liable for the withheld tax under the enterprise liability rules and may have personal assets attached if they knowingly signed remittance instructions that ignored the withholding obligation; a criminal conviction for failure to withhold exceeding RMB 100,000 can result in up to 3 years’ imprisonment, while any additional false statement of a tax officer can trigger the separate offense of tax fraud with a sentence of up to 7 years; departure bans are routinely imposed on the legal representative of a WFOE that has a significant uncollected withholding tax liability.
