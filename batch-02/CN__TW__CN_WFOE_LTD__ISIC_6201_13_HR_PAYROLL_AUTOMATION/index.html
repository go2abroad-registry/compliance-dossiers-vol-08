Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through provincial and municipal competent tax offices; online electronic portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Taxation) hosted on the China Tax Administration Information System (CTAIS).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The Taiwan investor incorporates the entity as a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE / limited liability company) with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtains the Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character code that serves as the mandatory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) for all PRC tax registrations. Step 2: Within 30 days of obtaining the license, the WFOE must complete tax registration and the supplementary taxpayer information filing through the Electronic Tax Bureau; the USCC is used as the TIN and VAT registration number, and no separate VAT certificate is issued. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no economic turnover threshold for VAT registration; every WFOE must register for VAT upon incorporation. The statutory small-scale taxpayer threshold is RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales; taxpayers above this threshold must be registered as general VAT taxpayers. The payroll SaaS platform service, being an information-technology/modern service, is generally subject to the standard 6% VAT rate before application of the export zero-rating.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The PRC WFOE qualifies for VAT zero-rating on exported payroll SaaS services supplied to an overseas customer, including a Taiwan-resident customer, when the recipient is located outside mainland China, the services are wholly consumed outside mainland China, and the services are within the statutory catalogue of cross-border zero-rated or VAT-exempt services under the VAT pilot regulations. The taxpayer must be a general VAT taxpayer, issue an export service invoice, receive the foreign-currency or cross-border RMB proceeds, and maintain contractual, delivery, and consumption evidence proving offshore supply. Physical goods exports qualify for 0% only after a completed customs export declaration showing departure from the PRC customs territory.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Taiwan maintains a managed-floating New Taiwan dollar regime with substantially liberalized current-account convertibility. Current-account transactions, including trade in goods and services, dividends, royalties, and employment remuneration, may be remitted freely after filing an electronic foreign-exchange declaration with an authorized bank; no prior central-bank approval is normally required provided the transaction is genuine and documentary evidence is retained. Capital-account items, including equity investment into Taiwan, intra-company loans, acquisition of real estate, and derivative transactions, require a valid prior approval or filing with the CBC, the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, or another competent authority. Export proceeds must be repatriated to Taiwan through authorized banks within five working days from collection and reported under the export declaration system; the proceeds may be sold to the bank for TWD or credited to a foreign-currency deposit account. Profit remittance by a foreign-invested enterprise is permitted after audited financial statements and tax clearance are presented, but the distribution must be supported by board minutes, a tax payment certificate, and the original foreign-exchange declaration files. In practice, cross-strait investments and remittances from mainland China are subject to additional MOEA Investment Commission screening under the Act Governing Relations between Peoples of the Taiwan Area and the Mainland Area, which can lengthen the compliance timeline and require sealed certificates and corporate authorization chains. Overall, TWD is convertible for legitimate business purposes, but the entity must establish a compliance calendar for prior approvals, declaration filings, and documentary retention to avoid delays and penalties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Measures Governing the Collection and Payment of Export Proceeds, an exporter must repatriate all export foreign-currency receipts to an authorized bank in Taiwan within five working days after the date of payment or the date of negotiation of the transport document, whichever occurs first. The exporter must present the export declaration form or its electronic equivalent and complete the CBC foreign-exchange classification declaration. There is no general mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter may elect to convert the proceeds into New Taiwan Dollars or to retain the full amount in a foreign-currency deposit account. For cash payments and settlements involving mainland Chinese parties, the bank may impose enhanced documentation and prior regulatory filing requirements before the proceeds are collected.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), also known as the Central Bank of Taiwan, acting with the Financial Supervisory Commission under the Management of Foreign Exchange Act and the Foreign Exchange Control Regulations

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI platforms are not a viable primary account solution for a Taiwan-registered foreign-invested entity. Taiwan's Banking Act and the CBC's designated-bank framework require multi-currency corporate settlement accounts to be maintained with a licensed domestic bank or an offshore banking unit of a domestic bank. Foreign EMIs are not supervised by the Financial Supervisory Commission and cannot issue TWD settlement accounts, submit CBC foreign-exchange declarations, or participate in the local ACH and interbank clearing systems needed for payroll automation. An EMI may be used only as an auxiliary collection and payout channel to the extent it acts as a white-label distributor of a duly licensed partner bank, and even then, the underlying settlement, FX declaration, and payroll funding must flow through the Taiwan-licensed institution.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the Taiwan WFOE must be routed through a licensed domestic bank or the bank's offshore banking unit using a SWIFT MT103 payment from the foreign parent. The remittance must cite the approved investment project number or, for a new foreign-invested company, the preliminary registration number issued by the MOEA Investment Commission. The receiving bank will prepare a foreign-exchange declaration using the capital-account purpose code applicable to foreign investment in Taiwan. The WFOE must present the MOEA approval or filing receipt, the parent's board resolution approving the capital injection, and the shareholder loan agreement if the injection is structured as a shareholder loan. Upon receipt, a Taiwan-licensed certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report confirming that the funds were received in TWD or in foreign exchange and credited to the WFOE's account; the bank will then file the inward remittance with the CBC and issue a foreign-exchange receipt certificate, which becomes the documentary basis for registration and future dividend remittance.Outbound payroll disbursements and vendor settlements must be executed on the domestic ACH bulk clearing system for New Taiwan Dollar payroll runs, using the automated bulk round-file submission window of the financial information service; cross-border payroll and vendor payments to overseas beneficiaries should be settled through SWIFT MT103, provided the remittance purpose code and payroll support files are retained. For trade-related disbursements where goods or services are documented, Commercial Letters of Credit and Documentary Collections may be used, with presentation documents sent through the collecting bank.
