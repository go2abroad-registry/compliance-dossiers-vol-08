Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under the Taiwan Company Act. However, the Investment Commission requires foreign or Mainland China shareholders to remit the full subscribed capital into a blocked escrow account with an authorized foreign exchange bank in Taiwan; the bank and a CPA verify the remittance before incorporation registration, and the funds remain blocked until the Company Registration Certificate is issued.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, operating the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) online portal at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

The CN WFOE, as a resident limited liability company, obtains its unified social credit code (USCC) automatically issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) upon incorporation; the USCC serves as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration number. Tax registration is completed through the e-Tax portal by logging in with the USCC and submitting the basic registration data within 30 calendar days after business licence issuance. For a foreign non-resident corporate shareholder (e.g., the Taiwanese parent) that derives China-source dividends or interest, the withholding agent must apply for a non-resident TIN with the competent tax authority by submitting the foreign incorporation certificate, a valid Certificate of Tax Residence, and the underlying payment documentation; upon approval, the authority issues a non-resident taxpayer identification number used exclusively for withholding filing and treaty benefit claims.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None under the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law; China does not provide a check-the-box election. The WFOE is statutorily taxed as a resident enterprise. Elective special tax treatment for tax-neutral corporate reorganisations and elective tax treaty benefit applications may be available via the competent tax authority, subject to documentary and substantive tests.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable turnover exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (CNY 5 million) triggers mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; below this threshold, the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer with simplified levy rates. All entities must still complete income tax registration regardless of VAT scale.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods qualifies for 0% VAT when the goods are physically exported from China, the exporter has completed customs clearance and obtained the customs export declaration, and the export proceeds are received and repatriated in foreign currency within the statutory period, with all supporting documents retained for verification. Qualifying cross-border services (including international transportation, R&D, software, and certain consulting services) supplied to overseas recipients are zero-rated or VAT-exempt provided the recipient is offshore, payment is made in foreign exchange, and the service is wholly performed outside China. Pure equity holding activities are outside the VAT scope and thus do not independently trigger zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC)

China has mandated fully digital electronic invoices (all-electronic invoices, also known as full digital e-invoices) issued through the nationwide e-Tax invoicing platform. Invoices must be uploaded in real time for centralised fiscal clearance and authentication; the system assigns a unique invoice number and applies an anti-fraud digital stamp before issuance. System-to-system integration via tax authority APIs is mandatory for high-volume issuers.

Export zero-rating is subject to ex-post audit and joint supervision by the STA and customs authorities. If the zero-rated or rebate claim is determined to be fraudulent, unverified, or not substantiated by the required customs and foreign-exchange paperwork, the tax authority will claw back all VAT refunded, impose late payment surcharge, and assess an administrative monetary penalty of between one and five times the false rebate amount under the Law on the Administration of Tax Levy and Collection; criminal liability may also arise under the Criminal Law for export-rebate fraud. Supporting documentation must be retained for at least five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed but not fully liberalized foreign exchange control regime. The RMB is convertible on current account for trade, services, dividends, and legitimate distributions, but capital account inflows and outflows remain subject to registration, approval, quota, and negative-list controls. For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise used as a passive equity holding SPV, inbound equity capital must be routed through the SAFE FDI registration framework and can only be settled in RMB after a designated bank verifies the underlying capital contribution, negative-list compliance, and enterprise status. Despite nominal RMB convertibility, de facto restrictions appear at the point of cross-border remittance: capital account income cannot be used for activities outside the approved business scope or for speculative investments, and any change in capital purpose requires SAFE registration. Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is treated as a current-account item after tax, so the bank will require audited financials, tax clearance certificates, dividend resolution, and evidence that the funds are genuine post-tax profits; delays or rejections occur when the passive holding structure lacks commercial substance, historical filings, or clean source-of-funds proof. The overall assessment is restricted current-account convertibility plus controlled capital-account access, with high documentary rigidity and strong bank-level KYC discretion.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Administration rules, export proceeds must be repatriated into mainland China and logged in the bank's SAFE electronic trade monitoring system within the applicable trade-receipt deadline, generally no later than 90 days from the export customs declaration date for standard open-account exports unless a SAFE-registered deferred collection or offshore retention approval is obtained. There is no mandatory conversion ratio in the current current-account regime: the exporting WFOE may retain 100% of export receipts in a foreign currency account or convert voluntarily at the onshore spot rate, but every receipt must be matched to a customs/foreign exchange declaration and supported by contracts, invoices, customs declarations, and logistics records.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, exercising the delegated foreign exchange administration authority of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: Not Viable (0/10). Offshore digital fintech EMIs and neobanks are legally barred from serving as the primary treasury or clearing bank for a mainland China WFOE. Under PBOC and SAFE licensing requirements, only domestic designated foreign-exchange banks and licensed RMB clearing banks may open corporate accounts, receive FDI capital, process cross-border wires, and convert RMB for the WFOE. An offshore EMI does not hold a SAFE foreign exchange settlement license and cannot clear SWIFT MT103 payments into the SAFE monitoring system; attempting to route capital or dividends through an EMI would create an unlicensed cross-border payment channel, trigger KYC red flags, and expose the structure to penalties. EMIs may only serve as a low-value notification or card issuer where there is a licensed domestic sponsor bank, but they cannot replace the Tier-1 commercial bank relationship.

The receiving Chinese bank validates the SAFE FDI registration code, investment contract, and negative-list classification; then the proceeds are booked using the foreign exchange payment purpose code for capital contribution to a foreign-invested enterprise. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on gross dividends paid by the Chinese WFOE to a non-resident Taiwan enterprise shareholder under EIT Law Articles 3 and 37, unless reduced by an applicable arrangement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Quarterly estimated CIT returns must be filed and paid within 15 days after the end of each quarter; the final annual Form A settlement must be filed and paid within 5 months after the fiscal year-end (May 31 for a calendar-year WFOE).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: required if related-party transactions in the tax year equal or exceed RMB 400 million for tangible property transfers, RMB 200 million for intangible property transfers, RMB 100 million for related services, or RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers. Master File: generally required when total related-party transactions reach RMB 1 billion or when the enterprise is selected by the tax administration for transfer-pricing monitoring. A pure equity holding SPV will usually be below these thresholds but must still report related-party information annually and maintain contemporaneous evidence of arm's length conduct and substance.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not apply a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations regime, but the general anti-avoidance rule (EIT Law Article 47) and the Beneficial Owner Rules of State Administration of Taxation Announcement 2018 No. 9 require the WFOE to demonstrate business rationale, real management and decision-making, office space, employees, and Core Income-Generating Activities in China if it wishes to claim treaty benefits or avoid recharacterization. A passive equity SPV without economic substance cannot rely on the Cross-Strait reduced rate and is exposed to anti-shell-entity and GAAR adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion through false CIT return / artificial shift of profits

Statutory sanction: Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41, 44, 47 and 48; Tax Collection and Administration Law Articles 32 and 63 apply: tax authorities may disregard arrangements without economic substance, make an upward transfer-pricing adjustment, impose 0.05% daily late-payment interest, and impose a penalty of 50%-500% of the underpaid tax. If the evaded amount reaches the criminal threshold, Article 201 of the PRC Criminal Law applies and the entity is fined, with responsible directors subject to imprisonment up to seven years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false annual returns or approve the profit-shifting arrangement may be ordered to make civil restitution; the tax bureau may withhold company and personal taxpayer credit clearance, impose exit control (travel ban) under Article 44 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law, and invoke PRC Company Law Article 20 to pierce the limited liability veil if the corporate structure is used to evade tax and prejudice creditors, making directors personally jointly and severally liable.
Failure to withhold and remit dividend WHT on outbound distribution to Taiwan shareholder

Statutory sanction: Tax Collection and Administration Law Article 69 requires the withholding agent to be fined 50%-300% of the amount it failed to withhold, and Article 32 imposes 0.05% daily late-interest on the overdue tax; the tax authority will demand the unpaid WHT from the WFOE. Public disclosure as a serious tax violation triggers credit blacklisting, restricted government procurement, and exit control.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, financial director, and actual controller who knowingly directed the non-withheld dividend are jointly obligated to compensate the corporate shortfall; the exit ban can be applied personally, and if the unpaid tax is substantial, criminal charges may be brought for tax evasion or misappropriation of state tax revenues.
Beneficial ownership fraud / treaty abuse under the Cross-Strait arrangement

Statutory sanction: State Administration of Taxation Announcement 2018 No. 9 (Administrative Measures for Beneficial Owner Identification) and EIT Law Article 47 authorize recharacterization and denial of treaty benefits; 10% (or the full statutory) WHT is collected with 0.05% daily interest and a penalty from 50%-500% of the underpayment; false declarations may additionally constitute tax fraud under PRC Criminal Law Article 201.

Director personal liability: The substance failure, if combined with personal approval of false treaty claim documentation, exposes directors to personal civil liability for the extra tax, interest, and penalties, and to criminal liability for fraudulent tax avoidance; corporate veil piercing applies when the WFOE and Taiwan shareholder operate a sham corporate form with no legitimate commercial purpose, and the Chinese court may order personal asset attachment and travel restriction.
