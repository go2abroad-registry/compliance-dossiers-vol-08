Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online through the National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) platform linked from the STA official portal www.chinatax.gov.cn and administered by the provincial/municipal competent tax authorities

        
E-government filing portal
For a foreign parent establishing a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE): (1) register the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the Business License carrying the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory taxpayer identification number for all tax purposes; (2) tax registration is completed automatically via government data sharing and must be confirmed on the National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau; no separate paper VAT registration certificate is issued; (3) complete VAT taxpayer status formalities, with mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below that threshold, small-scale taxpayer status applies; (4) apply on the e-invoice platform for VAT invoice issuance rights, invoice allocation, and anti-counterfeit tax control initialization; (5) for a foreign non-resident entity without Chinese incorporation deriving China-source income, file non-resident enterprise tax registration with the competent STA office and obtain a non-resident taxpayer identification number using the home-country registration certificate and tax residence certificate

Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation

        
Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
There is no turnover threshold for tax registration itself; all WFOEs must register with the STA upon incorporation. The mandatory general VAT taxpayer threshold is RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales; small-scale taxpayers below that threshold may benefit from the current monthly VAT exemption of up to RMB 100,000 (RMB 300,000 per quarter) for ordinary taxable activities

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Exported goods qualify for VAT zero-rating when physically exported from China under a valid GACC export declaration and sold to an offshore buyer. Cross-border services qualify only if supplied to a recipient outside China, wholly consumed outside China, and remunerated from outside China; the WFOE's 3D game-art outsourcing and character-rigging services may qualify as cross-border modern services under the VAT zero-rating/exemption rules for cross-border taxable activities if the offshore-consumption and payment-from-abroad tests are met. Zero-rating and export refund generally require general VAT taxpayer status and a properly filed export-service VAT rebate claim.

China operates the Golden Tax System Phase IV electronic invoice platform. VAT invoices, including fully digital electronic invoices, must be issued through the STA e-invoice platform, are transmitted in real time to the tax authority, and carry a unified invoice classification code, serial number, digital e-signature, and anti-counterfeit QR stamp. Criminal liability may arise under Article 204 of the Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax refund, and the taxpayer's compliance rating is downgraded, exposing prior periods to retrospective audit.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime with conditional current-account convertibility and controlled capital-account opening. Game 3D art outsourcing fees are current-account service-trade receipts: the WFOE may receive USD, TWD, or other convertible currencies in a designated FX settlement bank account, retain them, or sell them to the bank at the market rate after submitting the underlying service contract, invoice, and service-trade declaration to the bank. The capital account remains subject to SAFE and MOFCOM registration and approval: Taiwan-sourced equity must be injected through a SAFE-registered foreign-investment FX capital account and cannot be replaced by trade receivables or PayPal receipts. Dividend remittances are permissible but require audited financial statements, corporate income tax clearance, a board resolution, and a SAFE remittance declaration. The RMB is not freely transferable cross-border without an underlying real-economy transaction. Profit-stripping through misplaced royalties or transfer-pricing structures will be challenged by the bank. Banks must also verify the Taiwan investor's outbound investment approval from the relevant Taiwan competent authority as part of source-of-funds review. Overall, current-account proceeds are convertible, capital-account movements are strictly documented, and profit remittance is lawful only when all tax and SAFE requirements are cleared.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
For the WFOE's service-trade exports, there is no statutory conversion ratio and no mandatory percentage of export proceeds to surrender. Export proceeds must be received by a licensed designated FX bank under SAFE total-amount verification and repatriated to China; bank monitoring expects collection within the contractually agreed payment term, normally 90 to 180 days from invoice date or service completion. If proceeds are not collected without a valid commercial or legal reason, the bank will escalate a suspicious transaction report. For goods-trade exports, proceeds must be repatriated within the period required by the goods-trade verification system, typically 180 days from customs declaration; however, this WFOE's 3D art outsourcing is service trade, so the bank applies the service-trade reporting code and contract-based verification rather than physical customs documents.

      

      
Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and its State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) serve as the operative monetary and foreign-exchange authorities for the PRC-domiciled WFOE; Taiwan-side outward investment approval is also required from the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (Taiwan).

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            PayPal
            Fit: High
          

          
Rating: Low. Offshore digital EMIs cannot legally provide the core settlement services required by a PRC WFOE because a foreign non-bank institution is not a SAFE-designated foreign-exchange bank and cannot open a RMB basic deposit account or an FDI FX capital account in China. EMIs such as international neobanks cannot accept compliant capital-account injections or dividend remittances on behalf of the mainland entity. Attempting to use any unlicensed EMI for capital movement will trigger AML refusal and regulatory rejection.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Taiwanese corporate shareholder with nominee shareholders and no audited financial statements makes the ultimate beneficial owner opaque.
        
Root cause: Taiwanese corporate shareholder with nominee shareholders and no audited financial statements makes the ultimate beneficial owner opaque.

        
Defense evidence: Provide a government-certified extract from the Taiwan company registry, articles of incorporation, and share register; audited financial statements or CPA-reviewed accounts for the latest two years; a signed UBO disclosure naming all natural persons with 25 percent or greater interest and all senior management; passport copies; notarized board resolution approving the WFOE incorporation and capital injection; and, if the Taiwan company is an investment-holding vehicle, evidence of its underlying business and source of wealth.

      

      
      

        Initial capital is expected to arrive via a PayPal balance, a third-party wallet, or a third-country clearing account instead of the Taiwan shareholder's corporate bank account.
        
Root cause: Initial capital is expected to arrive via a PayPal balance, a third-party wallet, or a third-country clearing account instead of the Taiwan shareholder's corporate bank account.

        
Defense evidence: Require the Taiwan investor to fund the equity injection from its own corporate bank account via SWIFT; provide bank statements showing the original PayPal or wallet balances being withdrawn to the corporate account; provide PayPal or platform business settlement reports; include an affidavit explaining the source of those balances; provide a written commitment that no equity injection will be routed through unlicensed money transmitters.

      

      
      

        Gaming-art outsourcing could be used to shift payments between affiliates, creating transfer-pricing and fake service risks.
        
Root cause: Gaming-art outsourcing could be used to shift payments between affiliates, creating transfer-pricing and fake service risks.

        
Defense evidence: Prepare a full transfer-pricing local file if the related-party revenue threshold is met; supply executed master service agreements with explicit fee formulas, milestones, deliverables, and dispute mechanisms; provide per-project work orders, client acceptance emails, delivery receipts, and invoice schedules; maintain timesheets, payroll, and social-insurance records proving actual employees perform the art services; submit corporate income tax returns and CIT payment vouchers.

      

      
      

        The WFOE's registered address is a virtual office or the premises are still under preparation, causing the bank site inspection to fail.
        
Root cause: The WFOE's registered address is a virtual office or the premises are still under preparation, causing the bank site inspection to fail.

        
Defense evidence: Provide a good-standing lease agreement for a physical office in the WFOE's registered city, recent utility bills, photographs of the office interior, and an employer nameplate; if a serviced office is used, provide a sublicense agreement and landlord consent; present corporate seal custody logs and local employee contracts; cooperate fully with the bank's pre-onboarding site inspection and provide a business continuity plan.

      

      
    

    

      
Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Before any funding, the WFOE must complete the foreign investment information reporting and obtain the SAFE FDI registration certificate. The bank opens the foreign-investment FX capital account after verifying the certificate and constitutional documents. The Taiwan parent then sends a SWIFT MT103 from its corporate bank account to the WFOE's FX capital account; the message must carry the direct-investment equity-capital purpose code and the FDI registration number in the remittance information. Upon receipt, the bank performs source-of-funds and capital verification; the funds are then settled into RMB only for approved operating purposes using supporting vouchers. PayPal, personal accounts, and transfers from third parties are unacceptable for equity injection. After receipt, the WFOE must complete capital-account closing verification within the applicable SAFE reporting timeframe and must not leave unverified funds stranded in transit.The WFOE receives client service fees in its FX settlement account or cross-border RMB account. Direct service payments should be completed via SWIFT MT103 with a service-trade purpose code; commercial LCs and documentary collections are permitted where end-clients require bank undertakings, but direct transfers are more practical for digital art delivery. For outbound payments to overseas contractors, the WFOE must submit the contract, invoice, and proof of service or delivery to the bank. For profit repatriation, the WFOE should first complete the annual audit and tax filing, then submit audited financial statements, tax clearance certificate, board resolution approving dividends, and a SAFE dividend remittance application; the bank converts RMB and remits via SWIFT MT103 under the investment-income purpose code. Stage 06
Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

      
Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

    

    

      
Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
25% standard enterprise income tax rate under PRC EIT Law Article 4; reduced to 15% for qualified High/New-Technology Enterprises and to 5% effective rate for small low-profit enterprises within statutory thresholds

      
Dividend withholding tax (WHT)
10% statutory withholding tax under PRC EIT Law on dividends paid by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise; no reduced treaty rate is currently available for Taiwan because the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement (the 'Cross-Strait Tax Agreement on Avoidance of Double Taxation and Enhancement of Tax Cooperation') was signed in 2015 but has not entered into force.

    

    

      
Mandatory Return Breakdown

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency: 

        
The annual return and final tax payment must be filed and settled within 5 months after the end of the tax year, i.e., by May 31 of the following calendar year. Quarterly provisional returns and advance CIT payments are due within 15 days after the end of each quarter.

      

      
      

        
        
Deadline:  · Frequency: 

        
The tax withheld must be turned over to the State Tax Administration within 7 days after the withholding obligation arises. For dividends, the obligation arises on the date of the shareholders' resolution declaring the dividend or on the date of actual payment, whichever is earlier. For interest and royalties, the obligation arises on the date the income is paid or deemed paid under Chinese tax law.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Under SAT Bulletin 2016 No.42, a contemporaneous Local File is mandatory for a resident enterprise whose related-party transactions in the prior year exceed: (i) RMB 200 million for purchase/sale of tangible goods; (ii) RMB 100 million for transfer/licensing of intangible property; (iii) RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; (iv) RMB 40 million for provision/rendering of services. A Master File is required when the aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. The WFOE, even at a lower turnover, must prepare a Transfer Pricing Contemporaneous File if any service threshold is crossed; the file must be completed by May 31 of the following year and submitted within 30 days upon the tax authority's request.

      
Economic substance scope: China does not maintain an explicit UAE-style Economic Substance Regulation statute, but the 'substance over form' doctrine, PRC General Anti-Avoidance Rule under EIT Law Articles 47-48, and tax treaty 'beneficial ownership' principles require the WFOE to demonstrate real economic substance. The WFOE must have a physical office, full-time 3D artists and riggers, actual control over core production decisions, and genuine risk-bearing capability. Core Income-Generating Activities ('CIGA') for game-art outsourcing include concept finalization, 3D sculpting, texture creation, rigging, quality assurance, project management, and invoicing; these activities must be performed in China by the WFOE, not merely by a Taiwan parent. If the Taiwan parent controls the economic substance while the WFOE functions as a mere booking vehicle, the tax authority can recharacterize income, disallow deductions, and impose special tax adjustment interest at 5 percentage points above the benchmark on the underpaid tax.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion - Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Document Fraud
        
Statutory sanction: PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law Articles 41 and 47, Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63 (tax evasion), and SAT Special Tax Adjustment Measures: the tax authority may reallocate income, impose an additional 0.05% daily late surcharge, and assess a penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Criminal Law Article 201 provides for imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines when the evaded tax is relatively large and constitutes more than 10% of the tax payable.

        
Director personal liability: The corporate veil can be pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20 when the director abusively uses the limited liability entity to evade tax and prejudice creditors. Directors are personally liable for civil restitution of misappropriated assets, their personal bank accounts can be frozen, they are subject to exit bans and travel blacklisting ('seriously untrustworthy taxpayer' list), and they face criminal imprisonment for tax evasion for up to 7 years; financial officers who knowingly prepare false documentation are treated as accomplices.

      

      
      

        Tax Administration - Failure to Withhold and Remit Dividend WHT
        
Statutory sanction: Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69: the withdrawal agent must be fined 50% to 300% of the tax that should have been withheld; the tax remains collectible from the withholding agent together with daily late surcharge at 0.05%; the competent tax authority may also issue a rectification notice requiring payment within 15 days. If the company wilfully disposes of assets to avoid collection, the persons in charge may be criminally prosecuted under Criminal Law Article 203 for tax evasion and obstruction of collection.

        
Director personal liability: The legal representative and financial controller who directed the non-withholding can be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid withheld tax to the extent the company's assets are insufficient; the tax authority may list the directors in the exit-control database, allowing border police to prevent departure from China. Directors may face administrative detention for refusal to comply and criminal fines; if the withheld sums were diverted to personal use, imprisonment of up to 10 years for misappropriation or embezzlement is possible.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance (GAAR and AML Risk)
        
Statutory sanction: EIT Law Article 47 (General Anti-Avoidance Rule) and Article 48 allow the tax authority to recharacterize the entire scheme, deny the incentives, adjust the taxable income, and impose special tax adjustment interest increased by 5 percentage points. If fraudulent, TCAL Article 63 applies with a 50%-500% penalty; the Anti-Money Laundering Law and Criminal Law Article 191 also impose fines and confiscation on shell companies involved in the laundering of illegal proceeds.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who commingle personal assets with the shell company's funds can be personally liable for the company's debts under Company Law Article 20. They face civil asset restitution orders, permanent blacklisting from serving as directors in China, travel bans, and criminal liability for tax fraud or money laundering with imprisonment of up to 10 years in aggravated cases; where illegal proceeds exceed RMB 10 million, administrative fines of up to RMB 5 million may b e imposed personally.
