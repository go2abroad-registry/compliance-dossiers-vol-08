Governing statute

Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required; at least one share must be subscribed and paid. The capitalization and payment schedule are fixed in the bylaws; if installments are used, full payment may not exceed two years from incorporation unless otherwise validly agreed. No escrow account is required for cash contributions, but foreign capital contributed must comply with the exchange registration regime of the Banco de la República.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety certification (Certificado de Bomberos), structural/electrical load inspection for any storage or printing facility, workplace risk management (SG-SST) registration with an ARL, and municipal occupancy/use-of-soil clearance before opening to the public or installing printing equipment.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — National Tax and Customs Directorate; principal portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; RUT/e-services portal: https://muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

For a Colombian S.A.S. (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada): (1) execute a notarial public deed of incorporation or private deed filed with the Cámara de Comercio; (2) register the company with the Chamber of Commerce, which generates the provisional NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria); (3) complete the Registro Único Tributario (RUT) through DIAN's electronic portal or a DIAN office, confirming the legal form, principal activity (ISIC 4791 under CIIU), and head office; (4) obtain the electronic signature (firma electrónica) and a DIAN user password to access telematic services; (5) select the tax profile as 'responsable del IVA' and 'declarante de renta'; (6) register if required for foreign trade and exports through the customs procedure (registro como exportador). Foreign non-resident entities without a permanent establishment may obtain a passive NIT through a resident agent or by direct electronic application under DIAN regulations, but for the Colombian S.A.S. the NIT is assigned domestically at incorporation.

Foreign non-resident entities without a permanent establishment may obtain a passive NIT through a resident agent or by direct electronic application under DIAN regulations, but for the Colombian S.A.S. the NIT is assigned domestically at incorporation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Colombia does not have a U.S.-style check-the-box election for Colombian S.A.S. entities. An S.A.S. is by law a distinct legal person subject to entity-level corporate income tax under the Estatuto Tributario (articles 12 and 240). The company may adopt certain internal forms of tax administration, such as opting to have tax filings prepared by DIAN-authorized accountants, choosing accrual basis methods as prescribed by law, and, where applicable, electing among statutory depreciation and inventory methods within ET parameters. Colombian tax law recognizes no general pass-through election for the S.A.S.; hybrid mismatch rules under Law 2010 of 2019 apply to prevent inconsistent fiscal treatment across jurisdictions, but no taxpayer election is available to reclassify the entity's default taxable status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For legal entities: no VAT registration turnover threshold — any S.A.S. effecting taxable commercial operations within Colombian territory must obtain a NIT, complete RUT registration, and register as a VAT responsible person. For natural-person micro-retailers, a simplified 'no responsable' exemption may apply if gross income does not exceed 3,500 UVT (approximately COP 174,296,500 for 2025); once the threshold is exceeded or when invoicing obligations arise, VAT registration is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT treatment is available for physical merchandise sold by a Colombian S.A.S. to non-resident purchasers if the goods are physically exported from Colombia under a valid customs exit operation, title and risk pass to the foreign buyer, and the sales are duly recorded in the export declaration and electronic commercial invoice. Services incidental to POD (e.g., design, printing, logistics services separately rendered to non-residents) may qualify for the export-of-services zero rate where the recipient is a non-resident, the service is wholly used or exploited abroad, and no Colombian VAT is chargeable to the foreign customer. For goods, the export must be evidenced by DIAN's accepted Declaración de Exportación, and the exporter must maintain possession of the transport document and proof of foreign-exchange channeling.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación / DEX) accepted by DIAN
  • Bill of Lading (for sea freight) or Air Waybill (for air freight) evidencing physical exit from Colombia
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Canalización de Divisas) proving receipt of foreign currency through an authorized intermediary

Mandatory real-time/near-real-time electronic invoicing applies to all VAT-responsible taxpayers, including an S.A.S. engaged in e-commerce retail. Invoices must be transmitted to DIAN via authorized technological operator or DIAN's free invoicing system and validated before delivery to the buyer. The electronic invoice uses the 'CUFE' (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) or 'CUDS' (Código Único de Documento Soporte) and includes a QR code enabling DIAN to query invoice authenticity. Such invoices are classified under factura electrónica de venta and are subjected to structural and tax validation via DIAN web services. In addition, POD sales exported from Colombia are documented through electronic export invoices and the DIAN foreign-trade clearance system.

DIAN rigorously audits export zero-rating claims. Any export found to be artificial, simulated, or lacking physical exit from Colombia, or whose currency proceeds are not repatriated through the foreign-exchange mechanism, triggers retroactive assessment of the full 19% VAT on the exported goods plus default interest and applicable fines. Under Article 646-1 of the Estatuto Tributario and related customs penalties, fraudulent export declarations or falsified documentation may give rise to penalties of up to 200% of the amounts omitted, criminal liability for customs fraud, and suspension or cancellation of the exporter's NIT status. The statutory review period for DIAN to retroactively adjust VAT credits and zero-rated exports is generally five years, extendable in cases of non-reporting or fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) is a free-float currency with full convertibility for current-account transactions, including digital service exports and e-commerce receipts, which require no prior license and may be channeled through authorized intermediaries. Capital-account operations, including foreign direct equity injection into a S.A.S. and profit remittances, are legal but conditional: the investment must be registered in the Banco de la República's Registro Nacional de Inversiones Internacionales (RNII), and dividend remittances must satisfy tax-clearance and withholding-tax rules. Profits are freely remittable only after the equity investment has been formally registered and the underlying earnings reflect locally taxable income.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory conversion ratio applies; Colombia does not compel exporters to sell export receipts to the central bank. However, proceeds from exports of goods and services that exceed the de minimis channeling threshold (USD 10,000 or its equivalent in other currencies per operation) must be canalizados, i.e., brought into the institutional foreign-exchange market through authorized commercial banks, within the statutory timeline: six (6) months from shipment or export declaration for goods, and four (4) months from the invoice or service-performance date for services, subject to extension mechanisms in the Banco de la República Foreign Exchange Manual. Below-threshold receipts may be settled directly or voluntarily registered with an authorized intermediary. The annual consolidated declaration of changes and associated Formularios de Declaración de Cambio must be filed with the receiving bank.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), acting as the sole monetary and foreign-exchange authority under Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1068 of 2015 and Resolution Externa 4 of 2020; operational enforcement is shared with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia for regulated intermediaries and with the DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) for trade and customs documentation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-medium viability as a stand-alone solution. Foreign digital banks and EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, Payoneer) are not authorized intermediaries in Colombia's institutional foreign-exchange market and cannot lawfully satisfy the mandatory channeling obligation for export proceeds. They remain legally usable only as ancillary collection and disbursement rails (e.g., Stripe/PayPal settlement aggregation, supplier payouts) when the underlying transactions are duly reported to an authorized local bank and the exceeding-threshold proceeds are still routed through a regulated intermediary within the statutory window. Thus, they may support liquidity velocity but not replace a tier-1 Colombian bank for FX compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires a SWIFT MT103 in USD or EUR from the foreign shareholder's bank to the S.A.S.'s account at an authorized Colombian intermediary. The wire reference field must carry the statutory purpose or concept code for a direct foreign-investment equity contribution (inversión internacional directa) and the beneficiary NIT. The receiving bank validates the remitter identity, origin of funds and the underlying shareholders' agreement, records the operation in the institutional FX market, and accepts the Formulario de Declaración de Cambio declaration; thereafter, the S.A.S. must file the international-equity registration with the Banco de la República within six (6) months. Unreported or miscoded injections attract FX-infringement penalties under the Colombia Foreign Exchange Statute.For a POD merchandise operation, outbound payments are cleared through (i) SWIFT MT103 credit transfers for direct supplier invoices (printing, blank merchandise, logistics), each registered under the applicable import-of-goods or import-of-services concept code; (ii) documentary collections or commercial letters of credit under UCP 600 for high-value recurring procurement, with the proceeds and covers channeled through the FX market when the transaction value exceeds USD 10,000; (iii) Stripe/PayPal linked rails for micro-disbursements to integrated fulfillment partners, reconciled daily to avoid breaching the channeling window; and (iv) SEPA, Fedwire or ACH transfers for SaaS, hosting and digital-advertising expenses. Each disbursement file, together with the underlying contract or invoice, must be preserved for DIAN audit and bank origin-of-funds verification.Onshore deliverable COP/USD forwards (contratos a término) executable only through licensed Colombian banks, locking conversion rates for future USD settlements from Stripe/PayPal and direct POD sales.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on COP/USD permitted for offshore hedging with foreign banks, cash-settled in USD, with onshore execution restricted to authorized intermediaries under the FX regime.Cross-currency swaps (swap de moneda) between COP and USD for medium-term treasury hedging of injected equity, debt-service flows and dividend remittance windows.FX put and call options on USD/COP offered by local banks for asymmetric coverage of annual profit repatriation and supplier-payable exposure.Central-bank instruments (foreign-exchange swaps and swap cambiario auctions) available to authorized intermediaries as an indirect liquidity-hedging layer for corporate treasury arrangements.

Foreign digital banks and EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, Payoneer) are not authorized intermediaries in Colombia's institutional foreign-exchange market and cannot lawfully satisfy the mandatory channeling obligation for export proceeds. They remain legally usable only as ancillary collection and disbursement rails (e.g., Stripe/PayPal settlement aggregation, supplier payouts) when the underlying transactions are duly reported to an authorized local bank and the exceeding-threshold proceeds are still routed through a regulated intermediary within the statutory window. Thus, they may support liquidity velocity but not replace a tier-1 Colombian bank for FX compliance.
