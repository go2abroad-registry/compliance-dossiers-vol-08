Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered by the local in-charge tax office where the WFOE is domiciled; online filing and taxpayer services are conducted through the STA National Electronic Tax Bureau unified e-Taxation portal.

E-government filing portal

For the Chinese WFOE: (1) incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number; (2) complete tax registration activation through the national e-Taxation portal within 30 days of incorporation; (3) choose or submit to VAT taxpayer identity classification (small-scale taxpayer or general taxpayer) at registration, with general taxpayer status being required for export zero-rating and input-VAT refund claims. For a foreign non-resident entity such as the Taiwanese shareholder without a permanent establishment in mainland China: no standard Chinese TIN is issued; the non-resident must complete non-resident enterprise tax filing/withholding registration with the in-charge tax authority, obtain a non-resident taxpayer code, and identify the Chinese resident payer as withholding agent for China-source income.

Default tax classification

Available election forms

None comparable to U.S. check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections; the PRC Enterprise Income Tax Law taxes resident enterprises at entity level on worldwide income. Available statutory elections are limited to VAT treatment: status as small-scale taxpayer until mandatory RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales threshold, simplified VAT calculation method, and the software-product VAT immediate refund election for self-developed software (13% output VAT with refund of the tax burden exceeding 3%).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales for mandatory general taxpayer VAT registration; all Chinese legal entities must complete tax registration immediately upon incorporation regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods qualify for 0% VAT when they physically leave Chinese customs territory under an export customs declaration naming the WFOE as exporter, are sold to an overseas buyer, and the foreign-currency proceeds are repatriated through approved banks. Digitally delivered game software sold through Steam/PlayStation may be treated as an offshore software/service export and zero-rated when the recipient is outside mainland China, delivery takes place electronically outside China, the underlying contract is with the foreign store/operator, and the WFOE submits the statutory cross-border zero-rating/offshore-transaction filing with supporting platform payout and user-territory data. Exporters must also be general VAT taxpayers to claim input-VAT refunds on export.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export goods customs declaration) accepted by Chinese Customs
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent transport/courier delivery proof for physical exports; for pure digital game exports, the Steam/PlayStation store payout report and digital delivery log
  • Commercial and export invoice issued by the WFOE
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or authorized bank foreign-exchange collection voucher evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Export collection/trade-in-goods foreign exchange monitoring records matched to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange
  • Cross-border software/service zero-rating filing receipt, underlying publishing/licensing contract, and platform settlement statement

China operates a national e-invoicing regime under the Golden Tax Phase IV system, with Full Digitalized Electronic Invoices issued in XML through the e-Taxation invoice platform; invoice data is submitted to the State Taxation Administration in near-real time for automated fiscal clearance, matching of sales and purchases, and fraud detection. Digital invoice authorization/tax-control numbers must be obtained from the platform before issuance; physical fiscal stamping is not used.

The State Taxation Administration and State Administration of Foreign Exchange cross-check export declarations, electronic delivery evidence, bank foreign-exchange receipts, and foreign-platform payout records. If zero-rating is claimed without genuine export or repatriation, or with falsified documents, the tax authority will claw back all VAT zero-rated or refunded amounts plus daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day and impose an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the evaded tax. Serious fraudulent export refund cases are referred to criminal prosecution for export-tax-fraud offenses, with additional fines and imprisonment; the recovery limitation period is unlimited for concealment-based fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB operates under a managed floating exchange rate regime with bifurcated convertibility architecture. Current account transactions, including cross-border software licensing settlements from Steam and PlayStation storefronts, are freely convertible and may be settled through bank-executed bilateral FX conversions, subject to authentication and truthfulness documentation covering platform agreements, sales statements, and SAFE services-trade declarations. Capital account transactions, however, remain heavily regulated. The initial equity injection from the Taiwanese parent into the WFOE requires an FDI foreign exchange registration completed by the designated account-opening bank, after which the registered capital is deposited into a restricted capital account and converted to RMB only for permitted expenditure items such as procurement of self-use equipment, payroll, operating expenses, and subsequent shareholder dividends. Profit remittance to Taiwan is permissible only after the annual statutory audit, the complete payment of corporate income tax and dividend withholding tax, the appropriation of the required legal surplus reserves, and the submission of audited financial statements, tax payment proofs, a shareholders resolution, and the specific dividend remittance instruction to the bank compliance desk. Since 2012 there is no surrender obligation for export proceeds; the WFOE can retain 100 percent of its foreign exchange receipts in a settlement account or convert to RMB at market rates. Nevertheless, outbound investment, external debt, and asset monetisation remain quota-limited and require individual SAFE approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since the 2012 abolition (SAFE Notice [2012] No. 4), the WFOE faces no export surrender quota and no mandatory conversion percentage. For Steam and PlayStation digital publishing receipts, the entirety of the foreign currency may be retained in a current-account FX settlement account, or converted to RMB at the bank prevailing spot rate, at the WFOE sole discretion. Two statutory controls persist: receipts must be repatriated to a domestic SAFE-monitored account within the reasonable services-trade credit period, generally no more than 90 days from the platform settlement statement issuance; and each receipt batch must be matched with the platform sales report, the underlying publishing agreement, the bank cross-border receipt declaration, and the WFOE inclusion in the SAFE services-trade monitoring directory. SAFE retains post-event verification rights and may sample high-frequency digital revenue streams.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), the foreign exchange administration arm under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), exercising delegated control over cross-border capital account transactions

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury clearing. Under the PBOC Measures for Non-bank Payment Institutions and SAFE cross-border payment licensing, offshore EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, and PayPal do not hold Chinese banking licences and are legally barred from opening local RMB settlement accounts for WFOEs. They may act only as auxiliary collection aggregators for overseas marketplaces when a licensed cross-border payment institution is interposed, yet even then the final settlement must be routed to a designated domestic bank. Using an unlicensed offshore EMI as the primary settlement rail would breach SAFE declaration requirements and risk adverse AML classification. For this Steam and PlayStation publishing WFOE, all cross-border flows must clear through a Chinese commercial bank with direct SWIFT membership, while a PBOC-licensed payment institution is acceptable only for sub-quota marketplace collections below the regulatory review threshold.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where annual related-party tangible property transactions equal or exceed RMB 200 million; where intangible or financial asset related-party transfers equal or exceed RMB 100 million; or where related-party services and other transactions equal or exceed RMB 40 million. Master File is mandatory when the group's aggregate annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, but PRC tax authorities apply a substance-over-form doctrine and General Anti-Abuse Rules under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law. Treaty entitlement also depends on economic substance: the Taiwan resident claiming dividend, interest, or royalty relief must perform substantive business activities in Taiwan, not merely hold PRC company shares or register a mailing address. In the videogame publishing chain, Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) that must be substantiated include negotiation and control of the Valve/Steam and PlayStation Store publishing agreements, ownership of platform account assets, pricing of game content, management of player purchase, refund and promotional decisions, control over software updates, and strategic management of distribution territories.
