Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (Taiwan), acting through the Taxation Agency and the applicable Regional National Taxation Bureau; the electronic registration and filing portal is the Ministry of Finance Tax E-Portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity establishing a Taiwan branch or subsidiary must first obtain business registration approval from the Ministry of Economic Affairs. Within the statutory period after business registration, and before commencing taxable sales if earlier, the entity must file for tax registration with the National Taxation Bureau having jurisdiction over its registered business address. The application must include the approved articles/memorandum and registration certificate, the appointed tax and fiscal representative's identification, and the branch or company applicant data. Upon successful registration, the Bureau issues the statutory Unified Business Number (Tax ID Number), which is then used for all Business Tax, Uniform Invoice, filing, and payment obligations. The foreign entity must maintain a resident tax agent for service of process and tax notices.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign non-resident entity establishing a Taiwan branch or subsidiary must first obtain business registration approval from the Ministry of Economic Affairs. Within the statutory period after business registration, and before commencing taxable sales if earlier, the entity must file for tax registration with the National Taxation Bureau having jurisdiction over its registered business address. The application must include the approved articles/memorandum and registration certificate, the appointed tax and fiscal representative's identification, and the branch or company applicant data. Upon successful registration, the Bureau issues the statutory Unified Business Number (Tax ID Number), which is then used for all Business Tax, Uniform Invoice, filing, and payment obligations. The foreign entity must maintain a resident tax agent for service of process and tax notices.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

None. Taiwan does not offer a check-the-box regime or hybrid mismatch elections; a Taiwan-registered limited company is automatically subject to entity-level profit-seeking enterprise income tax and standard Business Tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold for standard Business Tax registration; any person making taxable sales of goods or services in Taiwan must register before commencement. Small-scale dealers with average monthly turnover below NT$80,000 for goods or NT$40,000 for services may instead be subject to the simplified 1% non-value-added tax collection regime rather than standard VAT filing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Article 7 of the Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act to goods physically exported from Taiwan and to services sold to foreign customers where the service is not consumed in Taiwan and consideration is received in foreign currency. For the public commercial web scraping and proxy API business model, the zero-rating claim is available upon proof that the recipient is a non-Taiwan entity, the service is performed or consumed outside Taiwan, and the export proceeds are remitted to and repatriated into Taiwan.

  • Customs Export Declaration (export customs entry) as approved by the Taiwan Customs Administration
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export of goods
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Foreign Exchange Proceeds Receipt Certificate / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) issued by an authorized domestic bank

All VAT-registered businesses must issue Uniform Invoices for taxable sales. Qualified businesses must adopt Electronic Uniform Invoices and transmit invoice data to the Ministry of Finance Electronic Invoice Integrated Platform, either directly via the Turnkey interface or through an approved Common Carrier/Value-Added Service Center. Taiwan does not apply real-time continuous fiscal clearance or digital stamping; anti-fraud protection is provided by mandatory serialized Uniform Invoice numbering, electronic data reconciliation, consumer encryption codes, and the platform's validation controls.

The National Taxation Bureau may conduct documentary and transactional audits of zero-rated exports. If the export customs declaration, transport documentation, or foreign exchange inward remittance certificate is missing, untrue, or insufficient, the Business Tax previously zero-rated is clawed back at 5% with a surcharge. Under the Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act, an underpayment caused by false records is subject to a penalty of up to 3 times the tax underpaid. Forging customs declarations or remittance documentation may also trigger criminal liability under the Tax Collection Act and applicable customs-related criminal statutes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible for current-account items, including cross-border service-trade receipts from the commercial scraping-API business and the remittance of dividends derived from genuinely realized after-tax profits, subject to genuine-verification documentation. Capital-account items remain qualified and require prior SAFE registration: inbound equity capital injection, cross-border loans (foreign debt), and outbound guarantees all require registration or filing. Profit remittance hurdles are moderate: remittance of dividends requires (i) audited statutory financial statements, (ii) WFOE board profit-distribution resolution, (iii) the SAFE Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate or its online record, (iv) corporate income tax completion proof or tax-payment certificates, and (v) a mandatory tax/filing record (Service Trade and Other Items Foreign Exchange Payment Tax Filing Record) where the remittance equals or exceeds the USD 50,000 equivalent threshold. Because the WFOE's commercial model is service and data-pipeline exports, there is no customs declaration gate, but banks and SAFE will apply enhanced genuine-trade verification to the underlying API usage and revenue trail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The mandatory export-proceeds surrender system was abolished in 2012. The WFOE may retain 100% of export receipts in a domestic foreign-currency operational account; no statutory conversion ratio exists. Nevertheless, repatriation of service-export proceeds to onshore bank accounts is mandatory and must be collected within the trade-monitoring verification period (typically 180 days for goods; service receipts are assessed on a contract-by-contract basis). Proceeds may be converted to RMB at the bank at the WFOE's discretion provided the receipt is traceable to a genuine service-trade transaction evidenced by contract, invoice, and delivery/usage logs of the scraping-API and proxy services.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC); foreign exchange administration is delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) with provincial branches executing registration, monitoring, and enforcement for the WFOE.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low feasibility as a primary local treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) cannot act as licensed domestic clearing banks in China, cannot hold statutory capital, and cannot satisfy SAFE capital-account registration or tax-refund linkage. They may serve only as an auxiliary collection point for overseas revenue received via Stripe or USDC conversions, but routing material service-trade receipts through unregulated EMIs will trigger enhanced AML scrutiny due to reduced traceability to SAFE's trade monitoring. All statutory capital, dividend remittances, and tax settlements must flow through a licensed tier-1 Chinese bank account in the WFOE's own name.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder injects equity capital in USD (TWD is not a broadly tradable onshore settlement currency) via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered Capital Fund Foreign Currency Account, using the balance-of-payments purpose code for direct-investment capital contribution (direct-investment category, e.g., BOP code 621011). Prior posting, the WFOE must complete (i) market-entry filing with the Ministry of Commerce or its delegated authority, (ii) SAFE FDI registration and receipt of the Foreign Exchange Business Registration Certificate, and (iii) bank-side verification of the filing code against the remitter and remittance narrative. Once credited, capital funds may be settled into RMB only for self-use operating purposes: payroll, rent, taxes, procurement of bandwidth and data-center services, and general administrative expenses. Prohibited deployments include equity investment in third parties, loans to related parties beyond permitted centralized-dealing arrangements, wealth-management products, and non-self-use real property. For qualifying WFOEs under the capital-account income payment facilitation pilot, the bank may allow self-verification payment with the WFOE retaining transactional evidence, but all documentation must be produced to SAFE within the statutory retention window.Operating disbursements to Taiwan vendors for software licenses, data-center bandwidth, and proxy infrastructure will clear via SWIFT MT103 against a service contract, commercial invoice, and the WFOE's bank payment instruction. A Commercial Letter of Credit is not appropriate for data and API services and should be reserved only for incidental goods procured under Incoterms-backed purchase orders. Service payments above the USD 50,000 equivalent require the Service Trade and Other Items Foreign Exchange Payment Tax Filing Record; payments to Taiwan residents are also subject to PRC withholding tax on service fees (VAT on cross-border taxable services plus WHT on the non-resident's income) unless a reduced rate applies under the cross-strait double-taxation arrangement or an applicable treaty. Outbound dividend remittances to the Taiwan shareholder clear by MT103 supported by (i) the board profit-distribution resolution, (ii) PRC audited annual financial statements, (iii) completed corporate income tax settlement and dividend withholding at 10% or the arrangement-reduced rate, and (iv) the tax filing record. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% profit-seeking enterprise income tax (營利事業所得稅)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

20% withholding tax on dividends paid to a non-resident enterprise without a fixed place of business or business agent in Taiwan; 21% for non-resident individuals.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: annual related-party transactions exceeding NT$20 million; Master File: multinational group consolidated revenue exceeding NT$30 billion (also the Country-by-Country Reporting threshold).

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations code equivalent to offshore centres, but Article 12-1 of the Tax Assessment Act codifies the substantive taxation principle. A WFOE lacking real R&D activity, operational staff, a management office, bank accounts, board decision records, or actual business functions in Taiwan may be re-characterized as a shell; its income can be re-attributed to the offshore parent and the Taiwan tax return disregarded for tax purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty under Income Tax Act Article 110 of not less than 10% and up to 3 times the amount of underpaid tax; criminal liability for tax evasion under Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act carrying imprisonment for up to three years and/or a monetary fine.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or direct the fraudulent return are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax and penalties; the National Taxation Bureau may issue travel bans and property freezes; criminal liability extends to the responsible corporate officer with potential imprisonment.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of intercompany deductions, upward transfer pricing adjustment, re-characterization and reassessment under Article 12-1; penalty of up to 3 times the underpaid tax plus interest for intentional avoidance.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may be assessed as actual taxpayers after piercing the corporate veil; travel restraint orders and restrictions on exiting Taiwan apply to responsible persons with outstanding tax exceeding statutory thresholds.
AML / Unlicensed VASP Operation

Statutory sanction: De-registration, administrative fines under the Money Laundering Control Act, confiscation of virtual assets, business suspension, or closure by the competent authority.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors face criminal liability for knowingly facilitating money laundering, punishable by imprisonment of up to seven years and fines; travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed during investigation.
Withholding and Repatriation Violation

Statutory sanction: The tax authority collects the un-withheld amount directly from the withholding agent; a surcharge of 1.5 to 2 times the un-withheld amount applies for willful non-withholding, together with penal interest from the original payment date.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorize un-withheld cross-border remittances are personally liable for additional tax and penalties; responsible financial officers may be subject to prosecution and travel bans for unpaid corporate withholding tax.
