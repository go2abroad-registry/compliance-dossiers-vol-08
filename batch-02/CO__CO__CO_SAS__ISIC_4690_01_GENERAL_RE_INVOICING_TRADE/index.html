Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio de Bogotá — Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES); supervisión societaria by Superintendencia de Sociedades de Colombia; tax registration by DIAN.

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), arts. 5, 9, 17-26; Código de Comercio (Decreto 410 de 1971), arts. 110, 124, 126; Decreto 1074 de 2015, arts. 2.2.2.1.1-2.2.2.43.2 (RUES); Ley 222 de 1995 (Superintendencia de Sociedades).

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución (private incorporation instrument with notarized or electronically signed bylaws) registered in the Registro Mercantil; a public deed (escritura pública) is mandatory only if real property is contributed to the company.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital; capital is freely set in the bylaws and may be zero at formation. Payment of subscribed capital is fixed in the bylaws and the unpaid balance must be fully paid within 2 years from the date of incorporation. No escrow account or bank deposit certificate is required to register the S.A.S.

Funding & unlock path: Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos de Bogotá: fire-safety certification for the office and warehouse areas (extinguishers, evacuation signage, emergency plan); structural load inspection of rented warehouses under Ley 400 de 1997 / NSR-10; ARL affiliation and SG-SST program under Decreto 1072 de 2015, with COPASST constituted when the workforce is 10 or more employees.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — official portal: www.dian.gov.co; electronic filing and registration platform: MUISCA/DIAN Transaccional

E-government filing portal

An S.A.S. is incorporated by private document registered with the Cámara de Comercio, which assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) as the statutory TIN. The legal representative must then register or update the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) with DIAN in advance of the first taxable transaction, selecting the VAT responsibility code for export-oriented supplies. If the direct or indirect foreign shareholder is a non-resident entity, it must appoint a Colombian resident attorney-in-fact and provide proof of foreign tax residence or incorporation documents when requested by DIAN. No separate VAT identification number exists for the S.A.S.; the NIT/RUT combination serves as TIN and VAT ID. The S.A.S. must also obtain an electronic signature and an invoicing resolution from DIAN before issuing electronic invoices.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — An S.A.S. is incorporated by private document registered with the Cámara de Comercio, which assigns the NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) as the statutory TIN. The legal representative must then register or update the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) with DIAN in advance of the first taxable transaction, selecting the VAT responsibility code for export-oriented supplies. If the direct or indirect foreign shareholder is a non-resident entity, it must appoint a Colombian resident attorney-in-fact and provide proof of foreign tax residence or incorporation documents when requested by DIAN. No separate VAT identification number exists for the S.A.S.; the NIT/RUT combination serves as TIN and VAT ID. The S.A.S. must also obtain an electronic signature and an invoicing resolution from DIAN before issuing electronic invoices.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist under Colombian law; the S.A.S. is per se an entity-level income tax and VAT taxpayer. Only micro and small taxpayers meeting the statutory gross-income test may elect the Régimen SIMPLE de Tributación under Articles 903–916 of the Colombian Tax Statute, subject to activity eligibility and the 100,000 UVT annual gross-income cap. Re-invoicing intermediation operations are presumed to remain under the ordinary entity-level tax regime unless SIMPLE eligibility is expressly confirmed with DIAN.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold applies: VAT registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply or import performed in Colombian territory (COP 0 threshold). For optional SIMPLE-regime eligibility, annual gross income must not exceed 100,000 UVT (approximately COP 4.7 billion for tax year 2025).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for the 0% rate when they are physically exported from Colombian customs territory under an accepted export customs declaration, sold to a non-resident buyer, and paid in foreign currency through the Colombian foreign exchange market. Services, including software development, IT consultancy, digital services, and offshore commercial re-invoicing support, qualify when rendered to a non-resident without Colombian domicile, economically used or exploited entirely outside Colombia, and not linked to Colombian real estate or tangible property located in Colombia. The 0% rate is claimed in the periodic VAT return (Formulario 300) and the export invoice must expressly indicate 'Exportación' and tariff code; supporting contracts, export declarations, delivery evidence, and FX repatriation records must be retained for inspection.

  • Declaración de Exportación (DEX) accepted by DIAN
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or equivalent transport document evidencing physical departure from Colombian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or Colombian bank certificate proving foreign-currency repatriation within the permitted term
  • Commercial invoice and underlying export or re-invoicing contract
  • Packing list and, if applicable, certificate of origin

All Colombian merchants, including S.A.S. re-invoicing hubs, must issue electronic sales invoices (factura electrónica de venta) in XML format through authorized technology providers or DIAN's free web platform. Each invoice must be transmitted to DIAN in real time, validated against the authorized numbering range issued by DIAN resolution, and carry the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica) as the mandatory cryptographic digital stamp. Subsequent lifecycle events (acknowledgment, rejection, debit/credit notes) must be exchanged through DIAN web services/APIs. An invoice without DIAN validation and CUFE is not legally valid for VAT credit, deductible cost, or export zero-rating support.

DIAN may audit the full export substantiation file and require proof of shipment, service performance, contract, invoicing, and foreign-currency collection within the 180-day repatriation term. If zero-rating is unsupported or documents are false, DIAN will assess 19% VAT on the gross transaction, charge monthly default interest, and impose the 20% penalty for undue VAT refunds or compensations under Article 670 of the Colombian Tax Statute. A higher inexactitude penalty of up to 100% of the resulting tax difference may apply under Article 647 where the VAT return contains false or inaccurate information. Fraudulent export zero-rating may also trigger criminal tax evasion proceedings under Article 434A of the Tax Statute, and the DIAN clawback period generally covers the prior five tax years, extendable in cases of criminal fraud, concealment, or omissive conduct.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso is freely convertible under the free foreign-exchange market. Current-account transactions, including trade-related receipts and payments, do not require prior central bank approval; they must be executed through authorized intermediaries and supported by the relevant declaration of exchange. Capital-account operations such as foreign direct investment, portfolio investment, and foreign loans require registration with Banco de la República but are not subject to a concession or prior license regime. Profit remittances are legally permitted without exchange surrender, provided the foreign investment is duly registered, distributable reserves are evidenced by audited financial statements, and Colombian dividend withholding and income-tax obligations are satisfied. The practical hurdles for a re-invoicing S.A.S. are documentary rather than exchange-control-based: each inward and outward flow must bear a precise economic concept, be booked through a supervised bank, and be reconcilable with the tax and customs filings of the Colombian entity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Colombia no longer imposes compulsory surrender of export proceeds to the central bank; the mandatory conversion ratio is zero percent, and exporters may keep and use foreign currency subject to channelization rules. The fundamental obligation is to canalize export earnings through an authorized Colombian foreign-exchange intermediary as soon as they are received, and within the maximum statutory intervals established by Banco de la República regulatory practice: for goods generally the earlier of the commercial payment term or a period of up to 13 months from the date of shipment where genuine supplier credit exists, and for services generally up to four months from the contractual maturity date, with extensions permitted only for registered external credit. The exporter must present the DIAN export declaration, the collection proof, and the relevant foreign-exchange declaration; failure to meet the deadline triggers exchange-infraction proceedings and monetary fines.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (Central Bank of Colombia), with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (Financial Superintendency of Colombia) as the bank conduct supervisor and DIAN as the customs and foreign-exchange enforcement agency. The regulatory backbone is Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1735 of 1993, and Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium for purely offshore digital EMIs. Foreign e-money institutions are not licensed Colombian banks and cannot open onshore current accounts, access the local clearing systems operated under Banco de la República, or submit declarations of exchange. Under Colombian law, an entity that seeks to provide payment accounts in Colombia must be authorized by the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia as an electronic payment corporation (SEDPE/PES). For a re-invoicing S.A.S., an offshore EMI is therefore only a supplementary outbound execution rail where the actual regulated account remains at a tier-one Colombian bank; every transaction must still be captured in the official FX market and accompanied by the corresponding invoice and purpose code. Attempting to use an unlicensed EMI as the primary Colombian treasury channel would violate the channelization rules and create AML and exchange-control exposure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing for a capital contribution to the Colombian S.A.S. consists of: (1) registration of the foreign direct investment with Banco de la República using the applicable foreign-investment registration form, processed through the receiving Colombian bank; (2) issuance of an incoming remittance instruction from the non-resident shareholder using SWIFT MT103 with the S.A.S.'s precise registered name, the locally issued foreign-currency or COP account number, the bank BIC, and the economic concept described as direct investment capital contribution; (3) validation by the bank of the underlying shareholder resolution and apostilled corporate documentation; and (4) issuance of the local certificate of exchange, evidencing the conversion and the official statistical classification. The certificate is retained for the DIAN audit file and reconciled against the equity roll-forward in the statutory financial statements. If the contribution is made instead as an intercompany loan, the maturity, interest rate, and amortization schedule must be reviewed for external-credit registration when the term exceeds twelve months; short-term trade credit must nevertheless be itemised in the relevant exchange declaration.Outbound trade disbursements should use two principal rails: SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire transfers and documentary letters of credit issued under UCP600. For ordinary supplier payments, the S.A.S. instructs its Colombian bank to remit funds via MT103 with invoice, purchase-order, and LC references; the bank will pre-validate the underlying commercial documents and assign an approved foreign-exchange concept, usually linked to a prior import declaration or goods import program. For larger or formalised purchases, the S.A.S. opens an irrevocable commercial LC through the tier-one bank, with settlement at sight or deferred tenor in USD, EUR, or invoice currency. All disbursements must be archived together with the bank's exchange settlement receipt and be traceable to the shipment or service documentation. The use of local ACH rails inside Colombia is limited to domestic operating expenses; cross-border clearing must remain on correspondent-banking channels.
