Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) – official portal: https://www.dian.gov.co; transactional portal: https://muisca.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Colombian S.A.S. must obtain a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) and register in the Registro Único Tributario (RUT). Registration is completed electronically through DIAN’s MUISCA platform by a legal representative (appointed by private deed) using the foreign entity’s incorporation documents, power-of-attorney, and a local address. The NIT is issued immediately upon RUT approval; the RUT must be updated for any subsequent changes and contains the VAT and withholding tax classifications. For advance VAT registration, a pre-registration request is submitted through MUISCA before the first taxable operation.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The Colombian S.A.S. is subject to entity-level income tax at the standard corporate rate and is not eligible for U.S.-style pass-through treatment. For Colombian tax purposes, the S.A.S. is a separate tax resident if incorporated under Colombian law, regardless of the residence of its shareholders. A Colombian simplified joint-stock company is also a resident for worldwide income taxation; its shareholders are taxed only on dividends distributed.

Available election forms

Colombia does not provide a check-the-box regime. The S.A.S. is by law subject to entity-level taxation and no pass-through election is available. However, the company may elect the simplified taxation regime ('Régimen Simple de Tributación – SIMPLE') if it meets annual gross income thresholds and business type requirements, which replaces ordinary income tax, VAT in limited integrated form, and municipal industry-and-commerce tax with one consolidated payment; this election is irrevocable for 2 years and is filed with DIAN. Hybrid-mismatch elections are generally not recognized, and Colombian tax rules include controlled-foreign-company and anti-hybrid measures that override purely elective classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Persons selling goods or services are obligated to register as VAT-responsible parties in the RUT when expected annual gross income exceeds 3,500 UVT (Unidad de Valor Tributario). For the 2024 UVT of COP 47,065, this threshold equals approximately COP 164,727,500 (COP 164.7 million); the threshold is annually adjusted by DIAN based on UVT indexation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods, including titanium orthopedic implants, are expressly zero-rated (gravados a tarifa 0%) under Colombian VAT law, provided the goods are physically removed from Colombian territory and sold to a foreign purchaser. The exporter must hold (i) the official export declaration accepted by DIAN, (ii) the bill of lading or equivalent transport document, and (iii) the foreign currency collection evidence. Services exclusively used in the export of goods (e.g., freight, insurance, logistics, and product adaptation services) may also qualify for zero-rating if contractually linked to the export transaction and documented as such. The zero-rating does not exempt the company from VAT on domestic purchases; the exporter may offset such input VAT against output VAT and claim a refund ('Devolución') of the resulting credit balance under Article 850 of the Colombian Tax Statute.

  • Conocimiento de Embarque (Bill of Lading / Airway Bill issued by the carrier)
  • Remesa de Ingreso (Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign exchange settlement form registered with the foreign exchange market through an authorized intermediary bank)
  • Factura Electrónica de Exportación (Electronic Export Invoice with CUFE digital stamp)
  • Certificado de Procesamiento / Certificado de Origen (when preferential tariff claims apply)
  • Contrato de Compraventa Internacional (International Sales Contract or Purchase Order)

All VAT-responsible taxpayers and exporters must issue electronic invoices (factura electrónica) validated in real time by DIAN through the MUISCA web services (SOAP/REST APIs) under Resolution 000165 of 2023 and implementing regulations. Each invoice must include a mandatory electronic invoice number (CUFE or CUDS) and a digital stamp (alphanumeric code) generated from DIAN’s clearance process. Export sales must be electronically filed using the 'mandatory electronic invoice with export validation' (Factura Electrónica de Exportación) and the export declaration (vías) must be cross-matched with the customs export form and DIAN systems in real time. The company must also issue electronic supporting documents (documento soporte en adquisiciones) physical records and maintain EDI messaging integrity for large transactions.

DIAN and the National Customs and Tax Authority conduct post-shipment audits cross-checking export declarations, electronic invoices, and foreign exchange collections. If an apparent export is found to be non-genuine, incomplete, or not repatriated within the 180-day term (or an authorized extension), the zero-rating is retroactively revoked and the exporter must pay the 19% VAT, indexation, weekly interest (equal to the judicial lending rate), and a penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded. Individuals who knowingly submit forged transport documents or false export declarations are subject to criminal sanctions under Article 402 of Law 1564/2012 (customs fraud) and Article 26, 2017 Colombian Penal Code amendments, with imprisonment of 4–8 years and fines of up to 1,000 SMLMV. Additionally, the exporter may be charged with money-laundering related offenses for fictitious export transactions under Law 1762 of 2015. Any claim for VAT refund is subject to enhanced scrutiny, a 30% limitation reserve period, and blockage of the refund until the fiscal credit is proven valid; where the auditor identifies a deliberate false claim, the refund is denied and a clawback action plus punitive sanctions follows.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia operates a free-market exchange regime for current-account transactions through authorized intermediaries. Capital-account inflows, including non-resident equity contributions to a Colombian S.A.S., require prior registration in the Banco de la República 'Registro de Inversiones Internacionales'. Profit remittance and dividend outflows are legally permitted after the issuance of financial statements, but a 10% Colombian dividend withholding tax applies to distributions to non-resident shareholders, and the remittance must be channeled through a bank authorized by the Central Bank. There is no general currency freeze or forced price control; however, each operation must be declared to the Central Bank and a 'Declaración de Cambio' must be filed by the receiving authorized intermediary.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All proceeds from exported goods (ISIC 3250 orthopedic implant devices and titanium components) must be repatriated to Colombia within a period of six (6) months from the date of the export declaration, as provided under the Colombian external exchange regime. The funds must be channeled through an authorized intermediary and either surrendered into local currency at the market reference rate (TRM) or retained in a Colombian authorized foreign-currency account, subject to the same reporting duties. There is no mandatory fixed conversion ratio for the entire invoice; the exporter may lawfully maintain balances in foreign currency only to the extent permitted by the Banco de la República's compensation-account regulation. Late settlement without just cause exposes the exporter to fines and the suspension of export incentive regimes, so a digital reconciliation of each export airway bill and SWIFT credit advice is essential.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (the Central Bank) is the principal monetary, exchange-rate, and international-reserve authority; the Comité de Asuntos Aduaneros, Arancelarios y de Comercio Exterior and the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) have complementary administrative roles in trade-flow verification and foreign-exchange control enforcement.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore digital banks, EMIs, and payment settlement firms (e.g., Wise/TransferWise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) are not authorized by the Banco de la República to provide local corporate accounts or to render foreign-exchange services for Colombian export/import settlement. Their service is limited to low-value incoming remittances or to feeding a SWIFT credit into a local bank account; they cannot act as a treasury rail for USD-denominated orthopedic-implant trade because they cannot issue Colombian trade ID codes, cannot file Declaración de Cambio, and cannot operate an active account with a Colombian NIT. Therefore the entity must maintain a minimum of one tier-1 domestic commercial bank account as the primary clearing anchor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All foreign investment capital must enter through an authorized Colombian financial intermediary. Upon receipt of the SWIFT MT103 from the overseas parent, the local bank assigns a foreign-exchange operation code and requires the submission, before booking, of either an 'Informe de cambio' (Declaración de Cambio) in the Banco de la República format for the capital-injecting operation. The proper investment purpose code for a new equity capital contribution in a non-resident parent to a Colombian S.A.S. is '1101 – Importaciones de servicios, mercancías en general' not applicable; applicable code is '1002 – Inversión extranjera en el país mediante aportes al capital social' and the local bank must issue a 'Certificado de Inversión'. The MT103 should contain the beneficiary account (COP or USD), the beneficiary NIT, and the short payment detail 'Inversión Extranjera Directa Fase II - Capital Social SAS'. The investment must be physically received within 10 calendar days of filing the declaration and must be registered with the Banco de la República 'Encuesta de Inversión Extranjera Directa' in the following quarter. No re-domiciliation of funds is permitted before 12 months without a formal change of the investment register, and repatriation of capital triggers a capital-gains tax obligation together with the relevant dividend withholding rules.For trade settlements, the preferred clearing rail is a SWIFT MT103 for payment of import invoices under USD 1 million. To reduce fraud risk, for high-value orthopaedic implant materials or dedicated electron-beam premium components, the entity should advise the supplier to open and use a Commercial Letter of Credit issued by the Colombian bank via a SWIFT MT700; the bank will then honor the presenting documents (commercial invoice, airway bill, insurance certificate, certificate of origin) through a DPU or at-sight structure. All outbound disbursements must be mapped to a 'Declaración de Cambio' and to the appropriate DIAN import category for titanium powder (HS 8108.30), noting that payment for raw material cannot take place before the goods are declared at customs unless the bank receives a letter of guarantee. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low feasibility. Offshore digital banks, EMIs, and payment settlement firms (e.g., Wise/TransferWise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) are not authorized by the Banco de la República to provide local corporate accounts or to render foreign-exchange services for Colombian export/import settlement. Their service is limited to low-value incoming remittances or to feeding a SWIFT credit into a local bank account; they cannot act as a treasury rail for USD-denominated orthopedic-implant trade because they cannot issue Colombian trade ID codes, cannot file Declaración de Cambio, and cannot operate an active account with a Colombian NIT. Therefore the entity must maintain a minimum of one tier-1 domestic commercial bank account as the primary clearing anchor.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

35%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% for dividends distributed to foreign-resident shareholders out of profits that were already taxed at the 35% corporate level (Art. 408 E.T.); if the company distributes untaxed/exempt profits, the dividend WHT is 35% (which the payer must gross-up or withhold from the gross dividend). Colombian resident individuals receive dividends under a progressive scale that does not reduce the company's obligation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File obligations for tax year 2025 are triggered if gross income ≥ 100,000 UVT (COP 4,979,900,000) or net worth at year-end ≥ 200,000 UVT (COP 9,959,800,000), or — irrespective of the amount — where the S.A.S. engages in controlled transactions with related parties abroad or with entities in non-cooperative/low-tax jurisdictions. The TP informational return (Form 120) and the Informe Local/Maestro must be submitted electronically by the same date as Form 210.

Economic substance scope: Colombia does not have a standalone Gulf-style ESR, but the following substance doctrines bind the S.A.S.: (1) general anti-abuse rule (Art. 869 E.T.) authorizing recharacterization of schemes without a valid business purpose; (2) CFC rules (Articles 261-268 E.T.) taxing Colombian shareholders on passive income of foreign subsidiaries; (3) veil-piercing (Law 1258/2008, Art. 42) for legal forms used to evade taxes or defraud creditors; and (4) OECD/DTA 'beneficial ownership' standards. For the business model at hand, the Colombian entity must maintain an operational EBM facility, inventory, qualified technical staff, administrative governance, and substantive contracts in Colombia; empty delegation to a foreign parent or the absence of local decision-making on production/pricing will trigger re-assessments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Criminal defraudación fiscal (Art. 298 Código Penal): 48-108 months imprisonment and a fine equal to the evaded tax for evasion exceeding 250 SMMLV (≈ COP 325,000,000 in 2024); administrative 'inexactitud' penalty: 100% of the omitted tax (Art. 648 E.T.) plus default moratory interest; seizure, bank-account freeze and embargo by DIAN.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and the majority shareholder may be personally liable for the full tax deficiency (corporate veil pierced under Art. 42, Law 1258/2008); criminal travel bans (prohibición de salida del país) and preventive detention can be imposed; the accountant/revisor fiscal who signs false financial statements faces professional disqualification and penal sanctions.
