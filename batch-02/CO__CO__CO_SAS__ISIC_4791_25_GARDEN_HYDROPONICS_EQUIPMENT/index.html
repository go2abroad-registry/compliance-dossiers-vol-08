Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum authorized or paid-up capital under Ley 1258 de 2008. At least one share must be subscribed and issued; capital may be paid in instalments up to two years unless paid-in capital is agreed to be immediate. No statutory escrow account rule is imposed; any escrow is voluntary unless bylaws so provide.

Funding & unlock path: For home-office and pure e-commerce operations, no broad fire-safety or structural-load certificate is required. If a warehouse or storage space of more than 30 m² is operated, it must comply with Resolution 0312 of 2019 (minimum occupational health and safety implementation), NFPA or Colombian fire-safety standards (NTC 5246), and obtain an inspection certificate from the local fire department ('cuerpo de bomberos') for the storage area.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN); official portal for registration and electronic services: https://www.dian.gov.co

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must obtain a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) through registration in the DIAN single registry (Registro Único Tributario – RUT). The SOP is: (1) appoint a resident legal representative or agent with an authenticated power of attorney; (2) legalize the company's incorporation documents in Colombia through apostille/consularization and sworn translation; (3) register with the Chamber of Commerce if exercising commercial activities or use DIAN's virtual RUT pre-registration for non-commercial foreign suppliers; (4) validate identity and submit the RUT declaring the elected income tax and VAT responsibilities; (5) upon approval, obtain the certified RUT containing the assigned NIT; and (6) obtain a digital signature certificate issued by a certification authority licensed for electronic filing and electronic invoicing with DIAN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A Colombian S.A.S. is a juridical person and national resident taxpayer; it is taxed at entity level on its worldwide income and gains under ordinary corporate rules, not as a pass-through. There is no minimum capital requirement, but all shareholder, legal representative and company ownership information must be included in the RUT and the Chamber of Commerce registry.

Available election forms

Colombian law does not provide a check-the-box election; a S.A.S. cannot elect pass-through treatment. Eligible taxpayers may opt for the optional SIMPLE regime (Régimen Simple de Tributación) through DIAN on an annual basis where gross revenues do not exceed 80,000 UVT (approximately COP 3.79 billion for the 2024 UVT) and the economic activity is eligible; this regime substitutes certain income tax, VAT and other taxes with a combined installment payment. There is no standalone local hybrid mismatch election; hybrid structures are addressed through general BEPS-aligned transfer pricing, substance and anti-abuse rules, and no elective domestic treatment is available for hybrid mismatches.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general monetary threshold exists: any person engaged in taxable sales, services, or imports must register in the RUT as an IVA responsible party before undertaking the first taxable operation. For goods sold through e-commerce, VAT liabilities arise when the goods are imported or delivered into the Colombian customs territory. For the optional SIMPLE regime, the ceiling is 80,000 UVT (approximately COP 3.79 billion in 2024). Non-resident suppliers of digital services are separately required to register with DIAN from the first B2C sale without any minimum turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from Colombian customs territory by registered exporters are excluded from IVA (0%), and the exporter may offset or claim refund of input VAT attributable to the exported goods and directly related production or marketing costs. To qualify, the operation must satisfy general export substance requirements: a genuine sale to a non-resident purchaser, physical departure of the goods from Colombia under a valid customs export declaration (DEX), compliance with Colombian foreign-exchange regulations through repatriation of the export revenues, and documentary evidence of delivery, payment and transfer of title to the foreign buyer. Export of services is similarly eligible where rendered to a foreign beneficiary from Colombian territory and meets the statutory conditions for exportable services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX) validly lodged with DIAN and accepted by the customs clearance system
  • Transport document: Bill of Lading (sea) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing shipment from Colombian customs territory
  • Commercial invoice and packing list identifying the buyer, goods, terms of sale and HS codes of the hydroponics/gardening equipment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Reintegro de Divisas) issued by an authorized Colombian foreign-exchange intermediary, proving collection of export proceeds

Colombia operates a mandatory pre-validation electronic invoicing system (facturación electrónica) through DIAN. Taxable sellers must issue invoices electronically using an authorized technology provider in XML or UBL format and transmit them to DIAN's validation/clearance platform in near real time in accordance with DIAN's operating schedule. Every valid invoice must include the CUFE (Código Único de Facturación Electrónica), the QR code and the DIAN digital stamp; export transactions must be reported through the relevant electronic export documentation, the electronic purchase ledger and the periodic IVA return. Invoices not validated by DIAN are legally ineffective and expose the issuer to penalties.

DIAN will verify export VAT exclusions by matching the DEX, transport documents, foreign-exchange settlement certificates, payment flows and the nature of the goods. If the export position is false, unsupported or undocumented, DIAN may assess VAT at 19% on the invoiced value, impose moratory interest, apply the inaccuracy penalty of up to 200% of the resulting tax difference depending on the severity, order full clawback of previously refunded or credited input VAT, and refer the matter to customs and criminal enforcement under the Colombian customs fraud and anti-contraband regime. Repeated violations may additionally result in enhanced penalties, suspension or cancellation of exporter registration and seizure of goods in transit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Colombia maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime. Current-account transactions are freely convertible through authorized foreign-exchange intermediaries (IMC). Capital-account transactions require registration with the Banco de la República; foreign direct investment (FDI) must be channeled through the exchange market and is subject to a two-year minimum permanence period for capital repatriation, except in liquidation scenarios. Profit remittances are permitted after submission of the official FDI return (Formulario No. 3) and satisfaction of applicable corporate taxes; no additional transfer taxes are imposed on remittances. The system is characterized by broad convertibility with targeted administrative controls for export proceeds and investment registration.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds from goods must be collected and repatriated within six months from the date of the bill of lading, and service proceeds within six months from the date of rendering. Mandatory surrender of 100% of export proceeds is required through an authorized IMC; the exporter must obtain a conversion of foreign currency into COP. Settlement is evidenced by a Comprobante de Compra de Divisas issued by the IMC, and a Declaración de Cambio must be filed in accordance with Banco de la República regulations (Circular DCIN-350).

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República (Central Bank of Colombia) and the Comisión de Cambios (Exchange Commission) under the Ministry of Finance and Public Credit

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise or Revolut do not possess a Colombian financial license and cannot accept deposits or provide formal FX settlement under the exchange market regime. They may be used only as auxiliary collection channels for occasional retail e-commerce payments, provided that the funds are subsequently routed to an approved IMC in compliance with the wiring regulations. SEdPES (Sociedades Especializadas en Depósitos Electrónicos) are the only locally authorized digital deposit institutions, but they do not yet support full cross-border corporate treasury functions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign investors shall transfer capital through SWIFT MT103 to the account held at the local IMC (e.g., Banco de Bogotá), using purpose code 'Foreign Direct Investment – Establishment of New Enterprise' (Código de Inversión: 410). The wire must include the name and tax ID of the Colombian S.A.S. The receiving bank will issue a Comprobante de Compra de Divisas, and the company must submit Formulario No. 3 (Declaración de Cambio por Inversiones Internacionales) through the IMC within 30 working days. Back-to-back documentation includes: the capital contribution resolution of the shareholder, certificate of incorporation of the S.A.S., a post-closing corporate bank statement, and the legalized passport of the contributor.For trade settlement with hydroponics equipment suppliers, payables are executed via SWIFT MT103 using import purpose code 'Import of Goods – Formulario No. 4' (Declaración de Cambio por Servicios y Transferencias). For large-value or recurring supply contracts, open an irrevocable standby or documentary letter of credit under UCP600 through the IMC using MT700/MT707; sender and beneficiary must provide commercial invoices, transport documents, and packing lists.
