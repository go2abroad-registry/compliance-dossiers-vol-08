E-government filing portal

A Taiwan-incorporated WFOE must: (1) obtain foreign-investment approval from the Investment Commission, MOEA, and complete company registration; (2) upon incorporation, apply to the competent National Taxation Bureau for taxpayer registration and obtain the Uniform Invoice Number (統一編號, UIN), which serves as the statutory TIN/VAT ID; (3) receive the Business Tax Registration Certificate; (4) complete payroll withholding and entity registration; (5) for an SFO with only investment holding activities, no separate license is required; the same UIN is used for corporate income tax, business tax, and e-invoice filings. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Taiwan has no 'check-the-box' or pass-through election. A WFOE is per se taxable as a profit-seeking enterprise. Available elections include: (i) foreign income tax credit election for foreign-source income under Income Tax Act Article 3; (ii) inter-corporate dividend exclusion election under Article 42; (iii) election to use cost-average or specific identification for securities sales for an SFO holding assets; and (iv) application for advance rulings/APAs. Hybrid mismatch structures are constrained by the controlled-foreign-enterprise and general anti-avoidance rules (Income Tax Act Article 43-3), so hybrid mismatch elections are not permissible.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

NT$200,000 average monthly sales. Above this threshold, a business must register as a full VAT taxpayer and issue Uniform Invoices; below the threshold, it is a 'small-scale business' subject to a 1% business tax on gross sales, but is still required to register and file returns.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rating applies when tangible goods are actually exported from the Taiwan area and the exporter holds an approved customs export declaration (出口報單) and shipping evidence. Services: zero-rating applies to services provided to a foreign client or for exclusive use outside Taiwan, provided the service is not treated as domestic use, the consideration is externally sourced, and the supplier retains documentation showing the offshore destination; the burden of proving offshore use is on the taxpayer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單)
  • Bill of Lading (提單)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (匯入匯款通知書)

Full VAT registrants must issue Uniform Invoices (統一發票). Electronic Uniform Invoices (E-Invoices) are mandatory for qualifying businesses and must be transmitted in real time through the Ministry of Finance E-Invoice Integration Platform using the Turnkey API gateway; digital stamping and encryption are applied to each invoice. There is no per-invoice administrative clearance, but real-time synchronization enables automated audit scrutiny and data matching.

The National Taxation Bureau may re-audit zero-rating claims within the statutory 5-year assessment period (7 years for fraud). Fraudulent or fictitious export claims trigger: (i) recollected output VAT at the standard 5% or higher; (ii) a penalty equal to five times the amount evaded under Business Tax Act Article 51; (iii) monetary fines under the Tax Collection Act; and (iv) criminal liability for willful tax evasion. Retrospective clawbacks include refunded input VAT plus interest and possible cancellation of the enterprise's e-invoice credentials.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

CNY is fully convertible for current-account items subject to authenticity and consistency review by the designated FX bank, but remains a regulated, registration-based regime for capital-account items. For the WFOE, inbound equity capital (FDI) requires a SAFE foreign-exchange business registration (direct bank-underwriting model) before the capital-account receipt is credited; proceeds may be retained as FX or voluntarily settled into CNY subject to the negative list. Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is treated as a current-account dividend and requires: (1) annual statutory audit confirming the existence of distributable profits, (2) corporate income tax clearance and 10% treaty-based withholding tax settled under the Cross-Strait Agreement on Avoidance of Double Taxation and Tax Cooperation, subject to the Taiwan shareholder satisfying beneficial-ownership and Taiwan tax-residency conditions, and (3) SAFE dividend remittance verification executed by the sponsor bank. The principal remaining hurdles are the capital-account negative list, which prohibits capital-settled CNY from being used for downstream equity investment, securities investment, entrusted loans, or wealth-management products unless a specific pilot-zone permission is obtained, and the strict 'real transaction' verification applied to each dividend remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The former statutory export-collection-verification deadline of 180 days was repealed in 2012 and replaced by the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring and Aggregate Verification system under the 'Administrative Measures for Foreign Exchange in Trade' (SAFE Document HuiFa[2012] No. 38, as amended). Under the current regime, all trade current-account FX receipts must be repatriated to a licensed domestic bank within each enterprise's declared expected collection date recorded on the Trade FX Monitoring System; a 90-day benchmark applies for standard open-account general exports and up to 180 days for documentary collection/letters of credit, with any extension requiring substantiation and possible A-list downgrade to B/C-list status. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; the former mandatory surrender obligation has been repealed and the exporter may retain receipts in a trade-FX account or freely convert into CNY at market rates. For the subject SFO/WFOE, registered as an investment-holding vehicle under ISIC 6430, no active trade-FX directory status is required; the entity must report the absence of export performance and maintain no idle trade receipts, and any incidental export proceeds would immediately trigger the repatriation and monitoring obligations.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China collectively exercise all FX administration, cross-border payment monitoring, and capital-account approval authority over the onshore WFOE. On the Taiwan side, the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) and the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) regulate the Taiwan-resident shareholder's outward remittance, including the applicable NT/USD conversion and cross-border transfer declaration thresholds.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for onshore domestic clearing and capital-account transactions; Moderate for ancillary cross-border trade-collection facilitation under a SAFE-registered payment-institution pilot. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, PingPong, Currenxie, Wise Business) hold no PRC banking licence, cannot open or host the WFOE's regulatory accounts, cannot receive the registered-capital escrow, and cannot replace the SAFE business-registration conformity check. Their permissible use is confined to genuine goods-trade collection corridors supervised by a licensed onshore payment institution, with final RMB clearing forced back into a regulated bank account. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Step 1: Complete SAMR business licensing for the WFOE and open the basic deposit account at the chosen sponsor bank. Step 2: Complete the SAFE FDI business registration directly at the receiving bank under the bank-direct model and obtain the FDI Business Registration Certificate containing the E-form verification code. Step 3: Open the Foreign-Exchange Capital Account (CAPI) at the designated branch of BOC or CMB; the bank will reference the E-form, not a separate SAFE approval file. Step 4: The Taiwan shareholder remits via SWIFT MT103 with Field 70 including the FDI registration certificate number and the stated remittance purpose 'investment in registered capital', matching SAFE purpose code 622; the remitter must be the registered investor itself, and third-party payer wires will be rejected or placed on hold. Step 5: The receiving bank authenticates the wire against the FDI registration and credits the CAPI; proceeds can remain in USD or be settled into CNY under the 'voluntary settlement' rule into a dedicated settlement-pending-payment account. Step 6: Disbursement from the settlement-pending account is confined to the WFOE's legal business scope; if the family office intends to deploy capital-settled CNY into downstream onshore equity investments, a specific pilot-zone approval or separate compliance path must be obtained before settlement. Step 7: Every disbursement requires supporting contracts, VAT/fiscal invoices, and the bank-filed payment-submission form in the FX monitoring system.For goods trade, the standard rails are SWIFT MT103/MT202 through the sponsor bank supported by the customs declaration and Trade FX Directory conformity; documentary settlements are executed through Irrevocable Letters of Credit (MT700 issued by BOC or CMB and advised by a Taiwan bank) with USD cleared via CHIPS and CNH cleared via the Taipei RMB Clearing Bank (Bank of China Taipei Branch) under the Cross-Strait RMB settlement mechanism. For services and related-party fees, the mandatory 'tax recordal for cross-border service-trade payments' must be electronically filed for payments equal to or exceeding the USD 50,000 equivalent, and the bank will not release the wire unless the tax recordal certificate and, where applicable, withholding tax payment vouchers are presented; royalties to Taiwan licensors must be cleared against withholding tax first. Dividend remittances follow the same SWIFT infrastructure but require audited annual financial statements, the dividend resolution, a confirmation of distributable profits, the corporate income tax settlement certificate, the 10% withholding tax certificate, and the SAFE current-account payment verification form before the MT103 can be released.
