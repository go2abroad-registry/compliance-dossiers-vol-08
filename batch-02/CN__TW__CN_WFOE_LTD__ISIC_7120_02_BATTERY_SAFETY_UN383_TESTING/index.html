Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, STA). Registration and compliance are executed through the National Unified Standard E-Tax Bureau (全国统一规范电子税务局, https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn), in conjunction with the State Administration for Market Regulation (国家市场监督管理总局, SAMR) one-stop incorporation platform and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局, SAFE) Digital Foreign Exchange Administration system for export-receipt monitoring.

E-government filing portal

A PRC WFOE obtains its statutory tax identifier automatically at incorporation: the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, USCC) printed on the SAMR business license is the exclusive TIN for Corporate Income Tax (CIT), VAT, withholding, and social insurance. SOP for CN_WFOE_LTD: (1) complete SAMR online incorporation (企业登记全程电子化) and obtain the USCC; (2) within 30 days of licence issuance, log into the National Unified E-Tax Bureau to complete tax registration information confirmation (税务信息确认), including taxpayer-type determination, accounting-standard filing (财务会计制度备案), deposit-account reporting (存款账户账号报告), and tax-officer designation; (3) register in the SAFE 'Trade in Goods' foreign-exchange directory (货物贸易外汇收支企业名录) through the settlement bank before receiving export proceeds; (4) apply for invoice-quota and maximum-issue-limit authorisation (发票票种核定与最高开票限额) and activate the Tax Digital Account (税务数字账户) for fully digitalised e-invoices (数电票); (5) file the offshore-service VAT zero-rating/exemption registration with the in-charge tax office for cross-border UN38.3 testing services.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a WFOE (limited liability company) is a PRC resident enterprise taxed at the legal-entity level on worldwide income at the standard 25% CIT rate; no pass-through, partnership, or disregarded-entity classification exists for PRC domestic purposes; outbound profit distributions attract 10% dividend withholding tax, potentially reduced to 5% under the Cross-Strait tax arrangement or applicable treaties; hybrid and controlled-foreign-company anti-avoidance rules apply.

Available election forms

PRC law provides no check-the-box entity election. Available statutory elections for the WFOE testing laboratory: (1) VAT status election — voluntary general-taxpayer registration (一般纳税人) available below the RMB 5 million threshold; small-scale simplified method at 3% (currently reduced to 1% through 2027); (2) CIT preferential applications — High-and-New-Technology Enterprise status for a 15% CIT rate, and Small Low-Profit Enterprise reduced-rate treatment (effective 5% on the first RMB 3 million taxable income for 2023–2027); (3) R&D super-deduction election (100% additional deduction for qualified testing-methodology R&D expenditure); (4) one-time pre-tax deduction election for fixed assets and equipment under RMB 5 million (accelerated depreciation); (5) cross-border service VAT zero-rating/exemption filing under Caishui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4 with ex-ante submission of the offshore testing service contract; (6) hybrid-mismatch perimeter — PRC debt/equity thin-capitalisation (2:1), CFC attribution, and 2023 CIT implementation-rule anti-avoidance provisions constrain hybrid financing; prudently structured foreign-tax-credit and tax-sparing elections are available but no hybrid check-the-box result is achievable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 in aggregate annual VAT-taxable turnover — mandatory VAT general-taxpayer registration applies once cumulative taxable supplies exceed RMB 5 million within any rolling 12-month period; below that threshold the WFOE may elect small-scale taxpayer status (3% levy, currently reduced to 1% through 31 December 2027) with a monthly RMB 100,000 VAT-exemption allowance for small-scale taxpayers; however, an exporting WFOE requiring input-VAT refunds will generally elect general-taxpayer status immediately. Applicable headline rate for the laboratory's technical testing services: 6%; goods supplies at 13%; logistics at 9%; qualifying exports at 0%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods zero-rating: export of UN38.3-tested lithium-battery samples/products qualifies for 0% VAT and an export input-tax refund (出口退税) when there is actual customs clearance out of PRC territory, an overseas buyer remits foreign currency, and the goods are not in the prohibited/restricted refund catalogue. Services zero-rating/exemption: cross-border technical testing services (UN38.3 drop, vibration, thermal, and CE-RED EMC tests) supplied to an overseas recipient, including a Taiwan-registered client, and 'completely consumed outside China' qualify for the 0% rate under Caishui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4 (offshore services / offshore outsourcing), subject to filing of the offshore service contract, overseas-party receipt confirmation, and inward-remittance proof; for Taiwan-destined work, the Cross-Strait tax arrangement applies for double-taxation relief on income, while the return of tested samples into Taiwan may attract Taiwan 5% business tax and customs duties, mitigated by temporary-export/ATA-carnet documentation for round-trip sample movements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible on the current account provided the designated bank verifies transaction genuineness; the capital account remains restricted and transaction-by-transaction monitored. The WFOE may freely receive and settle testing-fee income (UN38.3 drop testing and CE-RED electromagnetic lab services) under BOP declaration code 228050 for technical testing services. Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is permitted after statutory audit, enterprise income tax final settlement, board-approved distribution and tax clearance; withholding applies at 10%, reduced to 5% where the Taiwan corporate beneficial owner directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE under the Cross-Strait Avoidance of Double Taxation Agreement. No repatriation quota exists, but disposal of equity or liquidation proceeds requires a dedicated SAFE-verified capital account. The principal convertibility hurdles are documentary: tax filing certificates, SAFE registration codes, and the prohibition on using capital-account funds for non-business, equity, securities or real-estate uses outside the approved scope.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender applies; the forced conversion and export verification regime was abolished in 2012. The WFOE retains 100% of its export and service receipts in its FX settlement account and may voluntarily convert to CNY at any time. Service-trade receipts must be declared through the settling bank within 5 working days under the correct BOP receipt code, and the WFOE must be enrolled in the bank's FX data collection system and, for any goods-trade flows, in SAFE's Trade FX Monitoring List (贸易外汇收支企业名录). There is no statutory conversion ratio or repatriation deadline for services; however, irregular collection patterns, late declarations or invoice mismatches trigger SAFE on-site examination and reduction of FX convenience quota.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC / 中国人民银行) exercises apex monetary authority; the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE / 国家外汇管理局), a PBOC-affiliated regulator, enforces all FX control measures, FDI registration, trade monitoring and cross-border payment verification affecting the CN_WFOE_LTD treasury.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing protocol: (1) incorporate with SAMR and carve the corporate seals; (2) open the basic operating account and complete bank-administered SAFE FDI registration (业务登记凭证 with an 18-digit verification code); (3) the Taiwan shareholder wires the capital contribution via SWIFT MT103 with '投资款 / Capital Injection - [WFOE legal name]' in the remittance information field; the receiving bank credits the FX capital account under BOP declaration code 622011 (investment capital funds); (4) the WFOE may voluntarily settle the proceeds into CNY through the 意愿结汇 mechanism into a supervised pending-payment account (结汇待支付账户), with each disbursement verified against underlying contracts, invoices, lease, payroll or procurement documents; (5) capital funds may not be used for equity or securities investment, intercompany lending other than as permitted, real-estate purchases outside approved scope, or any non-operating use. Registered capital may be injected in instalments per the articles of association; no statutory minimum first-tranche percentage applies under the 2020 Foreign Investment Law, but the bank requires the injection schedule to align with the filed feasibility plan and the WFOE's operational needs.Equipment imports: SWIFT MT103 for T/T payments under BOP code 121010 (general trade), or an irrevocable at-sight or 90-180 day letter of credit with documents comprising commercial invoice, packing list, air waybill or bill of lading and inspection certificates; the WFOE must appear in SAFE's Trade FX Monitoring List for goods-trade payments. Service payments (IECEE CBTL certification fees, calibration services, laboratory software licences): MT103 under BOP code 228050, supported by contracts and invoices; any single payment above the USD 50,000 equivalent requires the pre-submitted '服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表' filed through the e-tax bureau before the bank executes the wire. Profit remittance: statutory audit report, enterprise income tax annual return filing, dividend distribution resolution, and WHT certificate (5% or 10%) must be bundled and remitted exclusively from the operational FX account. No commercial disbursement may be cleared through personal accounts or unlicensed third-party payment channels.Deliverable FX forwards (远期结售汇) under SAFE's genuine-demand principle, with notional capped at actual expected receipts or paymentsFX swaps (外汇掉期) for temporary liquidity conversion between CNY and foreign currenciesCross-currency interest-rate swaps (货币掉期/交叉货币利率互换) for long-term equipment-finance or intercompany-loan hedgingVanilla and structured FX options and option-combination forwards (区间远期、组合期权) embedded in a documented corporate hedging programmeOffshore NDFs are permissible only for the Taiwan parent (Taipei or Hong Kong CNH markets) to hedge the equity value of the China investment; the mainland WFOE is ineligible to contract NDFs onshore

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for Wise as an onshore treasury rail: Wise Business does not accept mainland China-registered companies, and domestic CNY clearing is dependent on licensed PSP partners; Wise may be used by the Taiwan parent only for small disbursements routed via SWIFT into the WFOE's licensed bank account. Airwallex and similar licensed PSPs are Medium viable as supplementary collection channels for overseas customer payments, but testing-fee revenue must ultimately enter the WFOE's own SAFE-supervised bank account to satisfy the 'who exports, who collects' (谁出口谁收汇) integrity rule. Use of unlicensed offshore EMIs for business receipts constitutes illegal cross-border payment activity and exposes the entity to account freezing and SAFE penalties.
