Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital exists under the Taiwan Company Act for a limited company; however, the total subscribed capital must be paid in full before registration and may not be paid in installments. For a wholly foreign-owned entity, the capital must be remitted from overseas through banking channels, deposited into a temporary corporate account, and evidenced by a bank-issued certificate and a Taiwan certified public accountant capital verification report. No blocked escrow account is required by law, but the paid-in capital must not be withdrawn or refunded to the investor before lawful incorporation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, accessible via the Electronic Tax Office (e-Toilet / 电子税务局) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity establishing a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) must first register the company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) to obtain a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the de facto Tax Identification Number (TIN). Thereafter, the WFOE must complete tax registration with the State Taxation Administration (STA) within 30 days of obtaining the business license, submitting: (i) the SAMR-issued business license, (ii) articles of association, (iii) lease or property ownership documents for the registered address, (iv) identification of the legal representative and senior officers, (v) bank account opening certificate in China, and (vi) a completed tax registration form such as the 'Tax Registration Form (税务登记表)' (not a generic code). Non-resident entities without a physical presence that earn China-source income must obtain a tax registration via the STA's withholding agent mechanism under the Provisional Regulations on Enterprise Income Tax, using tax file number assigned by the STA upon filing of the withholding contract or under Double Tax Agreement claim procedures.

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for WFOE: China does not permit check-the-box pass-through elections for limited liability foreign-invested entities. A WFOE is subject to entity-level Corporate Income Tax (CIT) at 25%, with potential treaty or preferential lower rates (e.g., 15% for encouraged industries in certain zones). VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5 million annual taxable sales (turnover) for general VAT taxpayer status; below this threshold, entities are generally registered as small-scale taxpayers and may apply a simplified VAT rate of 3% (subject to prevailing preferential reductions). A newly incorporated WFOE may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer by meeting the threshold or by satisfying the STA's administrative criteria (e.g., sound accounting and fixed place of business).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating for exports of goods applies when the WFOE physically exports goods from China to Taiwan (or other jurisdictions) and satisfies the STA's export VAT refund (出口退税) criteria under the Measures for the Administration of Export Tax Refund (Exemption) on Goods and Services (国家税务总局公告). The exported goods must: (i) be declared to customs under the appropriate export customs regime (e.g., General Trade 一般贸易), (ii) receive the customs export declaration evidence (出口货物报关单) with a valid export customs record, (iii) be substantiated by a sales contract, commercial invoice, packing list, and transportation documents showing delivery outside the customs territory of China, (iv) have the foreign exchange received in a timely manner and verified via the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) foreign exchange monitoring system, and (v) be supported by a valid VAT special invoice or ordinary invoice used for the export sale. Digital / cross-border services zero-rating is narrower: export of services outside China can be zero-rated only if the services are fully consumed outside China and are specifically listed in the STA's Catalogue of Zero-Rated and Tax-Exempt Cross-Border Services

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (出口货物报关单) with assigned customs declaration number
  • Bill of Lading (海运提单) or Airway Bill (空运单), as applicable
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Foreign Exchange Inward Remittance Proof (出口收汇核销单 or SAFE-verified bank receipt confirming repatriation)
  • Export sales contract between WFOE and overseas buyer
  • VAT special invoice or ordinary invoice for export sale

China mandates the use of the nationwide electronic invoice system '税控系统' (Tax Control System) operated by the STA. General VAT taxpayers must issue invoices through the '全面数字化电子发票' (All-Electronic Invoice, 'ShuPiao' system) which requires real-time transmission of invoice data to the STA's platform using official APIs. Each electronic invoice carries a unique 20-digit invoice code and an anti-fraud digital stamp (税务数字证书). For high-risk transactions or export goods, the 'Golden Tax Phase IV' (金税四期) system enforces cross-checking of invoice amounts, tax rates, and customs declaration data, with automated risk flags for anomalies. Physical tax control devices (税控盘) remain accepted for some offline issuance, but the STA prioritizes API-based digital fiscal clearance. All invoice data must be synchronised to the STA central database; failure to transmit data in real-time may result in suspension of VAT invoice issuance privileges

Export VAT refund claims are subject to post-clearance audits by the STA, customs anti-smuggling bureaus, and SAFE. Where a zero-rated export is found unsubstantiated, fraudulent, or the foreign exchange is not repatriated within the statutory period (generally 180 days, or as extended by SAFE under special circumstances), the STA may impose: (i) withdrawal of the VAT refund entitlement and additional VAT payable equal to the zero-rated / exempted tax, (ii) late payment surcharges of 0.05% per day on the underpaid tax, (iii) administrative penalties up to 1-5 times the tax evaded (or a statutory fine), (iv) forfeiture of the right to use export tax refund facilitation for up to 2 years, (v) referral to criminal authorities if fraud exceeds RMB 50,000 or constitutes '虚开增值税专用发票' (falsely issuing VAT special invoices) or '骗取出口退税' (obtaining export tax refund by fraud), leading to imprisonment and fines under PRC Criminal Law Articles 204 and 205. Internal controls must ensure documentation retention for at least 5 years and modernisation of export records in the single-window customs system.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Taiwan maintains a managed floating exchange-rate regime in which the New Taiwan Dollar (NTD) is freely usable within the domestic payment system and current-account transactions are broadly liberalized without prior approval. Remittances related to trade in goods, services, profits, wages, and intra-company funding may generally be effected through authorized domestic banks, subject to declaration thresholds and supporting documentation. Capital-account transactions, by contrast, are treated as controlled: inbound investment, outward direct investment, cross-border loans, equity injections, and private-equity buyout contributions require prior approval or filing with the CBC and/or the Investment Commission of the MOEA, depending on industry restrictions and transaction size. Export proceeds are subject to collection and settlement rules but there is no automatic full surrender requirement; exporters may retain foreign currency in authorized foreign-currency deposit accounts or sell the proceeds to designated banks. Profit remittance by a foreign-invested WFOE requires audited or reviewed financial statements, dividend distribution board resolutions, and proof of the underlying foreign investment permitted status; withholding tax must be settled, and remittance is then routed through the local settlement bank using the designated foreign exchange purpose code. In practice, the greatest hurdles are capital-account pre-approval, the absence of a blanket current-account convertibility guarantee, and the need to demonstrate a real economic nexus and proper investment licensing before repatriating proceeds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds must be collected within 180 days from the date of shipment unless an extension is granted by the CBC or the authorized foreign-exchange bank. There is no mandatory conversion ratio under the current managed-float framework; an exporter may keep the foreign currency in a designated foreign-currency deposit account or convert it to NTD at a bank negotiated rate. All settlements must be reported to the CBC through the Banking Department's Foreign Exchange Data Processing System using approved purpose codes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC), operating under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act and administered in cooperation with the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) and the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility is Low to Nil for a Taiwan-domiciled WFOE. Taiwan's banking law reserves deposit-taking, remittance, and foreign-exchange dealing for licensed banks and the Post Office; an offshore electronic money institution or digital EMI cannot lawfully maintain a NTD settlement account, provide local clearing, or act as a designated foreign-exchange bank for the WFOE. An unlicensed EMI would be unable to satisfy CBC reporting obligations, capital-verification requirements, or the statutory documentary chain for corporate disbursements. At most, a licensed foreign bank operating a Taiwan offshore banking branch or an international securities firm may provide ancillary services, but the primary operating account must be held at a domestic Tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the Taiwan WFOE must be routed from overseas through a designated domestic foreign-exchange bank after the Investment Commission of the MOEA grants the foreign-investment approval or post-approval filing is complete. The remitter must use the CBC-approved foreign exchange purpose code for inbound remittance of foreign investor capital (generally 'raise' or 'use and remittance of foreign capital' under the investment guidelines), execute a foreign exchange transaction/declaration form with the bank, and provide the wire instruction referencing the investment approval number. The local bank will credit a temporary capital escrow account or directly the WFOE's NTD capital account; the actual paid-in capital must then be verified by a Taiwan-licensed CPA, who issues a capital verification report and files it with the Company and Commercial Registration authorities. For a leveraged buyout SPV, the sponsor's equity contribution and, if applicable, shareholder loan must be separately identified; shareholder loans above the registration threshold require CBC approval and must comply with the debt/equity regulations and interest withholding rules.For settlement of acquisition consideration, advisory fees, professional legal/accounting costs, or related-party payments, the WFOE should use the SWIFT MT103 standard for clean telegraphic transfers to the beneficiary bank account, with the full remitter and beneficiary name, address, account numbers, and the CBC-approved foreign exchange purpose code for outward remittance. For documented trade transactions, an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit opened by the WFOE's Taiwan bank in favor of a Chinese or other export seller, with usance or sight clauses, provides a secured institutional clearing rail; documentary collections (D/P and D/A) can be used where the parties accept collection risk. All outward remittances must be accompanied by invoices, contracts, or merger-and-acquisition agreements and reported to the CBC under the daily foreign exchange transaction reporting threshold; any remittance exceeding the statutory exemption requires an application and supporting documentation. Same-day CHIPS or local NTD clearing rails are not available for cross-border flows; settlement is processed through the Taiwan clearing house for domestic legs and through correspondent banks for USD/EUR payments.Outright forward contracts (NDF-style deliverable and non-deliverable FX forwards) are permissible when executed with an authorized domestic bank and based on a genuine underlying commercial or financial exposure, such as expected export proceeds or a foreign-currency capital injection.FX swaps (spot vs forward) and cross-currency swaps are permitted for liquidity management in a covered and documented manner, provided the counterparty is a CBC-authorized bank and exposure is limited to the SPV's actual debt service or acquisition commitments.Plain-vanilla currency options are allowed where a real exposure is demonstrated; speculative or uncovered derivative positions are generally prohibited and would require special CBC approval.NDFs are not freely available for the NTD market in all cases; the CBC maintains restrictions on high-frequency speculative NTD trading, and any non-deliverable hedge must be routed through a local bank's offshore banking unit or approved international branch if not prohibited.

Profit-Seeking Enterprise Income Tax Annual Return, Audited/Statutory Financial Statements and Transfer Pricing Disclosure Calendar-year taxpayers must file and pay between May 1 and May 31 following the fiscal year-end; approved non-calendar fiscal years must file within five months after the fiscal year-end. $0 USD Delinquent filing triggers a surcharge of 3% of unpaid tax for each two-month period, capped at 9%; if the return is filed after discovery or not filed, an additional penalty of 15% of unpaid tax applies; if no tax is due, a fixed fine of NT$1,500 to NT$7,500 applies. Late payment interest accrues monthly using the two-year postal time-deposit rate, and continued non-payment leads to tax levy, bank account attachment and a tax clearance block preventing voluntary deregistration.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under Company Act Article 20 and related MOEA/FSC threshold rules, a Taiwan WFOE must have its annual financial statements independently audited by a certified public accountant if, at fiscal year-end, it meets any one of the following: (a) paid-in capital of NT$30 million or more; (b) total annual revenue of NT$50 million or more; (c) headcount of 100 or more employees; or (d) it is a public company or designated financial/securities institution. For this private equity buyout SPV, the balance-sheet test is typically decisive: the SPV will more likely than not hold acquisition loans and portfolio investments exceeding NT$30 million, thereby making a full CPA audit mandatory even if the entity has zero operating revenue.
