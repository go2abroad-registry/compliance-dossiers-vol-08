Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); taxpayer-facing online services are delivered through the National Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) platform at the official STA portal (www.chinatax.gov.cn) and the local electronic tax bureaux.

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor establishing a PRC wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) must first apply for a Foreign-Invested Enterprise business licence at the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) under the Foreign Investment Law. The licence carries a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which automatically becomes the taxpayer identification number for all PRC national tax, local tax, social insurance and VAT purposes; no separate TIN is issued. The SAMR registration data is synchronised to the competent local office of the State Taxation Administration, completing the statutory tax registration without a separate application. The WFOE must then complete supplementary VAT onboarding in the e-Tax platform: select the VAT taxpayer status (general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer), apply for invoice allotment in the National VAT Invoice System, configure the electronic invoice/digital invoice module, and appoint a tax representative/accounting entity. If a foreign non-resident enterprise itself has a taxable presence or engages in cross-border supply without incorporation, it must complete a non-resident tax registration at the local STA, obtain a temporary taxpayer code for withholding purposes, and file China-source income tax and VAT through the withholding agent mechanism.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None for entity classification: the WFOE is automatically treated as a PRC resident enterprise taxed at entity level; no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under PRC tax law. For VAT purposes, the WFOE may elect between small-scale taxpayer treatment and general taxpayer treatment subject to the RMB 5 million annual turnover threshold, and may elect to apply VAT zero-rating rather than exemption for eligible cross-border services.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no monetary turnover threshold for basic tax registration because the WFOE is registered at incorporation. The mandatory turnover threshold for VAT general taxpayer registration is RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales; below that, the small-scale VAT taxpayer rate applies. The remote BPO call-centre and tele-sales service, as a modern service, is normally chargeable at 6% VAT domestically and at 0% when qualifying as an exported service.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the PRC VAT cross-border taxable conduct rules (the VAT pilot programme and its cross-border supply annex, as amended), a PRC VAT general taxpayer supplying business-process-management services, including call centre operations, inbound helpdesk and outbound tele-sales BPO, to an overseas recipient located in Taiwan (jurisdiction TW) may apply the 0% VAT rate if the services are completely consumed outside the PRC, the recipient is not a PRC unit or individual, the service is supplied under a written contract, and the export consideration is received in foreign currency and repatriated to the PRC. The export service must be recorded as zero-rated sales in the VAT return, and the taxpayer must retain documented evidence supporting offshore consumption, including client tax-status evidence, service delivery records, call logs and payment flows. If the service is provided to a PRC-related party's Taiwan establishment or is partially consumed in China, zero-rating is denied.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) - required where physical goods or ancillary equipment are exported from the PRC customs territory
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill or equivalent transport document - required for physical export movements; not applicable to pure remote BPO call-centre services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipt voucher evidencing receipt and repatriation of the export consideration in foreign currency from Taiwan
  • Cross-border service contract, statement of work or purchase order identifying the Taiwan recipient and the offshore service scope
  • Evidence of offshore consumption: customer tax/corporate registration certificate, service delivery records, call detail records, and telephony/IP-based delivery logs demonstrating no PRC taxable consumption
  • PRC VAT zero-rated/special invoice issued through the e-invoice system or invoice management platform
  • Foreign exchange settlement and declaration records from the designated bank, including the export proceeds repatriation monitoring record

The PRC uses a nationwide VAT invoice management system (the National VAT Invoice System). All special VAT invoices and digital e-invoices are issued through the taxpayer's authorised invoicing software or the fully digitalised electronic invoice (digital e-invoice) platform and are uploaded in real time to the State Taxation Administration's central data warehouse for automated cross-matching, anomaly detection and anti-fraud analysis. Invoice numbers are centrally allocated by the tax authority; digital invoices carry cryptographic verification codes and electronic signatures (anti-fraud digital stamping). There is no public third-party fiscal-clearance API comparable to Latin American electronic invoicing; instead, qualified taxpayers integrate with the official e-Tax/e-invoice service interfaces for invoice issuance, red-letter reversal, input VAT certification, and export rebate filing.

The STA may conduct post-export audit and require production of the full substantiation file. If zero-rating is improperly claimed, the tax authority will claw back the zero-rated output VAT, deny or recover input VAT refunds, exact late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and impose an administrative penalty of 50% to five times the underpaid VAT under the Tax Administration Law of the PRC. Fraudulent export or circular transactions are additionally subject to inclusion in the abnormal taxpayer list, tightening of invoice allotment, export rebate suspension, and criminal liability for tax fraud under the Criminal Law of the PRC if the amounts or intent meet statutory thresholds.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with RMB convertibility restricted on the capital account while substantially liberalized on the current account. The Chinese WFOE can freely settle legitimate service-export revenues and pay current-account operating expenses through designated banks, but every cross-border transaction must be supported by genuine commercial documents and reflected in the SAFE foreign exchange monitoring system. On the capital side, FDI equity injection from the Taiwan parent requires approval or filing with the Ministry of Commerce and SAFE; once remitted, funds are credited to a foreign-currency capital account and can be settled into RMB for qualifying use only after presenting a capital verification report and a payment-purpose application. Profit remittances to Taiwan are permitted after audited financials, corporate income tax settlement, and board declaration are completed, but are treated as capital-account dividend remittances and must be routed through a Chinese bank with the correct purpose code. A WFOE cannot freely convert CNY into foreign currency for cross-border lending or overseas investments without prior SAFE registration; hence treasury planning must pre-approve each major flow. The RMB is not fully convertible offshore, and the Taiwan parent should expect settlement lags and documentary scrutiny.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's trade-monitoring scheme, the WFOE must collect export and service proceeds through a domestic designated foreign-exchange bank and match the incoming funds to underlying contracts and invoices in the SAFE foreign exchange monitoring system. Repatriation should generally occur within 180 days of the service performance date unless SAFE has approved a later collection. There is no fixed percentage surrender obligation; once received, the enterprise may retain the foreign currency in its current-account foreign-exchange account or voluntarily convert into RMB, but the retained funds may only be used for genuine operating needs. Delayed collection, partial write-offs, or settlement through third-party accounts require prior notification or filing to the local SAFE branch and must be supported by the client contract, service invoice, completion report, and a statement of non-offshore retention.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must remit investment capital via SWIFT MT103 from a Taiwan regulated bank to the WFOE's Foreign Exchange Capital Account at a Chinese designated bank such as Bank of China. The remittance message should include the SAFE-compliant foreign direct investment purpose code for equity injection, together with the MOFCOM filing certificate, articles of association, and board resolution. After receipt, the bank issues a capital-entry voucher, a PRC registered accountant performs capital verification, and the SAFE FDI system records the shareholder equity registration. Any subsequent conversion from USD to RMB must be supported by a capital-account use form and may only be applied to permitted business purposes.Domestic operating disbursements for salaries, rent, and telecom are paid in RMB through the WFOE's corporate online banking platform and CIPS for any cross-border RMB elements. For physical imports such as telephony hardware or IT equipment, the WFOE may use commercial irrevocable letters of credit or documentary collections (D/P or D/A) through the issuing Chinese bank, supported by customs declarations, shipping documents, and inspection certificates. Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Under Article 20 of the Taiwan Company Act and the MOEA certification rules, an independent CPA audit is statutorily mandatory when the company's paid-in capital exceeds NT$30,000,000 (approximately US$950,000). This is the sole general corporate threshold; there are no separate statutory audit triggers based solely on turnover or employee headcount for a standard BPO call-center WFOE. Publicly-listed/OTC companies and regulated financial institutions are subject to additional mandatory audit regimes; if a non-listed company applies for bank credit, tax loss carryforwards, or certain subsidies, an audited report may be contractually or administratively required.
