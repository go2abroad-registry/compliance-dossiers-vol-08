Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None under Taiwan Company Act for a general foreign-invested limited company; the Articles must fix a par value and authorized share capital, all issued shares must be subscribed, and the paid-in capital must be verified by an independent certified public accountant. No statutory blocked escrow account is required; however, the foreign capital injection must be approved by the Investment Commission, remitted to a bank account in Taiwan, and committed to the Company before the incorporation registration is completed. Withdrawals prior to registration may be restricted until the capital verification report is signed and deposited.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable for physical chemical, manufacturing, assembly, or warehousing operations. However, the registered office is a commercial workplace and must maintain the building occupancy permit, obtain a fire safety inspection certificate under the Fire Services Act, install portable fire extinguishers and emergency exit lighting, and maintain an evacuation plan. General workplace safety obligations under the Occupational Safety and Health Act apply to office ergonomics, electrical safety, and emergency preparedness.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The renminbi (CNY) is fully convertible on the current account for trade, services, dividends, and interest without ex-ante SAFE approval; capital-account transactions remain governed by a registration/approval matrix under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations (State Council Decree 532, as amended). For a Taiwan-invested WFOE acting as a VC GP (ISIC 6499): (i) inbound equity capital is a capital-account FDI item requiring SAFE FDI registration, a dedicated foreign-exchange capital account (外汇资本金账户), and compliance with the payment-based settlement regime (支付结汇), including the negative list that prohibits settlement into entrusted loans, wealth-management products, securities investment outside permitted vehicles, and non-self-use real estate; (ii) profit and carried-interest remittances to the Taiwan parent are classified as current-account 'direct investment income' for BOP purposes but are procedurally conditioned on audited financial statements, a board-approved profit-distribution resolution, the FDI registration record, and the applicable tax withholding/clearance voucher; (iii) any cross-border conversion of proceeds from downstream portfolio exits is monitored as a discrete SAFE capital-flow event and may require registration amendments; and (iv) Taiwan investors are treated as foreign investors for FX purposes under the Taiwan Compatriots Investment Protection Law, enjoying no preferential FX quota and no autonomous convertibility, though they are eligible for pilot vehicles (e.g., Shanghai QFLP/R-QFLP) and RMB cross-border remittance channels for qualified private-fund structures. The practical remittance hurdle is not convertibility per se but documentary discipline: each CNY/FX leg must be matched to an underlying business record, a cross-border payment declaration, and the correct SAFE transaction-code family.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable to this mandate: the WFOE is a venture-capital GP with no physical export trade, and China abolished compulsory export-proceeds surrender in October 2012; exporters retain FX under a five-business-day receipt declaration obligation only. All inbound receipts for this entity are capital-account FDI items (investment capital), which carry no surrender or mandatory conversion ratio. Should the GP later repatriate proceeds from an offshore portfolio exit, those receipts would be classified as ODI repatriation and must follow the annual SAFE foreign-exchange stock-and-equity registration (存量权益登记) and the originating ODI registration, not trade-surrender rules.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局), operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行, the central bank); SAFE municipal sub-branches administer FDI registration and capital-account convertibility, executing day-to-day control through delegated Approved Foreign Exchange Banks (AFEBs) at the WFOE's registered address.
