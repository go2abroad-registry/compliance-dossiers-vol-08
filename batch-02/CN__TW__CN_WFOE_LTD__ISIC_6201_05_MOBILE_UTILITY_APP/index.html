Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

The use of a virtual office or commercial mailbox as the registered office address for a WFOE engaged in mobile utility software development is highly restricted and generally not acceptable for tax and bank registration. The Company Law of the PRC and the Regulations on the Administration of Market Entity Registration require a registered address (住所) that is a fixed, verifiable physical location within the administrative area of the registration authority, and the address must correspond to a building unit or room that can receive legal documentation and be used for actual business activities. Local SAMR offices, especially in first-tier cities such as Shanghai, Beijing, Shenzhen, and Hangzhou, may permit a 'concentration registration' (集中登记) or 'one address multi-license' (一址多照) arrangement in designated business parks or government-supported incubation centers; however, this does not equate to a pure virtual mailbox and requires the park manager to provide physical letterboxes, the capacity for regulators to perform inspections, and a lease agreement of at least one year. For banking purposes, virtually all licensed commercial banks under PBOC supervision reject purely virtual addresses for corporate account opening: the bank compliance officer must visit or verify the place of business, confirm a site lease or property ownership certificate, and see physical servers, computers, staff work stations, and accounting records that demonstrate genuine commercial substance. For an ISIC 6201 mobile utility app company, the tax authority may accept a shared office or a serviced office with dedicated meeting rooms and an actual reception desk, but the local tax bureau will still require an on-site inventory verification before issuing value-added tax invoices. Consequently, a pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is rejected for this industry at the WFOE registration, tax registration, and bank account opening stages; the most prudent approach is a physical lease of a Class B or Class C office suite of at least 15 square meters, with a separate desk for the legal representative and archives for the company seal in accordance with the company seal archival regulations.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online electronic portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

For the China-resident WFOE, incorporation before the local Administration for Market Regulation automatically produces the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), an 18-character identifier that serves as the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number. The local market administration transmits the company record to the competent State Taxation Administration branch through the interagency data-sharing platform, so no separate tax registration visit is required; within 30 days of the business license, the legal representative must complete the initial tax registration confirmation, bank-account information, and accounting-institution reporting in the Electronic Tax Bureau. If a foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment is in the structure (for example the Taiwan shareholder), it is not required to obtain a separate TIN merely for shareholding or passive Chinese-source income; the China WFOE as payer must withhold enterprise income tax and VAT at source. If the foreign non-resident entity acquires a permanent establishment, PRC real estate, or another taxable presence, it must file a Non-resident Enterprise Tax Registration application through the e-Tax portal or the local tax service hall to obtain a non-resident taxpayer identification number, and it may later apply for treaty or cross-strait agreement relief by submitting a Tax Residence Certificate to the tax authority.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Taiwan operates a managed-float foreign exchange regime with a two-tier statutory framework: current account transactions are generally free and convertible, while capital account movements remain strictly declared and, in many cases, pre-approved. The CBC delegates dealing power to Authorized Foreign Exchange Banks (AFEBs), and all FX receipts and disbursements must be lodged through the bank's Foreign Exchange Declaration System (FXDS). Export receipts from the mobile utility app's App Store and Google Play sales are current account inflows, promptly convertible into New Taiwan Dollars once the purpose code is declared. However, capital injections from the China mainland parent, share capital increases, and subsequent dividend repatriation to mainland China are capital account events. Dividend remittance requires an audited financial report, a valid Investment Commission approval for the original investment, and a bank-level declaration under the monetary authorities' General Rules Governing Foreign Exchange Businesses; if the amount exceeds the paid-in capital as registered, a prior CBC or Investment Commission permit is necessary. Practically, the WFOE should maintain thorough transfer pricing documentation and tax payment certificates, because banks will withhold remittance if tax clearance is incomplete. The CBC also monitors NTD for offshore settlement; all onshore FX conversion must take place through AFEBs, and offshore digital EMIs cannot act as settlement agents for NTD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods export proceeds must be collected and declared within 180 days from the shipment date; service export proceeds, including App Store licensing royalties, must be credited and declared within 180 days from the service performance date. The exporter may either surrender the foreign currency to an AFEB for conversion to NTD or hold it in a designated foreign-currency deposit account, but full declaration via FXDS is mandatory; no mandatory conversion ratio is imposed by the CBC, allowing 100% retention in foreign currency.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC), Foreign Exchange Department

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMI viability is Low. An offshore digital EMI, such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, or Payoneer, is not licensed by the Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission and cannot operate as an Authorized Foreign Exchange Bank. Such an EMI cannot hold NTD deposits, issue NTD clearing codes, or process CBC-mandated FX declarations for the WFOE. These EMIs can only serve as auxiliary international collection or payout channels for non-NTD currencies, but the statutory treasury layer, capital verification, tax clearance, and dividend remittance must run through a domestic tier-1 commercial bank. Offshore digital EMIs are therefore legally barred from functioning as the primary settlement rail for Taiwan-domiciled entities.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound FDI capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the mainland parent's bank to the Taiwan WFOE's Capital Escrow Account with an AFEB, using the remitter's bank wire authorization, CBC purpose code 5701 for paid-in capital of foreign investment, and the Investment Commission approval number in the narrative field. The AFEB will accept the funds only after the foreign investment approval and will provisionally freeze the amount until a Taiwan CPA issues the capital verification report and the bank updates the CBC's FXDS registration. Exchange into NTD for operational use is permitted only after capital verification, and any excess over the approved amount must be returned to the offshore remitter within the statutory time frame.For payment of licensing fees, hosting charges, and overseas advertising, the preferred clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 with CBC declaration codes 2710 for royalties or 2720 for technical service fees, supported by invoicing. For settlement of hardware procurement or large vendor contracts, a commercial documentary letter of credit, referencing MT700 and MT707, or a documentary collection can be used, but the bank will require the underlying purchase order, transport documents, and tax clearance. Because the core activity is software licensing, MT103 direct telex transfers from the Taiwan operational account are the most efficient rail; all outbound disbursements must be preceded by a tax-withholding calculation, such as 20% on royalties, and a valid purpose-code declaration.Outright deliverable FX forward contracts with an Authorized Foreign Exchange Bank for a period up to 12 months, supported by documented underlying trade or investment exposure.Cross-currency swaps limited to genuine hedging of long-term foreign-currency obligations, requiring a signed ISDA and CSA with a licensed AFEB and a board treasury resolution.Non-deliverable forwards are not permitted for onshore Taiwan entities without a physical delivery underlying; offshore NDF books may only be accessed through the AFEB's offshore branches, and settlement in NTD for speculative purposes is strictly prohibited.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Fintech EMI viability is Low. An offshore digital EMI, such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, or Payoneer, is not licensed by the Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission and cannot operate as an Authorized Foreign Exchange Bank. Such an EMI cannot hold NTD deposits, issue NTD clearing codes, or process CBC-mandated FX declarations for the WFOE. These EMIs can only serve as auxiliary international collection or payout channels for non-NTD currencies, but the statutory treasury layer, capital verification, tax clearance, and dividend remittance must run through a domestic tier-1 commercial bank. Offshore digital EMIs are therefore legally barred from functioning as the primary settlement rail for Taiwan-domiciled entities.
