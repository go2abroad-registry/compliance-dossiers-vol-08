Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, STA) with decentralized local tax service offices (各省/市税务局); primary portal is the nationwide e-Tax platform (电子税务局) accessible at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; invoice management and fiscal clearance are operated through the Golden Tax Phase IV system (金税四期) and the E-Invoice Service Platform (电子发票服务平台).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE as a limited liability company at the local Market Regulation Administration (市场监督管理局, SAMR), obtaining the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码), which functions as the taxpayer identification number for all tax purposes under the five-certificate integration policy (五证合一). Step 2: Within 30 days of the business license issuance, the WFOE must complete tax registration information supplementation through the e-Tax portal or the competent local tax service office, covering taxpayer status registration (税费种认定), legal representative and foreign investor details, registered address, banking account filing (银行账户报告), accounting policy filing (财务会计制度备案), and authorized finance officer (办税人员). Step 3: Elect VAT classification (small-scale taxpayer or general VAT taxpayer), apply for invoice quotas, and obtain digital certificate authentication (税务UKey or digital certificate) for issuance of fully digitalized electronic invoices (数电票). Step 4: For a foreign-invested enterprise, complete foreign direct investment (FDI) registration with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) through the receiving domestic bank before any capital injection or shareholder loan remittance; the bank reports the FDI information to SAFE. Step 5: If the WFOE intends to claim preferential EIT treatment (e.g., encouraged industry 15% rate, Hainan Free Trade Port 15% rate, or technology-advanced service enterprise 15% rate), file the corresponding qualification recordation and supporting documentation with the tax authority; cross-strait tax treaty benefits (Cross-Strait Agreement on Cooperation in Tax Collection and Mutual Administrative Assistance) must be claimed via the non-resident withholding agent or through annual self-adjustment procedures.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE (LLC) is a PRC resident enterprise (居民企业) under Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax (EIT) Law, taxed on worldwide income at the standard 25% EIT rate; it is not a pass-through entity for PRC tax purposes. Territorial scope: worldwide income is within the tax net, while foreign-source dividends received by the WFOE may be exempt under the indirect foreign tax credit participation exemption conditions (Article 24 EIT Law implementing rules); distributions of dividends from the WFOE to a non-resident Taiwan parent or shareholder are subject to cumulative withholding income tax at 10% on the gross dividend, subject to reduction to 5% where the Taiwan shareholder holds at least 25% of the WFOE equity and meets the cross-strait agreement's beneficial ownership test; interest, royalties and service fees paid to non-resident parties by the WFOE are subject to 10% withholding tax plus VAT and surcharges unless treaty or cross-strait agreement relief applies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE (LLC) is a PRC resident enterprise (居民企业) under Article 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax (EIT) Law, taxed on worldwide income at the standard 25% EIT rate; it is not a pass-through entity for PRC tax purposes. Territorial scope: worldwide income is within the tax net, while foreign-source dividends received by the WFOE may be exempt under the indirect foreign tax credit participation exemption conditions (Article 24 EIT Law implementing rules); distributions of dividends from the WFOE to a non-resident Taiwan parent or shareholder are subject to cumulative withholding income tax at 10% on the gross dividend, subject to reduction to 5% where the Taiwan shareholder holds at least 25% of the WFOE equity and meets the cross-strait agreement's beneficial ownership test; interest, royalties and service fees paid to non-resident parties by the WFOE are subject to 10% withholding tax plus VAT and surcharges unless treaty or cross-strait agreement relief applies. No tax-exempt zone status is available to the WFOE under this business scope except explicitly designated zone regimes (e.g., Hengqin, Nansha, Hainan) requiring substantive operations and encouraged-industry certifications.

Available election forms

China does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; however, the WFOE may exercise the following statutory or administrative elections: (i) VAT status election — the WFOE may elect small-scale taxpayer treatment (3% levy, currently reduced to 1% under the continuation policy through December 31, 2027) if annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000, or may voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer (6% financial-service rate / 13% goods rate) with input VAT crediting; registration as general taxpayer is mandatory upon annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000. (ii) Deemed taxation (核定征收) election for EIT in limited circumstances, requiring STA approval and being disfavored for financial services and licensing businesses. (iii) Preferential rate elections: encouraged industry 15% (Catalog of Encouraged Industries for Foreign Investment), Hainan Free Trade Port 15%, Technology Advanced Service Enterprise 15%, and small-and-low-profit enterprise 5% effective rate (annual taxable income not exceeding RMB 3,000,000). (iv) Hybrid financing instruments — characterization follows economic substance over form under Article 3 EIT Law and CaiShui [2009] No. 2, No. 46; there is no formal check-the-box election for equity/debt hybrid instruments, and thin capitalization (2:1 debt-to-equity safe harbor for non-financial entities, 5:1 for financial entities) plus interest deduction caps apply; hybrid mismatch rules under BEPS Action 2 are applied through the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR) and specific STA circulars, disallowing double-deduction or deduction-without-inclusion outcomes. (v) For outbound royalty and financing transactions, the WFOE may elect the deemed profit method or assessable income method for transfer pricing only under an advance pricing arrangement (APA) or intra-group service cost-sharing arrangement approved by the STA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All enterprises holding a business license must complete tax registration within 30 days of SAMR issuance irrespective of turnover; for VAT classification, annual taxable sales not exceeding RMB 5,000,000 qualify for small-scale taxpayer status (optional), while annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 mandate general VAT taxpayer registration; small-scale taxpayers enjoy monthly sales exemption up to RMB 100,000 (quarterly RMB 300,000) for VAT but such exemption is generally unavailable to financial/royalty income streams; the revenue-based financing fund's commission and royalty income is standard-rated at 6% VAT with input-VAT crediting available only to general taxpayers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: 0% VAT with refund of input VAT is available for exports of goods by the WFOE where the export transaction is a genuine sale to a non-resident party, goods physically leave the PRC customs territory, the export declaration (报关单) is in the WFOE's name, the export proceeds are repatriated, and the export tax refund declaration is filed by the statutory deadline (fourth VAT filing period of the following calendar year or within 210 days of export per STA Announcement 2013 No. 30, with the 180-day FX receipt requirement as the general bank/SAFE standard). Services: cross-border taxable services, including revenue-based financing, royalty/licensing and financial services supplied to non-resident enterprises for consumption wholly outside China, qualify for 0% VAT or VAT exemption under CaiShui [2016] No. 36 and subsequent STA circulars; the WFOE must evidence that (i) the service recipient is a non-resident enterprise without a permanent establishment in China receiving the service, (ii) the service is rendered entirely offshore (no domestic value-added activity), (iii) consideration is paid directly by the offshore counterparty to the WFOE's PRC bank account, and (iv) the cross-border taxable service exemption/zero-rating declaration (跨境应税行为免税/零税率申报) is submitted through the e-Tax platform; for royalty streams under a technology licensing sub-model, the zero-rating claim additionally requires recordal of the technology import/export contract with the relevant authorities and STA certification.

Since 2022-2024, China has implemented Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期) and fully digitalized electronic invoices (数电票, 'full-electronic invoices') nationwide. Issuance must occur exclusively through the STA's E-Invoice Service Platform (电子发票服务平台) with API-based real-time transmission, digital stamping, and mandatory fiscal clearance at the moment of issuance; no pre-printed blank invoice stock is permitted for newly established general taxpayers; invoices are generated with a unique invoice code and QR/status verification; real-time data on invoice flows, bank settlements, customs declarations, and FX receipts is cross-matched by the STA's big-data tax risk monitoring system; the WFOE must maintain API connectivity or use the e-Tax portal for invoice issuance, red-letter (credit note) processing, and monthly VAT reconciliation; failure to clear invoices in real time or using off-platform software triggers automated risk alerts and invoice quota freezes.

Export zero-rating and cross-border service exemption claims are subject to intensive audit by the STA Golden Tax IV data-mining system, which cross-matches export declarations, B/L data, VAT invoices, bank FX receipts, and SAFE remittance records. Penalties for false claims: (i) administrative clawback of the refunded VAT/EIT benefit plus interest at 0.05% per day; (ii) tax evasion penalty under Article 63 EIT Law and Article 52 of the VAT Implementation Rules of 50% to 500% of the tax underpaid; (iii) fraudulent export tax refund under Criminal Law Article 204 — threshold RMB 50,000 (primary liability for the entity; responsible officers also exposed) — with fines of 1 to 5 times the amount fraudulently refunded and imprisonment of up to life; (iv) loss of export tax rebate qualification for a minimum 36-month remediation period, suspension of invoice issuance, downgrade of taxpayer credit rating to D, and inclusion in the STA public blacklist with joint cross-agency restrictions (Customs, SAFE, market regulation, banking, and public security); (v) retrospective clawback may reach back a full 10-year statute of limitations for fraudulent acts, and restitution orders require repayment of refunds plus surcharges before any administrative reinstatement of the WFOE's export or invoicing status.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current-account items, including royalty distributions, trade disbursements, and day-to-day operational payments, subject to SAFE authenticity and conformity verification. Profit remittance by a WFOE requires audited local financial statements, evidence of corporate income tax settlement or prepayment, and board authorization; no implicit conversion quota applies to such remittances. Capital-account transactions, including foreign direct investment and cross-border financing, remain restricted and must undergo SAFE registration through a designated FX capital account. For cross-strait flows, Taiwan-sourced funds are treated as onshore cross-border remittances, and settlement can be conducted through designated banks using the Cross-Strait RMB/TWD clearing framework; however, principal convertibility is still governed by the current-account versus capital-account classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds in goods or services must be repatriated to a designated domestic foreign-exchange account and verified through SAFE's trade administration monitoring system. The statutory repatriation timeline is generally within 90 days of the customs declaration date for goods or the completion date for services, with a possible extension up to 180 days upon application for special trade terms. There is no mandatory sale-conversion ratio under the current current-account convertibility framework; the WFOE may retain 100% of export proceeds in foreign currency or convert to RMB at any time. For cross-border digital brands using e-commerce platforms, export proceeds may be aggregated and settled through a licensed cross-border payment institution, subject to underlying transaction authenticity and platform statement evidence.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium only when a licensed domestic partner bank or licensed cross-border payment institution is used; direct standalone operation by an offshore digital EMI is Low or Prohibited. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Airwallex, PingPong, or Payoneer cannot independently provide onshore CNY clearing or maintain domestic settlement accounts without obtaining a PBoC cross-border RMB payment institution license or partnering with a licensed bank. The legal constraints include the scope of the Payment Institution License, foreign ownership restrictions under the Payment Rules, SAFE registration requirements for cross-border aggregated settlement, and the prohibition of acting as a pool of customer funds without an authorized clearing contract.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For capital injection: the Taiwan shareholder must remit funds via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account held with a domestic bank, with the remittance narrative referencing 'Contribution of registered capital of WFOE' and the SAFE FDI registration code. The receiving bank verifies the FDI control number and updates the SAFE Foreign Direct Investment system post-receipt. For current-account inbound flows such as royalty or financing distribution payments from Taiwan, use MT103 with a service-trade purpose code and provide the Service Trade Contract registration reference and proof of withholding tax, if applicable. For revenue-based financing collections from local merchants, domestic ACH/RDFI rails are used; for cross-border merchants, licensed cross-border payment institutions can aggregate settlements subject to authentic transaction data and platform-backed evidence.The primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 for direct trade and service disbursements, with supporting invoices, contracts, and SAFE verification codes. For larger procurement or standby arrangements, a Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700 series) may be issued by the WFOE's bank against a confirmed purchase order or master supply contract. For RMB-denominated outbound disbursements, CIPS (Cross-border Interbank Payment System) can be used in parallel with SWIFT if the counterparty bank is a participant.
