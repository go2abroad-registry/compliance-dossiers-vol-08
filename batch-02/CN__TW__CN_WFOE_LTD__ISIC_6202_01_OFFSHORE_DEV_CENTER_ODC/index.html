Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office, commercial mailbox, or mail-forwarding-only address is rejected by PRC tax authorities and major commercial banks as the registered office of a WFOE engaged in the ISIC 6202 offshore software development center business because the company registry requires a lawful and verifiable operating address and tax registration presupposes proof of actual business premises. In government-sanctioned pilot Free Trade Zones, a nested address or sublease seat leased from an officially authorized incubator or zone administrator may be accepted for registration, but a physical presence with employees, equipment, and records is still required for a credible bank KYC profile. Banks will decline account onboarding if the address is an unqualified virtual office, and the State Taxation Administration may reclassify the taxpayer as having low commercial substance on-site. The prudent structure is a fully leased physical office with payroll, computing infrastructure, and management oversight.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Taiwan Ministry of Finance, National Taxation Bureau having jurisdiction over the entity's registered office, supported by the Ministry of Economic Affairs company registration system and the Ministry of Finance eTax Portal.

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor must first establish or register the Taiwan legal vehicle with the Ministry of Economic Affairs or the competent local authority, obtain foreign investment approval where applicable, complete company registration, secure a registered office, and complete business registration with the competent National Taxation Bureau before commencing taxable activities. The authority issues an eight-digit Unified Business Number (統一編號), which functions as the principal tax and business identification number. VAT registration is incorporated into business registration. Required materials generally include the approved company or branch registration documents, articles of incorporation, registered-agent and office information, responsible person's identification, foreign investor and ownership documents, capital verification or remittance evidence where required, business-scope information, and power of attorney for local filing. Additional registration may be required for employees, withholding tax, customs, import-export operations, and electronic invoices. The submitted entity profile is internally inconsistent: Taiwan does not use the Mainland China Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise legal form. A Taiwan subsidiary should ordinarily be registered as a company limited by shares or a company limited by guarantee or shares under Taiwan law, while a foreign company branch is registered as a branch. The Unified Business Number and tax registrations must therefore correspond to the legally approved Taiwan form.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Taiwan generally taxes companies as separate legal persons and does not provide a United States-style check-the-box election. A Taiwan company is subject to Taiwan corporate income tax on Taiwan-source income and, subject to applicable rules, offshore income under the alternative minimum tax regime. A foreign company branch is taxed on Taiwan-source income attributable to the branch. Partnerships and certain other transparent arrangements may receive flow-through treatment under their specific statutory rules, but a limited-liability company or subsidiary cannot elect ordinary pass-through treatment merely by filing a tax classification election. Taiwan's transfer-pricing, controlled foreign company, permanent establishment, and anti-avoidance rules may apply irrespective of the entity's accounting classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Ordinary businesses must generally complete business and VAT registration before commencing business; there is no general turnover threshold that permits an ordinary taxable company to operate without registration. Small businesses and certain exempt operators may be subject to special assessment or exemption rules, commonly associated with monthly sales thresholds of approximately NT$100,000 for goods and NT$50,000 for services, subject to statutory classification and current administrative rules. A software engineering and maintenance company should not assume that these small-business thresholds apply.

Taiwan's electronic invoice system is administered through the Ministry of Finance platform and approved software or service providers. VAT-registered businesses must issue government-compliant Uniform Invoices, with electronic invoicing required or phased in for specified taxpayers and transaction types. Electronic invoices generally require transmission to the Ministry of Finance electronic invoice platform within the prescribed reporting period, including buyer, seller, transaction amount, tax, invoice, and carrier information. Taiwan does not generally operate a universal real-time pre-clearance model equivalent to Brazil's NF-e or Italy's SDI for every invoice. Digital signatures, approved formats, platform transmission, retention, cancellation, allowance, and credit-note controls must follow Ministry of Finance requirements. Customs-linked electronic declarations apply separately to imports and exports.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a bifurcated convertibility regime. Current account items (software service exports, import of goods, travel, dividends remitted from lawfully accumulated profits) are de facto freely convertible upon bona fide documentary verification under SAFE Circular 28/2013 (Sale of Foreign Exchange for Current Account). Capital account items—including foreign direct investment (share capital injection), external debt, and outbound investment—remain subject to mandatory registration, approval, or filing with SAFE and/or the National Development and Reform Commission and the Ministry of Commerce. Profit remittance for a WFOE requires: (i) audited financial statements, (ii) evidence of corporate income tax final settlement (annual CIT filing, Chinese corporate income tax payment certificate), (iii) shareholders' dividend distribution resolution, and (iv) bank deferential verification of no unpaid statutory reserves (surplus reserve fund must be allocated at 10% of profit until reaching 50% of registered capital). Since the 2015–2019 SAFE reforms, repatriation of bona fide capital is permitted, but the parent must complete FDI Pay-Out Registration and the local bank handles the remittance against the validated dividend documentation. There are no residual quantitative dividend caps, but effective taxation (10% withholding on dividends unless reduced under the PRC–Hong Kong or PRC–Singapore tax treaties, or within the WFOE's own preferential bracket) acts as a de facto remittance cost. Restrictions may intensify under macro-prudential FTZ or capital-flow monitoring windows; treasury should maintain dual-scenario liquidity planning.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The statutory mandatory surrender obligation for export proceeds was formally abolished by SAFE's 2012 reform (implemented in SAFE Circular 2/2019 and Banking Guidance). Exporters are no longer required to convert foreign currency into RMB at a fixed ratio or within a statutory eight-day surrender window. However, all export proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic settlement foreign-exchange account (出口收入待核查账户 concept ceased, replaced by direct settlement account) within the legally permitted 'reasonable period'—interpreted administratively as proceeds should normally arrive within 180 days of the customs clearance date for goods trade; for service trade (software/ODC), the 'deemed receipt' standard is 90 days from service completion absent a contractual deferral. Proceeds may be retained indefinitely in a foreign-currency account at a designated bank, but banks must report to SAFE via the Foreign Exchange Monitoring System (as-verified settlement). The enterprise is free to sell the currency at market rates; however, SAFE applies the 'converted use' verification doctrine: retained foreign currency may only be used for approved current or capital expenditures, and any cross-border outflow requires matching underlying documentation. For software service exporters (ISIC 6202), each inbound SWIFT receipt must be matched to a 'Service Trade Foreign Exchange Payment/Receipt' transaction in the bank system, supported by a service contract, commercial invoice, delivery acceptance certificate, and proof of technology/software export type (online transmission or physical media), with payment exceeding USD 50,000 triggering the enhanced verification scale.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Overall feasibility rating: Medium-Low for direct CNY settlement; High as an auxiliary collection rail. Wise (TransferWise Ltd) is an EU-licensed EMI but does not hold a Chinese cross-border payment license and cannot open a Mainland Chinese CNY domestic account; it may only be used for inbound foreign-currency collection (USD/EUR) into a dedicated pass-through account held with a licensed Chinese bank partner (e.g., Citibank China or Bank of Shanghai joint-service model). Payoneer holds a State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) entity and operates via a licensed cross-border payment institution (Currencycloud or China UnionPay partner), but its China service is restricted to collection of e-commerce or freelance proceeds; a software ODC invoicing B2B services to offshore parents would face strict scope limitations because Payoneer's Chinese MPI partners impose a cap of USD 50,000 per aggregate receipt and require underlying e-commerce screen evidence—inadequate for a professional services WFOE. Legal constraint: under the 2021 'Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Cross-Border Payment Institutions' (PBOC), all cross-border RMB/foreign-currency settlement must be routed through banks or PBOC-opinionated licensed payment institutions; non-licensed EMIs cannot directly execute settlement in China. Therefore Wise/Payoneer should be used only as sub-aggregation for incidental small-volume transactions; the primary treasury rail must remain a domestic commercial bank SWIFT account.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Foreign shareholder is a Taiwan holding company or BVI/offshore entity—PBOC 'Sanctioned and High-Risk Jurisdiction' red flag

Root cause: Foreign shareholder is a Taiwan holding company or BVI/offshore entity—PBOC 'Sanctioned and High-Risk Jurisdiction' red flag

Defense evidence: (1) Certificate of Incorporation and Memorandum/Articles of Association of the Taiwan parent and each intermediate offshore entity; (2) Ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) declaration on PBOC Form A covering >25% natural persons, with passport/TAI Compatriot Permit copies; (3) Three-year audited financial statements of parent proving substantive operations and source of wealth; (4) Notarized explainer letter on commercial rationale (ODC labor arbitrage); (5) Proof that no entity in the chain appears on UN, EU, UK, OFAC, or PRC sanctions lists; (6) Taiwan parent's bank reference letter from a reputable Taiwan bank (Chinatrust, Cathay United, or Fubon) issued within 3 months.
High-volume outbound/inbound service trade flows with intra-group counterparties (parent-company service payments)—'Transfer Pricing & Circular Flow' AML overhang

Root cause: High-volume outbound/inbound service trade flows with intra-group counterparties (parent-company service payments)—'Transfer Pricing & Circular Flow' AML overhang

Defense evidence: (1) Cost-plus transfer pricing documentation (TPD) compliant with PRC State Administration of Taxation Bulletin 16/2017, including a full functional analysis and interquartile arm's-length benchmarking of the ODC profit margin; (2) Signed intra-group software development master agreement and each statement-of-work; (3) Delivery evidence (git repository reports, JIRA/Defect tracking cycle, UAT sign-offs) matched to the invoiced scope; (4) SAFE Service Trade Verification Form showing transaction code '228020' (Software Development, Implementation and Maintenance); (5) Full bank statements of WFOE proving no un-cleared dormant balance.
Rapid surge in inbound capital (capital injection exceeding RMB 10 million in one calendar quarter) without prior FDI registration

Root cause: Rapid surge in inbound capital (capital injection exceeding RMB 10 million in one calendar quarter) without prior FDI registration

Defense evidence: (1) FDI registration certificate issued by SAFE/State Administration for Market Regulation (FDI Quasi-Asset License); (2) Board resolutions of both parent and WFOE approving the capital increase with bank funding schedule; (3) Share transfer or contribution contract authenticated by a PRC notary; (4) Declaration of currency of contribution (USD/RMB) and utilization plan; (5) Undertaking not to circumvent the 'negative list' under the 2020 Foreign Investment Law; (6) Updated Articles of Association filed with SAMR.
Account opening by a Taiwanese or foreign legal representative without PRC social-security/visa evidence—immigration/tax resident mismatch flag

Root cause: Account opening by a Taiwanese or foreign legal representative without PRC social-security/visa evidence—immigration/tax resident mismatch flag

Defense evidence: (1) Valid work residence permit (外国人工作许可证 or 台港澳人员就业证/exempt scenario under statutory ODC foreign-director allowance), or (2) sufficient evidence of non-work nature: Board resolution appointing a local Chinese general manager with actual operational control; (3) Tax circular showing withholding tax filings on director fees; (4) Hotel/residence proof and entry stamps; (5) Written explanation that the foreign legal representative holds only a non-executive oversight role and a local senior manager will sign the account mandate.
Commercial bank internal policy flag for 'Section 311/China Cross-Border IT Services' if entity invoices for intangible software services—possible IP export review

Root cause: Commercial bank internal policy flag for 'Section 311/China Cross-Border IT Services' if entity invoices for intangible software services—possible IP export review

Defense evidence: (1) Technical classification certificate (软件产品登记 or IT assessment) demonstrating the WFOE performs R&D and not mere royalty collection; (2) Export software contract with the parent expressly stating deliverables pass limitations, no source-code IP assignment outside China, and title remains with WFOE unless transferred per PRC Technology Import/Export regulations; (3) CIT withholding clearance (clearance letter) on any bundled royalties; (4) Integrity pledge that service fees do not embed disguised dividend or royalty components; (5) IT inventory—servers physically located inside China with PBoC/MLPS level-2 filing where applicable.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) Setup—parent executes a capital contribution plan and wires USD/EUR from the Taiwan offshore bank (or HK/US intermediary bank) to the WFOE's 'FDI Capital Account' (资本金账户) at a domestic designated bank; the bank requires SAFE FDI Registration Number (previously 'FDI Card') and China's Bank for International Settlement reporting code to accept the credit. (2) Purpose/message code—SWIFT field 23B is CRED; Remittance Information field 70 must state 'INVESTMENT CAPITAL INJECTION FOR SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT COMPANY [WFOE NAME]—FDI REGISTRATION NO. XXXXX'; banks reject ambiguous descriptions such as 'loan' or 'payment'. (3) Under the 2019 SAFE outsourcing of FDI verification, the bank uses the 'Capital Project Information System' real-time match; funds are credited as restricted-category and cannot be released for working capital until the WFOE files the new FDI ER-Form and, if capital is fully paid and the entity is operational, converts to CNY using a spot transaction. (4) Timing—foreign exchange settlement of the foreign-currency capital account into RMB for operational rents, salary, and tax payments is allowed immediately, but each conversion must be tagged '资本金结汇' in the bank system, with the bank validating that the converted RMB is used for genuine business expenditures within 5 working days. (5) Parent must not inject via a third-party payer—the funding entity on SWIFT must match the registered investor exactly; any intermediary bank charge causes shortfall and requires supplementary injection amendment. (6) If equity injection is staged, each tranche must be evidenced to a capital audit; if postponed beyond the statutory 5-year business term, the bank may freeze un-utilized balance.Primary clearing rails for the ODC WFOE: (a) SWIFT MT103 direct instruction from the WFOE's domestic bank account to offshore suppliers and vendors (for software licensing renewals, cloud infrastructure (AWS/Google Cloud), contractor sub-payments, and Taiwan-parent administrative sub-chargebacks) executed under current-account 'Service Trade' purpose codes with SAFE verification; (b) Commercial Letters of Credit (at-sight / 30–60 days usance) are NOT applicable to software service outflows and should be avoided; rather purchase-order-backed telegraphic transfer (T/T) with enhanced due diligence is used for high-value IT procurement; (c) RMB cross-border clearing through CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) for trade settled in CNY with Hong Kong/Taiwan clearing participants (CTBC/Chinatrust as Taiwan participants); MT103 CNY routing uses the CNAPS CNY settlement code; (d) For outbound dividends, the rail is always SWIFT MT103 from the corporate RMB or FOREIGN-CURRENCY account through the bank to the Taiwan parent via a U.S. dollar correspondent (e.g., Citibank NY, JPM) or directly into a Taiwan NTD account via the bank's Taiwan cross-strait real-time clearing arrangement (银行间跨境人民币清算行 in Taipei—Bank of China Taipei Branch / TBK). Settlement integrity: each outbound > USD 50,000 triggers mandatory bank stamp on the 'Service Trade Exchange Settlement/Receipt Application', ACC comparison, and retention of contract number, tax withholding voucher, and completion evidence; transaction matching to the corresponding MIT/MOT (import/export) forms is not required for services but the WFOE must file an annual SAFE enterprise report via SAMR.Onshore CNY/USD deliverable Forward Contracts (SHIBOR-based, 1M–12M tenor, available to any current-account-verified WFOE from designated banks)Cross-currency Interest Rate Swaps (CCIRS) qualified under SAFE for a WFOE with both foreign-currency assets and liabilitiesOffshore CNH/USD Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDF) executed through the Taiwan parent treasury center at BOCI/HK interbank desks, matched to net CNH exposure (permissible so long as no PRC-funding leakage avoids SAFE verification)FX Swap (SPOT + FORWARD combination) for converting FDI capital inflows without residual open positionLimited vanilla FX Options (call/put) on CNY/USD transacted with domestic banks under the 2011 SAFE 'Cross-border structured FX' framework, provided the transaction is commercial-hedging—not speculative—and the underlying exposure exceeds the notional

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Overall feasibility rating: Medium-Low for direct CNY settlement; High as an auxiliary collection rail. Wise (TransferWise Ltd) is an EU-licensed EMI but does not hold a Chinese cross-border payment license and cannot open a Mainland Chinese CNY domestic account; it may only be used for inbound foreign-currency collection (USD/EUR) into a dedicated pass-through account held with a licensed Chinese bank partner (e.g., Citibank China or Bank of Shanghai joint-service model). Payoneer holds a State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) entity and operates via a licensed cross-border payment institution (Currencycloud or China UnionPay partner), but its China service is restricted to collection of e-commerce or freelance proceeds; a software ODC invoicing B2B services to offshore parents would face strict scope limitations because Payoneer's Chinese MPI partners impose a cap of USD 50,000 per aggregate receipt and require underlying e-commerce screen evidence—inadequate for a professional services WFOE. Legal constraint: under the 2021 'Measures for the Supervision and Administration of Cross-Border Payment Institutions' (PBOC), all cross-border RMB/foreign-currency settlement must be routed through banks or PBOC-opinionated licensed payment institutions; non-licensed EMIs cannot directly execute settlement in China. Therefore Wise/Payoneer should be used only as sub-aggregation for incidental small-volume transactions; the primary treasury rail must remain a domestic commercial bank SWIFT account.
