Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up capital is required by Colombian law. The incorporators freely fix the authorized, subscribed, and paid-in capital in the bylaws. Deferred cash contributions may be scheduled but the payment period cannot exceed two years. Colombian law does not require a blocked escrow account for S.A.S. formation; cash contributions are evidenced by bank transfer or deposit statements which must be retained in the corporate accounting file.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety inspection and certification by the local Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos; structural load verification and engineering clearance for racking, mezzanines or warehouse fixtures; municipal concept of land use for any physical premises; SG-SST occupational health and safety program with ARL affiliation under Resolución 0312 de 2019; workplace-occupancy clearance is required before employees or operators begin activities.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

DIAN - Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (Sede Electrónica: www.dian.gov.co; transactional portal: MUISCA)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must obtain a Colombian NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) by filing an application with DIAN through a locally appointed legal representative (apoderado) under a notarized power of attorney. The representative executes the pre-registration in MUISCA, obtains the NIT, then completes the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) enrolment to update tax status, e-invoicing qualification, and economic activity classification (e.g., ISIC 4791). Non-resident shareholders are allowed; no minimum capital is required for the S.A.S.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Colombia has no check-the-box election. The S.A.S. is automatically a domestic corporation subject to Colombian income tax on its worldwide income as a separate entity. Eligible taxpayers may elect under Law 1943/2018 and Decree 1625/2016 to apply either the standard corporate income tax regime or the SAR (Simplified Taxation Regime) where turnover limits and activity tests are met. No domestic hybrid mismatch election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general VAT registration threshold: every individual or entity engaged in selling taxable goods or services in Colombia is required to register in the RUT before commencing activities. A reduced threshold applies only to certain informal retailers; for standard SAS e-commerce and marketplace operations, immediate RUT registration is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of physical goods from Colombia are subject to VAT rate 0% (excluded from VAT) if they are legally exported in accordance with the Colombian Customs Statute and the goods leave the country within the allowed timeframe. Services provided exclusively for use outside Colombia, including digital marketplace support services, may be zero-rated as export services where the beneficiary is not located in Colombia and the requirements of Decree 1625/2016 and DIAN Concept 01745/2018 are met. For cross-border e-commerce under an Amazon FBA model, physical goods exported from Colombia qualify for 0% VAT provided the exporter receives the export proceeds within the statutory exchange settlement periods and all customs formalities are completed.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DEX) accepted by DIAN
  • Bill of Lading or Airwaybill evidencing definitive export
  • Commercial invoice issued in the name of the foreign buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Recibo Electrónico de Compensación) issued by Colombian bank, or certificate/dispatch document proving foreign currency entering through authorized foreign exchange market
  • FBA shipment, warehouse receiving documentation, and customs broker proof for goods delivered to Amazon fulfilment centers

Mandatory e-invoicing (factura electrónica) is phased in by DIAN. The SAS must issue electronic sales invoices through ICT providers approved by DIAN. The invoice XML must be validated, transmitted, and registered with DIAN in near real-time via web services. Invoices must include CUFE or CUDE code, DIAN assigned validation status, and QR code. Debit/credit notes must follow the same clearance mandate. Exports and FBA marketplace sales require electronic export invoice and electronic invoice with customs export annexes.

DIAN may disregard zero-rating where export documentation, foreign exchange repatriation, or service-use tests are incomplete or contradictory. In fraud cases, the authority reverses the 0% treatment, demands the 19% VAT plus default interest, and imposes penalties of up to 200% of the omitted tax amount under Article 648 of the Colombian Tax Statute, along with income tax recapture and potential criminal liability for tax evasion (Law 1762/2015, Article 24). Exporters must retain the complete electronic dossier for at least 6 years for audit and potential clawback procedures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian peso (COP) operates under a managed free-floating exchange-rate regime. Current-account operations — export collections, import payments, services, royalties, and dividend/profit remittances — are freely convertible in the official exchange market, but all export proceeds must be channeled through authorized intermediaries of the foreign-exchange market (IMCs) or a compensation account registered with Banco de la República. Capital-account operations — foreign direct investment (FDI), external loans, and portfolio inflows — are also convertible, subject to registration with Banco de la República through the IMC at the time of channeling. Profit remittance abroad is lawful where (i) the paid-in capital was registered as foreign investment, (ii) distributable earnings were approved by the shareholders' meeting and the Colombian CIT/withholding obligations were satisfied, and (iii) the remittance is executed through an IMC with the corresponding exchange declaration. The operative hurdles are the documentary burden of FDI registration, source-of-funds verification for offshore shareholders, and the strict prohibition on settling export-generated flows through unregistered channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Pursuant to Decree 1735 of 1993 and Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83, export proceeds from goods must be reintegrated (brought into the exchange market) within six (6) months from the export declaration (DMA), extendable to twelve (12) months with Banco de la República prior authorization; service-export proceeds must be reintegrated within six (6) months from the provision of the service. A one-hundred-percent (100%) channeling obligation applies to the foreign currency generated by exports; however, the exporter is not necessarily required to convert the foreign currency into pesos because proceeds may lawfully be applied to extinguish the exporter's own foreign-currency liabilities (e.g., import payments or FBA logistics obligations) through a declared compensation. Amazon FBA settlements represent aggregated export collections that must be mapped to the underlying DMAs and entered in the 'reintegro' declaration filed by the IMC. Failure to reintegrate on time, under-declaration, or use of unregistered offshore wallets for export collections subjects the S.A.S. to exchange fine procedures, customs/DIAN penalties, and exclusion from the IMC relationship.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (the central bank of the Republic of Colombia), acting jointly with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) as financial-market supervisor and with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Tourism (MINCIT) and the DIAN customs/foreign-trade agency as co-regulators of cross-border flows. The foreign-exchange regime is set out in Law 9 of 1991, Decree 1735 of 1993, Decree 2555 of 2010, and Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83 of Banco de la República.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High with material legal constraints. Stripe may be used as the Colombian-incorporated S.A.S.'s e-commerce collection/payment gateway for card transactions, settling in COP through local acquiring banks, but it is not an authorized Intermediario del Mercado Cambiario (IMC) and cannot lawfully replace the statutory channel for Amazon FBA export proceeds. Airwallex is a non-resident global EMI: appropriate for non-FX working-capital disbursements (cloud services, contractor payments), but unusable as the sole collection point for export settlements. Payoneer, frequently used by Amazon sellers, may serve only as a complementary wallet whose balances are swept to a local Tier-1 IMC or a compensation account registered with Banco de la República. Nequi and Daviplata (SEDPEs) offer local e-wallet rails with transactional limits and cannot function as corporate treasury accounts. Recommended design: the local Tier-1 IMC account is the legal anchor for reintegro; Stripe, Payoneer, and Airwallex remain auxiliary rails excluded from export-reintegration unless a compensation-account registration is in place.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant sequence for a non-resident capital contribution: (i) the shareholder executes the S.A.S. capital-payment minutes and, if demanded by the bank, a notarized contribution act; (ii) funds are remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's own bank account (clearly identifying the investor as remitter) to the S.A.S.'s Tier-1 IMC account in Colombia, denominated in COP or USD; (iii) the IMC files the Declaración de Cambio under the foreign-direct-investment capital category and registers the investment with Banco de la República's international-investment records; (iv) after crediting, the bank issues the exchange certificate for the UBO/audit file. Purpose codes should reference 'Inversión extranjera directa — aporte de capital'; the wire message must not commingle personal expenses or third-party transactions. Verification package: MT103 confirmation, exchange declaration, updated Chamber of Commerce excerpt showing paid capital, and the shareholder registry book.For operating disbursements, the S.A.S. should use the following rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers in USD/COP from the local IMC account to Chinese, U.S., or regional suppliers, with the invoice and import declaration (Declaración de Importación) referenced in the wire narrative; (ii) sight commercial letters of credit (L/C at sight) issued by the Tier-1 bank for high-value FBA inventory, protecting against documentary risk; (iii) direct card-based or account-to-account payouts to Amazon for FBA logistics, storage, and advertising, which are treated as service/import payments and must be reconciled against Amazon charges; and (iv) exchange-market offset (compensation) where USD export proceeds are applied to extinguish supplier obligations without passing through COP, declared as compensation to the IMC. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium-High with material legal constraints. Stripe may be used as the Colombian-incorporated S.A.S.'s e-commerce collection/payment gateway for card transactions, settling in COP through local acquiring banks, but it is not an authorized Intermediario del Mercado Cambiario (IMC) and cannot lawfully replace the statutory channel for Amazon FBA export proceeds. Airwallex is a non-resident global EMI: appropriate for non-FX working-capital disbursements (cloud services, contractor payments), but unusable as the sole collection point for export settlements. Payoneer, frequently used by Amazon sellers, may serve only as a complementary wallet whose balances are swept to a local Tier-1 IMC or a compensation account registered with Banco de la República. Nequi and Daviplata (SEDPEs) offer local e-wallet rails with transactional limits and cannot function as corporate treasury accounts. Recommended design: the local Tier-1 IMC account is the legal anchor for reintegro; Stripe, Payoneer, and Airwallex remain auxiliary rails excluded from export-reintegration unless a compensation-account registration is in place.
