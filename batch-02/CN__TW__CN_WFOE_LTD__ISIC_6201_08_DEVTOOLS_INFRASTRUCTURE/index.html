Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); the official online electronic filing portal is the China Electronic Tax Administration e-Tax platform (Electronic Tax Bureau), accessible through the STA and provincial tax service bureaus. For this CN_WFOE_LTD, Taiwan (TW) is the cross-strait investor/offshore jurisdiction; unless a Taiwan branch or permanent establishment is separately registered, Taiwan is not the registration jurisdiction for this China-domiciled WFOE.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the business license containing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) for all People's Republic of China tax filings, including Corporate Income Tax (CIT), Value-Added Tax (VAT), and withholding tax. Step 2: Within 30 calendar days from the date of business license issuance, complete the tax registration information confirmation in the provincial Electronic Tax Bureau platform; the system automatically synchronizes the SAMR registration to the tax authority and activates the tax registration file. Step 3: Register the entity's VAT taxpayer status with the competent tax service bureau; the default is small-scale taxpayer if annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000, but the WFOE may apply for general VAT taxpayer status to claim input VAT credits and support export zero-rating. Step 4: If the ultimate parent or Taiwan investor will receive China-source dividends, royalties, interest, or service fees, the WFOE as payer must complete a non-resident enterprise filing in the electronic tax bureau and, where required, obtain/record the non-resident taxpayer identification number for withholding CIT and VAT on cross-border payments. Step 5: For export zero-rating of cloud/software services, complete the cross-border taxable services zero-rating/exemption record-filing with the competent tax authority by uploading the service contract, non-resident recipient evidence, and anticipated payment terms. Step 6: Maintain a dedicated tax account and compliance folder for invoice issuance, general ledger, export documentation, and foreign-exchange receipts (FIRC/SAFE records).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the China WFOE is a resident enterprise of the People's Republic of China and is taxed on worldwide income at the entity level under the Corporate Income Tax Law; it is not an eligible pass-through entity. VAT is collected separately on taxable supplies; the WFOE is not a tax-exempt zone entity.

Available election forms

None for US-style check-the-box; China has no domestic check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election under normal Chinese tax law. Available statutory elections are: (a) election to become a general VAT taxpayer when below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold; (b) election to remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer using simplified levy rates when eligible; (c) election to apply the reduced small-and-low-profit CIT regime if the statutory thresholds for annual taxable income, asset value, and headcount are met; (d) record-filing election for cross-border taxable services zero-rating/exemption. No hybrid mismatch election exists.

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover threshold for tax registration: every WFOE must register with the STA within 30 days of SAMR incorporation. The mandatory economic threshold triggering general VAT taxpayer registration is annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (five million yuan); below that threshold the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer. Domestic cloud/software services are generally subject to 6% VAT, while domestic goods sales are generally subject to 13% VAT.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a controlled but progressively managed convertibility regime. Current account transactions, including software subscription exports, service fees, and dividend remittances, are broadly convertible and do not require SAFE pre-approval after the underlying real-business authenticity is verified by a designated FX bank. Profit repatriation by a WFOE to overseas shareholders is permitted once annual statutory audit, tax clearance, and a board dividend resolution are completed; a withholding tax of 10% applies unless a double tax agreement lowers the rate. There is no mandatory surrender or fixed conversion ratio for export proceeds, but all trade-related FX receipts must be repatriated into a domestic FX settlement account within the statutory period (normally 90 days from export or service performance) and can be converted into RMB under the bank booking model. Capital account convertibility remains constrained: inbound FDI requires SAFE foreign exchange registration and a dedicated capital account; funds credited to the capital account can be converted to RMB only for transactions within the WFOE's business scope and are prohibited from being used for equity investment or securities speculation unless specifically approved. Outbound capital flows for acquisitions or lending are subject to SAFE approval and project filing. Therefore the WFOE should treat its domestic RMB books as operationally current-account convertible, but must maintain strict documentation of the OFDI/FDI reporting trail for any capital account movement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE regulations, all foreign export receipts must be repatriated to a designated bank account in the People's Republic of China. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio; the WFOE may keep the earnings in foreign currency or convert to RMB. For goods exports, statutory collection is generally 90 days from the customs declaration date, unless a longer credit term is reported to SAFE. For service exports (software/cloud), receipts must be collected in accordance with the underlying contract and presented to the bank with invoice and service evidence. Receipts not collected within 90 days (or the reported expected date) require a revised trade credit report and supporting documents.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% profit-seeking enterprise income tax, plus a separate 5% retained-earnings surcharge on undistributed earnings if profits are not distributed; the effective aggregate cost structure for a Taiwan WFOE is therefore 20% corporate tax plus either the 5% retained-earnings surcharge or the 21% non-resident dividend withholding tax at distribution.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

21% withholding tax on dividends paid or distributed to non-resident shareholders (including non-resident individuals and non-resident profit-seeking enterprises) under Income Tax Act Article 88 and Article 26; dividends paid to a resident corporate shareholder are generally not subject to 21% withholding, while resident individuals receiving dividends are integrated into the personal income tax system with the imputation credit mechanism abolished and no separate dividend withholding at source.
