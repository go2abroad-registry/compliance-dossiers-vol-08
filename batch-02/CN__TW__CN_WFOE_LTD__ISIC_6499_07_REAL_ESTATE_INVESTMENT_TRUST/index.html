Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For a WFOE engaged in ISIC 6499 real estate investment trust activities, a virtual office or commercial mail forwarding address is not acceptable. The PBOC, SAMR, and tax authorities require a physical business premises that corresponds to the registered address, with retained original documents (incorporation record, articles, lease of real property, shareholder registers), a dedicated company seal, and identifiable personnel. Using a registered agent-only address triggers a presumption of false registration under the PRC Company Law and can lead to cancellation, serious credit punishment, and bank account closure. A real estate trust business must demonstrate local operational substance, such as an executed commercial office lease for at least one year and the presence of at least one compliance officer, to secure banking licenses and investment permissions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT export treatment applies to goods physically exported from China that receive customs release, and to designated cross-border services consumed wholly outside China, including certain technology, R&D, and business process services. For a REIT, domestic real estate rental and disposal income is not zero-rated; only cross-border supplies satisfying the 'fully consumed outside China' test qualify.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration (export Bill of Entry equivalent) with electronic customs release confirmation
  • Bill of Lading / sea waybill / airway bill evidencing cross-border carriage
  • Commercial invoice showing export price and delivery terms
  • Packing list
  • Bank export proceeds settlement record / Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or equivalent SAFE foreign exchange collection receipt
  • Export VAT special invoice or procurement invoice for input VAT refund support

Mandatory use of the STA-authorized electronic invoice platform. VAT invoices must contain a digital signature, unique invoice number and QR code, and invoice data is reported to the STA in near real time. Red-blue invoice reconciliation and automatic clearance controls are required before any refund or credit is granted.

Unverified or fraudulent export zero-rating claims are retrospectively clawed back by the STA, with daily late-payment interest and penalties under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, generally 50% to 500% of the evaded tax; deliberate false export VAT refund claims also expose senior officers to criminal liability under PRC criminal law.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime with substantial administrative control over the capital account. The WFOE's current account, comprising service fees, dividends from operations, trade payments, and operating expenses, is generally convertible on a bona fide basis, but every payment must pass through a designated foreign exchange bank that verifies documentation and files transaction codes with the SAFE monitoring system. The capital account—equity injection, shareholder loans, outward transfers, offshore investment, and liquidation proceeds—is not freely convertible and requires either SAFE registration, prior approval, or a specific quota. Profit remittance to a Taiwan shareholder is permitted only after the annual statutory audit is completed, the foreign investor's share of distributable profits is determined using Chinese GAAP, and deemed tax obligations are settled; the bank will require the SAFE FDI registration certificate, audited financial statements, and the board's profit distribution resolution. Export surrender obligations are currently non-mandatory in percentage terms but receipts must be repatriated within 180 days after the export declaration date, and enterprises may either keep foreign exchange or voluntarily sell to banks. Cross-strait remittances receive enhanced regulatory review because of the political sensitivity, and banks must ensure that the intended economic purpose matches the reported purpose code.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated to China through bank channels within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration. There is no statutory mandatory conversion percentage; the WFOE may either retain the proceeds in its foreign currency current account or voluntarily sell them to a designated bank. Repatriation must be reported under the Trade Monitoring System with matching electronic customs data, invoices, and logistics records.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMIs and non-bank payment institutions are legally barred from providing onshore RMB settlement accounts, foreign exchange capital accounts, SAFE registration services, or profit remittance processing for a mainland China WFOE. Such EMIs may support ancillary cross-border e-commerce collection in certain open pilot zones, but they cannot act as the primary treasury bank for FDI injections, capital verification, tax clearance, or dividend repatriation under China's capital account controls. All treasury-grade foreign exchange flows must be executed through a licensed designated onshore bank connected to SAFE's monitoring systems.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must wire the equity capital through a SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's foreign currency capital account at a designated onshore bank, using the SAFE FDI registration code and the purpose code reflecting foreign direct investment capital injection. The bank will execute real-name verification, check the negative list for restricted industries, and perform foreign exchange settlement strictly in accordance with the registered capital item. After the funds arrive, the WFOE obtains a capital verification report from a licensed CPA firm and files the FDI registry update.
