Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital: an S.A.S. fixes subscribed capital freely in the by-laws, payable within two years of incorporation (Law 1258/2008, Art. 4). No escrow account is mandated. Recommended subscribed capital for bitumen wholesale: COP 200,000,000 (≈ USD 50,000) to support Swift LC/TT settlement lines.

Funding & unlock path: Certificado de Bomberos (fire safety certificate) covering warehouse and drum storage zones per NFPA 30 and Colombian RETIE; structural load inspection certificate for storage areas (Decree 945/2019); occupational health and safety program (SG-SST) registered under Decree 1072/2015 with mandatory ARL affiliation and worker exposure controls for bitumen fume and heat during handling.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital: an S.A.S. fixes subscribed capital freely in the by-laws, payable within two years of incorporation (Law 1258/2008, Art. 4). No escrow account is mandated. Recommended subscribed capital for bitumen wholesale: COP 200,000,000 (≈ USD 50,000) to support Swift LC/TT settlement lines.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN), through the Muisca and DIAN Virtual Portals

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must engage a Colombian legal representative or local attorney in fact to register at the DIAN through the Muisca portal. The applicant must upload the entity's constitutive documents (bylaws or equivalent), evidence of legal existence from the home jurisdiction with apostille or consular legalization, power of attorney, and proof of a local address for tax domicile. Assignment of a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) is issued once the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) is approved, including the applicable tax responsibilities such as IVA and ICA (industry and commerce tax) where required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity must engage a Colombian legal representative or local attorney in fact to register at the DIAN through the Muisca portal. The applicant must upload the entity's constitutive documents (bylaws or equivalent), evidence of legal existence from the home jurisdiction with apostille or consular legalization, power of attorney, and proof of a local address for tax domicile. Assignment of a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria) is issued once the RUT (Registro Único Tributario) is approved, including the applicable tax responsibilities such as IVA and ICA (industry and commerce tax) where required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box regime exists in Colombia. A Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada (S.A.S.) is mandatorily treated as a tax-resident corporation under Colombian tax law, taxed on worldwide income at the entity level. No general hybrid mismatch or entity classification election is available; some elective mechanisms exist only for specific regimes such as holding companies (sociedades holding colombianas) under certain conditions and for consolidated or group filing regimes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Persons making taxable sales or rendering taxable services in the previous calendar year exceeding 3,500 UVT are required to register and be responsible for IVA (2024 UVT equals COP 47,065, thus a threshold of approximately COP 164,727,500). Importers and exporters must generally register regardless of turnover where they operate in international traffic.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods are taxed at 0% IVA, including the export of containerized drummed or bagged bitumen. Zero rating applies when the sale meets the legal definition of export under the Customs Regime (Decree 1165/2019), including definitive export from Colombian customs territory, payment in foreign currency where applicable, and the goods leaving Colombia. Offshore services rendered to foreign users may also be zero-rated when performed entirely outside Colombian territory or used exclusively abroad, subject to the 'offshore services' requirements in the IVA statute.

  • Declaración de Exportación (DEX) accepted by DIAN customs
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing the goods leaving Colombian customs territory
  • RUT and export invoice (factura de exportación) with CUFE/CUDE validation
  • Packing list and weight/volume certificate for containerized bitumen
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (certificado de cobro de divisas /Foreign Exchange Certificate) as required by Banco de la República
  • Documentary evidence of receipt in the destination market where applicable under the DEX regime

Under DIAN Resolution 165/2023 and related rulings, all registered persons must issue electronic invoices (factura electrónica de venta) through DIAN-authorized software or a free DIAN tool. Invoices must be transmitted to DIAN before issuance and must obtain prior validation through a numerical authorization, status 'validated' and the assigned CUFE/CUDE digital identifier. Digital stamping and mandatory real-time or pre-clearance validation apply to sales, export invoices, and support documents; API-based integration with DIAN's electronic invoicing environment is mandatory for in-house or ERP billing systems.

Under Article 481 and related provisions of the Colombian Tax Statute, exporters must substantiate the actual export through DIAN customs records and evidence of foreign exchange repatriation. Restitution of the global differential (IVA difference at 19%) plus interest is assessed when zero-rated exports are grounded in false declarations, evasive documentation schemes, or non-compliant customs formalities. Penalties include a fine equivalent to 10% or 20% of the invoiced value for irregularly claimed export refunds (Articles 647 and 648 of the Tax Statute), potential indictment for tax fraud under Criminal Law 599/2000, the cancellation of export certification, and the retrospective clawback of VAT refunds for up to the statute of limitations period (generally 5 years; extended to 10 years for unregistered transactions or non-declared operations).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Colombian Peso (COP) is fully convertible under a managed-float regime. Current-account transactions (trade, services, transfers) are free from prior license requirements, and capital-account entries (foreign direct investment, external loans, portfolio flows) are admitted subject to mandatory ex-post registration with the Banco de la República through authorized IMCs. No capital-control hurdles block routine trade: the practical constraints are the TRM (Tasa Representativa del Mercado) daily conversion rule, the FX declaration forms, and the 5% withholding? - No, I'm drifting into tax. Profit remittance hurdle: dividend/profit remittances by a non-resident shareholder have no FX quota or prior permit; they require (i) the foreign investment to be registered in the Registro Internacional de Inversiones (RII) and (ii) a Formulario No. 4/No. 3 (as applicable) declaration at the paying IMC. Dividend tax withholding (10% standard for non-residents) applies at the bank level but is a tax, not an FX, restriction. Conversion to USD for overseas distribution is executed at the day's TRM without administrative approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Colombia applies a 'channeling' (canalización) rather than a surrender regime. There is no mandatory conversion ratio into COP: exporters may retain USD/EUR proceeds in SFC-approved foreign-currency accounts (cuentas de compensación) or local accounts, provided the funds are channeled through an IMC and declared. Statutory timeline: proceeds from goods exports must be received and channeled within 12 months from the date of the export (fecha de la declaración de exportación), under Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83 (the former six-month term was extended and standardized to 12 months by Banco de la República). For bitumen (HS 2713) containerized wholesale, the importer's SWIFT remittance must be matched, by wire reference, to the DEX (Declaración de Exportación) filed at DIAN; de minimis relief applies to shipments whose FOB value does not exceed USD 10,000.

Central bank supervision: Banco de la República de Colombia (the Central Bank of Colombia); foreign exchange (FX) regulation is issued by its Junta Directiva under Law 9 of 1991 and Circular Reglamentaria DCIN-83. Enforcement is shared with the DIAN (Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales) for trade declarations and export-proceeds checks, and with the Superintendencia Financiera de Colombia (SFC) for supervised Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario (IMCs).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW (rating: 2/5) for the operating treasury of the Colombian S.A.S. Offshore EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, Payoneer) are not authorized Intermediarios del Mercado Cambiario and cannot legally open COP treasury accounts or receive/deliver export-import proceeds that must be canalizadas through an IMC under Law 9/1991 and Circular DCIN-83. An offshore EMI's local partner bank can only support retail-level transfers; any commercial-use conversion or FX settlement outside a registered IMC creates a sanctionable 'canalización por fuera del mercado cambiario' violation (Resolution 3019/2021, DIAN). EMIs are viable solely for immaterial pre-incorporation expenses and intra-group operational overhead outside Colombia; the logical treasury design isolates such flows from the statutory FX channels.

Annual corporate income tax return (Declaración de Renta y Complementarios) Annually according to the DIAN tax calendar based on the last two digits of the company's NIT, normally due in August of the year following the taxable year $0 USD Late filing generates an extemporaneous filing penalty of 5% per month or fraction of month on tax payable, subject to a 100% tax cap, plus default interest; continued non-filing may lead to DIAN official assessment and additional penalties.
Annual beneficial owner report (Información de Beneficiario Final) Annually on the date fixed by DIAN in the official tax calendar; a no-change confirmation is also mandatory $0 USD Failure to file, incomplete filing, or incorrect UBO information may be penalized with fines up to 15,000 UVT per report under the Colombian Tax Code, in addition to potential registry negative consequences.
