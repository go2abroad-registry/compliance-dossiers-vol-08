Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For a WFOE engaged in study-abroad placement and immigration visa consultancy, a virtual office or commercial mailbox is not operationally viable and will be rejected by Chinese tax authorities, local SAMR, and Chinese banks for account opening under AML/KYC because the industry requires a physical licensed premises for customer reception, contract execution, and regulatory inspection. Additionally, the PRC Social Insurance Law and the local education bureau's licensing criteria mandate a fixed business address with lease registration and actual personnel presence. Therefore, a physical office lease with at least a 12-month term and a rent invoice is strongly advised.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) limited company. Declared capital must be deposited in a Taiwan bank account and a bank-issued certificate of deposit must accompany the incorporation filing; the capital may be denomicated in NTD or foreign currency and must equal the amount approved by the MOEA.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局), official portal https://www.chinatax.gov.cn, e-tax filing platform https://tpass.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

Within 30 days of obtaining the business license, the WFOE must register with the local State Taxation Administration office via the e-tax platform, obtaining the Unified Social Credit Identifier (USCI) which serves as the statutory TIN/VAT ID. Required filings include articles of association, domicile proof (lease or ownership certificate), bank account information, legal representative and financial officer identification, and de facto office photographs. Post-registration, the taxpayer must activate VAT general taxpayer or small-scale status and apply for e-invoice quota through the e-tax portal.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise; taxed on worldwide income at 25% corporate income tax; no pass-through treatment) — Within 30 days of obtaining the business license, the WFOE must register with the local State Taxation Administration office via the e-tax platform, obtaining the Unified Social Credit Identifier (USCI) which serves as the statutory TIN/VAT ID. Required filings include articles of association, domicile proof (lease or ownership certificate), bank account information, legal representative and financial officer identification, and de facto office photographs. Post-registration, the taxpayer must activate VAT general taxpayer or small-scale status and apply for e-invoice quota through the e-tax portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise; taxed on worldwide income at 25% corporate income tax; no pass-through treatment)

Available election forms

None; PRC law does not provide check-the-box entity classification elections. A foreign-invested enterprise is always treated as a resident enterprise. The only election available is VAT taxpayer status: small-scale taxpayer (3% levy, simplified method) versus general taxpayer (6%/9%/13% rates with input credit), based on annual taxable turnover; no hybrid mismatch elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Basic VAT registration is mandatory upon incorporation without turnover threshold; the mandatory general taxpayer threshold is annual taxable turnover exceeding RMB 5,000,000. Small-scale taxpayer status applies up to RMB 5,000,000 annual turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may apply 0% VAT to cross-border services exported to Taiwan if (1) it is a VAT general taxpayer, (2) the recipient is an overseas (Taiwan) enterprise or individual, (3) the service—including international student placement, study-abroad consulting, and immigration visa advisory—is supplied entirely for overseas consumption, and (4) the service falls within the 'advisory services' category listed in CaiShui [2016] No. 36 Annex 4. The tax authority requires that the service contract clearly states the overseas recipient, that payment is received from abroad, and that evidence of the service being consumed outside China (e.g., enrollment confirmation, visa grant notice, immigration application record) is retained.

  • Cross-border service contract or agreement between the Chinese WFOE and the Taiwan-side client
  • VAT ordinary invoice or special invoice issued for the zero-rated exported service
  • Inbound foreign exchange settlement receipt (国际收支涉外收入申报单) or cross-border RMB payment voucher from the Taiwan client
  • Overseas consumption certificate: enrolment confirmation, study/immigration visa approval record, or formal service delivery report proving the service is consumed outside China

China mandates fully digitalized e-invoicing (数电票) with real-time synchronization to the State Taxation Administration's consolidated e-invoice platform. General taxpayers must issue all invoices digitally through the e-tax portal; no physical fiscal stamping or manual clearance is required except for specific industries under approved pilots. Enterprise ERP integration through official API is available and standard practice for WFOEs.

Fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger retrospective clawback of VAT refunds within 10 years, plus late payment surcharge at 0.05% per day, a fine of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax under the PRC Tax Administration Law, and criminal tax evasion liability if the evaded amount exceeds RMB 50,000 or the entity has prior tax violations. Tax authorities will also examine the authenticity of the overseas consumption evidence and may re-characterize the services as taxable domestic consultancy if the service is partially performed in mainland China.
