5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For a fixed-place-of-business branch: mandatory registration from the first day of operations, with no turnover threshold. Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Article 7 of the Value-Added and Non-Value-Added Business Tax Act zero-rates goods exported from Taiwan and services provided in Taiwan that are actually used or consumed outside Taiwan. For the PCT patent prosecution agency, the services must be supplied to a non-resident client and the substantive benefit/use of the service must occur outside Taiwan; services that support only Taiwan domestic filings remain taxable at 5%. The zero-rating must be evidenced by contemporaneous contracts, invoices, work product, and payment records.

  • Taiwan Customs Export Declaration (export declaration number and status) for any physical goods component
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export movement from Taiwan
  • Commercial export invoice referencing the zero-rated Article 7 Business Tax classification
  • Foreign buyer purchase order or service contract identifying the non-resident client, place of performance, and place of use
  • Bank foreign exchange collection statement, inward remittance advice, or foreign exchange settlement receipt proving repatriation of export proceeds to Taiwan

Taiwan requires VAT-registered businesses to issue Uniform Invoices for taxable sales. Authorized e-invoice issuers operate through the MOF e-Invoice Integration Service Platform by transmitting invoice data via web or Turnkey XML application interface; the platform validates the business number, invoice number, and aggregate data, and archives invoices for automated cross-checking with the periodic VAT return. Taiwan does not currently impose an invoice-by-invoice real-time fiscal counter-stamping or digital anti-fraud stamping module; controls are centralized invoice number allocation, controlled procurement of paper Uniform Invoices from authorized printing plants, encrypted Turnkey transmission, and electronic return matching.

If zero-rating is claimed without the required export and exchange substantiation, the tax authority will claw back the 5% output tax and disallow the related input tax credits, adding interest from the original filing date. Under Article 51 of the Business Tax Act, underpayment may subject the taxpayer to a penalty of one to three times the underpaid tax; forged export documents, false invoices, or fraudulent refund claims can trigger higher evasion penalties, criminal referral, and extension of the tax assessment period under the Tax Collection Act.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed float with substantial current account convertibility but strict capital account control. For the Taiwan-owned WFOE, current account transactions, including service fees for patent prosecution and IP licensing, are freely convertible upon submission of authentic commercial documents, tax vouchers, and customs declarations. Capital account items such as registered capital injection, shareholder loans, and overseas investment are not freely convertible; they require prior SAFE registration and are monitored through a dedicated capital account (FDI) at the bank. Profit remittances are permitted only after annual audits, tax clearance, and verification of retained earnings; the remittance is considered a current account item and is processed without a mandatory conversion surplus. Exchange surrender requirements have been relaxed over time, yet all foreign exchange receipts must be repatriated to a domestic SAFE-designated bank distance, although the enterprise may hold foreign currency or convert it voluntarily. Taiwan is a separate customs territory under the WTO framework, and all financial flows between Taiwan and mainland China are treated as cross-border, requiring cross-strait remittance documentation, enhanced due diligence, and additional compliance screening by the designated bank. As a result, a highly structured liquidity environment is in place where each capital flow must align with a registered commercial purpose and be supported by a closed loop of contractual, invoice, and payment evidence.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods trade export proceeds, the WFOE must repatriate the full invoice amount to a domestic SAFE-designated bank within 90 days after the date of export declaration. For service trade including patent prosecution and IP licensing, proceeds must be repatriated according to the contractual payment schedule, but no later than the agreed settlement date. There is currently no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds; the WFOE may freely retain foreign currency in its current account or sell it to the bank at the prevailing market rate. All receipts must be logged in the bank's foreign exchange monitoring system and reconciled against the customs, tax, and contract documentation. Any extended delay or non-repatriation without an approved extension will trigger a compliance inquiry and potential administrative penalty.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) acting under the delegated authority of the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, or Revolut Business are not licensed by SAFE or the PBOC to provide primary corporate bank accounts, hold client funds, or facilitate capital account transactions for a mainland Chinese WFOE. These platforms lack the statutory registration and custody framework required to receive FDI capital injections, verify export proceeds, or support SAFE-controlled current account conversions. They may be used only as ancillary non-bank payment channels for micro outbound disbursements outside the statutory banking system, but even then, legal risk remains because all cross-border trade settlement must flow through a SAFE-designated bank. Therefore, the overall viability rating for using any fintech EMI as the main treasury rail is Low, and the treasury blueprint must rely exclusively on Tier-1 domestic commercial banks for all regulated FX and clearing activity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must route via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 through a designated SAFE-approved bank in mainland China. The remitter in Taiwan must include the SAFE registration certificate number and the appropriate purpose code for foreign direct investment equity injection in the payment narrative of the wire transfer. The WFOE must complete the FDI filing with the local SAFE branch before the capital transfer is initiated. Once the funds arrive in the foreign exchange capital account, the amount is blocked until a certified public accountant provides a capital verification statement. After capital verification, funds may be transferred to the RMB basic settlement account for operational deployment under the 'payment after confirmation' regime, which requires that each disbursement be matched against the approved business scope of the WFOE. Any misuse of capital account funds is treated as a severe compliance violation and may result in fines under PRC foreign exchange regulations.For patent office fees, translation costs, and foreign legal services, the preferred clearing rail is a direct SWIFT MT103 message initiated from the WFOE’s domestic bank to the beneficiary’s bank, carrying the proper current account purpose code for professional and legal services or royalties and license fees. For high-value annual multi-jurisdiction PCT portfolio settlements, the WFOE may instruct its bank to issue a Commercial Letter of Credit through a confirming bank, or to arrange a Documentary Collection. Because the business is service-oriented, open account settlement with electronic invoice reconciliation is acceptable for low-risk counterparties; however, official patent office fees must be remitted to the designated official bank account of each patent office or settled through the patent office’s electronic payment gateway using the national payment reference. All outbound payments must be supported by the underlying service contract, the tax certificate for withholding where applicable, and a detailed invoice or fee schedule to satisfy SAFE’s current account audit trail.Deliverable FX forward contracts with a domestic SAFE-designated bank, used to hedge USD, TWD, or EUR receivable and payable exposures arising from service fees and patent office disbursements, provided the underlying transaction is genuine and the settlement is backed by commercial documentation.Cross-currency interest rate swaps and FX swaps for longer-dated exposures, subject to demonstrated hedging need and annual SAFE registration where the notional exceeds the voluntary filing threshold.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are generally not permissible for onshore WFOE entities outside a Free Trade Zone account framework; even within a FTZ, explicit SAFE authorization is required, and such authorisation is rarely granted to patent prosecution agencies. Therefore, the treasury blueprint does not include NDFs as a standard hedging instrument.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore fintech EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, or Revolut Business are not licensed by SAFE or the PBOC to provide primary corporate bank accounts, hold client funds, or facilitate capital account transactions for a mainland Chinese WFOE. These platforms lack the statutory registration and custody framework required to receive FDI capital injections, verify export proceeds, or support SAFE-controlled current account conversions. They may be used only as ancillary non-bank payment channels for micro outbound disbursements outside the statutory banking system, but even then, legal risk remains because all cross-border trade settlement must flow through a SAFE-designated bank. Therefore, the overall viability rating for using any fintech EMI as the main treasury rail is Low, and the treasury blueprint must rely exclusively on Tier-1 domestic commercial banks for all regulated FX and clearing activity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: NT$200 million in annual related-party transactions (approximately USD 6.7 million) generally triggers the mandatory preparation of Local File and Master File; for businesses operating at a loss or in certain designated industries the threshold can be lower.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan has no separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code; however, the tax authority applies a real substance requirement in the actual management place test and in the practice under Taiwanese corporate and tax law. A patent prosecution agency claiming treaty benefits or preferential incentive regimes must maintain substantive economic presence in Taiwan, including permanent offices, qualified professionals, full-time staff, board meetings, and core IP prosecution decision-making (CIGA) physically taking place in Taiwan.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Article 110 of the Income Tax Act, an administrative penalty of up to three times the underpaid tax is imposed; Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act criminalizes the conduct with imprisonment for up to five years, detention, or a fine.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed or took part in the false return are personally liable to restore unpaid taxes, assets may be frozen, travel may be blocked under Article 24 of the Tax Collection Act if arrears exceed NT$2 million, and criminal imprisonment may be imposed for fraud.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may disregard the taxpayer's structure, re-determine the arm's length profit using the most reasonable method, and impose a penalty of up to three times the understated tax amount; failure to submit required documentation may also trigger a separate fine.

Director personal liability: If the TP disclosure failure is deliberate and causes tax underpayment, directors can be held to have violated the tax law and become personally liable for underpaid taxes, interest, and penalties; they may also be blacklisted from leaving Taiwan and criminally prosecuted.
Shell Company / DTA Abuse Without Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty relief, re-characterization of the transaction as a sham, inclusion of the income in Taiwan corporate income, and an additional penalty of up to three times the evaded tax; serious cases are referred as tax fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly and severally liable for the aggregate tax, interest, and penalties after piercing the corporate veil; personal travel bans, asset seizure, and imprisonment apply where the shell arrangement is proven fraudulent.
