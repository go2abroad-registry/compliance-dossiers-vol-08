A pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is rejected by the local SAMR and tax authorities for this B2B enterprise SaaS WFOE. The registered address must be a physical premises in mainland China that is leased or owned and capable of receiving official correspondence. Free-trade-zone virtual-address schemes are available only for approved industrial categories and not for a standard WFOE generating ordinary VAT and CIT filings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The People's Republic of China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime under PBOC/SAFE. The current-account item is broadly convertible for bona fide trade and services: the WFOE may receive B2B SaaS subscription revenue from overseas clients and, after bank verification of contract, invoice, and service delivery, may retain the foreign exchange in a current-account deposit or settle it into RMB. Capital-account transactions, however, are not freely convertible. FDI capital contributions from the Taiwan parent must enter through a SAFE-registered capital account and cannot be settled into RMB until the bank confirms the underlying investment documents and intended use under the payment-to-use negative list. Prohibited uses include entrusted loans, repayment of onshore RMB loans from non-affiliates, equity investment outside the approved scope, and non-owner-occupied real estate except with SAFE exception. Profit remittance is lawful but gated: the WFOE must complete the annual statutory audit, file corporate income tax, settle tax liabilities, document distributable profits, and present a board resolution to the remitting bank. The bank will verify that the dividend is not persistently funded by hedge or circular flows and will apply the applicable dividend withholding tax for the Taiwanese shareholder before outward remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE trade and services foreign exchange administration, export and software service-trade receipts must be repatriated into mainland China within 180 days after customs declaration for goods or after service completion/invoice date for B2B SaaS services, unless SAFE has approved offshore retention. There is currently no mandatory conversion ratio: 100% of collected foreign exchange may be kept in a current-account foreign currency deposit or sold to a designated bank for RMB at market rate. The bank must complete trade verification using the executed software contract, commercial invoice, access/delivery evidence, and VAT/tax documentation before settlement.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low viability as a primary treasury rail. The PRC legal framework reserves foreign-exchange settlement, payments, and current/capital account clearing to licensed designated banks. Offshore digital EMIs (including Wise B2B and Stripe Billing) cannot open a recognized domestic settlement account in the WFOE's name, cannot provide SAFE-compliant FX purpose codes, and would cause a violation of repatriation if used as the final receiving rail. The configured Stripe_Billing and Wise_B2B rails may be used only as front-end collection gateways for the offshore group, and funds must ultimately be wired to the mainland WFOE's designated bank through SWIFT with correct trade documentation. A licensed third-party payment institution may be engaged for small-value current-account collection, but it must clear through a domestic bank and cannot replace the corporate banking relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must transmit paid-in capital by SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI capital account at the designated bank, citing the purpose of payment as 'FDI registered capital injection for CN_WFOE_LTD' with the applicable SAFE FX transaction code. The WFOE must complete FDI information registration before the remittance, and the bank must verify the business license, articles of association, board resolution, and FDI registration certificate. Upon receipt, the bank records the credit as paid-in capital and, if required by the bank or by a future lender, a Chinese CPA capital verification report is issued. Settlement of the capital into RMB must follow the payment-to-use negative list and be applied only to payroll, cloud infrastructure, software development, taxes, and ordinary operating expenses, not to entrusted loans, equity investment, or real estate outside the approved business scope.For legitimate outbound SaaS disbursements—cloud provider charges, overseas subcontractors, Taiwan parent license fees, and technology royalties—the WFOE must instruct a SWIFT MT103 remittance from its designated bank and submit the service contract, invoice, tax withholding certificate, and proof of actual service delivery for SAFE service-trade verification. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are not suitable for ordinary SaaS subscription transactions because the service is intangible; they may be used only in hybrid software distribution arrangements that involve physical media, master license ownership, or organized goods movement.
