Competent corporate registry

Cámara de Comercio (local Chamber of Commerce) acting through the Registro Único Empresarial y Social (RUES) online portal, with the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) issuing the NIT tax identification number

Governing statute

Ley 1258 de 2008 (Sociedad por Acciones Simplificada), Articles 1-46; supplementary application of Código de Comercio (Decreto Ley 410 de 1971), Articles 110-120 on merchant registration; Ley 1429 de 2010 on formalization incentives

Formation / constitutional filings

Documento Privado de Constitución registered with RUES; Notary-attested public deed (escritura pública) is required only if real property or in-kind contributions exceeding specified thresholds are made

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum subscribed or paid-up capital is required for an S.A.S. under Ley 1258 de 2008. Capital may be zero or symbolic, may be paid in cash or in kind, and the payment schedule may extend up to two years with deferred payments recorded in the deed. No escrow account or bank deposit is mandated

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate issued by the Cuerpo Oficial de Bomberos based on inspection of the physical site; structural load inspection by a certified civil engineer for any warehouse; workplace occupational health clearance reviewed by the ARL and aligned with the Ministry of Labour health and safety standards (Resolution 0312 of 2019)

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN) — Colombia's National Tax and Customs Authority; principal portal: www.dian.gov.co; transactional filing system: MUISCA (Modelo Único de Ingresos, Servicio y Control Automatizado) at www.muisca.dian.gov.co. Legal basis: Estatuto Tributario (Decreto 624 de 1989, as amended by Ley 2277 de 2022) and Decreto Único Reglamentario 1625 de 2016.

E-government filing portal

For a locally incorporated S.A.S.: (1) upon registration of the company at the Cámara de Comercio, the system automatically assigns a NIT (Número de Identificación Tributaria, 10 digits plus verification digit) and issues the preliminary RUT (Registro Único Tributario); (2) the legal representative must enter MUISCA with the Cámara credentials within 30 business days to confirm the RUT data, register the tax responsibilities (IVA 'responsable' and income-tax payer), and arrange the company's digital certificate (firma electrónica) with an DIAN-certified certification authority (e.g., GSE, Certicámara, Andes SCD) for e-filing and e-invoicing; (3) any foreign parent or non-resident shareholder needs no separate Colombian tax ID unless it is itself engaged in Colombian-source activities; the S.A.S. acts as its own taxpayer. For a foreign entity without a Colombian branch or establishment that must still obtain a NIT (e.g., VAT on digital sales or import customs clearing through a broker): appoint a resident legal representative and a tax agent (agente de retención), file the RUT application with apostilled or legalized documents translated into Spanish, and obtain a NIT through DIAN/MUISCA; the resulting 'RUT de responsable del IVA' allows the foreign entity to file periodic VAT returns and issue electronic invoices.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Colombian S.A.S. is a resident, separate legal entity taxed on worldwide income at the entity level; the standard corporate income tax rate is 35% for fiscal years 2023-2024 and 30% from fiscal year 2025 (per Ley 2277 de 2022). No pass-through treatment is available to an S.A.S.; the company also bears Colombian VAT as a 'responsable del IVA' for its local e-commerce retail sales, and for exports it acts as the exporter of record for VAT zero-rating purposes.

Available election forms

Colombian law contains no US-style 'check-the-box' regime; entity-level taxation is compulsory for an S.A.S. The statutory elections relevant to this business profile are: (i) 'Régimen Simple de Tributación' (SIMPLE) — an optional simplified composite tax regime available to eligible businesses with prior-year gross income not exceeding 100,000 UVT (COP 4,979,900,000 at UVT-2025 of COP 49,799); election is exercised in the first two months of the fiscal year (Form 2593) and replaces the ordinary income-tax/ICA model with a single aggregate rate (2.2% to 14.5% depending on the gross-income band) while simplifying VAT handling for retail operations; (ii) VAT refund election — the exporter may elect between refund (devolución de saldos a favor) and offset (compensación) of the export VAT surplus against other national taxes (arts. 850-857 E.T.); (iii) optional accelerated-depreciation and amortization elections under the E.T. for e-commerce logistics assets; (iv) the hybrid-mismatch rules of arts. 49-52 E.T. are mandatory anti-avoidance adjustments without elective relief; and (v) once the S.A.S. exceeds the statutory size thresholds, it must file transfer-pricing documentation and may adjust its results by 'opción de ajuste integral' (inflation adjustment) only when the law expressly permits it — generally unavailable for recent-year corporations.
