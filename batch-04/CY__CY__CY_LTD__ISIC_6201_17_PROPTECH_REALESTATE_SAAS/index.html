Paid-in capital rule: None – Cyprus private limited companies may be incorporated with any nominal share capital (commonly EUR 1,000 divided into EUR 1 shares); no minimum paid-up capital and no escrow account requirement for general PropTech SaaS activities.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus; principal e-portal is the 'Ariadne' Tax Information System (TIS) at taxportal.mof.gov.cy. Corporate incorporation (HE77 Private Limited Company) is administered by the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DRCOR) via efiling.drcor.mcit.gov.cy. VAT administration sits within the same Department of Taxation (Nicosia HQ).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the HE77 Private Limited Company at DRCOR and obtain the HE registration number. Step 2: Within 60 days of incorporation, file the Tax Department registration application (TD2001) through Ariadne/TIS to obtain the eight-digit Tax Identification Code (TIC/TIN) and open the corporate tax file, which governs annual IR4 returns and employer PAYE filings. Step 3: File VAT Form 101 (Application for VAT Registration) electronically before commencing taxable activity where the €15,600 threshold will be exceeded, or immediately on starting intra-Community acquisitions/distance sales; the VAT registration number takes the format CY prefix plus eight or nine characters with check digit. Step 4: For foreign-owned structures, appoint a Cyprus tax-resident secretary and certified public accountant/auditor, and file the statutory auditor appointment form (HE30). Non-resident sellers without a Cyprus fixed establishment are not ordinarily required to obtain a local TIC unless they hold Cyprus real estate or elect the OSS scheme, in which case a Cyprus VAT/OSS registration is mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus tax-resident Ltd (managed and controlled in Cyprus) is taxed on worldwide income at the uniform 12.5% corporate rate, with a participation exemption for qualifying dividend and capital gain income and no sub-national corporate tax. The unit is treated as a standalone taxable person for VAT and cannot be treated as a pass-through entity under domestic law.

Available election forms

Cyprus has no US-style check-the-box election; entity classification is statutory. Available elections include: (1) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) on new qualifying equity contributions at the ten-year government bond yield plus 50 basis points; (2) IP-Box regime election granting an effective 80/92.5% deemed deduction on qualifying software/patent income subject to the OECD nexus limitation; (3) group loss relief election (75% affiliation, Cyprus/EU/EEA resident); (4) VAT group registration election under the Cyprus VAT Law for closely linked persons; (5) Union and Non-Union One-Stop-Shop (OSS) scheme election for B2C electronically supplied services, shifting VAT compliance to destination Member States; and (6) election to claim capital allowances and R&D-enhanced deductions on SaaS platform development costs.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. Cyprus has been an EU member since 2004 and adopted the euro in 2008; therefore there is no FX licensing, no prior-exchange-control approval, and no central bank permission requirement for currency conversion or cross-border transfers. Profit remittance is unrestricted: dividends paid by a Cyprus LTD to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Cypriot withholding tax, and dividends to EU/EEA parent companies are protected under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive. All residual crisis-era capital controls imposed in 2013 were fully lifted by 2016 and no standby restrictions remain. The only residual obligation is statistical: banks report cross-border flows to the CBC under its International Transactions Reporting System (ITRS), and transfers above EUR 10,000 in cash are subject to the EU cash control regulation (EU) 2018/1672. Convertibility is therefore de jure and de facto unrestricted for a standard low-risk PropTech SaaS treasury.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Cyprus imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion into EUR, and no time limit for repatriating or holding export proceeds. A Cyprus LTD may retain USD, EUR, GBP, or multi-currency operating balances in CBC-licensed banks without any FX conversion obligation. The only compliance duties are indirect: export receivables must be recorded in the audited financial statements for the 12.5% corporate income tax calculation, and transfer pricing documentation must support intra-group international service flows.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), operating within the European System of Central Banks and the ECB Single Supervisory Mechanism for credit institutions; the CBC supervises licensed banks, monitors balance-of-payments statistical reporting, and enforces EU anti-money laundering directives through its AML Directive issued under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Law (L.188(I)/2007, as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM-HIGH as a supplementary rail, LOW-HIGH as a primary settlement rail. EEA-licensed and passported Electronic Money Institutions (PSD2-authorized, e.g., Wise, MangoPay or CBC-licensed EMIs) may be used for low-value cross-border disbursements, Stripe payout concentration, and US ACH receipts, but the primary treasury and statutory settlement accounts must be held with a CBC-licensed credit institution to satisfy AML supervision, corporate banking covenants, and Cyprus tax residency evidence. Pure offshore/non-EEA digital EMIs without a correspondent banking relationship will be rejected by Cypriot banks in account relationships, and crypto-denominated rails or stablecoin settlement are not permissible for operating or escrow flows.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Certified extract from the Cyprus Registrar of Companies; UBO declaration filed under the Cyprus Beneficial Ownership Register framework; an unambiguous corporate structure chart covering every intermediate entity to the natural person; certified passport copies of the UBO; and a signed source-of-wealth statement with corroborating evidence (historical tax returns, sale-of-shares contracts, liquidation distributions, or bank reference letters).
Sanctions exposure — UBO, shareholders, or material counterparties connected to US, EU, UN, or UK sanctions lists, or to jurisdictions with negative AML/CFT reputational standing.

Root cause: Sanctions exposure — UBO, shareholders, or material counterparties connected to US, EU, UN, or UK sanctions lists, or to jurisdictions with negative AML/CFT reputational standing.

Defense evidence: Completed sanctions/PEP questionnaires for all directors and UBOs; automated OFAC/EU/UN consolidated list screening results; current tax residence certificates (for Cyprus: T.D. 101 or equivalent); bank reference letters from the UBO home bank covering at least three years; and a written board undertaking confirming that no sanctioned proceeds or designated persons will benefit from the treasury accounts.
Source-of-funds mismatch — inbound share-capital injection disproportionate to the stated SaaS revenue model or early-stage financial statements.

Root cause: Payment-flow red flags — collection model that could mask third-party funds (e.g., landlord/tenant deposits passing through the corporate account without a client-money framework).

Statutory registry non-compliance — gaps in annual filings, unreported director/UBO changes, or inactive registry status of the Cyprus entity or its parent.

Root cause: Statutory registry non-compliance — gaps in annual filings, unreported director/UBO changes, or inactive registry status of the Cyprus entity or its parent.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections flow through TARGET2/SEPA for EUR transfers and the SWIFT correspondent network for non-EUR. For equity injection: SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank to the Cyprus entity's EUR account with field 70 clearly stating 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — CY_LTD — board resolution dated DD/MM/YYYY'. SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) may be used where the shareholder is in the EEA and the EUR amount is within SEPA limits; USD or GBP injections require MT103. The CBC/EU balance-of-payments statistical framework requires the bank to classify the transaction under the BPM6 direct investment equity category (the ECB '5D' code) or shareholder loan category ('5E') for related-party debt. The bank will request: (i) certified board resolution authorizing the increase or call of share capital; (ii) updated share register and share certificates; (iii) UBO declaration and source-of-funds proof; (iv) if the injection exceeds USD 100,000 or originates outside the EEA, the full evidentiary defense dossier; and (v) confirmation that no cash above EUR 10,000 is used, per EU cash control rules. No CBC license, FX approval, or convertibility permit is required at either inbound or outbound stage.The base clearing rail is SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT/instant SEPA) for all EUR disbursements inside the EEA, covering vendor payments, payroll, cloud infrastructure, and Irish/Polish tech subcontractors at zero FX cost. For non-EEA and non-EUR payments (e.g., US cloud providers, international marketing agencies, USD-denominated debt service), the rail is SWIFT MT103 with standard debtor/beneficiary information and invoice references. Commercial Documentary Credits (MT700 LC at sight or confirmed) are permissible for high-value physical-asset or licensed-software acquisitions, but the low-indentity SaaS model defaults to open-account MT103 transfers supported by supplier invoices. US-side subscription receipts from Stripe Billing and ACH Direct may be aggregated through a US partner bank or authorized EMI and periodically swept to the Cyprus EUR account, using a USD account held at the Cypriot bank to reduce conversion slippage. All outbound flows above bank-specific thresholds are captured by the CBC's International Transactions Reporting System; the corporate client should submit an annual anticipated payment schedule so the bank can assign correct balance-of-payments statistical codes.EUR/USD Outright Forward (delivery-based) — executed with the on-boarding Cypriot bank under ISDA 2002 or European master agreement; suitable for hedging USD-denominated SaaS revenue into EUR; typical minimum notional EUR 250,000 for a corporate relationship.FX Swap (spot-forward or tom-next) — available through the bank treasury desk for short-dated liquidity matching between USD inbound receipts and EUR operating costs; recorded under CBC statistical reporting and not subject to any convertibility restriction.FX Option (plain vanilla or barrier) — permitted to protect a defined USD/EUR budget rate while retaining upside from favorable conversion; requires ISDA/CSA master agreement with margin posting consistent with the bank's credit policy.Cross-Currency Swap — appropriate for longer-dated, USD-denominated venture debt or intra-group loan exposures where principal and interest are USD and operating revenues are EUR; requires a formal board-approved FX risk policy.Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) — permissible only for residual emerging-market exposures such as CNY, KRW, INR, or other restricted-convertibility currencies where the Cypriot bank's global desk provides synthetic deliverability; EUR/USD NDF is neither available nor required because EUR/USD is fully convertible. Any NDF usage requires explicit board sign-off, confirmed economic substance, and documented hedge accounting treatment.

Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: A Cyprus private limited company must appoint an auditor and undergo a statutory audit unless it qualifies for the small-company audit exemption under Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113. The exemption is available for small companies meeting low-turnover and small-balance-sheet criteria, generally turnover not exceeding EUR 50,000 and balance sheet total not exceeding EUR 100,000, provided no shareholder holding 10% or more of the issued shares requests an audit.
