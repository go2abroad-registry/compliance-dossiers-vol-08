Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (T.A.C. Portal: https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy)

E-government filing portal

For a Cyprus private limited company incorporated under the Companies Law, the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) is automatically issued by the Registrar of Companies upon incorporation and is recorded with the T.A.C. A foreign entity establishing a fixed establishment or permanent establishment in Cyprus must register with the T.A.C. using Form T.A.C. 001 (for corporate income tax) and Form VATA (for VAT) through the T.A.C. online portal; non-resident entities supplying goods or services in Cyprus are generally required to register for VAT from their first taxable transaction, with the option to appoint a tax representative for VAT compliance. Supporting documents include certificate of incorporation, memorandum and articles of association, registered office address, ultimate beneficial ownership declarations and, where applicable, a certificate of residence from the foreign tax authority.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not operate an elective check-the-box regime comparable to the U.S.; companies are taxed at entity level at 12.5% corporation tax. Available elections include: group VAT registration; VAT grouping under the Cyprus VAT Act; the notional interest deduction (NID) election for new equity; the intellectual property box regime election under s.37 of the Income Tax Law; tonnage tax election for qualifying shipping entities; foreign tax credit pooling election; and group relief elections allowing transfer of losses among Cypriot group companies. Cyprus applies EU ATAD anti-hybrid rules, and hybrid mismatches are neutralised through statutory mandatory provisions rather than elective mechanisms.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€15,600 annual taxable turnover for taxable persons established in Cyprus; non-established taxable persons generally must register from the first taxable transaction with no threshold for supplies not covered by the Union/Import One Stop Shop, and distance-selling of goods to Cypriot consumers exceeding €10,000 per calendar year triggers Cypriot VAT registration unless the OSS regime is elected.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Cyprus zero-rates the export of goods to destinations outside the EU and certain supplies treated as exports, including goods exported within 90 days of supply subject to documentary evidence. For a cross-border payment gateway service, zero-rating may apply under the EU place-of-supply rules where the service is provided to a taxable person established in another member state (B2B cross-border service; reverse charge applies in the recipient's state) or to a non-EU taxable person or non-taxable consumer outside the EU, in which case the place of supply is outside Cyprus and the supply falls outside the scope of Cypriot VAT (effectively 0% with entitlement to input tax deduction if attributable to taxable activities). To claim zero-rating, the supplier must hold valid evidence of the recipient's VAT identification number, business status and location, and, for physical exports, the goods must leave the EU within the statutory period.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / Electronic Single Administrative Document)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or Bank Credit Advice showing receipt of foreign currency
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Contract or Purchase Order
  • Proof of Delivery and Export Cargo Movement Records

As of the current rules, Cyprus has no general mandatory B2B real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping system for electronic invoices. Electronic invoicing is permitted and requested for all B2G relationships under applicable EU e-invoicing standards (EN 16931/PEPPOL), and Cyprus is preparing for the EU-wide ViDA e-invoicing framework which will introduce mandatory structured e-invoicing and reporting for cross-border B2B transactions in due course. The T.A.C. provides an electronic filing platform for VAT returns and toll collections; a transaction-level real-time clearance mandate is not yet applicable.

The T.A.C. may retrospectively claw back zero-rated input VAT and impose assessments if an exporter fails to maintain and produce the required export and foreign currency repatriation documentation within the statutory period or if the goods are diverted to free circulation within the EU. Penalties include additional tax up to 100% of the tax underdeclared, interest at 6% per annum or such rate as imposed by law, and monetary penalties up to €10,000 for incorrect returns, with higher penalties and imprisonment for fraudulent claims. The limitation period for assessments is generally six years; this may be extended to ten years in cases of fraud or deliberate suppression.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility for EUR and all freely transferable currencies under EU Treaty Article 63 and the liberalized Cypriot Exchange Control Law. Profit remittance is unrestricted; dividend distribution is subject to corporate solvency and audited-profit conditions, not to FX approval, provided the payer maintains proper transfer pricing and economic substance. Compliance friction points arise from CBC balance-of-payments statistical reporting, EU sanctions screening, and anti-money-laundering / counter-terrorist financing filing obligations. Capital or current account transactions do not require prior central bank authorization unless they involve sanctioned jurisdictions or trigger sectoral restrictions under EU/EU financial sanctions regulations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies for export receipts. Export proceeds in EUR or foreign currency may be retained offshore or in domestic accounts indefinitely. Banks apply a 10-business-day reconciliation period for trade settlement to satisfy AML reporting if transaction values exceed EUR 100,000 or the DNFBP risk-matrix threshold. Reporting is statistical only for balance-of-payments purposes; no mandatory conversion to EUR is imposed.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza Kyprou)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Regulated EU/EEA Electronic Money Institution (EMI) passported into Cyprus: Medium-High viability, subject to CBC registration under the Payment Services Law (L. 219(I)/2018) and SEPA access. Offshore digital EMIs without a Cypriot or other EU/EEA branch license: Low viability; they cannot directly access SEPA or CBC clearing infrastructure and will be treated as unlicensed payment institutions, creating AML, regulatory, and reputational non-compliance for the PSP's settlement channel. The preferred structure is a Cyprus-licensed EMI or a Payment Institution authorized by the CBC with dedicated client-segregated accounts.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate income tax on the chargeable income of Cyprus tax-resident companies and, for non-resident companies, on Cyprus-sourced income only; no municipal or provincial corporate surcharges.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Cyprus imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Cypriot company to either resident or non-resident shareholders. The 17% Special Defence Contribution applies only to Cyprus-domiciled resident recipients of dividends and is therefore entirely outside the scope of a foreign-owned cross-border payment gateway. Distributions of gateway profits to non-resident shareholders can be made with no Cypriot WHT layer and no DTA filing requirement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return for a financial period must be filed by the fifteenth month after the accounting period end, e.g. a 31 December 2025 year-end is due by 31 March 2027. Final self-assessed tax is payable on the same due date. During the year, provisional self-assessment instalments are required: 75% by 31 July and 25% by 31 December.

Box 13 – Gross turnover Total global acquirer, gateway, interchange, clearing and settlement fee revenue before chargebacks and float adjustments. All Visa/Mastercard direct-acquiring and Fedwire clearing services attributed to the Cyprus entity are included; intra-group settlement set-offs require fully documented contracts. Late filing: automatic administrative fine of EUR 100 plus EUR 50 per day of continuing default, capped at EUR 500 per return. Late payment: default interest of 2% per annum accrues on unpaid tax for each month or part of month outstanding, with distraint, court enforcement and statutory travel restrictions against the responsible director in cases of prolonged default.
Box 20 – Chargeable income Accounting profit adjusted for capital allowances, non-deductible expenses, exempt income and transfer-pricing adjustments. Cyprus residents pay tax on worldwide income. Intercompany fees paid to a foreign parent (brand, software, back-office) must satisfy the arm's-length standard and be evidenced before submission. Late filing: automatic administrative fine of EUR 100 plus EUR 50 per day of continuing default, capped at EUR 500 per return. Late payment: default interest of 2% per annum accrues on unpaid tax for each month or part of month outstanding, with distraint, court enforcement and statutory travel restrictions against the responsible director in cases of prolonged default.
Box 35 – Tax payable Chargeable income multiplied by 12.5%; credits for foreign tax and withholding tax are claimed in adjacent boxes. Where the parent is a DTA/EU resident, attach the foreign Tax Residence Certificate to claim the foreign tax credit; absent a valid TRC the credit is refused. Late filing: automatic administrative fine of EUR 100 plus EUR 50 per day of continuing default, capped at EUR 500 per return. Late payment: default interest of 2% per annum accrues on unpaid tax for each month or part of month outstanding, with distraint, court enforcement and statutory travel restrictions against the responsible director in cases of prolonged default.
Box 51 – Declaration and signature Certification by an authorised officer or certified tax agent that the return is complete and accurate. Signature must be by a Cyprus-resident executive director or a licensed accountant; a purely nominee signature from foreign shareholder-directors is a clear substance red flag. Late filing: automatic administrative fine of EUR 100 plus EUR 50 per day of continuing default, capped at EUR 500 per return. Late payment: default interest of 2% per annum accrues on unpaid tax for each month or part of month outstanding, with distraint, court enforcement and statutory travel restrictions against the responsible director in cases of prolonged default.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR – A full Local File and Master File are mandatory where the Cyprus tax-resident entity's gross revenue reaches EUR 7,500,000 in a tax year AND the aggregate annual value of controlled transactions with associated enterprises (parent licensing, merchant acquiring services, settlement support, financing) exceeds EUR 750,000. The documentation must be produced to the Cyprus Tax Department within 60 days of written demand. Country-by-Country Reporting obligations attach to the MNE group only when consolidated group revenue is at least EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not operate a Gulf-style Economic Substance Regulation; it applies an equivalent shell-company doctrine built on the central management and control test (Income Tax Law L.118(I)/2002 s.2), the 2015 Tax Compliance Measures and the EU ATAF/ATAD anti-letterbox rules. A PSI/EMI-licensed online merchant acquirer must maintain a Cyprus registered office, full-time executive management in Cyprus, board minutes adopted in Cyprus, a Cyprus bank account, resident executive directors and merchant acquiring contracts governed by Cyprus law. A licence holder without such substance is treated as a shell: tax residency is revoked, treaty and EU directive relief is refused and income is reallocated to the jurisdiction of the foreign parent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / fraudulent under-declaration of PSP revenue

Statutory sanction: Criminal conviction under s.57 of the Income Tax Law: imprisonment of up to five years and/or fines; additional administrative penalty of up to 100% of the under-assessed tax plus default interest of 2% per annum; parallel money laundering prosecution under L.188(I)/2007 where proceeds are moved abroad.

Director personal liability: Every director who signed or caused a false return, including shadow directors managing the company from abroad, is jointly and severally liable for the additional tax, penalties and interest; courts may order civil restitution, seizure of personal assets, travel bans and extradition in aggravated cross-border fraud cases.
Shell company / sham economic substance

Statutory sanction: Refusal or revocation of the Cyprus Tax Residence Certificate and all DTA and EU Directive relief; reassessment of the entire fee income at source; strike-off by the Registrar of Companies; revocation of the payment institution licence by the Central Bank of Cyprus; administrative fines for false declarations.

Director personal liability: Nominee and whole-time directors face personal liability for breach of fiduciary duties under the Companies Law (Cap.113 ss.169-170), disqualification orders, and personal responsibility for fraudulent trading where the shell strips payment outflow to the foreign parent. The managing director and the foreign shadow director are both exposed to contributions from personal assets.
Transfer pricing non-compliance and base erosion

Statutory sanction: Primary tax adjustment to arm's-length profits plus fines and default interest under the Cyprus Transfer Pricing Direction; recharacterisation of excessive fees as deemed dividends or distributions; disallowance of the royalty and interest deductions; penalty of up to 100% of the adjusted tax in cases of deliberate behaviour.

Director personal liability: Managing directors who approved the non-arm's-length pricing are personally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties where the company is insolvent; the Cyprus Tax Department may pierce the corporate veil and recover directly from directors' assets where profits were systematically diverted abroad.
AML / payment-processing misconduct by the licensed PSP

Statutory sanction: Under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law (L.188(I)/2007 as amended), legal persons face criminal fines of up to EUR 1,000,000 and/or imprisonment of responsible individuals of up to five years; the Central Bank of Cyprus may additionally impose administrative fines, revoke the payment institution licence and disqualify compliance officers.

Director personal liability: Directors, AML compliance officers and beneficial owners who consented, connived in or negligently permitted breaches face criminal imprisonment, personal fines, confiscation of illicit proceeds, disqualification from management of regulated entities, and international travel bans under FATF countermeasure procedures and Interpol red notices.
