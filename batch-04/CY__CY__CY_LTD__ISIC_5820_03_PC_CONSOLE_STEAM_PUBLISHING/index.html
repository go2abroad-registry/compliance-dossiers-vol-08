Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; electronic Tax Portal (Taxisnet) at https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy handles TIC registration, VAT returns, OSS/IOSS declarations and EC Sales Lists; company-level incorporation and TIN creation is effected through the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver at https://efiling.companies.gov.cy.

E-government filing portal

For a Cyprus Private Limited Company (CY_LTD): the statutory Tax Identification Code (TIC) is generated automatically upon incorporation through the Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver; no separate direct-tax application is needed for a Cyprus-resident company. The registered auditor must then (1) activate the corporate tax file on the Tax Portal; (2) submit VAT registration Form VAT 104 before making taxable supplies where the EUR 15,600 threshold is expected to be exceeded, or immediately for supplies where VAT is chargeable (digital gaming sales to EU consumers are handled through the OSS module rather than local registration); and (3) register for social insurance and obtain a branch/TIC number for any foreign permanent establishment. A foreign legal entity creating a Cyprus PE or making taxable supplies files Form VAT 104A via the Tax Portal with certified charter/incorporation documents, a notarised translation, proof of foreign address, and details of the appointed local tax representative.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: a Cyprus-resident CY_LTD is taxed at the statutory headline rate of 12.5% on worldwide income; profits of a foreign PE, foreign dividends, and qualifying foreign interest are exempt, and a broad participation exemption applies. No pass-through or US-style check-the-box classification exists for a Limited company; however, the qualifying IP Box regime grants an 80% notional deduction on qualifying video-game software profits, producing an effective rate of approximately 2.5% net of the deduction.

Available election forms

Cyprus provides no check-the-box election; the relevant statutory elections under the Income Tax Law N.118(I)/2002 and EU ATAD are: (i) IP Box election (80% qualifying-intangible deduction); (ii) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on new equity; (iii) group relief election for losses of 75%+ group members; (iv) restructuring/rollover deferral election; (v) depreciation method election (straight-line vs. reducing balance); (vi) hybrid-mismatch neutralisation elections under CIT legislation aligning with Council Directive (EU) 2016/1164; and (vii) indirect-tax elections: OSS registration vs. local VAT registration for intra-EU B2C digital supplies, small-business annual accounting election, and annual vs. quarterly VAT return filing under the Cyprus VAT Law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 - annual taxable turnover in Cyprus above which a Cyprus-established supplier must register for VAT (registration below the threshold is optional). Non-established (foreign) suppliers have no exemption: they must either register for Cyprus VAT from the first euro of Cyprus supplies or use OSS for EU B2C digital services. The special EUR 10,000 annual intra-EU threshold for distance sales of goods and electronically supplied services (ESS) to other member states is applicable, above which the customer-country VAT rules/OSS apply to a Cyprus-established publisher.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical exports: 0% VAT applies to goods dispatched/transported from Cyprus to a destination outside the EU, provided lawful proof of removal is held within statutory deadlines (evidence at time of invoice/return) and the exporter files the SAD/export declaration. Digital video-game publishing (Steam/PlayStation) is not zero-rated as goods: (i) B2B sales of licences/rights to a VAT-registered EU customer are subject to reverse charge; (ii) B2C sales to EU consumers are taxed at the customer-country VAT rate through OSS; (iii) sales to non-EU consumers are outside EU VAT scope and require two non-contradictory place-of-establishment proofs (IP address, billing address, bank country, SIM MCC). Royalties received from Valve/PlayStation (B2B) follow recipient place-of-supply rules and are outside Cyprus VAT if the customer is non-EU or an EU reverse-charge buyer; domestic CY direct-to-consumer sales of digitally downloaded games are standard-rated at 19%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / electronic ECS export entry) or Intrastat dispatch declaration
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB) / CMR consignment note / courier shipment manifest proving physical removal from Cyprus and the EU
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and any export licence/certificate required for the consignment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or certified bank statement) evidencing repatriation of the export sales proceeds
  • For digital supplies: OSS quarterly returns, EC Sales Lists (ESL), reverse-charge invoices, and retained two-proof place-of-supply records (IP address, billing address, bank country)

Cyprus has no real-time fiscal clearance, Continuous Transaction Control framework, or digital stamping mandate as of the current cycle. Mandatory B2G e-invoicing applies to public contracts (EN 16931/Peppol); VAT returns (VAT 4/5/6), EC Sales Lists, Intrastat dispatch declarations (reporting threshold EUR 90,000/year) and OSS/IOSS filings must be submitted exclusively via the Tax Portal. Taxable persons must retain electronic records for six years and grant the Tax Department API-accessible access; the EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) programme will introduce compulsory structured e-invoicing for cross-border B2B supplies by 2030, so API/Tax-Portal readiness is advisable.

The VAT Department may audit export records up to six years (extended to ten years in cases of fraud or gross negligence). A failed or unsupported zero-rating/outside-scope claim is re-assessed at 19% with statutory interest of 1% per month pro rata; the erroneously refunded input VAT is clawed back with the assessed output VAT. Administrative fines apply per missing/incorrect invoice or record, and fraudulent export refusals may attract criminal prosecution under the Cyprus VAT Law and the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law (fines and custodial penalties), plus retrospective revocation of rulings and correction of OSS declarations to each affected member state.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Euro is fully convertible across both current and capital account transactions. The repealed Exchange Control Law (Cap. 92) leaves no residual exchange-control approval gate. Profit remittances, dividends, and capital repatriation are unrestricted, subject only to Cypriot corporate tax compliance and any withholding tax reductions under the applicable double tax avoidance treaty. No central bank licensing or prior authorization is required for ordinary cross-border transfers related to trade or royalties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cyprus imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion requirements for export receipts. All export proceeds may be retained freely in EUR or foreign currency accounts. However, Cyprus entities must comply with ECB and Central Bank of Cyprus reporting obligations, including quarterly Balance of Payments reporting (Form C18) for individual transactions exceeding an aggregate threshold of EUR 50,000, and the EU-wide Transparency Directive administrative reporting for designated capital movements.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα Κύπρου), operating within the European System of Central Banks and subject to ECB supervisory and monetary policy frameworks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with significant legal constraints. Non-Cyprus EMIs may offer EU IBANs and faster onboarding, but most tier-1 Cypriot banks require a full bank relationship for the company's primary transaction flows, including Steam and PlayStation Store payouts. Under PSD2, an EU-licensed EMI can provide payment services passportable to Cyprus, yet a foreign EMI without a Cyprus branch or appointed agent faces practical delays in clearing ACH/SEPA for gaming royalty flows and may not support Commercial LC settlement. A Cyprus-licensed EMI is preferable, though full corporate onboarding is still less comprehensive than a mainstream bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing requires SWIFT MT103 wire transfers from the parent company or investor, using purpose code 'CAPT' (Capital Contribution) or 'INVN' (Investment). Funds must be transmitted through a credit institution with access to TARGET2 and SWIFT. For amounts exceeding EUR 10,000, the receiving Cypriot bank requires a Payment Information Sheet declaring the underlying transaction, together with the subscribed capital resolution and evidence of registration of the increase at the Registrar of Companies. Verification of the remitting bank's authorization and the UBO's identity is mandatory under the Prevention of Money Laundering Law (A.188(I)/2007).Outbound trade disbursements are cleared primarily through SWIFT MT103 for royalty payments, developer advances, and digital-store settlement transfers. For high-value transactions exceeding EUR 10,000 or where a direct payment to a foreign supplier requires assurance of delivery, Cyprus banks support commercial documentary credits (DC) and standby letters of credit.
