Governing statute

Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113 (as amended), ss. 4-8 (incorporation), s. 5 (share capital), s. 57 (registered office), s. 139 (directors and secretary); residual provisions of Table A incorporated by default.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association submitted with Form HE1 (Application for Incorporation) and Certificate of Incorporation issued under Cap. 113, s. 8

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 17 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cap. 113; shares may have any agreed par value and no escrow or banker's capital confirmation is required before registration.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate required for any occupied office/warehouse from the Cyprus Fire Service under the Fire Prevention and Fire Fighting Law; a structural adequacy and building entry certificate from the District Office; an occupational safety and health clearance under the Safety and Health at Work Laws (Law 89(I)/1996) including workplace risk assessment and employee training records.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cap. 113; shares may have any agreed par value and no escrow or banker's capital confirmation is required before registration.

Category: Not applicable — a general business registration with the Tax Department and employer registration with Social Insurance Services are sufficient for freight-forwarding agency services; no statutory industrial operating permit is required.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cyprus Tax Department (Tmima Forologias), Ministry of Finance; digital portal: Cyprus Tax Portal (Tax Portal) at taxportal.mof.gov.cy and ARIADNI e-filing platform; the VAT Service operates within the Tax Department under the VAT Law No. 94(I)/2000.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation of the Cyprus Private Limited Company (HE77), the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DROCOR) issues the company's Tax Identification Number (TIN), which coincides with the VAT registration number (CY + 8 digits + check). Step 1: File Form TD100/01N (incorporation/corporate income tax registration) with the Tax Department within 60 days of incorporation; obtain a corporate tax certificate. Step 2: File VAT Form TD100/02N for VAT registration if annual taxable turnover of goods/services supplied in Cyprus exceeds EUR 15,600, or voluntarily to exercise input VAT deduction rights or to perform intra-Community acquisitions. Step 3: Non-resident foreign entities with a Cyprus permanent establishment, Cyprus-source income, or local taxable activity must appoint a Cyprus tax representative (as required for non-EU entities) and complete the same TD100 registration forms; they obtain a TIN/VAT ID without Cyprus tax residence status. The Cyprus TIN is the same identifier used for all indirect tax filings, Customs (EORI, if applied separately), and the VIES system.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; a Cyprus-resident company (management and control in Cyprus) is taxed on worldwide income at the corporate rate of 12.5%; non-resident companies are subject to Cypriot tax on Cyprus-source income only (Territorial_Scope); no sub-federal tax distinctions (Unitary_National); participation exemption (100% exemption on qualifying foreign dividends and foreign branch profits) applies. — Upon incorporation of the Cyprus Private Limited Company (HE77), the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DROCOR) issues the company's Tax Identification Number (TIN), which coincides with the VAT registration number (CY + 8 digits + check). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a Cyprus-resident company (management and control in Cyprus) is taxed on worldwide income at the corporate rate of 12.5%; non-resident companies are subject to Cypriot tax on Cyprus-source income only (Territorial_Scope); no sub-federal tax distinctions (Unitary_National); participation exemption (100% exemption on qualifying foreign dividends and foreign branch profits) applies.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Cyprus. Statutory elections relevant to the freight forwarding Ltd include: (1) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on new equity introduced after 1 January 2015; (2) group loss relief election for 75%+ group companies (Form TD); (3) deemed dividend distribution (DDD) rule for accounting years commencing 1 January 2022 - election to apportion and declare profits; (4) exemption election for intra-group foreign dividends under the participation exemption; (5) EU VAT OSS/IOSS scheme election for intra-Community distance sales; (6) for international transport, the option to zero-rate ancillary handling services directly linked to export/import journeys.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for supplies of goods and services within Cyprus; EUR 10,000 threshold for intra-Community distance sales of goods under the OSS special scheme; no threshold for non-resident/non-EU suppliers required to register for Cyprus VAT when making taxable supplies of services or import transactions subject to reverse charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Qualification under Article 41 of the Cyprus VAT Law (transposing Article 146 of the EU VAT Directive): (1) supplies of goods dispatched or transported to a destination outside the EU are zero-rated provided the goods physically leave Cyprus within 90 days of dispatch; (2) transport services relating to the international carriage of such exported goods, including transcontinental container block train and intermodal rail freight services (ISIC 5229), are zero-rated when the transport is directly linked to the exported goods and supplied to the consignor/consignee; (3) ancillary services (handling, cargo consolidation, customs clearance, storage) qualify for zero-rating only where the value is included in the transport charge or supplied as part of the single export transaction; (4) the freight forwarder must hold and preserve conclusive documentary evidence of the removal (export customs declaration, railway consignment note, B/L, and proof of payment in foreign currency); (5) zero-rating also extends to services of international freight forwarding intermediaries under the same evidentiary conditions.

  • Customs Export Declaration – Single Administrative Document (SAD/IE-529) accepted by the Cyprus Customs Authority
  • International Railway Consignment Note (CIM/SMGS) or Combined Transport Waybill evidencing movement from EU to non-EU territory
  • Bill of Lading (or multimodal transport document) covering any maritime/road leg beyond the rail network
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List issued by the Cyprus entity
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds within the 180-day repatriation period
  • Certificate of Origin (and EUR.1 or A.TR certificate if preferential origin is claimed)

Mandatory structured electronic invoicing for business-to-government (B2G) transactions using the European standard EN 16931 (PEPPOL format) under the Cyprus B2G e-Invoicing framework; no mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping for B2B/B2C invoices as of 2024; voluntary e-invoicing is accepted; the Tax Department may require SAF-T style digital audit files on request; VAT returns must be filed electronically via the Cyprus Tax Portal (Tax Portal/ARIADNI); EORI numbers are issued separately by the Cyprus Customs (under the EU UCC).

The Tax Department may conduct retrospective audits up to 6 years (extended to 10 years in cases of fraud or wilful default) and will claw back incorrectly zero-rated VAT with statutory interest at 1% per month (compounded) and administrative penalties of up to 2 times the evaded tax. Additionally: (1) fraudulent export refund claims expose the company to criminal penalties (fine up to EUR 500,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years for directors/officers); (2) false customs declarations trigger UCC-related penalties under Cyprus Customs Law and potential revocation of the Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) status; (3) input VAT previously deducted on export purchases is recoverable by clawback assessment; (4) documentary deficiencies (missing SAD, consignment note or remittance certificate) shift the burden of proof to the exporter, resulting in reversal of the zero rate and VAT liability at 19% plus penalties. Cyprus abolished exchange controls in 1994, so the 180-day repatriation period is applied as an administrative/transfer pricing safe harbour rather than a statutory currency control deadline.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies. Cyprus is a Eurozone member and applies the acquis communautaire, including the free movement of capital under Article 63 TFEU. No exchange-control law distinguishes capital versus current account transactions. Profit and dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted, subject to corporate solvency requirements under the Cyprus Companies Law (Cap. 113), confirmation of distributable reserves, standard anti-money-laundering screening, and any applicable withholding-tax formalities (generally 0% under the Parent-Subsidiary Directive or the domestic participation exemption). There is no Central Bank approval, permit, or registration step for remittances; only statistical reporting through the annual balance-of-payments and direct-investment surveys applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Cyprus imposes no statutory timeline for repatriating export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export receipts may be retained in foreign-currency accounts at licensed credit institutions, converted freely, or held offshore. For intra-EU dispatches, only Intrastat statistical thresholds apply; for non-EU exports, the Cyprus Customs electronic declaration (IFEM) and customs export documentation must support the underlying cross-border fund flows, and anti-money-laundering transaction monitoring applies at the account level.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), operating within the European System of Central Banks and the European Central Bank framework; the Cyprus Ministry of Finance retains legislative competency over financial market acts, while the Central Bank acts as the licensing and supervisory authority for credit institutions, payment services, and the balance-of-payments statistical regime.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

High for ancillary euro disbursements, SEPA/instant payments, and FX conversion, provided the electronic money institution is authorized in an EEA member state and passporting into Cyprus under the Payment Services Directive (PSD2). Medium for primary settlement and large-volume rail-freight clearing: EMIs lack full correspondent banking depth for non-EEA currencies (CNY, TRY, KZT, INR) and do not carry banking-license balance-sheet strength. Legal constraints include prohibition on taking deposits or granting commercial credit, mandatory safeguarding of client funds, and full customer due diligence alignment with the Cyprus AML Law and Central Bank of Cyprus AML Directive. Contracts should expressly require the EMI to be licensed by its home state competent authority and passported to Cyprus, with audit rights over safeguarding arrangements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital is routed via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the remitting shareholder's bank, with the payment narrative field (field 70 or 72) explicitly stating 'Capital Injection', 'Share Capital Contribution', or 'Shareholder Loan', together with the relevant ECB balance-of-payments statistical purpose category for direct investment equity where the receiving bank's system requires it. No prior Central Bank of Cyprus authorization is needed. The company should file the share-capital increase at the Department of Registrar of Companies and retain the certified updated share register; for shareholder loans, a written loan agreement at arm's length and board approval must be maintained. Receiving banks will apply standard anti-money-laundering verification of the remitter and may require the source-of-funds evidence before crediting to the operational or escrow account.Outbound rail-freight settlements clear primarily through TARGET2 (euro) and the SWIFT network. Direct supplier, carrier, and terminal payments are executed via SWIFT MT103 with the trade purpose narrative and are settled in euro or converted to required currencies through approved counterparties. For high-value or documentary transactions, Commercial Letters of Credit issued under UCP 600 via MT700/MT707 are recommended, particularly for rolling-stock acquisitions, transit-equipment purchases, and carrier deposits; electronic presentation is supported under eUCP for digital waybills. Open-account flows may also use Bank Payment Obligations under the ICC URBPO framework. Intra-EEA disbursements below large-value thresholds are cleared through SEPA Credit Transfers, while instant payments (SCT Inst) are appropriate for low-value urgent operational costs. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Cyprus Transfer Pricing Return (TPR) and Local File are required when the aggregate value of reportable related-party transactions exceeds EUR 750,000 in a fiscal year; Master File and Country-by-Country (CbC) Report obligations attach to multinational groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not have an offshore-style standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute, but tax residency rests on management and control exercised in Cyprus. The Tax Department applies substance-over-form, EU ATAD, and DAC6 anti-abuse rules. A rail freight forwarding entity whose only activity is a letterbox banking arrangement with no staff, premises, or active board decision-making in Cyprus cannot claim Cyprus residency or treaty benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Assessment and Collection of Taxes Laws, criminal conviction attracts imprisonment up to 5 years and monetary fines; the tax authority may additionally assess evaded tax up to 200% of the amount under-declared, plus interest and surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false returns or direct the evasion are personally liable for the tax shortfall; courts may pierce the corporate veil, order civil restitution, impose travel bans, and disqualify directors under the Companies Law Cap. 113.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of double tax treaty benefits, re-characterisation of the company as non-Cypriot tax resident, imposition of a 12.5% tax on deemed Cyprus-source income, penalties for late assessments, and compulsory deregistration under the Companies Law.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil liability for all unpaid group tax and may be treated as having traded fraudulently; they can be prohibited from leaving Cyprus pending recovery proceedings and are exposed to personal asset forfeiture where the corporate facade is found to be a sham.
AML / Sanctions / KYC Violations

Statutory sanction: Under Cyprus AML Law L.188(I)/2007 and EU sanctions regulations, the corporate penalties can reach EUR 20 million or 10% of global turnover; the company may be struck off and its payment-rail privileges (SWIFT, Letters of Credit) revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for AML compliance failures; legal persons and directors may be jointly fined, prohibited from holding directorial positions, subjected to travel restrictions, and, where the money laundering is connected to the transport of restricted cargo, prosecuted with imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Cyprus tax authorities may arm's-length reprice the transactions, assess underpaid tax at 12.5% plus interest, impose penalties of up to 50% of the additional assessment, and reject the transfer pricing return, triggering criminal sanctions for false documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false transfer pricing declarations or knowingly approve non-arm's-length pricing are personally assessed for the additional tax and interest; courts may lift the corporate veil to attach personal assets and issue a travel ban where economic substance is shown to be fictitious.
