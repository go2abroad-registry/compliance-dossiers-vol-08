Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cyprus Tax Department (Department of Taxation), Ministry of Finance; single online portal: 'Tax For All' (TFA), https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy. The Tax Department also administers VAT and customs duties.

E-government filing portal

Domestic Ltd: (1) Incorporate with the Department of Registrar of Companies (DRCOR) to obtain the HE registration number; (2) through TFA, obtain the 8-digit Tax Identification Code (TIC) upon registration of the company with the Tax Department; (3) file VAT Form TD2001 before first supply or within 30 days of turnover exceeding EUR 15,600. Foreign entity: no VAT threshold; register before first taxable supply, appoint a Cyprus fiscal representative (joint and several VAT liability), and obtain a CY-prefixed VAT number; no TIC required for income tax if no permanent establishment, in which case Cyprus-source royalties are subject to domestic withholding tax subject to treaty relief.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. Resident Cyprus Ltd taxed at 12.5% corporate income tax on worldwide profits, with territorial exemptions (participation exemption on dividends and disposal gains, foreign branch exemption, notional interest deduction). Not pass-through; no general tax-exempt zone regime (tonnage tax limited to shipping). — Domestic Ltd: (1) Incorporate with the Department of Registrar of Companies (DRCOR) to obtain the HE registration number; (2) through TFA, obtain the 8-digit Tax Identification Code (TIC) upon registration of the company with the Tax Department; (3) file VAT Form TD2001 before first supply or within 30 days of turnover exceeding EUR 15,600. Foreign entity: no VAT threshold; register before first taxable supply, appoint a Cyprus fiscal representative (joint and several VAT liability), and obtain a CY-prefixed VAT number; no TIC required for income tax if no permanent establishment, in which case Cyprus-source royalties are subject to domestic withholding tax subject to treaty relief.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. Resident Cyprus Ltd taxed at 12.5% corporate income tax on worldwide profits, with territorial exemptions (participation exemption on dividends and disposal gains, foreign branch exemption, notional interest deduction). Not pass-through; no general tax-exempt zone regime (tonnage tax limited to shipping).

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. Available elections: (a) VAT group registration (Art. 12A, VAT Law 95(I)/2000) for closely connected persons; (b) group relief loss surrender under s.27 Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 (75% group); (c) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) under s.9B on new equity; (d) tonnage tax election for shipping; (e) tax transparency for registered UCITS/AIFs. Hybrid mismatch: ATAD anti-hybrid rules apply; no elective mismatch mechanism is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 per annum of taxable supplies for persons established in Cyprus; EUR 0 (no threshold) for non-established taxable persons; EUR 10,000 annual threshold for intra-Community distance sales/acquisitions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies where goods physically leave the EU within 90 days from supply and are exported to a non-EU destination (Art. 28, VAT Law), and to intra-Community supplies to EU VAT-registered customers. Franchise royalty/licence fees invoiced by CY Ltd to non-Cyprus B2B franchisees fall under place-of-supply rules (customer's jurisdiction; reverse charge or outside scope), not Cypriot zero-rating; royalties charged to Cyprus franchisees are standard-rated 19%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / ECS electronic export declaration) certified by Cyprus Customs
  • Transport document evidencing departure from the EU (Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR)
  • Commercial invoice and underlying franchise/licence agreement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT/MT103 credit) evidencing collection of export proceeds
  • Packing/weight list and customs exit record (for goods-related export services)

B2G e-invoicing is mandatory in EU-standard (EN 16931/PEPPOL) format for supplies to Cypriot public contracting authorities. Retail/food-service POS operators must use Tax-Department-approved electronic fiscal devices and issue serial-numbered fiscal receipts. Cyprus has no SDI-style real-time B2B/B2C invoice clearance API and no digital stamping regime; a ViDA-aligned e-invoicing rollout is pending.

Unsubstantiated zero-rating triggers retrospective VAT assessment plus interest, civil penalty up to 100% of the VAT evaded, and criminal liability (VAT Law 95(I)/2000): fines and/or imprisonment up to 5 years for fraudulent evasion; Customs Code (EU Reg. 952/2013) penalties for false export declarations; asset seizure, director disqualification, and publication of serious offences. Limitation period for assessment is extended from 6 to 10 years in fraud or gross negligence cases.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus has been a euro-area member since 1 January 2008, and the euro is fully and unconditionally convertible. Under Article 63 TFEU there are zero exchange controls on current-account flows (royalties, dividends, interest, management fees) and on capital-account flows (foreign direct investment, intercompany loans, liquidation proceeds, repatriation of capital). Profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is unrestricted: Cyprus does not withhold dividend tax on distributions to non-residents absent a Cyprus permanent establishment, and royalty payments to non-resident licensors are made gross under domestic law and the EU Interest and Royalties Directive, subject to substance requirements and the domestic and EU general anti-abuse rules. The practical convertibility hurdles are source-side, namely PRC SAFE ODI registration and NDRC/MOFCOM approval for Chinese outward investment, HK-subsidiary funding documentation, and bank-level AML/KYC and sanctions verification; the Cypriot FX regime itself imposes no convertibility restriction on the entry, holding, or exit of capital.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cyprus imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline. Exporters, service providers, and licensing businesses (including CY_LTD's franchise-royalty model) may retain foreign-currency receipts (CNY, USD, HKD) indefinitely in multi-currency accounts at CBC-licensed credit institutions and convert them at commercial rates at their discretion. The only binding obligations are: (i) CBC balance-of-payments statistical returns using the ITRS/BoP classification, including royalty income under the services rubric and direct-investment equity or loan flows; (ii) AML/CFT transaction monitoring and sanctions screening; (iii) full payer and beneficiary information under Regulation (EU) 2015/847 for wire transfers, including intra-EEA transfers below EUR 1,000; and (iv) beneficial-ownership register filings (Cyprus Beneficial Ownership Register under the Companies Law, and the separate UBO register for express trusts). All legacy exchange-control restrictions were abrogated upon euro adoption, and the 2013 crisis-era capital controls were fully lifted by 2016.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου / Central Bank of Cyprus, 'CBC'), a Eurosystem member operating within the European System of Central Banks (ESCB). The CBC is the national competent authority for licensing and prudential supervision of credit institutions, payment institutions, and electronic-money institutions, for balance-of-payments and international transaction reporting (ITRS), and for AML/CFT supervision of less significant institutions; significant Cypriot credit institutions are supervised directly by the ECB Single Supervisory Mechanism (SSM) in cooperation with the CBC.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplemental API-driven collection and payment rail; Low as a sole primary banking relationship. Cyprus-licensed electronic-money institutions (CBC under the Electronic Money Law L.81(I)/2012, transposing EMD2/PSD2) and EEA EMIs passporting into Cyprus can provide CNY/EUR/USD virtual IBANs, low-cost cross-border money transfer, and FX conversion (for example Wise, Airwallex, Revolut). Legal and structural constraints: (i) client funds held at an EMI are safeguarded and not covered by the deposit protection scheme, so capital escrow, statutory deposits, and secured accounts must remain at a CBC-licensed credit institution; (ii) non-EEA EMIs require an EEA licence or an EEA-authorized agent arrangement; (iii) CySEC-licensed FX/CFD brokers are not banking counterparties and cannot hold operational treasury balances; and (iv) passported EMIs often restrict franchise-fee collection volumes under their business-model risk appetite. Recommended deployment: an EMI as a low-value multi-currency collection and procurement settlement layer, with daily sweeping of balances to the Bank of Cyprus or Hellenic Bank operational account to preserve CBC ITRS reporting, audit trail, and tax payment infrastructure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound structure for CY_LTD with PRC/HK shareholders: (1) Equity injection route: the PRC shareholder chain completes NDRC, MOFCOM, and SAFE ODI registration before remittance; the remitting Hong Kong holding company issues an SWIFT MT103 with full payer information under Regulation (EU) 2015/847 and the CBC balance-of-payments statistical purpose code for direct investment-equity capital; the receiving CBC-licensed bank credits the segregated capital escrow account at Hellenic Bank or Ancoria after matching the bank-certified board allotment resolution, updated register of members, share-purchase agreements, and evidence that no Cyprus capital duty is payable (the former 0.6% share-capital duty was abolished with effect from 1 January 2016, leaving only the EUR 350 incorporation fee and the EUR 350 annual levy). (2) Intercompany shareholder-loan injection route: classify the flow for CBC ITRS purposes as direct investment-intercompany debt, support the remittance with a duly executed loan agreement, arm's-length interest rate (EURIBOR plus margin), repayment schedule, and consolidated group financial statements; the loan proceeds may be swept to the operational account after completion of CDD source-of-funds verification. All EUR inbound flows settle through TARGET2; CNY or USD injections are converted at the receiving bank's commercial rate using the ECB euro reference rate as the compliance benchmark; the bank's CDD file must retain the CBC ITRS return form and an AML clearance note before funds are released.CY_LTD will clear outbound treasury through four rails: (1) Eurozone operational payments, management fees, and royalty redistribution: SEPA Credit Transfers using the ISO 20022 pain.001 format via the Bank of Cyprus operational account. (2) High-value trade payments to PRC equipment, dry-goods, and central-kitchen machinery suppliers: documentary sight or usance letters of credit issued under UCP600 at Eurobank Cyprus or the National Bank of Greece (Cyprus) branch, transmitted by SWIFT MT700 with amendments via MT750, and confirmed or advised through Bank of China, ICBC, or other PRC banks. (3) CNY-denominated vendor payments: cross-border CNY clearing via Bank of China (Hong Kong/China) correspondent accounts or CIPS, using MT103 with the trade-in-goods purpose code and full documentary support (customs declarations, commercial invoices, packing lists). (4) Outbound licensor royalties to the PRC brand-owner: MT103 wire with the services purpose code, a transfer-pricing memorandum, and the signed licence agreement attached, with domestic VAT and withholding tax treatment verified in advance. All outbound wires are pre-cleared against EU, UN, OFAC, and Cyprus Ministry of Finance sanctions filters, the CBC balance-of-payments declaration, and the approved treasury policy; any intercompany zero-balance cash pooling requires a signed cash-pooling and set-off agreement acceptable to the CBC for large-exposure and anti-abuse review.EUR/USD spot and outright FX forward contracts (maturity up to 12 months) executed with a CBC-licensed credit institution or CySEC-licensed investment firm under MiFID II for operational, procurement, and royalty-flow hedging.Offshore CNY (CNH) non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) through London or Hong Kong CNH market makers to hedge PRC royalty remittance exposure without requiring onshore CNY deliverability.EUR/CNH cross-currency swaps (CCS) with a Tier-1 counterparty for medium-term shareholder loan and restaurant-equipment capital-expenditure hedging over a two-to-five-year horizon.Bought vanilla EUR/USD and EUR/CNH FX options, zero-cost collars, participating forwards, and barrier forwards to cap adverse exchange-rate movements on scheduled disbursements and forecast franchise-fee receipts while preserving upside.EURIBOR or €STR-linked interest rate swaps where the intercompany shareholder loan or any project finance facility carries a floating reference rate; all hedging must be executed on a documented risk-management and non-speculative basis under an ISDA/CSA or Cyprus master agreement.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplemental API-driven collection and payment rail; Low as a sole primary banking relationship. Cyprus-licensed electronic-money institutions (CBC under the Electronic Money Law L.81(I)/2012, transposing EMD2/PSD2) and EEA EMIs passporting into Cyprus can provide CNY/EUR/USD virtual IBANs, low-cost cross-border money transfer, and FX conversion (for example Wise, Airwallex, Revolut). Legal and structural constraints: (i) client funds held at an EMI are safeguarded and not covered by the deposit protection scheme, so capital escrow, statutory deposits, and secured accounts must remain at a CBC-licensed credit institution; (ii) non-EEA EMIs require an EEA licence or an EEA-authorized agent arrangement; (iii) CySEC-licensed FX/CFD brokers are not banking counterparties and cannot hold operational treasury balances; and (iv) passported EMIs often restrict franchise-fee collection volumes under their business-model risk appetite. Recommended deployment: an EMI as a low-value multi-currency collection and procurement settlement layer, with daily sweeping of balances to the Bank of Cyprus or Hellenic Bank operational account to preserve CBC ITRS reporting, audit trail, and tax payment infrastructure.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 for goods transactions, EUR 250,000 for services, EUR 100,000 for other transactions. Master File required if group consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 50 million.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to relevant activities (IP, finance, leasing, etc.). For a food franchise, ESR is not applicable. However, general substance requirements for tax residency (management and control in Cyprus) must be met.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine up to €50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years. Additional civil penalties up to 100% of tax evaded.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for unpaid taxes if they knowingly participated in evasion. Travel bans and asset freezes may be imposed.
Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax residency status, retroactive taxation at 12.5% on worldwide income, and penalties up to €50,000. Potential strike-off from the register.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for taxes and penalties if they fail to ensure substance. Travel bans and disqualification as directors for up to 5 years.
AML (Anti-Money Laundering)

Statutory sanction: Fines up to €500,000 for the company. Criminal prosecution of responsible officers with imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors can be held personally liable for AML breaches, including civil restitution and criminal penalties. Travel bans may be imposed during investigation.
