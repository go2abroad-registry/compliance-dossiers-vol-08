Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cyprus Tax Department (Tμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance; electronic services portal: Tax Portal via Ariadne (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must apply for a Cyprus Tax Identification Number (TIN) at the competent District Tax Office (DEO). For VAT operations, file Form VAT-101 (Application for Registration under the Value Added Tax Law) with the VAT Service of the Tax Department. Supporting documents include certified certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, lease/ownership evidence of premises, proof of director/shareholder identity, and any existing foreign tax registration certificates. Upon approval, the company receives a 9-digit TIN and, for VAT, an assigned VAT number in the format CY12345678X.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity must apply for a Cyprus Tax Identification Number (TIN) at the competent District Tax Office (DEO). For VAT operations, file Form VAT-101 (Application for Registration under the Value Added Tax Law) with the VAT Service of the Tax Department. Supporting documents include certified certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, lease/ownership evidence of premises, proof of director/shareholder identity, and any existing foreign tax registration certificates. Upon approval, the company receives a 9-digit TIN and, for VAT, an assigned VAT number in the format CY12345678X.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists. Cyprus-incorporated and centrally managed-and-controlled companies are tax residents subject to entity-level corporate income tax of 12.5%. Available elections include group relief elections, overseas permanent establishment exemption elections, notional interest deduction elections, and the intellectual property box regime. Hybrid mismatch rules under the Multilateral Instrument constrain deduction/non-inclusion outcomes, but no independent pass-through classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 in annual taxable turnover of goods or services; reduced registration thresholds apply for intra-EU distance sales and certain cross-border services under VAT e-commerce rules; voluntary registration is permitted from establishment date.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate may be claimed where the supplier proves that goods are dispatched or transported by the supplier, the customer, or a third party outside the territory of the EU (or to another EU member state subject to intra-community supply rules), and that the goods leave Cyprus within 90 days of supply. Offshore B2B services to non-EU recipients are outside the scope of VAT where the customer belongs outside the EU; digital scanning and virtual-office services qualify as electronically supplied services to non-EU business recipients. Zero-rating requires sufficient commercial documentation to evidence export movement and the non-EU status of the customer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Electronic Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document SAD)
  • Bill of Lading (or equivalent transport document: Air Waybill, CMR consignment note, or post-customs evidence of dispatch)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign currency bank transfer credit evidence

No mandatory nationwide real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping regime for B2B invoices. Cyprus has adopted mandatory e-invoicing for B2G transactions under PEPPOL/European Standard EN 16931, with a phased public-procurement mandate. Private-sector electronic invoicing remains optional and subject to general VAT invoice rules requiring authenticity of origin, integrity of content, and legibility.

Cyprus VAT authorities can retrospectively claw back zero-rated VAT if the claimed export cannot be substantiated within any statutory examination period. Civil penalties include late tax equal to 10% of the tax due, fixed penalties up to EUR 500 per violation, and interest at statutory rates. Fraudulent zero-rating or deceptive export documentation may attract criminal prosecution with imprisonment, fines up to EUR 10,000, and mandatory VAT payment plus penalties. Transfer pricing documentation and FX records are also scrutinised in refund audits.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 in aggregate intra-group transactions for the fiscal year requires a Local File; Master File required for groups with consolidated revenue at or above EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus follows the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and the arm's length principle. Cyprus is not classified as a zero-tax jurisdiction with standalone Economic Substance Regulations, but the Cyprus Tax Department requires real economic substance for tax residence, treaty access, and GAAR avoidance. A virtual office / mail scanning CMRA company must prove local substance: staff, premises, management decisions, and operational expenditures in Cyprus.
