Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual mailbox is not accepted for a private equity buyout SPV seeking local bank accounts, Cyprus tax residency or substance. Registered office service providers may offer a physical address, mail handling and meeting room, but the SPV must demonstrate management and control in Cyprus through board meetings, senior management and local accounting. For a non-resident SPV with no Cyprus activities, a virtual office may be formally accepted by the Registrar, but it will raise red flags for banks, CySEC and the Cyprus Tax Department.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DRCOR), Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Republic of Cyprus — electronic filing via the DRCOR e-Filing Portal.

Governing statute

Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113 (as amended, incl. the Companies (Amendment) Law of 2021 which removed minimum capital requirements for private companies); relevant provisions: s.3 (mode of incorporation), s.5 (object and liability clauses), s.10 (Certificate of Incorporation), s.46 (registered office), s.48 (notice of directors and secretary), and s.118 (articles of association).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Incorporation Form HE1, together with the Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association and a Statement of Compliance; the incorporation is completed by issuance of the Certificate of Incorporation (Form HE2 is thereafter filed for officers).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 13 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private limited company in Cyprus; shareholders may fix authorised capital (e.g. EUR 1,000) and the paid-up portion may be zero or as agreed in the Articles; no escrow deposit or blocked bank account rule applies; any injected capital must reflect commercial substance under EU anti-abuse principles.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety: the registered office must obtain a Fire Risk Assessment certificate and routine inspection clearance from the Fire Service of the Deputy Ministry of Civil Protection; structural load inspection: not applicable for a third-party leased office, relying on the landlord's existing building certification; workplace clearance: registration with the Ministry of Labour and Social Insurance under the Safety and Health at Work Laws 89(I)/1996, with a written workplace risk assessment and, for office-only use, exemption from regular surprise inspection unless a notifiable accident occurs.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private limited company in Cyprus; shareholders may fix authorised capital (e.g. EUR 1,000) and the paid-up portion may be zero or as agreed in the Articles; no escrow deposit or blocked bank account rule applies; any injected capital must reflect commercial substance under EU anti-abuse principles.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, as a special resolution of the incorporators, that the unconditional share subscription set opposite each subscriber's name in the Company's Memorandum be ratified and the issued share capital allotted in accordance with the Articles of Association. RESOLVED FURTHER that the registered office of the Company be established and maintained at a registered address in the Republic of Cyprus pursuant to the Companies Law, Cap. 113. RESOLVED FURTHER that the natural persons named in the consent lists be and are hereby appointed first directors and company secretary to hold office until the first general meeting. RESOLVED FURTHER that the directors be authorized to open bank accounts, adopt the share certificate form, and execute all ancillary filings.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable — no industrial licensing board authorisation is triggered; the Cyprus industrial licensing regime (relevant industrial installations legislation) applies only to defined manufacturing, energy, or mineral installations, and a purely office-based private equity buyout SPV (ISIC 6499) falls outside the industrial register.

Category: Not required — no operational or manufacturing permit; the entity operates under the Certificate of Incorporation and the Taxpayer Registration Certificate (Form TAX 200) issued by the Cyprus Tax Department; no CySEC authorisation is needed for a passive holding/SPV unless regulated fund management or investment services are exercised.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance; online portal: ARIADNE Integrated Tax Information System (externally accessible through portal.mof.gov.cy)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity deriving Cyprus-source income or establishing a permanent establishment must register for income tax with the Tax Department via ARIADNE. The application is submitted using TD2000 together with certified articles of incorporation, a Cyprus registered address, proof of identity and tax residence of directors/beneficial owners, and economic substance details. For VAT purposes, a non-established taxable person must apply for VAT registration before making any taxable supply in Cyprus, regardless of turnover, using the same TD2000 procedure and appointing a Cyprus tax representative where no local financial establishment exists. The Tax Department issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, a Cyprus VAT number.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity deriving Cyprus-source income or establishing a permanent establishment must register for income tax with the Tax Department via ARIADNE. The application is submitted using TD2000 together with certified articles of incorporation, a Cyprus registered address, proof of identity and tax residence of directors/beneficial owners, and economic substance details. For VAT purposes, a non-established taxable person must apply for VAT registration before making any taxable supply in Cyprus, regardless of turnover, using the same TD2000 procedure and appointing a Cyprus tax representative where no local financial establishment exists. The Tax Department issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, a Cyprus VAT number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus has no check-the-box election. A private limited liability company is automatically taxed at the entity level under the Income Tax Law. Available statutory elections include VAT grouping (Art. 49 VAT Law), group relief for losses (Art. 21 Income Tax Law), the Notional Interest Deduction (NID) as a reduction of taxable income, the IP Box/Amended IP Box regime (Art. 23A), and tonnage tax election for qualifying ship management or shipping operations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover threshold for persons established in Cyprus; non-established taxable persons must register before first taxable supply irrespective of threshold

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) the supply of goods exported outside the EU, evidenced by a valid customs export declaration; (ii) intra-Community supply of goods to a VAT-registered customer in another member state where the customer's EU VAT number is recorded and the goods move to the customer's member state; (iii) certain internationally related services, including transport and ancillary services connected with exported goods or through transit abroad; and (iv) the supply of services to non-EU customers typically outside the scope or zero-rated under place-of-supply rules. The exporter must show physical departure of the goods from CY in conformity with the EU VAT Directive and maintain sufficient evidence of delivery within the relevant reporting period.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document - SAD)
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill for air freight)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank confirmation of receipt of foreign currency export proceeds)

Cyprus does not currently impose a mandatory real-time electronic invoicing, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control (CTC) pre-clearance regime. E-invoicing may be used with customer consent, preferably using EN 16931 compliant formats. VAT returns and Intrastat filings are submitted electronically through the ARIADNE portal; API integration is available in principle but not mandatory, and no real-time Government clearance of individual invoices exists.

Zero-rating claims are subjected to compliance audit and risk-based verification. If substantiation is deficient, the Cyprus Tax Department re-assesses output VAT at 19% on the export value, imposes interest at 1.25% per month on the arrears, and applies administrative fines pursuant to Articles 44-56 of the VAT Law. Fraudulent export claims may attract a penalty of up to EUR 10,000 under Article 55, further penalties equal to the tax evaded under the Tax Procedures Law, and custodial sanctions upon criminal conviction. Wrongfully obtained refunds are clawed back with interest calculated from the date the refund was paid.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File preparation is mandatory for a Cyprus tax-resident company with related-party transactions exceeding EUR 750,000 in a fiscal year; a Transfer Pricing Summary Form must be submitted to the Cyprus Tax Department when the threshold is EUR 250,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus applies an anti-abuse substance doctrine that requires a Cyprus-resident SPV to have real management and decision-making functions in Cyprus, adequate premises, resident staff, and independent financial and treasury records. A private-equity buyout SPV that merely holds an invoiced or booking function with no active supervision and control in Cyprus risks being treated as not tax-resident under the Cyprus Income Tax Law, losing DTT benefits, or being subject to deemed dividend anti-abuse rules. There is no separate ESR regime comparable to the UAE; however, OECD Base Erosion and Profit Shifting (BEPS) substance requirements and the principal purpose test under the MLI will be applicable.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Return

Statutory sanction: A penalty equal to the tax under-assessed plus financial penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded, together with interest at the statutory rate; judicial proceedings may lead to a fine of up to EUR 50,000 and/or imprisonment for up to five years on indictment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed the false return, authorised concealment or engaged in criminal tax evasion face personal civil liability for the unpaid tax, criminal fines, travel bans, and a criminal record; in serious fraud cases, the court may order custodial sentences and restitution of shareholder distributions made with knowledge of the evasion.
Shell Company and Sham Arrangements

Statutory sanction: The Tax Department may recharacterise transactions, deny the SPV the rights to treaty benefits, impute the relevant income as deemed dividend income to the Cyprus shareholder, and apply a 17% deemed dividend distribution levy plus penalties; transfer pricing adjustments may also be raised, resulting in consequential tax, penalties and interest.

Director personal liability: Under Cyprus Companies Law (Cap. 113), a court may lift the corporate veil where the company is a mere sham; directors who authorised operations without economic substance can be ordered to pay the company's unpaid tax debts and be disqualified for up to five years, with travel bans imposed in serious cross-border structured abuse cases.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Failures

Statutory sanction: Civil fines of up to EUR 350,000 for each breach; imprisonment for up to five years; substantial administrative fines per day of continued non-compliance; the SPV may be struck off and its licence (if any) revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for AML failures unless they prove all reasonable steps were taken; directors may be subject to criminal prosecution, disqualification, travel bans and freezing of personal assets, alongside the financial penalties imposed on the company.
Transfer Pricing Documentation and Filing Defaults

Statutory sanction: The Tax Department may impose a transfer pricing adjustment increasing the SPV's taxable profit, apply interest on the under-declared taxes, raise a penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax, and reject the SPV's tax losses carryforward; the failure to submit the summary form triggers an administrative penalty of EUR 3,000 per year or higher upon formal notice.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly caused or allowed missing documentation or deliberate transfer-price mispricing may be personally liable for penalties if the company is insolvent; directors may face supplementary assessments, disqualification, and, in aggravated cases, criminal prosecution for reckless preparation of public accounts.
