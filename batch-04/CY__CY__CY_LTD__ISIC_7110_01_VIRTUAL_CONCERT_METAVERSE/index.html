Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is permissible for statutory registered-office purposes for an unlicensed virtual-performance and digital-ticket company, provided the address is a real physical address in Cyprus, not a P.O. Box, and service of process can be effected at that address. However, the company must be able to evidence a Cyprus board-meeting process, local directors and decision-making substance in Cyprus in order to sustain Cyprus tax residence and to withstand anti-avoidance attack on the 12.5% corporate tax rate and network treaty benefits. A registered-agent/secretary address must be disclosed in the register; the metaverse business model itself does not require any particular physical premises.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver, Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Nicosia, Republic of Cyprus.

Governing statute

The Companies Law, Cap. 113 (Cyprus), as amended, particularly ss. 4, 9, 21, 32, 121, 123 and the Companies (Fees) Rules; supplemented by the Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (Registration) Rules and the Cyprus Business Portal E-Filing Regulations.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form HE1 (Application for Registration of a Company) together with the executed Memorandum and Articles of Association.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital for a private company limited by shares; incorporation is permitted with a single share of EUR 1 or USD 1 par value. No escrow account, bank certificate, or notarized capital deposit is required, although the corporate bank account opening will trigger standard AML source-of-funds verification for any issued shares.

Funding & unlock path: Occupational health and safety clearance under the Health and Safety at Work Law 89(I)/1996 requires a workplace risk assessment and, where there are more than five employees, appointment of a safety officer and submission of a safety policy. Fire-safety certification of the registered office is required by the relevant Municipality and the Fire Service where the premises are physically occupied; for remote-only metaverse operations, a self-certified remote workplace statement and compliance with cybersecurity hygiene standards are sufficient. No structural load inspection applies to non-industrial office or virtual operations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up share capital for a private company limited by shares; incorporation is permitted with a single share of EUR 1 or USD 1 par value. No escrow account, bank certificate, or notarized capital deposit is required, although the corporate bank account opening will trigger standard AML source-of-funds verification for any issued shares.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable for pure virtual service businesses; the general commercial regulator is the Department of Commerce of the Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry for e-commerce consumer protection matters. No manufacturing or operating industrial permit is required for ISIC 7110 virtual performance hosting.

Category: None required — statutory license is not mandatory for virtual event hosting and digital ticket tokenization. Operators should obtain an optional e-commerce service registration, comply with the Electronic Commerce Law 156(I)/2004, and, if issuing tokenized tickets with secondary-market trading, obtain a no-action confirmation or CASP registration under the CySEC crypto-asset services regime.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department, Ministry of Finance of Cyprus (Φορολογικό Τμήμα); official portal: www.taxdepartments.mof.gov.cy (Tax For All e-portal); VAT e-returns and VIES reporting via the same portal; Customs EXD (Customs & Excise Department) operates THESEAS export declaration system under the Ministry of Finance.

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company (HE77) receives its statutory Tax Identification Number (TIC – Tax Identification Code, 8 digits) automatically upon incorporation with the Registrar of Companies. A foreign legal entity establishing a branch or otherwise exercising an economic activity in Cyprus must apply for a TIC on form TD2018 through the Tax Department's Tax For All portal after filing the Certificate of Registration of Overseas Company. For VAT, a taxable person must submit form VAT 101 to the Tax Department. Non-established suppliers of digital services (virtual concerts, metaverse ticketing) may register for Cyprus VAT directly or elect to use the EU VAT One-Stop-Shop (OSS) for intra-EU and non-EU B2C digital supplies.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (Cyprus resident companies are subject to corporate income tax at 12.5% on worldwide income; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income and profits attributable to a Cyprus permanent establishment; no sub-national tax distinction exists in the unitary national regime). — A Cyprus private limited company (HE77) receives its statutory Tax Identification Number (TIC – Tax Identification Code, 8 digits) automatically upon incorporation with the Registrar of Companies. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Cyprus resident companies are subject to corporate income tax at 12.5% on worldwide income; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income and profits attributable to a Cyprus permanent establishment; no sub-national tax distinction exists in the unitary national regime).

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election exists under Cyprus law. Available statutory elections include: group relief election for 75% subsidiaries; overseas permanent establishment loss relief election; notional interest deduction (NID) election on new qualifying equity; special defence contribution deemed dividend distribution election for close companies; and anti-hybrid elections pursuant to EU ATAD (implemented via Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 amendments).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

General VAT registration threshold for Cyprus-established taxable persons: EUR 15,600 of annual taxable turnover; separate EUR 10,000 threshold for cross-border intra-EU acquisitions and distance sales of goods; non-established digital service suppliers are required to register or use the OSS once they supply services to Cyprus consumers (no monetary threshold).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for 0% VAT where the goods are dispatched or transported by the supplier or a third party to a destination outside the EU, satisfying Article 146 EU VAT Directive (transposed as Section 61 of Cyprus VAT Law), provided the export occurs within 90 days of the time of supply. For services: B2B and B2C supplies of digitally delivered concert access, metaverse ticketing, and virtual performance hosting to customers outside the EU are zero-rated / outside the scope of Cyprus VAT under the place-of-supply rules (Sections 58, 59 and 59A of the VAT Law); B2B supplies to EU customers are subject to the reverse-charge mechanism.

  • Single Administrative Document (SAD) – customs export declaration lodged through the Cyprus Customs THESEAS electronic system
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR transport document evidencing physical removal from the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/MT103 proof of payment from the non-EU customer
  • Digital sales record, ticket ledger, or smart-contract audit trail evidencing the non-EU identity and location of each purchaser
  • VAT invoice and, where applicable, OSS or VIES listings

Mandatory business-to-government (B2G) e-invoicing via the Cyprus Government e-Invoicing platform based on PEPPOL and European standard EN 16931 (Law on Electronic Invoicing, 2022). No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-mandated e-reporting is in force for B2B/B2C VAT invoices in Cyprus; VAT returns and VIES EC sales lists are filed electronically through Tax For All. Standard business invoices require sequential numbering and a valid Cyprus VAT registration number.

Fraudulent export zero-rating or unjustified VAT refund claims render the full VAT amount immediately clawed back by assessment, plus statutory interest and late-addition penalties of 10% per tax period. Criminal liability arises under Cyprus VAT Law: false export declarations or forged substantiation are punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years and/or fines of up to EUR 34,000, and courts may additionally impose a penalty equal to double the VAT evaded. Retrospective reassessment is permitted for 6 years, extending to 10 years in cases of fraudulent conduct, under the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible. Cyprus applies EU Treaty freedoms under Article 63 TFEU, meaning both current account and capital account transactions are unrestricted in law. Profit remittance and dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are free, with a 0% Cyprus withholding tax on dividends paid to non-residents and no exchange control approval requirements. The only practical barriers arise from EU/UN sanctions, anti-money laundering due diligence, and the 50% deemed dividend distribution concept for Cypriot tax-resident shareholders.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Exporters may retain balances in euro or foreign currency in any licensed Cypriot bank or abroad. The CBC only requires statistical balance-of-payments reporting when material export settlements are selected for data collection.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (CBC) as the national banking and AML supervisor, operating within the European System of Central Banks and the eurozone framework; there is no separate foreign exchange administration in Cyprus.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High for EEA-licensed Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) that have passported into Cyprus under PSD2 and maintain segregated client funds. Legal constraints: an offshore EMI cannot provide payment services to Cyprus-domiciled clients unless it is licensed or passported through the CBC notification mechanism. Virtual ticket or USDC-denominated flows are additionally subject to Cyprus crypto-asset service provider (CASP) registration, and many correspondent banks reject such flows when processed through unregulated digital EMIs. Production treasury should therefore prefer a CBC-regulated bank or an EEA-regulated EMI with a confirmed Cyprus passport to avoid frozen rails.

Defense evidence: Submit a board-approved business plan and AML/KYC policy, sanctions and PEP screening procedure, token issuance and redemption mechanics, smart-contract audit where applicable, customer terms of service, third-party exchange due diligence, and a legal opinion classifying the token under Cypriot and EU law.
Cyprus company has limited local substance or lacks a physical office and employees.

Root cause: Cyprus company has limited local substance or lacks a physical office and employees.

Defense evidence: Provide a commercial lease or serviced office agreement, payroll records or director service agreements, Cyprus bank mandate, original Board meeting minutes held in Cyprus, tax residency certificate, and an economic substance narrative aligned with management and control in Cyprus.
Inbound funding immediately passes through the company to a third-country beneficiary or distributor.

Root cause: Inbound funding immediately passes through the company to a third-country beneficiary or distributor.

Defense evidence: Deliver an expected transaction-flow matrix, source-of-funds affidavit from the ultimate investor, signed commercial contracts or pro-forma invoices covering each onward payment, full KYC/AML forms for all counterparties, and a written explanation of commercial purpose linked to the virtual concert event.
Counterparty is a cryptocurrency exchange or wallet provider not regulated in Cyprus or another EU jurisdiction.

Root cause: Counterparty is a cryptocurrency exchange or wallet provider not regulated in Cyprus or another EU jurisdiction.

Defense evidence: Obtain a VASP/CASP registration certificate or MiCA authorisation evidence, audited SOC-type or security report, wallet ownership attestation, transaction-hash trail for movements of digital assets, and an independent due diligence report assessing counterparty sanctions and financial crime controls.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use direct bank transfers only. For EUR injections from the EU, use SEPA Credit Transfer into the company's Cyprus euro account; for USD or GBP injections, use SWIFT MT103 quoting purpose code or narrative such as CAPITAL INCREASE or SHAREHOLDER LOAN and referencing the Cyprus HE registration number. The originating bank must be the same as the shareholder or lender, not a third-party intermediary. CBC approval is not required, but the receiving bank will require a board resolution, signed share subscription or loan agreement, UBO declaration, source-of-funds evidence, and CRS self-certification for tax purposes.Clearing rails are TARGET2 and SWIFT. Use SWIFT MT103 for ordinary supplier and rights-holder payments; use MT700/MT707 for commercial letters of credit or documentary trade settlement; use SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant for euro clearing where speed is essential.
