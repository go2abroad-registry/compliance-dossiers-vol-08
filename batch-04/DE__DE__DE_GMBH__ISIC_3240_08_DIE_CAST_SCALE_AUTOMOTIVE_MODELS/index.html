Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Federal Central Tax Office (Bundeszentralamt für Steuern – BZSt) for VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.); local Finanzamt for the Steuernummer; electronic filing portal ELSTER (www.elster.de).

E-government filing portal

Upon notarial incorporation and registration in the Commercial Register (Handelsregister), the GmbH must file the 'Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung' (tax registration questionnaire) with the competent local Finanzamt. The Finanzamt issues the income-tax/trade-tax number (Steuernummer). Separately, an online application for the VAT identification number (USt-IdNr.) is submitted to BZSt under §27 UStG; this is required for intra-community supplies and certain cross-border services. Normal processing time is 4–8 weeks; foreign beneficial owners must submit applicable anti-money-laundering/KYC attestations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Germany has no check-the-box election. A GmbH is per se opaque and subject to corporation tax, solidarity surcharge, and trade tax on worldwide income with treaty relief. The principal statutory elections are: (i) Organschaft (fiscal unity) under §6a KStG and §2 Abs. 2 Nr. 2 UStG, requiring financial, economic, and organisational integration; (ii) VAT small-entrepreneur status under §19 UStG if turnover limits are met (prior-year EUR 22,000 / current-year EUR 50,000), though practically disadvantageous for a trading GmbH; (iii) mandatory anti-hybrid notifications/deduction disallowances under §4k EStG (no elective avoidance). Foreign hybrid mismatch elections are not available for a domestic GmbH.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Resident GmbH: no economic activity threshold; VAT registration is mandatory upon commencement of taxable business. For foreign suppliers: no threshold for domestic supplies of services/immovable work; intra-EU distance sales of goods trigger German VAT when total EU-wide distance sales exceed EUR 10,000; import VAT applies from the first euro. The small-business threshold (EUR 22,000 prior-year / EUR 50,000 current-year) is not a registration exemption for a fully incorporated GmbH operating as a going concern.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under §4 Nr. 1 in conjunction with §6 UStG applies where the supplier (GmbH) exports goods, including 1:18 scale die-cast automotive replicas, from German customs territory to a destination outside the EU, and the supplier (or an agent) ships the goods while retaining the statutory proof of export (Ausfuhrnachweis). B2B services with the place of supply in a third-country territory are also zero-rated under §4 Nr. 3 UStG if the recipient is an entrepreneur. The goods must leave the EU within the statutory dispatch period, and all transport/customs evidence must be consistent.

  • Customs Export Declaration and electronic export release (ATLAS / E-Customs / Zollbescheinigung)
  • Commercial invoice stating 0% VAT (or 'VAT not applicable – export') and Incoterms
  • Bill of Lading, Airwaybill, or CMR transport document showing departure from EU territory
  • Proof of Export (Ausfuhrnachweis) – customs endorsement or electronic confirmation in ATLAS (AE)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 credit evidence evidencing foreign payment
  • Packing list and HS/ISIC description (die-cast scale models / toys, ISIC 3240)
  • Any proof of third-country import (e.g., customs entry of destination country) where available

Germany does not currently require real-time fiscal clearance or tax-authority digital stamping. Mandatory structured e-invoicing (E-Rechnung) for domestic B2B supplies takes effect 1 January 2025 under §14 UStG. Invoices must be received and be capable of structured electronic processing in an EN 16931-compliant format (XRechnung or ZUGFeRD 2.3). VAT returns are transmitted periodically through ELSTER; there is no transaction-level API clearance as of 2025. EU ViDA measures may introduce digital reporting obligations around 2030.

The Finanzamt will retrospectively disallow export zero-rating if the documentary export proof is missing, false, or materially inconsistent. VAT at 19% is then reassessed on the invoiced export amount under §14c UStG, plus late-payment interest (0.5% per month, §233a AO). Extended assessments remain possible within the 10-year limitation period for attempted evasion. Falsifying export documents is tax evasion under §370 AO, punishable by imprisonment for 5 years (aggravated: up to 10 years) and fines. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day period, unless timely reported to Deutsche Bundesbank under the Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (§§66 AWV), risks fines up to EUR 30,000, in addition to correction of external trade statistics.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Germany operates a fully liberalized FX regime: the euro is completely convertible for both current and capital account purposes under the Außenwirtschaftsgesetz (AWG) and EU primary law (TFEU Art. 63). No exchange-control license, central bank approval, or capital transfer authorization is required for inbound share capital, shareholder loans, trade settlement, profit distribution, or equity repatriation to non-resident owners. The sole latent friction is statistical: every cross-border payment (inward or outward) exceeding EUR 12,500 or its foreign-currency equivalent must be classified with a Deutsche Bundesbank statistical purpose code and reported under Sections 67-73 AWV, either by the remitting bank via the electronic reporting procedure or by the resident company directly. Misreporting or non-reporting constitutes an administrative offense punishable by fine. GmbH dividend payments to foreign shareholders remain subject to separate German withholding tax (Kapitalertragsteuer plus solidarity surcharge) with potential treaty relief, but no FX approval is ever required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. German law imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no mandatory repatriation period for export proceeds. A German resident exporter may lawfully hold, retain, or freely dispose of foreign currency balances in Nostro accounts without any obligation to convert into EUR. The only required compliance acts are (i) statistical declaration of each export-related cross-border payment exceeding EUR 12,500 under the Bundesbank purpose-code catalogue (Warenverkehr/goods category), and (ii) customs declaration of all physical shipments of 1:18 scale die-cast models via ATLAS for extra-EU exports, supplemented by Intrastat declarations for intra-EU dispatches once the annual threshold is exceeded. Failure to maintain the statistical reporting trail is the single most common German export compliance deficiency and must be remedied by a block-audited payment-pairing procedure.

Central bank supervision: Deutsche Bundesbank (Frankfurt am Main) as the competent authority for foreign exchange statistics and balance-of-payments reporting under the German Foreign Trade and Payments Ordinance (Außenwirtschaftsverordnung - AWV); concurrently, Bundesanstalt für Finanzdienstleistungsaufsicht (BaFin) supervises banking and payments conduct, and Generalzolldirektion executes customs and cross-border cash controls.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Statutory routing protocol: (i) EU/EEA shareholders fund the GmbH initial capital via SEPA Credit Transfer (ISO 20022 pain.001), while non-EU shareholders remit via SWIFT MT103 with charges option OUR/BEN and the unambiguous beneficiary reference 'STAMMEINLAGE DE_GMBH' plus the correct Bundesbank statistical purpose code for equity/capital formation; (ii) funds must be credited to the German capital escrow account (Gründungskonto) and remain at the free disposal of the Geschäftsführer, certified by the notary's capital-contribution certificate (Kapitalaufbringungsbescheinigung) and confirmed by the company's entry in the Handelsregister; (iii) a minimum of EUR 12,500 of the EUR 25,000 statutory minimum capital must be paid in cash prior to registration, which the chosen tier-1 bank verifies against the notarial documentation; (iv) shareholder-loan financing must be documented by a notarially secure loan agreement, a capital-flow purpose code, arms'-length interest, and subordination if required, to eliminate any risk of recharacterization as hidden equity contribution; Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% Körperschaftsteuer (federal corporate income tax) plus 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag on that tax, yielding a combined federal rate of 15.825%; municipal Gewerbesteuer (trade tax) is applied at a 3.5% federal assessment base multiplied by the municipal Hebesatz (typically 340%–490%, i.e., an effective trade tax of 11.9%–17.2%), producing an aggregate German corporate tax burden of approximately 30%–33% on taxable income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Statutory rate 26.375% = 25% Kapitalertragsteuer plus 5.5% Solidaritätszuschlag on that tax (§43a(1) Nr.1, §32d EStG), withheld and remitted by the German GmbH to the Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) at the moment the profit appropriation resolution is passed. Treaty rates commonly 5% for corporate shareholders holding at least 10% of the capital (e.g., Germany–China DTA 2014: 5% if beneficial owner holds ≥10% of the capital; otherwise 10%; Germany–US DTA: 5% for ≥10% corporate shareholders, 15% otherwise). Under the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive implemented in §43b EStG, the dividend exemption (0% withholding) applies if the EU/EEA parent holds at least 10% of the GmbH’s share capital for an uninterrupted period of at least 12 months and meets the substance requirement of §50d(3) EStG.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: German documentation triggers are found in §90(3) AO and the Gewinnabgrenzungsaufzeichnungsverordnung (GAufzV). The statutory documentation trigger points are: (i) extraordinary, non-recurring cross-border transactions with a non-resident related party: documentation required when the consideration for the individual transaction exceeds EUR 100,000; and (ii) ordinary, routine business relationships with non-resident related parties (e.g., ongoing purchase of zinc alloy, tooling, tampo printing supplies, or sales of finished die-cast models): documentation required when the aggregate annual consideration of such transactions exceeds EUR 5,000,000. Below these thresholds, contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is still mandatory upon written demand by the tax office during a tax audit, and the burden of proof is reversed, with an income estimation under §162(3) AO, if documentation is not presented within 60 days for routine records and 30 days for extraordinary transactions.
