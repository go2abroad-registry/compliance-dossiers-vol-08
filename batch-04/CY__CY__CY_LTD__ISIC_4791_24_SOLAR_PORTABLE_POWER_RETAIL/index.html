Competent corporate registry

Governing statute

Companies Law, Cap. 113 (Cyprus), as amended: ss. 3-6 (incorporation, memorandum and articles), s. 11 (registrar's power to refuse misleading names), s. 34 (private company requirements), s. 119 (company records), and s. 382 (registration and fees), together with the Companies (Amendment) Law of 2007 and EU Directive (EU) 2017/1132 on company law as transposed.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form HE1 (Application for Registration of a Private Limited Company) filed together with the subscribed Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association, Form HE2 (Notice of Registered Office) and Form HE3 (Notice of Directors and Secretary); the Registrar issues the Certificate of Incorporation (HE5).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 18 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital exists under Cap. 113; the share capital is fixed by the Memorandum. A nominal authorised capital of EUR 1,000-EUR 2,000 divided into ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each is recommended. No escrow account rule applies; shares may be allotted as fully paid without cash deposit, although a one-off 0.6% capital contribution duty is levied on the market value of paid-up capital and share premium under current Cyprus tax rules, making a modest fully paid capital advisable. Non-resident founders may lawfully hold 100% of the shares without exchange-control approval.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus (Τμήμα Φορολογίας); online portal: TaxisNet (Ariadni) at taxisnet.mof.gov.cy. VAT is administered by the VAT Service (Υπηρεσία ΦΠΑ) within the Department of Taxation; export customs controls are administered by the Customs & Excise Department (Τμήμα Τελωνείων).

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company (HE77) is automatically registered for income tax upon incorporation; the Registrar of Companies notifies the Department of Taxation, and the company receives its Cyprus Tax Identification Number (TIN). VAT registration is a separate step: mandatory once taxable supplies exceed EUR 15,600 in any consecutive 12-month period, or for non-established foreign suppliers from the first euro of taxable B2C supplies or distance sales in Cyprus (unless the EUR 10,000 EU OSS threshold applies). Applications are lodged through TaxisNet or the VAT Service's prescribed registration forms; foreign companies may register as non-established taxable persons and may be required to appoint a fiscal representative in Cyprus for distance sales of goods or for electronic services. There is no minimum share capital requirement and non-resident ownership is fully permitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus has no check-the-box entity-classification election. Available elections and regimes include: (i) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) on qualifying new equity contributions at the 10-year government bond reference yield plus 10 percentage points, capped at 20%; (ii) group loss-relief election among 75%-owned Cyprus-resident and EEA-resident group entities; (iii) IP Box regime election granting an 80% exemption on qualifying intangible profits, producing an effective tax rate of 2.5%; (iv) tonnage tax election for qualifying shipping operations; (v) EU One-Stop Shop (OSS) and Import One-Stop Shop (IOSS) elections for cross-border distance sales and low-value imported consignments not exceeding EUR 150; and (vi) option-to-tax elections for immovable property transactions. Hybrid mismatch rules under the EU Anti-Tax-Avoidance Directive (ATAD) apply mandatorily, with no elective override or check-the-box mitigation available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; EUR 10,000 EU cross-border distance-sales threshold for OSS election; zero threshold for non-established taxable persons supplying goods or electronic services in Cyprus.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated VAT applies to (i) exports of goods, including portable solar battery generators and solar panels, physically dispatched or transported to destinations outside the EU, provided the goods leave EU customs territory; (ii) intra-EU dispatches to VAT-registered customers in another Member State when the customer's valid VIES-registered VAT number and Intrastat reporting requirements are satisfied; and (iii) services directly connected to exported goods and qualifying international transport services. The exporter must hold contemporaneous customs and payment documentation; physical exit must be achieved within the customs export window (normally 90 days from export declaration) and export evidence must be obtained within one year from the time of supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document - SAD) electronically filed through the EU Automated Export System (AES/ECS) using the exporter's EORI number
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical dispatch and exit from EU customs territory
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT/MT103 or bank statement) proving receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency from the overseas purchaser
  • Intrastat dispatch declaration and the customer's valid VAT identification number for intra-EU dispatches
  • Customs exit confirmation (AES/ECS export result) evidencing that the goods have left the EU customs territory
  • E-commerce fulfilment and payment gateway transaction logs, including Stripe and Affirm BNPL settlement records

Cyprus does not currently enforce a real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction-control system equivalent to the Italian SDI. Invoicing must comply with VAT Directive 2006/112/EC and the Cyprus VAT Law, including electronic invoicing integrity-and-legibility requirements for cross-border e-commerce. B2G electronic invoicing via the PEPPOL network is mandatory under the Cypriot law transposing Directive 2014/55/EU. Cyprus is preparing for the EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) mandatory e-invoicing and digital-reporting regime. E-commerce payment and settlement records (Stripe, Affirm/BNPL) must be retained on file and are accessible to the Department of Taxation for audit purposes.

Zero-rating claims are subject to post-export documentary audit by the Department of Taxation and the Customs & Excise Department, reinforced by EU platform reporting and DAC7 data. Deficient or false documentation triggers retrospective reversal of the 0% rate, assessment of unpaid VAT at the 19% standard rate, statutory late interest, and penalty assessments not exceeding twice the tax evaded where dishonest intent is established. Knowingly false export declarations carry criminal liability, fines and imprisonment, and input VAT previously credited may be clawed back. Under EU VAT rules, zero-rating is forfeited if export proof is not obtained within one year of the supply or if the goods re-enter the EU without proper declaration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus maintains full and unrestricted currency convertibility as a eurozone member; the euro is legal tender and no exchange control authorisation is required for current account or capital account flows under Article 63 TFEU (free movement of capital). Profit remittance is legally unrestricted for a Cyprus Private Limited Company: dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Cyprus withholding tax, subject to CRS/FATCA reporting and anti-money laundering due diligence. The Central Bank retains only statistical reporting obligations (Balance of Payments, International Investment Position) for transactions exceeding de minimis thresholds, plus cash declaration duties of EUR 10,000 at the border.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender rules apply. Cyprus abolished mandatory export proceeds repatriation and conversion ratios following EU accession; a resident exporter may retain export receipts indefinitely in any freely convertible currency in Cyprus or foreign bank accounts. Only balance-of-payments statistical reporting is imposed on the exporter through the Central Bank of Cyprus's electronic reporting portal for transactions above the prevailing notification threshold, and invoicing in USD, EUR or third currencies is unrestricted.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou), acting as the national monetary authority and prudential supervisor under the European System of Central Banks; foreign exchange administration falls within the Ministry of Finance and the Central Bank of Cyprus in accordance with the Exchange Control Law (Cap. 199) as historically modified and effectively abrogated upon EU accession (2004) and euro adoption (2008).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

High feasibility rating subject to PSD2 legality constraints. Digital EMIs authorised under the EU electronic money directive (2009/110/EC) in the EEA may passport into Cyprus and hold client segregated funds in a Central Bank of Cyprus-registered branch or as cross-border service providers; they may not take repayable deposits or extend credit. Use of EMIs such as Payoneer, Wise Business or Airwallex is legally permissible for merchant settlement and supplier payouts, provided the EMI holds a full EEA licence, observes CBC AML registration and the underlying treasury flows conform to the company's BOP reporting duty.
