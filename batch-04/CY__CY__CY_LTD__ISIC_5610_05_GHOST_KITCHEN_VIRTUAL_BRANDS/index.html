Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus (Tax Department); e-portal: TAXISnet (taxportal.taxdep.mof.gov.cy); Registrar of Companies for incorporation and CRN/HE registration.

E-government filing portal

For a Cyprus-incorporated Ltd, the Tax Identification Number is the Company Registration Number (CRN/HE code) issued by the Registrar of Companies; the company must additionally obtain a Taxpayer Registration Certificate and, where applicable, a VAT identification number via TAXISnet (forms TWC106 and ancillary schedules). Foreign entities not incorporated in Cyprus must apply to the Tax Department as non-resident companies, submitting certified memorandum and articles of association, identity documents of directors and shareholders, proof of registered office or authorized representative in Cyprus, and declarations of Cyprus-source income or permanent establishment activity; VAT registration is compulsory once taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a Cyprus-incorporated Ltd, the Tax Identification Number is the Company Registration Number (CRN/HE code) issued by the Registrar of Companies; the company must additionally obtain a Taxpayer Registration Certificate and, where applicable, a VAT identification number via TAXISnet (forms TWC106 and ancillary schedules). Foreign entities not incorporated in Cyprus must apply to the Tax Department as non-resident companies, submitting certified memorandum and articles of association, identity documents of directors and shareholders, proof of registered office or authorized representative in Cyprus, and declarations of Cyprus-source income or permanent establishment activity; VAT registration is compulsory once taxable turnover exceeds the statutory threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus is not a check-the-box jurisdiction; entity classification follows domestic corporate law. Available elections include Notional Interest Deduction (NID) on new equity, IP Box notional deduction (80% exemption on qualifying intellectual property income), group loss relief elections, tonnage tax election for shipping, VAT group registration, OSS/MOSS non-Union scheme election for cross-border digital services, and ATAD-compliant hybrid mismatch arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for domestic supplies of goods and services; EUR 10,000 for intra-EU distance sales and cross-border digital services under OSS; EUR 10,000 for intra-Community acquisitions by non-taxable legal persons

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported to non-EU destinations, evidenced by a customs export declaration and transport documentation; ancillary services to exported goods (loading, international transport) are zero-rated; B2B services supplied to non-EU taxable persons fall outside Cyprus VAT under place-of-supply rules; intra-EU dispatches qualify for zero-rating only where the customer provides a valid EU VAT ID and the supply is reported in the EC Sales List; domestic ghost-kitchen meal deliveries remain taxable at reduced rates (9% restaurant/catering or 5% specified foodstuffs) and do not qualify for export treatment.

  • Customs Export Declaration via EU Single Administrative Document (SAD) / AES electronic export system
  • Bill of Lading, CMR international consignment note, or Air Waybill evidencing physical transport from Cyprus to the export destination
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and purchase order identifying the foreign buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/MT103 bank credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds
  • Signed delivery and acceptance confirmation from the foreign consignee or cooperative buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or mandatory B2B/B2C e-invoicing API regime is currently enforced in Cyprus for ghost-kitchen or food-delivery operators; B2G e-invoicing to public-sector entities follows the Peppol/European standard; VAT returns are filed through TAXISnet; EU ViDA proposals for mandatory e-invoicing and real-time digital reporting are pending and not yet effective.

Cyprus VAT authorities may deny input VAT recovery and claw back refunds where export zero-rating is unsupported, incorrectly documented, or fraudulent. Re-assessments may be raised within 5 years (extended to 10 years in fraud or omission cases); statutory judicial interest accrues on overdue VAT, and administrative penalties up to twice the tax evaded may be imposed. Fraudulent export claims are also subject to criminal prosecution under Cap. 262, with fines and imprisonment. Exporters must retain the full commercial and customs documentation bundle for at least 6 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The euro is fully convertible in Cyprus; both capital and current account transactions are liberalized under Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. No exchange controls or balance-of-payment restrictions exist for Cypriot private limited companies. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and liquidation proceeds may be repatriated without quantitative limits, subject only to corporate law formalities, tax clearance certificates, transfer-pricing documentation, and standard anti-money laundering verification by the resident bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or forced conversion rules apply in Cyprus. Export receipts may be retained in EUR or in any freely convertible foreign currency, may be transferred abroad, and are not subject to mandatory conversion timelines. Reporting obligations arise only through Central Bank of Cyprus statistical data collection and EU balance-of-payments reporting thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου) acting within the Eurosystem and European Central Bank framework

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasible with material caveats. EU-licensed Electronic Money Institutions and Payment Service Providers may passport into Cyprus under PSD2, and may provide IBANs, payment cards, FX conversion, and payout collection from delivery aggregators. However, the legal viability for a Cyprus limited company is rated Medium-High: local Tier-1 banks remain necessary for EUR TARGET2 clearing, capital escrows, documentary credits, and correspondent banking through SWIFT. EMI accounts are often rejected by upper-tier counterparties and high-value marketplaces. Additional constraints include shorter preservation of funds, no lending, and higher compliance scrutiny around delivery-platform contractual chains.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound equity or shareholder-loan funding must be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the transferring bank using standardized purpose codes for inward direct investment and equity capital. Within the European Economic Area, SEPA Credit Transfer may be used for euro-denominated capital injections. The receiving Cypriot bank requires a completed CBC declaration for statistical purposes, proof of the identity of the originating party, a board resolution authorizing the capital contribution, and documentation of the share allotment or loan agreement to segregate the funds into a Capital Escrow Account until the share capital is registered. Funds should never be co-mingled with operating revenues.Supplier and subcontractor payments are cleared through SWIFT MT103 messages for international currency settlements, through SEPA Credit Transfers for EU vendors, and through Commercial Letters of Credit where import of commercial kitchen equipment or large inventories occurs. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory transfer pricing documentation is triggered where controlled non-financial transactions exceed EUR 500,000 in aggregate or per counterparty; financing transactions exceed EUR 250,000; or total controlled transactions exceed EUR 750,000, requiring preparation of Local File and Master File and filing of the annual transfer pricing summary with Form 400.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not have a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but the Cyprus Tax Commissioner applies place-of-effective-management residence, EU ATAD GAAR, CFC legislation, and transfer pricing substance jurisprudence. A delivery-only ghost kitchen must maintain real local assets and functions: a physical kitchen lease, employed chefs and delivery coordination staff, active management decisions taken in Cyprus, and direct contracts with UberEats/DoorDash under the Cyprus LTD. Without such substance, the entity can be recharacterized as a shell, tax residence may be denied, treaty benefits rejected, and profit reallocated to the economic owners at 12.5% tax.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Best-of-assessment tax on undeclared profit at 12.5%, late-payment interest of 10% per annum, and an evasion penalty of up to 100% of the additional tax; upon criminal conviction, fines up to EUR 10,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or cause the filing of a false return are jointly and severally liable; personal assets may be seized, travel bans may be imposed, and the court may disqualify such directors from holding office for up to 5 years.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization under Cyprus GAAR and EU ATAD CFC rules; denial of IP Box, treaty benefits and participation exemption; reallocation of taxable profit to the Cyprus principals at 12.5%; civil penalty of up to 50% of additional tax.

Director personal liability: Nominee and de facto directors may be personally assessed for the avoided tax, stripped of director status, and criminally convicted for knowingly facilitating a sham arrangement, with imprisonment of up to 5 years.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer-pricing adjustment to taxable income; deduction denied for the excessive portion; 10% interest on the deficiency; specific TP documentation penalty of up to EUR 5,000; repeat violations may lead to enhanced administrative fines.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve related-party intercompany contracts without economic review may be personally liable for restitution to the Cyprus company, exposed to Revenue negligence assessments, and subject to personal tax assessments in cases of deliberate mispricing.
