Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of Cyprus (Cyprus Tax Department, Ministry of Finance), administered through the 'Tax For All' electronic portal at taxportal.mof.gov.cy. The Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver issues incorporation; the Tax Department assigns the Tax Identification Code (TIC) for Cyprus Ltd companies as an integrated part of the registration file.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Cyprus Ltd (HE77) is incorporated online with the Registrar of Companies; the company's registration number functions as the tax identification code (TIC). No separate TIN application for the company exists. The Tax Department must be notified within 60 days via Form TD2001 on the Tax For All portal; a Cyprus-registered professional (auditor or tax representative) is required. Non-resident ownership and directorship are unrestricted. VAT registration is separately filed electronically (Form TD100) once taxable turnover exceeds €15,600 or voluntarily for input VAT recovery. The CySEC crowdfunding licence under Law 146(I)/2021 and EU Regulation 2020/1503 (ECSPR) must be obtained prior to operating, and the licence number referenced in the tax registration file.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Cyprus-resident companies (managed and controlled in Cyprus) are taxed at 12.5% corporate income tax on worldwide income, subject to statutory exemptions; non-resident entities are taxed on Cyprus-source income. No pass-through eligibility, unified territorial regime, or special tax-exempt industrial zone applies to Cyprus Ltd entities. — A foreign-owned Cyprus Ltd (HE77) is incorporated online with the Registrar of Companies; the company's registration number functions as the tax identification code (TIC). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — Cyprus-resident companies (managed and controlled in Cyprus) are taxed at 12.5% corporate income tax on worldwide income, subject to statutory exemptions; non-resident entities are taxed on Cyprus-source income. No pass-through eligibility, unified territorial regime, or special tax-exempt industrial zone applies to Cyprus Ltd entities.

Available election forms

Cyprus does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box regime. Available elections and mechanisms relevant to a crowdfunding portal: (1) group relief election for 75%+ subsidiaries under Art. 16 of the Income Tax Law; (2) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) on new equity capital; (3) IP Box regime with an effective ~2.5% CIT rate on qualifying intangibles; (4) VAT grouping election (Form TD500-P); (5) OSS/MOSS registration for B2C digital services to EU consumers; (6) foreign tax credit and treaty elections; (7) hybrid mismatch alignment under the ATAD-conforming provisions of the consolidated Income Tax Law as amended by Law 62(I)/2022.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€15,600 (annual taxable turnover of supplies in Cyprus) — mandatory registration within 30 days of crossing. A €10,000 threshold applies to intra-EU distance sales. Exempt financial-intermediation fees from equity crowdfunding generally do not count toward the threshold unless the entity conducts mixed taxable activities.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Cyprus to non-EU destinations qualify for 0% VAT under Art. 53 of VAT Law N.94(I)/2000. Intra-EU supplies to VAT-registered customers qualify for zero-rating only with the customer's valid EU VAT number and corresponding EC Sales List declaration. Crowdfunding intermediation services supplied to non-Cyprus taxable persons are outside the scope of Cyprus VAT (place of supply at the customer's location) and are not 'export zero-rated'; B2C digital services to EU consumers are governed by the OSS scheme. Equity-crowdfunding commission fees typically qualify as VAT-exempt financial intermediation under Art. 135(1)(d)-(f) of the EU VAT Directive, with no input VAT recovery entitlement; zero-rating is reserved for demonstrable physical export transactions only.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document — SAD)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill plus proof of exit from Cyprus/EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or alternative SWIFT MT103/MT202 payment evidence (Cyprus does not issue an official IRC)
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and underlying sale contract
  • EC Sales List and customer's valid EU VAT number for intra-Community zero-rated supplies

Mandatory B2G e-invoicing applies under EU Directive 2014/55/EU for invoices to Cyprus public authorities. B2B e-invoicing is voluntary but accepted in XML/PDF formats. VAT returns (Form 101) must be filed electronically via the Tax For All portal within 40 days of the end of each quarterly VAT period, with payment due on the same date. No real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API stamping, or digital pre-approval of invoices is currently in force; Cyprus is expected to phase in EU ViDA digital reporting and e-invoicing mandates from 2030. Platform transaction records, investor onboarding files, and supporting documentation must be retained electronically for at least six years.

No statutory FX repatriation deadline exists in Cyprus (Eurozone member; capital movements fully liberalized under Art. 63 TFEU); the value 0 denotes the absence of a mandatory deadline, although export-finance banking practice may reference a 180-day norm. Zero-rating claims are audited against customs exit evidence, remittance records, and underlying contracts. Fraudulent claims attract: retrospective assessments within 10 years (5 years in ordinary cases); monthly interest at 2% on undisclosed VAT; administrative fines up to €10,000 per offence; imprisonment up to 5 years on conviction; clawback of all zero-rated input VAT refunds and denial of future zero-rating; and, for regulated crowdfunding platforms, CySEC administrative sanctions up to €500,000 or 10% of annual turnover under Law 146(I)/2021 with possible licence suspension or revocation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Republic of Cyprus has used the euro as its sole legal tender since 1 January 2008, and the euro is fully convertible in both the current and capital accounts under Article 63 TFEU (free movement of capital) and the EU Capital Movements Directive; there are zero exchange-control authorizations, no requirement for Central Bank of Cyprus pre-approval for inward equity crowdfunding investments, and no restriction on profit remittance; outward dividends to EU/EEA shareholders are free of any Cyprus withholding tax, while dividends to third-country shareholders also incur zero Cyprus withholding tax as Cyprus does not levy such tax at source, and the only substantive prior hurdle to repatriation is the completion of corporate tax filings and CRS/FATCA reporting; for treasury purposes, the portal can freely convert EUR to any deliverable major currency and maintain multi-currency accounts with CBC-licensed banks, subject only to standard bank counterparty limits and the ECB/CBC statistical reporting regime rather than any exchange-control gate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable - Cyprus maintains no statutory export-proceeds surrender regime, no mandatory repatriation timeline for current-account receipts, and no obligation to convert any percentage of export proceeds into EUR; a Cyprus private limited company engaged in cross-border crowdfunding services retains all service revenues in EUR or foreign currency without conversion thresholds; the only administrative requirement is ECB Balance of Payments (BoP/IIP) and MIRIA statistical reporting to the Central Bank of Cyprus via the commercial bank's e-reporting channel, with BoP direct-investment and financial-account transactions reported within five business days where they exceed the applicable counterparty or transaction materiality threshold (commonly EUR 12,500 per customer per year); no timeline or ratio exists that would compel surrender of inward capital or export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Greek: Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou; Turkish: Kibris Merkez Bankasi), operating as a member of the Eurosystem and the European System of Central Banks, in coordination with the European Central Bank (ECB) on monetary policy, TARGET2 settlement, and Balance of Payments statistics collection; the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission (CySEC) acts as the supervising authority for the crowdfunding service provider licence and client-asset conduct, while the Central Bank of Cyprus remains the sole licensing, banking-supervision, and foreign-exchange-statistics authority for banks and payment/e-money institutions in the Republic of Cyprus.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Medium. EEA-authorised EMI and PI entities (Lithuanian, Maltese, or Dutch licences) can lawfully issue EUR IBANs and execute SEPA instant transfers for the portal pursuant to the EEA passport under PSD2/EMD2; however, their underwriting appetite for a High_Risk_Special_License equity crowdfunding portal is inconsistent, and most Tier-1 EMIs (e.g., Payoneer, Revolut Business, N26 Business) apply categorical prohibitions against crowdfunding and pass-through escrow models; viable corridors are limited to specialised B2B EMIs with explicit high-risk acceptance policies (e.g., ClearBank/Modulr-style rails, Transact Payments in Malta, and Cyprus-licensed EMIs supervised by the Central Bank of Cyprus); legal constraint: if the EMI is non-Cyprus, the portal must demonstrate to the CBC-licensed settlement bank that no independent payment-institution authorisation is triggered for the portal itself, and the EMI must comply with the CBC pass-through fund segregation circular, requiring a Cyprus-based principal signatory for operational control; overall rating Medium - usable as a collection-only channel, not for statutory escrow.
