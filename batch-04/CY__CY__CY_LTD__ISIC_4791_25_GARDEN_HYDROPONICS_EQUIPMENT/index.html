Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (RoC), Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Republic of Cyprus.

Governing statute

Companies Law, Cap. 113 of the Republic of Cyprus, as amended (principally ss. 3, 6, 7, 8, 29 and Table A); supplemented by Companies Law (Amendment) No. 98(I)/2015 regarding the General Register of Companies and Beneficial Owners.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association of a private limited company and Form HE1 incorporation application, filed with the RoC.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 31 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private limited company under Cap. 113, s. 8; recommended 1,000 ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each fully paid to evidence substance. No escrow account or capital verification deposit applies to general e-commerce retailing; payment rails operate purely on standard bank KYC.

Funding & unlock path: Where a physical warehouse or office is used, obtain a Fire Safety Certificate from the Cyprus Fire Service under the Fire Defence Law, a structural load inspection certificate under the Streets and Buildings Regulations for the chosen premises, and a workplace health-and-safety registration with the Labour Inspection Department under the Factories (Health, Safety and Welfare) Law; employer registration at the Social Insurance Services is activated before any staff recruitment.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private limited company under Cap. 113, s. 8; recommended 1,000 ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each fully paid to evidence substance. No escrow account or capital verification deposit applies to general e-commerce retailing; payment rails operate purely on standard bank KYC.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Consumer Protection Service, Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Republic of Cyprus (for distance-selling compliance); Department of Electronic Communications and Postal Regulation (OCECPR), Ministry of Transport, Communications and Works (only where hydroponics equipment contains a radio transceiver module); EU Single Market free circulation competences otherwise apply.

Category: No sector-specific operating permit is required for online retail distribution of garden hydroponics equipment under Cypriot or EU law; general economic freedom applies under Articles 49 and 56 TFEU. If a device contains Wi-Fi, ZigBee or Bluetooth control, the product module must be notified to OCECPR under Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU, and CE-marking under the New Legislative Framework (Regulation (EC) 765/2008) becomes a product-compliance obligation, not a company licence.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation (Τμήμα Φορολογίας) of the Republic of Cyprus — Ministry of Finance; e-filing and VAT administration via the Tax Department e-Services portal (taxportal.mof.gov.cy); export customs procedures administered separately by the Cyprus Customs Department.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Cyprus incorporation: a Cyprus Ltd receives its Tax Identification Number (TIN) automatically through the Registrar of Companies; no separate TIN application is required for a Cyprus-resident company. Step 2 — Foreign entity or branch: the non-resident seller files Form TD2001 (Foreign Company / Branch Registration) through the Tax Department e-Services portal or at the District Tax Office, attaching certified constitutional documents, certificate of good standing from the home registry, board resolution appointing a local tax representative, and notarized power of attorney; the Tax Department issues the 8-digit TIN upon complete filing. Step 3 — VAT registration: the entity must file VAT-101 (Cyprus-established) or the non-established taxable person registration form where it makes or intends to make taxable supplies in Cyprus; registration is effective from the first domestic taxable supply or establishment of a fixed establishment. Step 4 — Alternative for distance sales: non-resident e-commerce sellers may rely on the Union OSS (intra-EU distance sales) or IOSS (third-country imports up to EUR 150) instead of a Cypriot VAT ID, although a CY VAT number remains mandatory for goods warehoused or delivered from Cyprus.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus Ltd is a distinct taxable person; tax residence is based on management and control in Cyprus; resident companies are taxed on worldwide income at 12.5% CIT; non-resident companies with a Cyprus permanent establishment are taxed only on PE-attributable Cyprus-source income; no tax-transparent classification is available for a Ltd under Cyprus law. — Step 1 — Cyprus incorporation: a Cyprus Ltd receives its Tax Identification Number (TIN) automatically through the Registrar of Companies; no separate TIN application is required for a Cyprus-resident company. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus Ltd is a distinct taxable person; tax residence is based on management and control in Cyprus; resident companies are taxed on worldwide income at 12.5% CIT; non-resident companies with a Cyprus permanent establishment are taxed only on PE-attributable Cyprus-source income; no tax-transparent classification is available for a Ltd under Cyprus law.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election. Hybrid mismatch treatment follows EU ATAD implemented by the Income Tax Law amendments (Laws 117(I)/2020 and 58(I)/2022). Available elections: (i) Cyprus IP Box one-off election granting an 80% qualifying deduction; (ii) Notional Interest Deduction election on new equity; (iii) group loss relief election; (iv) VAT grouping election under the VAT Law; (v) advance tax ruling procedure to confirm classification and fiscal treatment of hybrid instruments and cross-border structures before filing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for Cyprus-established taxable persons; no registration threshold for non-established taxable persons making taxable supplies in Cyprus; intra-EU distance sales of goods may be declared under the Union OSS once the EUR 10,000 EU-wide threshold is exceeded; IOSS applies to B2C imports of consignments not exceeding EUR 150.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Cyprus VAT Law implementing Article 146 of Directive 2006/112/EC, the 0% rate applies to: (1) goods exported by the supplier or for its account to a destination outside the EU, physically leaving Cyprus or the EU within three months of supply; (2) services connected with exported goods, including international transport, loading, handling and customs agency; and (3) services supplied where the place of supply is outside the EU, including B2C digital services. For the exporter of garden-hydroponics equipment, the zero rate is allowed only where the goods are physically delivered to a non-EU customer with transport evidence; dispatch to a Cyprus or EU address, even if invoiced to a foreign customer, is taxable at 19%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD / Electronic Export System exit confirmation with movement reference)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR consignment note evidencing physical departure from Cyprus and arrival in the third country
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 payment confirmation from the foreign buyer

Mandatory e-invoicing under Law 63(I)/2024 based on the European standard EN 16931: B2G invoices from 1 October 2024; B2B/B2C e-invoicing phased in for large businesses during the 2025 roll-out and for all Cyprus-established VAT traders no later than 1 May 2026 (as adjusted by Tax Department announcements). Invoices must be exchanged through certified PEPPOL access points using UBL or CII syntax and include the full invoice reference; Cyprus does not operate real-time tax authority clearance, digital fiscal stamping, or a continuous transaction control engine. The Tax Department performs post-baseline audit matching using VAT returns and e-invoice data, with a Cypriot SAF-T reporting framework under development.

The Cyprus Tax Department and Customs Department conduct post-clearance and desk audits that reconcile AES export declarations, VAT returns, transport documents and FX remittances. If the statutory export bundle is not produced, or the goods did not actually leave the EU, the zero rating is retrospectively clawed back and assessed at 19% VAT, plus statutory interest at 0.5% per month (6% per annum) and administrative penalties. Fraudulent or abusive zero-rating claims, including round-tripping, fake export documentation or artificial non-EU invoicing, are prosecuted as tax evasion with criminal fines up to double the tax evaded and imprisonment for the responsible officers; in fraud cases the assessment period is extended up to 10 years, and the local VAT representative and directors may be jointly and severally liable.
