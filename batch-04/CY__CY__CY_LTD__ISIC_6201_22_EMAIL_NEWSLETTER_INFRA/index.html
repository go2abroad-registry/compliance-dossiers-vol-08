Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus, Ministry of Finance; VAT registration and administration coordinated with the Department of Customs and Excise of the Republic of Cyprus. E-filing portal: Cypriot Tax Portal 'Ariadne' (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy).

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Cyprus private limited companies obtain a Certificate of Incorporation from the Registrar of Companies, which issues the HE company number. The company must then register in the Tax Department's Company Tax Register within 60 days of commencing business to receive the Cyprus Tax Identification Number (TIC), which for a Cyprus Ltd is aligned with the HE registration number. VAT registration requires submission of Form VAT 101 to the Department of Customs and Excise, together with the company formation documents, shareholders' and directors' details, registered office address, evidence of actual or expected taxable turnover, and identity documents of the signatory. A foreign entity without a Cyprus fixed establishment may also be required to appoint a tax representative for VAT and duty compliance. Upon successful registration the Tax Department issues a VAT registration number beginning with the prefix 'CY' followed by the HE number.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. A Cyprus private limited company is a separate taxable legal entity. Cyprus tax-resident companies are subject to corporation tax at 12.5% on worldwide income; non-resident companies are externally taxable only on Cyprus-source income. No pass-through status is available to private limited companies, and no general entity classification election exists under Cypriot direct tax law.

Available election forms

Cyprus does not apply the US-style check-the-box entity classification system. Available statutory elections and relief mechanisms relevant to corporate taxpayers include group relief election under the Income Tax Law, election for notional interest deduction on new equity, election to offset trading losses against profits of the same or preceding years, terminal loss relief elections, and EU ATAD compliant hybrid mismatch neutralisation arrangements. For indirect tax, eligible taxable persons may elect for VAT grouping under the Cyprus VAT Law, elect to use the special scheme for electronically supplied services where applicable under EU OSS rules, and may elect for annual VAT accounting in limited qualifying circumstances.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility. Cyprus adopted the euro in January 2008 and, as an EU Member State, is bound by the free movement of capital under Article 63 TFEU. Current and capital account transactions are fully liberalised: there are no exchange controls, no prior FX licensing, no repatriation restrictions and no differentiated regimes for resident versus non-resident shareholders. Profit remittance in the form of dividends is unlimited, carries zero Cyprus withholding tax for non-resident shareholders, and requires no Central Bank approval; payment is executed by ordinary wire through the commercial banking system. All post-2013 crisis emergency capital controls (Decretos) were fully lifted by April 2015 and no residual banking or FX restrictions remain. Cross-border transfers are monitored solely for Anti-Money Laundering screening and for Central Bank of Cyprus balance-of-payments statistical collection under Regulation (EC) No 184/2005, not for convertibility approval.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Cyprus imposes no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for the repatriation or surrender of export proceeds. A Cyprus-incorporated exporter may retain foreign-currency receipts in USD, GBP or other denominations in a commercial bank foreign-currency account, may convert them to EUR at the market rate, or may hedge them. The only compliance burden is statistical: the receiving bank must report external payments equal to or exceeding EUR 12,500 to the Central Bank of Cyprus balance-of-payments collection framework, and must apply standard AML verification of the issuing counterparty. No export-licence or FX permit is triggered for email-infrastructure or SaaS services.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα Κύπρου / Kentriki Trapeza Kyprou), acting within the Eurosystem and subject to the European Central Bank's single monetary policy for EUR foreign-exchange and reserves operations; the Central Bank of Cyprus is the licensed credit institution supervisor, sole authority for bank licensing/AML inspections, and the national competent authority for balance-of-payments and external statistics reporting in Cyprus.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM as a supplementary collection and settlement rail, not as a substitute for a local Cypriot bank relationship. Cyprus-licensed Electronic Money Institutions are supervised by the Central Bank of Cyprus under the Electronic Money Law, 2012 (Law 88(I)/2012) and PSD2; EEA passporting EMIs are lawful in Cyprus without a branch licence and may provide EUR IBANs for SEPA traffic. Legal constraints: funds held at an EMI are e-money claims, not bank deposits, and are not covered by the Cyprus Deposit Protection Fund; onshore Cypriot Tier-1 banks generally classify unlicensed or non-bank payment institutions as higher-risk counterparties, which may trigger enhanced due diligence or rejection of payments originating from offshore fintech accounts. For this ISIC 6201 email-newsletter SaaS, an EU-licensed EMI is compliantly viable for receiving Stripe/credit-card settlement flows and for paying low-value EU suppliers, but must be ring-fenced from the primary treasury relationship and disclosed to the Cypriot relationship manager to avoid a negative AML perception.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) the Cyprus board passes a written resolution approving the share issuance, contribution or shareholder loan; (2) the remitting corporate treasury executes a SWIFT MT103 (or SEPA credit transfer for EUR) to the Cyprus company's EUR IBAN at its Tier-1 Cypriot bank; (3) the remitting bank must populate the purpose/reference field with a clear economic descriptor such as 'Share Capital Contribution – EGM Resolution dated DD.MM.YYYY' or 'Intercompany Shareholder Loan Agreement dated DD.MM.YYYY'; (4) the receiving Cypriot bank records the balance-of-payments statistical purpose code for equity capital or intercompany financing and, for any payment equal to or above EUR 12,500, submits the required Central Bank of Cyprus BoP return; (5) the Cyprus company delivers to the bank a source-of-funds declaration identifying the remitter, the UBO of the remitter, the relationship to the company, and copies of the board resolution, the shareholder register update and any loan agreement. No exchange-control permit, central-bank approval or prior licence is required for any component of this routing; verification is purely AML/statistical.EUR-denominated disbursements to EU/EEA beneficiaries clear via SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (IBAN-to-IBAN) with final settlement in TARGET2 central-bank money. Non-EUR or non-SEPA payouts are executed by SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers through the Cypriot bank's correspondent network (typically Citi, J.P. Morgan, Standard Chartered for USD, and major London clearers for GBP), using SWIFT gpi for real-time tracking. Trade settlements requiring documentary assurance are issued by the Cypriot bank's trade-finance desk as Commercial Letters of Credit transmitted by SWIFT MT700, with amendments via MT707 and reimbursement/telex releases per UCP 600. For the SaaS email-newsletter model, recurring vendor bills, refunds and subscription revenue should be consolidated through the local operational account, with a segregated escrow or capital account reserved for statutory capital and share-premium held until the project capex is clear.Outright FX Forward contracts (deliverable) in freely convertible pairs — EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/CHF — transacted with the Cyprus Tier-1 bank under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or bank master-forward confirmation, used exclusively to hedge forecast SaaS/email-infra subscription receivables or supplier payables.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs) referencing restricted or non-convertible currencies (e.g., CNY, INR, NGN, KRW) permitted only where the company holds a documented commercial exposure to customers/suppliers in those currencies; settlement is in EUR via the executing bank and the NDF must align with the Cyprus group treasury policy on speculative-transaction prohibition.Cross-Currency Swaps (EUR-USD, EUR-GBP, EUR-CHF) permissible for hedging both principal and interest flows on shareholder loans or intercompany balances; require a collateral arrangement (ISDA CSA or credit-support annex) and a commercial end-user designation, not a CySEC licence, because the Cyprus company hedges its own proprietary exposure rather than providing investment services to third parties.
