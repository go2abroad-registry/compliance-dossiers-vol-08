Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Cyprus law requires a registered office address in Cyprus at all times. For an ISIC 6420 pure equity holding SPV, a virtual office address or registered-agent address can satisfy the strict statutory minimum in some administrative contexts, but it is not sufficient to establish Cyprus tax residence or treaty eligibility under the management-and-control test. A purely passive equity-holding SPV should have a real physical registered office in Cyprus, substantive local administrative records, and a credible local management structure. A licensed CySEC entity cannot rely on a virtual office. For this unlicensed holding SPV, virtual office is permissible only as a temporary/administrative accommodation and will heighten AML and tax-substance review.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cyprus law. The company may incorporate with a nominal subscribed share capital of at least EUR 1.00. There is no statutory escrow or capital verification requirement; subscribed capital may be deposited in the company's Cyprus corporate bank account upon incorporation.

Funding & unlock path: Office premises require a Fire Safety Compliance Certificate issued by the Cyprus Fire Service following an inspection, a valid structural occupancy permit from the local municipality, and periodic maintenance of fire extinguishers. No industrial workplace clearance is required for a passive holding company.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Cyprus law. The company may incorporate with a nominal subscribed share capital of at least EUR 1.00. There is no statutory escrow or capital verification requirement; subscribed capital may be deposited in the company's Cyprus corporate bank account upon incorporation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a fully euroised jurisdiction. The euro is freely convertible for both capital and current account transactions. No exchange control restrictions apply to cross-border transfers to EU/EEA recipients, and only limited monitoring obligations apply to extra-EU payments under EU free-capital-movement rules. Profit remittances can be distributed as dividends without any foreign-exchange authorisation, subject only to Cyprus corporate formalities and beneficial 0% domestic withholding tax under the Parent-Subsidiary Directive. Banks may apply internal anti-money-laundering screening, but no governmental conversion or approval requirement exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender rule exists in Cyprus. Exporters may retain export receipts in any convertible currency, and there is no mandatory conversion ratio or repatriation timeline. All proceeds must be properly recorded in corporate books and accepted for tax purposes in accordance with ordinary Greek accounting standards. Transfer pricing and thin capitalisation rules remain applicable; no certificate of surrender is required by the Central Bank of Cyprus.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), operating within the European System of Central Banks and subject to the monetary policy and supervisory framework of the European Central Bank

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Cyprus permits locally authorised Electronic Money Institutions and EU passporting under the E-Money Directive, but offshore digital EMIs without a Cyprus licence cannot provide settlement or cleared rails for capital injections, dividend flows, or intercompany debt in a manner acceptable to tier-1 financial institutions. EMIs may be used for small operational payments, but for this pure equity holding SPV, SWIFT-capable commercial banks are operationally and regulatory required as the primary treasury rail. Legal constraints include AML transaction thresholds, restrictions on correspondent banking, and elevated verification burdens for non-EU EMI counterparties.

Defense evidence: Detailed treasury business plan, board-approved investment mandate, underlying share purchase and cooperation contracts, supporting invoices, legal opinion from Cyprus counsel, and a full purpose-code narrative for each SWIFT field 70 remittance instruction
Lack of economic substance: registered office only, no local directors, no local employees, no actual operational footprint

Root cause: Lack of economic substance: registered office only, no local directors, no local employees, no actual operational footprint

Defense evidence: Tenancy and lease agreement for the Cyprus registered office, local director appointment deeds and consent letters, outsourced corporate administration agreements with a licensed service provider, and an economic substance file demonstrating compliance with Cyprus, EU, and OECD requirements, including projected payroll and local management activity

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route all inbound capital injections using SWIFT MT103 (or TARGET2 for euro transfers) to the Cyprus IBAN with the exact corporate legal name as beneficiary. Use ISO 20022 purpose code 'EQUI' where offered, otherwise the EPC remittance narrative 'Shareholder equity injection under Investment Agreement dated [date]'. Provide concurrently to the remitting bank a certified board resolution affirming the contribution, a signed deed of contribution or share subscription agreement, and a statement of no adverse encumbrance. For extra-EU payments, include full correspondent banking details and complete legal address of the beneficial owner. Ensure the ultimate funding party's bank applies standard AML verification and the Cyprus receiving bank obtains the remitter's passport and financial statements for funds-above-EUR-50,000.For operational disbursements and downstream investments, use SWIFT MT103 for direct settlement. For trade needs, use Documentary Letters of Credit (MP700) where the underlying flow is commercial and collateralised. For dividends and intercompany loans, SWIFT MT103 with ISO 20022 purpose codes 'DIVD' (dividend), 'INTC' (intercompany payment), or 'LOAN' (repayment of principal), accompanied by board minutes and intercompany agreements.
