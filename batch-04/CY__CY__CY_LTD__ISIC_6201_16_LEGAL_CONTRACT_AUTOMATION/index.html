Competent corporate registry

Department of Registrar of Companies and Intellectual Property Rights (RCIPR), Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Nicosia, Cyprus

Governing statute

The Companies Law, Cap. 113 (Cyprus), as amended; ss. 3-12 (incorporation, memorandum, share capital, registration and certificate of incorporation), ss. 118-119 (directors and secretary), s. 201 and s. 202 (registered office), and Table A (Articles of Association).

Formation / constitutional filings

Registration Folder comprising Form HE1 (Application for Registration), Memorandum and Articles of Association, and Form HE2 (Statutory Declaration of Compliance); the issued instrument is the Certificate of Incorporation.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 29 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up share capital or statutory escrow deposit is required for a private limited company under Cap. 113. Standard practice is an authorised and issued share capital of EUR 1,000 (1,000 ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each); banking institutions commonly expect EUR 1,000-2,000 to be paid up for corporate account opening, and no escrow account is mandated.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification is issued by the Cyprus Fire Service of the Ministry of Justice and Public Order; for a low-risk office the employer must complete a fire-risk assessment and, where the premises exceed the thresholds in the Fire Safety Law, obtain a fire certificate prior to occupancy. Under the Safety and Health at Work Law, L. 89(I)/1996, as amended, the employer must commission a workplace risk assessment and obtain the compliance clearance of the Department of Labour Inspection. No structural load inspection is triggered where the registered office occupies an already-certified commercial building under standard office loading.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up share capital or statutory escrow deposit is required for a private limited company under Cap. 113. Standard practice is an authorised and issued share capital of EUR 1,000 (1,000 ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each); banking institutions commonly expect EUR 1,000-2,000 to be paid up for corporate account opening, and no escrow account is mandated.

Not applicable — ISIC 6201 (computer programming, cloud document automation and e-signature authentication) is a non-industrial, non-manufacturing service activity. Category: Not applicable — no industrial operating permit or manufacturing licence is required. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; official portal: Tax Portal (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy); e-filing via Ariadni system (https://ariadni.mof.gov.cy); VAT ID validated on VIES.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Cyprus Limited Company is first incorporated with the Registrar of Companies, obtaining an HE registration number. The company is then automatically issued an 8-digit Tax Identification Number (TIN) upon submission of Form IR101 (Commencement of Business) to the Tax Commissioner. For VAT, every taxable person engaged in taxable economic activity must register on Form F.101 via the Ariadni portal; non-established EU and third-country persons must register regardless of turnover. The VAT registration number comprises CY + 8 digits + 1 letter (e.g., CY12345678L). Processing takes 15-30 days and requires certified memorandum/articles of association, certificate of incorporation, registered office address, board resolution, passports of directors/shareholders, and a projection of Cyprus economic activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: a Cyprus-resident company is taxed on worldwide income at 12.5% corporate income tax; a non-resident company is taxed only on Cyprus-source income. Limited liability company is not pass-through. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: a Cyprus-resident company is taxed on worldwide income at 12.5% corporate income tax; a non-resident company is taxed only on Cyprus-source income. Limited liability company is not pass-through. Key statutory reliefs include the dividend participation exemption, foreign PE exemption, Capital Gains Tax limited to Cyprus immovable property and shares in companies owning Cyprus immovable property, and Notional Interest Deduction on new equity under Section 9C of Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002.

Available election forms

Group relief election under Section 31 of Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 available to companies resident in Cyprus with 75% direct or indirect shareholding; election for unilateral foreign tax credit under Section 36; Notional Interest Deduction election under Section 9C; election for Cyprus Tonnage Tax (maritime) and IP Box regime under Sections 13-14; intra-group transfer of losses is elective; EU ATAD hybrid mismatch rules are implemented and impose mandatory disclosure and denial of deductions on hybrid arrangements not consistently treated; no US-style check-the-box election exists in Cyprus law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for supplies of goods; EUR 7,800 for services; nil threshold for non-established taxable persons; EUR 10,000 intra-EU distance sales/digital services cross-border threshold triggers registration in the destination member state via the OSS scheme

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to destinations outside the EU are zero-rated under Article 58 of the Cyprus VAT Law, subject to proof of physical departure within 90 days of supply. Cross-border supplies of services to business customers outside the EU, including SaaS, cloud document automation, e-signature authentication, and software licensing, are zero-rated under Article 62 B2B place-of-supply rule, provided the supplier obtains the customer's foreign business registration number and two independent items of evidence of the customer's non-EU establishment. Intra-EU B2B supplies are zero-rated upon validation of the customer's EU VAT number and inclusion in the EC Sales List.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document lodged via the EU AIS/ICS2 system)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / CMR consignment note evidencing physical dispatch
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (SWIFT MT103 / bank account credit advice)
  • Export manifest, packing list, and supplier purchase order
  • Retained digital delivery logs, geolocation data, and non-EU customer registration evidence

Cyprus mandates PEPPOL BIS 3.0 e-invoicing for business-to-government supplies following transposition of EU Directive 2014/55/EU; full continuous transaction controls and near-real-time digital reporting for intra-EU B2B supplies are being phased in pursuant to the EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) agreement, with mandatory e-invoicing expected from 1 July 2030; no real-time fiscal stamping API is currently operative for SaaS/software services, but digital service providers must retain full transactional logs and IP-location evidence for audit purposes.

Unjustified zero-rating claims are subject to retroactive assessment by the Tax Commissioner for up to 10 years in cases of fraud or willful default, and 6 years otherwise; the tax department claws back the VAT not accounted for plus statutory interest at 2.75% per annum, and imposes administrative penalties of up to 200% of the tax expended under Section 46 of the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law 4/1978. Criminal conviction for VAT fraud may result in imprisonment for up to 5 years and fines up to EUR 150,000. The Tax Department systematically cross-checks customs export declarations against the ECS system, bank FX remittance records, and corporate tax returns to detect sham export schemes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a full Eurozone member (Euro adopted on 1 January 2008). There is no regulatory distinction between capital-account and current-account convertibility; the euro is fully and freely convertible, and all EU/EEA capital movements are unrestricted under Article 64 TFEU and the Cypriot Exchange Control Law (Cap. 93, repealed in 1994). The emergency restrictive measures imposed in March 2013 were completely lifted effective 6 April 2015. Profit and dividend remittance is not subject to exchange-control authorisation, central bank approval, or statistical prior permit; repatriation is subject only to standard CRS/FATCA disclosure obligations, the solvency and distribution tests under the Cyprus Companies Law (Cap. 113, s.169), and AML/KYC verification of the ultimate beneficial owner. Intra-EU inbound and outbound EUR payments settle freely in TARGET2 via SEPA.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Cyprus imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts, and no prescribed timeline for repatriating foreign-currency export revenue. Export settlements for ISIC 6201 SaaS licensing may be retained in EUR or foreign currency in any Cyprus-licensed credit institution or EU/EEA-passporting bank. ECB/CRD IV foreign-currency open-position limits apply exclusively to prudentially supervised credit institutions, not to non-financial corporate exporters. No Central Bank of Cyprus filing or approval is required for export repatriation; the only obligations are mandatory euro-area balance-of-payments statistical reporting (ITRS/ESS codes) through the reporting bank for transactions exceeding the EUR 12,500 threshold.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Greek: Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου; Turkish: Kıbrıs Merkez Bankası), operating as the national monetary authority, resolution authority, and prudential supervisor under the ECB Single Supervisory Mechanism within the Eurosystem.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High, subject to two mandatory conditions. First, the EMI must hold a valid Electronic Money Institution authorisation in the EEA (ESMA/EBA passport) and, if operating in Cyprus, must have notified the Central Bank of Cyprus under Article 18 PSD2. Second, the EMI may hold only an operational/turnover sub-account; it cannot serve as the exclusive account for subscribed share capital, statutory audit confirmations, or creditor-protection escrow under Cap. 113. Cyprus-licensed FinTech EMIs are functional for cheap EUR clearing, corporate card issuance, and API-driven liquidity routing. Offshore (non-EEA) EMIs without EEA passporting are not recognised as credit institutions under the Cyprus Banking Law (Cap. 105); channelling client deposits or share capital through an unlicensed EMI constitutes unlawful deposit-taking under Articles 2 and 30 Cap. 105 and will be rejected in annual CBI/IFU licensing or CySEC capital verification. Mitigation: maintain Bank of Cyprus or Hellenic Bank as the Tier-1 relationship bank and use an EEA-passporting EMI solely for payment-acceptance pass-through.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Routing protocol: SWIFT MT103 with TARGET2/SEPA EUR settlement for value dating on T+1, using the ordering bank domiciled in an EU/EEA jurisdiction to avoid extra correspondent layers. Required purpose-code classification under the Central Bank of Cyprus ITRS/ESS statistical framework: use 'Direct investment in Cyprus resident enterprise — Equity capital / Share capital subscription' (BPM6 category DI3), with narration field 72 containing 'CY_LTD HE77 Share Capital Injection — UBO CY_LTD'. The confirmation ticket must include the corporate authorisation references (board resolution, share allocation, UBO register file) for the bank's transfer audit. Where the incoming transfer exceeds EUR 100,000, banks automatically trigger an EDD review of the beneficial owner and destination account; the company must hold the documentary defence dossier pre-cleared with the Compliance Officer before the wire crosses, which reduces hold time to 24 to 48 hours. EUR inbound from an EEA bank may also use SEPA SCT instant where the remitter holds a European IBAN, but a MT103 is preferred for audit trail and because the SEPA message cannot carry the formal BPM6 purpose code required by the Central Bank of Cyprus.Clearing rails for the LegalTech SaaS model: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct credit for B2B subscription refunds, vendor payments, intercompany fees, and director remunerations, utilising the Central Bank of Cyprus ITRS code for 'computer services' (ISIC 6201 / software licensing services), not a goods-trade code; (2) SEPA SCT for EUR-only disbursements to EEA beneficiaries, executed on the same calendar day with the ISO 20022 'company name + invoice reference' tag for reconciliation; (3) card-network rails (Visa/Mastercard) via the Stripe or co-acquiring merchant account for end-consumer refunds and chargebacks, with the merchant record showing the precise SaaS service description and the appropriate MCC 5734 or equivalent software-services MCC; (4) Commercial Letter of Credit (SWIFT MT700) is not economically relevant for a digital SaaS exporter but remains available for later physical procurements; where used, it must be irrevocable and confirmed by a EU/EEA issuing bank, with presentation documents reduced to freight-invoice/customs declarations. All outbound USD-denominated disbursements above EUR 10,000-equivalent must include the exact purpose code and the underlying contract/purchase-order number in field 70 of the MT103 to avoid anti-money-laundering intervention.OTC outright FX forward (EUR/USD, EUR/GBP, EUR/CHF) executed with an EU/EEA MiFID II investment firm or Cyprus-licensed credit institution under an ISDA 2002 master agreement, with mandatory EMIR reporting to a registered trade repository (non-financial counterparty exemption from clearing where volumes stay below the EUR 3 billion threshold).Non-deliverable forward (NDF) in USD/EUR for exposures to non-euro sovereign counterparties in jurisdictions with restricted convertibility, permitted only where the counterparty is an EU-authorised bank and the contract is cash-settled in EUR; must be documented as a hedging, not speculative, instrument in treasury policy.Cross-currency swap (EUR/USD) for balance-sheet hedging of intercompany loans or proposed USD-denominated share capital injections, executed under an ISDA/CSA, with swap payments netted through the Tier-1 clearing bank; internal treasury policy must require sensitivity analysis at +/−5% FX movement.Plain-vanilla bought FX options (zero-premium collars and call-spreads) for downside protection on receivables, permitted where the option is purchased from an EU-authorised counterparty and the company holds no net short option position; writing of naked options is not permitted under standard MiFID II client-appropriateness assessment for a non-financial corporate.Microhedge execution via single-bank electronic foreign-exchange platforms (e.g., 360T, LSEG FX matching) of the Tier-1 bank, limited to spot outrights and same-day forwards with maximum tenor of 12 months, aligned to the natural cash-conversion cycle of Stripe settlement (T+2 settlement, 180-day refund window).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions in a tax year, triggering mandatory preparation of the Local File and the submission of the TP Summary with the TD4 return; a Master File is required when the worldwide group's consolidated turnover exceeds EUR 750 million.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) registration as in the UAE or Bermuda; however, substance is rigorously enforced through: (i) management and control rules for tax residency, (ii) the ATAD-aligned General Anti-Abuse Rule (GAAR), (iii) Cyprus CFC rules, (iv) the nexus requirement of the IP Box regime, and (v) the 'sufficient substance' condition for the Notional Interest Deduction and treaty benefits. The LegalTech SaaS entity must maintain an office, qualified staff, board meetings held in Cyprus, and strategic decision-making in Cyprus; otherwise the Tax Department may re-domesticate the company abroad, deny the TRC, and reclassify profits as subject to full 12.5% taxation without reliefs.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Income Tax Law (L.118(I)/2002) and the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law, conviction is punishable by a fine not exceeding EUR 5,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 3 years; the Civil Tax Debt is assessed with a 50% penalty plus statutory interest of 1.75% above EURIBOR; the company is also debarred from holding CySEC or public-subsidy licences while a conviction stands.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false tax returns are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties; courts may impose travel bans and issue arrest warrants; each director faces imprisonment of up to 3 years per false declaration; the corporate veil is pierced under Section 305 of the Companies Law, Cap. 113, ordering personal restitution of diverted funds.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the company's tax residence to the management-and-control jurisdiction, nullifying Cyprus residence and the 12.5% regime; denial of Tax Residence Certificate and consequent loss of treaty benefits; a Cyprus assessment on the repatriated profits (12.5% plus 10% non-filing surcharge) despite reclassification; deregistration or strike-off from the Cyprus Registrar of Companies as a non-performing entity.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the tax differences if they acted in bad faith; an international travel ban and asset freeze may be imposed under the Tax Procedures Law; the directors can be disqualified from managing any Cyprus company for 3 years; each director is additionally exposed to criminal liability for the offence of aiding non-tax-resident trading.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: A formal transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length profit; an immediate 10% discretionary penalty on the adjustment; interest on the full additional assessment from the original due date; rejection of the Local File if not contemporaneous, thereby shifting the burden of proof to the taxpayer; repeated mispricing can trigger an audit of all open years and a 50% deliberate-penalty.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the TP Summary and knowingly certify non-arm's length pricing are subject to a personal fine of EUR 3,500 and/or imprisonment of up to 3 years; if group funds have been shifted overseas, the directors are liable in civil restitution to the company's creditors; under the savings clause of the Cyprus TP law, the personal assets of directors can be seized for the tax difference.
AML Violation and Unlicensed Payment Handling

Statutory sanction: Breach of the Cyprus Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing Laws (L.188(I)/2007, as amended) exposes the entity to a corporate fine of up to EUR 500,000, a freezing order on all bank accounts, and a temporary ban on the provision of financial or SaaS processing services; the Central Bank of Cyprus may revoke the payment-processing permission of any relevant banking counterparty handling the proceeds.

Director personal liability: Every director and the appointed AML Compliance Officer may be personally fined up to EUR 100,000 and/or imprisoned for up to 5 years; the court may issue a travel ban and residential seizure orders; directors can be ordered to personally compensate the frozen funds where they caused the company to continue transacting with the flagged counterparties; a serious AML conviction triggers personal insolvency and prohibition from serving as a director of any EU financial entity.
