Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus — official portal: https://tax.mof.gov.cy; VAT and ARIADNE e-service portal: https://mece-reporting.mof.gov.cy

E-government filing portal

For a foreign entity establishing a Cyprus Ltd: (1) incorporate with the Registrar of Companies; (2) the Registrar automatically notifies the Tax Department, which issues an income tax TIN upon finalisation of incorporation and registered-office confirmation; (3) for VAT, file the prescribed VAT registration form (Form TD0700/VAT101) with the District Tax Office or via ARIADNE/online portal; a non-EU established enterprise must appoint a Cyprus tax representative; (4) submit certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, notice of registered office, details of directors/shareholders, and projected Cyprus taxable turnover; (5) if registering for intra-EU/OSS operations, separately apply for the One Stop Shop (OSS) scheme via the Cyprus Tax Portal and provide evidence of existing TIN/VAT ID from the home Member State.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Cyprus-resident companies are taxed as separate legal entities on worldwide profits; non-resident companies are taxed on Cyprus-source income only; no composite taxation or look-through treatment available to Ltd companies. — For a foreign entity establishing a Cyprus Ltd: (1) incorporate with the Registrar of Companies; (2) the Registrar automatically notifies the Tax Department, which issues an income tax TIN upon finalisation of incorporation and registered-office confirmation; (3) for VAT, file the prescribed VAT registration form (Form TD0700/VAT101) with the District Tax Office or via ARIADNE/online portal; a non-EU established enterprise must appoint a Cyprus tax representative; (4) submit certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, notice of registered office, details of directors/shareholders, and projected Cyprus taxable turnover; (5) if registering for intra-EU/OSS operations, separately apply for the One Stop Shop (OSS) scheme via the Cyprus Tax Portal and provide evidence of existing TIN/VAT ID from the home Member State.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Cyprus-resident companies are taxed as separate legal entities on worldwide profits; non-resident companies are taxed on Cyprus-source income only; no composite taxation or look-through treatment available to Ltd companies.

Available election forms

Cyprus does not maintain an IRS Section 7701-style check-the-box election. Available elections include election for group tax relief (surrender of losses), election for notional interest deduction on new equity, election to amortise intangible assets on a straight-line basis, and election to apply the tonnage tax regime. Hybrid mismatch elections and alignment under EU ATAD are governed by Cyprus Income Tax Law and do not permit flow-through classification for corporate entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 taxable turnover in any rolling 12-month period for Cyprus-established businesses; any distance-sales or digital-service supplier exceeding an EU-wide annual threshold of EUR 10,000 must register/use OSS; non-established businesses supplying goods or services in Cyprus must register from the first supply irrespective of turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for (1) export of goods physically removed from Cyprus to a destination outside the EU, evidenced by a valid customs export declaration and shipping/commercial documentation; (2) intra-Community supplies of goods dispatched to a taxable EU consumer holding a valid VAT identification number, subject to EC Sales List reporting; (3) supplies treated as exported in accordance with the EU place-of-supply rules, including shipments of goods to third states. For digital services (MMORPG in-app purchases under a B2B arrangement), the service is taxed where the customer is established; where the customer is established outside Cyprus/EU, the place of supply is outside Cyprus and the supply is outside the scope of Cypriot VAT without being formally zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document/AES export evidence)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill showing export from Cyprus to a third-party destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Swift/bank payment receipt proving actual consideration received
  • Commercial invoice and packing list identifying goods and export destination
  • Proof of export within 90 days from supply (delivery/customs registration date)
  • For intra-EU dispatches: copy of the customer's taxpayer VAT registration number and the vendor's EC Sales List (VIES) record
  • Dispatch/transport documentation validated by customs or appointed carrier

No mandatory real-time electronic clearance or digital stamping regime is currently in force for B2B invoices in Cyprus. B2G e-invoicing is mandatory under EU Directive 2014/55/EU using PEPPOL BIS Billing 3.0. VAT reporting is electronic: monthly/quarterly VAT returns via ARIADNE, periodic EC Sales Lists (ESL/VIES) and Intrastat declarations. No continuous transaction control (CTC) or API pre-clearance requirement is operational at present.

The Cyprus Tax Department subjects export zero-rating claims to documentary scrutiny and may conduct retrospective verification. If the exporter cannot produce the required export proof within 90 days of supply, the VAT is treated as due and the zero rating is reversed with statutory interest from the date of the original return. Deliberately fraudulent zero-rating claims attract civil penalties of up to 100% of the under-declared VAT, fines, and potential criminal imprisonment; the VAT assessment and reassessment window extends to ten years in cases of fraud, deliberate default, or incorrect return information.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. Cyprus is a euro-area member state; there are no exchange control restrictions on inbound or outbound payments, profit remittances, dividend distributions, loan repayments or liquidation proceeds. Non-resident investors may freely convert and repatriate euro or foreign currency, subject to standard anti-money-laundering verification and, where relevant, Central Bank of Cyprus balance-of-payments statistical reporting. No withholding tax is imposed on outbound dividends paid by a Cyprus tax-resident company, but treasury documentation should evidence economic substance and commercial rationale.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement to surrender export proceeds to the Central Bank of Cyprus or to convert foreign currency receipts into euro. Export proceeds from global MMORPG sales and app-store payouts may be retained in designated foreign-currency accounts. Transactions exceeding reporting thresholds must be reported to the Central Bank of Cyprus for balance-of-payments and international investment position purposes; failure to file accurate reports can create AML reputational exposure but does not trigger mandatory conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou) as the national macroprudential, foreign exchange statistics and financial stability authority, operating within the European System of Central Banks and subject to the European Central Bank's guidelines and EU Treaty capital movement rules.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility with legal guardrails. An EEA-incorporated electronic money institution with a valid e-money licence may passport into Cyprus under the Revised Payment Services Directive (PSD2) and provide payment collection or virtual IBAN services. Non-EEA digital EMIs have low standalone viability unless they establish a Cyprus or EU branch or cooperate with a Cyprus-licensed sponsor. EMIs are acceptable for receiving Apple/Google payout flows and making low-value disbursements, but the treasury must maintain a primary Tier-1 commercial bank account for liquidity concentration, escrow, and FX settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For EEA shareholder contributions, use SEPA Credit Transfer in euro to the Cyprus operational account; for non-EEA contributions, use SWIFT MT103 with a clear purpose code such as 'CAPL' or 'BOND' under ISO 20022 as appropriate, plus the treasury instruction referencing the share subscription and board resolution. The bank should receive prior notice, a board-approved capital injection plan, and certified KYC documents to avoid trust or money-laundering flags. Commercial Letters of Credit are not applicable to digital MMORPG publishing services and are reserved for physical goods or documentary trade; the treasury will use open-account MT103 wire transfers for developer royalty distributions, advertising network settlements, cloud hosting fees and subcontractor payments. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if related party transactions exceed: €750,000 for goods, €250,000 for services, €100,000 for royalties, €100,000 for interest. Master File required if group consolidated revenue exceeds €750,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus applies Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) per EU Code of Conduct and OECD BEPS. Companies must demonstrate adequate premises, employees, and management in Cyprus. Specific substance requirements for IP companies.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, plus interest; criminal prosecution with fines up to €50,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for tax debts if they knowingly participate in evasion; civil restitution, travel bans, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax residency status, reclassification of income, and penalties for non-compliance with ESR; fines up to €100,000 and possible criminal charges.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for fines and penalties if they fail to ensure substance compliance.
AML Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to €500,000 for companies; imprisonment up to 5 years for individuals; regulatory sanctions including license revocation.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally fined and imprisoned for AML violations.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income, penalties up to 10% of the adjustment, and interest; in severe cases, criminal penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors may be liable for penalties if they knowingly disregard documentation rules.
