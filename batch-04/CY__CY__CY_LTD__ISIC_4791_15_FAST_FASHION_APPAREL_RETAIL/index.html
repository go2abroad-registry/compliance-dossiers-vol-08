Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver, Ministry of Energy, Commerce and Industry, Nicosia, Cyprus.

Governing statute

Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113 (as amended): ss. 4-8 (formation, Memorandum and Articles of Association), s. 24 (registered office), Part IV (directors) and applicable registration provisions; supplementary Tax identification and VAT registration under Income Tax Law 118(I)/2002 and VAT Law 95(I)/2000.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form HE1 (Application for Incorporation) filed together with the notarized Memorandum and Articles of Association; formal registration evidenced by the Certificate of Incorporation and publication in the Official Gazette.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 25 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for private limited companies under the Cyprus Companies Law (Cap. 113). The subscribers freely set the authorized share capital; a standard fast-fashion retail distributor typically registers EUR 1,000 (nominal) with initial paid-up of EUR 100-500. No escrow account or capital verification is required for non-regulated e-commerce activity; escrow rules apply only to CySEC-licensed, banking, or other regulated sectors.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of Cyprus, Ministry of Finance; online portal: Taxisnet (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy); customs matters administered by the Department of Customs and Excise.

E-government filing portal

1) Register the Private Limited Company with the Registrar of Companies to obtain the HE registration number. 2) The company is automatically registered for income tax with the Tax Department; a Tax Identification Number (TIN) is allocated upon incorporation notification. 3) For VAT, file Form VAT-101 with the Tax Department, attaching the certificate of incorporation, certified memorandum and articles of association, registered office certificate, director and secretary details, and evidence of business premises; for non-EU-established persons, a local tax representative is mandatory. 4) The VAT number is issued in the format CY+8 digits+1 check letter, generally within 30 days. 5) If engaged in intra-EU distance sales or B2C digital services, also register for the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) via Taxisnet.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope: Cyprus tax-resident companies are taxed on worldwide income at a 12.5% corporate rate with a foreign tax credit; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income. A Cyprus Ltd is not eligible for pass-through treatment. — 1) Register the Private Limited Company with the Registrar of Companies to obtain the HE registration number. 2) The company is automatically registered for income tax with the Tax Department; a Tax Identification Number (TIN) is allocated upon incorporation notification. 3) For VAT, file Form VAT-101 with the Tax Department, attaching the certificate of incorporation, certified memorandum and articles of association, registered office certificate, director and secretary details, and evidence of business premises; for non-EU-established persons, a local tax representative is mandatory. 4) The VAT number is issued in the format CY+8 digits+1 check letter, generally within 30 days. 5) If engaged in intra-EU distance sales or B2C digital services, also register for the EU One-Stop-Shop (OSS) via Taxisnet.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope: Cyprus tax-resident companies are taxed on worldwide income at a 12.5% corporate rate with a foreign tax credit; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income. A Cyprus Ltd is not eligible for pass-through treatment.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election; Cyprus implements ATAD hybrid-mismatch rules under Law 37(I)/2019. Available elections include: board-management tax-residence determination, Notional Interest Deduction on new equity, IP-Box regime producing a 2.5% effective rate on qualifying intangibles, tonnage tax for shipping, and OSS / IOSS registration elections for cross-border e-commerce. No VAT group registration exists in Cyprus.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for Cyprus-established taxable persons; no threshold for non-established foreign suppliers, who must register from the first euro of taxable supply; EUR 10,000 Union-wide threshold applies for intra-EU distance sales of goods and B2C digital services when using the OSS scheme.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to: (1) exports of goods to non-EU destinations under section 55 of the VAT Law, subject to proof that goods left the EU within 90 days of supply; (2) intra-Community supplies of goods to a customer holding a valid EU VAT identification number, reported in the VIES statement under section 59; (3) supplies of ancillary services directly linked to exports. B2C distance sales of fast-fashion goods to EU consumers are not zero-rated and must be taxed at destination via OSS. Domestic B2C retail sales remain subject to 19% VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / summary declaration) filed via Cyprus Customs ECS
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR evidencing physical movement out of the EU
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank transfer evidence of export payment, where applicable
  • Commercial Invoice containing Incoterms, full description, quantity and value of goods
  • Customer's VAT registration number and contemporaneous VIES validation report for intra-Community supplies
  • Proof of delivery signed by the customer or carrier tracking document

No mandatory B2B e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance regime as of 2025. E-invoicing is voluntary; B2G invoices to public-sector buyers must be deliverable in the European Standard EN 16931 on request. VAT reporting is via periodic Taxisnet returns and VIES recapitulative statements. Cyprus is preparing for the EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) proposal; real-time digital reporting and mandatory e-invoicing are expected to be phased in from 2030.

The Tax Department may raise VAT assessments within 6 years of the tax period, extended to 10 years in cases of fraud or wilful neglect. False export zero-rating claims attract a 10% late-payment penalty on the unpaid VAT plus an additional 1% per month for continuing default, default interest, and retrospective clawback of any refunds paid. Fraudulent evasion can lead to civil penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded and criminal prosecution with imprisonment of up to 5 years. Customs enforcement measures include seizure of goods, asset distraint and Winding-up proceedings. Note: Cyprus itself imposes no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day reference reflects the ultimate beneficial owner's home-country exchange-control obligations, non-compliance with which may trigger reversal of export refunds in the source jurisdiction.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a Eurozone member using the Euro (EUR) with full capital and current account convertibility under Article 63 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. There is no Cypriot exchange-control law, no Central Bank of Cyprus prior-approval requirement for equity injection, profit repatriation, dividend distribution, or trade settlement, and no restriction on holding foreign currency. The only regulatory layers are: (i) balance-of-payments statistical reporting under Regulation (EC) No 1849/2005, administered by the Central Bank of Cyprus for individual cross-border transactions at or above the prescribed threshold; and (ii) AML and travel-rule verification under Regulation (EU) 2023/1113. Profit and dividend remittance requires a valid shareholder resolution, sufficient distributable reserves under the Cyprus Companies Law, and up-to-date tax filings, but does not require any FX authorization or conversion license.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio apply in Cyprus. Export receipts from the fast-fashion e-commerce business may be freely retained in the original currency (USD, GBP, etc.), freely converted into EUR, or used offshore. The Central Bank of Cyprus merely imposes balance-of-payments statistical reporting on banks for transactions above the reporting threshold; there is no repatriation deadline and no mandatory exchange of export proceeds into EUR.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High as a complementary treasury and cross-border payout rail, but Low as a sole settlement infrastructure. EU-licensed Electronic Money Institutions, including those passporting into Cyprus under PSD2 and the EMD2 regime, may aggregate e-commerce proceeds and provide SWIFT-comparable disbursement rails. However, an EMI cannot maintain a capital escrow account or take deposits, must safeguard client funds in segregated accounts at a Tier-1 credit institution, and requires a sponsoring correspondent bank. For the target rails (Klarna BNPL and Shopify Payments), an EMI overlay is viable only where the merchant acquirer holds an EU banking or payment-institution license; routing PSP waterfalls directly into an omnibus EMI account creates AML mixing flags and may trigger payment-institution licensing issues under the Central Bank of Cyprus.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing is via SWIFT MT103 with the ECB balance-of-payments purpose code for 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' and ISO 20022 purpose code 'CAPI' (Capital Payments), executed over TARGET2 for EUR or CHIPS/Fedwire for USD. The accompanying documentation bundle includes the notarized shareholder and board allotment resolutions, share premium ledger entry, issued share certificates, and the bank's KYC forms. The receiving bank is obligated to transmit transaction details to the Central Bank of Cyprus balance-of-payments statistical system where the amount exceeds the reporting threshold. Travel-rule compliance under Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 requires complete originator and beneficiary information in MT103 fields 50 and 59. No FX conversion or Central Bank approval is required for the capital injection.Primary clearing rails: SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier payments and intercompany disbursements; SWIFT MT700 Commercial Letters of Credit for documented fast-fashion purchase orders above EUR 100,000; SEPA Credit Transfer and SEPA Instant for EU suppliers; and cross-currency settlement to Chinese manufacturers via EUR/USD or EUR/CNY NDF-funded disbursements using Tier-1 correspondent banks. Reconciliation is performed through SWIFT FIN field 72 narration and ISO 20022 pain.001/pain.002 messages.
