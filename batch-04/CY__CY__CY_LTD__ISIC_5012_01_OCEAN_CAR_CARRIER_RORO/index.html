Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cyprus Tax Department, Ministry of Finance; online portal: ARIADNE (Tax Department e- Filing Portal) at taxdep.gov.cy

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must first register its corporate presence with the Cyprus Registrar of Companies and obtain a Certificate of Incorporation. Subsequently, it must file Form T.D. 101 (Tax Registration Application) with the Cyprus Tax Department within 60 days of commencement of business. The Tax Department issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, if taxable supplies are being made, a VAT registration number (Eparchiako Kefalio). A non-resident entity without a fixed establishment may register directly for VAT in respect of its Cyprus taxable transactions; a tax representative may be required where no permanent establishment exists.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. Cyprus resident companies are taxed at the corporate income tax rate of 12.5% on worldwide income; non-resident entities are taxed only on Cyprus-source income. The entity is classified as a separate taxable person, not eligible for pass-through treatment as a default.

Available election forms

Cyprus does not apply a check-the-box pass-through election. Relevant statutory elections include: (a) VAT group election under Article 32 of the Cyprus VAT Law (fiscal unity), (b) tonnage tax election for qualifying Cyprus-flagged or EU-flagged shipping activities in place of standard corporation tax, (c) election for intra-group transfer of losses under Income Tax Law Article 9, and (d) hybrid mismatch disallowance elections consistent with EU ATAD. No entity-level U.S.-style classification election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 for taxable turnover during any consecutive 12-month period; for non-established foreign suppliers, mandatory VAT registration from the first supply absent any threshold; distance sales EU threshold also applies for cross-border supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) applies to goods physically exported from Cyprus to a destination outside the EU, and to certain internationally related services under the VAT Law, including transport of goods by sea between Cyprus and third countries, and services directly related to the operation or chartering of seagoing vessels for commercial use. The Ro-Ro vessel transport of vehicles qualifies where the recipient is established outside the EU and the service is directly connected with the export or import of goods on board a seagoing vessel. Documentary proof of export or dispatch from EU customs territory must be retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document - SAD / E-Customs)
  • Bill of Lading (through bill of lading for Ro-Ro cargo vessels)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Bank Payment Confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration
  • Commercial Invoice with export zero-rating legend
  • Packing List
  • Cargo Manifest / Load List for the seagoing vessel
  • Voyage Order or Charter Party where applicable

No real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory e-invoicing clearance system is currently operational in Cyprus. Electronic invoicing is permitted under the VAT Law, and the country is aligning with EU e-invoicing interoperability standards (EN 16931) for B2G invoices; the Tax Department runs a voluntary portal (ARIADNE) for e-tax filing and VAT returns. Full real-time digital stamping and transactional clearance mandates are not yet introduced.

The Cyprus Tax Department will impose VAT, interest at 1.5% per month or part thereof, and penalties up to the amount of VAT due in cases of unjustified zero-rating. Knowingly submitting false export declarations attracts criminal penalties: up to €17,000 and/or imprisonment up to five years. Retrospective clawback of input VAT is mandatory if export documentary proof is incomplete or fabricated, including a clawback for prior tax periods under statute of limitation of eight years in matters of fraudulent or wrongful omission, and five years in other cases. Additional administrative fines apply for missing e-invoicing or documentation under the VAT Procedures Assessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Republic of Cyprus is a Eurozone member; the Euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account. There are no pre-approval requirements for trade settlements, profit remittances, or dividend distribution to non-resident shareholders (only anti-money laundering and EU sanctions screening apply). Profit repatriation is further tax-friendly: no withholding tax on dividends for non-resident shareholders in qualifying circumstances, and the tonnage tax regime for shipping income provides an attractive 0% tax on qualifying vessels. The only practical hurdle is the requirement to use a Cyprus-licensed bank or EU-authorized payment institution to effect payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Cyprus has abolished all exchange control surrender or conversion requirements for export proceeds. Shipping receivables denominated in USD or other foreign currencies may be retained freely in offshore or onshore USD accounts. However, for statistical purposes, export receipts above EUR 12,500 must be reported to the Central Bank's balance of payments statistics unit through the reporting bank.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (CBD) in coordination with the European Central Bank for Eurosystem monetary policy; under the Exchange Control framework, the Central Bank is the designated authority for licensing and oversight of cross-border payment institutions, EMIs, and investment firms.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-to-High for operational payments and FX conversion, but Low for capital escrow and standby LC purposes. Legally, non-Cyprus EU/UK EMIs (e.g., Marion Pay, CurrencyCloud, Airwallex) can open multi-currency accounts and provide international payment services under the electronic money institution passport. However, the Central Bank of Cyprus requires all shipping/trade payments to be transacted through a Cyprus-authorized credit institution or a fully authorized EU EMI with an established branch/agent in Cyprus. Moreover, for documentary credits (LC) and bill of lading financing, a full commercial bank remains indispensable.

EU statistical reporting (Balance of Payments) must be completed by the remitting bank, and the recipient bank in Cyprus will authenticate the transfer against the Cyprus Registrar of Companies share capital register. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% flat corporate income tax (CIT) on all taxable profits under the Cyprus Income Tax Law (L.118(I)/2002), as amended.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Cyprus imposes no domestic withholding tax on outbound dividends paid by a Cyprus company to its shareholders, whether resident or non-resident. The Special Defence Contribution (SDC) of 17% on deemed dividend distributions applies only to Cyprus tax-resident shareholders and is not a dividend withholding tax; profits distributed to non-residents are entirely free of Cyprus withholding tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The IR 601 must be lodged within 12 months after the end of the tax year; for a 31 December year-end, the statutory deadline is 31 December of the following calendar year. The Transfer Pricing Return (Form TP-1) and any tonnage tax election must accompany the annual filing; provisional tax payments are due on 31 July and 31 December.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 for controlled purchases of goods and EUR 750,000 for controlled sales of goods; EUR 250,000 for controlled intra-group services, intangible property transactions and financing/interest transactions, tested on an annual aggregate basis per related party. A Cyprus Local File is required where any category exceeds the threshold, a Master File applies to multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of at least EUR 750 million, and Form TP-1 (Transfer Pricing Return) must be filed annually with the IR 601 CIT return.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not maintain a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) filing regime, but the Cyprus Tax Department and Cypriot courts apply the doctrines of substance-over-form, central management and control, and beneficial ownership. EU anti-tax-avoidance rules (ATAD), the GAAR under the Income Tax Law, and the MLI Principal Purpose Test require genuine economic activity to access treaty benefits, the participation exemption, the IP Box and the Notional Interest Deduction. For the Cyprus Tonnage Tax regime, the Cyprus Shipping Deputy Ministry requires substantive ship management, crewing and technical operations to be performed from Cyprus, and failure to satisfy these economic substance conditions results in revocation of the tonnage tax election, denial of shipping tax exemptions and potential re-characterization of the Cypriot vehicle as a foreign-managed shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law (L.4/1978) and Income Tax Law (L.118(I)/2002), conviction for tax evasion is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years and/or fines of up to EUR 35,000; the Tax Commissioner additionally recovers the underpaid tax with interest at 10% per annum, imposes administrative fines and may issue default assessments and attachment orders against bank accounts and vessels.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve false returns are criminally liable and may be personally assessed for the unpaid tax; courts may pierce the corporate veil under the Companies Law (Cap. 113) for fraudulent trading, imposing personal civil liability for the company's tax debts, making directors subject to travel bans (departure prohibition orders), disqualification from acting as directors, and imprisonment.
Shell Company Operations and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Loss of Cyprus tax residence, denial of treaty benefits under the MLI Principal Purpose Test, disallowance of participation exemption, NID and IP Box reliefs, revocation of the tonnage tax election, and re-assessment of Cyprus-source shipping income; administrative fines under the Companies Law and potential de-registration of the entity; shareholders may be taxed as if the company were transparent.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held jointly and severally liable for the additional taxes and penalties where they knowingly permitted the artificial arrangement; the court may issue disqualification orders up to 15 years, travel bans, and personal restitution orders for monies transferred out of the shell structure to affiliates or shareholders.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Defaults

Statutory sanction: The Cyprus Tax Department may make an arm's length adjustment to taxable profits, disallow overstated intra-group deductions, impose administrative fines of up to EUR 500 for TP documentation failures, and charge default interest at 10% per annum on the additional tax; tax treaty MAP protection may also be suspended for non-compliant taxpayers.

Director personal liability: Directors certifying false or incomplete TP-1 declarations face personal fines and criminal liability for aiding tax evasion; in cases of aggressive profit shifting, directors may be made personally liable for the adjusted CIT, interest and fines where they knowingly signed the transfer pricing return without a reasonable basis.
AML, Sanctions and CDD Violations on Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under L.188(I)/2007, legal persons may be fined up to EUR 1,000,000 and individuals imprisoned for up to 14 years on conviction for money laundering offenses; regulatory supervisors (Cyprus Bar Association, ICPAC, or CYSEC where applicable) may impose additional professional fines, suspend the company's license or flag registration, and MOKAS may freeze assets; the Cyprus Shipping Deputy Ministry may revoke the vessel's Cyprus flag or operation licence for sanctioned trades.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and MLROs are personally liable with joint and several fines, imprisonment, travel bans and confiscation orders under the Proceeds of Crime legislation; professionals who knowingly facilitate sanctioned cargo movements face barring from practice, personal disqualification orders and criminal prosecution.
Tonnage Tax Non-Compliance and Shipping Licensing Violations

Statutory sanction: The Cyprus Shipping Deputy Ministry may revoke the vessel's registration, impose administrative penalties per vessel, and the Tax Department may withdraw the tonnage tax election retrospectively, reassessing all profits at the 12.5% CIT rate with interest at 10% per annum; unpaid tonnage taxes may be recovered through in rem arrest of the vessel by the Admiralty Court and enforcement of maritime liens.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly operate outside the tonnage tax licence or cause the company to default are personally liable for the unpaid tonnage tax and penalties, may be personally reached under the Companies Law for insolvent trading where the vessel's operations continue while debts are unpaid, and may face travel bans and director disqualification proceedings initiated by the Shipping Deputy Ministry or the Tax Commissioner.
