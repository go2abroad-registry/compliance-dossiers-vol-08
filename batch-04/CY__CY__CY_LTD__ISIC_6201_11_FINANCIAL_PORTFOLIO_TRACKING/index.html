Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private limited company; nominal capital may be one or more shares of EUR 1.00; no escrow or deposit account is required. A capital duty of 0.5% (EUR 5 per EUR 1,000) applies to registered share capital.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification and annual fire inspection by the Cyprus Fire Service for the registered office; structural load certification for the lease premise; occupational health and safety workplace clearance under the Safety and Health at Work Law 89(I)/1996,including appointment of a competent safety officer.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a private limited company; nominal capital may be one or more shares of EUR 1.00; no escrow or deposit account is required. A capital duty of 0.5% (EUR 5 per EUR 1,000) applies to registered share capital.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus has full de jure and de facto current and capital account convertibility under the euro and EU Treaty free-movement-of-capital provisions. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, capital repatriation, and liquidation proceeds to any EU or bona fide third-country recipient are unrestricted, provided the company meets Cyprus Companies Law solvency tests, submits tax compliance certificates (Form TD4/withholding disclosure), and passes standard AML source-of-funds validation. The only practical hurdles are banking counterparty de-risking and, for sanctioned jurisdictions, EU/IOSI restrictions; a Cyprus Ltd operating financial analytics software has no additional FX licensing requirement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender, conversion, or mandatory repatriation timeline exists in Cyprus. Export receipts may be retained in EUR or any convertible currency, in full, and may be disbursed at a company's discretion. As a euro-area member, the only obligation is the reporting of international transaction data to the Central Bank of Cyprus for balance-of-payments statistical purposes; specific threshold-reporting under the ECB Regulation (EU) 1849/2005 as implemented by CBC Circular requires designation of a local declaration agent and submission of transaction-level records above €50,000.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Trapeza Kyprou), an integrated member of the European System of Central Banks, together with the Cyprus Ministry of Finance and the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission (CySEC) for payments and FX policy; the Central Bank administers balance-of-payments reporting, payment statistics, banking supervision, and anti-money-laundering oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Cyprus domestic banks treat offshore payment-financial-technology companies with sensitivity; an unregulated software-only portfolio tracking business is generally acceptable, but an EMI-style cross-border license expectation is not. Legal constraints arise from the EU E-Money Directive (EMD2) and Central Bank of Cyprus EMI licensing Circular: an overseas digital EMI may not solicit Cyprus clients or hold euro e-money without Corsagistrative substance. In practice, Cyprus banks will demand a local registered office, local AML MLRO, and a genuine board presence before granting a master/omnibus account. Pure offshore EMI rails are not viable as the primary treasury channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Optimum compliant rail is a SEPA Instant Credit Transfer or standard SEPA Credit Transfer into a EUR-denominated Cyprus IBAN for EU/EEA originators; for non-EU/EEA, use SWIFT MT103 with proper purpose code and detailed beneficiary line. In the narrative/purpose field, use 'Investment capital – private equity injection into CY Ltd' rather than a generic transfer; the Central Bank requires a valid BoP statistical reason. Process: obtain a formal board resolution; identify the contributing shareholder and their KYC pack; execute payment order with reference to the share subscription or capital increase; submit to the bank a copy of the share certificate, notarized corporate approval, and proof of tax payment or an explicit exempt-status memorandum for capital duty (Cyprus capital duty was abolished in 2019). In anticipation of suspicious-transfer queries, attach the audited valuation of the portfolio-tracking software IP and the current commercial contract.For routine AP, SaaS licensing, and software procurement, use SEPA Direct Debit or SWIFT MT103; for interbank reimbursements arising from trade finance, use MT202 COV. For large technology licensing or M&A disbursements, Cyprus banks routinely issue commercial Letters of Credit via MT700/MT701/MT720 governed by UCP600, and demand drafts may be negotiated by the counterparty. All outbound payments must carry a payment purpose code compatible with CBC's International Transaction Reporting System; where the beneficiary is an EU bank, use IBAN and BIC; for non-EU banks, require a validating correspondent with clear sanctions coverage. Late filing of Form TD4 triggers an administrative penalty of EUR 100 plus EUR 50 per month or part-month of continued default; unpaid tax bears interest at 1.75% per annum until settlement; negligent or fraudulent returns may attract penalties of up to 100% of the understated tax and criminal fines of up to EUR 10,000.

Core R&D activities, headcount tracking and expenditure ledgers must remain in Cyprus; outsourced or related-party R&D is excluded from the nexus calculation. Local File is required where controlled transactions exceed EUR 750,000 in aggregate for goods, services or financing, or EUR 100,000 for royalties; all non-resident related parties must be listed with country of residence. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 aggregate for controlled goods, services and financing transactions per tax year; EUR 100,000 for controlled royalty/IP transactions per tax year. A Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million. The TP Summary Information Table must be attached to Form TD4 in all cases where the thresholds are exceeded.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus applies a management-and-control residency doctrine: a Cyprus Ltd is tax resident only where board meetings, central management and key strategic decisions occur in Cyprus. The IP Box regime imposes substantial-activity nexus conditions requiring core R&D functions, qualified personnel and expenditure in Cyprus. Although Cyprus does not operate an Emirates-style ESR filing system, the EU ATAD CFC rules, the EU list of non-cooperative jurisdictions and OECD substance principles deny treaty, NID and IP Box benefits to letterbox or shell structures. The fintech SaaS business utilising Stripe and Apple IAP payment rails remains outside CySEC licensing at standard low AML risk, but must maintain full substance evidence (offices, payroll, board minutes and accounting records) in Cyprus.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Underdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Full reassessment at 12.5% plus interest at 1.75% per annum and civil penalties up to 100% of the tax understated; on criminal conviction under the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law, fines up to EUR 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to three years.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign false TD4 returns face personal criminal liability, civil restitution of evaded tax, departure (travel) bans for outstanding tax debts, and disqualification under the Companies Law (Cap 113).
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the entity as non-Cyprus tax resident, denial of treaty benefits, disallowance of NID and IP Box deductions, upward transfer-pricing adjustment, and attribution of profits to the foreign parent under CFC rules; administrative fines may also be imposed under substance-enquiry procedures.

Director personal liability: Nominee and local directors may be pursued for breach of fiduciary duties under the Companies Law (Cap 113), disqualified from acting as directors, and managing shareholders may be treated as shadow directors for restitution and winding-up exposure.
AML / Counter-Terrorist Financing Breach

Statutory sanction: Administrative and criminal fines for legal persons, including aggravated penalties for systemic failures, asset freezing, suspension of payment collection activity, and mandatory CySEC authorisation under the Payment Services Law once volume thresholds are crossed.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly fail to prevent money laundering are personally liable to fines and imprisonment of up to three years, and may be subject to travel bans and confiscation orders.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Upward adjustment of taxable profit with 12.5% corporate tax, interest at 1.75% per annum, TP-specific administrative penalties of EUR 500 for each omitted TP filing element, and risk of unrelieved double taxation where no corresponding adjustment is obtained in the foreign state.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally accountable for knowingly false TP declarations attached to Form TD4; personal liability may arise for secondary tax debts and for fraudulent misrepresentation to the Cyprus tax authorities.
