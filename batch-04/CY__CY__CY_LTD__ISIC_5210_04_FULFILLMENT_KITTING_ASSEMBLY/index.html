Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; online portal: https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy (Ariadne tax information system)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity without Cyprus incorporation must apply in writing to the Cyprus Tax Department for a Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) and, if making supplies liable to VAT, for a VAT registration number using Form VAT 101, supported by constitutional documents, contract/lease, copy of passport or corporate extract, and declaration of director details. Registration is immediate and mandatory before commencement of taxable activity for non-established taxable persons; established persons must register within 30 days of exceeding the VAT registration threshold. The competent officer issues the TIN/VAT number through the Tax Department portal.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign entity without Cyprus incorporation must apply in writing to the Cyprus Tax Department for a Cyprus Tax Identification Code (TIC) and, if making supplies liable to VAT, for a VAT registration number using Form VAT 101, supported by constitutional documents, contract/lease, copy of passport or corporate extract, and declaration of director details. Registration is immediate and mandatory before commencement of taxable activity for non-established taxable persons; established persons must register within 30 days of exceeding the VAT registration threshold. The competent officer issues the TIN/VAT number through the Tax Department portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election akin to the US. A private limited company is automatically subject to entity-level taxation at 12.5% corporate income tax and is resident where central management and control is exercised in Cyprus. Available elective mechanisms include: (i) Cyprus VAT group election allowing related persons to be treated as one taxable person for VAT, (ii) group relief election for the surrender of losses between 75%+ subsidiaries, (iii) elective notional interest deduction on new equity, and (iv) optional application of the IP Box regime. No hybrid mismatch neutralisation election is available; the participation exemption on qualifying dividends and capital gains is generally mandatory.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for taxable persons established in Cyprus; non-established foreign suppliers have no registration threshold and must register before making taxable supplies in Cyprus, including foreign supplies covered by e-commerce distance-selling and import rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods may be zero-rated if they are dispatched or transported out of the EU by the supplier or by a third party acting on his behalf, or by the customer established outside the EU, and are proven by a customs export declaration (SAD) accepted by the exporting member state. Services exported to non-EU customers are generally zero-rated where the place of supply lies outside Cyprus or the services are directly connected with export/import of goods. The supplier must retain evidence of physical export within the statutory period and be able to establish the identity and non-EU status of the recipient.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document – SAD)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Bill of Lading (B/L), CMR Waybill or Air Waybill
  • Proof of shipment/export from EU (customs exit stamp, electronic ECS record or trader's own controlled records)
  • Bank statement or Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing receipt of export proceeds

Cyprus currently does not mandate real-time e-invoicing clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction controls. Invoices must satisfy authenticity and integrity requirements, which may be met through electronic invoicing. VAT returns and EC Sales Lists are filed electronically through the Tax Department portal. E-invoicing using PEPPOL formats is accepted in public procurement under EU Directive 2014/55/EU. No country-wide real-time reporting or fiscal device clearance mandate is in force.

The Cyprus Tax Department may scrutinise zero-rating via documentary review, exporter audits and EU administrative cooperation. If conditions are not met, the VAT is assessed at 19% with statutory interest from the date of supply. Civil penalties may be imposed up to 100% of the tax evaded; criminal sanctions for fraudulent VAT evasion can include imprisonment and fines. Limitation periods for assessment are generally 5 years, extended to 10 years in cases involving fraud or deliberate evasion, and cross-border recovery is available under EU Regulation (EU) No 904/2010 and the Mutual Assistance Directive.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus applies the euro as legal tender; there are no general exchange control restrictions on current or capital account transactions. Profit remittance, dividends, and repatriation of capital are freely permitted for resident and non-resident investors, subject only to standard anti-money laundering compliance, tax clearance where applicable, and the EU Free Movement of Capital regime. The Capital Movements Directive provides for transitional derogations only in exceptional circumstances; none are currently applicable to Cyprus.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cyprus imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements and no mandatory conversion ratio. All export receipts in foreign currency (including USD, GBP, or other non-EUR currencies) may be retained in foreign-currency accounts, converted at market rates, or used directly for cross-border payments at the discretion of the exporting entity. Settlement of trade invoices through licensed banks or authorised payment institutions does not require central bank approval.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), operating within the Eurosystem and European System of Central Banks; external transaction and foreign exchange matters are also subject to European Central Bank regulations and EU Capital Movements Directive

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility is Medium-High for established European Economic Area Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Airwallex, Multitude, and Railsr, which may provide multicurrency IBAN accounts and the target payment rails of ACH Direct and Airwallex. Legal constraints require the service provider to hold a valid EEA EMI or Payment Institution licence passported into Cyprus, maintain a physical or authorised agent presence, and comply with client asset safeguarding, AML, and data protection obligations. Non-EEA offshore EMIs without a Cyprus passport are generally not viable for the operational account structure.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5% on the worldwide chargeable income of a Cyprus tax-resident private limited company (Income Tax Law, Law 118(I)/2002, as amended).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% withholding tax on outbound dividends paid by a Cyprus Ltd to non-resident shareholders; Cyprus imposes no domestic dividend WHT. Cyprus tax-resident individuals remain subject to Special Defence Contribution (SDC) at 17% on dividends received; Cyprus tax-resident companies may be charged 17% SDC on deemed dividend distributions where 70% of after-tax profits are not distributed within two years.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

TD200 must be filed within 15 months of the fiscal year-end (for a 31 December year-end: 31 March of the second following calendar year); any final tax balance is payable by the same deadline. Provisional instalments fall due on 31 July and 31 December of each calendar year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR. Per the Cyprus Transfer Pricing Decree (2022), a Local File is mandatory where related-party transactions exceed EUR 750,000 (goods), EUR 250,000 (services or financing), or EUR 100,000 (intangible/royalty transactions); a Master File is mandatory where group consolidated revenue exceeds EUR 750 million; a Summary Information Table (TP declaration) is attached to the corporate income tax return for any controlled transaction above EUR 100,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not operate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, tax residence is based on central management and control, and the Cyprus Tax Department applies OECD BEPS standards: substantive local premises, employees, Cypriot board meetings, and active commercial risk management are required to retain a TRC, access treaty benefits, and sustain IP Box or participation-exemption claims. A fulfilment/kitting operation must therefore maintain genuine warehouse staff, client contracts, and locally documented decision-making.
