Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus (Tμήμα Φορολογίας); operational e-portal 'Ariadne' (ariatne.mof.gov.cy) for integrated TIN/VAT and income tax administration; Customs & Excise Department for export clearance.

E-government filing portal

Domestic company: upon incorporation, the Registrar of Companies issues the registration number (HE77) and the Tax Department automatically allocates a Tax Identification Number (TIN); VAT ID is additionally allocated when VAT registration is triggered. Non-resident foreign entity with a Cyprus permanent establishment, a fixed place of business, or Cyprus-source income must apply to the District Tax Office via Ariadne using Form TIN 101, submitting certified constitutional documents, register of directors and shareholders, power of attorney, and proof of registered/subsidiary premises; VAT registration is compulsory (Form VAT 101) once taxable turnover of supplies exceeds €15,600 within any 12 consecutive months, and may be made voluntarily on the Ariadne portal.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (Cyprus-resident Ltd taxed at entity level on worldwide income at 12.5%; non-resident company taxed on Cyprus-source income only; no pass-through default) — Domestic company: upon incorporation, the Registrar of Companies issues the registration number (HE77) and the Tax Department automatically allocates a Tax Identification Number (TIN); VAT ID is additionally allocated when VAT registration is triggered. Non-resident foreign entity with a Cyprus permanent establishment, a fixed place of business, or Cyprus-source income must apply to the District Tax Office via Ariadne using Form TIN 101, submitting certified constitutional documents, register of directors and shareholders, power of attorney, and proof of registered/subsidiary premises; VAT registration is compulsory (Form VAT 101) once taxable turnover of supplies exceeds €15,600 within any 12 consecutive months, and may be made voluntarily on the Ariadne portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Cyprus-resident Ltd taxed at entity level on worldwide income at 12.5%; non-resident company taxed on Cyprus-source income only; no pass-through default)

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box regime. Available statutory elections and claims: (1) Cyprus group taxation election for 75%+ group companies (surrender of trading losses, notional interest on intra-group loans); (2) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on new equity (per Article 9 of Income Tax Law); (3) IP Box regime election granting 80% deduction on qualifying intellectual-property profits; (4) Tonnage Tax election for qualifying shipowners/charterers (generally unavailable to NVOCC freight forwarders); (5) anti-hybrid notifications under ATAD-relevant provisions (Law on Anti-Hybrid Rules), including deemed ratio changes; (6) VAT option to tax on certain financial/land supplies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€15,600 (mandatory registration once the value of taxable supplies in any 12 consecutive months exceeds this threshold; voluntary registration permitted below threshold)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated supply of goods where the goods are dispatched or transported outside the EU (export) from Cyprus by the supplier, the customer, or a third party; also zero-rated are supplementary services directly connected to ocean transport of goods destined for export, including NVOCC freight-forwarding services, cargo handling, and port storage incidental to international carriage, provided the service is rendered for the direct needs of the export goods and is evidenced by transport documents; zero rating applies to the supply of services for the international transport of goods between Cyprus and third countries. The NVOCC exporter must fiscally justify each zero-rated invoice; otherwise 19% VAT is due.

  • Electronic Export Summary Declaration (EXS) cleared by the Cyprus Customs & Excise Department, with export verification/printout
  • Bill of Lading (ocean B/L, NON-VESSEL OPERATING COMMON CARRIER house B/L or master B/L) evidencing carriage of the goods from the EU
  • Inward Foreign Currency Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or bank transfer credit evidence covering the invoice value from the non-EU customer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Sale contract / purchase order incorporating INCOTERMS
  • Delivery or insurance documentation (e.g., cover note, cargo insurance policy) corroborating the international voyage

Mandatory B2G e-invoicing in EU standard EN 16931 via the national PEPPOL-compliant platform for invoices to Cypriot public-sector bodies; no general B2B e-invoicing or real-time fiscal clearance mandate; no API-based pre-issuance approval or digital stamping requirement in the Cyprus VAT regime.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to ex-post risk-based audit by the Cyprus Tax Department and post-clearance verification by Customs. Unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating gives rise to immediate VAT reassessment, reversal of any refund or credit, statutory interest from the original due date, and penalty regimes under the VAT Law: administrative penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded, fixed penalties for failure to file, and criminal liability (fines and imprisonment) for recklessly or knowingly furnishing false documentation; retrospective clawback applies to open assessments for up to the general six-year limitation period, extended in cases of fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a full Eurozone member (Euro introduced as legal tender on 1 January 2008); the Euro is completely freely convertible. There are no capital controls or foreign-exchange licensing restrictions in normal operation. Current-account transactions — including dividend repatriation, operational profit remittances, management fees, freight commissions, and trade payments — are unrestricted and may be settled in EUR or any freely convertible currency. Residual statutory friction arises exclusively from: (i) EU/UN/OFAC sanctions screening obligations; (ii) anti-money-laundering KYC verification under the Cyprus Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law (L.188(I)/2007, as amended); (iii) Central Bank of Cyprus statistical balance-of-payments reporting for corporate receipts and payments exceeding EUR 50,000 per reporting period; and (iv) creditor-protection / solvency verification for corporate distributions governed by the Cyprus Companies Law (Cap. 113). No exchange-control approval is required for profit repatriation; the only blocking point would be a failed AML/CFT or sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion regime applies to export proceeds in Cyprus. EUR is the domestic currency and is freely usable; NVOCC export and freight-forwarding commission receipts may be retained indefinitely in the exporter's EUR or foreign-currency account. The Central Bank of Cyprus imposes only a passive statistical disclosure duty: resident corporate exporters must report aggregate outward and inward transactions of EUR 50,000 or more per calendar month through the CBU Monthly Questionnaire Reporting (MQR) channel or the equivalent direct reporting module. No minimum conversion ratio, no repatriation timeline, and no invoice-linked surrender rule is prescribed for export receipts or freight collections.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou) acting as the national competent authority for banking supervision, payment systems, and balance-of-payments statistics within the European System of Central Banks; monetary policy authority is delegated to the European Central Bank (ECB).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW for fully offshore third-country EMIs and MEDIUM-HIGH for Cyprus-licensed EMIs (authorised under the Electronic Money Institution and Payment Institution Laws, L.122(I)/2017, transposing EMD2/PSD2). A Cyprus-licensed EMI may open an operational wallet/IBAN for the NVOCC and provide SEPA/instant-payment rails, but such EMIs do not offer SWIFT correspondent capacity for commercial LCs, and they impose tight transaction velocity limits on freight-forwarding traffic. EMIs incorporated in the UK (FCA-regulated), Lithuania, or Ireland may provide ancillary disbursement accounts, but most restrict aggregate throughput to EUR 250,000 per calendar year for a medium-risk NVOCC and require a segregated freight-desk compliance module. The decisive legal constraint is substitutability: an EMI account is not a full credit institution account and cannot be used for letter-of-credit settlements, documentary collections, or CBU statistical submissions; EMIs are therefore viable only as a Treasury disbursement accelerator and payroll-clearing adjunct, not as the primary clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection is executed via SWIFT MT103 (for direct beneficiary credit) or MT202 COV (for interbank cover) from the shareholder or an affiliated group treasury account. The SWIFT purpose/remittance code must identify the transaction as either: (i) SIOS — direct investment in capital (equity injection); or (ii) shareholder loan (with the narrative 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN — CY_LTD_NVOCC' cited in the field 72). Funds arriving from an EU Member State in EUR are treated as domestic payments within TARGET2/SEPA and are not subject to CBU prior authorisation; inbound transfers from third countries in USD or other non-EUR currencies are credited after standard sanctions and AML screening. Under CBU balance-of-payments rules, any single or aggregated injection of EUR 50,000 or more must be reported by the bank through the monthly statistical questionnaire; the bank's compliance unit will require: (i) a board resolution approving the injection; (ii) identity of the remitting shareholder; (iii) source-of-funds evidence; (iv) a share-register extract or signed loan facility agreement; and (v) an AML/source-of-wealth declaration for the shareholder. No foreign-investment approval, no exchange-control licence, and no CBU transaction authorisation is required for non-resident ownership of a Cyprus Ltd in the freight-forwarding sector.Outbound disbursement rails: (a) SWIFT MT103 for operational payments — freight charges, carrier charges, port and terminal charges, demurrage and detention fees to foreign shipping lines and port operators; (b) TARGET2 / EURO1 clearing for EUR-denominated settlements to EU-based carriers and EU ports (free-of-cost same-day value); (c) irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit issued under UCP 600 for container-leasing programmes, slot-charter charter-hire, or large port-concession payments; (d) MT202 / MT202 COV for correspondent USD transfers routed through global correspondent banks with straight-through processing; and (e) SEPA Credit Transfer for domestic payroll, crew handling, and Cyprus fixed operating costs.
