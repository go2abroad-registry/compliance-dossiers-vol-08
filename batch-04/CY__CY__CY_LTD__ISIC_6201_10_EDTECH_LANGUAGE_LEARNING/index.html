Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113, ss. 3-7 and 10, as amended by the Companies (Amendment) Laws; registration of private company limited by shares.

Formation / constitutional filings

Form HE1 Application for Registration of a Company, together with the Memorandum and Articles of Association and the Statutory Declaration of Compliance.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 16 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement; a Cyprus private company limited by shares may be incorporated with one ordinary share of EUR 1.00 nominal value. No escrow or capital deposit account is required by DRCORO; the subscribed capital must be disclosed in the Statement of Capital and fully paid or credited as fully paid.

Funding & unlock path: Occupational fire safety certification and means-of-escape clearance for the registered office must be obtained from the District Fire Service in accordance with the Fire Protection (Special Provisions) Laws; the office premises must also satisfy structural load inspections and workplace safety checks under the Factories (Health, Safety and Welfare) Law and the Safety and Health at Work Law.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital requirement; a Cyprus private company limited by shares may be incorporated with one ordinary share of EUR 1.00 nominal value. No escrow or capital deposit account is required by DRCORO; the subscribed capital must be disclosed in the Statement of Capital and fully paid or credited as fully paid.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department of the Republic of Cyprus (Tμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance; official electronic portal: TAXISnet at taxportal.mof.gov.cy; corporate registry functions are administered by the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (Cyprus Business Portal).

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company (HE77...) is automatically issued a Tax Identification Number (TIN) equal to its Registration Number at incorporation, without a separate application for income tax purposes. For VAT, the entity must file Form VAT101 via TAXISnet within 30 days of exceeding the compulsory registration threshold of EUR 15,600 in taxable turnover, or may apply for voluntary registration before commencement. Non-resident entities making taxable supplies in Cyprus must appoint an approved tax representative and register for VAT through the same portal. Corporate income tax registration with the local District Tax Office is simultaneous with incorporation; annual tax returns are submitted electronically via TAXISnet File IT/2023 template on a 12-month accounting basis.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Cyprus private limited company (HE77...) is automatically issued a Tax Identification Number (TIN) equal to its Registration Number at incorporation, without a separate application for income tax purposes. For VAT, the entity must file Form VAT101 via TAXISnet within 30 days of exceeding the compulsory registration threshold of EUR 15,600 in taxable turnover, or may apply for voluntary registration before commencement. Non-resident entities making taxable supplies in Cyprus must appoint an approved tax representative and register for VAT through the same portal. Corporate income tax registration with the local District Tax Office is simultaneous with incorporation; annual tax returns are submitted electronically via TAXISnet File IT/2023 template on a 12-month accounting basis.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists in Cyprus; an incorporated entity is always taxed at entity level and cannot elect pass-through treatment. Available elections: (1) Group taxation under Income Tax Law Art. 16 for resident companies with 75% or greater ultimate ownership, allowing losses to be offset within the group; (2) VAT group registration under VAT Law Art. 11 for closely-connected taxable persons resident in Cyprus; (3) OSS registration for EU cross-border B2C e-services, permitting a single VAT filing in Cyprus at destination-country rates; (4) VAT refund election for partial-exemption calculation. Hybrid mismatch rules implement ATAD and are mandatory/non-elective, employing automatic disallowance-based adjustments rather than taxpayer elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 annual taxable turnover for domestic supplies of goods/services; EUR 7,800 for intra-Community acquisitions; EUR 35,000 for intra-EU distance sales of goods into Cyprus; EUR 10,000 EU-wide cross-border threshold for B2C telecom/broadcasting/electronic services (EdTech subscriptions must always be declared, with mandatory Cyprus VAT registration or OSS once exceeded in any EU Member State)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% VAT applies to (a) physical export of goods to non-EU third countries, and (b) intra-Community supplies, provided the goods physically depart Cyprus within 3 months (extendable to 6 months upon justification) and full transport documentation is retained. Services: 0% VAT applies to services directly related to export, international transport, and services supplied to a non-resident B2B customer established outside Cyprus where the supply is not effectively used in Cyprus. Digital EdTech subscription services: B2B sales to non-EU businesses fall outside Cyprus VAT scope; B2C sales to non-EU consumers are outside scope if effective use is abroad; B2C sales to consumers established in the EU must be declared at the destination Member State's rate via the OSS scheme and cannot be zero-rated. All zero-rating claims require input export documentation to be retained for at least 6 years.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document – EX/A declaration approved by Cyprus Customs)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR cargo manifest evidencing physical departure from EU customs territory
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Proof of payment (Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit transfer receipt showing FX inflow)
  • Customer's valid EU VAT identification number confirmed via VIES (for intra-Community zero-rating)
  • Signed service agreement/contract evidencing customer establishment location and effective use (for export of services)

No real-time continuous transaction control (CTC) clearance, API filing, or digital stamping regime is currently in force for B2B or B2C invoices. E-invoicing is permitted and commercially used; B2G invoices must comply with the European e-invoice standard EN 16931 routed through a PEPPOL access point. All VAT returns, VIES listings, Intrastat declarations, and OSS returns are mandated to be filed electronically exclusively via the TAXISnet portal. Cyprus has publicly committed to implementing mandatory structured B2B e-invoicing reporting under the EU ViDA package with an expected application date from the early 2030s; no fiscal device requirement applies to EdTech digital subscription services.

Cyprus Customs and the Tax Department conduct joint audits cross-referencing zero-rated export claims against customs export manifests, VIES records, and inward banking flows. Unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger immediate administrative reassessment recovering the unpaid VAT plus clawback of any refunded input VAT, with a 6-year assessment window (unlimited in cases of deliberate fraud). Penalties: automatic 10% surcharge on under-declared VAT; late-payment interest determined annually by the Government (currently computed from the assessment date); civil penalties up to EUR 500 per invoice for VAT law breaches; conviction on summary basis may impose fines up to EUR 10,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years, and on indictment fines up to EUR 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 2 years for directors and officers personally. Repeated abuse additionally triggers the denial of the right to deduct input VAT under EU anti-fraud case law, publication of penalties, and disqualification from OSS registration.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies to both current and capital account transactions. Euro membership and EU law, including Article 63 TFEU on free movement of capital and payments, eliminate exchange-control permits, foreign-currency transfer authorisations, and capital import/export licensing. Profit remittance is unrestricted for non-resident shareholders: Cyprus imposes no dividend withholding tax at source, and no Central Bank approval is required for dividend distribution. The only hurdles are corporate-law formalities such as board resolution, solvency test, audited financial statements, and beneficial-ownership disclosure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. There is no statutory deadline, mandatory conversion ratio, or surrender obligation for export receipts, including revenue from EdTech subscription services. Exporters may retain foreign currency in domestic or offshore accounts. The only reporting layer is Central Bank / European Central Bank balance-of-payments statistical reporting under the International Transactions Reporting System (ITRS), under which the resident receiving bank must accurately code inbound receipts.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou), acting within the Eurosystem, with the European Central Bank holding primary monetary and exchange-rate competence for the euro.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Conditional-High for an EU/EEA-licensed electronic-money institution passported into Cyprus under Directive 2009/110/EC and the revised Payment Services Directive (PSD2); Low for offshore/non-EU EMIs, which cannot lawfully provide payment accounts, hold segregated client funds, send SEPA transfers, or access TARGET2 settlement without a Cypriot/EU authorisation. Offshore EMI payment rails may only be used as supplementary collection channels and never as the primary treasury account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Use SWIFT MT103 (or equivalent ISO 20022 pain.001) from the parent/shareholder with beneficiary account at a CBC-licensed bank. For equity capital, the ISO purpose code should be SHAR (shareholder/equity payment) and field 70 must state 'Equity capital contribution under Companies Law Cap.113, no goods/services'. No central-bank permit is required, but the receiving bank must verify against the full corporate bundle: board resolution, share allotment form, updated register of members, CR16 and beneficial-ownership register. For intercompany loans, use purpose code INTC and attach the executed loan agreement, pricing schedule and transfer-pricing memorandum. Charges should be OUR, value undated, and payments must never be split artificially to avoid BOP reporting thresholds.For euro-denominated supplier or content-developer payments, route through SEPA Credit Transfer or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer via TARGET2. For non-EUR settlement such as USD or GBP, use SWIFT MT103 with MT202 COVER and SWIFT gpi tracking, coding purpose TRAD for ordinary trade disbursements. For high-value physical merchandise or equipment procurement, use a commercial documentary letter of credit issued under UCP600 via SWIFT MT700, confirmed where necessary; reserves and documentary discrepancies must be cleared before presentation. Alternative collection rail is documentary collection (DP/DA) for low-risk recurring counterparties.Outright deliverable EUR/USD, EUR/GBP and EUR/CNH forwards, with ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or bank-form dealer confirmation so that subscription revenue from US/UK markets is economically hedged without speculation.FX swaps for temporary liquidity needs, including same-day and tomorrow-next swaps between EUR and USD, combined with MT300 confirmations and ECB reporting obligations.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for currencies with restricted deliverability such as offshore renminbi (CNH), permitting hedging of Chinese payment receipts without requiring physical EUR/CNY settlement.Plain-vanilla cross-currency swaps (CIRS) for long-term intercompany loans or large capital liabilities where fixed or floating receivables and payables are swapped fully on an interest-rate and notional exchange basis.
