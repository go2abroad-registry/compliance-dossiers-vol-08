Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance of the Republic of Cyprus (Tax Department; Τμήμα Φορολογίας); official portal: Tax Portal / Ariadne at https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy and the Republic of Cyprus public services portal.

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus-registered Ltd obtains its Tax Identification Number automatically during incorporation through the Department of Registrar of Companies and Tax Department registration file activation. A foreign non-resident entity not incorporated in Cyprus but subject to Cyprus income tax or VAT must apply to a District Tax Office using the prescribed TIN 101 income-tax registration form, attaching the apostilled certificate of incorporation, constitutional documents, director/shareholder identity evidence, proof of registered address, power of attorney, Cyprus tax representative details, and a description of intended Cyprus activities. For VAT, the entity files Form VAT 101 (simplified Form VAT 102 for non-established taxable persons) with the VAT Service of the Tax Department. Tax returns, assessments, and VAT filings are managed through the Tax Portal. Cyprus VAT numbers take the form 'CY' followed by eight digits and a check letter.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. Cyprus private limited companies are separate taxable persons and are not fiscally transparent. Cyprus-tax-resident companies are taxed on worldwide income at the 12.5% corporate income tax rate; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income. Cyprus does not provide pass-through treatment for a Ltd; dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are generally not subject to Special Defence Contribution.

Available election forms

Cyprus has no US-style check-the-box or entity classification election; all capital companies are taxed at entity level. Statutory elections available to a qualifying Cyprus Ltd include group relief allocation for 75% group members, tonnage tax election for qualifying shipping activities, the IP box regime election for qualifying intangible assets (80% exemption, effective rate 2.5%), the notional interest deduction on new equity, and the One-Stop-Shop (OSS) VAT election for cross-border EU distance sales or digital services. Hybrid mismatch check-the-box elections are not recognised; anti-hybrid rules follow the EU ATAD implementation of the OECD/G20 BEPS minimum standard.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For Cyprus-established taxable persons, compulsory VAT registration arises where annual taxable turnover exceeds EUR 15,600 (excluding VAT), or is expected to exceed that level in a 12-month period. A non-established foreign supplier generally has no threshold and must register for Cyprus VAT from the first taxable supply in Cyprus unless it uses the OSS non-Union scheme. Intra-EU distance sales and electronically supplied services to consumers in other Member States are covered by an EU-wide EUR 10,000 threshold; crossing the threshold shifts the place of supply to the destination Member State, with VAT accounted through OSS.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to supplies of goods dispatched or transported to destinations outside the EU by the supplier, the customer, or a third party on their behalf, provided the goods leave the EU within the statutory period and the exporter holds contemporaneous documentary proof of export. The 0% rate also covers certain directly export-linked supplies, such as international transport and ancillary services, and supplies to qualifying diplomatic bodies. For the commercial stock-photo and AI visual-assets business, digital image licensing is treated as an electronically supplied service; where the customer is outside Cyprus, the supply is generally outside the scope of Cyprus VAT under place-of-supply rules, not zero-rated. Intra-EU B2B supplies must be reported on the EC Sales List, and B2C digital services to EU consumers are taxable in the consumer's Member State through OSS.

  • Customs Export Declaration using a Single Administrative Document (SAD) or the electronic AES export entry
  • Commercial invoice stating the nature, quantity, value, and export destination of the goods
  • Packing list

Cyprus does not currently impose mandatory real-time B2B e-invoicing clearance, continuous transaction control, or digital stamping. The national Ariadne interoperability network supports Peppol-based e-invoicing for public procurement, while private-sector e-invoicing remains optional by mutual consent. VAT returns, EC Sales Lists, and VIES statements must be submitted electronically through the Tax Portal, and e-invoices and source records must be retained for at least six years. Cyprus is preparing for the future EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) real-time digital reporting requirements.

The Tax Department, together with Cyprus Customs, audits zero-rated exports against customs exit data, transport records, VAT returns, and payment evidence. Where zero-rating is not substantiated, the assessment is reversed and VAT at 19% is demanded, with statutory interest from the time the VAT was originally due. The supplier bears the burden of proving export; retroactive clawback may cover the ordinary assessment period, extended in cases of fraud or negligent misstatement. Incorrect returns or false export evidence may trigger financial penalties, denial of associated input VAT, and criminal prosecution for VAT fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a full euro-area member (since 1 January 2008); both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible under Article 63 TFEU on free movement of capital. The Exchange Control Law was repealed in 2004, so no prior approval, quota, declaration, or tax-clearance certificate is required for profit remittance, dividend distribution, repayment of shareholder loans, or repatriation of equity capital by non-residents. Dividends, interest, and royalties paid to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Cypriot withholding tax. Residual practical hurdles are limited to AML/KYC source-of-funds verification by the receiving bank, central bank Balance-of-Payments statistical reporting for single transactions above EUR 100,000, and EU/UN/OFAC sanctions screening. The euro is the statutory functional currency; USD/GBP/CHF balances may be held offshore or in multicurrency accounts indefinitely.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Cyprus abolished all foreign-exchange surrender and export-proceeds repatriation requirements upon EU accession and definitively with euro adoption. Export service proceeds arising from stock-photo licensing and generative visual-asset royalties may be retained in any freely convertible currency, held offshore in non-resident accounts, or netted without any statutory conversion ratio, mandatory timeline, or notification to the Central Bank.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου), acting as the national banking and payments regulator within the Eurosystem; foreign-exchange reserve policy and euro liquidity management are delegated to the European Central Bank (ECB). No separate national Foreign Exchange Administration exists in Cyprus.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-to-High for operational payout rails, Low for primary treasury custody. Under PSD2 and the Electronic Money Directive (EMD2), EU-licensed electronic money institutions (Revolut, Wise, Paysera, Modulr) can passport into Cyprus via a Central Bank of Cyprus notification and may lawfully execute mass low-value contributor disbursements, FX conversion, and SWIFT payouts in 20+ currencies. These rails are viable as T+1 sweep vehicles, but their safeguarding accounts do not constitute a regulated corporate capital escrow and carry single-provider settlement and concentration risk; the inbound capital injection and long-term reserve must remain with a tier-1 commercial credit institution. The stock-photo platform itself does not require a CySEC/CBC payment-institution licence because it settles merchant transactions solely under its own content-licence contracts through regulated PSPs.

Under PSD2 and the Electronic Money Directive (EMD2), EU-licensed electronic money institutions (Revolut, Wise, Paysera, Modulr) can passport into Cyprus via a Central Bank of Cyprus notification and may lawfully execute mass low-value contributor disbursements, FX conversion, and SWIFT payouts in 20+ currencies. These rails are viable as T+1 sweep vehicles, but their safeguarding accounts do not constitute a regulated corporate capital escrow and carry single-provider settlement and concentration risk; the inbound capital injection and long-term reserve must remain with a tier-1 commercial credit institution. The stock-photo platform itself does not require a CySEC/CBC payment-institution licence because it settles merchant transactions solely under its own content-licence contracts through regulated PSPs.

The Cyprus tax year is the calendar year (1 January - 31 December). Form TD4 must be filed within 12 months after the end of the tax year, i.e. by 31 December of the following year. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Documentation is mandatory when the aggregate value of controlled transactions exceeds: (i) EUR 750,000 for financing and financial arrangements (including shareholder loans); (ii) EUR 750,000 for intra-group services, royalty, and use of intangible assets; and (iii) EUR 2,000,000 for the acquisition and disposal of goods in the tax year. A Cyprus Local File must be maintained generally where such aggregated transactions exceed the above limits; a Master File is required when the multinational group of which the entity forms part has consolidated group revenue exceeding EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: The Economic Substance Doctrine is applicable: Cyprus imposes a minimum-substance requirement through the Income Tax Law (Management and Control rule), the EU ATAD GAAR, and OECD nexus conditions for the IP Box regime. For a stock-photo licensing and AI-generated visual assets business, the Cyprus company must demonstrate: (a) local board meetings and strategic control in Cyprus; (b) qualified local employees executing core income-generating activities (procurement of image portfolios, licensing and contract negotiation, billing management through bank accounts in Cyprus); (c) expenditure incurred in Cyprus; and (d) for the IP-Box regime, a nexus ratio of qualifying R&D costs (tracking documentation) of at least 30% of the relevant development costs.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Fraudulent Under-Declaration of Stock Photo Royalties

Statutory sanction: Civil: additional assessment of tax evaded, 10% surcharge (non-negotiable), statutory interest accruing monthly; criminal: conviction under Articles 15 and 16 of the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law results in a fine of up to EUR 10,000 and imprisonment for up to 3 years; a court order for disqualification of the corporate from tax incentives for 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign inaccurate undistributed-profit declarations or who instruct concealment of revenue files are exposed to personal criminal conviction; the court may pierce the corporate veil under the Cyprus Companies Law Cap 113 allowing creditors to claim personal restitution, in addition to travel bans, and to the extent fraud is proven, imprisonment of up to 3 years.
Shell Company – Sham Substance / Letter-Box Arrangement

Statutory sanction: The entity is re-domiciled as a non-resident deemed to be managed and controlled offshore; all Cyprus-source IP income is reassessed, deductions are denied, a 10% surcharge applies, and the beneficial treaty status is withdrawn. In flagrant cases the tax is escalated to 25% (the standard rate applicable to non-filers), plus penalties of 50% of the assessed tax.

Director personal liability: Under EU ATAD anti-abuse rules and Cyprus jurisprudence, the directors are treated as having formed an artificial arrangement; they can be held jointly liable for the corporate tax and surcharges, subjected to a travel ban isolating assets, and, if they knowingly participated, to criminal fines and imprisonment in addition to civil restitution to revenue authorities.
AML – Know Your Customer and Monitoring Shortfalls in Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under the Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law N.188(I)/2007: administrative fines from €5,000 up to €1,000,000 for entities; criminal money-laundering conviction leads to unrestricted fines and imprisonment up to 14 years. MOKAS may also issue freezing orders on all bank and payment-scheme accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors are classified as 'covered persons' subject to personal CDD obligations; willful blindness to the provenance of stock-media licensing proceeds triggers personal criminal liability with imprisonment of up to 14 years, personal civil confiscation of proceeds, travel bans, and disqualification from acting as company directors for up to 10 years.
