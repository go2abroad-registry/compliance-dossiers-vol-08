Governing statute

Companies Law, Cap. 113 of the Laws of Cyprus (as amended through 2021) — formation provisions (ss. 4–13), memorandum and articles of association (ss. 6–9), certificate of incorporation and effect of registration (ss. 11–13), directors and company secretary (Part VII, notably ss. 110, 114 and 124), and registered office filing obligations; electronic incorporation implemented through the eCompanies (e-registry) system.

Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under the Companies Law Cap. 113 and no escrow or paid-up deposit is required before incorporation. The Memorandum typically declares authorised share capital of EUR 1,000 (1,000 ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each); at least one share must be issued and fully paid on incorporation and any remaining shares may be issued later. The annual Cyprus company levy of EUR 350 is payable separately to the Registrar.

A standard office occupational clearance will be issued for the Nicosia registered address.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Republic of Cyprus (Tax Portal: www.taxportal.mof.gov.cy)

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus private limited company obtains its Tax Identification Number (TIN) automatically upon incorporation and, if not already designated, files the appropriate registration notice with the local District Tax Office together with certified incorporation documents, registered office address, and anticipated activity statements. A foreign entity earning Cyprus-source income or establishing a Cyprus permanent establishment must apply to the Department of Taxation, attaching the foreign incorporation certificate, an English translation if applicable, board resolution appointing a local tax representative, and audited financial statements or an opening balance sheet as required.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Cyprus private limited company obtains its Tax Identification Number (TIN) automatically upon incorporation and, if not already designated, files the appropriate registration notice with the local District Tax Office together with certified incorporation documents, registered office address, and anticipated activity statements. A foreign entity earning Cyprus-source income or establishing a Cyprus permanent establishment must apply to the Department of Taxation, attaching the foreign incorporation certificate, an English translation if applicable, board resolution appointing a local tax representative, and audited financial statements or an opening balance sheet as required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus law does not provide a US-style 'check-the-box' election. A Cyprus incorporated private limited company is subject to entity-level corporate income taxation at 12.5%. Certain partnerships and joint ventures may benefit from tax-transparent treatment; a company may also elect for the tonnage tax or intellectual property box regimes where statutorily eligible. Hybrid mismatch elections are not separately available under Cyprus domestic law; such arrangements are instead denied relief under the EU Anti-Tax Avoidance Directive implementation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

EUR 15,600 taxable turnover within the preceding 12 months for general supplies of goods and services; EUR 10,000 annual intra-Community distance sales threshold; and compulsory registration from the first supply for non-established electronic service providers.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: zero-rating applies where goods are physically removed from Cyprus to a destination outside the European Union and the export is evidenced to the satisfaction of the Customs Department within the prescribed period. For digital health-app services supplied to non-EU customers: such supplies fall outside the scope of Cyprus VAT under the place-of-supply rules; supplies to EU consumers via Apple IAP or Google Play Billing may instead fall under the OSS Non-Union Scheme or the consumer's local VAT regime.

  • Validated electronic Customs Export Declaration (Electronic SAD / ECS customs receipt)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, CMR, or other transport document evidencing departure from the EU
  • Commercial invoice issued to the non-EU buyer
  • Purchase order or contract establishing the commercial export transaction
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds

Cyprus does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance, SAF-T submission, or digital stamping for B2B sales invoices. Electronic invoicing is legally valid and common between taxable persons, while invoices to Cypriot public authorities must be submitted via the government e-invoice platform. There is no API-based continuous transaction control regime in force.

Where export evidence is absent, inaccurate, or fraudulently produced, the Department of Taxation will deny zero-rating retroactively, render the VAT due with interest from the date of supply, and impose administrative penalties of up to 200% of the tax evaded. The Cyprus Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law permits assessments up to six years retrospectively, extending to ten years in cases of fraud or willful default; criminal prosecution may lead to fines and imprisonment of up to five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility in practice: the euro is the legal tender of Cyprus, and the Republic maintains no exchange-control restrictions on current or capital account transactions for residents or non-residents, consistent with Articles 63-65 of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. The emergency capital controls imposed in March 2013 during the banking crisis were fully abolished by April 2015. Inbound equity injections, dividend profit remittances, intercompany loans, intragroup guarantees, royalty payments, and trade settlements are unrestricted and require no CBC prior approval; the only operative requirements are commercial-bank KYC/AML verification, balance-of-payments statistical reporting by the receiving credit institution, and corporate-law compliance under the Cyprus Companies Law, Cap. 113. Profit remittance to a non-resident shareholder is not subject to Cyprus dividend withholding tax (0% WHT) and is not subject to the Special Defence Contribution (SDC), which applies only to Cyprus tax-resident recipients; dividend payments to EU/EEA and treaty-resident shareholders may additionally benefit from the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive and applicable double-tax treaties. Outbound royalties to non-residents are likewise free of Cyprus withholding tax, with the EU Interest and Royalties Directive providing a further safe harbour for EEA resident payees. The 12.5% unified corporate income tax rate applies to chargeable profits, and the notional interest deduction and IP box rules are available to optimise the treasury structure while maintaining arm's-length substance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable: Cyprus imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory foreign-currency conversion ratio, and no prescribed timeline for the repatriation or conversion of export proceeds. A Cyprus resident company may retain 100% of its export receipts in euros or any permitted foreign currency in accounts held with licensed credit institutions or CySEC-authorised EMI client accounts. The health-fitness app company must nonetheless ensure (i) each inbound settlement from App Store Connect or Google Play Console is classified by the receiving bank under the CBC's ITRS services-export item for digital content and computer services; (ii) original App Store Connect financial reports and Google Play payout statements are retained as underlying audit evidence for the CBC and the Cyprus Tax Department; (iii) any cash or bearer negotiable instruments of EUR 10,000 or more crossing the EU border are declared pursuant to Regulation (EU) No 1889/2005 as transposed in Cyprus; and (iv) the operational treasury applies standard VAT reverse-charge treatment for cross-border B2B digital services under the EU VAT One-Stop-Shop regime and the 12.5% corporate tax return filings, because no currency-surrender deferral or exemption election exists to manage.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (CBC), operating as the national competent authority within the Eurosystem and the European System of Central Banks; the European Central Bank (ECB) exercises monetary policy and currency oversight for the euro, while the CBC performs prudential supervision of Cyprus credit institutions, foreign-exchange statistical reporting via the International Transactions Reporting System (ITRS/BOP), and AML/CFT supervision of banks and electronic money institutions under the Cyprus Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law, L.188(I)/2007, as amended.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Rated Medium as a primary bank substitute but High as a secondary liquidity rail. CySEC-authorised Electronic Money Institutions under the Electronic Money Law L.81(I)/2012, transposing EU Directive 2009/110/EC, and EU/EEA-passported EMIs (for example Revolut Business, Monese, and similar PSD2/EMD2 passport holders) may legally provide client accounts, IBANs, and SEPA Instant payout rails to a Cyprus private limited company. The legal constraint is that such EMIs are not deposit-taking credit institutions and cannot provide committed credit facilities, commercial letters of credit, or full-scope treasury margin lending; in addition, several EMIs restrict pass-through settlement flows from App Store and Google Play aggregators without supplementary UBO and source-of-funds documentation. The recommended dual-core treasury architecture is therefore: (i) a Cyprus Tier-1 bank holding the escrowed capital and the primary operating EUR account, and (ii) a CySEC-authorised or EU-passported EMI used solely for instantaneous contractor payouts and SEPA CT Inst sub-discharge, with daily treasury sweeps back to the Tier-1 account to preserve a complete audit trail and to avoid any perception of shadow-banking fragmentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital is routed without CBC pre-approval, but with strict wire-protocol and purpose-code discipline. For EU/EEA-resident shareholders, the compliant channel is a SEPA Credit Transfer (SCT) or SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (SCT Inst with ten-second settlement) from the shareholder's verified account to the company's Capital Escrow Account at the chosen Tier-1 bank, using the remittance reference 'CY Ltd subscription of share capital at par' and the shareholder's account reference. For non-EU shareholders, the compliant channel is a SWIFT MT103 from the remitting bank designating the inward remittance as 'Direct Investment - Equity Capital (capital subscription)' under the CBC's ITRS/BOP statistical classification, which the receiving Cyprus bank records automatically; no CBC form or licence is required before disbursement. The receiving bank will verify the source-of-funds and UBO dossier, clear the funds typically T+0 for SEPA credits and T+1 for MT103 wires, and the company must then file the share-allotment return (Form HE32) with the DRCOR within the statutory filing period following allotment under Cap. 113. Where the injection is denominated in USD, the MT103 clears through the bank's nostro account, is converted to EUR at the ECB euro reference rate plus a contracted spread, and the conversion ticket is retained as audit evidence; a formal board resolution and a subscription contract should pre-exist the transfer to document arm's-length substance.Three clearing rail families are available without currency-control constraints. First, for EUR-denominated disbursements within the EEA, SEPA Credit Transfer (D+1 value) and SEPA Instant Credit Transfer (up to ten seconds) serve contractor payroll, marketing agency fees, and cloud-host payouts. Second, for cross-currency supplier payments, SWIFT MT103 wires with the BoP service-item classification for digital services or software licensing are settled through the bank's FX desk or correspondent network at T+1/T+2. Third, for documentary trade obligations, Commercial Letters of Credit issued at sight under UCP 600 provide secured settlement for physical-device imports or infrastructure acquisition, with the Cyprus bank mandating no FX surrender and requiring only ICA/ISBP-compliant documentation. Platform partner remittances to Apple Distribution International Ltd (Cork) and to Google Ireland Ltd (Dublin) flow as SWIFT MT103 or SEPA credits classified under the BoP item 'charges for the use of intellectual property' or 'computer services', reflecting Cyprus's 0% royalty-withholding position; all outbound wires must be supported by underlying invoices or platform distribution agreements and by an OECD-compliant transfer-pricing file, so that the operational treasury retains CBC-supervision-ready audit records and Cyprus Tax Department arm's-length documentation.EUR/USD vanilla deliverable FX forwards (tenors up to 12 months, T+2 settlement, executed under the bank's ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or standard FX framework agreement).EUR/USD and EUR/GBP FX spots and same-day settlement forwards for the conversion of App Store and Google Play USD/GBP net proceeds into the EUR operating account.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in emerging-market and restricted-convertibility currencies such as INR, TRY, EGP, and NGN, transacted with Tier-1 counterparties under an ISDA 2002 Master and Credit Support Annex, used only where subscription revenue originates from those jurisdictions.Cross-currency swaps (EUR/USD fixed-for-fixed principal exchange) for medium-term treasury alignment against commercial letters of credit and capital facilities of 12-36 month tenor.Purchased European FX options (EUR/USD calls and puts matching the app-store settlement cycle) for asymmetric protection of platform subscription revenue, strictly long-volatility structures with no short-option or exotic auto-callable features.Synthetic FX forward replication via matched EUR/USD money-market deposits and loans, elected where the non-financial counterparty wishes to avoid derivative classification and EMIR clearing-threshold complexity.All executed OTC derivatives are subject to EMIR trade reporting to a registered trade repository;
