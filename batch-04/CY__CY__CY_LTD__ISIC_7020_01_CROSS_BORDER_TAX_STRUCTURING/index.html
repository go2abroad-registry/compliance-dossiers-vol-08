Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Cyprus Tax Department (Τμήμα Φορολογίας), Ministry of Finance — electronic filing portal 'Tax For All (Ariadni)'; corporate registry is administered by the Department of Registrar of Companies and Official Receiver (DROC), Nicosia.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Cyprus Private Limited Company at DROC to obtain an HE registration number. Step 2: File the new-company tax registration dossier (Form TD2001) with the local District Tax Office within 60 days of incorporation to obtain the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN). Step 3: Where taxable turnover exceeds the VAT threshold, submit VAT registration (Form VAT-101) to the VAT Service of the Tax Department; acceptance results in a distinct VAT registration number (CY prefix), issued electronically within 5-10 working days. Non-resident shareholders are permitted; no minimum capital or local director residency requirement applies. Foreign branch registrations (HE-19) follow analogous sequence with a local appointed agent.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Step 1: Incorporate the Cyprus Private Limited Company at DROC to obtain an HE registration number. Step 2: File the new-company tax registration dossier (Form TD2001) with the local District Tax Office within 60 days of incorporation to obtain the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN). Step 3: Where taxable turnover exceeds the VAT threshold, submit VAT registration (Form VAT-101) to the VAT Service of the Tax Department; acceptance results in a distinct VAT registration number (CY prefix), issued electronically within 5-10 working days. Non-resident shareholders are permitted; no minimum capital or local director residency requirement applies. Foreign branch registrations (HE-19) follow analogous sequence with a local appointed agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus applies an opaque entity-level corporate income tax (12.5%) with no US-style check-the-box system. Available statutory elections: (i) Group loss relief — surrenderability of losses among 75% connected companies; (ii) Optional VAT group registration (single taxable person); (iii) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on new equity capital, 2025 indicative rates apply; (iv) IP box nexus-compliant 80% profit exemption election; (v) Management and control residency election for Cyprus-registered companies managed from abroad, enabling non-resident tax status; (vi) Deemed Distribution Charge mechanism for undistributed profits to resident shareholders — optional mitigation via certification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€15,600 (taxable turnover over any preceding 12-month period; €10,000 for intra-Community acquisitions; €0 for non-established taxable persons supplying taxable goods or services intra-Community)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies where substantive proof of export exists: (i) physical export of tangible goods transported outside the European Union, evidenced within 3 months from the time of supply; (ii) intra-Community supply of goods delivered to a registered business in another Member State, supported by the customer's valid ARIADNI VAT number and periodic listing (VIES); (iii) services directly related to exported goods (e.g., freight, agency, packing); (iv) B2B services supplied to non-EU business customers (place of supply outside Cyprus); (v) international passenger and air/sea transport. Advisory services qualifying under ISIC 7020 are zero-rated only when the recipient is a taxable person established outside the EU and the benefit is effectively consumed outside Cyprus — documented service contracts and OECD model place-of-supply analysis are required.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document — SAD) with electronic IES confirmation
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill showing physical removal of goods from Cyprus/EU customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice, contract, and Incoterms evidence
  • Customer's EU VAT number and VIES validation for intra-Community dispatches

Mandatory electronic submission of VAT returns (VAT-4) and Intrastat declarations via the Tax For All (Ariadni) portal. No real-time B2B fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate in force. Cyprus participates in the Peppol network and mandates Peppol-compliant B2G e-invoicing for supplier transactions to Cypriot public bodies. Structured invoices with e-signature or EDI interchange are recognized. Proposed ViDA real-time digital reporting is not yet implemented — transmission via standard file upload with envelope controls.

Export zero-rating is heavily scrutinized by the Cyprus Tax Department and EU anti-fraud authorities. Failure to substantiate physical export within the statutory period triggers mandatory reassessment at 19% VAT on the entire consideration, plus interest at 3.5% per annum. Civil penalties under Articles 60-64 of the Assessment and Collection of Taxes Law 4/1978 reach 200% of the undeclared tax (100% for negligence, 200% for fraud), with criminal prosecution carrying imprisonment up to 5 years and fines up to €50,000. VAT refunds on surplus input credits are payable within 4 months from the electronic claim; delayed refunds accrue monthly penal interest at 1%. Retrospective clawback (5-year statute of limitation, extendable to 10 years for deliberate evasion) with cross-border exchange of information via the EU Administrative Cooperation Regulation 904/2010 is routinely applied.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a Eurozone member state and, as such, applies full current and capital account convertibility under the EU Treaty and the Central Bank of Cyprus (CBC) regulatory framework. Profit remittance and dividend repatriation are not subject to statutory conversion limits or exchange control licensing. However, practical banking execution requires submission of audited financial statements, a Cyprus tax residency certificate, current beneficial ownership declarations, and anti-money laundering source-of-funds evidence under L.188(I)/2007, as amended. Dividends paid to EU/EEA corporate shareholders are generally exempt from withholding under the Parent-Subsidiary Directive (90/435/EEC as codified), provided the 2-year holding period and substance requirements are met. Non-EU remittance may attract a 5% withholding tax unless a double-tax treaty reduction applies. No central bank approval is needed for ordinary operational FX conversion.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements and no mandatory conversion ratios in Cyprus. Exporters may retain 100% of export receipts in EUR or in foreign currency accounts. Settlements for trade transactions are reported to the CBC for balance-of-payments statistics only when individual foreign-currency receipts or payments exceed EUR 12,500. Non-EU exporters must provide commercial invoices, bills of lading, and an exporter registration reference to support wire clearing; no portion of proceeds is required to be converted into EUR.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα Κύπρου)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Cyprus-incorporated EMIs licensed by the Central Bank of Cyprus, or EU/EEA EMIs passporting into Cyprus under the Payment Services Directive (2015/2366/EU), may provide operational payment accounts, FX conversion, and cross-border disbursements. Offshore digital EMIs without EU/EEA passporting or CBC registration may not lawfully hold client funds or provide regulated payment services in Cyprus. Such EMIs cannot replace a tier-1 Cypriot bank for capital escrow, documentary credit, or substantial treasury intermediation. The recommended architecture is a Cyprus licensed bank as the primary settlement account, with an EU-authorized EMI used only as a secondary low-value disbursement channel. Any digital EMI must comply with CBC client-money segregation rules and provide an official payment institution licence or passporting notification.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital contributions must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign parent or investor to the Cyprus operational or capital escrow account. To ensure CBC balance-of-payments classification, the wire narrative must include 'Share Capital Subscription – CY_LTD' and the applicable EU balance-of-payments purpose code, typically 'G301 – Direct Investment: equity capital' or 'P701 – Other investment liabilities' for intercompany loans. Amounts above EUR 100,000 require a board resolution, signed subscription agreement, and completed source-of-funds / source-of-wealth forms. EUR transfers clear via Target2 through correspondent banks; USD or third-country currency transfers rely on the correspondent bank's nostro account and must include the full legal name of the beneficiary and the Cyprus Bank Identifier Code (BIC) plus IBAN. Strike-off trigger

Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: All Cyprus private companies must appoint a qualified statutory auditor under Section 69 of the Companies Law (Cap. 113). A mandatory audit is compulsory unless the company qualifies as a small company under two of three criteria (total assets not exceeding EUR 350,000; annual turnover not exceeding EUR 700,000; average employees not exceeding 10) AND its annual gross revenue does not exceed EUR 70,000. The exemption is unavailable to public-interest entities, credit institutions, insurance undertakings, and entities failing the EUR 70,000 revenue test. A cross-border tax structuring consultancy with ordinary professional billings normally exceeds the EUR 70,000 threshold and is therefore subject to a compulsory independent statutory audit; additionally, shareholders holding at least 10% of the issued capital may requisition an audit even where the exemption applies.

12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 1,000,000 aggregate value of controlled transactions in respect of goods and financial transactions; EUR 500,000 aggregate for services, intangible/royalty transactions. A Cyprus Master File is required when the consolidated group revenue exceeds EUR 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus applies OECD and EU substance-over-form principles through its tax residence rule (management and control), CFC rules (ATAD), and a general anti-abuse principle. A Cyprus Ltd must maintain a physical Cyprus office, local board meetings, resident directors with actual decision-making authority, adequate staff, and business records in Cyprus. Absence of these elements will result in denial of a Cyprus Tax Residence Certificate, recharacterisation as a shell, loss of treaty benefits, and retrospective reassessment of profit in the beneficial owner's jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of the full tax due at 12.5%, civil penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, interest at 3% per annum, and on criminal conviction fines of up to EUR 100,000 and/or imprisonment up to five years

Director personal liability: Directors and shadow directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax and penalties; courts may impose director disqualification, travel bans, and asset confiscation orders.
Shell Company / Non-Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Refusal or withdrawal of the Cyprus Tax Residence Certificate; denial of participation exemption and IP Box relief; reassessment treating the Cyprus entity as a pass-through or transparent vehicle; imposition of late-payment and documentation penalties

Director personal liability: Nominee directors are personally liable for filings they sign and may be disqualified for aiding a sham; beneficial owners risk personal assessment in the actual jurisdiction of management and control, with civil recovery proceedings and cross-border travel restrictions.
AML / Money Laundering Through CY Ltd

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Anti-Money Laundering Law, fines up to EUR 500,000 per contravention, freezing of bank accounts, revocation of operating licences, and criminal penalties upon conviction including imprisonment up to 14 years

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners exposed to criminal money-laundering charges; senior management can be criminally prosecuted individually, receive confiscation orders, and be barred from holding any financial-services position in Cyprus.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment re-computing taxable profit at arm's length; additional tax at 12.5%; penalty up to 20% of the adjustment; interest at 3% per annum; potential denial of domestic loss and participation exemptions

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or sign transfer-pricing documentation knowing it to be artificial are jointly liable for the additional tax; in serious cases, professional misconduct referrals and criminal prosecution for aiding tax evasion may follow.
