Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Department of Taxation, Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus (Cyprus Tax Department / Φορολογικό Τμήμα); official digital portal: 'Tax Portal' (https://taxportal.mof.gov.cy) and the 'Ariadne' e-filing system. VAT matters are administered by the same Department's VAT Service (Υπηρεσία ΦΠΑ).

E-government filing portal

A foreign or newly incorporated entity must (1) obtain incorporation documents and the Certificate of Registration from the Cyprus Registrar of Companies; (2) physically or electronically submit Form VAT 101 (Application for VAT Registration) and/or the TIN application via the Tax Portal, accompanied by the Certificate of Incorporation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, certified passport copies of directors/shareholders, proof of registered office in Cyprus, and (for non-resident entities) a Certificate of Tax Residence issued by the home jurisdiction; (3) for non-established digital suppliers, register directly under the EU Non-Union OSS scheme through the Cyprus Tax Portal, appointing a fiscal representative if required by EU rules; (4) await issuance of the 9-character TIN/VAT number with 'CY' prefix, typically within 15 working days for standard local applications; (5) activate the Ariadne e-portfolio for electronic submission of VAT returns, OSS filings, and Intrastat declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a Cyprus Private Limited Company is a separate taxable person; companies managed and controlled in Cyprus are tax resident and subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income at 12.5%; non-resident companies are taxed only on Cyprus-source income. No pass-through treatment is available. There is no income-tax-free zone unless the entity is an international trust or qualifies under the IP Box regime, which is a preferential income rate, not a territorial exemption. Digital micro-drama production/distribution income from foreign streaming platforms constitutes trading income taxable in Cyprus under the worldwide basis.

Available election forms

Check-the-box entity classification is not available under Cyprus law, as it is a unitary corporate tax jurisdiction implementing EU ATAD. Available statutory elections: (1) VAT grouping election under Article 42 of Law 95(I)/2000 (Form VAT 118); (2) Notional Interest Deduction (NID) election on new equity contributions under section 9K of the Income Tax Law; (3) IP Box regime election under section 9A/9B (Qualifying Intangible Assets); (4) group tax loss relief election under sections 17 and 18; (5) deemed dividend distribution election (notional distribution at 70% of undistributed profits under Defence Law) with a 50% reduction alternative for reinvestment; (6) hybrid mismatch disallowance adjustments under ATAD (EU Directive 2016/1164), which override any mismatching payment/payable elections. None of these allow pass-through taxation for the Ltd entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CY€15,600 annual taxable turnover for established Cyprus businesses (Art. 11, VAT Law 95(I)/2000). For non-established entities and suppliers of digital services to Cyprus consumers, no threshold applies — registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply. For intra-Community acquisitions, registration is required above CY€10,251.61 (€10,000 digital distance sales threshold if selling to Cyprus consumers from another Member State).

Cyprus does not yet operate a real-time transactional clearance or digital stamping system. Mandatory e-invoicing is limited to B2G supplies via the PEPPOL network per EU Directive 2014/55/EU; B2B e-invoicing is voluntary and must remain authentic, integral, and legible under VAT rules. The Tax Department uses the Ariadne e-portal for VAT return filing (submitted by the 10th of the following month with payment by the 10th), OSS (One-Stop-Shop) quarterly digital service returns, and EC Sales Lists/Intrastat. No API-based fiscal data exchange or government pre-clearance is currently enforced for private B2B/B2C streams.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus has been a Eurozone member since 1 January 2008; the euro is fully convertible on both current and capital account for residents and non-residents, with no exchange-control approval required for cross-border remittances. Profit remittance is free: dividends to non-resident shareholders carry a 0% withholding tax (Special Defence Contribution applies only to Cyprus tax-resident shareholders), while dividend outflows to persons resident in blacklisted jurisdictions are disallowed for the dividend-received deduction or ordinary tax reliefs. The principal practical convertibility hurdle is banking rather than legal: Cypriot banks require documentary evidence of substance, source of funds and the underlying commercial contract before releasing or converting large USD/CNH flows, and digital micro-transaction revenue from Apple/Google/Stripe must be documented as merchant-of-record receipts to achieve proper current-account classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Cyprus imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion or repatriation timeline for export proceeds. A Cyprus company may lawfully retain USD, GBP or CNH receivables in domestic or EEA bank accounts without converting to euro. The only associated duties are tax recognition of the receivable in euro equivalent for VAT/corporate income tax purposes and the Bank of Cyprus Balance-of-Payments statistical reporting performed by the company's commercial bank. For digital services (vertical micro-drama in-app sales), Apple, Google and Stripe settlements are consideration for services supplied and require no export/import documentation or FX surrender.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Kentriki Trapeza tis Kyprou), operating within the European System of Central Banks and Eurosystem; CySEC has no role in FX controls, and payment/e-money institutions are supervised by the CBC under Law 103(I)/2018.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Revolut Fit: High

Medium-High. Cyprus-licensed or EEA-passported electronic money institutions (e.g., Revolut Business, Payoneer, Monese) can lawfully provide remote onboarding, multi-currency holding, low-cost FX and mass payouts to content creators under PSD2 safeguarding rules. Legal constraints: EMIs cannot take deposits on their balance sheet, must safeguard customer funds in segregated accounts at partner commercial banks, and typically do not offer documentary LCs, escrow, overdrafts or large-ticket settlement. For the vertical micro-drama treasury, the compliant architecture is a licensed Cypriot bank as the principal treasury nexus with an EMI/PSP as a secondary collection and mass-payout rail; a pure-EMI holding structure creates AML questions and weakens substance proof in Cyprus.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: use a regular corporate wire - SWIFT MT103 for extra-EEA funding or the Eurosystem T2 SEPA credit transfer (ISO 20022 pacs.008) for EEA funding - from the parent/promoter's corporate bank account to the Cyprus receiving bank. Equity injections must carry a purpose statement in the remittance field reading 'Increase of share capital - Cyprus Ltd HE77 registration number - board resolution date'; shareholder loans must state 'Shareholder loan disbursement pursuant to facility agreement dated DD-MM-YYYY'. The receiving bank files the Central Bank of Cyprus BoP statistical return classifying the flow as Direct Investment - Equity (inward) or Other Investment - Intercompany Loan, respectively. Pre-funding verification requires submission of the corporate resolution, updated shareholder register, facility agreement (for loans) and evidence of appointment of a Cyprus secretary and registered office; internal bank thresholds (typically EUR 100,000 per day) may trigger a pre-notification form and four-eyes compliance approval, but the CBC imposes no legal ceiling on capital injections.EU suppliers (post-production, local creative agencies, EU platform partners): T2-reachable SEPA credit transfer (ISO 20022 pacs.008, optional MT103) to the beneficiary IBAN referencing the PO and supplier invoice; for intra-EU digital acquisition services, the company must issue a reverse-charge VAT note (Form VAT 312A) and retain the supplier's invoice for the CBC BoP statistical categorization 'Trade in Services - Audiovisual Licensing'. Non-EU studios and distributors (US, HK, SG, CN): SWIFT MT103 in the deal currency - USD via the bank's New York correspondent over CHIPS/Fedwire, EUR via T2 cross-border credit, CNH via offshore renminbi market settlement to the studio's offshore account or USD clean payment against a USD-equivalent commercial invoice to a mainland account. A documentary commercial letter of credit under UCP600 (irrevocable, confirmed) is permissible and appropriate for high-ticket minimum guarantees (EUR 500,000 and above), but episodic micro-drama licensing is normally settled via telegraphic transfer with an acceptance certificate and commercial debit note. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 – a Cyprus tax resident entity is required to prepare and retain a Local File where the aggregate annual value of controlled transactions with related parties exceeds €750,000. A Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated annual turnover exceeds the equivalent of €750 million. Regardless of threshold, all Cypriot resident entities with controlled transactions must submit a TP Summary Table (Form FAT1) annually through TAXISnet; documentation must be contemporaneous and produced within 60 days of a written request.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not impose a stand-alone Economic Substance Regulation comparable to the UAE. However, corporate tax residence requires genuine management and control in Cyprus. Under the EU ATAD General Anti-Abuse Rule, the Cyprus Tax Department may disregard a structure lacking domestic substance. For a vertical micro-drama distributor claiming IP Box or DTA benefits, the taxpayer must demonstrate qualified employees, decision-making functions, specific development or improvement of intellectual property, and controllable risks in Cyprus. A pure shell company with minimal staff and no active management will be denied treaty benefits and may be deemed non-resident, triggering domestic and EU anti-abuse penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Misstatement of Digital Revenue

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Law (Cyprus), fraudulent or negligent avoidance of tax exposes the company to additional tax, a surcharge of up to 100% of evaded tax, interest at statutory rate on overdue amounts, and criminal fines up to €20,000. The Tax Department may act within 6 years of the end of the assessment year (12 years for deliberate default).

Director personal liability: Civil recovery may be pursued against directors who signed false returns. In flagrant cases, directors are personally liable for unpaid corporate tax under the Companies Law (Qualifications of Director) provisions, and the court may impose travel bans, asset-freezing orders, director disqualification for up to 5 years and imprisonment for up to 3 years.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: The Tax Commissioner can disregard the separate personality of the shell company under the anti-abuse provisions and tax profits directly in the hands of the beneficial owners at applicable rates. The company may be struck from the Cyprus Registrar of Companies for non-compliance with economic substance checks; the Cypriot Tax Department can also retrospectively apply Cyprus residency rules and levy penalties for misrepresentation. DAC6 and the EU GAAR require disclosure and levy penalties of up to €1,500 per infringement for propeller-type arrangements.

Director personal liability: Directors of a shell company are exposed to joint and several liability for unpaid corporate taxes plus a fine of up to €5,000 each. A director's travel ban may be issued under the Tax Collection Law where tax debts exceed €50,000, and a criminal conviction for knowingly maintaining a sham company can result in imprisonment for up to 4 years.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty for missing documentation is €150 for each document not delivered within the statutory 60-day response period. Where the Tax Department proposes a transfer pricing adjustment, an additional 10% assessment surcharge applies; in cases of gross negligence the adjustment may be increased to 25% of the taxpayers' resulting tax difference. Interest accrues at statutory rates until full payment.

Director personal liability: Directors signing tax returns with deliberate mispricing can be jointly liable for the additional TP adjustment plus interest. In criminal cases, the court may impose disqualification from serving as a director for up to 3 years and a personal penalty equivalent to 2% of the aggregated controlled transactions.
Anti-Money Laundering / Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Cyprus Prevention and Suppression of Money Laundering Activities Law, natural and legal persons face administrative fines of up to €250,000 for each non-AML compliance breach. The Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission or the Social Insurance and Tax authorities may additionally impose penalties equal to double the amount laundered, and criminal conviction carries imprisonment of up to 5 years

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly facilitate money laundering through the vertical micro-drama platform are personally exposed to criminal imprisonment of up to 5 years and confiscation of personal assets derived from the offence. Directors may be barred from holding office and subjected to a travel ban where civil recovery proceedings are initiated.
