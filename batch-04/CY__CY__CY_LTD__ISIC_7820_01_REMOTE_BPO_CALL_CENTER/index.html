Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Department (Department of Taxation), Ministry of Finance, Republic of Cyprus; e-services via the Tax Portal at taxportal.portal.gov.cy.

E-government filing portal

A Cyprus-incorporated Ltd is automatically issued a corporate tax identification number (TIN) by the Registrar of Companies at incorporation and must be registered with the Tax Department for income tax. A foreign entity with a Cyprus taxable presence must apply to the Tax Department for non-resident TIN registration before commencing activity. VAT ID registration for all entities, including non-established foreign suppliers, is made through the Tax Department's official VAT registration application, submitted directly or through an authorized tax representative where no Cyprus permanent establishment exists. Supporting documents include certificate of incorporation, articles of association, proof of address, description of taxable supplies, and expected turnover.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A Cyprus-incorporated Ltd is automatically issued a corporate tax identification number (TIN) by the Registrar of Companies at incorporation and must be registered with the Tax Department for income tax. A foreign entity with a Cyprus taxable presence must apply to the Tax Department for non-resident TIN registration before commencing activity. VAT ID registration for all entities, including non-established foreign suppliers, is made through the Tax Department's official VAT registration application, submitted directly or through an authorized tax representative where no Cyprus permanent establishment exists. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Cyprus has no US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections and follows ATAD. Available taxpayer elections include: (i) group relief for losses between 75%+ related group members; (ii) VAT grouping with the Tax Commissioner's consent; (iii) election for notional interest deduction (NID) on new equity; and (iv) IP box partial exemption election where eligible.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

19%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

€15,600 annual taxable turnover for established taxable persons in Cyprus. Non-established foreign suppliers have no threshold and must register before the first taxable supply in Cyprus; EU distance sales to Cyprus consumers are subject to the €10,000 cross-border distance-sales threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Cyprus/EU to a third-country destination qualify for the 0% VAT rate if removal is evidenced within 90 days and the exporter retains all statutory export documents. Cross-border B2B services, including BPO call-center services supplied by a Cyprus Ltd to foreign taxable customers, are generally outside the scope of Cyprus VAT, not officially zero-rated; the place of supply is the customer's jurisdiction. Contracts, substantive invoices, bank statements, and cost documentation must evidence the true business-to-business nature. Cyprus law does not impose a general statutory FX-repatriation deadline; the 180-day figure is maintained as the filing and audit substantiation benchmark for export proceeds.

  • Customs export declaration (Single Administrative Document) lodged in the TARES/ECS system
  • Transport document proving physical departure from Cyprus/EU (Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or CMR)
  • Commercial invoice and supply contract
  • Packing list and delivery/cargo documentation
  • Bank remittance or inward payment confirmation (SWIFT MT103/inward remittance certificate) evidencing receipt of export proceeds

Cyprus does not currently impose a real-time fiscal clearance, electronic invoicing API, or digital stamping mandate for B2B businesses. E-invoicing is voluntary for private transactions; B2G suppliers must submit e-invoices through a PEPPOL-compliant access point for public procurement. VAT returns and VIES recapitulative statements are filed electronically via the Tax Portal.

The Tax Department and Cyprus Customs verify export evidence rigorously before accepting zero-rating or granting related refunds. Where documents are false, missing, or inconsistent, zero-rating is retrospectively clawed back and VAT is assessed at 19% plus statutory interest from the original due date. Under the VAT Law, tax evasion or irregular invoicing triggers surcharges, fines up to two times the VAT evaded in severe cases, possible criminal liability, and exposure to EU-wide OSS/refund audit measures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Cyprus is a eurozone member state and has fully adopted the euro as legal tender. Both current and capital account operations are freely convertible under the free movement of capital provisions of the Treaty on the Functioning of the European Union. Profit remittance through dividends is unrestricted and generally subject to no Cyprus withholding tax, provided the paying Cyprus tax-resident company satisfies economic substance requirements and anti-abuse rules. No prior exchange control approval is required for foreign direct investment, inbound loans, dividend distributions, liquidation proceeds, or service fee remittances, except for targeted EU financial sanctions and counter-terrorism asset freezes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Cyprus abolished statutory export proceeds surrender and mandatory conversion requirements upon EU accession. No timeline exists for repatriation of export receipts, and there is no minimum conversion ratio into euros. A Cyprus private limited company engaged in cross-border remote BPO services may retain export proceeds in foreign currency accounts, convert at freely negotiated commercial spot rates, or use them directly for foreign operating disbursements, subject only to corporate tax, transfer pricing, and payment transparency obligations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Cyprus (Κεντρική Τράπεζα της Κύπρου, CBC) as the national competent authority for banking authorisation, payment systems oversight, FX regulatory surveillance, and anti-money-laundering supervision of credit institutions; HM Treasury/Cyprus Ministry of Finance for exchange control legislation repeal; European Central Bank (ECB) for eurosystem monetary policy and TARGET2 payment systems.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: High as an ancillary payment rail, but Low as a sole corporate banking solution. Cyprus-recognised Electronic Money Institutions and Payment Institutions authorised in the EEA, such as Wise, Revolut Business, and specialised EU payment institutions, may lawfully service a Cyprus Ltd under the EU passporting regime. Offshore or non-EEA digital EMIs without a valid passport cannot operate as principal clearing institutions for the Cyprus entity, cannot receive statutory share capital, and should be restricted to petty displacement, employee expense reimbursements, or correspondent-supported B2B disbursements. The primary domestic bank account remains mandatory for statutory capital, tax payments, and audit trail integrity.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

12.5%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Cyprus domestic law imposes no withholding tax on dividends paid by a Cyprus Ltd to non-resident shareholders. The Special Defence Contribution (SDC) on deemed dividend distributions was abolished for distributions made on or after 1 January 2024, eliminating the prior 17% imputation baggage for resident individual shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

IR4 return and final self-assessed tax must be submitted and paid within 12 months after the end of the tax year, i.e., by 31 December of the year following the fiscal year-end (e.g., FY 2024 return due 31 December 2025). Provisional tax: 75% of the estimated liability payable by 31 July of the tax year and the remaining 25% by 31 December of the tax year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Provisional estimate and first installment (75%) due by 31 July of the tax year; second installment (25%) due by 31 December of the tax year. Companies incorporated in the current year may estimate conservatively and settle 100% by 31 December.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: EUR 750,000 per category of related-party transactions (goods; services; financial transactions; royalties/intangibles) in a tax year triggers the Cyprus Local File obligation for non-SME taxpayers. Master File is mandatory when the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750 million; CbC reporting applies at the same EUR 750 million group threshold. Documentation must be submitted within 30 days of a written Tax Department request and updated annually.

Economic substance scope: Cyprus does not administer UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR). Substance is instead tested through the Cyprus tax-residence rule (management and control), the conditions for NID, the IP Box nexus factor, and the OECD/ATAD GAAR. A remote BPO call center must maintain a physical office in Cyprus, employ resident staff performing the core helpdesk/telesales functions, keep books and records in Cyprus, and hold board meetings in Cyprus to support tax residence, TRC issuance and treaty-based income exemptions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Fraudulent or Negligent Return

Statutory sanction: Administrative: additional assessment up to 100% of the under-declared tax, 5.35% per annum late interest and a 5% surcharge. Criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Law and Criminal Code: fine of up to EUR 300,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; permanent revocation of the Tax Clearance Certificate and deregistration risk.

Director personal liability: Directors, shadow directors and signatories of false returns are prosecuted personally; civil restitution of evaded amounts; travel bans and freezing of Cyprus bank accounts; disqualification under Cyprus Companies Law Cap. 113.
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement and Nominee Abuse

Statutory sanction: Loss of Cyprus tax residence and denial of the Tax Residency Certificate; recharacterization of the full BPO profit to the jurisdiction of effective management with back-tax, interest and penalties; ATAD/GAAR reassessment of artificially structured transactions; EU listing and enhanced disclosure of the arrangement.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal assessment for corporate tax where the corporate veil is pierced for fraud or sham; misrepresentations in TRC applications trigger individual fines; FATCA/CRS exchange exposes UBOs to civil penalties and criminal referral.
