Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-in capital under Article 156 of the Companies Act. However, a Mainland Chinese investor must obtain a pre-incorporation investment permit from the MOEA Investment Commission, remit the full declared investment amount in foreign currency into a dedicated NT-dollar capital verification account at an authorized Taiwanese foreign-exchange bank operating as a statutory escrow-type account, and procure a licensed CPA capital verification report before the MOEA registration application; capital and share ownership may not be reduced or transferred without subsequent Investment Commission approval.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, SAT), administered through provincial and municipal tax service bureaus; e-filing portal: 全国统一规范电子税务局 (Nationwide Unified Electronic Tax Bureau) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; export clearance and customs declaration linkage via the China International Trade Single Window (中国国际贸易单一窗口, https://www.singlewindow.cn).

E-government filing portal

Under the multi-license integration reform (多证合一), the WFOE obtains its PRC tax identity automatically upon incorporation: the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码) issued by the SAMR business registry is the statutory TIN and VAT taxpayer identification number. Within 30 days of incorporation, the WFOE must activate tax registration through the Electronic Tax Bureau, complete real-name verification (实名办税) for the legal representative and financial officer, file bank account reporting (银行账户报告), accounting system filing (财务会计制度备案), and invoice type/quota determination (发票票种核定). Foreign shareholders supply notarized and legalized incorporation documents and file the Foreign Investment Information Report (外商投资信息报告) via the MOFCOM platform. No separate VAT ID is issued; the USCC is used for all VAT, CIT, customs, and export refund filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; a domestically incorporated WFOE is a PRC tax resident under Art. 2 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, taxed at 25% CIT on worldwide income with foreign tax credit relief; Territorial_Scope is worldwide. VAT classification defaults to small-scale taxpayer if annual turnover is below CNY 5,000,000, unless general taxpayer status is elected or mandatory; no pass-through election exists; cross-border e-commerce retail exporters may additionally apply a deemed taxable income ratio of 4% for CIT purposes under SAT Announcement [2019] No. 36.

Available election forms

PRC law provides no check-the-box or hybrid mismatch pass-through election; available mechanisms include: (i) election of VAT general taxpayer status for small-scale taxpayers with sound accounting, enabling input VAT refunds at the 13% output rate; (ii) cross-border e-commerce retail export election between 'exemption with input VAT refund' (有票退税) and 'exemption without refund' (无票免税) under Caishui [2018] No. 103, extended through 2025; (iii) export refund management category (一/二/三/四类) affecting pre-refund examination and FX substantiation; (iv) transfer pricing APA and contemporaneous documentation elections; (v) GAAR-based hybrid mismatch self-adjustment — hybrid entities are not recognized for PRC tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 annual taxable sales (rolling 12-month cumulative 应征增值税销售额) is the mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration threshold; below this threshold the WFOE registers as a small-scale taxpayer with a 3% levy rate (temporarily reduced to 1% through 2027). All entities must obtain a VAT/TIN identity (USCC) from business commencement; there is no economic turnover de minimis exemption from registration. Taiwan-side: a foreign enterprise selling goods to Taiwan consumers via Taiwan online platforms with annual sales above TWD 480,000 may require Taiwan Business Tax registration, although for physical goods this obligation generally falls on the Taiwan importer.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sale of China-origin automotive aftermarket parts to Taiwan qualifies for VAT zero-rating (零税率) and export tax refund (出口退税). General-taxpayer WFOEs apply the 免抵退 (exemption-offset-refund) method for self-manufactured goods, recovering input VAT up to the applicable export rebate rate (standard 13% for auto parts). Cross-border e-commerce retail exports under customs supervision codes 9610 (B2C parcels), 9710 (B2B direct), and 9810 (overseas warehouse) may elect 'refund with purchase invoices' (有票退税) or, absent valid VAT special invoices, 'exemption without refund' (无票免税), provided that: (i) the exporter is a registered cross-border e-commerce enterprise, (ii) goods are physically exported through PRC customs, (iii) payment is received from overseas consumers or entities, and (iv) per-transaction value limits are satisfied. Foreign exchange must generally be received within 180 days from the customs export date; the operative export refund declaration deadline is April 30 of the year following the export year, and refund rights lapse if the filing window is missed absent special approval.

Mandatory fully digitalized e-invoicing (数电票/全电发票) through the national Electronic Invoice Service Platform under Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期): every VAT invoice is issued with a 20-digit invoice number, QR code, and real-time upload to the tax authority's central invoice database at the moment of issuance. No physical tax-control device (税务UKey) is required for 数电票; enterprises integrate via the VAT Invoice Comprehensive Service Platform using API/XML interfaces for purchase and sales reconciliation. E-invoices are the only compliant invoice type for general VAT taxpayers. Customs export declarations are cleared through the Single Window system and automatically matched in real time with invoice data and bank FX receipts.

SAT Golden Tax IV performs automated cross-matching of customs export declarations, e-invoice issuance, and bank FX receipts. Anomaly triggers — abnormal FX settlement routing, inflated invoice prices, mismatched HS codes, excessive conversion-cost ratios (换汇成本), and false customs declarations — expose the WFOE to: (i) rejection of the refund application and re-classification as deemed domestic sale (视同内销补税) with a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge; (ii) retrospective clawback of all refunded VAT; (iii) administrative penalties of 100%–500% of the fraudulently obtained refund, suspension of export refund rights, and downgrade to the most severe Category IV credit rating under Art. 66 of the PRC Tax Administration Law; (iv) criminal liability under Art. 204 of the PRC Criminal Law (骗取出口退税罪), with a prosecution threshold of approximately RMB 100,000 defrauded, escalating to 5–10 years' imprisonment for 'huge amounts' and 10 years to life imprisonment (无期徒刑) with fines of 100%–500% or property confiscation for 'especially huge amounts'; and (v) unlimited retrospective recovery — fraud claims are not subject to the ordinary 3/5-year limitation periods — plus joint disciplinary blacklisting (联合惩戒) barring responsible individuals from new export refund registrations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is convertible for current-account items (goods, services, and dividends legally derived from after-tax profits) subject to transaction-authenticity review at the settlement bank; capital-account items remain tiered and require SAFE registration or case-by-case approval. The WFOE may open a capital-goods account for inbound equity injection, noting that PRC law currently imposes no statutory minimum paid-in capital for this entity type. Capital conversion follows the voluntary-settlement (意愿结汇) model with payment-by-use authenticity checks and a negative list that prohibits diversion to securities investment, entrusted loans, or non-owner-occupied real-estate purchases. Profit-remittance hurdles: completion of the annual statutory audit by a PRC CPA firm, corporate income tax settlement and clearance, 10% dividend withholding for Taiwan-resident shareholders (no cross-strait double-tax agreement is in force, so no treaty reduction is available), adoption of a board resolution, and SAFE verification of undistributed profits against audited financial statements at the paying bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mandatory foreign-currency surrender has been abolished; the statutory conversion ratio is 0%, and the WFOE is free to retain export proceeds in foreign currency or to convert them voluntarily. However, proceeds must be repatriated to China and reconciled within the bank's trade-forex monitoring file; SAFE's trade-in-goods regime treats proceeds collected later than 90 days after the customs declaration date as delayed-collection items requiring self-reporting through the Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System, and retention of export proceeds offshore beyond the prescribed repatriation window (generally 6 months from the customs declaration date) requires prior SAFE approval. For marketplace e-commerce exports collected through Stripe/PayPal rails, the WFOE must be registered in SAFE's Trade Foreign Exchange Income and Expenditure Enterprise Roster, maintain A-class status, and pass all settlements through either a licensed Chinese cross-border payment institution conducting centralized collection with segmented settlement reporting, or an onshore export-proceeds account matched to customs declarations under the 9610/9710/1210 e-commerce customs regimes.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局, SAFE), operating under the People's Bank of China (中国人民银行, PBOC). SAFE holds exclusive regulatory competence over current-account trade settlement verification, capital-account registration (FDI, external debt, outbound investment), and the licensing of cross-border payment institutions; PBOC exercises bank-supervisory authority over corporate account opening, AML/KYC, and payment-system licensing.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH as an offshore collection rail, LOW as an onshore account substitute. A foreign EMI without a PBOC Payment Business License (支付业务许可证) cannot open a domestic RMB settlement account, acquire onshore merchants, or perform RMB clearing. PayPal is lawful only through PayPal China's licensed acquisition platform; Stripe holds no PRC license and cannot onboard a mainland WFOE directly, so the WFOE must either operate a Hong Kong/Singapore merchant entity to connect to Stripe, or use a licensed Chinese cross-border collection processor (PingPong, LianLian, Airwallex, Payoneer) that provides a virtual foreign-currency collection account in the processor's name and settles export proceeds into the WFOE's commercial bank account under SAFE transaction-level reporting. Each settlement batch must carry matching e-commerce transaction data, customs declarations, and logistics evidence to withstand bank and SAFE authenticity review.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: NT$50,000,000 in aggregate related-party transactions triggers the full Transfer Pricing Report/Local File. NT$20,000,000 triggers simplified contemporaneous documentation, and NT$10,000,000 is the relevance threshold for cross-border transactions with tax-haven related parties. An MNE group with consolidated revenue above Taiwan's CbCR filing threshold (NT$27,000,000,000) must maintain a Master File and file country-by-country reports.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not maintain a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime like the UAE. However, the general anti-avoidance rules in Income Tax Act Articles 43-1, 66-8, and the taxation of resident entities on worldwide income impose a substance-over-form doctrine. A Taiwanese WFOE must demonstrate real business management locally, keep accounting records in Taiwan, have local employees or physical infrastructure, and make substantive business decisions in Taiwan. A shell WFOE without local economic activity will be denied treaty benefits, may be treated as a tax haven vehicle under CFC rules, and can have its taxable profit recharacterized to the beneficial owner.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fictitious Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under Income Tax Act Article 110, intentional underreporting gives rise to an additional tax of up to 200% of the underpaid CIT. Criminal conviction under Tax Collection Act Article 41 carries imprisonment of up to five years, detention, and fines up to NT$6,000,000. The tax authority is authorized to examine bank deposits and third-party payment settlement records to reconstruct income.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns or direct the concealment are personally liable for the underpaid tax to the extent of unjust enrichment. The National Taxation Bureau may impose leave restrictions and asset attachment under Tax Collection Act when corporate arrears exceed NT$2,000,000. Individual prosecution and imprisonment apply when fraud is proven.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authorities may apply general anti-avoidance rules to disregard the shell entity, reattribute the e-commerce sales income to the true economic owner, deny the treaty/TRC claim, and reassess 20% CIT with an additional penalty of generally not less than 2x the underpaid tax until the deficiency is paid. The commercial authority may also revoke company registration for a dormant filing company.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced in cases of fraud or sham arrangements; directors who participated in the shell structure may be personally assessed for unpaid corporate taxes. Travel bans, suspension of passport rights, and, in egregious cases, criminal liability for tax evasion may follow. Liquidation distribution from a shell company to foreign shareholders without settling tax liabilities creates personal recourse against the liquidator.
Improper Transfer Pricing / Non-Arm's-Length Transactions

Statutory sanction: The National Taxation Bureau may adjust the transfer price under Income Tax Act Article 43-1, recalculate taxable income, and assess an underpayment penalty plus daily statutory interest. Failure to maintain a Transfer Pricing Report where required results in an administrative fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000 and materially aggravates the penalty on any adjustment. For AMT purposes, the adjusted amount could also trigger the 12% basic tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or execute non-arm's-length related-party contracts may be jointly liable for the resulting tax deficiency if the contract is found to be a sham or has no valid business purpose. The tax authority may seek to attach personal assets of the responsible person when the corporate entity is insolvent or was used as the alter ego of the shareholder.
Withholding Tax Violations on Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: The Taiwanese entity, as withholding agent, becomes liable for the unwithheld tax and is fined an amount not less than the equivalent of 15% and not more than three times the unwithheld tax. Interest accrues from the original due date. Intentional mischaracterization is viewed as tax evasion under Tax Collection Act and transfers criminal exposure to the signatories.

Director personal liability: The responsible officer and finance director are personally liable for the unremitted withholding tax if they knowingly signed the remittance instruction. The National Taxation Bureau may order a travel ban and prohibit property transfers. Criminal imprisonment of up to five years is possible for deliberate failure to withhold or remit.
Money Laundering Through E-Commerce Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Control Act, conviction for money-laundering activities involving illegal tax proceeds is punishable by imprisonment of up to seven years and a fine of up to NT$50,000,000. The financial intelligence unit may freeze the company's Stripe/PayPal and bank accounts on suspicion, and the tax authority may impute unreported income using bank deposit analysis and third-party payment settlement data.

Director personal liability: Directors are statutory AML compliance officers for the entity even in online commerce. Personal liability attaches for failing to implement transaction monitoring. In money-laundering convictions, personal assets derived from the offence are confiscated; travel bans and extradition risk may apply. The 'responsible person' can be imprisoned separately from the corporate entity.
