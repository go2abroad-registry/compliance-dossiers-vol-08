Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Taiwan Companies Act does not impose a minimum paid-up capital on an ordinary company limited by shares. For CN_WFOE_LTD, the sole shareholder will subscribe 1,000,000 ordinary shares at NT$10 per share, for an aggregate initial paid-in capital of NT$10,000,000. No blocked escrow account is required; however, the subscribed capital must be remitted from overseas through an authorized foreign-exchange bank into a temporary Taiwan investment account, remain intact until the CPA issue of the capital verification report, and not be withdrawn before company registration. Withdrawal, asset misappraisal, or false payment before registration triggers criminal liability and personal liability of directors under Articles 9 and 11 of the Companies Act.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety: the warehouse must obtain a Fire Safety Facility Approval Certificate from the local Fire Department covering fire alarm systems, smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, emergency lighting, exit signage, and fire-resistant storage; the company must file a fire prevention plan and an emergency evacuation plan as part of the Civil Defense program. Occupational Safety: the company must perform a baseline occupational hazard assessment under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, provide electrical safety inspection for battery charging areas, maintain ventilation and thermal-runaway control, obtain structural certification for storage racks, and conduct employee emergency-response training. The Civil Defense clearance includes registration of hazardous chemical storage locations with the local fire department if lithium-battery stock exceeds the threshold requiring emergency response planning, and the company must maintain first-aid supplies and an accident-spill response kit.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign non-resident entity (e.g., a Chinese WFOE) establishing a taxable presence in Taiwan, the entity must apply for a Business Registration (營業登記) with the local National Taxation Bureau. The procedure includes: (1) submitting a completed Business Registration Application Form (營業登記申請書) to the competent tax authority in the jurisdiction of the business premises; (2) providing the original and copies of the company's incorporation certificate, articles of incorporation, and proof of foreign registration; (3) submitting a power of attorney (授權書) authorizing a local representative or tax agent; (4) providing the lease agreement or proof of ownership of the business premises; (5) obtaining a Uniform Invoice Number (統一編號) which serves as the Tax Identification Number (TIN) for VAT and income tax purposes. The application can be filed online via the Ministry of Finance's e-tax portal or in person. Approval typically takes 5-10 business days. The entity must also register for the electronic invoice system if it engages in B2C retail sales.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Monthly sales exceeding NT$80,000 (or annual sales exceeding NT$960,000) triggers mandatory registration for Business Tax purposes. However, for foreign e-commerce sellers, registration is required regardless of threshold if they have a fixed place of business or a dependent agent in Taiwan.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the export of goods from Taiwan to foreign countries (including mainland China, Hong Kong, Macau) and to services provided to foreign enterprises or individuals that are used outside Taiwan. For goods, the seller must provide proof of export, such as a customs export declaration (出口報單) and a bill of lading. For services, the seller must provide a contract and evidence of foreign currency receipt. The zero-rating is claimed on the Business Tax return, and input VAT on related purchases can be refunded.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單)
  • Bill of Lading (提單) or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (外匯收入證明) or Foreign Exchange Receipt (結匯證明)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Export Permit (if applicable)

Taiwan mandates the use of Electronic Uniform Invoices (電子發票) for all B2C retail transactions. Sellers must integrate with the Ministry of Finance's Electronic Invoice Integration Platform (財政部電子發票整合服務平台) to transmit invoice data in real-time or within 48 hours. For certain high-risk industries, real-time clearance via API is required. Anti-fraud digital stamping (QR code and random number) is mandatory on all electronic invoices. Failure to comply may result in penalties and suspension of business.

The National Taxation Bureau conducts rigorous audits on zero-rated transactions. If the required documentation is incomplete or fraudulent, the zero-rating is disallowed, and the tax authority will assess the 5% Business Tax retroactively, plus a penalty of up to 5 times the underpaid tax. Additionally, interest is charged from the original due date. In cases of deliberate fraud, criminal liability may be pursued under the Tax Collection Act, leading to imprisonment or fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is freely convertible on the current account once the supporting trade or service documentation is verified by the domestic bank, while capital-account transactions are strictly controlled. For a CN_WFOE_LTD receiving equity subscription proceeds from a Taiwan shareholder, the funds must be wired into a foreign exchange capital account opened after the FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE. The bank monitors the use of every capital injection and permits conversion into RMB only for genuine business expenditures under the payment-use mechanism after contracts and invoices are reviewed. Dividend remittance to the Taiwan parent is treated as a current-account outflow, but the bank will require an audited annual financial statement proving that the profit is distributable, a board resolution declaring the dividend, enterprise income tax clearance of the dividend withholding obligation, and documentary evidence that prior audited losses have been fully recovered. There is no legal prohibition on remitting the entire after-tax profit to Taiwan, but without the required audit and bank-level economic substance review, the remittance will be held. The WFOE should not attempt to convert capital-account receipts into RMB and later remit them as dividends before the scheduled operational usage; that would be deemed a disguised capital outflow and trigger penalties.

Export proceeds surrender rules

In accordance with SAFE current-account regulations, export proceeds from the sale of solar portable power products must be repatriated to the domestic collecting bank within 180 days after export declaration, unless an extension is approved by SAFE for certified receivables. There is no mandatory conversion percentage; the WFOE may keep the full amount in foreign currency in a current-account foreign exchange settlement account or convert 100% to RMB at the daily price. The bank must cross-match each MT103/CIPS credit to the customs declaration and the e-commerce transaction background in the cross-border payment database before settlement. Failure to meet the 180-day receipt requirement subjects the entity to a lower trade facilitation category and blocks export tax rebate claims.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), exercising delegated foreign-exchange administration under the People's Bank of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Not viable as a primary banking channel. PRC law requires the WFOE to hold its operating RMB account and SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account at a licensed domestic commercial bank. Offshore digital EMIs are legally barred from opening domestic Chinese bank accounts or settling SAFE-controlled transactions. Stripe and Affirm are merchant checkout rails only; their settlement funds must be transferred into a licensed third-party payment institution and then swept into the WFOE tier-1 bank account. The EMI cannot be used as the corporate treasury account, FX conversion point, or payment clearing backbone.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must execute a SWIFT MT103 using the beneficiary bank details of the CN_WFOE foreign exchange capital account, with the remittance purpose code stated as Foreign Direct Investment Equity Subscription and the SAFE FDI registration reference quoted in the payment instructions. The receiving tier-1 bank validates the incoming credit against the SAFE filing before booking it, and then a Chinese registered CPA performs capital verification and issues a legal capital verification report confirming paid-in capital. After verification, the foreign currency can be converted into RMB and transferred to the RMB basic operational account only as the WFOE presents underlying invoices, contracts, payroll schedules and tax filings supporting the approved purpose. Remittances must not be split to circumvent SAFE threshold filings, and any excess contribution over the registered foreign-invested amount requires an amended FDI registration before funding.For operating and trade payments, the WFOE issues a SWIFT MT103 payment instruction through its tier-1 bank, quoting the correct SAFE current-account payment purpose code and attaching the commercial invoice, purchase order, bill of lading or air waybill and customs declaration. The bank debits the RMB account, performs spot FX conversion, and sends the wire through the correspondent clearing network. For major import shipments of solar panels and portable power systems from Taiwan, the preferable rail is an irrevocable commercial letter of credit opened under UCP600 with a clean bill of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, quality inspection certificate and commercial invoice as required documents. Documentary collections on D/P or D/A terms can be used for recurring low-value deliveries once a clean settlement history exists. For RMB-denominated trade, CIPS may be used instead of SWIFT MT103 if the Taiwan counterpart bank participates in CIPS, reducing correspondent bank costs and keeping clearing within the PBC system.Onshore deliverable FX forwards with a licensed domestic commercial bank, limited to the amount and tenor of underlying USD/RMB current-account exposure from export sales or import purchases.Onshore FX swaps between RMB and foreign currency to manage short-term trade-liquidity timing, supported by the real trade documents and board-approved treasury policy.Currency options and zero-premium collar structures for the WFOE actual settlement flows, restricted to genuine exposure and approved by the bank compliance desk.Cross-currency swaps are permissible only when the underlying is a SAFE-registered loan or long-term trade payable; speculative or leveraged swap structures are prohibited.Offshore RMB non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted for the onshore WFOE; an NDF hedge, if needed, must be executed by the Taiwan parent outside the China entity and cannot be booked, paid or reimbursed by the WFOE.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable as a primary banking channel. PRC law requires the WFOE to hold its operating RMB account and SAFE-registered foreign exchange capital account at a licensed domestic commercial bank. Offshore digital EMIs are legally barred from opening domestic Chinese bank accounts or settling SAFE-controlled transactions. Stripe and Affirm are merchant checkout rails only; their settlement funds must be transferred into a licensed third-party payment institution and then swept into the WFOE tier-1 bank account. The EMI cannot be used as the corporate treasury account, FX conversion point, or payment clearing backbone.
