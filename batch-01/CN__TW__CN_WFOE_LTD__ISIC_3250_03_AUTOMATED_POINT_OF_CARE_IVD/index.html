Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China, exercised through provincial and municipal Tax Service Bureaus; online filing and administrative procedures are completed via the Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal, with VAT invoice controls through the National VAT Invoice Management System operated under Golden Tax Phase IV.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise with the State Administration for Market Regulation and obtain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code, which is the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number for all PRC tax purposes. Step 2: Tax registration is automatically completed through the SAMR-tax authority information sharing mechanism within 30 days of issuance of the business license; no separate paper tax registration certificate is issued. Step 3: Log onto the Electronic Tax Bureau and complete new taxpayer formalities, including selection of VAT taxpayer status as small-scale taxpayer or general taxpayer, determination of VAT filing period, accounting standard confirmation, and registration for electronic invoice issuance entitlement. Step 4: Apply for a digital Tax U-Key and electronic invoice authorization so that the WFOE can issue special and ordinary VAT invoices through the National VAT Invoice Management System. Step 5: For a genuinely foreign non-resident enterprise with no permanent establishment in China, the Chinese withholding agent, or the non-resident enterprise itself where required, must complete a non-resident enterprise tax registration with the competent Tax Service Bureau and obtain a temporary non-resident TIN; the application typically requires the foreign enterprise registration certificate, the cross-border service or royalty contract, and appointment of a PRC tax representative. This SOP therefore covers both the resident WFOE and the non-resident foreign-parent scenario.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise incorporated under PRC Company Law and is subject to enterprise income tax on worldwide income; no pass-through treatment is available).

Available election forms

None for entity-classification purposes: PRC law does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch entity election. The WFOE may, however, elect general VAT taxpayer status if below the mandatory threshold, and may apply for High and New Technology Enterprise status to access the reduced 15% enterprise income tax rate; both are procedural or incentive elections and do not alter entity-level tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no turnover threshold for basic tax registration because all established business entities must complete tax registration at incorporation. The mandatory VAT general-taxpayer registration threshold is RMB 5,000,000 of cumulative taxable sales over a rolling 12-month period; below that threshold the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer using the simplified VAT rate of 3% (subject to temporary preferential reductions), while crossing the threshold obliges conversion to general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods from mainland China, including exports to the Taiwan jurisdiction as a separate customs territory, is eligible for the 0% VAT rate and refund of attributable input VAT where all of the following conditions are met: (1) the WFOE is registered as a PRC VAT general taxpayer; (2) the manufactured IVD goods physically leave mainland China and are declared to China Customs in the WFOE's own name; (3) the export sales are supported by an export customs declaration with electronic release data; (4) the export proceeds are received and repatriated through lawful foreign-exchange channels within 180 days of the export customs declaration date; (5) the WFOE files the export tax rebate declaration through the Electronic Tax Bureau within the statutory filing period; and (6) the exported IVD goods are lawfully registered or licensed under PRC medical-device and customs export-control rules. Qualifying offshore digital services may also be zero-rated if listed in the cross-border taxable-services catalogue, but the principal export here is physical goods.

All VAT invoices, including ordinary, special, and fully digitalized electronic invoices, must be issued through the National VAT Invoice Management System (Golden Tax Phase IV) and transmitted in real time to the competent Tax Service Bureau. The tax authority centrally clears each invoice by assigning a unique invoice code and number, and purchasers can verify authenticity on the VAT Invoice Public Service Platform. There is no transactional fiscal stamping model; instead, invoice clearance is achieved through centralized electronic data matching, and export sales are separately flagged as zero-rated in the e-invoice system and reconciled electronically with China Customs export release data.

If the export customs declaration is false, the goods are re-imported or diverted into domestic PRC consumption without lawful procedures, or the foreign-exchange proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days without timely regulatory approval, the tax authority may disallow the zero-rating, claw back any export VAT refund already granted, and reclassify the export sale as domestic taxable supply subject to 13% VAT plus late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day. A fraudulent claim for an export tax refund is treated under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection as a serious tax violation: the tax authority will recover the refunded amount and impose a penalty of 50% to 5 times the amount involved. Where the amount is large or the scheme is organized, criminal liability for fraudulent export tax refund under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China may arise, with penalties including imprisonment, fines, and confiscation of property. Enhanced audit scrutiny is expected where SWIFT LC or TT payment patterns, high-risk destination markets, invoice values, or customs quantities are inconsistent with the timing and amount of foreign-exchange repatriation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The PRC maintains a hybrid convertibility regime. Current account transactions, including trade-related payments for goods and services, are largely freely convertible after banks perform documentary authenticity verification. Capital account transactions remain controlled: inbound foreign direct investment into the WFOE is permitted under the Foreign Investment Negative List, but requires SAFE foreign exchange registration, an onshore capital account, and a bank pre-review of the capital injection contract. Received FDI can be converted into RMB through settlement under the capital account, but must be applied to bona fide business expenditures; speculative use or outbound lending is prohibited. Profit remittances are treated as current account payment of investment returns, subject to audited financial statements, corporate income tax completion or withholding certificates, board resolution, and the absence of pending SAFE penalties. The offshore Taiwan parent may not freely re-inject fungible funds after receipt; each injection must be traceable to the registered capital schedule. Currency conversion for outward remittances is conditioned on genuine transaction documents, and no full capital account liberalization exists. The WFOE should therefore structure each invoice, equity injection, and dividend declaration as a separately docketed compliance file.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished forced foreign currency surrender, but 100% of export foreign currency receipts from IVD sales must be repatriated into an onshore bank account within 180 days of the export customs declaration, unless a SAFE exemption is approved for specific offshore settlement practices. No mandatory conversion ratio applies; the WFOE may retain export proceeds in a foreign currency current account or voluntarily convert them into RMB at market rates. The exporter must report each receipt through the SAFE digital foreign exchange platform and maintain the underlying customs declarations, bills of lading, commercial invoices, and packing lists for bank authenticity review.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, acting under the delegation of the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with the PBOC holding ultimate monetary authority and SAFE administering all cross-border foreign exchange, FDI, trade settlement, and capital account transactiobs.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI platforms are not viable as the primary treasury or statutory capital account receptor for the China WFOE. Such EMIs are not licensed by SAFE and cannot perform FDI registration, capital verification, export surrender reporting, or onshore RMB clearing under the PRC Foreign Account regulations. PBoC and SAFE require WFOEs to maintain primary settlement relationships with licensed domestic banks that are direct participants in CNAPS, CIPS, and SWIFT correspondent rails. Fintech EMIs may be used only as ancillary collection channels for overseas distributor payments, and only if the EMI controls cross-border remittance data in compliance with PBoC rules and does not accept the WFOE's statutory equity capital. Legal constraint: offshore EMIs generally cannot offer CN domestic account numbers in the WFOE's name, and their payment aggregator status does not equal a PRC banking license.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must initiate an MT103 wire transfer to the WFOE's SAFE-registered capital account at Bank of China Limited or another tier-1 domestic bank. The payment purpose code must be classified under the SAFE balance of payments transaction code for inward direct investment equity capital, corresponding to registered capital injection, and the bank must complete the foreign exchange receipt declaration form for the capital account. The bank will verify the FDI registration certificate, the capital injection schedule, the board resolution, and the equity subscription agreement before crediting the receipt as capital. After the foreign currency arrives, the WFOE may settle the funds into RMB using a real transaction declaration, and the local certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report in accordance with China's Company Law and the Administration for Market Regulation filing rules. The WFOE must then only deploy the RMB proceeds into payroll, raw materials, capex, or other permitted operating expenditures; sweeping or relending the capital to the parent is prohibited.For settlement of raw material imports, component purchases, royalty payments, and other international trade obligations, the WFOE should use SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for open-account trade, commercial letters of credit issued by the onshore bank under MT700/MT707 for larger or risk-controlled transactions, and documentary collections under URC522 for occasional settlements where LC is not required. For RMB-denominated cross-border payments to suppliers in Taiwan and other regions, the WFOE should route through CIPS via the domestic bank with the same SAFE trade purpose code and full supporting documents: customs declarations for goods, commercial invoices, packing lists, transport documents, insurance certificates, and tax withholding vouchers for cross-border service fees. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital EMI platforms are not viable as the primary treasury or statutory capital account receptor for the China WFOE. Such EMIs are not licensed by SAFE and cannot perform FDI registration, capital verification, export surrender reporting, or onshore RMB clearing under the PRC Foreign Account regulations. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared when annual related-party tangible property transfers exceed RMB 200 million; financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million; intangible asset ownership transfers exceed RMB 100 million; or other related-party transactions aggregate more than RMB 40 million. Master File must be prepared when annual cross-border related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion or when the enterprise is designated by the State Taxation Administration. The WFOE must also file the Annual Related Party Transaction Report with the annual CIT return whenever any related-party transaction occurs.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not apply OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as a separate statute, but the tax authorities apply a substance-over-form doctrine, the General Anti-Abuse Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, and the beneficial ownership rules under State Taxation Administration Public Notices to deny treaty/double-taxation relief where the Taiwanese shareholder or WFOE lacks commercial substance. For the WFOE, Core Income-Generating Activities for IVD manufacturing must be performed in mainland China: product design, production, quality control, regulatory licensing, inventory management, and revenue booking should be supported by local personnel and premises. The Taiwanese parent must likewise demonstrate real business activity in Taiwan to be recognized as beneficial owner of any dividend, interest, or royalty.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Artificial Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The in-charge tax authority may recharacterize the entire structure under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and Article 120 of the Implementing Regulations; apply a deemed taxable income adjustment; deny the cross-strait tax treaty or arrangement benefit; impose the 10% withholding tax on the deemed dividend; impose a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; and assess a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law. The entity may also be listed as a serious-tax-dishonest party, triggering joint punishment including travel restrictions and public blacklisting.

Director personal liability: Directors and the legal representative who knowingly participated in the shell arrangement may have the corporate veil pierced under Article 20 of the PRC Company Law and be held jointly and severally liable for the WFOE's unpaid corporate income tax and penalties. The legal representative will face an exit-entry control measure (travel ban) for outstanding tax liabilities; assets may be frozen; and criminal liability may arise under Article 201 of the Criminal Law for tax evasion, with imprisonment of up to seven years and fines.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / BEPS Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: The State Taxation Administration may apply Article 41 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, make a transfer pricing adjustment, increase taxable income, add interest on the underpaid tax, and impose a penalty for failure to provide contemporaneous documentation under Article 70 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law. Fraudulent transfer pricing reporting may be treated as tax evasion under Article 63, attracting a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax and daily late-payment surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors and the financial officer who sign the false transfer pricing documentation and related-party schedules can be subject to civil liability for the WFOE's tax adjustment when the company is unable to satisfy the liability; Chinese courts have applied the corporate-veil piercing doctrine to hold controlling shareholders personally liable; the legal representative is tagged as a tax-dishonest person and banned from leaving China; if the understatement is deliberate and large, criminal prosecution under Article 201 of the Criminal Law can result in imprisonment for three to seven years and criminal fines.
Disguised Dividend Distribution and Withholding Tax Failure

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may deem the shareholder loan or excessive payment to be a dividend distribution under Article 39 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law Implementing Regulations and apply Article 37 withholding tax. As withholding agent, the WFOE is liable for the unwithheld tax; Article 69 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law imposes a penalty of 50% to 300% of the amount that should have been withheld. Thin-capitalization disallowances under Article 46 and the General Anti-Abuse Rule under Article 47 may also operate to deny the interest deduction and recharacterize the transaction.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the disguised distribution and fail to discharge the withholding obligation may be personally liable for the withholding penalty if the WFOE is unable to pay. The legal representative will be subject to exit restrictions and high-consumption curbs; assets of the company and the Taiwanese parent may be attached if the corporate veil is pierced; criminal exposure includes imprisonment for tax evasion or fraud where the failure to withhold is part of a scheme to conceal income, with a maximum term of seven years and additional fines.
