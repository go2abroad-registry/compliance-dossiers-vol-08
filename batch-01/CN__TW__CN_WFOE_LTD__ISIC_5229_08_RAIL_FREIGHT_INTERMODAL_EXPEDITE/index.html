Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA). Administratively exercised through the nationwide Unified Electronic Tax Bureau portal (etax.chinatax.gov.cn) and the Comprehensive Digital E-Invoice Platform; export verification is co-administered with the General Administration of Customs via the China International Trade Single Window; foreign exchange monitoring is coordinated with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange.

E-government filing portal

Under the 'One License, One Code' (Yi Zhao Yi Ma) reform, the WFOE obtains its statutory tax identification automatically upon issuance of the business license from the Administration for Market Regulation: the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code serves as both the company registration number and the tax ID. Within 30 days after formation, the entity must complete online tax registration confirmation on the Electronic Tax Bureau, activate the tax profile (production and operational taxpayer status), elect general or small-scale VAT taxpayer status, apply for digital invoice quota, and conclude a three-party automatic tax deduction agreement with its bank. Cross-border payments to the Taiwan parent trigger non-resident withholding registration and beneficial ownership documentation for Cross-Strait treaty relief.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Under the 'One License, One Code' (Yi Zhao Yi Ma) reform, the WFOE obtains its statutory tax identification automatically upon issuance of the business license from the Administration for Market Regulation: the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code serves as both the company registration number and the tax ID. Within 30 days after formation, the entity must complete online tax registration confirmation on the Electronic Tax Bureau, activate the tax profile (production and operational taxpayer status), elect general or small-scale VAT taxpayer status, apply for digital invoice quota, and conclude a three-party automatic tax deduction agreement with its bank. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China maintains a per-se entity-level taxation system without check-the-box pass-through or hybrid entity elections. The WFOE is a resident enterprise taxable on worldwide income at 25% CIT. Statutory elections available: (a) general VAT taxpayer status even below the RMB 5,000,000 small-scale threshold, with a 36-month lock-in after election; (b) small low-profit enterprise (small-micro) CIT concession at a 20% reduced rate with an effective 2.5% to 5% burden on qualifying income up to RMB 3,000,000, maximum RMB 50,000,000 total assets and 300 employees (extended by policy to 2027); (c) one-time pre-tax deduction of qualifying fixed assets below RMB 5,000,000; (d) accelerated R&D expense super-deduction election; (e) quarterly prepayment based on actual taxable profit versus an estimated annual installment; (f) application of the Cross-Strait Agreement on Avoidance of Double Taxation with the Taiwan administration, including mutual agreement procedure and reduced dividend/interest/royalty withholding; no hybrid mismatch election exists, and thin capitalization (2:1 debt-to-equity) plus transfer pricing contemporaneous documentation rules govern related-party funding.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic entities are VAT-registered from formation under the unified social credit code. The small-scale taxpayer line is annual taxable sales not exceeding RMB 5,000,000; small-scale taxpayers benefit from a levy rate of 3% (temporary reduced to 1% under current policies) and a monthly exemption of up to RMB 100,000 (RMB 300,000 per quarter) on periodic sales. Sectoral headline VAT rates are 13% for goods, 9% for transport and rail-related freight carriage, and 6% for modern services (including freight forwarding agency, logistics ancillary services, and international agency work). A non-resident service supplier without a permanent establishment in China has no fixed local registration threshold: the Chinese resident customer is statutorily required to withhold VAT through reverse charge on payments for taxable services performed by the offshore supplier.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT with preservation of input VAT credit/refund) is available to a general VAT taxpayer for: (A) goods physically exported from the PRC customs territory, including rail freight on transcontinental block trains exiting China, where the WFOE is the seller/exporter and the customs export declaration identifies the rail mode of exit; (B) international transportation services involving carriage of goods from a place within China to a place outside China (and the reverse), or between two overseas places, where the WFOE acts as the principal carrier on an international rail journey; and (C) cross-border services wholly consumed outside China, which are zero-rated or exempt under the cross-border taxable services catalogue of CaiShui [2016] No. 36. Pure agency freight forwarding services consumed overseas are VAT-exempt rather than zero-rated unless the WFOE issues the multimodal bill of lading and assumes carrier liability. The claim requires filing the Cross-Border Taxable Services Zero-Rate/Exemption Return on the Electronic Tax Bureau and evidencing the overseas recipient, actual foreign exchange receipt, and the international rail itinerary.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic customs data) evidencing exit of the goods from PRC territory by rail
  • International Railway Consignment Note (SMGS/CMIS rail waybill) and/or multimodal transport bill of lading issued by the WFOE
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank export foreign exchange collection voucher evidencing receipt from the overseas counterparty
  • Export sales contract, commercial invoice, and packing list
  • Special VAT e-invoice for the zero-rated export or cross-border service supply
  • Cross-Border Taxable Services Zero-Rate/Exemption Declaration (Kua Jing Ying Shui Xing Wei Shi Yong Zeng Zhi Shui Ling Shui Lv Mian Shui Shen Qing Biao) for service-based claims
  • Export tax rebate application form with supporting customs electronic data matching file

The nationwide Golden Tax Phase IV and Full Digital E-Invoice (Shu Dian Piao) mandate applies. All VAT invoices, including special VAT invoices and export zero-rated invoices, must be created on the STA-controlled e-invoice platform, either through the official browser interface or by application-programming-interface integration of the WFOE's accounting system with the Electronic Tax Bureau. Each invoice carries a unique 20-digit invoice number, an electronic signature/seal, and is cleared in near real-time to the national Golden Tax data warehouse before buyer-side verification is possible. The system performs automatic invoice-to-return-to-tax-declaration reconciliation and risk scoring (governance by data), eliminates physical stamping, requires same-period issuance corresponding to the underlying supply, and flags all export invoices for the joint customs-tax-foreign-exchange matching engine.

All zero-rating and export rebate claims are screened by the joint STA-General Administration of Customs-State Administration of Foreign Exchange data engine and may be subject to documentary verification, on-site inspection, and container-level physical examination. If export proceeds are not repatriated within the statutory 180-day ceiling, the documentation set is incomplete, the rail waybill and customs data do not reconcile, or the substantive export is not proven, the refund is clawed back with interest and a late-payment surcharge (0.05% per day) and the supply is re-characterized as domestic taxable at the applicable 9% or 6% VAT rate. Fraudulent export rebate claims attract an administrative penalty of 0.5 to 5 times the refunded VAT (Tax Collection Administration Law, Article 63), forfeiture of illegal gains, downgrade of the enterprise tax credit rating to Grade D, suspension or permanent loss of export rebate qualification, inclusion on the joint disciplinary blacklist, and referral to criminal prosecution for fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds (Criminal Law Article 204), which carries imprisonment up to life plus confiscation of property; responsible officers and beneficial owners face parallel individual liability.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed floating exchange-rate system under which the RMB is fully convertible for current-account items, including trade in goods and services, freight forwarding, logistics fees, and rail transport charges, provided that the underlying transaction is genuine and documentation is retained. Capital-account transactions, including foreign direct investment into a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), shareholder loans, equity transfers, and outward investment, remain subject to SAFE registration, project approval, or filing. For a Taiwan-invested WFOE engaged in ISIC 5229 rail freight and intermodal expediting, inbound capital must be injected through the foreign-currency capital account and may only be converted into RMB for uses consistent with the approved business scope. Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder requires completion of the annual statutory audit, corporate income-tax final settlement and tax clearance, a board resolution certifying distributable profits, and a bank-facilitated SAFE profit-remittance procedure. Service-trade remittances, such as payments to overseas railway operators and terminal handlers, are eligible for simplified current-account verification under SAFE rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since SAFE's 2019 reform, China has abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds. All export and service-trade proceeds must be repatriated onshore within statutory time limits: generally 30 days after the service is completed for service trade, and 90 days from customs declaration for goods-related trade, unless a contracting party requires an alternative cash-flow timing. The WFOE may retain such foreign exchange in an onshore current-account balance or convert it to RMB voluntarily. For rail-freight and intermodal expediting services, receipts must be supported by transport contracts, rail waybills, multimodal bills of lading, freight invoices, customs declarations, and the bank's verification of cover under the SAFE current-account transaction-monitoring system. Any retention of export/service proceeds in an offshore account, including under an onward freight-billing model, requires a prior SAFE approval or filing.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE); local SAFE branches supervise and enforce foreign exchange controls on behalf of the PBoC.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMIs and neobanks holding EU, UK, or Hong Kong licences cannot serve as the primary treasury or settlement account for a mainland China WFOE because they lack a valid Payment Business Licence issued by the PBoC or SAFE. Such EMIs are not permitted to hold RMB, execute domestic payments, or perform tax and statutory fund deposits for the WFOE. They may only perform ancillary collection or cross-border exchange functions through licensed correspondent banks, and even then must use SWIFT/CNAPS gateways with a full AML/KYC trail. The WFOE must open its main operating and capital accounts with a domestically licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Registered capital from the Taiwan shareholder must be remitted to the WFOE's FDI Capital Account at a licensed domestic bank via SWIFT MT103/MT202 using the SAFE inbound direct-investment purpose code. The remitter must be exactly the Taiwan shareholder named in the approved investment, and the remittance currency may be USD, EUR, RMB, or other major currency. The bank requires the SAFE FDI Registration Certificate or electronic filing number before crediting the capital account. After arrival, funds may be converted into RMB for approved business purposes under the bank's foreign-currency capital-account settlement rules, and surplus conversion requires additional SAFE filing. For Taiwan investment, the applicable registration is under the Mainland-Taiwan investment framework, requiring notarised certification of the Taiwan parent and the Taiwan Compatriots Investment Certificate. A complete verification file must include the audited balance sheet, approved articles of association, business-scope licence, and the SAFE-issued file code.For payments to overseas railway undertakings, terminal operators, feeder carriers, customs brokers, and intermodal subcontractors, the WFOE shall use bank-to-bank SWIFT MT103/MT202 for standard service-trade settlement. High-value or term-based infrastructure and rolling-stock payments above USD 500,000 may be structured under an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit (LIC) or standby letter of credit to secure performance and provide negotiable payment assurance to the counterparty. Every payment must be matched to an underlying invoice and transport document, including a rail waybill or multimodal bill. The bank will verify the transaction in the SAFE Service-Trade Exchange / Current-Account Verification system; no customs registration is required when the freight service is rendered independently of goods import/export, unless bundled with goods.
