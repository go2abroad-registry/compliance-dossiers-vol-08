Physical registered office is mandatory for a WFOE engaged in commercial printing and trading card publishing. A virtual office or commercial mail-forwarding mailbox is not acceptable to SAMR, tax authorities, or commercial banks for this industry. The enterprise must possess an actual, exclusive-use office or production/operating premises evidenced by a lease agreement with real property ownership proof, rental invoice, and a red-stamped landlord qualification document. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局; headquarters portal: www.chinatax.gov.cn; taxpayer electronic operations portal: etax.chinatax.gov.cn). Local district-level offices of the STA exercise day-to-day registration, invoice-quota and audit powers over the WFOE's registered place of business. Sales into the Taiwan jurisdiction do not trigger a separate PRC tax registration; Taiwan import VAT (5% business tax) is administratively collected by Taiwan Customs from the importer, and any Taiwan fixed place of business would require separate registration with the National Taxation Bureau of Taiwan.

E-government filing portal

Upon incorporation, the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which functions as the single statutory TIN, VAT registration number and customs registration code under the Multi-Certificate Integration reform. Tax registration is created ex officio from the SAMR record; within 30 days the WFOE must complete the following steps: (1) log in to the electronic tax bureau, perfect the taxpayer profile, elect PRC VAT status (small-scale or general taxpayer), and file accounting/financial rules (Chinese GAAP; tax accounts strictly PRC-law compliant); (2) register payroll withholding and individual income tax; (3) complete export-operator filing and obtain the customs importer/exporter code; (4) apply for e-invoice quotas and fully digitalized electronic invoice (数电票) issuance authorization; and (5) appoint the statutory representative and tax responsible person for the electronic tax account.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — a China-incorporated Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is a Chinese tax resident taxed at entity level on worldwide income under the Corporate Income Tax Law (25% headline CIT rate; 15% for certified High and New Technology Enterprises; 20% reduced rate for qualified small and low-profit enterprises on taxable income up to CNY 3,000,000). The WFOE is within the territorial scope of Chinese entity-level taxation on global profits with foreign tax credit relief; no pass-through to the foreign shareholder is permitted, and outbound profit distributions are subject to 10% withholding tax unless reduced by applicable treaty. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a China-incorporated Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise is a Chinese tax resident taxed at entity level on worldwide income under the Corporate Income Tax Law (25% headline CIT rate; 15% for certified High and New Technology Enterprises; 20% reduced rate for qualified small and low-profit enterprises on taxable income up to CNY 3,000,000). The WFOE is within the territorial scope of Chinese entity-level taxation on global profits with foreign tax credit relief; no pass-through to the foreign shareholder is permitted, and outbound profit distributions are subject to 10% withholding tax unless reduced by applicable treaty. Manufacturing sites situated in ordinary industrial zones are not within an exempt zone; customs-supervised bonded/export-processing zones carry distinct deferral/refund mechanics.

Available election forms

PRC law contains no check-the-box entity classification election. Available statutory elections: (1) VAT taxpayer-type election — annual turnover up to CNY 5,000,000 permits small-scale status at a 3% levy rate, with mandatory upgrade to general taxpayer status above the threshold; (2) export method election for general taxpayers between exemption-collection-refund (免抵退) for manufacturing enterprises and exemption-refund (免退税) for trading companies; (3) High and New Technology Enterprise certification (15% CIT rate and 100% R&D super-deduction); (4) small and low-profit enterprise preferential elections; (5) fixed-asset input VAT credit timing elections; and (6) offshore services zero-rating elections conditioned on complete export documentation. No hybrid mismatch or look-through elections are available under PRC tax law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

General VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory once annual assessable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; below that threshold the WFOE may elect small-scale taxpayer status (3% levy rate with restricted input credit mechanics) or voluntarily register as a general VAT taxpayer — registration as a general taxpayer is a precondition for claiming input-VAT refunds on exports. Election constraints follow uniform-duration rules under the VAT implementing regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of collectible trading cards manufactured by the general-VAT-taxpayer WFOE to the Taiwan jurisdiction qualify for a 0% VAT rate with input-VAT refund under the exemption-collection-refund method, provided that: (i) the WFOE holds valid export-operator and customs registration; (ii) the goods physically depart mainland China under a formal electronic export customs declaration; (iii) export sales are separately invoiced and recorded at arm's length, supported by irrevocable letters of credit, wire transfers or other documented payment evidence; (iv) foreign currency proceeds are repatriated and verified through the SAFE monitoring system within the statutory deadline; and (v) the refund application and electronic data-matching submission lapse are filed before the statutory deadline (generally the 15th day of the month following the export month, with annual centralized refund declaration available for prior-year exports). Exports to Taiwan are recognized as exports for VAT zero-rating purposes; ECFA/early-harvest tariff preferences do not alter the VAT treatment.

PRC mandates fully digitalized electronic invoices (数电票) issued exclusively through the taxpayer's e-tax account on the STA's unified national electronic tax-bureau platform. General VAT taxpayers must register invoice quotas, transmit invoice datasets in real time through the official platform API (电子发票服务平台), and embed the encrypted tax-control code and digital QR clearance mark replacing physical VAT stamping. Buyer-side verification occurs through the STA national invoice verification portal (全国增值税发票查验平台); private third-party fiscal clearance operators are not recognized, although the STA supplies the standard UKey/SDK enabling ERP integration so that invoices generated from ISIC 3240 production and sales orders must flow through the platform API before delivery to customers.

The STA, General Administration of Customs (GACC) and SAFE jointly cross-match customs declarations, banking FX flows, invoice log data and refund claims under the Golden Tax system. Material discrepancies trigger on-site audits and physical reconciliation of card serial numbers, collection-set inventory and production records. Where export zero-rating is improperly claimed, the refunded tax is clawed back with statutory interest; an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax evaded is imposed; export-tax-refund qualification and customs credit status are suspended; and, under Criminal Law Article 204, fraudulent export tax refunds are punishable by imprisonment of up to 10 years (or life imprisonment for aggravated amounts) with fines, plus potential smuggling penalties and legal-representative disqualification upon repeat violations.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current account convertibility for trade-related payments. Capital account items remain subject to prior approval, filing, and registration through SAFE. Profit remittance for WFOE dividends is permissible after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax clearance, and currency registration via the domestic custodian bank; no separate SAFE approval is required, but documentary evidence of distributable profits derived from audited financial statements is mandatory. Hurdles include ensuring the payment purpose code is accurate, the WFOE has a valid Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) registration, and there are no unresolved tax arrears.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE Notice No. 28/2019 and the current trade facilitation framework, export proceeds are no longer subject to mandatory foreign exchange surrender. Proceeds must nevertheless be repatriated to China within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration. The exporter may retain the foreign currency in its current account or voluntarily settle into CNY at the spot rate, subject to trade authenticity verification by the receiving bank. For collectible trading card exports, the bank will require the export contract, export customs declaration forms (报关单), commercial invoices, and transport documents (B/L, air waybill) to release funds to the operating account.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwanese shareholder must instruct its bank to wire the registered capital via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's dedicated Capital Escrow Account (资本金账户) at a Chinese domestic bank. The remittance should carry the purpose code corresponding to 'Equity Injection' under the PRC Balance of Payments classification, accompanied by the remittance narrative referencing the FDI registration number, the investment approval certificate number, and the WFOE's unified social credit code. The receiving bank will verify the remittance against the SAFE FDI registration and the foreign exchange business report (涉外收入申报), and will credit the funds to the escrow account. Subsequent conversion to CNY is permitted only for operating expenses, procurement of raw materials, employee salaries, and other approved corporate outlays; the funds cannot be used for equity investment, entrusted loans, or non-operating investments without additional PBOC/SAFE filings.For settlement of import trade payables (e.g., acquisition of holographic film, specialty inks, and packaging materials), the WFOE will instruct its domestic bank to send SWIFT MT103 payments using the current account purpose code for goods trade after attaching the import customs declaration, signed contract, commercial invoice, and shipping documents to the bank's trade authenticity verification system. For larger or long-term contracts with overseas suppliers, the entity may open a Commercial Letter of Credit via the domestic bank (SWIFT MT700, with amendments by MT707), with payment terms against shipping documents, issued in accordance with UCP600 rules. All outbound wires and documentary settlements are automatically matched against the entity's import/export declarations in the State Administration of Foreign Exchange's goods trade monitoring system (货物贸易外汇监测系统).Deliverable FX forward contracts (onshore CNY against USD/EUR/JPY, with trade or foreign debt underlying)FX options (European-style plain vanilla, and barrier/corridor options permitted with SAFE product registration)FX swaps (deliverable swap of CNY against foreign currency, both legs settled)Cross-currency interest rate swaps (for hedging interest rate and currency risk on approved external debt)CNY non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) in offshore markets (not permissible as onshore hedging instruments; may be used only as a valuation benchmark and require bank confirmation that no onshore delivery is made)

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low-to-Moderate feasibility. Offshore digital EMI services (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, Currenxie) may only be used as supplementary collection rails for foreign consumer card payments from Stripe or Authorize.Net. They cannot lawfully maintain the WFOE's principal corporate bank account in mainland China, cannot provide CNY clearing access, and are not licensed by the PBOC to handle domestic cross-border settlement. Any flows between an offshore EMI and the WFOE's domestic bank account must be supported by genuine cross-border trade documentation (contracts, invoices, customs declarations) and reported in SAFE's foreign exchange monitoring system. Therefore, the primary treasury relationship must remain with a PBOC-licensed commercial bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when related-party transactions in the tax year exceed: RMB 100 million for tangible asset transfers (purchases/sales of goods); RMB 20 million for intangible asset transfers and royalties; RMB 20 million for provision of services and other transactions (including interest and management fees). Master File is mandatory when the WFOE belongs to a multinational group with consolidated revenue in the preceding fiscal year exceeding RMB 5 billion and the WFOE has related-party transactions. The annual Related-Party Transaction Schedule must be filed regardless of whether the documentation thresholds are met.

Economic substance scope: China does not maintain a stand-alone Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to certain offshore centres, but the same effect is achieved through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under PRC CIT Law Article 47, the substance-over-form principle, and the beneficial-ownership condition for treaty relief. The WFOE and its Taiwanese parent must demonstrate actual personnel, premises, decision-making capability, business risk assumption, and genuine operational purpose; a conduit or letterbox company is denied treaty benefits and its intercompany transactions may be recharacterized as equity or dividend distributions.
