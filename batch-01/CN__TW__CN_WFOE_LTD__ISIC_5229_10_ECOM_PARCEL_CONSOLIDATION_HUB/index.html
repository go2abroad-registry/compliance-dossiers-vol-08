Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a limited liability company under the R.O.C. Companies Act. The Investment Commission, however, assesses the adequacy of the declared registered capital against the operational scale of the cross-border parcel consolidation hub, including warehouse lease, equipment, staffing, insurance, and customs guarantee exposure. The subscribed capital must be remitted from abroad and admitted into a bank account opened in the WFOE's name at an R.O.C. licensed bank, and a capital verification certificate is required before registration. There is no statutory blocked escrow account rule for a general WFOE; the capital becomes usable for lawful operating expenses once the MOEA Company Registration Certificate is issued and tax registration is completed.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online and in-person filings are made through the National Electronic Tax Bureau ('e-Tax') and the applicable local provincial/municipal/county tax service bureau at the WFOE's registered address.

E-government filing portal

For the China WFOE: (1) incorporate with the local Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) under China's Company Law; the business license will contain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is statutorily recognised as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for VAT, Corporate Income Tax, customs, invoicing and foreign-exchange reporting under the multi-certificate integration reform. (2) After the SAMR certificate is issued, the STA receives the registration data from the inter-departmental information-sharing system; the WFOE must then complete supplementary tax registration on the e-Tax portal, file the legal representative, financial officer and tax accountant identity information, register bank deposit accounts with the tax authority, select the VAT taxpayer type, and configure the VAT invoice parameters before issuing invoices. (3) No separate VAT identification number is issued; the USCC is printed on VAT invoices and is the unified TIN. For a foreign non-resident investor or a non-resident enterprise that earns China-source income but does not have a permanent establishment in China, the non-resident must obtain a Non-Resident Enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number from the local STA office that supervises the withholding agent; the China WFOE, as withholding agent, applies for that identifier with the foreign entity's home-country tax registration and duly legalised entity documents.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - the China WFOE is a PRC tax resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at the statutory 25% Corporate Income Tax rate; it is not eligible for pass-through treatment and cannot be disregarded as a transparent entity.

Available election forms

None for entity classification; the PRC does not provide an entity check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch election. The only statutory elections that apply are VAT elections: a taxpayer with annual taxable sales below RMB 5,000,000 may choose small-scale simplified taxation or may apply for general taxpayer status, and a general VAT taxpayer may use the election and deemed-credit rules prescribed by the Ministry of Finance and the STA for specific industries where available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No pre-registration economic-threshold exemption for the WFOE; tax registration with the STA is mandatory from incorporation. The VAT turnover threshold is RMB 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales, above which general VAT taxpayer registration is compulsory; below that amount, small-scale VAT treatment may apply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating applies to goods actually exported from mainland China's customs territory, including parcels consolidated in the hub and shipped to offshore destinations (including Taiwan customs territory), provided the WFOE is a registered VAT taxpayer, the goods are declared to China Customs under an export customs declaration, the export sale is evidenced by commercial invoice, transport document, item-level parcel manifest and bank foreign-exchange receipt, and the exporter receives and repatriates the export proceeds within the statutory window. Cross-border services and international freight-forwarding services may also be zero-rated or exempt when supplied to an offshore recipient and consumed entirely outside China, with contracts, service documentation, foreign-exchange settlement and tax authority-prescribed records. The WFOE must report the zero-rated sales on the VAT return and apply for any resulting VAT refund/export rebate within the statutory filing period; failure to satisfy the documentary conditions converts the supply to taxable at the applicable rate.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration filed electronically with China Customs
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing export from mainland China
  • Export Proceeds Collection / bank export-settlement receipt issued by a designated foreign-exchange bank
  • Item-level cross-border e-commerce export declaration list or consolidated parcel manifest for the parcel consolidation hub

China's Golden-Tax System Phase IV and the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform require all VAT invoices to be issued through tax-controlled UKey/tax-disk equipment or through the fully digitalised e-invoice service. Invoice issuance is limited by tax authority-authorised monthly invoice quota and invoice type; issuance data flows to the STA in near real time, enabling automated cross-checking against purchase invoices, customs declarations and foreign-exchange settlement records. There is no transaction-by-transaction pre-clearance approval by a tax officer, but the system uses the national unified invoice encryption and QR-code digital stamp, and high-volume taxpayers integrate billing systems with the e-invoice platform through STA-defined data-exchange interfaces or by filing invoice batches through e-Tax. Non-compliant invoice issuance may lead to invoice quota blocking, administrative fines and disqualification of the claimed input credits.

The STA, China Customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange jointly audit export zero-rating files. If the export declaration, bill of lading, parcel manifest or foreign-exchange collection evidence is false, incomplete or not verified, the tax authority will claw back the zero-rated VAT exemption/refund plus a daily late-payment surcharge (0.05% per day on the omitted tax), impose a fine of one to five times the fraudulent export-tax refund under the Tax Collection Administration Law, suspend the taxpayer's export-tax-refund treatment (commonly up to six months) and refer the matter for criminal prosecution if the amount meets the threshold for the criminal offence of fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates. Customs may impose separate penalties for false export declaration or illegal import/export of goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible for current-account trade transactions (goods and services) under the IMF Articles of Agreement Article VIII framework; SAFE enforces authenticity-based verification, not prior approval, via the Trade Foreign Exchange Supervision and Administration System. Capital-account operations (FDI equity injection, cross-border loans, liquidation and exit proceeds) remain controlled: each inflow must be registered with SAFE under the FDI Information Registration framework and any conversion or payment from the capital escrow account is subject to the Capital Account Income Payment Convenience Policy (capital account income payment facilitation) with bank-side authenticity review. Profit remittance to the foreign parent is permitted annually after tax settlement: the WFOE must submit a board resolution, audited financial statements, the CIT annual reconciliation clearance certificate (企业所得税汇算清缴), evidence of tax payment, and a bank payment application; the remittance must not exceed distributable after-tax profits and bank review typically completes within five business days. Taiwan-leg NTD convertibility is not deliverable onshore in China; settlement is cleared offshore through the designated cross-strait clearing banks because NTD is not a listed clearing currency under the PBOC payment system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

All export proceeds, including Taiwan-bound parcel-logistics service revenue, must be repatriated to a domestic current-account foreign-currency account of the WFOE within 180 days from the customs export declaration date (or within the contractual collection period where a bank-verified extension is filed), and each repatriation is automatically matched against electronic customs data in the Trade FX Supervision and Administration System. No mandatory conversion ratio applies; since the 2012 nationwide liberalization, the WFOE may freely retain export receipts in foreign currency or convert to RMB through its designated bank. Uncollected export vouchers older than 180 days trigger SAFE on-site verification (现场核查) and possible inclusion in the national trade-credit risk list; retrospective adjustment filings are required if the actual collection period deviates from the declared trade payment term.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Bank of China (PBOC) is the exclusive FX regulator for the China-domiciled WFOE; the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) and the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC) govern the Taiwan clearing leg under the 2009 Cross-Strait Financial Cooperation Memorandum of Understanding and the Cross-Strait Currency Clearing Agreement, with Bank of China Taipei Branch designated as the RMB clearing bank in Taiwan.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-High feasibility for Airwallex (上海空中云汇持牌支付机构) as a PBOC-licensed non-bank payment institution and SAFE-registered cross-border payment institution; it is viable for cross-border e-commerce collection, vendor payout automation, and parcel line-haul settlement, but it is not a substitute for the Tier-1 bank treasury account because client funds are held in segregated commingled trust accounts at licensed commercial banks, not as bank deposits. Legal constraints: (1) all outbound/inbound transfers must be reported to SAFE under the Cross-Border Payment Institution Regulations (汇发〔2023〕5号) with per-transaction purpose codes, adding reconciliation overhead for high-frequency parcel-level flows; (2) TWD settlement for Taiwan counterparties cannot be executed onshore by the Mainland entity and must be cleared through Airwallex's offshore licensed entities (HKMA-licensed Money Service Operator), which cannot provide local NTD clearing without a Taiwan FICE-approved presence; (3) the WFOE must maintain a full operational account at a Tier-1 domestic bank for RMB conversion, tax wire, and customs tariff payments.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required if related-party transactions in the fiscal year reach NT$50 million or more; Master File required when the consolidated global group revenue reaches NT$30 billion.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan has no formal ESR title, but Article 12-1 of the Tax Collection Act and Article 43-3 of the Income Tax Act apply an economic substance and beneficial ownership doctrine: transactions lacking commercial rationality may be recharacterized. The WFOE must maintain payroll, office leases and management records proving substance in Taiwan; a pure letterbox entity may be disregarded and the Taiwan profits reattributed to the mainland parent, triggering an additional 10% penalty and punitive assessments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under Article 110, Income Tax Act: deficiency tax is recovered plus a penalty of up to 4 times the underpaid amount; criminal prosecution under Article 41, Tax Collection Act may impose up to 5 years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Director/officer (responsible person) jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax penalty; a travel ban is imposed on the statutory representative; criminal conviction may lead to disqualification from corporate office.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may deny the Taiwanese entity's corporate identity, disallow intercompany expenses, and recharacterize all Taiwan-source income as attributable to the mainland PE; a penalty of 30% of the recharacterized net profit may be imposed, and the registration may be revoked by the Ministry of Economic Affairs.

Director personal liability: Directors may be sued for civil restitution in the amount of tax avoided; orders restrict departure from Taiwan while the case is under investigation; substantial compliance failure triggers criminal penalties for abuse of legal personality.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty up to NT$5 million plus disqualification of the compliant officer; the substance review office may order suspension of operations until a real beneficial owner declaration is filed.

Director personal liability: The Taiwan WFOE's director of record is personally liable for regulatory penalties in the event of concealment; the company's dividend withholding tax refunds will be blocked and a legal representative travel ban is automatic for overdue AML penalties.
