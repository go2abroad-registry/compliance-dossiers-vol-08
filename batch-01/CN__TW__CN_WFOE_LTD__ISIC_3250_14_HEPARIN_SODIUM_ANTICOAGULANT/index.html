Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), with its electronic tax filing portal (e-Tax) accessible through the STA official website (chinatax.gov.cn); export customs clearance is administered by the General Administration of Customs through the International Trade Single Window and the SAFE-authorized banks handle foreign exchange verification.

E-government filing portal

The PRC WFOE obtains its statutory Tax Identification Number automatically as the 18-character Unified Social Credit Code issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation at incorporation. The WFOE must then complete registration on the STA e-Tax portal, register for VAT, file its tax status with the local competent tax office, and apply for electronic export tax rebate filing rights. A foreign non-resident entity that has not created a PE in the PRC is not issued a Unified Social Credit Code; the PRC paying party must file the non-resident registration and withholding record through STA using the non-resident entity's home-country Tax Identification Number, contract details, and the STA-generated withholding file identifier. If the foreign non-resident creates a PE or establishes a branch in the PRC, it must complete a separate tax registration and obtain an official tax identification number from the STA.

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold applies to a PRC WFOE; VAT registration is mandatory from incorporation. The small-scale VAT taxpayer classification applies to taxable annual sales not exceeding RMB 5,000,000; exceeding RMB 5,000,000 creates mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of the export sale of heparin sodium anticoagulant is available when the WFOE is a VAT-registered general taxpayer, the goods physically leave the PRC Customs territory and arrive in Taiwan under an export contract, the export is declared through China Customs, and the foreign exchange or otherwise approved cross-strait settlement amount is received via SAFE-regulated channels. The input VAT attributable to the exported pharmaceutical goods may be refunded under China's export tax rebate regime. The Taiwan-bound export is an export for PRC VAT purposes and therefore not subject to PRC output VAT; the import into Taiwan remains a separate Taiwan Business Tax event generally assessed by Taiwan Customs at 5% on the import value.

  • China Customs Export Goods Declaration with validated export-clearance status
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the CN WFOE
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing shipment from PRC to Taiwan
  • SAFE-designated bank foreign exchange receipt voucher, including the cross-border receipt declaration and bank remittance advice
  • STA electronic export tax rebate filing report with goods/item and tax-invoice cross-reference

China operates a mandatory digital fiscal invoice system under the Golden Tax IV architecture. VAT special invoices are generated digitally, encrypted with tax authority anti-counterfeiting controls, and transmitted in near-real time to the STA invoice verification system through the national electronic invoice platform. Export transactions must be reflected in the STA export tax rebate module, and physical export clearance is obligatory through China Customs' International Trade Single Window. No manual clearance is permitted for VAT special invoice issuance; all invoice data are reconciled with customs export data and bank FX collection records.

The STA, China Customs and SAFE-designated banks will compare the export declaration, logistics trail, pharmaceutical export licensing, export invoices, and actual receipt of funds. Where the claimed zero-rating or rebate is not substantiated, or where proceeds are not repatriated within the required period, the VAT refundable amount is subject to clawback, statutory late-payment surcharge interest, and administrative penalties under the PRC Tax Administration and Collection Law and Customs Law; where false export declarations or fraudulent rebate claims are involved, criminal liability for export tax refund fraud under PRC Criminal Law may be pursued, with fines and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China maintains a managed but compliant partial-convertibility regime for the WFOE. Current account transactions, including goods trade proceeds, royalties, and service payments related to the heparin sodium business, are generally freely convertible once the underlying transaction is verified by a designated foreign-exchange bank under the authenticity-and-consistency framework. The capital account is not fully convertible: foreign direct investment equity injections must be embedded in an SAFE FDI registration, routed through a dedicated Capital Account, and settled under SAFE's real-need principle before tracked use. Export proceeds no longer face mandatory surrender; the WFOE may retain the foreign currency in a current-account settlement account or voluntarily sell it to a designated bank. Profit remittance requires audited financial statements, tax payment proofs, and a SAFE-authorized bank verification of distributable profits, and any historical unreconciled capital verification is normally escalated. Overall, the convertibility framework is workable but not open-market; there is no freely floating CNY for every capital purpose, and each cross-border flow must carry a commercial background, invoice, customs record, or statutory filing to pass the bank's compliance screen.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory export surrender ratios. The WFOE may retain 100% of its heparin sodium export receipts in a registered foreign-exchange current-account settlement account or convert them into CNY voluntarily. SAFE's trade-goods verification framework generally requires export proceeds related to customs-cleared consignments to be repatriated into China within 180 days of customs declaration, unless the bank accepts a delayed-repatriation filing; funds must flow through a designated bank account and be matched to the relevant customs declaration in the Trade Monitoring System.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the foreign-exchange regulator of mainland China, which together govern the WFOE's cross-border banking and capital-flow regime. The Taiwan-sourced investment is treated as an external foreign-investment flow requiring SAFE registration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for a mainland China WFOE. Offshore fintech EMIs are not licensed deposit-taking institutions under PBOC/NFRA rules, do not have a local banking license to hold RMB corporate deposits or issue onshore settlement accounts, and cannot serve as the primary treasury rail for inbound FDI, customs clearance, tax payment, or SAFE-regulated trade flows. At most, a licensed domestic bank could use an EMI as an ancillary collections front-end in supported corridors, but the WFOE's statutory books, capital verification, and export reconciliation should remain with a qualified onshore Tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent should remit equity subscription funds through SWIFT MT103 from its corporate bank account in Taiwan, with the remittance purpose and instructions clearly flagged as an FDI equity contribution to a wholly foreign-owned enterprise in mainland China. Before receipt, the WFOE and the designated domestic bank must complete SAFE's FDI foreign-exchange registration, usually through the SAFE digital system. Funds are then credited into a dedicated Capital Account, not a regular operating account. After receipt, the bank verifies authenticity under the real-need principle, the funds may be settled into RMB for permitted operating uses, and a mainland China CPA issues the capital verification report for the company registration and future profit-remittance calculations.For the heparin sodium trade, the preferred clearing rails are: direct SWIFT MT103 telegraphic-transfer settlements against invoices and transport documents; commercial Letters of Credit via SWIFT MT700 issuance and MT754 negotiation for high-value consignments to pharmaceutical distributors; and documentary collections under D/P or D/A terms for open-account customers when controlled documents are used. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual corporate income tax return: generally due from 1 May through 31 May of the following year for a calendar-year company. A fiscal-year company generally files within five months after the end of its accounting year. Provisional return: generally due during 1 September through 30 September for a calendar-year company, subject to statutory applicability and available filing methods.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Withheld tax is generally remitted by the statutory deadline following the payment or withholding event, and annual withholding statements are generally filed by the statutory January deadline for the preceding calendar year. The exact deadline depends on payment type and recipient status.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Taiwan local-file documentation generally applies where the taxpayer or controlled transactions meet prescribed thresholds, commonly including annual net sales and non-operating income of at least NTD 300 million or controlled transactions of at least NTD 200 million, subject to transaction-specific exemptions. A master file is generally relevant to a Taiwan entity that is a member of a multinational enterprise group with consolidated revenue of at least NTD 3 billion and cross-border controlled transactions. Thresholds, exemptions, and filing dates must be confirmed for the relevant fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not operate a UAE-style standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime. Nevertheless, Taiwan applies substance-over-form principles, general anti-avoidance rules, transfer-pricing rules, controlled foreign company rules, beneficial-ownership concepts, permanent-establishment principles, and anti-abuse provisions. A Taiwan pharmaceutical entity should maintain real personnel, premises, production capability, regulatory functions, quality-control functions, decision-making, risks, assets, and records consistent with its reported profits. A principal structure that allocates residual profit to an offshore affiliate without corresponding functions, assets, and risks is high risk.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Return

Statutory sanction: Assessment of unpaid tax, late-payment surcharges, interest, administrative penalties, enforced collection, and possible criminal prosecution for tax evasion. Criminal consequences may include fines and imprisonment under applicable Taiwan tax legislation.

Director personal liability: The responsible person and individuals who knowingly direct, approve, prepare, or assist the conduct may face criminal liability, civil restitution, tax recovery, asset attachment, and restrictions on departure where legally ordered. Limited liability does not protect intentional personal wrongdoing.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax authority adjustment to arm's-length income or deduction, denial of unsupported expenses, additional corporate income tax, withholding tax, penalties, interest, and possible audit expansion to customs and indirect taxes.

Director personal liability: Directors and responsible officers may incur civil liability for deliberate misstatements, breach of fiduciary duties, or unlawful distributions and may face criminal exposure if the pricing scheme forms part of fraud or evasion.
Withholding Tax Failure

Statutory sanction: Recovery from the payer of tax that should have been withheld, late-payment charges, interest, administrative fines, denial of treaty relief, and possible liability for inaccurate withholding statements.

Director personal liability: The responsible person and withholding agent may be personally exposed where they intentionally or negligently fail to withhold or remit tax, subject to statutory defenses and allocation of duties.
Shell Company and Beneficial-Ownership Abuse

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, recharacterization of transactions, withholding-tax assessment, transfer-pricing adjustments, anti-avoidance penalties, beneficial-owner investigations, and potential criminal proceedings for fraud or tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Controllers, directors, and officers who knowingly participate may face personal civil claims, criminal liability, confiscation or attachment of assets, and legally authorized restrictions on travel.
AML, Sanctions, and Suspicious Payment Activity

Statutory sanction: Suspicious transaction reporting, account restrictions, transaction rejection, regulatory investigation, administrative fines, asset freezing where legally applicable, and criminal penalties for money laundering or prohibited transactions.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and responsible persons may face criminal imprisonment, fines, confiscation, civil restitution, and management or licensing consequences where they knowingly facilitate or fail to prevent unlawful conduct.
Pharmaceutical and Manufacturing Licensing

Statutory sanction: Product seizure, recall, suspension or revocation of licenses, production shutdown, administrative fines, civil product liability, and criminal penalties for illegal manufacture, sale, adulteration, or unsafe products.

Director personal liability: Responsible persons and technical managers may face criminal prosecution, personal compensation claims, disqualification, and imprisonment where unlawful manufacture or unsafe-product conduct is attributable to their decisions.
Customs and Import-Export Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Additional customs duty, import VAT, penalties, seizure, forfeiture, suspension of customs privileges, and criminal sanctions for deliberate smuggling or fraudulent declarations.

Director personal liability: Individuals approving false customs documents may face personal fines, criminal liability, confiscation, and civil recovery for resulting duties and losses.
Unlawful Profit Repatriation and Capital-Control Breach

Statutory sanction: Recovery of unlawful distributions, withholding-tax assessment, corporate and tax penalties, shareholder repayment claims, regulatory reporting, and possible foreign-exchange or company-law sanctions.

Director personal liability: Directors may be jointly or personally liable for unlawful distributions, breach of capital-maintenance rules, creditor prejudice, and deliberate tax or foreign-exchange violations.
Accounting, Records, and Data Integrity Failure

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of deductions, estimated assessment, loss of tax incentives, administrative fines, audit adjustments, license consequences, and adverse evidentiary inferences.

Director personal liability: The responsible person, finance head, and designated accounting personnel may face statutory penalties and civil liability for intentional destruction, falsification, or concealment of records.
