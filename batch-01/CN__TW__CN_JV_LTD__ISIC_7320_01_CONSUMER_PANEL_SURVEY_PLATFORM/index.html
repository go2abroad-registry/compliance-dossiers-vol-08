Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (Taiwan), National Taxation Administration; administered through regional National Taxation Bureaus. Primary online portals: Tax Portal (paytax.nat.gov.tw) and the Electronic Invoice Integrative Services Platform (einvoice.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor establishing a sino-foreign equity JV limited company in Taiwan must first obtain investment approval from the Department of Investment Review, Ministry of Economic Affairs. Upon incorporation with the Company Registry of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the company is automatically assigned a Unified Business Number (UBIN), which serves as its tax identification number. The JV must then file a Business Tax Registration (營業登記) with the local National Taxation Bureau within 15 days after business commencement; the tax bureau issues a tax registration certificate and validates the UBIN for VAT and corporate income tax purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; the JV is a resident profit-seeking enterprise subject to 20% corporate income tax on Taiwan-sourced and worldwide net income, with foreign tax credits on foreign-source income. The entity is not a pass-through vehicle; Taiwan does not recognize check-the-box partnerships for limited companies. — A foreign investor establishing a sino-foreign equity JV limited company in Taiwan must first obtain investment approval from the Department of Investment Review, Ministry of Economic Affairs. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the JV is a resident profit-seeking enterprise subject to 20% corporate income tax on Taiwan-sourced and worldwide net income, with foreign tax credits on foreign-source income. The entity is not a pass-through vehicle; Taiwan does not recognize check-the-box partnerships for limited companies.

Available election forms

Taiwan has no check-the-box entity classification election. A JV may elect optional simplified export VAT accounting for zero-rated sales, apply for advance pricing agreements, select IFRS as its accounting standard for tax alignment, and may claim tax credits under the Enterprise Free Trade Zone Act or Statute for Industrial Innovation if eligible. Hybrid mismatch and CFC regimes under Articles 43-3 and 43-4 of the Income Tax Act apply automatically; no elective hybrid mismatch rule is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

NT$200,000 monthly average sales for standard VAT-payer registration with full invoice obligations; smaller-scale enterprises with NT$80,000 to NT$200,000 monthly average turnover must register but may apply the 1% special VAT rate; businesses under NT$80,000 monthly average turnover are conditionally exempt with MOF approval.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating is available under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act for goods physically exported from Taiwan customs territory and for services supplied by Taiwan enterprises to persons outside Taiwan if the services are used, rendered or enjoyed outside Taiwan and paid for in foreign currency. The survey platform must demonstrate that the consumer-panel services and associated data outputs are furnished to overseas clients and do not involve local Taiwan consumers; a partial zero-rating election may apply to separately identifiable offshore service streams.

  • Customs export declaration (出口報單 with proof of physical exit)
  • Matching commercial invoice and packing list
  • Ocean/air bill of lading or electronic logistics proof
  • Service contract and foreign-client acceptance evidence for offshore services
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank settlement record proving foreign-currency consideration received
  • Unified invoice (B2B or B2C) annotated with 0% tax rate

Taiwan mandates electronic invoicing (Cloud Invoices) through the MOF E-Invoice Platform. Enterprises meeting prescribed turnover thresholds are required to use Turnkey service (API) to transmit electronic unified invoices to the National Taxation Bureau within statutory timeframes. B2C invoices use a common carrier cloud-invoice loop, and B2B invoices require near-real-time registration of invoice numbers and amounts. Digital time stamping and cryptographic validation are performed through the MOF platform; there is no physical fiscal stamp but a legally recognized electronic fiscal storage system with penalty sanctions.

The National Taxation Bureau audits zero-rated export claims by cross-checking customs clearance records, shipping manifests, bank foreign-exchange reports and service delivery logs. Any misdeclaration, forged documentation, or failure to repatriate export proceeds within the 180-day statutory settlement window results in reversal of the zero-rating plus underpaid VAT at 5%. Under Article 51 of the Business Tax Act, the taxpayer is punishable by an additional penalty of up to five times the underpaid tax; where willful fraud is established, criminal liability under the Tax Evasion and Penalty Act may attach, and the Ministry of Finance may claw back refunds for a seven-year assessment period.
