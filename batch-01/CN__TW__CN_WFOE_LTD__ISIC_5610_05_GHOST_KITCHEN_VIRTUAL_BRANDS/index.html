Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

National Taxation Administration (NTA), Ministry of Finance, Taiwan (R.O.C.); online portal: E-Tax Portal (https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw); local execution through regional national tax bureaus (國稅局) and revenue service offices (稽徵所).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — before incorporation, the Mainland China investor must obtain Taiwan investment approval from the Investment Commission, Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) under the Statute for Investment by Foreign Nationals; Step 2 — incorporate the WFOE as a Limited Liability Company through MOEA's Company Registration Online, receiving an 8-digit Unified Business Number (UBIN/統一編號) issued automatically by MOEA; Step 3 — complete mandatory tax registration with the local Revenue Service Office of NTA within 15 days after commencement of business under Article 28, Business Tax Act; Step 4 — exchange the UBIN for the Business Tax Registration Certificate and, if the WFOE carries on export activities, apply to the Taipei Customs, Customs Administration, for a Customs Exporter Code. For the Taiwan WFOE, the UBIN is the single statutory TIN/VAT ID and must be printed on all Uniform Invoices and customs declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Taiwan WFOE is a taxable resident company incorporated under the Taiwan Company Act; it is taxed on worldwide income at the 20% Corporate Income Tax rate (with an additional 5% retained-earnings surtax on undistributed earnings at certain levels); it is not a pass-through or transparent entity; any distribution to the Mainland China shareholder is subject to withholding tax at the applicable rate under the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Agreement, subject to qualification.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is available under Taiwan law; statutory classification is factual, not elective. Available elections relevant to entity income tax include: (1) application for exemption of the retained-earnings surtax on reinvested income under the Statute for Industrial Innovation; (2) election by a foreign investor-owned WFOE to adopt a January-December fiscal year if the parent uses a different fiscal year, subject to NTA approval; (3) selection of the profit-contribution method under Article 25 of the Income Tax Act for deemed service income from international transactions; and (4) execution of an Advance Pricing Agreement (APA) or unilateral business tax rulings for cross-border related-party transactions. Tax treaties are not applicable to Mainland China investors except under the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Agreement.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

NT$0 registration threshold for a Taiwan-incorporated WFOE with a fixed place of business (e.g., a ghost kitchen facility); registration is mandatory as soon as the WFOE commences taxable sales. For an offshore foreign supplier without a Taiwan fixed place of business that sells only cross-border electronic services to Taiwanese consumers, the threshold is NT$480,000 in annual taxable sales from Taiwan-source services; the physical ghost-kitchen structure eliminates threshold applicability.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating is available under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act for: (i) goods physically exported from Taiwan to Mainland China or third countries, evidenced by Customs export declaration; and (ii) services supplied by the WFOE that are used or enjoyed wholly outside Taiwan, including offshore franchise/know-how, cloud-kitchen software/management, and delivery-platform ancillary services, provided the services are not substantially performed within Taiwan. The WFOE must be a general VAT taxpayer for the refund mechanism; input VAT on export-related purchases becomes creditable and refundable under Article 39, and export sales are reported on the quarterly Business Tax return at a zero rate rather than at 5%.

  • Commercial Invoice

All sellers must issue sequential Uniform Invoices under Article 32 of the Business Tax Act; for delivery-only, multi-brand ghost kitchens, the WFOE must issue electronic Uniform Invoices (E-Invoice) or use an NTA-approved food-delivery platform (e.g., UberEats, DoorDash) that issues electronic receipts on the seller's behalf. The E-Invoice details must be transmitted through the government E-Invoice Platform digital API (Turnkey/API) within 48 hours after issuance; the system applies encrypted QR-code stamping and sequential invoice number clearance, but Taiwan does not implement a per-transaction government real-time approval. Failing to upload, using non-approved POS, or generating off-book numbers is a Business Tax Act violation punishable by one to five times the evaded tax.

Any fraudulent zero-rating claim, including forged customs declarations, fictitious Bills of Lading, or sham offshore service contracts, is subject to retrospective reassessment of the 5% Business Tax on the entire export sale, plus a penalty of one to five times the underpaid tax under Article 51, and a late-payment surcharge of 1.5% for every two days. The NTA and Customs Administration may issue joint audit examinations using UberEats/DoorDash payouts, cross-border payment rails, and Taiwan foreign-exchange settlement records. Criminal liability may arise under the Tax Collection Act and Criminal Code for tax evasion with imprisonment up to five years; refunds previously issued are clawed back with statutory interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is current-account convertible for genuine trade and service transactions under China's IMF Article VIII obligations, while capital-account transactions (equity injection, cross-border loans, dividend/principal repatriation) remain registration- and authenticity-based. A Taiwan-invested WFOE must complete the 外商投资信息报告 (foreign-investment information filing) with the market-regulation authority and bank-executed SAFE FDI registration (境内直接投资外汇登记) before receiving inbound equity. Profit remittance requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement proof (完税证明), board resolution on profit distribution, and bank-side authenticity review under the '展业三原则' (KYC, authenticity, due diligence); unrestricted convertibility for speculative or non-trade flows is prohibited.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mandatory export-proceeds surrender was abolished in 2012 (汇发〔2012〕20号); no statutory timeline or conversion ratio currently applies. Export/service-trade receipts must be credited to a registered 经常项目外汇账户 and reported under BOP rules before conversion to RMB beyond the registered intended use. For a delivery-only ghost-kitchen WFOE, platform payouts (Meituan/Ele.me) are domestic RMB and outside surrender rules; cross-border service receipts or royalties from the Taiwan parent or overseas licensees above USD 50,000 require a 服务贸易外汇业务办理表 and the tax-filing certificate (服务贸易等项目对外支付税务备案表) at the paying bank.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行); regulated by the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations of the PRC (2008, as revised) and the SAFE '汇发' circular series; cross-border payments and capital-account registrations are executed bank-side under SAFE delegation and monitored through the 外汇局资本项目信息系统 and BOP (国际收支) direct reporting.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium. Mainland-licensed digital banks (WeBank, MYBank) may open RMB settlement accounts only after physical KYC of the legal representative and cannot maintain SAFE capital-account escrow relationships for FDI; offshore EMIs (Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) hold no CBIRC/SAFE banking license and cannot hold capital-account funds or perform onshore FX conversion for a PRC WFOE. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Taiwan parent remits via SWIFT MT103 (or CIPS for direct CNH) to the WFOE's SAFE-registered 资本金账户, quoting purpose code 621011 (Investment Capital / 股权投资款) and the FDI registration reference; the receiving bank verifies the SAFE FDI confirmation, 外商投资信息报告 receipt, and negative-list restrictions before crediting. Foreign-currency inbound funds are settled into RMB through a 结汇待支付账户 in accordance with the registered intended-use positive list. Where the Taiwan parent provides intercompany debt, a 外债 (cross-border loan) registration under PBOC macroprudential management (跨境融资宏观审慎管理) is required, including borrowing-contract registration and FX-hedging documentation; equity injection is preferred for the ghost-kitchen WFOE to reduce debt-service and repatriation risk.Clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103/MT202 for open-account and service-trade payments to the Taiwan parent (royalties, technology fees, management fees), supported by the 税务备案 table where the amount exceeds USD 50,000; (ii) documentary letters of credit or clean collection/DP for import of commercial kitchen equipment from third-country suppliers; (iii) domestic RMB rails (CNAPS 大额支付系统, 网上支付跨行清算系统) for procurement of raw materials and platform-operating expenses inside mainland China; (iv) CIPS for cross-border RMB royalty or supplier payments when counterparties agree to CNH settlement. All outbound FX conversions must be supported by authentic trade documents (invoices, contracts, customs declarations, delivery-platform statements) and reported under BOP rules with bank application of the 展业三原则.远期结售汇 - physically settled forward FX contracts against real current-account or registered capital-account exposure外汇掉期 - CNY/USD FX swaps for liquidity rebalancing and cash-flow timing mismatches货币掉期 - cross-currency and interest-rate swaps (including structured forms) permitted only where a genuine underlying borrowing or investment exposure exists, e.g., on an intercompany 外债 facility人民币外汇期权 - plain-vanilla FX options purchased from a licensed bank under a documented trade-risk mandateExcluded onshore: CNH/CNY non-deliverable forwards (NDF) are not permitted for PRC-resident entities; speculative FX trading and open-ended derivatives positions are prohibited under PBOC and SAFE derivative rules; all hedging must be mark-to-market tracked with underlying exposure evidence.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low-Medium. Mainland-licensed digital banks (WeBank, MYBank) may open RMB settlement accounts only after physical KYC of the legal representative and cannot maintain SAFE capital-account escrow relationships for FDI; offshore EMIs (Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) hold no CBIRC/SAFE banking license and cannot hold capital-account funds or perform onshore FX conversion for a PRC WFOE. A Taiwan parent may use offshore EMI accounts for treasury overlay, but all compliant inbound equity, outbound royalties, and platform settlements of the mainland WFOE must flow through a licensed Chinese commercial bank; cross-border e-commerce licensed payment institutions (LianLian, PingPong) are supplementary collection channels only and not a substitute for a corporate settlement account.
