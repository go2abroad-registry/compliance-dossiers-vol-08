Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For a WFOE engaged in export distribution of pre-packaged spicy tallow hotpot base bricks under ISIC 4638, a pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally not permissible for the registered address because the PRC Company Law and the Market Entity Registration Regulations require a registered domicile that is a genuine, legally usable address capable of receiving legal documents and official correspondence. In practice, however, the registered address may be either an owned or leased physical commercial premises; many local SAMR bureaus accept address registration on a licensed incubator, government-sponsored entrepreneurship base, or a registered office service provider if such provider is on the local approved list. The critical limitation is taxation and banking: the local tax bureau and the foreign-exchange bank will require an actual business premises or a verifiable central office for on-site inspection during opening of bank accounts, issuance of VAT invoice quotas, and export tax rebate filing. A virtual mailbox alone will be rejected by the tax authorities for export tax rebate registration and by the bank for foreign exchange settlement and Letter of Credit negotiation. Therefore, for the hotpot base export distribution WFOE, the entity must lease a physical office and ideally a licensed food/condiment storage warehouse if it conducts any domestic purchase, storage, repackaging, or consolidation of export goods; if the WFOE operates as a pure trading/purchasing agent without domestic warehousing, a physical office suite with a lease agreement, property ownership certificate or title extract of the lessor, and a front-desk receiving arrangement is strongly recommended and is the practical benchmark for an unannounced physical inspection. The virtual office may only serve as an auxiliary executive mailbox, never as the registered address.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Taiwan prescribes no general statutory minimum paid-in capital for a limited company. The foreign investor must obtain a Foreign Investment Approval Letter before remitting capital, deposit the approved capital into a newly opened Taiwan bank account, and obtain a bank-issued capital balance certificate and, where required, a certified public accountant capital verification report for filing with MOEA. No blocked escrow account is statutorily required, but the paid-in capital must remain in the account and may not be withdrawn before the Company Registration Certificate is issued.

Funding & unlock path: Civil defense fire safety and fire prevention certification from the local fire bureau is mandatory for the warehouse, including installation of fire extinguishers, alarms, emergency lighting, and evacuation signage. Structural load inspection and shelf-stability certification are required for all racking systems. Workplace safety approval shall be obtained under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, covering manual handling, forklift operation, electrical safety, and emergency equipment. The Company must also register employees for labor insurance, health insurance, and pension contributions with the local competent authority before commencing operations.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), with online filing through the provincial Electronic Tax Bureau portals integrated with the national e-Tax system (central portal www.chinatax.gov.cn); export customs data are exchanged with the General Administration of Customs of the PRC (GACC).

E-government filing portal

The China WFOE is incorporated with the State Administration for Market Regulation and receives a Business License containing the Unified Social Credit Code. Within 30 days after the Business License is issued, the WFOE must complete tax registration with the local STA office through the Electronic Tax Bureau or on-site service hall, thereby obtaining a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN) identical to the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code. The next steps are: (1) register for VAT and determine small-scale vs general VAT taxpayer status; (2) complete the export tax refund/exemption filing with the STA after GACC customs registration; (3) register in the trade foreign exchange receipt system with a designated bank and SAFE by completing the Trade Foreign Exchange Receipts and Payments Registry; and (4) if cross-border e-commerce export fulfillment is used, complete the applicable cross-border e-commerce retail export supervision filing. For a foreign non-resident entity without a permanent establishment in China, China-source taxation is enforced through a PRC withholding agent, and the non-resident entity should obtain/confirm its foreign taxpayer identification number and include tax residence details in the withholding registration records; a separate TIN is not required for a WFOE because the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported pre-packaged hotpot soup base is eligible for the PRC VAT zero/export-rebate regime when: (a) the seller is a VAT general taxpayer; (b) the goods are actually cleared by GACC and transported from the customs territory of mainland China to a destination outside that territory, including Taiwan as a separate customs territory; (c) the export sale is supported by a valid customs export declaration and purchase contract with an overseas buyer; (d) the sales proceeds are repatriated to China through a designated foreign exchange bank and reported; and (e) the input VAT chain relating to tallow, packaging materials, seasoning and logistics is supported by lawful special VAT invoices. The exported goods are not subject to output VAT at the point of export, and the residual input VAT is refundable or creditable under the export tax rebate procedure.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form with customs release and electronic port data
  • Bill of Lading or multimodal transport document showing the Taiwan consignee and port of discharge
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank-issued foreign exchange receipt/export proceeds settlement voucher
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Export sales contract or purchase order for the pre-packaged hotpot soup base
  • VAT special invoice(s) for domestic input purchases and export sales-related invoicing records
  • Export tax refund/rebate declaration submitted through the STA e-Tax system with the customs declaration number

All VAT invoices, including export sales invoices, must be issued in the STA’s digital invoicing platform or through an approved integrating system using the taxpayer’s digital certificate/electronic seal. Invoice metadata must be transmitted to the Golden Tax Phase IV system, which performs real-time reconciliation of issuance, declaration and payment data; the GACC and STA exchange export manifest data through the electronic port. Special VAT invoices and fully digitalized e-invoices carry a centrally issued invoice code, QR anti-counterfeiting code and must be filled using the STA-specified data schema. There is no mechanical paper fiscal stamping in mainland China, but the issuance right and invoice block are centrally allocated and any abnormal invoice triggers automatic tax-risk control.

The STA cross-checks the customs declaration, invoice issuance, logistics documents and bank foreign exchange receipts against the export rebate claim. If zero-rated exports are found to be based on false customs declarations, forged FIRC/foreign exchange vouchers, circular invoicing, or non-repatriation of export proceeds within the prescribed 180-day FX repatriation period, the zero-rating is reversed, the refunded VAT is clawed back with daily late-payment interest of 0.05% of the unpaid tax, and a penalty of one to five times the fraudulently refunded tax may be imposed under the Law on the Administration of Tax Levying and Collection. Where criminal threshold for fraudulent export tax rebating or VAT invoice fraud is met, the case is referred for criminal prosecution; the retroactive assessment limitation is extended for fraudulent conduct and can be unlimited.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed floating exchange-rate system with a current-account convertibility regime for genuine trade and service transactions. The RMB is convertible for trade settlements and ordinary service payments, but the capital account remains regulated. A CN WFOE established by a Taiwanese investor must obtain SAFE FDI registration before inbound equity capital is received; outbound profit remittances require an audited annual financial statement, a completed foreign exchange settlement and sale application, CIT tax clearance, and a prior SAFE foreign-exchange payment declaration. Registered capital funds received into the Foreign Exchange Capital Account cannot be freely used or converted; they must be settled on a pay-as-you-go basis, with banks testing the authenticity of the underlying expenses. The Taiwan leg is subject to opposite rules: Taiwan does not strictly control trade-related NTD conversion, but the mainland side requires bank verification of customs declarations, invoices, and logistics records. No automatic full conversion exists for capital account transactions such as intercompany loans or shareholder advances; those must be separately registered and covered by the SAFE framework. Profit repatriation is not barred but is effective only if the company can evidence real operations, tax filing, and statutory capital preservation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE goods-trade monitoring system, the WFOE must repatriate export proceeds to a domestic foreign-currency account; trade receipts must normally be collected within the contract's payment term and, if outstanding beyond 90 days from the date of customs declaration, must be reported as delayed collection in the SAFE digital monitoring system. The former mandatory percentage-based surrender was abolished; the company may retain export income in foreign currency or sell it to the bank at the prevailing market rate. No percentage conversion quota is applied unless a specific bank internal policy or a tax collection cross-check requires a documentary explanation for retained balances.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Not viable as a primary account, and only marginally usable as a supplementary rail. Under the PRC Banking Supervision Law, overseas and internet electronic-money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions and cannot open corporate accounts or hold SAFE capital account registrations for a mainland WFOE. SAFE-licensed cross-border payment institutions may digitize small-value open-account trade flows, but they must ultimately toll through a Tier-1 licensed bank and cannot handle FDI capital, L/C negotiation, FX derivatives, or large T/T settlement. Feasibility rating: Restricted / Supplementary Use Only.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwanese/Hong Kong investor executes a contribution resolution and wires foreign currency, preferably USD or RMB, to the WFOE's dedicated Foreign Exchange Capital Account maintained at Bank of China or ICBC. The remittance instruction must include the SAFE purpose code for foreign direct investment capital injection and reference the WFOE name and underlying FDI contract. The receiving bank verifies against the SAFE FDI registration certificate and the PRC business license before crediting. After receipt, the bank applies pay-as-you-go capital settlement; each conversion into RMB is tested against the intended use, such as lease agreements, supplier invoices, payroll, tax payments or production overhead. A PRC-licensed certified public accountant issues a capital verification report after each tranche or at full paid-in capital; the report is filed with the market regulation authority and supports subsequent SAFE and industrial-commercial updates. Capital account funds cannot be freely on-loaned or transferred to third-country related parties without prior SAFE approval.For procurement of tallow, spices, packaging and other inputs from overseas suppliers, the WFOE can settle through SWIFT MT103 T/T transfers after submitting the contract, proforma invoice, customs declaration and a bank foreign-exchange purchase application; for larger bulk trade, it may open an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit through Bank of China or ICBC, with documents examined under UCP600; for low-risk repeat deliveries, Documentary Collections (D/P at sight or D/A with tenor) are acceptable. On the export side, the same rails operate in reverse: a Taiwanese importer remits via SWIFT MT103 in USD or RMB, or issues an L/C naming the WFOE as beneficiary; the WFOE's advising/negotiating bank checks invoice, packing list, bill of lading, health certificate, certificate of origin and customs declaration. All receipts and payments must be reconciled in the SAFE goods-trade monitoring system; mismatching purpose codes, missing logistics proof or non-trade related narrative in the SWIFT field 70 will cause rejection or suspension.Deliverable RMB-USD forward settlement-and-sale contracts executed with a licensed onshore bank, supported by documented export/import cash-flow exposure and SAFE trade-monitoring records.FX swaps and RMB-USD cross-currency interest rate swaps covering principal and interest on a SAFE-registered external loan or shareholder advance, provided the underlying debt is registered and the derivative is executed with a domestic Tier-1 bank under its SAFE derivatives qualification.Cross-currency swaps for long-term intra-group financing to align USD investor funding with RMB operational cash flows, subject to the bank's internal product approval, SAFE negative-list review and board-level derivative risk authorization.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible onshore; the mainland WFOE may not execute or accept an NDF, so any NDF hedging must be centralized in the offshore parent in Taiwan or Hong Kong and must not be linked to the WFOE's capital or trade settlement accounts.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Not viable as a primary account, and only marginally usable as a supplementary rail. Under the PRC Banking Supervision Law, overseas and internet electronic-money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions and cannot open corporate accounts or hold SAFE capital account registrations for a mainland WFOE. SAFE-licensed cross-border payment institutions may digitize small-value open-account trade flows, but they must ultimately toll through a Tier-1 licensed bank and cannot handle FDI capital, L/C negotiation, FX derivatives, or large T/T settlement. Feasibility rating: Restricted / Supplementary Use Only.
