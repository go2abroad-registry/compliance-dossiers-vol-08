Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity classification election exists under Taiwan law. Entity status is determined solely by the Company Act (limited liability company or company limited by shares). No hybrid mismatch elections are available; however, foreign enterprises may elect to be taxed under the branch profit remittance regime if registered as a Taiwan branch (Article 25, Income Tax Act) and may apply for an advance ruling on transaction results or profit attribution under the Income Tax Act. For indirect tax, a taxpayer may elect to apply for the ‘monthly filing’ or ‘quarterly filing’ schedule depending on turnover and may voluntarily register for VAT even when below the statutory threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Average monthly taxable revenue exceeding NT$80,000 for sale of goods or NT$40,000 for sale of services; an enterprise with no fixed business place and selling goods/services through e-commerce platforms may be required to register if sales to Taiwan customers exceed NT$480,000 per year (per Ministry of Finance guidance for foreign e-commerce providers)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Value-Added and Non-Value-Added Business Tax Act, sales of goods exported abroad , goods re-exported after processing, sales to export processing zones or free trade zones, and sales of internationally provided services are subject to zero percent VAT. To qualify, the taxpayer must be registered as a business, issue a zero-rated uniform invoice (統一發票), document the export transaction, and provide transport and foreign-exchange evidence showing the goods or services were actually supplied to a foreign person or delivered outside Taiwan. The claim must be supported by customs export declarations and payment settlement records.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單, usually via the Ministry of Finance Customs Administration)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Taiwan
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (出口結匯實書 / Foreign Exchange Earnings Certificate) from an authorized foreign-exchange bank

Taiwan mandates the use of government-integrated electronic invoices (電子發票) for most VAT-registered businesses. The e-invoice platform (電子發票整合服務平台) requires real-time transmission of invoice data through the Turnkey API in the government-defined XML/JSON format, with digital signature and timestamping. Certain industries are subject to mandatory real-time electronic invoice clearance; for other businesses, bulk filing with the National Taxation Bureau via the API is generally permitted. The e-invoice system supports carrier-consolidated digital receipts and must retain the standardized 電子發票證明聯 evidence for audit and refund claims.

Zero-rating claims are subject to strict documentary audit by the National Taxation Bureau. If a taxpayer fraudulently or erroneously claims zero-rating without actual export or with fabricated documents, the assessment authority will retroactively impose the standard 5% VAT, unpaid tax interest, and a penalty under Article 51 of the Business Tax Act of 5% to 5 times the amount of tax evaded. In serious cases involving forged customs documents or false declarations, criminal liability may arise under the Tax Evasion and Tax Collection Measures, with imprisonment and fines. Additionally, refund claims (出口退稅) may be denied if all mandatory export and foreign-exchange evidence is not retained for at least five years and reconciled with the quarterly or monthly business tax filings.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current-account items are fully convertible under IMF Article VIII: payment for imports (ABS resin, precision steel molds, packaging), freight, commissions, and license fees is settled on a 'real invoice plus customs matching' basis with no monetary cap. Capital-account items remain administratively controlled: inbound equity injections require SAFE FDI Business Registration (直接投资外汇登记) and must be credited only to a designated Capital Escrow Account (资本金账户); outbound dividend remittances are permitted after statutory audit, dividend WHT settlement under the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement (5% WHT at ≥25% shareholding), and SAFE profit-remittance filing. Practical hurdles: banks apply total-amount trade-background matching (contract amount vs. FX remittance vs. customs declaration) with 70% deviation screening; shareholder loans are capped by the Investment-Debt ratio (投注差) mechanism; and any deviation beyond the registered purpose is screened as a capital-account violation with penalties of up to 30% of the unlawful amount under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations (2008).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since SAFE's 2012 reform (汇发[2012]59号), there is NO mandatory conversion ratio or forced surrender of export proceeds; however, statutory repatriation (调回境内) remains absolute. A-class exporters must collect and remit export proceeds to an onshore account; the SAFE Trade Monitoring System (贸易监测系统) benchmarks collection within 90 days after the customs export declaration (for general trade), and any delay beyond 180 days or any unrepatriated offshore balance triggers an automatic total-amount mismatch (总量核查) anomaly, followed by on-site inspection, downgrade to B/C class, or administrative penalties. Enterprises may retain earned FX in a foreign-currency settlement account or convert voluntarily (意愿结汇); e-commerce proceeds collected via licensed payment institutions must be settled to the onshore operator account and reported under the 2019 Payment Institution FX Business framework (汇发[2019]13号).

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局, SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC); SAFE delegates 'bank-direct FX registration' power to designated commercial banks for routine FDI and goods-trade verification, while PBOC retains macro-prudential authority under the Full-Caliber Cross-Border Financing framework (全口径跨境融资宏观审慎管理). For the Taiwan leg, the Taiwan Central Bank (中央銀行, CBC) governs TWD-denominated cross-strait remittance reporting and balance verification.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium feasibility, restricted scope. Offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, PingPong, WorldFirst, Stripe, PayPal Business) are payment institutions, not banks: they cannot open or hold onshore settlement accounts, escrow capital contributions, or lawfully park export FX offshore beyond the SAFE repatriation window. They are permissible only as collection rails for e-commerce and marketplace receipts (跨境电商收款) to the extent the provider operates under a SAFE license (汇发[2019]13号) or partners with a licensed acquiring bank; funds must be swept to the WFOE's domestic settlement account with full order-level reconciliation. Rating: Medium for micro-order receivables (PayPal-scale), Low for capital injection and B2B trade settlements, and Not Permitted as the sole treasury rail because the PBOC 'three-consistency' principle (contract party = customs consignee = FX receiver) cannot be satisfied by offshore EMI aggregation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) SAFE FDI Business Registration obtained (or bank-direct registration filed) yielding the FDI business registration certificate number (业务登记凭证编号) and the 6-digit FDI identity code; (2) the Taiwan parent remits via SWIFT MT103 through its Taiwanese bank, quoting in Field 70 the SAFE FDI registration number, the stated purpose 'Investment Capital Contribution / 注册资本入资', and the Chinese WFOE's account name; BOP transaction code 621011 (直接投资—投资资本金) is auto-assigned by the receiving bank; (3) the beneficiary bank verifies the remittance against the registered capital quota and the WFOE's articles of association investment schedule, credits the Capital Escrow Account, and generates the FX Receipt Declaration (涉外收入申报单) within T+1; (4) funds may then be transferred to the operational account for permitted working-capital uses, with restrictive negative-list monitoring (no equity/real-estate speculation unless expressly licensed). Payments in RMB from Taiwan can alternatively settle through CIPS using the cross-strait RMB clearing banks (BOC and CCB Taipei branches), removing USD correspondent conversion costs.Supplier and CapEx settlements: SWIFT MT103 wires for recurring raw-material purchases (ABS/PC resin, steel plates) carrying SAFE purpose code 1101 (general trade) plus the customs and contract references; documentary Letter of Credit (UCP600) issuance via MT700/MT707 for high-value mold-making equipment and bulk resin contracts, with the WFOE as applicant and the Taiwan/overseas supplier as beneficiary; MT760 standby guarantees where performance bonds are required. Receivables side: inbound MT103 collections from distributors, export L/C confirmation through confirming banks (MT707/MT710), and clean collections under URBPO (MT400 series). For RMB-invoiced orders, all settlements are cleared via CIPS participant banks with just-in-time liquidity from the WFOE's RMB settlement account, eliminating the FX intermediation step. PayPal/EMI e-commerce collections are batched to the onshore operator account and, where applicable, topped-up or swept via the licensed acquiring bank's netting cycle.Deliverable FX Forwards (远期结售汇): licensed commercial banks, strict real-demand (实需) underwriting against export-forecast or import-cost exposure; tenors matched to trade cycles, settled on delivery date at agreed rate.FX Swaps (外汇掉期): near-dated spot-vs-forward rollovers for short-term USD/RMB working-capital conversion, real-demand documented.Cross-currency Interest Rate Swaps (货币利率掉期): for long-term equipment-finance USD liabilities, converting floating USD risk into fixed RMB-equivalent cash flows, subject to bank ISDA/NAFMII master agreements.Plain-vanilla FX Options and Range/Collar Structures (外汇期权及组合): enterprises may buy options or enter risk-reversal collars with premium payment; zero-premium collars permissible, but writing uncovered options is prohibited for non-financial corporates.Natural-hedge and settlement-delay techniques (延期结汇): permitted retention of export FX to time voluntary conversion when forward pricing is unfavorable.NDFs (offshore non-deliverable forwards): NOT PERMITTED for onshore WFOEs — SAFE allows only onshore deliverable forwards against real trade; access to offshore CNH NDF markets is legally unavailable to mainland-resident enterprises, and any such synthetic exposure must be booked by the Taiwan parent outside the PRC treasury perimeter.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium feasibility, restricted scope. Offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, PingPong, WorldFirst, Stripe, PayPal Business) are payment institutions, not banks: they cannot open or hold onshore settlement accounts, escrow capital contributions, or lawfully park export FX offshore beyond the SAFE repatriation window. They are permissible only as collection rails for e-commerce and marketplace receipts (跨境电商收款) to the extent the provider operates under a SAFE license (汇发[2019]13号) or partners with a licensed acquiring bank; funds must be swept to the WFOE's domestic settlement account with full order-level reconciliation. Rating: Medium for micro-order receivables (PayPal-scale), Low for capital injection and B2B trade settlements, and Not Permitted as the sole treasury rail because the PBOC 'three-consistency' principle (contract party = customs consignee = FX receiver) cannot be satisfied by offshore EMI aggregation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: CNY 100,000,000 for tangible-asset transfers; CNY 100,000,000 for financial-asset transfers; CNY 10,000,000 for intangible-asset transfers; CNY 40,000,000 for other related-party transactions. Master File preparation is required when the MNE group's consolidated revenue for the prior fiscal year reaches CNY 5,000,000,000; Country-by-Country Report filing is required when group consolidated revenue exceeds CNY 5,500,000,000, per SAT Announcement [2016] No. 42.

Economic substance scope: Mainland China does not impose offshore-type Economic Substance Regulations (ESR). However, Article 47 of the CIT Law and the SAT's General Anti-Avoidance Rules (Announcement [2017] No. 6) authorize recharacterization where an entity lacks real management, staff, assets, or business purpose. The WFOE must demonstrate actual manufacturing substance in China through local payroll, plant premises, equipment usage, and board decision-making. Taiwanese intermediary or shell holding structures (BVI/HK layers) face substance audits and may be disregarded for tax purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment to arm's length profit; penalty of 25% of the additional tax where contemporaneous documentation is not provided, or 50%–300% of the underpaid tax under Article 44/63 CIT Law; interest at the benchmark lending rate plus 5 percentage points.

Director personal liability: Willful TP fraud may trigger corporate veil piercing; directors and legal representatives can be jointly and severally liable for the underpaid CIT. Criminal tax evasion prosecution carries up to seven years' imprisonment and fines, and the court may impose travel bans and asset freezing preventing the director from leaving China.
Withholding Tax Evasion on Dividends and Royalties

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the unwithheld amount from the Chinese payer; daily late surcharge of 0.05%; penalty of 50%–100% of the amount evaded; retroactive assessment can extend three years, or ten years in cases of fraud.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and finance officer responsible for the remittance may be personally assessed for failure to withhold; deliberate evasion is a criminal offense under Article 201 of the Criminal Law of China, carrying up to seven years' imprisonment and financial penalties. Exit restrictions are frequently applied before case closure.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: GAAR recharacterization of the WFOE as a conduit; deemed profit adjustment; penalty of up to 300% of the underpaid tax; cancellation of tax registration, business license revocation, and inclusion in the tax non-compliance blacklist affecting bank accounts and customs clearance.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held joint and severally liable for tax debts when the corporate form is proven a sham. They may be prohibited from serving as a legal representative or senior manager in China for up to three years, and may face civil restitution orders, bank account freezes, and criminal sanction if assets are concealed offshore.
AML / Cross-Border Payment Irregularities

Statutory sanction: PBOC-AML fines up to RMB 2,000,000; freezing of bank accounts; customs penalties up to five times the value of undeclared goods; tax underpayment penalties; referral to public security for illegal foreign exchange trading or smuggling.

Director personal liability: Directors can be prosecuted for money laundering (up to ten years' imprisonment), illegal cross-border remittance, smuggling, and tax fraud, with confiscation of personal assets. A travel ban is automatic during criminal investigation, and civil recovery actions against personal property are common.
Failure to File Corporate Income Tax Returns

Statutory sanction: Fixed penalty of up to RMB 5,000 per unfiled return; daily surcharge of 0.05% on outstanding tax; forced collection via bank garnishment, seizure of inventory and equipment; tax authority refusal to issue a Clear Tax Certificate for the legal representative to exit the country.

Director personal liability: The legal representative or director in control of the financial affairs may be held personally liable for obstruction of tax collection; willful non-filing is treated as tax evasion, exposing the responsible director to criminal prosecution and imprisonment, as well as personal asset attachments to satisfy the WFOE's tax debt.
