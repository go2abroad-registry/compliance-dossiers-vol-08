Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Taiwan has no general statutory minimum paid-up capital for an ordinary company limited by shares or limited company. Capital must be fully subscribed and paid before registration, and the company must provide bank payment evidence and obtain a certified capital verification report from a Taiwan certified public accountant. Additional capital, ownership, or approval requirements may apply to restricted industries or foreign investors.

Funding & unlock path: A standard office generally requires no special industrial civil-defense clearance. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Fiscal Information Agency (財政部財政資訊中心) and the National Taxation Bureaus (各地區國稅局); online portal: https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw

E-government filing portal

A foreign-invested entity must first complete company incorporation with the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) and obtain the approved company registration. Thereafter, the entity must complete business registration (營業登記) with the local National Taxation Bureau within 15 days of approval, submitting the incorporation certificate, articles of incorporation, lease or ownership proof of the business premises, identity documents of the responsible person and shareholders, and the uniform invoice usage application (統一發票使用申請). The National Taxation Bureau will assign a Unified Business Number (統一編號, UBN) VAT registration number and an associated tax file number; the entity must then register for electronic uniform invoice (電子發票) issuance and, if applicable, apply for the export sales zero-rated VAT approval under Article 7 of the Value-Added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election under Taiwan law for a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture. An entity may elect simplified business tax filing under Article 23 of the Business Tax Act if annual turnover is below NT$3,000,000, but such election is not available for exporters of goods or services who must claim input VAT credits. For income tax purposes, a JV organized as a limited company is an entity-level taxpayer subject to the 20% corporate income tax rate on worldwide income if managed and controlled in Taiwan; no hybrid mismatch entity election is available. A foreign entity with a Taiwan permanent establishment may elect to allocate profits attributable to the Taiwan branch under the Advance Pricing Agreement procedure or Mutual Agreement Procedure, but such election does not alter the entity classification itself.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

NT$40,000 per month for sole proprietors with no uniform invoice obligation; NT$200,000 per month for businesses eligible for simplified filing; mandatory VAT registration with uniform invoice issuance is required when monthly sales of goods or services exceed NT$200,000. A foreign company with a fixed place of business or electronic commerce nexus in Taiwan is required to register regardless of threshold if making taxable supplies in Taiwan.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of tangible goods qualifies for 0% VAT under Article 7(1) of the Business Tax Act if the goods are exported and customs export declaration is made, or shipped directly by the seller to an overseas buyer; export-related services, including software, SaaS, telematics and fleet-management technology services, qualify for 0% VAT under Article 7(8) if the services are provided to a foreign client, used entirely outside Taiwan, and the consideration is remitted into Taiwan in foreign currency; the service provider must obtain a foreign exchange certificate or bank remittance record evidencing receipt of foreign currency. Cross-border e-commerce electronic services provided to overseas enterprises also qualify for zero-rating where the place of use and performance is outside Taiwan.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Foreign Exchange Revenue or Inward Remittance Certificate (出口結匯水單)

Taiwan mandates the use of electronic uniform invoices (電子統一發票) for VAT-registered businesses, with mandatory transmission of invoice data to the Ministry of Finance E-Invoice Platform (財政部電子發票整合服務平台) via API or web-based service within 48 hours of issuance; the platform assigns a unique invoice number and performs real-time validation; VAT on all invoices must be reported through the platform and the quarterly VAT return must be transmitted electronically. Physical uniform invoice books remain permitted for approved cases, but all B2B and B2C electronic invoice data must be cleared by the National Taxation Bureau for reconciliation; the Turnkey API solution is required for high-volume issuers.

Zero-rated sales must be substantiated within the statutory filing quarter; if the foreign exchange proceeds are not received within 180 days from the export date, the taxpayer must provide evidence of reasonable cause to the National Taxation Bureau or the zero-rating will be retroactively reversed. Fraudulent zero-rating claims are subject to a penalty of five times the amount of the tax underpaid plus an additional fine of up to NT$1,000,000 for false invoices under Article 44 of the Business Tax Act; criminal liability under the Tax Evasion and Penalty Act (稅捐稽徵法) includes imprisonment of up to 5 years and confiscation of the unpaid tax plus delinquent interest. The National Taxation Bureau maintains a dedicated export-verification audit ladder requiring cross-matching of customs export records, FX inward remittance records, and electronic uniform invoice data; where documentation is incomplete or the foreign exchange proceeds are not repatriated, the VAT assessed will be increased by 5% retroactively and penalties will be levied.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is convertible on current-account items (software licensing, SaaS subscriptions, logistics information services) subject to bank-side truthfulness and consistency review by direct SAFE-designated banks. Capital-account items are non-convertible without registration: FDI equity injection requires FDI foreign-exchange registration (FDI外汇登记), shareholder loans require the all-round cross-border financing macro-prudential registration (全口径跨境融资宏观审慎管理), and liquidation or distribution flows require SAFE verification in the Capital Projects Information System. Profit remittance hurdles for the Taiwan-invested JV are: (i) annual statutory audit certified by a PRC CPA; (ii) corporate income tax final settlement with a payment or zero-tax certificate; (iii) the online cross-border payment tax filing certificate (对外支付税务备案电子凭证) issued via the tax administration portal; (iv) a board resolution declaring the dividend; and (v) BOP reporting with the profit-distribution purpose code 223022. NTD is not internationally convertible; the Taiwan shareholder will typically convert distributed RMB to CNH, USD, or HKD before wiring onward from its Taiwan treasury desk. Taiwan CBC regulations require banks to report any single corporate remittance above NT$50 million or its USD/HKD equivalent to the CBC on an automated declaration basis.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Since the 2012 foreign-exchange administration reform, mainland China imposes no mandatory surrender or statutory conversion ratio on export proceeds. All export and software-service receipts must, however, be repatriated into the mainland within 180 days of the underlying customs declaration or invoice date and be held in a foreign-currency settlement account or spot-converted to RMB at the bank's daily market rate. Verification is performed through the Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System (货物贸易外汇监测系统) using total-volume verification (总量核查) against the enterprise's customs and invoice flows; Class A enterprises receive low-touch pass-through treatment, while Class B or C enterprises face per-transaction documentary pre-approval. For the SaaS and fleet-dispatch export model, receipts are booked under BOP item computer and information services (227522); the JV must submit the Designated Foreign Exchange Receipts Declaration Form (涉外收入申报单) and a commercial invoice reflecting the corresponding subscription contract.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), a deputy-ministerial administration under the People's Bank of China (PBC), is the supreme authority over all Renminbi and foreign-currency transactions in mainland China. The Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC) independently administers New Taiwan Dollar convertibility and cross-border reporting within the Taiwan jurisdiction. No reciprocal SAFE-CBC settlement or surrender treaty exists; cross-strait flows settle via SWIFT, CHIPS, CIPS, and offshore CNY (CNH) clearing corridors.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW for inbound capital injection and export-receipt collection; MEDIUM for outbound disbursements to overseas logistics subcontractors and IoT hardware vendors. Legal constraints: non-bank payment institutions and offshore EMIs (including Airwallex, Currenxie, Wise Business, Dukascopy, and similar virtual treasury rails) are not licensed by SAFE to open capital-account or foreign-currency settlement accounts for a mainland-registered JV. SAFE requires all trade-related collection and capital flows to pass through a bank holding a full foreign-exchange license. Taiwan-side fintech channels such as CTBC Bank corporate digital portals and Cathay United Bank treasury APIs can initiate MT103 instructions but cannot replace mainland OFAC/SAFE compliance. Practical deployment: maintain a small executive treasury wallet in an approved offshore EMI for paying foreign fleet contractors of not more than USD 10,000 per transaction, backed by an invoicing flow and replicated in the onshore accounting ledger; the JV must keep its primary legal treasury in licensed onshore banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Taiwan-side equity must be remitted to the JV's SAFE-registered capital account (资本金账户) using a SWIFT MT103 whose field 72 narrative contains the FDI foreign-exchange registration voucher number (for example FDI-3200-000012345) and the purpose code INVEST-IN-CHINA. The receiving bank validates the enterprise registration in the Capital Projects Information System, matches the payer to the Taiwan investor, and posts the funds under the capital-account regime; any single injection exceeding 30% of registered capital also triggers a CPA capital-verification report. For shareholder loans, the JV completes the all-round cross-border financing macro-prudential registration to obtain a business-registration voucher and remits to a dedicated loan account; RMB loans use an onshore NRA-based arrangement. Current-account software-licence revenue and SaaS subscription receipts route to the foreign-currency settlement account with a completed Designated Foreign Exchange Receipts Declaration, the underlying agreement, and a digital tax-registration record; the bank then credits USD, HKD, or EUR, or converts to RMB at the daily central-parity-referenced rate.Primary clearance rails for supplier payments, royalty remittances, and dividend distributions are SWIFT MT103 with the correct SAFE BOP purpose code (for example 223022 profit distribution to the Taiwan shareholder, 230050 royalties, or 227522 computer-services import) attached; the bank verifies the corresponding tax certificate, board resolution, and contractual evidence before posting. For logistics hardware procurement the JV may open a commercial letter of credit via MT700 through its trade-finance counter, with a maturity profile aligned to platform cash flow and incoterms backed by the supplier invoice and bill of lading. For renminbi-denominated cross-strait obligations to suppliers registered in Shanghai or Fujian pilot zones, the JV can use the CIPS-II participant-bank channel to achieve direct CNY settlement without US-dollar conversion delays; USD flows otherwise clear via standard Fedwire and CHIPS correspondent lines maintained inside each tier-1 bank. A redundant execution endpoint is the Bank of China Taiwan branch or Hong Kong CHATS HKD clearing when the Taiwan counterparty cannot accept CNY directly; all outbound wires retain the documentary chain of invoice, contract, customs record where goods move, and payment proof.Onshore CNY forward foreign-exchange contracts (远期结售汇) with a licensed SAFE bank on genuine current-account trade or capital flows; requires the underlying contract, invoice, and the JV's risk-need statement filed quarterly.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) between CNY debt and USD or HKD funding legs, executed onshore under a China-recognised ISDA master agreement and reported to the PBC and SAFE where applicable.FX swaps (外汇掉期) for short-dated treasury re-alignment between the foreign-currency capital or settlement account and the RMB operating account, permissible under the JV's foreign-currency possession limit.Covered FX options and collars (外汇期权组合) purchased from the tier-1 relationship bank with physical settlement; premiums and payoff must remain proportional to the JV's underlying exposure.Offshore CNH NDFs are not permitted on the JV's balance sheet and cannot be booked through a mainland bank; the Taiwan parent may execute CNH NDFs at its own offshore treasury only, with no element of the derivative booked, guaranteed, or funded by the mainland JV.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: LOW for inbound capital injection and export-receipt collection; MEDIUM for outbound disbursements to overseas logistics subcontractors and IoT hardware vendors. Legal constraints: non-bank payment institutions and offshore EMIs (including Airwallex, Currenxie, Wise Business, Dukascopy, and similar virtual treasury rails) are not licensed by SAFE to open capital-account or foreign-currency settlement accounts for a mainland-registered JV. SAFE requires all trade-related collection and capital flows to pass through a bank holding a full foreign-exchange license. Taiwan-side fintech channels such as CTBC Bank corporate digital portals and Cathay United Bank treasury APIs can initiate MT103 instructions but cannot replace mainland OFAC/SAFE compliance. Practical deployment: maintain a small executive treasury wallet in an approved offshore EMI for paying foreign fleet contractors of not more than USD 10,000 per transaction, backed by an invoicing flow and replicated in the onshore accounting ledger; the JV must keep its primary legal treasury in licensed onshore banks.
