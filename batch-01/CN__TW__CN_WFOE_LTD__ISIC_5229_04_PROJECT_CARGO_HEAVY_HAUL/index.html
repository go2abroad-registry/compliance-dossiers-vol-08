Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (resident enterprise 居民企業 under Enterprise Income Tax Law Art. 2; worldwide CIT exposure at 25%; VAT general taxpayer (一般納稅人) by default because WFOEs are incorporated entities with annual sales expected above RMB 5 million; territorial VAT on PRC-sourced service supply; Taiwan business tax does not apply unless a Taiwan PE/fixed place of business is established).

Available election forms

Available elections: (1) VAT general taxpayer vs. small-scale taxpayer election — small-scale status applies only if annual taxable sales are below RMB 5 million; general taxpayer status is mandatory above the threshold. (2) Zero-rating vs. exemption election for cross-border taxable services (零稅率或免稅選擇) — international transport segments may elect zero-rating with input VAT refund offset under the 免抵退 method, while logistics auxiliary segments are statutorily exempt without refund. (3) Simplified CIT assessment (核定徵收) election is statutorily unavailable for WFOEs engaging in licensed/regulated logistics activities without reliable books. (4) Hybrid mismatch: China's CIT Law Art. 46 and the General Anti-Avoidance Rules (特別納稅調整) override hybrid arrangements; no elective 'check-the-box' regime exists; cross-border payment structures must pass the anti-hybrid substance test.

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

China: RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales (RMB 500万) above which general VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory; below this, small-scale VAT taxpayer status at 1%-3% levy. Taiwan: New Taiwan Dollar (TWD) 80,000 average monthly sales threshold for mandatory business tax registration; under TWD 80,000/month the provider is exempt; Taiwan 小規模營業人 pay a 1% deemed profit-based business tax up to TWD 200,000/month. Cross-border reverse-charge: Taiwan recipients of imported services self-assess 5% business tax regardless of threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

China zero-rating: (1) Exported goods — 0% VAT with refund of prior-stage input VAT; the WFOE must have actual export customs declarations for the OOG cargo. (2) Cross-border services — zero-rating applies only to international transport services (國際運輸服務) and eligible cross-border services specifically classified as zero-rated under the STA catalogue (財稅〔2016〕36號 Annex 4); for ISIC 5229 project-cargo heavy-haul logistics, the ocean/air international carriage element is zero-rated, whereas logistics auxiliary services (裝卸搬運、貨運代理、打捆加固、倉儲) are VAT-exempt (免稅) without refund entitlement. (3) Eligibility documentation requires the cross-border service contract, proof of service fully rendered to non-PRC recipients, and evidence the consideration is collected from non-PRC parties. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible on current account items (trade, services, dividends) subject to genuineness verification and reporting. Capital account items are administered under a positive-list regime; FDI equity injections and shareholder loans require SAFE registration and are confined to designated onshore FX accounts. Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is permissible after annual audit, tax filing, and bank verification, and is generally subject to withholding tax at 10% (reduced to 5% under the Cross-Strait tax cooperation arrangement where the Taiwanese corporate beneficial owner directly holds at least 25% of the WFOE and meets substance/anti-abuse conditions). Remittance can be executed only after payment of enterprise income tax and compliance with the foreign exchange 'round-trip investment' restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished mandatory export proceeds surrender; the statutory mandatory conversion ratio is zero. All export receipts must nonetheless be repatriated into a designated export receipts foreign exchange account via a SAFE-licensed bank. Under the SAFE Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring framework, ordinary export proceeds must be verified against customs declarations within 90 days of customs clearance; advance receipts must be registered with the bank as 'advance collection' if settlement occurs more than 90 days before export; delayed receipts beyond 90 days require a delayed-collection registration. Failure to repatriate or verify within those windows can trigger administrative penalties and suspension of current-account FX operations.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as central bank, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the dedicated foreign exchange regulator exercising delegated oversight.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for offshore digital EMI banking as a full substitute; an offshore EMI (e.g., Revolut or Airwallex-style platform) is not a licensed domestic settlement institution and cannot host onshore RMB/FCY accounts for the WFOE. Cross-border fintech payment companies may operate only as licensed third-party payment facilitators through a Chinese commercial bank or through a licensed cross-border RMB clearing partner, and they are not permitted to open capital-account FDI escrow accounts. Feasibility rating: Low on all capital-account flows; Medium only for ancillary low-value current-account collections.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires the Taiwan investor to first complete SAMR registration of the WFOE, obtain the Foreign Enterprise Investment Certificate / FDI filing, and open a dedicated RMB or foreign currency Capital Settlement Account (capital escrow account) at a SAFE-designated bank. The equity remittance is transmitted via SWIFT MT103 from the Taiwan bank to the WFOE's capital account using a recognized cross-border remittance purpose code (typically 'Foreign direct investment / equity injection'); the bank carries out pre-entry FDI registration, authenticates the board resolution and investment contract, and credits the funds to the escrow account. Funds may only be released into operational accounts after the bank verifies the capital verification report and actual business use; use of the funds must comply with the negative list for FDI, and no citizen guarantee or security is permitted for Taiwanese inbound investment without full SAFE security registration.Outbound disbursements for heavy-haul project cargo operations (freight carrier payments, port handling, equipment rental, subcontractor settlements, insurance) are executed primarily through onshore bank SWIFT MT103 wire transfers and, for import of capital equipment, through commercial Letters of Credit (L/C) issued by the WFOE's bank. Every outbound payment requires the presenting bank to verify the underlying commercial invoice, contract, customs declaration (for goods), transport evidence and, where relevant, withholding tax certificate. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory when annual related-party transactions with a party exceed RMB 40 million for services (the relevant limb for OOG haulage, charters and management fees); RMB 200 million for tangible property transfer; RMB 100 million for intangibles and RMB 100 million for financial assets. Master File: mandatory when group-wide consolidated global related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion and the Chinese entity has cross-border controlled transactions. Documentation is due within 5 months of the fiscal year end, simultaneously with the annual CIT return.

Economic substance scope: The PRC has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations as in BVI/UAE. Substance requirements operate through (i) the independent-transaction principle under EIT Law Art. 41 and its Implementation Rules; (ii) the Beneficial Ownership doctrine in SAT Announcement [2018] No. 9, which denies treaty relief to a recipient that lacks real personnel, premises, economic-decision functions and risk-bearing activity; (iii) the general anti-avoidance rule in EIT Law Art. 47; and (iv) the place-of-effective-management test that may relocate the tax status of a nominal Taiwanese entity. A 100% Taiwanese-owned WFOE must maintain actual personnel, payroll, risk-taking, equipment procurement contracts and operational control in Mainland China to retain its deductibility and its status as the source-country taxpayer.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (corporate income tax)

Statutory sanction: Under TAL Art. 63: recovery of the unpaid tax plus 0.05% daily surcharge; administrative penalty of 0.5-5 times the evaded tax; blacklisting on the serious-tax-default list; if the evaded amount exceeds 10% of taxable income and is 'relatively large', the company faces fines under Criminal Law Art. 201 and confiscation of assets.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, CFO and finance manager are criminally liable under Criminal Law Art. 201, with imprisonment up to 7 years for huge amounts; travel/exit bans per TAL Art. 44 until the tax is settled; civil liability and disqualification follow if the director signed false statutory accounts.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Missing Documentation

Statutory sanction: Transfer-pricing reassessment on an arm's length basis may impose additional CIT plus interest at the institutional benchmark rate plus 5 percentage points where Local File/Master File documentation is absent; administrative fines up to RMB 50,000 for failure to preserve documentation; a further 0.5x-5x fraud penalty if false evidence is submitted.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve subsidies of cross-border profit diversion to the Taiwanese parent breach fiduciary duty under Company Law Art. 192, exposing them to civil damages; in egregious cases of intentional fund outflows, bank account freezes and exit bans apply; the director could be treated as an accomplice to tax evasion with imprisonment.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Beneficial-ownership denial under SAT Announcement [2018] No. 9, reclassifying the Taiwanese recipient as taxable at the full 10% WHT; the mainland WFOE may be treated as a conduit, with revenue re-attributed to the true owner, resulting in CIT, VAT, fines of 0.5x-5x and possible forced deregistration; the enterprise is removed from the Local Tax Clearance certificate for outbound remittances.

Director personal liability: Under Company Law Art. 23 (2024 revision), the court may pierce the veil and hold the Taiwanese shareholder personally liable for all entity debts and tax arrears; the legal representative's personal ability to exit China is prohibited; if the shell is instrumentalised for evasion, prosecutorial action follows.
False Fapiao Issuance/Receipt (VAT Invoice Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Art. 205: for issuing or obtaining false special VAT invoices, the company is fined and the responsible personnel face imprisonment of 2 years up to life for 'extremely huge' amounts; a simultaneous CIT adjustment with 0.5x-5x penalty is imposed; the entity is added to the serious tax violation database and blocked from export rebates.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and CFO face direct criminal liability as the responsible persons of the unit crime, with aggravated sentences where the director ordered or endorsed the fapiao fraud; travel bans and 3-year disqualification from directorship are ancillary.
Anti-Money Laundering and Cross-Border Foreign Exchange Violations

Statutory sanction: AML Law fines up to RMB 5 million for an institution and RMB 500,000 for responsible individuals; foreign-exchange penalties of 0.5-1 times the illegal amount under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations; frozen accounts, mandatory repatriation and loss of banking facilities; criminal money-laundering liability under Criminal Law Art. 191 carries up to 10 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: The legal representative and compliance officer are personally fined, banned from being directors or senior managers for 3 years, subject to exit bans, and, in laundering cases, criminally prosecuted as the controlling person; civil liability for the entity's losses applies.
