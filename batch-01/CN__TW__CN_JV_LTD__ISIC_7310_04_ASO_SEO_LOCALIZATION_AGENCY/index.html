Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Taiwan has no general statutory minimum paid-up capital for a limited company. The capital must be sufficient for the proposed business and operating costs. Capital is normally deposited into a preparatory company bank account after name approval, followed by a Taiwan certified public accountant capital verification report. Foreign capital must be remitted through an approved banking channel and foreign investment approval must be obtained before subscription or capital injection.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Taiwan-incorporated Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture is a resident profit-seeking enterprise (營利事業) taxable at the entity level on worldwide taxable income at 20% corporate income tax (Income Tax Act Article 3), subject to the 12% Alternative Minimum Tax where applicable; no pass-through treatment is available to shareholders; undistributed retained earnings above statutory investment limits are subject to a 5% retained-earnings surtax; dividends to non-resident shareholders attract 21% withholding tax, and no consolidated/group taxation regime exists, so the JV is a separate taxpayer from its foreign parents and affiliates.

Available election forms

Taiwan does not administer a check-the-box or entity-classification election resembling IRC Section 7701. Relevant statutory elections for this JV include: (i) Income Tax Act Article 25 deemed-profit election, available to foreign enterprises without a Taiwan fixed place of business deriving Taiwan-source technical or service fees, under which taxable income is deemed at a Ministry-of-Finance-approved percentage (generally capped at 15%) of gross contract consideration in lieu of branch accounting; (ii) retained-earnings reinvestment election under Article 66-9 to defer the 5% undistributed-surplus surtax when earnings are reinvested within statutory timeframes; (iii) Alternative Minimum Tax 'basic tax' alternative-basis computation with AMT credit carryforward; (iv) unilateral foreign tax credit relief for PRC-source income; the Cross-Strait Agreement on the Avoidance of Double Taxation and Tax Cooperation (signed 2015) is not yet in force, so no treaty-based withholding relief is currently claimable.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Domestic sales threshold: full 5% VAT registration is mandatory when average monthly sales exceed NT$200,000; businesses with monthly sales between NT$80,000 and NT$200,000 qualify for the small-scale business tier (1% tax with quarterly returns); businesses with sales at or below NT$80,000 per month are exempt unless voluntary registration is sought. For foreign enterprises supplying cross-border electronic services (B2C digital marketing/SaaS-type services) to Taiwan consumers, VAT registration and collection are compulsory once annual turnover to Taiwan reaches NT$480,000. Genuine export supplies (goods or services) are zero-rated with no minimum turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under Business Tax Act Articles 7 and 8-1 for: (i) goods physically exported from the Taiwan customs territory supported by customs declarations; and (ii) services sold to foreign enterprises or foreign individuals where the services are wholly consumed or used outside Taiwan and the consideration is received in foreign currency. For this ASO/SEO localization agency, offshore digital marketing deliverables (app store optimization, multilingual organic search, and localization services) provided to non-resident app developers/publishers qualify as zero-rated exports of services if the contract designates offshore delivery, the counterparty has no Taiwan VAT registration, the primary benefit accrues outside Taiwan, and no taxable use occurs domestically. The zero-rated turnover must be reported in the zero-rate schedule of the VAT return; any local onboarding support or services supplied to Taiwan-based affiliates remain taxable at 5%. Input VAT attributable to zero-rated exports is refundable quarterly through the normal VAT refund application, generally processed within one month.

Taiwan mandates the Uniform Invoice (統一發票) system for all VAT-registered businesses. Electronic invoicing is effectively compulsory: B2C e-invoices must be transmitted to the National Taxation Bureau's E-Invoice Turnkey platform or an E-Invoice Value-Added Center via API within 48 hours of issuance, and B2B e-invoices within 7 calendar days; the platform performs near-real-time receipt and digital stamping, assigning a unique cloud-return identification. QR-code and mobile-carrier barcode integration are required for B2C e-invoices. Failure to transmit within the statutory window exposes the taxpayer to fines per occurrence of NT$1,500–NT$15,000 under Business Tax Act Article 48, escalating to suspension of business for continued violations.

Export zero-rating claims are a priority audit area for export-intensive service agencies. Taiwan does not prescribe a formal statutory grace period beyond operational benchmarks, but tax assessment practice expects foreign-currency consideration to be collected within 180 days of invoice issuance; longer deferrals require documented commercial justification or the zero-rated supply may be re-characterized as a domestic taxable supply. Supporting records (customs data, remittance watermarks, contracts, workpapers, and deployment evidence) must be retained for at least 5 years. Upon a failed substantiation, the tax authority retroactively reverses the zero rate, assesses 5% output VAT on the gross consideration, and imposes a fine of up to 5 times the underpaid tax under Business Tax Act Article 51, together with daily interest from the original due date; deliberate or fraudulent schemes attract aggravated penalties, sealing of accounting books, suspension of business, and criminal referral under Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act (imprisonment up to 5 years) and Article 62 of the Business Tax Act. The general assessment period is 5 years from filing, extended to 7 years where fraud is proven. The authority operates a dedicated zero-rating cross-check program that reconciles customs declaration records, central-bank foreign-exchange settlement data, and business tax returns to detect fictitious exports or exchange manipulations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

TWD is convertible but not freely internationalized; convertibility is administered through authorized dealer banks. Current-account flows (non-merchandise trade such as ASO/SEO localization services, advertising procurement, SaaS licensing, and contractor fees) are freely convertible upon declaration; each foreign-exchange settlement equivalent to NT$500,000 or above requires an individual declaration (結匯申報書) filed by the settling bank with the CBC under the applicable transaction-nature code. Capital-account flows are restricted: paid-in capital injection from mainland shareholders requires a prior MOEA Investment Commission permit, bank processing through a declared equity-investment purpose code, and China-side ODI foreign-exchange registration by the mainland parent, while dividend and profit repatriation to the mainland shareholder requires audited profit evidence, CBC-filed investment-income remittance documentation, and non-resident dividend withholding in the absence of an effective cross-strait tax agreement. Practical hurdles include the prohibition on using fintech EMI rails (Wise/Payoneer) for capital-account remittances, the unavailability of offshore TWD settlement, and the requirement that all cross-strait remittances be supported by genuine trade or investment documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan does not impose a fixed mandatory conversion ratio for export or service-export proceeds; proceeds may be retained in a foreign-currency deposit account or voluntarily converted into TWD. The statutory discipline is repatriation: goods-export proceeds must be received within four months of customs export release, and service-export proceeds (including ASO/SEO agency fees) must be credited to the JV's designated foreign-currency account in accordance with the payment schedule of the signed service contract; overdue proceeds require a written extension application to the authorized dealer bank with CBC filing. The shipper/service provider must declare the transaction nature at settlement and retain service agreements, invoices, acceptance reports, App Store/Google performance reports, and settlement confirmations for at least five years for CBC verification; no surrender ratio applies to retained USD, EUR, or RMB balances in the JV's foreign-currency deposit account.

Central bank supervision: The Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (the CBC / 中央銀行), exercising foreign-exchange administration under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (管理外匯條例) and the CBC's Regulations Governing the Declaration of Foreign Exchange Receipts and Disbursements or Transactions; banking supervision is vested in the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC / 金融監督管理委員會), whereas approval of mainland Chinese equity investment is vested in the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA / 經濟部投資審議委員會) under the Act for Relations between the People of the Taiwan Area and the Mainland Area (臺灣地區與大陸地區人民關係條例). The CBC operates a managed-float regime for the New Taiwan Dollar (TWD), with all authorized dealer banks required to transmit settlement data through the CBC's foreign-currency data reporting system.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Conditionally viable (B+ feasibility rating). Wise Business and Payoneer may be onboarded as complementary collection and payout rails for small-value overseas client receipts (App Store optimization retainers, Google Ads fee pass-throughs, freelancer localization invoices) and for contractor disbursements under USD 10,000 per transaction, provided the balances are swept to the primary domestic bank at least weekly. Legal constraints: neither provider is a licensed bank in Taiwan; they operate as payment/E-Money service providers through local partner-bank clearing, and the JV's domestic bank will require disclosure of these channels as documented collection sources. EMI rails cannot carry capital-account flows, cannot substitute for SWIFT-backed service-export settlement evidence for CBC declaration and tax filing, and must never be used to remit dividends back to the mainland parent, as China's SAFE requires such inbound investment receipts to be settled through regulated bank channels under the parent's ODI registration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Precondition chain: (i) the mainland parent completes China-outbound investment approvals (NDRC filing, MOFCOM or provincial commerce authority certificate, and SAFE foreign-exchange registration) and (ii) the Taiwan JV obtains the MOEA Investment Commission permit to accept mainland investment. Execution: SWIFT MT103 remittance from the mainland parent's own bank account, denominated in USD or in CNY through the Taipei RMB clearing mechanism operated by Bank of China's Taipei branch, to the JV's newly opened capital escrow account at the Taiwan deposit bank; the receiving bank files the CBC declaration under the equity-investment transaction-nature code (inward investment in an onshore enterprise) and holds funds in escrow. Release: after MOEA registration of the paid-in capital and delivery of the accountant's capital verification report to the bank, funds are re-designated to the operational NT$/USD account and become deployable working capital. Compliance safeguards: no third-party remittance is accepted as shareholder capital; funds must arrive in disclosed tranches matching the approved investment amount; the mainland parent must evidence the lawful exit of foreign exchange from China; any subsequent capital increase or shareholder loan requires a fresh Investment Commission approval and CBC-filed declaration.Primary rail: SWIFT MT103 from the JV's NT$/USD operational account for vendor payments above USD 10,000 equivalent, covering Apple Search Ads, Google Ads, Meta, enterprise SaaS subscriptions, and overseas localization subcontractors; each transfer is backed by a contract/invoice and a CBC service-import declaration.
