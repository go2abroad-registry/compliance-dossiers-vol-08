Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Taxation Agency, Taiwan (財政部賦稅署); principal operating contact is the local National Taxation Bureau of the seat of incorporation. Primary portals: MOF Tax Administration Portal (https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw) and E-Invoice Integration Platform (https://www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

For a newly incorporated Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture: (1) complete company/branch registration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs and obtain the company registration number and corporate identification code; (2) within 15 days after incorporation, file for tax registration at the competent National Taxation Bureau pursuant to Article 28 of the Business Tax Act; (3) receive the Unified Business Number (統一編號) and, if taxable, the VAT/Business Tax registration certificate; (4) where electronic invoices will be issued, register for the E-Invoice Integration Platform and obtain a Turnkey/API credential; (5) if goods are to be exported, register separately with Customs as an exporter/importer; (6) if paying foreign royalties or non-resident service fees, register as a withholding taxpayer and obtain the relevant withholding rate approvals.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; resident Taiwan-incorporated JV is taxed on worldwide profit-seeking income under the Income Tax Act, at 20% corporate income tax, plus a 5% surtax on undistributed retained earnings; the JV is not a pass-through vehicle. A branch of a foreign entity remains separately classified as a non-resident branch, subject to 20% income tax plus 15% branch-profits remittance tax if profits are remitted out of Taiwan, unless reduced by a tax treaty.

Available election forms

Taiwan does not provide a US-style check-the-box election; tax classification follows legal entity form under the Company Act. Relevant elections include: financial-holding-company consolidated filing (only for qualified financial holding groups); election to use functional currency for tax accounts; election to calculate export zero-rated VAT refund without physical retail invoices; election to use the simplified 1% rate applicable to small-scale business taxpayers (only if monthly sales remain below the threshold); and formal Advance Pricing Agreement with the tax authority for cross-border royalty flows. Hybrid mismatch elections are not generally recognized; Taiwan has not adopted comprehensive hybrid mismatch legislation, but anti-avoidance principles under Article 43-1 of the Income Tax Act and the Corporate Income Tax Anti-Tax Avoidance Rules may recharacterize abusive royalty arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

For a licensed corporate entity, business tax registration is mandatory before business commencement. Small-scale business operators with monthly taxable sales below NT$80,000 for goods or NT$40,000 for services may apply for registration exemption; foreign cross-border electronic service suppliers must register for simplified business tax when annual Taiwan-sourced digital services revenue exceeds NT$480,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act for: (i) exports of goods accompanied by customs outward declarations; (ii) domestic services or intellectual-property licenses provided to, or used by, foreign enterprises or individuals outside Taiwan, including royalty income under a patent/trademark sub-licensing arrangement where the licensed IP is exploited abroad; and (iii) services in connection with exported goods. For the IP HoldCo, the licence agreement must demonstrate that the licensee is a foreign entity, the licensed territory and use are outside Taiwan, and the consideration is received in foreign currency; the VAT on input purchases attributable to the zero-rated export services is then creditable or refundable.

  • Uniform Invoice issued as an export zero-rated invoice (外銷零稅率發票/中英文OPT invoice)
  • Patent/trademark licence agreement and sub-licence chain evidencing foreign beneficial use and territory
  • Foreign exchange receipt or inward remittance certificate (央行外匯水單/賣匯證明) evidencing collection of royalty proceeds
  • Customs export declaration (出口報單) and bill of lading / airway bill — required only where patented goods, prototypes, or media are physically exported

Taiwan mandates the issuance of Uniform Invoices (統一發票) by all VAT-registered business entities. Large and designated business entities must issue cloud e-invoices through the Ministry of Finance E-Invoice Integration Platform, with approved software or Turnkey API transmission to the fiscal authority. There is no general real-time fiscal-stamp requirement for each invoice, but electronic invoices must be transmitted to the platform within the prescribed filing cycle, and every Business Tax return must be filed every two months along with the summary uniform-invoice data. Export zero-rated sales must be reported separately and substantiated on the return; failure to file accurately triggers penalty review.

The National Taxation Bureau cross-checks zero-rating claims against customs export data, banking foreign-exchange settlement records, and e-invoice filing data. If an export is recharacterized as a domestic taxable supply, the 5% VAT is assessed retrospectively with interest, and under Article 51 of the Business Tax Act, a penalty of 1 to 5 times the evaded tax may be imposed. Fabricating customs declarations, remittance certificates, or licence agreements triggers criminal liability under the Tax Evasion Punishment Act, and for deliberate evasion the statutory assessment period is 7 years; immediate VAT refunds are clawed back with interest before any penalty decision.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible for genuine current-account service transactions, including IP licensing royalties, but all conversions must pass through a designated foreign-exchange bank's authenticity and legality review. Capital-account items, such as the initial FDI equity injection, intercompany loans and post-investment liquidation proceeds, remain subject to SAFE registration or approval and cannot be freely swapped or settled without the required regulatory evidence. Profit remittance by the JV to the Taiwan shareholder is operationally treated as a current-account 'investment income' payment but carries meaningful hurdles: the JV must complete enterprise income tax final settlement, present audited statutory financial statements showing accumulated distributable profits, pass a board resolution for the profit distribution, and provide a valid Taiwan tax-residency certificate if treaty/tax-agreement relief is claimed; absent that certificate a 10% dividend withholding tax will apply. Onshore TWD/CNY conversion has no liquid direct market, so cross-currency flows are normally settled through USD or via offshore CNH/TWD platforms at the Taiwan treasury level.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-receipt surrender obligation or mandatory conversion ratio in China. The JV may retain 100% of export and service-trade proceeds in foreign currency in its domestic current-account FX account, or convert them into RMB onshore at market rates. All receipts must nevertheless be repatriated through an SAFE-licensed bank and declared in the Balance of Payments / trade monitoring system. Receipts classified as trade-in-goods collections must be received within the contractual trade term; advance receipts and abnormally deferred receipts must be flagged in the SAFE digital monitoring platform to avoid becoming a 'focus verification enterprise'. For IP licensing royalties, export verification is replaced by concurrent documentary matching of agreements, invoices, tax filings and beneficial-ownership evidence at the bank; no fixed percentage of incoming FX must be converted into RMB.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC); for the Taiwan counterparty, the central monetary authority of Taiwan (Central Bank of China, Taiwan, 'CBC') and the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: NT$100 million aggregate related-party transactions in one tax year triggers a full Local File; NT$500 million aggregate controlled transactions additionally requires a Master File. A simplified Local File is accepted when total controlled transactions are between NT$50 million and NT$100 million. CbC reports are required for MNE groups with consolidated revenue exceeding NT$270 billion.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not apply OECD-style Economic Substance Regulations, but its GAAR, CFC and PEM rules impose a substance-over-form test. A Taiwan IP licensing holding company must have real employees, a physical office, independent board authority, complete books, and active IP management functions; otherwise the tax authority may disregard the entity, deny the royalty deduction, and attribute income to the ultimate principals.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty of the short-paid tax of one to three times under ITA Art. 110; criminal liability under Tax Collection Act Art. 41 with imprisonment up to five years and fines up to NT$1,000,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or direct the false return are jointly and severally liable for the deficiency, subject to civil restitution, asset attachment, travel bans and imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Abuse

Statutory sanction: Reallocation of income under ITA Art. 43-1; denial of the royalty deduction; statutory 20% WHT on the disallowed payment; penalty of up to twice the additional tax; criminal prosecution where deliberate.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorize the sham pricing can be personally liable for the additional tax and penalties, subject to civil suit and travel bans.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the entity; denial of intra-group deductions; 20% withholding tax on deemed dividends; registration revocation under the Company Act; cumulative late-filing and underpayment penalties up to 30% plus twice the tax, as applicable.
