Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is not acceptable as the sole statutory registered office for a corporate-secretarial and registered-agent business in Taiwan. The tax authority, bank account-opening teams, and AML supervisors reject standalone virtual addresses because the ISIC 8299 business is considered a medium-risk shell-company enabler and must demonstrate physical management, accessible records, identifiable persons, and a genuine employment or agent presence. A serviced office with an actual lease, signboard, meeting space, local correspondence handling, and on-site personnel is the minimum compliant arrangement. A mailbox may be used only as a secondary correspondence address.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) with statutory delegation to the local Municipal/District Market Supervision and Administration Bureau (MSA); filings are accepted at the district-level one-stop administrative service center or through the national 'Market Entity Registration Online Platform'.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 revision, effective 1 July 2024), Chapter II Arts. 42-101; Foreign Investment Law of the PRC (2020), Arts. 5, 22 and 29; Implementing Regulations of the Foreign Investment Law (2020), Chapter III; Regulation on the Administration of Market Entity Registration (State Council Decree No. 746), Arts. 8-19; Measures for the Implementation of Company Registration in Foreign-Invested Enterprises; Law of the PRC on Protecting Taiwan Compatriots' Investment and its Implementing Rules.

Formation / constitutional filings

Official company registration package comprising the Application for Market Entity Registration (公司登记（备案）申请书), the Articles of Association, and the Joint Venture Contract, culminating in the issuance of the Business License (营业执照) bearing the Unified Social Credit Code.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum registered capital for a general service-sector Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture under the 2020 Foreign Investment Law; the 2024 Company Law requires shareholders to pay the subscribed capital in full within five years of incorporation. No national escrow account is mandated, but a SAFE-monitored FDI capital base account must be opened with a designated bank, and the local MSA practises an expectation of at least RMB 100,000 paid-in capital to support the corporate-agency and secretarial licensing scope.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety record filing at the local Fire and Rescue Division (消防救援机构) under GB 50016-2014, including a simple fire declaration for office floor areas below 300 m², fire-extinguisher placement, and evacuation signage; building occupancy clearance from the local housing authority confirming lawful commercial use via the structural completion acceptance record (建筑工程竣工验收备案) and the load-capacity certificate of the leased floor; occupational workplace clearance under the Work Safety Law, Articles 42 and 98, comprising a first-aid kit, emergency exit audit, and annual declaration through the national workplace-safety reporting system.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (財政部, MOF) acting through the Directorate-General of Taxation and the territorial National Taxation Bureaus (各地區國稅局); central taxpayer portal: MOF Tax Entry Portal (財政部稅務入口網, eiv.etax.nat.gov.tw); e-invoicing portal: E-Invoice Integration Service Platform (電子發票整合服務平台, www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

For a new Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture: (1) obtain foreign-investment approval from the Investment Review Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (投審會) under the Statute for Investment by Foreign Nationals; (2) obtain company-name pre-check and file incorporation with the MOEA Central Region Integration Service Center through the One-Stop Company and Commerce Online Application system; (3) the National Taxation Bureau automatically assigns the 8-digit Unified Invoice Number (統一編號, 'GUI number') as the statutory TIN/VAT registration identifier through the integrated registration channel; (4) complete tax domicile registration (稅籍登記/營業登記), income-tax withholding registration, and business tax (VAT) registration with the local National Taxation Bureau; (5) activate e-invoicing on the E-Invoice Integration Service Platform and select the Turnkey/API transmission channel. Fee: none for TIN issuance; legalization by a Taiwan court-affiliated notary or an overseas representative office may be required for the investor's corporate certificates.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box entity classification regime exists. Statutory elections and special rules available: (i) consolidated C.I.T. filing election for a domestic parent holding at least 90% direct/indirect equity in its Taiwan subsidiaries for 12 consecutive months (Income Tax Act Art. 39-1, effective tax year 2023); (ii) hybrid-mismatch deduction disallowance for payments involving dual-deduction or deduction-without-inclusion structures (Income Tax Act Art. 43-4, effective tax year 2024); (iii) CFC inclusion rules for controlled foreign companies; (iv) pre-approved non-calendar fiscal-year election; (v) small-scale simplified VAT assessment election for monthly turnover ≤ TWD 200,000; and (vi) for MNE groups with group revenue ≥ EUR 750 million, the global minimum top-up tax / IIR and QDMTT regime applies to Taiwan entities from 2025-2026. No pass-through election is available for corporate JVs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover-based exemption: every business engaged in taxable activity must apply for VAT and tax domicile registration before commencing business. Small-scale businesses (小規模營業人) with average monthly turnover of TWD 200,000 or less may be assessed at the special 1% rate with simplified quarterly filing; full standard registration, 5% output tax, and bi-monthly filing are mandatory once average monthly turnover exceeds TWD 200,000. Foreign e-commerce platforms and vendors making B2C digital services into Taiwan must register via the simplified online foreign-unit platform.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Business Tax Act Art. 7 zero-rating applies to: (i) exports of goods conducted through approved customs export procedures; (ii) goods sold for export with foreign-currency consideration; and (iii) services rendered to foreign enterprises or individuals for international transport or services provided to foreign clients and not used within Taiwan — this includes offshore corporate-secretarial, registered-agent, and compliance-administration services performed for non-resident clients, provided the benefit of the services is localized entirely outside Taiwan and consideration is received in foreign currency. Zero-rating is claimed period-by-period in the bi-monthly VAT return (filed by the 15th of the following month) with full substantiation; excess input VAT attributable to zero-rated supplies is refundable and is not subject to the monthly TWD 500,000 rolling-credit ceiling.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) with verified shipment evidence
  • Commercial invoice in triplicate form (三聯式統一發票) tied to e-invoice platform records
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for physical goods)
  • Foreign-currency inward remittance certificate (出口結匯水單) or bank FX receipt statement evidencing collection from the foreign buyer
  • Executed service agreement specifying offshore performance and non-Taiwan use (for services)
  • Proof of the counterparty's non-resident status (e.g., overseas registration certificate or tax residence certificate)
  • E-Invoice Integration Service Platform output record matching the declared zero-rated transaction
  • Intercompany or agency documentation proving the JV performed the substantive secretarial and registered-agent functions (economic substance evidence)

Mandatory e-invoicing: all VAT-registered businesses must issue uniform invoices through the MOF E-Invoice Integration Service Platform and transmit invoice records to the National Taxation Bureau electronically. Transmission deadlines: generally within 48 hours of issuance; businesses with annual sales ≥ TWD 300,000,000 must transmit in near real-time (next business day) via Turnkey or web-service API. Required data fields include the seller's GUI number, buyer GUI number where applicable, item codes, taxable amounts, VAT amounts, and the auto-generated random-code digital stamp (隨機碼). Paper unified invoices remain lawful only for qualifying small-scale micro-businesses. The e-invoice platform record is the authoritative root file for zero-rated export reporting and input-credit refunds.

The National Taxation Bureau may retroactively reassess zero-rating claims within the 7-year assessment period (核課期間) for non-reported or understated transactions and 5 years for fully reported transactions (Tax Collection Act Art. 21). If zero-rating is not substantiated, output VAT is restated at 5% with daily late interest from the original due date, input credits previously refunded are clawed back with interest, and an additional penalty of 5 to 7.5 times the evaded tax applies for fraudulent or willfully misstated claims (Business Tax Act Art. 51). Willful tax evasion by the responsible person carries criminal liability of imprisonment up to 5 years and/or a fine (Tax Collection Act Art. 41). Where export structures involve paper shell companies, sham transactions, or round-tripped funds, the tax authorities apply substance-over-form recharacterization, deny the zero-rating, and pursue joint liability against directors and ultimate shareholders.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Current account: TWD is fully convertible for bona fide service-trade receipts and disbursements under the Foreign Exchange Control Act (管理外匯條例), with Authorized Banks verifying underlying contracts, tax invoices, and CBC Remittance Classification Codes. Capital account: convertibility is materially restricted for mainland-origin investors under the Regulations for Permission of Investment in Taiwan by Mainland Area People; capital injection, equity acquisition, and increase of paid-in capital require prior approval from the MOEA Investment Review Department, and proceeds must be converted and settled only through the appointed Authorized Bank with CBC balance-of-payments reporting. Profit-remittance hurdles: dividend or fee remittances to the Chinese parent require (i) audited Taiwan financial statements and tax clearance; (ii) 20% withholding tax on dividends, interest, royalties, and reportable service fees paid to the mainland corporate shareholder under Article 25 of the Act Governing Relations between the People of the Taiwan Area and the Mainland Area; (iii) registration and verification at the Chinese parent's SAFE-located bank before the inbound dividend can be credited, including proof of source funds and the underlying Taiwan outbound remittance advice. PRC current-account convertibility is unrestricted for cross-border service-trade fee payments, but the Taiwan party must present a service contract, commercial invoice, and proof of tax payment to the remitting bank to pass SAFE bank-level authenticity review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan: no statutory surrender ratio or mandatory conversion applies. Export and cross-border service-export receivables must be collected into the Taiwan entity's foreign-currency deposit account at an Authorized Bank within the commercial settlement period agreed in the underlying contract (bank-enforced at no later than 180 days unless extended), declared to the CBC through the bank upon receipt, and may thereafter be retained 100% in original currency or converted to TWD voluntarily at market rates. Mainland parent: SAFE imposes no mandatory conversion ratio since the 2012 settlement reform; however, goods-trade proceeds must be repatriated to China within 90 days of customs declaration, service-trade proceeds must be repatriated 'in a timely manner', every receipt must clear the Digital Foreign Exchange Registry, and the payment must be classified under the applicable PRC international-balance-of-payments service code for legal, accounting, and management consultancy services.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC, 中央銀行) as the foreign-exchange and clearing-settlement authority in the Taiwan jurisdiction, supervised in banking conduct by the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC); counterpart mainland authority: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, which governs the PRC EJV shareholder's outbound investment registration, capital remittance, and repatriation of profits.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility with material legal constraints. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, Revolut Business) are not licensed under the Taiwan Banking Act, cannot hold TWD deposits, provide no Taiwan-located account number, and cannot act as the CBC-designated Authorized Bank for remittance classification, mainland-investment escrow, or tax withholding. They may be deployed only as auxiliary collection channels for small-value fees from offshore clients after the local core banking relationship is active, subject to FSC rules on payment services and to Taiwan Personal Data Protection Act restrictions on transferring client beneficial-owner data to the PRC parent or third-country processors.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% (applies to taxable income exceeding NT$120,000; the first NT$120,000 is taxed at 50% of the taxable amount, equivalent to a maximum of NT$60,000 on that bracket; effective standard rate on commercial-scale profits is 20%)

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Form 1, Section A – Company Identification (納稅義務人名稱及統一編號欄) Legal name of the Taiwan profit-seeking enterprise, unified business number (統一編號), industry code, registered address, and responsible-person identification. The JV must be registered as a Taiwan tax resident entity; the mainland Chinese parent must not be listed as the taxpayer. The responsible person (managing director) must sign and certify; a mainland director should be identified by passport and Taiwan-resident certificate if applicable. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Line 01 – Gross Operating Revenue (營業收入總額) Total invoiced consideration for company-secretarial, registered-office, nominee-director, offshore-administration, and ancillary consulting services; reconciled to the VAT uniform-invoice monthly filing (401 form). Recognize revenue on an accrual basis and issue uniform invoices for all fees, including those invoiced to the mainland parent. Service fees paid by the mainland parent must be benchmarked at arm's length; mischaracterization as royalties would expose the income to a 20% royalty withholding characterization issue. Omission of cash or wire fees constitutes fraudulent understatement. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Line 02 – Operating Costs (營業成本) Direct costs including outsourced secretarial filings, statutory agent fees paid to external law firms, licensing fees, and direct staff assigned to client engagements. Deductible only with supporting vouchers (contracts, invoices, bank-debit evidence); payments to the mainland parent for subcontracted services must satisfy the benefit test and TP documentation. Nondeductible payments to shell suppliers will be added back. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Line 05 – Operating Expenses (營業費用) Deductible expenses: wages, rent, electricity, telecommunications, insurance, depreciation, professional indemnity premiums, and tax-exempt penalties (government fines are nondeductible). Salaries of expatriate directors/staff are deductible only if employment services are actually rendered in Taiwan and monthly withholding tax (6%/18%) has been remitted; expenses paid to related parties abroad require an arm's-length benchmark and economic-substance justification. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Line 33 / 53 – Operating Net Profit and Non-Operating Income (營業淨利及營業外收支) Reconcile gross profit minus expenses to operating profit; then add non-operating items: bank deposit interest, FX gains/losses, and penalty income. Interest income from Taiwanese bank deposits is subject to 15% final withholding and is creditable against CIT; loan interest receivable from the mainland parent must be priced at arm's length and reported as taxable interest income. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Schedule 13 (帳外調整) and Loss Carryforward Schedule – Taxable Income (課稅所得額) Compute taxable income after statutory book-tax differences, nondeductible expenses, exemptions, and offset of net operating losses (NOL) carried forward. NOL may offset up to 10 years. A JV whose mainland parent conducts related-party transactions must disclose book-tax differences; failure to maintain accounting books triggers deemed assessment under the same-industry profit standard, which for secretarial services is materially disadvantageous. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Line – Tax Payable Computation (應納稅額) and AMT Comparison Apply the progressive bracket (50% of first NT$120,000; 20% of the excess), then subtract statutory credits (R&D, foreign tax credits, investment credits) and compare with the 12% AMT. The mainland parent cannot claim the JV's credits or losses through flow-through; no foreign tax credit is available to the Taiwanese entity on profits already taxed in mainland China. If the computed tax is below the AMT floor, the AMT difference is payable. Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
Schedule on Undistributed Surplus (未分配盈餘申報書) Compute the 5% surtax on undistributed retained earnings in excess of legally distributable surplus, after offsetting deficits and statutory reserves. If the JV resolves to distribute dividends to the mainland parent before the filing date, the retained-earnings surtax base is reduced; dividend distribution is then subject to the 21% non-resident withholding (see treaty-relief protocol for possible 10%/5% reduction, subject to implementation caveats). Late filing: a delinquency penalty equal to 3% of the assessed tax for each month of delay, capped at 30%, plus interest at the postal savings one-year fixed rate; if the return is filed but tax is understated, a penalty of 2x the underpaid tax (2–3x in cases of fraud) plus interest; failure to file at all triggers ex-officio assessment by the tax authority with compulsory execution against corporate and responsible-person assets.
