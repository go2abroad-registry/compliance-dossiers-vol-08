Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Taiwan has no general statutory minimum paid-up capital for a company limited by shares or company limited by shares wholly owned by foreign investors. The subscribed capital must be sufficient for the proposed business, and a Taiwan-certified public accountant must verify the capital contribution after remittance. Capital is normally remitted into a designated bank account and converted into Taiwan dollars; there is no universal statutory escrow requirement, but the bank account and remittance evidence must be maintained for capital verification and foreign-investment reporting. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, 'SAT'), headquartered in Beijing; administrative supervision is exercised by the municipal SAT branch at the WFOE's registered address. All registrations, filings, invoice clearance and export refund functions are executed through the SAT Electronic Tax Bureau portal ('电子税务局', https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn), the national VAT invoice service platform, and the Golden Tax IV ('金税四期') system.

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the WFOE at the Municipal Market Supervision Administration (市场监督管理局) to obtain the business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code ('统一社会信用代码'), which simultaneously becomes the statutory tax registration number via SAT-SAMR data linkage; tax registration is deemed completed upon license issuance, with a 30-day supplementary filing grace period. (2) File taxpayer commencement and tax-type determination (税种认定) through the E-Tax Bureau to fix the applicable VAT taxpayer type (General or Small-Scale), Enterprise Income Tax, urban maintenance and education surcharges, stamp duty, and payroll withholding taxes. (3) Register the legal representative, financial officer and tax agent; bind the corporate digital certificate; procure the e-Tax UKey (税务UKey); and activate fully digitalized electronic invoicing (数电票). (4) For export operations: complete the MOFCOM foreign-trade operator filing (对外贸易经营者备案), customs exporter registration (海关进出口收发货人备案), NMPA medical-device export recordation, and bank goods-trade FX account settlement. (5) File the export tax refund qualification ('出口退税备案') and designate the 'exemption-credit-refund' (免抵退) method applicable to manufacturing exporters.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (worldwide-income resident enterprise; no pass-through eligibility). The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise ('居民企业') taxed at the all-in 25% Enterprise Income Tax rate on worldwide income; it is a separate taxable person distinct from its foreign shareholders and cannot consolidate with them. Dividends remitted to a Taiwan-resident shareholder attract 10% withholding, potentially reduced to 5% only where the Cross-Strait Tax Cooperation Agreement applies and Taiwan-resident beneficial ownership is substantiated. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (worldwide-income resident enterprise; no pass-through eligibility). The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise ('居民企业') taxed at the all-in 25% Enterprise Income Tax rate on worldwide income; it is a separate taxable person distinct from its foreign shareholders and cannot consolidate with them. Dividends remitted to a Taiwan-resident shareholder attract 10% withholding, potentially reduced to 5% only where the Cross-Strait Tax Cooperation Agreement applies and Taiwan-resident beneficial ownership is substantiated. Indirectly, the WFOE is a VAT taxpayer, customs duty payer, urban surcharge taxpayer and stamp duty taxpayer. No tax-exempt-zone entity status attaches merely from location in a bonded zone; any zone-based preferences operate at transactional level only.

Available election forms

(a) VAT status election: annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 compel General VAT Taxpayer status; smaller enterprises may elect General status voluntarily or remain Small-Scale Taxpayers (3% levy with periodic preferential reductions). (b) Export method: manufacturing WFOEs are statutorily bound to the 'exemption-credit-refund' (免抵退) formula; no election exists to use the trading-company 'exemption-refund' (免退) method unless the entity is restructured as a trading company. (c) EIT planning: no check-the-box regime in the U.S. sense; available elections are limited to special tax treatment of qualifying asset reorganizations, statutory 5-year EIT loss carryforward utilization, and Advanced Pricing Arrangements for related-party export pricing; hybrid instrument and beneficial-ownership positions are governed by the General Anti-Avoidance Rule and filing disclosures. (d) Small-scale taxpayers may elect to waive statutory VAT exemptions in order to issue special VAT invoices deductible by customers.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Every WFOE with a business license is automatically VAT-registered; China imposes no economic-turnover threshold permitting exemption from registration. The operative threshold is the General versus Small-Scale taxpayer status line: annual taxable sales exceeding RMB 5,000,000 (approximately USD 690,000) compel General VAT Taxpayer registration; below this line the entity defaults to Small-Scale Taxpayer status (3% levy) unless it elects General status. For the titanium implant manufacturer, General Taxpayer status is functionally indispensable because only General Taxpayers may claim input VAT credit and export refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% output VAT with credit and refund of input VAT) is available only where all of the following criteria are satisfied: (i) Exporter status — the WFOE holds a valid foreign-trade operator filing, customs exporter registration, NMPA Medical Device Production License (医疗器械生产许可证), and export recordation for the porous titanium hip implant classified under HS 9021.31; (ii) Physical export — the goods are declared at a PRC customs port and leave the customs territory of the People's Republic of China for Taiwan or another destination; Taiwan is treated by PRC customs as a separate customs territory, so a full mainland export clearance is mandatory; the customs declaration names the WFOE as consignor and states the true FOB value; (iii) Genuine foreign sale — a valid export contract, commercial invoice and packing list with an overseas or Taiwanese purchaser, identical in quantity, unit price, product description and value; (iv) FX repatriation — export proceeds are received through a designated bank and reported under the goods-trade Foreign Exchange monitoring system; (v) Input VAT chain — the WFOE maintains compliant special VAT invoices for titanium alloy powder feedstock, electron-beam 3D-printing consumables, electricity and production equipment so that the refund is confined to VAT economically borne on the exported production; (vi) Timely filing — the monthly 'exemption-credit-refund' application is filed within the statutory period following the month of customs export; separate cross-border-service zero-rating rules apply to any offshore engineering or consulting services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口货物报关单) — electronic customs record with HS 9021.31 classification, FOB value and export port, naming the WFOE as exporter
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (海运提单/空运单) — third-party transport document evidencing that the goods left PRC customs territory and were destined for the Taiwanese buyer
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Income Declaration (出口收汇确认凭证/涉外收入申报单) — bank confirmation that export proceeds were received and settled through the goods-trade FX monitoring system
  • Commercial Invoice, Packing List and Export Contract (商业发票、装箱单、外销合同) — complete product description, unit prices and export terms reconciling with the customs declared value
  • Medical Device Production License and Export Recordation File (医疗器械生产许可证及出口备案) — NMPA-issued documentary proof of lawful manufacture and market authorization for the titanium hip implant
  • Certificate of Origin and cross-strait trade documentation (原产地证书及两岸贸易单证) — required where Taiwan customs or the buyer-side certification demands origin evidence

Mandatory real-time fiscal clearance under the fully digitalized e-invoice (数电票) and Golden Tax IV regime: every VAT invoice is issued through the E-Tax Bureau using the taxpayer's digital certificate; the national platform validates each invoice block online, assigns a 26-digit anti-counterfeit code, and records it instantaneously for the buyer's input-credit eligibility. Physical stamping is not required for fully digitalized invoices; paper-plus-seal invoices survive only in transitional scenarios. Large taxpayers may apply for the '乐企' direct-connection API integrating SAT clearance inside the enterprise ERP; all other taxpayers use the web portal. The system performs real-time triangulation of invoice flow, payment flow, logistics flow and, for exports, customs declaration data; any anomaly automatically suspends invoice quotas or export refund processing.

Export refund claims are machine-audited by the 'four-flow matching' (四流合一) engine reconciling contract flow, goods flow, invoice flow and payment flow; any mismatch triggers manual inspection or on-site audit. Legal consequences for fraudulent zero-rating or over-refund include: (i) clawback — SAT orders repayment of the entire refunded VAT plus a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; (ii) monetary penalty — 50% to 5 times the fraudulent refund amount under Articles 63 and 66 of the Tax Administration Law; (iii) unlimited recovery period — the ordinary 3-to-5-year statute of limitation does not apply to fraud or evasion, permitting indefinite retrospective clawback; (iv) criminal liability — Article 204 of the Criminal Law punishes fraudulent export tax refund with imprisonment and fines; (v) administrative sanctions — suspension or cancellation of the export tax refund qualification, revocation of foreign-trade operator status, inclusion in the national tax blacklist with consequential restrictions in banking, credit and customs AEO treatment; (vi) FX penalties — failure to repatriate proceeds within 180 days without a SAFE-approved exception attracts fines of 0.5% to 30% of the unrepatriated amount and retrospective denial of refund claims; (vii) EIT reassessment — if export prices are recharacterized as related-party transfer pricing misstatements, additional Enterprise Income Tax, interest and penalties are superimposed on the VAT clawback.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible for current-account items (goods trade, services trade, wages, and qualifying profit/dividend remittances after tax settlement) pursuant to China's IMF Article VIII obligations, but every receipt or payment is subject to genuine-transaction verification (真实性审核) by a designated FX bank. Capital-account items remain selectively convertible: inbound FDI is liberalized and requires only post-establishment FDI registration (delegated to banks) plus compliance with the Foreign Investment Negative List (2023 edition), whereas outbound portfolio investment, cross-border guarantees, intra-group on-lending, and securities transactions remain quota- or approval-gated. Profit remittance to the Taiwan parent is a current-account transfer but carries filing hurdles: (i) the WFOE must evidence corporate income tax settlement via the tax payment receipt (完税证明); (ii) the board must pass a dividend declaration based on audit-adjusted PRC GAAP financials; (iii) the remitting bank requires the audited financial statement, tax clearance, and board resolution; and (iv) the remittance is legally subject to PRC withholding tax of 10% absent treaty relief (the 2015 Cross-Strait Tax Agreement is not yet ratified, so no effective reduction to 5% currently applies). These conditions must be satisfied before the bank will release the SWIFT MT103 outbound transfer.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Mandatory FX surrender was abolished on 1 August 2012. Current rules: (1) no statutory conversion ratio applies to export receipts - an A-class goods-trade enterprise may freely retain hard currency in an FX current account or convert into RMB; (2) repatriation remains mandatory - export proceeds must be collected onshore through a designated FX bank within generally 180 days from the customs export declaration date (出口收汇期限), with any delay beyond 180 days requiring documented justification (e.g., extended open-account credit terms or payment disputes) and bank declaration; (3) the WFOE must complete SAFE goods-trade list registration (名录登记), operate under the total-quantity verification/balance-checking regime (总量核查), and promptly file all BOP receipts and payments; (4) if downgraded to B/C category, export proceeds must flow through a pending-receipt FX account (待核查账户) and the WFOE must present customs declarations, contracts, and invoices per transaction before settlement; (5) non-compliance triggers SAFE warnings, administrative penalties, suspension of FX settlement convenience, and downgrade to B/C status.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC / 中国人民银行) as the central monetary authority; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE / 国家外汇管理局) as the direct foreign-exchange regulator; National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA / 国家金融监督管理总局) as the banking licensing and on-balance-sheet supervisor; Ministry of Commerce (MOFCOM / 商务部) and National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC / 国家发展和改革委员会) as the investment-access gatekeepers under the Foreign Investment Law and the nationwide Negative List. SAFE administers the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations (外汇管理条例), the Measures for the Administration of Foreign Exchange in Goods Trade (汇发〔2012〕38号), and the bank-delegated FDI registration framework.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared when any category of related-party transactions exceeds RMB 200 million for tangible assets, RMB 100 million for intangible assets, RMB 100 million for financial assets, or RMB 40 million for other transactions; Master File is mandatory if annual cross-border related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: China does not operate formal Economic Substance Regulations of the kind seen in offshore finance centres; however, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (EIT Law Art. 47) and the Beneficial Ownership Test (Announcement 2018 No. 9) require the WFOE to demonstrate genuine business substance in China, including physical production facilities for titanium orthopedic implants, resident operational management, sufficient employees, and actual risk-control and decision-making functions. Failure to maintain substance can result in denial of tax incentives, recharacterisation of transactions, and imposition of a 25% tax on adjusted profits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (逃税)

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid corporate income tax plus daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Art. 201 provides imprisonment of three to seven years and fines.

Director personal liability: Directors, the statutory representative, and financial officers are jointly and severally liable for restitution of the unpaid tax if corporate formalities are abused; courts may impose exit bans, freeze personal assets, and order imprisonment of responsible individuals.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment under EIT Law Art. 123 with secondary adjustment, 50%–500% underpayment penalty, daily surcharge at 0.05%, an administrative fine up to RMB 50,000 for non-filing, and a ten-year retroactive assessment period for transfer pricing adjustments.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute fraudulent intercompany agreements or approve fictional royalties may be personally liable; in aggravated cases the authorities impose asset freezes, exit controls, and criminal liability for intentional mispricing.
Shell Company / Lack of Commercial Substance

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may disregard the legal entity under GAAR, recharacterise the transactions, impose 25% EIT on imputed profits, revoke tax-preference treatment, levy penalties and interest, and refer the matter to customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange for blacklisting.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil under Company Law Art. 20 exposes directors and shareholders to joint and several liability for unpaid corporate taxes; in serious cases, travel bans and imprisonment for illegal business operations or fraudulent dissolution are available.
