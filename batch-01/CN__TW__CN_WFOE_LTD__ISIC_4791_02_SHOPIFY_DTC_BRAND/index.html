Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

PRC law does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch entity classification; a WFOE is per se a Chinese resident enterprise taxed under the Enterprise Income Tax Law at a headline rate of 25% on worldwide income. Available elections: (i) voluntary registration as a General VAT Taxpayer when annual VAT-taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000, or retention of Small-Scale Taxpayer status (levy rates 1–3%); (ii) foreign tax credit election for overseas taxes (direct and indirect credit) under the EIT Law; (iii) small low-profit enterprise concession producing an effective 5% EIT rate for taxable income not exceeding RMB 3,000,000 with no more than 300 employees and RMB 50,000,000 in assets; (iv) installment and quarterly prepayment election for CIT; (v) no binding cross-strait double taxation agreement is currently in force with Taiwan (the 2015 Cross-Strait Agreement remains unratified in Taiwan), hence no treaty-based check-the-box or tie-breaker election is available, and the General Anti-Avoidance Rules (特别纳税调整) govern hybrid arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PRC: RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable VAT sales — compulsory General VAT Taxpayer registration; below the threshold the WFOE may remain a Small-Scale Taxpayer. Taiwan nexus: a mainland remote seller of goods or digital services to Taiwan consumers must register for Taiwan Business Tax when annual taxable sales of digital services exceed TWD 480,000; low-value goods imports with a customs value not exceeding TWD 2,000 per shipment are exempt from import duty and business tax at Taiwan customs clearance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may apply the 0% export VAT rate with input-VAT credit/refund (出口退(免)税) where: (1) it holds General VAT Taxpayer status; (2) goods are physically exported from mainland China under customs supervision, including shipments to Taiwan as a separate customs territory via cross-strait logistics or the 9610 retail-export / 9710 B2B e-commerce / 9810 overseas-warehouse regimes; (3) each shipment is covered by an electronic customs export declaration evidencing that the goods left China; (4) export proceeds are collected from the overseas buyer in foreign currency and repatriated within the statutory FX deadline; (5) the goods are not on the prohibited or restricted export duty-rebate negative lists; and (6) Shopify order records, payment-rail settlement data (Shopify Payments, Stripe, PayPal), and logistics manifests reconcile to the declared customs value. Offshore services rendered to overseas customers may qualify for zero-rating or exemption when supplied from mainland China to recipients abroad with no domestic consumption.

Golden Tax Phase IV fully digitalized e-invoice (数电票/全面数字化电子发票) mandate: all VAT invoices issued by the WFOE are generated in real time through the Electronic Tax Bureau with mandatory 24/7 API-based fiscal data transmission, tax-authority electronic digital stamping, and no paper invoice or physical stamping for registered general taxpayers. Export and e-commerce parcel clearance is conducted electronically through the China International Trade Single Window with no paper customs documentation. If the WFOE triggers Taiwan remote-seller registration, it must issue Taiwan electronic invoices (電子發票) through an approved platform and file bimonthly Business Tax returns with the Taiwan National Tax Administration.

Export VAT refunds are subject to prior risk-screening and joint special-task-force scrutiny by tax, customs, foreign-exchange and public-security authorities (四部门联合打击骗税机制). The tax authority may retrospectively claw back the full refunded VAT, impose a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%, levy fines ranging from 1x to 5x of the fraudulently obtained refund (Tax Collection and Administration Law Art. 66), downgrade the exporter's customs credit rating, suspend or cancel export-tax-refund eligibility and foreign-exchange collection rights, and may refer the case for criminal prosecution. Fraudulently obtaining export tax refunds constitutes the crime of 骗取出口退税罪 (Criminal Law Art. 204) punishable by imprisonment up to life for especially huge amounts; administrative termination of cross-border e-commerce export filing status and payment-rail settlement may also follow. On the Taiwan side, evasion of remote-seller registration triggers back-assessment of the 5% Business Tax, a 5% late payment surcharge, and fines up to three times the evaded amount under the Value-added and Non-value-added Business Tax Act (加值型及非加值型營業稅法).

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible for current account items (goods, services, dividends of a real nature) under IMF Article VIII, as implemented through SAFE's verified-transaction regime: every current account receipt or payment must have genuine, documented underlying trade or service activities (真实性、合规性审核). Capital account items remain restricted: inbound foreign direct investment is permitted after market regulation filing (外商投资信息报告) and SAFE FDI registration; profit remittance is classified as a current account

Export proceeds surrender rules

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC / 中国人民银行) as the central bank, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE / 国家外汇管理局) as the exclusive foreign exchange administration for the mainland-operating WFOE. The Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan, CBC / 中央銀行) separately governs NT dollar flows for the Taiwan parent shareholder, but does not control the WFOE's onshore RMB or FX treasury.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% (Profit-Seeking Enterprise Income Tax; a further 5% surtax applies to undistributed retained earnings under the Income Basic Tax Act)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

21% on gross dividends paid to a non-resident corporate shareholder, including a Mainland Chinese parent (Art. 88 and Art. 89 Income Tax Act). The Taiwan WFOE must withhold and remit the tax within 10 days from the distribution date. Undistributed earnings remain exposed to the 5% retained-earnings surtax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: TWD: mandatory Transfer Pricing Report (Local File) when annual related-party revenue-type transactions exceed TWD 50 million or expense-type transactions exceed TWD 20 million; Master File and Country-by-Country notification are required where the MNE group has consolidated revenue above TWD 27 billion. In all cases, cross-border related-party transactions must be disclosed on Attachment 27 of the annual return.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not apply the offshore-center Economic Substance Regulations model. However, for Mainland-invested WFOEs, the Mainland Investment Regulations require genuine local substance: a real office lease, at least one full-time Taiwan payroll employee, local books and board records, and actual management of the webstore in Taiwan. A WFOE used as an invoicing shell without local operating substance is recharacterized under Art. 43-1 Income Tax Act (general anti-avoidance), with income reattributed to the PRC parent, and the Investment Commission may revoke the investment permit and order divestment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Suppressed Online Sales

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment of 20% CIT, 5% retained-earnings surtax and VAT under-reporting penalties; civil penalty of up to 3 times the evaded tax under Art. 110 Income Tax Act; criminal liability under Art. 41 Tax Collection Act with imprisonment of up to 5 years and fines up to TWD 10 million.

Director personal liability: The responsible director is the named offender for criminal tax evasion; conviction results in imprisonment; once tax is confirmed and unpaid above TWD 100,000, a court-ordered travel ban applies (Art. 24 Tax Collection Act); the corporate veil may be pierced if the director diverted corporate assets overseas.
Shell Company - Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Revocation of the Mainland investment permit and mandatory divestment by the MOEA Investment Commission; tax authority disregard of the WFOE under Art. 43-1 Income Tax Act, reattributing the e-commerce income to the PRC parent; deemed 21% withholding on any actual repatriation; refusal of VAT input credit on purchasing invoices.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false substance declarations face criminal liability for filing false documents; the PRC parent shareholder cannot invoke the corporate veil defense under Art. 154 Company Act where the corporate form is abused; directors may be jointly liable for restitution of unwarranted tax benefits.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Discretionary TP adjustment of taxable income to the arm's-length result; civil penalty of up to 3 times the underpaid tax (Art. 110); separate fixed fines of TWD 3,000-30,000 for failure to produce required TP documentation, with the burden of proof reversed for transactions with related parties in tax-haven jurisdictions.

Director personal liability: Knowingly false TP attestations are treated as fraudulent tax declarations, exposing directors to criminal sanctions under the Tax Collection Act; directors may also owe indemnification to the company if the TP adjustment creates capital deficiency or triggers insolvent trading claims.
AML / Cross-Border Settlement Abuse and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Money Laundering Control Act: corporate fines up to TWD 5 million and imprisonment of up to 7 years for responsible persons engaged in intentional money laundering; Uniform Invoice Punishment Regulations: fines up to TWD 1 million, suspension of business, and publication of the company name; VAT evasion penalty of up to 5 times the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: The director personally receiving diverted payments is the primary money-laundering actor and faces imprisonment; directors authorizing false e-invoicing are jointly and severally liable for the evaded VAT and CIT, and such tax claims survive bankruptcy discharge.
