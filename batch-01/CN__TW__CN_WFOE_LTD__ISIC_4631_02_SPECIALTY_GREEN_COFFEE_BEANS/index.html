Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is mandated by the Companies Act of Taiwan; however, the Investment Commission will require actual remittance of the approved capital from the foreign parent to a Taiwanese NT-dollar bank account, and this remittance must be evidenced by a foreign exchange settlement certificate at the time of incorporation. There is no blocked escrow account requirement, but the funds must remain in the company account and be backed by the capital remittance certificate issued by the designated foreign exchange bank; failure to remit within three months of the Investment Commission approval will cause the permission to lapse unless an extension is sought.

Funding & unlock path: Civil defense and occupational clearance requires a Fire Safety Equipment Inspection Certificate issued by the local fire department, renewed annually, and a Fire Safety Management Plan for any warehouse over 300 square metres, including certified emergency lighting, fire extinguishers, clear aisle access and a named fire-safety manager. The building must have a lawful occupancy permit confirming structural load and fire resistance for warehousing use. Under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, the WFOE must also file a workplace hazard assessment and obtain a Labour Inspection and Occupational Clearance for warehouse racking, forklift operations, and manual lifting; any findings must be corrected and re-inspected within 30 days.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is mandated by the Companies Act of Taiwan; however, the Investment Commission will require actual remittance of the approved capital from the foreign parent to a Taiwanese NT-dollar bank account, and this remittance must be evidenced by a foreign exchange settlement certificate at the time of incorporation. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局), operating through provincial, municipal, and county-level tax bureaus. Online portal: Electronic Tax Bureau (电子税务局) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; statutory website: www.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) limited liability company, the unified Unified Social Credit Code (USCC, 统一社会信用代码) is automatically allocated upon registration with the Market Regulation Administration and constitutes the primary Tax Identification Number (TIN/纳税人识别号). Within 30 days after obtaining the business license (营业执照), the WFOE must complete tax registration (税务登记暨税种认定) with the competent local tax bureau via the E-Tax Bureau portal. Required filings include: business license, articles of association, legal representative identity, foreign investor identity/certification, registered address lease or ownership certificate, and bank deposit account information. The tax bureau will then confirm applicable tax categories (CIT, VAT, stamp duty, etc.), electronify the taxpayer profile, and issue the tax registration certificate equivalently through the electronic registration system.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; the CN WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise (居民企业) subject to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income at the standard 25% CIT rate. It is a limited liability company with independent legal personality; pass-through eligibility does not apply. Territorial scope: Chinese source and worldwide income of the WFOE is taxable in the PRC; foreign income tax credits are available under the PRC CIT Law. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the CN WFOE is a Chinese resident enterprise (居民企业) subject to Enterprise Income Tax on worldwide income at the standard 25% CIT rate. It is a limited liability company with independent legal personality; pass-through eligibility does not apply. Territorial scope: Chinese source and worldwide income of the WFOE is taxable in the PRC; foreign income tax credits are available under the PRC CIT Law. Tax-Exempt Zone: none applicable by default; preferential zones (e.g., Hainan Free Trade Port, certain bonded areas) may offer reduced CIT rates or customs privileges only upon qualifying application, which is not presumed for this standard WFOE.

Available election forms

PRC tax law does not provide check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections comparable to US/European regimes. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT general taxpayer voluntary election after meeting accounting and turnover criteria (annual taxable sales > RMB 5,000,000 is compulsory; below threshold, small-scale taxpayer status is default, but voluntary general taxpayer status may be chosen with robust accounting); (ii) simplified tax collection (核定征收) for income tax in exceptional cases, subject to stringent approval and generally not available for WFOE; (iii) accelerated depreciation or specific depreciation methods under CIT; (iv) loss carryforward utilisation election (standard 5-year carryforward, with extended periods for qualifying industries); (v) foreign tax credit choice between direct and indirect credits; and (vi) VAT input credit selection for dutiable and zero-rated operations. No entity classification election is permitted for a WFOE; it is automatically a ring-fenced resident entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All enterprises, including WFOEs, must register with the tax authority within 30 days of incorporation regardless of turnover. For VAT general taxpayer status, compulsory registration applies when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000 (or when expected turnover exceeds that threshold); below RMB 5,000,000, small-scale taxpayer status applies with an option to elect general taxpayer treatment on fulfilling accounting/control requirements.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

An export of goods from the PRC is eligible for VAT zero-rating (0% VAT with refund of input VAT) if: (i) the seller is a registered VAT general taxpayer and a Foreign Trade Operator registered with the Ministry of Commerce and Customs; (ii) the goods are physically exported and declared to Customs with an approved export declaration; (iii) the sale is a genuine commercial export to an overseas buyer (including excluded cross-border transactions with Taiwan only where permitted under PRC Cross-Strait trade instruments; mainland WFOEs exporting directly to Taiwan follow standard export procedures); (iv) the exporter has obtained and maintained an export tax refund category (出口退（免）税企业管理类别); and (v) the export proceeds are repatriated to China within the statutory period. For re-export of imported green coffee beans, any import VAT paid may be refunded after satisfying the same substantive requirements, subject to the 'examination of export difficulties' exception and no prior processing in unrestricted domestic consumption.

China has implemented the National Fully Digital Electronic Invoice (全电发票) regime, integrated with the Golden Tax Phase IV system. All VAT invoices (General and Special) must be issued through the official Electronic Invoice Platform, encrypted with taxpayer UKeys, and transmitted in real time to the STA database. For customs and export transactions, electronic export declarations and digital clearance are mandatory; no physical stamping is applied. The system automatically reconciles invoice data, customs data, and foreign exchange receipts, creating a real-time fiscal audit trail for indirect taxation and export refunds.

The STA uses Golden Tax Phase IV and cross-agency data analytics (customs declarations, invoice issuance, foreign exchange receipts, and export manifest) to identify fraudulent export VAT refunds. In case of false export declarations, forged documentation, or failure to meet the FX repatriation deadline, the tax authority denies or claws back the refunded VAT, imposes a penalty of 0.5 to 5 times the tax amount involved, levies late-payment surcharges (0.05% per day), downgrades the exporter's risk classification, and suspends or revokes export tax refund status. Serious fraud constitutes the criminal offence of export tax fraud under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law (punishable by imprisonment up to life and fines), and may also trigger customs smuggling charges under the PRC Customs Law.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Renminbi is fully convertible for bona fide current account transactions, including the import of green coffee beans and related logistics costs, subject to documentary verification by a licensed Chinese bank. Capital account items remain subject to prior SAFE registration and supervision; a WFOE receives foreign equity investment through the FDI capital account mechanism after obtaining SAFE's Foreign Direct Investment Registration and the bank-issued e-certificate. Profit remittance by a WFOE is treated as a current account transaction and is legally permissible once the enterprise has completed annual audit, tax clearance, and the bank has verified audited financial statements, the foreign exchange settlement account records, and a board or shareholders' resolution approving the distribution. Cross-strait Taiwan investor participation triggers additional beneficial ownership and source-of-funds scrutiny but does not alter the underlying classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE trade-monitoring regulations, export proceeds for goods must be repatriated into a domestic designated foreign exchange settlement account within the time limit agreed by the bank, usually not exceeding 90 days from the date of customs export declaration. There is no mandatory statutory conversion ratio; an exporter may voluntarily settle into RMB or retain the proceeds in a foreign currency settlement account, provided the inflows reconcile with customs declared values and the SAFE Trade Monitoring System. The exporter must report the receipt with the correct trade purpose code and maintain supporting customs declarations, commercial invoices, bills of lading, and bank receipts to satisfy the verification and write-off procedures. For this WFOE, the primary business is importation rather than export, so the compliance focus is import-payment verification and outward remittance controls, while any incidental sample or re-export of green coffee beans would be subject to the 90-day repatriation norm.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity subscription and paid-in capital shall be routed by SWIFT MT103 wire from the Taiwan parent or investing shareholder to the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI capital foreign exchange account at the chosen domestic bank. The remittance must be accompanied by a purpose declaration consistent with SAFE's FDI code for inward equity investment; the receiving bank will credit the account only after verifying the SAFE e-foreign-direct-investment registration certificate, board resolution, formation and business license of the WFOE, and the investor's KYC/passport pack. On the same day of credited arrival and after the bank's capital-golden term monitoring, the WFOE may convert into RMB under the bank's foreign exchange settlement system, subject to payment of the applicable RMB and USD interbank exchange price and reporting under the capital project monitoring system. The funds can be released to fund working capital for green coffee inventory, logistics, and operating expenditure only after providing a lawful use-circumstances declaration and, if required, the bank's invoice-level verification for payment in support of import costs.Disbursements for the import of specialty green coffee bean containers and related ocean or air logistics shall be executed via (i) SWIFT MT103 direct telegraphic transfer from the WFOE's foreign exchange settlement account to the overseas supplier, or (ii) an irrevocable documentary commercial letter of credit (LC) issued by the WFOE's Chinese bank, payable at sight or on a 90-day usance basis. Both methods require submission to the issuing bank of the commercial invoice, full set of bills of lading, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin, packing list, and, in the case of LC, a compliant presentation through the negotiating bank. For outward remittances, the WFOE must declare the trade purpose code for goods imports and the bank will perform matching against the customs import data, ensuring the amount, currency, and beneficiary are consistent with the underlying trade contract; no foreign-exchange purchase for these payments is available without the relevant import documents, and any advance payment exceeding the bank's threshold requires the commercial contract and, where necessary, a bank guarantee from the supplier.SAFE-approved onshore RMB/USD FX forwards with documentary trade-background verification, used to lock the imported green coffee cost against USD invoice exposureNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed offshore through the Taiwan treasury center or an eligible offshore financial institution, subject to cross-strait remittance limitations and internal corporate hedging policy, applicable only for macro-hedge of non-RMB settlement exposureCross-currency swaps between RMB and USD under the bank's derivative framework for hedging long-tenor working capital and trade financing interest rate and currency risk, permissible only when the underlying exposure is evidenced by trade receivables, inventory, or capital-flow obligations

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for core treasury operations. Offshore digital EMI platforms and cross-border payment fintechs (e.g., Airwallex, Payoneer, PingPong) are acceptable only as auxiliary collection or disbursement channels for small-value, platform-driven cross-border trade settlements, and cannot replace a licensed Chinese domestic bank for FDI capital holding, SAFE trade verification, RMB settlement, corporate income tax compliance, or documentary letter of credit issuance. Any use must be structured as a secondary receivable-payment layer beneath a primary domestic bank account, with all funds ultimately repatriated to the WFOE's own China-based settlement account.

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

21% withholding tax on dividends distributed by the Taiwan WFOE to a non-resident corporate shareholder. Mainland Chinese shareholders cannot claim treaty relief because the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement, signed in 2015, has not entered into force; the 21% statutory rate applies. Undistributed earnings are also subject to a 5% retained earnings surtax at the corporate level.

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Transfer Pricing Report, or Local File, applies where aggregate controlled transactions reach NT$200 million or more in a taxable year.
