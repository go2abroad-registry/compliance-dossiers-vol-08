Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — a Taiwan charitable endowment foundation is a separate juridical person taxed at entity level under the Income Tax Act; taxation applies in principle to Taiwan-source income only (territorial scope). A conditional income-tax exemption applies to qualifying educational, cultural, public-welfare or charitable organizations under Article 4(13) of the Income Tax Act and the MOF exemption criteria, which require that a stated ratio of annual revenue be expended on mission-related charitable or grant-making purposes.

Available election forms

Taiwan follows a civil-law statutory framework and has no check-the-box or pass-through election. Available statutory elections for the foundation: (i) application for income-tax-exempt status of the charitable organization (exemption-certificate route in lieu of the standard taxable return); (ii) small-scale business-tax assessed-rate election (1% on goods / 2% on services) where average monthly turnover is between NT$80,000 and NT$200,000, in lieu of the 5% VAT rate; (iii) e-invoice transmission architecture selection (Ministry of Finance Turnkey direct connection versus approved SaaS platform); and (iv) no hybrid-mismatch or CFC election is available because the entity is domestic and qualifies for charitable exemption; the CFC and anti-hybrid provisions of the amended Income Tax Act are inapplicable to this exempt scope.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Average monthly taxable sales up to NT$80,000 are exempt from business tax; monthly turnover of NT$80,000–NT$200,000 subjects a small-scale business to an assessed output amount at 1% (goods) or 2% (services) without input-VAT credit; turnover exceeding NT$200,000 per month mandates full 5% VAT registration and uniform/e-invoicing. The foundation's endowment administration, grant distributions, interest and dividend income are outside the taxable turnover base; only ancillary commercial sales of goods or services count toward the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Article 7 of the Business Tax Act zero-rates: (i) goods actually exported and leaving Taiwan customs territory; (ii) services rendered by a Taiwan entity to foreign enterprises and used or consumed outside Taiwan, with consideration received in foreign exchange; (iii) deemed exports to export-processing zones, free-trade-zone enterprises, science-park companies and bonded operators; and (iv) international air and sea transport. The foundation's endowment-management and scientific-grant administration services performed for offshore grantors qualify under category (ii) where the economic benefit is realized abroad; fund-level interest, dividends and grant income are exempt financial income under Article 8 rather than zero-rated. Excess input VAT attributable to zero-rated supplies is refundable through the regular monthly or quarterly return, subject to Bureau verification.

  • Customs export declaration (出口報單) or goods-export certificate bearing the customs serial number (for goods)
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and, where applicable, the grant-services or endowment-management contract
  • Ocean bill of lading, air waybill or courier delivery receipt evidencing physical export
  • Foreign-exchange settlement certificate (結匯水單) or inbound remittance advice evidencing receipt of foreign-currency proceeds
  • Offshore-use substantiation pack: client certificate, service-rendering documentation and foreign-site evidence (for zero-rated exported services)

Taiwan mandates uniform invoices (統一發票) for all standard VAT taxpayers. E-invoices (電子發票) are issued and transmitted to the Ministry of Finance e-Invoice Consolidation Service Platform in near real-time via the Turnkey application using signed XML/JSON messaging with transport-layer security, platform-signed acknowledgement and digital sequence control; the current mandatory schema is the EV2021 2.0 e-invoice standard. A charitable foundation conducting taxable sales above the NT$200,000 monthly threshold must transmit each issued invoice without delay and remains visible to the National Taxation Bureau in the platform; foundations holding an approved tax-exemption certificate for pure grant-making activities are outside the e-invoice clearance mandate.

Zero-rating claims are cross-verified by the National Taxation Bureau against the Customs export ledger and the Central Bank foreign-exchange declaration database; incomplete or inconsistent documentation triggers reclassification of the supply at the 5% output rate with deficiency assessment. In cases of fraud or false documentation: (i) an administrative penalty of up to five times the evaded tax under Article 51 of the Value-Added and Non-Value-Added Business Tax Act; (ii) criminal liability under Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act (2021 amendment) of one to seven years' imprisonment and/or a fine of up to NT$10 million; (iii) retrospective clawback of refunded VAT plus penal interest with the ordinary five-year assessment period extended to seven years for fraudulent cases; and (iv) for a charitable foundation, revocation of the income-tax exemption certificate and potential dissolution proceedings by the supervising competent authority.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible on the current account (trade in goods/services, dividends, interest, ordinary operational remittances) where a genuine transaction background and supporting documentation are presented; the capital account remains regulated: inbound FDI requires SAFE-registered foreign exchange registration (FDI外汇登记) and deposit of the capital into a designated FX capital account (资本金账户), with discretionary FX settlement (意愿结汇) enabling conversion to RMB upon a payment instruction; profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is permitted after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax finalization, and a bank-level review of the cross-strait tax agreement (2015 Agreement) benefits, with no separate SAFE approval needed for qualified distributions; structural hurdles remain for round-tripping, portfolio investment, and unrestricted outflow, meaning the treasury must align intra-group payments to real grant-making and scientific procurement transactions to maintain lawful convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory export surrender obligations in 2012 as part of the goods trade FX administration reform (SAFE 2012 No. 1 and the 'Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Management Guidelines' 汇发[2012] No. 38); export receipts must be repatriated into a domestic FX settlement account generally within 180 days of the export customs declaration, but there is no mandatory conversion ratio — an enterprise may retain 100% of its export proceeds in foreign currency or convert optionally at its discretion; SAFE uses a dynamic A/B/C classification to monitor receipts and payments, and listed/registered business scope must cover the export. For the CN_JV_LTD charitable endowment entity whose ISIC 6430 scope is non-commercial grant-making, this rule applies only to incidental export-derived licensing or fundraising asset sales; the treasury policy will adopt a zero-surrender posture with voluntary conversion aligned to budgeted grant disbursement cycles and will maintain the A-classification status through timely reporting under the enterprise's designated bank branch.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central monetary authority; State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated foreign exchange control agency responsible for FDI registration, capital account supervision, and bank-delegated verification of cross-border flows; China Banking and Insurance Regulatory Commission (CBIRC) merged into the National Financial Regulatory Administration (NFRA) covers banking licensing and conduct supervision.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low — PRC banking law requires corporate settlement accounts to be opened with licensed domestic banks; offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in China and cannot open the mandatory SAFE-registered capital account or serve as the primary treasury rail; their limited role may be an auxiliary receiving channel for small-value foreign donations if processed through a licensed Chinese bank partner under the trade facilitation pilot, but this cannot replace the constitutional SWIFT/CIPS banking rails of the JV; the legal recommendation is to use domestic banks as the sole account infrastructure and to establish one offshore settlement account (in Hong Kong or elsewhere) only for the Taiwan grantmaker's inward funds, with full disclosure to SAFE.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder wires the registered capital (in CNY via cross-border RMB clearing, USD/EUR, or convertible currencies) through a correspondent bank using SWIFT MT103 to the Bank of China capital account (No. 2200); prerequisites are: (1) SAFE FDI foreign exchange registration (FDI外汇登记) completed at the local SAFE branch through the bank, obtaining a FDI number; (2) statutory business account opened at the same designated bank; (3) remittance purpose declared as transaction code 622011 (foreign-investment capital contribution); the receiving bank validates the underlying investment background, pays funds to the capital account, and thereafter allows discretionary FX settlement (意愿结汇) into the 结汇待支付 escrow account for restricted use (grant funding, procurement, wages, land/lease, taxes); all receipt vouchers and the SAFE registration form must be retained for five years; for a Taiwan investor, cross-border RMB remittance via the cross-strait RMB clearing mechanism (China Construction Bank Taipei or Bank of China Taipei branch correspondent arrangement) avoids TWD-FX conversion delays; the capital schedule must be completed within the period defined in the SAMR-registered JV contract, and any deferred contribution requires a bank filing of an amended capital schedule.Clearing rails for outflows are governed by the real-transaction principle: (1) RMB grants to a Taiwan foundation are cleared via CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System) or SWIFT MT103 from the domestic account, with the bank verifying the grant agreement, board approval, tax filing certificate (对外支付税务备案表), and the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement reduced withholding, if any; (2) FX disbursements (USD/EUR) for international scientific grants are charged against the FX settlement account, reported via SAFE's current-account monitoring system, and require the grant agreement plus proof of registration of the foreign recipient organization; (3) for procurement of equipment funded by the endowment, the treasury may issue a commercial letter of credit (MT700) from the operational account margin or instruct an electronic transfer — LC is not the dominant rail but remains permissible for high-value scientific instruments; (4) each outbound payment above the exemption threshold (currently RMB 50,000 for tax-filing purposes, with some facilitation thresholds) should be supported by the online tax filing certificate, and all payments are screened against PRC OFAC-style sanctions lists and the bank's internal AML list; (5) any outbound transfer to a jurisdiction not having a tax treaty or information exchange is subject to enhanced due diligence and, for an endowment entity, an annual foreign-payment quota assessment.Onshore RMB foreign-exchange forwards (远期结售汇) — permitted under SAFE's hedging framework for genuine contract/grant exposures, executed with any licensed domestic bank.Onshore FX options (外汇期权) and option combination strategies (e.g., collars) — permissible only with a real transaction background and appropriate business-backed authorization; speculative option-writing is prohibited.Cross-currency swaps (货币掉期) — available for CNY/USD/EUR principal and interest exchange between the JV and its funding counterparty, subject to SAFE registration and real cross-border debt/equity underlying.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on CNY — prohibited for the onshore JV treasury without SAFE approval; the Taiwan shareholder may enter NDFs offshore, but the domestic entity must rely on onshore deliverable forwards and options to avoid regulatory penalty.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low — PRC banking law requires corporate settlement accounts to be opened with licensed domestic banks; offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) are not licensed deposit-taking institutions in China and cannot open the mandatory SAFE-registered capital account or serve as the primary treasury rail; their limited role may be an auxiliary receiving channel for small-value foreign donations if processed through a licensed Chinese bank partner under the trade facilitation pilot, but this cannot replace the constitutional SWIFT/CIPS banking rails of the JV; the legal recommendation is to use domestic banks as the sole account infrastructure and to establish one offshore settlement account (in Hong Kong or elsewhere) only for the Taiwan grantmaker's inward funds, with full disclosure to SAFE.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% profit-seeking enterprise income tax (Income Tax Act Art. 5); 12% Alternative Minimum Tax (Basic Income Amount) recapture under the Income Basic Tax Act; 5% additional tax on undistributed retained earnings (Art. 66-9) where 2018-and-later profits are not distributed.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

21% gross-basis withholding on dividends paid to a non-resident corporate shareholder (Income Tax Act Arts. 88 and 73-2). No branch-remittance tax applies to Taiwan branch profits (branch pays only the 20% CIT). Undistributed earnings are additionally taxed at 5% (Art. 66-9).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing must occur within 5 months of fiscal year-end: 31 May for calendar-year taxpayers; a one-month CPA-assisted extension to 30 June is available; the 5% retained-earnings surtax return is due on the same date as the principal return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: NT$200,000,000 (NT$200 million) of annual controlled related-party transactions triggers the mandatory contemporaneous Transfer Pricing Report (Local File standard); NT$50,000,000 for taxpayers using tax incentives or loss carryforwards; the annual Related-Party Transaction Disclosure Table is mandatory if any controlled transaction exists; group Master File and CbCR reporting apply at Taiwan-headquartered group consolidated-revenue thresholds under the TP Examination Rules.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan has no Gulf-style ESR. Substance is enforced via: (1) the general anti-abuse 'substance-over-form' rule (Tax Collection Act Art. 12-1); (2) the charitable-foundation operative-substance test, requiring a real Taiwan office, staff, independent board discretion over grants, and no private inurement; a foundation serving as a mere investment/conduit shell loses the Art. 4(13) exemption and is taxed retroactively at 20% plus penalties; (3) beneficial-ownership documentation for treaty-based WHT relief; (4) NPO registration and source-of-funds record-keeping under the Money Laundering Control Act for foundations receiving cross-strait endowment wires.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (詐術逃漏稅捐)

Statutory sanction: Understated-tax penalty of up to 2x the amount evaded (Income Tax Act Art. 110); 1%-per-2-days delinquency surcharge; criminal conviction under Tax Collection Act Art. 41 punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years or a fine.

Director personal liability: The responsible director/chairperson is the statutory taxpayer representative; criminal liability attaches personally, and pending arrears trigger a departure-restriction order (travel ban) and seizure of personal assets where the company is insolvent; the founder/shareholder can lose charitable-approval status.
Private Foundation Abuse / Dividend-WHT Bypass (公益法人規避)

Statutory sanction: Revocation of tax-exempt status with retroactive taxation of all exempt years at 20%; 2x understatement penalty; refusal or clawback of grants; deregistration by the competent authority; disgorgement under the Civil Code for unlawful foundation acts.

Director personal liability: Directors are liable for breach of fiduciary duty (Civil Code/articles on juristic-person management) and for criminal breach of trust or embezzlement (Criminal Code Arts. 336/342, up to 10 years' imprisonment); travel bans and asset-preservation orders are routinely issued in fraud investigations of charitable foundations.
Transfer Pricing and Thin-Capitalization Stripping (移轉訂價及過度槓桿)

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment increasing taxable income, disallowance of excess interest (Art. 43-2), secondary penalty of up to 2x underpaid tax, and assessed interest; failure to file the TP report without reasonable cause attracts separate fines under the TP Examination Rules.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false TP disclosures face personal criminal exposure for aiding tax evasion; the company's inability to pay after adjustment subjects responsible persons to departure restrictions and contribution claims in liquidation.
Shell Company / False Invoicing (虛設行號、虛開發票)

Statutory sanction: VAT and CIT penalties (up to 100%-300% of the fraudulent invoice amount under the VAT Act for issuing false invoices), disallowance of deductions, corporate registration revocation, and criminal sanction for document forgery; the entity may be treated as a 'false establishment' with collective assessment of the responsible person.

Director personal liability: The legal representative is jointly and severally liable for taxes, fines, and restoration of fraudulent VAT credits; criminal forgery/embezzlement charges carry up to 5-7 years' imprisonment; a court may freeze personal bank accounts and impose exit bans.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance (洗錢防制及實質受益人申報)

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Money Laundering Control Act (up to NT$1,000,000 for registration/reporting failures and enhanced due-diligence breaches), refusal of bank wire facilities, and referral of suspicious grant flows to the Anti-Corruption/economic-crime prosecutors.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly facilitate undisclosed PRC-source funding face criminal money-laundering charges (Money Laundering Control Act, up to 7 years' imprisonment) and confiscation orders; travel bans and personal surety requirements are standard in cross-strait financial-crime investigations.
