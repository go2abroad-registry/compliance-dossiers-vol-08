Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a WFOE limited liability company under PRC law. The registered capital is set in the Articles of Association and the full subscribed amount must be contributed within five years from the date of establishment under Article 47 of the 2023 revised Companies Law. A blocked escrow account is not generally required, but contributed capital must be received into the company's basic bank account and the contribution must be reported under the foreign investment information system. Because the business scope covers Class III medical device manufacturing, the paid-in capital must be sufficient to equip and operate the intraocular acrylic lens production facility at the time the Medical Device Production License is audited.

Funding & unlock path: Fire protection design review, fire acceptance filing, and fire safety certification from the local fire and rescue authority; civil defense approval and structural load inspection where the production area is below grade; occupational hazard pre-assessment and control-effect evaluation under the Law on the Prevention of Occupational Diseases covering noise, volatile organic compounds, and micromachining dust; work-safety facility three-simultaneous approval under the Work Safety Law; cleanroom environmental conformity inspection for controlled manufacturing areas.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA); online portal: Electronic Tax Service Platform (E-Tax) operated by STA and provincial tax service bureaus, accessible through www.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

The Taiwan-resident investor forms the Chinese WFOE through the Administration for Market Regulation. The issued business license contains the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which functions as the WFOE statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration identifier under the multi-license integration system. Within 30 days of license issuance, the legal representative and finance officer must complete supplementary tax registration through the Electronic Tax Service Platform by confirming the competent tax authority, industry classification (medical device and intraocular lens manufacturing), accounting standards, fiscal period, bank deposit accounts, and controller details. The platform issues an electronic filing password, CA certificate and tax-control U-Key for e-filing and invoice issuance. The WFOE must then apply for general VAT taxpayer status if estimated annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000 or on a voluntary basis, apply for invoice quota and electronic invoice issuance rights, and complete export tax rebate registration in the E-Tax platform. For the Taiwan shareholder as a non-resident enterprise without a Chinese establishment, the WFOE as withholding agent must complete non-resident enterprise registration with the STA to obtain a non-resident TIN for dividend, interest and royalty withholding; the Taiwan shareholder may also be required to provide a Taiwan tax residency certificate when invoking cross-strait tax arrangement relief.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the Chinese WFOE is a resident enterprise for Corporate Income Tax purposes, taxed at entity level on worldwide income at the statutory 25% CIT rate, and is not eligible for pass-through treatment.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under PRC CIT. Under VAT, a small-scale taxpayer may elect to register as a general VAT taxpayer if accounting and compliance conditions are met, and a general VAT taxpayer may elect simplified VAT calculation only for enumerated categories at 3% or 5% as statutorily specified; no pass-through election is available for the WFOE.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 in annual taxable sales in any continuous 12-month period triggers mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; there is no separate VAT registration turnover threshold for the WFOE because the USCC automatic tax registration applies from incorporation, and the threshold determines small-scale versus general taxpayer classification.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A general VAT taxpayer exporting intraocular acrylic lenses from mainland China, including sales to Taiwan, is eligible for the 0% VAT export rate, with input VAT refundable, provided the goods are physically exported from mainland Chinese customs territory; the export is made by the WFOE with a valid customs export declaration; the export sale is supported by an export invoice, sales contract, transport document, and input VAT special invoices; and the export proceeds are received from the Taiwan or other overseas buyer in foreign exchange, repatriated to China through a designated foreign exchange bank, and recorded in the SAFE trade-in-goods system. The Taiwan buyer is generally liable for Taiwan import business tax at importation, and the WFOE does not acquire a Taiwan indirect-tax registration solely by exporting goods to Taiwan unless it creates a fixed place of business in Taiwan.

  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing physical departure from mainland China

China operates the Golden Tax Project Phase IV, requiring all VAT invoices to be issued through the tax-authority-controlled e-invoice system using the taxpayer U-Key or CA certificate. Fully digitalised electronic invoices are generated in the STA electronic invoice service platform and carry a real-time digital anti-counterfeit signature before transmission to the buyer; invoice data, VAT returns, customs export declarations, and SAFE foreign-exchange collection records are automatically cross-matched. A VAT special invoice for input credit is valid only if it has cleared through the national invoice system and is included in the taxpayer invoice verification records.

The STA and China Customs use Golden Tax data analytics to cross-check export invoices, customs declarations, and SAFE foreign-exchange collection data. If export zero-rating or refund substantiation fails, the STA disallows the refund, claws back the refunded VAT, and charges late-payment surcharge at 0.05% per day. Under the Tax Collection Administration Law, fraudulent export tax refund imposes an administrative penalty of one to five times the amount fraudulently refunded; false customs declarations may also trigger Customs penalties. Where the fraudulent amount reaches RMB 50,000, the case is referred for criminal prosecution for fraudulent export tax refund, carrying imprisonment and heavy fines, with life imprisonment for especially large amounts. The recovery period for fraudulent refund claims is unlimited, while non-fraud errors are generally subject to the statutory three-year assessment limitation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a dual-tier foreign exchange convertibility regime. The RMB is fully convertible for current account transactions, including cross-border trade in goods, services, trade-related insurance, and profit remittances for this CN_WFOE_LTD, subject to genuine transaction background and SAFE-verified documentation. Capital account items remain tightly controlled. The Taiwan parent's registered capital contribution is classified as foreign direct investment and must be registered with SAFE, released through the FDI capital foreign-exchange account, and settled into RMB only against real operating expenses under the real-transaction settlement principle. Profit remittances are permissible after the annual audit is completed, corporate income tax is paid, and SAFE declaration formalities are fulfilled; the WFOE must demonstrate actual business substance, submit audited Chinese GAAP financial statements, and obtain a tax clearance certificate from the PRC tax authority. Export proceeds from the sale of intraocular acrylic lenses are current account items and may be settled voluntarily, but the foreign-exchange income must be repatriated to China and reported in the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. Major hurdles arise where capital injections exceed the registered FDI amount, where shareholder loans are used to circumvent capital controls, or where cross-border guarantees create outbound contingent liabilities requiring prior SAFE filing or registration.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Administration Rules, export proceeds arising from customs-cleared exports must be repatriated to China and credited to the exporter's domestic foreign-exchange settlement account or foreign-exchange account within 180 days from the date of export customs declaration, unless SAFE grants an extension for documented force majeure, payment dispute, or abnormal trade-cycle reasons. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0% for Class A exporters, who may retain the foreign currency or voluntarily sell it to a domestic bank at market rates. For Class B or C exporters, SAFE may impose enhanced verification, require full supporting documentation for every collection, and in practice require 100% purchase-and-surrender or a verifiable settlement justification for each export receipt until the enterprise's compliance status is upgraded.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally barred as primary banking rails. Offshore digital EMIs cannot open a domestic RMB current account, cannot hold a SAFE-registered FDI capital account, cannot perform foreign exchange settlement under China's Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, and cannot issue or confirm commercial letters of credit under PRC banking law. Such platforms may be used only for ancillary cross-border receivables collection through a licensed PRC third-party payment institution, subject to settlement limits and full sweeping to the onshore Tier-1 bank account. Any attempt to operate the WFOE's corporate treasury, capital injection, or trade settlement exclusively through an offshore EMI would create an unlawful unlicensed payment structure and cause SAFE and PBOC to reject the account relationship.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low and legally barred as primary banking rails. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (contemporaneous documentation) is mandatory if any of the following thresholds are met in a fiscal year: RMB 200 million for related-party tangible asset transfers; RMB 100 million for financial asset transfers; RMB 100 million for intangible asset transfers; or RMB 40 million for other related-party transactions (including service fees, interest, and royalties). A Master File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion.

Economic substance scope: Under PRC CIT Law Art. 47, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) and the doctrine of substance-over-form apply. The tax authority may reclassify or disregard an arrangement lacking economic substance. For a WFOE engaged in high-precision manufacturing, Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) — including production planning, quality assurance, key equipment operation, and supply-chain decisions — must be performed within China to justify the profit attributable to the WFOE. If the Taiwan parent retains decision-making power over critical functions and risks without compensating the WFOE adequately, the tax authority may allocate a higher share of profits to the WFOE under the profit split method. Additionally, under the 2017 Transfer Pricing Circular (Bulletin No. 6 of 2017), the WFOE must prepare annual TP documentation and include a TP analysis on related-party transactions in the annual filing.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Under the Levying and Collection of Tax Law (TAL) Art. 44, the tax authority may apply a deemed profit method and adjust the entity's taxable income; additionally, a penalty of RMB 10,000 or more may be imposed under TAL Art. 62 for failure to keep conforming books. If the documented data is false, the adjustment is treated as evasion under TAL Art. 63 – the taxpayer is liable for 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax, plus daily overdue surcharge at 0.05%.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly signed or instructed the preparation of false transfer pricing files are personally liable for the resultant underpaid tax and penalties. Civil asset recovery may be enforced against a director's personal assets if the company is used as a mere instrument ('piercing the corporate veil' under PRC Company Law Art. 20 / 2023 Art. 23). The director may be banned from leaving China (travel ban) under the exit control mechanism, and criminal liability for tax evasion under Criminal Law Art. 201 – up to 7 years' imprisonment – may attach.
False Invoicing / VAT Fraud

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Art. 205 imposes a fine ranging from RMB 50,000 up to 5 times the tax amount involved on the entity; for persons directly in charge, life imprisonment can be imposed for particularly large amounts. Tax authorities may also invoke TAL Art. 63, imposing 50%–5 times the underpaid tax and full denial of the claimed deductions. If the scheme involves export refund fraud, additional penalties under the Export Tax Refund Administration rules apply (Art. 22 of the State Council Order No. 401).

Director personal liability: Directors, CFOs, or tax agents who orchestrated the false invoice scheme are directly exposed to criminal liability for tax evasion and invoice fraud. Corporate veil piercing is readily applied if bank accounts or assets were commingled with personal accounts. A director's personal property may be seized for unpaid taxes and criminal fines. A travel ban and suspension from serving as a corporate officer are mandatory court measures; imprisonment of up to life can be imposed for leaders.
Illegal Profit Repatriation / WHT Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under CIT Law Art. 37, the WFOE is jointly and severally liable for the withholding obligation. Failure to withhold triggers a penalty of 50% to 3 times the tax that was not withheld under TAL Art. 69, plus the unpaid WHT itself and daily surcharge. If the conduit arrangement is detected, the GAAR (CIT Law Art. 47) will recharacterise the Hong Kong entity as a shell – denying treaty benefits and treating the entire amount as a Chinese-source dividend subject to 10% WHT, plus an additional 50% penalty for fraud/evasion.

Director personal liability: For design or facilitation of the non-withholding scheme, directors are personally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties. Courts may pierce the veil if the company acts as an alter ego of the Taiwan shareholder, allowing creditors or the tax authority to satisfy the department's claims out of directors' personal assets. Directors who signed the dividend resolution or foreign exchange transfers may additionally face criminal prosecution for tax evasion (Art. 201) and for illegal foreign exchange dealings (Art. 175 / 225), carrying imprisonment of 3 to 10 years and substantial fines. They are also subject to a court-ordered travel ban and blacklisting in personal credit records.
