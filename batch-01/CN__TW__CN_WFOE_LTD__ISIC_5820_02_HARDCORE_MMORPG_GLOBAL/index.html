Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The Taiwan Company Act imposes no minimum paid-in capital for limited liability companies. However, the subscribed capital must be actually paid in cash into a Taiwan bank account in the company name and verified by a Taiwan certified public accountant before the establishment registration is completed. The capital received from the foreign shareholder must match the amount approved by the Investment Commission. The funds are not permanently blocked after registration, but they must be held in a designated account until the CPA capital verification report is issued, and the inward remittance must be declared to the Central Bank of Taiwan.

Funding & unlock path: Not Applicable for an industrial civil-defense manufacturing or warehouse permit. For the leased commercial office, obtain the Fire Safety Equipment Inspection Certificate and formulate a fire safety management plan as required by the Fire Services Act. No structural load certification is required for a software publishing office beyond the landlord's building occupancy certificate, and the company must comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Act for routine workplace safety and fire drills.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered through the Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) online portal at www.chinatax.gov.cn; local tax filing is under the STA branch at the WFOE's statutory incorporation address.

E-government filing portal

For a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) incorporated in mainland China, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) serves as the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and VAT registration number; no separate TIN application is required. The WFOE must then complete taxpayer registration information through the STA e-Tax portal, file for VAT General Taxpayer status where annual turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000 or on an elective basis, and complete export tax refund/zero-rating registration if it intends to export services or goods. A foreign non-resident entity (including a Taiwan-resident parent) that has no PRC permanent establishment must separately complete Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Registration through the STA e-Tax system; the STA assigns a non-resident Tax Identification Number used for Chinese withholding obligations (dividends, royalties, service fees). The Taiwan-resident parent must provide a certificate of its foreign tax identification and, where applicable, a Certificate of Residence for treaty benefits before any PRC withholding is computed.

The WFOE must then complete taxpayer registration information through the STA e-Tax portal, file for VAT General Taxpayer status where annual turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000 or on an elective basis, and complete export tax refund/zero-rating registration if it intends to export services or goods. Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The People's Republic of China does not provide a check-the-box election, partnership pass-through election, or hybrid entity classification election for a WFOE. Enterprise Income Tax is levied on the entity as a resident taxpayer on worldwide income; controlled foreign corporation and hybrid mismatch anti-avoidance rules apply mandatorily. Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 0 - a mainland China-incorporated WFOE must register for VAT upon commencement of taxable activity; RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover is the statutory threshold above which VAT General Taxpayer registration is mandatory, and below which Small-Scale Taxpayer status applies unless the WFOE elects General Taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical software carriers sold overseas, 0% VAT applies to goods exported from mainland China where a customs export declaration is accepted and export proceeds are repatriated in foreign currency. For digital delivery of the MMORPG and in-app purchases through Apple App Store and Google Play, zero-rating is available where the services or digital content are supplied to customers outside mainland China, are wholly consumed outside the customs territory, are paid for with foreign currency, and are supported by platform payout documentation. The WFOE must demonstrate that the sale is an export of services rather than a domestic supply; absence of an overseas end-customer or foreign-currency consideration will reclassify the supply as taxable at the applicable domestic VAT rate. Pure digital transactions do not require customs clearance, but the exporter must retain platform sales/payout evidence and cross-border bank receipts.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) - export goods customs declaration for physical software carriers, with the customs office endorsement and unique customs declaration number; not applicable to pure digital downloads
  • Bill of Lading or airway waybill - evidencing physical delivery of exported goods to the overseas consignee; omitted where the export is purely digital
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) - or, where the foreign exchange administration requires it, the bank-issued Cross-Border Receipts Declaration - evidencing repatriation and settlement of foreign-currency consideration through a Chinese bank
  • Export contract or online distribution agreement with Apple App Store and Google Play showing overseas territories, user market, and revenue-sharing mechanism
  • Apple App Store and Google Play payout statements and financial dashboard reports for the tax period, reconciling gross sales, platform commissions, and net remittance to the WFOE
  • Software copyright registration certificate or intellectual property ownership/royalty documentation (supplementary evidence for intangible export treatment)

All VAT invoices (including Special VAT Invoices and digital VAT invoices) must be issued through the STA-approved e-Invoice Service integrated with the Golden Tax System Phase IV. Invoice data are encrypted, assigned a unique tax invoice code, and transmitted to the STA in real time before the counterparty may claim input VAT credit. B2B purchasers cannot deduct input VAT without an STA-cleared electronic invoice matching the transaction. Export zero-rated sales must be invoiced through the same e-Invoice platform using the export sales tariff code and supporting export documentation; unregistered offline invoicing is prohibited.

The STA may audit export zero-rating applications through the Golden Tax System cross-matching customs, foreign exchange, invoice, and platform payment data. If documentation is incomplete, the sale is consumed in mainland China, or foreign-currency receipts are not repatriated within 180 days, the WFOE loses zero-rating and must output VAT at the applicable domestic rate (13% or, for qualifying electronic services, 6%), plus daily late-payment surcharges of 0.05% of the unpaid tax and an administrative penalty ranging from 50% to five times the tax underpaid under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Serious cases involving fabricated documents or false export refund claims are subject to criminal prosecution for defrauding export tax rebates under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China, with fines and imprisonment, confiscation of the fraudulently obtained refund, and mandatory reversal of any input VAT previously credited.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Taiwan operates a managed-float foreign exchange regime with liberalised current-account convertibility and regulated capital-account convertibility. The Taiwan WFOE will be permitted to receive payments for digital game publishing, settle royalties, hold foreign-currency deposits, and convert New Taiwan dollars at market rates through Authorized Banks without a bureaucratic exchange allocation mechanism. However, the capital account is not fully open: inbound equity contributions for the WFOE must be approved by the competent foreign-investment authority and matched against the approved investment amount before conversion into TWD. Export receipts, including app-store proceeds from Apple and Google, are treated as service export earnings and must be evidenced by platform payout statements, underlying contracts, and proper foreign-exchange declarations; there is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio. Profit remittance to overseas shareholders is feasible after delivering audited financial statements, tax payment confirmation, corporate board resolutions, and documentary proof of the underlying distributed profit, with the primary obstacle being evidentiary completeness rather than rationing. For an ISIC 5820 hardcore MMORPG global operation, the key convertibility sensitivity lies in proving that income from Taiwan-source players is properly separated from cross-border software sales and that royalties paid to the mainland China parent or related technology licensor are at arm's length. The Central Bank requires each settlement to be classified under the correct current-account or capital-account purpose code, and banks will reject unsupported FX transactions that resemble round-tripping, fake cross-strait trade, or unapproved financing. Overall, the regime is workable for a professionally structured WFOE, but capital injections, shareholder loans, and any TWD hedging transactions must be pre-documented and filed with the Authorized Bank before execution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan does not impose a statutory percentage surrender requirement for export proceeds. Export foreign-currency receipts may be retained in foreign-currency deposit accounts at Authorized Banks, but they must be declared and settled through the bank in accordance with the underlying trade or service contract, normally within the agreed credit term, commonly under 180 days from invoice or service completion. For digital game publishing revenue received from Apple App Store and Google Play, the WFOE must maintain the platform payout statement, merchant agreement, and tax invoice to demonstrate service export character; conversion into TWD is optional and executed at the prevailing market rate once the bank has recorded the foreign-exchange declaration.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), acting through its Foreign Exchange Administration, under the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act and supervised through Authorized Foreign Exchange Banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally unsuitable as the primary treasury spine. Taiwan's Foreign Exchange Regulation Act confines foreign-exchange dealing, FX deposit-taking, trade settlement declaration, and remittance reporting to Authorized Banks supervised by the Central Bank. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed Authorized Banks for Taiwan domestic FX purposes, cannot hold the WFOE's statutory capital escrow, cannot issue capital verification certificates, and cannot lawfully convert TWD or process Taiwan-source export settlement. A fintech EMI may be used only as an auxiliary collection wallet in a foreign jurisdiction for non-Taiwan ancillary transactions, but it must not be used for capital subscription, dividend remittance, statutory audit confirmation, or the main USD payouts coming from Apple and Google. The recommended solution is to route all material inbound and outbound FX through Bank of Taiwan or Mega International Commercial Bank, reserving the fintech layer strictly for small operational payments outside Taiwan where such EMI services are locally permitted.
