Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum registered capital applies for a Wholly Foreign-Owned Limited Liability Company in this ordinary wholesale sector. E-government filing portal

For a Taiwan-established company, complete company establishment and obtain the company registration documents, then apply to the competent National Taxation Bureau for tax registration and a business administration number before commencing business. A foreign company establishing a Taiwan branch must first complete branch registration with the competent authority and then register for tax. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold exempts an ordinary company conducting taxable business from business registration and tax registration. The NTD 600,000 annual sales threshold commonly referenced for certain foreign electronic service suppliers is not a general threshold for this goods-trading activity. Confirm registration obligations against the taxpayer's actual Taiwan presence and activities.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Qualifying exports of goods may be zero-rated if the statutory export conditions are met and the taxpayer retains evidence linking the sale to the goods' export from Taiwan. Certain services supplied for use outside Taiwan may qualify subject to statutory conditions. A mainland China export is not a Taiwan export merely because the entity or task record refers to Taiwan; the transaction's actual supplier, place of supply, customs movement, and taxpayer registration determine treatment.

  • Taiwan export declaration or other customs evidence showing export clearance and identifying the exported goods
  • Commercial invoice, sales contract, and shipping records, including bill of lading or air waybill, sufficient to link the export to the zero-rated sale
  • Evidence of payment and settlement retained under applicable accounting and tax-record rules; an inward remittance certificate is not a universally prescribed condition for Taiwan export zero-rating

Taiwan uses Government Uniform Invoices, including electronic invoices for taxpayers subject to the applicable e-invoice rules. Invoice issuance, transmission, and reporting are governed by Ministry of Finance requirements and applicable taxpayer/category rules. Taiwan does not impose a universal real-time pre-clearance or digital-stamping regime equivalent to systems that require tax-authority clearance before every invoice is issued.

Taiwan does not impose a general 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation deadline as a condition of export zero-rating. Unsupported or incorrectly claimed zero-rating may result in assessment of business tax, interest, administrative penalties, and recovery of improperly obtained refunds under applicable tax laws. False documents or intentional tax evasion may trigger more severe statutory sanctions. Penalties depend on the facts, applicable provisions, and any correction or disclosure procedures.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Renminbi is fully convertible for current-account items (goods trade, services, and investment income including dividends) subject to genuine-transaction verification, documentary evidence, and BOP declaration; capital-account items remain controlled and conditional: inbound FDI capital contributions are permitted against SAFE registration, outbound capital (equity transfer proceeds, capital reduction, liquidation distributions, external debt repayment) requires prior SAFE registration or quota approval, and all capital-account settlements follow the discretionary settlement with a negative list (no securities investment, no entrusted loans, no real-estate use outside prescribed scope). For profit remittance to a Taiwan shareholder, the legal hurdles are: (i) a PRC CPA audited statutory financial report for the fiscal year ending, (ii) a valid profit-distribution resolution of the board/shareholders, (iii) proof of 10% dividend withholding tax paid to the PRC tax authority on the gross dividend (the statutory reduced rate for non-resident passive income; the Cross-Strait Tax Cooperation Agreement, though signed, is not effectively in force in practice, so the standard 10% rate applies without a recognized treaty reduction to 5%), and (iv) execution of the remittance through the current-account investment-income BOP category with SAFE/currency-monitoring system verification; dividend remittance is not subject to capital-account licensing but banks will refuse payment where tax clearance or audit evidence is incomplete.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished all mandatory foreign-exchange surrender and mandatory conversion ratios; the exporter may hold export receipts in its current-account FX settlement account up to the SAFE-assigned quota or settle them into RMB at the market rate at its discretion. Statutory repatriation obligations remain binding: export proceeds must be received in China, as a general rule, no later than 90 days after the customs export declaration date; where collection is later than 90 days after customs declaration, the exporter must file a deferred-receipts report (延期收款报告) in the SAFE trade-credit monitoring system within 30 days after the actual receipt; where collection is received more than 30 days before shipment, an advance-receipts report (预收货款报告) may be required for amounts exceeding the USD 50,000 equivalent threshold; banks refuse or escalate simplified settlement where the monitoring system flags overdue or unmatched receipts against customs records, and the trade-credit reporting obligation is a precondition to repeated settlement of goods-trade FX transactions.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行) as the monetary authority, with the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局) as the exclusive administrative authority for foreign-exchange regulation, cross-border receipts and payments, BOP reporting, trade-credit monitoring, and FDI capital registration; SAFE's regulatory powers for goods-trade and capital flows are delegated to commercial banks acting as direct agents under the 'bank handling' (银行直接办理) regime, requiring every cross-border flow to be authenticated against customs and SAFE monitoring systems before execution.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for purely offshore digital EMI and virtual-account providers (Airwallex, Currencycloud, Wise Business, or non-PRC neobanks) used as the primary collection or disbursement rail for the WFOE; such structures bypass the SAFE goods-trade customs-verification chain, produce unverified cross-border receipts in a foreign entity account of a PRC resident exporter, trigger monitoring flags for inconsistent BOP reporting and customs-data mismatch, and expose the exporter to administrative penalties up to 30% of the unlawful foreign-exchange amount or criminal exposure for illegal FX business and evasion of verification. Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for PRC-licensed cross-border payment fintechs (e.g., LianLian Global, PingPong, XTransfer and comparable PBOC/SAFE-licensed entities) acting as a supplementary layer that provides transaction matching, trade-document digitization, and FX advisory, so long as the actual settlement leg is executed through a licensed onshore custodian commercial bank; for bulk dehydrated garlic and chili pepper export settled by LC or T/T wire, the onshore custodian bank remains the mandatory clearing counterparty and the fintech layer only accelerates reconciliation. Recommendation: do not route export proceeds to any offshore account of the WFOE or any related offshore entity; all export receipts must land in the WFOE's onshore current-account FX settlement account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for the Taiwan investor's injection of registered capital into the PRC WFOE: (1) complete the foreign-investment information reporting with the MOFCOM/market-regulation system and obtain the unified social credit code-linked foreign-investment record; (2) execute SAFE FDI registration through the receiving bank's direct-dealing system to obtain the SAFE business-registration voucher; (3) open a dedicated foreign-currency capital FX account at the Tier-1 custodian bank, distinct from the current-account FX settlement account; (4) the Taiwan parent remits via SWIFT MT103 with remittance field specifying 'investment in registered capital' and the receiving bank performs the BOP receipts declaration under the direct-investment capital-increase code; (5) after receipt, obtain a PRC CPA capital-verification report confirming paid-in capital; (6) settle the capital into RMB under discretionary settlement with a negative list, executing a payment-instruction letter (支付命令函) for each deployment into permitted operating uses (procurement of garlic and chili raw materials, processing, rent, wages, domestic logistics), with funds prohibited from securities investment, entrusted loans, or real-estate use outside prescribed scope; (D) any management fee, royalty, external-debt interest, or other service remittance requires its own contract-based current-account verification or SAFE registration before execution, and all outward remittances are screened for sanctions, round-tripping, and consistency with the enterprise's registered business scope and customs data.Onshore deliverable FX forward contracts (远期结售汇) executed with the custodian bank against genuine and quantifiable export-receipt and import-payment exposures under SAFE's real-trade-background aggregation rules, with maturities generally capped at 12 months and no cash-settlement without actual trade flows.FX currency swaps (外汇掉期) between the WFOE and the custodian bank to manage temporary liquidity mismatches between USD and RMB balances arising from the timing differences of export collections and import disbursements.Vanilla RMB-versus-FX currency option combinations (risk-reversal, call/put spreads) with real trade exposure, full upfront premium settlement, and bank-policy loss-limit covenants; the options must be exercised or cash-settled only against actual FX flows and cannot be used as leveraged speculation.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) strictly limited to hedging principal and interest service of registered SAFE external debts or long-term FX liabilities, never as a standalone speculative derivative for an entity without qualifying FX liabilities.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are NOT permissible for an onshore PRC corporate as its primary hedge instrument; NDF usage is confined to the offshore interbank market and ring-fenced tail-risk structures of the PRC bank's own offshore book, so the WFOE must use deliverable forwards and vanilla options in the onshore market for all trade-related hedging.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW for purely offshore digital EMI and virtual-account providers (Airwallex, Currencycloud, Wise Business, or non-PRC neobanks) used as the primary collection or disbursement rail for the WFOE; such structures bypass the SAFE goods-trade customs-verification chain, produce unverified cross-border receipts in a foreign entity account of a PRC resident exporter, trigger monitoring flags for inconsistent BOP reporting and customs-data mismatch, and expose the exporter to administrative penalties up to 30% of the unlawful foreign-exchange amount or criminal exposure for illegal FX business and evasion of verification. Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for PRC-licensed cross-border payment fintechs (e.g., LianLian Global, PingPong, XTransfer and comparable PBOC/SAFE-licensed entities) acting as a supplementary layer that provides transaction matching, trade-document digitization, and FX advisory, so long as the actual settlement leg is executed through a licensed onshore custodian commercial bank; for bulk dehydrated garlic and chili pepper export settled by LC or T/T wire, the onshore custodian bank remains the mandatory clearing counterparty and the fintech layer only accelerates reconciliation. Recommendation: do not route export proceeds to any offshore account of the WFOE or any related offshore entity; all export receipts must land in the WFOE's onshore current-account FX settlement account.
