Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, acting through provincial and municipal tax services. The online electronic portal is the STA National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax), including the export tax refund module.

E-government filing portal

For the PRC WFOE: (1) obtain establishment registration from the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and receive the business license showing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is the mandatory Chinese Tax Identification Number; (2) within 30 days after issuance of the business license, complete tax registration through the local STA tax service office or the Electronic Tax Bureau platform, linking the USCC to Corporate Income Tax, VAT, stamp tax, and withholding obligations; (3) if annual taxable VAT sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, apply for mandatory general VAT taxpayer status, or elect general taxpayer status voluntarily when eligible; (4) apply for the electronic tax certificate and digital certificate needed to access e-Tax, issue invoices, and file export tax refund declarations; (5) for the non-resident Taiwan parent or other foreign non-resident entity without a Chinese permanent establishment, a separate Chinese TIN is not required unless the entity derives China-source income; in that case, non-resident tax registration and withholding registration must be completed with the STA branch administering the payer or the income source.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise subject to Corporate Income Tax on worldwide income, is not a pass-through entity, and is not located in a tax-exempt zone. Non-resident shareholders remain subject to PRC dividend withholding tax on distributions.

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election for entity classification. The only available tax-status election under PRC law is the VAT classification election between small-scale taxpayer and general VAT taxpayer, which does not alter entity-level income tax treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT registration is mandatory for all newly formed WFOEs from the date of establishment; no monetary exemption applies. Compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000. Below the threshold, the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer using the simplified VAT collection method.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from China are eligible for VAT zero-rating and export tax refund only when (a) the goods are physically exported and released under a PRC Customs export declaration; (b) the exporter is the PRC WFOE and holds general VAT taxpayer status with corresponding VAT special invoices for purchases; (c) export proceeds are repatriated in accordance with State Administration of Foreign Exchange rules; and (d) the goods are not on the prohibited-export list. Cross-border e-commerce export modes recognized by customs are available for oversized home-furniture shipments, including direct B2C, B2B, and overseas-warehouse modes, and may use simplified export clearance and refund procedures. Offshore digital services are not part of this goods-based entity; China applies separate rules to qualifying cross-border digital services.

  • Customs Export Declaration (official PRC export goods customs declaration record)
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or international logistics proof of export shipment
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List for the exported furniture
  • Export sales contract or purchase order, if required for customs valuation
  • Bank receipt or foreign exchange settlement record evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Export tax-refund declaration data filed through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau export tax refund module

VAT invoices must be issued through the STA-approved electronic invoice platform; the Golden Tax Phase IV system receives invoice data in near real time. Fully digitalized electronic invoices are recognized and carry a unique invoice number and an anti-counterfeit tax control code or QR code. General taxpayers may use API-based integration for ERP-to-tax-system transmission. Special VAT invoices used for input tax credit are verified in the national VAT invoice verification database. There is no separate physical fiscal stamp; the STA digital system performs anti-fraud validation.

The STA, with General Administration of Customs data, automatically cross-checks export declarations, logistics, VAT invoices, and foreign exchange receipts. If a zero-rating or export tax refund claim is fraudulent or lacks substantiation, the tax authority must recover the refunded amount, impose a late-payment surcharge, and levy a penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax evaded under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law. Serious cases constitute criminal tax fraud and may lead to criminal liability, suspension or revocation of export tax refund qualifications, and extended retrospective clawback generally up to five years for fraudulent acts.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is conditionally convertible under China's managed floating exchange-rate regime. Current-account transactions, including e-commerce sale of furniture, freight, warehousing, platform fees, and ordinary trade payments, are generally freely convertible; the bank must still perform a veracity check against the commercial contract, invoice, customs declaration, and logistics evidence. Capital-account transactions remain under strict SAFE control. An inbound equity investment from Taiwan must first complete the FDI foreign exchange registration; the contribution is then received into a foreign exchange capital account and can be settled into RMB under the corporate negative-list rules for permitted working-capital expenditures. Profit remittance to the Taiwan parent is lawful only after the WFOE's annual corporate income tax settlement, based on an audited distributable profit amount, with proof of withholding tax and no past-due SAFE reporting. Repatriation of registered capital beyond accumulated profits requires capital reduction, deregistration, and prior SAFE approval. Surplus funds cannot be used for speculative cross-border transfers. All FX flows must be supported by underlying business truthfulness; a bank that detects documentation mismatches will freeze the payment and report to SAFE. Therefore, treasury planning must separate current-account receipts, capital-account injections, and dividend outflows into distinct audit trails.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China has abolished the historical forced surrender ratio for export goods in foreign currency. The WFOE may keep USD or RMB export proceeds in a current-account foreign exchange account or convert the entire amount into RMB at the onshore spot rate; no minimum conversion percentage is mandated. The statutory discipline is receipt matching and reporting: if the export proceeds do not arrive within 90 days from the customs export date, the enterprise must file a deferred-collection trade credit report with SAFE, usually by the 30th day after the 90-day threshold. If the delay exceeds 180 days, the bank will treat the transaction as a high-risk anomaly and may require item-by-item proof of collection schedule. For cross-border e-commerce, the WFOE must reconcile platform settlements with customs e-commerce lists and cannot leave export receipts offshore without SAFE approval.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) as the central bank, acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) as the delegated foreign-exchange administration; the Taiwan-side outward remittance approval is governed by the Central Bank of Taiwan (CBC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Fintech EMIs are not legally viable as a primary treasury channel for a PRC WFOE. Offshore digital banks and electronic money institutions such as Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, and Airwallex do not hold Chinese banking licenses and cannot open onshore corporate settlement accounts, hold client RMB deposits, or perform capital account verification in China. PBOC and SAFE restrict corporate account takeover and client fund custody to licensed Chinese commercial banks and qualified clearing institutions. A small number of licensed cross-border payment institutions may handle export collection for e-commerce, but they are sub-channels and cannot substitute for the Tier-1 domestic bank account required for statutory capital, tax payment, payroll, and profit repatriation. Therefore, the blueprint should use Tier-1 Chinese banks as the primary rail and integrate fintech platforms only as downstream collection and card-settlement tools.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must first obtain the outward remittance approval or facility headroom required by the Central Bank of Taiwan, then transmit USD or RMB via SWIFT MT103 directly to the WFOE's foreign exchange capital account at a Chinese Tier-1 bank. The bank will verify the SAFE FDI registration code and match the remittance amount against the approved registered capital. The Field 70 remittance information should carry the SAFE registration number and the China BOP purpose code for inbound direct investment equity injection. After receipt, the bank performs capital-account income verification and may allow the funds to be converted to RMB on a willing-settlement basis for permitted operating expenditures. If the local administration for market regulation or the bank's internal policy requests an accounting confirmation, a certified public accountant capital verification report or a funds-in statement audited by a licensed CPA must be added before excepted payments.For domestic supplier and freight payments, the WFOE should use RMB transfers through the Chinese national payment clearing system. For cross-border payments to foreign factories, carriers, or overseas warehouses, permitted rails include SWIFT MT103 for direct payment of confirmed invoices, commercial letters of credit (sight or usance) for large-container furniture purchases, and documentary collections (D/P or D/A) for moderate-risk counterparties. The bank will require the underlying trade contract, pro-forma or commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, customs declaration, and if applicable the insurance certificate before booking the FX. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on gross dividends paid by a China resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder. Under the Cross-Strait Agreement on Avoidance of Double Taxation on Income, the rate is reduced to 5% if the Taiwan beneficial owner is a resident of Taiwan and holds directly not less than 25% of the paid-up capital of the WFOE; otherwise 10% remains applicable.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File documentation is required under SAT Announcement 2016 No. 42 when annual related-party transactions exceed RMB 200 million for tangible assets, RMB 100 million for intangible assets, RMB 100 million for financial asset transactions, or RMB 40 million for provision or receipt of services. The Master File is required when the multinational group had consolidated revenue in the preceding year of at least RMB 5.5 billion and the enterprise engaged in related-party transactions. The Country-by-Country Report is required at the same RMB 5.5 billion group revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: China applies the substance-over-form doctrine and the general anti-avoidance rule under CIT Law Article 47. For Cross-Strait treaty benefits, the Taiwan shareholder must be the beneficial owner and must not be a shell or conduit. The economic substance analysis tests whether the Taiwan entity performs core income-generating activities, has real employees, premises, decision-making authority, asset ownership and risk assumption. Formal registration alone is insufficient; a paper entity used only to access the 5% dividend or 7% royalty rate will be disregarded and full domestic withholding will be applied with penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 41, 44, 47 and 48; Tax Collection and Administration Law Articles 35 and 63. Tax authorities can reassess income by arm's-length pricing, add 0.05% daily late-payment interest, and impose a penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid CIT. Where the evasion exceeds the criminal threshold, Criminal Law Article 201 imposes criminal fines and imprisonment up to 7 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false transfer pricing contracts or caused the understatement of income may have the corporate veil pierced under PRC Company Law Article 20; they can be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and restitution. Criminal responsibility attaches to the persons who organized and directed the evasion, which can include imprisonment and criminal fines. The director may be placed on the major tax violation blacklist, triggering travel bans, exit restrictions and denial of government procurement or banking facilities.
Withholding Tax Violation on Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Articles 37 and 40; TCAL Article 69. The withholding agent is liable for the undeclared withholding tax and subject to a penalty of 50% to 300% of the tax that should have been withheld. TCAL Article 62 additionally allows a penalty up to RMB 10,000 for failure to file the withholding return. The tax authority may also collect the unpaid tax directly from the Taiwan shareholder beneficiary.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly approved a dividend declaration without legal retained earnings or without withholding and remitting the WHT may be personally liable to compensate the company for losses under PRC Company Law Article 149. Where company assets are diverted abroad leaving creditors unpaid, courts may pierce the limited-liability veil and impose personal asset restitution. Serious failures involving concealment of taxable income are criminal tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201; the responsible director faces imprisonment up to 7 years, criminal fines, blacklisting and exit bans.
Treaty Abuse and Shell Company Arrangement

Statutory sanction: CIT Law Article 47 and SAT Announcement 2018 No. 5. Treaty benefits are denied, the shell is disregarded, and the income is recharacterized as taxable to the China WFOE or otherwise subject to full 10% WHT, plus late-payment interest and a 50% to 500% evasion penalty. If the royalty payments are supported by fraudulent invoices, Criminal Law Article 205 on false invoicing may apply.

Director personal liability: Directors who created and operated the sham structure can be criminally liable for organizing tax evasion under Criminal Law Article 201, with imprisonment up to 7 years and fines. Civilly, the corporate veil is pierceable because the shell is used to abuse legal form; directors may be ordered to return misappropriated royalty or dividend monies from personal assets. The responsible directors may be blacklisted as major non-compliant taxpayers, effectively barring cross-border travel and freezing bank accounts until the tax liability is settled.
