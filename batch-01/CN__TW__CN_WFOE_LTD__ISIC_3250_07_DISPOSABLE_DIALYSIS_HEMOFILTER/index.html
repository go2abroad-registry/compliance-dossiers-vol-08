Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Taiwan limited liability company. However, the capital amount stated in the Investment Commission approval letter must be actually paid in and deposited into a bank account opened in Taiwan in the name of the company before the incorporation registration is filed. A bank certificate of paid-in capital must be produced. No separate blocked escrow account requirement is imposed by Taiwan law; however, withdrawal or repayment of capital prior to lawful registration may render the registration defective and subject the directors to administrative sanctions.

Funding & unlock path: The manufacturing facility must obtain a fire safety equipment inspection certificate and fire safety management plan approval from the local fire department before occupancy, as well as civil defense and emergency evacuation compliance where required by the local municipality. The building must pass structural load and public safety inspection and receive a lawful building use permit. Under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, the employer must implement risk assessment, monitor ethylene oxide and other chemical agents, certify cleanroom pressurization and ventilation systems, provide personal protective equipment, and obtain all necessary workplace safety approvals before operators commence work. No production line may be activated until the fire department, municipal building authority, environmental authority, and occupational safety inspector have confirmed compliance.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online service via the STA's national unified Electronic Tax Bureau (E-Tax) platform.

E-government filing portal

After SAMR business license issuance, the WFOE must complete tax registration on the STA E-Tax platform within 30 days, upload the business license, articles of association, legal representative identity, registered address proof, bank account certificate, and Taiwanese shareholder registration/identity documents. The STA will assign the Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC), which serves as the taxpayer identification number (TIN), VAT registration number, and enterprise income tax registration number. For export operations, the WFOE must also complete foreign trade operator filing with the Ministry of Commerce, customs registration, and SAFE registration. A foreign non-resident entity (including the Taiwanese investor) that earns only China-source withholding-type income is not required to obtain a PRC TIN; the WFOE as withholding agent registers for withholding and files on its behalf. If direct filing is required, the foreign non-resident entity may obtain a temporary TIN from the competent STA tax service office by submitting evidence of China-source income and the relevant withholding agreement.

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable turnover triggers compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration; all newly incorporated WFOEs must complete basic tax registration within 30 days of obtaining the business license irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered VAT general taxpayer exporting physical goods (dialysis hemofilters and similar ISIC 3250 medical consumables) from mainland China to destinations outside the PRC customs territory, including Taiwan, can apply the 0% export VAT rate and export tax rebate under the manufacturing export 'exemption, credit, and refund' method. Eligibility requires actual customs clearance and departure from mainland China, title and risk transfer to the overseas buyer, export sales invoicing, valid foreign trade and customs registrations, no restriction/exception in the export rebate catalogue, and foreign exchange receipts collected through designated banks.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (export rebate special copy) with customs release endorsement
  • Commercial invoice / export sales invoice issued by the WFOE to the Taiwan or overseas buyer
  • Bill of Lading (ocean) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing physical removal of the goods from mainland China customs territory
  • Packing list, export contract, and, where applicable, health/product license for Class II/III medical devices
  • Electronic export proceeds collection/foreign exchange repatriation record transmitted through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) and the designated foreign exchange bank

The STA's Golden Tax Phase IV system and national unified e-invoice platform require invoice data to be transmitted in real time. Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices (FDEIs) are issued through the tax authority's platform with unique fiscal invoice codes and anti-counterfeit QR codes, replacing manual invoice authentication. Large taxpayers must integrate ERP/API interfaces for automated issuance and fiscal clearance; VAT special invoices require digital certificates and are subject to centralized VAT invoice management verification.

The STA crosschecks export data through Golden Tax Phase IV against customs release information and SAFE foreign exchange receipts. If export documentation is false, goods do not actually leave the customs territory, or export proceeds are not repatriated by the 180-day deadline, zero-rating is revoked, VAT refunds or credits are clawed back with overdue interest, and a fine from 50% to 500% of the evaded tax may be imposed under Article 63 of the Law on Administration of Tax Levy and Collection. Fraudulent export tax refund is a criminal offence under Article 204 of the Criminal Law; false customs declarations are additionally subject to penalties under the Customs Law and SAFE foreign exchange administration rules.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with strict capital-account controls. The Renminbi (RMB) is convertible for current-account transactions when genuine trade or service documentation is presented, but every capital-account transaction requires prior SAFE approval, filing, or registration. A Taiwan-owned WFOE manufacturing dialysis consumables must obtain an FDI Business Registration from SAFE, open an FDI capital account, and instruct the remitting bank to credit the equity injection as an FDI equity investment before any use of the funds is permitted. RMB converted from capital contributions may then be used for working capital and procurement, subject to the negative-list rules and post-transaction monitoring. Profit remittance to a Taiwan shareholder is treated as a current-account outflow requiring audited financial statements, proof of profit distribution, tax payment vouchers, and a positive annual SAFE foreign-exchange compliance inspection. Full capital-account convertibility is unavailable; direct cross-border capital repayments, refunds, and share transfers need separate authorisations. Under the current goods-trade framework, export receipts must be repatriated into domestic foreign-exchange accounts, but no mandatory conversion percentage exists for ordinary trade enterprises, allowing the WFOE to settle or retain foreign currency. Penalties, restrictive B/C status, and enhanced monitoring are imposed if capital account codes are misused or trade documents are fabricated.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's goods-trade foreign-exchange monitoring, export proceeds must be repatriated to a designated domestic bank account within 180 days of customs declaration, or within an approved extended period for long-conversion contracts. The mandatory conversion ratio is 0% for an A-class enterprise: 100% of export foreign-currency receipts may be retained in the WFOE's current foreign-exchange account or converted into RMB at the prevailing market rate. B/C-class enterprises may face temporary forced conversion, restricted account scope, and stricter documentary scrutiny until their classification is restored.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low viability for primary corporate banking. PRC foreign-exchange law requires trade receipts, export funds, and capital injections to pass through SAFE-designated bank accounts or licensed payment institutions with SAFE cross-border filings. Unlicensed offshore digital EMIs cannot open a local RMB account, cannot act as an FX window with SAFE, and cannot issue commercial letters of credit. The WFOE may only use an offshore EMI as an ancillary collection tool for small foreign-currency receivables, and all funds must be swept back to a domestic bank account; it is not a compliant substitute for bank clearing or capital-account operations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder wires equity through a SWIFT MT103 into the WFOE's FDI capital account at a qualified China-side bank. The remittance instruction must cite the SAFE FDI registration number and use the purpose code for direct-investment equity injection. Upon receipt, the bank verifies the name, amount, and registration reference, then either retains funds as foreign currency or settles into RMB under the capital-account settlement policy. The WFOE must complete the CPA capital verification and SAFE system update within the statutory reporting window, and must not co-mingle capital funds with ordinary trade receipts.For export shipments of dialysis hemofilters, the WFOE may use SWIFT MT103 for prompt payment transactions under USD, EUR, or TWD trade agreements; commercial documentary letters of credit (SWIFT MT700) for more secure settlement; and documentary collections (D/P and D/A) for established buyer bases. Every clearing method must be processed through the bank's trade-services engine and reconciled with the China Customs electronic export declaration, the commercial invoice, the bill of lading, and the certificate of origin. Imported raw materials, especially polyethersulfone (PES) hollow-fiber stock, should be cleared via inbound MT103 or LC drawings matched to customs import data.Deliverable CNY forward trades against USD or TWD, supported by genuine current-account trade exposure and evidenced by underlying export/import contracts.Zero-cost FX collars combining a long call and short put on CNY/USD to cap adverse movements while preserving some upside.Cross-currency swaps or interest rate swaps linked to an underlying SAFE-registered foreign-currency loan only, with matching maturity and principal notional.Onshore RMB FX options with bank-approved real-demand verification are permissible; onshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for Chinese WFOEs are not permissible, and any offshore CNH NDF activity must be executed outside the onshore account structure.
