Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under the Companies Act, no general statutory minimum paid-in capital applies to a company limited by shares; however, the FSC requires a licensed securities broker (證券經紀商) to maintain paid-in capital of at least NT$200 million (approximately US$6.3 million), fully paid in cash into a dedicated capital account at a Taiwanese bank, verified by a licensed CPA capital-verification report. Funds must be escrowed and remain unwithdrawn until the FSC issues the operating permit; additional statutory operating bond and settlement fund deposits apply under TWSE/TDCC requirements.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Taiwan (R.O.C.) – National Taxation Administration; e-service portal: https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw; e-invoice portal: https://www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw

E-government filing portal

A non-resident entity must establish a fixed place of business (branch) and file an Application for Registration of Profit-Seeking Enterprise with the regional National Taxation Bureau before commencing taxable operations. Submit the joint-venture contract, approval/licence evidence, power of attorney appointing a local tax representative, and lease or place-of-business proof. The Administration verifies the documents and issues the 8-digit Uniform Invoice Number (GUI) for VAT and income tax purposes. If no fixed place of business exists, the foreign entity may obtain a TIN through withholding-based registration and must appoint a withholding agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists in Taiwan. Corporate income tax applies at the entity level under the Income Tax Act. A profit-seeking enterprise may elect, subject to NTA approval, to carry forward net losses against future income for up to 10 years, but this does not create pass-through treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.05%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold applies to businesses with a fixed place of business in Taiwan; registration is mandatory before taxable activity begins. For a non-resident seller of cross-border electronic services to Taiwan resident individuals, VAT registration is required only if annual taxable turnover exceeds NT$480,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to export services sold in Taiwan for use outside Taiwan. For offshore securities brokerage, zero-rating requires: (i) the client is a non-resident juridical person or foreign individual, (ii) the trades are executed on an offshore exchange or otherwise the brokerage service is fully performed outside Taiwan, and (iii) the commission is invoiced in foreign currency and received by inward remittance. The service contract, trade confirmations, and exchange settlement records must demonstrate foreign consumption and the non-resident status of the principal.

  • Customs Export Declaration (required only if the brokerage transaction involves physical securities certificates or goods; otherwise N/A)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (same dual-purpose limitation used as evidence when goods are embedded)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate and Foreign Exchange Settlement Record for the commission, supplemented by the brokerage contract, issuer trade confirmations, and the client's non-resident certification

Taiwan mandates Uniform Invoices (統一發票) for all taxable businesses. Certified e-invoices must be transmitted to the Ministry of Finance E-Invoice Platform through an approved API or the Cloud Invoice APP within 48 hours of issuance. Real-time government fiscal clearance is not used; instead, the system applies digital encryption, GUI embedment, and QR-code verification. Securities brokerage commission income must be invoiced using the prescribed category for financial services and retained in electronic records for inspection.

The National Taxation Bureau may audit zero-rated export brokerage claims within 4 statutory limitation years (extended to 7 years in fraud cases). If the zero-rating claim is denied or fraudulent, the taxpayer must repay the evaded tax, plus interest, and is subject to an administrative penalty of one to five times the evaded amount. Deliberate fraud or evasion is additionally referred for criminal prosecution under Article 41 of the Tax Evasion Act, carrying imprisonment of up to five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The New Taiwan dollar is generally convertible for legitimate current-account transactions, including documented trade payments, service fees, salaries, taxes, interest and dividends. Capital-account transactions, foreign direct investment, capital injections, intercompany loans, securities investments and repatriation of capital remain subject to foreign-exchange declarations, bank documentary review, applicable annual thresholds, and any required regulatory approval. A Taiwan securities-brokerage vehicle must separately satisfy Financial Supervisory Commission licensing, capital, client-asset segregation, prudential, reporting and anti-money-laundering requirements. Dividend and profit remittances normally require audited financial statements, shareholder and corporate approvals, tax clearance or withholding-tax evidence, evidence of distributable profits, and confirmation that regulatory capital and statutory reserves remain compliant. A PRC-origin shareholder or control structure may trigger additional Cross-Strait restrictions, beneficial-owner review and Investment Commission requirements. Offshore digital payment institutions cannot be used to bypass Taiwan banking, foreign-exchange, securities, client-money or AML controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan does not generally impose a universal mandatory conversion ratio requiring all export proceeds to be surrendered into New Taiwan dollars. Exporters must maintain appropriate customs, commercial-invoice, shipping, banking and foreign-exchange records, and banks report or process qualifying foreign-exchange transactions under Central Bank rules. Foreign-currency export receipts may generally be retained in permitted foreign-currency accounts, subject to transaction documentation and bank controls. No single universal statutory conversion deadline applies to every export transaction; the applicable timing depends on the underlying trade, collection method, bank reporting category and current Central Bank rules. The proposed entity is a securities brokerage business rather than an exporter, so export-proceeds rules should not be treated as authority for receiving client securities funds or brokerage revenue.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) for foreign-exchange administration, together with the Financial Supervisory Commission for securities, banking, anti-money-laundering, customer-asset and licensed-brokerage supervision. Cross-Strait investment and remittance matters may also require review or approval by the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs under applicable Taiwan regulations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for core treasury, client-money and securities-settlement functions. An offshore electronic-money institution or digital wallet may support limited corporate operating payments only where its licensing, Taiwan payment-service permissions, correspondent-bank arrangements, transaction limits and AML controls permit. It cannot lawfully replace a Taiwan bank account for regulated brokerage client assets, segregated money, capital injection, securities settlement, foreign-exchange reporting or licensed financial intermediation. Visa and Mastercard acquiring or payout arrangements require licensed acquiring, payment, merchant, consumer-protection, chargeback and AML structures and do not authorize securities brokerage.
