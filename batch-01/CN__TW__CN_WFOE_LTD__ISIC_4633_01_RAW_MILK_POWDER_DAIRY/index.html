Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (Guojia Shuiwu Zongju); the mandated online registration and filing portal is the National Unified Standard Electronic Tax Bureau (Quanguo Tongyi Guifan Dianzi Shuiwuju) accessible at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn; cross-strait import-side tax administration for the Taiwan jurisdiction resides with the Taxation Agency, Ministry of Finance, Taiwan.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE is a Chinese resident legal person. Upon incorporation, the State Administration for Market Regulation issues the Business License bearing the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC, pinyin: Tongyi Shehui Xinyong Daima), which serves as the exclusive statutory Taxpayer Identification Number for income tax, VAT, stamp tax and export tax rebate filings. The foreign/Taiwanese non-resident shareholder is not separately issued a Chinese VAT registration number. Procedure: (1) within 30 calendar days of licence issuance, complete tax registration on the provincial National Unified Standard Electronic Tax Bureau portal by submitting the Business License, Articles of Association, registered office lease, legal representative identification, and the investor's foreign/Taiwan corporate existence evidence; (2) the competent tax authority creates the taxpayer master file and assigns tax categories, whereupon the WFOE elects its VAT identity (small-scale taxpayer applying the simplified 3% levy, or general taxpayer applying the standard 13% rate and input VAT credit); (3) for dairy export trading, obtain the Customs Import and Export Goods Consignor Recordal (Hai Guan Beian) and apply for the export VAT Exemption-Credit-Refund qualification (Mian Di Tui) in the electronic tax system; and (4) complete the SAFE current-account trade registration, open a foreign-exchange settlement account, and enrol in the Foreign Exchange Monitoring System to clock the 180-day export proceeds repatriation period.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. The PRC does not offer check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. The WFOE is mandatorily classified as a Chinese tax resident enterprise taxed at entity level on worldwide taxable income at 25% corporate income tax; the only material statutory elections are the VAT small-scale/general taxpayer choice and, for qualified exporters, the zero-rated export refund route.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration is triggered immediately upon incorporation for any WFOE, without any turnover de minimis. Compulsory conversion from small-scale to general VAT taxpayer status applies when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000, or when the exporter otherwise elects general taxpayer status; export-oriented dairy commodity traders are normatively required by the tax authority to operate as VAT general taxpayers in order to access input-VAT credit and export zero-rating.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The WFOE may apply the 0% VAT export rate for whole and skimmed milk powder exported from mainland China to Taiwan via the Exemption-Credit-Refund (Mian Di Tui) method, provided all statutory criteria are met: (i) VAT general taxpayer status together with Customs Import-Export Recordal; (ii) the milk powder is actually declared to mainland customs, physically exits the China customs territory, and is received beyond it; (iii) the cross-strait export sale is genuine, contractually documented, and arm's-length; (iv) export proceeds in foreign currency or cross-strait RMB settlement are collected, repatriated, and settled in a mainland Chinese bank and verified under the SAFE Foreign Exchange Monitoring System within 180 days; and (v) the exported goods are backed by creditable input VAT from documented purchases of raw milk powder and logistics. Sales to Taiwan are treated as exports for PRC VAT purposes and remain eligible for zero-rating/refund despite the special cross-strait customs channel.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bill of Entry) - PRC export goods customs declaration (Chu Kou Huo Wu Bao Guan Dan) with electronic customs clearance stamp
  • Bill of Lading (ocean/combined transport) or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from mainland China customs territory and arrival in Taiwan
  • Inward Remittance Certificate - export proceeds collection voucher (Chu Kou Shou Hui Ru Zhang Tong Zhi) issued by the settling bank in China
  • Commercial export invoice (Chu Kou Fa Piao) and packing list for the shipment
  • Sales contract or purchase order between the WFOE and the Taiwan buyer
  • SAFE Foreign Exchange Monitoring System export proceeds verification record (Huo Wu Mao Yi Wai Hui Jian Ce Xi Tong)
  • Milk powder quality/health certificate and certificate of origin as optional-risk substantiating evidence

China imposes a mandatory real-time electronic invoicing and fiscal clearance regime. The WFOE must issue all VAT invoices through the State Taxation Administration's National Unified E-Invoice Service Platform; fully digitalised e-invoices (Dianzi Fapiao / Shudian Piao) are transmitted live to the tax authority, each bearing a unique national serial number and a State Taxation Administration-supervised anti-counterfeiting digital seal. Billing systems must integrate with the tax authority's public APIs to enable automatic invoice clearance, line-item reconciliation with customs export declarations, and continuous audit-data feeding; the legacy tax-control black-box equipment is obsolete in the fully digitalised channel.

Export zero-rating is subject to ex-post verification triangulating customs export declarations, e-invoice platform data, transport bills, sales contracts, and bank/SAFE foreign-exchange collection records. Where the export is not substantially verified, goods are not proven to have left the customs territory, proceeds are not repatriated and settled within the statutory 180-day deadline (barring an approved SAFE extension), or any document is forged, the tax authority retroactively claws back the full refunded/exempted VAT, imposes daily late-payment surcharge interest, and levies an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the tax evaded under the Tax Collection Administration Law. The criminal offence of defrauding export tax refunds (pian qu chu kou tui shui zui) may additionally expose the entity and its responsible officers to criminal fines, asset seizure, and imprisonment up to life imprisonment in aggravated or organised cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The operational WFOE is domiciled in mainland China, so the PRC foreign exchange control regime governs the treasury blueprint. The RMB is convertible for current account purposes, including trade-related milk powder imports and exports, ordinary operating disbursements, and service-related remittances, subject to transaction authenticity rules under the Foreign Exchange Control Regulations. Capital account operations, however, remain restricted and require case-by-case SAFE approval or registration: FDI equity injection, external borrowing, and cross-border guarantees each carry separate filing thresholds. Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is permitted under the current-account dividend line once the WFOE has made RMB or foreign-currency profit distributions from audited retained earnings, has paid the relevant Chinese corporate income tax including any withholding tax on dividends, and submits to the remitting bank a completed SAFE/FDI profit remittance form, audited financial statements, tax payment certificates, and a board resolution declaring the dividend. There is no statutory mandatory conversion ratio for genuine export proceeds after they are collected; the WFOE may hold accepted foreign currency in a designated current-account FX account or convert into RMB through the onshore market at the prevailing rate. Direct RMB-FX conversion outside bank intermediation is prohibited; any hedging must be grounded in a real underlying current-account exposure. Capital-side conversion of FDI proceeds into RMB is governed by the negative list and the permitted-use list, and converted RMB must be spent on bona fide business purposes such as payroll, domestic raw materials, rent, and taxes, but not injected into domestic loans or real estate outside the approved business scope. These hurdles materially increase bank onboarding documentation requirements and operational lead time.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For goods-trade export receipts, the statutory collection period is generally 90 calendar days after the customs export declaration date; any payment falling outside this window, whether an advance receipt or deferred collection beyond 90 days, must be reported through the receiving bank under SAFE's trade-in-goods monitoring system. There is no fixed mandatory conversion ratio; the exporter retains 100% of export proceeds in its current-account foreign exchange settlement account or may settle them into RMB at market rate at its discretion. If export proceeds are not received within the regulatory monitoring tolerance, the bank will suspend trade foreign exchange operations and the WFOE must provide the original contract, customs declaration, logistics documents, and a written explanation to SAFE.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC) acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), which is the designated authority for foreign exchange regulation and cross-border payment verification in mainland China.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low. Foreign digital EMI platforms such as Wise, Revolut, Payoneer, and Airwallex standalone do not hold PRC banking licenses and are not permitted to open local checking or clearing accounts, nor to maintain onshore RMB current accounts for a mainland WFOE. PBOC and SAFE require all goods-trade foreign exchange and cross-border RMB payments to be settled through SAFE-designated bank branches. An EMI may only provide auxiliary collection or disbursement technology via a licensed PRC partner bank, but even then the WFOE must maintain the principal clearing and custody relationship with a domestic Tier-1 commercial bank. Consequently, this blueprint mandates domestic banks as legal account keepers and treats EMIs solely as non-liable technical gateways that cannot replace a regulated local bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent should remit the registered capital in USD or RMB via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's Foreign Exchange Capital Account, also known as the FDI Capital Account, at a SAFE-registered PRC bank. The remitting bank must use the standard FDI remittance purpose code with a clear narrative stating that the transfer is an equity investment for a wholly foreign-owned enterprise constituted under PRC corporate law. The receiving bank will complete the national FDI information registration, and the WFOE must submit the signed business license, Articles of Association, FDI Basic Information Form, and board resolution evidencing the capital contribution. After funds are credited, the bank may convert them into RMB at onshore rates under the capital account settlement mechanism, subject to the permitted-use list; converted RMB must be transferred to the WFOE's RMB basic settlement account and used only for permitted business purposes. A licensed PRC certified public accountant should issue a capital verification report confirming the actual amount and value date of the contribution, which will later support registry filings and future profit remittances.For import and export trade disbursements, the WFOE will use bank-intermediated clearing rails. First, SWIFT MT103 straight telegraphic transfer is available for post-shipment payments, urgent commercial remittances, and settlements after verification of the contract, invoice, customs declaration, and balance-of-payments filing. Second, commercial letters of credit issued under UCP600 use MT700 and MT707 messages, with the WFOE as applicant and payment released against compliant shipping documents presented through the negotiating bank. Third, documentary collections, including DP/UA and DA terms, use MT400 and MT410 messages through the presenting bank when open-account terms are impractical but full LC cover is not necessary. All outgoing trade payments must be reported under SAFE's goods-trade monitoring system with the appropriate goods-trade payment purpose code, and the underlying trade file must be retained for at least five years. If the counterparty accepts RMB, the same rails can be used through SWIFT MT103 CNY settlement or CIPS routing, but the commercial invoice and customs data must still evidence the real underlying dairy trade.FX forwards with a documented real current-account trade or capital exposure, executed under a bilateral ISDA/CSA with a licensed onshore bank and subject to SAFE's real-need rule and prohibition of speculative net open positions.FX swaps combining spot and forward legs, limited to cash-flow management of trade receipts and disbursements for the underlying goods-trade and operating cash flows.Plain-vanilla FX options, including collars, risk reversals, and zero-cost structures, used only where a quantified and verifiable onshore RMB versus foreign-currency exposure exists.Cross-currency swaps used to hedge long-term capital structure exposure, such as converting a foreign-currency external loan drawdown into RMB, provided the underlying external debt is registered with SAFE and used in compliance with the approved loan purpose.Onshore NDFs are prohibited for a domestic PRC WFOE; non-deliverable forward contracts cannot be booked onshore. Any NDF would have to be executed offshore by the non-resident parent or affiliate and would not constitute a permissible onshore hedge for the Chinese operating entity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: CNY 200 million annual related-party tangible-property transactions, CNY 10 million for intangible or financial transactions, and CNY 40 million for other related-party transactions trigger the mandatory contemporaneous Local File. Master File and Country-by-Country reporting are required where the ultimate parent group's consolidated revenue exceeds CNY 5.5 billion.

Economic substance scope: Although China does not apply the UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, the PRC GAAR, the beneficial ownership doctrine, and the OECD transfer pricing framework require the WFOE's core income-generating activities in the cross-border dairy trade to be performed in China. The WFOE's board decisions, office premises, procurement functions, quality inspection, inventory ownership, logistics control, invoicing, treasury management, and risk-taking must demonstrate economic substance. A WFOE that is a mere shell or conduit without real functions, assets, and risks will fail the beneficial ownership analysis and may have treaty or cross-strait relief denied, with income recharacterised and additional tax imposed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: PRC Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63 imposes a penalty from 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax, plus daily late-payment surcharge. Where false VAT invoices are used, Criminal Law Art. 205 imposes severe criminal fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment in egregious cases; Criminal Law Art. 201 punishes tax evasion by fines and custodial sentences.

Director personal liability: Directors who instigate, approve, or participate in false invoicing are directly responsible persons and face restitution of illicitly dissipated corporate assets, blacklisting by tax authorities, travel bans and exit refusal under Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 44. Courts may pierce the corporate veil under PRC Company Law Art. 20 where the abuse of limited liability harms the State revenue claim, exposing directors to personal liability and criminal imprisonment up to seven years.
Transfer Pricing / Shell Arrangement

Statutory sanction: PRC CIT Law Art. 41 and Art. 47 authorise the tax authority to make transfer pricing adjustments and disregard the shell arrangement. The adjusted tax becomes immediately due with interest. If the taxpayer fraudulently conceals related-party transactions, TCAL Art. 63 penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax applies; failure to maintain documentation may lead to fines under administrative enforcement and denial of treaty or cross-strait withholding relief under SAT Public Notice No. 35 of 2019.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally assessed where they failed to ensure arm's length documentation and knowingly allowed the WFOE to function as a shell. Civil asset restitution may be ordered against directors for unlawfully extracted dividends or bonuses. Tax authorities may apply an exit blacklisting order preventing directors from leaving China. Criminal tax evasion charges under Criminal Law Art. 201 are possible where the transfer pricing misstatement is fraudulent, with imprisonment up to seven years.
Cross-Border Withholding / AML Red-Flag

Statutory sanction: Under TCAL Art. 69, the WFOE as withholding agent is ordered to pay the tax that was not withheld, and a penalty from 50% to 300% of the unwithheld amount is imposed; overdue daily surcharge continues to accrue. State Administration of Foreign Exchange rules impose civil liability for unlawful outward remittance. The PRC Anti-Money Laundering Law sanctions failure to perform customer due diligence on beneficial owners and suspicious transaction reporting, with monetary fines and revocation of licences.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives who caused the payment to be made without statutory withholding become liable for the unpaid tax to the extent the corporate veil is pierced; their personal assets may be subject to attachment and restitution. Tax authorities will place the WFOE and its responsible persons on the taxation blacklist, triggering travel bans and exit refusal. Criminal liability for tax refusal or evasion may be pursued under Criminal Law Art. 202 or Art. 201, with imprisonment up to seven years and criminal fines.
