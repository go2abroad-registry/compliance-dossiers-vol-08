Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Taiwan limited company. The subscribed capital must be fully paid in before registration and verified by a Taiwan-certified public accountant; no blocked escrow bank account is mandated by company law. However, if the Investment Commission approval conditions prescribe a capital amount or capital injection schedule, the capital must be remitted from the PRC WFOE into the Taiwan account and supported by a foreign exchange receipt certificate prior to filing with the registry.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable to pure online retail. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China, with the electronic tax service administered through the China Electronic Tax Administration portal; foreign-trade customs registration is coordinated through the General Administration of Customs of the People's Republic of China via the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For a China Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), the procedure is: incorporate the limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR); the business license bears a Uniform Social Credit Code, which functions as the company Tax Identification Number and is recognized by tax, customs, foreign-exchange and statistics authorities. Complete post-incorporation tax registration through the China Electronic Tax Administration portal within 30 days of business-license issuance. For export operations, register with the General Administration of Customs as an import/export cargo declarant through the China International Trade Single Window and obtain a customs registration code. Complete SAFE-compliant direct-investment foreign-exchange registration with a Chinese designated foreign-exchange bank. Foreign non-resident entities without a Chinese establishment obtain a Chinese taxpayer identification number only through withholding tax registration arranged by a Chinese payer; they are not classified as resident enterprises.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. China does not provide check-the-box, partnership-style pass-through, or hybrid mismatch elections for a WFOE. A WFOE is a resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income. For VAT administration only, the WFOE may be a general taxpayer or small-scale taxpayer depending on annual taxable sales, but this does not alter the entity-level tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No VAT registration threshold applies to an established WFOE; tax registration is mandatory upon incorporation. RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales is the threshold above which VAT general taxpayer status is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from China to Taiwan by a WFOE, including dietary supplements sold through cross-border e-commerce, qualify for the 0% VAT rate if title in the goods transfers to a non-resident purchaser, the goods leave China customs territory under an approved export supervision mode, including 9610 cross-border e-commerce retail export, 9710 cross-border e-commerce B2B export, or 9810 overseas-warehouse export, and the export has economic substance supported by complete customs, logistics, platform transaction, payment and foreign-exchange repatriation records.

  • Export customs declaration accepted by the General Administration of Customs through the China International Trade Single Window
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Cross-border e-commerce order record and electronic platform transaction data showing the Taiwan end-purchaser
  • Bill of lading, airway bill, or cross-border logistics waybill proving physical export from China
  • Export-proceeds repatriation documentation, including bank foreign-exchange inflow records or a settlement report from a licensed cross-border payment institution

General taxpayers must issue fully digitized all-electronic invoices through the China Electronic Tax Administration platform. Invoice data is transmitted in real time to the State Taxation Administration, and anti-fraud digital verification is handled through the national electronic invoice service platform. Export declarations are integrated with the China International Trade Single Window for customs clearance and tax-customs data matching.

The State Taxation Administration, the General Administration of Customs, and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange jointly audit export zero-rating claims. Missing, internally inconsistent, or fraudulent documentation results in denial or clawback of the 0% VAT rate, reassessed output VAT, daily late-payment interest, and penalties of up to five times the tax evaded under the Law of the People's Republic of China on Administration of Tax Collection. Customs declarations made falsely are separately penalized under the Customs Law of the People's Republic of China, and serious fraudulent export tax-refund schemes carry criminal liability under the Criminal Law of the People's Republic of China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed floating exchange rate regime in which the renminbi is convertible for current account transactions, including trade in goods, services, and profit distributions, consistent with its IMF Article VIII obligations. However, capital account items remain extensively controlled and each cross-border flow must be treaty-backed and documented through SAFE's digital monitoring platforms. For a China-domiciled WFOE engaged in cross-border nutraceutical e-commerce, the Taiwan investor's registered capital injection is classified as foreign direct investment and must be registered with SAFE through the FDI system before proceeds are converted into renminbi. Export revenues from Taiwan-bound supplement sales may be retained in foreign currency settlement accounts or converted at the onshore market rate, but all receipts must be reconciled against customs declarations. Profit remittance is permissible after annual audit, tax clearance, and application of withholding taxes, though the bank requires board resolutions and audited financials. Cross-border guarantees, loans, and derivatives are permitted only under specific SAFE registrations or approvals. The overall framework is functional for treasury operations but imposes strict evidentiary handling, verification windows, and continuous monitoring on every capital flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the PRC Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Administration Rules, the WFOE must repatriate all export proceeds to its Chinese bank account within 90 days from the date of customs export declaration. There is no statutory mandatory conversion percentage; the WFOE may retain foreign currency in a designated export settlement account or convert it to renminbi at the onshore market rate. The repatriated amount must be reconciled with customs filings, and any extension or discrepancy beyond SAFE tolerance requires submission to the local SAFE branch through the Trade Monitoring Platform.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Direct onboarding of offshore fintech electronic money institutions is not legally viable for the China-domiciled WFOE. Offshore EMIs do not hold a PBOC payment business license and are not authorized by SAFE to maintain settlement accounts for mainland entities. The WFOE must rely on licensed domestic commercial banks for its primary clearing infrastructure. PBOC-licensed cross-border payment platforms, such as LianLian Pay or PingPong, may be used as supplementary collection channels for marketplace receipts, but funds must ultimately settle through a Chinese commercial bank under the goods-trade forex monitoring framework.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan investor must remit registered capital via SWIFT MT103 from its Taiwanese corporate bank account to the WFOE's designated FDI capital account held at an approved Chinese commercial bank. The remittance must carry the correct foreign exchange purpose code corresponding to the FDI equity investment, and upon receipt the bank verifies the funds against the SAFE FDI Confirmation Form and the WFOE's foreign exchange registration record. The bank will validate the payment against the SAFE goods-trade monitoring system, and the WFOE must retain full transactional records for at least six years. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory contemporaneous documentation is triggered when the China WFOE's total related-party transactions in the tax year reach or exceed RMB 100 million under SAT Announcement No. 42 of 2016, with category-specific thresholds for tangibles, intangibles, financial transactions, and services; Master File: the Chinese entity must prepare a Master File where the multinational group's prior-year consolidated revenue exceeds RMB 4 billion and the Chinese WFOE has related-party transactions.

Economic substance scope: China does not apply DIFC-style Economic Substance Regulations or a separate CIGA filing; however, the EIT Law general anti-avoidance rule (Article 47) and SAT Announcement No. 16 of 2019 on beneficial ownership require the Taiwan-related counterparty to perform core income-generating activities such as strategic commercial decisions, risk management, asset ownership, and substantive functions. A bare shell, passive nominee, or treaty-shopping Taiwan parent will be disregarded for tax purposes; the State Taxation Administration will recharacterize the transaction, deny treaty rate benefits, and tax the WFOE as the principal entity. The WFOE should maintain board minutes, employment records, physical office evidence, intercompany agreements, and value-chain documentation in the local file.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion through Off-Book Cross-Border E-Commerce Revenue and Related-Party Profit Shift

Statutory sanction: Tax Administration Law Articles 32, 63, and 64 impose a 50% to 500% penalty on the tax shortfall plus a 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge; Criminal Law Article 201 criminalizes fraudulent tax declarations, with imprisonment up to three years for relatively large evasion and up to seven years where the evaded amount is unusually large or exceeds a prescribed threshold, plus fines and confiscation of illegal gains.

Director personal liability: Directors who order, approve, or conceal off-book revenue or false intercompany payments can be prosecuted as directly responsible persons, and the corporate veil may be pierced where the WFOE acts as a mere instrument; directors face civil restitution to the tax authority, personal fines, a tax-related travel and visa ban under the serious tax violation blacklist mechanism, and imprisonment for tax evasion.
Treaty-Shopping Shell Parent and Denial of Cross-Strait DTA Protection

Statutory sanction: Under EIT Law Articles 41, 47, and 48 and SAT Announcement No. 16 of 2019, the tax authority may disregard the shell, recharacterize the transaction, impose the statutory 10% WHT on a deemed dividend or interest/royalty, and assess late surcharges plus penalties of 50% to 500% of under-withheld tax; fraudulent or forged TRC declarations may lead to criminal fraud liability and blacklisting.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false beneficial-ownership certificates or structure a conduit chain with no economic substance are jointly liable for tax losses and civil restitution; they are placed on the significant-tax-violation blacklist, face asset freezes and travel restrictions, and can be convicted of fraud or tax evasion if they knowingly transmitted false treaty claims, leading to imprisonment for up to seven years.
Unlicensed Nutraceutical E-Commerce and High-Risk Merchant-Account AML Exposure

Statutory sanction: Food Safety Law Article 122 imposes fines of RMB 50,000 to RMB 100,000 or 10 to 20 times the value of the goods for unlicensed food business operations and can cancel licenses; Criminal Law Article 225 criminalizes illegal business operation, with imprisonment up to five years and property confiscation in serious cases; the Anti-Money Laundering Law permits monetary penalties and confiscation of illegal gains; all unreported revenues are reassessed to CIT with late interest and penalties under the Tax Administration Law.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who use the WFOE as a facade for illegal unlicensed supplement sales and payment laundering may be pursued criminally, with the corporate veil pierced if the corporation is used to commit crimes; personal civil liability attaches for tax restitution and consumer harm, asset freezes and travel bans are imposed under AML and tax enforcement measures, and directors may be sentenced to imprisonment for illegal business operation or money laundering, with serious cases exceeding five years.
