Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA / 国家税务总局); national taxpayer portal: STA Electronic Tax Bureau (电子税务局) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn. Import VAT and customs duties are co-administered with the General Administration of Customs of the PRC (GACC / 海关总署) through the China International Trade Single Window (中国国际贸易单一窗口).

E-government filing portal

Incorporate the WFOE at the local Administration for Market Regulation to obtain the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC / 统一社会信用代码), which is the sole statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) for tax and customs. Complete tax registration (税务登记) at the competent in-charge STA office within 30 days of the business license, including bank account filing. Register separately as an importer/exporter (进出口货物收发货人备案) with GACC for the frozen beef and seafood import scope. VAT status is then designated as General VAT taxpayer (一般纳税人) when annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000, or Small-scale taxpayer (小规模纳税人) below that level, with voluntary general registration permitted to allow input VAT recovery on import.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law (25% headline EIT on worldwide income); no pass-through or transparency applies. Territorial scope: worldwide income with a per-jurisdiction foreign tax credit for foreign-source taxes; Taiwan (TW) cross-strait flows are governed by domestic rules absent an effective comprehensive double taxation agreement, with 10% withholding on payments to Taiwanese beneficiaries. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed at entity level under the Enterprise Income Tax Law (25% headline EIT on worldwide income); no pass-through or transparency applies. Territorial scope: worldwide income with a per-jurisdiction foreign tax credit for foreign-source taxes; Taiwan (TW) cross-strait flows are governed by domestic rules absent an effective comprehensive double taxation agreement, with 10% withholding on payments to Taiwanese beneficiaries. The entity is not a tax-exempt zone entity; bonded warehousing or customs supervision zone status may be separately elected to defer import duties and import VAT.

Available election forms

The PRC does not maintain a US-style check-the-box election. Statutorily available elections include: (i) voluntary General VAT taxpayer registration below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold (irrevocable for 12 months), enabling input VAT crediting and export VAT refund eligibility; (ii) simplified VAT calculation at the collection rate for small-scale taxpayers (3% standard, temporary 1% relief); (iii) EIT elections on advance payment methods, foreign tax credit sourcing, loss carryforward (5-year standard; 10-year for qualifying high-tech enterprises), and financing structure; (iv) special tax treatment elections for reorganisations and advance pricing arrangements with STA; and (v) anti-hybrid mismatch adjustments imposed via the general anti-avoidance rule (EIT Law Art. 47) and STA BEPS Action 2 guidance rather than as elective regimes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory General VAT taxpayer registration at RMB 5,000,000 of annual taxable sales (RMB/CNY local currency). Below the threshold, the WFOE may remain a small-scale taxpayer or elect general status. For imports, no separate registration threshold applies: import VAT (9% for the frozen meat and pelagic fish subject goods) is payable at customs clearance for every inbound shipment entering domestic consumption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods — including re-export of imported deep-frozen beef and pelagic fish after bonded storage, regional processing, or direct re-export — qualify for the 0% VAT rate with input VAT refund (出口退税) if: (i) the exporter is a General VAT taxpayer holding valid GACC export registration; (ii) the goods physically leave the PRC customs territory; (iii) export proceeds are collected in foreign currency through designated banks; and (iv) the customs export declaration, e-invoice, and logistics records are consistent. Offshore cold-chain services supplied to non-resident customers are zero-rated only where wholly consumed outside China. Pure domestic distribution of imported goods does not qualify for zero-rating; the WFOE charges output VAT at 9% and credits the import VAT paid at customs. Exports to Taiwan (TW) are administratively treated as exports for VAT refund purposes, subject to cross-strait trade control documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口报关单) with HS code, quantity, and customs release
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from the PRC customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / export proceeds collection record from a designated foreign-exchange bank
  • Export invoice and VAT refund declaration submitted through the STA export tax refund module
  • Commercial contract, packing list, and cold-chain temperature records substantiating the frozen cargo

Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期) mandates full digital e-invoicing (全面数字化电子发票 / 数电票) via the STA Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform, with real-time issuance reporting, a 20-digit e-invoice number, and a trusted digital certificate replacing physical stamping. Import clearance is mandatory through the China International Trade Single Window with electronic customs declarations, automatic risk scoring, and digital payment of duties and import VAT. VAT invoice data is cross-matched with customs declarations and foreign-exchange collection records; API-based electronic clearance is embedded in the customs release process.

Export VAT refund claims are subject to intensive STA/GACC cross-verification of customs declarations, e-invoices, and foreign-exchange receipts. Fraudulent zero-rating or over-refund claims trigger mandatory clawback of refunded amounts, 0.05% daily late-payment surcharge, administrative penalties of 50% to 500% of tax evaded (Tax Administration Law Art. 63), and suspension or disqualification from the export refund regime for up to three years. Serious cases constitute criminal VAT fraud (Criminal Law Art. 204) punishable by imprisonment up to life and confiscation of property; the assessment period is extended to 10 years for fraudulent conduct. Customs Law penalties separately apply for false declarations, HS misclassification, or cold-chain integrity violations in the frozen beef and seafood trade.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible on the current account, covering trade, services, wages, remittances of investment profits, and transfers for personal use, subject to authenticity verification and anti-money laundering checks. The capital account remains strictly controlled. A WFOE may remit after-tax profits to its foreign shareholder under current account rules provided that the enterprise has completed statutory annual audits, paid corporate income tax, and provides board resolutions, audited financial statements, tax certificates, and valid supporting trade documents. Capital injections, equity transfers, and loans require separate SAFE registration and special capital account opening; cross-border RMB investment is permissible after registration. Practical hurdles include bank-level compliance for verification of beneficial ownership, tax certificates, and the requirement that capital account funds be used strictly in accordance with the approved investment purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE 'Measures for the Administration of Foreign Exchange in Trade in Goods', export proceeds must be repatriated to China within 180 days after the date of export customs declaration unless an exceptional extension is registered. There is no mandatory conversion ratio; enterprises may retain up to 100% of export proceeds in their designated foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily sell them for RMB within the approval scope. All export receipts must be reported to the Trade Monitoring System, with supporting contracts, customs declarations, invoices, and logistics evidence available upon bank and SAFE request. For food import and cold-chain wholesale operations, consistent documentary chains are required to avoid false export/trade misinvoicing risk.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) onboarding viability is Low inside Mainland China territory. Foreign non-bank EMIs are not permitted to issue local RMB accounts, maintain nostro/vostro accounts, or conduct foreign exchange settlement services on behalf of resident enterprises without a PBOC payment institution license and SAFE approval. A WFOE must use a licensed commercial bank for capital accounts, trade settlements, and FX derivatives. Non-bank payment institutions may only support domestic RMB collecting/payments; they cannot act as the primary treasury clearing rail for large cross-border meat/seafood trade and would not fulfill SAFE trade monitoring obligations. Foreign payment processors may be used solely as a sub-channel, but the legal account and SWIFT settlement must remain at a licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant inbound routing for a Taiwan shareholder injecting capital into a WFOE begins with completion of SAFE/FDI registration and receipt of a Foreign-Enterprise Code and FDI ID record. The Taiwan shareholder then remits via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's dedicated Capital Escrow Account (Account Type 1) at the licensed bank, using 中国银行 code 821011 (Foreign Direct Investment – Registered Capital) and a detailed payment purpose denoting 'Capital injection for CN_WFOE_LTD under FDI'. The bank will verify the FDI registration certificate, the payment instruction sender's IBAN/BIC, end-to-end beneficial ownership, and the required thin-capitalization ratio. After the funds are credited, the bank will complete the payment authenticity check according to SAFE, and the enterprise may settle the foreign currency into RMB within the scope of 'capital conversion' use (purchase of imported goods, logistics, cold-chain warehousing, salaries, and rent). Any domestic usage not supporting the import trade scope must be disposaled only after a further SAFE 'Payment of Capital Account' application and submission of the underlying contracts.For outbound settlement to overseas suppliers of frozen beef and seafood, the permissible clearing rails are: (i) Direct SWIFT MT103 / MT202 telegraphic transfers from the WFOE's current account to suppliers for payments on open-account terms, subject to SAFE 'Trade in Goods' reporting, contract, invoice, and customs declaration matching; (ii) Commercial documentary letters of credit (sight or usance) opened through the account bank, with MT700/MT707 issuance and negotiation handled by the same SWIFT network; (iii) Cross-border RMB settlement using the CIPS or an approved SWIFT/RMB channel, which allows settlement in RMB outside mainland China; and (iv) Cash-in-advance payments using MT103 where suppliers require pre-payment.
