Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally not acceptable for a company engaged in professional services such as game art outsourcing. The tax authorities and banks require a physical address for verification, audit, and credit assessment purposes. The MOEA may accept a registered agent address, but the company must maintain a physical office for operational and regulatory compliance. Banks typically reject virtual offices for account opening, and the tax authority may impose penalties for non-compliance. Therefore, a physical office is strongly recommended.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed for an ISIC 7490 professional creative service company under the Taiwan Company Act. However, the capital subscribed under the Articles of Incorporation must be fully paid by the founding shareholders and, in the case of a foreign-invested joint venture, the foreign shareholder's contribution must be remitted from abroad through a licensed Taiwan bank, converted into New Taiwan Dollars where applicable, and verified by a Taiwan-certified public accountant capital verification report. No blocked escrow account or capital quarantine arrangement is required by law for this business scope.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for eligible current-account transactions, including trade in services and recurring service fees, subject to genuine-transaction documentation and bank-based verification. Capital-account convertibility remains restricted: all cross-border equity investment, shareholder loans, and capital injections must be registered with SAFE through the Direct Investment Management System before funds are admitted into the PRC. For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, dividend and profit remittance to a Taiwan shareholder is treated as a current-account transaction or as remittance of lawful earnings, permitted after annual audit completion, corporate income tax clearance, and the submission of the SAFE Foreign Exchange Registration for Direct Investment. The primary hurdles are (i) proof of authenticity through invoices, tax certificates, and audited financial statements; (ii) SAFE registration of each capital-account movement; and (iii) compliance with the Negative List, which does not restrict 3D game art outsourcing but may require disclaimers on content production and data security.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The PRC abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds in 2012. Export-related receipts, including cross-border payments for 3D art asset creation, character rigging, and related BPO services, must be repatriated to a domestic bank account through a licensed settlement bank. The exporter or service provider may retain the foreign currency in a current-account foreign exchange settlement account or convert it to RMB at market rate. Statutory verification: export and service receipts must be matched and verified via SAFE's 'Trade Foreign Exchange Receipt and Payment Monitoring System' within the prescribed timelines, generally 30 days for advance payments and 90 days for ordinary shipment or service completion receipts. No minimum conversion ratio applies, but every inbound remittance must bear a clear service description and be declared under the correct SAFE trade-in-services code.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and its administrative arm, the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Low to Medium. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Payoneer, Wise Business, PayPal Business) are not licensed banks in the PRC and cannot serve as the primary treasury vehicle. They may be used as auxiliary collection channels for low-value overseas clients, but every receivable derived from export of services must ultimately be routed into an onshore bank account subject to SAFE monitoring. Legal constraints: the JV must not hold operating receivables offshore; any funds parked with an unlicensed EMI will create a false declaration issue and may trigger AML scrutiny for unverified cross-border receipts. The only robust use for these EMIs is inbound connectivity to a Chinese settlement bank through an approved payment aggregator, with full underlying contract and tax invoice flows.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Underwriting red flag: Incomplete or opaque disclosure of the beneficial ownership chain, especially when the Taiwan shareholder is a holding company or an offshore trust.

Root cause: Underwriting red flag: Incomplete or opaque disclosure of the beneficial ownership chain, especially when the Taiwan shareholder is a holding company or an offshore trust.

Defense evidence: Provide a certified org chart from the ultimate individual beneficial owner to the PRC JV; register certificates of ultimate beneficial owners in the bank's AML system; include the Taiwan entity's audited financial statements, business license, minutes of shareholders' meetings, and notarized statements confirming no government ownership or sanctioned-party ties; and provide a written explanation of the funding pathway for each capital contribution.
Underwriting red flag: Use of PayPal or other digital EMI for B2B trade invoicing, causing a mismatch between declared service export code and payment channel profile.

Root cause: Underwriting red flag: Use of PayPal or other digital EMI for B2B trade invoicing, causing a mismatch between declared service export code and payment channel profile.

Defense evidence: Supply the complete client contracts, work orders for 3D asset creation, delivery logs, invoice and payment routing matrix, and an explanation that PayPal is used only as an invoicing convenience for small-ticket overseas clients. Confirm in writing that all proceeds are repatriated within the statutory verification window and that no digital EMI holds corporate funds overnight for treasury purposes. Include the bank's own FATCA and CRS forms to demonstrate tax transparency.
Underwriting red flag: Cross-border receipts denominated in USD or EUR for gaming art outsource services where the industry code (ISIC 7490) could be perceived as intangible software licensing instead of professional BPO services.

Add a certified statement from the tax advisor explaining why the receipts are service income, not royalty or license income, and provide SAFE service trade declaration codes consistent with the 7490 scope.
Underwriting red flag: Capital injection from a Taiwan entity into a Chinese JV without clear proof of the source-of-funds in the Taiwan entity's bank account.

Root cause: Underwriting red flag: Capital injection from a Taiwan entity into a Chinese JV without clear proof of the source-of-funds in the Taiwan entity's bank account.

Defense evidence: Provide bank statements of the Taiwan investing entity for at least six months, audited financial statements, tax filings proving lawful accumulation of the capital, a board resolution from the Taiwan entity approving the investment, and a certified certificate of deposit or asset valuation letter. Where funds originate from a third-party lender, provide the executed loan agreement, the lender's due-diligence pack, and the on-lending structure to the JV, with an explanation of the interest rate and repayment terms.
