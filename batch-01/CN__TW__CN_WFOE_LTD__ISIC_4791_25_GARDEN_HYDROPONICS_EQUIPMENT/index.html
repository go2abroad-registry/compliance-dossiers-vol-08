Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, 'SAT'), administering tax registration, VAT, enterprise income tax, invoice control, and export tax refunds under the Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期) system, with enforcement through provincial, municipal, and county-level tax service bureaus and the unified Electronic Tax Bureau (电子税务局). Authoritative portal: https://www.chinatax.gov.cn; national service hotline: 12366. The entity CN_WFOE_LTD is a mainland Chinese wholly foreign-owned enterprise whose operative tax domicile is exclusively the PRC; the 'TW' flag in the composite key identifies Taiwan as the counterparty export market or investor reference and confers no Taiwanese tax residence on this entity.

        
E-government filing portal
Step 1: Incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Business License bearing the 18-character Uniform Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, 'USCC'), which is legally recognized as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for all PRC tax purposes. Step 2: Tax registration is triggered automatically through the multi-certificate integration (多证合一) data-sharing reform; within 30 days of license issuance, the WFOE must complete supplementary filings via the e-Tax Bureau, including accounting/financial system and software filing, bank deposit account reporting, legal representative and controlling shareholder information, and payroll records. Step 3: Determine VAT status — general taxpayer (一般纳税人) or small-scale taxpayer (小规模纳税人); an exporter seeking output-VAT refunds must obtain general taxpayer status or export through a licensed agent, and must complete the Export Tax Refund/Exemption Filing (出口退（免）税备案) before the first export declaration. Step 4: Complete customs registration as an import/export consignee/consignor (海关进出口收发货人备案), effect the foreign trade operator automatic filing with the Ministry of Commerce, and open trade foreign-exchange settlement accounts with a SAFE-designated bank linked to the Trade in Goods Foreign Exchange Monitoring System. Step 5: Apply for the Fully Digital Electronic Invoice (数电票) quota through the SAT invoice platform. No statutory minimum paid-in capital applies (subscription-based registered capital system), and online retail of home hydroponics and gardening equipment does not require a statutory license or an import/export permit.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (no pass-through eligibility). The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise (居民企业) taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law at the standard 25% rate, with foreign tax credit relief for overseas taxes on the same income. Territorial scope: worldwide taxation; not a tax-exempt zone. Preferential EIT regimes available: 15% rate for certified High and New Technology Enterprises; effective 5% EIT on qualifying taxable income for Small Low-Profit Enterprises (taxable income not exceeding RMB 3 million, employees not exceeding 300, total assets not exceeding RMB 50 million, policy extended to 31 December 2027); 15% rate for encouraged industries in Western Development regions. Outbound dividends to non-resident shareholders are subject to 10% withholding tax; the Cross-Strait tax arrangement with Taiwan is signed but not in force as a comprehensive double-taxation treaty and provides no general withholding reduction. The entity is a VAT taxpayer on domestic sales and a zero-rated exporter on qualifying exports; local surtaxes (urban maintenance and construction tax at 7%/5%/1% by locale, plus education surcharge 3% and local education surcharge 2%) are assessed on VAT actually paid. — Step 1: Incorporate with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Business License bearing the 18-character Uniform Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, 'USCC'), which is legally recognized as the taxpayer identification number (TIN) for all PRC tax purposes. Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation (no pass-through eligibility). The WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise (居民企业) taxed on worldwide income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law at the standard 25% rate, with foreign tax credit relief for overseas taxes on the same income. Territorial scope: worldwide taxation; not a tax-exempt zone. Preferential EIT regimes available: 15% rate for certified High and New Technology Enterprises; effective 5% EIT on qualifying taxable income for Small Low-Profit Enterprises (taxable income not exceeding RMB 3 million, employees not exceeding 300, total assets not exceeding RMB 50 million, policy extended to 31 December 2027); 15% rate for encouraged industries in Western Development regions. Outbound dividends to non-resident shareholders are subject to 10% withholding tax; the Cross-Strait tax arrangement with Taiwan is signed but not in force as a comprehensive double-taxation treaty and provides no general withholding reduction. The entity is a VAT taxpayer on domestic sales and a zero-rated exporter on qualifying exports; local surtaxes (urban maintenance and construction tax at 7%/5%/1% by locale, plus education surcharge 3% and local education surcharge 2%) are assessed on VAT actually paid.

        
Available election forms
China has no check-the-box entity classification mechanism; all limited companies are statutorily taxable at the entity level. The available elections are: (1) VAT status election — entities with annual taxable sales below RMB 5 million may elect general taxpayer status, and existing general taxpayers with annual sales not exceeding RMB 5 million may elect conversion to small-scale status through the time-limited window extending to 31 December 2027 (small-scale levy rate 3%, temporarily reduced to 1% through 31 December 2027); (2) export tax method election — manufacturing exporters use the 'exemption, credit and refund' (免抵退) method, whereas trading/merchant exporters use the 'exemption and refund' (免退税) method; (3) cross-border e-commerce elections in comprehensive pilot zones — the 'exemption without invoice' (无票免税) election for export sales lacking valid input VAT invoices, and the deemed taxable income assessment for EIT at 4% of export sales revenue under SAT Announcement 2019 No. 36; (4) Small Low-Profit Enterprise EIT preferential rate claimed at the annual EIT settlement; (5) anti-hybrid and thin-capitalization rules — interest deductions are limited by the 2:1 debt-to-equity thin-capitalization ratio and by hybrid-mismatch restrictions under SAT Announcement 2017 No. 2 where the counterparty jurisdiction does not tax the corresponding interest income.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
China maintains current-account RMB convertibility in principle, but capital-account movements remain restricted and require SAFE registration or pre-approval. Export e-commerce proceeds and service/current-account receipts are generally convertible after truthfulness review; however profit remittance by a WFOE is characterised as 'investment income remittance' and requires audited financial statements, tax clearance or tax payment certificates, a board resolution declaring distributable profits, and completion of the annual foreign investment information and balance-of-payments verification filings. Capital repatriation by deregistration remains a capital-account matter. Cross-border e-commerce sales received in foreign currency must be settled into a licensed bank or SAFE-registered payment institution; funds may not bypass the PRC banking system. Any Taiwan-side settlement flows remain subject to separate cross-strait banking rules, but the underlying WFOE treasury exposure is governed by PBOC/SAFE.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
There is no blanket statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts; exporters may retain foreign exchange in designated trade foreign-exchange accounts within approved quotas. However, the WFOE must repatriate export proceeds to China and reconcile them under SAFE's Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System against the matching customs declaration. As a baseline rule, payment for goods exports should be collected within 30 days after export customs clearance unless a deferred-payment arrangement is filed with SAFE in advance. Settlement into RMB must be supported by genuine underlying trade documentation, platform settlement reports, customs manifests, and logistics evidence; accounts maintained with banks or third-party payment institutions must ensure that e-commerce receipts are at all times attributable to the exporter's own trade registration data.

      

      
Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), with delegated verification authority to designated Foreign Exchange Banks
