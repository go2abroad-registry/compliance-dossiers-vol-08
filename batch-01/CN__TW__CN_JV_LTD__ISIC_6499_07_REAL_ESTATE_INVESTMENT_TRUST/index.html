Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance (財政部, MOF); regional National Taxation Bureaus (各地區國稅局) administer profit-seeking enterprise income tax, Business Tax (VAT) and e-invoice compliance; online services via the Tax Portal (財政部稅務入口網, etax.nat.gov.tw) and the E-Invoice Integration Platform (電子發票整合服務平台, einv.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: obtain Foreign Investment Approval from the MOEA Investment Commission; Step 2: incorporate the Taiwan company (股份有限公司) under the Company Act via MOEA Company Registration (公司登記); Step 3: secure Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) approval for the REIT under the Real Estate Securitization Act; Step 4: file profit-seeking enterprise income tax and Business Tax registration with the competent National Taxation Bureau within 15 days of business commencement to obtain the Unified Business Number (統一編號, GUI) and VAT registration certificate; Step 5: register for Cloud E-Invoice (電子發票) and obtain the MOEA company digital certificate (工商憑證) for e-filing and Turnkey transmission.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Step 1: obtain Foreign Investment Approval from the MOEA Investment Commission; Step 2: incorporate the Taiwan company (股份有限公司) under the Company Act via MOEA Company Registration (公司登記); Step 3: secure Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) approval for the REIT under the Real Estate Securitization Act; Step 4: file profit-seeking enterprise income tax and Business Tax registration with the competent National Taxation Bureau within 15 days of business commencement to obtain the Unified Business Number (統一編號, GUI) and VAT registration certificate; Step 5: register for Cloud E-Invoice (電子發票) and obtain the MOEA company digital certificate (工商憑證) for e-filing and Turnkey transmission.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

REIT pass-through distribution election: distributed REIT trust income is taxable at the beneficiary level by withholding at source, leaving the REIT entity untaxed on distributed income; undistributed trust income remains taxable at entity level at the 20% profit-seeking enterprise income tax rate. VAT filing-frequency election: monthly VAT filing may be elected instead of the standard bi-monthly filing to accelerate input VAT refunds on zero-rated export transactions. Taiwan has no US-style 'check-the-box' election; entity classification is fixed by company law and the Real Estate Securitization Act, and hybrid mismatch avoidance is effected through the CFC rules (Income Tax Act Article 43-3) and PEM rules (Article 43-4).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Corporate entities must register for VAT within 15 days of business commencement with no turnover threshold; simplified-tier registration applies above NT$80,000 (goods) / NT$40,000 (services) average monthly sales; full VAT and Uniform Invoice (統一發票) registration becomes compulsory once average monthly sales exceed NT$200,000. A REIT vehicle earning Taiwanese real estate rental and management income is therefore mandatorily VAT-registered from inception.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act, 0% VAT applies to: (i) goods exported from Taiwan customs territory; (ii) goods sold to bonded export zones or offshore clients; (iii) services supplied for use entirely outside Taiwan or rendered overseas; and (iv) international transport services. A Taiwan REIT may claim zero-rating only for rental or property-management income sourced from real estate located outside Taiwan or for services rendered to non-resident principals for offshore use; income from Taiwan real estate (lease and management fees) is taxable at the standard 5%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) or approved customs export clearance record
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill (提單或航空貨運單) evidencing physical export
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List (商業發票及裝箱單)
  • Export proceeds settlement certificate or foreign-currency inward remittance certificate (出口結匯水單或外匯收入證明)
  • Executed sales contract or service agreement with the non-resident counterparty

Enterprises with average monthly sales above NT$200,000 must issue Uniform Invoices, predominantly in Cloud E-Invoice (電子發票) form; invoice XML data must be transmitted via the MOF 'Turnkey' (t-k) gateway to the E-Invoice Integration Platform within 48 hours of issuance for both B2B and B2C transactions, with digital validation through a unique invoice number, random code (隨機碼) and QR code/2D barcode printed on the e-invoice proof (電子發票證明聯). The MOF platform performs central clearing, cross-matching and Uniform Invoice lottery validation; no separate physical fiscal safe or real-time stamping hardware is mandated.

Zero-rating is denied where export documentation or foreign-exchange settlement is incomplete or inconsistent: the 5% VAT is retroactively assessed with interest, and a penalty of up to five times the evaded tax is imposed under the Business Tax Act; the statutory assessment period extends to seven years for under-reported VAT. Willful evasion carries criminal liability under the Tax Collection Act (imprisonment up to five years); repeated false claims trigger enhanced audit status, invoice-issuance restrictions, suspension of e-invoice/refund privileges, and Customs penalties for false or fabricated export declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The New Taiwan Dollar (TWD) is fully convertible for current-account trade and service transactions, requiring settlement through a Designated Foreign Exchange Bank (指定外匯銀行) with supporting commercial documents; no CBC pre-approval applies to trade remittances. Capital-account convertibility is residence-based and quota-controlled: CBC places an annual net settlement ceiling of USD 5,000,000 per natural person and USD 50,000,000 per corporate entity for transactions without individual CBC sanction; amounts above these thresholds require CBC approval. For a mainland-China-funded JV, the Act for Relations between the People of Taiwan and the Mainland Area (台灣地區與大陸地區人民關係條例) and the Rules Governing Investment in Taiwan by Mainland-Area People (大陸地區人民來臺投資許可辦法) impose an additional pre-clearance requirement: the MOEA Investment Commission must approve the initial inward equity investment, any capital increase, the approved business scope, and the shareholding structure. Profit repatriation is permitted only after the commission's pre-registration, tax clearance by the National Taxation Bureau, and physical verification of the approval letter by the remitting bank. REIT distributions to non-resident beneficiaries are treated as trust income under the Income Tax Act; a 15-20% withholding is applied depending on the treaty or Taiwan's statutory domestic withholding rate, and the CBC declaration threshold (USD 500,000 per remittance or 20 aggregated remittances per year) always applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan has abolished mandatory export-proceeds surrender; an exporter is not required to convert foreign currency receipts into TWD but must repatriate all export receivables within the payment terms declared to Customs at export (30-180 days for D/A, D/P, and O/A arrangements) and must settle the collection exclusively through a Designated Foreign Exchange Bank to allow CBC cross-verification against the export declaration. The exporter may retain the collected FX in a domestic foreign-currency deposit account. Each export settlement on the Forex Declaration Form (外匯收支或交易申報書) is mandatory where the amount equals or exceeds USD 500,000. For the REIT itself, rental income arises in TWD domestically and therefore attracts no surrender or repatriation obligation; the applicable obligation is only on the JV sponsor's trading revenue if any export activity is later appended to the approved business scope.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (中央銀行, CBC), acting jointly with the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC) for REIT licensing and with the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) for mainland-China capital admission

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Taiwan does not issue an offshore EMI license; the Electronic Payment Institution Act (電子支付機構管理條例) regulates domestic e-payment warehousing, not cross-border corporate treasury. No offshore digital bank (e.g., Revolut, Wise) can hold TWD clearing accounts or access the Taiwan Clearing House (台灣票據交換所) or the CBC interbank transfer system (CIFS). An offshore EMI may function only as an upstream USD/EUR float vehicle for pre-funding the Taiwan FX spread, with settlement completed via correspondent bank SWIFT MT103 into an onshore designated FX bank; this introduces dual AML/KYC checks and surplus economic cost. For a regulated REIT with mandatory FSC-governed trust accounts, use of an offshore EMI as the primary treasury rail is not legally viable. The recommended architecture is a direct account at a tier-1 Taiwan clearing bank with a separate USD reserve account at a correspondent bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire protocol: (1) MOEA Investment Commission permit for formation or capital increase; (2) remittance from the mainland parent's bank via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for a pre-funded intermediate bank) to the designated FX bank in Taiwan with the settlement instruction identifying the beneficiary as the JV's capital escrow account; (3) bank completion of the CBC Foreign Exchange Declaration Form citing purpose code 'Inward Investment Remittance - Equity Subscription (投資股本匯入)' and quoting the MOEA approval number; (4) TWD conversion at the designated bank's buying rate recorded on the CBC foreign-exchange transaction system; (5) issuance of a bank foreign-exchange transaction memorandum as evidence for the CPA capital verification; and (6) registration of the paid-in capital with the Ministry of Economic Affairs after submission of the final equity allocation statement. All underlying contracts, valuations, and board minutes for the underlying Taiwan property portfolio must be uploaded to the bank's compliance portal before execution; the CBC retains the right to re-settle any suspicious transaction above the USD 500,000 reporting threshold.Outbound treasury rails: (a) Dividend and REIT beneficial-interest distributions: executed only through SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'Outward Investment Income Remittance (投資收益匯出)'; the remittance file must append the CPA audited statutory financial statements, the withholding tax payment voucher (15% for corporate beneficiaries, 20% for individual beneficiaries absent a reduction under a tax treaty), the MOEA Investment Commission's record of return, and the CBC Forex Declaration Form. (b) Trade settlement for property construction and fit-out: UCP600-compliant Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) issued through the designated FX bank with the corresponding shipping/acceptance documents, or direct supplier MT103 payments tagged with an import/export license number when the underlying is machinery; RMB-denominated supplier settlements are lawful via the Taiwan subsidiary's designated FX bank using the TWD/CNY offshore clearing window operated by the Taiwan Clearing House. (c) REIT asset-level cash distributions: TWD rents are pooled in the trust account and converted to USD/CNY through one designated bank using a deliverable FX forward matched to the distribution declaration dates; each distribution batch remains subject to the corporate USD 50,000,000 annual net settlement ceiling, and breach of this ceiling requires a CBC prior-approval letter.Deliverable FX Forwards (遠期外匯) booked onshore with a designated FX bank, backed by the executed REIT distribution schedule or verified CAPEX payables; tenor strictly limited to the maturity of the underlying real cash-flow exposure.Cross-Currency Swaps (CCS / 換匯換利) between TWD and USD where the REIT's rental cash flows are TWD-denominated and the mandated dividend leg to foreign beneficial-interest holders is USD-denominated.FX Currency Swaps (換匯交易) for bridging CNY share capital to USD dividend obligations without an uncovered forward position.Vanilla Physical-Settlement FX Options (買權/賣權) quoted by the designated FX bank for committed distribution dates, with premium paid from the operational account and exercised only to hedge an actual foreign-currency payment.Prohibited instruments: NDFs (Non-Deliverable Forwards) and all offshore TWD forwards referencing the offshore CNY/TWD spread are banned for onshore Taiwanese corporates; the CBC has prohibited domestic corporate access to the NDF market since 1998 and will void uncovered or speculative transactions, imposing administrative fines and suspension of the bank's FX authorization.
