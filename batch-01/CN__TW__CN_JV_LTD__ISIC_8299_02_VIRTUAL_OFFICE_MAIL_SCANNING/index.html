Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A perpetual mailbox or third-party commercial mail receiving address is categorically impermissible as the JV's statutory registered office under Taiwan tax and banking interpretation. The registered office must be a genuine physical suite where books, records, mail-scanning equipment, and personnel are present. Because the JV itself sells virtual-office and mail-scanning services (ISIC 8299 / CMRA), Taiwan's National Taxation Bureau and banks supervised by the Financial Supervisory Commission specifically reject a 'substance-less' registered address, as it indicates circular accommodation registration. A third-party virtual office may be used for auxiliary marketing correspondence and as a delivery-of-process point, but only if the JV maintains a separate, exclusive physical head-lease with utility records, and that physical address is disclosed during corporate bank account opening and VAT registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / No_General_Tax_Exempt_Zone

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists in Taiwan. A foreign investor may elect to operate through a taxed branch (non-resident) or a resident JV subsidiary; resident companies are subject to entity-level Profit-Seeking Enterprise Income Tax at 20% plus a 5% surtax on undistributed retained earnings (an election to distribute avoids the surtax). Sole proprietorships and partnerships (not applicable to a JV limited company) may elect pass-through treatment; hybrid mismatch elections are limited to recognized group/treaty structures and must be disclosed with the annual corporate income tax return.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory business-tax registration applies when average monthly turnover exceeds the statutory commencement point (NT$80,000 for sales of goods, NT$40,000 for services); full VAT registration with mandatory uniform invoice issuance applies when average monthly sales reach or exceed NT$200,000; monthly sales between the commencement point and NT$200,000 are taxed at the 1% special small-scale rate. Non-resident online and cross-border service suppliers to Taiwan customers have separate VAT-registration duties measured in the same New Taiwan Dollar thresholds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act, goods actually exported from Taiwan are zero-rated upon evidence of shipment. Cross-border services, including virtual-office and mail-scanning services, are zero-rated only where the services are supplied to a named overseas taxable business or offshore recipient for use wholly outside Taiwan and consideration is receivable in foreign currency; domestic onward-use or Taiwan-recipient services are subject to 5% VAT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) or processing-export equivalent bearing the approved customs serial number
  • Commercial invoice and zero-rated sales schedule (零稅率銷售額清單) reconciling the VAT return period
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or international express consignment note evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (結匯水單) or bank FX settlement statement evidencing payment received within the permitted period
  • Signed export/service contract and customer onboarding records (CMRA mail-scan service logs) showing the offshore recipient and foreign use

All VAT-registered businesses must issue uniform invoices (統一發票); the MOF mandates electronic invoices (電子發票) for qualifying taxpayers through the E-Invoice Integration Platform (財政部電子發票整合服務平臺). Government-assigned serial number blocks ('字軌') are stamped digitally before issuance; completed e-invoice data, including void and void-void records, must be transmitted to the Platform via the Turnkey direct connection or an authorized Value-Added Service Center API within the statutory filing deadlines (generally the tax-period return date for B2B cloud e-invoices, with a faster 48-hour filing requirement for carrier-held citizen digital receipts). No physical fiscal stamp is required for cloud e-invoices.

National Taxation Bureau audits verify zero-rating by matching customs declarations, e-invoice data, and foreign-exchange receipts. Missing, inconsistent, or fraudulent documentation triggers disallowance of the 0% rate, reassessment at 5% VAT on gross consideration, interest, and a penalty of up to 5 times the tax evaded under Article 51 of the Business Tax Act; fraudulent schemes also attract criminal penalties under the Tax Evasion Punishment Act and anti-money-laundering sanctions given the CMRA designation, with a statutory assessment window of up to 7 years for unreported or underreported zero-rated supplies.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Under PRC foreign exchange control, RMB is freely convertible for current-account items (trade, services, dividends) but remains tightly managed on the capital account. For a Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, profit remittance requires audited financial statements, corporate income tax settlement clearance, bank verification of the underlying distribution resolution, and SAFE reporting. Capital injections, equity transfers, and liquidation proceeds require separate SAFE registrations and purpose-specific accounts. With respect to the Taiwan jurisdiction reference, TWD is generally convertible for current-account trade and services, but cross-strait RMB remittances must comply with the Cross-Strait Financial Cooperation Framework and local Currency Clearing Office protocols. The principal hurdle is documentary verification rather than exchange rationing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Export receipts must be repatriated into China through designated banks and recorded in SAFE's export revenue verification system. There is no enacted percentage conversion requirement; exporters may hold foreign currency in settlement accounts or convert to RMB at market rates. SAFE monitors repatriation timelines based on commercial contract cycles, typically requiring reconciliation against customs declarations and payment receipts within the normal trade credit period. Overdue or unreconciled proceeds may trigger enhanced scrutiny and administrative penalties.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low to Medium feasibility. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, WorldFirst) cannot provide licensed domestic RMB account or CIPS access without a Chinese banking or payment institution license. They may issue multi-currency virtual IBANs for inbound collections in USD/EUR, but onward settlement into the Chinese JV must route via SWIFT to a SAFE-designated onshore bank. Such EMIs are acceptable only for ancillary foreign-currency receipts, not for statutory capital, dividend remittance, or official tax-cleared trade flows. Their use requires an explicit risk waiver and strict reconciliation controls.
