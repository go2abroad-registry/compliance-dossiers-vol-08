Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

Under Taiwan Company Act, there is no statutory minimum paid-in capital for a limited liability company; the paid-in capital must be at least equal to the issued share capital declared in the Articles of Incorporation and may be evidenced by a capital verification bank statement. No blocked escrow account is statutorily required unless the foreign investment approval imposes capital injection conditions; in that case, the foreign investor's remitted funds must be cleared through the Central Bank of China (Taiwan) foreign exchange approval process.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: A Taiwan-incorporated company is a separate taxpayer under the Income Tax Act (所得稅法), subject to Profit-Seeking Enterprise Income Tax at 20% on net worldwide income if resident in Taiwan. For a pure equity-holding SPV, dividends received from a domestic profit-seeking enterprise are exempt from the income tax base under Article 42 of the Income Tax Act where the shares are directly held at or above 20% and continuously for at least 12 months; this exemption does not alter the entity-level classification. No pass-through treatment is available to limited companies; the corporate liability shield is respected. Territorial scope: resident status attaches to companies incorporated or having their head office in Taiwan; non-resident foreign corporations are taxed only on Taiwan-source income via branch filing or withholding.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or free entity-classification election exists under Taiwan domestic law; tax classification is governed by the Company Act form. Available elections: (1) election of small-scale trader treatment at the 1% Business Tax rate where average monthly sales do not exceed TWD 80,000 (Article 13, Business Tax Act); (2) election to carry forward net operating losses for up to 10 years under Article 39 of the Income Tax Act, conditioned on approved financial accounts and records; (3) election to claim investment tax credits under the Statute for Industrial Innovation for qualifying R&D, smart machinery, and green energy equipment; (4) election of standard 5% VAT filing where a business would otherwise fall under the small-scale trader regime; (5) election to apply for input VAT refund on zero-rated export transactions under Article 39 of the Business Tax Act; (6) no consolidated or group income tax election is available to ordinary holding companies; hybrid mismatch elections are not recognized in Taiwan domestic law, and any cross-border mismatch outcome is governed by the applicable bilateral tax treaty.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

TWD — No statutory de minimis turnover threshold for ordinary Business Tax registration; every business selling goods or services in Taiwan must complete 稅籍登記 before commencement (Article 28, Business Tax Act). Special regimes: small-scale traders (小規模營業人) with average monthly sales below or equal to TWD 80,000 (approximately TWD 960,000 per annum) are taxed at 1% on deemed revenue and may be exempted from issuing uniform invoices; businesses with monthly sales between TWD 80,001 and TWD 200,000 pay 1% unless they elect standard 5% VAT accounting. A pure equity-holding SPV receiving only exempt dividend income is outside the Business Tax sales base and is not required to file periodic VAT returns, but must complete 稅籍登記 or be registered as a withholding entity if it maintains a Taiwan business presence.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Article 7 of the Business Tax Act grants a 0% rate to: (1) goods physically exported, supported by a customs export declaration; (2) services supplied to foreign enterprises for use wholly outside Taiwan with consideration paid in foreign currency and actually received in Taiwan; (3) international transportation services; (4) services rendered to foreign vessels and aircraft; (5) sales to duty-free shops, foreign embassies, and international organizations; (6) goods and services directly related to export transactions. A pure equity-holding SPV may claim zero-rating on qualifying offshore management, advisory, or administrative services only if a written service contract exists, a zero-rated uniform invoice is issued, foreign-currency consideration is received and credited in Taiwan, and the economic use of the service occurs wholly outside Taiwan. Dividend receipts are not 'sales of goods or services' and are therefore outside the Business Tax base: they generate neither output VAT nor zero-rated output tax.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) authenticated by the Customs Administration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (提單/空運提單) evidencing physical export
  • Inward Foreign Exchange Remittance Certificate (結匯水單) or bank statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency
  • Commercial Invoice (商業發票) issued in the contract currency
  • Packing List (裝箱單)
  • Zero-Rated Sales Register (零稅率銷售額清單) filed with the periodic Business Tax return

Mandatory digital e-invoicing (電子發票): all VAT-registered enterprises must issue e-invoices through the Ministry of Finance E-Invoice Integrated Platform (財政部電子發票整合服務平台) or a certified Value-Added Center (加值中心) using Turnkey/API interfaces. Issuance data must be uploaded to the platform within 48 hours of invoice issuance for B2B and B2C invoices; B2C carrier (載具) storage and the Uniform Invoice Lottery are integrated. Export zero-rated sales must be declared in the periodic Business Tax return together with the zero-rated sales register (零稅率銷售額清單); customs export declaration data are automatically transmitted from the Customs Administration to the National Taxation Bureau. Failure to timely upload e-invoice data is subject to penalties under Articles 48 and 52 of the Business Tax Act.

Joint audits by the National Taxation Bureau and the Customs Administration compare customs export declarations, e-invoice data, foreign-exchange receipts, and bank flows. If zero-rating is claimed without physical export, with misdeclared goods or services, or in respect of export proceeds not repatriated within the statutory period, the VAT is retrospectively clawed back with interest. Article 51 of the Business Tax Act imposes a penalty of up to 5 times the evaded tax; Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act, as amended in 2021, imposes criminal liability of up to 7 years' imprisonment for fraudulent evasion. The statutory assessment period is 7 years for non-filing or fraudulent cases and 5 years for ordinary under-declarations (Article 21, Tax Collection Act); repeated offenses may result in suspension or revocation of the exporter's customs registration and loss of zero-rating privileges.

Non-resident shareholders do not file Form 17; the Taiwan-resident SPV acts as withholding agent on distributions to non-residents. For the SPV itself, report only Taiwan-source operational revenue. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.
Form 17 Line 4 / Box 04 (Cost of Goods Sold) For an equity holding SPV, cost of goods sold is zero unless the entity derives service or trading income outside the passive holding scope. If the SPV is a pure holding company, no COGS entry. Non-resident parent should ensure no intercompany cost allocations without transfer pricing documentation. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.
Form 17 Line 5 / Box 05 (Operating Expenses) Report deductible administrative expenses: audit, legal, registered office, bank fees, local directors' remuneration. Support with invoices and withholding tax certificates. Management fees paid to the Chinese parent are deductible only if arm's length, supported by a Taiwan transfer pricing report, and subject to applicable withholding (20% or treaty rate, unless a Taiwan branch of the foreign recipient). Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.
Form 17 Line 8 / Box 08 (Non-operating Income and Gains) Report interest income, FX gains, and other non-operating income. Dividends from the China subsidiary are tax-exempt under Article 42 if the SPV holds at least 20% equity for at least 12 months; record in the exempt income memorandum account. Exempt dividends must still be disclosed on Schedule 5. The China subsidiary's dividend distribution is subject to Chinese withholding tax of 10% under the Cross-Strait Agreement; the Taiwan SPV may claim a foreign tax credit only to offset Taiwan tax on that income, which is generally nil. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.
Form 17 Line 8a / Exempt Dividend Reconciliation Reconcile Article 42 exempt dividends: payor name, tax ID, amount, date paid, and shareholding percentage. Document 12-month holding period and direct investment qualification. Attach China subsidiary's dividend resolution, Chinese tax withholding certificate, and China Tax Residency Certificate for the subsidiary. The Chinese WHT is a final cost unless a foreign tax credit can be used against other Taiwan-source income (normally unavailable to a pure SPV). Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.
Form 17 Line 13 / Box 13 (Net Taxable Income before Loss Carryforward) Compute taxable income after adding back non-deductible expenses: penalties, retained earnings surtax, and entertainment over statutory limits. A pure holding SPV may report a tax loss if operating expenses exceed nil revenue. Tax losses are available only to the Taiwan-resident entity and may be subject to continuity-of-business review if the shareholding structure changes. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.

Loss carryforward benefits remain available if the SPV continually files returns and maintains its status as a Taiwan-resident company. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. Even a nil return must be filed timely to avoid penalties on the imputed minimum.
Form 17 Lines 17-19 (Basic Tax / AMT) Apply the Alternative Minimum Tax (basic tax) at 12% to taxable income plus preferential deductions. Exempt dividend income under Article 42 generally does not trigger AMT because it is derived from qualifying profit-seeking enterprises. AMT applies to the Taiwan-resident SPV; non-resident shareholders are not subject to Taiwan AMT on dividends. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Understatement or fraud penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax under Article 110; responsible person may face travel bans. The China subsidiary's dividends must be clearly traced from source to the memorandum account for future distribution to shareholders. Late filing: 3% per month of assessed tax for the first 30 days, then 0.5% per month thereafter, capped at 15% of unpaid tax. Non-payment surcharge: 1% every 2 days up to 30 days. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: TWD 50 million in aggregate annual related-party transactions for mandatory Local File preparation; small business exemption applies where annual operating revenue is below TWD 30 million, but a pure holding SPV must nevertheless maintain a TP report if intercompany loans, service fees, or guarantee fees exceed TWD 10 million or if the tax authority requests documentation.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not have a separate ESR law, but the Income Tax Act (Articles 12-6-1, 43-1, 43-3) and the Cross-Strait Agreement's Limitation-on-Benefits provision require the SPV to demonstrate real business purpose, local management, employees/office, independent decision-making, and actual exercise of shareholder rights. A mailbox company used merely to route dividends to the Chinese parent may be disregarded, with the 10% treaty rate denied and the 21% statutory rate retroactively imposed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (虛報漏稅)

Statutory sanction: Article 110 Income Tax Act: fine of up to 3 times the underpaid tax; Tax Collection Act Article 62: imprisonment for 1-7 years and fine up to TWD 30 million; travel ban imposed under Article 24 for unpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns are jointly liable for the underpaid tax; serious fraud may lead to imprisonment for the actual decision-maker; courts may pierce the corporate veil to hold directors personally liable for restitution.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse (紙上公司)

Statutory sanction: Tax authority disregards the entity and reattributes income to the beneficial owner; 21% WHT imposed retroactively with penalties up to 3 times underpaid tax; company may be deregistered for false declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for the differential tax if the entity lacks assets; travel bans and civil restitution orders can be imposed; criminal liability under the Company Act for false registrations.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Deficiency (洗錢防制缺失)

Statutory sanction: Money Laundering Control Act: fine of TWD 1-20 million for AML/CFT violations; criminal penalties up to 5 years imprisonment and fine up to TWD 10 million; suspension of bank operations possible.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability on directors and AML officers for intentional or grossly negligent BO filing failures; travel bans and asset freeze orders; civil liability for facilitated money laundering.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure (移轉訂價文據缺失)

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment plus penalty of 1x to 2.5x underpaid tax; failure to produce TP documentation may lead to estimated assessment with penalty up to 3x; late filing correction fines.

Director personal liability: Directors are liable for maintaining contemporaneous documentation; intentional manipulation of intercompany pricing may lead to personal joint liability and imprisonment for tax fraud.
Withholding Tax Violations (扣繳違規)

Statutory sanction: Withholding agent liable for uncollected tax plus penalty of 1x-3x; collected but unpaid: penalty 1x-2x; late Form 31 penalties of TWD 3,750-45,000.

Director personal liability: The responsible person in charge of withholding is jointly liable under Article 25-1 if the entity cannot pay; false documentation for reduced WHT may trigger forgery and tax evasion charges with up to 7 years imprisonment.
Retained Earnings Surtax Evasion (未分配盈餘規避)

Statutory sanction: Surtax assessed under Article 66-9 at 5%; penalty up to 3 times the underpaid surtax; interest accrues at postal savings rates; memorandum account compliance failures carry separate penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors personally liable for false equity declarations; if the SPV cannot pay, responsible persons face personal liability and travel bans.
