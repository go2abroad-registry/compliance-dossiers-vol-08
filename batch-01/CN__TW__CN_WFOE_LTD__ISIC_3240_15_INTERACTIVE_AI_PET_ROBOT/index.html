Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

There is no general statutory escrow requirement. Foreign investment approval is generally required before foreign capital is remitted and invested, subject to applicable exceptions.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Taiwan (target jurisdiction): Ministry of Finance (財政部, MOF), administered by the five Regional National Taxation Bureaus (國稅局); unified e-tax portal: https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw. China (entity home jurisdiction): State Taxation Administration (國家稅務總局, STA); Electronic Tax Bureau portal: https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

China WFOE home registration: (1) obtain a Unified Social Credit Code (統一社會信用代碼) on the SAMR business license, which is the statutory tax ID for all invoicing and tax filing; (2) complete tax registration with the local STA office within 30 days of license issuance, submitting taxpayer status, accounting basis, legal representative, and bank account particulars; (3) apply for VAT general taxpayer status when annual taxable sales reach RMB 5 million (compulsory threshold) or voluntarily below it. Taiwan market-side registration: (1) for pure physical goods export into Taiwan with no Taiwan fixed place of business, the WFOE is not required to obtain a Taiwanese tax identification number; Taiwan Business Tax on imported robotic pet toys is collected by Taiwan Customs from the Taiwan importer/purchaser; (2) if a Taiwan branch is established, apply for branch registration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs, then register for profit-seeking enterprise income tax and business tax with the local National Taxation Bureau to obtain a Unified Business Number (統一編號); (3) if the WFOE directly supplies cross-border electronic/offshore services to Taiwanese individuals, it must complete online business tax registration once annual turnover exceeds NT$480,000.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the China WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at 25% (15% if certified as a High/New Technology Enterprise); no pass-through election exists for limited liability entities. Taiwan: a Taiwan branch or PE of the WFOE is taxed at the entity level at 20% profit-seeking enterprise income tax on Taiwan-source income only; Territorial_Scope applies because non-Taiwan-source manufacturing profits are excluded absent a Taiwan PE. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the China WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise taxed on worldwide income at 25% (15% if certified as a High/New Technology Enterprise); no pass-through election exists for limited liability entities. Taiwan: a Taiwan branch or PE of the WFOE is taxed at the entity level at 20% profit-seeking enterprise income tax on Taiwan-source income only; Territorial_Scope applies because non-Taiwan-source manufacturing profits are excluded absent a Taiwan PE. Tax_Exempt_Zone status is not available for this ISIC 3240 manufacturing/export business in either jurisdiction.

Available election forms

China: no check-the-box election; available elections include (1) VAT general taxpayer vs. small-scale taxpayer status below the RMB 5 million annual turnover threshold, (2) special reorganization tax treatment under Caishui [2009] No. 59, and (3) the standard export 'exemption, credit, and refund' (免抵退) election arising from general taxpayer status. Taiwan: no check-the-box election; available elections include (1) Advance Pricing Agreements for related-party cross-border transactions, (2) CFC/PEM exemption declarations under the Income Tax Act Articles 43-3 and 43-4 for bona fide offshore business structures, and (3) election of branch vs. subsidiary legal form at MOEA registration. Both jurisdictions apply OECD-aligned hybrid mismatch and general anti-avoidance rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Taiwan: NT$480,000 annual taxable sales of cross-border electronic services by non-resident sellers, NT$80,000 per month for goods / NT$40,000 per month for services for domestic small-scale enterprises, no registration threshold for physical imports which are taxed at customs. China: RMB 5,000,000 annual turnover is the compulsory VAT general taxpayer registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The AI bionic pet robot is manufactured in mainland China and exported to Taiwan; China's VAT export zero-rating applies under the 'exemption, credit, and refund' (免抵退) mechanism provided the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer, the goods are physically shipped out of PRC customs territory, and a valid export customs declaration is filed. The applicable export rebate rate for HS 9503 toy/robotic products is the full 13% statutory VAT rate. Taiwan Business Tax is not zero-rated on this particular cross-strait flow because the taxable import event occurs in Taiwan: Taiwan Customs assesses a 5% business tax on the imported AI pet robot at importation. Subsequent re-export from Taiwan would qualify for zero-rating under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act.

  • PRC Customs Export Declaration (出口貨物報關單) matching HS code 9503 (robotic toy animals / ISIC 3240)
  • VAT special invoice or qualifying export invoice issued by the WFOE reflecting the export sale
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment to a Taiwan port
  • Bank inward remittance certificate / SAFE foreign exchange settlement record proving collection from the Taiwan buyer
  • Export sales contract, commercial invoice, and packing list
  • Taiwan import clearance record (進口報單) and proof of Taiwan customs duty and business tax payment (完稅證明)

Taiwan: mandatory e-invoice (電子發票) regime for all VAT general taxpayers; e-invoice data must be transmitted via MOF-certified Turnkey Service Centers (加值服務中心) to the Integrated E-Invoice Platform (電子發票整合服務平台) on a continuous basis, with a consolidated VAT return filed every two months; B2B API/GUI machine-readable submission is mandatory. China: fully digitalized electronic invoices (數電票/全面數字化電子發票) are mandatory; invoices are issued through the STA E-Invoice Platform with near-real-time data upload, automatic red-invoice control, and mandatory invoice verification for input VAT credit claims.

China: fraudulent export VAT refund claims are criminalized under Article 204 of the Criminal Law, with imprisonment of 5-10 years (extendable to life in severe cases) and fines of 1-5 times the refund defrauded; administrative clawback under the Export Tax Refund Administration Measures requires repayment of the refund plus interest surcharge, with a 5-year assessment limitation period (10 years for fraud); STA and the General Administration of Customs jointly monitor abnormal export pricing, HS code misclassification, and round-tripping of identical goods. Taiwan: any fraudulent zero-rating claim, import duty misdeclaration, or fabrication of export documentation exposes the taxpayer to a 1-10x underpaid business tax penalty under Article 51 of the Business Tax Act, criminal liability under Article 41 (imprisonment up to 5 years), and a 5-year retroactive audit window (7 years in fraud cases). The 180-day foreign exchange repatriation deadline for export proceeds is enforced under SAFE rules for the Chinese exporter; late collection must be documented with bank corroboration to avoid exchange-control and export refund clawback reviews.

A100000 Line 23 (应纳税额) Tax computed at 25% (or 15% for High and New Technology Enterprise) on the adjusted taxable income. The tax rate applied affects the distributable surplus available for the Taiwan shareholder. Late filing: RMB 2,000–10,000 (serious: RMB 10,000–50,000); daily late-payment surcharge 0.05% of overdue tax; penalty for under-declared tax: 50%–500%; criminal liability for false filing under Criminal Law Article 201 (tax evasion: imprisonment up to 3 years, or 3–7 years for huge amounts exceeding 30% of taxable income plus fines).
A100000 Line 29 (实际应纳所得税额) Tax payable after foreign tax credits and treaty relief offsets. Domestic tax credits should not be conflated with the Cross-Strait Agreement dividend reduction, which is applied at the withholding stage. Late filing: RMB 2,000–10,000 (serious: RMB 10,000–50,000); daily late-payment surcharge 0.05% of overdue tax; penalty for under-declared tax: 50%–500%; criminal liability for false filing under Criminal Law Article 201 (tax evasion: imprisonment up to 3 years, or 3–7 years for huge amounts exceeding 30% of taxable income plus fines).
A100000 Line 32 (应补（退）所得税额) Settlement balance: annual tax liability minus quarterly prepayments. A persistent refund position with a profitable Taiwan parent may trigger tax audit and transfer-pricing risk. Late filing: RMB 2,000–10,000 (serious: RMB 10,000–50,000); daily late-payment surcharge 0.05% of overdue tax; penalty for under-declared tax: 50%–500%; criminal liability for false filing under Criminal Law Article 201 (tax evasion: imprisonment up to 3 years, or 3–7 years for huge amounts exceeding 30% of taxable income plus fines).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File (本地文档): annual related-party tangible-asset transactions exceeding RMB 200 million; related-party intangible-asset transactions exceeding RMB 100 million; related-party financial-asset transactions exceeding RMB 100 million; other related-party transactions exceeding RMB 40 million. Mandatory Master File (主体文档): total annual related-party transactions exceeding RMB 1 billion. Any related-party transaction triggers the annual related-party disclosure schedules (A102000 series) filed with the annual CIT return. Local File must be prepared by the documentation deadline and submitted to the tax authority within 20 days of a statutory notice.

Economic substance scope: The PRC does not apply British-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, substance requirements are enforced through (i) the Beneficial Owner test in SAT Announcement 2018 No. 9 requiring substantive business activities, personnel, assets, and risk control in Taiwan; (ii) the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) in EIT Law Article 47 and implementing rules; (iii) the substance-over-form doctrine allowing disregard or recharacterization of entities and transactions lacking bona fide business purpose; (iv) special tax adjustment rules on thin capitalization, related-party debt, and excessive royalty payments. A Taiwan intermediate holding company without real operations or with profits largely entitled to reduced taxation will be denied the 5% dividend rate and subjected to deemed dividend and transfer-pricing adjustments.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (逃税)

Statutory sanction: Tax collection with daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; penalty of 50%–500% of the unpaid tax (Tax Collection Administration Law Articles 40, 63); criminal prosecution under Criminal Law Article 201: imprisonment up to 7 years plus fines of 1–5 times the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, financial officer directly responsible, and actual controllers face criminal prosecution; PRC courts and tax authorities may impose travel bans and exit restrictions under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 44 until tax liabilities are settled; civil restitution is recoverable from directors who participated in fraud or dissipated company assets.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disregard or recharacterization under GAAR (EIT Law Article 47); denial of treaty benefits and the 5% dividend rate; deemed-dividend adjustment of the diverted profits; penalty of 50%–500% of short-paid tax plus 5% special-tax-adjustment interest per annum for up to 10 years; possible revocation of the WFOE's business license.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and actual controllers are jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax; they may be subject to personal asset freezing, exit bans, and criminal liability for fraudulent registration and evasion-related offenses.
Fraudulent Special VAT Invoices (虚开增值税专用发票)

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Articles 204–205: fines and imprisonment up to life sentence for fraudulent issuance of special VAT invoices; confiscation of property for particularly serious cases; administrative revocation of VAT invoice quota and export refund eligibility.

Director personal liability: In a unit crime, the legal representative, financial executives, and procurement officers who executed the scheme are criminally liable even if the company is fined; courts issue asset freezes and travel bans during investigation; civil compensation is available to the customs and tax authorities within the statutory framework.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Transfer-pricing adjustment to arm's-length result with 5% per annum special tax adjustment interest on the underpaid tax; penalty of 50% of the underpaid tax (Tax Collection Administration Law Article 63); dual-filing non-compliance penalty of RMB 2,000–10,000 for the annual CIT return and up to RMB 100,000 for refusal to provide contemporaneous documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors and general manager who approved the pricing policy are exposed to being named in the tax adjustment notice; personal liability attaches if the transfer pricing scheme was used to evade tax and the director knowingly caused the WFOE to default, triggering joint and several liability for the tax debt and potential criminal complicity.
Failure to Withhold Dividend WHT and Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: The WFOE as withholding agent is jointly and severally liable for the un-withheld tax under Tax Collection Administration Law Article 69, with penalties of 50%–300% of the un-collected tax; excess interest is disallowed as a deduction and recharacterized as a deemed dividend subject to WHT; late-payment surcharge accrues from the distribution date.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the unlawful distribution may be personally ordered to repay the distributed funds to the company for satisfaction of tax debts; the legal representative is subject to exit restriction until the tax is settled; civil fraud liability against the board exists where the distribution was made with knowledge of unpaid taxes.
