Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online electronic portal: National Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) and the Electronic Invoice Service Platform; customs/tax data integration via the China International Trade Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For a PRC WFOE, after incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation and issuance of a business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code, the entity must complete tax registration information confirmation through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 calendar days. The 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code is the statutory Tax Identification Number and VAT registration number; no separate VAT ID is issued. Required items include the foreign investor's identity and beneficial ownership documents, the articles of association, a registered operating address proof, bank account details, and representative person identification. For a foreign non-resident enterprise without an established entity or permanent establishment in China, a non-resident taxpayer identification number may be obtained by application through the withholding agent at the competent tax authority under the PRC Non-Resident Enterprise Tax Administration Rules.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a PRC WFOE, after incorporation with the State Administration for Market Regulation and issuance of a business license bearing the Unified Social Credit Code, the entity must complete tax registration information confirmation through the Electronic Tax Bureau within 30 calendar days. The 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code is the statutory Tax Identification Number and VAT registration number; no separate VAT ID is issued. VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory general VAT taxpayer registration; however, every WFOE must complete tax registration upon incorporation regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A VAT-registered PRC WFOE may apply the 0% export VAT rate to physical goods, including medical nitrile glove shipments to Taiwan and other destinations, only where the goods are physically exported from PRC customs territory, filed in the Export Goods Customs Declaration Form through the China International Trade Single Window, sold to a non-resident purchaser, and the foreign currency proceeds are repatriated through bank channels to the WFOE's PRC bank account within the statutory time limit. Qualifying cross-border services supplied to overseas clients and wholly consumed outside the PRC may also be zero-rated if the recipient is offshore and the documentation demonstrates true offshore consumption.

  • Export Goods Customs Declaration Form (export-tax-rebate special copy) generated via the China International Trade Single Window
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Export Sales Contract
  • VAT Special Invoice evidencing the input VAT on the exported goods
  • Bank Foreign Exchange Receipt Declaration Form / Export Proceeds Collection Information matched to the export declaration

Mandatory use of the PRC tax control system. Fully digitalized electronic invoices must be issued through the State Taxation Administration's Electronic Invoice Service Platform; invoice data are cleared in near-real time through the national electronic invoice clearing system. Each digital invoice carries an anti-forgery tax control code and digital signature. Paper VAT invoices must be issued through tax control equipment with real-time reporting to the tax authority.

Export tax authorities and customs share export data through the China International Trade Single Window. If zero-rating or refund is not substantiated by the customs declaration, bill of lading, VAT special invoice, or export proceeds receipt, the STA will claw back the refunded VAT plus interest from the date of refund and impose a late-payment surcharge. Where false declaration or fraudulent export refund is established, penalties under Article 63 of the Tax Collection and Administration Law range from 50% to five times the tax involved; criminal liability for fraudulent export tax rebate under Article 204 of the Criminal Law may result in detention, imprisonment up to life, fines, or confiscation of property. Exporters placed on the tax untrustworthy party list may also be suspended from export tax rebate privileges.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

For the mainland WFOE, RMB is convertible for current-account items such as goods trade, service fees, dividends, and tax remittances after the designated bank verifies the underlying authenticity, documentation, and SAFE transaction codes; no prior SAFE approval is required for current-account remittances. Capital-account inflows, including FDI registered-capital contributions, shareholder loans, and equity issuance, remain subject to mandatory prior SAFE registration and the 'payment-to-bank' settlement principle, whereby foreign-currency capital can only be converted to RMB for actual enterprise uses such as raw-material purchases, salaries, rent, and tax payments, not for speculative financial purposes. Profit remittance by a WFOE is treated as a current-account outflow once the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax finalisation, and full tax payment or withholding (including the 10% dividend withholding tax for non-resident shareholders, potentially reduced to 5% under the applicable cross-strait tax arrangement if beneficial-ownership certification is obtained) are completed; the remitting bank will require the tax-clearance certificate, audited financial statements, and a valid shareholder dividend resolution. Taiwan-side NT$ funds are freely convertible and remittable subject to FSC anti-money-laundering and large-amount reporting, but all Taiwan-origin funds destined for the mainland must enter through the mainland's FDI registration and banking verification pipeline.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Rules on the Foreign Exchange Administration of Trade in Goods (汇发[2012]38号, as amended), export proceeds must be repatriated onshore and credited to the WFOE's foreign-exchange collection account or settled into RMB with a designated bank within the statutory period, generally not later than 30 days after receipt, and in all cases within the customs/SAFE export-receipt verification cycle. There is no mandatory percentage surrender to the central bank; the WFOE may retain export receipts in a permitted foreign-exchange settlement account, but the entire amount must be captured in SAFE's monitoring system and match customs declarations. If converted, each conversion must be supported by a truthful payment-purpose declaration. The WFOE may also use its foreign-exchange capital account to pay overseas vendors directly in foreign currency, but this does not extinguish the repatriation-timeline obligation. On the Taiwan side, the CBC similarly requires export proceeds to be settled through a licensed bank with periodic declaration of overseas remittances.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China, operating under the People's Bank of China (PBOC); Taiwan-side cross-strait flow oversight by the Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC) and the Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility for onshore treasury use. Offshore digital EMIs such as Airwallex, Currenxie, or Payoneer are not licensed by PBOC under the Payment Administration Rules and cannot open local settlement accounts, undertake RMB clearing, or perform statutory tax/customs/SAFE reporting for the mainland WFOE. Such EMIs may only serve as payment facilitators for inbound collections if they operate through a licensed domestic third-party payment institution (e.g., Alipay, WeChat Pay, UnionPay), but they cannot replace the WFOE's mandatory domestic commercial-bank relationship. Direct use of an unlicensed overseas EMI as a treasury counterparty would present a significant AML/KYC red flag and may breach PRC payment-clearance regulations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound FDI capital from the Taiwan shareholder is routed by SWIFT MT103 (single-leg customer wire) or MT202 COVER through a USD or RMB clearing channel to the WFOE's FDI capital account opened with a SAFE-designated bank. The remittance narrative must state 'FDI capital contribution to registered capital of [CN_WFOE_LTD] – no commercial trade invoice'. The receiving bank validates the SAFE FDI registration in the National Foreign Exchange Administration Platform and requires supporting documents including the business licence, the foreign-exchange registration certificate, the articles of association, the capital contribution schedule, and a shareholder board resolution. The funds cannot be mingled with operational funds in the basic RMB account. Subsequent conversion of the capital to RMB must be executed through the 'Capital Settlement Bank' under the 'payment-to-bank' principle; for each drawdown the WFOE must submit a signed authenticity declaration and underlying-use documentation such as raw-material purchase contracts, payroll schedules, tax certificates, or utility invoices. Deferred capital contributions, prepayment of share capital, or conversion of debt into equity require additional SAFE and MOFCOM/registration approvals and cannot be processed under standard MT103 instructions.For export settlements of sterile examination nitrile gloves, the primary clearing rail is a SWIFT MT103 T/T remittance from the overseas buyer to the WFOE's export collection account; each receipt is verified against the customs export declaration using SAFE's goods-trade monitoring system. For high-value or credit-supported shipments, an irrevocable documentary letter of credit is advised through SWIFT MT700 and settled under UCP600 by presenting conforming documents, including commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin, quality certificate, and any buyer-required inspection certificates. After settlement, export proceeds are subject to the 30-day repatriation timeline and must be declared under SAFE transaction code 121010 for goods exports. Disbursements to upstream suppliers, such as nitrile latex or packaging-material vendors, may be made directly from the FDI capital account in foreign currency or by purchasing foreign exchange from the operational RMB account under current-account self-payment rules, with supporting import customs declarations and invoices.Deliverable onshore RMB/FX outright forwards with a licensed Chinese bank under a SAFE-approved master agreement and genuine current-account or foreign-debt exposure.RMB/FX foreign-exchange swaps used to manage cash-flow mismatches between import payments and export receipts while maintaining the underlying trade exposure.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps (CCIRS) for medium- to long-term hedging of foreign-currency liabilities or intrinsic capital-equivalent positions arising from the cross-strait treasury structure.European-style vanilla FX options (call or put) with expiry aligned to the underlying export order or import payment flow, permitted for hedging and not for speculative gain.Offshore RMB NDF contracts executed by the Taiwan parent or an offshore treasury affiliate (not by the mainland WFOE) to hedge NT$/USD and RMB exposure outside SAFE's onshore deliverable-forward regime; the WFOE itself cannot conclude NDFs onshore because SAFE mandates physical delivery and genuine underlying documentation.
