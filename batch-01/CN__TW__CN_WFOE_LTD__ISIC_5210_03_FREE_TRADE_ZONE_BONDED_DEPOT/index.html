Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is fully convertible for current-account transactions and only conditionally convertible on capital account. For this Taiwan-owned WFOE operating a bonded warehouse in an FTZ: (i) inbound FDI equity capital is permitted only after SAFE-authorized FDI registration by the domestic bank and (ii) outbound profit/dividend remittance to the Taiwan shareholder requires completion of the annual statutory audit, PRC enterprise income tax final settlement (汇算清缴), issuance of the Tax Payment Certificate (完税证明), and, where the amount exceeds the equivalent of USD 50,000, the electronic cross-department tax filing for outbound service-trade payments under the State Taxation Administration and SAFE 2021 No. 19 announcement. The FTZ 'inside border, outside customs' bonded status does not create capital-account convertibility; it relaxes customs documentation for goods flows only. Capital-account proceeds may be used on the convenience payment basis under SAFE Circular 汇发〔2023〕28号, subject to the negative list (prohibition on equity/securities investment, entrusted loans, real estate development outside the ordinary course, and illegal lending). Warehouse storage and handling fee receivables from overseas clients are current-account service trade and may be settled in RMB or foreign currency at the enterprise's will.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Statutory mandatory surrender of export proceeds was abolished by the 2008 PRC Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations and the 2012 SAFE goods-trade administration reform (汇发〔2012〕38号). No minimum conversion percentage or fixed statutory surrender timeline applies; all export and current-account goods/service proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic onshore FX account of the WFOE (offshore retention requires SAFE approval) and may be converted into RMB at the enterprise's discretion (意愿结汇). The operative discipline is SAFE's ASONE goods-trade aggregate monitoring framework: export receipts are matched against customs declarations, and receivables outstanding beyond 90 days from the customs release date must be justified and registered on the trade-monitoring system; failure to do so can downgrade the entity to B/C class status and curtail invoice-matching privileges. For bonded-zone depot transactions, cross-border payments executed against goods entering or leaving the zone must be supported by the customs 核注清单/报关单 records; the simplified bonded-zone FX administration rules apply and the bank will archive the electronic customs matching reference before processing settlement.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange of the People's Republic of China (国家外汇管理局, SAFE) operating under the People's Bank of China (中国人民银行, PBOC); SAFE is the operative licensing, monitoring, and enforcement authority for all foreign exchange receipt, payment, settlement, and conversion activity of the WFOE, with local FTZ sub-bureaus supervising the bonded-depot entity through the ASONE digital foreign-exchange administration platform.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-Medium as a primary treasury rail and strictly prohibited as an escrow/settlement layer without a PBOC payment-institution licence. Offshore digital EMIs (Airwallex, PingPong, XTransfer, and HK-licensed MSOs) may provide front-end trade-route orchestration, invoice matching, and small-value collection attribution, but underlying funds must settle into the WFOE's licensed onshore bank account or through the EMI's licensed Chinese payment affiliate under the SAFE cross-border payment institution regime (汇发〔2019〕13号). Customs 核注清单 and the bonded warehouse ledger cannot be intermediated by an offshore EMI. The legal classification is therefore an ancillary receivable-collection tool, not the clearing backbone for FDI, LC, or customs-duty flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent transmits equity via SWIFT MT103 (or CIPS if denominated in CNY) to the WFOE's capital-proceeds account (资本金账户) at the onshore bank designated in the SAFE FDI information registration. The remittance instructions must carry the purpose code for FDI equity investment capital (SAFE 涉外收支交易分类 code 622013/622023 series, 外国来华直接投资—投资资本金), the FDI registration reference number, and the WFOE's full name. The receiving bank verifies the SAFE capital-account system record and the 外商投资信息报告 filed with the market-regulatory authority, credits the capital account, then converts into RMB at the enterprise's will (意愿结汇) into an RMB capital-proceeds account under 汇发〔2023〕28号 convenience policy. Proceeds may fund bonded-depot build-out, shelving and equipment, rental, staff costs, customs-bonded fees, and related operating expenditures; prohibited uses (securities investment, entrusted loans, real estate development outside the ordinary scope) are bank-monitored through ex-post random inspection. USD is the recommended bridge currency because TWD is not a freely deliverable clearing currency onshore for settlement legs; the Taiwan parent should convert TWD to USD before remission. Supplementary liquidity can be obtained through a SAFE-registered external debt (shareholder loan) from the Taiwan parent under the macro-prudential debt ceiling, settled via the same capital account with a separate external-debt registration record.(1) SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier, contractor, logistics, and service-fee payments, tagged with SAFE trade/service purpose codes and supported by the customs 核注清单 for goods-related legs plus invoice, contract, and transport documents; (2) Commercial letters of credit (sight or usance, USD or CNY) issued by the onshore bank for high-value imported goods consigned into the bonded warehouse, with proceeds to the beneficiary bank via MT103/MT202 within the LC terms; (3) Border-tax stage payments: while goods remain bonded, duty/VAT is deferred, so when goods leave the zone for domestic customs clearance the treasury must pre-fund the payable through the 单一窗口 海关税费电子支付 module to avoid detention; (4) Domestic clearing: zone-to-onzone supplier and client transfers use RMB local clearing (大额/小额支付系统 or commercial bank e-banking), while intra-zone counterparty transactions may be denominated in FX or RMB under bonded-zone FX rules; Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% corporate income tax on taxable income of a Taiwan resident company; a Taiwan branch of a foreign company is generally taxed at the same rate on Taiwan-source income attributable to the branch.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

21% Taiwan withholding tax generally applies to dividends distributed by a Taiwan company to a non-resident shareholder. Taiwan companies generally do not impose a separate branch-profit remittance withholding tax in the same manner as a dividend, but Taiwan-source payments to a foreign head office may be subject to withholding according to their legal characterization.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return is generally due from 1 May through 31 May of the following year. A company using a non-calendar fiscal year generally files within the statutory filing period following the end of its fiscal year. Provisional income tax filing, where applicable, is generally due by the end of the ninth month of the fiscal year, with payment of one-half of the preceding year's assessed tax or the estimated current-year tax under the applicable rules.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Taiwan transfer-pricing documentation is not governed by a single universal threshold. A Local File is generally required for a company with annual net operating revenue exceeding the prescribed threshold, commonly NT$500 million, or with controlled transactions exceeding the applicable prescribed threshold, commonly NT$200 million in aggregate for the relevant category or reporting period, subject to current regulations and exemptions. A Master File is generally required for a Taiwan entity that is part of a multinational enterprise group and meets the applicable annual revenue and cross-border controlled-transaction conditions, commonly including annual net operating revenue of at least NT$3 billion and prescribed cross-border related-party transactions. Country-by-Country reporting may apply to a Taiwan constituent entity of a multinational group meeting the group-wide consolidated revenue threshold, generally EUR750 million, subject to Taiwan rules and filing requirements.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not operate a universal standalone economic-substance regime identical to the United Arab Emirates or certain offshore jurisdictions. Nevertheless, Taiwan applies substance-over-form, general anti-avoidance, controlled-foreign-company, transfer-pricing, beneficial-ownership, permanent-establishment, and anti-tax-avoidance principles. A warehouse company should maintain genuine Taiwanese personnel, decision-making, operational capacity, leased or owned premises, inventory-control systems, customs records, risk assumption, and third-party commercial evidence consistent with its declared functions. A nominal warehouse with no personnel, no operational control, circular invoicing, or artificial related-party charges may face income reallocation, denial of deductions, withholding reassessment, penalties, and permanent-establishment or beneficial-ownership challenges.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Jurisdictional entity mismatch and unlicensed operation

Statutory sanction: Business suspension, revocation of customs or warehouse privileges, seizure or reassessment of goods, customs penalties, back taxes, administrative fines, and possible criminal liability for unauthorized operation or customs violations.

Director personal liability: Directors and responsible persons may face civil compensation, joint liability for customs or tax losses where statutory conditions are met, disqualification or administrative sanctions, and criminal prosecution for deliberate unauthorized operation. Travel restrictions or imprisonment are not automatic and require statutory authority and judicial or enforcement procedures.
Tax evasion and concealed revenue

Statutory sanction: Back corporate income tax, value-added tax, withholding tax, interest, surcharges, invoice penalties, loss of deductions or tax losses, and criminal tax-evasion penalties in aggravated or intentional cases.

Director personal liability: Responsible persons may incur criminal fines or imprisonment under applicable tax statutes, civil restitution, and liability for unpaid taxes or penalties where personal participation, instruction, gross negligence, or statutory responsible-person liability is established.
Customs and bonded-goods breach

Statutory sanction: Import duty, commodity tax, business tax, customs fines, confiscation, forfeiture, suspension or cancellation of bonded status, and criminal smuggling or customs-fraud liability where intentional conduct is proven.

Director personal liability: Warehouse managers, customs declarants, directors, and other responsible persons may face personal administrative or criminal exposure for intentional false declarations, concealment, or unauthorized removal. Civil recovery may include duties, taxes, penalties, and losses.
Transfer-pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax authority adjustment to arm's-length income, denial of deductions, additional corporate income tax, withholding tax, interest, documentation penalties, and possible repeat-audit or assessment consequences.

Director personal liability: Directors generally do not incur automatic personal liability solely from a transfer-pricing adjustment, but deliberate falsification, tax evasion, breach of fiduciary duty, or knowing approval of sham transactions may create civil and criminal liability.
Withholding tax failure

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the tax that should have been withheld, withholding penalties, interest, payer liability, denial of treaty relief, and possible penalties for inaccurate withholding returns or certificates.

Director personal liability: The withholding agent and responsible persons may be liable for unpaid withholding tax and statutory penalties. Criminal exposure generally requires intentional falsification, evasion, or other aggravated conduct.
Permanent establishment and source-income misclassification

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of Taiwan-source income, corporate income tax, withholding tax, value-added tax, penalties, interest, and denial of unsupported expense allocations.

Director personal liability: Responsible persons may face civil and criminal consequences where the misclassification was intentional or involved false records. Ordinary technical disagreement without fraud does not automatically create imprisonment or travel restrictions.
Anti-money laundering and suspicious-payment failures

Statutory sanction: AML administrative fines, enhanced supervision, transaction restrictions, account freezing, reporting failures, confiscation or forfeiture of criminal proceeds, and criminal prosecution for laundering or assisting illicit funds.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and responsible employees may face personal fines, civil restitution, asset confiscation, travel restrictions, and imprisonment where they knowingly assist, conceal, or facilitate money laundering or terrorist financing.
Corporate governance and accounting falsification

Statutory sanction: Accounting and company-law fines, tax reassessment, loss of filing privileges, director sanctions, license consequences, civil damages, and criminal liability for forgery, fraud, or tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may face civil restitution, shareholder or creditor claims, disqualification, asset preservation, travel restrictions where legally ordered, and imprisonment for intentional falsification or fraud. The corporate veil is not pierced merely because the company cannot pay; personal liability requires a statutory or proven wrongful basis.
Improper profit repatriation

Statutory sanction: Withholding and corporate tax reassessment, disallowance of deductions, foreign-exchange or company-law penalties, repayment orders, and possible unlawful dividend or breach-of-duty liability.

Director personal liability: Directors approving unlawful dividends or sham payments may be personally liable for repayment, creditor or shareholder damages, and criminal breach-of-trust or fraud charges where the statutory elements are satisfied.
