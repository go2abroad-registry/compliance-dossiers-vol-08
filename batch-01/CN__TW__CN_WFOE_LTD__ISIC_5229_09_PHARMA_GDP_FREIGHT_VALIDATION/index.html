A virtual office or commercial mailbox is effectively rejected for this industry. Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-in capital applies to an ordinary limited company under the Company Act of Taiwan; the subscribed capital must be fully contributed before registration and verified by a certified public accountant. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (STA), administered through the nationwide electronic e-Tax online service platform and the STA official web portal at www.chinatax.gov.cn

E-government filing portal

(1) Foreign corporate shareholders submit notarized and apostilled constitutional documents, board resolutions, and passports/officer identification to the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) through the local integrated government service platform. (2) Upon incorporation of the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) as a Limited Liability company, SAMR issues a Business License bearing the Uniform Social Credit Code (USCC), which is automatically recognized by the STA as the enterprise Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN). (3) SAMR electronically notifies the STA of the new legal entity, and the WFOE must complete supplementary tax registration through e-Tax within 30 days, confirming permanent address, bank account information, legal representative, accounting standards, and the intended VAT taxpayer status. (4) The WFOE may then elect to register either as a small-scale VAT taxpayer or as a general VAT taxpayer in accordance with the PRC VAT regulations. (5) For foreign non-resident entities with no Chinese establishment, a non-resident taxpayer registration must be completed through e-Tax where a direct Chinese-source withholding obligation arises, and the foreign parent company may additionally apply for a Certificate of Tax Residency in its home jurisdiction to claim treaty benefits. (6) Any change to registration data, including capital investors, directors, business scope, or bank account, must be reported to the STA through e-Tax within 30 days of the change.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None - the PRC does not provide a US-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election. The only material election available to the WFOE is the voluntary registration as a general VAT taxpayer before exceeding the statutory threshold; corporate income tax classification remains entity-level and mandatory for all Chinese resident enterprises.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

CNY 5,000,000 of annual taxable turnover: exceeding this threshold makes general VAT taxpayer registration mandatory; businesses below the threshold may remain small-scale VAT taxpayers, while non-resident suppliers with Chinese-source taxable services may be required to register through e-Tax or rely on a withholding agent

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies where goods are physically exported from the PRC customs territory under a valid customs export declaration, title and risk have passed to an overseas buyer, the export consideration is received and repatriated in the invoiced currency within the statutory period, and the exporter retains the full documentary chain proving the export and payment. Qualifying offshore services, including international freight transportation and logistics/services supplied to non-resident customers where the benefit is wholly received outside China, may also be zero-rated under applicable STA export VAT rules (including Caishui [2016] No.36), provided the service recipient is a non-resident entity, the service is consumed outside China, and foreign exchange is received for the service. For GDP pharma freight, the exporter must additionally evidence the controlled-temperature logistics service through GDP-compliant cold-chain monitoring records.

  • Customs Export Declaration Form (customs clearance bill of entry)
  • Master and house Bill of Lading, airway bill, or equivalent cargo receipt evidencing physical departure from Chinese customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or the SAFE foreign exchange collection verification record evidencing actual repatriation of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice issued in accordance with PRC export invoicing rules and VAT zero-rating documentation requirements
  • Packing list
  • Original export sales contract and, for GDP pharma freight, the freight/logistics service agreement
  • Temperature excursion-free GDP monitoring logs and quality validation records substantiating the certified pharmaceutical logistics service

China mandates the issuance of fully digitalized electronic invoices (TDEI) through the STA national electronic invoice platform, requiring near-real-time API transmission of invoice data to the tax authority. Each invoice carries a unique nationwide invoice number and digital QR anti-fraud mark, and the Golden Tax Phase IV system performs automated cross-verification of invoice issuance, VAT filings, customs export declarations, and foreign exchange collection records.

The STA, in coordination with customs and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange, may deny or claw back any zero-rated VAT refund where physical export, service offshoring, or currency repatriation cannot be substantiated. The exporter must return the VAT refund received, pay interest and late-payment surcharges, and is exposed to administrative penalties ranging from 50 percent to five times the tax evaded under the PRC Tax Collection and Administration Law. Fraudulent or fictitious export VAT refund claims may also constitute criminal export tax refund fraud under Article 204 of the PRC Criminal Law, carrying imprisonment up to life in extremely serious cases, fines, and confiscation of property. The statutory audit scrutiny period generally extends three years, or ten years for fraud or deliberate evasion, and the Golden Tax Phase IV data-matching system continuously cross-checks invoices, customs exit records, logistics bills, and foreign exchange collection records.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The currency convertibility framework applicable to a China-incorporated WFOE is governed by the People's Bank of China and its foreign exchange administrator, SAFE. China maintains current account convertibility under Article VIII of the IMF Articles of Agreement; trade-related payments, freight charges, insurance, and logistics fees can be converted and remitted with genuine supporting contracts and customs declarations. The capital account, by contrast, remains heavily managed. Equity capital received from the Taiwan shareholder must be injected through a designated foreign exchange capital account after FDI registration, and any subsequent conversion into RMB is subject to the 'FDI capital settlement' regime and the negative list for foreign investment. Dividend remittances are permissible after annual audit, tax clearance, and proof of distributable profits, but require formal board resolutions and tax refund or withholding certificates. Surplus capital pending use cannot be diverted into securities, entrusted loans, or domestic real estate without specific SAFE approvals. The WFOE will also need to comply with SAFE's cross-border payment reporting, entity annual FDI reports, and bank-based verification for every current account transaction. Overall, the RMB is not freely convertible on capital account; operational liquidity inside China is stable, but exits require documentary substantiation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory conversion ratio applies to export foreign currency receipts under the current SAFE trade-in-goods reform; 100% of proceeds may be retained in a licensed foreign exchange settlement account or voluntarily converted into CNY. Export proceeds must be repatriated into a domestic foreign exchange account by the contractually agreed payment maturity, and the receipt and payment data aggregate is monitored by SAFE. Where a payment is not received within 90 days after export customs clearance, the exporter must register the overdue or deferred trade receivable in the SAFE Trade Monitoring System and provide relevant credit-term documentation; otherwise the bank may suspend current-account settlement.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) with delegated foreign exchange administration by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI entities are not viable as a primary banking rail for a China WFOE. PBOC and SAFE do not recognise foreign fintech EMI licences as bank licences in China, and such EMIs are legally barred from opening domestic RMB or foreign exchange settlement accounts, from holding FDI capital, and from performing SAFE capital verification, tax filing, or regulatory reporting. They may only function as an ancillary low-value collection utility where they partner with a licensed Chinese payment institution, but they cannot replace the Tier-1 PRC bank for statutory capital injection, clearing, or treasury concentration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder should effect equity injection by SWIFT MT103 wire to the WFOE's SAFE-registered FDI foreign exchange capital account at a licensed PRC Tier-1 bank. The wire must include the unique FDI business registration code obtained through SAFE's foreign direct investment system, state the payment purpose as Inbound Direct Investment Equity Capital, and use the SAFE-designated 6-digit transaction nature code for Inbound Direct Investment Equity Capital Injection as mapped in the settlement bank's reporting menu. The receiving bank verifies the credit against SAFE's electronic data extracts, after which the public accountant issues an independent capital verification report; the funds may then be converted into RMB under the FDI capital settlement regime.Outbound trade and freight disbursements may be cleared via SWIFT MT103 for standard cross-border wires, Commercial Letter of Credit (MT700/MT707) for high-value documentary payments, and Documentary Collections (D/P and D/A) for lower-risk settlement. CNY cross-border payments can be routed through the Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS) with same-day settlement.
