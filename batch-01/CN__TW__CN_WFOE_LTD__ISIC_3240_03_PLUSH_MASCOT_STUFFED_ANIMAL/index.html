Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Taxation Administration, Ministry of Finance, Taiwan (R.O.C.), administered through the regional National Taxation Bureaus; the online electronic filing and information services are provided through the MOF Tax e-Filing Portal and the Electronic Invoice Integration Service Platform.

        
E-government filing portal
For a foreign non-resident entity forming a WFOE Ltd in Taiwan, first obtain investment approval from the Ministry of Economic Affairs Investment Commission and complete MOEA company registration. After incorporation, file Business Tax Registration with the local National Taxation Bureau, submitting the articles of incorporation, foreign investor certificate, board resolution appointing a local statutory representative, address proof or lease for the business premises, bank account information, and foreign investor remittance certificate. The Taxation Bureau then issues the eight-digit Unified Number, which is the national Taxpayer Identification Number, together with a Business Tax Registration Certificate. Where the foreign non-resident entity has no fixed place of business or agent in Taiwan and supplies only cross-border electronic services, the MOF Taxation Administration registers the foreign entity in the simplified non-resident electronic-services taxpayer system and issues a withholding payment account or Unified Number for reverse-charge compliance. The WFOE must notify the Taxation Bureau of any registration amendments, address changes, or representative changes within 15 days.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a foreign non-resident entity forming a WFOE Ltd in Taiwan, first obtain investment approval from the Ministry of Economic Affairs Investment Commission and complete MOEA company registration. After incorporation, file Business Tax Registration with the local National Taxation Bureau, submitting the articles of incorporation, foreign investor certificate, board resolution appointing a local statutory representative, address proof or lease for the business premises, bank account information, and foreign investor remittance certificate. The Taxation Bureau then issues the eight-digit Unified Number, which is the national Taxpayer Identification Number, together with a Business Tax Registration Certificate. Where the foreign non-resident entity has no fixed place of business or agent in Taiwan and supplies only cross-border electronic services, the MOF Taxation Administration registers the foreign entity in the simplified non-resident electronic-services taxpayer system and issues a withholding payment account or Unified Number for reverse-charge compliance. The WFOE must notify the Taxation Bureau of any registration amendments, address changes, or representative changes within 15 days.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation

        
Available election forms
None for check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election purposes. A qualifying enterprise may elect the special small-business Business Tax regime at 1% if average monthly taxable sales remain below NT$200,000, but that regime is not available to a WFOE exporting zero-rated goods; the standard VAT invoice-and-input-credit regime is mandatory for exporters.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
5%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
NT$200,000 average monthly taxable sales is the threshold above which standard Value-Added Business Tax registration and Uniform Invoice issuance are mandatory; below this threshold a small-business special tax status at 1% may apply. There is no blanket exemption from registration for any person carrying on a business selling goods or services in Taiwan.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act, goods actually exported from Taiwan after Customs clearance, goods supplied outside Taiwan, and services used entirely outside Taiwan and paid in foreign currency qualify for the 0% Business Tax rate. A WFOE may claim export zero-rating only if the goods physically leave Taiwan, the Taiwan Customs export declaration is in the WFOE name, title and risk pass to the overseas buyer, and the sale proceeds are received in foreign currency. Offshore digital services can qualify where the place of supply is outside Taiwan and the recipient is a foreign territory; services consumed in Taiwan remain taxable at 5%.

Standard VAT-registered businesses must issue Uniform Invoices. The MOF Electronic Invoice Integration Service Platform receives e-invoice data through the Electronic Invoice Turnkey service and machine-readable API transmission. E-invoices carry an electronic encryption token and invoice QR anti-tampering mechanism. B2C e-invoices from designated electronic-commerce and internet businesses must be uploaded to the national platform within the statutory period, generally within 48 hours of issuance, while B2B invoices must be transmitted within the applicable filing window. Taiwan does not use real-time fiscal stamping equipment; anti-fraud control is achieved through central platform receipt, cryptographic integrity, QR verification, and periodic Business Tax filing reconciliation.

      
On audit, if the customs export proof or foreign exchange repatriation evidence is missing, incomplete, or false, the zero-rating is reversed, the 5% Business Tax is reassessed, and interest runs from the original tax filing due date. Under the Business Tax Act and the Tax Collection Act, the taxpayer may face a penalty of up to five times the short-paid tax. Deliberate falsification of an export declaration or inward remittance certificate constitutes tax evasion and may trigger criminal liability, asset preservation measures, and suspension or revocation of business tax registration.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The PRC maintains a managed floating exchange rate regime with a central parity fixing and a daily trading band against the USD. Current account items, such as export proceeds, service fees, wages and lawfully distributed dividends, are effectively convertible through authorized banks under an authenticity and rational-necessity review. Capital account items are not freely convertible. Inbound foreign direct investment must be registered with SAFE through a domestic designated bank, after which the WFOE may open a capital account facility and convert paid-in equity into RMB only under bank supervision. Outbound capital movements, including shareholder loan repayments, equity disposal proceeds, and liquidation distributions, require separate SAFE approvals or deregistration procedures. Profit remittance to the Taiwanese shareholder is achievable only after the WFOE completes its statutory audit, files corporate income tax returns, resolves retained earnings via shareholder resolution, and settles any dividend withholding tax. The remitting bank will require the audit report, tax payment vouchers, board/shareholder resolution, and a valid foreign exchange payment declaration. The RMB is not freely convertible outside the onshore clearing system, although cross-border RMB settlement is permitted for bona fide trade and FDI structures where the Taiwanese counterparty agrees to RMB.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
China abolished mandatory export proceeds surrender and there is no minimum conversion ratio. The WFOE may retain 100% of its export foreign currency receipts in an approved foreign currency settlement account, or it may convert them into RMB at the onshore market rate. If receipts are brought onshore or retained without an offshore-holding approval, the full amount must be declared under the trade current account and matched against customs declarations. The banking system performs the SAFE trade payment and collection declaration, normally on the day of receipt, with correction and verification obligation completed no later than five business days after settlement. No statutory forced conversion timeline applies. Delinquent declaration can result in the suspension of trade payment functions.

      

      
Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China. Although the parent shareholder sits in Taiwan, the WFOE is an onshore PRC entity and is therefore governed by PRC foreign exchange regulations, not Taiwan's central bank rules.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Low viability and legally barred for onshore WFOE clearing. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not licensed by the PBOC to open CNY-denominated accounts, hold deposits for a PRC entity, execute SAFE trade/financial declarations, or settle capital account flows. They cannot participate in CNAPS, CIPS, or the onshore interbank FX market. The WFOE may use such EMIs only as informal information dashboards or for expenses outside China, but all statutory liquidity clearing, FX conversion, and AML-controllable payments must route through a licensed domestic Tier-1 commercial bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        Before remittance, the Taiwanese shareholder must complete any applicable Taiwan Investment Commission authorization for mainland investment. The WFOE then completes FDI foreign exchange registration with SAFE through the nominated domestic bank and opens a capital account facility. The Taiwan parent must instruct a SWIFT MT103 wire to the WFOE capital account using the bank-declared SAFE foreign exchange purpose code for direct investment equity contribution, with the remitter name and beneficiary account precisely matching the FDI registration. The receiving bank verifies the SWIFT header, source jurisdiction, transaction purpose code, and registration reference. After credit and bank-level capital verification, an onshore certified public accountant issues the capital verification report confirming paid-in capital. Any RMB conversion from the capital account requires a separate bank-supervised settlement instruction evidencing the lawful capital usage purpose.Outbound payments to Taiwanese suppliers and third-party component vendors must be processed through the current account trade settlement rail. For open account arrangements, the WFOE instructs its domestic bank to send a SWIFT MT103 remittance carrying the applicable SAFE trade payment purpose code, supported by the sales contract, commercial invoice, packing list, customs declaration, and logistics documentation. For larger procurement or where the supplier requires bank assurance, the WFOE may open an irrevocable commercial letter of credit via SWIFT MT700; documentary collections with documents against payment or documents against acceptance are also permissible for established counterparties. All outbound disbursements must reconcile with the customs database and tax filing status. For cross-border RMB settlements to Taiwan, the payment can clear through CNAPS and CIPS, with the Taiwan beneficiary receiving RMB through an authorized RMB clearing bank. US dollar and other foreign currency settlements clear through SWIFT, correspondent banking, and CHIPS/Fedwire networks.Onshore deliverable CNY/USD forward contracts for genuine trade and approved capital-account exposures, executed through a bank with interbank FX market membershipOnshore foreign exchange swaps and cross-currency swaps for balance-sheet hedging of approved foreign-currency debt or FDI-related financial exposures, limited to the notional and maturity of the underlying exposurePlain-vanilla European-style CNY/USD options purchased from authorized banks on a real-demand basis for hedging, not for speculative open positionsNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permitted onshore for a PRC WFOE because the RMB delivery obligation must be settled onshore; offshore NDFs also cannot be used to hedge the WFOE's onshore trade or capital flows under PRC foreign exchange law
