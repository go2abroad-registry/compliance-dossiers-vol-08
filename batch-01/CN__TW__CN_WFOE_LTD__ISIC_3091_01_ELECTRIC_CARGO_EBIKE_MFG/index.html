Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

For this manufacturing WFOE engaged in ISIC 3091 electric cargo e-bike production, a virtual office or commercial mailbox is not permissible as the sole registered office. SAMR requires the registered address to correspond to actual business premises with manufacturing, warehousing, and operational capacity. Municipal tax authorities require a physical place of business for VAT registration, invoice issuance, and export refund verification, and banks conducting CDD will reject a mailbox-only address for account opening. A correspondence or registered-agent address may be used solely for service of process only while the physical factory address remains the registered office. The entity must secure an industrial factory lease of at least 36 months and maintain full commercial substance documentation before filing.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (国家税务总局, STA) of the People's Republic of China; official national portal: http://www.chinatax.gov.cn; electronic tax filing portal: https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn (provincial e-tax bureaus).

E-government filing portal

After the WFOE is incorporated with the Administration for Market Regulation and receives its Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), it must complete tax registration at the competent local STA office within 30 calendar days. The registration packet must include the business license, articles of association, legal representative identification, registered address proof, financial accounting system description, banking account information, and completed tax registration forms. The USCC serves as the entity's taxpayer identification number for enterprise income tax, VAT, and customs declaration purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box, pass-through, or hybrid classification election is available to a PRC WFOE; resident enterprises are taxed at entity level on worldwide income. A VAT general taxpayer election is available after annual taxable sales reach RMB 5,000,000 or voluntarily for businesses meeting accounting requirements. Hybrid mismatch and treaty override outcomes are governed by the STA's general anti-avoidance rules implementing BEPS Action 2.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales for mandatory registration as a general VAT taxpayer; all enterprises with taxable activities, irrespective of turnover, must complete tax registration and obtain a VAT taxpayer identification number.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports by a VAT general taxpayer are zero-rated for VAT when the sale is a genuine cross-border transaction, title and risk pass to an overseas buyer, the goods physically leave PRC customs territory, and the export declaration is duly logged in the customs system. Electric cargo e-bikes classified under ISIC 3091 qualify for the 0% export rate and corresponding export drawback, subject to the statutory documentation and foreign-exchange repatriation requirements.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口货物报关单) with customs endorsement
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (提运单) evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank foreign-exchange collection receipt (银行收汇凭证)
  • Export VAT invoice (出口发票)
  • Foreign trade contract and signed sales order
  • Packing list and cargo weight manifest
  • Certificate of Origin when preferential customs treatment is claimed

The PRC operates the Golden Tax System (金税工程) with mandatory near-real-time VAT invoice data transmission to the STA. Comprehensive digital e-invoicing (全电发票) is being rolled out nationally, and all VAT general taxpayers must issue invoices through STA-approved digital invoice platforms with live API/network integration. There is no physical fiscal stamping; each invoice is centrally verified and recorded by the STA.

False export zero-rating claims trigger retrospective clawback of all VAT refunded, plus late-payment surcharges and fines from 50% to 300% of the amount fraudulently refunded under the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection. Persistent or serious violations may result in suspension of export-rebate qualification, customs penalties, referral to public security for tax evasion or smuggling crime, and inclusion in the STA's serious-tax-credit-violation blacklist with cross-border information exchange.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Chinese WFOE operates under partial capital account convertibility with SAFE approval required for most capital transactions and profit remittances to the foreign parent in Taiwan. Current account items such as trade settlements are generally convertible subject to repatriation and reporting obligations. Profit remittance to Taiwan is subject to SAFE review, potential withholding taxes, and documentation of legitimate business expenses, with typical approval timelines of 30-60 days depending on transaction size and compliance history.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export receipts must be surrendered to the designated foreign exchange bank within five working days of receipt. Mandatory conversion to RMB at the prevailing exchange rate is required with 100% surrender of export proceeds. Failure to comply can result in penalties and restrictions on future export activities. For cross-border trade with Taiwan, additional reporting to both SAFE and Taiwanese authorities is mandatory.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China for the Chinese WFOE entity; Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) for the Taiwanese parent jurisdiction

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium to High. Offshore digital EMIs are permissible but subject to stringent Chinese AML/KYC standards, SAFE cross-border transfer reporting, and potential restrictions on digital payment platforms. Legal constraints include mandatory registration of cross-border payment information and prohibition on using unlicensed fintech for FX transactions.
