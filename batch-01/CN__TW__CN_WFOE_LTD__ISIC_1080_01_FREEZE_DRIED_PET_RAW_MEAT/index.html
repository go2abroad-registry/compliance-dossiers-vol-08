Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None under current PRC Company Law; the WFOE Articles of Association may stipulate any reasonable registered capital in CNY. However, the capital contribution schedule must be consistent with the subscribed capital stated in the Articles of Association; capital may be paid in within five years of establishment unless otherwise approved. For ISIC 1080 freeze-dried pet meat manufacturing, operational capacity and food production licensing will require demonstrated paid-in or committed capital sufficient for industrial premises, vacuum freeze-drying equipment, and environmental compliance. Funds for initial capital contribution must be remitted from the overseas investor through the designated foreign exchange account and reported to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) unless an exemption applies. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The New Taiwan Dollar (TWD) is not a fully free-convertible global reserve currency; it operates through the domestic interbank foreign exchange market administrated by the CBC under a managed floating exchange rate regime. Taiwan applies a dual-account framework stated in the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act. Current-account transactions, including export-import trade settlements, service fees, interest, and dividend remittances, are generally eligible for direct handling by authorized banks without need for central bank pre-approval, provided that correct supporting documents and CBC foreign exchange transaction declarations are submitted. Capital-account transactions, especially inbound foreign direct investment into a Taiwanese Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE), require prior approval from the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, followed by a CBC foreign exchange declaration and settlement at a designated bank. There is no general surrender mandate for export earnings: an exporter may legally retain foreign currency proceeds in a Foreign Currency Deposit Account at an authorized bank or may sell the foreign currency to a bank at the prevailing market rate. Profit remittance by a WFOE to its overseas shareholders is permitted only from verified after-tax retained earnings, evidenced by audited financial statements, tax settlement, and an application via an authorized bank after withholding tax payment. Because the upstream ownership in this case may be routed through a mainland China parent, the Investment Commission and the CBC will apply additional cross-strait capital review, though ordinary trade-related current account flows remain liquid for financing genuine production and pet food export requirements.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBC administered regulations, proceeds from exports of goods must be repatriated to Taiwan within 180 days from the date of customs export declaration; service export proceeds must be collected within 120 days unless extended approval is obtained. All inward export proceeds must be reported through an authorized bank with the proper foreign exchange transaction declaration. There is no mandatory conversion ratio into TWD; exporters may retain the foreign currency proceeds in a Foreign Currency Deposit Account or may sell the proceeds to the bank at market rate. Failure to meet the repatriation timeline or to file complete declarations may result in CBC fines, suspension of simplified foreign exchange procedures, and adverse customs/corporate banking restrictions.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

LOW. Offshore fintech EMIs are not legally authorized to open corporate accounts for a Taiwan-registered WFOE without first acquiring a banking branch or trading licence from the Financial Supervisory Commission under the Banking Act. The FinTech Innovative Experimentation Act only permits time-limited sandbox trials, not permanent EMI servicing. Taiwan-licensed digital banks such as LINE Bank and Rakuten International Commercial Bank are predominantly retail-oriented and generally do not offer full corporate banking, foreign exchange, escrow, and documentary trade services for a foreign-invested manufacturing entity. Accordingly, the treasury blueprint must reject fintech EMI reliance and use a Tier-1 domestic bank as the settlement and compliance anchor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The PRC parent must first secure Taiwan Investment Commission approval and obtain the relevant foreign exchange registration certificate. Funds should be remitted by SWIFT MT103 from a mainland China bank directly to the WFOE's designated Taiwanese Tier-1 bank, with the CBC inward remittance purpose code representing foreign direct investment and the payment narrative matching the approved investment amount. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A transfer pricing Local File and Master File are mandatory when the aggregate related-party transaction amount during the fiscal year exceeds NT$200 million; if exceeding NT$30 million but below NT$200 million, only a structured disclosure in the tax return is required.

Economic substance scope: The tax authority applies Economic Substance Regulations in substance-over-form assessment. For the WFOE manufacturing freeze-dried pet raw meat, the Core Income-Generating Activities must be locally evidenced by (i) physical freeze-drying plant operations in Taiwan, (ii) local management decisions regarding procurement and pricing, (iii) Taiwan bank accounts for invoicing and collection, and (iv) local employees executing payroll, QC and compliance. Absence of any one of these activities can trigger CFC earnings inclusion or a finding that the Taiwan WFOE is a sham shell, piercing the shareholder's limited liability.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing / Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Under the Income Tax Act art. 110, the tax deficit is assessed and the taxpayer is penalized an amount not exceeding 3 times the underpaid tax. If found to be criminal concealment, the Tax Collection Act art. 41 authorizes a fine of TWD 300,000 to TWD 3,000,000 and imprisonment of 1 to 5 years. The TP report is also void, with ROC customs fines for mispriced imports.

Director personal liability: Directors will face (1) joint and civil liability for the unwarranted tax refund or underpayment, (2) a travel ban via the National Taxation Bureau's blacklisting, (3) seizure of personal assets to satisfy the assessed amount, and (4) criminal liability for fraud if the court finds they knowingly ordered or accepted the falsified return.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: The Taiwan tax authority will recharacterize the WFOE as an agent of the mainland parent and assess a 20% profit tax on deemed Taiwan-source revenue, plus a penalty up to 3 times the additional tax (Income Tax Act art. 110). Under Taiwan's anti-abuse rules, the entity's tax-payment identity may be dissolved without legal standing to invoke the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil is applied under the Company Act art. 154; the director is personally liable for the tax arrears if the WFOE's capital is found to be fictitious or if the company was used to defraud creditors. Article 9 of the Company Act imposes criminal imprisonment of up to 2 years for a director causing the company to issue dishonest records; assets outside Taiwan may also be frozen through judicial cross-border comity.
Dividend Repatriation / Withholding Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The payor is assessed 20% (or 21% if the reclassified dividend is paid to the non-resident corporate shareholder) on the disguised distribution; in addition, a penalty equal to 1 to 3 times the tax withheld may be imposed under Tax Collection Act art. 51. Failing to file the Quarterly Withholding Tax Statement is a separate violation with a fine of TWD 4,500 to TWD 10,000 per month until correction.

Director personal liability: The managing director is a person in charge of the de facto tax liability; the court can pierce the corporate veil and order restoration of remitted profits plus punitive civil compensation. The director concurrently receives a travel ban, a blacklisting declaration in the tax bureau's enforcement list, and imprisonment of 1 to 5 years under Tax Collection Act art. 41 for the intentional commission of withholding evasion. If the payment is made through platform pay with no invoice, the AML regime reinforces forensic tracing and asset confiscation.
