Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Taiwan Ministry of Finance, administered through the competent National Taxation Bureau; tax registration and electronic tax services are available through the Ministry of Finance eTax Portal and the National Taxation Bureau e-invoice platforms.

E-government filing portal

A Taiwan subsidiary must first complete company incorporation or branch registration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Department of Commerce, and obtain foreign-investment approval where applicable. The entity then files business tax registration with the competent National Taxation Bureau before commencing business. The authority issues an eight-digit Unified Business Number, which functions as the principal tax identification number and business tax identifier. Required materials generally include the incorporation or branch registration certificate, articles of incorporation, registered office evidence, responsible-person identification, foreign-investment approval documents, lease or premises documentation, business scope information, bank information where requested, and an authorized-agent power of attorney. A foreign company without a Taiwan establishment generally does not obtain a Taiwan business tax number solely because it has non-resident sales; registration may arise if it creates a Taiwan fixed place of business, appoints a qualifying local agent, or falls within a specific cross-border digital-services regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Taiwan has no United States-style check-the-box election and generally does not permit an incorporated company to elect transparent treatment for Taiwan tax purposes. A locally incorporated company is taxed as a separate legal entity. A foreign company may operate through a branch, which is taxed on Taiwan-source income attributable to the branch, or through a subsidiary, which is separately taxed on its income. Taiwan has no broad statutory hybrid-mismatch election that converts a limited liability company into a pass-through entity. Group taxation and consolidated-return treatment are generally unavailable except for narrowly defined statutory arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold applies to business tax registration. A business must register before commencing taxable business activities in Taiwan. The NT$200,000 monthly-sales benchmark is generally relevant to classification under the small-scale business tax regime and is not a general exemption from registration. Exported goods and qualifying exported services are generally zero-rated rather than exempt.

Taiwan uniform invoices may be issued electronically through the Ministry of Finance e-invoice platform or an approved private service provider. Businesses must use approved invoice formats, maintain invoice and transaction records, and transmit required invoice data in accordance with applicable e-invoice rules. Taiwan generally does not operate a universal real-time pre-clearance model for every business invoice comparable to certain clearance jurisdictions; digital transmission, invoice validation, buyer notification, and platform reporting obligations apply according to taxpayer type and transaction category. Business-to-business transactions ordinarily require compliant uniform invoices and purchaser tax identification information. Point-of-sale systems and electronic records must be capable of audit production and prescribed data retention.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China achieves full current-account convertibility, with trade-related FX settled freely upon documentary verification; capital-account transactions remain heavily controlled, including foreign direct investment (FDI) equity injections, shareholder loans, and profit repatriations. A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) may remit distributable profits to its Taiwan parent only after completing annual audit, tax clearance, and SAFE registration, and must evidence the remittance with a board resolution, audit report, tax settlement certificate, and the FIEs capital-account information. The renminbi (CNY) is convertible for trade on the domestic FX market, but cross-border capital flows are subject to SAFE aggregate-quota and post-verification monitoring. Profit remittance is permitted via the bank after the annual independent audit is filed with the Foreign Exchange Bureau; funds may be converted to original investment currency or retained as RMB subject to the 'Foreign-Invested Enterprise' (FIE) operating status.

Export proceeds surrender rules

SAFE abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds as of 2012; exporters under trade foreign-exchange category A (the default for WFOEs in low-risk designer toy manufacturing) may retain 100% of export receipts in a current-account FX or RMB settlement account and voluntarily convert when needed. However, trade verification requires that physical export receipts be matched to customs declarations via the 'Trade in Goods' Electronic Port System within 90 days of customs clearance; deferred receipt beyond 90 days must be declared to SAFE. No mandatory conversion ratio applies, but receipts must be directed back to China within 12 months for normal open-account terms unless a legitimate exception (e.g., pre-payment or consignment) is registered. For incomplete documentation or high-risk trades, SAFE can require full or partial surrender to the designated bank pending investigation.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low for primary treasury operation due to direct digital EMI licensing not available onshore; offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Airwallex, Currenxie, WorldFirst) may partner with Chinese licensed banks to act as collection/virtual account services for e-commerce export receipts, but cannot replace a domestic WFOE bank account. Such rails are feasible only as supplementary settlement channels for designer toy sales on Shopify, Stripe, and local Chinese POS, subject to the bank’s notification and SAFE registration for the cross-border e-commerce trade category (Paying-for-Closing on E-commerce). Strict restrictions exist on foreign EMI holding WFOE funds or providing lending/hedging directly in CNY; all FX conversion must occur through a domestic licensed bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Structured corporate chart showing fully transparent ownership (individual or Taiwan company) up to natural persons, notarized certificate of incumbency, audited parent financial statements, and a written explanation of lawful source of funds for the FDI capital injection. Include registered UBO lists, passport copies, and Taiwan MOEA corporate registration extracts.

Capital account funding from Taiwan parent with recurring monthly injections structured as 'shareholder loans' instead of formal equity investment

Root cause: Capital account funding from Taiwan parent with recurring monthly injections structured as 'shareholder loans' instead of formal equity investment

Defense evidence: FDI registration certificate issued by SAFE, capital injection plan approved by the provincial branch, capital verification report by a licensed Chinese CPA (including proof of foreign currency conversion), original board resolution, and written shareholder loan agreement with arm’s-length interest and repayment schedule registered on the Foreign Exchange Business System. Present a clear dividend distribution and capital-structure policy to avoid misuse.
IP royalty or brand licensing payments to Taiwan-parent for designer blind box character rights, creating circular or layered transaction flows

Root cause: IP royalty or brand licensing payments to Taiwan-parent for designer blind box character rights, creating circular or layered transaction flows

Defense evidence: IP license agreement filed with the State Administration for Market Regulation, tax withholding certificates on royalty payments, strong transfer-pricing documentation using a Comparable Uncontrolled Price (CUP) analysis or cost-plus method, and evidence of beneficial ownership (e.g., trademark/design registrations in the Taiwan-parent’s name). Include independent IP valuation and business rationale write-up showing the arm's-length royalty rate.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Taiwan parent injects registered share capital via an external FX wire to the WFOE's designated Capital FX Account at the onshore bank. The remitting bank must use purpose code '622011' (Direct Investment – Paid-up Capital) or equivalent China-specific code '2202' (Foreign Exchange Settlement of FDI). The Taiwan remitter must provide a clear narrative referencing the SAFE FDI registration number and the 'FDI Statistics Platform Business Registration Form' issued by the onshore bank. After the funds arrive, the WFOE’s bank verifies the CFA 'Capital Account Magnet' system record, completes foreign currency settlement into CNY via a 'Capital Account – RMB Settlement Deposit Account', and requires a CPA capital verification within 15 business days for filing with SAFE. For loans from Taiwan parent, the routing must use a separate 'Shareholder Loan' heading with a filled-in FX liability registration and the debt-to-equity ratio ceiling (typically 1:1 for non-financial FIEs); repayment must follow a pre-registered schedule and pass the bank's offshore-related transaction review.Regular trade settlements for raw material imports (plastic granules, paints, moving parts) use Swift MT103 over the onshore bank's cross-border fast payment rail, with the required trade background documents (invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading/AWB, and customs declaration) submitted to the bank within 30 days before or after remittance. For high-value molding-material purchases exceeding USD 200,000 per transaction, an irrevocable Documentary Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C) is the preferred rail, with the WFOE as applicant instructing the bank to issue through a Taiwan or Asian beneficiary bank. Additional clearing rails include China's Cross-Border Interbank Payment System (CIPS) for RMB-denominated trade with Taiwan counterparties under the Cross-Strait Currency Clearing Framework, and the SWIFT 'Trade+With electronic bank guarantee' service for order-based supplier financing. Account disbursements to China-based suppliers and logistics partners are executed via domestic RMB real-time gross settlement (HVPS), preserving a clean audit trail for SAFE's trade verification system.SAFE-approved Bank FX Forward (Deliverable) – matching a documented current-account trade exposure (e.g., USD collections from designer toy overseas sales against CNY operating costs) with a maximum tenor of one year and real trade background at deal settlement.Cross-Currency Swap (CNY/USD) – used for medium-term conversion of an FDI capital deposit into working-capital liquidity or to hedge a shareholder loan repayable in original currency, but only after bank-level counterparty credit approval and SAFE notification.FX Option (CallorPut on USD/CNY) – purchased through a licensed onshore bank to hedge price volatility on confirmed export orders without delivery obligation, with the underlying transaction identified in the customer verification process and subject to post-trade monitoring by the bank's hedging desk.Interest Rate Swap paired with FX Swap – for synchronising the FX and interest-rate exposure of the capital injection and trade loans, permitted only for real economic exposure documentation.NDF (Non-Deliverable Forward) – not permissible onshore for this WFOE; onshore entities are prohibited from entering into CNY NDFs when they have legitimate trade deliverable exposure. Offshore NDF activity in Taiwan/Hong Kong is only allowed for hedging non-deliverable CNH exposure and requires separate approval from the enterprise’s board and group treasury; CNH deliverable forwards are handled at the HK-DMA or Taiwan RMB clearing bank.
