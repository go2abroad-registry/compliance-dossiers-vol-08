Available election forms

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Registration is mandatory for all sellers of taxable goods/services with no de minimis VAT registration threshold. General taxpayer registration becomes mandatory once annual sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; below this threshold, small-scale status may be retained (generally 3% collection rate, currently reduced to 1% through 31 December 2027), and small-scale taxpayers benefit from a monthly sales exemption of RMB 100,000 (quarterly RMB 300,000). An exporting manufacturer such as the WFOE should elect general taxpayer status to utilize input-VAT credits and export refunds.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The manufacturing WFOE may claim the 0% export VAT rate and refund through the 'exempt, offset, refund' (免抵退) method if: (1) it is a registered VAT general taxpayer; (2) the surgical suture needles are manufactured by the WFOE, physically exported, declared to GACC, and leave the PRC customs territory; (3) export proceeds are received in foreign currency and repatriated within the statutory period (generally 180 days from export declaration); (4) genuine input VAT special invoices are held for purchased materials; (5) the export tax refund filing is on record and monthly export refund reports are filed on the Electronic Tax Bureau; and (6) the goods are not on a prohibition/refund-exclusion list. The refund is capped by the input VAT legally attributable to the exported goods that remains after offset against domestic output VAT.

Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices (数电票) are mandated for all newly established enterprises under Golden Tax Phase IV, issued through the national Unified Invoice Service Platform on the Electronic Tax Bureau. Each e-invoice carries a unique 20-digit invoice number and a cryptographic digital authentication (税务数字证书), is uploaded to the STA database in real time at issuance, undergoes centralized invoice/rationality clearance matching (发票稽核比对) against customs, VAT offset, and bank data, and does not require a physical stamp. Issuance is available via the government portal, mobile app, or enterprise ERP/API interfaces; legacy paper special VAT invoices apply only to pre-existing conversion arrangements.

Export refund claims are audited under the STA–GACC–SAFE joint data platform and 'anti-fraud' campaigns. Failure to collect export proceeds within 180 days of export declaration, absent one of the nine statutory exceptional circumstances, recharacterizes the export as a deemed domestic sale (视同内销) requiring supplementary output VAT, forfeiture of the refund, daily late-payment surcharges at 0.05%, and penalties under the Tax Administration Law. Document forgery or fraudulent over-claiming exposes the WFOE to an administrative clawback of the refunded tax and a penalty of 50%–500% of the tax defrauded; criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law (骗取出口退税罪) attaches where the fraud exceeds RMB 50,000, with imprisonment escalating from up to 5 years to life imprisonment for aggravated amounts above RMB 2,500,000, alongside fines of 1–5 times the defrauded tax and asset forfeiture.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

RMB is convertible for current-account transactions such as cross-border trade in goods, service fees and ordinary profit remittances, subject to genuine transaction documentation and bank authenticity review. Capital-account transactions remain regulated: inbound FDI equity injection requires an FDI foreign-exchange registration with SAFE, disbursement into a designated capital settlement account, and post-settlement declaration of use. Dividend/profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is treated as investment income under the current account only after the WFOE has completed its statutory audit, paid or settled corporate income tax, and allocated the statutory surplus reserve at 10 percent of after-tax profits until the reserve reaches 50 percent of registered capital. The remitting bank requires the audited financial statements, CIT payment vouchers, board resolution and the relevant SAFE report; the RMB amount is converted into USD and remitted via SWIFT MT103 under BOP purpose code 322011. Liquidation proceeds, shareholder loans and outward guarantees require separate capital-account approvals or registrations and are not freely remittable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's goods-trade foreign-exchange administration regime (SAFE Order [2012] No. 1 as amended), export proceeds must be received into the WFOE's domestic current-account foreign-currency account and reconciled with Customs export declarations. There is no mandatory conversion ratio or forced surrender requirement for ordinary manufactured exports; the WFOE may retain USD export earnings or convert them to RMB at the onshore interbank rate. The SAFE monitoring system applies a deferred-collection reporting threshold of 180 days after Customs export declaration: if the underlying payment terms exceed 180 days, the bank must record the transaction in the goods-trade monitoring system and obtain the required deferred-receipt registration before settlement. For exports to Taiwan, the receiving bank will match the incoming SWIFT credit against the Customs declaration ID, export invoice, packing list and transport documents.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), is the monetary and foreign-exchange authority for the mainland-incorporated WFOE. The Taiwan Financial Supervisory Commission and Central Bank of Taiwan do not regulate the onshore banking or FX position of this CN-WFOE.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for substantive onshore statutory flows. Offshore digital EMI/e-money platforms cannot open a compliant RMB current account, receive export proceeds, or hold the WFOE's FDI capital settlement account. Such platforms may provide offshore collection convenience for overseas distributors, but funds must still be repatriated through a SAFE-licensed onshore bank to satisfy goods-trade FX verification. Routing statutory export proceeds through an offshore EMI without a domestic underlying bank creates false-trade red flags, AML/SAR exposure and SAFE enforcement risk. The WFOE should use licensed onshore banks as the only clearing channel for trade, capital and treasury flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injection from the Taiwan shareholder must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the direct investor's own corporate bank account to the WFOE's designated FDI capital settlement account at a SAFE-licensed onshore bank. The remitter name must match the entity recorded in the SAFE FDI registration; third-party payments are not permitted. The wire should state 'Equity injection into registered capital of CN_WFOE_LTD' and use BOP transaction code 621011 for new foreign direct investment. TWD is not a freely deliverable onshore clearing currency; the Taiwan shareholder should remit USD or CNH/RMB via CIPS or a corresponding Taiwan RMB clearing bank. After crediting, the onshore bank verifies the FDI registration certificate and may convert the foreign currency into RMB for permitted operating purposes. Each settlement must be supported by a capital-proceeds payment-use declaration, and the WFOE may not use the funds for prohibited uses such as securities investment, entrusted lending or real estate beyond scope.For export of surgical suture needles and assemblies from mainland China to Taiwan distributors or end customers, use SWIFT MT103 TT settlement with BOP purpose code 121010 for general-trade export collection. The bank must receive the Customs declaration number, commercial invoice, packing list and transport documents, and the recipient name must match the trade counterparty. For import of PGA resin, surgical needles, packaging materials or capital equipment, the WFOE may open a commercial letter of credit via SWIFT MT700/MT707, requiring documents such as commercial invoice, bill of lading, packing list, origin certificate, insurance certificate and quality certificate. For cross-border RMB trade with Taiwan, the WFOE may clear via CIPS through an onshore designated bank if the Taiwan counterparty participates through a RMB clearing bank. Such platforms may provide offshore collection convenience for overseas distributors, but funds must still be repatriated through a SAFE-licensed onshore bank to satisfy goods-trade FX verification. Routing statutory export proceeds through an offshore EMI without a domestic underlying bank creates false-trade red flags, AML/SAR exposure and SAFE enforcement risk. The WFOE should use licensed onshore banks as the only clearing channel for trade, capital and treasury flows.
