Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No minimum paid-up capital is imposed by Taiwan Company Act for a limited liability company. However, for the ISIC 5221 cross-border TIR trucking licence, the Ministry of Transportation and Communications requires paid-in capital of at least NT$5,000,000, fully subscribed and paid in cash at par, verified by a certified public accountant. The capital is deposited in the company's Taiwan bank account and is not escrow-blocked; it may be deployed only after registration and the transport licence are obtained, and it cannot be withdrawn below the regulator's solvency requirement.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense fire-safety approval is required for the registered office, terminal garage, and any warehouse used for bonded cargo staging. The licensed fire authority must issue a Fire Safety Certification after inspection of extinguishers, hydrants, emergency lighting, signage, and evacuation routes. If vehicles are serviced on site, an Occupational Safety clearance under Taiwan Occupational Safety and Health Act is required for pits, lifts, ventilation, and hazardous material storage. Structural load inspection is required for any mezzanine or racking above 2 meters.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China, administered locally by the provincial and municipal competent tax bureaux; online electronic portal: STA Electronic Tax Hall (e-Tax) plus provincial e-Tax modules; official gateway: chinatax.gov.cn.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) under the integrated market-entity registration procedure, including the Foreign Investment Information Report for the foreign shareholder. Step 2: Through the multi-certificate one-stop integration, the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) printed on the Business License is automatically registered with STA and becomes the taxpayer identification number (TIN). Step 3: Complete supplementary tax registration in the Electronic Tax Hall within 30 days of issuance: select VAT taxpayer status (general or small-scale), file legal representative real-name authentication, designate the competent tax bureau, register bank account and report expected taxable scope. Step 4: Activate the e-identification digital certificate (CA) for electronic declarations and e-invoice issuance. Step 5: For a foreign non-resident enterprise that has no permanent establishment or WFOE status, the person paying the China-source income must perform withholding registration; if the foreign investor nonetheless needs a direct Chinese TIN, apply to the competent tax bureau where the income arises for non-resident registration and treaty-based relief. The WFOE itself is a Chinese resident taxpayer and does not obtain a separate non-resident TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the WFOE limited liability company is a Chinese resident enterprise taxed on worldwide taxable income under the Enterprise Income Tax Law; no pass-through or transparent classification is permitted.

Available election forms

None for entity classification: no check-the-box elections and no hybrid mismatch election under Chinese tax law. The only meaningful election is VAT taxpayer status: an enterprise with annual taxable turnover below RMB 5,000,000 may elect general VAT taxpayer status to recover input VAT, while mandatory general registration applies above that threshold. Export zero-rating election is available if the statutory export evidence package is complete.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable turnover. Any WFOE making taxable supplies must complete VAT/tax registration upon establishment; below RMB 5,000,000 small-scale taxpayer status may apply, and above RMB 5,000,000 registration as a general VAT taxpayer is mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export of goods is zero-rated only if the goods are declared and physically leave the customs territory of mainland China, the WFOE is a general VAT taxpayer, a legitimate export sales contract exists, and the export proceeds are repatriated through banks. For international road freight transport under TIR Carnets, the cross-border transport service provided to a non-resident customer and commencing or ending outside mainland China is zero-rated as an international transport service; however, any domestic portion must be segregated or treated as part of the zero-rated international carriage only under a single cross-border contract supported by TIR and CMR documentation. The claim must be supported by customs exit records, TIR Carnet data and foreign-exchange collection evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration (China Export Goods Declaration Form; export counterpart of a Bill of Entry) with TIR Carnet customs endorsement
  • TIR Carnet and matching TIR-EPD (TIR Electronic PreDeclaration) acceptance record
  • CMR international road consignment note or equivalent Bill of Lading/waybill evidencing cross-border movement
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Export freight transport contract or invoice with the zero-rated VAT entry
  • Bank-issued Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or SAFE trade foreign-exchange collection and settlement verification record
  • Export VAT zero-rating declaration submitted through the STA Electronic Tax Hall

China operates a fiscally controlled e-invoicing environment administered by STA. General VAT taxpayers must issue Special Value-Added Tax Invoices or fully digitalized electronic invoices (FDEI) through the STA electronic invoice service platform or approved tax-control software. Invoice data must be transmitted to the STA's Golden Tax System III in real time or near-real time. Each invoice carries a unique tax-control serial number and anti-fraud verification code, commonly described as 'one invoice, one code', and can be verified on the national VAT invoice verification portal. For export zero-rating, the zero-rated e-invoice must cross-reference the customs export declaration number and the foreign exchange collection record.

The STA and China Customs jointly audit export zero-rating claims. If the export declaration, TIR/CMR route evidence, commercial invoice, or foreign-exchange repatriation record is false or incomplete, the zero-rating is reversed and the services/goods are recharacterized as domestic taxable supplies. The taxpayer must then pay unremitted VAT at the applicable rate (for freight transport, 9%) plus any unremitted city maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day, and administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the underpaid tax if tax evasion is found. Any VAT refund already paid is clawed back with interest. Fraudulent export-refund claims can be prosecuted under the PRC Criminal Law for export tax-refund fraud; the statutory recovery period is unlimited for fraud, and shifts to the general three-year (or five-year if the amount exceeds RMB 100,000) limitation for non-fraud administrative recovery.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains current account convertibility under the PBOC-SAFE framework, while capital account transactions remain subject to approval and registration controls. CNY is freely convertible for trade and service payments upon genuine transaction documentation, but not for full capital-account fungibility. A CN WFOE must open current-account foreign exchange accounts at designated banks, and every inbound equity remittance is treated as FDI and tracked in SAFE's capital account information system. Profit repatriation by the Taiwan shareholder is permitted after the annual audit and tax clearance, but requires the bank to verify the distribution resolution, audited financial statements and tax withholding certificates; flexibility of currency conversion is at market rate but subject to anti-avoidance review. Surplus capital and loan principal repayments require SAFE registration and foreign debt quota limits. Overall, the regime is a managed float with daily reference rate fixing; the WFOE faces high compliance overhead for cross-border fund flows and must maintain a fully documented treasury file for each remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's Measures for Foreign Exchange Administration of Trade in Goods, export receipts from TIR trucking services and ancillary cargo charges are not subject to a fixed mandatory surrender ratio. A Chinese WFOE may retain foreign currency in its current-account FX account or sell on demand to designated banks. The statutory collection period is generally within 90 days after customs export declaration, with a permitted extension to 180 days for legitimate commercial delays. There is no compulsory conversion ratio to CNY; however, the receiving bank must perform genuine-transaction verification and report receipt amounts into SAFE's goods trade monitoring system. Receipts that remain outstanding beyond the prescribed timeframe without adjustment declaration will be flagged as overdue verification and may trigger regulatory review.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Limited and conditional: a pure offshore EMI is not authorized by PBOC or SAFE to open local RMB or foreign exchange settlement accounts for a Chinese WFOE, and therefore cannot serve as the primary banking rail. A PBOC-licensed non-bank cross-border payment institution may be engaged only as an ancillary front-end for small ticket receipts or low-value disbursements, and all such flows must clear through a licensed domestic commercial bank for SAFE reporting, trade verification and capital account control. The WFOE should not outsource statutory FX compliance, TIR trade documentation or FDI capital supervision to any offshore EMI.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

20% for dividends paid to a non-resident corporate shareholder; 21% for dividends paid to a non-resident individual. The Taiwanese resident company also suffers a 5% retained earnings surtax on undistributed net profits if earnings are accumulated without distribution.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual settlement return must be filed between 1 May and 31 May of the year following the fiscal year; the provisional return is due from 1 September to 30 September of the current year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: NT$100 million in aggregate related-party transactions (approximately USD 3.3 million) triggers mandatory Local File preparation; a Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue above NT$20 billion, or where the Taiwanese entity's related-party transactions exceed NT$100 million in high-risk categories. Even below the threshold, a contemporaneous transfer pricing policy for TIR trucking revenue sharing is best practice.

Economic substance scope: Although Taiwan does not operate offshore ESR, the substance-over-form doctrine under Income Tax Act Article 12-1 and Article 43-1, together with the Cross-Strait Tax Agreement's treaty-beneficiary testing, require the Taiwan WFOE to demonstrate real board management in Taiwan, active contracts, local employees, truck ownership or qualifying leases, TIR carnet management, and actual Core Income-Generating Activities (CIGA) in Taiwan. The tax authority may ignore a shell WFOE and reattribute profits to the mainland parent; treaty relief is denied if the beneficial owner is merely a pass-through.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The tax authority invokes Income Tax Act Article 12-1 (substance-over-form) and Article 43-1 (reallocation), disallowing expense deductions and assessing CIT at 20% on the reallocated net profit. An evasion penalty of 2 to 3 times the underpaid tax may be imposed under Article 110, and interest is added at the prescribed rate until payment.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the company's unpaid tax if the company is adjudged an artificial arrangement; civil actions permit asset seizure and restitution. Under Tax Collection Act Article 24, controlling directors can be blacklisted, given travel bans, and have personal bank accounts and properties attached for tax debts exceeding NT$1 million.
Transfer Pricing Evasion and False Invoicing

Statutory sanction: Under Income Tax Act Article 43-1, the tax authority reallocates profit and adds a penalty of 2 to 3 times the underpaid tax. Business Tax Act Article 51 also imposes a penalty of up to 5 times the sales tax evaded for false e-invoices, and the Ministry of Finance may suspend invoicing privileges or prohibit operations.

Director personal liability: Directors face criminal charges under Tax Collection Act Article 41 (tax evasion by fraud): imprisonment of up to 5 years and a fine of up to NT$6 million. The corporate veil is pierced under Company Act Article 154 if the directors acted with bad faith, making them jointly and severally liable for all tax debts, compensation, and legal costs; a court may appoint a receiver to enforce against personal assets.
Withholding Tax Evasion and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Income Tax Act Article 114, the company as withholding agent is liable for the full underwithheld tax plus a penalty equal to 20% of the underwithheld amount, or 40% for intentional failure; interest accrues from the date the tax should have been paid. Treaty abuse triggers reassessment at the statutory 20% rate, and the fraudulent TRC may be reported to tax authorities for criminal sanction.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the withholding declaration or allowed payment without withholding are personally liable as withholding agents. Criminal liability under Tax Collection Act Article 42 can lead to imprisonment of up to 3 years for intentional withholding violations. Directors may also be disqualified as directors, banned from leaving Taiwan if the outstanding tax debt exceeds NT$1 million, and held personally liable for the corporation's tax debts where a court finds the distribution and fake documentation were a sham.
