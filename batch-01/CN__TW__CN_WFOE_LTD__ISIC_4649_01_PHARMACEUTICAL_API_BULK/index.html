Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

National Taxation Administration of Taiwan (Ministry of Finance) — official tax e-filing portal 'Tax e-Filing System' (etax.nat.gov.tw); company registration administered via the MOEA One-Stop Online Service (onestop.nat.gov.tw).

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor establishing a WFOE in Taiwan must first apply to the Ministry of Economic Affairs for company incorporation. Upon approval, the company must apply for registration of profit-seeking enterprise tax (營利事業所得稅) and business tax (營業稅) at the local National Taxation Administration branch. The tax authority issues a Unified Business Number (統一編號) which serves as the VAT registration number and income tax identification number. Application requires submission of: (1) incorporation certificate, (2) board/shareholder resolution on legal representative, (3) tax registration application form (營業人設立登記申請書), (4) lease or ownership certificate of business premises, (5) identification documents of the legal representative and shareholders, (6) bank account details. Foreign-invested WFOEs must also complete foreign exchange filings through a designated bank. The tax authority will complete registration within a statutory period after verifying the documents; a tax ID card is issued. Registration must be completed before commencing business operations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Taiwan WFOE is a domestic taxable entity subject to corporate income tax on worldwide income at 20%, plus a 5% retained earnings surtax at distribution; it is not eligible for pass-through treatment.

Available election forms

Taiwan does not provide a check-the-box regime equivalent to the U.S. tax classification system. However, the WFOE can elect between actual costing and deemed costing for transfer pricing purposes, and may elect to claim export zero-rating by filing relevant documentation. Special status elections are available for foreign corporate shareholders under the Statute for Industrial Innovation and the Free Trade Zone Act, but these are incentive-based deductions, not classification elections. No CFC or hybrid-mismatch election is available; Taiwan applies mandatory anti-deferral rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Monthly sales greater than NT$200,000; an entity making sales below this threshold may apply for simplified registration, but a WFOE with expected or actual pharmaceutical API export sales will exceed the threshold and must register as a full business tax payer.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to sales of goods that are exported from Taiwan, including pharmaceutical APIs, provided the seller is registered for business tax, the goods are actually shipped abroad, and the exporter retains documentary evidence. Services exported to foreign clients and used outside Taiwan qualify if not ancillary to domestic use. The WFOE must be named as the exporter on the customs export declaration.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) — the official customs clearance document generated from the Customs NET system
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — issued by the carrier evidencing shipment from Taiwan to the foreign buyer
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List issued by the WFOE
  • Sales contract or purchase order evidencing the foreign transaction
  • Certificate of Origin / Free Sales Certificate for pharmaceutical APIs if required by the importing country
  • Foreign exchange inward remittance certificate (export proceeds evidence)
  • Zero-Rating Export Sales List, filed with the monthly/periodic business tax return
  • GMP / pharmaceutical license evidence to substantiate the nature of the API goods when requested by Customs

The Ministry of Finance mandates e-invoicing (電子發票) through the government e-Invoice Service Platform. Businesses must adopt an approved Electronic Invoice Transmission System (Turnkey Solution) using the common e-invoice standard (Cloud Invoice), and real-time or near-real-time transmission is required for all e-invoices. For export, customs export declaration data is transmitted through the Customs Administration's 'Customs NET' system; e-invoice data for zero-rated export sales must be filed in the periodic business tax return. No separate stamping system is used, but every e-invoice carries an electronic barcode (QR code) and unique invoice number. The API-based cloud e-invoice integration is mandatory for companies with annual taxable sales above NT$2.4 million.

The tax authority performs cross-matching between customs export data, e-invoice records, and foreign exchange remittance records. In case of unsubstantiated zero-rating or non-repatriation of proceeds, the tax authority will claw back the zero-rated benefit and assess business tax at 5%. Additionally, penalties range from 5% to 15% of the understated tax amount for filing errors; intentional false declarations or fraudulent export schemes are subject to penalties up to double the amount of tax evaded, imprisonment under the Tax Evasion Act, and suspension of business. False documentation (e.g., forged bills of lading or export declarations) triggers criminal prosecution with up to 7 years' imprisonment and fines. In case of non-repatriation of export proceeds within 180 days without justifiable cause approved by the Central Bank, the revenue will be treated as taxable business revenue, the zero rate is reversed, and additional late payment interest and penalty are imposed.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% China Enterprise Income Tax (EIT Law Art. 4)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory China withholding tax on dividends distributed by a Chinese resident enterprise to a non-resident enterprise shareholder (EIT Law Arts. 3, 37, 59; CA 2008 No.1). Temporary reinvestment relief: qualified foreign investors reinvesting distributable profits directly into China enjoy a deferral of the withholding tax on the reinvested portion (MOF-SAT Ann. 2018 No.102, extended by 2023 No.28 through 2027).

Late filing: RMB 2,000 per return up to RMB 20,000 plus 0.05% daily surcharge; systemic under-prepayment triggers tax-bureau risk inspection and mandatory annual adjustment.
Form A200000 Line 13 (本年累计已缴税额) Cumulative CIT prepaid in earlier quarters of the fiscal year. System-carried figure; verify against bank payment records, particularly where cross-border FX receipts fund the WFOE's tax payment account. Late filing: RMB 2,000 per return up to RMB 20,000 plus 0.05% daily surcharge; systemic under-prepayment triggers tax-bureau risk inspection and mandatory annual adjustment.
Box: 所得项目 Income classification: '股息、红利等权益性投资收益' (dividends), '利息' (interest), or '特许权使用费' (royalties). Classify cross-strait debt interest separately from dividends because different treaty rates apply; do not combine mixed payments on one return. Failure to withhold: the WFOE is personally liable for the under-withheld tax plus a 50% to 3x penalty (Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 69) and 0.05% daily surcharge; without a filed return and tax clearance certificate, SAFE and the bank jointly block the cross-border wire.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (本地文档) triggered under SAT Ann. 2016 No.42 when annual related-party transactions with Taiwan affiliates reach: (a) tangible property transfers (API goods) > RMB 200 million; (b) intangible property transfers > RMB 100 million; (c) financial asset transfers > RMB 100 million; (d) any other related-party transactions (management fees, service fees, royalties) > RMB 40 million. Master File (主体文档) triggered when annual aggregate related-party transactions exceed RMB 1 billion. A WFOE purchasing pharmaceutical API bulk from a Taiwan parent with annual procurement ≥ RMB 200 million is automatically in Local File scope; the Taiwan parent qualifying as a Chinese-resident group entity is required to prepare a Master File where cross-strait aggregate turnover exceeds RMB 1 billion. Contemporaneous documentation must be in place by the annual filing deadline (May 31); the full file must be submitted within 20 working days of inclusion in an audit list.

Economic substance scope: China does not maintain offshore-style Economic Substance Regulations but enforces equivalent substance-over-form discipline through: (1) the beneficial-owner doctrine (SAT Ann. 2018 No.9) disqualifying conduit shareholders lacking substantive business functions; (2) the General Anti-Avoidance Rules (EIT Law Art. 47; SO 5/2009 Chapter 10) permitting recharacterization of tax-motivated structures without commercial rationale; (3) the place-of-effective-management residency rules (CA 2009 No.82) that can deem a WFOE managed from Taiwan to be a Chinese tax resident; (4) sector-specific substance expectations: the WFOE must maintain a physical office, a valid drug business license (药品经营许可证), GSP certification (药品经营质量管理规范), licensed pharmacy personnel, warehouse or cold-chain logistics as required, independent invoicing and payment functions, and full Chinese regulatory filings. Failure to maintain this substance exposes the WFOE to recharacterization as a nominee: the Taiwan parent is treated as the real taxpayer, dividend withholding at 10% is imposed on deemed distributions, and penalties under Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 63 apply.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (逃税罪, Criminal Law Art. 201)

Statutory sanction: Criminal Law Art. 201: fines of 0.5x to 5x the evaded tax; imprisonment up to 7 years where the evaded amount is enormous (unpaid tax ≥ RMB 500,000 and ≥ 10% of the payable). Administrative: 50% to 5x penalty plus 0.05% daily late interest.

Director personal liability: The legal representative, financial director, and responsible tax and customs managers are subject to criminal detention and imprisonment; courts may impose exit bans (出境限制) and freeze personal assets; Taiwan-resident directors of the parent are not shielded and may be detained upon entry into PRC territory under mutual legal assistance.
Falsely Issuing VAT Special Invoices (虚开增值税专用发票罪, Criminal Law Art. 205)

Statutory sanction: Art. 205: up to 3 years' imprisonment plus fine for amounts within RMB 2 million; 3–10 years for RMB 2–5 million; 10 years to life imprisonment with property confiscation above RMB 5 million. Administrative: cancellation of VAT general taxpayer status, blacklisting, and 50% to 5x penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or authorized the invoicing scheme are prosecuted as principal offenders; the corporate veil is pierced for restitution of evaded tax; the WFOE's pharmaceutical license is revoked and the responsible individual is permanently barred from senior roles in pharmaceutical enterprises.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Thin Capitalization (特别纳税调整)

Statutory sanction: EIT Law Arts. 41–48 and SAT Ann. 2016 No.42: arm's-length recharacterization, upward income adjustment, an interest surcharge of 5% above the central bank benchmark rate on the underpaid tax, and a 50% to 5x penalty where the non-arm's-length pricing is intentional.

Director personal liability: The financial director who signed TP documentation and the CEO who approved pricing policy bear joint and several liability when the veil is pierced under Company Law Art. 20; travel restrictions apply during special tax investigation and remain until full settlement.
Shell Company / Conduit Abuse (受益所有人否定 and GAAR Recharacterization)

Statutory sanction: SAT Ann. 2018 No.9 and EIT Law Art. 47: denial of treaty benefit, reimposition of full 10% withholding on past dividends, 5% late interest, 50% penalty on under-withholding; all payments to Taiwan counterparties are disallowed as deductions, effectively taxing the entire gross margin; the business license may be revoked for lack of genuine operations.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable as statutory withholding agents for unpaid tax (Tax Collection Administration Law Art. 69); personal exit bans are imposed; egregious cases are prosecuted as duty evasion with the PRC nominee director convicted for facilitating the cross-border scheme.
Anti-Money Laundering and Sanctions Violations (反洗钱, 反制裁法)

Statutory sanction: AML Law (2024 revision): entity fines of RMB 500,000 to RMB 5 million; responsible-person fines of RMB 50,000 to RMB 500,000; account freezes, denial of all FX remittances, revocation of the WFOE's operating license; sanctions under the PRC Anti-Foreign Sanctions Law (2021) and export control laws trigger asset freezes and criminal referral.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability for money laundering (Criminal Law Art. 191) includes imprisonment up to 10 years; compliance officers and legal representatives are personally barred from financial services; conviction entails automatic disqualification from any PRC directorship and a permanent travel ban.
