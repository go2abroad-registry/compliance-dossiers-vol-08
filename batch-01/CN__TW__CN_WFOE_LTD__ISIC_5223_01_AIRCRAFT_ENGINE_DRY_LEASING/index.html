Stage 03
Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局; SAT) via the National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau (全国统一规范电子税务局) at https://etax.chinatax.gov.cn and taxpayer hotline 12366; export-rebate clearance is administered jointly with the General Administration of Customs through the China International Trade Single Window (中国国际贸易单一窗口). Sub-national tax distinctions (urban maintenance and construction tax, education surcharges, and local levies) are administered by provincial and municipal tax bureaus under the SAT's vertical leadership.

        
E-government filing portal
A foreign investor incorporates the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The SAMR business license carries the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, USCC), which is the statutory TIN and VAT ID for all tax, customs, and foreign exchange filings. Tax registration is effected automatically through inter-agency data sharing within 30 days of incorporation; the WFOE must then complete the supplementary taxpayer information return, appoint a tax officer, and apply for invoice quotas and general VAT taxpayer qualification when applicable. Non-resident foreign enterprises without a permanent establishment in mainland China do not obtain a separate VAT ID; the domestic payer or recipient is registered as a withholding agent, and a foreign enterprise that becomes directly taxable may obtain a temporary tax registration code (临时税务登记) from the competent local tax authority.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise subject to Enterprise Income Tax at the statutory 25% rate on worldwide income; no pass-through eligibility for an LLC-type WFOE; dividends distributed to the foreign parent are generally exposed to 10% dividend withholding tax, subject to treaty relief and reinvestment deferral). — A foreign investor incorporates the WFOE with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The SAMR business license carries the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, USCC), which is the statutory TIN and VAT ID for all tax, customs, and foreign exchange filings. Tax registration is effected automatically through inter-agency data sharing within 30 days of incorporation; the WFOE must then complete the supplementary taxpayer information return, appoint a tax officer, and apply for invoice quotas and general VAT taxpayer qualification when applicable. Non-resident foreign enterprises without a permanent establishment in mainland China do not obtain a separate VAT ID; the domestic payer or recipient is registered as a withholding agent, and a foreign enterprise that becomes directly taxable may obtain a temporary tax registration code (临时税务登记) from the competent local tax authority.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation (PRC resident enterprise subject to Enterprise Income Tax at the statutory 25% rate on worldwide income; no pass-through eligibility for an LLC-type WFOE; dividends distributed to the foreign parent are generally exposed to 10% dividend withholding tax, subject to treaty relief and reinvestment deferral).

        
Available election forms
Available elections include: (a) election to register as a general VAT taxpayer before reaching the mandatory RMB 5,000,000 turnover threshold, thereby allowing input-VAT credits; (b) election between the export 'exemption and refund' method and the 'exemption, credit and refund' method for VAT rebates, in accordance with exporter type; (c) small-scale taxpayer election to apply the temporarily reduced 1% VAT levy in lieu of 3% (policy in force through 31 December 2027); and (d) election by a non-resident investor under Caishui 2018 No. 102 to defer dividend withholding on reinvested retained earnings of the WFOE. There is no check-the-box or other elective entity-classification regime under PRC tax law for limited-liability companies; hybrid mismatch outcomes are countered by the statutory General Anti-Avoidance Rule, thin-capitalisation, and controlled-foreign-enterprise provisions rather than elective mechanisms.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
13%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
Annual aggregate taxable turnover exceeding RMB 5,000,000 makes registration as a general VAT taxpayer compulsory. Below that threshold the entity may remain a small-scale taxpayer (simplified levy at 3%, temporarily reduced to 1% until 31 December 2027), and small-scale taxpayers with monthly sales up to RMB 100,000 (quarterly up to RMB 300,000) are currently VAT-exempt. Dry leasing of aircraft engines to domestic lessees is a taxable supply of tangible-movable-property leasing services taxed at 13%; cross-border leases are zero-rated and therefore do not trigger the threshold.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rating (0% VAT with entitlement to input-VAT refund or rebate) is available on two bases. (a) Physical export of goods: aircraft engines sold by the WFOE and exported through a PRC customs port, supported by a customs export declaration and transportation out of the PRC customs territory. (b) Cross-border supply of tangible-movable-property leasing services: under the cross-border taxable services list (Caishui 2016 No. 36 and Announcement 2016 No. 29), a dry lease of an aircraft engine to an offshore lessee, including a lessee in Taiwan, qualifies only if the lessee is located outside mainland China, the engine and the leasing service are wholly consumed outside the PRC customs territory, and the lease agreement and foreign-payment evidence indubitably demonstrate the offshore character. Engines remaining registered in mainland China or used on domestic routes disqualify the lease from zero-rating, leaving the rental taxable at 13% with no refund entitlement.

Full-digital electronic invoicing is mandatory nationwide under the Golden Tax System IV (金税四期). All VAT invoices must be created in the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform, each carrying a nationally unified invoice number, an embedded QR verification code, and a digital seal; invoice data are cleared in near real time into the SAT central data pool. Large taxpayers are obliged to integrate their billing systems with the platform using standard open APIs, transmitting invoice metadata (seller/buyer USCC, amount, tax rate, and goods or service classification code) for online validation before an invoice number is released. Export invoices for physically exported goods must reference the customs export declaration number; cross-border zero-rated services require a specially flagged zero-rate e-invoice. The China International Trade Single Window effectuates real-time customs, inspection, and export-tax-rebate data clearance through a single electronic submission channel.

      
Export zero-rating refunds are subject to pre-refund automated risk screening and post-refund administrative audit by the SAT export-rebate risk-control centre, with high-risk claims suspended pending verification. Fraudulent zero-rating claims (骗取出口退税) trigger mandatory retrospective clawback of the VAT refunded, late-payment surcharge, administrative fines of one to five times the tax defrauded under the Law of the PRC on the Administration of Tax Collection, downgrade of the taxpayer's credit rating to the lowest grade 'D' (entailing suspension of invoice facilitation and export-rebate priority for up to 36 months), and denial of further refund applications until all amounts are repaid. Recovery is time-barred at three years for ordinary understatements, five years in special circumstances, and is unlimited for fraudulent refunds and evasion. Criminal liability arises under Article 204 of the Criminal Law of the PRC for the crime of fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates, punishable with imprisonment up to life imprisonment and confiscation of property; responsible officers and beneficial owners may be jointly prosecuted. Reliance on forged bills of lading, false customs declarations, circular foreign-exchange arrangements, or sham offshore lessees is treated as presumptive fraud, and the burden of substantiating offshore consumption lies entirely with the WFOE.

            BoxField
            Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
          

          
            
            

              Box 01 - Gross Operating Revenue
              Gross lease rental and service fee income from dry-leasing turbofan aircraft engines to Chinese and international airlines.
              Report only Taiwan-source revenue allocable to the WFOE. Rental from engines physically located outside Taiwan must be supported by invoices and customs/contractual evidence.
              Late filing: 1% surcharge per month (or segment of a month) on the unpaid tax amount, up to a maximum of 10%.               Net operating loss deduction claimed under the 10-year carryforward rule.
              Losses must be traceable to Taiwan books and records; a non-resident parent cannot apply foreign losses to the Taiwan WFOE's tax return.
              Late filing: 1% surcharge per month (or segment of a month) on the unpaid tax amount, up to a maximum of 10%.               Box 31 - Taxable Net Income
              Statutorily adjusted income after excluding exempt income and applying all tax adjustments.
              For a non-resident entity without a PE, taxable income is deemed to be 20% of total Taiwan-source revenue if books are not recognized by the tax authority.
              Late filing: 1% surcharge per month (or segment of a month) on the unpaid tax amount, up to a maximum of 10%. Intentional failure to file permits a presumed assessment and an additional 100% penalty on the resulting underpayment.

              20% regular income tax on taxable net income, reduced by prepayments and investment tax credits.

If the return is filed after a formal demand from the tax authority, a 3% surcharge plus an additional 1% monthly penalty applies up to 10%. Intentional failure to file permits a presumed assessment and an additional 100% penalty on the resulting underpayment.
            

            
            

              Box 38 - Alternative Minimum Tax
              Income Basic Tax on the basic income amount; excess of basic tax over regular tax is payable.
              Aircraft leasing may generate large tax-exempt components (foreign branch dividends, asset disposal gains); regardless of book treatment, include them in the AMT computation if above the NTD 500,000 deduction.
              Late filing: 1% surcharge per month (or segment of a month) on the unpaid tax amount, up to a maximum of 10%. If the return is filed after a formal demand from the tax authority, a 3% surcharge plus an additional 1% monthly penalty applies up to 10%. Intentional failure to file permits a presumed assessment and an additional 100% penalty on the resulting underpayment.
            

            
            

              Box 41 - Retained Earnings Tax
              5% tax on unappropriated earnings remaining undistributed at the end of the following year.
              A non-resident parent's directive to keep profits in the WFOE will trigger this entity-level tax unless the China parent receives a formal dividend.
              Late filing: 1% surcharge per month (or segment of a month) on the unpaid tax amount, up to a maximum of 10%. If the return is filed after a formal demand from the tax authority, a 3% surcharge plus an additional 1% monthly penalty applies up to 10%. Intentional failure to file permits a presumed assessment and an additional 100% penalty on the resulting underpayment.
            

            
          
        

      

    

    

      
Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Local File required when annual related-party controlled transactions equal or exceed NTD 30 million. Master File and Country-by-Country Report are required for multinational enterprise groups with consolidated group revenue of at least NTD 27 billion.

      
Economic substance scope: Taiwan applies a substance-over-form doctrine and statutory anti-avoidance rules (Income Tax Act Art. 66-8; Tax Collection Act Art. 12-1). For an aircraft-engine dry-leasing WFOE to be respected, the entity must maintain real commercial substance in Taiwan: permanent office or registered premises, locally resident management and technical staff, board meetings and strategic decisions in Taiwan, possession of engine operating records, exposure to and management of engine risk (lease default, maintenance, repossession), and a properly capitalized bank account. A pure contractual structure or a paper 'SPV' without commercial activities will be disregarded, and income will be re-allocated to the mainland Chinese parent under the IRIS (Inappropriate Realization of Income by Settlement in Foreign Exchange) principle.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / False Invoicing
        
Statutory sanction: Fraudulent tax evasion is punishable under Tax Collection Act Art. 41 with imprisonment for not less than six months and not more than five years, plus a fine of up to NTD 5,000,000. The tax authority will also collect the evaded tax with daily interest and impose a penalty of up to three times the underpaid tax.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who execute or approve false invoices are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and penalties. A criminal court may impose travel restrictions, asset freezes, and, upon conviction, imprisonment.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance
        
Statutory sanction: The tax authority may disregard the Taiwan WFOE by invoking Art. 66-8 of the Income Tax Act, re-attribute the full lease income to the parent, levy 20% CIT on the deemed net profit, and impose a 30% penalty on the underpaid tax. The WFOE may also lose its license under the Foreign Investment Act.

        
Director personal liability: The de facto director (whether Taiwanese or foreign) may be held personally liable for the re-attributed tax debt, barred from leaving Taiwan until the debt is settled, and subject to disqualification as a director for up to five years.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance
        
Statutory sanction: The tax authority will make a transfer pricing adjustment from the related-party transaction amount to the arm's length price. The resulting additional tax is assessed at 20%, and an administrative penalty of 30% of the underpaid tax applies. In cases of intentional misstatement, the penalty may reach 100%.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who sign the TP report or cause the filing of fraudulent documentation face personal fines, disqualification, and criminal liability for aiding tax evasion. In severe cases, a court order can attach personal bank accounts and impose a travel ban.

      

      
      

        Unreported Cross-Border Payments / Withholding Failure
        
Statutory sanction: A withholding agent who fails to withhold is required to pay the withheld tax itself. Additionally, a fine of one to three times the amount of withholding tax is imposed, along with daily interest. The payment to the non-resident is treated as a taxable distribution if not rectified.

        
Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable with the company for unpaid withholding tax and fines. A director's personal assets may be attached, and the director can be prohibited from leaving Taiwan while the tax debt remains outstanding.

      

      
      

        AML / Beneficial Ownership Transparency
        
Statutory sanction: Under the Money Laundering Control Act and Company Act beneficial ownership rules, a legal entity can be fined up to NTD 5,000,000 per violation, and the responsible officer can be fined up to NTD 5,000,000. Failure to report a substantial controller triggers a fine of NTD 200,000 to NTD 500,000 per instance.

        
Director personal liability: Directors may be disqualified and banned from serving as directors for up to five years. In money laundering prosecutions, the court may impose imprisonment of up to five years, confiscation of the leased engines, and a travel ban pending trial.
