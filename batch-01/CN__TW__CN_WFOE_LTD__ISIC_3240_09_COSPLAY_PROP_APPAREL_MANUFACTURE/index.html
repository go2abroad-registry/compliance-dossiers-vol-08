Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; official national portal at https://www.chinatax.gov.cn; electronic filing and digital invoice services through the Electronic Tax Bureau (etax.chinatax.gov.cn); national taxpayer service hotline 12366; export/customs data exchange through the China International Trade Single Window (singlewindow.cn). A WFOE registers with the local competent tax authority at its place of business in mainland China.

E-government filing portal

A Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) is established under the Foreign Investment Law and the PRC Company Law by registration with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). Upon issuance of the business license, the WFOE automatically receives an 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which constitutes its statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) and VAT registration identifier. SAMR transmits the registration to the STA in real time under the one-certificate-one-code reform. The WFOE must then complete, within 30 days of the license date: (1) verification of the auto-populated tax registration profile (VAT, corporate income tax, stamp duty, urban construction tax, education surcharges, and social insurance) in the Electronic Tax Bureau; (2) status determination as a general VAT taxpayer where annual taxable sales will exceed RMB 5 million; (3) export tax rebate (exemption) registration with the competent tax authority, including risk-grade assessment; (4) China Customs exporter registration through the Single Window, using the same USCC; (5) opening of a basic deposit account and a foreign-exchange settlement account with a designated bank, execution of the tripartite tax-bank-enterprise direct withholding agreement, and appointment of a financial officer; and (6) enrolment, where applicable, for cross-border e-commerce customs supervision codes 9610 (B2C) and 9710 (B2B) for sales made through Stripe or Shopify Payments to Taiwan or other markets. Taiwan-resident investors may hold 100% of WFOE equity under cross-strait investment rules; no minimum registered capital is required, and capital contribution records remain relevant for the financial accounting profile behind the TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China does not operate a check-the-box entity-classification election. A PRC WFOE is mandatorily a resident enterprise for corporate income tax (CIT), taxed at entity level on worldwide income. Available statutory elections relevant to an exporting manufacturer include: (1) the VAT status election between small-scale taxpayer and general VAT taxpayer, with mandatory general status above RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales and voluntary registration below that threshold; (2) the VAT simplified levy method for selected out-of-scope transactions (e.g., a 3% deemed levy rate, with the temporary 1% levy for small-scale taxpayers in force to 31 December 2027); (3) the export tax rebate method election, under which production-export enterprises apply the exemption-credit-refund model and trading-export enterprises apply the exemption-refund model, with a restricted one-off election for mixed manufacturing-trading operations subject to separate accounting; (4) the small low-profit enterprise (SLPE) CIT election, effective when taxable income does not exceed RMB 3,000,000, employees do not exceed 300, and total assets do not exceed RMB 5,000,000, yielding a 5% effective CIT rate; (5) the election to claim a 100% additional CIT deduction for eligible R&D expenditure on fabric technology and costume manufacturing innovation; and (6) hybrid mismatch treatment, under which the Special Tax Adjustment Measures and Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law allow the STA to re-characterize or disallow deductions for cross-border payments excluded from the recipient's taxable income, thereby neutralizing hybrid financial instrument outcomes; no unilateral entity-classification election is available in China.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

CNY is fully convertible on current-account items (export proceeds, dividends, service fees) subject to bank authenticity and consistency verification, but capital-account items remain controlled: FDI equity injection requires SAFE FDI registration and is credited to a dedicated capital account with use restrictions (no direct remittance for non-operating purposes such as real estate or equity investment outside the approved business scope). Profit remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is open-account permissible after each fiscal year end, conditioned on a licensed CPA audit, corporate income tax withholding clearance (10% dividend withholding, reduced to 5% where the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Arrangement of 2015 applies for a Taiwan resident company beneficially holding ≥25%), a shareholders' dividend resolution, and the bank's verification against the audited net profit. Capital repatriation by equity transfer or liquidation requires SAFE/AMR approval, state tax clearance, and a post-action reporting filing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

China abolished mandatory surrender of export receipts in 2012; the WFOE may retain export earnings in a foreign-currency current account or convert to CNY at prevailing market rates at any time. Under the trade-in-goods total-amount verification regime, proceeds from customs-cleared exports must be collected generally within 90 days of the customs declaration date; where collection is expected beyond 90 days (deferred receipt), the WFOE must file a deferred-receipt report with SAFE through the banking system within 30 days of the scheduled maturity, and any such deferred outstanding balances count against the enterprise's trade-credit quota (A-class ceiling of 30% of prior-year export volume). Advance payments received before shipment must be reported within 15 days of receipt and liquidated against customs declarations within the prescribed 180-day turnaround for A-class enterprises. No mandatory conversion ratio applies to the retained hard-currency balances.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE, 国家外汇管理局) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC, 中国人民银行); SAFE provincial/municipal branches administer FDI registration, trade-in-goods verification, and capital account settlement for the WFOE's manufacturing site within the Jiangsu/Zhejiang/Guangdong export corridor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent remits USD or HKD through SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's FDI Foreign-Exchange Capital Account at ICBC/BOC, referencing the SAFE FDI Registration Certificate (FDI业务登记凭证) and transaction purpose code 622 (foreign direct investment capital injection). The receiving bank must match the remittance against the SAFE system, settle the foreign currency into CNY under the capital settlement mechanism at the prevailing central parity-based spot rate, and credit the RMB expenditure account, strictly monitoring designated-use compliance (capex, raw materials, payroll, and ordinary overhead; prohibited for securities, wealth-management products, or real estate outside the registered business scope unless approved). All injection tranches must be completed within the five-year statutory contribution period and reported via the monthly SAFE cross-border flow monitoring file.For payments to third-party suppliers (fabric, wig fiber, zippers, packaging, freight forwarders): SWIFT MT103 for amounts up to USD 500,000; Commercial Letters of Credit (sight or usance 90–180 days) issued through BOC/ICBC trade desks for amounts above USD 500,000, ensuring two-party authenticity verification against contracts and customs declarations. Cover / Basic Information (基本資料) Taxpayer identification number (統一編號), company name, address, fiscal year-end, business registration code, industry code (ISIC 3240 / local Taiwan industry code), and the responsible person's ID. Enter the Taiwan WFOE's own tax ID and local detail. The non-resident parent is not named in this return; parent details are recorded in the company registry and in withholding forms at distribution. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 01 (Account Basis) Accounting principles adopted, bookkeeping basis, and currency. Taiwan IFRS / local GAAP with New Taiwan Dollar functional currency is mandatory. Reconciliation to foreign parent GAAP is not accepted; the foreign parent's accounting policies do not govern the Taiwan statutory books. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 10 (Net Sales Revenue 營業收入) Total net operating revenue from fabrication and tailoring of cosplay costumes, theatrical robes, synthetic wigs, props, and accessories, including online sales settled through Shopify/Stripe and other cross-border payment rails. Report all Taiwan-source and effectively-connected revenue. Sales directly executed by the foreign parent without a Taiwan PE are not reportable here; if the WFOE merely assists the parent, only commission/service income is reportable. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 20 (Cost of Goods Sold 營業成本) Direct materials (fabrics, fibers, dyes), licensed-character input costs, direct labor, factory overhead, and freight-in attributable to the cosplay apparel manufacturing operations. Related-party purchases must be priced at arm's length and substantiated by a Transfer Pricing Local File where thresholds are met; no arbitrary uplift of intercompany purchase prices is permitted. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 30 (Gross Operating Income 營業毛利) Net sales revenue minus cost of goods sold. If the WFOE carries no inventory risk and acts as a buy-sell agent for the parent, this line should show only the distribution margin, not full gross margin. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 40 (Operating Expenses 營業費用及損失) Selling, general and administrative expenses: salaries, factory rents, utilities, depreciation, platform transaction fees (Stripe/Shopify fees), customs duties, insurance, and professional service fees. Service fees paid to the non-resident parent or affiliated parties are deductible only if genuine, arm's-length, and substantiated; the payer must withhold and remit 20% gross-basis WHT on taxable Taiwanese-source service fees of non-residents. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 50 (Operating Income 營業淨利) Net profit derived solely from the Taiwan manufacturing and sales activity of the WFOE. Allocation of head-office expenses is not permitted for a domestically incorporated subsidiary; the WFOE is a separate taxable entity and cannot claim the parent's home-office costs. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 60 (Non-Operating Income 非營業收入) Interest income, foreign-exchange gains, rental income, licensing royalties received, and other non-operating income. Royalties received by the Taiwan entity from foreign licensees are taxable as Taiwan-source profit-seeking income if the exploitation/bearing of right is attributable to the Taiwan business; foreign tax credits may apply. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 70 (Non-Operating Expenses 非營業損失) Interest expense, foreign-exchange losses, casualty losses, and other non-operating deductions. Interest paid to a non-resident related lender is deductible only at arm's-length rates and subject to 15% gross-basis WHT; excessive interest from tax-haven affiliates may be disallowed pursuant to interest-stripping rules. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 80 (Net Income Before Tax 全年所得額) Total profit after all operating and non-operating items, before applying loss carryforwards and tax exemptions. This is the starting point for the statutory 20% headline rate; no parent-consolidated group relief is available. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 90 (Taxable Income After Loss Carryforwards 課稅所得額) Net income reduced by tax-exempt income and prior-year qualifying operating-loss deductions (10-year carryforward under Article 39). Losses are available to the WFOE only, not to the foreign shareholder; losses of a foreign head office cannot be used. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Line 100 (Income Tax Payable 應納稅額) Taxable income multiplied by 20%, reduced by R&D tax credits, foreign tax credits, AMT differential, and provisional tax installments previously paid. Shareholder-level dividend withholding of 21% on distributions to non-residents is computed on a separate Withholding Tax Return (Form 10/17), not in this annual return. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest. Willful evasion triggers criminal prosecution under the Tax Collection Act Article 41 (imprisonment up to five years, detention, and/or fines).
Schedule M (Minimum/AMT Reconciliation 所得基本稅額) Computation of alternative minimum tax where preferential exemptions cause the effective rate to fall below 12%; basic income exceeds NT$6.5 million and AMT liability is compared with regular tax. A typical manufacturing/retail WFOE with no tax-holiday preference is ordinarily not subject to AMT; any foreign tax exemption claims must still be scheduled. Late filing: surcharge of 10% of assessed tax for each two-month period or fraction thereof of delay, up to a maximum of 30% of assessed tax; where no tax is due, a fine of NT$3,000 to NT$30,000. Underreporting or false return: fine from two times to three times the underpaid tax, plus overdue interest.
