For a recycled scrap copper wire trading WFOE, a pure virtual office or commercial mailbox is generally rejected by both tax authorities and banks. SAMR may accept a law-firm address for initial registration, but upon tax registration the tax authority will require a physical operating address evidenced by a lease, rent invoices, property tax payment records, on-site signage, and employee presence. Banks will refuse to open corporate accounts for a medium-risk metals-trading entity whose registered office is only a virtual mailbox because AML/CFT site-inspection obligations cannot be satisfied. A virtual office may be used only as a supplementary communication address, never as the principal registered office for VAT ordinary taxpayer registration or documentary LC facilities.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No mandatory minimum paid-in capital applies under the Companies Act for a limited company; however, the Investment Commission will assess whether the declared capital is adequate for the proposed cross-border scrap trading and warehousing business. Capital must be remitted into a Taiwan foreign-currency deposit account or NT account, settled through an authorized foreign-exchange bank, and verified by a licensed CPA. No general blocked escrow account is required, although the Investment Commission may impose capital utilization milestones on the approved investment plan.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification under the Fire Services Act: the warehouse or storage facility must obtain a Fire Safety Equipment Inspection Certificate, maintain unobstructed egress, install automatic alarms and extinguishers, and maintain fire insurance. Structural clearance: a licensed architect or structural engineer must certify floor loading for stacked copper wire bundles and racking. Occupational safety: the Ministry of Labor, R.O.C. (Taiwan), requires a workplace hazard assessment under the Occupational Safety and Health Act, training for forklift and crane operators, personal protective equipment for copper slivers and dust, an emergency response plan, and regular worksite inspections.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

China operates a unified Enterprise Income Tax (EIT) system for WFOEs without a check-the-box pass-through election. The WFOE is a taxable resident enterprise at 25% EIT on worldwide income. No hybrid mismatch election is available under domestic law. However, a WFOE may elect small-scale low-profit enterprise (small low-profit enterprise) status for preferential EIT at effective rates (annual taxable income up to RMB 3 million for 2024/2025 policy window, taxed at 5% effective rate for qualifying small low-profit companies), and may elect the 'Small-Scale VAT taxpayer' simplified levy method (3% levy rate, or 1% temporary reduction where in force) instead of the General VAT taxpayer method, but such an election does not alter the entity-level taxable classification. Export zero-rating is only substantively available to General VAT taxpayers. No other statutory tax classification election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5 million (annual taxable sales of goods/services) for mandatory registration as a General VAT taxpayer; quarterly sales threshold under the small-scale VAT simplified regime is RMB 300,000 exempt from VAT (monthly RMB 100,000, with applicable policy thresholds) but registration threshold for any VAT liability is structurally unified with the business licence. In practice, any entity making taxable supplies is registered upon incorporation; however, the General/Small-Scale distinction is based on annual sales exceeding RMB 5 million.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of goods by a General VAT taxpayer WFOE qualifies for the 0% VAT rate under the 'Measures for the Administration of the Export Tax Rebate (Exemption) of Goods' (State Administration of Taxation Announcement No. 12 of 2022 and related STA circulars) where such goods are physically exported from China and customs export declaration is completed. The recycled scrap copper wire must qualify as a genuine exportation of goods (not domestic sale), and the export proceeds must be repatriated to China. Under the 'Export Tax Refund (Exemption)' legal framework, the WFOE must: (a) hold General VAT taxpayer status; (b) have completed the export tax rebate registration; (c) possess a valid Export Customs Declaration (出口报关单) for the exact HS codes under the export contract reflecting the ISIC 4669 recycled non-ferrous scrap categories; (d) have entered into a sales contract with the Taiwan buyer, issue an export commercial invoice and packing list; (e) have physically left the territory through a designated Chinese customs port with transferable customs supervision records; (f) have received the foreign exchange payment from an overseas buyer in a recognised manner and repatriated it through the repatriation banking system within the statutory deadline. Zero-rating is available for both physical goods exports and, for qualifying offshore services, under the 'cross-border taxable services zero-rating and exemption' rules announced under Notice 2016/36 of the Ministry of Finance and STA, but the target scope for recycled scrap copper wire is a physical goods export. Note that for certain recyclable metal scrap export, there are export quota/licensing restrictions and the exporter must verify that the recycled copper wire is not classified under prohibited waste categories for export; if the shipment is treated as 'recycled copper raw materials' under the 'Management Catalogue of Solid Wastes (Import/Export)', an export licence from MEE may condition eligibility. The zero-rated export must not have been previously sold domestically or entered the VAT taxable chain in China.

  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List issued by the WFOE
  • Official Export Contract or Sale Agreement between the WFOE and Taiwan buyer

China mandates the use of the 'New Generation Electronic Invoicing System' (through the E-Tax Bureau Online Platform), under which VAT invoices are digitally issued with a unique electro-magnetic invoice number and encrypted stamping controlled by the STA. Full digital invoices (全面数字化电子发票) must be issued in XML/OFD format through the national e-invoice service platform, and each invoice is transmitted in near-real-time to the STA central data warehouse; there is no additional independent fiscal stamping device. The VAT Integrated Management System performs cross-automated matching of input and output invoices and enforces the 'VAT invoice segments and lines' validation with anti-fraud detection via the 'Invoice Management System' (金税四期 / Golden Tax System Phase IV). Enterprises must issue invoices via the official e-invoice APIs, and special VAT invoices must be reconciled against the STA's 'VAT Invoice Check and Comparison' platform before input tax credit is claimed.

If the export zero-rating is claimed but the export transaction is unverified, fraudulent, or the foreign exchange is not repatriated within the statutory deadline, the tax authority will deny the zero-rated treatment, require the exporter to pay the output VAT at the standard rate (13%) on the full export value, and impose a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day on the unpaid VAT. For fraud involving fabricated export documents or false export declarations, the penalty ranges from 50% to 500% of the tax evaded under the Tax Collection and Administration Law; criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law for 'fraudulent export tax rebates' can impose imprisonment and fines where the defrauded refund exceeds RMB 50,000. Additionally, the competent tax authority may claw back any input VAT refund already paid, cancel the export tax rebate qualification for a period (from 6 months to 3 years), and publish the entity in the 'Tax Violation Blacklist' under the Joint Punishment Memorandum for Serious Tax Violations. Customs authorities may also impose administrative penalties, suspend the exporter's customs declaration qualification, and, for environmental non-compliance involving prohibited or unlicensed waste scrap exports, the MEE and customs may confiscate the goods and impose fines under the 'Solid Waste Law'.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating administered foreign exchange regime with a hard separation between current-account and capital-account convertibility. On the current account, goods-trade receipts and payments, service fees, and after-tax dividend distributions can be executed through designated banks once the bank verifies authenticity through SAFE's goods-trade and non-trade surveillance platforms. The WFOE is therefore able to receive Taiwan-sourced FDI equity, pay foreign suppliers for scrap copper, and remit declared dividends, but every transaction requires an underlying contract, invoice, customs declaration or audit trail. On the capital account, however, convertibility is strictly controlled: equity injections must be pre-registered with SAFE through the PRC bank, external borrowing must meet macro-prudential limits and be registered, and any outward remittance that is not a current-account item requires prior approval. Export proceeds are not subject to mandatory conversion, but they must be repatriated to a domestic foreign-exchange account and reconciled against customs records within the statutory collection window, normally 90 days. Profit remittance to the Taiwanese shareholder is permitted under current-account rules only after payment of corporate income tax, audited financial statements confirming distributable profits, and a legal dividend declaration. Banks treat large or frequent dividends from a trading WFOE as potential disguised capital flight; therefore the treasury must retain full documentation of cumulative profits, tax payments, and actual trade operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender percentage applies to export foreign-currency receipts under current SAFE rules; 100 percent retention in a designated foreign-exchange settlement account is permitted. The statutory duty is repatriation and verification: export proceeds arising from goods export must be brought into China and reported to SAFE's trade monitoring system, typically within 90 days from the date of customs declaration unless the enterprise has obtained an approved extension. The bank matches every FX credit or conversion against the underlying customs export declaration and invoices; any shortfall, delay, or settlement mismatch triggers enhanced AML review.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBoC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low and legally barred for primary settlement. Offshore fintech EMIs are not licensed by PBoC or SAFE to open domestic current-account or foreign-exchange settlement accounts for a PRC onshore WFOE. PBoC payment institution rules allow only licensed Chinese payment institutions to handle auxiliary collection and payment functions, and those licenses do not include full cross-border trade settlement, capital-account clearing, or FX conversion. The WFOE must therefore clear all inbound FDI, exports, imports, and dividend remittances through a domestic Chinese commercial bank; an offshore EMI may be used as a lightweight invoice-matching or treasury dashboard interface only if the underlying account remains at the Tier-1 PRC bank and all cash movement passes through SAFE-approved rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder must remit FDI equity via SWIFT MT103 to the WFOE's dedicated SAFE-registered foreign-exchange capital account at the Tier-1 PRC bank. The originator must use SAFE purpose code for foreign direct investment in China, commonly 622010 or the bank-mapped equivalent, and include the WFOE's FDI registration reference number in the remittance details. The PRC bank screens the remitter and checks the amount against the approved equity amount before crediting; afterwards, capital-settlement rules let the WFOE convert the foreign currency to RMB for permitted business use only. The bank will retain capital-account verification forms and the latest SAFE digital registration record, and the WFOE should obtain a bank-issued capital verification certificate or account statement for tax and audit purposes.For imports of shredded non-ferrous scrap copper, the primary rail is an irrevocable documentary letter of credit issued by the WFOE's PRC bank, either sight or usance with the Taiwanese seller as beneficiary; SWIFT MT103 is used for open-account settlements, repayments of trade loans, or low-value recurring consignments after approval. Documentary collections covering D/P and D/A can be used for captive supplier relationships and lower transaction values. Every outward payment must be processed through the goods-trade current account and matched to the corresponding import customs declaration, invoice, and logistics evidence in SAFE's trade monitoring system; payment terms, advance-deposit thresholds, and trade-finance limits must be approved by the bank's compliance and commodity-trade desks.SAFE-approved onshore CNY foreign-exchange forward contracts for genuine current-account exposure, limited to outstanding trade receivables and payables and evidenced by contracts, invoices, and customs declarations.SAFE-approved foreign-exchange swaps executed with the domestic PRC bank to repurchase or switch CNY and FX cash flows in support of the underlying trade settlement cycle.Cross-currency swaps and interest-rate swaps only where a real capital-account liability or long-term trade-finance facility has been registered with SAFE and the notional matches the outstanding debt.Boundary for NDFs: onshore non-deliverable forwards are not permissible for the WFOE as a direct stand-alone hedge; the use of offshore NDF or an indirect hedge through an offshore treasury vehicle requires SAFE approval and is generally reserved for multinational treasury centres, not a trading WFOE.
