Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; local competent tax service bureau at the WFOE's registered address; online portal: Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) at etax.chinatax.gov.cn and the National Electronic Invoice Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

A foreign investor does not obtain a separate Chinese TIN for the WFOE. Upon incorporation, the Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) issues the WFOE a Unified Social Credit Code (USCC), which is legally the taxpayer identification number and VAT registration number. Procedure: (1) notarize and apostille/legalize the foreign parent's incorporation documents and board resolutions; (2) file the name pre-approval and foreign investment registration with SAMR; (3) obtain the WFOE business license with the USCC; (4) complete tax status registration through e-Tax within 30 days of the business license date, which also opens the VAT taxpayer file and consumption tax/social insurance links; (5) confirm VAT classification as small-scale or general taxpayer; (6) activate fapiao issuance through the STA electronic invoice platform. A foreign non-resident parent without a permanent establishment in China does not apply for a Chinese TIN; it uses its home-country TIN when claiming treaty benefits and when the WFOE withholds tax on dividends, interest, or royalties.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All businesses with taxable activities must complete VAT registration. Mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration is triggered when annual taxable sales exceed CNY 5,000,000; below CNY 5,000,000 the taxpayer may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer, while basic VAT registration is required from incorporation regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the PRC Interim VAT Regulations and the export tax rebate (exemption, credit, refund) framework for exported goods, the WFOE, as a VAT general taxpayer and manufacturing exporter, may apply the 0% VAT rate to the export of canned boba tapioca pearls and claim a refund/credit of input VAT. Eligibility requires actual export from mainland China customs territory to Taiwan or another foreign customs territory; a completed customs export declaration; commercial export sales to an overseas buyer; receipt and repatriation of the export proceeds in foreign exchange within the statutory collection period; and submission of the export tax rebate declaration before the filing deadline. If the goods do not qualify for substantiated export zero-rating, the supply is reclassified as taxable at 13% output VAT.

  • Customs Export Goods Declaration Form (filed through the China International Trade Single Window, with customs release endorsement and export date)
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill or international cargo manifest for non-ocean transport) evidencing physical departure from mainland China to Taiwan or another foreign destination
  • Bank-issued Export Proceeds Collection Statement (foreign exchange receipt/remittance advice, or SAFE Goods Trade Monitoring System record) proving the export payment was received and repatriated to the WFOE's PRC bank account within the collection period

Fapiao issuance is mandatory. The WFOE must use the State Taxation Administration's National Electronic Invoice Service Platform under Golden Tax Project Phase IV, issuing VAT special invoices or fully digital electronic invoices with a unique invoice code, digital signature, and encrypted QR code transmitted to the STA at issuance in near real time. This enables nationwide tax clearance and cross-checking against customs, bank, and input invoice data. Traditional tax-control equipment is not used for fully digital e-invoice taxpayers; any private or ERP invoice arrangement must be interfaced to the STA-certified platform to have legal effect.

The STA performs post-refund audits using Golden Tax Phase IV data to cross-check the customs export declaration, logistics documents, bank foreign exchange receipt, and VAT invoices. If the export zero-rating is not substantiated or proceeds are not repatriated within the 180-day period, the refund is clawed back, the export is retroactively reclassified to 13% VAT, and late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day applies. Under the PRC Tax Collection Administration Law, a false claim gives rise to a penalty of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax; fraudulent export tax refund claims also constitute the criminal offence of fraudulently obtaining export tax rebates under the PRC Criminal Law, punishable by fines of 1 to 5 times the defrauded amount, imprisonment up to life, and loss of export tax rebate qualification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains a managed float and a strict separation between current and capital account items. Under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations, RMB is fully convertible for bona fide current account transactions, including trade in goods and cross-border services, conditioned on genuine documentation and bank review. Capital account items, however, remain heavily controlled under the State Administration of Foreign Exchange registration framework. A Taiwan-invested WFOE manufacturing tapioca pearls may freely convert export proceeds into RMB, pay imported raw materials, and remit profits after tax, provided it files the relevant international income and expense declarations and presents invoices, customs declarations, contracts, and audited financials. The principal hurdles are the need for FDI foreign exchange registration before capital injection, supervision of capital account settlement by SAFE, denial of full capital account convertibility for speculative purposes, and strict documentary truthfulness checks. Profit remittance requires an audited PRC statutory report, tax completion certificate, and board resolution. Therefore, treasury design must route all cross-border flows through licensed bank counters and maintain a complete trade and capital KYC file.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Current PRC law does not impose a fixed mandatory conversion ratio. All export foreign currency receipts must be repatriated to China and either retained in a designated export foreign-exchange settlement account or converted to RMB at the WFOE's discretion. SAFE's total trade monitoring generally benchmarks export collection against the customs declaration within a 90-day post-export window; if collection is delayed beyond this window, the bank must flag the discrepancy and the WFOE must provide contracts, dispatch documents, and payment term evidence. The export proceeds from canned boba tapioca pearls and related goods may therefore be retained 100% in foreign currency or settled immediately.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Non-viable. PRC payment-institution regulations require any entity servicing RMB payments, foreign-exchange settlement, or cross-border trade payments for a domestically domiciled WFOE to hold a PRC payment license and be supervised by the People's Bank of China and SAFE. Offshore digital EMIs are legally barred from opening domestic bank settlement accounts, handling capital account transactions, or performing FX conversion for a mainland-incorporated WFOE. The only compliant route is a licensed domestic tier-1 bank, with no reliance on foreign EMI banking rails.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Master File is required when aggregate related party transactions in a tax year exceed RMB 1 billion. Local File is required when tangible asset transfers with related parties exceed RMB 200 million, financial asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, intangible asset transfers exceed RMB 100 million, or other related party transactions exceed RMB 40 million. Related party transaction reporting schedules must be filed even below these thresholds whenever any related party transaction exists.

Economic substance scope: Although the PRC does not apply the offshore Economic Substance Regulations model, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, together with the Special Tax Adjustment Measures, requires the WFOE to demonstrate actual commercial substance and core income-generating activities in China. For canned boba tapioca pearls, CIGA includes procurement of raw tapioca, formula ownership, aseptic manufacturing, quality control, inventory risk, invoicing, treasury decisions, and operational management. A WFOE with no manufacturing plant, no staff in China, and board decisions made entirely in Taiwan can be recharacterized as a shell; profits may be adjusted, deemed dividends may be taxed, and the Taiwan parent may be denied treaty benefits for lack of beneficial ownership.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Under Article 41 and Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law, the tax authority may adjust related party pricing, recharacterize excessive royalties and fees as distributions, disallow interest deductions under thin capitalization, impose special tax adjustment interest, and apply Article 63 of the Tax Collection Administration Law, resulting in recovery of unpaid tax, daily interest at 0.05%, and an administrative fine of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly implement the profit-stripping arrangement face personal civil liability for the WFOE's unpaid tax and penalties to the extent that the corporate veil is pierced for abuse of limited liability. Directors may be placed on the tax authority blacklist, subjected to travel bans through the exit-entry control system, have personal bank accounts and assets frozen, and if the underpaid tax is relatively large and exceeds 10% of the total tax due, directors may be prosecuted under Article 201 of the Criminal Law for tax evasion with imprisonment from three to seven years for huge amounts.
False Export Invoicing, Export VAT Rebate Fraud, and AML Exposure

Statutory sanction: This triggers Article 204 and Article 205 of the Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax refunds and issuance of false special VAT invoices, with fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment for severe amounts. Under the Tax Collection Administration Law and Anti-Money Laundering Law, the tax authority and customs bureau can recover the fraudulently obtained refund, impose fines of 100% to 500% of the refunded amount, revoke export qualifications, and refer the case for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false invoices, instruct staff to create fake bills of lading, or route proceeds through underground channels are personally exposed to criminal arrest, travel bans, and blacklisting. Personal assets derived from the fraudulent refunds and false invoicing may be confiscated. Directors may also face unlimited joint and several liability for the WFOE's fraudulent VAT/CIT liabilities where the corporate form is used as a vehicle for crime.
Shell Company Structure and Absence of Economic Substance / Beneficial Ownership Abuse

Statutory sanction: Under Article 47 of the Enterprise Income Tax Law and the General Anti-Avoidance Rules, the tax authority may disregard the WFOE as a shell, recharacterize its transactions, impute taxable profits to the Chinese business operations, and deny the Cross-Strait Double Taxation Agreement relief. The resulting unpaid tax and deemed dividend withholding tax will attract the daily 0.05% surcharge and an administrative fine of 50% to 5 times the underpaid tax; the taxpayer will also be listed as a major tax violation case in the public blacklist system.

Director personal liability: Under Article 23 of the PRC Company Law, shareholders and directors who abuse the independent status of the limited liability company and shareholder limited liability to evade debts are jointly and severally liable for the WFOE's obligations. Directors are subject to travel restrictions, blacklisting by the tax authority, seizure of personal assets, and if the shell structure is used to evade CIT or WHT knowingly, prosecution for tax evasion under Article 201 of the Criminal Law with imprisonment of up to seven years may follow.
