Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; online filing and registration are administered through the China Electronic Tax Administration (e-Tax) portal available via the official STA website at www.chinatax.gov.cn, with consolidated enrollment through the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) Integrated Government Service Platform.

E-government filing portal

Upon SAMR issuance of the wholly foreign-owned enterprise (WFOE) business license, the WFOE completes tax information confirmation within the China Electronic Tax Administration no later than 30 calendar days after establishment. The Unified Social Credit Code printed on the business license is automatically accepted as the statutory taxpayer identification number (TIN) for all PRC national and local direct and indirect taxes, including VAT and corporate income tax. The system activates the taxpayer master file in the Golden Tax System; no separate physical VAT registration certificate is issued. For the non-resident foreign parent or investors, no Chinese TIN is obtained solely by reason of the WFOE formation; if the foreign non-resident entity itself derives China-source income and is not under the withholding mechanism, it must apply separately for a non-resident tax registration through the e-Tax platform by submitting certified documentation of incorporation, board resolution, power of attorney, evidence of the relevant transaction or permanent establishment, and the prescribed application for non-resident taxpayer registration.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China maintains robust current-account RMB convertibility while retaining material capital-account controls. The WFOE's ordinary trade receipts from musical instrument and audio gear e-commerce may be converted to RMB through a designated bank without case-by-case approval, provided the transactions are supported by true customs and logistics data. Capital inflows, particularly the Taiwan shareholder's equity injection, are governed by the Foreign Direct Investment regime and require SAFE's foreign exchange registration before funds enter the country. Once received, the foreign exchange capital account cannot be used for speculative securities, entrusted loans, offshore working capital loans, or real-estate investment outside the approved scope. Profit remittance to the Taiwan investor is a current-account dividend and is permitted after annual statutory audit, corporate income tax settlement, filing of the dividend withholding tax, and bank-level authenticity review. Repatriation of contributed capital upon liquidation is a capital-account event requiring separate SAFE filing and tax-clearance certificates. Therefore, the treasury must maintain strict segregation between capital-account and current-account fund flows, and every inward remittance must include a clearly stated purpose code and a matching documentary trail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory foreign exchange surrender is currently imposed by SAFE. The WFOE may retain export proceeds in foreign currency or sell them to the designated bank at market rates. Receipts from goods exports and cross-border e-commerce sales must be received within the agreed commercial payment term and must be reported through the Goods Trade Foreign Exchange Monitoring System; any receivable outstanding beyond 90 days from export declaration requires a documented justification and may be flagged as an abnormal trade item. There is no statutory minimum conversion ratio for offshore receipts; the WFOE may maintain surplus forex in its current account subject to the bank's authenticity review.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), with the SAFE branch of the WFOE's registered location responsible for foreign direct investment registration and cross-border trade monitoring.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs have low viability as primary treasury and custody venues for a PRC-domiciled WFOE because they are not licensed by PBOC or SAFE and cannot accept deposits in mainland China. Their practical role is limited to payment-routing and FX-orchestration services that ultimately settle into a Tier-1 domestic bank account. The WFOE may use licensed cross-border payment institutions, such as PayPal through its PBOC-licensed domestic partner, for retail collections, but must ensure that platform settlement is cleared into the corporate account and reported as cross-border e-commerce goods sales. Stripe's direct onboarding for mainland China entities is constrained; the WFOE should only integrate Stripe via the platform's acquiring partnership with a Chinese bank or a branch of a licensed international acquiring bank. Any attempt to bypass local banking rails through an offshore EMI will breach PBOC's payment-service licensing boundaries and may trigger the closure of the WFOE's capital account. Therefore, the treasury blueprint should treat EMIs as ancillary transaction engines, not as the legal account or liquidity layer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan shareholder must complete a SAFE FDI registration before remittance and obtain a foreign exchange registration return or a bank-aggregated code in the capital-account information system. The equity injection should be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 only, from a bank account under the shareholder's legal name, to the WFOE's designated foreign exchange capital account at the Tier-1 bank. The remittance instruction must use purpose code specifically for foreign direct investment equity capital contribution, not current-account trade or service code; the beneficiary reference should include the WFOE's registered name and the FDI contract number. The receiving bank will check the registered capital amount, negative-list prohibition, and the actual use plan. Upon clearing, the bank will report the transaction to SAFE under the direct-investment statistical category, and a PRC CPA must issue a capital verification report. For TWD remittances, the bank will convert through the Taiwan Strait currency clearing arrangement; USD and HKD are also acceptable.
