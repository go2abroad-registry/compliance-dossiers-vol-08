Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Taiwan to a foreign destination (including offshore Amazon FBA fulfillment centers) qualify for the 0% export VAT rate if the Taiwanese exporter contracts with a foreign buyer or authorized representative and ultimately proves actual shipment outside the local customs territory. Qualifying zero-rated categories also include services rendered to foreign enterprises in connection with exported goods and qualifying offshore digital services, provided the consideration is paid in foreign currency and no economic use of the service occurs in Taiwan.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Entry / Bill of Entry) filed electronically with Taiwan Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of goods from Taiwan
  • Commercial invoice and packing list issued by the exporting WFOE
  • Purchase order, contract, or written agreement evidencing the cross-border sale
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-currency receipt evidencing payment repatriation by the offshore buyer

All VAT-registered businesses must issue Uniform Invoices for every taxable sale. Businesses designated as electronic-invoice merchants must transmit each electronic Uniform Invoice to the MOF Integrated Electronic Invoice Cloud Platform (Turnkey Server) through official APIs or batch channels, attaching the platform-generated QR code and random anti-fraud invoice number. This cloud transmission constitutes the statutory real-time fiscal clearance/anti-fraud stamping obligation for both B2B and B2C transactions; non-transmission or material inaccuracies are treated in audit as issuance of a false or missing Uniform Invoice, subject to the same penalties.

The National Taxation Bureau may retroactively review the zero-rated export claims by demand-file audit. Taxpayers must maintain a seamless chain from export declaration, transportation documents, custody records at the overseas FBA warehouse, and foreign exchange receipt showing collection of export proceeds in foreign currency. If the foreign-exchange proceeds are not repatriated to Taiwan within 180 days of the export date (or approved extension) or if any export document is false, fabrication of export status, or otherwise unverifiable, the zero rating is disallowed and the tax authority will reassess 5% business tax on the sales consideration with statutory late-payment interest, apply the understatement/short-payment penalty under the Business Tax Act (up to roughly two and a half times the tax evaded for intentional fraud, with criminal liability for document forgery), and issue a demand order; persistent violations may additionally suspend the e-invoice credential and confiscate the evaded tax.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is freely convertible on the current account, encompassing cross-border trade in goods and services and therefore all Amazon FBA-related export proceeds may be settled through designated banks. The capital account, including FDI equity injections from the Taiwanese parent, remains subject to SAFE registration and quota controls. The WFOE must open a capital foreign exchange settlement account upon receipt of the inbound remittance, verify the funds with a certified public accountant, and convert RMB only for purposes within the registered business scope. Profit repatriation to the Taiwanese shareholder is permissible only after independent annual audit certification, payment of corporate income tax and distribution under the 10% withholding tax (or treaty-protected rate), plus production of a board resolution and evidence of natural-person ultimate beneficial ownership. No mandatory surrender of export proceeds currently exists, but all goods-trade receipts must be repatriated within 180 days of customs declaration and reported under the SAFE monitoring system. Dividend outbound transfers require a second foreign exchange account and must be executed through the bank's FX settlement price. Compliance with the People's Bank of China's anti-money laundering rules and the State Administration of Foreign Exchange's digital trading platform (ASOne) is mandatory before any cross-border conversion is approved.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods-trade export proceeds from the Amazon FBA operation must be repatriated to China through the settlement bank within 180 days of the date of departure of the shipment from the Chinese customs warehouse. The statutory mandatory conversion ratio is currently zero percent, but the WFOE must present the signed export verification form, logistics proof and customs declarations in the SAFE system. Retained foreign exchange is permitted within the approved annual credit ceiling, and any conversion from USD to RMB is purely at the enterprise's commercial election at the prevailing market rate.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) / State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) of the People's Republic of China

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Airwallex and Stripe are not licensed as banks in mainland China and are legally barred from maintaining the WFOE's capital account, receiving FDI injections, or providing direct FX conversion services on behalf of the WFOE. They may only serve as third-party payment processors for low-value e-commerce sales through a licensed Chinese partner bank, and all funds must ultimately settle into a domestic corporate bank account. For the required capital verification, profit remittance and trade-based FX hedging, a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank remains mandatory. The use of Airwallex or Stripe as an inbound channel for the Taiwanese parent's investment would constitute an unauthorized cross-border payment scheme and trigger immediate SAFE enforcement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The inbound capital injection must be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the Taiwanese parent's bank to the WFOE's FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Settlement Account at the designated Tier-1 bank in China. The remittance shall carry the explicit purpose code 1101 'return of investment' or 7210 'foreign direct investment' and include in field 70 the SAFE filing registration number and the chart of the capital verification account. Upon receipt, the WFOE must submit the capital verification report prepared by a certified public accountant, then complete the capital account reconciliation and RMB settlement in SAFE's ASOne system. The converted RMB balance can then be transferred to the basic deposit account for operating liquidity. A second SAFE registration is required for any subsequent increase in paid-in capital.Trade disbursements can be settled via SWIFT MT103 for open-account supplier payments above USD 5,000, using purpose codes 1210 (export trade settlement) or 1220 (import trade settlement) after submission of the underwriting contract and invoice. For large consolidated factory purchases, a Documentary Collection (D/P or D/A) through a Chinese negotiating bank is preferred, while a Commercial Letter of Credit may be issued against the WFOE's available credit line for high-value raw material imports. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low. Offshore fintech EMIs such as Airwallex and Stripe are not licensed as banks in mainland China and are legally barred from maintaining the WFOE's capital account, receiving FDI injections, or providing direct FX conversion services on behalf of the WFOE. They may only serve as third-party payment processors for low-value e-commerce sales through a licensed Chinese partner bank, and all funds must ultimately settle into a domestic corporate bank account. For the required capital verification, profit remittance and trade-based FX hedging, a Tier-1 domestic commercial bank remains mandatory. The use of Airwallex or Stripe as an inbound channel for the Taiwanese parent's investment would constitute an unauthorized cross-border payment scheme and trigger immediate SAFE enforcement.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File / Transfer Pricing Report: mandatory when annual related-party transaction amounts reach NT$200 million or more; Master File and Country-by-Country Report: mandatory when the multinational group's consolidated revenue reaches NT$27 billion or more.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan has not enacted a Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime, but the tax authority applies substance-over-form, general anti-abuse rules, and profit recharacterization principles. A Taiwan WFOE engaged in Amazon FBA must maintain real office premises, local employees, records and decision-making functions; lack of economic substance may result in disallowance of deductions, transfer pricing recharacterization, or application of CFC/imputation rules.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion: Under-Reporting / Offshore Concealment

Statutory sanction: Tax authority may assess taxable income under the deemed or highest-profit method; underpaid tax incurs a surcharge of 1% per two-day delay up to 15%, plus a penalty up to 3 times the underpaid tax; criminal tax evasion can lead to imprisonment of up to 5 years and monetary fines under the Tax Collection Act and related criminal provisions.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or knowingly cause false returns are subject to criminal prosecution, personal fines, travel bans, and asset freezing; courts may pierce the corporate veil where the WFOE is used as a sham for tax fraud.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Shell Company

Statutory sanction: Taiwan tax authorities may adjust related-party transactions to arm's length, impute additional taxable income, impose interest and late penalties up to 3 times the underpaid tax, and disallow related deductions; unresolved transfer pricing audits may lead to criminal tax evasion referrals in egregious cases.

Director personal liability: CFOs, tax directors, and approving directors may be jointly liable if they knowingly executed non-arm's-length transactions; travel restrictions and civil restitution may be ordered where corporate assets are stripped.
Withholding Tax Non-Compliance on Profit Repatriation

Statutory sanction: The Taiwan withholding agent is personally liable for the uncollected tax; penalties may range from one to three times the under-withheld amount, plus daily surcharges, interest, enforced collection, and potential criminal liability if intentional.

Director personal liability: Directors and payment officers who authorize unlawful remittances can be compelled to pay the withheld tax personally; in serious cases, travel bans and enforcement orders will prevent exit from Taiwan.
AML / Illicit Payment Rails Misuse

Statutory sanction: Violations of the Taiwan Money Laundering Control Act may result in imprisonment of up to 7 years and substantial fines; administrative penalties, asset forfeiture, and account seizure may also be imposed.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal exposure for willful blindness or deliberate circumvention of AML controls; travel bans and cross-border mutual legal assistance may be invoked by authorities.
