Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR), acting through the local Market Regulation Administration of the city where the WFOE's registered office is established; the electronic filing platform is the National Enterprise Registration One-Stop Service portal.

Governing statute

Company Law of the People's Republic of China (2023 Revision, effective 1 July 2024), including the sections on incorporation conditions, registered capital subscription and governance of limited liability companies (Articles 23, 24, 47 and 68); Foreign Investment Law of the People's Republic of China (2019), Articles 4, 12 and 31, as applied to Taiwan-area investors by reference; Regulations of the People's Republic of China on Registration Administration of Market Entities (2021), Articles 5, 9, 15 and 20; and the Foreign Investment Negative List, under which wholesale distribution of passive electronic components is an unrestricted permitted category.

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Establishment Registration of a Wholly Foreign-Owned Limited Liability Company, together with the duly executed Articles of Association, filed in the SAMR online enterprise registration system; the issuance of the Comprehensive Business License constitutes the statutory incorporation instrument.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. The PRC Company Law does not impose a minimum paid-up capital for a WFOE engaged in wholesale distribution of electronic components. The registered capital amount is determined by the Articles of Association and must be fully subscribed by the sole member, with actual contribution made within five years from establishment as required by Article 47 of the Company Law. No blocked escrow account is required for this activity; capital may be contributed in RMB or a freely convertible foreign currency through a SAFE-registered capital account, and the Taiwan-area investor must document the source of funds for bank compliance purposes.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; tax filings and proceedings are conducted through the STA's National Unified Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax Bureau) platform, with the local competent tax bureau determined by the WFOE's registered address.

E-government filing portal

The WFOE, as a PRC-resident limited liability company, obtains its statutory taxpayer identification number through the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The procedure is: (1) register the WFOE with SAMR and obtain the business license with a legally valid USCC; (2) within 30 calendar days of business license issuance, perform tax registration at the local competent tax authority via the e-Tax Bureau, linking the USCC to the tax filing account; (3) where export operations are contemplated, register the enterprise for customs declaration with China Customs and obtain an operating customs registration code; (4) enrol for VAT general taxpayer status in accordance with the statutory requirements through the e-Tax Bureau if annual taxable sales exceed the threshold or voluntarily; (5) obtain the required export foreign-exchange receipt accounts with a PRC bank and complete SAFE trade-in-foreign-exchange registration; (6) where the WFOE is not yet formed, a foreign non-resident investor cannot itself obtain a PRC TIN; the PRC TIN arises only after the WFOE has been incorporated and holds its USCC.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — The WFOE, as a PRC-resident limited liability company, obtains its statutory taxpayer identification number through the Unified Social Credit Code (USCC) issued by the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR). The procedure is: (1) register the WFOE with SAMR and obtain the business license with a legally valid USCC; (2) within 30 calendar days of business license issuance, perform tax registration at the local competent tax authority via the e-Tax Bureau, linking the USCC to the tax filing account; (3) where export operations are contemplated, register the enterprise for customs declaration with China Customs and obtain an operating customs registration code; (4) enrol for VAT general taxpayer status in accordance with the statutory requirements through the e-Tax Bureau if annual taxable sales exceed the threshold or voluntarily; (5) obtain the required export foreign-exchange receipt accounts with a PRC bank and complete SAFE trade-in-foreign-exchange registration; (6) where the WFOE is not yet formed, a foreign non-resident investor cannot itself obtain a PRC TIN; the PRC TIN arises only after the WFOE has been incorporated and holds its USCC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None; PRC corporate income tax law does not provide check-the-box or pass-through election mechanisms, and no hybrid mismatch election is domestically recognized. The only practical entity-level election is the VAT general taxpayer vs. small-scale taxpayer status election, which does not alter corporate tax classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All WFOEs must complete tax and VAT registration from establishment irrespective of turnover; the mandatory VAT general taxpayer registration threshold is cumulative annual taxable sales exceeding CNY 5,000,000. Below that threshold, a WFOE may be treated as a small-scale taxpayer subject to the simplified VAT rate, unless general taxpayer status is voluntarily chosen.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exported goods from the PRC customs territory, including shipped goods to the Taiwan area, may be VAT zero-rated if the WFOE is a VAT general taxpayer, the sale is a genuine export sale, goods are physically shipped out of PRC customs territory under export customs declaration, the export invoice and supporting documents are consistent, export proceeds are received from overseas buyers and repatriated to the PRC within the statutory deadline, and all required export tax rebate declaration data is submitted via e-tax and customs systems. The zero-rating covers output VAT at 0% and allows refund or credit of input VAT attributable to the export, subject to the verified export documentation bundle.

  • China Customs Export Goods Declaration (Customs export declaration / Bill of Entry)
  • Bill of Lading, sea waybill, air waybill, or multimodal transport document evidencing export shipment
  • Commercial invoice
  • Packing list
  • Export sales contract or overseas purchase order
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) or bank foreign-exchange receipt confirmation issued by a PRC bank under SAFE rules
  • Zero-rated VAT export invoicing record as required under STA electronic invoice rules

China's electronic invoicing regime is governed by the Golden Tax Project Phase IV and the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoice (FDEI) system. VAT special invoices and ordinary invoices are issued through the National Unified Electronic Invoice Service Platform, either via the web portal or through approved API-based integration, and are uploaded to the STA in near real time. Each FDEI carries a legally binding electronic signature, a unique invoice code and number, and is subject to STA's automatic anti-fraud verification and post-issuance fiscal clearance reconciliation against VAT filings and Customs export records. Paper invoices have been substantially replaced; no separate decal or digital stamp is required beyond the FDEI's cryptographic electronic mark.

If STA or China Customs determines that export zero-rating is unsupported or fraudulent, the output VAT will be restored at the statutory 13% rate, the refunded input VAT will be clawed back in full, plus daily late-payment surcharge (0.05% per day) and an administrative fine ranging from 50% to 500% of the tax refund under the PRC Tax Administration Law. Fraudulent export tax rebate cases may additionally trigger Customs penalties, revocation of export tax rebate and customs registration qualification, and criminal prosecution under the PRC Criminal Law for fraudulent export tax rebate, carrying fines and imprisonment up to life imprisonment in serious circumstances.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with effective convertibility of the renminbi (CNY) on current-account items, while capital-account transactions remain strictly licensed and supervised by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE). A WFOE is treated as a foreign-invested enterprise and may open an FDI foreign-exchange capital account after completing FDI registration. Injected capital received in foreign currency can be settled into RMB for legitimate operating purposes listed on the negative-list and under the capital-funding account use rules, but the settled funds cannot be used for non-operating financial investments, real-estate speculation outside the permitted scope, or on-lending. The current-account profit remittance route permits distribution of after-tax profits subject to a statutory audit, tax filing and clearance, and payment through a designated bank with a profits remittance declaration. The practical hurdles include the need to evidence the underlying trade for every goods receipt and payment, the opaque but disciplined reporting of receipt/payment dates in the trade monitoring system, and the inability to freely hold and move overseas proceeds outside the banking channel. Dividend remittances from Taiwan-invested WFOEs also require verification of the investor's identity and source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under SAFE's trade-in-goods foreign exchange administration, export earnings must be repatriated to a domestic current-account foreign-exchange account at a designated foreign-exchange bank. The standard statutory collection window is the export customs declaration date plus 90 days; if the receipt is not collected within 90 days, the WFOE must file a delayed-collection report in the SAFE Trade Monitoring System within 30 days after the due date. No fixed mandatory conversion ratio exists, so export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency for eligible current-account outflows or converted into CNY at the bank's offering rate.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC), with delegated administration by the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Viability is low. Fintech EMIs (e.g., global borderless banking providers and digital money issuers) are not licensed by PBOC as designated foreign-exchange banks, and they cannot legally open a PRC capital account or perform FDI SAFE registration on behalf of a WFOE. They can only supply ancillary payment cards or e-wallets for already-settled transactions where the underlying funds flow through a licensed domestic bank, and the legal responsibility remains with the WFOE. Therefore, the treasury should rely exclusively on tier-1 licensed banks for all cross-border access and FX flows.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan-based investor shall remit the paid-in capital via a standard SWIFT MT103 from its Taiwan bank to the WFOE's FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account at the chosen tier-1 bank. The remittance must specify the SAFE foreign-exchange purpose code for foreign-invested enterprise capital contribution (e.g., 622011), refer to the FDI Foreign Exchange Registration Certificate and the WFOE's Unified Social Credit Code, and be accompanied by the underlying board resolution and investment agreement. After receipt, the receiving bank will verify the funds against the SAFE registration, complete settlement into CNY if needed, and, where requested, provide a proof-of-capital receipt. A Chinese CPA capital verification report should be prepared for the paid-in capital component when the foreign-invested enterprise law has been complied with conventional banking practice. If the injection is denominated in RMB, it must flow through a cross-border RMB clearing channel (CIPS or designated agent bank) using the same FDI registration and purpose code.For outbound inventory payments and supplier settlements, the preferred rails are SWIFT MT103 direct wire for open-account obligations, Commercial Letters of Credit (L/C at sight) for the smallest counterparty risk, and Documentary Collections (D/P, D/A) for repeat and well-rated vendors. Each disbursement must be matched to a corresponding trade document set: invoice, packing list, transport document, customs declaration, and, where credit is available, the correct BOP transaction code for goods import/export. Cross-border RMB payments to qualified suppliers may be cleared through CIPS, but all forex payments must be executed through the designated bank and recorded in the SAFE trade monitoring system.Onshore USD/CNY deliverable FX forwards with genuine current-account trade exposure and SAFE/PBOC dealer bank documentation; speculative forwards without underlying trade are prohibited.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps to hedge foreign-currency debt or matching long-term inventory receivables, provided the exposure is verifiable and the swap is concluded with a designated bank and reported to SAFE.Offshore NDFs are not permitted onshore; the WFOE should not transact non-deliverable forwards offshore to hedge CNY, as SAFE does not recognize them for corporate treasury settlements.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

20% territorial net-income tax (Article 5 Income Tax Act), plus a 5% retained-earnings surtax on undistributed annual profits; low-rate AMT of 12% applies where the regular effective rate falls below 12%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

21% on dividends distributed to non-resident corporate shareholders (including the PRC parent); resident Taiwanese corporate shareholders are tax-exempt; no imputation credit system remains

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory when cross-border related-party purchases/sales of goods reach NT$100 million annually or other related-party transacts (service fees, royalties, interest) reach NT$50 million; Master File mandatory when annual revenue exceeds NT$500 million and cross-border related-party volumes reach NT$100 million; declaration of all related-party transactions is due with the CIT return and full documentation must be produced within 30 days of the tax bureau's request

Economic substance scope: Taiwan applies the substance-over-form doctrine and GAAR under Article 12-1 of the Tax Collection Act: a buy-sell distributor that holds title only on paper, carries no inventory risk, has no Taiwan warehouse, and executes no real procurement or logistic functions will be re-characterised as a mere conduit; the bureau may then impute a deemed profit margin (normally 10%–15% of wholesaling revenue), reject the intercompany mark-up, and add a 2x–3x penalty

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Fraudulent Understatement of Sales or Inflated COGS

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of up to 5 years (Tax Evasion and Tax Collection Act Article 41), detention and/or fine up to NT$60,000; civil assessment of the evaded tax plus interest and an administrative fine equal to 2x (negligence) or 3x (intentional concealment) of the underpaid tax; denial of any loss carry-forward for the fraudulent year

Director personal liability: The statutory representative (chairperson/general manager) is named in criminal summons as the responsible person; tax arrears of the WFOE exceeding NT$1 million trigger a cross-border travel ban and asset freeze; directors become jointly civilly liable for restitution of the evaded tax plus interest if they signed or directed the false return
Economic Substance Failure / Shell Company Re-characterisation

Statutory sanction: GAAR invocation under Tax Collection Act Article 12-1: re-characterisation of the transaction, deemed-profit method applied to the wholesale margin (10%–15% of gross invoices), additional tax assessed on the full difference, plus a 2x–3x administrative penalty; registrable deregistration of the WFOE by the Ministry of Economic Affairs

Director personal liability: Directors/statutory representatives are personally sued for recovery of the re-assessed tax and penalties on the grounds of appointment as legal representative; travel bans and restrictions on leaving Taiwan are imposed until settlement; a finding of sham structure is reported to Taiwan's FIU and may trigger parallel money-laundering prosecution
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Violation of customer due diligence and suspicious transaction reporting duties under the Money Laundering Control Act: administrative fine up to NT$5 million and criminal sanction of imprisonment up to 2 years for deliberate non-reporting; trade-based money laundering is predicate to an offence punishable by imprisonment up to 7 years and fine up to NT$50 million for the enterprise

Director personal liability: Directors who authorise or recklessly ignore red-flag wire flows face criminal liability as accomplices; corporate forfeiture of the laundered funds; personal travel bans, restraint orders, and extradition risks under cross-strait mutual legal assistance protocols; bank credit lines are immediately revoked and the directors become personally guarantors for the outstanding exposure
Transfer Pricing Mispricing and Non-Disclosure of Related-Party Transactions

Statutory sanction: The tax bureau adjusts the profit under the most appropriate arm's-length method (usually TNMM on operating margin); the underpaid tax is assessed with a 2x administrative penalty, monthly 1% delay surcharge, and forfeiture of the favourable deemed-profit option; intentional concealment of the relationship raises the penalty to 3x

Director personal liability: Statutory representatives are personally liable to settle the adjusted tax and penalties before deregistration or dividend distribution abroad; the bureau may petition the court for conservatory seizure of the directors' personal assets and impose travel bans; a final determination that the WFOE is a conduit also exposes the parent PRC entity to joint collection within Taiwan's enforcement scope
