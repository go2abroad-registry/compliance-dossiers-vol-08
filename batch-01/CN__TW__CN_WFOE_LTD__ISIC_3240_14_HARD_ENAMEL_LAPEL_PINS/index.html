Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None - Taiwan Companies Act does not impose a statutory minimum paid-up capital for a general limited liability WFOE. However, the total capital stated in the Articles of Incorporation must be fully subscribed and paid-in before registration. The paid-in capital must be deposited into a segregated capital verification bank escrow account at a Taiwanese bank, and a certified public accountant must issue a capital verification report confirming that the funds are not withdrawn prior to completion of registration unless for permitted early expenditures. Foreign investment approval may impose additional minimum capital conditions for manufacturing activities at the discretion of the Investment Commission.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Taxation Administration, Ministry of Finance (Taiwan) and its regional National Taxation Bureaus; electronic filing and integration services reside at the 'My Tax' portal (https://www.tax.nat.gov.tw) and the e-Invoice Platform (https://www.einvoice.nat.gov.tw) for rapid registration filing, VAT reporting, and export document reconciliation.

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident investor establishing a WFOE (limited liability) in Taiwan must first obtain approval from the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) under the Foreign Investment Act. After approval, the investor submits the company incorporation application to the MOEA Department of Company Registration, together with the articles of incorporation, the foreign exchange remittance proof of paid-in capital, and the investment approval letter. Upon incorporation, the MOEA automatically issues the ten-digit Unified Business Number (UBN / TIN), which is the single tax identification number for corporate income tax, VAT, customs, and export purposes. Immediately before starting commercial operations, the WFOE must file for business (VAT) registration with the local Revenue Service Office of the National Taxation Bureau in its jurisdiction under Article 6 of the Business Tax Act, using the UBN and supplying business premises, inventory, and accounting system details. The non-resident parent company without a Taiwanese permanent establishment does not require a separate TIN unless it derives Taiwan-sourced income requiring withholding or wishes to claim a tax treaty benefit, in which case it may request a Non-Resident Tax Certificate from the local National Taxation Bureau. A foreign expatriate manager involved in daily operations needs to register for an Alien Tax Identification Number through the National Immigration Agency and the local National Taxation Bureau.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

TWD 80,000 average monthly taxable sales of goods (TWD 40,000 for services); when a business is engaged in export sales, VAT registration and zero-rating entitlement are mandatory regardless of the sales volume threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from Taiwan to a foreign destination qualify for the 0% VAT rate under Article 7 of the Business Tax Act. The sale must be made to a foreign buyer, the goods must physically leave Taiwan customs territory, and the Taiwan seller must be the customs declarant or be expressly identified on the export export declaration. For enamel pins merchandising made in Taiwan and sold through Etsy/Direct Pay or Stripe to international consumers, the goods-export route applies: the seller must generate a commercial invoice for export, file a customs export declaration at the port of departure, and retain documentary proof of actual shipment. Qualifying offshore digital services are not part of this physical-product transaction and would require additional evidence of consumption abroad if used; for physical enamel pins, the zero-rating is substantiated solely by customs-verified export and FX collection.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Export Declaration form issued by the Customs Administration, Ministry of Finance, with the barcoded of entry release number)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment from a Taiwanese port to a foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Foreign Exchange Settlement Certificate) issued by a licensed bank confirming receipt of the sales proceeds from the overseas buyer

Taiwan mandates the issuance of Uniform Invoices by all VAT-registered businesses. Under the current New Generation E-Invoicing regime, each VAT-registered business must obtain an official digital certificate and integrate with the Ministry of Finance e-Invoice Platform through an accredited Turnkey/API channel. Invoice data (including the export-specific zero-rated invoice) must be transmitted to the Platform in the prescribed XML format within the statutory filing cycle, and the Platform performs automatic data matching with customs export declarations, logisistics manifests, and foreign exchange remittance records. There is no separate anti-fraud fiscal stamping hardware, but the National Taxation Bureau enforces inter-system real-time cross-checks and issues audit alarms on discrepancies between e-invoice amounts, customs declarations, and bank inward remittances.

The National Taxation Bureau cross-validates every zero-rated export invoice against the Customs Administration's export declaration database and the foreign exchange bank's inward remittance records. If the required customs or FX documents are missing, incomplete, or contradict the e-invoice data, the tax authority will retroactively assess VAT at 5% from the date of the underlying sale, impose daily interest under the Tax Collection Act, and apply a statutory penalty of up to five times the evaded tax under the Tax Evasion and Irregularity Penalty Act. Where forged customs declarations, false shipping documents, or collusive bank certificates are detected, criminal liability under the Customs Act and the Criminal Code applies, all previously claimed export VAT refunds are clawed back with compound interest, and the exporter's zero-rating authorization is revoked prospectively.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China operates a managed floating exchange-rate regime with current-account convertibility on a genuine-transaction basis and strict capital-account controls. The Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise may convert USD, EUR, and RMB through licensed domestic banks for trade-related receipts and payments, but every conversion must be supported by authentic commercial documentation entered into SAFE's trade monitoring system. Capital-account inflows, including equity injections from the Taiwan shareholder, require FDI foreign exchange registration before remittance, and the received foreign capital must be used for the registered business purposes only. Dividends and profits may be repatriated to Taiwan after the WFOE completes annual statutory audits, files and pays corporate income tax, and presents the banks with profit distribution resolutions and tax clearance proofs, while the banks perform current-account verification. The RMB is not freely convertible outside mainland for capital positions, and the Taiwan parent should use USD or other freely convertible currency for capital injections and NDF-style hedging arrangements outside China when necessary. Surplus capital in WFOE settlement accounts cannot be used for offshore securities, speculative real estate, entrusted loans, or unauthorized intercompany lending, and any restricted use must be handled through a separate compliance procedure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under the SAFE trade foreign-exchange administration framework, export proceeds of hard enamel lapel pins and other merchandise must be repatriated to a foreign-exchange settlement account or RMB settlement account at a licensed domestic bank within 180 days from shipment or within the payment terms of the underlying sales contract, commercial invoice, letter of credit, or documentary collection instruction; overdue receivables beyond the agreed trade period must be reported to the bank. There is currently no mandatory fixed ratio requiring conversion of export foreign currency into RMB; the WFOE may choose to settle into RMB or retain the foreign currency within its approved current-account balance, provided the bank validates the transaction using the trade verification code. The retention period for unrealized export receivables is monitored against export customs declarations and logistics documents, and if the proceeds are retained abroad for a valid commercial reason, a SAFE filing must be made; no unauthorized surrender or delayed repatriation is permitted.

Central bank supervision: State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE) under the People's Bank of China (PBOC), acting through the local SAFE branch where the China WFOE is registered.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital banks and electronic money institutions are not legally authorized to open corporate treasury accounts or perform RMB clearing for a mainland China WFOE; therefore their feasibility as the WFOE's primary banking venue is LOW to NIL. Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe can function only as payment aggregators for collecting overseas retail receipts; the underlying settlement must ultimately be remitted by licensed acquiring/payment institutions into a bank account held by the WFOE at a SAFE-regulated Chinese bank. The WFOE must keep the platform settlement reports, transaction-level CSV files, refunds, and chargeback data to support trade authenticity; otherwise the bank may freeze the incoming remittance. Any arrangement using a foreign EMI to maintain liquidity offshore or pre-fund overseas disbursements on behalf of the WFOE would breach SAFE retention rules and should be rejected by the compliance committee.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The Taiwan parent must first obtain approval for mainland investment and then complete the FDI Foreign Exchange Registration with the local SAFE / delegated bank using the WFOE's business license and the investment approval document. After registration, the bank opens an FDI Foreign Exchange Capital Account; the Taiwan parent wires USD or another freely convertible currency by SWIFT MT103 with the SAFE capital account purpose code for foreign-invested enterprise equity capital contribution or capital increase. The bank credits the capital account and applies the proceeds under the FDI registration scope; the WFOE can then settle into RMB for operating expenses. A Chinese certified public accountant prepares the capital verification report and the Receipt and Payment Confirmation for FDI Foreign Exchange Capital is filed; the legal representative's identity, UBO dossier, and source of funds are re-verified at each drawdown.For procurement of raw materials, molds, and packaging materials, the WFOE uses SWIFT MT103 for direct payments to suppliers when payment terms are open account; Commercial Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700/MT707) are used for high-value mould-making or large batch orders with trusted overseas suppliers; and Documentary Collections at sight or term are used for lower-risk intermediate deliveries. All outbound payments must be matched by import customs declarations, purchase contracts, and warehouse inbound records, and banks apply the domestic trade verification code to meet SAFE's anti-money-laundering and trade-finance reconciliation requirements. For retail sales, Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe remit gross proceeds through licensed payment gateways to the WFOE's designated bank account, and the bank verifies the platform settlement as export current-account receipts.Onshore plain-vanilla RMB/USD forward contracts for genuine trade receivables and trade payables, with maturity based on the underlying sales/procurement contract and within the bank's credit line.Onshore cross-currency swaps (RMB/USD or RMB/EUR) that strictly match the timing and amount of domestic operating cash flows and inbound FDI settlement, approved by the bank as real-demand hedging.Fee-based exchange rate options for current account hedging, subject to SAFE real-demand verification and bank suitability assessment.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) involving the China WFOE as a direct counterparty are not permissible in the onshore market; the Taiwan parent may hedge RMB portfolio exposure outside mainland with NDFs only to the extent permitted by Taiwan regulations, but such instruments must not be booked in the WFOE.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore digital banks and electronic money institutions are not legally authorized to open corporate treasury accounts or perform RMB clearing for a mainland China WFOE; therefore their feasibility as the WFOE's primary banking venue is LOW to NIL. Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe can function only as payment aggregators for collecting overseas retail receipts; the underlying settlement must ultimately be remitted by licensed acquiring/payment institutions into a bank account held by the WFOE at a SAFE-regulated Chinese bank. The WFOE must keep the platform settlement reports, transaction-level CSV files, refunds, and chargeback data to support trade authenticity; otherwise the bank may freeze the incoming remittance. Any arrangement using a foreign EMI to maintain liquidity offshore or pre-fund overseas disbursements on behalf of the WFOE would breach SAFE retention rules and should be rejected by the compliance committee.
