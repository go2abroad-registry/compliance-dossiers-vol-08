Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration of the People's Republic of China (国家税务总局, STA); operational filings executed through the unified China Electronic Tax Bureau (电子税务局, etax.chinatax.gov.cn) under the local competent national tax service bureau at the WFOE's registered address; medical-device export licensing coordinated with the National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) and its provincial bureaus.

E-government filing portal

For a newly incorporated WFOE: (1) obtain the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (统一社会信用代码, USCC) on the business license issued by the Administration for Market Regulation; the USCC legally serves as the TIN for tax, customs, banking, and social-insurance purposes. (2) Complete mandatory tax registration within 30 days from business-license issuance through the e-Tax Bureau portal, filing registered address, legal representative real-name verification, bank account filing, and tax categories (CIT, VAT, stamp duty, urban-maintenance surtax, education surcharge, and withholding tax on non-resident payments). (3) Apply for general VAT taxpayer status; a manufacturer-exporter of medical devices normally elects general status to unlock zero-rated export refund mechanics. (4) Activate digital-invoice issuance credentials under the STA Golden Tax 'fully digital e-invoice' (全电发票) platform. (5) Complete export-tax-rebate registration (出口退(免)税备案) with the competent tax bureau and customs registration in the China International Trade Single Window.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — A PRC-incorporated WFOE is a Chinese tax resident under Enterprise Income Tax Law Article 2 and is taxed at entity level at the statutory 25% CIT rate on worldwide income; no pass-through or check-the-box transparency exists for LLC-type WFOEs. Cross-strait investment is protected under the Law on the Protection of Investment by Taiwanese Compatriots, but the WFOE remains a PRC resident enterprise in all mainland tax filings. Distributions to the Taiwan corporate shareholder are subject to 10% final dividend withholding (no formal cross-strait double-tax agreement, so no treaty-rate reduction); foreign tax credits are granted unilaterally for qualified foreign-source income.

Available election forms

China operates no check-the-box regime. Available elections include: (i) small-scale (3% statutory, temporarily reduced to 1% through 2027) versus general VAT taxpayer status (13%/9%/6%), with mandatory general registration above RMB 5 million annual sales; (ii) export-rebate method election: 'exemption-credit-refund' (免抵退) for manufacturers versus 'exemption-refund' (免退税) for trading agents; (iii) High-and-New-Technology Enterprise status at a 15% reduced CIT rate, with R&D-to-sales ratios of 5% (sales ≤ RMB 50 million), 4% (RMB 50–200 million), or 3% (above RMB 200 million) and at least 60% high-tech revenue concentration; (iv) accelerated depreciation for fixed assets under Cai Shui 2018 No. 54; (v) no hybrid-mismatch or CFC deferral election: PRC CFC attribution under EIT Law Article 45 applies to passive income parked in Taiwan-owned offshore vehicles, and thin-capitalization restricts related-party debt to a 2:1 debt-to-equity ratio; (vi) 10% withholding on outbound dividend/service remittances, verified against the applicable DTA (none available for Taiwan).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

RMB 5,000,000 annual taxable sales: a WFOE whose forecast or actual annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5 million must register as a general VAT taxpayer at the 13% goods rate; turnover at or below RMB 5 million may elect small-scale taxpayer status (simplified 1% levy through 2027). A manufacturing WFOE engaged in export zero-rating must adopt general taxpayer status because small-scale taxpayers cannot claim input-VAT refunds on exports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Physical export of domestically manufactured color-Doppler ultrasound consoles (HS 9018.12) qualifies for zero-rating under the VAT Export Rebate rules (Cai Shui 2012 No. 39 and Interim VAT Regulations Article 15): output VAT at 0% and full refund of the input VAT attributable to the export, applied at the 13% rebate rate (a full rebate for this medical-device category). Conditions: the exporter is a general VAT taxpayer; the goods actually depart PRC customs territory; the WFOE holds a valid NMPA medical-device production registration for the console; sales proceeds are received through a designated foreign-exchange bank within the statutory collection period; and electronic matching among the customs declaration, VAT invoice, and FX settlement is verifiable. Offshore installation, training, and remote-diagnostic services supplied to a non-PRC buyer qualify for zero-rating only where the service is not consumed within China and the recipient is outside the PRC (Cai Shui 2016 No. 36).

Golden Tax Phase IV (金税四期): every VAT special invoice issued by the WFOE must be created in real time on the STA digital invoice platform, carry the taxpayer's digital certificate and the tax authority's electronic stamp/QR code, and be transmitted to the national database in the same session; invoice numbers are centrally allocated, and manual off-platform issuance is effectively abolished for general taxpayers. Export customs clearance is fully paperless through the China International Trade Single Window (中国国际贸易单一窗口) with real-time electronic matching of export declarations, VAT invoices, and foreign-exchange receipts. The zero-rated export VAT return and export-rebate application are filed electronically as an integrated schedule of the general VAT return, with auto-verification by the rebate risk-control system.

The STA export-rebate risk-control engine continuously matches customs declarations, VAT invoices, logistics bills, and SAFE foreign-exchange receipts; anomalies trigger written verification (函调) to the supplier's tax bureau and on-site inspections of production capacity and cost structure. A false or fraudulent export zero-rating claim causes: (i) mandatory repayment of the refunded VAT with a daily late-payment surcharge of 0.05%; (ii) an administrative penalty of 50% to 500% of the falsely refunded tax under Article 63 of the Law on the Administration of Tax Collection; (iii) downgrade of the taxpayer credit rating to Level D and suspension of export tax-rebate privileges; and (iv) criminal prosecution under Article 204 of the Criminal Law for fraud in export tax refunds (骗取出口退税罪), carrying imprisonment up to five years for basic fraud, up to ten years for 'huge' amounts, and life imprisonment for 'especially huge' amounts, together with forfeiture of the illegal proceeds and monetary fines.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current-account transactions (China accepted IMF Article VIII obligations in 1996), so China WFOE LTD may remit dividends, royalties, and trade settlements with bank-based verification rather than SAFE pre-approval. Capital-account convertibility remains a managed, negative-list regime: inbound FDI equity injections require a one-time SAFE FDI registration executed at the accepting bank, while outbound capital movements (intercompany loans, guarantees, offshore M&A) sit on an approval/negative-list track. Profit remittance hurdles: (1) distributable profits must exist under the PRC GAAP audited balance sheet; (2) a board/owners' profit-distribution resolution must be passed; (3) corporate income tax and the 10% dividend withholding tax must be settled - reducible to 5% under the Cross-Strait Agreement on Avoidance of Double Taxation where the Taiwan shareholder holds at least 10% equity and passes beneficial-ownership/substance verification; and (4) the bank must verify the annual Foreign Investment Information Report and the negative-list position of the business. The manufacturing of Color Doppler Ultrasound Console equipment (ISIC 3250 / NAICS 334510) is outside the negative list and eligible for encouraged-industry treatment, so no industry-level convertibility prohibition applies. Dividend conversion from RMB into USD is executed at the receiving bank against the audited financial statements, board resolution, tax payment certificates, and the BOP transaction code 322033 (profit repatriation), ordinarily within one to two business days for Class-A enterprises.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The historic fixed-percentage mandatory surrender of export proceeds was abolished by SAFE Circular HuiFa [2012] No. 38. There is therefore no statutory conversion ratio: China WFOE LTD may retain 100% of export receipts in foreign currency within its current-account FX account, or settle them voluntarily into RMB (意愿结汇), including into a RMB settlement-pending account. The binding duties are: (a) all export receipts must enter an SAFE-registered current-account FX account; (b) receipts must reconcile with the corresponding customs declaration (报关单), bill of lading, and invoice under the total-amount verification (总量核查) methodology; (c) if collection occurs more than 90 days after the export customs-clearance date, the exporter must file a deferred-collection notification (延期收款报告) in the Trade Monitoring System within the statutory reporting window (typically before the 15th day of the month following expiry of the 90-day period); (d) advance receipts for exports that are more than 30 days before shipment or that exceed the equivalent of USD 300,000 must be reported to the bank; and (e) Class-A trade status, granted by default to newly registered WFOEs without sanctions or prior irregularity, enables streamlined single-transaction or batch verification with no mandatory conversion timeline.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC / 中国人民银行) as the central bank, acting through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE / 国家外汇管理局) as the delegated foreign-exchange regulator. SAFE administers the Trade Monitoring System (货物贸易外汇监测系统), the Foreign Exchange Capital Project Information System, the cross-border receipt/payment reporting framework, and the FDI foreign-exchange registration regime for inbound equity and capital-account flows. For RMB crossing with Taiwan, the PBOC Cross-Border RMB Clearing Arrangement (2012 Cross-Strait Currency Clearing Cooperation Memorandum) applies through the Taipei RMB clearing bank (Bank of China Taipei Branch) plus the CIPS reporting node.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury or capital management; LOW-to-MEDIUM for ancillary export collection only. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currenxie, Payoneer) hold no PBOC banking licence and no SAFE foreign-exchange settlement licence. They cannot legally open a PRC capital-account, cannot hold a RMB settlement-pending account, and cannot issue the CNP-format (人民币跨境收付) payment recording that SAFE accepts for trade verification. A China-domiciled WFOE may use an EMI only as an ancillary collection aggregator within the cross-border e-commerce pilot via a cooperating licensed PRC payment institution/bank, but that channel is incompatible with the SWIFT LC / commercial TT clearing rails specified for this medical-device export business: the LC advising/confirming chain requires SWIFT member PRC banks. Routing capital injections or outbound trade disbursements through an offshore EMI would create third-party payment mismatches under HuiFa [2012] No. 38 and PBOC payment-account rules, risking downgrade of trade classification from Class A to Class B and forced per-transaction documentary examination. Recommended architecture: primary accounts at licensed banks; EMI exposure limited to low-value complementary collections fully reconciled to invoices and customs records through the primary bank's virtual-account integration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Step 1: China WFOE LTD completes SAFE FDI registration through the accepting bank's direct-investment online module, obtaining the FDI code and replacing the former MOFCOM-approval system with the informational Foreign Investment Report. Step 2: The bank opens the PBOC-registered RMB basic deposit account (基本存款账户), then the designated FX capital account (资本金账户) in USD / RMB / TWD, matching the Articles of Association currency denomination. Step 3: The overseas shareholder, with an exact name match to the AMR registration, remits registered capital via SWIFT MT103 (USD) or CIPS/CNP (RMB) using the BOP transaction code 622014 (foreign direct investment - new investment in foreign-invested enterprises); the remitter's bank account must belong to the shareholder. Step 4: Incoming funds are credited to the capital account and matched in SAFE's Foreign Exchange Capital Project Information System; the bank then verifies paid-in equity against the registered capital amount and the board resolution. Step 5: The WFOE exercises voluntary FX settlement (意愿结汇) converting the USD capital into RMB in a ring-fenced settlement-pending account (结汇待支付账户); disbursement is then made on a payment-against-invoice basis or, for compliance-validated enterprises, under the nationwide capital-account income payment facilitation pilot. Step 6: Permitted uses include raw materials, equipment, payroll, rent, R&D, and taxes; prohibited uses are securities investment beyond bank wealth-management product quotas, entrusted loans or RMB-loan repayment outside pilot carve-outs, investment in real estate not for self-use, and investment in negative-list industries. For Taiwan-investor injections denominated in RMB, same-day reporting to RCPMIS (人民币跨境收付信息管理系统) through the accepting bank and, where applicable, notification to the Taipei RMB clearing bank are required.Clearing rails for the CN-WFOE medical-device export trade to Taiwan and third markets: (i) TT/Wire: outbound SWIFT MT103 from the WFOE's current-account FX account with the bank receiving commercial invoice, packing list, customs declaration (报关单), and transport documents; the cross-border payment declaration (对外付款申报单) must carry the BOP transaction code 121010 (general trade). Class-A trade status allows single-transaction electronic verification or a standing streamlined framework under HuiFa [2012] No. 38, enabling intraday execution. (ii) Commercial Letter of Credit: LC issuance via SWIFT MT700 by the PRC issuing bank, advised/confirmed by a Taiwan bank or by Bank of China Taipei Branch for RMB-denominated LCs; proceeds settle under UCP600 over SWIFT (USD/EUR), CIPS/CNAPS (RMB), or the cross-strait RMB clearing arrangement following the 2012 Currency Clearing Cooperation Memorandum. (iii) RMB trade settlement: the onshore bank uses its CIPS network to credit the Taiwan beneficiary's RMB account at a CIPS participant or via the Taipei clearing bank, with the identical documentary file. Every outbound RMB payment is reported by the bank to RCPMIS, and the WFOE must preserve the 'matching party' principle - the beneficiary must be the customs-declared trading counterparty.Onshore deliverable FX forwards (远期结售汇) executed with a licensed PRC bank under SAFE real-demand verification against documented physical export receivables or import payables; speculative and one-sided open positions are prohibited.FX options and option combinations (人民币外汇期权及期权组合), including vanilla calls, vanilla puts, and zero-cost collars, with notional capped at the amount of the hedged trade cash flow and premium payable in RMB.FX swaps (外汇掉期) with an onshore bank for temporary currency and liquidity bridging between the USD receipt date and the RMB payment date in the same trade cycle.Cross-currency swaps (货币掉期) for longer-dated FCY-denominated obligations linked to equipment import settlement or scheduled dividend remittance timelines, provided the SAFE documentation of the underlying current-account exposure is retained.Structuring restriction: offshore NDFs with non-resident counterparties are not permissible for the onshore WFOE; any RMB/USD hedge must be onshore and deliverable, with trade documents retained in the FX derivatives vault. For Taiwan-related invoices, hedge pricing references the onshore CNY (CNY), never the offshore CNH or NDF fixing.
