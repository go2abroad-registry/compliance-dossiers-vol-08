Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required under the Taiwan Company Act. The proposed capital must nevertheless be reasonably correlated with the stated business plan for ISIC 4661 energy commodity trading, and must be supported by an inward remittance certificate from a Taiwan bank and a certified public accountant capital verification report. No blocked escrow account requirement is imposed by the general company registration regime; however, the Investment Commission may require source-of-funds disclosure and may restrict withdrawal or transfer of contributed capital until the approved investment project is confirmed. If the project includes physical fuel storage, the Petroleum Administration Act licensing authority may require additional financial capacity evidence, insurance bonds, or environmental remediation security.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certification issued by the local Fire Bureau certifying automatic alarm and extinguishing systems, fire walls, hazardous material separation, emergency exits, and evacuation plans; structural engineer inspection and load certification for fuel tanks, pressure vessels, and pipe racks; Occupational Safety and Health Administration approval based on workplace hazard assessment, confined-space entry control, personal protective equipment, monitoring equipment, and a major accidental release prevention plan coordinated with civil defense authorities and the local emergency response command center.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

State Taxation Administration (STA) of the People's Republic of China; electronic taxpayer filing through the STA Electronic Tax Bureau (e-Tax) portal

E-government filing portal

The CN WFOE is a PRC resident enterprise, not a non-resident branch. Step 1: incorporate the Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) as a limited liability company with the State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) and obtain the Business License, which includes the 18-digit Unified Social Credit Code (USCC). Step 2: the USCC constitutes the statutory TIN and is automatically synchronized to the STA; however, the entity must complete supplementary tax registration on the e-Tax portal within 30 days of establishment, appoint a tax accountant/representative, and register for VAT and export tax rebate status if exporting. Step 3: no separate TIN certificate is issued under the one-license-one-code system. For a foreign non-resident shareholder or counterparty without a Chinese establishment, the withholding agent files a non-resident tax registration via the e-Tax portal to obtain a non-resident TIN for China-source income. For the Taiwan trade lane, a pure export sale does not require a Taiwan tax ID; a Taiwan permanent establishment or branch would require separate registration with the Taiwan Ministry of Finance tax collection authority.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None. PRC law does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid entity election for a WFOE; the WFOE is mandatorily taxed at entity level as a PRC resident enterprise. For indirect tax, the WFOE may elect small-scale VAT payer treatment if annual taxable sales do not exceed RMB 5,000,000, but must become a general VAT taxpayer once turnover exceeds that threshold. No hybrid mismatch election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

13%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

General VAT taxpayer registration is mandatory once annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000; every WFOE with taxable sales must complete VAT tax registration regardless of turnover, while below the RMB 5,000,000 threshold the WFOE may remain a small-scale VAT taxpayer.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% VAT export zero-rating is available only for marine fuel oil and refined distillates that are physically exported from mainland China customs territory, with title passing to a non-mainland buyer and export customs clearance evidenced by the customs release. Bunker fuel supplied to international voyages under customs-supervised bonded procedures may qualify as an export when actually shipped to an outbound vessel. The WFOE must be a general VAT taxpayer to obtain the export rebate; small-scale taxpayers receive exemption without rebate. Pure brokerage services without physical export do not qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Goods Declaration for Exportation) bearing customs release stamp
  • Bill of Lading or seaworthy waybill for the exported crude oil/refined products
  • Commercial invoice and export contract
  • Bank receipt or Inward Remittance Certificate (FIRC) evidencing foreign-currency proceeds received from the overseas buyer
  • Export tax rebate application filed through the STA e-Tax export rebate module
  • Certificate of origin or inspection certificate required by the Taiwan buyer, if applicable

The WFOE must issue invoices through the STA tax control system and the Fully Digitalized Electronic Invoices (FD-E-Invoice) platform; invoice data is transmitted to the STA in near-real time under the Golden Tax Phase IV system, with API-based issuance for system-to-system integration, digital authentication, and anti-fraud digital stamping. Only invoices cleared through the STA platform are valid for input VAT deduction or export rebate claims.

Tax and customs authorities cross-check customs declarations, bank FX receipts, and invoice data. If the zero-rating claim is unsubstantiated, the refunded VAT is clawed back, a late-payment surcharge of 0.05% per day is assessed, and administrative penalties may be imposed under the Law of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Tax Collection. Fraudulent export refund claims of high value can trigger criminal liability, retrospective adjustment across open tax years, and loss of future export rebate eligibility.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is convertible for current-account purposes subject to genuine trade documentation, but full capital-account convertibility is not yet achieved. A China-incorporated WFOE with Taiwanese shareholders may open foreign-currency capital accounts only after SAFE foreign-exchange registration for foreign direct investment is completed at the receiving bank. Once registered, current-account items such as trade settlements, commissions, and profit remittances are generally available against a real transaction, while capital-account items including capital increases, equity transfer proceeds, offshore loans, and cross-currency swaps remain subject to SAFE approval or registration and are not freely available. Export receipts must be repatriated into Chinese banks and cannot be left offshore without SAFE authorization; they may be retained in foreign currency or converted into RMB in whole or in part at the enterprise's discretion. Profit remittance to the Taiwanese parent is possible only after the annual statutory audit, corporate income tax filing and settlement, withholding-tax payment on dividends, and submission of the board resolution, audited financial statements, tax vouchers, and the remitting bank's transaction forms. The central constraint is not an absolute prohibition but the need to demonstrate the economic substance and documentary chain behind each cross-border fund movement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For a PRC-domiciled WFOE in commodity wholesale, all export foreign-currency proceeds must be repatriated into the PRC banking system within the SAFE-prescribed trade-monitoring cycle. The receiving bank must record the receipt in the Trade Monitoring System and match it to the corresponding customs declaration. Unmatched receipts are escalated after 30 days, and receipts remaining unreconciled for 90 days beyond the export date trigger high-risk trade-behavior monitoring unless the WFOE provides a written explanation. There is no universal mandatory conversion percentage; the statutory obligation is repatriation and traceability, not mandatory surrender. The exporter may retain qualifying proceeds in a foreign-currency settlement account or sell them to a licensed bank at its election; for the crude-oil and refined-products trading book, the bank may additionally require an enhanced documentary package, including bill of lading, customs export declaration, cargo inspection certificate, and broker commission statement.

Central bank supervision: The People's Bank of China (PBOC), operating through the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE), is the central monetary and foreign-exchange authority for the PRC-incorporated WFOE; the Taiwanese parent's capital remittances are treated as foreign direct investment into mainland China and require SAFE registration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: Not viable as the WFOE's principal treasury account or FX execution venue. Offshore digital EMIs such as Revolut Business, Airwallex, and Wise are not licensed as domestic deposit-taking institutions by PBOC or CBIRC and cannot maintain a RMB basic deposit account, an FDI capital account, or an external-debt account for a PRC WFOE. These EMIs cannot file SAFE balance-of-payments reports, cannot issue China-standard account-opening documentation packages, and are not participants in the onshore FX clearing system. They may be used only by the Taiwanese parent as an offshore collection vehicle before funds are remitted into the WFOE's domestic licensed bank. For capital calls, margin calls, and physical petroleum settlements into and out of the WFOE, only licensed domestic Chinese banks are permissible clearing rails.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Provide a notarized and apostilled beneficial-ownership declaration, the Taiwanese parent's full shareholder register from the Ministry of Economic Affairs, articles of incorporation, minute book, bank references, passport or national-ID copies of each ultimate natural person, a clear organizational chart to the final natural persons, and, if the parent is listed, the audited annual report and current shareholder registry.
