Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

None. No statutory minimum paid-up capital or blocked escrow account requirement applies to a Wholly Foreign-Owned Enterprise (WFOE) under current PRC law. The registered capital amount and contribution schedule are determined by the shareholder in the Articles of Association. The Taiwan investor may contribute in RMB or eligible foreign currency through approved banking channels, and the contribution must be received by the company before the expiry of the contribution period specified in the Articles of Association. For the proposed dental zirconia block manufacturing activity, the relevant NMPA production license does not impose a minimum capital threshold, but the facility, equipment, and quality system must be commensurate with the declared registered scope.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Protection Design Review and Fire Protection Final Acceptance Certificate or Record Filing for the factory premises must be obtained from the local Fire and Rescue Division before production begins. The civil defense obligation is limited to the construction of any underground or basement space and is confirmed with the local civil defense office. Structural load inspections must be certified by a licensed engineer for areas carrying sintering furnaces, milling machines, and dense zirconia block storage racks. Occupational health requirements include registration of the project with the local Health Commission, workplace monitoring for respirable dust, noise and heat stress under GBZ 2.1-2019 and GBZ 2.2-2007, provision of approved personal protective equipment, pre-employment and periodic medical examinations, and implementation of a safety production management system under the Work Safety Law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Mainland China is not a jurisdiction with full capital-account convertibility. The current account is generally convertible for genuine trade, services, and dividend remittances when the underlying transaction is verified by a licensed bank; the capital account is subject to case-by-case SAFE registration or approval. A Taiwan-owned WFOE must first complete FDI foreign exchange registration at the appointed bank, open a foreign exchange capital account, and only then receive equity funds from its Taiwanese shareholder. The RMB conversion of the capital injection is legal only for the WFOE's own operational purposes supported by invoices and business contracts. Export proceeds are no longer subject to mandatory surrender; however, they must be repatriated to China within the prescribed time period and may be retained in a foreign-currency settlement account or converted to RMB voluntarily. Profit remittance to the Taiwanese parent is permitted after the WFOE has paid or externally withheld corporate income tax and other taxes; the remitting bank will require the audited financial statements, tax payment certificates, the foreign exchange registration code, and a clear board resolution declaring dividends. Liquidation proceeds remittance requires SAFE deregistration and tax clearance. These controls make bank selection and documentation discipline critical.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Goods exports: proceeds must be repatriated to mainland China within 90 days from the date of customs export declaration, or within the commercial settlement period for open-account trade, in no case exceeding 90 days unless a deferred collection registration is made with SAFE's Trade Monitoring System. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0 percent; there is currently no fixed percentage of export proceeds that must be converted into RMB. Enterprises may retain 100 percent of foreign exchange in an export proceeds account or sell it to a designated bank at their discretion. Trade-in-services and cross-border e-commerce receipts follow separate SAFE codes but are also subject to repatriation to the domestic entity's account.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (PBOC) and State Administration of Foreign Exchange (SAFE)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility: Low. Under current PRC rules, a WFOE must use a bank authorized by SAFE/PBOC to receive capital injections, settle FX, pay import duties, and collect export proceeds. Offshore digital EMI licensees such as virtual banks and e-money institutions do not hold a mainland banking licence and cannot maintain CNY basic deposits or provide SAFE-compliant FX current and capital account clearing. They may not obtain the foreign exchange registration code or manage FX purpose-of-payment codes. Their legal position is limited to serving as an ancillary payment wallet for small cross-border e-commerce where the money is ultimately routed through a licensed domestic third-party payment institution; such routing is fragile and may create tax invoice mismatches. It is therefore not suitable as a primary clearing rail for a dental manufacturing WFOE. Use domestic tier-1 banks as the only fully compliant clearing rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injection rail for the Taiwan-owned WFOE: (1) Complete FDI foreign exchange registration through an SAFE-designated bank and obtain the FDI registration code. (2) Open a Foreign Exchange Capital Account in the WFOE's name at the same bank. (3) The Taiwanese shareholder remits the equity subscription via SWIFT MT103, with field 70 containing the FDI registration code and the purpose marked as foreign equity investment. (4) The receiving bank verifies that the remitter matches the registered overseas investor and that the board minutes and WFOE charter authorize the capital contribution. (5) The WFOE presents the bank with actual-burn documentation such as supplier contracts, lease agreements, payroll records, and purchase orders before converting foreign exchange into RMB under SAFE's real-use principle. (6) A Chinese certified public accountant issues the capital verification report after funds are credited and converted, which is then filed with the local Administration for Market Regulation. Large injections above USD 5 million may trigger enhanced due diligence, including source-of-wealth inquiry from the Taiwan investor.Outbound clearing rails for the WFOE's commercial trade include SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for import advance payments and open-account transactions, supported by the import contract, supplier invoice, and where applicable the customs declaration. Commercial letters of credit, MT700 or MT707, are recommended for larger zirconia powder and milling-equipment purchases requiring transport document control. Documentary collections remain a viable low-risk settlement alternative for dental ceramic consumables. For export sales to overseas dental laboratories and distributors, the WFOE receives LC or TT settlements into its export proceeds current account; the bank reconciles each receipt against the corresponding goods customs declaration and SAFE trade monitoring data. Direct RMB clearing with Taiwan counterparties can be executed through Bank of China's Taipei branch or other RMB clearing participants, but the underlying trade must still be documented with SAFE trade registration codes and full invoice evidence. All treasury payments must include the correct purpose-of-payment code and be supported by complete order-to-cash documentation.Onshore deliverable FX forward contracts with a SAFE-licensed Chinese bank, backed by actual trade invoices and committed working-capital or trade-cycle exposures; speculative forwards are prohibited.FX swaps for short-term liquidity matching between import payments and export receipts, used strictly to convert and re-convert RMB and foreign currency cash flows arising from genuine trade.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) permitted only where the WFOE has an underlying foreign-currency loan or debt instrument with matched notional and maturity.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for the onshore WFOE as a direct hedging instrument; the Taiwan parent may use offshore NDFs only to hedge its own CNY economic exposure outside mainland China, not to create a de facto onshore NDF market channel.

Offshore digital EMI licensees such as virtual banks and e-money institutions do not hold a mainland banking licence and cannot maintain CNY basic deposits or provide SAFE-compliant FX current and capital account clearing. They may not obtain the foreign exchange registration code or manage FX purpose-of-payment codes. Their legal position is limited to serving as an ancillary payment wallet for small cross-border e-commerce where the money is ultimately routed through a licensed domestic third-party payment institution; such routing is fragile and may create tax invoice mismatches. It is therefore not suitable as a primary clearing rail for a dental manufacturing WFOE. Use domestic tier-1 banks as the only fully compliant clearing rails.
