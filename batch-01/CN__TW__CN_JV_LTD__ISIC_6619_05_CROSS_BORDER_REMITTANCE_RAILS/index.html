Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. CN_JV_LTD is a limited liability resident enterprise for PRC Corporate Income Tax (CIT) purposes, taxed on worldwide income at the statutory rate of 25%. It is not a pass-through entity and does not qualify for flow-through or transparent treatment under PRC tax law. Non-resident branches or Taiwan-source income would be brought into the CIT net through the ordinary resident-enterprise framework, subject to bilateral treaty and anti-deferral rules.

Available election forms

PRC law does not provide a US-style check-the-box or general entity classification election; tax classification is statutorily determined. Available elections include: (1) the VAT general taxpayer election, with compulsory general taxpayer status where annual taxable turnover exceeds RMB 5,000,000; (2) for qualifying cross-border taxable services, the election between VAT zero-rating with input credit and VAT exemption without input credit; (3) CIT preferential elections, such as the 15% reduced rate for qualifying Advanced Technology or Advanced Technology Service Enterprises; and (4) fixed-ratio or actual-flow methodologies under transfer-pricing documentation. No hybrid mismatch election exists; PRC anti-hybrid rules in CIT Law Article 46 and the Special Tax Adjustments regime apply.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

6%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold for VAT identity; every taxpayer must register on establishment. Compulsory general VAT taxpayer registration applies where annual taxable sales exceed RMB 5,000,000. Small-scale taxpayers may elect general taxpayer status, and cross-border zero-rating claims ordinarily require general taxpayer status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under CaiShui [2016] 36 and SAT Notice 2016 No. 29, zero VAT rating is available for cross-border taxable services supplied by a PRC VAT general taxpayer where: (1) the service is provided to an overseas entity or individual; (2) the service is consumed entirely outside the PRC; (3) the consideration is received from overseas and settled in foreign currency or through offshore RMB accounts; and (4) the taxpayer maintains contemporaneous evidence of overseas consumption. For remittance services, zero-rating applies only where the service recipient is outside the PRC and the payment transmission service is wholly performed outside China. If any local cash-pickup or domestic settlement leg occurs in China, that portion is generally treated as domestic VATable service at 6% rather than zero-rated.

  • PRC Customs Export Declaration (出口报关单) — required where tangible ancillary goods are exported; not applicable to pure remittance services
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill — required for any associated cargo movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Receipt Voucher (收汇核销单) issued by the settlement bank
  • Cross-Border Service Trade Contract and ICA/EFT evidence identifying the overseas service recipient and scope of services
  • VAT zero-rated export service filing and proof of overseas consumption, including the foreign entity's certificate of incorporation or tax registration
  • Foreign exchange declaration in the SAFE trade-in-services monitoring system

All VAT invoices, including zero-rated export service invoices, must be issued through the STA electronic invoice platform integrated with the Golden Tax Phase IV system. Fully digitalized electronic invoices (全电发票) are generated with a unique blockchain-backed invoice code, transmitted in real time to the e-Tax platform, and cleared through automated fiscal authentication. No physical tax stamp is required; issuance is legally effective only after digital clearance, and API-based line-item matching reconciles invoice data against VAT returns and cross-border payment evidence.

Improper or fraudulent zero-rating claims are subject to retrospective clawback of the uncollected output VAT, daily surcharges at 0.05% of the arrears, administrative fines of up to five times the tax evaded, and criminal liability under PRC Criminal Law Article 204 for fraudulent export VAT refunds where the amount exceeds RMB 50,000. The STA matches export service contracts, foreign exchange receipt data, and invoice records through Golden Tax Phase IV; late repatriation beyond the 180-day benchmark may trigger denial of zero-rating, with reassessment and clawback of any previously claimed input VAT credit.

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The RMB is fully convertible for current-account items (trade in goods and services, dividends, interest, royalties, and salaries) under the Foreign Exchange Administration Regulations (State Council Order No. 532). Capital-account items - equity paid-in capital, inward shareholder loans, equity transfers, and liquidation proceeds - are only conditionally convertible and require SAFE registration or approval before the receiving bank may credit funds. For this JV, a dividend remittance to the Taiwan shareholder is permitted on the current account without a separate quota provided: (1) the annual financial statements have been audited by an onshore licensed CPA; (2) statutory surplus reserves have been accrued under the Company Law; (3) all corporate income tax, VAT, and dividend withholding tax (10% standard, or a reduced cross-strait treaty rate, generally 5%, where the Taiwan ultimate beneficial-owner certificate is accepted) have been settled; and (4) the outward amount is not disguising capital-account flows such as repayment of intercompany loans or advance repatriation of paid-in capital. Key remaining hurdle: the JV's ISIC 6619 activity is a regulated cross-border payment service; SAFE taxes client-fund flows and the JV's own commission income under separate classifications, and the JV may not treat client float as treasury liquidity. Liquidation and equity repatriation additionally require a capital-account registration and a tax-clearing certificate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no mandatory percentage surrender of export proceeds to the central bank in the PRC; the former surrender requirement has been abolished. However, SAFE still applies the repatriation and verification rules of the goods- and services-trade regimes. The JV's commission income derived from overseas agents and sponsors is treated as service-export proceeds that must be repatriated onshore into a domestic foreign-exchange or RMB settlement account; offshore retention is permitted only under an approved closed-end cash-pool arrangement. SAFE's trade-classification system designates the firm as A/B/C class: an A-class firm settles and verifies receipts without per-transaction limits, whereas B-class and C-class firms must register each collection and may be obliged by the bank to repatriate and settle within 60 days and 30 days, respectively. For the remittance-agency line of business, client funds are not export proceeds at all: PBOC and SAFE require instruction-by-instruction matching of collected RMB against each customer's payout, daily position settlement, and zero proprietary use of client float. The JV's own service-revenue component may be retained in foreign currency or settled into RMB at the JV's discretion, subject to the A/B/C classification and the bank's post-transaction verification window.

Central bank supervision: People's Bank of China (中国人民银行, PBOC); exchange-control administration is delegated to the State Administration of Foreign Exchange (国家外汇管理局, SAFE). CN_JV_LTD is a mainland-China Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture, so the PRC PBOC/SAFE regulatory regime governs all treasury, capital-account, and payment-rail matters. The Taiwan-headquartered shareholder enjoys no exemption from SAFE filing, bank designation, or transaction coding requirements.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: LOW / effectively unavailable for direct onboarding. A foreign digital EMI (including Taiwan-based payment firms, Wise/Airwallex-style platforms, and other non-bank electronic money institutions) cannot independently maintain a RMB settlement account, client-funds account, or SAFE capital account in mainland China unless it has obtained the PBOC non-bank payment institution licence under the Regulation on the Supervision and Administration of Non-bank Payment Institutions (effective May 2024) and SAFE cross-border payment registration. There is no EU/UK-style EMI passporting for a Taiwan or Hong Kong entity in the PRC. Therefore the JV must operate through its own licensed mainland bank accounts, or, at most, use an overseas EMI as a technology-only front-end that routes cleared funds to a mainland-licensed payment institution. Any design in which the offshore EMI holds or commingles settlement funds for PRC-destination customers is a regulatory hardening failure and will be rejected by the clearing bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Injection from the Taiwan parent to the mainland JV is made exclusively by SWIFT MT103 credit to the JV's SAFE-designated capital account (资本金账户). Prior to any wire, the JV must complete the Foreign-Investment Information Report and obtain the bank-side FDI registration reference. The outward instruction from the Taiwan shareholder must state its purpose as 'equity investment - capital contribution to a foreign-invested enterprise' and must carry the BOP direct-investment transaction code published by SAFE for that purpose; the numeric code is displayed by the receiving bank's foreign-exchange system at the point of registration because SAFE updates the coding list in its periodic reporting circulars. Funds must be remitted from the shareholder's own corporate bank account; cash, third-party-funded, or commingled instructions are rejected by the receiving compliance system. After the capital account has been credited, the JV may settle the proceeds into RMB within two business days or retain the foreign currency, subject to the negative-list restrictions. Paid-in capital must be recorded at the historical spot rate, disclosed in the annual information report, and supported by the industrial and commercial receipt voucher and the board resolution. Any subsequent return or withdrawal of equity capital requires a separate SAFE capital-account registration and liquidation/tax clearance.The JV uses tiered clearing rails as follows: (1) SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers for commissions, dividend remittances, and customer payouts to overseas beneficiaries; (2) SWIFT MT202/MT202COV bank-to-bank cover where the beneficiary bank is not directly connected; (3) commercial letters of credit issued under UCP600 through the MT700/MT707 series, funded at maturity by MT103/MT202; (4) for cross-border RMB, the mandatory rail is CIPS (Cross-Border Interbank Payment System); where the counterparty bank is in Taiwan, settlement may alternatively clear through the Taiwan RMB clearing arrangement operated by Bank of China (Taipei branch); (5) for domestic last-mile cash pickup, the JV batched client-payout files over CNAPS/IBPS to the local agent network, with the overseas portion transmitted via SWIFT gpi to the paying agent. Every outbound payment must be accompanied by the underlying contract, invoice, and SAFE-compliant purpose code; dividend and profit outflows additionally require the tax-payment vouchers and the audited financial certificate; trade-related disbursements must be matched in the bank's post-transaction surveillance system against the customs declaration or the service-trade transaction certificate within the prescribed verification window.Deliverable CNY forward (远期结售汇) - permitted only for genuine current-account or capital-account exposure and executed with the designated FX bank under SAFE's real-demand principleCross-currency interest-rate swaps (货币掉期) - permitted to hedge long-term USD/TWD/RMB debt and investment cash flowsFX swaps (外汇掉期) - permitted for short-term liquidity and rollover managementPlain-vanilla FX options (汇率期权) - permitted for hedging purposes, generally on a bought-option basis; sold-option or structured strategies require documented hedging rationaleOffshore RMB deliverable forwards (CNH forwards) - not permitted as onshore instruments for the JV; onshore RMB non-deliverable-forward (NDF) trades are prohibited, so any CNH hedge must be executed offshore through the Taiwan parent's treasury

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Rating: LOW / effectively unavailable for direct onboarding. A foreign digital EMI (including Taiwan-based payment firms, Wise/Airwallex-style platforms, and other non-bank electronic money institutions) cannot independently maintain a RMB settlement account, client-funds account, or SAFE capital account in mainland China unless it has obtained the PBOC non-bank payment institution licence under the Regulation on the Supervision and Administration of Non-bank Payment Institutions (effective May 2024) and SAFE cross-border payment registration. There is no EU/UK-style EMI passporting for a Taiwan or Hong Kong entity in the PRC. Therefore the JV must operate through its own licensed mainland bank accounts, or, at most, use an overseas EMI as a technology-only front-end that routes cleared funds to a mainland-licensed payment institution. Any design in which the offshore EMI holds or commingles settlement funds for PRC-destination customers is a regulatory hardening failure and will be rejected by the clearing bank.
