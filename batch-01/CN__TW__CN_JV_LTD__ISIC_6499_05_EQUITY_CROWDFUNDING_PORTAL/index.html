Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Taiwan generally imposes no universal statutory minimum paid-in capital for a limited company. Capital must be sufficient for the proposed business and must be fully subscribed and paid before registration, with a certified capital verification report from a Taiwan-certified public accountant. Taiwan does not generally require a statutory incorporation escrow account; funds should be deposited into a company formation account or other traceable account and remain available for company operations. Foreign-invested capital must be remitted through an approved bank after Foreign Investment Commission approval. A regulated securities or crowdfunding operator may be subject to substantially higher capital, net-worth, and liquidity requirements under securities regulations.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Ministry of Finance, Taxation Administration (財政部賦稅署) via the regional National Taxation Bureaus (國稅局) and the MOF Integrated Tax Services Portal (財政部稅務入口網).

E-government filing portal

A Sino-Foreign Equity Joint Venture must first obtain a company registration certificate from the Ministry of Economic Affairs. Upon incorporation, the National Taxation Bureau automatically assigns a Unified Business Number (統一編號) that serves as the statutory tax ID (TIN/VAT ID). The foreign investor or its Taiwan licensed representative must file Profit-Seeking Enterprise Income Tax registration and Business Tax (VAT) registration on the Integrated Tax Services Portal within 15 days of incorporation. Supporting documents include the approval certificate, MOEA registration, company charter, foreign-investor capital remittance certificate, and a local tax representative appointment letter. A business license is issued once the tax registration is completed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope_Resident_Worldwide; Non_Resident_Taiwan_Source; No_Check_The_Box_Pass_Through

Available election forms

Taiwan does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election for limited companies. A JV is taxed as a resident profit-seeking enterprise on worldwide income. Available statutory elections include (i) group consolidated return filing for qualifying 100% domestic subsidiaries, (ii) foreign tax credit election under Article 3 of the Income Tax Act, (iii) expense recognition and depreciation/amortization methods, (iv) profit-seeking enterprise unappropriated earnings allocation election, and (v) application of the Hybrid Mismatch Arrangement rules as announced by the Ministry of Finance for cross-border payments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

NTD 80,000 monthly turnover for goods; NTD 40,000 monthly turnover for services. Once the JV invoices or has a business establishment, registration is mandatory regardless of threshold. Foreign e-service providers supplying digital services to Taiwan individuals are required to register if annual turnover exceeds NTD 4,000,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The JV may claim 0% VAT on export of services, provided the equity crowdfunding portal services are rendered to foreign clients (non-Taiwan resident enterprises or individuals) and the relevant contract confirms the service is not for Taiwan domestic use. The service fee must be paid in foreign currency and the transaction must have actual cross-border economic substance, evidenced by a written contract, offshore financing agreements, and the JV's internal records showing the foreign target company and foreign investors. The 0% rate applies to the service fee portion, not to any ancillary domestic revenue.

  • Customs Export Declaration (出口報單) or Customs Commodity Export Declaration for services if applicable
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) for goods, or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (銀行外匯收支或交易申報書)
  • Zero-Rated VAT Invoice (零稅率統一發票)
  • Foreign service contract executed between the JV and its overseas principal
  • Evidence of the actual delivery and completion of cross-border crowdfunding services

Taiwan mandates electronic invoice (電子發票) issuance for designated industries and businesses with high turnover. The portal must connect to the MOF E-Invoice Cloud Platform (電子發票整合服務平台) using a Turnkey system (AI-Generated/API-based) and transmit invoice data within two working days of issuance. Each invoice is assigned a unique invoice number and a digital stamp, with a real-time query and encrypted XML data structure. For the equity crowdfunding portal, transaction records and fee invoices must also be submitted to the Ministry of Economic Affairs for crowdfunding-specific monitoring; failure to transmit within the statutory period results in a compliance penalty.

The tax authority scrutinises export-zero-rating claims for sham contracts and round-tripping arrangements. If a claim is disallowed, the JV must repay the uncollected VAT plus interest calculated from the date of the original filing. Under Article 41 of the Tax Evasion and Penalty Act, a fraudulent zero-rating claim carries a penalty of up to 5 times the amount of the evaded tax, and criminal liability (imprisonment of up to 7 years) may be pursued. The Ministry of Finance also retroactively reviews export earnings for the prior 7 years, and non-repatriation of foreign exchange within 180 days will cause the zero-rating to be revoked automatically, restoring 5% VAT output liability and resulting in late-payment surcharges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The New Taiwan Dollar (TWD) is managed under a managed float administered by the CBC. Current-account transactions (trade in goods, services, and remittance of dividends and profits) are fully convertible and are processed through authorized foreign-exchange banks subject to a declaration threshold of TWD 500,000 per transaction; amounts above the threshold require a specific Foreign Exchange Receipts and Payments Declaration form (外匯收支或交易申報書) with supporting documentation. Capital-account transactions—including inward direct investment, shareholder loans, and any equity-linked remittance—require prior approval from the Investment Commission of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA Investment Commission) under the Statute for Investment by Foreign Nationals (外國人投資條例), followed by registration and reporting through the authorized bank to the CBC. Profit remittance by a foreign-invested enterprise is lawful only after the submission of audited financial statements, tax clearance certificates, and board resolutions approving dividend distribution; however, where the beneficial owner is a mainland China entity or resident, the remittance is additionally subject to the restrictions contained in the Act Governing Relations between the People of the Taiwan Area and the Mainland Area (APR). Equity crowdfunding proceeds collected on behalf of issuers are not corporate assets of the JV portal and are therefore not freely convertible; such funds must be held in a segregated client escrow trust account at a licensed Taiwanese bank, with no FX conversion permitted absent a specific FSC/TPEx directive for disbursement in TWD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Taiwan has abolished mandatory surrender of export proceeds. Under the Regulations Governing the Declaration of Foreign Exchange Earnings and Expenditures (外匯收支或交易申報辦法), an exporter must (i) collect the full value of export proceeds within 180 days from the date of the export customs declaration unless a written extension is granted by the CBC or the relevant authorized bank; (ii) file a Foreign Exchange Earnings Declaration (出口外匯申報) through the collecting bank for each receipt exceeding TWD 500,000 or its USD equivalent; (iii) may retain proceeds in a Foreign Currency Deposit Account (FDA) at an authorized bank or freely convert to TWD at the prevailing market rate; and (iv) must ensure that the inward remittance advice matches the export declaration number within the customs-bank reconciliation period of 10 working days. Failure to collect within the statutory timeline or to declare constitutes a violation of the Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (管理外匯條例) and may result in administrative fines of up to TWD 3,000,000.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan), also known as the Central Bank of China (Taiwan), 中央銀行 (CBC)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for principal settlement, Medium for ancillary collection. Offshore digital EMI / payment institutions (e.g., UK-FCA-authorised EMIs or Singapore MAS Major Payment Institutions) cannot open a true domestic TWD settlement account in Taiwan and are not permitted by the FSC to conduct stored-value or money transmission services for Taiwanese residents absent a local Electronic Payment Institution licence. A Singapore or Hong Kong EMI may only serve as a complementary inbound collection rail where (i) client funds are ultimately swept daily into a Taiwanese tier-1 bank escrow account, (ii) the EMI is contractually engaged as a sub-collection agent of the FSC/TPEx-licensed portal and not as the settlement institution, and (iii) all TWD disbursements to issuers are executed exclusively through a domestic clearing bank. The CBC cross-border pre-screening requirement prohibits the use of offshore EMI rails for capital account transactions such as paid-in capital injection. Overall viability rating: Low for the primary treasury function, Medium for collecting retail investor top-ups in foreign currency.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant routing for an equity capital injection from the Chinese parent company into the Taiwan JV is as follows: (i) obtain the required investment approval from the MOEA Investment Commission (either the Foreign Investor approval or the mainland-funded investment permission under the APR); (ii) execute the capital remittance via SWIFT MT103 from the parent's account, using purpose code 'CAP-INV' (inward direct investment in Taiwan) and a clear narrative referencing the MOEA approval file number and the JV name; (iii) the Taiwan receiving bank will convert or credit TWD to the Foreign Investor Investment Account or the specifically designated Capital Escrow Account and must issue the Foreign Exchange Receipts and Payments Declaration (外匯收支或交易申報書) to the CBC; (iv) remittances exceeding TWD 500,000 must be flagged as 'Direct Investment' with the MOEA approval letter attached as documentary support; and (v) all crowdfunding escrow receipts must be deposited separately into an investor trust account and never commingled with the corporate operational account; each individual investor remittance is declared under the purpose code for securities offering proceeds rather than the direct investment code. Within 15 days of receipt, the JV must obtain a CPA capital verification report and file the paid-in capital registration with the Ministry of Economic Affairs Company Registration Office.Outbound treasury disbursements shall be executed through the following rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 for all foreign-currency vendor payments, service fees, and parent-company recharges, with a corresponding Foreign Exchange Outward Declaration (匯出匯款申報) and underlying commercial invoice; (ii) Taiwan's domestic interbank clearing system (全國連線電腦系統 ACH) for TWD-denominated disbursements, including escrow release payments to issuing companies and payroll; (iii) a confirmed at-sight documentary commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) for technology licence fees or hardware procurement from mainland Chinese suppliers, with MT754/MT756 settlement advices; (iv) escrow disbursements to issuers only upon the TPEx/FSC release conditions being met and with dual approval from the escrow bank and the portal's compliance officer; and (v) shareholder dividend remittances via SWIFT MT103 using purpose code 'DIV' accompanied by audited financial statements, board resolution, tax withholding slips, and proof of the 10% withholding settlement. Every outbound remittance above TWD 500,000 must be submitted to the CBC's online foreign-exchange declaration system by the remitting authorized bank.Onshore deliverable USD/TWD forward contracts with an authorized FX bank, permitted only with a documented underlying trade, direct investment, service, or dividend exposure; the tenor is limited to a maximum of 12 months and is extendable to 18 months upon CBC consultation; no speculative or naked positions are allowed.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCIRS) used solely to convert USD-denominated shareholder loans or capital-account obligations into TWD floating-rate liabilities, subject to CBC reporting and audited real-exposure documentation.Plain-vanilla USD/TWD call and put options purchased at an authorized local bank for hedge-only purposes, with a cap on premium and a policy prohibition against leveraged or barrier structures.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are expressly prohibited for onshore Taiwan residents by CBC policy; offshore TWD NDFs may not be booked as a liability of the Taiwan JV. The Chinese parent may manage residual currency risk using NDFs outside Taiwan only where such transactions are not guaranteed, collateralised, or booked by the Taiwan entity, and are supported by a standalone parent treasury policy.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: New Taiwan Dollars (TWD / NT$): Master File documentation is mandatory where annual revenue exceeds NT$300 million. Local File / transfer pricing report is mandatory where annual controlled cross-border related-party transactions exceed NT$50 million or annual controlled domestic related-party transactions exceed NT$100 million. Additional country-by-country reporting thresholds apply to multinational groups with consolidated revenue above the statutory limit.

Economic substance scope: Taiwan does not maintain separate Economic Substance Regulations for ordinary onshore companies, but the Income Tax Act Article 12-6 and Article 43-1 permit tax authorities to disregard or recharacterize transactions or entities that lack commercial substance and business purpose. An equity crowdfunding JV earning Taiwan-source revenue must demonstrate in Taiwan a substantive office, local management, licensed operational staff, board decision-making, escrow oversight, and genuine monitoring of listed startup offerings; otherwise the tax administration may reattribute income to the actual non-resident beneficiaries and deny treaty or cross-strait tax relief.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Underreporting

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of omitted income plus a penalty of 1 to 3 times the underreported tax under Article 110 of the Income Tax Act. Criminal tax evasion under Article 41 of the Tax Collection Act carries imprisonment for up to 5 years and a fine of up to NT$600,000.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors and executives who sign fraudulent returns or direct concealment are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall derived from the fraud, face criminal imprisonment, travel bans pending resolution of national tax arrears, and civil restitution of any dividends paid out of misstated profits.
Shell Company / Abuse of Corporate Form / DTA Shopping

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may disregard the corporate form, recharacterize the income as attributable to the actual beneficial owner, impose full statutory 21% dividend withholding at source, and assess a penalty of up to 3 times the underwithheld tax. Article 43-1 of the Income Tax Act authorizes recharacterization of artificial arrangements.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors and de facto controllers face personal liability for the withheld tax, civil restitution of misappropriated funds, travel bans, and prosecution for fraudulent misrepresentation if the shell structure is used to evade withholding taxes.
AML/CFT and Unlicensed Crowdfunding Platform Operation

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to NT$5 million, suspension or revocation of the FSC license, and, under the Money Laundering Control Act, criminal money laundering penalties of up to 7 years' imprisonment and a fine of up to NT$5 million for moving illicit funds through the crowdfunding portal.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors and AML compliance officers suffer personal criminal liability for knowingly enabling money laundering; personal assets may be frozen, travel bans imposed, and civil damages awarded to defrauded investors.
Late or Non-Filing of Annual Corporate Income Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Late filing penalty of up to 10% of assessed tax, daily interest from the original due date, and if the return is not filed after statutory notice, estimated assessment plus an additional penalty of up to 20% of the tax due.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: the responsible director may be held personally liable for tax arrears if corporate filing failure is part of a scheme to strip corporate assets, and may be prohibited from leaving Taiwan until the outstanding tax and surcharges are resolved.
